Best time to surf La Santa

The best time to surf La Santa is February, March, December. The season runs all year, cleanest Dec to Dec. In December the water sits around 20°C, so pack a 2 mm shorty, and the typical wave is 1.5 m. The nearest airport is ACE (19 km) and the nearest town is Arrecife (20 km).
