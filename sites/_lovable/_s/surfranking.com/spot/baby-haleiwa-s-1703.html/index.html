Best time to surf Baby Haleiwa's

The best time to surf Baby Haleiwa's is January, November, December. The season runs all year, cleanest Dec to Jan. In January the water sits around 25°C, so pack boardshorts, and the typical wave is 1.4 m. The nearest airport is HNL (9 km) and the nearest town is Honolulu (2 km).
