Best time to surf Itauna

The best time to surf Itauna is March, May, June. The season runs all year, cleanest Mar to Mar. In May the water sits around 24°C, so pack a rash vest or 2 mm, and the typical wave is 1.7 m. The nearest airport is CFB (42 km) and the nearest town is Cabo Frio (45 km).
