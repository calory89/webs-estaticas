Best time to surf Telescopes

The best time to surf Telescopes is July, August, September. The season runs all year, cleanest Feb to Oct. In July the water sits around 30°C, so pack boardshorts, and the typical wave is 1.4 m. The nearest airport is RKI (18 km) and the nearest town is Padang (153 km).

What is under Telescopes

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor sits over 57 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 78 m against 57 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.

Modelled bathymetry: NOAA ETOPO 2022 global relief at 15 arc-seconds (about 460 m per cell), sampled along this spot’s swell window. At that scale the model represents broad passes and channels; it cannot see a reef ledge, so nothing here is said about how the wave breaks.
