Best time to surf Bill's Bowls

The best time to surf Bill's Bowls is January, November, December. The season runs all year, cleanest Oct to Mar. In November the water sits around 12°C, so pack a 5/4 with boots and hood, and the typical wave is 1.9 m. The nearest airport is STS (40 km) and the nearest town is Santa Rosa (50 km).

What is under Bill's Bowls

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is still shallow, 13 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 20 m against 13 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
