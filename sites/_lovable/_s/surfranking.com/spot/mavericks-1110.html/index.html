Best time to surf Mavericks

The best time to surf Mavericks is January, October, November. The season runs all year, cleanest Sep to Mar. In January the water sits around 14°C, so pack a 4/3 with boots, and the typical wave is 1.9 m. The nearest airport is SFO (18 km) and the nearest town is San Mateo (18 km).
