Best time to surf Pico Alto

The best time to surf Pico Alto is February, March, April. The season runs all year. In March the water sits around 20°C, so pack a 2 mm shorty, and the typical wave is 1.4 m. The nearest airport is LIM (46 km) and the nearest town is Chilca (23 km).

What is under Pico Alto

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is still shallow, 6 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 12 m against 6 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
