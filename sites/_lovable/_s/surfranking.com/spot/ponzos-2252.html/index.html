Best time to surf Ponzos

The best time to surf Ponzos is January, February, December. The season runs all year, cleanest Dec to Feb. In February the water sits around 13°C, so pack a 5/4 with boots and hood, and the typical wave is 3.0 m. The nearest airport is LCG (30 km) and the nearest town is La Coruña (28 km).

What is under Ponzos

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor falls away into open ocean, 536 m, deep right up to the door, so swell arrives with its energy intact.
