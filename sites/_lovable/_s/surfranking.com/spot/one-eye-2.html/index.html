Best time to surf One eye

The best time to surf One eye is May, August, October. The season runs all year. In August the water sits around 24°C, so pack a rash vest or 2 mm, and the typical wave is 2.4 m. The nearest airport is MRU (39 km) and the nearest town is Curepipe (26 km).
