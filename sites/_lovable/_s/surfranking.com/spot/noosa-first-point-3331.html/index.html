Best time to surf Noosa first point

The best time to surf Noosa first point is February, June, July. The season runs all year, but rarely clean. In July the water sits around 21°C, so pack a 2 mm shorty, and the typical wave is 0.9 m. The nearest airport is MCY (23 km) and the nearest town is Sunshine Coast (33 km).

What is under Noosa first point

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is still shallow, 20 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 28 m against 20 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
