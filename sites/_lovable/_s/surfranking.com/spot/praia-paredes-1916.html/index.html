Best time to surf Praia Paredes

The best time to surf Praia Paredes is January, March, November. The season runs all year, cleanest Jan to Jan. In March the water sits around 14°C, so pack a 4/3 with boots, and the typical wave is 2.6 m. The nearest airport is LIS (102 km) and the nearest town is Leiria (21 km).
