Best time to surf Ghost tree

The best time to surf Ghost tree is January, November, December. The season runs all year, cleanest Sep to Mar. In November the water sits around 14°C, so pack a 4/3 with boots, and the typical wave is 1.6 m. The nearest airport is MRY (10 km) and the nearest town is Monterey (7 km).
