Best time to surf El Confital

The best time to surf El Confital is February, November, December. The season runs all year, cleanest Dec to Dec. In December the water sits around 20°C, so pack a 2 mm shorty, and the typical wave is 1.2 m. The nearest airport is LPA (26 km) and the nearest town is Las Palmas (6 km).
