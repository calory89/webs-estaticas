Best time to surf Grindavik Lighthouse

The best time to surf Grindavik Lighthouse is January, March, November. The season runs all year, but rarely clean. In January the water sits around 8°C, so pack a 6/5 with boots, gloves and hood, and the typical wave is 2.6 m. The nearest airport is KEF (21 km) and the nearest town is Keflavík (23 km).
