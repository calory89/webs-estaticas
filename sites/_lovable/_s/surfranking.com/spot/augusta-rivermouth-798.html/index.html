Best time to surf Augusta Rivermouth

The best time to surf Augusta Rivermouth is May, August, October. The season runs all year, cleanest Mar to Oct. In August the water sits around 19°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 3.2 m. The nearest airport is BQB (74 km) and the nearest town is Busselton (76 km).

What is under Augusta Rivermouth

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is still shallow, 20 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 69 m against 20 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
