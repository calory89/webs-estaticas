Best time to surf Rotto Box

The best time to surf Rotto Box is March, April, May. The season runs all year, cleanest Jan to Jan. In April the water sits around 22°C, so pack a 2 mm shorty, and the typical wave is 2.2 m. The nearest airport is PER (50 km) and the nearest town is Perth (38 km).

What is under Rotto Box

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor drops to 92 m, shoaling steadily over the last kilometres. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 560 m against 92 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
