Best time to surf Brandon Bay

The best time to surf Brandon Bay is January, February, March. The season runs all year, cleanest Jan to Mar. In March the water sits around 10°C, so pack a 6/5 with boots, gloves and hood, and the typical wave is 2.3 m. The nearest airport is KIR (37 km) and the nearest town is Tralee (22 km).

What is under Brandon Bay

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor sits over 45 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 77 m against 45 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
