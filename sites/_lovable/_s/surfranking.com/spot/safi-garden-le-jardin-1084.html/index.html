What is under Safi - Garden (Le Jardin)

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor drops to 161 m, shoaling steadily over the last kilometres. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 310 m against 161 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
