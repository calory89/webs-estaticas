Best time to surf La Buraca

The best time to surf La Buraca is February, March, April. The season runs all year, cleanest Oct to Apr. In February the water sits around 12°C, so pack a 5/4 with boots and hood, and the typical wave is 2.8 m. The nearest airport is VGO (16 km) and the nearest town is Vigo (8 km).

What is under La Buraca

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is already 300 m deep, deep right up to the door, so swell arrives with its energy intact. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 1212 m against 300 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.

Modelled bathymetry: NOAA ETOPO 2022 global relief at 15 arc-seconds (about 460 m per cell), sampled along this spot’s swell window. At that scale the model represents broad passes and channels; it cannot see a reef ledge, so nothing here is said about how the wave breaks.
