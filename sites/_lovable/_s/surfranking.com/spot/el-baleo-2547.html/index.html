Best time to surf El Baleo

The best time to surf El Baleo is February, April, December. The season runs all year, but rarely clean. In February the water sits around 13°C, so pack a 5/4 with boots and hood, and the typical wave is 3.1 m. The nearest airport is LCG (44 km) and the nearest town is La Coruña (44 km).

What is under El Baleo

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor drops to 141 m, shoaling steadily over the last kilometres. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 2250 m against 141 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
