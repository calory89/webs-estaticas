Best time to surf Praia do Norte

The best time to surf Praia do Norte is January, March, October. The season runs all year, but rarely clean. In March the water sits around 15°C, so pack a 4/3 with boots, and the typical wave is 2.7 m. The nearest airport is TER (2 km) and the nearest town is Angra do Heroísmo (18 km).
