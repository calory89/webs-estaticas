Best time to surf Deadman's

The best time to surf Deadman's is February, April, November. The season runs all year, cleanest Jul to Jul. In April the water sits around 12°C, so pack a 5/4 with boots and hood, and the typical wave is 1.7 m. The nearest airport is ACV (106 km) and the nearest town is Mendocino (83 km).

What is under Deadman's

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor sits over 55 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 189 m against 55 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
