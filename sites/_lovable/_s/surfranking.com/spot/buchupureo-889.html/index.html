Best time to surf Buchupureo

The best time to surf Buchupureo is February, May, July. The season runs all year, cleanest Feb to Sep. In May the water sits around 12°C, so pack a 5/4 with boots and hood, and the typical wave is 1.9 m. The nearest airport is CCP (81 km) and the nearest town is Quirihue (33 km).

What is under Buchupureo

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor falls away into open ocean, 525 m, deep right up to the door, so swell arrives with its energy intact.
