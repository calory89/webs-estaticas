Best time to surf Cave Rock

The best time to surf Cave Rock is May, June, July. The season runs all year, but rarely clean. In July the water sits around 22°C, so pack a rash vest or 2 mm, and the typical wave is 1.6 m. The nearest airport is DUR (37 km) and the nearest town is Durban (8 km).

What is under Cave Rock

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is still shallow, 13 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 22 m against 13 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
