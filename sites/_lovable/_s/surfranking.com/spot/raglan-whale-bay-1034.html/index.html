Best time to surf Raglan - Whale Bay

The best time to surf Raglan - Whale Bay is January, May, November. The season runs all year, cleanest Jan to Feb. In January the water sits around 21°C, so pack a 2 mm shorty, and the typical wave is 1.5 m. The nearest airport is HLZ (47 km) and the nearest town is Hamilton (43 km).

What is under Raglan - Whale Bay

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor falls away into open ocean, 1,201 m, deep right up to the door, so swell arrives with its energy intact. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 1764 m against 1201 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
