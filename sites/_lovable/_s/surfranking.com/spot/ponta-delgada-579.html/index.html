Best time to surf Ponta Delgada

The best time to surf Ponta Delgada is January, February, March. The season runs all year, cleanest Oct to Apr. In January the water sits around 18°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 2.3 m. The nearest airport is FNC (25 km) and the nearest town is Funchal (23 km).

What is under Ponta Delgada

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor sits over 52 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 72 m against 52 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
