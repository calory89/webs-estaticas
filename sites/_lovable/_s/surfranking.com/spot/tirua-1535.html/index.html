Best time to surf Tirua

The best time to surf Tirua is May, July, September. The season runs all year, cleanest Mar to Jul. In September the water sits around 12°C, so pack a 5/4 with boots and hood, and the typical wave is 2.1 m. The nearest airport is ZCO (99 km) and the nearest town is Carahue (51 km).

What is under Tirua

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is already 262 m deep, deep right up to the door, so swell arrives with its energy intact. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 666 m against 262 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.

Modelled bathymetry: NOAA ETOPO 2022 global relief at 15 arc-seconds (about 460 m per cell), sampled along this spot’s swell window. At that scale the model represents broad passes and channels; it cannot see a reef ledge, so nothing here is said about how the wave breaks.
