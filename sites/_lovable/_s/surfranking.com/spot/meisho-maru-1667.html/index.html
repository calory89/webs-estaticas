Best time to surf Meisho Maru

The best time to surf Meisho Maru is April, June, August. The season runs all year, cleanest Apr to Apr. In June the water sits around 16°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 2.7 m. The nearest airport is CPT (158 km) and the nearest town is Bredasdorp (34 km).

What is under Meisho Maru

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor drops to 78 m, shoaling steadily over the last kilometres. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 107 m against 78 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
