Best time to surf Dungeons

The best time to surf Dungeons is May, June, August. The season runs all year, cleanest May to Jun. In May the water sits around 15°C, so pack a 4/3 with boots, and the typical wave is 2.5 m. The nearest airport is CPT (27 km) and the nearest town is Cape Town (19 km).
