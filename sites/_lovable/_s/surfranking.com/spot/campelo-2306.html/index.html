Best time to surf Campelo

The best time to surf Campelo is February, March, April. The season runs all year, cleanest Mar to Apr. In March the water sits around 13°C, so pack a 5/4 with boots and hood, and the typical wave is 2.6 m. The nearest airport is LCG (34 km) and the nearest town is La Coruña (33 km).
