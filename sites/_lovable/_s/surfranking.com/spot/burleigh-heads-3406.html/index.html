Best time to surf Burleigh heads

The best time to surf Burleigh heads is May, July, August. The season runs all year, but rarely clean. In July the water sits around 21°C, so pack a 2 mm shorty, and the typical wave is 0.9 m. The nearest airport is OOL (10 km) and the nearest town is Gold Coast (1 km).

What is under Burleigh heads

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor drops to 131 m, shoaling steadily over the last kilometres. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 188 m against 131 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
