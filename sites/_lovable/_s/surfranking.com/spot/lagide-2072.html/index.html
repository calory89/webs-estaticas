Best time to surf Lagide

The best time to surf Lagide is February, April, December. The season runs all year, cleanest Feb to Feb. In February the water sits around 14°C, so pack a 4/3 with boots, and the typical wave is 2.8 m. The nearest airport is LIS (68 km) and the nearest town is Santarém (58 km).

What is under Lagide

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is already 474 m deep, deep right up to the door, so swell arrives with its energy intact. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 786 m against 474 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.

Modelled bathymetry: NOAA ETOPO 2022 global relief at 15 arc-seconds (about 460 m per cell), sampled along this spot’s swell window. At that scale the model represents broad passes and channels; it cannot see a reef ledge, so nothing here is said about how the wave breaks.
