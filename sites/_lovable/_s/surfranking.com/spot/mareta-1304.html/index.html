Best time to surf Mareta

The best time to surf Mareta is January, March, November. The season runs all year, cleanest Jan to Mar. In November the water sits around 18°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 2.4 m. The nearest airport is PRM (35 km) and the nearest town is Portimão (39 km).
