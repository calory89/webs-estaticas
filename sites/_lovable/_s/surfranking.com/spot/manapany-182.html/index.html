Best time to surf Manapany

The best time to surf Manapany is May, August, October. The season runs all year. In August the water sits around 24°C, so pack a rash vest or 2 mm, and the typical wave is 2.5 m. The nearest airport is ZSE (18 km) and the nearest town is St.-Benoit (40 km).

What is under Manapany

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor sits over 32 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 45 m against 32 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
