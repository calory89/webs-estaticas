Best time to surf Playa Negra

The best time to surf Playa Negra is April, May, June. The season runs all year. In April the water sits around 30°C, so pack boardshorts, and the typical wave is 1.2 m. The nearest airport is NOB (31 km) and the nearest town is Liberia (65 km).

What is under Playa Negra

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor drops to 70 m, shoaling steadily over the last kilometres. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 99 m against 70 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
