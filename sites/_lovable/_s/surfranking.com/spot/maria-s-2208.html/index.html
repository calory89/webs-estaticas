Best time to surf Maria's

The best time to surf Maria's is January, October, December. The season runs all year, but rarely clean. In October the water sits around 30°C, so pack boardshorts, and the typical wave is 1.1 m. The nearest airport is MAZ (17 km) and the nearest town is Mayagüez (22 km).
