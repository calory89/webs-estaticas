Best time to surf Cape Northumberland

The best time to surf Cape Northumberland is April, May, June. The season runs all year, cleanest Apr to Jun. In June the water sits around 16°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 2.6 m. The nearest airport is MGB (37 km) and the nearest town is Mount Gambier (27 km).

What is under Cape Northumberland

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor sits over 36 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 58 m against 36 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
