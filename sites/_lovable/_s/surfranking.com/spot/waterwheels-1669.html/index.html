Best time to surf Waterwheels

The best time to surf Waterwheels is March, April, May. The season runs Aug to Jun, cleanest Apr to May. In April the water sits around 21°C, so pack a 2 mm shorty, and the typical wave is 2.9 m. The nearest airport is BQB (80 km) and the nearest town is Busselton (82 km).

What is under Waterwheels

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is already 356 m deep, deep right up to the door, so swell arrives with its energy intact. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 541 m against 356 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
