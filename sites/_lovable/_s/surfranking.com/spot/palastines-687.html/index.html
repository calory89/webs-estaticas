Best time to surf Palastines

The best time to surf Palastines is February, March, April. The season runs all year, cleanest Nov to May. In March the water sits around 21°C, so pack a 2 mm shorty, and the typical wave is 1.5 m. The nearest airport is BQB (35 km) and the nearest town is Busselton (30 km).
