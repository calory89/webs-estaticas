Best time to surf Lobitos

The best time to surf Lobitos is March, April, May. The season runs all year. In March the water sits around 25°C, so pack a rash vest or 2 mm, and the typical wave is 1.4 m. The nearest airport is TYL (14 km) and the nearest town is Talara (14 km).
