Best time to surf Elliot River

The best time to surf Elliot River is April, May, July. The season runs all year, cleanest Apr to Oct. In July the water sits around 14°C, so pack a 4/3 with boots, and the typical wave is 2.8 m. The nearest airport is AVV (111 km) and the nearest town is Colac (52 km).

What is under Elliot River

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is still shallow, 13 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 22 m against 13 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
