Best time to surf Waenhuiskrans Point

The best time to surf Waenhuiskrans Point is May, June, July. The season runs all year, cleanest May to Aug. In June the water sits around 16°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 2.6 m. The nearest airport is CPT (170 km) and the nearest town is Bredasdorp (26 km).

What is under Waenhuiskrans Point

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor drops to 80 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 110 m against 80 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
