Best time to surf Angourie Lefts

The best time to surf Angourie Lefts is June, July, August. The season runs all year, but rarely clean. In August the water sits around 20°C, so pack a 2 mm shorty, and the typical wave is 1.3 m. The nearest airport is LSY (74 km) and the nearest town is Yamba (7 km).
