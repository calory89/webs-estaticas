Best time to surf Mengkudu

The best time to surf Mengkudu is July, August, September. The season runs all year, cleanest Mar to Dec. In July the water sits around 26°C, so pack boardshorts, and the typical wave is 1.9 m. The nearest airport is WGP (74 km) and the nearest town is Waingapu (74 km).
