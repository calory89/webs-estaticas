Best time to surf Cape Lookout

The best time to surf Cape Lookout is January, April, October. The season runs all year, but rarely clean. In April the water sits around 11°C, so pack a 5/4 with boots and hood, and the typical wave is 2.0 m. The nearest airport is SLE (90 km) and the nearest town is Tillamook (17 km).

What is under Cape Lookout

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is already 186 m deep, shoaling steadily over the last kilometres. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 269 m against 186 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
