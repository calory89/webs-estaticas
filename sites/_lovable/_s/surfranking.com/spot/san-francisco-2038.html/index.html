Best time to surf San Francisco

The best time to surf San Francisco is January, March, November. The season runs all year, cleanest Jan to Jan. In November the water sits around 16°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 2.8 m. The nearest airport is VGO (32 km) and the nearest town is Vigo (25 km).
