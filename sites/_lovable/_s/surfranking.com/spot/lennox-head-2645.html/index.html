Best time to surf Lennox Head

The best time to surf Lennox Head is June, July, August. The season runs all year, but rarely clean. In August the water sits around 21°C, so pack a 2 mm shorty, and the typical wave is 1.3 m. The nearest airport is BNK (5 km) and the nearest town is Ballina (7 km).

What is under Lennox Head

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is still shallow, 21 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 42 m against 21 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
