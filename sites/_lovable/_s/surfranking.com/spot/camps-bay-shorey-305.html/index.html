Best time to surf Camps Bay shorey

The best time to surf Camps Bay shorey is April, May, August. The season runs all year, cleanest Jan to Feb. In April the water sits around 17°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 2.2 m. The nearest airport is CPT (21 km) and the nearest town is Cape Town (6 km).

What is under Camps Bay shorey

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is still shallow, 9 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 13 m against 9 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
