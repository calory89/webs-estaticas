What we are

Not a hospital. Not a spa.
A medical community with a method.

La Clinique Swiss brings together physicians, residents and final-year medical students from Switzerland around preventive medicine done properly: measured every year, acted on with the least intervention that works, and repeated for decades.

Measure

Thirty-one markers, taken the same way every year, so that small changes are seen early. The value is in the series, not the snapshot.

Act

A written plan for the year ahead, read with you by a physician. The least intervention that works, and nothing sold to you on the way out.

Repeat

Once a year, every year, in the same place, with the same team. Seventy years is the horizon we design for, because that is how long the series has to be to mean something.

Said plainly
  • We are not a hospital and we do not treat illness.
  • We are not a spa, and we are not a supplement brand.
  • We do not sell property. Our partner residences do, on their own terms.
  • We do not promise years of life. We promise the measurement, the plan, and the people.
Why we exist

Medicine knows how to add good years.
It is just not organised to do it.

I trained and worked alongside physicians who could see, in a set of numbers, what a person's next twenty years would look like — and who had eleven minutes and no mandate to do anything about it. Prevention is the part of medicine with the best evidence and the worst business model. Nobody pays for the illness that did not happen.

So we built the thing the other way round. A community of Swiss and Swiss-trained physicians, who measure the same people the same way every year, for decades, at a few residences chosen for the purpose. Not a clinic, because a clinic waits for you to be ill. Not a spa, because a spa does not measure. A method that repeats, and the people who hold it to that.

Switzerland, because that is where the training is and where the standard comes from. A community, because a method only survives if a group of physicians owns it, reviews it and is willing to put their names to it. If you are one of them, the application is below.

— The founder

Where the method lives

We do not build residences.
We choose them.

For medical students: the fellowship

Final-year and penultimate-year students at a Swiss faculty join as fellows: a supervised rotation at a partner residence, a research role in the community's longitudinal data with authorship for those who work on it, and a place in the community that continues after graduation.

Questions physicians ask
Is this a job?

No. It is a rotation of one or more weeks a year, around your practice at home, under a separate agreement with the residence that hosts you. Travel and stay are covered by the residence; the terms of the rotation are set out in writing before you commit to anything.

What does the residence cover?

Travel, accommodation and board during your rotation, and the clinical room, equipment and staff you work with. The detail is in the letter of intent and in the residence's agreement.

Do I need Swiss licensing to practise at a residence abroad?

You remain subject to the professional regulation of the jurisdictions where you are licensed. Each partner residence holds the local permissions for the clinical activity on its premises; we confirm them in writing before any rotation.

Who owns the data?

The members own their data and consent to its use. The community holds the longitudinal series under a data agreement; physicians and fellows who work on it have authorship on what is published.

Can I apply from outside Switzerland?

Yes, if you trained at a Swiss faculty or hold Swiss specialist training. The 80% commitment is about training, not residence.

What if I am a student?

Apply as a fellow. The review is the same; the rotation is supervised and the research role is real.

Questions

Questions future members ask.

Are you a clinic?

We are a medical community with a method, not a hospital or a curative clinic. We measure, we plan, we follow you year after year. We do not treat illness; your own physician does, and we work alongside them.

Where are the residences?

In warm places, chosen one at a time. Each partner residence is announced to the waiting list first, with its own terms and price. The medicine is the same everywhere.

Who are the physicians?

More than 80% are Swiss or trained at the Swiss faculties. Every clinician is named, with credentials and institution, in the members' dossier and here before the founding cohort closes.

How do I become a member?

Through a partner residence: you join the residence of your choice and bring your membership of the community with you. If you are not yet with one, join the waiting list and you will hear first.

Is this an investment?

No. We do not sell property, shares or financial products, and neither we nor our partner residences promise any return or resale value. A residence sells a right of use on its own terms; we provide the medicine.

What does a week include?

A full assessment of thirty-one markers, body composition and capacity testing, movement, heat and cold, real food, structured sleep, and a written plan for the year read with your physician. The same shape, every year.

How do physicians work with you?

On a rotation of one or more weeks a year at a partner residence, around their practice at home, with their own members to follow over decades. Applications are reviewed monthly.

Can medical students join?

Yes, as fellows: a supervised rotation, a research role in the community's data, and a place in the community from the first year.

Which languages?

English, German, French, Italian and Spanish, in consultations and in correspondence.

What happens between visits?

The written plan, a quarterly video check-in with your physician, and next year's comparison. Anything that needs local investigation is referred to your own doctor, with a letter from us.

Medical questions
I have a chronic condition. Can I still become a member?

In most cases, yes. Hypertension, treated diabetes, controlled cardiac disease, autoimmune conditions and cancer in remission are all common among the people we expect to serve. We are not a hospital and we do not take over your care: your own physician remains responsible for treating your condition, and we work alongside them. Suitability is assessed individually at application, and where the residence is not an appropriate setting we will say so before you pay anything beyond the reservation.

Where is the nearest hospital, and what happens in an emergency?

Every partner residence is accepted into the network only with a written evacuation protocol and a referral hospital within a defined transfer time. Each residence keeps a clinical room for stabilisation, staffed during your stay. The specifics — the referral hospital, transfer times and route, equipment held, staffing hours, transfer partners and insurance requirements — are set out in that residence's dossier rather than summarised here.

What should I do about my medication?

Bring everything you take, in its original packaging, with a current prescription list. The customs rules of the residence's country apply to certain drug classes; each residence's dossier sets out which ones need documentation. Your physician reviews your full list at intake — including supplements — and will write to your own doctor if anything warrants it, but we do not alter prescriptions issued elsewhere without their involvement.

What about vaccinations and travel insurance?

Vaccination advice depends on the residence's country; check with a travel clinic four to six weeks before you travel. Comprehensive travel and medical insurance, including repatriation cover, is a condition of membership at every partner residence. The minimum cover required is set out in each residence's dossier so that you can check your existing policy rather than buy a new one.
