Why the factory price lies

A $6,900 cabin can cost more than an $8,500 one.

One unit per container instead of two doubles your freight. Powder coat instead of hot-dip galvanizing rusts by the sea. Your country's duty and VAT can add a third. Our calculator puts every unit on the same footing: delivered and installed where you are, per m².

Our story

Built while buying 300 beachfront cabins

We started TheGlampings while choosing the cabin supplier for Eco Beach City, a beachfront community in Sumba, Indonesia. We asked more than 100 factories for quotes, compared specs line by line with engineers, and learned how different the real landed price is from the brochure. This site is that spreadsheet, made public and kept up to date.
