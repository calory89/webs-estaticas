Terms & refunds

These terms apply to the Best Sales Closers Annual Retreat ("the Retreat"). Payments are processed by Eco Beach City LTD (United Kingdom). Contact: carlos@ecobeachcity.com.

What you pay for

€1,500 per person covers 5 days of training and selection, accommodation at Eco Beach City (Sumba, Indonesia) and buffet meals during the Retreat. Flights, visas, travel insurance, transfers not stated and personal expenses are not included.

Application and acceptance

Paying locks your seat while we review your application. If we do not accept your application, we refund 100% of your payment to the original payment method.

Dates

The Retreat runs once a year. Exact dates are sent by email to accepted applicants.

Cancellations by you

Write to us by email. Any refund for cancellations after acceptance is assessed case by case and depends on how close the request is to the Retreat date.

Selection

Being selected for the Best Sales Closers roster after the Retreat is based on performance and is not guaranteed by attending.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for your own travel documents, health and travel insurance, and for behaving respectfully toward other participants and staff.
