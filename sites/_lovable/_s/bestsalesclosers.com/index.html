Questions

How does the €30,000 guarantee work?

After the retreat you get offered closing positions on high-ticket deals through Best Sales Closers. If you attend the full retreat, accept and actively work the positions we offer and follow the system, and you still don't earn €30,000 in commissions within 12 months, we refund the full €1,500. The exact conditions are written in your contract, which you receive when you're accepted.

Is this legit?

Payments run through Stripe and are processed by Eco Beach City LTD, a UK company (no. 15790012). You pay €100 to apply and nothing else unless you're accepted. The guarantee is in a signed contract, not just on this page.

Why €100 to apply?

It keeps out people who aren't serious, so every real applicant gets a proper review. Not accepted = €100 refunded in full. Accepted = it counts toward your €1,500.

What offers will I sell?

High-ticket offers from companies that work with Best Sales Closers. We match you on Day 3 based on your profile and performance during the retreat.

Is this a job?

You're trained and then offered commission-based closing positions. You earn commissions on what you close.

What's included?

3 days of live training, real call practice, placement on high-ticket offers and the €30,000 guarantee. In-person seats also include accommodation and all meals at Eco Beach City. Flights, visas and personal expenses aren't included.

Online or in person — which should I pick?

Same price, same training, same guarantee. You choose after you're accepted. In-person seats in Sumba are limited and go first come, first served.

What level of English do I need?

The retreat runs in English. You need to be comfortable holding a sales conversation in English.

Do I need a visa for Indonesia?

Many nationalities can enter Indonesia with a visa on arrival or e-visa. Check the official rules for your passport before you book your flight.

When is it?

Once a year. Accepted candidates receive the dates, the contract and the guarantee by email, with enough time to book flights.
