Questions

How does the €30,000 guarantee work?

After the retreat, you get offered closing positions on high-ticket deals through Best Sales Closers. If you accept and actively work the positions we offer and follow the system, and you still don't earn €30,000 in commissions within 12 months of the retreat, we refund the full €1,500. The exact conditions are written in your contract, which you receive when you're accepted.

Why €100 to apply?

It keeps out people who aren't serious, so we can give every real applicant a proper review. If we don't accept you, we refund the €100 in full. If we do, it counts toward your €1,500.

What's included?

3 days of live training, real call practice, placement on high-ticket offers and the €30,000 guarantee. In-person seats also include accommodation and all meals at Eco Beach City. Flights and personal expenses aren't included.

Online or in person — which should I pick?

Same price, same training, same guarantee. You choose after you're accepted. In-person seats in Sumba are limited, so they go first come, first served.

When is it?

Once a year. Accepted candidates receive the dates, the contract and the guarantee by email, with enough time to book flights.

Where is Sumba and how do I get there?

Sumba is an island in Indonesia, about an hour's flight from Bali. Our travel partner Happy Low Cost helps you find the cheapest flights.

Is this a job?

You're trained and then offered commission-based closing positions on high-ticket deals. You earn commissions on what you close.
