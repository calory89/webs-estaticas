Questions

Everything buyers ask us.

Do I really own the home?

Yes. Legal title in your name to the studio and its 240 m² of land — 100% transferable. Live in it, rent it, gift it or sell it. The passport of free nights is a benefit of ownership, not the product.

Is this a timeshare?

No. A timeshare sells you weeks of someone else's property. Here you own real property — and the growing network of clubs rewards you with free stays on top.

How do the free nights work?

Each new destination the Club opens, owners unlock free nights there every year. Founders keep the largest allowance, for life. Sumba is open today; new destinations join the roadmap as the Club grows.

How do I pay?

In instalments, with no interest: a small reservation first, then staged payments as we build. The exact schedule is in the dossier.

Who rents my home when I'm away?

Our on-site team manages guests, cleaning and maintenance. You keep 75% of rental income — estimated around €100 per night for the studio.

Can I visit before deciding?

Yes. The Club is open and operating. Book a call and we'll arrange your visit — or join a live video tour from the island.
