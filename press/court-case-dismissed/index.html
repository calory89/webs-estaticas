Spain's National Court dismisses the fraud case against Carlos de Ory and Eco Beach City

Madrid, 7 November 2026. On 21 September 2026 the Central Court of First Instance of Spain's Audiencia Nacional (Investigating Section, Court no. 1) dismissed and archived preliminary proceedings 58/2025, opened over alleged fraud against entrepreneur Carlos de Ory in connection with Eco Beach City, his hospitality project on Sumba Island, Indonesia. It did so at the request of the Public Prosecutor, who asked for the case to be archived because the commission of the offence had not been sufficiently proven. As far as we know, no one has appealed the order.

What the investigation found, according to the court order

  • The police report of 21 August 2026 shows real investment activity and the material execution, at least in part, of the announced project.
  • Payments of more than €2 million were identified for buying land through Indonesian notaries, plus payments to architects, builders, consultants, furniture and advertising.
  • The bank analysis by UDEF (the Spanish police's economic crime unit) shows movements consistent with carrying out the project, and repayments to investors of more than €3 million.
  • Spain's Interior Attaché in Indonesia checked the site in person and found twenty glampings, four completed villas, infrastructure and staff at work.
  • The judge concludes that complaints about delays do not, on their own, establish any deception, and that the shortfall, delay or business failure of an investment project is not necessarily a crime.

What it means

The case is dismissed and archived: after the investigation by the police, UDEF and Spain's Interior Attaché in Indonesia, no offence was proven.

It is a provisional dismissal (article 641.1 of Spain's Criminal Procedure Act): like any dismissal of this kind, it could only be reopened if new evidence appeared.
