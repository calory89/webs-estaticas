Bio

One line

Carlos de Ory is a Spanish founder who has been building companies since 2013 and now builds them from Sumba, an island one hour from Bali.

Short (about 60 words)

Carlos de Ory has been building companies since 2013. He founded Happy Low Cost, a cheap-flights community through which more than 300,000 flights have been booked, and StartupsMansion, a house for founders in New York covered by Antena 3. In 2022 Forbes España named him one of the 100 most creative people in business. He speaks in English and Spanish.

Long (about 150 words)

Carlos de Ory has been building companies since 2013 and is a dad. In 2014 he launched Happy Low Cost, a community that finds cheap flights before they disappear: more than 300,000 flights have been booked through its deals and its communities passed 420,000 followers. He went on to live and build with other founders in New York (StartupsMansion, covered by Antena 3), Berlin, Miami and Bali. In 2022 Forbes España included him in its list of the 100 most creative people in business. Today he lives between Europe and Sumba, where he is building Eco Beach City, a town by the sea with coworking and solar power, and runs AI products such as RealHunt and Jobersity under Equinox Investment Group LLC. He is writing a book, episode by episode, about building companies and a life you choose.
