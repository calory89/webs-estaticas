December in Dublin has a special charm: lit-up streets, pubs with live music and that festive buzz that's hard to resist. We've just found a return flight from Barcelona for €39, non-stop, with Ryanair.

The dates we spotted are 10 December 2026 for the outbound leg and 11 December for the return, so it's a compact weekend break. Compared with the usual price of around €90, that's a saving of close to 57%.

Keep in mind that airfares change constantly and this one could rise within hours. If it appeals to you, don't wait too long: check availability and the current price using the button below, which is where the offer is confirmed.

If you arrive early, we'd recommend visiting Trinity College to see the Book of Kells, wandering around Temple Bar and stopping by the Guinness Storehouse. If you'd prefer something more relaxed, a walk out to Howth on the north coast is well worth it.
