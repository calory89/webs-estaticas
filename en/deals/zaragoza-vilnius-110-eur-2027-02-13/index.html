Vilnius, the capital of Lithuania, is one of those cities that catch you off guard: a baroque Old Town, brightly coloured churches and a relaxed atmosphere unlike most other European capitals. We have just found a return flight from Zaragoza for €110, 52% less than its usual price of €230.

What to do in Vilnius: wander through the Old Town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and climb Gediminas Hill for good views over the city. Spend an afternoon in the bohemian district of Užupis, with its quirky self-declared "republic" and street murals. And if you have an extra day, head to Trakai Castle, about 30 km away, which looks like something from a fairy tale and is especially beautiful with snow.
