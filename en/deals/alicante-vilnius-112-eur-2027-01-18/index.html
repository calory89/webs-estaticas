Vilnius, the capital of Lithuania, is one of those winter city breaks that catches you off guard. We've found a return flight from Alicante for €112, which is about €58 less than its usual price of around €170, a saving of 34%.

The dates we've spotted are an outbound flight on 18 January 2027 and a return on 23 January, giving you five days to explore the city. The flight is operated by Wizz Air Malta with one stop.

Keep in mind that flight prices change quickly, so it's worth checking soon. Fares can shift at any time, so always confirm the price when you click the button below.

Once you're there, don't miss the Old Town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site with baroque churches and cobbled lanes. Then head across to Užupis, the bohemian district with its own playfully written 'constitution'. And if the weather cooperates, take a trip to Trakai Castle, less than an hour away by car, with its frozen lake and winter scenery.
