If you fancy a trip to Brussels without spending a fortune, this deal is for you. We've found a direct Vueling flight from Valencia to Brussels for €65 return, when the usual price sits around €130. That's a 50% saving.

The dates we've found are 19 to 21 February 2027: leave on Friday and return on Sunday, which makes it a perfect long weekend. Prices can change at any moment, so we'd suggest checking it as soon as you can. Deals like this tend to go quickly, so if it appeals to you, don't leave it until tomorrow.

Once in Brussels, we recommend starting at the Grand Place, one of the most beautiful squares in Europe, then wandering the streets of the city centre. Another must-see is the Atomium, that quirky landmark shaped like an enlarged iron molecule, with great views over the city.

And of course, you can't leave without trying Belgian chocolate and a good abbey beer at one of the terraces or beer halls in the centre. A simple, cheap and very appealing plan for a winter weekend.
