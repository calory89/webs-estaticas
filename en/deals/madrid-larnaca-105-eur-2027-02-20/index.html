Once you arrive, Larnaca has a lovely seafront promenade at Finikoudes, ideal for a drink at sunset. The medieval castle by the harbour is also worth a visit and, if the weather cooperates, a trip to the salt lakes, where flamingos often appear in winter, is a good idea.

If you have a few extra days, Ayia Napa is within a reasonable drive and makes a perfect coastal getaway.
