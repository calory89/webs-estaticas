This deal takes you from Athens to Seoul, the capital of South Korea, for €441 return. The usual price for this route is around €650, so you save 32%. The flight has one stop and is operated by Ethiopian Airlines.

The dates we found are 10 to 15 December 2026, leaving on the 10th and returning on the 15th. That gives you about five days in Seoul, enough to get a good feel for the city, though you'll probably want more.

Prices change often, so check the dates and book when it suits you. The fare may vary, so use the button to see the current offer.

If you make it to Seoul, don't miss Gyeongbokgung Palace, a must-see with its royal guard ceremony. Wander through Bukchon, the neighbourhood of traditional hanok houses, and visit Gwangjang Market to try local food. For views, the N Seoul Tower at sunset is a good choice.
