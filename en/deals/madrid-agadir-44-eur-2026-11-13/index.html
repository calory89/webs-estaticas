This deal takes you to Agadir, on Morocco's Atlantic coast, with a nonstop Ryanair flight from Madrid. The dates we found are 13 to 20 November 2026, at a return price of €44. In the searches we reviewed, the reference price sits around €80, so the saving is substantial.

If you go, Agadir is a great place to unwind. It has a long seafront promenade for an evening stroll and a wide beach that's perfect for relaxing. If you have time for more, the Kasbah of Agadir, with its views over the port and the city, deserves a morning. You can also wander through the local souk, where you'll find spices, handicrafts and plenty of good-humoured bargaining.

And if you fancy getting out of the city, a day trip towards the coastal villages or the Paradis Valley will show you another side of southern Morocco. It's a simple plan, with plenty of scenery and a lovely atmosphere.
