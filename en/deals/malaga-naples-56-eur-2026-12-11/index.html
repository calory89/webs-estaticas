We came across this offer: a return flight from Málaga to Naples with Ryanair for €56, nonstop. This route usually costs around €90, so the saving is roughly 38%.

The dates we found are 11 to 18 December 2026. They make a comfortable escape a week before Christmas, with time to enjoy the city at a slower pace.

Keep in mind that airline prices change constantly and this one could rise at any moment. If you're interested, don't overthink it: prices can change and are confirmed when you click the button, so it's best to check it as soon as you can.

Once in Naples, it's worth getting lost in the historic centre and trying a proper Neapolitan pizza. From there you can take the train to Pompeii and visit its ruins, one of the most famous day trips in the area. And if you have an extra day, the Amalfi Coast is within easy reach and absolutely stunning.
