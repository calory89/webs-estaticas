If you fancy a trip to Sicily without splurging, this deal is for you. From Zaragoza you can fly to Palermo and back from €86, with Wizz Air Malta and one stop along the way. Compared with the €140 it usually costs, the saving is around 39%.

The dates found run from 28 November to 3 December 2026, giving you five nights to explore the city at your own pace. Flight prices change very quickly, though, so if the offer appeals, don't wait too long. Also keep in mind that the price may vary, so it's worth checking the final, up-to-date amount when you click through to see it.

In Palermo, don't miss the Ballarò market with its street food stalls, or the Palatine Chapel, with its Byzantine mosaics that will take your breath away. If you fancy a half-day trip, Monreale Cathedral, a short distance from the centre, is an essential visit.

And of course, try Sicilian cannoli and a good lemon granita from a local granita shop. These small pleasures are what make this trip special.
