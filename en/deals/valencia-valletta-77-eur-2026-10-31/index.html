If you're craving a sunny escape among ancient stone without overspending, this deal is for you: a nonstop Ryanair flight from Valencia to Valletta for €77 return. That's well below the usual price. Our reference searches for this route show fares around €112 to €140, and the normal price for this leg is about €130.

The dates we found are an outbound flight on 31 October 2026 and a return on 11 November 2026, within the October to November window. Prices change constantly, so if this interests you, don't wait too long. Click the button to check the available dates and the current price, which may change by the time you book.

Once there, Valletta is a compact and very photogenic city, ideal for exploring on foot among colourful balconies and cobbled streets. We recommend heading up to the Upper Barrakka Gardens to watch the Grand Harbour at sunset.

If you have an extra day, visit Mdina, the island's old silent capital, and take a trip to the Blue Grotto on the south coast. You can also catch the ferry to Gozo, Malta's sister island, with quiet beaches and villages.
