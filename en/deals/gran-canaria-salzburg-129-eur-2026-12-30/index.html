If you fancy swapping the Canarian sun for a winter fairy tale, we have a great deal for you: a return flight from Gran Canaria to Salzburg for €129, operated by Eurowings and nonstop. Its usual price is around €240, so the saving is almost half.

The dates we found are outbound on 30 December 2026 and return on 6 January 2027. They're ideal for making the most of the Christmas holidays and starting the new year in one of Austria's most beautiful cities.

Keep in mind that flight prices change very quickly and this one could go up at any moment, so we recommend checking the offer soon. The final price is confirmed when you click the button, so what you see there is what it costs at that moment.

Once you're there, don't miss the Old Town of Salzburg, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, with Mozart's birthplace and its charming narrow streets. In December, the Christmas market on Residenzplatz is a must for a warm drink. It's also worth climbing up to Hohensalzburg Fortress for views over the city, or visiting Hellbrunn Palace and its gardens.
