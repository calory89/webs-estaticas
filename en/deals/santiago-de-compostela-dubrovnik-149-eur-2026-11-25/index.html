If you're dreaming of a trip to Croatia without spending a fortune, this deal is for you. We've found a return flight from Santiago de Compostela to Dubrovnik for €149, operated by Iberia with one stop along the way. Based on our data, the usual price sits around €210, so the saving is roughly 29%.

The dates we found are an outbound flight on 25 November 2026 and a return on 1 December 2026. That gives you six days to explore one of the most beautiful cities on the Mediterranean, without the summer crowds.

Keep in mind that flight prices change constantly, so if this appeals to you, don't wait too long. Fares can shift at any moment, so check the current price by clicking the button below, which takes you straight to these dates.

Once you're there, we recommend walking the city walls, which wrap around the entire old town and offer spectacular views over the sea. Then lose yourself in the stone-paved streets of the Stradun, the main thoroughfare, and set aside some time for a boat trip to Lokrum island, just a short distance from the harbour.

If you'd prefer something more relaxed, Cavtat, a coastal village a short way from Dubrovnik, is perfect for a seafront lunch and a slower pace.
