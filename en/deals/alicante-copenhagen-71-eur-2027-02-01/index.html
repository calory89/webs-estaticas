Once you're there, stroll along Nyhavn, the colourful canal that is the city's most famous postcard. Then wander through Christianshavn and its laid-back atmosphere, and finish in a cosy café, because in Copenhagen the winter pastime is settling in with a good cup of coffee and enjoying hygge. And if the weather cooperates, head to the Little Mermaid by the harbour for a classic photo.
