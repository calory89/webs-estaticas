If you're dreaming of a winter break packed with art, great food and beautiful streets, Florence could be your next destination. We've found a return flight from Gran Canaria (LPA) to Florence (FLR) with Volotea for €123, compared with a usual price of around €170, which works out to a saving of roughly 28%.

The dates we found are 23 to 30 January 2027, with one stop on the route. That gives you about seven days to explore the city at a relaxed pace, and winter has the bonus of fewer crowds than spring or summer.

Keep in mind that flight prices change constantly and this one could rise within hours, so if it appeals to you, don't leave it until tomorrow. The exact price and availability are confirmed when you click through to the booking page.

Once you're there, don't miss the dome of the Cathedral of Santa Maria del Fiore, which offers spectacular views from the top. Stroll across the Ponte Vecchio and, if you love art, book a visit to the Uffizi Gallery well in advance. For lunch, head to the San Lorenzo Market or cross the river to the quieter Oltrarno neighbourhood.
