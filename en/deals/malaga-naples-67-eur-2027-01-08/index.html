This deal takes you from Málaga to Naples nonstop with Ryanair for €67 return. That is well below the usual fare on this route, which tends to sit around €110, so it's worth a look.

The dates on offer are 8 January 2027 outbound and 15 January 2027 back. That gives you seven days to explore one of southern Italy's most characterful cities, and you'll be visiting in the quieter low season.

Airfares change constantly, so if this one appeals, don't wait too long. What you see today can shift within hours, and the final price is confirmed when you click through.

Once you're there, start with pizza in its birthplace, for example in the Spaccanapoli district or at a traditional pizzeria. Then take a day trip to Pompeii, which is truly jaw-dropping. And if the weather cooperates, catch a boat to the Amalfi Coast or Capri, both within easy reach.
