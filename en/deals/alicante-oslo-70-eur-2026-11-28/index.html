This time we're heading north: Ryanair has a nonstop flight from Alicante to Oslo for €70 return. The usual price for this route is around €130, so the saving is about 46%.

The dates we found are an outbound flight on 28 November 2026 and a return on 5 December. It's a great getaway for escaping into the early Nordic winter, with short days and plenty of charm.

That said, flight prices change constantly and this one could rise as soon as others notice the deal, so it's worth checking soon. The price is confirmed when you click the button, so whatever you see there is what's available at that moment.

Once you're in Oslo, it's worth strolling along the harbour and the Akershus Fortress, visiting the Vigeland sculpture park, and heading to the Opera House, where you can walk up its roof for views of the fjord. If the weather cooperates, the views from Holmenkollen or a wander through the centre with the December Christmas lights will complete the plan.
