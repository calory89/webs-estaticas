If you'd rather swap the turrón for a Nordic winter, this one is for you: a nonstop Ryanair flight from Málaga to Oslo for €62 return. The usual fare on this route is around €140, so the saving is more than 50%.

The dates we found are an outbound flight on 23 December 2026 and a return on 4 January 2027, which leaves plenty of time for the holidays and an unhurried return to everyday life. Fares move quickly at this time of year, though, so the price may change. Check it as soon as you can if it catches your eye.

Once you're in Oslo, take a walk through the Vigeland sculpture park, visit the Viking Ship Museum on the Bygdøy peninsula, and head down to Aker Brygge by the harbour to see the city lit up for the season.

If you're after something a little more adventurous, ride up to the Holmenkollen ski jump for views over the fjord, or wander the Christmas markets in the centre and warm up with a hot drink and some local food.
