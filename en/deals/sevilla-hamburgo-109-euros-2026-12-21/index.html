Hamburg has a lot to offer in winter. You can stroll through the Speicherstadt, the old warehouse district by the harbour, and visit the Elbphilharmonie to take in the city from its viewing platform. The Christmas markets fill the centre with lights and stalls serving warming food, perfect for the cold days.
