Today we have a bargain for a getaway to one of Italy's most characterful cities. We found Vueling flights from Santiago de Compostela to Naples with one stop, for €90 return. That is a very low price for a long weekend away.

The dates we found are outbound on 16 January 2027 and return on 19 January 2027. The usual price for this route is around €160, so the saving is considerable. Airline fares change often, though, so the price may vary, and you will confirm the final amount when you press the booking button.

Our advice: if the dates suit you, don't overthink it. Deals like this tend to disappear quickly, and anyone who waits a couple of days often finds a higher price.

Once there, make sure you wander the historic centre and its narrow streets, where you can try authentic Neapolitan pizza. With a free day, a trip to Pompeii lets you see the ruins of a Roman city just a short journey from Naples. And if you feel like it, the seafront walk to Castel dell'Ovo makes a perfect end to the afternoon.
