If you're craving a change from Seville's winter chill for the Sardinian sea without spending a fortune, this deal is worth a look. We found a direct Ryanair flight from Seville (SVQ) to Cagliari (CAG) for €83 return. The usual price for this route is around €110, so the saving is roughly 25%.

The dates we found are an outbound flight on 11 December 2026 and a return on 14 December 2026. That makes it a short trip of three nights, ideal for a long weekend or a few days off before Christmas.

Fares change constantly and cheap deals rarely last long, so if this one appeals to you, don't wait around. We suggest checking the price and booking as soon as you can. The fare may vary, so it's worth confirming it when you click through to the airline's site.

Once you're there, Cagliari has plenty to offer in winter. Wander through the historic Castello district, with its old walls and views over the gulf. Head to Poetto beach, which is perfect for a walk by the sea in December. If you enjoy the outdoors, climb to the Sella del Diavolo for panoramic views over the bay.
