This deal takes us to Riga, the capital of Latvia, for €96 return with Ryanair, nonstop from Málaga. That's 40% less than the usual price for this route, so it's worth a look before it disappears.

The dates we found are 4 to 8 February 2027, with departure and return separated by four nights. Keep in mind that prices can change at any moment, so check the fare when you click through and book as soon as you're happy with it.

If you like wandering without a schedule, Riga's Old Town (Vecrīga) is one of the best places to get lost, with cobbled streets and squares full of history. It's also worth seeing the Art Nouveau buildings, which are a real architectural treasure.

To warm up, set aside some time for the Central Market, housed in former zeppelin hangars, and try a traditional café or restaurant. February is cold, but the city has a very special charm in winter.
