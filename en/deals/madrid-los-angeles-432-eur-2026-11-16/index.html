If you've been thinking about crossing the Atlantic to the US West Coast, now could be the time. We've found a Madrid to Los Angeles flight for €432 return, operated by Royal Air Maroc with one stop along the way. That's roughly 30% less than the usual €620.

The dates we found run from 16 to 27 November 2026: outbound on the 16th, return on the 27th. That gives you eleven days to explore Southern California at an easy pace, and November often brings mild temperatures and plenty of clear days.
