Hamburg in December has a special charm. You can wander through Speicherstadt, the warehouse district of red-brick buildings listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and finish the afternoon at the Christmas markets by the town hall.

If you love music, make time for the Elbphilharmonie, the city's most iconic concert hall. For a livelier evening, head to St. Pauli and its famous Reeperbahn. And if you want some fresh air, a walk along the harbour and the Elbe river gives you some of the best views in the city.
