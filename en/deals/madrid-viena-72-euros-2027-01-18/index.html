This deal takes you from Madrid to Vienna for €72 return with Ryanair, on a direct flight. That is about 20% below what this route usually costs, which hovers around €90.

The dates we found are an outbound flight on 18 January 2027 and a return on 20 January 2027, giving you a long weekend to explore the Austrian capital. Prices change very often, so if this interests you, don't leave it for later: check availability as soon as you can. The price is confirmed when you click the button.

Once there, don't miss Schönbrunn Palace and its gardens, which are lovely to stroll through even in winter. Another option is to sit in a classic Viennese café with a slice of Sachertorte and a good coffee, as tradition demands.

If you enjoy music, catch a concert at the State Opera or in one of the city's other venues. And for an unhurried wander, the Naschmarkt and the historic centre are ideal for walking and soaking up the atmosphere.
