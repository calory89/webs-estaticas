If you've ever dreamed of trading Caribbean beaches for Amsterdam's canals and bikes, this deal is worth a look. We found a nonstop return flight from Willemstad (Curaçao) to Amsterdam for €498, operated by TUI fly Netherlands, which saves about 38% compared with the usual price for the route.

The dates we found are an outbound flight on 12 November 2026 and a return on 21 November. That gives you nine days to explore the Dutch capital in autumn, when the city has a special charm and the museums are a little quieter.

A quick warning: flight prices change constantly, and fares on deals like this tend to sell out fast. We'd suggest checking the dates as soon as possible. Prices can change, so confirm the fare using the button before making any decisions.

Once you're there, it's worth getting lost in the Jordaan, with its narrow streets and quiet cafés. You could also visit the Rijksmuseum to see the Dutch Masters, or take a boat trip along the canals at sunset. And if you have time, Haarlem is half an hour away by train, with a historic centre that's lovely for a stroll.
