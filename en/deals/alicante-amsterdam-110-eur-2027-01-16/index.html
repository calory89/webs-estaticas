If you fancy a short break in one of Europe's most beautiful cities, this deal could be for you. We found a return flight from Alicante to Amsterdam with Vueling, nonstop, for €110. The usual price for those dates is around €150, so that's a saving of roughly 27%.

The dates we've found are departing on 16 January 2027 and returning on 18 January 2027. That's just two nights, perfect for a long weekend without clogging up your diary.

Keep in mind that flight prices change quickly, and this one could rise or sell out as soon as someone else spots it, so it's worth looking sooner rather than later. The price is confirmed on the airline's website when you click through, so you'll see the exact figure at that moment.

Once you're there, it's worth wandering the canals of the centre and the Jordaan district, with its cafés and charming shops. You can also visit the Van Gogh Museum or the Rijksmuseum, which are very close to each other. And if you're feeling up for it, hire a bike: it's the most classic way to get around the city.
