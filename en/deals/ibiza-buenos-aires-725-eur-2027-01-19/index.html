If you've been dreaming of tango and Argentine asado, this deal could be the perfect excuse. You can fly from Ibiza to Buenos Aires and back for €725 with Iberia Express, with one stop, which is 33% below its usual price of around €1,090.

The dates we found are 19 to 24 January 2027. In our reference searches we have seen prices between €842 and €1,098, so this offer stands out quite a bit. Keep in mind that flight prices change very quickly, so we recommend not leaving it until tomorrow if you're interested.
