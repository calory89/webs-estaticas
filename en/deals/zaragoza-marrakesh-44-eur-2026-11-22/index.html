Fancy an exotic escape without spending a fortune? This deal is for you. We've found return flights from Zaragoza to Marrakesh for €44 with Ryanair, nonstop, and it's 56% cheaper than the usual price on this route.

The dates we've found are 22 to 27 November 2026, so you'll have five nights to explore the Red City. They're very appealing dates, as November weather in Marrakesh is usually pleasant for strolling around without the summer heat.

Airfares change constantly, so we'd advise not waiting too long. Prices can shift at any time, so check the final fare when you click the booking button before it sells out.

Once there, don't miss Jemaa el-Fnaa square at sunset, with its food stalls and musicians. Wander through the Majorelle Gardens too, a blue oasis that captivates everyone who visits. And if you're feeling adventurous, a day trip to the Ourika Valley lets you see the Atlas Mountains up close.
