Here's a bargain worth knowing about: a direct flight from Seville to Copenhagen with SAS for €117 return. That's well below what this route usually costs, a saving of roughly 31%.

The dates we found are an outbound flight on 29 December 2026 and a return on 11 January 2027, so it's a great option if you want to see out the year in northern Europe and stretch your Christmas holidays.

If you make it to Copenhagen, take a stroll along Nyhavn, the canal lined with brightly coloured houses that is the city's most famous postcard view. Then you could visit Rosenborg Castle, where the Danish crown jewels are kept, or climb the Round Tower for views over the rooftops.

And if the cold doesn't put you off, rent a bike like the locals do and explore the canals. Cosy cafés where you can warm up with a good hot chocolate complete the hygge experience.
