If you fancy a different city break before the year ends, we've found a bargain for you: a nonstop flight from Madrid to Belgrade with Wizz Air from €87 return. It's the kind of deal you don't see every day, with a saving of around 33% on the usual price of about €130.

Once you're there, Belgrade has plenty to offer in a few days. Wander around the Kalemegdan fortress, which overlooks the point where the Sava and Danube rivers meet. Then head to the bohemian Skadarlija quarter for a traditional dinner and a relaxed drink. If you prefer a quieter pace, cross the river to Zemun, with its streets full of Central European atmosphere.
