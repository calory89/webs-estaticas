Warsaw's Old Town was carefully rebuilt after the Second World War. Stroll around the Market Square and along Krakowskie Przedmieście, and enjoy the cafés and restaurants in the area. If you're interested in history, the Warsaw Uprising Museum is a must for understanding what happened in the city.

To unwind, Łazienki Park, with its palace on the water, is lovely even in winter if you dress warmly. For city views, head up to the observation deck of the Palace of Culture and Science. January in Warsaw means real cold, so pack a good coat and enjoy the city like a winter fairy tale.
