We've spotted a deal to escape to Tallinn from Alicante for €135 return. That's around 25% below the usual price of roughly €180, so it's well worth a look.

The dates we found are 24 January 2027 for the outbound flight and 25 January for the return, a one-night trip that suits a short weekend or a spontaneous getaway. The flight is operated by Ryanair and includes one stop along the way.

Flight prices change constantly, so if the offer appeals to you, don't overthink it. We recommend checking the dates and prices the moment you click through, as the fare may vary.

Once you're in Tallinn, it's worth wandering the Old Town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site with medieval streets and towers. You can also climb Toompea Hill for the views or head to Kadriorg Park. And if you enjoy a creative buzz, the Telliskivi district has lively cafés, shops and markets.
