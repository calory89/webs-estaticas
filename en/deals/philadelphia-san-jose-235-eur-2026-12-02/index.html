San José is the heart of Silicon Valley and a very practical base for getting around the region. You can stroll through its historic downtown and visit the Computer History Museum, which is ideal if you're interested in technology.

From here you can also make a trip to Santa Cruz, with its beach, its pier and its famous seaside amusement park. And if you fancy a longer excursion, the Monterey coast and Big Sur will leave you speechless.
