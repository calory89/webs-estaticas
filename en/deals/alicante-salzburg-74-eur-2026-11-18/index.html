If you're craving a different kind of city break before the real cold sets in, this deal is for you. We've found a non-stop Ryanair flight from Alicante to Salzburg for €74 return, when the usual price hovers around €130, which means about 43% off.

The dates we've found are 18 to 25 November 2026. That gives you seven days to explore one of Austria's most beautiful cities, with its historic centre listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site and the pre-Christmas atmosphere just starting to appear.

A quick warning: flight prices change constantly and low fares rarely last long. If you're interested, don't overthink it. The price can vary and is checked at the exact moment you click to book.

Once you're there, don't miss the Hohensalzburg Fortress, which overlooks the city from above and offers spectacular views. Stroll through the Mirabell Gardens, where scenes from The Sound of Music were filmed, and visit Mozart's birthplace in the old town. If you're still hungry for more, the Salzkammergut lakes are only a short trip away by train or car, making them ideal for a day excursion.
