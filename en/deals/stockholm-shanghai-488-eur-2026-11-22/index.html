Shanghai is a huge city full of contrasts. You can stroll along the Bund, the famous waterfront promenade on the Huangpu River, and watch the Pudong skyscrapers up close at sunset. Another essential stop is Yuyuan Garden, a classic Chinese garden in the old town, with pavilions, ponds and stalls selling local food around it.

If you fancy getting lost for a while, the former French Concession has cafés, shops and tree-lined streets that are very pleasant to wander without rushing. And for a dose of shopping and nightlife, Nanjing Road and its surroundings are a good choice.
