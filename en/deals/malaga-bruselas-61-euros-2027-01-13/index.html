Once there, Brussels is easy to see in a few days. You can wander around the Grand-Place, one of the most beautiful squares in Europe, and try the famous chocolates and some good waffles along the way. Another option is to head to the Atomium, the city's giant metal structure, or stroll through the Ixelles neighbourhood and its independent shops.

And to round off the trip, don't miss the comic-strip murals scattered around the centre, a nod to the city's history and its best-loved characters.
