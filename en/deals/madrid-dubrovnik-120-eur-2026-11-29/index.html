Here's a deal worth a look: a return flight from Madrid to Dubrovnik from €120, nonstop and operated by Iberia. Compared with the roughly €170 it usually costs, the saving is around 29%, so it's worth a closer look.

The dates we found are outbound on 29 November 2026 and return on 4 December. It's a late November to early December window, a much quieter time in this city than summer.

Prices change often, so we'd suggest not leaving it too long if you're interested. The price may vary, and what you see here is confirmed when you click the booking button.

Once there, start by wandering the old town and climbing the walls, which offer spectacular views over the Adriatic. Then you can take a boat out to Lokrum island, just a short distance offshore, for a relaxed stroll among pines and coves. And if you want to get out of the city, Cavtat, a charming coastal town, is a short bus ride away.
