If you make it to Seoul, it is worth wandering through Gyeongbokgung Palace at sunrise, when there are fewer people. Another great plan is getting lost in the Bukchon neighbourhood, with its traditional hanok houses, and ending the day with dinner at the food stalls in Gwangjang Market.

For the views, head up N Seoul Tower on Namsan, especially if you land on a clear December afternoon. When the cold bites, cafés and hot baths at the local bathhouses make a perfect refuge.
