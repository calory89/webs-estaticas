If you're tired of Montreal's winter and dreaming of turquoise water, this deal is for you. We found a return flight from Montreal (YUL) to Cancún (CUN) for €264, operated by Flair Airlines and nonstop. This route usually costs around €380, which makes the saving roughly 31%.

The dates for this offer are departure on 18 January 2027 and return on 21 January 2027, a short three-day trip made for switching off. Keep in mind that prices can change at any moment, so it's worth checking the dates as soon as you can. What you see when you click the button is the information available at the time you look.

Once you're in Cancún, you could spend a day on Isla Mujeres, just a few minutes away by ferry, enjoying quiet beaches and a stroll along its seafront promenade. If you'd prefer a bit more history, the Mayan ruins of Tulum are a little over an hour away by road and have spectacular views over the sea. You can also take a dip in one of the area's cenotes, whose crystal-clear waters are perfect for cooling off after a day in the sun.
