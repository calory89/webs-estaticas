Warsaw in January has its charm: snow, Christmas markets that are packing up for the season, and a city that feels wonderfully quiet. This deal flies nonstop from Malaga with Ryanair for €61 return, compared with the usual €90. The dates we found are an outbound flight on 16 January 2027 and a return on 26 January.

Flight prices change constantly, so this one could rise within hours. We'd recommend booking sooner rather than later. Clicking the button checks the price and availability in real time, which is the only figure that counts.

Once you're there, it's worth wandering the Old Town, rebuilt after the Second World War, with its colourful façades and the Market Square. You can also visit Łazienki Park, with its palace on the water and its peacocks, or the Warsaw Uprising Museum, which is very thorough and tells a story that stays with you.

To round off the trip, try pierogi at a traditional restaurant and duck into an old-school café to warm up. It's a perfect plan for a winter long weekend.
