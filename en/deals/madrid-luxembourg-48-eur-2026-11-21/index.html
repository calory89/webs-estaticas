This deal takes you from Madrid to Luxembourg for €48 return, flying nonstop with Ryanair. That's well below the usual price for this route, which is around €110, so it's worth a closer look.

The dates we found are an outbound flight on 21 November 2026 and a return on 28 November 2026. That gives you seven days to explore a small country with plenty of charm, and the direct flight makes the trip easy from start to finish.

Flight prices change quickly, so if this offer appeals to you, don't wait too long. The price may vary, and what we show is checked at the moment you click the button.

Once there, wander through the old town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and visit the Bock casemates for stunning views over the Alzette valley. It's also worth heading to the Grund neighbourhood by the river and trying a good plate of Luxembourgish food with a local beer.
