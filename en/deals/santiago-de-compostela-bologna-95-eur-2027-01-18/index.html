The dates we found are 18 January 2027 outbound and 19 January return. It's a short trip, ideal for getting away without disrupting your week.

Flight prices change constantly, so if this appeals to you, don't wait too long. The price may vary and is confirmed at the moment you click through.

Once there, take time to stroll under the city's porticoes, which are part of UNESCO's World Heritage list. Climb the Torre Asinelli for views over the rooftops, then end the day in Piazza Maggiore, with the Basilica of San Petronio in the background. And, of course, try the tortellini at a good local restaurant.
