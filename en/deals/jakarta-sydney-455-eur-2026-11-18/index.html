If you've been dreaming of a trip to Australia, this one is worth a closer look. We've found a return ticket from Jakarta to Sydney with Qantas for €455, flying nonstop. The usual price for this route is around €650, which makes the saving about 30%.

The dates we found are an outbound flight on 18 November 2026 and a return on 25 November. Prices change often and fares can shift at any moment, so check the details as soon as you click through and decide quickly if it suits you.

November is a great time to visit Sydney, when spring is in full swing. You can stroll from Circular Quay to the Opera House, walk across the Harbour Bridge or take the ferry to Manly for a dose of coastline.

If you have a little more time, a trip to the Blue Mountains, about an hour and a bit from the city, offers spectacular scenery. And the beaches at Bondi and Coogee are perfect for an afternoon of sun and sea.
