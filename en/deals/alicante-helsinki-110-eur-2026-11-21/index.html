This deal links Alicante with Helsinki for €110 return, flying with Ryanair and one stop. The dates we found are 21 to 23 November 2026, ideal for a long weekend in the low season. Compared with the around €160 this trip usually costs, the saving is about 31%.

Flight prices change constantly, so we suggest not waiting too long. The price may vary, and what we show is confirmed when you click the booking button, where you will see the updated offer.

Once you arrive in Helsinki, it is worth wandering around the Kauppatori market square by the harbour and stopping by the Lutheran cathedral, one of the city's most recognisable buildings. You can also take a ferry to Suomenlinna, a sea fortress with a rich history and sea views.

November days are short and the cold bites, but it is the perfect time to step into charming cafés and try a Finnish sauna, one of the most typical experiences in the country.
