Once you're in Nairobi, you can visit Nairobi National Park, one of the few national parks in the world located so close to a major capital, where you can spot giraffes, lions and wildebeest with the city skyline in the background. If you'd prefer something calmer, the Giraffe Centre lets you feed giraffes from a raised platform.
