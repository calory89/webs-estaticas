Right now you have a very good chance to go without it costing an arm and a leg. We've found a return flight from Gran Canaria (LPA) to Dublin (DUB) with Ryanair, nonstop, for €60.

Once you're there, a week gives you plenty of time. You can wander around Temple Bar, the liveliest area, though it's also worth visiting by day. If you're into history, the Trinity College Library and its famous Book of Kells deserve a quiet afternoon. And if you're after something different, a trip by train or bus to the coastal village of Howth or the landscapes of Glendalough will show you another side of Ireland.
