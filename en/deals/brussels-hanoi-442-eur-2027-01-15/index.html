This one is a real bargain: Brussels to Hanoi for €442 return with Juneyao Airlines. You leave on 15 January and head back on the 21st, which gives you a full week to explore the Vietnamese capital without spending a fortune. The usual price is around €610, so you save almost €170.

The flight includes one stop, which is perfectly reasonable at this price. Departure dates are available over the coming months, so you have time to decide. Keep in mind that fares like this can change within hours, so check the exact price on the booking site before you commit.

Hanoi is worth every euro. Wander through the Old Quarter's busy narrow streets, try the street food (pho, bánh mì, century eggs), visit the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum and take it easy by Hoan Kiem Lake. If you have a few extra days, Ha Long Bay is only a few hours away by bus.
