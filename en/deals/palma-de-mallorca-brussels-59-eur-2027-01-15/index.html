If you fancy a trip to Brussels without spending a fortune, this deal could be for you. We found a return flight from Palma de Mallorca with Ryanair, nonstop, for €59. Its usual price is around €90, so the saving is 34%.

The dates we found are outbound on 15 January 2027 and return on 18 January 2027, which gives you three days to explore the Belgian capital in the middle of winter. It's a quiet time of year, ideal for wandering the city centre at your own pace.

Prices change constantly, so if this one catches your eye, don't wait too long. The price may vary, and you can check it by clicking the deal button, where you'll see current availability.

Once you're there, don't miss the Grand-Place, one of the most beautiful squares in Europe, especially around sunset. Another must-see is the Atomium, a very photogenic monument on the outskirts of the centre. And of course, try the waffles and Belgian chocolate at one of the city's long-standing patisseries.
