This deal takes you from Alicante to Warsaw on a direct Ryanair flight for €54 return. That is well below what this route usually costs, a saving of about 46% on the regular fare.

The dates we found are an outbound flight on 14 January 2027 and a return on 21 January 2027. They suit a quiet trip outside the high season, with fewer crowds in the streets and a distinctive winter atmosphere.

Prices change constantly, so we recommend booking as soon as you can. The price may vary, and the fare shown here is confirmed when you click the button, where you can check the dates and the current rate.

Once in Warsaw, don't miss the Old Town, carefully rebuilt after the Second World War and declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Another essential visit is the Warsaw Uprising Museum, which is very thorough and moving. And if the weather allows, stroll through Łazienki Park, where the palace by the lake is impressive even in winter.
