If you fancy swapping turrón for Nordic chill, this deal is for you: a nonstop Ryanair flight from Alicante to Copenhagen for €42 return. That's 65% less than this route usually costs, which normally sits around €120.

The dates we found are departing on 19 December 2026 and returning on 4 January 2027, so you have plenty of time to enjoy the holidays in the Danish capital. Prices can change at any moment, so we recommend having a look as soon as possible: the exact price is confirmed when you click the button.

Copenhagen has a special charm in winter. You can stroll along Nyhavn, with its colourful houses by the canal, and then wander through the Christmas market at Tivoli, which fills with lights at this time of year. If you enjoy culture, the Glyptotek and its collections make a perfect stop to warm up.

And to recharge, nothing beats a break in a Danish café with a good coffee and a cinnamon bun. Copenhagen has a calm, very pleasant pace, ideal for switching off after the festivities.
