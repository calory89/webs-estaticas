The dates we found are 17 to 24 November 2026, though the travel window runs from November 2026 to January 2027, so other combinations may be available. Fares on long-haul routes like this change often, so if the deal appeals to you, it's worth not waiting too long.

Macau is a very distinctive city. It was a Portuguese colony until 1999, and that shows in its architecture, its baroque churches and especially its food. Stroll through the historic centre, which is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and don't miss the Ruins of St. Paul's, the city's most famous façade.

For something different, head to the island of Coloane for its quiet beaches and a much more relaxed atmosphere. And, of course, try the local pastéis de nata, which are a delight with a Cantonese twist.
