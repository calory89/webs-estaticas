Bonaire is one of the Dutch Caribbean islands, and it's a paradise for diving that starts right at the shoreline. You can snorkel or dive over reefs full of marine life just a few steps from the beach.

Don't miss Washington Slagbaai National Park, which is ideal for exploring the island by off-road vehicle and discovering wild beaches and very different landscapes. And if you love animals, head to the salt flats in the north to see pink flamingos in the wild.

Finally, Kralendijk, the capital, has a pleasant waterfront promenade where you can have dinner with sea views as the sun goes down. It's a perfect trip for switching off in just a few days.
