We've found a bargain flying nonstop from Palma de Mallorca to Hamburg with Eurowings for €64 return. This route usually sits around €140, so the saving is roughly 54%. It's a deal aimed at anyone looking for a short city break who wants to stretch their budget.

The dates we found are outbound on 30 January 2027 and return on 31 January 2027, which means one night in the city. It's a very compact trip, ideal if you only have a weekend free and fancy a change of scene without the fuss.

Low fares tend to disappear quickly, so we'd suggest checking the dates and deciding soon. Prices can change at any time, so the final amount is confirmed when you click through to book.

If you make it to Hamburg, it's worth wandering through the Speicherstadt, the old warehouse district along the canal, and stopping by the Elbphilharmonie, whose terrace has great views over the harbour. For something livelier, St. Pauli and its famous Reeperbahn are a must after dark. January is cold, so pack warm clothes.
