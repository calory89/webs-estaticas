If you're ready to swap the Norwegian cold for Thai heat, this deal is worth a look. We've found a return flight from Oslo to Bangkok for €395, nonstop and operated by Norse Atlantic Airways. Compared with the usual €640, that's a saving of around 38%.

The dates we've found are 3 to 13 December 2026. That gives you ten days to explore the Thai capital at your own pace. Prices move quickly, though, so we'd suggest not waiting too long. The price may change, and you'll confirm it when you click the booking button.

In Bangkok, start with the Grand Palace and Wat Pho, home to the impressive reclining Buddha. Then take a boat trip along the Chao Phraya River at sunset, one of the best ways to see the city.

Don't miss Yaowarat, the Chinatown district, which is ideal for street food after dark. And if you prefer something more relaxed, the floating markets on the outskirts of the city make a very pleasant half-day trip.
