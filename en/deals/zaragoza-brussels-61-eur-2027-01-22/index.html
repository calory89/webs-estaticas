We recommend checking the offer as soon as possible. Prices can vary, so always look at the live fare before booking to make sure you're seeing the current figure.

Once you're in Brussels, take a stroll around the Grand-Place, one of the most beautiful squares in Europe, and look for the Manneken Pis, the famous little statue of a boy that is almost an institution in the city. Don't miss some Belgian chocolate or a beer in one of its traditional bars either.

If you have time, visit the Atomium, the quirky monument shaped like an iron crystal molecule, or head to one of the city's museums, such as the one dedicated to Magritte. Brussels has plenty to offer in a weekend.
