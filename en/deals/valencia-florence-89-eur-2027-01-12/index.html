Good news for art lovers: we've found a Valencia to Florence flight for €89 return with Vueling. The usual price is around €130, so the saving is 32%. Keep in mind that the route has one stop.

The dates we found are 12 to 16 January 2027, leaving on Tuesday and returning on Saturday, which makes it a perfect short getaway. As these are low-season dates, the city tends to feel calmer than in spring or summer.

Fares change all the time, so if this one appeals to you, don't wait too long. We recommend checking the offer as soon as possible and booking once you see it. The price may vary and is confirmed when you click the button.

Once you're there, don't miss the dome of the Duomo and the sunset views from Piazzale Michelangelo. If you love art, the Uffizi Gallery and the Galleria dell'Accademia, home to Michelangelo's David, are must-sees. And for a relaxed wander, cross the Ponte Vecchio and stroll through the Oltrarno neighbourhood, which is much quieter than the centre.
