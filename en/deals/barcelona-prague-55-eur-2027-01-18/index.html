If you fancy a charming city break in central Europe without overspending, this deal is for you. Vueling flies nonstop from Barcelona to Prague for €55 return, against its usual fare of around €80. That works out at a saving of 31%.

The dates we found are 18 to 22 January 2027: you leave on Monday 18 and return on Friday 22, which gives you four nights in the city. January is low season, so expect fewer crowds and a calmer atmosphere, but also cold weather, so pack a warm coat.

Flight prices change constantly and the best fares sell out fast, so check the dates and book as soon as you can. The price may vary, so confirm it when you click through before you decide.

Once you arrive, make sure you visit Prague Castle and the area around it, cross Charles Bridge at sunset, and wander the cobbled streets of the Old Town. If you have time, head over to Malá Strana and enjoy a good Czech beer in one of its pubs.
