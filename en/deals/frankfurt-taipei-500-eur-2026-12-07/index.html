This deal takes you from Frankfurt to Taipei, the capital of Taiwan, for €500 return with Air China. The flight has one stop and, according to our data, usually costs around €950, so the saving is roughly 47%.

The dates we found are departure on 7 December 2026 and return on 18 December 2026, which gives you eleven days to explore the island. These dates are fixed, but prices can change at any moment, so we recommend not hesitating if the calendar works for you.

If you book quickly, you'll have time to see the best of the city: head up to the Taipei 101 observation deck, lose yourself in night markets such as Shilin, and try street food in Ximending. For a day trip, the village of Jiufen, with its narrow alleys lit by red lanterns, is almost a must.

For nature lovers, Yangmingshan National Park is an hour from the centre and offers trails, volcanic fumaroles and beautiful views. And after a walk, there's nothing better than relaxing in the hot springs of Beitou.

Remember that the price is checked when you press the button, so look at the dates, decide calmly, but don't take too long.
