Fort Lauderdale is a great place to switch off, with fine sandy beaches and a pleasant seafront promenade. You can stroll along Las Olas Boulevard, lined with terraces, shops and restaurants, or take a dip and watch the sunset along the coast.

If you fancy getting out of the city, the Everglades are within easy reach and offer boat trips to spot alligators and birds in their natural habitat. For something livelier, Miami is nearby and has the Wynwood neighbourhood, where murals and street art are everywhere.
