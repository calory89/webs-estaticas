Here's a bargain: a nonstop flight from Palma de Mallorca to Stockholm with Ryanair for €58 return. Its usual price is around €130, so that's a saving of roughly 55%.

The dates we found are 21 to 23 November 2026. It's a short trip, ideal for a different weekend away, leaving on Saturday and returning on Monday.

Airfares change constantly, so if you're interested, don't wait too long. The price may vary and is confirmed when you click the booking button, where you'll see the updated offer.

Once there, we recommend strolling through Gamla Stan, the old town, with its cobbled lanes and cafés. You can also visit the Vasa Museum, a spectacular 17th-century ship, or head to Djurgården to enjoy its parks and open-air museums.

And don't miss fika, the coffee-and-pastry break Swedes take every day. It's the perfect excuse to warm up in November.
