Today's bargain sounds like a postcard: a nonstop flight from Amsterdam to Willemstad, on Curaçao, for €541 return. This route usually costs around €830, so the saving is roughly 35%. The flight is operated by Corendon Dutch Airlines.

The dates we found are outbound on 15 November 2026 and return on 24 November 2026, during the winter season. That gives you about nine days to unwind with the Caribbean as your backdrop.

Prices on deals like this move quickly, so if it appeals to you, don't wait too long. Fares can change within hours. The price may change and is always confirmed when you click through, so check it before you decide.

Once you're there, wander through Willemstad's historic centre, with its brightly painted façades that look like they've stepped out of a painting and that are part of the UNESCO World Heritage list. Afterwards, head to one of the island's beaches, ideal for relaxing and snorkelling. If you enjoy exploring, Christoffel National Park offers nature trails and views across the island.
