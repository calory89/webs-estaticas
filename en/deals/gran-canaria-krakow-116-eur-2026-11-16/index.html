Kraków is one of the most beautiful cities in Europe. Stroll through the Rynek Główny, one of the largest medieval squares on the continent, then climb up to Wawel Hill to visit the royal castle and cathedral. If you want something different, head to Kazimierz, the historic Jewish quarter, full of cafés, galleries and street murals.

For a day trip, the Wieliczka salt mines are close to the city and well worth the visit. November also has a special atmosphere: the cold weather makes cosy cafés irresistible and, towards the end of the month, the first Christmas markets start to appear.
