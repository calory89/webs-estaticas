If you're craving a city break full of art, history and great pasta, this deal is hard to beat. Volotea is flying direct from Bilbao to Florence for €51 return, compared with the €90 it usually costs, a saving of roughly 43%.

The dates we found are outbound on 12 January 2027 and return on 16 January 2027, giving you four days to explore at an easy pace. Keep in mind that prices can change at any moment, so check the fare when you click through and book soon if it suits you.

Florence is best explored on foot. Climb the dome of the Duomo for views over the terracotta rooftops, lose yourself among the masterpieces in the Uffizi Gallery, and cross the Ponte Vecchio as the sun goes down.

To round off the trip, head up to Piazzale Michelangelo for one of the best views in the city, then take your time over an artisan ice cream in Oltrarno, away from the crowds of the centre.
