Muscat has plenty to offer on a short break. You can stroll through the Mutrah souk by the harbour and get lost in its narrow lanes and stalls selling incense and spices. The Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque is a must-see for its architecture and its carpets.

If you fancy getting out of the city, Nizwa, with its fort and its Friday market, is within a reasonable distance for a day trip. And if you're after scenery, the wadis in the area are ideal for a swim in a landscape very different from the desert most of us imagine.
