If you fancy swapping Mallorca's sunshine for Vienna's Christmas charm, this deal is for you. We've found a non-stop Ryanair flight from Palma de Mallorca to Vienna for €54 return, compared with the around €100 it usually costs on normal dates. That's a saving of roughly 46%.

The dates we've found are an outbound flight on 24 December 2026 and a return on 1 January 2027. They're ideal for a getaway between Christmas and New Year, when the city is at its most magical. Keep in mind that prices can change at any moment, so check the dates and book as soon as they suit you.

Vienna has plenty to keep you busy. Visit the Schönbrunn Palace and its gardens, which look especially lovely with a bit of snow. Wander the historic centre and enjoy a Viennese coffee in one of the city's traditional cafés. And if the cold bites, the Christmas markets are perfect for warming up with something hot in your hands.

To round off the trip, take a ride on the Prater Ferris wheel, one of the city's landmarks, then get lost in the streets at your own pace. Vienna is best enjoyed without rushing, so leave room to explore its corners without a fixed plan.
