If safaris and elephants are on your wish list, this deal is worth a look: a return flight from Miami to Nairobi for €865, operated by Turkish Airlines with one stop. The usual price for this route is around €1,560, which works out to a saving of roughly 45%.

The dates we found are an outbound flight on 25 January 2027 and a return on 4 February 2027, a good option if you want to travel at the end of January and the start of February.

Flight prices change constantly, so if this interests you, don't leave it until tomorrow. The fare can change at any moment and is confirmed only when you reach the booking page, so it pays to decide quickly.

Once you're in Nairobi, you can visit Nairobi National Park, one of the few national parks in the world located so close to a major capital, where you can spot giraffes, lions and wildebeest with the city skyline in the background. It's also well worth visiting the elephant orphanage run by the David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust, where you can see baby elephants up close. If you'd prefer something calmer, the Giraffe Centre lets you feed giraffes from a raised platform.
