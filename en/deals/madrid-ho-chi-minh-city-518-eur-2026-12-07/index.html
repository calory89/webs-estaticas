Here's a great deal: return flights from Madrid to Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon) from €518, with Sichuan Airlines and one stop. Compared with the roughly €760 this route usually costs, that's a saving of close to 32%.

The dates we found are outbound on 7 December 2026 and return on 21 December, so you'd have two weeks to explore southern Vietnam. Prices can change at any time, though, so don't wait too long. Check the fare by clicking the button and decide quickly if it suits you.

Once you arrive in the city, don't miss Ben Thanh Market, where you can try street food and pick up a souvenir. The Cu Chi Tunnels, just outside the city, are an essential trip for understanding the country's history.

Another idea is to stroll through Cholon, the Chinatown district, and enjoy a Vietnamese coffee with condensed milk on a café terrace. And if you have time, a day trip to the Mekong Delta will give you a very different view of the country.
