This deal takes you from Phoenix to Fort Lauderdale on Florida's Atlantic coast for €210 return. Frontier Airlines operates it with one stop, and the usual fare on this route is around €300, so the saving is roughly 30%.

The dates we found are 14 to 19 November 2026, leaving on the 14th and returning on the 19th. That gives you about five days of Florida sunshine at a time of year when many people are already itching to get away.

Keep in mind that flight prices change constantly and this one could rise within hours, so if the dates suit you, don't wait too long. Use the button below to check the current price and availability.

Once you arrive, Fort Lauderdale has plenty to offer. Stroll along Las Olas Boulevard for dinner and shopping, cruise the canals as if you were in a little Venice, and of course, spend some time on Fort Lauderdale Beach. If you have time to spare, Miami is less than an hour away by car, and the Everglades make a great day trip for spotting alligators and local wildlife.
