If you make it to Manila, it's worth spending a couple of days in Intramuros, the walled historic centre from the colonial era, with its cathedral and cobbled streets. In the evening, a walk along the Malecón by the bay is ideal for watching the sunset over the sea.

If you have a bit more time, you can head to Tagaytay, not far from the capital, where you'll find views of Taal Volcano and its lake. And if you're after beaches, the islands of Palawan, such as El Nido, are among the most beautiful destinations in the country.
