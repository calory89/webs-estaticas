Here's a fare worth a look if you've been dreaming of crossing the Atlantic: Venice to Fortaleza, in northeast Brazil, for €537 return with Iberia. The usual price on this route is around €1,010, which makes the saving close to 47%. The flight has one stop.

The dates we found are 1 to 9 December 2026. Fares like this move quickly, so if it appeals to you, it's best not to wait too long. Prices can change, and the final fare is confirmed the moment you reach the booking page.

Fortaleza has lively city beaches such as Praia do Futuro, ideal for a stroll, a swim and a snack from the coconut stalls or fresh fish. If you want something calmer, Beach Park in Aquiraz is a water park full of slides, perfect for a day of thrills.

For a change of scenery, Jericoacoara is the gem of the region, with dunes, crystal-clear lagoons and unforgettable sunsets. It's a few hours by road from Fortaleza, so it's worth setting aside a couple of days for it. And if you feel like going out at night, the forró and seafood in the city are well worth it.
