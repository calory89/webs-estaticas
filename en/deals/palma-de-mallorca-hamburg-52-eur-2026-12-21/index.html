This deal is a great way to close out the year: a nonstop Ryanair flight from Palma de Mallorca to Hamburg for €52 return. The route usually costs around €130, so the saving is about 60%.

The dates we found are an outbound flight on 21 December 2026 and a return on 1 January 2027, which makes it easy to spend Christmas away and welcome the new year in this atmospheric port city.

Keep in mind that airfares change often, and the cheapest seats tend to go quickly. If the plan suits you, don't leave it until later. The price may vary, so check it when you click through to book.

Once you arrive, make time for Speicherstadt, the historic warehouse district of red brick, and take a stroll around the Elbphilharmonie, the concert hall with a free public viewing platform. The Christmas markets fill the city with lights and the smell of mulled wine and gingerbread.

If you feel like going out at night, Reeperbahn is the city's best-known nightlife area, and the Landungsbrücken harbour is a lovely spot to watch the boats at sunset.
