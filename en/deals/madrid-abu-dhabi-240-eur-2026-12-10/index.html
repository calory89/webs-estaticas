If you fancy swapping the December chill for sunshine and skyscrapers, we have a bargain for you: Madrid → Abu Dhabi from €240 return, with Pegasus and one stop. That's 27% less than its usual price of around €330.

The dates we found are outbound on 10 December 2026 and return on 22 December, so you'll have about twelve days to explore the Emirati capital at your own pace.

Be quick, though: flight prices change very fast and this one could go up within hours. We recommend checking it as soon as you can and confirming the final price when you click through, because that's where the offer is confirmed.

Once you're there, don't miss the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, one of the most impressive buildings in the world. Then head to Yas Island to see the Formula 1 circuit and Ferrari World theme park. To unwind, take a stroll along the Corniche at sunset, with sea views and the city's lit-up buildings.
