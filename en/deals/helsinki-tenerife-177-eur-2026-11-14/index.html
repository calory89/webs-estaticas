If you're dreaming of swapping Finnish snow for Canarian sunshine, this deal is for you. We've found return flights from Helsinki to Tenerife from €177 with Ryanair, about €63 less than this route usually costs.

The dates on offer are 14 to 19 November 2026, departing on the 14th and returning on the 19th. November is a good time to escape the northern chill, as Tenerife keeps mild temperatures and plenty of sunshine.

Keep in mind that flight prices change constantly, so this fare could rise or sell out at any time. We recommend checking availability and booking as soon as you find a combination that suits you. Clicking through will show you the current price, which is the one that counts.

Once you're there, you can head up Mount Teide to see the volcanic landscape, stroll along Las Teresitas beach, with its golden sand brought from the Sahara, or visit Garachico and Puerto de la Cruz to wander their streets and enjoy the laid-back atmosphere.
