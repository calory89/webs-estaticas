This deal takes you from Málaga to Budapest on a nonstop Ryanair flight for €74 return. The usual fare on this route is about €120, so the saving is roughly 38%.

The dates we found are outbound on 17 January 2027 and return on 22 January 2027, giving you five days in the Hungarian capital. This is low season, so it's a good pick if you want quiet streets and low prices.

Keep in mind that flight prices change constantly, and this one could go up within hours. If it appeals to you, don't leave it for later. Check that the price and dates are still available, then book as soon as you can.

Once you're there, we suggest a walk along the Danube to see the Parliament lit up at night, a visit to Fisherman's Bastion, and the Buda Castle area. In January, the Széchenyi thermal baths are a great way to warm up. And don't leave without trying a proper goulash or a lángos from a local market.
