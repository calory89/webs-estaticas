Winter has its own charm in Stockholm. Wander through Gamla Stan, the old town with its cobbled lanes and colourful buildings, which is ideal for unhurried strolls and stopping in atmospheric cafés.

Another must-see is the Vasa Museum, which houses a 17th-century warship that sank on its maiden voyage and can now be seen almost intact. If you'd like some fresh air, Skansen brings together traditional Swedish houses and is a good way to understand how people once lived in the country.
