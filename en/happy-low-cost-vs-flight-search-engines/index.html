How is Happy Low Cost different from a flight search engine?

A search engine (Skyscanner, Google Flights, Kayak, Kiwi.com…) compares fares for the route and dates you choose. Happy Low Cost works the other way round: it searches for you and only publishes return flights that cost at least 40% less than usual on that route, already checked on Kiwi.com.

When is a search engine better?

When you already know where and when you want to go. The search engine shows every option for those dates, including the ones that are not a deal.

When is Happy Low Cost better?

When you are flexible on destination or dates and want an exceptional price. Also to know whether a price is good: each route shows the lowest price we saw, the usual price (median) and the cheapest month.

Does Happy Low Cost sell tickets?

No. You book on the airline or agency website we link to. We may earn a small commission from those links at no cost to you, and that does not decide what we publish.

Can I use both?

Yes, and that is what we recommend: a free Happy Low Cost alert to hear about the deal and, when booking, the search engine or the airline to check nearby dates.
