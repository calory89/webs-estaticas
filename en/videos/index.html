Which videos do you publish?

Short videos of a real trip: the place, a corner, the food, the hotel or the flight. Vertical and 15 to 60 seconds works best. We do not publish ads or other people's videos.

When does it go live?

We review every video by hand. If it fits, it goes live within a day or two, with your name or @handle. We also share the best ones on our Facebook page.

Can I take it down later?

Yes. Write to us from the contact page with the place and your @handle and we will remove it.
