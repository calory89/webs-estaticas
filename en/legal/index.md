## Prices and accuracy
Every deal shows when its price was checked. Fares change often and we cannot guarantee that a price is still available when you book. Amounts shown in currencies other than the euro are approximate conversions using the European Central Bank reference rate of the day.

## Affiliate links
Some links are affiliate links (for example to Booking.com through CJ). If you book through them we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Commissions do not decide which deals we publish.

## Personal data we collect
If you create a deal alert we store the contact you give us (email or WhatsApp number), the airports, destination and price you choose, the date, and the IP address and browser of the request, to send you the alerts you asked for and to prevent abuse. We do not sell your data.
