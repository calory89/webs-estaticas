Best time to visit

Nairobi and its surroundings are worth visiting year-round. The dry seasons (January–February and August–October) offer the best weather for safaris and outdoor activities. The rainy seasons (April–May and November–December) can affect some routes, but also bring advantages: fewer tourists and greener landscapes.

What to do in Nairobi and beyond

Nairobi is your gateway to Kenya. Visit the National Museum, Giraffe Centre, or Elephant Orphanage. From here you can easily reach major parks: Amboseli, Tsavo, Masai Mara. The city has modern neighbourhoods, authentic African food, and a vibrant cultural scene. Jomo Kenyatta Airport is about 15 km from the city centre.
