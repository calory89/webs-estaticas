Price and when to book your flight

You can grab a return ticket on this route from €50. The sample prices we've tracked are €50, €71, and €72, so expect to pay between €50 and €72 if you search flexibly across dates. Airline and connections

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight from Zaragoza to Marrakech. No plane changes—you travel nonstop. The sample outbound departs at 13:30 from Zaragoza and the return at 06:25 from Marrakech. Mind the early morning times: double-check your airport connection if your flight leaves at dawn.

Best time to travel

Marrakech works as a destination almost year-round. October to April offers the most comfortable weather (15–25°C). Summer, June to August, gets very hot. If your goal is saving money, travel in low season (summer or mid-August); school holidays and bank holidays push prices up. Check your region's calendar to spot cheap windows.

What to do when you arrive in Marrakech

Menara airport sits 6 km from the city centre. Taxi or bus will get you to the medina. Once there, explore Jemaa el-Fnaá square (the beating heart), the palaces in the old town, Majorelle Gardens, and the city walls. If time allows, Ait Benhadou Kasbah is a 3-hour drive away. Eat well and haggle in the souks—it's part of the experience.
