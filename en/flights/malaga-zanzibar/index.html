Best time to visit

Zanzibar has a tropical climate. The dry season and best weather run from June to October (comfortable temperatures, less rain). Avoid April and May if you don't want constant rain. December to March is hot and humid, peak tourist season. April to June is cheaper and quieter.

What to do when you arrive

Expect turquoise beaches at Paje or Nungwi straight away. Stone Town, the historic capital, blends Arab, Swahili and Indian architecture in its alleys. Visit the spice warehouses, dive at coral reefs, or take a trip to Pemba island. The international airport is 50 km from Stone Town with direct road connections.
