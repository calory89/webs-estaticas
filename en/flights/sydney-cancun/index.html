Best time to visit

Cancun has great weather almost year-round. The Caribbean hurricane season runs June to November, so many travellers prefer December to May. Off-peak travel can mean better accommodation deals, though flight prices are what you see here.

What to do when you arrive

Cancun is your gateway to beaches, cenotes and Mayan ruins. The Riviera Maya is just round the corner: Playa del Carmen, Tulum and Cozumel Island are must-sees. Cancun international airport is 20 km from the centre, well served by bus and transfer services.
