Best time to visit

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: mild weather, fewer crowds, and good prices. Summer is peak season and expensive; winter is cheaper but cold. For the best balance: May or September.

What to do on arrival

Ferenc Liszt Airport is 16 km from the city centre. Buses, metro, or taxi get you there easily. Once downtown, don't miss the Danube, Széchenyi Thermal Baths, St. Stephen's Basilica, and Parliament. Budapest is compact: two or three days cover the essentials.
