Best time to go

April–May and September–October are ideal: pleasant weather, fewer crowds than summer, and better prices. Skip July and August if you want to save. Winter is cheap and quieter, though it rains more; Naples is worth it any season.

What to do in Naples

The historic centre is unmissable (Spaccanapoli, Duomo, Castel dell'Ovo). Eat genuine pizza anywhere. Pompeya is 30 minutes by train. Climb Vesuvius if you're up for it. The National Archaeological Museum holds treasures from Roman digs. Bohemian neighbourhoods like San Gregorio Armeno are perfect for wandering.
