Best time to visit Mauritius

Mauritius has a tropical climate year-round. The dry season, from May to November, is ideal: pleasant temperatures and minimal rainfall. December to April is humid and hot, with cyclone risk. Plan your trip between May and October to enjoy the best weather and outdoor activities without disruptions.

What to do when you arrive in Mauritius

Mauritius is a paradise for beaches, diving and nature. Visit Beau Bassin and Trou aux Biches beaches in the north, or explore the south with its crystal-clear waters. Don't miss the Pamplemousses Botanical Garden, a historical gem. For adventure, climb Piton de la Petite Rivière Noire or snorkel the coral reefs. The capital, Port Louis, offers local markets and authentic cuisine. Mauritius International Airport (MRU) is well-connected with transport to the centre and beaches.
