Best time to visit

Cape Town is a year-round destination, but keep the Southern Hemisphere seasons in mind. Austral summer (November to February) brings good weather but more tourists. Winter (June to August) is cooler and drier. Choose based on your climate preference and date flexibility for better fares.

What to do when you arrive in Cape Town

Cape Town International Airport (CPT) is about 20 km from the city centre. The city offers Table Mountain, beaches like Camps Bay, the Victoria & Alfred Waterfront promenade, nature reserves and the Garden Route. Plan according to your interests: nature, culture, beaches or local food.
