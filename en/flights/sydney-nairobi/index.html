Best time to visit

Nairobi has subtropical weather year-round. Dry seasons (July–October and January–February) offer better visibility for safaris and less rain. These periods tend to see higher fares. For cheaper tickets, consider the rainy seasons (April–May, November–December), when tourism is lower.

What to do on arrival

Nairobi is the gateway to Kenya's national parks. From Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO) you can arrange safaris to Masai Mara or Amboseli. The capital itself has museums, art galleries and markets. Note that safaris take time; consider extending your stay to make the most of them.
