Best time to travel

The dry season in the Philippines runs November to May: pleasant weather and less rain. December and January are peak (pricier and busier), but ideal for beaches and outdoor activities. For lower costs and fewer crowds, try May or September-October; note that October still sees more rain. No month guarantees cheap prices: book early no matter when you go.

What to do on arrival

Manila is your gateway to the Philippines. Ninoy Aquino Airport (MNL) puts you right in the heart of the country. Explore Intramuros (the historic walled city), visit baroque churches, relax on nearby beaches, or catch a ferry to Boracay, Palawan, or Siargao. Local cuisine (sinigang, adobo, lechón) is excellent and cheap. The airport is roughly 14 km from the centre; take a taxi, Grab, or public transport to save.
