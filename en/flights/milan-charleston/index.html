Price and when to book

The cheapest tickets hover around €700 for return flights. We found prices from €700, €852 and €870 in recent samples. Book early: prices typically climb as departure approaches. Check fares with date variations of a few days; often a small shift saves you money.

Airline and connections

Ryanair operates this route with 2 stops on the outbound leg. It's not a direct flight, so expect plane changes. Ryanair is known for tight pricing, but remember they charge for extra hand baggage and checked luggage: calculate carefully what you need to avoid surprises.

Best time to travel

Charleston is pleasant year-round, but spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) are ideal: mild weather and less peak-season crowds than summer. Prices are often competitive outside school holidays, so plan with flexibility if you can.

What to do on arrival

Charleston is a gem of the American South: stroll the Historic District, visit Fort Sumter and enjoy local dining on King Street. Charleston Airport (CHS) is roughly 20 km from town; easy access by taxi, rideshare or car rental.

FAQ

The Historic District is essential, with colonial homes and charming squares. Fort Sumter, Rainbow Row (colourful houses) and local food are highlights. A sunset walk through Battery Park is not to miss.
