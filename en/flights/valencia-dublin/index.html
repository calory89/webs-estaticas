Best time to travel

Dublin is lovely year-round, but skip peak season (Christmas and summer) if price matters most. Spring and autumn offer a good balance of decent weather and lower fares. Ireland has Atlantic weather: expect frequent rain, so pack a waterproof jacket.

What to do when you arrive

Dublin Airport is 10 km from the city centre, with easy access by bus or train. Must-sees: Guinness Storehouse, Trinity College, the River Liffey and traditional pubs. Temple Bar is touristy but iconic. With extra time, venture to Cliffs of Moher or Wicklow's coastline.
