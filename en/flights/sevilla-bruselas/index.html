€88 return is the best deal you'll find on this route. Most searches land between €88 and €105. Book as early as you can; Ryanair operates this line regularly, so there are frequent departures to choose from.

Best time to visit

Brussels is worth visiting anytime, but spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) offer the most pleasant weather for sightseeing. Prices tend to be lower in shoulder seasons, away from public holidays and school breaks. January and February can offer deals if you skip Spanish bank holidays.

What to do in Brussels

You'll land at Brussels-Zaventem Airport (BRU), 15 km from the centre. The Grand Place is unmissable, plus Manneken Pis, the Royal Palace, and art museums. Belgian food—mussels, fries, beer—is reason enough to go. The city is compact and easy to explore on foot or public transport.
