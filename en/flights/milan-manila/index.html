Price and when to book

The lowest price recorded is €599 return, though most searches fall between €679 and €830. Book as far in advance as possible, fares tend to climb as your departure date approaches. Compare daily if you have flexible dates; even changing your travel day can save you money.

Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Ryanair, which keeps these prices low precisely because there are 2 stopovers on the route. Outbound departs Milan at 05:50 and return leaves Manila at 05:45, both early morning. Expect layovers in between, but the savings make up for it if you're not in a rush.

Best time to visit

Manila is a tropical destination year-round. The dry season runs November to April, ideal for avoiding monsoons. Skip July and August, heavy rain. For lower fares, travel in shoulder season (May-June or September-October), though expect more rainfall. The example checked in December is a solid choice.

What to do when you arrive

Manila has Intramuros (walled old city), the Pasig River, the National Museum and local markets. Tagaytay with Taal volcano views is minutes away, and the Visayas islands are reachable by short flights or ferry. Eat street food in Makati, explore temples and nearby beaches. Ninoy Aquino Airport is 12 km from the city centre.
