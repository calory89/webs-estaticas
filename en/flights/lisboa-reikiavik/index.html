Best time to visit

Iceland works year-round, but each season has its appeal. Winter (November to March): northern lights and snowy scenery, but short days and harsh weather. Summer (June to August): nearly constant daylight, milder conditions, and accessible hiking trails, though busier and pricier. Spring and autumn offer a middle ground with fewer crowds.

What to do from Keflavík

Keflavík airport is 50 km from Reykjavík. Grab a bus, hire a car, or book a transfer. Don't miss the Golden Circle (waterfalls and geysers), glaciers, Reynisfjara's black sand beach, the Blue Lagoon thermal pools, and (in winter) aurora hunting. Iceland is made for road trips and guided tours.
