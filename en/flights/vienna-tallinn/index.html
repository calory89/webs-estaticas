What to do when you arrive in Tallinn

Explore the medieval Old Town, a UNESCO World Heritage site. Walk the city walls, climb Oleviste church for panoramic views, and enjoy cafés in Raekoja square. The harbour is worth a visit too, it's the modern heart of the city. Everything is within walking distance.
