Best time to visit

Kuala Lumpur is warm year-round. Dry season runs May to September, ideal for sightseeing. July and August are wetter but see fewer tourists. Book early regardless of season: intercontinental flights don't drop by time of year, but by seat availability.

You land at Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA). Take the Ekspres Rakyat train to the city centre in 30 minutes. Visit the Petronas Towers, explore Batu Caves, walk Chinatown, and relax at Lake Gardens. Malaysia offers authentic street food, nearby beaches, and genuine cultural blend.
