Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route with 1 stop on the outbound leg. This means it's not a direct flight, but it remains a budget-friendly option. Check where the stopover is and how long you'll be on the ground during your booking.

Best time to go

Mykonos is a Mediterranean destination with peak season in summer. Prices tend to be higher from June to August. If you want to save, consider travelling in spring (April–May) or autumn (September–October), when the weather is still pleasant and crowds are lighter.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Mykonos International Airport (JMK). From there, hire a car, catch a bus, or take a taxi into town. Mykonos offers stunning beaches, vibrant nightlife, windsurfing at Kalafatis, and quiet coves like Elia. The island is minutes from Delos, birthplace of Greek mythology.

No. Ryanair operates this route with 1 stop. Check your booking for the intermediate airport and total journey time.

What can I do in Mykonos?

Crystal-clear beaches (Paradise Beach, Super Paradise), island walks, nearby archaeological ruins, nightlife, and Greek cuisine. You can also take day trips to nearby islands like Delos or Rhenea.
