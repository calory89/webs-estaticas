Best time to visit

Tenerife enjoys subtropical weather year-round. July and August are driest and sunniest, but also busiest. For fewer crowds and reliable weather, try April–May or September–October. Winter (November–February) is mild, perfect if you want to escape Irish cold without hitting peak-season prices.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Tenerife South (Reina Sofía), gateway to beaches like El Médano and El Duomo. Don't miss Mount Teide (Spain's highest peak), reachable by tour from north or south. Candelaria and its basilica offer culture. For pure beach: Costa Adeje, Los Cristianos, or Puerto de la Cruz in the north. Choose between coastal relaxation or volcanic landscapes.
