Best time to visit

Rio de Janeiro offers beach and cultural events year-round. The Southern Hemisphere summer (December to March) brings warm weather and peak tourism. If you prefer fewer crowds and potentially better deals, consider April to September, which covers autumn and winter locally. Always check flight availability for your preferred dates.

What to do when you arrive

Rio's international airport (Galeão - Antonio Carlos Jobim) connects to the city centre by bus, metro, or taxi. Once there, visit Christ the Redeemer and Sugarloaf Mountain for iconic views, relax on Copacabana and Ipanema beaches, and explore historic Lapa for nightlife and colonial architecture. Travel in December, January, or February to experience Carnival or the Rio summer.
