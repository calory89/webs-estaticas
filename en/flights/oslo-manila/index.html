Best time to travel to Manila

December and January are dry months in the Philippines, perfect for beach and island activities. May to October is the rainy season, with potentially lower prices but less comfortable trips. Christmas (late December) is festive but may push prices up. If you are looking to save without sacrificing comfort, January is a good option after the Christmas peaks.

What to do when you arrive in Manila

Land at Ninoy Aquino International Airport (MNL), about 7 km from the city centre. Manila offers museums, colonial churches and vibrant nightlife. Easy access from there to Boracay, Palawan and Coron Islands. Eat at local markets, try Filipino cuisine and explore neighbourhoods like Intramuros. Before leaving Oslo, download offline maps and check local transport connections.
