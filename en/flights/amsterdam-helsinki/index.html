Best time to travel

Helsinki shines differently depending on the season. In winter (December to February) you'll enjoy the shortest days and the chance to see the Northern Lights, though it's bitterly cold. Spring and autumn are mild. Summer (June to August) offers white nights and pleasant temperatures. Any season works depending on what you're after, but book in advance if you're travelling during peak demand.

What to do when you arrive in Helsinki

The airport is well connected to the city centre. Walk around the White Cathedral in Senate Square, visit Uspenski Church (with its unmistakable red bricks) and stroll through the Katajanokka district. Design and architecture museums are a must. Eat at street markets and try Nordic local cuisine. If it's winter, head to nearby Lapland.
