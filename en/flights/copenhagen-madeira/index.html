Airlines and stops

Ryanair operates this route with one stop on the outbound journey. There are no direct flights between Copenhagen and Madeira, so expect a connection. The stop adds travel time but significantly cuts costs. Ryanair runs this connection regularly.

Best time to visit

Madeira enjoys mild weather year-round, but October to March sees fewer tourists and steadier prices. February and March are often cheap if you book ahead. Skip school holidays and Christmas when fares spike. Spring is excellent if you plan early.

What to do on arrival

Madeira is a trekking paradise: Pico Ruivo (1,861 m) delivers stunning views. Walk the Levadas, traditional water channels now turned into scenic trails. Funchal, the capital, has vibrant markets and a charming historic quarter in Porto. Black-sand beaches, botanical gardens and cliff hikes round it out. Cristiano Ronaldo airport (FNC) is 20 minutes from Funchal.

Are there direct flights from Copenhagen to Madeira?

What to see in Madeira besides beaches?

Madeira is far more than beaches. Hiking Pico Ruivo, walking the Levadas, Funchal's market, the historic quarter, dramatic cliffsides and tropical gardens are must-sees. Nature is the island's true treasure.
