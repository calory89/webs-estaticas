Best time to visit

November is ideal: pleasant weather, warm seas and fewer crowds than summer. Cyprus has sunshine almost year-round, so winter trips work too if you prefer fewer tourists and lower prices.

What to do when you arrive in Cyprus

Larnaca is your gateway to fine sandy beaches and crystal-clear waters. Explore Nicosia's old town, trek the Troodos mountains, visit Kykkos monastery or savour local food in any coastal village. Cyprus blends beach, history and nature.
