Best time to visit

Maldives shines from November to April, the dry season with minimal rain and clear skies. June to September is monsoon and the cheapest period, but expect more showers. For a balance between weather and price, May, October and late April work well. November, when the example flight is scheduled, is ideal: stable climate and not yet peak season.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Velana International Airport (MLE), the gateway to Maldives. From there, take a water taxi or speedboat to your resort. The Maldives is built for diving, snorkelling and private beaches: colourful coral, tropical fish and crystal-clear waters. You'll also find luxury spas, all-inclusive resorts and trips to uninhabited atolls.
