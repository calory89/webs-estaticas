What to do when you arrive

Hamburg packs plenty in: the port (Hafen), canals, red-light district, lakes and world-class museums. Fuhlsbüttel Airport (HAM) is 10 km from the centre with good train and bus links. Plan 3–4 days minimum on your first visit; it's a city blending history, art and modern life at a relaxed pace.
