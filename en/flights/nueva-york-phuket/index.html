Best time to go

Phuket has a tropical climate with a dry season from November to February, when rainfall is low and the sea is calm. Perfect for beach activities and diving. The monsoon season (May to October) sees fewer tourists and lower prices, but weather can be more unpredictable.

What to do when you arrive in Phuket

Phuket is the gateway to Thailand's beaches. Visit nearby islands by longtail boat, explore Buddhist temples like Wat Chalong, wander Patong Beach, or spend an evening at night markets. Local food is essential: try som tam and authentic curries away from tourist spots.
