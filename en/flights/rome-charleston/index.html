Best time to visit

What to do when you arrive

Charleston is a historic city with colonial architecture, upmarket dining and nearby beaches. Explore the historic district, visit Fort Sumter, enjoy the waterfront and try Lowcountry cuisine. The international airport is well connected to the city centre. There are tours, museums and a distinctive Southern charm.
