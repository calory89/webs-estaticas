Best time to travel

Sofia shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather and fewer crowds. Winters are cold but less touristy. Skip July–August if intense heat and peak season don't appeal. Travel in low or shoulder season for better hotel and activity prices, though flights are already budget-friendly.

What to do in Sofia

Bulgaria's capital boasts Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, Boyana Church (Unesco World Heritage), varied museums and lively nightlife. The old town blends Ottoman, Austro-Hungarian and Soviet architecture. Try local food at markets and taverns. Vitosha mountain is 30 minutes away for hiking and skiing. Daily budget is very low: food and accommodation are cheap.
