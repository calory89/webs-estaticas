Best time to visit

Vilna shines in summer (June–August) and early autumn. If you want to avoid crowds and peak prices, spring (April–May) is quiet and pleasant. Winter brings snow and cold, but if you enjoy that, December to February has its own charm.

What to do on arrival

Vilna International Airport is about 4 km from the centre. The Old Town is a Unesco World Heritage site: stroll its cobbled streets, climb Gediminas Tower for panoramic views, and visit the Gate of Dawn. Day trip: Trakai and its castle. Try beetroot soup and Lithuanian rye bread.

What can I see in Vilna?

The medieval Old Town is a must (Unesco World Heritage), Gediminas Tower offers views across the entire city, and Trakai Castle is just over an hour away. Local food is excellent: try cepelinai (stuffed potato dumplings) and Lithuanian beer.
