Air Serbia connects Amsterdam and Dubrovnik with one stopover from €206 return. The median price sits around €219, making this route very competitive for reaching the Dalmatian Coast.

Price and when to book

The cheapest flight hovers around €206 return. Sample prices we've found range from €206 to €225, with a median of €219. For this route, the standard advice is to book 4–6 weeks ahead. Since prices are tight, monitor fares regularly if your travel dates are flexible.

Airline and stops

Air Serbia operates this route with one stopover. It's not a direct flight, but the fares make up for the connection time. Check where your stopover is (usually Belgrade) and how much time sits between flights. Air Serbia is a mid-range carrier with solid value for money.

Best time to go

Dubrovnik shines from April to October. Summer (July–August) brings more tourists and higher prices, so consider May–June or September for better value and weather. Winter months (November–March) see much lower fares, but expect cooler temperatures and more rain. Book based on your weather preference, not on chasing a seasonal 'deal'.

What to do when you arrive

Dubrovnik is famous for its walled old town, complete city walls, and Adriatic views. The port is 20 minutes from the airport. Walk the Stradun (main street), explore monasteries and Fort St. John. Ferries from here reach the Elafiti Islands and Lokrum. Three or four days let you explore the city and surroundings properly.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Amsterdam to Dubrovnik?

No, this Air Serbia route includes a stopover (usually in Belgrade). Direct flights are not available at these prices.

How much is the cheapest flight?

From €206 return all-in with Air Serbia. Sample fares we've seen are €206, €219, and €225 depending on dates and availability.

Which airline flies this route?

Air Serbia operates Amsterdam–Dubrovnik. It offers strong value for money and is known for reliable punctuality.

What's the best time to visit Dubrovnik?

April to October offers good weather. May–June and September are ideal to dodge summer peak heat and crowds. Book well ahead if you're travelling those months.
