Madrid to Zurich flights start from €115 return. Our sample prices range from €115 to €212, with a median of €143. Book as early as possible. Compare flexible dates: shifting your travel day by one or two days can cut costs. This route analysis was run on 21 October 2026, so prices move.

Airlines and stops

Air Europa operates this route with direct Madrid-Zurich flights. No stops means less travel time and fewer baggage worries. A typical outbound flight departs around 07:40 from Madrid; the return leaves Zurich around 18:30. Direct means you board at MAD and land at ZRH.

Best time to visit

Switzerland shines year-round, but choose by what suits you. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild weather and fewer crowds. Winter (December–February) draws skiers; summer (June–August) is peak tourism and pricier. Book well ahead, especially around bank holidays and long weekends.

What to do when you arrive

Zurich is 12 km from the airport with excellent public transport: trains, trams and buses connect everything. In the city, stroll the Old Town, visit Zurich Cathedral, walk the lakeside, or explore the Swiss National Museum. Trains get you to the Alps, Lucerne or Bern easily. Don't miss Swiss chocolate and watches—they're the essentials.

FAQ

What is the cheapest price for Madrid to Zurich?

The lowest we found is €115 return with Air Europa. That is the baseline; you'll also find €143 (median) and higher depending on dates and seat availability.

Is the Madrid to Zurich flight direct?

Yes, Air Europa flies direct with no stops. You depart Madrid and land in Zurich in one flight.

What can I see in Zurich in a week?

The Old Town, lake, churches and museums fill 2–3 days easily. With a week, you have time for trips to Lucerne, Bern or the Alps. Swiss trains are fast and reliable.

When are flights to Zurich most expensive?

Peak summer (June–August) and school holidays push prices up. Winter ski season (December–February) is also costly. Book several weeks ahead to lock in a good fare.
