Best time to visit

Copenhagen is lovely in spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October): mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. If you fancy something festive, Tivoli's Christmas market (October-December) is iconic. Winter months (November-February) see lower fares but it's very cold. Summer (June-August) is peak season and flights go up.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Copenhagen Airport (CPH), 8 km from the city centre. The train gets you there in 15 minutes. Don't miss Nyhavn (historic canals), Amalienborg Palace, Tivoli, and the Christianshavn neighbourhood. It's a compact city: everything's walkable or a bike ride away. Grab a Danish smørrebrød sandwich and soak up the Scandinavian design everywhere.
