Best time to visit

Helsinki in winter (January–March) is stunning but very cold, ideal if you're chasing the Northern Lights. June to August brings milder weather and endless daylight. Autumn (September–October) offers fewer crowds, beautiful colours, and still-pleasant temperatures. Skip July unless you enjoy peak season tourism.

What to do when you arrive

Helsinki is compact and walkable. Explore Senate Square, admire the golden domes of Uspenski Cathedral, browse the National Museum, or try a traditional Finnish sauna (a must-do experience). The airport is 20 km from the centre, well connected by train, bus, or taxi.
