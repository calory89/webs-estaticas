Best time to visit

Vietnam is best visited between October and April, when the weather is dry and pleasant. November (like your sample dates, 16-23) is ideal: mild temperatures, less rain and no Christmas tourism rush. If you travel then, you'll dodge both the monsoon and peak tourist crowds, though Hanoi is always busy.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

You'll land at Noi Bai International Airport (HAN), 30 km from the city centre. Head to the Old Quarter to wander narrow, chaotic streets, eat pho and bánh mì from street stalls, visit Ho Chi Minh's Mausoleum and the Temple of Literature. Hoan Kiem Lake is perfect for a sunset walk. If you have spare days, jump to Halong Bay (UNESCO World Heritage) or Sapa in the northern mountains.
