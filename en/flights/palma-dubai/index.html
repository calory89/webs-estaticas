Best time to visit Dubai

Dubai shines from October to April when temperatures are pleasant (25–30°C). Summer is brutally hot, so skip it if you hate extreme heat. Prices adjust with demand, but there's no fixed rule for cheap months, it depends more on which specific weeks you choose and when you book.

What to do when you arrive in Dubai

Dubai offers the Burj Khalifa (world's tallest building), beaches, shopping at Dubai Mall, desert safaris and yacht cruises. Visit the old Al Fahidi district or pop next door to Abu Dhabi. Dubai International Airport (DXB) sits about 15 km from the centre, well connected by metro, taxi and bus.
