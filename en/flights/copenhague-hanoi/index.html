Best time to visit

Vietnam is rewarding year-round, but October to April offers drier, more pleasant weather in the north. Hanoi in these months sees moderate temperatures (15–25°C), ideal for wandering the Old Quarter and surroundings. Skip June–September if you prefer less rain. Booking with some lead time lets you adjust dates to suit your weather preference.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

Noi Bai International Airport is 25 km from the centre. The Old Quarter is essential: Jade Temple, Hoan Kiem Lake, chaotic narrow streets and local food. Halong Bay (Unesco site) and Sapa mountains are close by. Three or four days in Hanoi is a minimum to enjoy it at a relaxed pace.
