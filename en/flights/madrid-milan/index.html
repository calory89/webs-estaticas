What to do in Milan

The Cathedral (Duomo) is a must-see, along with Leonardo's Last Supper in the church of Santa Maria delle Grazie. Stroll through the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, explore the Brera neighbourhood with its galleries and terraces, and spend time at Castello Sforzesco. For shopping, the Quadrilateral of Fashion area is reliable. Don't miss the Navigli canals at sunset.
