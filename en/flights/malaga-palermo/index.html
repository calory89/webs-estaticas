Best time to visit

Palermo and Sicily are worth visiting year-round, but spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) offer ideal temperatures without summer heat. Prices tend to be more accessible in low season, though this route stays fairly stable in pricing.

What to do when you arrive in Palermo

You'll land at Palermo Airport (Falcone-Borsellino), about 35 km from the centre. Explore Vucciria street market, the Norman Cathedral, the Palatine Chapel and enjoy Sicilian cuisine. The city connects well to coastal towns like Mondello and Monreale. Try arancini, pasta con le sarde and granitas.

When is the best time to visit Palermo?

Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal for weather. Sicily is lovely in winter too, though it rains more. Avoid July–August if you don't like intense heat and crowds.
