Best time to visit

Belgrade shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather and fewer crowds. Winters are cold but the city buzzes with bohemian energy. Skip July–August if you dislike heat and peak tourism.

What to do when you arrive

Kalemegdan Fortress overlooks the river with stunning views. Walk Knez Mihailova, the main pedestrian street packed with shops and cafés. Eat in Vračar neighbourhood, try local ćevapi, and soak up nightlife along the Danube. Nikola Tesla Airport is 12 km away and well connected to the city centre.
