Best time to visit

Dubai is a year-round destination, though the best weather is November to March when temperatures are lower. Summer (June to August) can be very hot. Plan with flexible dates to take advantage of lower prices outside peak travel periods.

What to do in Dubai

Dubai airport (DXB) connects to the city in 20-30 minutes. Visit the Burj Khalifa, Dubai Marina waterfront, the traditional souks, and the beaches. Dubai offers everything from luxury shopping to traditional culture in the historic Al Fahidi district. Allow 3-4 days to enjoy the city properly.
