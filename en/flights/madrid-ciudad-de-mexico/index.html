Best time to visit

Mexico City is a year-round destination. December to February offers dry weather and pleasant temperatures. Rain occurs May through October, but afternoons are cooler. Avoid dates near major Mexican holidays if you prefer fewer crowds at attractions. Check the city's events calendar before booking to plan around festivals or peak tourism periods.

What to do in Mexico City

The capital offers endless options: the Historic Centre with the Metropolitan Cathedral, National Palace, Paseo de la Reforma, world-class museums (Anthropology, Frida Kahlo, Museo Tamayo). Teotihuacán ruins are an hour's drive away. Coyoacán and San Ángel are bohemian neighbourhoods with cafés and galleries. Food is a major attraction: street tacos to fine dining. Getting around is easy with an efficient metro system.
