Airline and stops

Wizz Air Malta operates direct flights from Lisbon to Rome. Travelling without stopovers saves time and hassle. Outbound departs at 09:05 and return at 06:25, so plan your airport transfers well. Wizz Air is known for competitive fares, but watch the hand luggage allowance included in your ticket.

Best time to travel

Rome is brilliant any time of year, but skip July and August if you hate crowds and scorching heat. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer fine weather and fewer tourists. Winter is quiet and cheaper, though some days are chilly. Check regularly: bargains can pop up in any month.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Fiumicino Airport (FCO), Rome's main hub. The regional train takes you to the centre in about 30 minutes for little cost. The must-sees: Colosseum, Roman Forum, Pantheon, Trevi Fountain. Walk through Trastevere neighbourhood, eat at a local trattoria and climb St Peter's Basilica if time allows. A well-packed rucksack and comfy shoes are your best friends.
