Best time to visit

Fort Lauderdale has subtropical weather. Avoid June to September due to hurricane season. October to April offers pleasant temperatures (68–81°F) and less rain. January and February are peak season in Florida, so for competitive fares, consider November, December or March.

What to do when you arrive

Fort Lauderdale is the gateway to South Florida's beaches and the Florida Keys. Fort Lauderdale Beach is 30 minutes from the airport. Explore the historic downtown, visit Miami (45 minutes away), or take a day trip to the Everglades. FLL airport connects well with local transport.
