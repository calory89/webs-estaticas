Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €38 per person return. Prices rise from there: €51 at the median, €62 for pricier fares. Book early—Ryanair typically increases fares as your departure date approaches. Aim for bookings 7 to 30 days before travel to spot the best deals.

Airline and flight type

Ryanair is your sole option on this route, and it delivers: direct flights from Zaragoza to Milan with no intermediate stops. Cabin baggage is included; checked luggage costs extra. Flight times are convenient: afternoon departures with arrivals that let you make the most of your day.

Best time to visit

Milan shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): comfortable temperatures, fewer crowds than summer. Skip July and August if you're budget-conscious—locals leave but tourists flood in. Winter (November–February) is cheaper and charming, though cold.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Milan airport (Malpensa or Linate, depending on your date). The centre is 15–20 km away: direct train in 40 minutes or bus. Must-see: the Cathedral, the Duomo, Galleria Vittorio Emanuele, La Scala Opera House. Neighbourhoods like Brera, Navigli, and Porta Romana are perfect for strolling, eating, and sampling Lombard cuisine. Milan is the design and fashion capital—if you shop, check out nearby outlets.

FAQ

Why are Zaragoza–Milan flights so cheap?

Ryanair connects these two cities with good frequency and direct service. Secondary routes typically cost less than major capitals. Milan's accessibility from across Spain keeps competition and prices low.

Are these direct flights or do I need a stopover?

Direct. Ryanair flies Zaragoza to Milan non-stop, saving time and connection costs.

Which Milan airport will I land at?

It depends on your booking date. Ryanair uses Milan Malpensa or Milan Linate. Both are close to the city centre with direct train or bus links. Check your booking confirmation to arrange transport.

What's included in the Ryanair fare at this price?

Hand baggage (40×20×25 cm) is included. Checked luggage (up to 20 kg) costs extra. Standard seat is included; priority seating has an additional charge. Always review your confirmation for full details.
