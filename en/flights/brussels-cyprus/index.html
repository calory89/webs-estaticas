Airline and stopovers

Wizz Air operates this route with one stopover. It's not a direct flight, but it's the low-cost option available. The stopover adds time to your journey, but keeps the price affordable. Check connection times and the stopover location when you book.

Best time to travel

Cyprus is a Mediterranean destination with pleasant weather year-round. June to September are the warmest months but also the busiest. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) offer comfortable temperatures and fewer crowds at beaches and attractions. Winter is the quietest season.

What to do when you arrive in Cyprus

You'll land in Larnaca, an airport well connected to the main tourist zones. Ayia Napa and Protaras beaches are popular with visitors. Medieval Nicosia, the Troodos Mountains and traditional villages in the highlands appeal to those after culture and history. Don't miss Greco-Roman archaeological sites.
