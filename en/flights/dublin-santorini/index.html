Best time to visit Santorini

Santorini shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): perfect weather, fewer crowds than July–August, and often lower fares. Avoid winter if beaches matter; some facilities close. April and October strike the best balance between sunshine and affordable flight options.

What to do when you arrive

Santorini captivates with white-washed clifftop villages (Oia is the postcard-perfect one), black volcanic-sand beaches, local wines and legendary sunsets. Explore Fira, swim at Kamari or Perissa, sail to Nea Kameni volcano, or catch a ferry to nearby Paros. The airport is 7 km from Fira; buses and taxis run to town.

When is the best time to book this trip?

What to see in Santorini besides beaches?

Oia and Fira villages are must-sees for architecture and views. Visit black-sand beaches like Kamari, taste local wine at vineyards, sail to nearby volcanoes, or ferry to other Aegean islands such as Paros or Naxos.
