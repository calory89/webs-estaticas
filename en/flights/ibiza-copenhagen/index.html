Best time to visit

Copenhagen is beautiful year-round, but each season has its own charm. The warmest months (June to August) draw more tourists, so prices tend to rise. If you're looking to save, consider autumn or winter: Copenhagen with snow is spectacular. Spring is also a solid choice with less crowds than summer.

What to do on arrival

Copenhagen awaits with Amalienborg Palace, Rosenborg Castle and the famous Nyhavn canals. Don't miss the Botanical Garden, the bohemian Christiania neighbourhood or a bike ride (Danes love cycling). Try local food and relax in cosy cafés. The airport is well connected to the city centre, just 25 minutes by train.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Ibiza to Copenhagen?

Not in this search. The flight we're showing has one stop. Although it adds travel time, the price (from €170) more than makes up for it.

Which airline operates this route?

Vueling flies this route. It's a reliable low-cost carrier with good European connections. Check what's included in your fare before purchasing.

When is the best time to visit Copenhagen?

April–May and September–October are ideal: pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Winter is cold but magical. July–August is peak season, with higher prices.

What can I do in Copenhagen?

Nyhavn is a must-see, as is Amalienborg Palace. Cycle around, visit Rosenborg, explore Christiania and enjoy Nordic cuisine. Three days are enough for the essentials.
