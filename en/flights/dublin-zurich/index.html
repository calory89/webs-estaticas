Fly Dublin to Zurich with Aer Lingus from €113 return. The median price sits at €114, so you'll find solid options without nasty surprises. A direct route straight into Switzerland's heart.

Price and when to book

Fares in the sample range from €113–120 return. Our advice: book in advance (2–3 months ahead is ideal for this route) to lock in the best rates. Prices creep up as departure nears, so don't leave it to the last week. Check midweek flights; they're typically cheaper than Friday and Sunday departures.

Airline and non-stop flights

Aer Lingus operates this route without stops: departures from Dublin (DUB) landing direct into Zurich (ZRH). A straightforward journey, no plane changes or annoying layovers. The Irish carrier has a solid reputation for punctuality and service on this European leg.

Best time to visit

Zurich appeals year-round. For reliable weather and reasonable prices, May–September (spring and summer) work well. Winter (December–February) brings snowy views and Christmas markets, though it's bitingly cold. Autumn (September–October) is calm with fewer crowds than peak summer.

What to do when you arrive

The airport (ZRH) connects to the city centre in 10–15 minutes by train. Explore the Altstadt (old town), stroll along Lake Zurich, visit the Kunsthaus art museum, or shop on Bahnhofstrasse. Switzerland is pricey overall, but museums are reasonable and Alpine scenery nearby is worth every euro. Buy public transport from the airport: it covers unlimited city travel.
