Best time to travel

Bogotá has an equatorial mountain climate with rain throughout the year. The driest months are December–March and July–August. Peak tourist season pushes prices up. If your priority is saving money, travel during quieter months, but remember the weather is unpredictable year-round.

What to do when you arrive in Bogotá

El Dorado international airport is 15 km from the city centre. Bogotá boasts world-class museums, La Candelaria's colonial architecture, and excellent restaurants. The city sits at 2,600 metres in the Andes, so allow time to acclimatise in your first days. Direct public transport links the airport to the city.
