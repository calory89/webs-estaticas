Best time to visit

Munich is beautiful year-round. Winter (December to February) often brings competitive prices during low season. Summer, especially June to August, sees higher demand and pricier flights. For warm weather and events, try May or September; for budget-friendly options, aim for November to March.

What to do when you arrive in Munich

Munich airport is 28 km from the city centre; take the S-Bahn train into town in about 45 minutes. Visit Marienplatz and the Cathedral, stroll through Nymphenburg Palace, enjoy a beer at a traditional biergarten, or explore the German Museum. If you're travelling in winter, don't miss the Christmas markets.
