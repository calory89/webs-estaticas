Best time to visit

Munich shines in winter (Christmas markets, nearby skiing) and spring (mild weather, fewer crowds). Skip July and August if you're hunting bargain fares. Shoulder months like October-November and February-March offer good value. Book further ahead for more affordable options.

What to do when you arrive

Munich is your gateway to Bavaria. Explore the historic centre, Marienplatz with its Gothic town hall, world-class museums, and nearby Nymphenburg Palace. Winter brings famous Christmas markets. The airport is 40 km out; take the S8 train to the centre in about 40 minutes.
