Best time to visit

Dubrovnik is a beach and culture destination on the Adriatic. Skip July and August if you want lower prices and fewer crowds. April–May and September–October are ideal: good weather, accessible beaches and less mass tourism. Prices tend to be keener outside peak season. November (as in our example) is already low season, with more competitive fares.

What to do when you arrive

Dubrovnik is famous for its walled Old Town, a Unesco World Heritage site, and nearby beaches. Walk the walls, visit the cathedral and monasteries, or relax at Banje Beach. Day trips from Dubrovnik reach islands like Lopud and Sipan. The airport is 20 km from the centre; use a taxi, shuttle or bus to reach the city. Allow 1–2 hours for transfer.

What to see in Dubrovnik besides the walls?

The cathedral, Franciscan monastery, Onofrio's Fountain and Stradun street are must-sees in the Old Town. Outside the centre, nearby beaches (Banje, Lapad) and trips to Dalmatian islands are very popular. Allow at least 2–3 days to make the most of the city.
