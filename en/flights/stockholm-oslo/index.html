Best time to visit

Stockholm and Oslo are appealing year-round. Winter (December to February) may offer lower prices but days are very short; summer (June to August) has perfect weather but is peak season. Spring and autumn provide a good balance of pleasant climate and reasonable prices. Check specific dates when booking to find your best option.

What to do when you arrive in Oslo

Oslo is perfect for a short break or as a base to explore Norway. Visit the Munch Museum, walk through Grünerløkka district, explore the harbour, and enjoy Nordic museums. With more time, take trips to the fjords or mountains. Oslo Airport (OSL) is 20 minutes from the city centre by train. Remember Oslo is expensive: budget carefully for your stay.
