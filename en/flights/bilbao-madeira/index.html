Best time to visit Madeira

Madeira offers pleasant weather year-round, but October to April is especially comfortable with mild temperatures and less rainfall. Spring (March–May) brings lush greenery and flowers. Summer is busier with more tourists. Always check the forecast before you go—mountain weather can shift quickly.

What to see and do in Madeira

Cristiano Ronaldo Airport (FNC) is 17 km from Funchal, the capital. Walk through the historic centre, the Lavradores market and the harbour. The levadas (historic water channels) are perfect for hiking. Beaches, botanical gardens, viewpoints over the ocean and charming villages round out the experience. Hire a car or use taxis and buses to explore the island.
