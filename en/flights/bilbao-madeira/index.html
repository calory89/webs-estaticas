Madeira has mild weather year-round, making it a destination in all four seasons. December is viable, as shown in the example. If you want to avoid crowds, spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) tend to be quieter. Prices don't drop by specific month, but booking well ahead is key.

What to do when you arrive

Cristiano Ronaldo Airport (FNC) is about 15 km from central Funchal. Madeira is known for spectacular hiking trails (such as Pico Ruivo), dramatic cliffs, botanical gardens and Funchal's historic harbour. Don't miss the cable cars, traditional markets and seafront promenades. It's perfect for nature, hiking and relaxation with Atlantic views.
