Price and when to book

Flights start from €777 return. Prices vary depending on when you book and which dates you choose: we've seen fares ranging from €777 to €1,035 in our search. The more advance notice you give, the better. If you're after maximum savings, stay flexible with dates and check deals weekly.

Airline and stops

Best time to travel

Low season months (late January, September to November) offer better prices. If you can choose, avoid peak periods (Christmas, Easter, summer). Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Thursdays usually have more competitive fares than Fridays and Sundays.

Burbank is your gateway to the Los Angeles area. From the airport you have bus, car rental or transport services. The city opens doors to attractions like the Hollywood Sign, Griffith Observatory, Santa Monica beaches, and film studios. Allow at least 3–4 days to enjoy the area at a comfortable pace.
