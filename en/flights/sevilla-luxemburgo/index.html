Best time to visit

December is ideal: Luxembourg shines with Christmas markets and festive lights. Prices from October onwards are competitive. If you prefer warmer weather, avoid winter, but expect higher fares in other seasons.

What to do in Luxembourg

Luxembourg is small but packed with charm. Explore the Old Town (UNESCO), walk through the historic centre, climb the ramparts and enjoy the viewpoints. In December, Christmas markets round off the experience. Luxembourg-Findel Airport is 6 km from the city centre with good bus connections.

No. Ryanair operates this route with 1 stop on both outbound and return. Check where your stopover is to plan your journey properly.

What's the cheapest price I've seen?

Which airline am I flying with?

Ryanair, which offers this route from Seville to Luxembourg.

What can I see in Luxembourg in a week?

The Old Town (UNESCO), rampart viewpoints, the Grand Ducal Palace, parks like Pétrusse, and nearby towns such as Echternach or Vianden. Rent a car or use local buses to get around.
