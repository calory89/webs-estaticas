Best time to travel

Peru has two clear seasons: dry (May to October) and wet (November to April). May to October is best for clear skies and minimal rain. Lima stays warm year-round. Plan based on where you're going: if you include the Highlands or Machu Picchu, the dry season is ideal.

What to do when you arrive

You land in Lima, the capital. Visit the Historic Centre (Unesco World Heritage), the Anthropology Museum, Miraflores cliffs and Peruvian cuisine, rated among South America's best. From there, arrange trips to Machu Picchu, the Inca Trail, Nazca or the Amazon.
