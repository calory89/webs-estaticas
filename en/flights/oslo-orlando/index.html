Airline and stops

Best time to visit

Orlando shines year-round, but avoid June and September if you don't fancy monsoons. December is ideal: perfect weather and parks without extreme crowds. January to April and October to November are solid months too. Book in advance even when travelling off-season to lock in these fares.

What to do when you arrive

Orlando is theme park heaven: Disney World, Universal and SeaWorld dominate. The arrival airport is MCO (Orlando International), well connected. Beyond the parks, explore downtown, the lakes of Winter Park or the nearest beach at Cocoa Beach (one hour away). Rent a car to get around without hassle.
