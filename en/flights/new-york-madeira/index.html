Price and when to book

New York-Madeira flights start from €566 return. The median price is €592, though you can find options up to €613 depending on your dates. To get better fares, book at least 2 months ahead. Prices fluctuate with season and availability, so booking early gives you more chances to save.

Airline and stopovers

TAP Portugal operates this route with 1 stopover. Flights include a connection, typically in Lisbon, before reaching Madeira. TAP is Portugal's leading airline and offers a good balance between price and service on this Atlantic route.

Best time to visit

Madeira has subtropical weather year-round, but April to October is ideal with less rainfall and warmer temperatures. These months are pricier, so if you're budget-conscious consider November to March when flights are cheaper and fewer tourists visit, though expect more rain.

What to do when you arrive

Madeira is perfect for hiking, black sand beaches, exploring Funchal's historic centre and visiting botanical gardens. Don't miss the Levada paths (trails with stunning views), the Botanical Garden cable car and the harbour area. Madeira airport (FNC) is 17 km from Funchal with easy bus or taxi access.
