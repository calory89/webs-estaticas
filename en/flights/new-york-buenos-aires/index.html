Best time to visit

Buenos Aires shines during spring (September to November) and autumn (March to May in the Southern Hemisphere). Both seasons offer pleasant weather and lower rainfall. Skip July and August unless you fancy the austral winter. Lower demand in winter can mean cheaper fares, but pack warm clothes.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Ministro Pistarini (Ezeiza), about 35 km from the centre. Explore iconic neighbourhoods: La Boca, San Telmo, Recoleta. Don't miss Colón Theatre and live tango. Eat well: steakhouses, alfajores, and Argentine wine are essential. Note the local currency (Argentine peso) and check the official exchange rate before you go.
