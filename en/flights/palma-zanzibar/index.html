What to do on arrival

Zanzibar is renowned for white-sand beaches, coral reefs and historic Stone Town. The international airport (ZNZ) is about 50 km from the island centre: taxis, transfers and car hire are available. Once there, snorkel, dive, tour spice plantations, wander the Stone Town markets or unwind at a resort. The island is small and easy to navigate between attractions.
