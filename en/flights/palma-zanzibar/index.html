Best time to visit

Zanzibar is a tropical destination where weather and prices shift seasonally. Shoulder periods, transitions between seasons, typically offer a sweet spot between decent weather and lower fares than peak season. Check local climate before booking: rainfall and humidity can affect your experience. Plan 2–3 months ahead for more flexibility on price and availability.

What to do on arrival

Zanzibar is renowned for white-sand beaches, coral reefs and historic Stone Town. The international airport (ZNZ) is about 50 km from the island centre: taxis, transfers and car hire are available. Once there, snorkel, dive, tour spice plantations, wander the Stone Town markets or unwind at a resort. The island is small and easy to navigate between attractions.
