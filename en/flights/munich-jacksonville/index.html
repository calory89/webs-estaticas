Best time to visit

Jacksonville enjoys pleasant weather year-round. November and April–May are ideal: mild temperatures (20–25°C) with little rain and no hurricane risk. July and August are hot, humid and uncomfortable. June to October is hurricane season in Florida, though Jacksonville sits further north than other cities. Climate patterns don't always shift prices, so prioritise your own dates.

What to do when you arrive

Jacksonville is your gateway to North Florida beaches: Amelia Island, Jacksonville Beach and Ponte Vedra. The city boasts the Cummer Museum of art, a revitalised riverfront and a growing food scene. Rent a car and you're 45 minutes from St. Augustine or several natural parks. JAX airport is just 13 km from downtown.
