Best time to visit Belgrade

Belgrade is pleasant year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: mild weather and fewer crowds. Winters are cold, summers hot. Skip Christmas and New Year if you want to avoid peak tourism.

What to do when you arrive

Belgrade awaits with vibrant nightlife, Kalemegdán Fortress overlooking the Danube, museums like the Nikola Tesla Museum, and neighbourhoods with character such as Zemun. Eat well: Serbian food is hearty and affordable. Nikola Tesla Airport is 20 km from the centre; use public transport or a taxi.
