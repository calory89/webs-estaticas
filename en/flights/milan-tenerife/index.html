Ryanair connects Milan with Tenerife on direct flights from €60 return. Median price sits at €68, so booking early makes a real difference. Check daily: prices move constantly.

Price and when to buy

Prices range from €60 to €108 in the sample checked. The earlier you book, the better: Ryanair lifts fares as dates approach. Book 2–3 months ahead if you can. Check every few days to catch occasional deals.

Airline and stopovers

Fly Ryanair on a direct route with no stops. That saves time and hassle. Bear in mind Ryanair is budget: cabin baggage is included in base fare, but checked bags cost extra.

Best time to visit

Tenerife works year-round with decent weather. Winter (December–February) draws heavy European tourism, so fares and airport crowds rise. Spring and autumn tend to be cheaper and easier to get around.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Tenerife South. Volcanic beaches, Mount Teide, coastal towns like La Laguna. You don't need a car near the coast, but one opens more ground. Sort airport transport before you fly.

FAQ

Is Ryanair really cheaper than other airlines to Tenerife?

On this route, Ryanair is the operator from Milan. It's budget, so check what's in the base price: cabin bag included, checked baggage costs extra.

How much does a direct Milan-Tenerife flight usually cost?

Checked prices range from €60 to €108 return with Ryanair. Median is €68. No way to predict the dips, but early booking always helps.

Are there stopovers on the Milan-Tenerife flight?

No, it's direct. Ryanair runs this route nonstop, so you land just once.

What is there to see and do in Tenerife?

Mount Teide (volcanic peak), black-sand beaches, mountain villages, boat trips. Depends on your stay length. Coast is reachable by public transport; Teide needs more planning.
