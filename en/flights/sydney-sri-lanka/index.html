Price and when to book

The lowest recorded price is €624 return. Most tickets range between €699 and €1,088. For this route, book as early as possible, the sooner you purchase, the lower the fares. Check for deals regularly from 2–3 months before your trip.

Airline and stopovers

Batik Air Malaysia operates this route with 1 stopover on the outbound flight. Departure from Sydney is 12 November 2026 at 09:50; return from Colombo is 22 November at 10:25. One stopover is standard on this route and lets you stretch your legs without adding excessive journey time.

Best time to visit

December to March is dry season in Sri Lanka, perfect for beaches and outdoor activities. May to September brings more rain but also fewer tourists and potentially cheaper fares if you snag a deal. November, as in the example, marks the start of peak season, so book early.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Bandaranaike International Airport in Colombo (CMB), about 30 km from the city centre. From there, explore Buddhist temples on the south coast, swim at Mirissa beach, take a scenic train ride to Kandy, or spot wildlife in national parks. Sri Lanka is compact and easy to travel; a week is ideal.
