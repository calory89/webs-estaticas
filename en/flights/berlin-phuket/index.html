Best time to travel

Thailand has the best weather from November to February: mild temperatures and less monsoon rain. High season pushes prices up but everything runs at full capacity. If you're chasing savings, May to September are cheaper, though more rainfall. The sample flight shown is in December—a solid middle ground.

What to do in Phuket

Phuket is the gateway to stunning beaches: Kata, Karon, and Patong have warm waters and nightlife. Day trips run to Phi Phi Islands and Phang Nga Bay (The Morning After the Wedding's bay). The Old Town has authentic Thai temples. Eat at street markets and try pad thai fresh-cooked. August is peak tourists, but December and January are ideal: perfect weather and packed but manageable beaches.

The lowest fare we've tracked is €678 return (searched 22 October 2026). Median is €733. Prices climb as departure nears, so booking well in advance is essential.

Is it a direct flight Berlin to Phuket?

No, it has 1 stopover. That's standard on this long-haul route. The stopover lengthens the journey but it's how Berlin-Phuket flights typically operate.

Which airline operates this route?

Etihad Airways on the fares we're showing. Etihad connects Berlin and Phuket well, with stopovers at its Abu Dhabi hub or similar. It's a quality carrier on long-haul routes.

When is the best time to fly to Phuket?

November to February is peak season: dry weather, 25–32°C, clean beaches. Avoid June to October (monsoon). December and January see peak tourism but it's worth it for the weather. Travelling off-season saves on hotels but expect more rain.
