Best time to visit

San Francisco is lovely year-round, but skip July-August if you want to avoid crowds and peak prices. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer great weather and fewer tourists. Winter (December-February) can be rainy but brings lower fares. Your €652 budget suggests there are affordable flights in low season, make the most of it.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at San Francisco International Airport (SFO), 23 km from the city centre. From there, take BART (the rapid transit train) to town for around €10. Don't miss the Golden Gate Bridge, Alcatraz, Fisherman's Wharf, and explore Mission District. Hire a bike, it's the best way to discover the bay.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Málaga to San Francisco?

No, this route includes one stopover. TAP Portugal handles the connection, which is standard for this transatlantic journey. The price from €652 already includes the stopover.

What's the cheapest price you've found?

The lowest recorded price is €652 return. The average sits around €688, so if you search carefully and book in advance, you can get close to that figure.

How many days in advance should I book?

For a transatlantic flight like this, book at least 60-90 days before your departure date. The earlier, the better: waiting until 2-3 weeks before, prices rise sharply.

What else is there to do in San Francisco besides the Golden Gate?

Explore Chinatown, visit Alcatraz prison (book online), stroll the Ferry Building, check out Haight-Ashbury, and eat in Mission District. The weather is cool even in summer, so bring a jumper.
