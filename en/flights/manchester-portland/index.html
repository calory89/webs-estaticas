Best time to travel

November in Portland is excellent: mild temperatures (10–15 °C), moderate rain, and fewer tourists than summer. The city looks beautiful in late autumn. Want cheaper tickets? Avoid UK school holidays (half-term in February, Easter, summer break). Fares spike then.

What to do when you arrive

Explore downtown Portland, galleries, and Forest Park with its trails. Portland is famous for craft coffee, hit the local roasters. Don't miss the Portland Art Museum and the Saturday Market if timing works. PDX airport is 20 minutes from the city centre. Cheap buses, taxis, and car rentals available. The city is walkable and backpacker-friendly.
