Best time to visit

Milan is worth visiting year-round. Winter (December to February) suits Christmas trips, shopping and fewer crowds. Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to October) offer pleasant weather and moderate prices. Check multiple dates to see real variations.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in the Milan metropolitan area. The Cathedral (Duomo), Sforzesco Castle, La Scala Opera House and the Brera neighbourhood are must-sees. Milan is fashion, art and food; walk Navigli at dusk, try risotto alla milanese and visit museums like the Pinacoteca di Brera.
