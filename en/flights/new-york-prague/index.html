Best time to visit

Prague is worth visiting year-round, but spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer good weather without peak summer crowds. Fares vary by season, so look for flexible dates. November (like in the example) is low season, which can work in your favour on pricing.

What to do when you arrive

Prague is a UNESCO World Heritage site: Prague Castle, Charles Bridge, Old Town Square and the Astronomical Clock are must-sees. Václav Havel Airport (PRG) is 20 km from the centre; bus, train or taxi are common options. The local currency is the Czech crown, though euros are accepted in some places. The city is very affordable and perfect for 3-5 days.
