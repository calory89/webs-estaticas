Best time to visit

San Juan is a year-round destination, but the best period is December to March with dry weather and pleasant temperatures. Avoid September and October if possible due to hurricane season in the Caribbean. Prices tend to be higher during European holidays and school breaks.

What to do on arrival

San Juan offers beaches, Old San Juan with colonial architecture, and access to the Virgin Islands. Luis Muñoz Marín Airport is 10 km from the city centre. You can rent a car, take a taxi or bus from there. Don't miss El Yunque and beaches like Rincon or Culebra if you extend your stay.
