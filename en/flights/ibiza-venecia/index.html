Airline and stops

Best time to travel

Venice attracts visitors year-round, but fares are usually lower outside peak season (July, August, Easter and public holidays). If your dates are flexible, travel between October and April for cheaper flights. January, February and November typically offer lower prices.

What to do when you arrive

Marco Polo is Venice's main airport, about 13 km from the centre. From there, take a bus, vaporetto or water taxi into the city. Venice is known for its canals, St Mark's Basilica, the Rialto Bridge and St Mark's Square. Book accommodation on the main island or in cheaper nearby areas like Mestre.

What can I see in Venice?

St Mark's Basilica, Rialto Bridge, St Mark's Square, the Grand Canal and historic churches. It's best to explore the city on foot or by boat. From Venice you can take day trips to islands like Murano and Burano.
