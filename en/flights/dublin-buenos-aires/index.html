Best time to travel

Dublin has a temperate maritime climate; Buenos Aires offers pleasant spring and autumn (September–November and March–May in the Southern Hemisphere). Demand to Argentina peaks in European summer (June–August) and drops in Northern winter, which may affect prices. Choose dates outside local holidays for better availability of cheap seats.

What to do when you arrive in Buenos Aires

Ministro Pistarini International Airport (Ezeiza) is your gateway. Reach the centre by bus, remise or transfer. Buenos Aires awaits with stunning European-style architecture, La Boca, San Telmo, live tango, world-class beef and wine, and South America's most vibrant nightlife. Spend at least 3–4 days exploring the main neighbourhoods.

What's worth seeing in Buenos Aires?

La Boca (with Caminito), San Telmo (tango and culture), the Metropolitan Cathedral, Teatro Colón, Recoleta (cemetery and shopping), Puerto Madero, and local markets. Eat Argentine beef and try Mendoza wine. The city deserves at least 4–5 days.
