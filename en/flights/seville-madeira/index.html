Fly from Seville to Madeira from €125 return with Ryanair. The median price sits around €146, so you've got options across the board. Madeira awaits with beaches, mountains and eternal spring.

Price and when to book

Prices start from €125, with a median of €146. Book as early as possible: the sooner you reserve, the better your chances of grabbing those lower fares. All prices shown are return, no hidden fees.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with one stopover. It's not a direct flight, but the prices make it worthwhile. Use the stopover to stretch your legs and grab a quick bite.

Best time to visit

Madeira is a year-round destination, but October to March offers pleasant weather without the crowds. If you want to avoid peak season and higher prices, aim for the quieter months. The island enjoys mild temperatures thanks to its Atlantic location.

What to do when you arrive

Land at Madeira Airport (Cristiano Ronaldo) and hire a car for freedom of movement. Hike Pico Ruivo, stroll Funchal, visit Cabo Girao cliffs, relax on Praia da Calheta beach. Eat espada preta (local fish) and try poncha, the traditional Madeiran drink.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Seville to Madeira?

No, this route includes one stopover. Ryanair is the airline operating these flights from Seville to Madeira.

What's the lowest price I've seen?

From €125 return. The median sits at €146, so you'll find options in that range if the lowest fare sells out.

Which airline flies this route?

Ryanair connects Seville to Madeira. Check their baggage policy: hand luggage is free, but checked bags come with an extra charge.

Where will I land in Madeira?

At Madeira Airport (Cristiano Ronaldo), about 15 km from Funchal. From there, bus, taxi or car hire will get you into town.
