The cheapest ticket we found was €135, but expect around €148 as standard. Our sample also showed prices at €165. Book as early as possible for better fares. This route moves regularly, so don't leave it to the last minute.

Best time to visit

Copenhagen works year-round. Spring (May-June) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is cold but festive. Check Ryanair's calendar for flight availability on your preferred dates.

What to do on arrival

Walk through Nyhavn, the iconic colourful canalside neighbourhood. Visit Amalienborg Palace, Tívoli (the historic amusement park) and taste Nordic cuisine. Copenhagen Airport connects well to the city centre by public transport. Spend at least 3-4 days to enjoy it properly.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Zaragoza to Copenhagen?

No. Ryanair operates this route with one stopover. It's not direct, but it's the available option for price and frequency. Check your connection before travelling.

Which airline flies Zaragoza-Copenhagen?

Ryanair operates this route. If you want alternatives, Zaragoza Airport connects to other European hubs from where you can reach Copenhagen with other carriers, but it'll likely cost more.

What to see in Copenhagen in 3 days?

Nyhavn (canalside walk), Amalienborg (royal palace), Tívoli (historic park), Christiania (alternative neighbourhood), Strøget (shopping street) and Vesterbro's cafés. Eat smorrebrod (Danish open sandwiches) and try local Danish beer. It's a walkable and welcoming city.
