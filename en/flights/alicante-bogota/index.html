What to do on arrival

Bogotá is Colombia's capital, situated at 2,600 metres above sea level. Visit La Candelaria (historic centre), the Gold Museum, Monserrate and the city's cultural scene. El Dorado international airport is 15 km from the city centre. From there, you can use public transport, taxi or book a transfer in advance for a smooth journey to your accommodation.
