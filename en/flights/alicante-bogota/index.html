Best time to travel

Bogotá enjoys mild weather year-round due to its altitude (2,600 metres). The dry season (December–March and July–August) is ideal for visiting. Keep in mind flight prices vary with Spanish and Colombian holidays and school breaks. Check the calendar before fixing your dates.

What to do when you arrive

Bogotá is a cultural capital: world-class museums (Gold Museum, Botero), Monserrate with panoramic views, La Candelaria for colonial architecture. Eat well (excellent Colombian food), head to Usaquén for nightlife and shopping. With extra days, daytrips to Zipaquirá (Salt Cathedral) or Chia are nearby.
