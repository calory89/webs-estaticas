Airline and stopovers

Best time to visit

Amsterdam appeals year-round, but avoid peak months (June–August) if you want fewer crowds. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer good weather and more moderate fares. Winter is peaceful, though colder and with shorter days.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Amsterdam Airport Schiphol (AMS), 9 km from the city centre. Train or bus reach the centre in 15–20 minutes. In the city: stroll the canals, visit the museums (Van Gogh, Rijksmuseum), explore the Red Light District, try croquettes and stroopwafels. Amsterdam works well for 3–4 days.

What should I see in Amsterdam?

The canals are most iconic: explore them by boat or on foot. Don't miss the Van Gogh Museum and Rijksmuseum. Try local food (croquettes, fresh herring, stroopwafels). Three to four days gives you enough time to enjoy the city properly.
