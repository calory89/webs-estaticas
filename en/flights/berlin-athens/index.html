Best time to visit Athens

Athens appeals year-round, but October and November are ideal: mild weather, fewer crowds than summer, and lower fares. December to February is cooler, while June to September is peak season with intense heat and pricier flights. Plan outside summer if you want to save.

What to do when you arrive in Athens

Athens awaits with the Acropolis, the Parthenon, and world-class museums. Stroll through Plaka, the old neighbourhood, or climb Lycabettus Hill at sunset. Ferries from Piraeus port connect to Aegean islands if you fancy extending your stay. The international airport is 30 km from the centre; bus, metro, and taxi are quick transport options.
