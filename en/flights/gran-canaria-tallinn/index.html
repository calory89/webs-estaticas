Best time to visit Tallinn

Tallinn shines in summer (June to August) with long daylight hours and pleasant weather. Winter is spectacular if you love snow and ice (December to February). October and November are cheaper and quieter. Booking two to three months ahead gives you more flexibility on pricing.

What to do in Tallinn

The medieval Old Town is essential: city walls, stone churches and cobbled squares. Kiek in de Kök, a defensive tower, offers 360° views. Visit Kadriorg, a Baroque palace surrounded by parks. Contemporary art museums and the Occupation Museum are unmissable. Eat at traditional Old Town restaurants: try barley soup and local rye bread.
