Best time to travel

November is a solid window: mild weather, less rain than summer, and outside the cherry blossom tourist rush. The prices shown here reflect availability in that period. If you want flexibility, try travelling between October and November to dodge peak demand.

What to do when you arrive

Kyoto is Japan's cultural heart: ancient temples, geishas in Gion, the Arashiyama bamboo grove and Zen gardens await. From Kansai, you can take a train to central Kyoto in just over an hour. The city deserves at least 3–4 days to explore its heritage properly. Book popular sites and walks in advance.
