Best time to travel

Oslo is spectacular in winter if you fancy snow and northern lights, though it requires heavy clothing. Summer (June–August) offers long days and pleasant temperatures, perfect for exploring the city and the fjords. Spring and autumn are calm and less crowded. Choose based on your weather preference; prices don't fluctuate predictably by season.

What to do when you arrive

Oslo boasts world-class museums (The Scream Museum, Viking Ship Museum) and stunning parks like Vigeland, plus a thriving food scene. You can easily reach Norwegian fjords by train or excursion. A public transport pass is worth it for multiple days. Walk through Grünerløkka for authentic local life.
