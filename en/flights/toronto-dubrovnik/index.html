Toronto to Dubrovnik starts at €722 return with Air Transat. The median price hovers around €741, so you'll find competitive fares if you book in advance. Fly to the Dalmatian coast without breaking the bank.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €722 for a return trip, with a median of €741. Book as early as possible: flights to Croatia fill up quickly during peak season. October and November are good months to book without rush, as prices tend to climb closer to departure.

Airline and stopovers

Air Transat operates this route with 1 stopover on the outbound leg. It's not a direct flight, but it's the most competitive option from Toronto. Always check your booking: the stopover can vary by date, but your connection is included.

Best time to visit

Dubrovnik shines from May to October: perfect weather, warm sea, and plenty to do. Travel in shoulder season (May, June, September, October) to avoid crowds and get better prices than July and August. Winter is quieter but cooler; autumn is ideal.

What to do when you arrive

Dubrovnik is more than the medieval walls, though they're worth the visit. Walk the Old Town, cross the Lovrijenac bridge, take a ferry to the Elafiti islands or Lokrum. The Dalmatian coast offers hidden beaches, local wines, and first-rate food. The airport is 20 km away; a bus or transfer gets you to the centre in half an hour.

FAQ

What's the cheapest flight price from Toronto to Dubrovnik?

€722 return is the best rate we've found. The median sits at €741, so you'll often land around that price. Book early to secure the lowest fare.

Does Air Transat fly direct from Toronto to Dubrovnik?

No, there's 1 stopover on the outbound leg. Air Transat is the key carrier on this route and offers the best price, but you'll need to plan for that intermediate stop.

When is the best time to visit Dubrovnik?

May to October is ideal: good weather, warm sea, and plenty to see. September-October is especially good because it combines fine weather with fewer crowds than summer.

What to see in Dubrovnik besides the walls?

The Elafiti islands, nearby beaches, Lovrijenac bridge, the cathedral, local cuisine, and Dalmatian wines. It's a complete destination for beach, culture, and food.
