Cheap flights London to Hamburg: from €81 return

London and Hamburg are just over an hour apart by direct flight with Ryanair. Prices start at €81 per person return, with a median of €90. The best way to travel between these two cities is to book in advance and skip the stopovers.

Price and when to book

Base price is €81 return per person, though the median sits around €90. Our sample also shows fares of €154 on certain dates. As a general rule, the earlier you book, the better your price: don't wait until the last minute. The gap between the lowest and highest fares is significant, so planning ahead really matters.

Airline and direct flight

Ryanair operates this route without stops. The outbound departs London at 10:10 and arrives in Hamburg non-stop, saving you time and hassle. The return flight leaves at 17:10. Flying direct is more comfortable and cuts down the risk of delays or baggage issues.

Best time to visit

Hamburg is worth visiting in spring, summer and especially during the Christmas season for its festive markets. Winter is cold but quieter. Avoid school holiday periods if you're chasing the cheapest fares, though we can't guarantee that as a fixed rule.

What to do when you arrive in Hamburg

Hamburg airport (Fuhlsbüttel) is about 9 km from the city centre. Don't miss the port, the Elbe river, St. Michael's Church with panoramic views, the Speicherstadt canals and the Reeperbahn district. Local food is excellent: try rye bread and Hamburg-style herring. The city is walkable and well connected by public transport.

FAQ

What is the cheapest price from London to Hamburg?

From €81 return per person. That's the lowest we've seen in our search. Most fares hover around €90.

Is the Ryanair flight from London to Hamburg direct?

Yes, it's completely direct with no stops. Departure from London at 10:10 and arrival in Hamburg non-stop.

Which airline flies from London to Hamburg?

Ryanair operates this route. It's a good option if you want budget fares, though remember that free cabin baggage allowance is limited.

What to see in Hamburg?

The port, Speicherstadt (historic warehouse district), the Elbe river, St. Michael's Church, Reeperbahn (nightlife) and local markets. It's a very walkable city.
