Best time to travel

January is interesting for prices, but January in Prague is very cold (average -2°C). For better weather, aim for May, June or September, when temperatures are 15-20°C and there's less rain. Avoid December if you want base fare prices—the Christmas markets drive prices up, though if you love Christmas markets, December is magical.

What to do when you arrive

Václav Havel Airport (PRG) is 20 km from the centre. Head to Charles Bridge for classic views, climb up to Prague Castle and walk through Old Town Square. Eat at any local beer hall and try Czech beer—the best is in traditional pubs like U Flekú. The Modern Art Museum and the Old Synagogue are worth your time.
