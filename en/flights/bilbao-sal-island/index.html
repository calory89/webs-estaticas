Best time to travel

December is a good time to visit Sal Island: temperatures are pleasant and rainfall is low. The climate is dry and sunny, perfect for the beach. Rainy months are from August to October, so best avoided if you want to guarantee good weather. Keep in mind that December is peak season for sun and beach tourism, so accommodation can be pricier.

What to do when you arrive at Sal Island

Sal Island is known for its white sand beaches and crystal-clear waters. Santa Maria is the most touristy village, with bars and restaurants by the sea. You can dive, windsurf or simply relax on the beach. There's also Bufareira Nature Park if you're looking for something quieter. The international airport (SID) is close to the main tourist areas.
