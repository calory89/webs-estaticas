The cheapest flight is around €825 return. The median sits at €914, with tickets up to €995 during peak demand. Book as early as possible: like most routes, the last seats go up in price. Don't wait until end of month or public holidays if you want to save.

Airline and stops

Ryanair is the airline on this route. The flight has 3 stops from Zaragoza to Cancún, so it's not direct. Expect a longer journey, but the price makes it worthwhile. Multiple stops are part of the game when hunting low-cost fares on intercontinental routes.

Best time to visit

Cancún is a year-round beach destination, but high season runs November to April: dry weather, no rain and pleasant temperatures. June to October is rainy season, fewer tourists and potentially cheaper prices, though hurricane risk exists. Choose based on your heat tolerance and crowd preference.

What to do when you arrive in Cancún

Cancún is a gateway to the Riviera Maya: white-sand beaches, cenotes, Mayan ruins like Tulum and Chichén Itzá. Isla Mujeres and Playa del Carmen are short distances away. Eat tacos, snorkel in reefs, explore the jungle. The airport is 25 km from the centre, with direct buses and shuttles to resorts.
