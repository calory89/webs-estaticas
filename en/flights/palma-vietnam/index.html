Best time to visit

Vietnam has a tropical climate. October to April is ideal: less heat and less rain. These months usually see higher prices due to demand. To save, consider May-September, though it's rainy season in the north. Regardless of when you go, book well in advance.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

Noi Bai International Airport is 25 km from the centre. You can take a taxi, bus or private transfer. Don't miss the Old Quarter, the Temple of Literature, Ho Chi Minh's Mausoleum and Ha Long Bay (just hours away). Eat at street markets and try pho and Vietnamese coffee.

FAQ

What's the lowest price for Palma-Hanoi flights?

The lowest price is €646 return. This is the best price found in the sample. Prices may vary by date, availability and how far in advance you book.

Does Vueling fly direct from Palma to Hanoi?

No, the flight includes 2 stopovers. It's a route that requires connections, so prepare for a long journey. Connection times and total duration depend on where those stops are.

When is the best time to travel to Vietnam?

October to April is best: pleasant weather, less rain. May-September is rainy season, especially in the north, but fewer tourists. Book in advance regardless of when you travel.

What to see in Hanoi after landing?

Hanoi's Old Quarter is a must, plus the Temple of Literature, Ho Chi Minh's Mausoleum and city lakes. Ha Long Bay, a UNESCO site, is just hours away. Don't miss the street food scene.
