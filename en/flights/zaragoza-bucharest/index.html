Best time to visit

Bucharest is appealing year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winters are cold but festive, perfect for Christmas or New Year breaks. Plan several months ahead for cheaper fares.

What to do when you arrive in Bucharest

Romania's capital blends history, art and nightlife. Visit Stavropoleos Church, the Parliament Palace (the world's heaviest building) and wander the Old Town. The Museum of Modern Art and Calea Victoriei Avenue are must-sees. Restaurants serve local cuisine at very reasonable prices.
