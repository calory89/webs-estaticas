Best time to go

November is a good month for Stockholm: cool temperatures (5-10°C), less summer tourism and the first spectacular winter sunsets. December brings Christmas markets but colder weather. For lower prices, travel outside July-August and December. Book early regardless of season.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Stockholm-Arlanda airport. In the city, don't miss Gamla Stan (old town), the Royal Palace, the Vasa Museum and the archipelagos. Stockholm is perfect for walking and has good public transport from the airport. In November the city is peaceful and winter lights start to shine.
