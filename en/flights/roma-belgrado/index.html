Best time to visit

Belgrade is a year-round destination. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant weather and fewer tourists than summer. Winter is cooler but ideal if you prefer fewer crowds and local café culture. Travel based on your temperature preferences and planned activities.

What to do when you arrive

Belgrade is a Balkan capital with intense nightlife, interesting museums and affordable food. The bohemian Savamala district, Kalemegdan Fortress and Danube river cruises are essential visits. The city centre is well-connected by public transport from Nikola Tesla Airport, 12 km away.
