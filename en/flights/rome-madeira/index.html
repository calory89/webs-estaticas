Price and when to book

The base price is €139, though most flights hover around €159 return. We've seen fares up to €174. As with any flight route, booking early works best: 4–6 weeks ahead usually gives you more options. Avoid last-minute bookings if you're chasing the lowest fare.

Airline and connections

Wizz Air Malta operates this route with one stop. It's not direct, but that connection is standard for this pairing and the price makes up for it. Check your booking confirmation to see where the technical stop is so you can plan your times properly.

Best time to visit

Madeira enjoys mild weather year-round, but fares vary by season. Travel between November and March for fewer crowds and potentially better prices. If you want guaranteed sunshine, May–September is safest, though typically more expensive.

What to do when you arrive

Madeira is a nature lover's paradise: hiking, dramatic cliffs and volcanic beaches await. Explore Pico do Arieiro, the ancient levadas (water trails) or stroll through Funchal. The airport is 17 km from the capital and easy to reach.
