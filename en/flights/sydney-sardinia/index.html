Best time to visit

Sardinia shines from June to September with warm beaches and perfect weather. May and October are great too: good conditions and fewer crowds. Winter is quieter and flights may be slightly cheaper, though temperatures are milder. November in Sardinia is autumn with moderate weather, so the example date is feasible if you don't need peak summer heat.

What to do when you arrive in Cagliari

Cagliari is your gateway to Sardinia's beaches and interior. Visit the old town, walk Poetto beach just outside the city, explore coastal villages like Villasimius or Muravera. The northeast (Costa Smeralda) is famous but pricier. Rent a car for freedom of movement; buses connect main towns. Try local specialities: malloreddus and bottarga.
