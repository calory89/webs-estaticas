Best time to visit

Stockholm shines in summer (June–August) with long days and mild weather, but prices peak. Autumn (September–October) and spring (May) balance pleasant weather with fewer crowds. Winter (November–March) is cheaper and ideal if you want snow and northern lights.

What to do when you arrive

Stockholm blends islands, palaces, and modernity. Explore Gamla Stan (old town), visit the Vasa Museum, or relax in nearby archipelagos. Public transport is efficient. Try fresh fish and Swedish meatballs. Three to four days is ideal to capture the city's essence.
