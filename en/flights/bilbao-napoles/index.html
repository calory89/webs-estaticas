Best time to visit

Naples works well almost year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather without summer crowds. If you travel in January like the example (7–12 January), you'll benefit from lower prices and fewer tourists, though it's a bit cooler. Summer is pricier and busier.

What to do when you arrive

Naples is the gateway to southern Italy: the historic centre is unmissable (Spaccanapoli, cathedral, museums), authentic pizza is a must, and Mount Vesuvius and Pompeii are a short trip away. The Amalfi Coast and islands like Capri and Ischia are also within reach. The airport is 5 km away and well connected to the city centre by train and bus.
