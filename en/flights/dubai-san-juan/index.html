Royal Jordanian connects Dubai to San Juan from €935 return. Median fares hover around €1,126. Book in advance and fly mid-November for better options.

Price and when to book

The cheapest ticket is €935, but standard fares sit around €1,126. Prices climb to €1,233 on peak dates. Book 3–4 weeks ahead: intercontinental flights rise sharply as departure nears. Mid-November is a sweet spot, away from holiday peaks.

Airline and stopovers

Royal Jordanian operates this route with 2 stopovers. It's not direct, but it's the available option for this journey. Connections extend travel time but break up the fatigue of such a long flight. Check your stopovers when booking, they're usually planned to minimise connection times.

Best time to travel

November is ideal: dry weather in Puerto Rico, no hurricane risk (more likely August–October) and still reasonable fares. Avoid December–January for holidays and price spikes. February and March also offer good weather and moderate occupancy.

What to do on arrival

San Juan is your gateway to Puerto Rico. Old San Juan mixes cobblestone streets, galleries and food. Luquillo beaches are 45 minutes away. El Yunque rainforest and Cabalete waterfall are unique natural sites. For relaxation, Culebra and Vieques islands offer turquoise waters without crowds.

FAQ

Is Royal Jordanian safe for travel to San Juan?

Yes. Royal Jordanian is an internationally certified airline. Although it involves stopovers, it's an established carrier for Middle East–Caribbean connections.

What does 2 stopovers mean on my journey?

2 stopovers mean you stop at 2 intermediate airports before reaching San Juan. The journey is longer but typical for this route. Check total duration and connection times when you book.

Why are November prices lower?

November isn't peak season in the Caribbean (outside school holidays and festivities). Fares are cheaper than December or summer without sacrificing decent weather.

What airport is San Juan?

It's Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport (SJU), Puerto Rico's main hub. About 15 km from downtown San Juan, well served by taxi, car rental and public transport.
