Price and when to book

The lowest recorded price is €612, though fares typically hover around €737. Higher options are available above €900. Booking 2 months ahead is a smart strategy for long-haul routes like this; the earlier you lock in your ticket, the more competitive prices you'll find.

Airline and stops

Iberia Express operates this route with 2 stops. It's not a direct flight, but that's standard for journeys from the Canaries to Southeast Asia. Use the layover time to rest, eat, or move around the terminal.

Best time to travel

Kuala Lumpur enjoys tropical weather year-round. Avoid monsoon season (September to March on the west coast). Dry months (June to August) see peak tourism and flight activity. Travelling in April, May, or late August to early September means lighter crowds at your destination.

What to do when you arrive

Kuala Lumpur offers the iconic Petronas Towers, Petaling Street night markets, temples like Thean Hou, and green spaces such as the Bird Park. The international airport sits 75 km from the city centre; the airport train link is the fastest and most economical way into town.
