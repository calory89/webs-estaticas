Best time to visit

Bucharest appeals year-round. If you prefer mild weather and fewer crowds, aim for May-June or September-October. Winters are cold but the city shines with lighter tourist numbers. Avoid school holidays and public holidays if you're chasing lower prices.

What to do in Bucharest

Don't miss the Palace of Parliament, Europe's largest building after the Pentagon. Stroll the Old Town (Lipscani), visit Stavropoleos Orthodox Church, explore the Village Museum and savour Romanian cuisine. Henri Coandă Airport is 16 km from the centre; bus, taxi or transfer gets you there in 30-45 minutes.
