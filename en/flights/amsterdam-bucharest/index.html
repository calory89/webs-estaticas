Price and when to book

Amsterdam-Bucharest flights are available from €212 (return). We've recorded fares of €212, €212 and €222. Book as early as possible. Airline and stops

KLM Royal Dutch Airlines operates this route non-stop. You'll fly direct from Amsterdam (AMS) to Bucharest (OTP), saving you time and hassle. A non-stop flight is more comfortable and less prone to delays.

Best time to visit

Bucharest appeals year-round. If you prefer mild weather and fewer crowds, aim for May-June or September-October. Winters are cold but the city shines with lighter tourist numbers. Avoid school holidays and public holidays if you're chasing lower prices.

What to do in Bucharest

Don't miss the Palace of Parliament, Europe's largest building after the Pentagon. Stroll the Old Town (Lipscani), visit Stavropoleos Orthodox Church, explore the Village Museum and savour Romanian cuisine. Henri Coandă Airport is 16 km from the centre; bus, taxi or transfer gets you there in 30-45 minutes.

FAQ

Yes, KLM offers direct flights on this route. No stops: departure from Amsterdam (AMS) with arrival in Bucharest (OTP).

From €212 return with KLM. Prices vary between €212 and €222 depending on exact travel dates. Book in advance to secure the best fares.

What are the main attractions in Bucharest?

The Palace of Parliament, the Old Town (Lipscani), Stavropoleos Church, the Village Museum and traditional Romanian restaurants. The city also has lively nightlife and art galleries.
