Best time to travel

Bologna is lovely year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather without summer crowds and high prices. Winters are cold and grey, though less touristy. For the lowest fares, travel November–March, but expect shorter daylight hours and possible rain.

What to do when you arrive

Bologna is famous for its medieval old town, towers, arcades and food. Don't miss Piazza Maggiore, the Basilica of San Petronio, the two towers (Asinelli and Garisenda) and the Morandi Museum. Eat authentic tagliatelle al ragù (Bolognese), mortadella di Bologna and tortellini. Marconi Airport is 4 km from centre; reach it by bus, taxi or train in 15 minutes.

What should I know about Ryanair baggage?

Ryanair includes a small backpack or personal item. Large cabin bags (40x20x25 cm) and checked luggage are charged separately. Check their current fees before purchase to avoid surprises.

What to see in Bologna in one day?

Bologna is compact. Walk Piazza Maggiore, climb the towers, stroll the arcades (40 km total), visit the Basilica of San Petronio and eat well. One day covers the highlights; two or three days let you savour it.
