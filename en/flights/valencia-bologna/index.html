Fly Valencia to Bologna with Ryanair from just €50 return. Median prices sit around €64, so you'll find good deals if you book ahead. Bologna awaits with legendary food and medieval towers.

Price and when to book

Prices start from €50, with a median of €64. In our sample, you'll also find options at €109. The earlier you book, the better: Ryanair raises fares as the flight fills up. Don't wait until the last minute if you want those low prices.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight: no transfers, no hassle. Typical departure from Valencia at 16:40 and return from Bologna early morning (around 05:45). A straightforward journey in just over 2 hours.

Best time to visit

Bologna is pleasant year-round, though spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer ideal temperatures without peak crowds. Summer can be busier and pricier. December may bring good deals if you travel outside holiday dates.

What to do when you arrive

Bologna is the birthplace of fresh pasta, medieval universities, and arcaded streets. Don't miss Piazza Maggiore, the Garisenda and Asinelli towers, and the Basilica of San Petronio. The city is compact—most attractions are walkable. Eat tortellini, tagliatelle or ragù at any trattoria and you'll have a proper Bolognese experience.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for Valencia to Bologna?

The lowest fare we see is €50 return. It's important to book early because Ryanair raises prices as the flight fills up.

Is it a direct flight Valencia to Bologna?

Yes, completely direct. Ryanair flies non-stop between Valencia and Bologna—you board once and disembark once.

Which airline flies the Valencia to Bologna route?

Ryanair operates this route. Remember it's a budget carrier: limited baggage, no reserved seats on base fares, and food and drinks only available for purchase onboard.

How much time do I need to see Bologna?

Two to three days are enough to explore the historic centre. If you book an early return flight, make the most of your last day. The city is small and very walkable.
