TAP Portugal connects New York to Malta for €541 return (from 23 October 2026). Median prices hover around €587. With one stopover, it's the best value option to reach the Mediterranean island.

Price and when to book

Flights start from €541. If you spot this route around that price, go for it, that's the lowest on record. The median sits at €587, so waiting longer could cost you €46 more. Book at least 3-4 weeks in advance; New York to Malta isn't a peak route, but prices climb steadily as departure nears.

Airline and stopovers

TAP Portugal operates this route with 1 stopover. It's not a direct flight, but TAP is a solid European carrier with decent service. The typical stopover is in Lisbon, where connection time is manageable. Always check baggage allowance, TAP charges for carry-on bags depending on ticket type.

Best time to visit

Malta has Mediterranean weather: spring (April–May) and autumn (October–November) are ideal, warm but not scorching. November is an excellent choice: quiet beaches and fewer tourists. Skip July–August if you dislike intense heat and crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Malta Airport (MLA) is 5 km from Valletta. Visit the capital, UNESCO World Heritage fortress city with stunning fortifications. Don't miss Mdina (ancient capital), Ggantija temples in Gozo, Comino's Blue Lagoon, or diving, Malta is a submarine paradise. Eat rabbit stew (coniglio) and try pastizzi (local pastries).

FAQ

Is €541 the final price or are there extra fees?

€541 is the reference price we found on 23 October 2026. Airport taxes and possible baggage charges always apply (especially with TAP if your ticket doesn't include it). Check the breakdown on the booking platform before paying.

Is TAP Portugal a good airline?

Yes, TAP is reliable and well-regarded, Portugal's national carrier. Service depends on your fare: economy basic is tight, but economy regular offers good value. Keep in mind Lisbon is the natural connection hub from New York.

How long is the stopover connection?

I can't give you the exact stopover time, but with one stopover in Lisbon it's typically 2–4 hours. It varies per flight. When you book, verify the connection time suits you, especially if it's your first trip with TAP.

What to see in Malta in 3 days?

Day 1: Valletta, Three Cities and harbour. Day 2: Mdina, Dingli and temples. Day 3: Gozo (ferry from Malta) or Comino. Malta is tiny; everything's close. Diving, Dingli cliffs and local markets are unmissable.
