Best time to visit

Berlin is brilliant almost year-round. Summer (June to August) brings peak tourism and higher prices; spring and autumn offer good weather with fewer crowds. Winter works if you fancy the Christmas markets or festive vibe. Pick your moment based on what you want to do and avoid Easter and August if you prefer quieter streets.

What to do when you arrive

Berlin is essential: Brandenburg Gate, Holocaust Memorial, Reichstag (climb the dome for views), Jewish Quarter. Don't miss the Pergamon Museum, Museum Island, or street art on the former East side. Grab currywurst from a stand, grab a beer in a biergarten. Two or three days feel rushed; a week is better.
