What to see and do in Krakow

Krakow is a cultural gem: the Main Market Square (Rynek Główny) is Europe's largest medieval plaza, Wawel Cathedral dominates the skyline, and the Jewish Quarter (Kazimierz) blends history with vibrant contemporary life. The Wieliczka Salt Mine is 15 km away if you fancy a day trip. John Paul II Airport is 15 km south; take a bus or taxi to reach the city centre in 30–45 minutes.
