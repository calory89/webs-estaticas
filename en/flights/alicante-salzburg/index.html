Best time to visit

Salzburg shines in winter (November to February) when Christmas markets and snow transform the city. Spring (April-May) and early autumn are also excellent with fewer crowds. Summer months are pricier and busier. Travel outside peak season to snag better fares and enjoy the city properly.

What to do in Salzburg

Don't miss the Altstadt (old town), a UNESCO World Heritage site, with the Cathedral and cobbled streets. Climb Hohensalzburg Fortress for panoramic views. Mozart was born here: visit his birthplace museum. The Sound of Music has strong roots in the city. The Austrian Alps are nearby for hiking and day trips. Salzburg airport is about 4 km from the centre; buses and trains connect easily.
