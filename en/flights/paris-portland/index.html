Icelandair operates this route with 1 stopover, usually in Reykjavik. It's not a direct flight, but the connection is typically quick. The airline is reliable and has been connecting Europe to North America for decades. Check baggage allowance and terms at booking.

Best time to visit Portland

Oregon is beautiful in autumn (September–October) with mild temperatures and moderate rainfall. Spring (March–May) is also excellent. Avoid winter if you dislike constant rain, though the climate isn't extreme. Summer is dry but more expensive. Book well ahead regardless of season.

What to do in Portland and on arrival

Portland is known for its cultural scene, specialty coffee shops and outdoor activities. Visit the International Rose Garden, Powell's Books (the world's largest independent bookstore), the historic downtown and nearby Cascade forests. Portland Airport (PDX) is 10 km from the city with easy connections by bus, train and taxi.
