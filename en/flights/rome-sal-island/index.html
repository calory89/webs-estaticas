Best time to visit

November is peak season on Sal Island: dry weather and perfect for beach days. It's hot but without summer rains. If you want lower prices, try May or June (low season), though it will be hotter and more humid. Fares climb in December and January.

What to do when you arrive

Sal Island is all about beaches: Cape Verde's international airport connects you to Santa Maria and Espargos, the main villages. Dive in crystal-clear waters, try kitesurfing at Pedra Lume beach, or spend a day at the Blue Lagoon. Beaches are the main draw; the island is small but perfect for relaxing and swimming. Eat fresh fish on the coast and enjoy the laid-back Atlantic rhythm.
