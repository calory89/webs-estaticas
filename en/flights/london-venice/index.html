The lowest price we've found is £42, with typical fares around £44 return. These prices are based on sample dates 3 December 2026 (outbound) and 12 December (return). Generally, booking 2-3 months in advance offers better deals. Prices vary by availability and day of the week, so compare flexible dates if you can.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route with direct flights, meaning you'll reach Venice with no stops. It's the fastest and most efficient option for this connection. No plane changes or intermediate airports required.

Best time to visit

Venice is beautiful year-round, but May to September is peak season with the most tourists. For fewer crowds and better prices, consider April, October or early November. Winter (December to February) is quieter with fewer visitors, though the weather is colder and morning mist over the lagoon is common.

What to do when you arrive

Marco Polo Airport connects directly to the historic centre in 20 minutes by water bus (vaporetto). Once in the city, don't miss St Mark's Basilica, the Doge's Palace, or a gondola ride along the Grand Canal. Museums, galleries and Venetian food will keep you busy for days. The city is best explored on foot, it's small and everything is a monument.
