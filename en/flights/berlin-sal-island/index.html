Airline and stops

Best time to visit

Sal Island is a year-round destination, but November to June offers the best weather: pleasant temperatures (24–28 °C) and less rain. July and August are hotter. Avoid Christmas dates if you want lower fares; prices drop significantly after early January.

What to do when you arrive

Sal Island is known for white-sand beaches and diving. Santa Maria is the main town with shops and restaurants. You'll land at Amilcar Cabral International Airport (SID). Don't miss the pink salt flats, coral reefs and sunset boat trips. It's perfect for a genuine break.
