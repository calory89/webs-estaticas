Best time to travel

Phu Quoc is a beach destination, so choose based on climate. The dry and cooler season (November to March) is the most sought-after, with clear skies perfect for beaches and diving. Note that high demand can push prices up; if you travel in low season (May to October) you'll find cheaper flights, though with more rain.

What to do when you arrive at Phu Quoc

Phu Quoc is Vietnam's largest island: white sand beaches, marine parks, diving, snorkelling and luxury resorts. Explore the northern coast for Duong Dong (fishing village), visit the marine national park, and sample local cuisine at night markets. Phu Quoc International Airport is well-connected with transport to hotels and tourist areas.
