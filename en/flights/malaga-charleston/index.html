Best time to travel

Charleston has a subtropical climate: mild winters and warm, humid summers. The best season is autumn (September–November) and winter (December–February), when the weather is pleasant and rainfall is lower. Avoid June–August due to intense heat. Prices may vary by season based on demand, so compare flexible dates.

What to do when you arrive

Charleston is a historic city full of colonial charm. Stroll the historic district, visit the bay forts (Fort Sumter), enjoy lowcountry dining on King Street and Market Street. The international airport is about 20 km from downtown; you have bus, taxi and car rental options.
