Best time to visit

Sofia shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), when the weather is mild and pleasant without summer crowds. Winter is cold and grey, but prices drop significantly. Avoid New Year and local holidays if you're after peace and quiet plus cheaper flights.

What to do when you arrive

Sofia is Bulgaria's cultural capital: the Alexander Nevsky Cathedral is unmissable, with its golden dome dominating the skyline. The Basilica of Saint Sophia (6th century) is a historical gem. Explore the Vitosha neighbourhood, taste local food at markets, and climb Mount Vitosha for city views. The airport is 10 km from the centre; train, bus, or taxi are quick and affordable options.
