Best time to visit

Sri Lanka has tropical weather and two monsoons. December to March (northern winter) is ideal, less rain, stable temperatures. January and February are best for beaches and wildlife spotting. Avoid April, May and October if you dislike constant rain. Off-season travel (May to September) sometimes brings lower fares, but expect wet weather.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Colombo's Bandaranaike International Airport. From there, explore Colombo itself, visit Kandy and the Temple of the Tooth, ride the scenic trains through Nuwara Eliya's mountains, or relax on beaches like Mirissa and Unawatuna. Don't miss Sigiriya's rock fortress, Yala and Udawalawe national parks. Sri Lanka works brilliantly for culture, nature and beach lovers combined.
