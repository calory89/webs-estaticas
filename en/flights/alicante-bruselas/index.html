Fly from Alicante to Brussels for just €91 return with Ryanair. The median price is €104, so you have good options if you book in advance. A perfect weekend in Belgium without breaking the bank.

Price and when to book

Prices range from €91 to €134 for return flights. Booking 2 months ahead (from today, 22 October) is usually smart: you have time before peak dates. Keep an eye on price changes on Mondays and Tuesdays, when airlines adjust fares. This route isn't the priciest, but it's not fixed either: small date changes can add or subtract €15–20.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight. Departure from Alicante (ALC) with no stops until Brussels (BRU). A near-3-hour direct is ideal: no hassle with connections and no risk of lost luggage. Heads up: Ryanair charges extra for baggage, seat selection and other add-ons.

Best time to visit

Brussels is worth visiting most of the year. October–November and March–April offer mild weather (10–15°C) and fewer crowds than summer. December shines with Christmas markets at the Grand Place. July–August is peak season and pricier. Skip January–February if extreme cold isn't your thing. Prices tend to vary more in high season (June–August), so book ahead.

What to do when you arrive

Brussels is compact and easy to navigate. The Grand Place is unmissable (spectacular, free entry outside). Manneken Pis, the Atomium and the Saint-Gery quarter are classics. Eat Belgian fries, try local beers and buy chocolate. Leuven is 15 minutes by train; Bruges is 30. Perfect for combining cities on a short trip from Alicante.
