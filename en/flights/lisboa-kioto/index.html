What to do in Kyoto

Kyoto is Japan's cultural core. Visit Fushimi Inari Shrine with its thousands of orange torii gates, walk through the bamboo forest of Arashiyama, explore geishas in Gion, and wander historic temples like Kinkaku-ji. Local cuisine and traditional hot springs round out the experience.
