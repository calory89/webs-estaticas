Prices for this route range from €404, €456 and €536 depending on travel dates. Book as far in advance as you can: intercontinental flights drop significantly when purchased several weeks ahead. October, November and early December are ideal windows to find low fares to Central Europe.

AJet and stopovers: what to expect

You'll travel with AJet. This journey includes 1 stopover, so it's not direct, but it's the available option for this route. Your outbound flight departs Dubai at 05:40 and your return leaves Krakow at 15:35. Pack water and charge your devices during the stopover.

Best time to travel from Dubai to Krakow

Krakow shines in autumn and winter: September to November offers mild weather and fewer crowds, while December brings spectacular Christmas markets. Spring (April-May) is also excellent. Avoid July and August if you're after peace and lower prices.

What to do in Krakow when you arrive

Krakow Airport (KRK) is 20 minutes from the city centre. The Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage site: explore the Main Square, climb Wawel Cathedral and walk through Kazimierz, the Jewish quarter. The Wieliczka Salt Mines are an hour away and a must-see. Eat pierniki and try local beer.
