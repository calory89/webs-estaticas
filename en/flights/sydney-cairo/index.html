Best time to visit

Cairo is an excellent destination during the Northern Hemisphere winter (November to February), when temperatures are milder. Summer months (June to September) can be scorching. Avoid the spring sandstorm season. Plan your trip well in advance to secure the best prices.

What to do when you arrive

Cairo is the gateway to the Pyramids of Giza, one of the world's wonders. Explore the Egyptian Museum, wander through Khan el-Khalili bazaar and visit historic Islamic Cairo. The Nile offers cruises and spectacular views. The international airport is well connected to the city centre, making your arrival straightforward.
