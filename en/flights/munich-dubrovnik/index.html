Best time to visit

Dubrovnik is a coastal city with medieval walls and crystal-clear beaches. June, September and October are ideal: warm weather, swimmable sea and fewer crowds than July-August. Low season (November-March) means cheaper flights, but the sea is cold. December can surprise with deals outside Christmas dates.

What to do when you arrive

Explore the walled old town, walk the ramparts, swim at Banje or Lapad beaches, day-trip to Lokrum island or the Elafiti islands by ferry. Try Dalmatian food and wine. Airport is 20 km away; bus, taxi or car hire get you to the centre quickly.

Which month has the cheapest flights to Dubrovnik?

Low season (November-March) has lower fares, but fewer daylight hours. September and October strike a good balance: perfect weather and reasonable prices.

What can I do in Dubrovnik in 6 days?

Old town walls, beaches at Banje or Lapad, Lokrum island, Elafiti island ferries, Dalmatian food and wine. Six days lets you add a day-trip south to Cavtat or a further island excursion.
