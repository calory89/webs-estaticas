You'll fly with Saudi Arabian Airlines, offering good value for money on this route. You'll have 2 stopovers, so the journey takes longer than a direct flight, but the savings make it worthwhile. Expect reasonable connection times and quality service during your layovers.

Best time to visit

Mauritius is a year-round destination, but avoid the cyclone season from November to May. The safest and most pleasant months are June to October, with comfortable temperatures and less rainfall. These months are also peak season, so book further ahead if travelling then.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam Airport, 50 km from the capital Port Louis. Mauritius offers crystal-clear beaches, snorkelling and diving, parks like Pamplemousses, and the famous Le Morne mountain. Don't miss the turquoise lagoon at Belle Mare or Chamarel waterfall. Car hire or pre-arranged taxis are your best bets for getting around.
