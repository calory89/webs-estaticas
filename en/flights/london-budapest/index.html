Airline and stopovers

Best time to visit

Budapest shines in spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October): pleasant weather, fewer crowds than summer, and gentler prices. Winter is cheap but cold. Avoid peak season if you want better deals. January–March flights are usually more economical than June–August.

What to do when you arrive

Ferenc Liszt Airport is 16 km from the city centre. Reach it by bus, metro or taxi. Budapest has the Danube, Széchenyi thermal baths, Gothic Parliament, the Jewish quarter with historic synagogues, and ruin bars in cellars. Try palacsinta (crepes) and goulash soup. Three or four days cover the essentials.
