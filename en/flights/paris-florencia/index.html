Prices range between €91 and €142 depending on dates. Most searches cluster around €92, which is a solid indicator of fair value. Book at least 6–8 weeks ahead to secure the best fares. Tuesdays and Wednesdays typically have lower demand than weekends.

Best time to travel

Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) offer the best balance: pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Avoid July–August if you're chasing low prices; in winter (outside Christmas), fares drop. January is a solid choice if you don't mind cooler temperatures. What to do in Florence

Once you land, the historic centre is essential: the Cathedral, the Duomo, the Uffizi Gallery. Stroll the Ponte Vecchio at sunset, climb the Giotto Tower for 360° views, visit the Pitti Palace. Grab gelato on any corner and try bistecca alla fiorentina. Florence deserves 3–4 days minimum to see it properly.
