Airline and stopovers

Best time to visit

Cancún has a dry season from November to April, perfect for beaches and activities. July and August are hot and wetter. Hurricane season runs June to November, though Cancún has solid infrastructure. Avoid Christmas and Easter peaks if you want better value and fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Cancún International Airport is 20 km from the centre. Visit the ruins at Tulum or Chichén Itzá, swim with turtles in cenotes, or unwind on Playa del Carmen. The Riviera Maya offers Mayan cuisine, theme parks and nightlife. Bring sunscreen—Mexican sun is intense.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Stockholm to Cancún?

No. KLM operates this route with 2 stopovers, so it's a long journey. Stopovers are usually in Amsterdam or other European hubs. Check connection times when booking.

When is the best time to visit Cancún?

November to April is ideal: dry climate, pleasant temperatures and perfect beaches. July and August are very hot. Avoid Christmas and Easter if you want fewer crowds and better prices.

What can I do in Cancún?

Turquoise beaches, Mayan ruins (Tulum, Chichén Itzá), cenote diving, parks like Xcel-Há or Xplor, local food and nightlife. The airport is well connected to the centre and the Riviera Maya offers activities for all tastes.
