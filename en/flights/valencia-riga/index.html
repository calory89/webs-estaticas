Best time to visit

Riga shines in winter (December–February) with Christmas markets and snow-lined streets. Autumn (September–October) and spring (April–May) bring mild weather and fewer crowds. Prices drop outside peak season, so consider travelling November to March if you want to save.

What to do when you arrive

Riga is Latvia's capital and a UNESCO World Heritage site. Wander the Old Town (Vecrīga), admire Art Nouveau facades, climb St Peter's Church for city views, or stroll along the Daugava riverside. The airport is 13 km from the centre; buses, taxis and car hire are available.
