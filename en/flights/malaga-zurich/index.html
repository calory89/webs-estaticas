Price and when to book

Prices start from €161 return. For this route, booking in advance is worthwhile: fares are typically more competitive if you purchase 2-3 months ahead. Bear in mind January is peak season in Zurich for skiing, so if you're after better prices consider travelling outside that period or mid-week.

Airline and connections

Air Europa operates this route with one stop on the outbound leg. While not direct, connection times are usually reasonable. The return flight may vary in structure depending on your chosen date. With Air Europa you'll get a reliable Spanish airline serving European routes.

Best time to travel

Zurich is lovely year-round, but winter (December-February) is peak for skiers. For better prices and fewer crowds, choose autumn (September-October) or spring (April-May). Summer is also popular with pleasant temperatures. Book early, especially for school holidays or long weekends.

What to do in Zurich

Zurich is compact and walkable. Stroll the Altstadt (old town), visit Zurich Cathedral, explore the lakeside, and enjoy local cuisine. Zurich Airport (ZRH) is 13 km from the centre with excellent train links (10 minutes). Extend your trip with day trips to the Alps or nearby Swiss villages.
