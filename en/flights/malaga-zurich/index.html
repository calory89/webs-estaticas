Best time to travel

Zurich is lovely year-round, but winter (December-February) is peak for skiers. For better prices and fewer crowds, choose autumn (September-October) or spring (April-May). Summer is also popular with pleasant temperatures. Book early, especially for school holidays or long weekends.

What to do in Zurich

Zurich is compact and walkable. Stroll the Altstadt (old town), visit Zurich Cathedral, explore the lakeside, and enjoy local cuisine. Zurich Airport (ZRH) is 13 km from the centre with excellent train links (10 minutes). Extend your trip with day trips to the Alps or nearby Swiss villages.
