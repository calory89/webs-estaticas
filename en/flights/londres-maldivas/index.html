Best time to go

Maldives has a tropical climate. November, December and January are ideal: less rain, comfortable temperatures and calm seas. It's high season, but if you're chasing price, shoulder months like March–April or September–October can offer good deals with fewer tourists.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Velana International Airport (MLE), Maldives' main gateway. From there: snorkelling on coral reefs, diving at some of the Indian Ocean's best dive sites, white-sand beaches, resort-hopping across atolls, manta ray spotting. Maldives is a beach and underwater destination, so pack your swimwear.
