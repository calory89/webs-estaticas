Best time to visit

Agadir enjoys mild weather almost year-round. Autumn (September–November) and spring (March–May) offer ideal temperatures without peak summer heat or winter chill. Summer months draw more visitors and prices rise; book flexibly if you can.

What to do when you arrive

Agadir is a gateway to Atlantic beaches and the Sahara. Relax on the urban beach, head to Taghazout reef breaks for surfing, or trek into the Atlas Mountains and Berber villages. The fishing port and local souks are must-sees. Food is delicious and cheap: Moroccan cuisine is authentic and good value.
