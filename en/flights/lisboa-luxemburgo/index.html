Fly direct from Lisbon to Luxembourg with Ryanair from €77 return. The median price sits at €129. Fares stay low if you book in advance and avoid peak season travel.

Price and when to book

Flights start from €77 (return), though typical fares range between €77 and €141. For these competitive prices, the earlier you book the better: more availability and lower fares with 6-8 weeks' notice. Check on Tuesdays and Wednesdays when airlines usually update their pricing.

Airline and flight type

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight with no stops. You'll depart Lisbon early morning (example: 06:45) and arrive in Luxembourg the same day. On the return, evening flights are available (example: 21:30). Bring only a cabin bag if you want to avoid baggage fees.

Best time to travel

Lowest fares usually fall outside school holidays and bank holidays. Avoid December, Christmas, Easter and July-August if budget matters. February, March, October and November offer better value with pleasant weather to explore the city.

What to do when you arrive

Luxembourg is small but history-rich: the Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage site. Visit Notre-Dame Cathedral, the Grund (riverside quarter), and the old fortress remains. The airport is 6 km from the centre; taxi or bus gets you there fast. Spend 2-3 days to make the most of it.

What to see in Luxembourg for a weekend trip?
