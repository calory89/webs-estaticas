Best time to travel

Vietnam is best visited between October and April, when the weather is drier and temperatures comfortable. Avoid May to September if you're not keen on heat and humidity. Booking well ahead during these months will help you secure better prices before peak season rates climb.

What to do in Hanoi and surroundings

Hanoi is the perfect gateway to Vietnam. Explore the Old Quarter, visit the Temple of Literature and Ho Chi Minh's Mausoleum. A few hours by train or bus away are Ha Long Bay, Sapa's terraced rice fields, and Phong Nha caves. Local food is spectacular and very affordable.
