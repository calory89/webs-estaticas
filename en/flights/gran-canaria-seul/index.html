Best time to visit

Seoul is spectacular in autumn (September to November) and spring (April to May): mild temperatures and stunning scenery. Winter is cold but dry, great if you like extreme cold. Avoid the summer monsoon. March to May and October tend to be cheaper than Christmas or New Year dates.

What to do when you arrive in Seoul

You'll land at Incheon International Airport (ICN), about 60 km from the city centre. The express train gets you there in 43 minutes. Must-see: Gyeongbokgung Palace, Myeongdong district, Namsan Tower and Namdaemun Market. Eat at local Korean food canteens, try kimchi and explore Gangnam. Buddhist temples are a cultural gem.
