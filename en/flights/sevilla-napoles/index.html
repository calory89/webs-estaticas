Best time to visit

December is a solid choice: fewer tourists than summer, low prices, and Naples with festive vibes. Skip July and August if you want to save money. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) offer decent prices and good weather too.

What to do in Naples

Visit Cappella Sansevero, the National Archaeological Museum, and the UNESCO old town. Eat pizza al taglio at any corner. Pompeii is 25 km away by train. Mount Vesuvius watches over the city. The coast towards Sorrento and the island of Capri work well as day trips.

Is Ryanair the only airline on this route?

Is the flight direct from Seville to Naples?

What should I see in Naples over a weekend?

The historic centre (Spaccanapoli), Cappella Sansevero, the cathedral, strolls by the port. A day trip to Pompeii takes half a day. With more time, visit Capri or the Amalfi Coast. Pizza and coffee in a local piazza is essential.
