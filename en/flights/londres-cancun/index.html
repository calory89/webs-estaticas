Best time to visit

November to April is peak season in Cancun: dry weather, no hurricanes, perfect beaches. November (like the 12–16 example) is excellent because it's pre-peak tourism and prices are lower than December or January. Avoid September and October: hurricane risk.

What to do on arrival

Cancun opens the door to Riviera Maya beaches, cenotes, Mayan ruins in Tulum and Playa del Carmen. Cancun airport (CUN) is 20 km from town. Hire a car, take a bus or taxi to your hotel. The hotel zone is safe and tourist-friendly.

What to see in and around Cancun?

Playa del Carmen, Tulum (Mayan ruins), cenotes for diving, Isla Mujeres, Cozumel. Snorkelling and diving are stunning. Local food is excellent and cheap. November is ideal: water 28°C, no rain.
