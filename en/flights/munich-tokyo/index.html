Best time to visit

Japan shines in spring (March–May) with cherry blossoms, and autumn (September–November) with mild weather. Winter and summer are pricier and busier. December, like the example flight, is shoulder season: not peak but not rock-bottom either.

What to do when you arrive in Tokyo

You'll land in the Tokyo metropolitan area. Explore Shibuya, Shinjuku, and Asakusa, visit temples, grab sushi at conveyor-belt restaurants, or take the train to Kyoto. Public transport is excellent and efficient. Don't miss the museums and local food scene.
