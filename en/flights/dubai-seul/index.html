Best time to visit Seoul

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are perfect for Seoul: mild weather, clear skies and stunning scenery. Skip harsh winters unless you're keen on the cold, and summer tends to be humid with rain. Travel outside peak season and you might spot better prices too.

What to do when you arrive in Seoul

You land at Incheon International Airport (ICN), about 60 km from the city centre. The Airport Railroad express train gets you downtown in 43 minutes. Once there, visit Gyeongbokgung Palace, traditional temples, Gangnam, Namdaemun Market and Seoul's thriving nightlife. The city is safe, well-connected and easy to navigate.
