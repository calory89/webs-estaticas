Ryanair connects Rome and Mykonos with competitive prices starting from €214 for the full trip. The median price hovers around €221, so you have room to find good deals if you book in advance.

Price and when to buy

The lowest registered price is €214 return, with a median of €221. Prices rise to €233 in other periods. To get the best rates, book as far in advance as possible: Ryanair typically has lower fares several weeks before departure. Avoid dates near school holidays and local public holidays, when prices spike.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair is your only option on this route with one stop. Prepare for an intermediate connection that will extend your journey, but in return you'll get a significantly reduced price compared to direct flights from other carriers. Check the timetables carefully to ensure the stopover isn't too tight.

Best time to visit Mykonos

Mykonos is a high-season summer destination when flight prices rise considerably. The best value for money is found in May, September and October, when the weather is good but tourism is lower. If your budget is tight, these months will let you fly cheaper and enjoy the island without crowds.

What to do when you arrive in Mykonos

Mykonos airport (JMK) is just 2 km from town. From the terminal you can take a bus, taxi or hire a car. Once on the island, don't miss Paradise and Psarou beaches, the white villages of Chora (the capital), traditional windmills and the nightlife scene. It's ideal for combining beach time with Greek cuisine and cultural exploration.

FAQ

What is the cheapest price to fly from Rome to Mykonos?

The lowest recorded price is €214 return with Ryanair. While it's possible to find deals in low season or booking months ahead, this is the starting point. The median is €221, so that's a realistic price to expect.

Is the flight from Rome to Mykonos direct or does it have stops?

Ryanair operates this route with 1 stop. There is no direct flight option at these prices, but the connection is part of the savings you get by choosing this budget airline.

Which airline flies from Rome to Mykonos?

Ryanair is the airline offering this route with the most competitive prices (from €214). It's the main operator between these two cities with low-cost fares.

When is it cheaper to fly to Mykonos from Rome?

Generally, prices are lower outside the peak summer season (June, July, August). May, September and October tend to offer the best price-to-value ratio. Booking many weeks in advance is key to finding the best fares.
