Best time to visit

Naples has warm weather most of the year. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: comfortable temperatures, fewer crowds than summer, and moderate accommodation prices. Winter is mild, though some months can be rainy.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Naples International Airport (Capodichino), about 5 km from the city centre. The city is a perfect base to explore Pompeii, Herculaneum and the Amalfi Coast. Don't miss Naples Cathedral, the San Carlo Theatre, or an authentic Neapolitan pizza in the old town. Ferries also connect to Capri and Ischia from the port.
