Best time to visit

Jacksonville has a subtropical climate. The ideal period is October to April: pleasant temperatures (18-25°C) and less rain. Avoid June-September if you dislike hot, humid weather. Prices tend to rise in European winter and school holidays, so February-March can be a good window if you book ahead.

Jacksonville is Florida's largest city by area. Stroll through the historic downtown (Riverside), relax on local beaches, or visit the city aquarium. It's easy to day-trip to Daytona Beach (1 hour) or St. Augustine, America's oldest colonial town (45 minutes). The airport is about 20 km from the city centre.
