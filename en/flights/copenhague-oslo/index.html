Best time to visit

You can travel between Copenhagen and Oslo year-round. Autumn and winter offer a chance to see the Northern Lights in Oslo if you're lucky and far enough north. Spring and summer bring milder temperatures and longer daylight. Oslo in winter is magical but cold; pack accordingly.

What to do when you arrive in Oslo

Oslo is compact and highly walkable. Visit the Opera House (stunning architecture), Grünerløkka neighbourhood (shops and cafés), Bjørvika waterfront, and the Opera Museum. Nature lovers should explore nearby national parks for hiking any time of year. Food is pricey, but local pizzerias and cafés offer good value. Public transport is efficient but costs money.
