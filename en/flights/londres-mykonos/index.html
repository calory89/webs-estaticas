Price and when to book

Fares range from €216 to €266 depending on availability. Book as early as possible: the further ahead you reserve, the better price you'll lock in. Avoid school holidays and bank holidays if you're after the cheapest tickets. October, November and April are solid months for finding good deals.

Airline and connections

Ryanair operates this route with one stop. Both outbound and return flights include at least one connection, so factor in extra time for your journey. Always check the stopover airport when booking so you can plan accordingly.

Best time to visit

Mykonos is stunning from May to September, but prices peak and beaches get rammed. If you want cheap flights and a quieter experience, travel in the shoulder months (April-May or September-October). The weather is still brilliant and the vibe much more relaxed.

What to do in Mykonos

Mykonos is the Cyclades' beach destination. Paradise and Super Paradise are iconic, but the north coast has calmer alternatives too. Mykonos Town delivers nightlife, seafront bars and proper Greek food. The Venetian windmills and Little Venice are must-see photo spots.
