Best time to visit

Prague shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with pleasant temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. Winter can be cold, but snowy Prague has its own charm. Avoid July and August if you prefer fewer tourists and better prices. Check specific dates to find the best balance between fare and weather.

What to do on arrival in Prague

You'll land at Václav Havel Airport (PRG), 20 km from the centre. Charles Bridge, Prague Castle and the Old Town Square are must-sees. Explore the medieval streets, sample local Czech beer and stroll along the Vltava river. Prague is compact and walkable; three days cover the essentials.
