Price and when to book

Prices range from €772 to €862 for return tickets. Booking further ahead gives you more options and flexibility. Although this is a long journey with connections, it pays to search 2–3 months in advance for competitive fares. Prices fluctuate by date, so test different outbound and return combinations.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route with 3 stops on the outbound leg. Three connections significantly extend overall travel time, but that's the trade-off for the rock-bottom price. Check the stopover details when booking to see where you'll connect.

Best time to visit

San Francisco is a year-round destination. The climate is mild; autumn and spring are especially pleasant. Summer attracts higher prices. For budget fares and decent weather, aim for autumn (September–November) or spring (March–May). Avoid European school holiday periods to find better deals.

What to do in San Francisco

The Golden Gate Bridge is unmissable, as is Alcatraz (book in advance). Cable cars climb steep streets. Castro is an iconic neighbourhood; Mission District offers murals, bars and food. Chinatown and North Beach are historic. Twin Peaks delivers panoramic views. The coast and beaches like Ocean Beach are perfect for a break.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for flights from Zaragoza to San Francisco?

From €772 return based on our data. Prices vary by date, how far ahead you book and availability. Ryanair offers the market's lowest fares for this route.

Is the Zaragoza–San Francisco flight direct?

No, the outbound route includes 3 stops. Ryanair operates this journey with connections, which lengthens travel time but cuts costs significantly.

Which airline flies Zaragoza to San Francisco?

Ryanair is the reference airline for this route at the lowest prices. Verify availability and conditions before you book.

What should I see in San Francisco in 3–4 days?

Spend one day on the Golden Gate, Ferry Building and Embarcadero. Another on Alcatraz (if you have a ticket), North Beach and Chinatown. A third on Mission District, Castro and Twin Peaks. Use any remaining time exploring smaller neighbourhoods or nearby beaches.
