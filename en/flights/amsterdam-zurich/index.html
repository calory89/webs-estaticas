Best time to visit

Zurich is worth visiting year-round. Autumn (September–November) and spring (April–May) usually offer good weather and fewer crowds than summer. Choose based on your interests: skiing in winter, nature in spring-autumn, or city activities any time.

What to do when you arrive in Zurich

Zurich airport is well connected to the city centre. Explore museums (Beyeler Foundation, Kunsthaus), shop on Bahnhofstrasse and enjoy café culture. From Zurich you can easily reach the Alps, lakes like Lake Zurich and medieval villages. Try fondue, walk the old town and enjoy modern architecture alongside historic charm.
