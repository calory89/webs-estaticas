Fly Berlin to Tenerife for just €108 return. The direct route with Ryanair gets you to the Canary Islands with no stops. Prices average around €139, though you'll find options up to €236.

Price and when to book

The cheapest flight costs €108 return. The average sits at €139, so if you see that price, don't wait. Book at least 4–6 weeks ahead: that's the lead time Ryanair typically offers on this route. Prices vary by date; check the search tool to compare.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route nonstop. Direct departure from Berlin to Tenerife South. A five-hour direct flight saves you time and hassle. Ryanair charges for baggage: bring only a complimentary cabin bag within their size limits.

Best time to travel

Tenerife has sunshine year-round, but the cheapest fares typically fall in May–June and October–November (outside peak summer and winter seasons). December–March attracts Europeans escaping the cold, so flights rise. For savings, choose shoulder months.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Tenerife South (TFS), about 60 km from the main tourist hubs. Expect volcanic beaches, Mount Teide (national park), coastal villages and hiking. The north (Puerto de la Cruz) is greener and more traditional; the south (Playa de las Américas) is more developed and warmer.
