Best time to visit

Budapest appeals year-round. Autumn (September to November) and spring (April to May) offer mild weather and fewer crowds. Winters are cold but perfect if you want to enjoy the thermal baths without the rush. Summer brings warmth and peak tourism. Any time is good if you score a fare like this.

What to do in Budapest

Budapest is renowned for its thermal baths (Széchenyi, Gellért), St Stephen's Basilica and the Parliament building by the Danube. The Jewish Quarter, Buda Castle and a sunset boat tour are must-dos. Eat at the ruin bars in District VII, explore food markets and soak in the thermal baths. Three days are ideal to cover the essentials without rushing.
