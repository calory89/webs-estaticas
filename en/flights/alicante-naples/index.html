Naples enjoys pleasant weather most of the year. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer comfortable temperatures without summer crowds or winter chill. What to do when you arrive in Naples

The airport is 7 km from the city centre. Visit Naples Cathedral, explore the historic centre (UNESCO World Heritage), and try authentic Neapolitan pizza. Close by are Pompeii and the Positano coast. The main railway station connects you to the whole region, so you can easily reach beaches, museums and coastal villages from Naples as your base.
