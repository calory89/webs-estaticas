Price and when to book

Prices range from €54 to €70 in our sample checked on 22 October. The best strategy is to book 2-3 weeks ahead: cheap tickets sell out quickly. Check regularly from 30 days before departure to spot price drops.

Airline and direct flight

Ryanair operates this route non-stop. The outbound flight departs Paris around 22:25 and arrives in Porto in the early morning (example: 14 November). Return departs Porto at 11:10 (example: 21 November). No plane changes, faster and simpler.

Best time to visit

Porto is worth visiting year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather without peak-season crowds or high prices. November to February is quieter with frequent deals, though days are shorter. Avoid July and August if you're chasing low fares.

What to do in Porto

The Douro riverfront is essential, with its old buildings and views from Clérigos Tower. Tour the port wine lodges in Vila Nova de Gaia, taste custard tarts at Livraria Lello, explore the historic centre (Unesco World Heritage), and relax at Vidago thermal baths. The airport is 10 km north, well connected to the city.

FAQ

How much is the cheapest Paris-Porto flight?

From €54 return with Ryanair. In our October 22 sample, most prices ranged between €54 and €70.

Is the Paris-Porto flight direct?

Yes, Ryanair flies direct with no stops. The flight departs around 22:25 from Paris in the evening.

What's the best date to fly cheap?

Book 2–3 weeks in advance. Autumn and winter (September to February) typically offer better prices than summer. Avoid holidays and aim for Tuesdays and Wednesdays.

What should I see in Porto over a weekend?

Ribeira, the port wine lodges, Clérigos Tower, Livraria Lello and a stroll through Vila Nova de Gaia. With a late-night flight, you make good use of both days.
