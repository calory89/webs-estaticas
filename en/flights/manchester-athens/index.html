Best time to visit Athens from Manchester

Athens works year-round, but spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer the sweet spot between pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. If you're hunting for lower fares, consider winter months (November-February), when the city is quieter, though temperatures are cooler.

What to do when you arrive in Athens

The Acropolis and Parthenon are must-sees. The Plaka neighbourhood blends history with authentic local tavernas. The Ancient Agora, Temple of Olympian Zeus, and Acropolis Museum are top-tier visits. On Sundays, Psyrri and Gazi areas buzz with markets, shops, and young crowds. Just a few kilometres away, Piraeus port connects you to Aegean islands if you want to extend your trip.
