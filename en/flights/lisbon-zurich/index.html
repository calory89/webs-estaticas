Best time to travel from Lisbon to Zurich

Zurich appeals year-round. Spring and autumn offer mild weather and long days, ideal for walking. Winter is cold but perfect if you want snow and festive atmosphere. Summer is warm and busy (prices rise). Give yourself date flexibility: a few days' difference can save €30 or more. Compare dates before you book.

What to do in Zurich when you arrive

Zurich is compact and easy to navigate. Walk through the Altstadt (old town) along the Limmat, visit the Gothic churches (Grossmünster and Fraumünster), and eat by the riverbanks. World-class museums (Kunsthaus, Dalí Museum) deserve hours. If time allows, take the Uetliberg for city and lake views. Swiss trains connect well to other destinations if you want to explore further.
