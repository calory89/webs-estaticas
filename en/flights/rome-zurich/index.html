Best time to visit

Zurich shines year-round, but autumn (September–October) brings pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter draws those keen on snow in the nearby Alps. Spring (April–May) offers mild temperatures. Avoid July and August if you're after lower prices, peak season dominates. The flights we checked date to late October and early November, when it starts to cool down.

What to do when you arrive

Zurich is compact and walkable. The Old Town (Altstadt) deserves a stroll; the lakes are perfect for unwinding. Nature lovers will find the Alps an hour away by train. Renowned museums, luxury shopping on Bahnhofstrasse and Swiss cuisine are other draws. The airport connects easily to the city centre by public transport.
