Best time to fly to Kyoto

Kyoto shines in spring (March–May) with cherry blossoms, and in autumn (September–November) with red and gold foliage. These seasons are busier and pricier. For fewer crowds and potentially better fares, consider winter (December–February). Compare prices directly for your preferred dates.

What to do when you arrive in Kyoto

Kyoto was Japan's imperial capital. Visit Fushimi Inari Shrine with its thousands of bright red torii gates, the Golden Pavilion Temple, Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, and the Imperial Palace. Stroll through the traditional Gion district at dusk to spot geisha. Don't miss local markets and shops selling tea and ceramics.
