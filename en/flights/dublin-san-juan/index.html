Best time to go

Puerto Rico offers tropical weather year-round, but the dry season (December to April) is most pleasant and popular. Avoid September and October due to hurricane season. December is ideal: good weather and festive atmosphere, though pricier. February-March is also excellent and slightly cheaper.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at San Juan International Airport (SJU). Old San Juan has colonial history, city walls and great food. Luquillo and Flamenco beaches are a short distance away. Don't miss El Yunque, a protected tropical forest. The island also offers diving, hiking and vibrant nightlife.
