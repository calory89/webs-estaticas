Best time to visit

Istanbul is worth visiting year-round, though autumn (September to November) and spring (April to May) offer the most pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Winter is cooler and damper but quieter. Summer can be very hot. Pick based on what suits you: climate comfort or fewer tourists.

What to do when you arrive

Istanbul airport is modern and well-connected. From there, reach the centre by bus, metro or taxi. The city has Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, the Grand Bazaar and Bosphorus cruises. Don't miss the museums, local food and Turkish baths. Istanbul is large, so staying in a good location saves travel time.
