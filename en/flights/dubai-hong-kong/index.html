What to do when you arrive

Hong Kong blends futuristic skyscrapers with ancient temples and traditional markets. Don't miss Victoria Peak for panoramic views, Star Ferry to cross the harbour, and bohemian neighbourhoods like Soho. Hong Kong Airport connects to the centre by train in roughly 24 minutes. Eat dim sum at any local cha chaan teng; the food scene is essential.
