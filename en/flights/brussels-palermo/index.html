Best time to travel

Sicily is appealing year-round, but October and November offer pleasant weather without summer heat. April and May are also ideal. Avoid August if you prefer fewer crowds and lower prices. Winter months (December to February) sometimes show lower fares, though rainfall increases.

What to do in Palermo

Sicily's capital seduces with cultural blend. Walk the Metropolitan Cathedral, explore the chaotic Ballarò market, eat arancini and panelle from street stalls, and visit the Teatro Politeama. Don't miss the Palazzo dei Normanni and its Palatine Chapel mosaics. Mondello beach is 30 minutes away by bus.
