Airline and stopovers

easyJet operates this route non-stop with no stopovers. Departures from London arrive in Larnaca the same day. Fly continuously without getting off or changing aircraft. Check easyJet's baggage policy before buying: the basic fare may include only a small backpack.

Best time to visit

Cyprus is a year-round destination, but skip July and August if you want lower prices and fewer crowds. April-May and October-November offer good weather and less mass tourism. Winter (December-March) is mild, perfect for beach time without the rush, and prices often drop.

What to do when you arrive

Larnaca airport is 6 km from the city. Visit the Church of Saint Lazarus, stroll along the coast, or head to the Troodos Mountains to explore traditional villages. Nissi and Fig Tree Bay beaches are must-sees. Winter travel means hiking and local food take centre stage; summer means beaches and Mediterranean swims.
