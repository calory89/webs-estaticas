Best time to visit

December is peak season in the Mediterranean, though gentler than summer. Naples in winter is cool (8–14°C) but less crowded. For cheaper prices, travel between November and February, avoiding Christmas and New Year. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer the best balance of weather and crowds.

What to do when you arrive in Naples

Capodichino airport is 4 km from the city centre. Visit the Cathedral, explore the Unesco-listed historic centre, try authentic Neapolitan pizza, climb Vesuvius or take a train to Pompeii (30 minutes). The Amalfi Coast is just over an hour's drive away. Naples is chaotic, authentic and delicious: real Italy.
