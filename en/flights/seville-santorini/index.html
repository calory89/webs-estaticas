Best time to travel

Santorini shines April to June and September to October: perfect weather, less crowding than July-August. Flight prices tend to be lower in spring and autumn. If you travel in peak season (summer), there's more ticket supply but also more demand, so book early.

What to do on arrival

Santorini airport (JTR) is in the north of the island. From there, bus, taxi or car hire take you to iconic villages: Oia for stunning sunsets, Fira for clifftop views, Kamari for black sand beaches. Don't miss the volcano, thermal springs and local wineries.
