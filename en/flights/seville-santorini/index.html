Fly from Seville to Santorini from €275 per person return. The median price is around €303, giving you a solid benchmark for spotting real offers. Transavia France will get you to the Cyclades in just a few hours.

Price and when to book

Prices range from €275 at the lowest to €309 in the samples we've seen. The median sits at €303, so anything near that figure is a decent deal. Book in advance: the earlier you reserve, the better your chances of securing a low fare. Check the example flight: departure 7 January at 18:50 from SVQ, return 12 January at 14:55 from JTR.

Airline and stops

You'll be flying with Transavia France. The outbound journey includes 2 stops, so it's not a direct flight. Although the connections add to your total travel time, they're often what enable these competitive prices. Factor in the stopover time when planning your journey.

Best time to visit

Santorini works as a destination almost year-round. Summer (June to September) is peak season with more tourists and higher prices. For something quieter with better fares, spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer good weather without the July–August rush. Winter is cheaper, but weather is less predictable.

What to do when you arrive

Santorini awaits with its white-and-blue villages, dramatic cliffs and black-sand beaches. Don't miss Oia at sunset, the Palea Kameni hot springs, Ammoudi port and local wineries. The airport (JTR) sits just a few kilometres north of the island, so you'll reach anywhere quickly. Rent a car or use local buses to get around.

The lowest price recorded is €275 per person return. The median is €303. These are real fares we've documented; book as soon as you find something close.

Transavia France is the airline for this route based on our data. Always check the booking platform for specific details of your return journey.

What can I see in Santorini in 5 days?

Spend time in Oia and Fira (main villages), explore Perissa and Kamari beaches, visit a local winery, take a day trip to Delos and Milos if possible. The sample trip lasts 5 days, enough time for the essentials.
