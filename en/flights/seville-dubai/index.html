Best time to visit

Dubai shines from November to March, when heat is manageable. Avoid June to September due to intense desert temperatures. High season overlaps with European winter, so booking with lead time helps you find keener prices.

What to do when you arrive

Dubai blends modernity and tradition: the Burj Khalifa dominates the skyline, Deira souks buzz with spices and gold, and beaches are superb. Stroll the Marina Waterfront, visit the Jumeirah Mosque and explore traditional markets. The airport connects well to the city centre by metro and taxi.
