Best time to travel

November is a good choice: pleasant temperatures in Las Vegas (20–28°C), less heat than summer, and the city remains lively. Avoid July and August when it's very hot. Prices tend to rise in peak season (December, school holidays), so November or early December offers value.

What to do when you arrive

Las Vegas offers the Strip with legendary hotels, Broadway-style shows, world-class casinos and fine dining from top chefs. Outside the city, the Grand Canyon is just over 4 hours by car, Valley of Fire about 1 hour away. Museums, concerts and sporting events keep the city buzzing 24/7. Harry Reid Airport (LAS) is 6 km from the Strip.
