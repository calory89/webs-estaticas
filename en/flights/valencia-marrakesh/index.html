Best time to visit

Marrakech is an attractive destination year-round, though the climate is most pleasant between October and April when temperatures are moderate. Summer months can be very hot. If you're after low prices, watch for Ryanair sales in low season, though these prices are already very competitive.

What to do in Marrakech

Once you land at Marrakech Airport (RAK), you have plenty of options: the Medina with its traditional souks, Jemaa el-Fnaa Square full of life, Majorelle Gardens, the Koutoubia Mosque, or excursions to the Atlas Mountains. The city is accessible and very tourist-friendly, perfect for a short break.
