Airline and stops

Korean Air operates this route with 2 stops on the outbound flight. It's not a direct service, but Korean Air provides solid service and included baggage allowance. Two stops are standard on Canada-Indonesia routes; the extra hours are offset by the price saving.

Best time to visit

Bali has a tropical climate year-round. Dry season (May to October) is best: less rain, perfect beaches and stable prices. Rainy season (November to April) brings lower accommodation rates, but flight prices can fluctuate. Avoid local holidays if you prefer fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive in Bali

Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) is 13 km from Denpasar. Visit luxury beaches in Seminyak and Kuta, ancestral temples like Tanah Lot, rice fields in Ubud, volcano hikes, and diving in Amed. Rent a scooter, eat local (nasi goreng, satay) and haggle in markets. Cost of living is far lower than Toronto.
