Berlin and Paris are just a short hop away. With Transavia France you'll find direct flights from €87 return. Median price sits around €92, so you have room to pick your dates without overspending.

Price and when to book

Flights start at €87 return. Our sample shows variation: €87, €92 and €159. Book as early as possible. Check prices mid-week, they're usually lower than Friday and Sunday departures.

Airline and stops

Fly Transavia France on a direct route with no stops. Departure from Berlin (BER), arrival in Paris. Flight time is around two hours. Check what's included in your fare, as Transavia is a budget carrier.

Best time to visit

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: mild weather, fewer crowds than summer, and steadier prices. July–August is peak season and pricey. Winter is cold, but December offers festive deals.

What to do when you arrive

Paris is the City of Light: Eiffel Tower, Louvre, Notre-Dame, Seine strolls. Museums, cafés, galleries. From the airport (CDG or Orly) you have direct train and bus options into the city centre. Berlin to Paris is a classic trip, enjoy the short flight.
