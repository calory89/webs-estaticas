Best time to travel

Buenos Aires enjoys a temperate climate year-round. Spring (September–November) and autumn (March–May) offer pleasant temperatures and less rain. What to do when you arrive

Buenos Aires is your entry point. Argentina's capital has plenty to offer: the neighbourhoods of La Boca, San Telmo and Recoleta deserve days of exploration. The Teatro Colón is a must-see. From here you can travel to Mendoza (wine region), Córdoba or even Iguazu Falls. With 2 stops, you'll arrive late; rest on day one and plan your itinerary without rush.
