Best time to visit

February and March offer pleasant weather and less rain on the east and south coasts of Sri Lanka. Avoid May to September (southwest monsoon) if you want beaches. High season is December to March, so prices can rise; February may be a sweet spot if you find a good deal.

What to do when you arrive in Colombo

Colombo is your gateway. Rent a car or take a train to explore: Sigiriya (rock fortress), Kandy (Temple of the Tooth), beaches at Mirissa or Unawatuna, and Adam's Peak. Sri Lanka is compact; most spots are 2–4 hours by train. Don't miss the tea plantations in Nuwara Eliya or safaris at Yala.
