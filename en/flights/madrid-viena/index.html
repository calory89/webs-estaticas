Price and when to book

The lowest price we've seen is €122 return. Prices in the sample range from €122 to €132, with a median of €131. Book with a reasonable lead time (2-3 weeks ahead is a solid rule of thumb), though low-cost fares can shift quickly depending on seat availability.

Airline and stops

You'll fly with Ryanair, which has 1 stop on the outbound route. Flights depart Madrid (MAD) late evening (21:00 in the example) and arrive in Vienna (VIE). Always check the details when booking: Ryanair charges extra for luggage, seat selection or changes.

Best time to visit

Vienna is lovely year-round, but October to November is ideal: cool weather, fewer crowds and better fares than summer. December brings Christmas markets if that appeals, but prices rise. Avoid July-August if you're hunting for good deals.

What to do when you arrive

Vienna airport (VIE) is 16 km from the centre, with easy train and bus connections. Must-sees: St Stephen's Cathedral, Schönbrunn and Hofburg palaces, museums along the Ring, and traditional coffee houses. The city is compact and very walkable.
