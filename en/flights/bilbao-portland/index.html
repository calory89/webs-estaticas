Best time to visit

November is low season in Portland, meaning fewer tourists and better prices. Weather is cold and rainy—typical Pacific Northwest—but if you love nature without the crowds, it's ideal. December and January prices spike significantly. If you can be flexible with dates within November or early December, you'll find better fares than other months.

What to do in Portland

Portland is known for its cultural scene, indie shops and outdoor life. Walk through Pearl District, visit Powell's Books (legendary bookstore), explore Forest Park or head to the Oregon Coast. The city is walkable, has good public transport and works perfectly for 5-7 days. From there you can day trip to Multnomah Falls or the Oregon Coast.
