Price and when to book

Prices range from €678 to €757. The median price is €704. Book as early as possible. Ryanair keeps competitive fares on this route, but seats fill quickly near holidays. Search 2–3 months ahead to secure the base fare.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with 3 stopovers. This is not a direct flight, so expect intermediate stops that will extend your total journey time. That's the trade-off for the low price. Check that your cabin baggage meets Ryanair's limits (10 kg) if you want to avoid paying for checked luggage.

Best time to visit Seoul

Seoul is pleasant year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer ideal temperatures. Avoid winter if you don't like intense cold. Prices tend to rise in peak seasons, but the experience is worth it. Search with flexible dates to find deals outside the busiest periods.

What to do when you arrive in Seoul

Incheon (ICN) is the main airport. Direct train or bus connections to central Seoul take about 1 hour. Don't miss Gyeongbokgung Palace, Myeongdong district, Namsan Tower and Insadong's traditional markets. Korean food is spectacular, try authentic bibimbap and kimchi. Metro is cheap and runs everywhere.
