Best time to visit

December is excellent for Rio: it's summer in the Southern Hemisphere, warm and with less rain than January-February. The prices we're seeing (€810–€835) are competitive for that month. If you want pleasant temperatures and fewer peak-season crowds, December is a solid choice. Skip it if carnival season is not your thing.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Tom Jobim International Airport (Galeão), 20 km from the city centre. Buses, metro and taxis connect easily. Rio has Christ the Redeemer, Guanabara Bay, Copacabana and Ipanema beaches, and the Serra da Carioca range to explore. December means beach time without constant rain. Eat well in the old centre and pick up some Portuguese before you go.
