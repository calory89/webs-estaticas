Best time to visit

Thailand's dry season runs November to February with ideal weather and less rain. December and January are peak tourist months, so prices spike. February is a solid alternative if you want good weather without the crowds. Avoid May to October (monsoon season).

What to do when you arrive in Phuket

Phuket is your gateway to Thailand's islands. Reach Phi Phi, Phang Nga or Phhuket Bay from here. On the island: white-sand beaches, Buddhist temples, nightlife in Patong Beach and local markets. The airport is around 30 km north; grab a taxi, minibus or hire a car.
