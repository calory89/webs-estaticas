Airline and connections

Pegasus operates this route. The outbound flight includes 2 connections, so total journey time is longer than a direct service, but the price reflects this. Best time to visit

Zanzibar is worth visiting year-round, but February falls in the dry season with warm temperatures (25–30°C) and light rainfall. June to October is cooler. Skip April and May if you want to avoid constant rain. The sample flight departs 1 February, a solid choice if you're after that balance of good weather and seat availability.

What to do when you arrive

Zanzibar airport (Stone Town Airport, ZNZ) is 50 km from Stone Town. Explore the UNESCO-listed old town, eat at local markets, dive or snorkel the coral reefs, and relax on beaches like Nungwi or Paje. Rent a bike or scooter to get around the island; it's cheap and safe.

FAQ

Is this a direct flight from Seville to Zanzibar?

No. The outbound has 2 connections, so total flight time is longer than a direct service. This partly explains the competitive pricing versus other carriers.

Which airline flies this route?

Pegasus is the airline we've recorded on this Seville–Zanzibar route. Check their website for schedules and baggage policy before you buy.

What can I see and do in Zanzibar?

Stone Town (old quarter and UNESCO site), white-sand beaches (Nungwi, Paje, Kendwa), diving and snorkelling on reefs, local spice markets, and nearby islands like Pemba. February weather is dry and hot, perfect for beach days.
