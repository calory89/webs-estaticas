Best time to visit Bogotá

Bogotá has mild weather year-round thanks to its altitude. There's no strong peak tourist season like other destinations, so you can travel almost anytime. Keep in mind prices can vary during local holidays or Spanish school breaks. Check monthly offers to adjust your budget.

What to do when you arrive in Bogotá

You land at El Dorado international airport (BOG), about 13 km from the city centre. La Candelaria is the colonial district with essential museums, including the Gold Museum. Plaza de Bolívar, the cafés of Usaquén, and views from Monserrate are classic visits. The city has diverse food scenes and neighbourhoods like Chapinero and La Zona Rosa are great for dining and nightlife. Use public transport or taxi apps: it's safe and affordable.
