Best time to travel

Bogotá is a year-round destination, though it's an Andean city at 2,640 metres with temperate weather. Avoid heavy rain seasons (April–May and October–November) if you prefer outdoor activities. December to March and July to August usually have less rainfall. Bear in mind that European school holidays drive up prices, so travel in low season if your calendar allows.

What to do in Bogotá

Colombia's capital has world-class museums like the Gold Museum, La Candelaria with colonial architecture, and Monserrate for panoramic views. Eat in Usaquén, stroll Calle 82, and visit parks like Bolívar. Bogotá is a perfect base for day trips to Zipaquirá, Guatavita, or the coffee region. El Dorado International Airport is 15 km from the centre, with good public and private transport links.
