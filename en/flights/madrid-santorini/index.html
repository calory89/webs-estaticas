Best time to visit

Santorini is a year-round destination, but each season has its appeal. Summer is hot and crowded; winter offers lower prices and fewer tourists, though the weather is less predictable. December can be a good choice if you're after peace and value for money, although some restaurants and activities may have limited hours.

What to do when you arrive

Santorini is famous for its Aegean views, white and blue villages (Oia, Fira), and volcanic sand beaches. You can explore blue-domed churches, walk along the cliff edge, taste local wines, or take boat excursions. Santorini International Airport (Thira) is about 12 km from town; buses, taxis, and car rental are available.

What is there to see in Santorini?

Santorini stands out for its white and blue villages (Oia and Fira), dramatic cliffs, volcanic sand beaches, vineyards, and volcano views. There are also traditional churches, archaeological museums, and boat trips to the crater. It's perfect for enjoying the Aegean at a relaxed pace.
