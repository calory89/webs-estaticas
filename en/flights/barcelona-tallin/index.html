Best time to visit

Tallinn shines in summer (June to August) with long days and mild temperatures, ideal for exploring the Old Town. For fewer crowds and lower prices, consider May or September. Winters are harsh but magical if you fancy snow. Pack warm clothes for any season.

What to do when you arrive in Tallinn

Lennart Meri Tallinn Airport is 4 km from the city centre. Tallinn's Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage site: medieval alleyways, towers and walls. Walk around Toompea, climb Kiek in de Kök tower for the view, explore museums and enjoy local cafés and Estonian food. The city is compact, perfect for 2-3 days.

What's there to see in Tallinn besides the Old Town?

The Old Town is essential. Toompea (the castle hill), historic churches, museums of Estonian history and traditional cafés are also worth visiting. With more time, nearby islands offer beaches and nature.
