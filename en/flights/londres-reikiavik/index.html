Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €104 return, though the average hovers around €107. Prices range from €104 to €147 depending on dates and availability. It's ideal to book as far in advance as possible to access the lowest fares. This route fills up quickly, especially during peak season (summer and northern lights in winter).

Airlines and stops

easyJet operates this route as a direct flight with no stops. You get the complete journey without changing planes, which saves time and hassle. Flight time is short, perfect for a weekend break or a full week away. Travel light or add baggage if you need it.

Best time to visit

Iceland is a year-round destination. In summer (June to August) you enjoy the midnight sun and green landscapes; in winter (October to March) you might see the northern lights. Spring and autumn bring fewer crowds. Be prepared for changeable weather whatever the season—dress warmly and expect quick shifts.

What to do when you arrive

Keflavík Airport (KEF) is 50 km from Reykjavik. Explore the Golden Circle route (waterfalls, geysers, national parks), the Blue Lagoon, glaciers and coastal cliffs. If you travel in winter, spend time chasing northern lights away from city lights. Distances are large but roads are well signposted.

FAQ

What's the minimum price to fly from London to Iceland?

The lowest price we've recorded is €104 return. The average is €107, though we've also seen prices up to €147 depending on dates. The earlier you book, the more cheap options you'll find.

Are there direct flights from London to Iceland?

Yes. easyJet offers direct flights on this route with no stops. It's the fastest and most comfortable way to reach Iceland from London.

What's the best time to visit Iceland?

It depends what you want to see. In summer (June to August) there's light almost all day. In winter (October to March) you can see the northern lights, though it's colder and there are fewer daylight hours. Spring and autumn are quieter and cheaper.

What can I do in Iceland?

Iceland offers unique landscapes: waterfalls (Skógafoss, Seljalandsfoss), active geysers, the Blue Lagoon, glaciers and coastal cliffs. In winter, hunt for northern lights. Everything is within a few hours' drive from Reykjavik, ideal for a week-long trip.
