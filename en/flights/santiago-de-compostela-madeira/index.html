Best time to visit

Madeira has mild weather year-round, but October to May is the best season for pleasant temperatures and less rain. Avoid July and August if you're looking for fewer crowds and lower prices. Spring (March–May) is particularly lovely for walking and exploring the island.

What to do when you arrive

Madeira airport (Cristiano Ronaldo) is 20 minutes from Funchal. Walk the cliffs at Ponta de São Lourenço, try local cable cars, visit the Botanical Garden and enjoy views from Pico do Arieiro viewpoint. Levada trails (traditional water channels) are essential. Local gastronomy and Madeira wine complete the experience.
