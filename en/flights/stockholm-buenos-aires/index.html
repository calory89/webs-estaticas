Price and when to book

The cheapest ticket we found is €1,011 (return). Median pricing hovers around €1,093, with options going up to €1,367. The earlier you book, the better: Ryanair's international fares climb as departure dates approach. Don't leave it to the last minute. Airlines and stops

Ryanair operates this route with 3 stops. It's not a direct flight, so you'll change planes three times. The layovers extend your journey, but the price makes up for it if you're on a tight budget. Check connection times carefully: outbound departs around 20:50 and the return arrives after 05:45.

Best time to visit

Buenos Aires is worth visiting year-round. If you want to avoid extreme heat (December to February), go in spring (September–November) or autumn (March–May). Prices don't automatically drop in low season: what matters is booking well ahead, regardless of when you travel. Keep checking for surprise deals.

What to do when you arrive

Buenos Aires won't disappoint. The city buzzes with nightlife, world-class food, live tango, belle époque architecture, and neighbourhoods like La Boca, San Telmo, and Palermo. If you venture beyond the city, Argentina has Mendoza (wine country), Bariloche (lakes and mountains), and Iguazu Falls. The international airport (Ministro Pistarini) is 35 km from the centre; buses and taxis get you downtown in under an hour.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Stockholm to Buenos Aires?

No. This Ryanair route includes 3 stops, so it's a connecting flight. There is no direct option at the prices we checked.

How much does a one-way ticket from Stockholm to Buenos Aires cost?

The prices we found are return fares. The cheapest ticket is €1,011 for the full trip (outbound + return). A one-way would be less, but it depends on the date and airline available at the time of booking.

Is Ryanair safe for such long flights?

Ryanair is a regulated airline operating across Europe to international safety standards. For intercontinental routes, it uses long-haul aircraft. The low price doesn't affect flight safety, it impacts comfort (narrow seats, limited baggage).

What to see in Buenos Aires beyond the city centre?

The city itself deserves several days (tango, museums, parks, food). With more time, head to Mendoza (vineyards, 1,100 km away), Bariloche (lakes and hiking), or fly to Puerto Iguazú for Iguazu Falls. Each destination requires domestic flights or overland travel from Buenos Aires.
