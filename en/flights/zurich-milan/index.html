Best time to visit

November is ideal for Milan: mild temperatures (10–15°C), fewer tourists than summer, and autumn colours in the parks. Avoid July and August if you want lower prices and fewer crowds. Spring (April–May) also offers pleasant weather and competitive fares.

What to do in Milan

Milan is Italy's fashion and design capital. Visit the Duomo, Castello Sforzesco, Pinacoteca di Brera, and the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II. Navigli, with its canals, is perfect for strolling and dining. Don't miss Leonardo's Last Supper (book ahead). Take a day trip to Como or Bergamo.
