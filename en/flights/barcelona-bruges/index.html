Best time to travel

Bruges is lovely year-round, but autumn and winter see fewer crowds and less congestion on the canals. Winter (December-February) has a magical atmosphere with possible festive lighting. Spring (March-May) and autumn (September-October) usually offer good weather and decent prices. Book several weeks ahead to secure a low rate.

What to do when you arrive in Bruges

Bruges is small but packed: wander the historic centre, take a canal boat tour, visit the Basilica of the Holy Blood and the Belfry. Try Belgian waffles and local chocolates. It's only 15 km from the coast (De Panne, Ostende), so a beach day is doable. The centre is very walkable from the train station, which connects to Brussels Airport.
