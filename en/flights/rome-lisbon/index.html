Price and when to book

Prices start from €68 return. In our sample we found three options: €68, €70 and €87. Book as early as possible to secure the best fares. Ryanair has availability across multiple dates, so compare several consecutive days to find your best deal.

Ryanair: direct and straightforward

Ryanair operates this route with direct flights (no stops). You'll depart from Rome Fiumicino and arrive at Lisbon Humberto Delgado in just over two hours. Travel with hand luggage included in the base fare, or add a large bag when you book if needed.

Best time to travel

Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Good fares are often found during these months. Avoid July and August if you're looking for a bargain, as demand peaks then.

What to do in Lisbon

Explore the Alfama district, visit the Monastery of the Jerónimos and Belém Tower. Climb up to Saint George's Castle for panoramic views, ride a classic tram, try pastéis de nata in a local bakery. The Tagus river and Portuguese azulejo tiles are unmissable.
