Best time to visit

November is low season in Mykonos: fewer tourists, cheaper accommodation and restaurants, still pleasant weather. Beaches remain enjoyable though the sea is cooler. Want buzzy nightlife and crowded beaches? Go summer. Seeking calm and bargains? Autumn is your time. November temperatures hover around 18–20°C, perfect for exploring without heat.

What to do in Mykonos

Mykonos means beaches, whitewashed villages and nightlife. Explore Mykonos Town with shops and tavernas on the waterfront, swim at Paradise or Psarou Beach, visit the Armenistis lighthouse at sunset. Small archaeological museums are worth an hour. Eat by the sea, sip frappé at a clifftop café, savour Greek cuisine. The airport (JMK) sits 2 km from town—very manageable.
