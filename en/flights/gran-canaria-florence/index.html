Best time to visit

Florence is lovely year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer the best weather and fewer crowds than peak summer. Winter is quieter and cooler, but museums and monuments stay open. Skip July and August if you can't handle throngs of tourists.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Florence airport (Peretola), just 4 km from the city centre and easily reached by bus or taxi. Must-sees: the Cathedral, the Uffizi Gallery, and the Ponte Vecchio. Walk along the Arno, climb the Duomo, try bistecca alla fiorentina. The city is small and walkable in two or three days.
