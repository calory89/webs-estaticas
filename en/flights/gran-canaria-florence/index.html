Airline and stopovers

Vueling is your carrier on this route. All flights include one stop, so there's no direct option. That stopover saves you money and gives you schedule flexibility. Connection times vary, but Vueling manages transfers well. Pack light carry-on or budget for checked baggage fees.

Best time to visit

Florence shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with fewer crowds than summer. Winter is quiet but cold. Skip July and August unless you enjoy heat and tourist masses. From Gran Canaria, regular flights run year-round, so pick based on your weather preference.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Florence airport (FLR), 4 km from the city centre. Bus or taxi gets you downtown in 20 minutes. The Cathedral, Duomo and Uffizi Gallery are must-sees. Walk the Ponte Vecchio, eat at Oltrarno trattorias and climb Piazzale Michelangelo for sunset views over the city.

No, all flights on this route include one stop. That's standard between the Canary Islands and Italy: connections give you schedule options and lower fares than chasing rare direct routes.

Is Vueling cheap for this route?

Yes, with fares from €172 return, Vueling delivers good value. Remember extra baggage costs add up. If you travel with just a carry-on, your final price is very competitive.

How many days do I need in Florence?

3–4 days lets you see the essentials at a relaxed pace. Two days covers the Cathedral, Uffizi and Ponte Vecchio; 4–5 days add smaller museums, churches and day trips to nearby Tuscan towns.
