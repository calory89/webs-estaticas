Best time to travel

January and February see the lowest fares because Central Europe is in low season. Budapest is cold but beautiful in winter: fewer tourists, Christmas markets still running in early January, and thermal baths are the perfect activity. October and November are also budget-friendly with decent weather. Skip July and August if you want to keep costs down.

What to do in Budapest

The Hungarian Parliament is the city's icon. The Széchenyi and Gellért thermal baths are a must. Cross the Danube, explore the Jewish Quarter, eat paprika in any local restaurant, and catch views from Buda Hill. A long weekend covers the essentials. Ferenc Liszt airport is 20 km from the centre; bus, metro, or taxi take you straight downtown.
