Best time to visit Budapest

Budapest shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), when weather is mild and crowds are lighter than summer. Winter is cold but ideal if you want lower prices and the chance to see the Danube frozen. Skip July and August if you prefer fewer tourists.

What to do when you arrive

The Danube splits the city into Buda (with the Castle and stunning views) and Pest (commercial and cultural hub). Don't miss the Széchenyi Thermal Baths, the Hungarian Parliament Building, St Stephen's Basilica, or a browse through the Central Market. Ruin bars are perfect for cheap eats and meeting locals.
