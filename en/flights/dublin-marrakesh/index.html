Best time to visit

Marrakech is pleasant year-round, but October to April offers milder temperatures (15–25°C) and fewer crowds than July–August. November and February are quieter months with reasonable weather. Winter is ideal if you want fewer tourists, though occasional rain is possible.

What to do on arrival in Marrakech

Marrakech airport (RAK) is about 4 km from the city centre. Taxis, buses and pre-booked transfers are available. In the city itself, the Medina (old town) is essential: Jemaa el-Fnaa square, souks, palaces and Menara gardens. If time allows, Atlas villages are under 2 hours away by car.

FAQ

Is the Dublin-Marrakech flight really direct?

Yes, no stopovers. Ryanair flies non-stop from Dublin to Marrakech, saving travel time.

What's the cheapest price you've seen on this route?

€56 return. Fares range from €56 to €81 depending on date and availability.

What does Ryanair's base fare include?

Seat and small cabin bag only. Large luggage, seat upgrades, food and drinks are charged separately. Always check what you've added at checkout.

What must I see in Marrakech in a short stay?

The Medina and Jemaa el-Fnaa square are must-dos. Add Menara gardens, the Bahia Palace and a souk walk. With more time, a day trip to the Atlas mountains or Essaouira beach are very popular.
