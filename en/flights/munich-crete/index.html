Fares range from €209 to €280 depending on dates. The median sits at €230 return. As always, book early for the best prices. Don't wait until the last minute—fares rise as departure approaches.

Airline and stopovers

Lufthansa operates this route non-stop: no changes, no surprises. Departure from Munich (MUC) and arrival in Heraklion (HER). A straightforward, comfortable flight to reach Crete without complications.

Best time to travel

Crete enjoys warm weather almost year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant temperatures without extreme summer heat. Winter is quieter and cheaper, though some areas can see rainy days. Choose based on what you're after: beaches with fewer crowds or perfect weather.

What to do when you arrive in Heraklion

Heraklion airport is your gateway to Crete. From there, reach beaches like Ammoudara or Matala, visit the Palace of Knossos (one of the largest Minoan ruins), explore the old town of Chania, or relax in coastal villages like Rethymno. Crete has culture, history and sea in abundance.
