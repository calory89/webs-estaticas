Best time to visit

Stockholm is stunning in winter if you enjoy cold weather and short days (ideal for northern lights in January–February), though it's the priciest season. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer good weather and moderate prices. Summer has long days but is peak season.

What to do when you arrive in Stockholm

Explore Gamla Stan, the colourful Old Town with narrow streets, visit the Royal Palace or museums on Djurgården island. The metro is famous for its decorated stations. Eat freshwater crayfish in season or try Swedish meatballs. From Arlanda airport (STO) you reach the city centre in about 20 minutes by train.

Gamla Stan (Old Town), the Royal Palace, museums on Djurgården, historic churches and the famous Swedish metro. The city spans 14 islands, perfect for boat tours.
