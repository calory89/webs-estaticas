Best time to visit

Stockholm shines in summer (June to August) with long days and mild weather. In winter (December-February) prices drop, but cold and snow dominate, ideal if you want Christmas or cheap travel. Spring and autumn offer a pleasant middle ground.

What to do on arrival

You land at Arlanda, 20 km from the city centre. Gamla Stan (Old Town) is essential: medieval cobbled streets and colourful buildings. Visit the Royal Palace, grab a coffee in Norrmalm, and in winter enjoy the festive atmosphere. The metro connects everything in minutes.
