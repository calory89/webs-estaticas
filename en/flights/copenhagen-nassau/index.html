Best time to visit

Nassau enjoys pleasant weather year-round, but December to April is peak season: less rain, ideal temperatures and flawless beaches. Outside those months it's equally welcoming with fewer crowds. Avoid September and October due to hurricane season in the North Atlantic.

Things to do on arrival

You'll land at Nassau International Airport (NAS). The Bahamas capital offers turquoise beaches, resorts, nightlife and the famous Atlantis. Paradise Island and Blue Lagoon are minutes away by boat. For local flavour, explore the historic centre and markets, or island-hop to nearby cayes. Crystal-clear water is the main draw: snorkelling, diving and paddleboarding are must-dos.
