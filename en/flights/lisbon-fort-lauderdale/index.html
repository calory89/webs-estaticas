Best time to travel

Fort Lauderdale has good weather almost year-round, but the Northern Hemisphere winter (November to March) is high season: less rain, pleasant temperatures and perfect beaches. If you want to save, spring and autumn usually have lower fares. Avoid hurricane season (June to November).

What to do when you arrive in Fort Lauderdale

The international airport (FLL) is 30 km from the city. Beach is first: Las Olas Boulevard walks, shopping at The Galleria, or relax on Miami Beach (30 min by car). Diving and cruise enthusiasts depart from here. You also have the Everglades nearby if you fancy nature.
