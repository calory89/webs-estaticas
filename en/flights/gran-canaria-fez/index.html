Check your baggage allowance based on your fare type: Iberia Express usually includes cabin luggage, but confirm if you need to check a bag.

Best time to visit

Fez is lovely year-round, but October–November and March–April offer ideal temperatures (20–25 °C) without summer heat peaks or winter chill. These months also mean fewer crowds in the medina. If you travel then, explore the souks and the Al Quaraouiyine University without the rush.

What to do on arrival

Fez airport (Saïss) is about 15 km from the city centre. Arrange your transfer in advance: taxi, private transfer, or rental car to suit your budget. Once in Fez, the medina is essential: wander its narrow streets, visit the spice and leather souks, and catch the Blue Gate. Mérida el Bab also deserves a stop, and if you have time, Moulay Idris is under an hour away.
