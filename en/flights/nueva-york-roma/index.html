Best time to travel New York to Rome

Rome is stunning year-round, but prices vary by season. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: pleasant weather, fewer crowds, and cheaper fares than summer. Winters are quiet and cold; July and August see peak demand and prices rise accordingly.

What to do when you arrive in Rome

Rome needs no introduction. The Colosseum, Vatican, Trevi Fountain, Roman Forum, and Pantheon are must-sees. Linger in the squares, eat your way through Trastevere, climb St. Peter's Basilica, and wander the city on foot to stumble upon hidden gems. Spend at least a week to enjoy it without rushing.
