Best time to go

November is late autumn in Switzerland. Temperatures drop (5–10°C) and rain is likely, but tourist crowds are thin, which helps prices. If you want to see Zurich without masses, this date works well. The Alps start to snow late month, so pack warm layers.

What to do when you arrive

Zurich is Switzerland's economic and cultural hub. Walk the Altstadt (old town), visit Lake Zurich, explore museums like the Kunsthaus, and eat in waterside cafés. From the airport, you reach the centre by train in 10 minutes. Time permitting, the Alps and Lake Lucerne are close. November lets you enjoy the city without crowds.

What to see in Zurich in 5 days?

The Altstadt, the lake, Gothic churches, museums, shops on Bahnhofstrasse, and day trips to nearby towns or the Alps. Public transport is excellent and all is well linked from the airport.
