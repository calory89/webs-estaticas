Best time to visit

Mauritius Island has tropical climate year-round. Dry season (May–November) is most comfortable; July–August peak tourist months. Avoid January–February for cyclone risk. December–April brings rain but often lower prices. Travel when your calendar allows, there's no predictable sale season here.

What to do when you arrive

Mauritius Island is beaches, coral reefs and multicultural heritage. Visit Port Louis (capital), Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam market, Tamarin Bay for dolphins, Black River Gorges, or relax on Bénitiers beaches. MRU airport is 50 km from centre; rent a car or use taxis. Plan 5–10 days.
