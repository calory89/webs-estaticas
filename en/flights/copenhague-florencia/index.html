Best time to visit

Florence is lovely in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), when the weather is mild and crowds are thinner than in summer. Winter months (December–February) often have lower prices, though expect cold weather and rain. Skip July and August if you want to avoid peak season crowds and higher fares.

What to do when you arrive

From Florence airport (Peretola) you're just 20–30 minutes from the city centre. Don't miss the Duomo, the Uffizi Gallery, Ponte Vecchio and Palazzo Pitti. Florence is compact and walkable. If you have extra days, explore nearby Tuscan towns like Siena or San Gimignano.
