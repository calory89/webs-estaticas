Best time to travel

Mauritius is a tropical destination year-round. June to September offer drier weather and mild temperatures. Cyclone season runs November to April, so avoid that period if you prefer stable conditions. How far in advance you book matters more than the season for finding good prices.

What to do when you arrive

Mauritius awaits with white-sand beaches, coral reefs and volcanic landscapes. The international airport serves Port-Louis and surroundings. You can explore Black River Gorges, visit the Pamplemousses Botanical Garden, dive on the reefs, or discover the capital. There are also museums, local markets and hiking routes in the interior highlands.
