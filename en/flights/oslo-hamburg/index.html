Price and when to book

Flights start from €126 return. Prices range from €126 to €132 depending on dates and availability. Book two to three months ahead to lock in low fares; waiting until the last minute usually means higher prices. December is peak season, so if you must travel then, book early.

Airline and connections

Norwegian Air Shuttle operates this route with direct flights, no stops. Departures around 12:35 from Oslo, arrival in Hamburg near 14:00 local time. Return flights depart around 11:45. Flying direct means fewer hassles, less time on the ground, and better odds your luggage arrives without delays.

Best time to visit

Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild weather, fewer crowds, and competitive prices. Winter is pricier but Hamburg's Christmas markets are spectacular if you travel in December. Summer draws mass tourism. Avoid local holidays and long weekends if you want the best fares.

What to do when you arrive

Hamburg is a gateway to northern Europe. Walk through Speicherstadt, the historic warehouse district; the port is stunning. Visit Miniatur Wunderland if you love miniature models. The Philharmonic Hall is striking architecture. Eat fresh herring by the harbour. Hamburg Airport (HAM) is 9 km from the city centre; bus or train in 25 minutes.
