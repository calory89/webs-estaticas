Best time to travel

Sardinia shines in autumn and winter: fewer crowds than summer and pleasant temperatures for exploring. November is a solid choice, quieter beaches, lush countryside and lower prices than peak season. February and March are also budget-friendly if you're planning a long weekend.

What to do when you arrive

Cagliari, Sardinia's capital, greets you with urban beaches, the ancient Castello neighbourhood and views over the Gulf of Angels. The island offers secluded coves, inland trekking, nuraghe archaeological sites and local food. Rent a car or use buses to explore beyond the city. Head to Poetto or Chia beaches without leaving the south.
