Best time to visit Sardinia

Sardinia shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): clean seas, sunshine without the heat, and fewer crowds than July–August. If you travel in winter (like the 30 January example), you'll find solitude and even lower prices, but the water will be cold. The sweet spot for price and weather is May or September.

What to do when you arrive in Cagliari

Cagliari is your gateway to the island. Its old town, the cathedral, and views from the Bastion of San Remy deserve half a day. From the airport (4 km from the centre) there's a direct bus. The Costa Smeralda to the north and Chia beaches to the south are key spots. Rent a car if you want to explore beyond the city.
