Airline and stops

Transavia France operates this connection as a direct flight from Paris to Istanbul. No stops means less time at the airport, easier baggage handling and the chance to arrive fresh at your destination. The journey is short and comfortable.

Best time to visit

Istanbul works well in almost every season. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: mild weather and fewer crowds. If you travel in summer, expect heat and more tourism. Winter is mild but can have rainy days. Choose based on what you're after: peaceful culture or a livelier atmosphere.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Istanbul Airport (IST), well connected to the centre by metro and bus. Once in the city, visit the Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia and Topkapi Palace. Walk through the Grand Bazaar, stroll along the Golden Horn and sample Turkish food in the markets. Istanbul blends history, art and modernity in every neighbourhood.

Is it a direct flight?

Yes, Transavia France operates this route without stops. You depart from Paris (PAR) and arrive directly in Istanbul (IST), which reduces total travel time.

Which airline flies this route?

How many days is it best to spend in Istanbul?

Between 4 and 5 days is a good balance to see the main sights unhurried: historic monuments, bazaars, boat trips and local life. With more time, explore modern neighbourhoods and nearby islands.
