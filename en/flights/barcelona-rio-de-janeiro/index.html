Price and when to book

The cheapest flight costs €902, though most prices fall between €967 and €1,398. Book in advance: the earlier you purchase, the better the rate. Search for mid-week flights and avoid dates around Brazilian holidays. Check regularly to catch deals.

Airline and stopovers

TAP Portugal is your carrier. The journey includes 2 stopovers, so expect a longer trip but a more budget-friendly one. Stopovers become easier to handle when you book early and choose your connections wisely.

Best time to visit

Rio de Janeiro shines year-round, but the dry season (May to September) offers the best weather. If you travel these months, your trip will be more comfortable. December-January is peak season with higher prices and busier attractions.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Galeão (Tom Jobim). The city offers everything from Corcovado and Sugarloaf Mountain to the bohemian neighbourhood of Lapa. Copacabana and Ipanema beaches are unmissable. Eat along Rua Ateniense and soak up carioca culture at your own pace.
