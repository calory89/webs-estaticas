Airline and stopovers

Best time to visit

Sofia shines in spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November): mild weather and fewer crowds. Winter is cold but cheap. Summer is hot and pricier. Choose based on your cold tolerance and budget.

What to do when you arrive

Alexander Nevsky Cathedral is a must-see, with its golden dome and stunning mosaics. Walk through the Basilica of Saint Sophia and climb Sveta Nedelya Church. The bohemian Vitosha neighbourhood is great for food and drinks. Don't miss the archaeological museums or the thermal baths in Bansko if you extend your trip.
