Best time to visit

Japan has distinct seasons. Spring (March-May) with cherry blossoms is stunning but pricey. Autumn (September-November) offers mild weather and gorgeous colours at lower fares. Winter (December-February) is cold but quieter. Summer is hot and humid. Plan ahead: the earlier you book, the better the prices.

What to do when you arrive in Kyoto

Kyoto is Japan's ancient capital: temples like Fushimi Inari, the Golden Pavilion and Arashiyama Bamboo Grove are must-sees. Stroll the traditional Gion district, try kaiseki (haute cuisine) and explore zen gardens. Weather is mild year-round. Arrive early to adjust to the time difference.
