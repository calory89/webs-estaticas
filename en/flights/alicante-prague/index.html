Best time to visit

Prague is beautiful year-round. Winter (December to February) offers cheaper flights and fewer crowds, though it's cold. Spring (April to May) and autumn (September to October) are ideal: pleasant weather and moderate prices. Summer is peak season, more flights but pricier too.

What to do when you arrive

Prague awaits with Prague Castle, Charles Bridge, Old Town Square and St Vitus Cathedral. The historic centre is compact and walkable. Try Czech goulash, visit local breweries and climb Petrin Lookout Tower for stunning views. Five days covers the essentials comfortably.
