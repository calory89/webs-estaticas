Best time to visit

Nassau has tropical weather year-round. The rainy season runs from June to November, whilst December to May offers drier and more stable conditions. For hurricane avoidance, January to April are the safest months. Travel in low season (May-August) if you prefer fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Nassau International Airport (NAS), very close to the city centre. The city offers white sand beaches, underwater parks and vibrant nightlife. Paradise Island, just minutes away, has resorts and water activities. The historic centre features colonial architecture and duty-free shops. Crystal-clear waters are perfect for diving and snorkelling.

FAQ

Does Air Canada fly direct from Vienna to Nassau?

No, this route has 2 stops. Air Canada operates it with connections, not as a direct flight.

When is the best month to visit Nassau?

January to April offers the best weather: pleasant temperatures and less rainfall. Avoid June to November due to the rainy season and hurricane risk.

What to see in Nassau in a week?

Beaches like Cable Beach and Paradise Island, the Atlantis Aquarium, the historic centre with Fort Fincastle, diving and snorkelling on the reefs, and nightlife at Junkanoo Beach. Don't miss a boat tour to nearby islands.
