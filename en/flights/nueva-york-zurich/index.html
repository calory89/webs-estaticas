Price and when to book

Flights start from €533 return. The median price is €544, and we've seen fares up to €643. Book as far in advance as possible: the earlier you purchase, the better the price. Check regularly, as fares vary depending on availability and demand.

Airline and stopovers

Icelandair operates this route with 1 stopover. The flights are not direct, so you'll have one stop before reaching Zurich. It's common for the stopover to be in Iceland, which may give you the option to explore Reykjavik if you wish, though most connections are relatively quick.

Best time to visit

Zurich is beautiful year-round. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter (December-February) is perfect if you fancy snow and the Alps, though prices rise. Avoid school holidays if you're looking for lower fares.

What to do when you arrive

Zurich is the gateway to Switzerland. Explore the Altstadt (Old Town), walk along the shores of Lake Zurich, head to the nearby Alps, or take a train to Interlaken or Lucerne. The airport has excellent public transport connections. Don't miss the world-class museums and Swiss cuisine.
