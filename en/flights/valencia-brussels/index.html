Best time to travel

Flights to Brussels are relatively stable in price year-round. October, November and early December are usually good windows: autumn weather, fewer massive crowds and contained fares. Christmas and Easter will spike prices. For weekend breaks or bank holidays, book at least two weeks ahead.

What to do when you arrive in Brussels

You'll land at Brussels Airport (Zaventem). The Grand Place is a must: medieval architecture that takes your breath away. Walk through the Sablon neighbourhood, try Belgian beers at any café, visit the Royal Palace and, time permitting, the Basilica of the Sacred Heart. It's also a perfect jumping-off point for exploring Flanders and nearby towns.

Is €69 the final price or are there hidden fees?

It's the airfare price return. Ryanair adds costs for baggage, seat selection and other options, so your final total will be higher. Check the terms before you pay.

Is the flight direct from Valencia to Brussels?

Yes, Ryanair offers direct flights on this route. No stops, no plane changes.

Which airport is Brussels?

Brussels-Zaventem (BRU) is the main one. It's 12 km from the city centre; a train will get you there in 20 minutes. It's modern and well connected.

Is it worth booking now or waiting for sales?

If your dates are flexible, book soon: Ryanair occasionally drops prices but it's unpredictable. At €69 there isn't much room to drop further. If those are your only dates, book at least 15-20 days before your trip.
