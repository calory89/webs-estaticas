What to do in Brussels

Brussels is Belgium's capital and offers history, gastronomy and medieval architecture. Walk around the Grand Place, one of Europe's most beautiful squares; visit the St Michael's Cathedral; taste Belgian beers and artisan chocolate. Brussels Airport (BRU) is well connected to the city centre by public transport.
