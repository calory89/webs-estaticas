Best time to visit

December is an excellent month: less rain, pleasant cooler temperatures (5-15°C), and Tokyo shines with festive decorations. Avoid July and August if you dislike extreme heat and humidity. Spring (April-May) is also lovely but pricier. Mid-November to February tends to offer better value.

What to do on arrival

Tokyo awaits with traditional temples, Shinjuku's skyscrapers, neighbourhoods like Shibuya and Harajuku, and world-class gastronomy. No need to hire a car: the metro is efficient and cheap. Visit Senso-ji, stroll through Ginza, try karaoke. You'll arrive at Narita or Haneda airport, both well connected to the city centre.
