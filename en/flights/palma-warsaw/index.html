Best time to visit

Warsaw is worth visiting year-round, but December (as in the example) is cold with sub-zero temperatures. If you prefer milder weather, try spring (April–May) or autumn (September–October). Prices tend to spike in July–August and over Christmas, so aim for shoulder season to keep costs down.

What to do on arrival

Warsaw is Poland's capital: explore the Old Town (UNESCO site), the Palace of Culture and Science, and the Ghetto Uprising Monument. Visit war museums, try pierniki and borscht, stroll along the Vistula. Modlin Airport is 30 km from the centre; coaches and trains run direct to town.
