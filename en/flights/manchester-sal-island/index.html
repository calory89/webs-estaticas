Best time to visit

Sal Island shines year-round, but the driest and warmest months are October to June. During European winter (November to March), the climate is ideal: temperatures between 20–28°C, moderate winds, and minimal rainfall. Avoid September and October if you want the most stable weather. Book ahead during peak season.

What to do when you arrive

Sal Island is paradise for pristine beaches, kitesurfing, and diving. Santa Maria is the main tourist town, with beachfront bars and shops. Pedra Lume features a historic salt flat and calm waters. Nearby reefs are perfect for snorkelling. The international airport is 10 km from Santa Maria, quick taxi ride into town.
