Price and when to book

The lowest price is €573, but the median sits at €683 return. Prices in the sample range from €573 to €781. Book in advance: fares drop when you reserve early. Avoid the final weeks before departure if you're hunting for deals.

Airline and direct flights

Iberojet Airlines operates this entire route. Best part: it's a direct flight Madrid-Punta Cana, no stops. Departures from Madrid (MAD) to Punta Cana (PUJ). No time wasted on connections or layovers in other airports.

Best time to travel

Punta Cana is a beach destination year-round. Hurricane season in the Caribbean runs June to November, so December to April typically offers more stable weather. That said, prices depend more on Spanish holiday periods than the season itself.

What to do when you arrive in Punta Cana

Bavaro and El Cortecito beaches are must-sees. Don't miss reef diving, Isla Saona trips, or natural cenotes. Punta Cana Airport is around 20 km from the main resort areas. Many hotels include transfers in their packages.
