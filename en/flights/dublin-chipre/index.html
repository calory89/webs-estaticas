Best time to visit Cyprus

Cyprus shines April–May and October–November: guaranteed sun, mild temps (23–28°C) and warm sea. Summer (June–August) is scorching and pricey. Winters are mild but rainfall is on the cards. For value, travel outside peak season.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Larnaca (LCA), Cyprus's southern gateway. Rent a car or take a bus to Paphos or Nicosia. The coast offers white-sand beaches and fishing villages; inland, Byzantine churches and the Troodos mountains await. Kourion ruins and Kolossi Castle are worth a trip. Try local meze and Commandaria wine.

FAQ

Are there direct flights Dublin–Cyprus?

Not in this search. Ryanair offers a connection with one stop outbound. Direct flights on this route are rare and pricier.

Is Ryanair safe for flying to Cyprus?

Yes. Ryanair is a major European carrier. Remember its rules: free cabin bag, paid checked luggage, and strict policies on changes and cancellations.

When is it cheapest to fly to Cyprus?

Outside July–August and school holidays (Christmas, Easter). Late spring (April–May) and autumn (October–November) give good value and weather. Book 2–3 months ahead.

Where do I land in Cyprus?

Larnaca (LCA) is the main international airport. It's 30 km from the city; a rental car, taxi or bus gets you to town or the coast in under an hour.
