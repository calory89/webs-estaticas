Airline and stops

Juneyao Airlines operates this route with 1 stop. It's not a direct flight, but the price is competitive for such a long journey. The stopover gives you a break mid-trip, though it will add a few hours to your total travel time.

Best time to visit

Bangkok is hot and humid most of the year. The cool season (November to February) is best: less rain and more comfortable temperatures. These months are peak tourist season, so prices climb. If you're after budget fares, consider May to October, though you'll hit the monsoon.

What to do when you arrive

Bangkok awaits with stunning temples (Wat Arun, Wat Phra Kaew), floating markets and incredible street food. Suvarnabhumi International Airport (BKK) is about 30 km from the centre; take the airport train or a taxi. From Bangkok, you can hop to the islands (Phuket, Koh Samui) or head north to Chiang Mai.
