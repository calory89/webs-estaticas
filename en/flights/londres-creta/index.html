Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Swiss International Air Lines. This route has 1 stopover on the outbound leg, so it's not a direct flight. The stopover is standard on London-to-Crete routes, and Swiss typically offers good value.

Best time to visit

Crete is ideal in autumn and winter if you want to avoid crowds and extreme heat. November is excellent: quiet beaches, pleasant weather (18–22°C), and fewer tourists. June to September are peak summer months with higher prices. Spring (April–May) is also very pleasant.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Heraklion Airport (HER). Everything is within reach: Matala and Falasarna beaches, the Knossos archaeological site, traditional villages in the Lasithi mountains, and local food in Chaniá. Hire a car if you want to explore the island at your own pace.
