Airline and flight type

TAP Portugal is your carrier on this route. The highlight: it's a direct flight from New York to Porto with no stops. You save time and skip the hassle of changing planes. TAP is Portugal's flag carrier and often includes checked baggage on base fares, but always verify the details.

Best time to visit

Porto suits travel year-round, but October, April and May offer great weather with fewer crowds. Prices stay steadier outside summer (July–August) and Christmas. To spend less, sidestep Portuguese holidays and international school breaks.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Porto Airport (OPO), 11 km from the city centre. Take the E metro line (11 minutes) or a direct bus. In the city: wander the Ribeira district, sample Port wine in the cellars of Vila Nova de Gaia, climb the Clérigos Tower and admire the iconic blue tiles. A Douro river cruise is a must.
