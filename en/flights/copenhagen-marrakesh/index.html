Fly from Copenhagen to Marrakech from €131 return. Most routes fall between €131 and €228. With Ryanair and one stopover, you have affordable options to reach the Red City.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we found is €131 return, with the median at €211. Fares range from €131 to €228 depending on your travel dates. The key is booking as far in advance as possible: the earlier you purchase, the better the deal typically is. Check regularly if you're flying in November.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with one stopover included. It's not a direct flight, so you'll have an intermediate stop before reaching Marrakech. Always check the connection time when booking; a layover of 1 to 2 hours is standard on budget routes like this.

Best time to travel

Marrakech is attractive year-round, but October to April offers the most comfortable climate: mild temperatures without summer's extreme heat. November is particularly appealing for its pleasant weather and usually offers good availability of budget flights.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Marrakech Menara, the city's main airport. Quick connections to the city centre are available. Don't miss the Medina with its labyrinthine souks, Jemaa el-Fna Square, the Bahia Palace and Majorelle Gardens. Nearby: the Atlas Mountains and Sahara for multi-day excursions.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Copenhagen to Marrakech?

No, this Ryanair route includes a stopover. There is no direct option in the prices we found.

Why does the price vary so much, from €131 to €228?

Your travel dates, how far in advance you book and flight occupancy make the difference. Peak season trips or last-minute bookings cost more. Book 2–3 months ahead if you can.

Which month is best for finding cheap flights to Marrakech?

We cannot tell you that any specific month is always cheaper. What works is booking with maximum advance notice, regardless of season.

How long does the flight from Copenhagen to Marrakech with a stopover take?

Total time depends on your layover duration. The pure flight time is short, but with the connection you can expect around 6 to 8 hours of travel approximately.
