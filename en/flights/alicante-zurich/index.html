Best time to visit

Zurich works year-round. Winter months (December–February) bring festive markets or snowy landscapes, but hotel booking gets tight. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) are sweet spots: mild weather, fewer crowds and better accommodation deals. Summer is stunning but expensive for hotels and attractions.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Zurich Airport (ZRH), 12 km from the centre. The city is compact yet packed: Altstadt with luxury shops, world-class museums, lakeside walks. From Zurich it's quick train access to the Alps, Lucerne and Interlaken. Eat chocolate, try fondue and stroll the Limmat riverbanks. Budget carefully: Switzerland is pricey.
