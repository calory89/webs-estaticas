Best time to visit

Buenos Aires has a subtropical climate. The best seasons are autumn (March-May) and winter (June-August), with pleasant temperatures and less rain. Avoid January-February due to humid heat. Travelling in low season helps you find better prices and fewer crowds at attractions and museums.

What to do when you arrive

From Ministro Pistarini Airport (Ezeiza), take a remise or colectivo (shared taxi) to the city centre. Buenos Aires offers tango, museums, charming neighbourhoods (La Boca, San Telmo, Palermo) and world-class dining. The local currency is the Argentine peso; check the current exchange rate before travelling.
