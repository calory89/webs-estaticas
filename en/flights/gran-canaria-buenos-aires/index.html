Price and when to book

Prices on this route range from €1,112 to €1,488 depending on dates. The median sits at €1,154, so if you spot fares near that level, it's a reasonable time to buy. Airline and stopovers

Iberia Express operates this route with 3 stopovers. It's a long journey with multiple plane changes before you land in Buenos Aires, so be prepared for a marathon trip. The upside is the fares stay competitive for a route of this distance.

Best time to travel

Buenos Aires in December and January is summer: warm weather, long days, and the city buzzing. Perfect if you're chasing sunshine and escaping European winter. February starts to cool slightly. Spring (September-November) and autumn (March-May) are mild and pleasant. Winter (June-August) is cool but not harsh.

What to do when you arrive

Buenos Aires is vibrant: La Boca's famous Caminito, San Telmo for tango and weekend markets, and Palermo for world-class food. The Teatro Colón is unmissable. Try empanadas, asado, and a proper Malbec. Give yourself time, this capital demands several days to really explore it.
