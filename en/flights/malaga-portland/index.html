Best time to travel

Portland is a year-round destination. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild weather and beautiful scenery. Winter is rainy but appealing if you're interested in architecture and local food without crowds. Plan ahead to secure the best fares.

Things to do when you arrive

Portland is known for its cultural scene, independent shops and quality food. Visit Downtown, walk around Pioneer Courthouse Square, explore Southeast and Northeast neighbourhoods. The city is very walkable and public transport is good. Nearby you'll find hiking in the Cascades and Mount Hood, perfect for a day trip.
