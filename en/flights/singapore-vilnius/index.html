Flights from Singapore to Vilna cost from €694 return with Etihad Airways. The median fare sits around €794, so you have tight options if budget is key. This route involves 2 stops on the outbound leg, but the price makes it worthwhile if you're flexible with dates.

Price and when to book

The base price is €694, though most searches land between €794–797. Book 2–3 months ahead for the best chance at lowest fares. Check regularly: Etihad adjusts prices based on demand. Avoid peak travel dates if your budget is strict.

Airline and stops

You'll fly Etihad Airways, which balances price and service well. The outbound leg has 2 stops, adding travel time but cutting costs compared to direct options. The return flight in the example (19 Nov, 06:20 departure) is early morning, so check schedules when choosing dates. Always verify your full itinerary before booking.

Best time to visit

Vilna shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather and less rain. Winter (December–February) is cold but magical if you want snow and fewer crowds. Summer brings more tourists and potentially higher prices. Check fares regularly to find your ideal date and price combo.

What to do when you arrive

Vilna will charm you: the medieval Old Town is Unesco-listed, with stunning squares, baroque churches and street art. Don't miss Gediminas Tower, the castle and the historic Jewish quarter. The airport (VNO) is 4 km from the centre; taxis and buses connect you quickly. Vilna is budget-friendly versus other European capitals, perfect for stretching your money.
