Best time to visit

Phuket is a year-round beach destination. November and December bring sunshine and pleasant temperatures—perfect for travellers. January is peak season with higher prices. August to October is monsoon season with frequent rain, but fares drop if you're flexible. Travel mid-week to avoid crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Phuket offers white-sand beaches, Buddhist temples, and nightlife in Patong. Rent a scooter to explore Kamala, Karon, or Big Buddha. Catch a ferry to the Phi Phi or Similan islands for diving and snorkelling. The airport is 30 km from town; bus, taxi, or private transfer get you to your hotel in 45–60 minutes.

Which airline operates this route?
