Best time to visit

Belgrade appeals year-round. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Winters are cold but the city has festive charm. Avoid summer peaks if you want peace and lower fares.

What to do when you arrive

Belgrade blends history and modernity. Tour Kalemegdan Fortress, stroll Zemun's bohemian quarter, and eat local food along the Danube. Museums, nightlife and Austro-Hungarian architecture are major draws. Nikola Tesla Airport is about 20 km from the centre; buses, taxis and private transfers are available.
