Best time to visit Kyoto

Kyoto is beautiful year-round, but spring (March-May) and autumn (September-November) are ideal: mild weather and spectacular landscapes. Spring brings cherry blossoms; autumn turns trees red and orange across the mountains. Skip summer if you dislike humidity and heat. What to do when you arrive in Kyoto

You'll land at Osaka Airport (Kansai, code OSA), about 75 km from Kyoto. It's easy to reach via direct train (roughly 80 minutes) or bus. Once in Kyoto, explore ancient temples (Fushimi Inari, Kiyomizu-dera), wander the geisha district of Gion, stroll through the Arashiyama bamboo groves, and savour local cuisine. Kyoto is Japan's cultural heart.
