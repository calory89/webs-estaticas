Airline and stopovers

Lufthansa is the option on this search and takes you direct from Munich to Belgrade with no stopovers. That means a flight of just over two hours with no changes or connection queues. It departs at 11:20 and you arrive relaxed to make the most of the afternoon.

Best time to visit

November is a good bet: late autumn in Belgrade with mild temperatures (10-15°C), fewer tourists than summer, and the city with a more authentic feel. If you prefer warmer weather, spring (April-May) and early autumn (September) are equally ideal. Prices tend to stay similar outside the peak summer season.

Things to do when you arrive

Belgrade is a city of contrasts: Kalemegdan Fortress dominates the Danube, the Dorćol district is bohemian and packed with cafés, and Knez Mihailova is the main street for strolling and shopping. Nightlife is famous (floating bars on the river), Balkan cuisine is excellent and affordable, and everything is within walking distance. Three or four days give you time to breathe in the city at a leisurely pace.
