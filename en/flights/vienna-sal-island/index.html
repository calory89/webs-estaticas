Best time to travel

Sal Island enjoys stable weather almost year-round, but December to March offer more comfortable temperatures and less rainfall. January and February are most popular, so prices tend to rise. Consider travelling in November, December, or March-April for better value and comfort.

What to do on arrival

Sal Island is famous for its stunning beaches and sun-and-sea tourism. Santa Maria is the most touristy village with shops and restaurants. For more activity, try windsurfing at Ponta Preta or visit the Sal Nature Reserve. The international airport is well connected to main attractions, so hiring a car or using local taxis is straightforward.
