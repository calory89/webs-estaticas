The lowest price recorded is €868 for return flights. Prices range from €868 to €1,072 in the sample checked on 22 October 2026. Book well in advance for competitive rates: long-haul flights lock in better prices when purchased 2-3 months ahead.

Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Air Europa, offering 2 stopovers on the outbound journey. The sample departs Palma on 8 December at 10:35 and arrives in Mexico City the same day. The return leaves on 10 December at 01:15 from MEX. With two stops, total journey time increases, but the price is significantly lower than direct options.

Best time to visit

December is peak season in Mexico City due to festivities, so expect more tourists and higher prices. For savings, consider trips from May to September, outside Spanish school holidays. The weather is warm year-round in the capital, though June to September brings afternoon rainfall.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Mexico City International Airport (MEX), 5 km from the centre. Don't miss the Zócalo, Metropolitan Cathedral, Teotihuacán, the Anthropology Museum and Xochimilco's trajineras. Eat tacos al pastor, tamales and pan de muerto. Public transport is cheap and efficient. One week covers the main attractions.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Palma to Mexico City?

Not in this sample. Air Europa operates with 2 stopovers. Connections lengthen your journey but cut costs significantly versus direct alternatives.

What's the average price for these flights?

The median is €1,058. You'll find deals from €868 if you book ahead, but typical fares range from €1,000 to €1,100 for the full return trip.

Which airport do I arrive at in Mexico City?

You'll land at Mexico City International Airport (MEX), the capital's main terminal. It's 5 km from the centre, with metro, bus and taxi options available.

How many days do I need to visit Mexico City?

5-7 days is ideal to see the historic centre, main museums and nearby excursions like Teotihuacán. With less time, focus on the Zócalo, Cathedral and Anthropology Museum.
