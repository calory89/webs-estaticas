Best time to visit

Warsaw shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather, less rain, lively streets without tourist hordes. Winter is cold but stunning if you fancy snow and festive decor. Summer is pricier and crowded. Any dates outside Polish bank holidays and long weekends usually offer better fares.

What to do in Warsaw

The Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage site: walk the squares, visit the Royal Castle and Cathedral. Warsaw's uprising left memorials and history trails worth exploring. Quality museums like POLIN (Jewish history) and modern art galleries. Try piernik (spice cake), barszcz (beetroot soup), and local beer at any bar. Public transport is fast and cheap.

Which airline? Is it safe?

Ryanair, Europe's largest low-cost carrier. Perfectly safe, though expect their usual policies: paid luggage, optional fast boarding, limited flight changes. Check their website for hidden fees.

What's the best month to visit Warsaw?

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Winter is cold but magical with snow. Summer is pricey and busy. Steer clear of Polish national holidays and long weekends for better fares.
