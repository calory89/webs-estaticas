Best time to travel

Bogotá has mild weather year-round due to its altitude (2,640 m). The driest months are December–January and July–August, ideal for exploring the city and surrounding areas. Avoid peak season if you want to keep costs down.

What to do when you arrive in Bogotá

You'll land at Eldorado International Airport (BOG), 13 km from the centre. La Candelaria is the historic heart: visit the Metropolitan Cathedral, Palacio de Nariño and the Gold Museum. Take the cable car to Monserrate for city views, explore the galleries in Usaquén, and sample local cuisine in Zona Rosa's restaurants.

What can I see in Bogotá?

Colombia's capital offers the Gold Museum (the world's largest pre-Hispanic gold collection), La Candelaria with colonial architecture, Monserrate with city views, and creative neighbourhoods like Usaquén full of galleries, shops and nightlife. You can also take day trips to nearby towns like Zipaquirá.

Is there a train from Dublin to Bogota?

No. There is an ocean between Dublin and Bogota (about 8,140 km between the airports), so the practical way to get there is by plane.
