Best time to visit

Phuket has a tropical climate. The dry season (November to February) is best: less rain, pleasant temperatures, calm seas. December and January peak tourism, so busier beaches and higher prices. February offers a good balance. Avoid May to October when rainfall is heavy.

What to do when you arrive

Phuket is the gateway to Thailand's beaches. Patong Beach is the most famous but crowded. Try Kata and Karon for a quieter vibe, or spend a day visiting the Phi Phi Islands by boat. Old Phuket Town has markets and colonial architecture. The airport is 30 km from the centre; use taxi, tuk-tuk or airport shuttle services.
