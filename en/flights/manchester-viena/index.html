Fly Manchester to Vienna with Ryanair from €64 return. The median price sits around €75, so you've got room to find a bargain. Direct flight and quick: the best option to escape to Austria's capital without breaking the bank.

Price and when to book

Prices start at €64 return, with a median of €75. We've seen fares up to €109 depending on the exact date. The key is not to wait: book as soon as possible from today. Ryanair fares on this route tend to climb as departure approaches, so move fast if you spot a price you like.

Airline and stops

Fly with Ryanair direct from Manchester to Vienna. No stops, no connections, no surprises. Departure at 18:00 from Manchester (example: 14 November), arrival in Vienna mid-afternoon. Return is also direct: departure at 16:45 (example: 19 November). Ryanair is a budget carrier, so check what baggage your fare includes before you pay.

Best time to travel

Vienna is spectacular year-round, but if you're chasing cheap fares, visit in low-demand months: November, January, February. Winter brings lower prices and fewer crowds, though it's cold. For better weather, April–May and September–October are shoulder season: pricier than winter, but without summer's heat and tourists.

What to do when you arrive

Vienna awaits with imperial palaces (Schönbrunn and Hofburg), world-class museums and historic cafés where you can linger for hours. The city centre is 20 minutes from the airport by train. Walk along the Danube, try Sachertorte at Café Central and climb Stephansdom cathedral. If you have spare time, Bratislava (Slovakia's capital) is just 45 minutes by train.
