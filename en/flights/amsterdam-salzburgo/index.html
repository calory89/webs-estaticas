Best time to visit

Salzburg shines in winter with snowed-in old town and Christmas markets through January. Spring is perfect for sightseeing without snow and alpine walks nearby. Autumn brings golden light. Summer draws crowds and higher fares. Check each specific date to see what the flight costs that day.

What to do on arrival

Salzburg is Mozart's city: the Cathedral, the Residenz, and the medieval centre along the Salzach River. Climb Hohensalzburg fortress for panoramic views. Time permitting, the Austrian Alps (Großglockner) and Lake Königssee (Bavarian Germany) are next door. W.A. Mozart airport is 4 km from the city; bus or taxi takes 15–20 minutes.
