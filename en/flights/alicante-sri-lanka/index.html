Best time to travel

Sri Lanka shines December to March: dry weather, perfect beaches and pleasant temperatures. It's peak season, but it's the ideal window. Avoid May to September (monsoon). The search date shown here is mid-November, with an example flight in December: a good window. Check regularly to see price shifts by month.

What to do when you arrive in Colombo

Bandaranaike Airport (CMB) is 30 km from Colombo. From there, easy access to Buddhist temples, Mirissa or Unawatuna beaches, Nuwara Eliya highlands and national parks. It's a complete destination: culture, nature and beach. Hire a car or use taxis: the country is small and manageable.
