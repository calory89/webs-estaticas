Best time to visit

The Bahamas have tropical weather year-round. The dry season (November to April) is best: less rain and pleasant temperatures. Avoid June to October due to hurricane risk. Travelling in low season (May, September, October) may offer better accommodation prices, though flight fares don't always drop.

What to do when you arrive in Nassau

Nassau is the Bahamas' capital. Explore the historic centre, relax at Junkanoo Beach or Cable Beach. Take trips to the Exuma Islands, snorkel at coral reefs, or visit Atlantis Resort. Lynden Pindling airport (NAS) is 25 km from the city centre: use taxi, bus, or car hire.
