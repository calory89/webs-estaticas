Price and when to book

The lowest recorded price is €185 for return flights. Data shows a median of €207, with variations up to €213. Airline and connections

Ryanair operates this route with one stop on the outbound flight. A technical break is standard on services from Rome to Austria. Although it's not a direct flight, the low fares offset the extra travel time. Check your booking for the stopover city and waiting times before confirming.

Best time to visit

Salzburg shines in winter (Christmas markets) and summer. Bear in mind that January–March sees ski season and winter tourism, which can push prices up. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are shoulder seasons with fewer crowds. Prices tend to spike during high-demand periods, so search ahead.

What to do on arrival

Salzburg is Mozart's birthplace and gateway to the Alps. Don't miss the Altstadt with its Cathedral, Mirabell Palace and Hohensalzburg fortress with stunning views. The region is ideal for hiking, lake visits and alpine villages. Rome to Salzburg is a complete cultural leap: from Italian classical history to Austrian baroque.
