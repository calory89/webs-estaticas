Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €176 for a return trip. Most fares hover around €178, with some routes up to €224. Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with 2 stopovers on the outbound journey. There are no direct flights Zaragoza-Salzburg at these prices. Stopovers add time to your trip but make the price affordable. Always check your booking: hand baggage, changes and extra services may carry additional charges with this airline.

Best time to visit

Salzburg is beautiful year-round, though winter brings snow and cold (perfect for skating and Christmas if you travel then). Spring and autumn offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Summer sees peak tourism and higher prices. Book ahead whatever season you choose.

What to do when you arrive

Salzburg is compact and walkable. The old town (Altstadt) is Unesco heritage, with the Cathedral and Getreidegasse (the iconic shopping street). Hohensalzburg Fortress overlooks the city from above. If you love The Sound of Music, tours of filming locations are everywhere. Nearby lakes (Fuschlsee, Wolfgangsee) make perfect day trips.
