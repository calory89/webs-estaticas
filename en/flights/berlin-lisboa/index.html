Berlin and Lisbon are just over two hours apart by air, and Ryanair keeps fares competitive on this route. From €74 for the full trip, with a median of €79, it's an affordable getaway to Portugal's capital.

Price and when to book

Fares range from €74 to €184 return. The median sits at €79, so booking with some lead time (2–4 weeks) gives you solid options at sensible prices. The further ahead you plan, the better your budget control. October and November typically offer good rates before prices climb toward Christmas.

Airline and what to expect

Ryanair is the carrier on this route with direct flights. No stops: you leave Berlin and land in Lisbon without a connection. Outbound flights depart around midday, and returns leave early morning, so you have full days in Portugal's capital without wasting time in transit.

Best time to go

April, May, September and October are ideal: mild weather, fewer crowds than summer, and more moderate fares. July and August see higher prices. Winter (December to February) is cheaper but rainier; still, Lisbon keeps buzzing—museums, cafés and restaurants run at full tilt.

What to do in Lisbon

Alfama's tiled lanes and narrow streets, the Tower of Belém, the Jerónimos Monastery, and the city's many viewpoints (miradouros) are unmissable. Eat pastéis de nata at any pastelaria, try grilled octopus, and ride tram 28 through the city. Basílica da Estrela and the Botanical Garden are peaceful escapes.

FAQ

What's the lowest price you found?

€74 return with Ryanair. This is our lowest sample; on the right booking and travel dates, you can hit it.

Is this a direct flight Berlin to Lisbon?

Yes. Ryanair flies non-stop, roughly 2 hours direct between the two cities.

How many days should I spend in Lisbon?

Three or four days are ideal to see the highlights without rushing: Alfama, Belém, museums and local food. If you have longer, take a train to Sintra or Cascais.

Which Lisbon airport will I use?

Humberto Delgado Lisbon (LIS), the only one serving the metropolitan area. It's about 7 km from the centre; bus, train or taxi get you downtown quickly.
