Best time to visit

Edinburgh is lovely any time of year, but winter can be harsh with frequent rain. Spring and autumn offer milder weather and fewer crowds. Avoid peak festival season (Edinburgh Festival in August) if you're hunting for cheaper accommodation deals, though the city has a special buzz during these periods.

What to do when you arrive

Edinburgh Airport (EDI) is 15 km from the city centre and well connected by public transport. Spend a day exploring Edinburgh Castle and the Royal Mile, stroll through Princes Street Gardens, and climb Arthur's Seat for panoramic views. With more time, trips to Stirling or the Highlands are unmissable.
