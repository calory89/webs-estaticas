Quick answer: The cheapest return flight from Gran Canaria to Amsterdam we found costs 171 € (search on 25 Nov 2026, Kiwi.com). It is a nonstop flight with Transavia.

Transavia connects Gran Canaria to Amsterdam with a direct flight from €171 return. Average prices sit around €175, so you have good options for budget travel. Book in advance to secure the best fares.

Price and when to book

Prices range from €171 to €201 for a return ticket. The earlier you book, the better: booking in advance is the safest strategy. Avoid last-minute reservations, as fares spike closer to departure.

Airline and direct flights

Transavia operates this route as a direct flight with no stops. Departure from Gran Canaria (LPA) at 18:55 and arrival in Amsterdam (AMS) at 06:20 the next day (checked 25 November 2026). No aircraft changes means more comfort and saved time.

Best time to visit

Amsterdam is lovely year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather without peak summer crowds. January and February are cheaper if you don't mind Dutch cold. Book ahead regardless of season.

What to do when you arrive

Amsterdam needs no introduction: canals, museums (Van Gogh, Anne Frank), bicycles everywhere, typical bars. Schiphol Airport is 10 km from the city centre with good train links. You'll arrive early morning, perfect to make the most of your day.
