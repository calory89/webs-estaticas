Air Europa connects Alicante with Fez from €131 minimum return. The median price hovers around €140, making this route very affordable. Flying to Morocco from the Alicante coast is cheaper than you'd expect.

Price and when to book

Alicante-Fez flights range from €131 to €146 return depending on dates. Book as far in advance as possible: supply tends to shrink as departure approaches. October is not peak season, so decent options are usually available. Check regularly to catch fares below €140.

Airline and stopovers

Air Europa operates this route with one stopover (not direct). The outbound departs Alicante at 12:35 and arrives in Fez the next morning after a stop. The return departs Fez at 6:10. One stopover is standard on this connection and layover time is usually reasonable.

Best time to visit

Fez is best visited in autumn and winter: mild temperatures (15–20°C) and far less heat than summer. October and November offer ideal conditions. Avoid July and August when temperatures soar. Flight availability depends more on demand than season, so search with flexibility.

What to do when you arrive

Fez is one of Morocco's most captivating cities. The medina of Fez el-Bali (old town) is a labyrinth of winding streets, riads and souks. Visit the Chouara Tannery, Bou Inania Madrasa and the Royal Palace. Just 50 km away is the blue city of Chefchaouen, perfect for a day trip. The airport is about 15 km from the centre.

FAQ

From €131 return with Air Europa. The median is around €140. Prices vary depending on travel dates and how far ahead you book.

No, Air Europa operates this route with one stopover. The complete journey takes longer than a direct flight, but it's the available option and prices compensate.

Which airline flies Alicante to Fez?

Air Europa is the main carrier on this route. Check their website or flight search engines to confirm availability for your dates.

When is the best time to visit Fez?

October to April: pleasant temperatures (15–20°C) and dry weather. July and August are very hot. For better prices, compare multiple dates: fares vary independently of season.
