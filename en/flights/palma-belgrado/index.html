Best time to travel

Belgrade is worth visiting in autumn or spring to avoid extreme summer heat and crowds. December has its Christmas charm but days are short. If you want milder weather and possibly lower fares, consider March to May or September to November. Book early if you're travelling during peak dates.

What to do when you arrive in Belgrade

Belgrade charms with a blend of history and modernity. Nikola Tesla International Airport is 12 km from the city centre. Don't miss Zemun's old quarter with Danube views, Kalemegdan Fortress, the Temple of Saint Sava and the riverside bar scene. Local food is tasty and affordable. Three or four days give you time for the essentials.
