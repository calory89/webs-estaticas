Best time to visit Luxembourg

Luxembourg is worth visiting year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Winters are cold but festive, and summer draws more tourism. Check prices regularly: fares fluctuate based on demand and local holidays.

What to do when you arrive in Luxembourg

You'll land at Luxembourg Airport (LUX), 6 km from the city centre. The Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage site, with narrow streets and historic fortresses. Explore the medieval city, visit Notre-Dame Cathedral, stroll through Petrusse Gardens or take day trips to the Ardennes. Luxembourg cuisine and proximity to Belgium and France make it an ideal base for regional exploration.
