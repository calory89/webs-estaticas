Best time to visit

Budapest shines in spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is cold but ideal if you want thermal baths without the rush. Skip August if you're after peace and quiet.

What to do when you arrive

Budapest is pure heritage: Széchenyi thermal baths (historic hot springs), Fishermen's Bastion, and Parliament are must-sees. Cross the Chain Bridge, wander Buda's old town, and grab langos (fried bread). Don't miss a night cruise on the Danube.
