Price and when to book

The cheapest flight we've found is €707 per person (return), though most hover around €786. We have examples ranging from €707 to €793. To get these fares, book 2 months in advance; after that, prices tend to climb. The earlier you purchase, the better the deal.

Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Iberia Airlines on the entire route. The flight has 2 stopovers on the outbound leg, so the journey is longer, but the savings are significant. In the example we show, departures around 9 December with return on 14 December, you have flexibility depending on your dates.

Best time to visit

Fort Lauderdale is prime in winter (December to March): perfect weather, no hurricanes, and beautiful beaches. If you travel then, you get better value for money with more activities on offer. Avoid June to October due to hurricane season. Outside winter, prices drop, but the climate is hotter and more humid.

What to do on arrival

Fort Lauderdale is the gateway to Florida's coast. You have nearby beaches (Deerfield Beach, Pompano Beach), America's busiest cruise port, and easy access to Miami (30 km away). The international airport (FLL) is well connected to the city centre. Book tours, reserve water activities and accommodation in advance to secure availability at a good price.
