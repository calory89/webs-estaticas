Best time to visit

Tenerife enjoys subtropical weather year-round, so it's a destination for any season. May-June and September-October tend to be quieter if you prefer fewer crowds. Winter (November to March) draws more visitors but offers pleasant temperatures. Check your availability and budget; low season doesn't always mean low prices.

What to do when you arrive in Tenerife

Mount Teide is essential: hiking, cable car, or simply admiring Spain's highest volcano. Northern beaches (Puerto de la Cruz) and southern ones (Playa de las Américas) are popular. La Laguna, the historic capital, deserves a visit. Los Gigantes cliffs and traditional villages like Icod de los Vinos round out your options. Rent a car for freedom to explore the island.
