Best time to visit

Bruges is beautiful year-round, but March to May and September to October offer mild weather without peak summer crowds. Prices tend to be more reasonable outside July and August. Plan your trip with plenty of notice to secure availability at good prices.

What to do when you arrive

Bruges is a medieval city with canals and picturesque alleyways. Visit the Market Square (Markt), climb the Belfort tower, explore the Basilica of the Holy Blood, and take a boat tour along the canals. Try Belgian waffles and chocolate; if it's your first time, spend at least two days exploring at a relaxed pace. Brussels-Charleroi airport is about 60 km away; reach the city by bus or train.
