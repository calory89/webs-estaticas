Best time to visit

Bruges is beautiful year-round, but autumn (September–November) and spring (April–May) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is magical if you fancy Christmas markets, though it'll be cold. Skip July and August if you hate tourist masses. Prices rise in peak season, so off-peak months often deliver better fares.

What to do when you arrive

Brussels-Zaventem Airport is 30 km from Bruges; you'll reach the city by train or bus in 40–50 minutes. Once there, walk through Market Square, cross St John's Bridge, visit the Basilica of the Holy Blood and take a canal boat tour. Eat mussels, try Trappist beers and buy authentic Belgian chocolate. Bruges walks itself in 2–3 days without rushing.
