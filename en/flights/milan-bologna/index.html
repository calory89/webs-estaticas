Flights from Milan to Bologna start from €106 return. The median price sits around €109, making this route very affordable for travelling between two major northern Italian cities. Book in advance to secure the best fares.

Price and when to book

The lowest price found is €106 return. Sample prices range from €106 to €138. For this short route, we recommend booking at least 6-8 weeks ahead. Check regularly to spot price drops, as Ryanair adjusts fares based on availability. Avoid last-minute bookings on bank holidays and peak periods.

Airline and connections

Ryanair operates this route with 1 connection. Though Milan and Bologna are only around 200 km apart, the flight includes one stopover. Total journey times vary depending on the connecting airport. Check your booking confirmation for stopover details and layover times.

Best time to visit

Bologna is pleasant year-round, but spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer ideal weather and fewer crowds than summer. Avoid booking for July-August if you want a quieter experience and lower prices. Winter months can be more economical, though temperatures are cool.

What to do on arrival

Bologna deserves a stop: the historic centre with Piazza Maggiore, the Asinelli and Garisenda Towers, the Basilica of San Petronio and medieval arcades are must-sees. With limited time, spend at least half a day exploring the old town. If connecting from Milan, you might fit in a quick visit depending on your layover.
