Best time to visit

Munich shines year-round. Spring (April–May) brings mild weather and the city in full swing. Summer (June–August) is perfect for sunshine but it's peak season and pricey. Autumn (September–October) is ideal: pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter brings Christmas markets (November–December) worth experiencing, though it's cold. Skip July–August if you're watching your budget.

What to do in Munich

Marienplatz and the City Hall are essential. Visit Neuschwanstein castle if you have time (incredible and close by). Explore the Old Town, Nymphenburg Palace and its gardens. Beer lovers: Hofbräuhaus is a must. The German Museum and Pinacothek are top choices for rainy days. Outdoor beer gardens are the authentic Bavarian experience, especially in autumn. Don't miss Marienplatz at sunset.

What to see in Munich in 3–4 days?

Start at Marienplatz, then walk the Old Town and visit Nymphenburg Palace. Spend time in museums (German Museum, Pinacothek). If possible, take a day trip to Neuschwanstein castle nearby. Try Bavarian beer at traditional beer gardens and restaurants.
