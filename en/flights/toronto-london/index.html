Best time to travel

Toronto and London are pleasant destinations most of the year. October-April in Toronto is intense winter, while London has mild but grey winters. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer better weather in both cities and more interesting landscapes. Check in advance to spot the best-priced dates that suit your availability.

What to do when you arrive

London is a classic: Big Ben, Tower Bridge, free museums (British Museum, National Gallery), West End theatres, markets like Camden. If you have time, explore neighbourhoods like Shoreditch or Notting Hill. Toronto deserves a stop: Niagara Falls is less than 2 hours away, Lake Ontario has waterfront walks, and downtown offers galleries, restaurants and vibrant culture. Both cities blend history and modern life.
