Airline and direct flights

SAS (Scandinavian Airlines) operates this route nonstop. One flight: depart Oslo at 08:45, arrive in Bruges at 07:05 after roughly 2 hours airborne. No stops, no changes. SAS is the main Nordic carrier serving Belgium from Oslo.

Best time to visit

Bruges is lovely year-round, but October and November offer mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Autumn is ideal if you want to stroll the canals without July and August's tourist masses. Winter brings cold but a festive atmosphere.

What to do when you arrive

Brussels Airport (BRU) is about 90 km from Bruges; direct train from the terminal. In Bruges, walk the medieval centre, take a boat tour of the canals, visit the Basilica of the Holy Blood, and sample Belgian beer and chocolate. The city is small and walkable in 2–3 days.

The medieval Market Square, navigable canals, the Basilica of the Holy Blood, and the Belfry tower. Eat at a local brewery, try artisanal chocolate, and spend 2–3 days exploring the city at a relaxed pace. It's compact and pedestrian-friendly.
