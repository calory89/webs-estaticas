Best time to visit Warsaw

Warsaw is pleasant year-round. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter months (December-February) are colder but ideal if you want festive atmospheres or slightly lower prices. Avoid July-August if you dislike heat and peak tourism.

What to do when you arrive in Warsaw

Poland's capital blends history, culture and modern life. Explore the Old Town, a Unesco site, walk along the Vistula River, visit the Warsaw Uprising Museum and royal palaces. The city has good public transport and is very walkable. Try pierniki (pastries) and traditional Polish cuisine in local restaurants.
