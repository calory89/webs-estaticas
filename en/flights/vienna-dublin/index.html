Fly direct from Vienna to Dublin with Ryanair from €99 return. Median prices sit at €102, giving you real opportunities to grab a cheap ticket to Ireland without stopovers.

Price and when to book

Prices range from €99 to €105 return. To secure the €99 fare, book as far in advance as possible. Ryanair raises prices closer to departure, especially on popular routes like Vienna-Dublin. If your dates are flexible, compare several days: small variations in departure or return can save €3 or €5.

Airline and direct flight

Ryanair operates this route with no stopovers. Your flight departs Vienna and arrives direct in Dublin. You'll save time with no connections and won't need to worry about baggage transfers. It's the fastest way to reach Ireland's capital.

Best time to visit

Dublin is worth visiting year-round, but summer (June to August) and peak holidays attract more tourists and push prices up. For cheaper flights, consider autumn (September to November) or spring (March to May). Winter (December to February) can also offer bargains, though the weather is colder and days are shorter.

What to do in Dublin

Dublin deserves 3 to 4 days minimum. Don't miss Trinity College, Guinness Storehouse, Temple Bar, and the national museums. Dún Laoghaire port, 15 km south, is ideal for seaside walks. The city is compact and easy to explore on foot or by bus. Use public transport or book local tours: both are cheap and reliable.

FAQ

What is the minimum price Vienna-Dublin?

The lowest recorded price is €99 return with Ryanair. Median is €102, so finding a ticket for slightly more is realistic. Book early to access the lowest fares.

Is Vienna-Dublin a direct flight?

Yes, Ryanair flies direct with no stopovers on this route. Single flight from Vienna to Dublin.

Which airline flies Vienna to Dublin?

Ryanair operates this route. As a budget airline, note that hand luggage is limited and checked baggage is charged separately if needed.

How many days should I spend in Dublin?

3 to 4 days are enough to see the main highlights: historic centre, museums, pubs and nightlife. If you want to explore nearby areas (Howth, Malahide, Dún Laoghaire), add 1 or 2 extra days.
