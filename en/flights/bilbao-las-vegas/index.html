Best time to visit

Las Vegas is accessible year-round. January and February offer mild temperatures (highs of 15–20°C) and fewer crowds than summer. August is scorching (40°C+) and pricey. October and November are ideal: pleasant weather without extreme heat. Avoid New Year's Eve and US public holidays if you're after lower fares.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Harry Reid International Airport (LAS), 10 km from the city centre. Las Vegas offers far more than casinos: explore the Strip, visit Fremont Street Experience (old Vegas), or take day trips to the Grand Canyon, Valley of Fire, and Red Rock Canyon, all within a few hours' drive. Museums, shows and fine dining complete the experience.
