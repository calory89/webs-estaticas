Best time to visit

Salzburg is rewarding year-round. Winter is magical for snow and Christmas markets (December and January); spring (April-May) brings pleasant weather and fewer crowds; summer (June-August) is ideal for nearby lakes but expect busy tourist season. Autumn (September-October) combines good weather with moderate prices.

What to do when you arrive

Salzburg Airport (W. A. Mozart) is 4km from the city centre. The city is Mozart's birthplace: visit his birth house, Mirabell Palace and the Cathedral. Salzkammergut lakes lie 30km away. If you love 'The Sound of Music', follow the film locations tour. Walk along the Salzach riverside and explore the galleries of Getreidegasse.
