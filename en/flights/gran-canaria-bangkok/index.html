Fly from Gran Canaria to Bangkok from €689 return with Ryanair. The typical price range sits around €733. This is a one-stop route, perfect for budget travellers heading to Thailand.

Price and when to book

The lowest price on record is €689 return, with a median of €733. Fares can reach €950 during peak times. Book as far in advance as possible: Ryanair fares tend to climb as departure approaches. March to May and October to November are seasons with better price-to-availability ratios for Thailand travel.

Airline and stops

Ryanair is your carrier on this route, with 1 stop along the way. Flights are not direct, so you'll have an intermediate connection. Check your connection time when booking and remember that baggage can incur extra charges with Ryanair; always review your fare rules before purchase.

Best time to visit

Thailand is at its best from November to February, with dry weather and comfortable temperatures. May to October is the rainy season. Travelling in low season (May to September or March to April) often means lower fares, though the climate will be more humid. December and January are the most expensive and crowded months.

What to do when you arrive in Bangkok

Bangkok is lively: visit the Grand Palace and Emerald Buddha Temple, cruise the canals, eat at floating markets and enjoy street food. Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK) is about 25 km from the centre; use the Airport Rail Link train, bus or taxi to reach your hotel. From Bangkok, it's easy to reach Chiang Mai, Krabi or the islands.
