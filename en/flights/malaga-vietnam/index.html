Best time to visit

Vietnam is best visited between October and April, when the climate is drier and temperatures are pleasant. Avoid July to September due to monsoons. December is ideal: great weather and holidays, though prices tend to rise. Travel May to September for fewer tourists and potentially better fares.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

You'll land in Hanoi, the capital, full of temples, markets, and vibrant street life. From here, explore Ha Long Bay (UNESCO World Heritage), rice terraces in Sapa to the north, or travel south to Ho Chi Minh City and the Mekong Delta. Noi Bai Airport is 25 km from the centre; take a bus, taxi, or arrange transport in advance.
