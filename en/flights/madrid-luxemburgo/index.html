Best time to visit

Luxembourg is a year-round destination. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winters are cold but atmospheric if you're after Christmas or New Year. Avoid July–August if you prefer fewer tourists.

What to do when you arrive

Luxembourg is small but packed. Explore the UNESCO-listed Old Town with its fortresses and Pétrusse River views. Visit the Grand Ducal Palace, capital museums and, if you have time, nearby towns like Vianden or Echternach. The airport is 6 km from the city centre.
