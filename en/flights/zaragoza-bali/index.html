Best time to visit Bali

Bali's dry season runs April to October, with less rain and pleasant temperatures. Avoid November to March if you prefer clear skies, though rain falls as short bursts. Peak tourism is June-August: on a tight budget, shoulder seasons (April-May or September-October) may offer better price and comfort balance. Check local road conditions and festivals matching your travel dates.

What to do on arrival in Bali (DPS)

You'll land at Ngurah Rai international airport (DPS) in the south. From there: luxury beaches like Seminyak and Kuta, temples such as Tanah Lot and Besakih, rice fields in Ubud, and diving in the north. Local transport is cheap: taxis, scooter rentals or organised tours. On a budget, Bali offers affordable accommodation, food and experiences.
