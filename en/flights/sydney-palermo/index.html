Best time to travel

Sicily is a year-round destination, but October to April offers cooler weather and fewer crowds. May to September is hot. Since flying from Sydney is a long journey, plan ahead and choose dates that let you enjoy the destination without rushing.

What to do when you arrive in Palermo

Palermo is Sicily's capital: labyrinthine streets, street food markets (Ballarò, Vucciria), the Metropolitan Cathedral, and nearby beaches. Don't miss Palazzo dei Normanni or a trip to Mondello. Hire a car or use local transport to explore the whole island.
