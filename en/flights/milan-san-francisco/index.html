The lowest price we've seen is €551, though typical fares sit around €710. Prices range from €551 to €750 depending on your dates. Airline and stopovers

Air France operates this route with one stopover. The flight departs Milan at midday and arrives in San Francisco late afternoon (local time) the next day. One intermediate stop is standard on this connection and gives you a chance to stretch your legs without adding too much to your total journey time.

Best time to visit

San Francisco is a year-round destination, but the weather is most pleasant from May to September. The sample flight is in November, with mild temperatures (13-18°C) and fewer peak tourists. Prices tend to climb in peak summer, so if your schedule is flexible, travelling in autumn or spring works out cheaper.

What to do when you arrive

You land at San Francisco International Airport (SFO), 21 km from the city centre. BART (Bay Area Rapid Transit) connects you straight into town in 30 minutes. Once there, the Golden Gate Bridge, Alcatraz, the Mission District and the historic cable cars are must-sees. The city is compact and very walkable.
