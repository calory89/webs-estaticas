Price and when to book

Tickets start from €174 return, with an average price of €234. The golden rule applies here: book as early as possible for the best fares. Check two to three months before your trip to catch the best deals. October and November are quieter months with lower prices compared to summer.

Airline and stops

ITA Airways operates this route. You'll have 1 stop on the outbound flight, so it's not direct but quite manageable. The return flight varies depending on availability. These European stops are short and straightforward—nothing like long intercontinental layovers.

Best time to visit

Naples is worth visiting year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer perfect weather without peak summer crowds. Summer is pricier and busier. Winter is mild on the coast with lower fares, ideal if you're budget-conscious.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Capodichino airport, 4 km from the city centre. Take a bus, taxi or train into the old town. Don't miss the Cathedral, Castel dell'Ovo, and grab a slice of pizza al taglio on any corner. Pompeii and the Amalfi coast are an hour away by train. The National Archaeological Museum and Capodimonte Museum are must-sees.

FAQ

The lowest recorded price is €174 return. The average is €234. Book early to secure the lowest fares.

Are there direct flights from Munich to Naples?

No, the flight has 1 stop on the outbound journey. This is typical for this route and doesn't add too much travel time.

Which airline flies from Munich to Naples?

ITA Airways operates these cheap flights. It's a reliable Italian carrier.

What should I see in Naples besides the historic centre?

The National Archaeological Museum, Pompeii (Roman ruins), the Amalfi coast with picturesque towns like Positano, and the islands of Capri and Ischia. Everything is accessible from Naples by public transport.
