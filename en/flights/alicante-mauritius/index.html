Best time to visit

Mauritius is a tropical destination worth visiting year-round, but May to October brings the dry season with less rain and pleasant temperatures. Cyclone season runs November to May, so if you're after the best weather, plan your trip during the dry months. Always check the forecast before confirming.

What to do when you arrive

Mauritius boasts white-sand beaches, coral reefs for diving and snorkelling, and stunning views. Port Louis, the capital, has museums and local markets. Pieter Both mountain and Chamarel Waterfall are classic spots for hiking and photography. The international airport is about 65 km from the capital; hire a car or use public transport to get around.
