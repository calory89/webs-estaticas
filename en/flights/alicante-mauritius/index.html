Best time to visit

Mauritius enjoys tropical climate year-round. The dry season runs May to October (southern hemisphere winter), perfect for beaches and water sports without heavy rains. Southern summer months (December to February) are warm and humid, but the island remains appealing. Avoid cyclone season if you prefer maximum safety.

What to do when you arrive

Mauritius offers white-sand beaches, coral reefs and nature reserves. The north has resorts; the east, wild landscapes; the south, dramatic cliffs. Don't miss Port Louis (capital), Chamarel Waterfalls or Deer Island. Hire a car to explore at your own pace or use local taxis and tours.
