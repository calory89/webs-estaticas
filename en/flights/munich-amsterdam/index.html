Airline and stopovers

KLM Royal Dutch Airlines operates this route nonstop, with no stopovers. You'll fly from Munich Airport (MUC) to Amsterdam Schiphol Airport (AMS) in one go. Flight time is typically around 2 hours. KLM is a major European carrier with good connectivity.

Best time to visit

Amsterdam is lovely year-round. Spring (April–May) offers good weather and fewer crowds than summer. October–November is pleasant too. June through August are pricier and more touristy. Avoid Christmas and New Year dates if you're chasing low fares.

What to do when you arrive

Amsterdam is compact and bike-friendly. Visit the museums (Van Gogh, Rijksmuseum), cruise the canals, explore neighbourhoods like Jordaan or De Pijp. Schiphol is 9 km from the city centre with direct train service every 10–15 minutes. Consider the Amsterdam City Card for museums and transport.
