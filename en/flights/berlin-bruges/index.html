Price and when to book

Base price is €167 return. We checked this route on 22 October 2026 and prices range from €167 to €173 depending on dates. It's best to book 2-3 weeks in advance: this gives you leeway to find a good fare. November is a good time to travel at a reasonable price before the winter high season kicks in.

Airline and flight type

Brussels Airlines operates this route with direct flights. No stops means less hassle, less time in the airport and lower risk of delays. The journey is short (around 1h40 of flying) and the Belgian carrier is reliable for this Berlin-Bruges connection.

Best time to visit

November is ideal: mild temperatures (8-12°C), fewer tourists than summer and the city starts preparing Christmas decorations. December prices rise. Avoid July and August if you want low fares. Spring (April-May) is also good though prices are higher.

What to do when you arrive

Bruges is a UNESCO World Heritage site: stroll the medieval centre, visit the Market Square (Grote Markt), climb Bruges Belfry, boat tours along the canals. Eat Belgian waffles, chocolate and try local beers. Brussels airport (Zaventem) is 25 km away; bus or train get you to the city centre in 30 minutes. Bruges is 15 km further north.

FAQ

Can I find cheaper flights from Berlin to Bruges?

Current prices are around €167-173. It's hard to go lower because it's a short route with few airlines. Booking 2-3 weeks ahead is your best strategy. Set up price alerts so you don't miss a deal.

Are Berlin-Bruges flights direct?

Yes, Brussels Airlines flies direct with no stops. It's the quickest and most comfortable option for this route.

Which airline flies Berlin to Bruges?

Brussels Airlines operates this connection. It's the Belgian flag carrier and has a good reputation on short European routes.

How many days should I spend in Bruges?

Bruges looks good in 2-3 days. The historic centre is small and compact. If you want to combine it with Antwerp, Ghent or another Belgian city, 4-5 days is ideal.
