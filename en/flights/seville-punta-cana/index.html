Flights from Seville to Punta Cana start at €680 return. The average price hovers around €784. Air Europa offers this route with one stopover, perfect for enjoying beaches, cenotes and resorts in the Dominican Republic.

Price and when to book

Return fares start from €680, though most sit between €784–€786. Prices vary significantly by travel date. Book early once you've confirmed your preferred dates, the sooner you secure your tickets, the better. October 2026 is a reference point, so check availability for your exact travel dates.

Air Europa and stopovers

Air Europa operates this route with 1 stopover. It's not direct, but the airline has strong transatlantic connectivity as a Spanish carrier. The typical stopover hub varies by booking date and may include European or African connections. Always verify total journey time before you complete your reservation.

Best time to travel

Punta Cana works year-round, but the dry season (November to April) brings clear skies and less rainfall. July and August are busy and pricier with higher humidity. If you're budget-conscious, travel May to October outside Spanish school holidays to secure better fares.

What to do when you arrive

Punta Cana is your gateway to the Caribbean: pristine white-sand beaches, cenotes (natural freshwater caves), national parks and all-inclusive resorts. Punta Cana International Airport (PUJ) sits 30 km from main beaches. From there, you reach Playa Bavaro, La Romana and Higüey. No car needed if you're in a resort, but hiring one is affordable for exploring beyond.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for flights Seville to Punta Cana?

From €680 return with Air Europa. That's the lowest fare recorded; the median sits at €784. Prices shift greatly by date, travel outside peak season to save more.

Are there direct flights from Seville to Punta Cana?

No. Air Europa operates with 1 mandatory stopover. Total journey time usually runs 12–14 flight hours plus connection time, depending on the hub and layover length.

Is Air Europa reliable for this route?

Yes. Air Europa is a Spanish airline with solid experience on transatlantic services. Always confirm your booking on Air Europa's official website and ensure your travel documents are in order before departure.

What can I see and do in Punta Cana?

Dream beaches (Bavaro, Macao), natural cenotes for diving, parks like Manati Park, snorkelling on reefs, and spectacular marine life. Many resorts include activities in their packages. To explore further, hire a car or book tours through your hotel.
