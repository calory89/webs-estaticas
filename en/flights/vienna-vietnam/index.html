China Eastern Airlines operates this route with 1 stopover on the outbound flight. The typical stopover is usually Shanghai (PVG) or Beijing (PEI), though China Eastern may alter connection hubs. With one stop, expect a total travel time over 15 hours. Check your confirmation to see where your stopover is so you can plan connection times.

Best time to visit

Vietnam has a tropical climate. October to April offers pleasant temperatures and less rainfall, especially in the north where Hanoi is located. May to September is monsoon season with high heat and humidity. For the best experience, travel between November and March. Avoid local Chinese and Vietnamese holidays if you want fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

You'll land at Noi Bai International Airport (HAN), about 25 km from central Hanoi. The old town is essential: walk around Hoan Kiem Lake, visit the Temple of Literature and Thang Long Citadel. Don't miss Ha Long Bay (organised tours from Hanoi), Hoi An village and southern beaches. Use buses, taxis or local apps; transport is cheap.
