Price and when to book

The cheapest tickets on this route start from €674 return. The median price sits at €742, though you'll find pricier options around €1,669. Book as early as you can. Long-haul routes like this usually have better prices 2–3 months ahead. Don't wait until the last minute if you want to save.

Airline and stops

Transavia operates this route with 2 stops on the outbound flight. It's not a direct flight, so add between 8 and 15 extra hours depending on where the connections are. Check the layover times: some can be tight, so keep your luggage ready for plane changes. The return flight will follow a similar pattern.

Best time to visit

The Philippines has a tropical climate. December to May is the best season: clear skies, less rain and pleasant temperatures. If you travel during typhoon season (July to October), prices are usually lower but weather risk is higher. August and September are the wettest months. Travelling outside these peak dates may offer better fares.

What to do when you arrive

Manila is your gateway to the Philippines. Walk around Intramuros, the old walled city. Don't miss Rizal Park, perfect to unwind. For beaches, take a short domestic flight to Boracay, Palawan or Cebu. Eat at Quiapo market and try street food. The National Museum is a must if you like museums. Naia airport is about 7 km from the city centre; use authorised taxis or Uber.
