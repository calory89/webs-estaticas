Best time to visit

Kraków shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) with pleasant weather and fewer rainy days. Winter is cold but atmospheric if snow appeals to you. Summer is peak season with more tourists and higher potential fares. Book 1–2 months ahead for better rates, especially in shoulder or low season.

What to do when you arrive

Kraków is a medieval gem: explore Rynek Główny (the largest main square in Europe) and climb St Mary's Church Tower. Visit Wawel Castle, Poland's national symbol. For deeper history, Auschwitz-Birkenau camp is 65 km away (guided tours run from the city). Try pierniki (sweet dumplings) and żurek (rye soup). The Jewish Quarter and Kazimierz market are must-stops.
