Best time to visit

Cairo in November is excellent: mild temperatures (20-30°C), no summer heat, and fewer tourists than peak winter. It's perfect for exploring comfortably. Avoid July and August if you don't handle extreme heat well.

What to do when you arrive

The international airport is 25 km from the city centre. Once there, the Pyramids of Giza are unmissable, along with the Egyptian Museum. Coptic Cairo, Khan el-Khalili (the traditional bazaar) and a Nile cruise are classic must-dos. Plan at least 3-4 days to enjoy without rushing.
