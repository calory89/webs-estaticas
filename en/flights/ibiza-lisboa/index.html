Prices and when to book

The lowest price is €103 return. Sample fares range €103, €112 and €136. Advance booking remains your best strategy: reserve 5–8 weeks ahead if possible. Prices tend to climb as the date approaches, especially around bank holidays and weekends.

Airline and stops

You travel with Vueling on this route. The outbound flight includes 1 stop, so it's not direct but the connection is manageable. Vueling is reliable on this sector and base fares are competitive if you pack light.

Best time to visit

Lisbon shines in autumn and winter: less mass tourism, pleasant temperatures (15–20°C) and walks along the Tagus without crowds. October and November are ideal months. Avoid July and August if you want lower fares and fewer tourists.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Humberto Delgado Airport, 7 km from the centre. The metro connects you direct in 30 minutes. Stroll Baixa Pombalina, take Tram 28, explore Alfama's tiles, cross the Tagus by boat. Eat pastéis de Nata and taste Portuguese wines. Three days cover the essentials.

FAQ

What is the cheapest flight from Ibiza to Lisbon?

The lowest price is €103 return. It depends on dates and how far ahead you book. The sooner you reserve, the better your chances of catching that price.

Does Vueling fly direct from Ibiza to Lisbon?

No, the flight includes 1 stop. It's a short connection but adds to your total travel time. If you need direct, check other dates or airlines.

When is it cheaper to fly to Lisbon from Ibiza?

Autumn and winter are usually cheaper than summer. Day of the week and advance booking also matter: a Tuesday or Wednesday booked 6 weeks ahead is often cheaper than a Friday last-minute.

What must I see in Lisbon in 3 days?

Must-see: Baixa Pombalina, Alfama, Tram 28, Jerónimos, Belém, the Tagus. Eat at Ribeira Negra, try pastéis de Nata at Pastéis de Belém. A day trip to Sintra is worth it if you have extra time.
