Vienna and Marrakech are just over 1000 km apart and Ryanair connects both cities with one stopover. Prices hover around €124 return on the most economical dates, with a median of €143. Whether you want a short or long getaway, this route is affordable if you book with time.

Price and when to book

The lowest return fare found is €124, and the median sits at €143. These prices are with Ryanair. Book as early as possible: with 6–8 weeks' notice, your chances of scoring deals improve significantly. Low-season travel (outside summer and bank holidays) is cheaper; check directly with the airline for availability on specific dates.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair is your option on this route with one stop included. It's not a direct flight, but the price makes up for it. The airline operates from Vienna (Schwechat airport) to Marrakech (Menara airport). Travel light with hand baggage only to avoid extra charges; Ryanair charges for checked luggage.

Best time to visit

Marrakech offers the best weather in autumn (October–November) and spring (March–April): pleasant temperatures and less extreme heat than summer. Winter (December–February) is mild but with occasional rain. If you're after budget prices, these shoulder seasons are ideal because you dodge peak summer tourism.

What to do in Marrakech

Marrakech offers Jemaa el-Fnaa Square with street performers and food vendors, historic palaces and gardens in the Medina (UNESCO World Heritage), and the Atlas Mountains just under an hour away. The souks invite you to haggle for crafts, textiles and ceramics. Menara airport is 6 km from the city centre; bus, taxi or car hire get you downtown quickly.

FAQ

Is the Vienna to Marrakech flight direct?

No, it includes one stop. Ryanair operates this route with an intermediate stopover, so the overall journey is longer than a direct flight, but the price is very competitive.

What is the cheapest price you found?

The lowest return price is €124. This is the best offer; the median hovers around €143. Book in advance to access these fares.

Which airline flies this route?

Ryanair is the carrier on this connection. Check their baggage policy: the flight includes free hand luggage, but checked baggage carries an extra charge.

What to see in Marrakech in 4 or 5 days?

The Medina and historic palaces, Majorelle and Menara gardens, Jemaa el-Fnaa Square, artisan souks, and trips to the Atlas Mountains or Zagora desert are must-see. Marrakech is easy to explore on foot and by local taxi.
