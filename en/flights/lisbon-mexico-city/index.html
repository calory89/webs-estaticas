Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €933 return, though most tickets hover around €967. Sample prices range from €933 to €1,016. Booking 6–8 weeks in advance usually offers better fares than waiting. Set up price alerts on your flight search engine to catch significant changes.

Airline and stops

Lufthansa operates this route with 1 stopover on the outbound journey. It's not a direct flight, but one stop is standard for transatlantic routes Lisbon–Mexico. Always check your layover time when buying: some flights offer 2–3 hour connections, others longer.

Best time to travel

Mexico City has mild weather almost year-round. The dry season (October to April) brings clear days and no rain, ideal for sightseeing. Avoid peak Atlantic hurricane season (September–October) if you prefer stable flights. Travel outside Spanish and Mexican holiday periods to find better fares.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Mexico City International Airport (Benito Juárez), 5 km from the centre. Metro, bus and taxi connect to the city. Visit the Historic Centre, Basilica of Guadalupe, Xochimilco and the Frida Kahlo and Tamayo museums. Explore Coyoacán and consider Teotihuacán for a day trip. The time difference is –7 hours from Portugal.
