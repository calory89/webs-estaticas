Best time to visit

Phu Quoc is tropical. Dry season (November to March) delivers clear skies and minimal rain. November is ideal: pleasant weather and prices haven't peaked yet. Avoid monsoon season if you prefer stable weather.

What to do on arrival

Phu Quoc is an island: white-sand beaches, diving, pearl farms and floating markets are must-dos. The island has accessible resorts, local restaurants and water activities. The airport is just over 10 km from the centre. Many visitors combine Phu Quoc with other Vietnam destinations.

What else is there to see in Phu Quoc besides beaches?
