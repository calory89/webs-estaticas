Best time to visit

Portland is pleasant in summer (June to September) with long days and dry weather. If you prefer less rain and fewer crowds, spring (April–May) and early autumn (September–October) are excellent. Winters are rainy but the city remains interesting. Avoid booking last-minute; purchase in advance for better prices regardless of season.

What to do when you arrive in Portland

Portland is known for its cultural scene, local food, and indie vibe. Walk around downtown, visit Powell's Books (an iconic bookstore), explore different neighbourhoods (Alberta, Hawthorne, Pearl District). Don't miss Forest Park, one of the largest urban parks in the USA, or the numerous food carts and cafes. The city is very walkable or accessible by public transport.
