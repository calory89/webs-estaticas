Best time to visit

Bali's dry season runs April to October—ideal for beaches and hiking. Rainy season (November–March) brings lower prices but heavier showers. December is a sweet spot: fewer crowds than July–August, rain still building. Skip school holidays if you want better deals.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Ngurah Rai (DPS), the main airport. Explore beaches like Seminyak and Uluwatu, ancient temples, rice terraces in Ubud, and volcanoes. Bali is cheap for food, lodging and activities. Arrange local transport ahead (taxi, hire car or tours).
