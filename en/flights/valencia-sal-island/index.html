What to do on arrival

Sal Island is Cape Verde's gateway. Expect white sand beaches, snorkelling and world-class diving. Explore Santa Maria's coastal charm or lounge at Ponta Leme. Airport is 10 km from main resorts. Hire a car or use local taxis to get around.
