Airline and stops

Best time to visit

Stockholm shines in summer (June to August) with long daylight hours and mild weather, but it's peak season. Winter (December to February) is magical for snow and northern lights, though very cold. Spring and autumn balance price and comfort. Book 2–3 months ahead for better fares.

What to do in Stockholm

Gamla Stan, the old town, feels medieval. Stockholm City Hall has great views. Museums like the Vasa (a 17th-century warship) or ABBA Museum are worth time. Summer brings boat trips around archipelagos; winter offers frozen-lake ice-skating. The subway itself is an art gallery. A city transit pass covers everything.

Are there direct flights from Palma to Stockholm?

Yes, Ryanair flies direct with no stops. Departure from Palma (PMI), arrival in Stockholm (STO). It's the quickest option.

What should I see in Stockholm in 5 days?

Walk through Gamla Stan, climb Stockholm City Hall, visit the Vasa Museum. Spend a day on boat trips to islands and archipelagos. Explore Södermalm (cafés and shops) and Norrmalm (shopping and nightlife). The subway is an attraction itself. A city pass gives you unlimited transport.
