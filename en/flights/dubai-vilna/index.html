Best time to visit

Vilna shines in spring and autumn. Winters are harsh (sub-zero temperatures), though the city sparkles with holiday lights in December. Skip peak tourist seasons if you want a quieter experience. Check frequently: small date shifts can save you €30–€50.

What to do when you arrive

Lithuania's capital has a medieval UNESCO-listed Old Town with Baroque churches, historic squares and defensive walls. The international airport (VNO) is 4 km from the centre. Walk Gediminas Avenue, climb Gediminas Tower and try Baltic cuisine. The city is compact and walkable, with cheap public transport.
