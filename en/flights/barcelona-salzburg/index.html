Price and when to book

Flights start from €147 (return). We've seen prices of €147, €155 and €201 in searches from 21 October. Book early: fares climb as the date approaches. Don't leave it until the last minute.

Airline and stops

Vueling is your option on this route. You'll have 1 stop on the outbound flight, meaning one intermediate landing before Salzburg. It's a fair trade-off: you lose time, you save money.

Best time to visit

Salzburg shines in autumn (October-November) and winter (December-January), when Christmas markets fill the streets and Baroque architecture gleams under snow. Spring is also excellent if you prefer to avoid the cold. Prices tend to drop outside July and August.

What to do when you arrive

Hohensalzburg fortress dominates the skyline. Don't miss Mozart's birthplace, the Cathedral, or a stroll down Getreidegasse, the most picturesque street in town. If time allows, climb the fortress at sunset. The centre is compact and walkable; there's a direct bus from the airport.
