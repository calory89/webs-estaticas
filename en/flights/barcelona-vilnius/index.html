Barcelona-Vilna is on offer: from €97 for the whole trip with Ryanair. The median price is €116, so there's room to find deals if you book early. Lithuania is waiting for you on a budget.

Prices and when to book

The lowest fare is €97 return. Other available prices hover around €116 and €138. Book as soon as possible: demand grows closer to departure. October and November tend to have reasonable fares on this route, so if your travel dates are flexible, note those months.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight. Departure from Barcelona (BCN) with no changes until Vilna (VNO). Outbound around 09:30 and return around 16:45, though times vary by date. Always check exact times in your search.

Best time to travel

Vilna shines in autumn (September to October) with colours and pleasant weather, and spring (April to May). Winter is cold but charming if you like snow. Skip peak summer (July-August) if you want lower prices. Check deals in February and March too.

What to do when you arrive

Vilna airport (VNO) is 4 km from the city centre. The old town is a UNESCO site: stroll the cathedral, churches and markets. Climb Gediminas Hill for panoramic views. Nightlife and dining are affordable. Trakai Castle is easy to reach by train. Lithuania offers plenty on a tight budget.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Barcelona to Vilna?

Yes. Ryanair flies direct with no stopovers between Barcelona (BCN) and Vilna (VNO).

What's the minimum price for this flight?

From €97 return. The median is €116, so most dates fall around that price.

Which airline offers the cheapest fares?

Ryanair operates this route and offers the prices shown. Compare dates to find the lowest fare.

What to see in Vilna besides the old town?

Trakai Castle 28 km away is a must. Walks through Vilna's hills, art museums, and Lithuanian food in any tavern. Lake Galvė near Trakai is beautiful. Everything is affordable.
