Best time to visit

The Philippines enjoys good weather year-round. The dry season (November to April) is ideal to avoid heavy rain and get more sunny days at beaches and in cities. December and January are peak months with higher prices; consider February or March to balance weather and budget.

What to do when you arrive in Manila

Manila is the perfect entry point. From the airport you can reach the historic centre (Intramuros), museums, local markets and great food. Use Manila as a base to plan trips to nearby islands, Palawan, Boracay or the Cordilleras. Public transport is cheap and cost of living is far lower than Europe.

What to see in Manila besides beaches?
