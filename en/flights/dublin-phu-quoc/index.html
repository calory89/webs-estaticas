Best time to visit

Phu Quoc has a tropical climate. The best months are November to February, with less rain and comfortable temperatures. December is excellent if you don't mind travelling in high season, with beaches in perfect condition. Avoid May to October if you want to steer clear of monsoons.

What to do when you arrive

Phu Quoc is a beach destination: Sao Beach and Long Beach are the most popular. You can snorkel, dive, take boat trips or visit the marine park. The town of Duong Dong has local markets and seafood restaurants. It's a quiet island, ideal to unwind after a long flight.
