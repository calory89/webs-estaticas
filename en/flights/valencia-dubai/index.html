Fly from Valencia to Dubai for €465 return with Vueling. The median price hovers around €501. Book well in advance and secure the lowest fares on this route to the Emirates.

Price and when to book

Valencia-Dubai flights start from €465 return, with a median price around €501. Vueling offers options from this amount. To get the best price, book as far in advance as possible; fares typically rise as your departure date approaches. Prices range between €465 and €587 depending on availability and selected dates.

Airline and stopovers

Vueling is your option on this route. Flights include 2 stopovers, so you won't fly direct. Although this adds to your journey time, it allows access to more competitive prices than a non-stop flight. Always check connection times before confirming your booking.

Best time to travel

Dubai is accessible year-round, but the climate is most pleasant from November to March when temperatures are moderate. Prices tend to rise during school holidays and festive periods. If you travel outside peak demand times, you'll find more options near the median price.

What to do when you arrive in Dubai

Once you land at Dubai International Airport (DXB), you can explore the Burj Khalifa, visit the desert, walk along the waterfront (Beach Walk), shop at Emirates Mall or relax on public beaches. The metro connects the airport to downtown quickly. There's no single must-do: it all depends on your interests.

FAQ

What is the cheapest price for Valencia-Dubai?

The lowest price found is €465 return with Vueling. This price may vary depending on your exact travel date and real-time availability.

Is it a direct flight from Valencia to Dubai?

No, Vueling offers this route with 2 stopovers. This means you'll have two stops before reaching Dubai, which increases total journey time but reduces cost.

Which airline flies Valencia-Dubai?

Vueling is the available airline for this route based on current data. Check baggage policies before booking, as restrictions on luggage may apply.

What is the best time to travel to Dubai?

November to March offers the best climate (temperatures between 20–30°C). Avoid June to August if you can't handle extreme heat. Outside these months you'll also find more competitive prices.
