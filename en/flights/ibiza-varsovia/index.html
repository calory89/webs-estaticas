Price and when to book

The cheapest ticket is around €130 return, and most fares range between €130 and €161. Book early: the sooner you purchase, the better price you'll get. Avoid last-minute bookings and check Tuesday to Thursday, when fares tend to be more competitive.

Airline and stops

Vueling operates this route with one stop. There are no direct flights between Ibiza and Warsaw, so your journey includes an intermediate connection. Total travel time depends on your connecting airport, but allow enough time between flights.

Best time to travel

Poland shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with mild weather and fewer crowds. Winter (December–February) is cold but magical if you like snow. Avoid July–August if you want better prices and fewer tourists.

What to do when you arrive in Warsaw

Warsaw is living history: explore the Old Town (UNESCO World Heritage), visit museums on World War II, stroll along the Vistula River and try local cuisine in the Praga district. Chopin Airport is well connected to the city centre, 10 km away. Note January is very cold; pack warm clothes.

FAQ
