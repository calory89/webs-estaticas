Best time to travel

January is a good month to visit Kyoto: cold but dry weather, less peak-season tourism. Avoid the week before and after New Year if you want lower prices. Check Japan's local holidays to plan activities.

What to do when you arrive

From Kansai airport, trains reach Kyoto in just over an hour. Visit temples like Fushimi Inari or Arashiyama, walk through the geisha district of Gion, and taste local food. Kyoto is Japan's cultural heart: temples, gardens and tradition everywhere.
