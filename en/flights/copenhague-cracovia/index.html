Best time to visit

Krakow appeals year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winters are cold but magical if you enjoy snow. Avoid European school holidays and public holidays if you want cheaper fares.

What to do when you arrive

Krakow airport (Balice) is 20 km from the city centre. Follow the Royal Route: Wawel Castle, Market Square, and the Jewish Quarter. Visit Auschwitz as a day trip. Eat pierniki (dumplings) anywhere and try Polish beer. Krakow blends history, food, and nightlife at very affordable prices.
