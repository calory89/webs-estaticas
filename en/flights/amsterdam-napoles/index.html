Best time to visit

Naples works year-round, but October to April is cheaper and less crowded than summer. Avoid July and August if you're chasing deals. The city is active 365 days: focus on what you want to do (beaches or milder weather) rather than only price swings.

What to do on arrival

Naples Airport (Capodichino) is 4 km from the city centre. Express rail or bus gets you downtown in 20 minutes. Must-see: the Cathedral, the Unesco-listed old town, Pompeii 30 minutes by train, and the islands of Capri or Procida by boat. Eat pizza on Via dei Tribunali. Book restaurants ahead.
