Best time to visit Cancún

High season (December to April) is most popular: dry weather, warm waters and perfect beaches. For cheaper prices, consider May to November, though it's hurricane season (get good travel insurance). July and August are hot and humid but outside the peak hurricane window.

What to do in Cancún and nearby

Cancún is your gateway to Playa del Carmen, Tulum and the Riviera Maya. Explore cenotes, Mayan ruins like Chichén Itzá and white sand beaches. The international airport is 25 km from the centre; rent a car or use shared transfers to get around. The currency is the Mexican peso.
