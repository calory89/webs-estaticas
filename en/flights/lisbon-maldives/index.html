Best time to visit

Maldives has two monsoons: the northwest (November to March) brings clear skies and calm seas, while the southwest (May to October) brings more rain but fewer tourists. November to February is perfect for diving and beaches, though prices rise. For budget travel, consider April, May or October as shoulder seasons with good deals and decent weather.

What to do when you arrive

Maldives is all about beaches and reef. Land at Velana International Airport (MLE) and take a speedboat or seaplane to your resort. Dive, snorkel right from your bungalow, explore Malé. Baa, Ari and Malé atolls have the best wall dives. You don't need tours, the sea is the destination itself.
