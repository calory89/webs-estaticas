Best time to travel

November is ideal: pleasant weather in Las Vegas (moderate temperatures, no extreme heat) and still before peak holiday season. June to August offer cheaper fares but expect intense heat (45°C+). Avoid December and major holidays for better deals.

What to do in Las Vegas

Beyond casinos and shows, walk the Strip, ride the High Roller observation wheel, explore Valley of Fire State Park (48 km north), or visit the Neon Museum for vintage Vegas history. Red Rock Canyon (32 km away) is perfect for hiking and sunsets away from the city buzz.
