Airline and stops

Best time to visit

Sardinia shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): perfect weather, fewer crowds than summer, and equally stunning beaches. For lower fares, low season (winter and early spring) typically beats July–August prices.

What to do when you arrive

Sardinia means white-sand beaches, hidden coves and Roman heritage. Explore the Costa Smeralda, visit Cagliari (the capital), wander inland villages or take a boat to the La Maddalena Archipelago. Cagliari-Elmas airport is 4 km from town; grab a taxi or car rental from there.

FAQ

No, it includes 1 stop. Ryanair connects both islands via a flight with an intermediate layover, which is why the price is so budget-friendly.

What's the cheapest price I've seen for this route?

The lowest recorded price is €169 return. The median sits at €171, so if you spot fares near that level, it's a solid buy.

Which airline flies between Gran Canaria and Sardinia?

What can you visit in Sardinia if you're short on time?

With limited days, focus on Cagliari and surroundings, or the Costa Smeralda if you're after beaches. The inland region with traditional villages is also worth exploring if you rent a car.
