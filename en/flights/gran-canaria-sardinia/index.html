Best time to visit

November is an excellent choice: Sardinian autumn runs warm (18–22°C), beaches are quieter than summer and prices drop. The Mediterranean water is still swimmable. Harvest season brings good vibes to villages too. For guaranteed sunshine, August costs more; spring (April–May) is pricey but gorgeous. Winter (December–February) can be rainy and many facilities close.

What to do when you arrive

Cagliari airport (CAG) is 10 km from the capital. Hire a car or catch a bus from there. Must-sees: Costa Smeralda (dreamy northern beaches), Bosa (charming fishing village), Oristano (authentic Sardinia), Pula's southern beaches, or Bugerru's malachite mines. Try fregola pasta and Cannonau wine. Ten days gives you time to explore the island without rushing.
