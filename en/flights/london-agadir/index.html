Best time to visit

Agadir enjoys great weather year-round. The most pleasant months are October to April, when it's warm without the scorching summer heat. Winter (December–February) means fewer crowds. Since Ryanair keeps fares low all year, compare prices across your available dates rather than chasing a specific season.

What to do when you arrive

Agadir is Morocco's main beach destination with a stunning bay and full tourist infrastructure. Visit the kasbah, stroll the corniche at sunset, day-trip to Essaouira's medina, or simply relax on the beach. The fishing port offers authentic local life. Hire a car to explore the coast or the mountains inland.
