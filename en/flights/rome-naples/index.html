Fly from Rome to Naples from €154 return with ITA Airways. Prices typically hover around €156 on the median, so this route is well within budget. A quick and direct connection between two of Italy's most vibrant cities.

Price and when to book

The cheapest flight we've seen is €154 (return), with typical fares around €156. On this short route, price swings are minimal. Book 2-3 weeks ahead if you can; on domestic or short-haul routes, prices are less volatile than long-haul, but getting in early always helps.

Airlines and stopovers

ITA Airways operates this route direct: no stopovers, no connections. Departures around 21:45 from Rome, arrival in Naples past midnight (return typically at 15:00). A straightforward journey of just over an hour.

Best time to visit

Naples is pleasant year-round, but May-June and September-October are ideal: perfect weather, fewer crowds than July-August, and gentler prices. Avoid peak summer if you want peace and quiet.

What to do when you arrive

Naples airport (Capodichino) is 7 km from the city centre, reachable in 15-20 minutes. Don't miss the Cathedral, the Palazzo Reale di Capodimonte, the National Archaeology Museum (with Pompeian treasures), walks through the Mergellina port, and authentic Neapolitan pizza. With one extra day, Pompeii and Herculaneum are less than an hour away.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price Rome-Naples?

From €154 return. It's a very short route, so fares stay pretty steady around that mark.

Which airline flies Rome to Naples?

ITA Airways operates this route on direct flights.

Yes, it's a direct flight with no stopovers on ITA Airways.

What to see in Naples in 1-2 days?

The Cathedral, the National Archaeology Museum (with mosaics and sculptures from Pompeii), Palazzo Reale di Capodimonte, the port and the old town. With a second day, Pompeii or Herculaneum are a must-visit.
