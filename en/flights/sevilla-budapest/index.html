Best time to visit

November is ideal: cool weather but bearable, fewer crowds than summer. Christmas and New Year push prices up sharply. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) also offer good value. Avoid July–August if you want low fares.

What to do on arrival

Budapest is Central Europe's cultural hub. See the Parliament, Széchenyi Thermal Baths, the Danube from the Buda side. Eat authentic goulash, explore Art Nouveau architecture and walk around Pest. Ferenc Liszt Airport is 15 km from the centre; take a bus or shared minibus to save money.
