Price and when to book

Valencia-Fez flights start from €39 return, with a median of €53. We've seen fares up to €93. The earlier you book, the better: Ryanair increases prices as departure approaches. Aim for 4-6 weeks ahead to lock in the lowest fares.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair is the option on this route and flies direct. No stopovers means less hassle, shorter travel time and lower risk of delays. Departs Valencia, arrives straight into Fez, making your journey straightforward.

Best time to visit

October, November and March are ideal: pleasant weather, mild temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. Skip July and August if you can't handle heat (regularly exceeds 35 °C). Spring and autumn offer the perfect balance of price and comfort.

What to do when you arrive

Fez is Morocco's cultural gem: the medina, a Unesco World Heritage site, takes you centuries back through tea houses, souks and craftsmanship. Visit the Al Quaraouiyine University, traditional tanneries and colonial riads. Meknes, Ifrane and the Rif are within reach. Rent a car or use local taxis to explore.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price Valencia to Fez?

From €39 return. That's the lowest recorded fare; median sits around €53. Depends on dates, availability and how far ahead you book.

Is Valencia to Fez a direct flight?

Yes, Ryanair flies direct with no stopovers. It's the most convenient and quickest option between these two cities.

Ryanair operates Valencia-Fez. They offer competitive fares but check their baggage policies.

What to see in Fez in 3-4 days?

The old medina (essential), tanneries, museums, Al Quaraouiyine University and riads. With more time, day trips to Meknes, Ifrane or Volubilis are worthwhile.
