Fly Barcelona to Marrakech for €39 return with Ryanair. The median price is €40. Direct flight with no stops and frequent departures so you can choose the date that suits you.

Price and when to book

Flights on this route start at €39 return. Most tickets hover around €40. Whilst there's no magic day when prices drop, booking early reduces the risk of fares going up. Ryanair tends to keep fares low if you book in advance. Check deals on weekdays; in peak season (winter and spring) seats sell out quickly.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight (no stops). Early afternoon departure from Barcelona and evening return make it easy to maximise your time in Morocco. Luggage: check with Ryanair if you need to pay extra for checked baggage. Cabin baggage is included.

Best time to visit

Marrakech is ideal from October to April: mild weather (20–25°C daytime), no extreme summer heat. December and January offer good flight deals as many Brits travel over Christmas, but there are also promotions outside those peak dates. Skip July and August unless you fancy temperatures over 35°C.

What to do when you arrive

Marrakech airport (RAK) is 6 km from the centre. Bus, taxi or car hire are your options. Visit the Old Medina, Jemaa el-Fnaa square (with musicians and street food), historic palaces (Bahia, Badia), gardens (Majorelle) and souks. Sahara desert tours and Atlas Mountains trips are easy day excursions from here.

FAQ

Are flights really €39 from Barcelona to Marrakech?

Yes. We've seen prices from €39 return (from sample data dated 21 October 2026). The median is €40, so it's normal to find deals in that range. Higher prices in the sample reach around €56.

Is it a direct flight or does it have stops?

It's direct. Ryanair flies non-stop from Barcelona (BCN) to Marrakech (RAK). You leave in the afternoon and arrive in Morocco in the evening.

Which is the best airline on this route?

Ryanair is the one offering these bargain fares. It's a budget carrier (checked baggage isn't included unless you pay), but it works well for short hauls if you travel light or buy luggage allowance.

What can I see and do in Marrakech?

The Old Medina, Jemaa el-Fnaa square, historic palaces (Bahia, Badia), Majorelle Gardens, souks and traditional markets. Day trips to the Sahara desert, Atlas Mountains or Berber villages are all easy from here.
