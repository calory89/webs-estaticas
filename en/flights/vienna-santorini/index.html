Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €223 for a return trip. The median sits at €227, so you have some wiggle room. Book early: Ryanair fares tend to climb closer to departure. Search at least 6-8 weeks in advance to snap up these prices.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route with 1 stop each way. It's not non-stop, but the price makes up for it. Remember Ryanair charges for extras: luggage, seat selection and changes all cost money. Check their website to avoid surprises at the airport shop.

Best time to visit

Santorini is seasonal. Summer (June to September) is expensive and crowded. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer the best price-to-weather balance. Winter is cheaper but many businesses close. December still has good weather with fewer crowds than August.

What to do when you arrive

Joannis Daskalogiannis airport is on the island. From there, take a bus or taxi to your hotel. Spend time in Oia and Fira for sunset views, dive at volcanic beaches, taste local wine and relax in hot springs. No need for a rental car if you stay in the main village.
