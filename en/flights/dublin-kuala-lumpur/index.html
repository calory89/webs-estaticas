Best time to visit

Kuala Lumpur is a tropical destination year-round. The driest months are June to August, though rain is frequent. November to March offer fewer showers. Avoid the peak European holiday season if you want fewer crowds at hotels and attractions. Plan 2–3 months ahead for better seat availability.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA). The city features the Petronas Towers, KL Tower for 360° views, the cultural Chinatown district, Hindu temples like Thean Hou, and the Royal Palace. Outside the capital, Taman Negara National Park and the Batu Caves are accessible nature escapes. Local metro and bus transport is cheap and reliable.
