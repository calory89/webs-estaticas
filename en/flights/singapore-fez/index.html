What to do when you arrive

Fez is Morocco's cultural capital. The old medina with its souks, Al Quaraouiyine University, and the famous leather tanners are essential. Fez-Saïss airport sits about 15 km from the city centre. Taxi or transfer services get you there in 30–40 minutes. Spend at least 2–3 days exploring at a comfortable pace.
