Best time to visit

Palermo is pleasant most of the year, but May–June and September–October offer the best weather without summer extremes. November brings low season prices and fewer crowds. Winters (December–February) are mild but wetter.

What to do when you arrive

Palermo is Italy's food capital: grab arancini, pasta alla Norma and panelle at street markets. Visit Palazzo dei Normanni, the Cathedral and the Cappella Palatina for Byzantine mosaics. Don't miss Teatro Politeama or wandering the Vucciria neighbourhood. The airport is 35 km away; bus or hire car are affordable options.
