Best time to travel

Nairobi has pleasant weather year-round. Dry seasons (January-February and June-October) are best. Avoid April and May if you want less rain. Book your flight well in advance to lock in a good rate.

What to do when you arrive in Nairobi

Nairobi is the gateway to Kenya's safaris. The National Museum, Giraffe Centre and urban parks are worth visiting. With a few extra days, the Amboseli or Masai Mara parks are only hours away.
