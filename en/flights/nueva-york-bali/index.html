Best time to visit

Bali works year-round, but April to October is peak season: sunny days and less rain. November to March brings higher humidity and showers, though it's quieter. For avoiding crowds and finding better flight deals, try May–June or August–September, when prices often drop.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) in Denpasar. From there, hire a car or use local transport. Don't miss Ubud's temples, Seminyak and Canggu beaches, Tegallalang's rice terraces, and Mount Agung. Bali blends beach, culture and nature within affordable flying distance from New York.
