Best time to visit

Denmark is stunning in summer (June to August) and winter for the northern lights (November to February). Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild weather and fewer crowds. Check the airline's price calendar to see when fares drop for your preferred travel dates.

What to do when you arrive in Copenhagen

Copenhagen is iconic: stroll around Nyhavn with its colourful townhouses, visit Rosenborg Castle and enjoy Nordic cuisine. Museums (Ny Carlsberg Glyptotek, Statens Museum for Kunst) are worth your time. Copenhagen Airport is about 10 km from the city centre; trains or buses will get you into town quickly.
