Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €64 return; the median sits at €67. In our price sample, fares range from €64 to €81, so you have room to manoeuvre. Book at least 4–6 weeks in advance to secure a low fare. Cheap tickets sell out quickly on this popular route, especially during holiday periods.

Airline and layovers

Ryanair operates this route with non-stop flights (no layovers). The outbound departs Rome in the evening (19:30) and the return leaves in the early morning (06:00). Travel with just hand luggage to avoid extra baggage charges.

Best time to visit

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Fares tend to be more competitive outside July–August and public holidays. Avoid Christmas and Easter if you're after budget prices.

What to do when you arrive

Paris has two main airports: Charles de Gaulle (CDG) and Orly. Quick access to the city centre by train or bus. Explore the Rive Gauche, climb the Eiffel Tower, visit the Louvre, stroll through Montmartre. Both cities have good public transport links from their airports.
