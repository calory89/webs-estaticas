Best time to visit

Krakow is particularly lovely in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) with mild weather and fewer crowds. Winter is cold but festive; summer attracts more tourists. For budget hunting, travelling November to February (outside Christmas) and June–July often offers better value.

What to do on arrival

Krakow is a stunning medieval city. Don't miss the Main Market Square (Rynek Główny), Wawel Cathedral, the Jewish Quarter, and the Wieliczka Salt Mine. It's just 15 km from the airport. Strolling its cobbled streets, sampling local food, and exploring historical museums are essential.
