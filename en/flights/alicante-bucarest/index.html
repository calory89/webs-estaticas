What to do in Bucharest

Romania's capital blends communist-era architecture, stunning Orthodox churches, and a thriving food scene. Don't miss the Palace of Parliament, the medieval quarters of Lipscani and Obor, Stavropoleos and Radu Vodă churches, or simply wander the bars and cafés on Dorobanți Street. Romanian food is flavourful and cheap.
