Best time to visit

Krakow is stunning year-round, but May, June, September and October offer mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter (December–February) is bitterly cold with snow—ideal if you fancy Christmas markets or nearby Tatra skiing. July and August draw peak tourism; expect higher prices.

What to do in Krakow

The Main Market Square (Rynek Główny) is the historic heart, packed with cafés and shops. The Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage site. Don't miss Wawel Castle, St. Mary's Basilica, and Kazimierz, the former Jewish quarter. Krakow Airport (KRK) is 20 km from the centre; buses, taxis and private transfers reach downtown in 30–45 minutes.
