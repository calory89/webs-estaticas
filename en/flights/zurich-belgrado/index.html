Best time to visit Belgrade

Belgrade is accessible almost year-round. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Winters are cold but the city has Christmas charm. Avoid peak tourism seasons if you prefer a quieter experience.

What to do when you arrive

Belgrade won't leave you indifferent. Walk around Kalemegdan Fortress, explore the old town and relax on the splavovi (floating river bars on the Danube). Museums, art galleries, vibrant nightlife and authentic Balkan food await. A short trip from Zurich with plenty to explore.
