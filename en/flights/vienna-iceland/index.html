Best time to visit

Iceland is a year-round destination. Winter (November to February) brings northern lights, short days and snowy landscapes; it's the most magical season but with extreme weather. Spring and autumn offer moderate temperatures and fewer crowds. Summer features 24-hour daylight and is peak season. Whatever the season, pack warm clothing: Iceland is always cold.

What to do when you arrive at Keflavík

Keflavík International Airport (KEF) is 50 km from Reykjavik. From there, explore the Golden Circle (Þingvellir, Geysir, Gullfoss), the south coast with its waterfalls, or the northern fjords. If travelling in winter, book northern lights tours. The capital, Reykjavik, has museums, nightlife and Nordic gastronomy. Rent a car or join tours: many secondary roads require experience driving in snow.

FAQ

From €181 return with Ryanair. This is the lowest price we've recorded. Prices typically average around €230, so booking early and staying flexible with dates helps you grab the best deals.

Is the flight from Vienna to Iceland direct?

No, Ryanair includes 1 stopover on this route. It adds 2–4 hours to the total journey, but keeps the price low. Check where the stopover is when you book.

Which airline flies from Vienna to Iceland?

What to see in Iceland in a week?

With 6–7 days (like the example, 9th to 15th January), you can cover the Golden Circle, the south coast with waterfalls, and possibly the northern fjords. Plan road distances carefully: driving times are long. In winter, northern lights are the big draw if weather permits.
