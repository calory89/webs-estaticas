Best time to visit

Thailand is a sun-and-beach destination year-round, but October to February is the dry season (best weather). August-September sees more rain. Phuket is particularly crowded in December-January when prices spike everywhere. May-July offers better value if you don't mind higher humidity.

What to do on arrival

Phuket is the gateway to Thailand's beaches: Big Buddha, Patong Beach, Phi Phi Islands. Phuket International Airport is 30 km from the centre. Taxis, shuttles or bus from there. Sample local food, visit Buddhist temples and night markets. A week gives you time to explore without rushing.
