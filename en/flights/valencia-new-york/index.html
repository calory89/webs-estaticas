Best time to visit

New York is attractive all year round. Autumn (September–November) and spring (April–May) offer good weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winters are cold but magical if you like snow. Avoid peak tourist seasons if you're looking for lower prices.

What to do when you arrive

New York has three airports: LaGuardia (LGA), Newark (EWR) and JFK. LaGuardia is closest to the centre. Once in the city, explore Manhattan, visit world-class museums, walk the High Line, discover neighbourhoods like Brooklyn or Soho, and eat at renowned restaurants. Public transport is fast and affordable.
