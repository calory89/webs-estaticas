Best time to visit

New York is a year-round destination. Autumn (September to November) and spring (April to June) offer good weather and fewer crowds than summer. What to do when you arrive

New York boasts museums (MoMA, Metropolitan), Broadway, Central Park and distinctive neighbourhoods (SoHo, Brooklyn, Harlem). Times Square, the Empire State and One World Observatory are must-sees. Eat pizza in Greenwich Village, explore galleries in Chelsea and walk the High Line.
