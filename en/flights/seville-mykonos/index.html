Best time to visit

Mykonos is a beach and party destination, best from May to September when it's warm and dry. Outside peak season (October-April) there's less tourism, cheaper accommodation, and quieter beaches, though the sea is colder. January and February see fewer flight frequencies, so options may be limited in winter. The island operates year-round, but most bars and clubs close November to March.

What to do when you arrive

Mykonos airport (JMK) is 10-15 minutes from the port or town centre. The island is small and easy to explore. Visit Chora (the main village) for its white-washed streets and shops, Psarou and Paradise beaches, and the windmills at sunset. Eat at local tavernas away from the tourist hub for better value. Rent a quad or scooter to explore independently without relying on buses.

What is there to see in Mykonos besides beaches?

The village of Chora with its white-washed alleys is a must. Don't miss the historic windmills, the Paraportiani church, and the seafood tavernas in Little Venice. For nightlife, the island is famous, but there's something for every budget.
