Best time to go

Dubrovnik in autumn (October-November) is excellent: fewer crowds, mild weather and beaches still accessible. Spring (April-May) is also ideal. Avoid July-August if you want lower fares and less congestion. Check the fare calendar days before booking.

What to do when you arrive

Dubrovnik is the jewel of the Dalmatian coast: medieval walls, old town, nearby beaches and villages like Cavtat or Ston within easy reach. The airport is 20 km from the city. Buses, taxis and car hire connect you to the region.
