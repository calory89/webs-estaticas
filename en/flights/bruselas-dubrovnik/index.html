Price and when to book

Flights start from €128 return according to October 2026 data. Prices in the sample range between €128 and €151, with a median of €145. The earlier you book, the better: fares rise as your travel date approaches. Don't leave it to the last minute if you want to keep your ticket in the €130–140 range.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with 1 stopover. The outbound flight departs Brussels at 09:05, and the return flight leaves Dubrovnik at 09:15. Although there is a stopover, total travel times are usually reasonable. Always check your booking confirmation for the stopover location and connection time.

Best time to travel

Dubrovnik is worth visiting year-round, but the Dalmatian coast shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): pleasant temperatures, fewer crowds than summer, and more budget-friendly prices. Winter is peaceful and economical if you seek a quieter experience.

What to do on arrival

Dubrovnik Airport is 22 km from the city centre. The Pearl of the Adriatic awaits: medieval walls, a UNESCO-listed Old Town, beaches along the Dubrovnik Riviera, and excursions to nearby islands. Ferries depart from the port to Lokrum, the Elafiti Islands, and other Adriatic gems.
