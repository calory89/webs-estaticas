Price and when to book

Tickets start from €204 return, though most fares sit between €204 and €252. Book as early as possible—last-minute deals on this route are rare. The search from 22 October shows pricing variation, so compare alternative dates and stay flexible with your travel days to save money.

Airline and stopovers

Wizz Air Malta operates this route with 1 stopover on the outbound flight. There are no direct flights from Valencia to Crete, so you'll need to make a connection. Check connection times carefully when purchasing and allow enough time at the stopover airport. Wizz Air Malta has strict baggage policies, so verify what's included in your ticket.

Best time to visit Crete

Crete is a year-round destination, but May–September bring beach weather and intense heat (30–35°C in August). October–April is milder (15–20°C). Peak season brings crowds and higher fares. November is a smart compromise: still sunny, fewer tourists, and lower prices, as you can see from the late-November examples.

What to do when you arrive in Crete

Your airport is Heraklion (HER), 5 km from the city centre. Enjoy beaches, hiking, archaeology (Knossos, Malia) and coastal towns like Rethymno. The food is excellent—Cretan cuisine ranks among Greece's best. Hire a car if you want to explore the island freely without relying on buses.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Valencia to Crete?

No. This route includes 1 stopover, so it is not direct. Wizz Air Malta handles the connection.

Can I get a cheaper ticket if I book further in advance?

Yes, generally the earlier you book, the more low-fare options appear. On international routes like this one, booking 2–3 months ahead usually offers better prices than booking in the final month.

What does the Wizz Air Malta price include?

Wizz Air is a low-cost carrier, so baggage may not be included depending on your fare type. Check when you purchase what your ticket covers (seat, cabin bag, checked luggage). The prices shown may be base fare only.

When is the best time to fly to Crete for price and weather?

October–November and April–May offer the best balance: pleasant weather without extreme heat or crowds. August is peak season with more tourism and potentially higher fares. Flying mid-week often costs less than weekends.
