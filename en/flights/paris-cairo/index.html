Best time to visit

December is a good choice: pleasant temperatures in Cairo (20–25°C) and no scorching summer heat. Avoid July and August when temperatures exceed 35°C. Crowds at temples and museums are thinner in winter, and travelling mid-month (as in this example) lets you enjoy yourself without end-of-year crowds.

What to do on arrival

Cairo is the gateway to the Pyramids of Giza, the Egyptian Museum, and Khan el-Khalili bazaar. The airport is 20 km from the centre; take an official taxi or bus. Most Nile cruises and day trips to Saqqara depart from here. Note the time difference: Cairo is 1 hour ahead of Paris in winter.
