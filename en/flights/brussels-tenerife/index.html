Flights from Brussels to Tenerife start from €75 return with Ryanair. The median price sits at €99, giving you plenty of affordable options to escape to the islands. Check our sample dates and start searching for your ticket.

Price and when to book

We found flights from €75 return. Typical prices hover around €99, with some tickets reaching €118 depending on your travel dates. As always, booking in advance pays off: the earlier you lock in your trip, the better the price. Set up price alerts and check mid-week, when fares tend to drop.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair is your carrier on this route. The flight is direct with no stopovers, so you depart Brussels and arrive straight in Tenerife. That saves you time and hassle with connections.

Best time to travel

Tenerife enjoys pleasant weather year-round, but peak season (December to March) brings more visitors and higher prices. To save money, aim for May to October, though July and August can spike as well. Avoid school holidays and bank holidays if your schedule allows.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Tenerife South, the airport closest to the southern beaches. From there, explore Los Cristianos, Playa de las Américas, or hire a car to tour the island: Mount Teide, Anaga, northern beaches... Tenerife is big with something for everyone.
