Best time to visit Jacksonville

February is a good choice: pleasant temperatures (18–23°C) and less peak tourism than summer. Avoid hurricane season (June to November). Spring (March–April) and early autumn are also recommended. Check the weather before travelling; Jacksonville has mild winters but occasional rain.

What to do when you arrive in Jacksonville

Jacksonville is the largest city by area in the continental US, with wide beaches such as Jacksonville Beach and Neptune Beach. Visit the historic San Marco district, the Cummer Museum or enjoy the Metropolitan Park. Saint Augustine (45 mins away) has the oldest European settlement in the country. Also accessible: nature reserves and Florida's Atlantic coast.
