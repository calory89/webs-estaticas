Best time to visit

Amsterdam shines in spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October), when weather is pleasant and rainfall moderate. Winter is cold and grey, but less crowded. Summers are warm with peak tourism. Choose based on your preference for weather and visitor numbers.

What to do when you arrive

Amsterdam offers unique canals for cycling, world-class museums (Van Gogh, Rijksmuseum), neighbourhoods like Jordaan for cosy cafés, and the Red Light District as a cultural attraction. Amsterdam Airport (AMS) has excellent public transport links to the city centre.
