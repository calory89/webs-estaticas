Best time to visit

Bali has tropical weather year-round. The dry season (May to October) is peak tourist season with clearer skies and less rain, though prices rise. The rainy season (November to April) is cheaper and quieter, perfect if you're budget-conscious and don't mind occasional showers. November marks the start of this season with still-competitive fares.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS), about 13 km south of Denpasar. From there, head to Seminyak or Canggu beaches, explore ancient temples like Tanah Lot or Besakih, trek up Mount Agung, or wander the rice terraces around Ubud. Bali blends culture, nature, and beach leisure all within easy reach and affordable prices.
