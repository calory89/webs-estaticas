Best time to visit

Bali has a tropical climate with two distinct seasons. The dry season (April to October) offers better weather and less rain, ideal if you prefer outdoor activities. The wet season (November to March) has more rainfall but fewer tourists and potentially lower prices. Choose based on your climate tolerance and crowding preference.

What to do when you arrive in Bali

Bali is famous for its beaches, terraced rice fields, and ancient temples. Visit Ubud to immerse yourself in local culture, relax in Seminyak or Kuta, or explore the temples of Tanah Lot and Besakih. Options exist for everyone: diving, trekking, yoga, spas, and local food. Rent a scooter for flexible movement or book organised tours.
