Best time to visit

Bali has a tropical climate. The best season is April to October, when there's less rain and weather is more stable. November to March is wetter with higher rainfall chances, though still visitable. January and February tend to be rainier. Consider these conditions when picking your travel dates.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) in southern Bali. From there, explore temples like Tanah Lot or Besakih, beaches such as Seminyak or Canggu, rice terraces in Ubud, or simply relax on the coast. Hire a scooter or car to get around, or use local taxis. Bali is affordable for food, accommodation and activities.
