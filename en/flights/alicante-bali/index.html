Best time to visit

Bali has a tropical climate: fly during the dry season (April to October) to avoid constant rain. July and August see peak tourism, so consider May-June or September-October for fewer crowds. Check the forecast close to your travel dates to pick the best weather window.

What to do when you arrive

Bali is far more than beaches: ancient temples like Tanah Lot, rice terraces in Ubud, hiking on Mount Batur, diving in Amed, and local markets await. Denpasar airport (DPS) has good transport links to the south. Spend at least 5-7 days to explore without rushing.
