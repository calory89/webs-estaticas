Best time to travel Milan to Vienna

Vienna is stunning year-round. Winter (December–February) is magical for Christmas markets and New Year vibes. Spring and autumn offer mild weather and fewer crowds. Summer is peak season: more tourists, busier streets, but clear skies for sightseeing. Choose what suits you and book ahead for the best prices.

What to do when you arrive in Vienna

Vienna is the heart of classical music and imperial architecture. Don't miss Schönbrunn Palace, St. Stephen's Cathedral and the State Opera House. Stroll the old town, grab traditional Viennese coffee at a historic café, and explore the Danube. Vienna Airport is about 16 km from the centre; train, bus or taxi will get you there easily.
