Price and when to book

Prices start at €179 return. We've seen samples ranging from €179 to €213. The earlier you book, the better the rate usually is: don't miss it if you spot a low price. Check regularly if your dates are flexible.

Airline and stops

easyJet is your carrier on this route. Keep in mind the journey includes 1 stop, so it's not direct. That said, it's a factor that helps keep prices competitive.

Best time to travel

Tenerife is a year-round destination thanks to its mild climate. If you travel in low season (November to March, except holidays), prices tend to be tighter. Avoid December and school holidays if you're chasing the best deal.

What to do in Tenerife

You'll arrive at Tenerife North or South Airport depending on your connection. Don't miss Mount Teide, Spain's highest volcano. On the coast, there are black sand beaches, towns like Icod de los Vinos, or Siam Park if you're travelling with family. A week gives you time to explore both north and south.

From €179 return. It's the lowest price we've recorded. The median sits at €207.

How many days do I need in Tenerife?

7–10 days gives you plenty of time to explore beaches, hike Mount Teide, and visit traditional towns. With fewer days, focus on either the north or south.
