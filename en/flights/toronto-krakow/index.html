Price and when to book

The lowest price is €629 for a return flight. The median sits at €634, meaning half the available fares hover around that figure. We've seen prices up to €648. Book 2–3 weeks ahead to secure the best rate. Flights booked 1–2 months in advance are typically cheaper than last-minute bookings.

Airline and stopovers

This route is operated by SAS (Scandinavian Airlines). The outbound flight includes 1 stopover. Example: departure on 11 November at 17:10, arriving on 18 November at 10:15 on the return. Travel knowing you'll have a connection en route to Krakow.

Best time to travel

November and early December offer good weather without summer price peaks. Avoid July–August (peak prices) and the weeks around Christmas. Spring (April–May) is also worth considering for better weather. Travel outside school holidays to find better fares.

What to do in Krakow

The Market Square is the historic heart of the city. Don't miss Wawel Castle, St. Mary's Basilica and the Jewish Quarter. If you have time, nearby Auschwitz deserves a solemn visit. Local food is excellent and affordable. Krakow airport is 15 km from the centre with good public transport links.
