Fly from Gran Canaria to Cyprus from €209 return with Iberia Express. The median price sits at €233, so you'll find real deals below average. With one stopover, it's the most economical option to reach Larnaca.

Price and when to book

Flights start from €209 (return), though most hover around €233. We've seen prices reach €245 in the same search. The sweet spot is booking 2–3 months ahead: buy in July–August if you're travelling in October, or September–October for December trips. Prices spike in peak season (summer and Christmas holidays).

Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Iberia Express, which delivers solid value for the fare. Your outbound flight includes 1 stopover, so it's not direct. This intermediate stop is what makes these prices so competitive. The usual hub is a major European capital, but always confirm the routing when you book.

Best time to visit

Cyprus has sunshine year-round: mild winters (15–20°C) and hot summers (30–35°C). May to October is peak season and pricey; November to March is cheaper and quieter, though occasional rain. December and January prices rise for the holidays. January–February and October–November offer the best mix: pleasant weather and reasonable fares.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Larnaca (LCA), with easy connections into town. Visit Nicosia (the divided capital), the beaches of Limassol or Paphos, and Troodos (mountains and traditional villages). Don't skip archaeological sites like Kourion and Salamina. Eat souvlaki and halloumi; taste local wine. Bus and car hire are budget-friendly options.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Gran Canaria to Cyprus?

No, available flights have 1 stopover. That's why the price is so keen (from €209). There are no regular direct routes on this connection.

Which airline flies this route?

Iberia Express operates these flights. It's the low-cost brand within the Iberia group, with good ratings for basic baggage and tight pricing.

When is it cheapest to fly to Cyprus from Gran Canaria?

Prices drop in January–February and October–November (outside peak season). Book 2–3 months ahead to lock in the best rate. Avoid July, August, December and school holidays.

What's the main airport in Cyprus?

Larnaca (LCA) is the largest and where you'll arrive. It's 10 km from the city and well served by bus and taxi. Paphos (PFO) is the other option, but these flights land at Larnaca.
