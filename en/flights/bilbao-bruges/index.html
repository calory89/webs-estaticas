Price and when to book

Prices range from €108 to €184 in our sample. Book as early as possible: 2–3 months ahead usually offers the best availability for low fares. February is low season in Belgium, so fewer tourists and tighter pricing than summer.

Airline and connections

Brussels Airlines operates this route as a direct flight with no stops. Departure from Bilbao is in the afternoon, arriving in Brussels in the evening. It's the quickest and most practical way to reach Bruges.

Best time to visit

February and March are ideal for low prices and fewer crowds. April to May brings good weather and garden flowers. October is another excellent choice: mild temperatures and lighter tourism than summer. Avoid July and August if you want peace and quiet.

What to do when you arrive in Brussels

From Brussels airport, take a train or bus to Bruges (30 minutes). Once there: explore the Grand Square, take a boat tour through the canals, visit the Basilica of the Holy Blood, and sample Belgian beers and artisan chocolates. The old town is a UNESCO World Heritage site.
