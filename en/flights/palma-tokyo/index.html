Best time to visit

Tokyo shines in spring (March–April) and autumn (October–November), when weather is mild and crowds lighter. Winter (December–February) brings cold temperatures but clear skies, perfect for sightseeing. Summer is hot and humid. Check the booking calendar: flights outside peak season are often cheaper.

What to do when you arrive

Tokyo is endless: historic temples like Senso-ji, buzzing districts like Shibuya and Shinjuku, world-class museums and unbeatable food. Haneda Airport puts you 14 km from the centre; the Narita Express train connects well if you land at Narita. Plan 5–7 days minimum to make it count.
