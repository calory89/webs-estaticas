Best time to visit

Istanbul shines in spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October), with pleasant weather and less rain than winter. Summer is hot and crowded. For lower fares, travel in low season (November-February), though expect cooler temperatures.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Istanbul Airport (IST), north of the city centre. Head to the Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia and Topkapi Palace in Sultanahmet. Take a Bosphorus cruise, sip Turkish coffee at a çay bahçesi and haggle in the Grand Bazaar. Don't miss Balat's colourful houses and the views from Galata Tower.

What to see in Istanbul in 3-5 days?

Visit Sultanahmet (Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia, Hippodrome), the Bosphorus, Balat, Galata and the Grand Bazaar. If time allows, cross to the Asian side (Uskudar). Eat at a local waterfront restaurant and try Turkish street food.
