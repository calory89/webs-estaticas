Best time to visit

What to do when you arrive

Mexico City boasts world-class museums, exceptional food, and colonial architecture. Explore the Historic Centre, Paseo de la Reforma Avenue, and neighbourhoods like Coyoacán and La Roma. Frida Kahlo Museum, Metropolitan Cathedral, and traditional markets are must-sees. Mexico City International Airport (MEX) is 12 km from the centre with easy metro or taxi access.

What to see in Mexico City besides the Historic Centre?
