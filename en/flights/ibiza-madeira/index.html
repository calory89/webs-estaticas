Best time to travel

Madeira enjoys sun year-round. October to May brings pleasant weather for exploring without extreme heat. Spring (March–May) is especially gorgeous with flowering landscapes. Plan ahead to secure better prices through early booking.

What to do in Madeira

Funchal, the island's capital, blends colonial heritage with cosmopolitan buzz. Don't miss the levadas (clifftop hiking trails), viewpoints in Madeira National Park, and the cable car experience. Beach, trekking, local food and coastal villages round out an ideal 4-5 day escape.

What to see in Madeira in 5 days?
