Best time to travel

Cancún shines from November to April—dry weather, no hurricanes. December and January are peak season (and prices). Consider May, September or October for better fares. Avoid June to October, hurricane season.

What to do when you arrive

Cancún is your gateway to the Caribbean. White-sand beaches, cenotes, Mayan ruins like Chichén Itzá or Tulúm, and theme parks await. The airport is 25 km from the city centre: hire a car, take a shuttle or book a private transfer based on your plans.
