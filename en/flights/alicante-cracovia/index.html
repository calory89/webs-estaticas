Best time to visit

Krakow is beautiful in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winters are cold but the city has Christmas charm. Check prices regularly: fares vary by demand and seat availability.

Krakow airport is 15 km from the city centre. Explore the medieval Main Market Square, visit the Jewish quarter of Kazimierz, tour Wawel Castle, and consider a trip to Auschwitz. Polish cuisine is worth trying: sample pierniki and kielbasa at local markets.
