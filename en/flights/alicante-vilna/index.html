What to do when you arrive

Vilna blends medieval history with modern vibrancy. Explore the Old Town (Unesco World Heritage), climb Gediminas Tower for city views, and wander Uzupis, the bohemian quarter full of street art. Don't miss the Cathedral or the Church of St Peter and St Paul. Rent a bike—Vilna is very cycle-friendly.
