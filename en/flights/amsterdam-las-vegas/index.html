Price and when to book

Flights start from €800 return. Most searches cluster around €829, with examples up to €870. Don't leave it until the last moment: the earlier you book, the better fare you'll find. easyJet tends to fill seats quickly, especially during peak season.

Airline and layovers

easyJet is your carrier for this route. These flights include 2 layovers on the outbound journey, so it's not a direct service. The layovers add time to your trip, but they bring the final price down. Check your booking to see where the connections are made so you can plan your layover time.

Best time to travel

Las Vegas is a year-round destination for gambling, shows and dining. The winter season is popular thanks to mild weather, which beats the intense summer heat. Avoid peak tourist seasons if you're chasing lower fares and smaller crowds.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Harry Reid International Airport. Once in the city, the Strip is essential: casinos, the Bellagio fountains, Cirque du Soleil shows. It's also worth venturing out to see Red Rock Canyon or Valley of Fire. Eat at celebrity chef restaurants without breaking the bank inside the casino resorts.

FAQ
