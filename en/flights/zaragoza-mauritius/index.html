Best time to visit

Mauritius has a tropical climate. Avoid the rainy season (January to March). The driest months are May to September, ideal for beaches. Temperature hovers around 25–30°C year-round. Travel in European winter (December to February) for warmth, but expect more tourists and slightly higher prices.

What to do in Mauritius

White-sand beaches at Beau Bassin and Trou aux Biches, coral reef diving, and hiking in Black River Gorges National Park. Port Louis's old town is worth a day trip. Don't miss Chamarel waterfall or Île aux Cerfs. Local food blends Indian, French, and African influences.
