Best time to visit

Puerto Rico enjoys warm weather year-round. If you prefer to avoid intense heat, November to April tends to be more pleasant. Keep in mind that hurricane season runs June to November, though it's less frequent on the island. Travelling in shoulder season (May–October) may offer slightly better rates on accommodation and activities.

What to do upon arrival

San Juan is Puerto Rico's capital and offers plenty to explore: Old San Juan with its colourful colonial streets, historic forts, nearby beaches and a vibrant food scene. From Luis Muñoz Marín Airport (SJU) you're close to the city centre. Enhance your trip by visiting Flamenco Beach (Culebra) or exploring El Yunque, the tropical rainforest. Don't miss Paseo de la Princesa at sunset.
