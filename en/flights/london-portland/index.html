Best time to visit

November is ideal: summer tourism has passed, winter rain hasn't fully arrived, and Oregon shows autumn colours. Prices are lower than in summer or the Christmas holiday period. March to May (spring) is also attractive if you prefer more daylight, though fares rise then.

What to do when you arrive in Portland

Portland is known for its cultural scene, independent cafés, bookshops and urban parks like Forest Park. Visit the Portland Art Museum, Powell's City of Books or the Oregon Coast (90 minutes away). Portland International Airport (PDX) is 16 km from the centre; buses and taxis are readily available. A great base to explore Oregon.
