Best time to visit

February is a good window: escape the Canary winter and reach Bruges when prices haven't spiked. March to May offers milder weather in Belgium. To dodge crowds, travel midweek. Prices tend to climb during cruise season (May–September) and European public holidays.

What to do when you arrive

Bruges is pure medieval: the UNESCO-listed old town with canals, squares and Gothic architecture. Walk the Markt, climb Belfort Tower. Eat Belgian waffles (called 'gaufres') on every corner, try Trappist beers. From Bruges, it's easy to reach Antwerp by train (20 mins) or the coast at Ostend (1 hour). A weekend covers the basics; 3–4 days lets you enjoy it at ease.
