Fly from Ibiza to Maldives from €824 return. Median price sits at €921, so you have room to spot good deals. With two stops and Iberia Express, this distant destination is within reach.

Price and when to book

Lowest price recorded is €824, though median hovers around €921. For such a long route, booking 2–3 months ahead typically yields better fares. Prices shift depending on departure and return dates, so compare a few days to find your best option.

Airline and stops

You'll fly with Iberia Express on a route with 2 stops. It's not direct, but this setup is standard for intercontinental routes from Ibiza. The stops lengthen your journey but unlock competitive pricing. You'll arrive at Malé (MLE), Maldives' main airport.

Best time to travel

Maldives is a beach and diving destination year-round. Dry season (November to April) is most popular and tends to be pricier. To save, consider May to October, though it rains more. Note that January hits peak season: book further ahead if travelling then.

What to do when you arrive

Malé, the capital, is your entry point. From there you'll connect by boat or seaplane to island resorts. The archipelago is famous for white-sand beaches, snorkelling and world-class diving. On your first trip, explore the city, then unwind on the islands.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Ibiza to Maldives?

No, this route includes 2 stops. Direct flights from Spain to Maldives don't exist; connections are standard even from larger cities.

Which airline operates this route?

Iberia Express. It's the carrier recorded for these prices from Ibiza to Maldives.

Can I find cheaper flights?

The lowest price is €824. Fares shift with dates; compare different departures and return dates on flight search engines to find the best deal.

What is there in Maldives besides beaches?

Diving, snorkelling on coral reefs, resort island hopping, and the capital Malé with local markets. It's primarily a beach and marine nature destination.
