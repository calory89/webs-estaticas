Best time to travel

Nassau and the Bahamas are a year-round destination, but November to April is the dry season with stable weather. Summer (June to October) has higher hurricane risk and is low tourist season. November specifically offers good weather without peak winter visitor crowds.

What to do on arrival

Nassau is your gateway to Bahamas beaches. Lynden Pindling International Airport is 8 km from the centre. Visit Paradise Island, relax at Cable Beach, explore historic downtown, or take a ferry to nearby Exuma or Eleuthera. Turquoise waters and local food are the main draws.

What is there to visit in Nassau?

Paradise Island with resorts and beaches, Cable Beach for relaxation, the colourful historic centre and the harbour. From Nassau you can take excursions to nearby islands such as Exuma (famous for white sand beaches) or go snorkelling. The tropical climate and crystal-clear water are the main attractions.
