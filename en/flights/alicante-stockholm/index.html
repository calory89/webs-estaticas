Best time to visit

Stockholm is stunning in winter if you fancy the cold and short days, but spring and autumn offer better weather and more daylight. Avoid July and August if you're after low prices: it's peak season. February to April and September to October tend to be more affordable.

What to do when you arrive

Stockholm deserves at least 3 days. Stroll through Gamla Stan (the old town), climb the City Hall for panoramic views, visit the Vasa Museum, and relax in one of its parks. The airport (Arlanda) is 20 km from the city centre; bus, train, or taxi get you there easily.
