Best time to travel

Orlando is a destination year-round, but flight prices drop outside school holiday peaks (July-August and Christmas-January). Spring (March-May) and autumn (September-October) offer good weather with fewer crowds and cheaper fares. Avoid peak season if you want to save on flights.

What to do when you arrive

Orlando is theme park heaven: Disney World, Universal Studios and SeaWorld are the top attractions. Beyond parks, Florida's beaches are just over an hour away, the Everglades offers nature and wildlife, and outlet malls are perfect for shopping. MCO airport is 25 km from the centre, well served by public transport and car hire options.
