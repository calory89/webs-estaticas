Price and when to book

Flights start from €132 return, though most hover around €177. Booking at least 8 weeks ahead gives you better odds of finding a cheap fare. The earlier you purchase, the more low-cost options you'll find.

Airline and stopovers

Iberia Airlines operates this route with one stopover. There are no direct SCQ-BRU flights, so expect 2 to 4 extra hours compared to a direct flight. The stopover is brief and well-connected from Santiago.

Best time to travel

Brussels is ideal between April and October when the climate is mild and daylight abundant. Avoid December and January if you're after low prices—holidays inflate ticket costs. The most competitive fares appear in shoulder season (May, September).

What to do when you arrive

Brussels is small and easy to explore on foot. Grand Place is essential, plus the Atomium, art museums and Belgian breweries. Eat local waffles and chocolate. Brussels-Zaventem airport is 12 km from the centre, well served by train and bus.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Santiago to Brussels?

No. Iberia Airlines operates this route with one stopover. Direct flights between these two airports don't exist.

What's the best time to fly cheap to Brussels?

Shoulder season, especially May and September, offers cheaper flights than summer and Christmas. April and October also have good prices and pleasant weather.

Grand Place, Atomium, Sablon neighbourhood, markets, museums and local food (waffles, chocolate, beers). Everything is nearby and the city is compact.
