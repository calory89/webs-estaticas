Best time to visit

Warsaw shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather and fewer crowds. Winter (December–February) is cold but atmospheric. Summer (June–August) is peak season with more tourists. Choose based on your weather preferences and what you want to do.

What to do when you arrive

Warsaw is your gateway to Polish culture. Explore the Old Town (UNESCO World Heritage site), the Warsaw Uprising Museum, the Palace of Culture and Science, or walk along the Vistula riverside. Local food is affordable and tasty. Many travellers go on to Krakow, Gdańsk or Auschwitz from here.
