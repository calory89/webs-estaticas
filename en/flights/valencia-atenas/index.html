Fly from Valencia to Athens for less than you'd expect: from €130 return. Median price sits around €166, so you have affordable options if you search well. With one stop, you'll reach Greece's capital quickly.

Price and when to book

The lowest price found is €130 for the complete journey. Data shows typical fares range between €130 and €172. The key is booking in advance: the earlier you purchase, the better rates you'll see. October, November, March and April usually offer good price-to-availability ratios. Avoid dates around Easter and summer if you're chasing low fares.

Airline and stops

You'll fly with Wizz Air Malta on this route. The journey includes 1 stop, so it's not direct, but total travel time is reasonable. Wizz Air is a budget carrier known for competitive pricing. Check baggage policies before buying: you might need to pay extra for an oversized cabin bag or checked luggage.

Best time to visit Athens

Athens shines in spring (March-May) and autumn (September-November), when weather is mild and crowds are lighter. Summer months (July-August) are very hot. Winter is cheaper but can be rainy. If you want low price plus decent weather, aim for April, May, October or November.

What to do when you arrive

Athens airport is about 30 km from the centre; bus, metro and taxi are available. In the city, don't miss the Acropolis and Parthenon, the ancient agora, the Acropolis Museum and Plaka. Eat at a traditional taverna, explore the Anáfiotika neighbourhood and take a ferry to nearby islands like Hydra or Aegina if you have extra days.
