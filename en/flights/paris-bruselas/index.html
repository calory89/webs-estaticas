Best time to visit

Brussels is lovely year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: mild weather, less rain, fewer crowds. Avoid December if you want low prices; it draws holiday shoppers and festive tourists. Winter is grey but often cheaper if weather doesn't bother you.

What to do when you arrive

Brussels deserves 2–3 days: the Grand Place is unmissable, museums are excellent, and food will surprise you. Belgian beers, chocolate, and waffles are on every corner. From Brussels-Zaventem Airport, direct transport reaches the city centre (bus, train). It's a short but dense destination, perfect for a European weekend.
