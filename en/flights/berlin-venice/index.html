Fly from Berlin to Venice from just €44 return. With Ryanair you get direct options and prices averaging around €55. Book in advance to lock in the best fares.

Price and when to book your flight

The lowest price we found is €44 return, though the average sits at €55. You'll also find options at €115 depending on your dates. Book as far in advance as possible: cheap flights sell out quickly, especially during peak season. We recommend reserving 4 to 8 weeks ahead for the best prices.

Airline and flight type

Ryanair is your carrier on this route, operating direct flights with no stops. This means you travel from Berlin to Venice without intermediate landings, saving time and hassle. Bear in mind Ryanair charges for checked baggage and carry-on luggage, so review the baggage policy before booking.

Best time to visit

Venice is beautiful year-round, but avoid peak cruise season (July-August) if you want lower prices and fewer crowds. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer the best balance of pleasant weather and fewer tourists. Winter is cheaper but greyer and damper.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Marco Polo Airport, 13 km from the city centre. Take a bus, vaporetto (water bus) or water taxi into town. Once there, visit St Mark's Basilica, the Doge's Palace, and take a gondola ride through the canals. Eat at restaurants away from St Mark's Square for better value. Day trips to the Lido and the islands of Murano and Burano are a must.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for Berlin-Venice flights?

The lowest price we found is €44 return. The average hovers around €55. We also recorded a fare at €115. Prices depend entirely on your travel dates and how far ahead you book.

Does Ryanair fly direct from Berlin to Venice?

Yes, Ryanair operates this route non-stop. It departs Berlin Airport (BER) and arrives directly at Venice Marco Polo Airport (VCE).

When should I book flights to Venice?

Reserve as far in advance as you can, ideally 4-8 weeks before travel. This gives you the best chance of finding cheap fares like €44 or €55. Prices tend to rise as your departure date approaches.

What can I do in Venice?

Visit St Mark's Basilica, the Doge's Palace, explore the canals, take a gondola ride, and day-trip to Murano (famous for glass) and Burano (famous for lace). Try local specialities like squid risotto and cicheti (Venetian tapas).
