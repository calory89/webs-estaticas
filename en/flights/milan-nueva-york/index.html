Best time to visit

New York is worth visiting year-round. Winter (January–February) sees fewer tourists and may offer better flight prices. Spring and autumn shine for climate and experience. Avoid Christmas and New Year weeks if budget is tight.

Walk the streets, grab street food and tick off your must-sees. You'll land in the New York metropolitan area, the main east coast hub.
