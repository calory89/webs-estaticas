Best time to visit

Bucharest is attractive year-round. Autumn and spring offer good weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is ideal if you prefer cooler temperatures and festive charm. Avoid July and August if you prefer fewer tourists.

What to do in Bucharest

Don't miss the Palace of Parliament, Europe's largest administrative building. Stroll through the Old Town (Lipscani) with its period cafés and shops. Visit museums like the National History Museum or Art Museum. Enjoy local food: ciorbă, mici and sarmale. The city has vibrant nightlife and is perfect for a European weekend break.
