Best time to travel

Bogotá is a year-round destination with mild, mountain-altitude weather. December to March and July to August are drier. Flying in low season (May, June, September, October) brings lower prices and fewer crowds. Plan according to your dates and compare prices within the booking window we provided.

What to do when you arrive

Bogotá awaits with its historic centre, the Gold Museum and La Candelaria's nightlife. Monserrate and the hills of Usaquén offer city views worth the trip. Extra days? Guatavita and Zipaquirá day trips are worthwhile. El Dorado airport is 13 km from downtown; taxis and buses get you there easily.
