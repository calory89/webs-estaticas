Best time to visit

Tokyo is a year-round destination. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) are ideal: mild weather and less rain. Winter brings cold temperatures but clear skies; summer is hot and humid. Factor in your budget and what experience suits you best before choosing dates.

What to do when you arrive

Tokyo offers ancient temples like Senso-ji, modern districts such as Shibuya, museums and world-class dining. Haneda airport (TYO) has direct train links to the city centre. Consider day trips to Kyoto or Osaka. Explore by neighbourhood to make the most of your time.
