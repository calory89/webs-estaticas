Best time to visit Palermo

Sicily is appealing nearly year-round. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer great weather without summer crowds. If you want warm beaches, July and August are ideal but busier. Winter (December-February) sees fewer tourists and potentially lower prices, though daylight hours are shorter.

What to do when you arrive in Palermo

Palermo Airport (Falcone-Borsellino) is about 35 km from the city centre. Once there, visit the Cathedral, Palazzo dei Normanni, and traditional markets like Ballarò and Capo. Local food is essential: try arancini, panelle, and pasta con le sardine. You can also explore Mondello Beach or take day trips to Monreale and Mondello.
