Airline and stops

easyJet operates this route non-stop: no changes or connections. Departure from Manchester around 06:30, arrival in Munich around 10:40 (local time). Flight time is just over two hours, perfect to make the most of your day once you land.

Best time to visit

Munich is lovely year-round. Autumn (September–October) and spring (April–May) bring pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. December is special for Christmas markets, though demand peaks then. Low season (January–February) may offer better fares if your outbound date is flexible.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Munich airport (MUC), 28 km from the city centre. Train or bus takes you into town in 40–45 minutes. Visit Marienplatz and the Cathedral of Our Lady, explore the English Garden, grab a beer at a traditional beer hall, and if you're there in winter, catch the Christmas markets. The city centre is very walkable.
