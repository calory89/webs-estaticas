Price and when to book

Flights found range from €72 to €88 return. For this route, the smartest move is to book 4-6 weeks in advance: Ryanair tends to push prices up as departure approaches. Check the same day of the week you plan to travel, as prices sometimes vary between weekdays and weekends.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight. You'll depart from Málaga without stops and arrive straight in Copenhagen. No layovers, less time wasted and easier with luggage. Check your booking for Ryanair's baggage policy: they charge extra for oversized items.

Best time to visit

Copenhagen shines in summer (June-August) if you want canal walks and outdoor life, but prices spike. Spring (April-May) offers good weather without crowds. Winter is cheaper, especially November to January, though it's cold. Autumn is also affordable and sees less rain than winter.

What to do when you arrive

Copenhagen airport is 8 km from the city centre; metro gets you there in 13 minutes. Don't miss Nyhavn with its coloured townhouses, Rosenborg Castle, the bohemian Christiania neighbourhood and Strøget's cafés. Rent a bike: it's the local way around. The city is pricey for restaurants, but eat at markets and local cafés to save money.
