Best time to visit

Mykonos is a summer destination, but it also attracts visitors in spring and early autumn. July and August see more tourism and higher prices. If you want beach access, good air connections and fewer crowds, consider June or September. Winter reduces flight availability and island activities.

What to do when you arrive

Mykonos airport (JMK) is 2 km from town. Mykonos is known for its beaches (Psarou, Paradise), nightlife and Cycladic architecture in the villages. It's also a gateway to Delos, a Greek archaeological site worth visiting. Eat at local tavernas and watch the sunset from the windmills area.

FAQ

Can I find cheaper flights than €240?

€240 is the lowest price recorded in this sample. It's hard to go lower, though date changes or frequent searches may reveal occasional offers. Set up price alerts if you have flexible dates.

How many stops does Wizz Air Malta have to Mykonos?

The outbound flight has 2 stops. It's not direct. Stopovers are normal on this route and don't affect the final price, which remains competitive even with connections.

What is the airport code for Mykonos?

The airport code is JMK (Mykonos International 'John Mykonios'). It's the island's only commercial airport, small but well connected. Buses and taxis run from there to the town centre.

What can I do in Mykonos besides the beach?

Mykonos offers nightlife in Little Venice, Cycladic architecture in the old town, famous beaches like Paradise and Psarou, and access to Delos (ancient ruins). There are also sunset bars, local restaurants and shopping at the port.
