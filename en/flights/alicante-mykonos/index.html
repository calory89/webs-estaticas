Price and when to book

The most affordable flights hover around €246, though typical prices land at €253. We also found options at €278. For this route, booking 6-8 weeks ahead usually gives the best value. Avoid buying in the final week before departure if you want a better deal.

Airline and connections

Vueling operates this route with 2 stops, so it's not a direct flight. Total journey time depends on where the layovers occur. If you prefer fewer stops, check other options, though connecting flights often come with lower fares.

Best time to visit

Mykonos is hugely popular in summer when prices spike. To save money, consider May–June or September–October, when the weather is still excellent and crowds are lighter. Winter is cheaper but has shorter daylight hours.

What to do when you arrive

Mykonos airport is around 2 km from the port and town centre. Don't miss Psarou and Paradise beaches, Little Venice, the windmills, and the nightlife. The island blends beaches, Greek food, and a vibrant atmosphere perfectly.
