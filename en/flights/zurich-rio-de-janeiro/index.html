Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €860 return. The median sits at €864, giving you a realistic idea of what you'll pay. In our sample, prices range from €860 to €1,287, with considerable variation. Book as early as possible: these long-haul flights tend to climb in price as departure approaches. Two months ahead is a solid benchmark.

Airline and stopovers

You'll travel with Air France, which operates this route with 1 stopover. It's not direct, but it's what's available. The full journey is quite long (Zurich to Rio crosses the Atlantic), so the stopover is standard on this route. Air France connects well from Zurich, typically with a stop in Paris or another hub.

Best time to visit

Rio de Janeiro is a year-round destination, but keep in mind that December to March is summer in Brazil (heat, humidity, tropical rain). April to October is Rio's winter (drier, pleasant temperatures of 20-25°C). Prices can fluctuate with local holidays and European school breaks. Check the calendar before fixing your dates.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Tom Jobim International Airport (RIO), about 20 km from the city centre. From there, central Rio is accessible by bus, metro or taxi. Beaches like Copacabana and Ipanema, Christ the Redeemer, Sugarloaf Mountain and Guanabara Bay are must-sees. Spend time wandering historic neighbourhoods like Lapa. If travelling in summer, pack sunscreen; if in winter, bring a light jacket for the evenings.
