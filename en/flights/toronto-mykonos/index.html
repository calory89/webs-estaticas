Price and when to book

The cheapest flight is €804 return. Prices in the sample range from €804 to €916, with a median of €814. Book as far ahead as you can: the sooner you secure your ticket, the better your chances of locking in a low fare. Avoid last-minute decisions; supply shrinks and prices climb.

Airline and stops

You'll fly with SAS (Scandinavian Airlines). The journey includes 2 stops, so it's not a direct flight. This is standard for long-haul intercontinental routes. Total journey time will depend on where SAS routes the stops, but expect a trip lasting several hours.

Best time to visit

Mykonos in November (like the 10 November example) is low season: fewer tourists, cheaper hotels and restaurants, mild weather but occasional rain. If you want guaranteed beach and heat, go in summer, but expect to pay more for both flights and accommodation. October and November offer the sweet spot.

What to do when you arrive

Mykonos is famous for white-sand beaches (Psarou, Paraga), whitewashed villages with narrow lanes and nightlife. Visit the Church of Panagia Paraportiani, stroll Chora's harbour at sunset or take a boat to nearby Delos. Eat at seaside tavernas and catch the sunset from the old windmills.

FAQ

Is the Toronto to Mykonos flight direct?

No, it includes 2 stops. SAS operates this route with intermediate stops, so expect several hours of total journey time.

What's the lowest price you found?

€804 return is the cheapest in the sample. The median sits at €814, so there are options in that range if you're flexible with dates.

When is the best season to visit Mykonos?

October and November are ideal: less crowding than summer, pleasant weather and lower prices. For guaranteed beach and heat, go June to September, though everything will be pricier.

What can I see and do in Mykonos?

White-sand beaches, the whitewashed town of Chora with its winding lanes, the Church of Panagia Paraportiani, historic windmills, boat trips to Delos, Greek food at the harbour and nightlife. There's something for everyone.
