Best time to visit

Madeira is a year-round destination, but October to March offers pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Avoid Spanish school holidays if you want lower fares. Summer months (July-August) tend to be pricier. Check real-time price calendars for your specific travel dates.

What to do when you arrive

Madeira's airport is in Funchal, the capital. The island is perfect for hiking, quiet beaches and dramatic cliffs. Walk the levadas (ancient water channels), visit the Botanical Garden, try espetada (local grilled fish) and explore local cuisine. Rent a car to explore freely or use public transport.
