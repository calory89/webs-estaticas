Price and when to buy

The lowest recorded price is €68 return. The median stands at €83, with occasional deals up to €89. For these bargain fares, book well in advance: 4 to 8 weeks before departure is typically the sweet spot. The earlier you purchase, the fewer surprises at checkout.

Airline and connections

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight. Departure from London around 12:10 and return from Vilna around 05:55. No stops means less hassle, shorter transit time and straight to your destination. Perfect for a long weekend or quick getaway.

Best time to visit

Vilna shines year-round, but autumn (September–October) and spring (April–May) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Winter is magical if you fancy snow and Christmas markets. Summer is warm but busier. Prices fluctuate with season, so check fares regularly.

What to do on arrival

Vilna International Airport (VNO) is just 4 km from the city centre. The Old Town is a Unesco World Heritage site: wander the squares, visit the Cathedral and Gediminas Tower. The artsy Užupis district is packed with bars and galleries. Enjoy authentic Lithuanian cuisine anywhere without breaking the budget.
