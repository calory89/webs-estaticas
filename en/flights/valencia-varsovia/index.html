Best time to visit

Warsaw is appealing year-round. May to September offer the best weather for exploring the city and surroundings. Winter (December to February) is cold, but the city has Christmas charm. Avoid peak season if you're looking for lower prices.

What to do when you arrive in Warsaw

Chopin Airport (WAW) is 10 km from the city centre. Once there, explore the restored Old Town (UNESCO World Heritage), the Palace of Culture and Science, and the Jewish Quarter. Warsaw offers quality museums, vibrant nightlife and authentic Polish food at very reasonable prices.

FAQ

What to see in Warsaw?

The restored Old Town, the Palace of Culture and Science, Second World War museums and the Jewish Quarter are essential. The city is very walkable and public transport is efficient.
