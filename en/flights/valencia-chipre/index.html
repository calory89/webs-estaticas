Price and when to book

Tickets from Valencia to Cyprus start from €159 return, though most searches hover around €174-176. Book as early as possible: the sooner you purchase, the better fare you'll secure. Avoid booking in the final weeks before travel, when prices rise significantly.

Airline and stopovers

You'll travel with Wizz Air, which connects Valencia to Larnaca via one stopover. It's not a direct flight, but it's the most economical option for this route. Wizz Air is a Hungarian budget airline that balances price with service quality. Expect one intermediate stop on your journey.

Best time to visit

Cyprus enjoys warm weather almost year-round. Spring (March-May) and autumn (September-November) are ideal: great weather and fewer crowds compared to summer. If you choose winter, you might find lower fares, though daylight hours will be shorter. Book well in advance regardless of season.

What to do when you arrive in Larnaca

Larnaca is your gateway to Cyprus. Explore Larnaca Beach, stroll along the seafront promenade, and visit the Church of Saint Lazarus. From here you can take excursions to Nicosia, the beaches of Ayia Napa, or villages in the Troodos mountains. The airport has good public transport and taxi connections to the city centre.
