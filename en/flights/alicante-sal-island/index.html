Best time to visit Sal Island

Sal enjoys warm, dry weather year-round, perfect for beaches. Rainfall is minimal. Check the calendar before you go to avoid periods of strong winds if you're planning diving or windsurfing. The island has no heavy peak season, so choose based on your availability.

What to do when you arrive at Sal Island

Sal is small but packed: stunning beaches at Santa Maria and Ponta Leme, an abandoned shipwreck at Buracona (natural pool with blue water), and the salt lagoon where you float effortlessly. The international airport (SID) is in Praia, close to everything. Eat fresh seafood on the coast and try local Creole food. Rent a car or use shared taxis to get around.

What's there to see on Sal Island besides beaches?
