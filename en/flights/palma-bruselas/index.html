Best time to visit

Brussels is accessible year-round. April to May and September to October offer pleasant weather with fewer crowds than summer. Winter is colder but ideal if you want lower prices and to enjoy the Christmas decorations in December.

Things to do in Brussels

Belgium's capital won't disappoint: the Grand Place is a must-see, the Royal Palace is worth visiting, and the medieval old town around it is perfect for wandering. Eat waffles, taste Belgian beer and explore the historic centre. Museums and art galleries play an important role in the city's cultural life.
