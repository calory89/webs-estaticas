Best time to visit

February offers low prices. Sofia in winter is cold but perfect if you want fewer tourists and authentic city vibes. Spring (April–May) brings better weather. Skip July and August if you want to avoid heat and peak season crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Sofia is compact and walkable. Visit Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, the Basilica of Sofia (UNESCO site), and the bohemian Vitosha neighbourhood. Eat proper Bulgarian food in local taverns. Day trips to Rila Monastery or the Rila mountains are easy. The airport is 10 km from the city centre; taxis or buses get you there quickly.
