Best time to visit Miami

November is an excellent month for Miami: pleasant temperatures (25–28°C), less rainfall than summer, and it's not yet peak cruise season. December and January are pricier due to European winter holidays. For lower fares, travel outside Spanish school holidays and bank holidays.

What to do when you arrive in Miami

Miami offers beaches at Miami Beach, nightlife in South Beach, street art at the Wynwood Walls, shopping in the Design District, and Cuban food in Little Havana. Miami International Airport (MIA) is 30 km from the centre; use Metrorail, taxi, or car hire to get around.
