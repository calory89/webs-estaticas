Best time to visit

Hong Kong has a subtropical climate. October to November brings pleasant temperatures and less rainfall, perfect for sightseeing. Skip summer (June to August) due to extreme heat and humidity, and typhoon season. December to February is also pleasant, though prices may be higher.

What to do when you arrive

Don't miss Victoria Harbour, temples like Man Mo, and traditional markets such as the Bird Market. Victoria Peak offers spectacular views. You can also explore nearby islands by ferry, wander colonial neighbourhoods, or savour local cuisine at street stalls and dim sum restaurants.
