Price and when to book

The lowest price is €533 for return flights. Prices we've seen range from €533 to €653, with a median of €544. Airline and stops

You'll fly with Etihad Airways, which offers a good balance between comfort and price. The outbound flight includes 1 stop, so it's not direct. This is normal for this route: direct flights from Dublin to Singapore are rare. Etihad will get you to Singapore with a layover in between, which extends the journey but reduces the cost.

Best time to visit

Singapore is a tropical destination with little seasonal variation. The best time is November to February, when it's cooler and there's less rain. These months also align with lower prices if you book ahead. Avoid September and October, when monsoon season is stronger. Travelling outside peak season (July-August) will also save you money.

What to do in Singapore

Singapore is an unmissable city-state: Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay, Chinatown, and Little India are must-sees. Eat at hawker centres (cheap street food markets), visit the Menara Tower observation deck, and explore Sentosa island. The airport (Changi) is well-connected and public transport is excellent. Three to four days are enough to see the main sights.
