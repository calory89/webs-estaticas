Best time to visit

Naples is lovely year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. For lower prices, skip July and August. December can surprise you: less beach tourism and quieter city streets.

What to do in Naples

Naples is the gateway to Campania. Visit the National Archaeological Museum, the Cathedral of San Gennaro, and the UNESCO-listed old town. Excursions to Pompeii, Herculaneum, and the Bay of Naples depart from here. Neapolitan pizza tastes best nowhere else: eat it in Spaccanapoli, the pulse of the centre.
