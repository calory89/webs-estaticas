Best time to travel

Dubai offers pleasant weather from November to March, avoiding peak summer heat. Mexico City is best visited during spring and autumn (March to May and September to November). Plan your trip outside peak season to secure better fares and avoid crowds at your destination.

What to do when you arrive in Mexico City

The capital is unmissable: Historic Centre, Zócalo, National Palace, Templo Mayor. Don't skip the museums (Frida Kahlo, Museum of Anthropology), Coyoacán and Xochimilco with its traditional boats. The food scene is world-class. From the airport, public and private transport take you to the centre, about 30 km away.
