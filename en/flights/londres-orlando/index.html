Best time to visit

Orlando is a year-round destination. The quieter months—and potentially cheaper fares outside peak season—are May to August and September to November. January and December draw heavy crowds. Hurricane season in Florida runs June to November, but Orlando sits inland and isn't directly affected. Pick based on your weather and crowd preferences.

What to do when you arrive

Orlando is the gateway to Disney World, Universal Studios, Islands of Adventure and the world's biggest theme parks. If you prefer nature, the Everglades and natural springs are within reach. Beaches on the coast—Cocoa Beach, Clearwater—are 1–2 hours by car. Orlando International Airport (MCO) is 25 km from the city centre. Rent a car, book a transfer or use transport services to get around.
