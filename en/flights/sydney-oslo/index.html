Best time to travel

Oslo in autumn (September–October) offers clear days and mild temperatures. Winter (November–February) brings snow and the Nordic concept of 'koselig', but with fewer daylight hours. Spring (April–May) and summer (June–August) are peak season with higher prices. If you're looking for savings without extreme cold, autumn is your sweet spot.

What to do when you arrive in Oslo

Oslo Gardermoen Airport (OSL) is 50 km from the city centre. Once in the city, don't miss Oslo City Hall, the Opera House, the Munch Museum, and nearby fjords. Strolling through Grünerløkka and sampling Nordic cuisine are must-dos. From Oslo, you can also explore further north into the Norwegian countryside via efficient public transport.
