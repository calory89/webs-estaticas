Price and when to book

The lowest price we've seen is €48 return, with a median of €56. We've tracked fares ranging from €48 to €64. Booking well in advance remains your best bet: the earlier you purchase, the higher your chances of snagging a good rate. Check regularly if your trip is coming up soon.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight. No stopovers, no plane changes, no waiting around. You'll travel without interruption. Example: departure from Rome at 21:15, arrival in Marrakech at 06:05 (local time).

Best time to visit

Marrakech is pleasant almost year-round. Autumn and winter months (October to March) offer moderate temperatures, perfect for walking through the medina without extreme heat. Spring is also excellent. Avoid July and August if you don't handle intense desert heat well.

What to do when you arrive

Marrakech airport (Menara) is 6 km from the centre. In the medina, don't miss Jamaa El Fna Square, the souks, the Bahia Palace and the Menara Gardens. From the city centre you can also take day trips to the Atlas Mountains, Ouarzazate or the Sahara Desert.

FAQ

Is the Rome-Marrakech flight really direct?

Yes, Ryanair flies direct between Rome and Marrakech with no stopovers.

Which airport do flights depart from in Rome?

Flights on this route depart from Rome's airports (code ROM, which covers Fiumicino and Ciampino). Check your booking to see which one you're using.

Is €48 the final price or are there taxes to add?

The price from €48 is a guide. Ryanair adds airport taxes and other charges during checkout, so your final total will be higher. Use our prices as a reference for comparison, but always confirm on the airline's website.

What can you do in Marrakech in a few days?

Three to four days is enough for the medina, Jamaa El Fna Square, the souks and gardens. With more time, add a trip to the Atlas Mountains or the desert.
