Best time to visit

Prague is beautiful year-round, but autumn (September–October) and spring (April–May) offer pleasant weather without summer crowds. Low-season prices tend to be more competitive, though it depends on your exact travel dates.

What to do when you arrive

Václav Havel Airport is 17 km from the city centre. Reach it by train, bus, or taxi. In the Old Town, don't miss Old Town Square, the Astronomical Clock, Charles Bridge, and Prague Castle. Czech beer and local cuisine are a must.
