Price and when to book

Vienna-Hamburg flights start from €121 (return), though you'll typically pay between €121 and €152. There's no fixed rule about which day prices drop lowest, but booking in advance usually works better than last-minute. Check across several days to spot trends.

Airline and stops

Eurowings operates this route as a direct flight with no stops. The sample flight departs Vienna at 08:45 and arrives in Hamburg early enough to make the most of your day. Direct flights like this are comfortable and save time waiting around.

Best time to visit

Hamburg is worth visiting year-round. Summer (June–August) is peak season with higher prices and more tourists. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer good weather without summer peaks. Winter is cheaper, though it rains more.

What to do when you arrive

Hamburg is a port city with plenty to explore: the Hafen (harbour) is essential, Reeperbahn for nightlife, historic churches like Michaeliskirche, and varied museums. Public transport is reliable, so getting from the airport to the city centre is straightforward.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for Vienna-Hamburg flights?

€121 return with Eurowings. It's the lowest registered, but the median is €132, so keep an eye out for that fare.

Is the Vienna-Hamburg flight direct?

Yes, completely direct with no stops. Eurowings operates it that way, making your journey more comfortable and quick.

Eurowings operates this route. Check their website or flight comparison sites directly for schedules and availability.

What can I see in Hamburg?

The harbour is the most-visited area, especially for walks and sunsets. Michaeliskirche offers tower views, there are art and history museums, and Reeperbahn has nightlife and entertainment.
