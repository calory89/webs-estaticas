Price and when to book

The lowest price found is €835 return. The median stands at €909, so expect fares between €835 and €1,443. As a rule of thumb, book 2–3 months ahead to access the best rates. The earlier you reserve, the better the deal.

Airlines and stops

TAP Portugal is your option with 2 stops on the outbound. The journey takes longer than a direct flight, but the savings are significant. Connections typically happen in Lisbon, TAP's main hub. Always check your connection times: with two stops, allow at least 2–3 hours between flights.

Best time to travel

Rio de Janeiro is a year-round destination, but avoid December–February (Brazilian summer and Carnival season) if you want fewer crowds and lower fares. November–October and May–September offer better value. Mid-season typically brings more competitive pricing.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Rio's International Airport (Galeão). From there, reach the city centre by bus, taxi or rental car. Rio won't disappoint: Copacabana Beach, Sugarloaf Mountain, Christ the Redeemer, Guanabara Bay. The city is vibrant with excellent food and nightlife. Plan at least 3–4 days.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Munich to Rio de Janeiro?

No. With TAP Portugal you have 2 stops. These are the most common on this route. Direct flights from Munich don't exist or are extremely rare; most connections go via Lisbon or other European hubs.

What is the cheapest price found?

€835 return. This is the minimum in the sample; the median sits around €909. Some fares reach €1,443 depending on dates and availability.

Which airline operates this route?

TAP Portugal. It's a reliable carrier with strong presence in Brazil. Always check baggage policies and change rules according to your fare type.

What should I see in Rio de Janeiro?

Christ the Redeemer (cable car from Corcovado), Sugarloaf Mountain, Copacabana and Ipanema beaches, Botanical Garden, and Lapa neighbourhood. Dedicate 4–5 days minimum to enjoy it properly.
