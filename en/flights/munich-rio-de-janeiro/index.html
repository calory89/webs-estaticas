Airlines and stops

TAP Portugal is your option with 2 stops on the outbound. The journey takes longer than a direct flight, but the savings are significant. Connections typically happen in Lisbon, TAP's main hub. Always check your connection times: with two stops, allow at least 2–3 hours between flights.

Best time to travel

Rio de Janeiro is a year-round destination, but avoid December–February (Brazilian summer and Carnival season) if you want fewer crowds and lower fares. November–October and May–September offer better value. Mid-season typically brings more competitive pricing.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Rio's International Airport (Galeão). From there, reach the city centre by bus, taxi or rental car. Rio won't disappoint: Copacabana Beach, Sugarloaf Mountain, Christ the Redeemer, Guanabara Bay. The city is vibrant with excellent food and nightlife. Plan at least 3–4 days.
