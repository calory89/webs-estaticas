Best time to travel

Vietnam has a tropical climate. October to April is best: less heat and less rain. May to September is very humid and wet. Avoid peak holiday dates if you prefer quieter travel.

What to do in Hanoi and around

Hanoi is your gateway to Vietnam. Explore the Old Quarter, visit Ho Chi Minh's Mausoleum, the Temple of Literature and Halong Bay (a few hours away). Street food is excellent and very affordable. From Noi Bai Airport you can reach the city centre by bus or taxi.
