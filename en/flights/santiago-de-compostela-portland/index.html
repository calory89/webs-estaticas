Best time to visit

October and November bring pleasant weather to Portland: mild temperatures (10–15°C), moderate rainfall, and fewer crowds than summer. These months typically show stable pricing compared to June–August. Winter months (December–February) are rainy but viable on a tight budget. Avoid July–August if you're hunting bargains.

What to do in Portland

Portland is known for its arts scene, independent bookstores (Powell's Books is a must), craft breweries and diverse food. Pioneer Courthouse Square is the downtown hub. Forest Park offers hiking with city views. PDX airport is 10 km from centre; the MAX light rail is the cheapest option. Book ahead if visiting during holidays.
