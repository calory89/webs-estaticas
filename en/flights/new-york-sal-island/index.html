Best time to visit

November is excellent for Sal Island: dry weather, less rain than summer, and temperatures around 26–28°C. Waters are warm and perfect for swimming. This is the start of Cape Verde's peak tourist season, so expect great beach conditions throughout the month. Prices tend to be more reasonable outside Christmas and New Year.

What to do when you arrive

Sal Island is known for white-sand beaches and crystal-clear waters. Visit Praia de Santa Maria for swimming and water sports. The village of Palmeira has charm with its fishing port. Don't miss the Pedra Lume Salt Pans where you float naturally in thermal waters. Eat fresh fish at local beach shacks and enjoy the laid-back Cape Verdean pace.
