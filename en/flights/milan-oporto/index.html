Best time to visit

Porto is appealing year-round. Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) offer good weather and fewer crowds than summer. Prices tend to be more competitive outside July and August, so plan ahead.

What to do in Porto

The old town (Ribeira) is essential: wander its winding streets, cross the Luís I Bridge, and have authentic port wine at the lodges. Clérigos Tower, Lello Library, and the Douro River are must-sees. Grab a francesinha (local sandwich) and spot Portuguese tiles around every corner.

What to see in Porto in 3 days?

Start with Ribeira and the Douro River, climb Clérigos Tower, visit Lello Library, try francesinha and port wine at the lodges, and spend an afternoon admiring the azulejo tiles at Igreja de São Ildefonso. A trip to the Douro Valley or Aveiro rounds out your stay.
