Best time to travel

Zurich is lovely any time of year, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer stable weather and fewer crowds. In winter, if you like snow and Christmas markets, December is magical but pricey. Book well in advance regardless of season.

What to do when you arrive

Zurich is compact and easy to explore on foot. Stroll through the Altstadt (old town), walk around Lake Zurich and climb Uetliberg for panoramic views. Don't miss the Swiss National Museum and local cuisine. Zurich Airport is well connected by public transport to the city centre (30–40 minutes).
