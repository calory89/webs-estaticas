Best time to visit

The Maldives are tropical. The dry season (November to April) is ideal: less rain and better underwater visibility. To find lower fares, avoid European school holidays and peak seasons like Christmas and Easter.

What to do when you arrive

Malé International Airport is your gateway. Resorts are reached by speedboat or seaplane. The Maldives are known for beaches, diving and snorkelling in crystal-clear waters. Relax, explore coral reefs, or try water sports. It's pure escape.
