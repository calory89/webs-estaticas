Best time to visit

Bucharest is lovely in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with mild weather and less rain. December brings cold temperatures but festive Christmas events. For more stable prices, travel midweek rather than weekends or public holidays.

What to do in Bucharest

Visit the Palace of Parliament, one of the world's largest buildings. Stroll the Old Town (Lipscani) with its cafés and craft shops. Explore Stavropoleos Church and Dimitrie Gusti Village Museum. Eat at local restaurants and taste authentic Romanian cuisine. Bucharest is cheap: accommodation and food cost far less than other European capitals.
