Best time to visit

Marrakech suits travel year-round. Spring (March–April) and autumn (October–November) are perfect: mild temperatures without intense heat. Summer (June–September) draws crowds and higher prices. Winter is peaceful but cool at night in the medina.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Marrakech Menara Airport. Head straight to the medina for souks, Jemaa El Fnaa square, and traditional riads. Day trips to the Atlas Mountains or the Sahara Desert are close by. Local hammams and Moroccan food are must-tries.
