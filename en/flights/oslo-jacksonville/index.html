Best time to visit

Jacksonville has a subtropical climate. Winter (November–March) offers pleasant temperatures of 15–22°C, perfect for outdoor activities without intense heat. Summer is humid and hot. October and April are agreeable shoulder seasons. Avoid June–September if you prefer cooler weather and lower hurricane risk.

What to do when you arrive

Jacksonville is the largest city by area in the continental US. Enjoy Jacksonville Beach for swimming, the St. Johns River for boat tours, the historic San Marco neighbourhood with shops and restaurants, and the MOSH (Museum of Science and History). It's considerably more affordable than other Florida destinations.
