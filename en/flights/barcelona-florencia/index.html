Best time to visit

Florence is worth visiting year-round, but avoid peak season (June to August) if you want fewer crowds and lower prices. April, May, September and October offer perfect weather and manageable tourism levels. Winter (December to February) is quieter and often has cheaper flights, though daylight hours are shorter.

What to do when you arrive

Florence airport (FLR) is 4 km from the city centre, reachable by train or bus in 20 minutes. The city is essential for art lovers: Duomo, Uffizi Gallery, Accademia, Ponte Vecchio. Book museums ahead. Trains from Florence reach Siena, Pisa and Cinque Terre cheaply for exploring Tuscany.
