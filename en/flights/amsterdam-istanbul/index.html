Best time to visit

Istanbul is worth visiting any time, but October to November and April to May are ideal: mild weather, fewer crowds than summer, and better prices. Avoid July and August if you want peace and value for money. December works too if you're okay with cooler temps and want to see festive decorations.

What to do when you arrive

Istanbul airport (IST) connects easily to the city centre. Spend time at the Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia, and the Spice Bazaar in the historic quarter. Beyoğlu is the place for nightlife and cafés. Take a ferry across the Bosphorus for stunning views of the city from the water. Eat at street stalls: kebab and Turkish food are cheap and delicious.
