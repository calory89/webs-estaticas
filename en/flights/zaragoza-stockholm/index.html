Best time to visit

Stockholm shines in summer (June to August) with long days and pleasant weather, and in winter (December to February) if you're chasing the Northern Lights and snow. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild climate and fewer crowds. Check availability for your preferred dates.

Things to do in Stockholm

Sweden's capital blends modernity with heritage. Don't miss the Old Town (Gamla Stan), the City Hall with panoramic views, the Royal Palace and world-class museums. The nearby archipelago is perfect for boat trips. In winter, the frozen Lake Mälaren offers unique activities. Eat at local markets and try Nordic cuisine.
