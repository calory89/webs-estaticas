Airline and stops

Best time to visit

Lisbon is pleasant year-round, but April to June and September to October offer the best weather and fewer crowds than July and August. Winter months (November to February) often have more competitive fares if you're budget-conscious. Avoid Spanish and Portuguese bank holidays for better prices.

What to do when you arrive

Humberto Delgado airport is 7 km from the city centre. Take the Aerobus (line 91) or train to get downtown quickly. Don't miss Alfama, Belém Tower, Jerónimos Monastery and the Tagus. Grab pastéis de Nata everywhere. Rent a bike to explore or hop on the vintage trams up the steep streets. Three or four days is a good length of stay.
