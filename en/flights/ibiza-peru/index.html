Best time to visit

Peru is worth visiting year-round, but the dry season in the highlands (May to October) is perfect if you're heading to Machu Picchu or the Sacred Valley. November marks the start of the rains but remains a good month with attractive fares. Avoid Christmas and New Year weeks for lower prices. Plan 2–3 months ahead.

What to do when you arrive in Lima

You'll land at Jorge Chávez, Peru's main airport. Lima offers world-class cuisine, museums like the National Museum, and neighbourhoods such as Miraflores and Barranco. Connections to Cusco (for Machu Picchu), Arequipa and other cities depart from here. Spend at least 2–3 days in the capital before exploring the rest of the country.
