Price and when to book

The cheapest flight found costs €1,426 return. The median price is €1,497, with options up to €1,683 depending on dates. Book as early as possible: this route has significant price variation by availability. If your trip is flexible, compare dates across 2-3 weeks to find the best value.

Airline and stopovers

Royal Jordanian operates this route with 2 stopovers on both legs. Outbound: departs Dubai at 22:45 on 16 November. Return: leaves Lima at 11:35 on 23 November. The 2 stopovers extend travel time but cut costs significantly versus non-stop alternatives.

Best time to travel

November is ideal for Peru: dry season in the highlands (Machu Picchu, Cusco) with clear skies and pleasant temperatures (15–25°C). Lima's coast is warm but not oppressive (25–30°C). Avoid peak tourism June–August if you prefer fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Jorge Chávez International Airport, 16 km from Lima city centre. Public transport and taxis are available. Lima offers world-class museums (Gold, National), outstanding food, and interesting neighbourhoods (Miraflores, San Isidro). From here it's straightforward to travel to Cusco, Sacred Valley or Machu Picchu by plane or coach.
