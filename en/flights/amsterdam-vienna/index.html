Price and when to book

The most budget-friendly trip costs €210 return. The median price is €213, with samples reaching €262. Book in advance: the sooner you purchase, the better your odds of grabbing those low fares. Bookings made 1-2 months ahead tend to be safer.

Airline and flight type

Fly with KLM Royal Dutch Airlines. This is a direct Amsterdam-Vienna flight, no stops. You board in AMS and land in VIE without changing planes, saving you time and hassle.

Best time to visit

Vienna shines at Christmas, but spring (March-May) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Summer months tend to be pricier. Winter can deliver lower fares if you don't mind the cold, though it's the coldest season.

What to do when you arrive in Vienna

Vienna is a musical and cultural capital: visit St. Stephen's Cathedral, Schönbrunn Palace and the State Opera. Stroll through the UNESCO-listed old town. Try the famous Sacher Torte in a Viennese café. Vienna airport (VIE) is well connected to the centre by train and bus.

FAQ

Is €210 the final price or are there hidden charges?

The price shown is what the market offered; always check the breakdown on KLM's site to confirm whether airport taxes or other charges are added at checkout.

Is the Amsterdam-Vienna flight direct?

Yes, it is direct. KLM operates this route non-stop—you board in Amsterdam and arrive in Vienna without any plane changes.

What's the best date to book this trip?

The sooner you book, the better. With 4-8 weeks' notice, you'll usually find more availability at low prices. Avoid searching right after an offer is announced—prices climb fast.

What to see in Vienna beyond the main attractions?

Besides Schönbrunn and the Cathedral, don't miss the Hofburg Museums, the Church of St. Charles and traditional local markets. If travelling at Christmas, the festive markets are spectacular.
