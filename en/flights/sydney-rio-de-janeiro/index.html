Best time to travel

Rio de Janeiro offers excellent conditions most of the year. Southern summer (December to February) brings beaches and warm weather, though it's peak season. Autumn (March to May) and spring (September to November) are pleasant months with fewer crowds. Travel when it suits your dates and budget best.

What to see and do in Rio

Don't miss Christ the Redeemer, Sugarloaf Mountain, and the beaches of Copacabana and Ipanema. The Lapa neighbourhood offers nightlife and dining. If you travel during carnival season (February–March), you'll experience something truly unique. Explore museums, bay tours, and hiking trails in the mountains.
