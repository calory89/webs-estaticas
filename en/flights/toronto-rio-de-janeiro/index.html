Best time to travel

Rio de Janeiro is warm year-round. Local summer (December to March) offers perfect beach weather but is peak season. Australian spring (September to November) and autumn (March to May) are quieter periods with slightly lower fares. Skip peak times if you want a more relaxed and budget-friendly trip.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Antônio Carlos Jobim International Airport (Galeão), 20 km from the centre. Christ the Redeemer, Sugarloaf Mountain, and the beaches of Copacabana and Ipanema are must-sees. Lagoa Rodrigo de Freitas, Lapa neighbourhood and the Museum of Modern Art round out a first visit. Use the metro and local buses to get around the city.
