Quick answer: The cheapest return flight from Oslo to Vietnam we found costs 616 € (search on 23 Oct 2026, Kiwi.com). It has 2 stops with Norwegian Air Shuttle.

Fly from Oslo to Hanoi from €616 return with Norwegian Air Shuttle. The median price is €627, so you've got solid options. With two stops, the journey takes longer, but the savings are significant.

Price and when to book

Fares start at €616 for a return ticket. We've tracked prices at €616, €627 and €693. Book early, the lowest fare can sell out quickly. Booking 3–4 weeks ahead tends to work well on this route.

Airline and stops

You'll fly with Norwegian Air Shuttle, which operates this route with 2 stops. The outbound departs 10 November at 07:00 and the return on 20 November at 12:25. The stops extend journey time but keep costs well below direct options.

Best time to travel

November is excellent for Vietnam: mild temperatures, less rain than other seasons and scenery at its best. Avoid typhoon months (September–October) and extreme heat (June–August). Travelling outside peak tourist dates helps secure better fares.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in Hanoi, the capital in the north. See the Old Quarter, Hoan Kiem Lake and the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum. From Hanoi, Ha Long Bay (UNESCO site), Sapa's rice terraces and central Vietnam are easy to reach. November is perfect for trekking and exploration.

FAQ

What's the cheapest flight from Oslo to Vietnam?

From €616 return with Norwegian Air Shuttle. We've seen this fare available, though dates shifting by a few days can affect the price.

Does Norwegian Air Shuttle fly direct Oslo to Hanoi?

No, this route includes 2 stops. Direct flights on this route are rare and much pricier. The stops add travel time but keep fares low.

What's the average return fare?

Around €627. That's a solid benchmark: if you see something close to that, it's a representative price for this route.

What should I see in Hanoi and around it?

The Old Quarter, Hoan Kiem Lake, museums and the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum are in the city. Outside: Ha Long Bay, Sapa (mountains and rice fields) and Ha Giang are essential. November is the ideal month to explore without intense heat.
