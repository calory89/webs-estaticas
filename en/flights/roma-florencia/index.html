Best time to visit

Florence shines year-round. June to September draw peak tourism and higher prices. Autumn (October–November) and early winter (December–January) tend to be cheaper, though weather is cooler. Spring (March–May) strikes a balance: pleasant climate, moderate fares and fewer crowds than summer.

What to do when you arrive

Florence is the heart of the Renaissance: the Cathedral of Santa Maria del Fiore, the Uffizi Gallery and Ponte Vecchio are essential. Visit the Palazzo Pitti, climb Brunelleschi's dome or stroll along the Arno. Rome is an hour away, so the reverse route works too. Spend at least 2–3 days to explore properly.
