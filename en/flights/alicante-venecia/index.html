Best time to travel

Venice is beautiful year-round, but October, November, and April are ideal: fewer crowds than summer and pleasant weather. If you prefer to avoid tourists, travel in winter, though some canal services may be reduced. Prices tend to be more economical outside peak tourism season.

What to do when you arrive

Venice awaits you with St. Mark's Basilica, the Doge's Palace, and its famous canals. Cross the Rialto Bridge, wander through narrow streets, take a gondola ride or simply stroll at sunset. Venice airport is connected to the city centre by vaporetto, bus or water taxi. Book museums and attractions in advance during high season.
