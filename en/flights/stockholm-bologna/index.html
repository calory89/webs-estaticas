Best time to visit

Bologna is beautiful year-round. December offers Christmas markets and festive atmosphere, though it's peak season. For fewer crowds and stable prices, spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal. Winter is often cheaper overall, but demand matters more than season.

What to do when you arrive

Bologna is the city of towers, arcades and fresh pasta. Don't miss the Basilica of San Petronio, Asinelli Tower, the Two Towers, and stroll through the historic centre. Eat tortellini, mortadella and Bolognese pasta at any trattoria. Guglielmo Marconi airport is 4 km from the city centre, well served by bus and taxi.
