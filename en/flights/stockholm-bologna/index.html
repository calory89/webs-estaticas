Price and when to book

The lowest price we've seen is €115 return, with a median of €137. Prices go up to €159 depending on availability and date. There's no clear pattern for cheaper days of the week, so set up price alerts and compare flexible dates. Book early: fares tend to rise as your departure date approaches.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route with one stop. The outbound flight departs Stockholm (STO) at 09:50 on 5 December 2026, and the return leaves Bologna (BLQ) at 06:50 on 11 December. One stop adds travel time but keeps prices competitive. Check the total duration and connecting airport when you book.

Best time to visit

Bologna is beautiful year-round. December offers Christmas markets and festive atmosphere, though it's peak season. For fewer crowds and stable prices, spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal. Winter is often cheaper overall, but demand matters more than season.

What to do when you arrive

Bologna is the city of towers, arcades and fresh pasta. Don't miss the Basilica of San Petronio, Asinelli Tower, the Two Towers, and stroll through the historic centre. Eat tortellini, mortadella and Bolognese pasta at any trattoria. Guglielmo Marconi airport is 4 km from the city centre, well served by bus and taxi.
