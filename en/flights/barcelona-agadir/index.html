Best time to visit

Agadir enjoys good weather almost year-round. October to April is the most pleasant season, with mild temperatures and plenty of sunshine. June to September is very hot. Travelling outside peak season (summer and Christmas) also helps you find better prices on accommodation and activities.

What to do in Agadir

The beach is the main draw: wide sand, palm trees and a seafront promenade lined with terraces. The port offers local atmosphere and a fish market. If you fancy the mountains, the Anti-Atlas is nearby. Shops and restaurants dot the centre; the old medina is worth exploring. Escape to coastal towns like Essaouira if you have extra days.
