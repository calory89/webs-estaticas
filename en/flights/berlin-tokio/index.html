Things to do in Tokyo

Tokyo offers ancient temples, cutting-edge modernity and unique experiences. Don't miss Senso-ji, Shibuya, Shinjuku, museums and local food culture. Narita or Haneda international airport are well connected to the city by train and bus. Spend at least 5-7 days to enjoy the capital and surroundings.
