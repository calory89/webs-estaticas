Prices range from €154 to €245 in the sample checked. To get the best fares, book as early as possible: the sooner you purchase, the better the price you'll find. Most searches centre around €156, so that's a solid benchmark. Avoid leaving it to the last minute; cheap seats sell out fast.

Airline and connections

Transavia operates this route with direct flights: no plane changes needed. Departure from Amsterdam is early morning (example: 07:20) and arrival in Larnaca is around midday local time. The return flight leaves in the afternoon (example: 13:35). Perfect for maximizing time on the ground without losing hours to connections.

Best time to travel

Cyprus enjoys sunshine almost year-round. Winter (December to February) is mild and prices are lower—ideal if you're budget-conscious. Spring (March to May) offers the sweet spot: pleasant temperatures without summer's extreme heat. If you want beaches and guaranteed warmth, go in summer, but expect crowds and higher fares.

What to do when you arrive

Larnaca is your gateway to the island. Explore Mackenzie Beach, stroll the seafront, or visit the Church of St. Lazarus. Cyprus offers pristine beaches, mountain villages, Greco-Roman ruins, and excellent food. Rent a car at the airport for freedom: the island is small and journeys are short.

What is there to see in Cyprus?

White-sand beaches, the Akamas cliffs, traditional villages like Omodos, ancient ruins, and local cuisine. Larnaca is the perfect base to explore the entire island.
