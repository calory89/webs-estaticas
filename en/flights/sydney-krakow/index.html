Base prices start at €930, but typical range spans €930 to €1,385. Book as soon as possible from today: this is a long intercontinental route and seats fill up quickly. Compare multiple departure and return dates to find the best fares; small calendar shifts can mean €300–400 differences.

Best time to visit

Krakow is a year-round destination. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) bring pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter (December–February) is magical with snow, but it's peak season. Summer can be pricier and busier. Avoid school holiday dates if you want a quieter experience.

What to do when you arrive in Krakow

The Rynek Główny (Main Market Square) is the medieval heart, surrounded by galleries and museums. St Mary's Basilica and Wawel Castle are must-sees. The Jewish Quarter of Kazimierz has a unique vibe, and nearby Auschwitz-Birkenau is a historically significant site. Gothic and Renaissance architecture permeates the city: stroll, eat pierniki (dumplings), and relax in cosy cafés.
