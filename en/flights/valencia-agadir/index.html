Best time to visit

Agadir has mild weather year-round. June to September is warmest but busier with tourists. For fewer crowds and potentially better prices, try spring (April–May) or autumn (October–November). Avoid peak season if you prefer a quieter break.

What to do when you arrive

Agadir is famous for its long beach and seafront promenade. Explore the medina (traditional market), climb the Casbah for panoramic views, or take day trips to Tafraoute (painted rock mountains) and nearby coastal towns. The fishing port is worth a visit to see local life.
