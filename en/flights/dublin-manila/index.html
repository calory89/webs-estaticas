Best time to visit

The Philippines has a dry season from November to April, the ideal period to visit. June to October brings rain and typhoons. If you want to enjoy the climate, travel between November and March. Prices may vary during this window, so track offers in those months. December and January are peak season and more expensive.

What to do on arrival in Manila

Manila is your gateway to the Philippines. Explore the old town (Intramuros), visit colonial landmarks and relax by the Bay. From here you can easily reach destinations like Boracay, Palawan or the Cordillera. Manila airport (MNL) is on the outskirts; use authorised taxis or ride-sharing to reach the city centre. Don't miss exploring beyond the capital: the Philippine islands are the real treasure.

What to see in Manila besides the old town?

The National Museum, Rizal Park botanical garden and San Agustín Church (Unesco World Heritage) are must-sees. To escape the city, Boracay (via domestic flight or ferry) and Palawan beaches are spectacular. Try local food: lechón and adobo are signature dishes you shouldn't miss.
