Best time to visit

Peru is rewarding year-round, but May to September (dry season) is the sweet spot. The weather in the mountains is more stable and rainfall is lower. December to March is the rainy season, especially in the Amazon and highlands. Book well ahead if you're travelling in peak season.

What to do when you arrive in Lima

You'll land at Jorge Chávez International Airport, 16 km from the city centre. From there, explore Lima's historic centre, visit pre-Hispanic art museums, sample local food in Miraflores or Barranco. Many travellers then fly or bus to Cusco for Machu Picchu or trek the Sacred Valley.
