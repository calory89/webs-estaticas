February is a good time to escape intense cold in much of Europe, though Bologna is still winter. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) are ideal: mild temperatures, less rain than winter, and cities less crowded than summer.

What to do when you arrive

Bologna awaits with its medieval towers (Asinelli and Garisenda), the Basilica of San Petronio, and Italy's longest arcades. Walk the historic centre, try local food in Piazza Maggiore, and don't miss Emilian art museums. Bologna airport (Marconi) is 10 km from the centre, well connected by public transport.
