Best time to visit

Bologna is lovely year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: mild temperatures, less mass tourism than summer and no winter cold. July–August gets hot. If you want low fares plus good weather, aim for shoulder season. The data checked on 22 October suggests availability in autumn.

What to do in Bologna

Bologna, capital of Emilia-Romagna region, is a university city with medieval charm. Stroll Piazza Maggiore (the heart), climb Torre Asinelli for views, enter the Basilica of San Petronio. Eat authentic tagliatelle al ragù (real Bolognese sauce), try tortellini and mortadella. The porticoed arcades are unique: over 40 km of historic covered walkways run through the city. Guglielmo Marconi airport is 4 km from centre; direct bus to old town.

What to see in Bologna in 3 days?

Day 1: Piazza Maggiore, Basilica of San Petronio, arcades. Day 2: Torre Asinelli, Ancient Art Museum, local food. Day 3: Basilica of Santo Stefano, shopping in the centre, more Bolognese cuisine. It's a compact, walkable city.
