Best time to visit

Kuala Lumpur has a tropical climate year-round. The driest season is May to September, though heat and humidity are constant. If you prefer less rain and fewer tourists, those months are ideal. February, when these sample prices appear, falls in monsoon season but also sees fewer visitors.

What to do when you arrive

Kuala Lumpur welcomes you with the iconic Petronas Towers, cinemas, shopping at Pavilion and Midvalley, the stunning Batu Caves temple, and traditional markets. The international airport (KLIA) is about 70 km from the city centre and well connected by train and bus. From there it's easy to explore the city and take trips to the Perhentian Islands or Cameron Highlands.
