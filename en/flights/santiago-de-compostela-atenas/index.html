Best time to visit

Athens is pleasant year-round, though May to September see more tourists and higher fares. October, April and spring offer good weather with fewer crowds. Winters are mild, though rain occurs some days. Skip July and August if you want better value and less congestion.

What to do when you arrive

Spend at least 3-4 days exploring. Visit the Acropolis and Parthenon, stroll through Plaka, climb Lycabettus Hill at sunset, and eat in Psirri. Don't miss the Acropolis Museum. The international airport is 30 km away; reach the city centre by metro, bus or taxi in 45-60 minutes.
