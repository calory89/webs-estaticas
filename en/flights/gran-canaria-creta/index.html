Best time to visit

Crete enjoys pleasant weather almost year-round. Summer (June to September) means beaches and sunshine, but also peak crowds. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer great weather without the rush. Winter is quieter and cheaper, though some rain is possible.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Heraklion Airport (HER) on the north coast. Crete offers Minoan ruins (Knossos), sandy beaches, coastal towns like Chania, and mountain trails. Eat at local tavernas, taste Cretan cuisine, and explore both coast and inland peaks.
