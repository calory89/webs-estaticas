Flights Dublin-Azores: from €231 return

Fly Dublin to the Azores with Ryanair from €231 per person return. The median price sits around €232, so you'll find solid options if you're flexible with dates. In under 2 hours plus a stopover, you're in the Portuguese archipelago.

Price and when to book

The lowest price on record is €231, with a sample ranging from €231 to €264. There's no fixed pattern for price drops on specific days or months, but booking 2–3 months ahead generally secures lower fares. March, April and September tend to balance out well: less peak tourism than summer, but better weather than winter.

Airline and stopovers

You'll travel with Ryanair. The flight has 1 stopover, so it's not direct. Ground time at the intermediate stop varies, but expect around 3–4 hours total journey time. Ryanair works best if you're carrying just cabin baggage; checked luggage has an extra charge.

Best time to visit

Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: pleasant temperatures (18–22°C), less rain and quieter beaches than summer. July and August have perfect weather but higher prices and more crowds. Winter is cooler and damper, though still manageable. The archipelago sits in the North Atlantic, so bring a jacket regardless of season.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at João Paulo II Airport (PDL) in Ponta Delgada, on the island of São Miguel. Explore the hot springs at Caldeiras Velhas, swim in natural thermal pools, trek volcanic craters and caldera lakes. Birdwatching is excellent. Try local cuisine: scabbardfish, braised octopus and tropical fruit. Note that car rental isn't dirt cheap; buses and organised tours are more practical.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for Dublin-Azores flights?

The lowest price found is €231 return. Other sample prices are €232 and €264. Flexible dates and early booking help you nail the best deals.

Does Ryanair fly direct Dublin to Azores?

No, it's not direct. Ryanair operates this route with 1 stopover. Total journey time is around 3–4 hours including the stop.

Which airport serves the Azores?

The main one is João Paulo II (PDL) in Ponta Delgada, São Miguel. It's your gateway to the archipelago.

When is the best time to fly Dublin-Azores?

Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) deliver solid weather and fewer crowds than summer. Avoid last-minute bookings; the earlier you book, the better your price tends to be.
