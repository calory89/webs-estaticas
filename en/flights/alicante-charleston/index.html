What to do when you arrive in Charleston

Charleston is a charming historic and coastal destination. Stroll the historic Rainbow Row district, visit forts like Sumter, enjoy nearby beaches, or taste Lowcountry food. Charleston airport (CHS) connects well to the city centre via public transport and car hire. It's small in scale but big in character.
