Best time to visit

Bucharest is pleasant year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) deliver ideal weather and fewer crowds. Winters are cold but perfect if you fancy a Christmas or New Year break without the mass tourism. Skip July–August if you prefer quieter streets.

What to do in Bucharest

Romania's capital blends communist architecture, grand palaces and vibrant nightlife. The Palace of Parliament is stunning, the Old Town (Lipscani) bursts with bars and restaurants, and Stavropoleos Church impresses with its beauty. Walk the main avenues, eat at authentic local taverns and spend an evening exploring the cultural scene.
