Airline and direct flight

Best time to visit

Amsterdam is lovely year-round, but April–May and September–October offer good weather and fewer crowds than summer. The lowest fares tend to be in low season (November–February), though the weather is grey. Avoid July–August if you want to dodge peak tourism and higher prices.

What to do in Amsterdam

Walk the canals, visit the Van Gogh Museum or Rijksmuseum, cycle like a local, grab stroopwafels at Albert Cuyp Market, climb the Basilica of St Nicholas. The airport is 10 km from the centre; bus, train, or taxi get you downtown in 30 minutes. The city is compact and walkable.

What should I see in Amsterdam in a few days?

The canals, the main museums (Van Gogh, Rijks), Anne Frank House if you're interested in history, a bike ride around the city, and the street markets. You can cover the centre in 2–3 days without rushing.
