Best time to travel

Peru is a year-round destination, but the dry season (May–October) offers better weather in the highlands. December is peak season on the coast and in Lima. For better value, avoid July–August and European school holidays. Prices jump around bank holidays.

What to do in Lima and beyond

Lima is your entry point. Explore the historic centre, Miraflores and San Isidro. The food scene is world-class. With more time, Machu Picchu (Cusco), Nazca, and the Andes are worth every euro. The international airport is 16 km from the city centre.
