Airline and stops

You'll fly with Wizz Air UK, and the best part is there are no stops: it's direct from start to finish. Departure from Valencia, arrival in London. No plane changes, no transit waits, no running around the airport.

Best time to travel

February is a good window to find low prices if you're flexible. Spring months (March–May) also offer decent fares and better weather. Skip July–August if you're chasing savings: it's peak season and prices climb. December isn't recommended for tight budgets either.

What to do in London

Once you land, there's Big Ben, Parliament, Tower Bridge, the British Museum (free entry to main galleries), Hyde Park and Portobello Road markets. Shoreditch and Camden neighbourhoods are worth a stroll. The Underground is fast and covers the whole city. Grab fish and chips anywhere: it's still the best.
