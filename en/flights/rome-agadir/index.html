What to do when you arrive in Agadir

Agadir offers golden sandy beaches, a lively seafront promenade, and bustling local markets. The port is your gateway to Atlas mountain excursions. If you surf, there are waves all year. Eat fresh seafood at the fish market and explore the rebuilt medina. The mild climate makes outdoor activities possible almost year-round.
