Best time to travel

Sicily appeals year-round, but spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer the best blend of pleasant weather and moderate crowds. Summers are hot, winters mild but occasionally grey. Prices climb during peak season, so book ahead if travelling then.

What to do in Palermo

Sicily's capital is unmissable: the Gothic cathedral, Teatro Politeama, street markets at Ballarò and Vucciria, and nearby beaches like Mondello. Trips to Monreale and the Tyrrhenian coast depart from here too. Mix local food, Norman history and seaside in a compact space.
