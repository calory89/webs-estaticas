Best time to visit

February to April is the dry season in the Philippines: ideal weather, less rain, and prices tend to be stable. Avoid typhoon season (June to November). Mid-season months usually offer fewer crowds and a better balance between climate and fares. Check the forecast before travel.

What to do in Manila

Ninoy Aquino International Airport (MNL) is 7 km from the city centre. Intramuros (walled old city), Roxas Boulevard waterfront, national museums, and Makati district are must-sees. Eat at local markets, take ferries to nearby islands, or relax at spas. Nightlife is lively. Jeepney rides are typical and cheap.

What can I see in Manila in a week?

Intramuros, Rizal Park, the National Museum, Makati, Mall of Asia, and a day trip to Corregidor or beaches near Tagaytay. Street food and Malate bars are essential. A week covers the basics; with more time, explore nearby islands in the archipelago.

How far is Manila from Toronto?

About 13,220 km in a straight line between YTO and MNL airports.

Is there a train from Toronto to Manila?

No. There is an ocean between Toronto and Manila (about 13,220 km between the airports), so the practical way to get there is by plane.
