Best time to visit

Istanbul is worth a visit any time of year. December is winter: mild by day (8–13°C), cold at night, fewer tourists than summer. If you want better weather, aim for May, June or September. Prices tend to drop in low season (November to March), so this December route is a smart opportunity.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Istanbul airport (IST). The city awaits: the Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia, the Spice Bazaar, the Golden Horn. Take a taxi, bus or metro from the airport. Eat at local restaurants, drink Turkish tea and try halva sweets. Istanbul blends tradition and modernity: explore both sides of the Bosphorus.
