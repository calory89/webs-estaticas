Best time to visit

Jacksonville has a subtropical climate: mild winters (15–20°C) and warm, humid summers. December is low season, which explains the affordable prices you see here. Avoid June to September if you prefer less extreme heat. Spring (March–May) is another solid choice if you want pleasant weather without higher fares.

What to do when you arrive in Jacksonville

Jacksonville is a gateway to Florida's beaches, with 22 miles of coastline. Explore River Walk, a scenic riverside promenade with dining and views along the St. Johns River. JAX airport is 13 km from the city centre, accessible by taxi, bus or rental car. The city blends nature (parks and reserves) with urban entertainment, perfect for recovering from a multi-stop journey.
