Best time to visit

Singapore is tropical and warm year-round. Avoid monsoon months (November to January) if you prefer less rain, though it's not a dealbreaker. December and January attract more tourists, so flights can be pricier. Consider travelling in the dry months of February to September for better fares and weather.

What to do when you arrive

Singapore is cosmopolitan and compact. Explore Marina Bay, ride the Singapore Flyer, visit Chinatown temples, stroll through the Botanic Gardens and enjoy street food. Changi Airport has good public transport connections to the city centre.
