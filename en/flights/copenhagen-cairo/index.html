Best time to visit

Egypt is most pleasant from October to April, when temperatures are moderate and the heat isn't overwhelming. Cairo in winter (December to February) is ideal for tourists. Avoid June to August if you don't tolerate extreme heat well. Booking well in advance will help you secure better prices.

What to do when you arrive in Cairo

Cairo International Airport (CAI) is about 25 km from the city centre. From there you can visit the Pyramids of Giza, the Sphinx, the Egyptian Museum and Khan el-Khalili bazaar. The city is the perfect starting point for excursions to the Nile, Saqqara or Memphis. Plan at least three days to cover the main sights.
