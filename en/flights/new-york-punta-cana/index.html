Best time to visit

December to March is the dry season and peak time in Punta Cana, with ideal temperatures (25–28°C). July and August are hot and humid. For lower fares, travel in May, June or September, though rainfall is higher. December can get pricey just before New Year's.

What to do when you arrive

Punta Cana is your gateway to stunning beaches and all-inclusive resorts. The international airport (PUJ) is well connected to the east coast. Playa Blanca, Cap Cana and Bavaro Beach are the most famous. For culture, Santo Domingo's colonial zone is 2–3 hours by car. Diving, snorkelling and nature parks are all available.
