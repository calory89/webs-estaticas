Price and when to book

The lowest price we've tracked is €272 per person return. Prices rise quickly: samples at €288 and €302 on nearby dates. Book at least 2–3 months ahead for best rates. October and November offer better fares before peak season. Check mid-week (Tuesday–Thursday) for lower prices than Friday and Sunday.

Airline and stopovers

Vueling operates this route with 1 stopover. Direct flights from Ibiza to Cairo don't exist, so expect one stop. Layover times typically range from 2 to 4 hours, giving you a proper break without major delays. It's a solid trade-off for the budget price.

Best time to visit

October to April is peak season: pleasant temperatures (20–25°C) and no scorching heat. Skip June to September if you can't handle extreme warmth. Ramadan dates vary yearly and affect restaurant and shop hours, check before you go. Winter brings cheaper fares and cooler mornings, though not freezing. December and January balance climate and price nicely.

What to do when you arrive

Cairo is your gateway to Giza pyramids, the Egyptian Museum and Saladin's Citadel. Stroll Khan el-Khalili bazaar at sunset. With extra days, day trips to Saqqara, Memphis or the Nile are within reach. The airport is 20 km from the city centre; take a taxi, Uber or arrange a private transfer. Ibiza to Cairo opens centuries of archaeology and culture without breaking the bank.
