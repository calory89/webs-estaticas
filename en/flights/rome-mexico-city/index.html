Best time to travel

Mexico is a year-round destination, but the dry season (November to April) offers better weather. Avoid the rainy season (May to October) if you prefer sunny days. Keep in mind December, Christmas and New Year tend to be pricier. Spring (March-April) and autumn (October-November) are good periods with fewer crowds.

What to do in Mexico City

Mexico's capital is vast: Historic Centre, Templo Mayor, National Palace, Zócalo. Visit Frida Kahlo at La Casa Azul, world-class museums (Anthropology, Tamayo), markets like La Merced, trendy neighbourhoods (Coyoacán, Roma, Condesa). Food is exceptional and affordable. Book at least 4-5 days to explore properly. The airport (MEX) is 5 km from centre, well connected by metro.
