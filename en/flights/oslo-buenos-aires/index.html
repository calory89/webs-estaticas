Best time to visit

Buenos Aires has a subtropical climate. Spring (September–November) and autumn (March–May) offer pleasant weather and less rain. Summer months (December–February) are hot and humid; winter (June–August) is mild. Choose based on your weather preference. What to do on arrival

Argentina's capital has plenty to offer: iconic neighbourhoods like La Boca and San Telmo, live tango, world-class museums, and top-tier food. Ministro Pistarini Airport (Ezeiza) is 35 km from the city centre; reach it by bus, remise or train. Explore the port, taste empanadas and local asado, and soak up the porteño energy.
