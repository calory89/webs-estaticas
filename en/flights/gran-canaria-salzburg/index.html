Best time to visit

Salzburg shines in winter (December to February) if you want snow and Christmas markets. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer perfect weather and fewer crowds. July and August are the most expensive and busiest months. January is a solid choice: post-New Year escape, reasonable prices, and the alpine forests at their best.

What to do in Salzburg

Explore the old town (UNESCO heritage site), visit Hohensalzburg Fortress from the hilltop, stroll Getreidegasse where The Sound of Music was filmed. The alpine lakes around Hallstatt are 1.5 hours away, perfect for a day trip. Austrian cuisine (apfelstrudel, schnitzel) won't disappoint.
