Best time to visit

Hamburg shines from May to September, with long days and pleasant temperatures. Autumn (September-October) offers beautiful landscapes. Winter is harsh but magical if you enjoy snow and Christmas atmospheres. Prices drop in low season, though weather is more severe.

What to do in Hamburg

Walk through the Speicherstadt canals, the city's most romantic area. Visit the port and the Philharmonie, stunning modern architecture. Elbe Beach is perfect for strolls. Eat at street markets and try Aalsuppe (eel soup). The Miniatur Wunderland museum delights curious minds.
