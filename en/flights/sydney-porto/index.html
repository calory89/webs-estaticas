Best time to visit

Porto shines in autumn (September to November) and winter (December to February), with mild temperatures and less rain than spring. Spring (March to May) is also pleasant. Avoid summer if you're chasing lower fares—it's peak season. The route is viable year-round.

What to do when you arrive in Porto

Explore the Ribiera, the medieval riverside quarter along the Douro. Taste Porto wine at traditional cellars in Vila Nova de Gaia, across from the city centre. Climb the Torre dos Clérigos for panoramic views. The airport is 11 km from the centre; bus or taxi are quick options. Don't miss the cobbled streets and Portuguese azulejo tiles.
