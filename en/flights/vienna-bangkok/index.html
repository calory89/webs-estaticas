Best time to travel

November is excellent for Bangkok: pleasant temperatures (25-32°C), moderate humidity and less rain than other months. It's peak season but for good reason. If you prefer fewer crowds, consider early December, though prices climb.

What to do when you arrive

Bangkok has iconic temples like Wat Pho and the Grand Palace, floating markets outside the city, nightlife on Khao San Road and spectacular street food. Suvarnabhumi airport connects well to the city by train or taxi. Spend at least 3-4 days to enjoy it properly.
