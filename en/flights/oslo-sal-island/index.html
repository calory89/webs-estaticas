Best time to travel

Sal enjoys warm, dry weather year-round. November to June are ideal if you prefer moderate temperatures and less rain. December and January are popular but may see higher demand. What to do on arrival

Sal is a beach destination: natural pools, kitesurfing at Ponta Preta, and white-sand beaches like Santa Maria. Explore the island interior, visit Palmeira or discover the Marine Natural Park. Sal is compact, perfect for 5–7 days of relaxation. The international airport (SID) is 15 minutes from Santa Maria centre.
