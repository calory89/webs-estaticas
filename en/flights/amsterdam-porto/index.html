Best time to visit

Porto is pleasant almost year-round. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. December is also viable if you want festive atmosphere. Avoid July–August if you dislike heat and peak tourist numbers.

What to do in Porto

The historic centre is UNESCO World Heritage: walk the Ribeira, head down to the Douro waterfront, cross the iconic Dom Luís Bridge. Sample Port wine in the lodges of Vila Nova de Gaia. Costa Dourada beaches are 30 km away. Museums, Livraria Lello bookstore and local food round out your stay.
