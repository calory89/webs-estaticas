Ryanair connects Dublin and Brussels on a direct flight from €44 return. The median price hovers around €51, with options up to €59 depending on dates. Book early and get the best fares.

Price and when to book

The cheapest flights (€44) sit at the lower end, though prices climb to €59 on certain dates. The average stands at €51. Book as far in advance as possible: Ryanair's fares are cheaper the earlier you reserve, especially for short-haul routes like this one.

Airline and flight type

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight with no layovers. This cuts travel time and simplifies your logistics. Note that Ryanair has strict baggage rules: cabin bag is included in the base fare, but checked luggage costs extra. Review their terms before booking.

Best time to visit

Dublin and Brussels enjoy pleasant weather in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October). Avoid peak summer months (July–August) if you're chasing lower prices. December can offer good deals, though expect colder weather. Check the fare calendar for your specific travel dates.

What to do when you arrive in Brussels

Belgium's capital is perfect for a weekend trip. Visit the Grand Place, wander the Sablon district, taste Belgian beer and croquettes, and explore chocolate shops. Brussels-Zaventem Airport connects easily to the city centre by public transport. From there, train day trips to Bruges, Ghent, or Antwerp are straightforward.
