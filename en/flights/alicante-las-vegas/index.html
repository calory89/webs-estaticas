Best time to visit Las Vegas

Las Vegas runs year-round. October, November, March and April offer milder weather and less crowding than summer. Airfares can be cheaper in low season. December rises sharply for Christmas. Avoid July and August for extreme heat. Your airfare depends on booking timing rather than the month itself.

What to do in Las Vegas

The Strip is the hub: casinos, luxury hotels and shows. Fremont Street downtown has a vintage, budget-friendly vibe. The Neon Museum and art galleries are less touristy. The Grand Canyon is 30 km away for day trips. Valley of Fire and Marble Park are short drives. Eat at food halls for better value than touristy restaurants. The Bellagio Fountains and Mirage attractions are free.
