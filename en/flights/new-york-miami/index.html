Best time to visit

Miami works year-round. Skip September and October if you want to avoid hurricane season; the dry, comfortable months run November to April, though prices climb then. Consider May–August for fewer crowds and lower fares, though heat and humidity are intense. Prices were last checked on 23 October, so monitor changes as your travel dates get closer.

What to do when you arrive

Miami offers white-sand beaches (South Beach, Wynwood), street art, nightlife and Cuban food. The international airport (MIA) is about 10 km from the city centre; bus, taxi or hire car get you in fast. Spend at least 3–4 days exploring Little Havana, Wynwood Walls and the beaches of Key Biscayne.
