Best time to visit

Japan offers brilliant experiences year-round. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) are peak seasons for mild weather and stunning landscapes. For lower fares, consider off-season travel in winter or summer. The October query date doesn't mean October is cheaper; shop around different travel dates.

Things to do when you arrive

Kyoto is Japan's historic heart, packed with ancient temples, traditional gardens and geishas in the Gion district. Must-sees include the Golden Pavilion (Kinkaku-ji), Fushimi Inari Shrine and Arashiyama Bamboo Grove. Eat local cuisine and explore traditional markets. You'll arrive at Osaka Airport (OSA), roughly 75–90 minutes by train from Kyoto.
