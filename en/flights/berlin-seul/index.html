Best time to travel

Seoul has four distinct seasons. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: mild weather, clear skies and less rain. Winter (December–February) is cold but dry; summer (June–August) is humid and hot. Check prices regularly to find the sweet spot between climate and fares.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Incheon (ICN), one of Asia's major hubs. Seoul stands out for its traditional temples, modern neighbourhoods like Gangnam, street markets and unique cuisine. Public transport is excellent: trains, metros and buses connect the entire city. Explore Bukchon, visit royal palaces and sample street food.
