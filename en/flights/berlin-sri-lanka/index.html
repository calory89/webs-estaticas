Best time to travel

December is ideal if you want to escape European winter: Sri Lanka has warm weather and is dry season in many areas. But it's also when most tourists arrive and prices rise. If you want to save and don't mind rain, consider May to October (southwest monsoon, but cheaper flights). Either way, from Germany this route always involves a long flight with a stop.

What to do when you arrive in Colombo

You'll land at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB), about 30 km from Colombo. The capital is your gateway: visit Colombo Lighthouse, Kelaniya Temple, museums or Galle Face seafront. From Colombo you can travel to other highlights: southern beaches, central highlands with tea plantations, or ancient palaces like Sigiriya and Polonnaruwa. Hire a car or use local buses to explore the island.

When do the flights depart and arrive?

What should I know before travelling to Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka is a safe and accessible destination. Colombo has all services: transport, hotels, restaurants. December is dry season in south and north (good for beaches), while the centre is pleasant year-round. Bring cash (Sri Lankan rupee), though ATMs are available. The island is small (65,000 km²) and connected by roads, trains and local buses.
