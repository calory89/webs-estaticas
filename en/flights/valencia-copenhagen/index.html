Best time to visit Copenhagen

Copenhagen shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) with mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter (December–February) brings the famous Christmas atmosphere and the illuminated Tivoli Gardens, though it's colder. Every season has its charm, so choose based on your preferred activities and climate.

What to do when you arrive

Copenhagen is compact and walkable. See Amalienborg Palace, stroll through colourful Nyhavn with its picturesque houses, explore the bohemian Freetown Christiania neighbourhood. Tivoli Garden is essential. Cycle the city like locals do and you'll discover hidden corners. Try smorrebrod (open-faced sandwiches) everywhere and browse Danish design in galleries and shops.
