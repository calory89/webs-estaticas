Best time to visit Nassau

Nassau works year-round, but bear in mind hurricane season in the Caribbean runs June to November. Best months are December to April: dry weather and perfect temperatures. These months are also pricier. For savings without sacrificing weather, aim for May, September or October, though hurricane risk persists in October. Always check the forecast before you travel.

What to do in Nassau and beyond

Nassau is the capital of the Bahamas and your gateway to paradise beaches, islands and diving. Cable Beach and Paradise Island are most accessible from town. The historic centre has museums and the famous Straw Market for local crafts. Don't miss Atlantis Resort (even just to see it), and if you can, day-trip to the Exuma Cays or Blue Lagoon Island. Lynden Pindling Airport is 20 minutes from the centre by taxi or bus.
