Best time to visit

Nassau has tropical weather year-round. Hurricane season in the Caribbean runs from June to November, so consider travelling between December and May for greater safety and weather stability. The dry season is most comfortable for enjoying beaches and outdoor activities.

What to do on arrival

Nassau is your gateway to the Bahamas. From Lynden Pindling airport (NAS) you'll land in the heart of the capital. Explore Cable Beach, visit the Atlantis Resort if you appreciate luxury architecture, stroll through the historic centre, and take day trips to nearby beaches and coral reefs. The Exuma Cays are a short distance away and worth the trip.
