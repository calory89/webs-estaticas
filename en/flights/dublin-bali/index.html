Best time to travel

Bali in November is excellent: end of the dry season, pleasant temperatures (28–30°C) and less rain than winter months. The prices you see now (October–November 2026) are competitive because it's not peak European holiday season. Avoid July–August if you're chasing low fares; December prices rise sharply.

You'll land at Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS), about 15 km from Denpasar. Transfers to areas like Seminyak, Ubud or Kuta are straightforward. Bali has beaches, temples, rice fields and a vibrant food scene. A flight with a stop gives you more recovery time before exploring the island.
