Best time to visit

Tallinn shines in summer (June to August) with long daylight hours and mild temperatures. If you want to skip the crowds, spring (May) and early autumn (September) are ideal with good weather. Winters are cold and dark, but if you're after the Northern Lights, December to February is your shot.

What to do when you arrive

Tallinn Airport (Lennart Meri Tallinn Airport, TLL) is 4 km from the city centre. Medieval Old Town is a Unesco site: walk the historic streets, visit Alexander Nevsky Cathedral and the city walls. Tallinn Bay offers stunning views, and the centre has cafés, shops and galleries. From here you can also take day trips to Lahemaa or ferry to Stockholm.
