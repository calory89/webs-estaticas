Best time to go

Cancún works year-round, but hurricane season runs June to November, so consider December to May for better weather. Those months are busier and pricier though. Shoulder seasons often offer the best value for money.

What to do in Cancún

Beach, diving and Maya ruins. Explore the Riviera Maya, cenotes in Tulum, or the archaeological sites at Chichén Itzá. Cancún International Airport is about 25 km from the centre. Shuttle buses, car hire and private transfers get you to your hotel easily.
