Best time to travel

Helsinki shines in summer (June–August) with nearly endless daylight, but prices climb. For budget travel, consider spring (April–May) or autumn (September). Winter (December–February) is colder and darker, though it can offer deals if you fancy the Nordic experience. February often sees fewer tourists than December–January.

What to do in Helsinki

Finland's capital blends Nordic modernity with design and history. Visit the white Cathedral, the Church in the Rock carved into granite, the indie shops and cafés of Kallio, and the Velho harbour with its markets. If you travel in summer, enjoy public saunas and ferries to the Stockholm Archipelago. Ateneum and Kiasma museums showcase contemporary art.
