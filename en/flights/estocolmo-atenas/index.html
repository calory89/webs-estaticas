Best time to visit

Athens enjoys pleasant weather most of the year. April to June and September to October offer comfortable temperatures and fewer crowds than July and August. These shoulder-season periods often have lower fares than peak summer, making your trip more enjoyable.

What to do when you arrive

Athens is the birthplace of Western civilisation: the Acropolis, the Parthenon and the Agora await. Stroll through Plaka, the old quarter with cobbled streets and authentic tavernas. The National Archaeological Museum holds classical treasures. Grab a coffee in Syntagma or climb Lycabettus at sunset. Athens International Airport 'Eleftherios Venizelos' connects well to the centre by metro (blue line, 40 minutes).
