Best time to visit

Singapore has tropical weather year-round. February is dry and pleasant with less rain. Avoid November to January if you prefer lower humidity. Low tourism demand in spring (February–March) usually means cheaper flights than summer or peak holidays.

What to do when you arrive

Singapore is perfect for 4–5 days: Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay, Merlion, Chinatown, Orchard Road for shopping, Hindu temples and night markets. Changi Airport is 20 km from centre; bus, MRT train or taxi are quick and cheap.

FAQ

When should I book to pay less?

What else is there to see in Singapore besides Marina Bay?

The Merlion, Gardens by the Bay, Chinatown with temples and markets, Orchard Road for shopping, Sentosa Island for beaches and theme parks, and Hindu temples in Little India.
