Get flights from Rome to Iceland from €185 return. The median price is €209, so you have good availability at a fair rate. Ryanair operates this route with one stopover, ideal if you're after the most budget-friendly option.

Price and when to book your Rome-Iceland flight

The lowest price is €185. Fares range between €185, €209, and €214 depending on availability. Ryanair usually offers better prices if you book well in advance, so don't leave it until the last minute. Check regularly and set up price alerts to catch good deals. Flexibility on dates is key: changing your travel week can make a real difference.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair is the airline on this route. The flight includes one stopover, so it's not a direct flight. Bear in mind the total travel time: the stopover adds hours, but the savings are substantial. Ryanair departs from Rome Fiumicino (FCO) and arrives at Keflavík (KEF), Iceland's main international airport.

Best time to visit Iceland

Travel season affects price. Summer months (June to August) tend to be pricier as it's peak season. For the best value, consider spring (April-May) or autumn (September-October), when you get better price and weather balance. Winter is cheaper but daylight hours are very short and conditions more severe.

What to do when you arrive in Iceland

You'll land at Keflavík, about 50 km from Reykjavík. The capital and surroundings have plenty to offer: the Blue Lagoon is unmissable, the northern fjords are stunning, and you'll find waterfalls, glaciers and sea cliffs. If you travel in winter, hunt for the Northern Lights. Rent a car to explore the Ring Road. Iceland is expensive, so eat at supermarkets when you can.

FAQ

Is the Rome-Iceland flight direct?

No, it includes one stopover. Ryanair operates this route with an intermediate stop, which increases total travel time but significantly reduces the ticket price.

What is the nearest airport to Reykjavík?

Keflavík (KEF) is the main international airport. It's about 50 km from Reykjavík. From there you can rent a car or take a bus to the capital.

Why is this flight so cheap?

Ryanair is a low-cost airline that cuts costs across the board. Plus, the stopover reduces the fare. If you want direct flights or fewer stops, you'll pay more, but this is the most budget-friendly option available.

When should I book to get the best price?

The earlier you book, the better. Ryanair releases fares months in advance. Ideally, book 2-3 months before your travel date. Avoid booking close to departure when prices spike.
