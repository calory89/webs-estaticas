Best time to visit

Tokyo is beautiful year-round, but spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) offer the most pleasant weather and less rain. Skip summer if you don't handle heat and humidity well. November is particularly attractive: mild temperatures and fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Tokyo won't leave you indifferent: walk through Shibuya, visit temples in Asakusa, explore Ginza for shopping, and eat at street markets. Narita or Haneda airport connects you quickly to the city centre by train. Plan at least 5–7 days to enjoy the city at a relaxed pace.
