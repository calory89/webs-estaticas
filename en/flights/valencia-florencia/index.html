Best time to visit

Florence is worth visiting year-round, but choose based on your preference: spring (March–May) offers good weather and fewer crowds; summer (June–August) is peak season with heat and tourists; autumn (September–October) has pleasant weather and fewer visitors than July–August; winter has fewer tourists but cold temperatures and shorter days.

What to do when you arrive

The Cathedral of Santa Maria del Fiore is Florence's defining landmark—visit it first. Cross the Ponte Vecchio, explore the Uffizi Gallery if you're patient with queues, wander the medieval centre, climb Brunelleschi's dome (if you don't mind stairs), and dine in a trattoria in Oltrarno. Peretola Airport is 4 km from the centre; the SITA bus runs directly into town.
