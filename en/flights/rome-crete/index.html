Price and when to buy

The lowest price we've found is €177 return. Sample prices range from €177 to €212. Booking 6–8 weeks in advance usually beats last-minute fares. The earlier you purchase, the more flight times and dates you'll have to choose from.

Airlines and stops

Ryanair is the airline on this route. The outbound flight includes 1 stop, so it's not direct. This means a longer journey, but it allows access to very competitive fares. Always check the connection time when you book.

Best time to visit

Crete is worth visiting year-round. May to October bring peak heat and zero rain, but also peak prices and crowds. April and November offer good weather at better rates. Winter (December to February) is cheaper, with mild temperatures and empty beaches.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Heraklion Airport (HER). The island has stunning beaches (Elafonissi, Balos), Minoan palaces (Knossos), Samaria Gorge, and whitewashed villages with sea views. Rent a car or use local buses. Eat where locals eat: cheese boards and retsina wine.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price Rome to Crete?

€177 return is the lowest we've tracked. The median sits at €209, so look for date flexibility and book early.

Does Ryanair fly direct Rome to Crete?

No, it has 1 stop on the outbound. The return may vary. Always check your booking confirmation to see the connection airport and layover time.

Which month is cheapest to fly to Crete?

November, December, January and February are usually the best value. May to September prices climb steeply. Check the fare calendar when searching to see exact variations.

What must-see sites are there in Crete?

Knossos Palace (Minoan ruins), Samaria Gorge, Elafonissi and Balos beaches, and towns like Rethymno. Three days is the minimum to enjoy without rushing.
