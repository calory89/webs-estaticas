Price and when to book

Flights range from €665 to €853 depending on your chosen dates. Book 2–3 months ahead and stay flexible with departure and return dates to grab the lowest fares. The sooner you lock in your ticket, the better your chances of a good price.

Air Canada: stopovers and flight details

All flights operate with Air Canada and include 2 stopovers. There is no direct option on this route. Outbound departs early morning (example: 07:00 from Vienna) and return leaves mid-morning (example: 11:50 from Fort Lauderdale). Factor in extra time due to stopovers; total transit time will be lengthy.

Best time to visit Fort Lauderdale

Fort Lauderdale shines November to March: expect pleasant temperatures (20–25°C) and lower humidity. It's peak season, so prices climb. Hunt for deals in May or September if you can handle heat and rain. Avoid hurricane season (June–October).

What to do in Fort Lauderdale

Fort Lauderdale boasts miles of beaches, the iconic Strip promenade, diving and cruise ports. Inland you'll find museums, shopping and nightlife. The Everglades are nearby for wild adventures. Fort Lauderdale airport (FLL) sits 30 km from the city centre with solid public transport and car rental links.
