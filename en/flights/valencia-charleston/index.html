Best time to visit

Charleston is pleasant almost year-round. November to March brings milder weather, though that's hurricane season peaks August to October. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer good weather with fewer crowds than summer. Avoid July and August if you dislike intense heat and peak tourism.

What to do in Charleston

Charleston is a charming coastal city with plenty of character. Walk through the Historic District, visit Fort Sumter, enjoy nearby beaches, and savour local cuisine. The Flower Market is iconic, and there are museums and galleries for everyone. It's a perfect blend of history, beach life, and excellent food.
