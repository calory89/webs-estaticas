Best time to travel

Bulgaria shines in spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October): mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winters are cold but ideal if you seek lower prices and fewer tourists. Avoid July and August if you don't want extreme heat and peak season chaos.

What to do when you arrive in Sofia

Bulgaria's capital blends medieval history with modern life. Visit the Alexander Nevsky Basilica, stroll through the old town and taste local cuisine. Sofia is your gateway to Rila and Bachkovo monasteries, just a few hours' drive away. Prices are very affordable compared to other European destinations.
