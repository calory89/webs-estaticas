Best time to visit

Athens is attractive year-round, but spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer perfect weather without summer heat or July-August crowds. If you travel in low season (November-February), you'll find tighter prices and fewer tourists, though some attractions may have reduced hours.

What to do in Athens

The Acropolis and Parthenon are unmissable. Explore Plaka, climb Lycabettus Hill at sunset, visit the Acropolis Museum and wander through the Ancient Agora. Don't miss eating at a local taverna, trying Greek food and relaxing on nearby islands like Hydra or Aegina if you have extra time.
