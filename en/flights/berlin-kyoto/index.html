Airline and stopovers

Finnair is your carrier on this route. Expect 1 stopover, typically in Stockholm or Helsinki, which is standard for Germany-to-Japan flights. There are no direct flights on this route, so plan for around 15–18 hours total travel time including connection.

Best time to visit

December works well: Kyoto glows with temple illuminations and fewer crowds. Spring (March–April) brings cherry blossoms but higher prices. Autumn (October–November) is more affordable with perfect weather. Avoid summer due to heat and humidity.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Osaka's Kansai Airport, 75 km from Kyoto. Visit the temples of Fushimi Inari and Kinkaku-ji, walk through Arashiyama's bamboo grove, and explore the traditional Gion district. Local trains are fast and cheap; buy a JR Pass if you plan to travel around Japan.
