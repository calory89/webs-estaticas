Best time to visit

Milan is pleasant year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer great weather with fewer crowds. Avoid August if you don't enjoy peak season. Winter is quiet and fares can be competitive.

What to do when you arrive

The Duomo is a must. Galleria Vittorio Emanuele is a must-walk. Museums like Pinacoteca di Brera are worth your time. Eat at small restaurants in Brera or Navigli. Spare a day? Lake Como is an hour away.
