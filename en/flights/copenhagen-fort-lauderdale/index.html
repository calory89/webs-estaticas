Best time to visit

Fort Lauderdale shines in the Northern Hemisphere winter (November to March): perfect weather, no tropical rain. Summer (June to September) is hot, humid and hurricane-prone. Shoulder season (April-May, October) offers good balance between price and climate. Avoid peak tourist months if you prefer quieter surroundings.

What to do on arrival

You land in Fort Lauderdale, the gateway to Broward's coast. Explore white-sand beaches, stroll the marina with its shops and restaurants, enjoy the waterfront atmosphere. The town centre offers nightlife and shopping; natural attractions like the Everglades are just over an hour away. It's an ideal base to relax and soak up Florida's vibe.

Does Air Canada fly direct from Copenhagen to Fort Lauderdale?

What should I see in Fort Lauderdale?

Broward's beaches are the main draw: sand, sea and water sports. The marina offers walks, shops and dining. Don't miss the town centre's nightlife and, if nature appeals, the Everglades are nearby.

When is the best time to visit Fort Lauderdale?

Northern Hemisphere winter (November to March) is ideal: dry, pleasant weather. Summer is very hot and humid, with hurricane risk. Shoulder months (April-May, October) offer good alternatives if you want fewer crowds and reasonable prices.
