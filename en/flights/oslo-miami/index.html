Best time to travel

Miami is a year-round beach destination, but October to April offer the sweetest weather: less swelter and less rain. July and August are humid and hot. Prices adjust with demand, so compare flexible dates if you can. December and January peak with tourists and prices may climb, but they also deliver perfect dry weather.

What to do when you arrive

Miami Beach and South Beach are non-negotiable for sand and nightlife. Vizcaya Museum and Wynwood Walls tick the culture box. For nature, the Everglades are an hour out. Little Havana gives you the Cuban heartbeat of the city. If you travel in December (like the sample date), expect 25–26°C with clear skies, perfect for exploring on foot.
