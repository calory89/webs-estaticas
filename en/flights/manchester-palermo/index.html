Best time to visit

Sicily is wonderful year-round. Summer (June–September) brings peak tourism and higher prices. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Winter is quieter and cheaper, though rain is more common.

What to do when you arrive in Palermo

Palermo airport (Falcone-Borsellino) is 35 km from the centre, with easy access to the city. Visit the Cathedral, Palazzo dei Normanni, Teatro Politeama. Eat at street markets (Capo, Vucciria). Head to Mondello for beaches. Sicily offers Greek archaeology, distinctive food, and coastal towns for day trips.
