Best time to visit the Maldives

The Maldives has tropical weather year-round. The dry season runs from November to April, perfect for beaches and snorkelling. These months are busier and prices tend to be higher. For lower fares, consider May to October, though you'll face more rain. Check the weather for your specific dates before deciding.

What to do when you arrive in the Maldives

Velana International Airport (MLE) is in Malé. Most resorts offer boat or seaplane transfers from there. The Maldives is known for coral reefs, diving, white-sand beaches and pristine islands. Explore the capital Malé, visit local islands or stay at a private resort. You won't need a car; most travel is by water.
