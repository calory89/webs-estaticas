Best time to visit

Athens shines in autumn (September to November) and winter (December to February), with pleasant temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. Spring (March to May) is equally lovely. Skip July and August if you prefer to avoid intense heat and peak tourist masses. Off-season prices drop too, making winter your sweet spot for both budget and enjoying the city properly.

What to do when you arrive

Athens airport (Eleftherios Venizelos) is about 30 km from the centre. Public transport is fast and cheap. In the city, explore the Acropolis and the Parthenon, stroll through Plaka, climb Lycabettus Hill at sunset, and visit museums like the Acropolis Museum or the National Archaeological Museum. If you rent a car, day trips to Delphi, Meteora, or nearby islands are within reach.
