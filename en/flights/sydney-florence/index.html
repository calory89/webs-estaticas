Best time to visit

Florence is beautiful in spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October), with mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. June-August are peak months and pricier. If you're budget-conscious, winter (November-February) offers lower fares, though with cooler conditions. A 10-day trip from Sydney is reasonable to manage jet lag.

What to see and do in Florence

The Florence Cathedral (Duomo), the Uffizi Gallery, and Palazzo Vecchio are must-sees. Cross the Ponte Vecchio, explore the San Nicolás neighbourhood, and reserve time for museums and churches. Climbing the dome is free with a pre-booked ticket. Florence Airport (Peretola/FLR) connects to the city centre by bus and fast train. Eat fresh pasta, try bistecca alla fiorentina, and browse antique bookshops.

What else is there to see in Florence besides the Duomo?

The Uffizi Gallery (book online), Palazzo Vecchio, Ponte Vecchio, the Basilica of Santa Maria Novella, and the Convent of San Marcos. South of the river (Oltrarno) is less touristy and very photogenic. Allow 3-4 days minimum to avoid rushing.
