Best time to visit

Phu Quoc has a tropical climate. Dry season runs November to April with less heat and rainfall. Avoid May to October due to monsoons. Dubai is scorching in summer (June–August), so travel in winter or spring for better weather.

What to do when you arrive in Phu Quoc

Phu Quoc is an island paradise with white-sand beaches, national parks and diving. Explore Duong Dong village, visit local pearl farms and savour Vietnamese cuisine. There are all-inclusive resorts and budget-friendly options for everyone.
