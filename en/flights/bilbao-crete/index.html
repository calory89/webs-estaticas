Best time to visit

Crete is a beach and culture destination year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer perfect weather without summer crowds. Winter (November–March) is cheaper and quieter, though it rains more. Check the forecast and choose by priority.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Heraklion Airport. Bus and taxi connect to the city centre. Visit the Palace of Knossos, explore Vai and Balos beaches, wander Rethymno's old town, or discover mountain villages inland. Crete deserves at least a week. Car hire gives you freedom to explore.
