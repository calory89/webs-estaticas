Best time to visit Vietnam

Vietnam is best visited between November and April, when the weather is drier and temperatures are mild. This is peak season, so prices may be higher, but you'll travel in optimal conditions. If you're looking to save, consider May or June, although there will be more humidity. Avoid September and October due to typhoons.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

Hanoi is your gateway to Vietnam. Walk through the Old Quarter, visit Ho Chi Minh's Mausoleum and stroll around Hoan Kiem Lake. From here you can take excursions to Halong Bay, mountain villages in the north or travel by train to the south. Local cuisine is exceptional: don't miss pho and egg coffee.
