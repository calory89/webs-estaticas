Best time to visit

Fez is comfortable almost year-round. Autumn (September–November) and spring (March–May) are ideal: mild weather, fewer crowds and perfect visibility in the medina. Summer is scorching and winter can bring rain. If you're hunting for lower fares, try travelling off-season (January–February or November), though flight availability will be lower too.

What to do when you arrive

Fez is one of North Africa's most authentic cities. The medina Fes el Bali is a UNESCO World Heritage site: maze-like alleys, leather work, perfumeries and the buzz of centuries-old souks. See the leather tanners (one of the most mesmerising sights), Al Quaraouiyine University and the blue gates of Bab Boujloud. From Fez you can explore Meknes (1 hour away) or the Rif mountains.
