Best time to visit

Marrakech is worth visiting year-round, but autumn and spring offer pleasant temperatures without summer's intense heat. October and November are ideal with mild weather and fewer crowds. Skip July and August if you don't handle extreme heat well.

What to do when you arrive

Marrakech's medina is a must: Jemaa el-Fnaa square, the souks and Bahia Palace. Don't miss Majorelle Gardens or Koutoubia Mosque. For relaxation, traditional hammams are affordable and accessible. Menara airport is close to the city centre, about 6 km away.
