Cheap flights Brussels to Rome from €55

55 € returnTypical price: 59 €See flights and dates ✈️

Fly Brussels to Rome from €55 return with Ryanair. The median price hovers around €59, so you have solid options if you're after savings. Here's how to make the most of them.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €55 return. Fares range from €55 to €67 depending on dates. To grab the bottom rate, book as far ahead as possible: Ryanair rewards early bookers. If you have flexibility, try shifting your dates by a day or two; small changes can cut costs by several euros.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight both ways. No connections, so you travel uninterrupted. Keep in mind Ryanair's strict baggage policy: a small cabin bag is free, but large rucksacks and suitcases carry extra fees. Check their T&Cs before purchasing.

Best time to visit

Rome is busy most of the year, but November to February offers lower fares and fewer crowds at monuments and museums. July and August peak in price. For good weather without the premium, aim for April–May or October. The sample flight (14–18 November) hits a sweet spot.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Rome Fiumicino or Ciampino (depending on Ryanair). Train, bus and taxi link the airport to the city centre. Don't miss the Colosseum, Vatican, Trevi Fountain and Trastevere. Most of Rome is walkable; a 3-day Roma Pass saves on museums and transport.

FAQ

Why are Brussels to Rome flights so cheap?

Ryanair competes hard on major European routes and prices aggressively to fill seats. In return, it charges for baggage and add-ons. They're budget flights, but if you travel light, the savings are real.

Is Ryanair safe?

Yes. Ryanair holds standard EU safety certification. Its 'low-cost' reputation doesn't affect safety standards, only comfort and extra services.

Do these €55 fares include luggage?

The base price covers the ticket and a small cabin bag. Checked baggage, seat selection and onboard food cost extra. Budget carefully before booking to know your real total.

What's the best date to book from now?

We checked on 23 October; November flights (like the 14th) still have booking lead time. If flexible, try different date combos on Ryanair.com, sometimes moving a day saves €5–10.
