Flights from Brussels to Seoul cost from €434 return, with a median of €561. Air China offers the best options with 1 stopover. If you travel in December, this route is more affordable than peak season.

Price and when to book

The base price is €434. Most bookings sit around €561, with options up to €635. Prices climb as departure approaches, so book 2–3 months ahead for the lowest fares. December is a good window because it's low season on this route. Avoid April–May and October–November, peak demand periods for Asia travel.

Airlines and stopovers

Air China operates this route with 1 stopover included. There are no direct flights from Brussels to Seoul, so expect at least one connection. The typical stop is at a major Chinese hub. Though it means longer travel time, the price remains competitive against routes with multiple connections.

Best time to visit

Seoul shines in spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October), with mild temperatures and clear skies. December is cold but dry, perfect if you want to avoid crowds and peak prices. Summer (June–August) is hot and humid, though fares remain reasonable. Winter (January–February) is harsh, but offers lower prices if you can handle the cold.

What to do when you arrive in Seoul

You'll land at Incheon, Seoul's main international airport (35 km from the city centre). It's well served by rail and direct coaches into town. Explore Gangnam, the modern district; Myeongdong, packed with shops and food; Gyeongbokgung Palace; and Dongdaemun Design Plaza. Korean cuisine, themed cafés, and nightlife make Seoul unmissable. Book accommodation early in areas like Hongdae or Insadong for better rates.
