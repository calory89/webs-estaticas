Best time to visit Vietnam

Vietnam is a year-round destination, but dry and cool months (October to April) offer the best weather to explore the country. Avoid the southwest monsoon if you want to see Hanoi and the north without constant rain. Peak tourist season is December and January, so expect bigger crowds and slightly higher prices in those months.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

You'll land at Noi Bai International Airport, about 25 km from the city. Once in Hanoi, don't miss the Old Quarter, the Temple of Literature and the colonial opera house. From there, day trips and longer excursions are easy and cheap: Ha Long Bay, Sapa for trekking, or Hue and Da Nang further south. Domestic transport is affordable and straightforward.

October to April is the best weather window. December and January see peak demand, so consider October, November, February or March if you want potentially lower fares and fewer tourists.
