Best time to visit

Iceland is stunning year-round. Winter (Nov–Feb) brings aurora borealis potential and extreme weather; summer (Jun–Aug) offers nearly endless daylight and better conditions. Choose based on what appeals: frozen landscape or green wilderness. Flight availability at good prices depends on many factors beyond season alone.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Keflavík (KEF). Easy access to Reykjavík from there. Iceland offers the Golden Circle (geysers, waterfalls, tectonic plates), Jökulsárlón glacier lagoon, Ásbyrgi canyon, and dramatic cliffs. Rent a car if you're up for it: Icelandic roads are unique but demanding.
