Price and when to book

The cheapest flight is €268 return; other examples sit at €277 and €288. The gap between the lowest and median prices is tiny, signalling consistent availability at good rates. For this route, book 2–3 months ahead. Skip last-minute purchases; prices climb fast. Check Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday mornings, when airlines refresh fares.

Airline and stopovers

Wizz Air is your option here, with 2 stopovers on the outbound. No direct Berlin-Mykonos flights exist, so expect intermediate stops. This extends your journey but keeps costs down. The return in the sample departs at 08:30, so get up early if you want that slot. Choose your stopovers based on available time: quick connections (1.5–2 hours) work fine if you've checked luggage; if not, you can comfortably handle 3–4 hours.

Best time to travel

Mykonos peaks in summer (June–August), with higher prices and crowds. For value and calm, try May, September or October: decent weather, fewer tourists, moderate fares. January–March is low season; expect cooler, wetter conditions, but you'll find the year's lowest prices. The sample flight is in January, perfect if you're budget-conscious and don't mind variable weather.

What to do when you arrive in Mykonos

Mykonos airport (JMK) is 2 km from town; take a local bus or taxi to your hotel. Mykonos shines for its sandy beaches (Psarou, Paradise, Super Paradise), seafront tavernas and nightlife. Walk the old town's whitewashed lanes and Venetian windmills, hop to nearby Delos (birthplace of Apollo in Greek myth) or unwind on quieter south-coast beaches. Fresh fish and feta appear on every menu; try the local wine too.

FAQ

What's the cheapest flight Berlin to Mykonos?

€268 return with Wizz Air. This isn't a promotion, it's the base fare for this route. Other options are available at €277 and €288.

Are there direct flights from Berlin to Mykonos?

No. Wizz Air operates this route with 2 stopovers on the outbound leg. Direct flights between these cities aren't available regularly, so stopovers are standard and help keep prices low.

How far in advance should I book for a good price?

Book 2–3 months ahead. The sooner the better; wait too long and fares rise, especially for summer travel.

What's there to do in Mykonos besides beaches?

Explore the old town with whitewashed streets and windmills, visit Paraportiani Church, take a ferry to archaeological Delos (45 minutes away), and eat fresh seafood at waterfront tavernas. For peace, skip weekend nights in town; head to southern or northern beaches instead.
