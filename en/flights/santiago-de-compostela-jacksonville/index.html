Best time to visit

Jacksonville is pleasant nearly year-round. Winter (December to February) is ideal if you want to avoid intense summer heat. Hurricane season runs August to October, so skip those months if possible. Search for flexible date options: small changes can save you €50–€100.

What to do when you arrive

Jacksonville offers beaches, history and plenty to explore. The Historic District is a classic stop; St. Augustine, America's oldest city, is 45 minutes away. Enjoy local coffee, walk along River Street and don't miss the Atlantic beaches. The airport is about 20 km from the centre: car hire or public transport get you around easily.
