Best time to visit

Istanbul is appealing year-round. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. July and August are pricier and more packed. Winter (December-February) is cold but low season with fewer tourists.

What to do when you arrive in Istanbul

You'll land at Istanbul Airport (IST), with good connections to the city centre. The Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia, Topkapi Palace and the Grand Bazaar are must-sees on the European side. Don't miss a sunset Bosphorus cruise or explore Galata and Beyoglu. Public transport is accessible and affordable.
