Best time to visit

Riga shines in winter for Christmas markets (December) and New Year. Summer (June to August) brings long days and mild weather. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer fewer crowds and steadier fares. Skip peak dates if you want to save money.

What to do in Riga

Latvia's capital blends medieval Old Town with early 20th-century Art Nouveau architecture. Stroll along the Daugava river, explore Riga Cathedral and taste Baltic cuisine. Winter Christmas markets fill the squares. The airport is 15–20 minutes from the city centre.
