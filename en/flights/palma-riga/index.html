Price and when to book

Tickets range from €154 to €164 based on the sample checked on 22 October 2026. For this route, booking 6–8 weeks ahead works well if you have flexible dates. Ryanair typically offers better fares on weekdays, so avoid Fridays, Saturdays and public holidays if you're hunting the lowest price.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this connection with 2 stops on the outbound leg. The example flight departs Palma at 05:55 and returns from Riga on 12 December at 13:45. With two breaks, expect 8–10 hours total journey time. Get to the desk early: Ryanair is strict on schedules and hand luggage limits.

Best time to visit

Riga shines in winter for Christmas markets (December) and New Year. Summer (June to August) brings long days and mild weather. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer fewer crowds and steadier fares. Skip peak dates if you want to save money.

What to do in Riga

Latvia's capital blends medieval Old Town with early 20th-century Art Nouveau architecture. Stroll along the Daugava river, explore Riga Cathedral and taste Baltic cuisine. Winter Christmas markets fill the squares. The airport is 15–20 minutes from the city centre.
