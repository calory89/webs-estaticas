Best time to visit

Agadir has pleasant weather almost year-round, with mild winters (15-22°C) and warm summers (25-30°C). Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to October) are ideal: beautiful beaches without intense heat. Avoid July and August if you're not keen on very hot weather.

What to do when you arrive

Agadir is a beach destination: sandy shores, a lively seafront promenade and a relaxed vibe. Walk through the medina, try local food at the souks, or take trips to Souss-Massa National Park. Evenings are quiet and safe in the tourist centre. Perfect for unwinding without hassle.
