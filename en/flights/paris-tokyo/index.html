What to do when you arrive in Tokyo

Tokyo is vast: Senso-ji in Asakusa, Shibuya Crossing, Shinjuku district, museums and temples everywhere. Narita or Haneda airport connects you quickly to the city centre by train. Plan by neighbourhood, eat anywhere (the food is incredible) and use public transport with confidence.
