Best time to visit

Sal Island has stable weather year-round, but the most pleasant months are November to June, when temperatures are milder and rainfall is lower. July and August can be very hot. Avoid peak season if you prefer fewer crowds, though prices on this route tend to be consistent throughout the year.

What to do when you arrive

Sal Island is renowned for its white sand beaches and turquoise waters. Visit the town of Santa Maria, dive in coral reefs, or relax at Ponta Leme. Amílcar Cabral International Airport connects directly to the island; transfers to hotels are quick. The island is small but perfect for a real getaway.
