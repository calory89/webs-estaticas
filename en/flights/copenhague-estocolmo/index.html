Best time to visit

Stockholm shines in summer (June–August) with long daylight and mild weather. Spring (April–May) and early autumn (September) are less crowded and equally lovely. Winter is magical if you're chasing the Northern Lights, though pricier. High season pushes fares up, so plan around your budget and preferences.

What to do when you arrive

Stockholm offers islands to explore, the Vasa Museum, Gamla Stan's old town, and an excellent food scene. Arlanda Airport (ARN) is 20 minutes from the centre by train. Copenhagen won't disappoint either: Tivoli, the Little Mermaid, Nyhavn's canals, and trendy neighbourhoods like Nørrebro. The Skåne region between both cities offers great day trips.
