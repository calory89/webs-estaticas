Best time to travel

Tokyo is a year-round destination, but spring (March–April) and autumn (October–November) offer perfect weather and stunning scenery. These periods also see higher demand, so prices tend to rise. Summer is warm and humid; winter is cold but dry. Travel in low season if you're hunting for the best price.

What to do in Tokyo

Tokyo is a megacity with neighbourhoods for everyone: Shibuya and Shinjuku for nightlife, Asakusa for traditional temples, Ginza for luxury shopping. Don't miss the Imperial Palace, Tsukiji Market, or the famous pedestrian crossing. Get around on the bullet train (Shinkansen) to Kyoto, Osaka, or Hiroshima. Eat at ramen shops, conveyor belt sushi bars, and tonkatsu restaurants.

What should I see in Tokyo in 5 days?

Spend days exploring Shibuya, Asakusa, and Ginza in central Tokyo; visit temples like Senso-ji. Climb Tokyo Tower or Skytree for panoramic views. Eat at markets and traditional alleys. If you have time, take a day trip to Nikko or Mount Fuji.
