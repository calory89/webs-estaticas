Airline and stops

Best time to visit

Rome is a year-round destination, but October and November are ideal: pleasant weather, fewer crowds than summer, and cheaper fares. March to May are also excellent. Skip July and August if you dislike queues and high prices. Christmas and Easter drive costs up.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Fiumicino or Ciampino. The Colosseum, Roman Forum, Trevi Fountain and the Vatican are essential. Spend 3–4 days walking the historic centre, eating authentic pasta, and exploring areas like Trastevere. Rome is best explored on foot: wear comfortable shoes.

FAQ

Can I find Bilbao–Rome flights cheaper than 56€?

Unlikely. 56€ return is the lowest recorded. It's hard to beat, but possible if you have complete date flexibility and book well in advance. The median is 62€, a more realistic target.

Does Vueling fly direct from Bilbao to Rome?

Yes. It's a direct flight with no stops. Departure is early morning from Bilbao (example: 07:30) and you arrive in Rome the same day.

What's the cheapest month to fly to Rome?

October, November, March, April and May usually cost less than summer. Avoid July, August, Christmas and Easter if you want budget fares.

Which airport do I land at in Rome?

Vueling flies to Rome Fiumicino (FCO) or Rome Ciampino (CIA). Fiumicino is closer to the city centre. Both have easy public transport links downtown.
