Cheap flights Copenhagen to Bruges

241 € returnTypical price: 254 €See flights and dates ✈️

The Copenhagen-Bruges route starts from €241 return with SAS, with a median of €254. It's a short, direct journey perfect for a week-long getaway between Denmark and Belgium.

Price and when to book

Prices on this route range between €241 and €255 for return flights. The median sits at €254, giving you an idea of a fair price. As a general rule, book 6-8 weeks in advance to secure better fares. The longer you wait from the search date, the more expensive it typically becomes.

Airline and number of stops

SAS (Scandinavian Airlines) operates this route as a direct flight with no stops. You travel from CPH (Copenhagen) to BRU (Brussels) without any aircraft changes, which greatly simplifies your journey and avoids unnecessary delays.

Best time to travel

Copenhagen is spectacular in summer (June to August), but also wonderful in spring and winter. Bruges looks beautiful all year: in winter with Christmas decorations and fewer tourists, in spring and autumn with ideal weather. Avoid August if you prefer smaller crowds.

What to do when you arrive

In Bruges, stroll the canals and Market Square, visit the Basilica of the Holy Blood and the Belfry. From Brussels you can take trips to nearby cities like Ghent or Antwerp. Try Belgian beer and local waffles.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for this route?

The lowest price recorded is €241 return. The median is €254, so if you find something around that range, it's a good deal.

Does SAS fly direct Copenhagen to Bruges?

Yes, SAS operates direct flights on this route without stops, which makes your journey much simpler.

When are there fewer tourists in Bruges?

November to March (except Christmas) sees fewer visitors. Spring and autumn are also good times with milder weather and smaller crowds than summer.

How good is travelling from Brussels-Zaventem airport to visit Bruges?

Excellent. From Brussels airport there are direct trains to Bruges every 30-60 minutes taking 20 minutes. It's very convenient and affordable.
