Best time to visit Berlin

Berlin is worth visiting at any time, but the most pleasant months are May–June and September–October, when the weather is warm without being stifling. Winter (December–February) has its charm if you fancy Christmas markets or snow, though temperatures drop. July–August see peak tourism and prices may rise.

What to do when you arrive

Berlin has endless options: world-class museums (Museum Island), the Berlin Wall and East Side Gallery, bohemian neighbourhoods like Kreuzberg and Neukölln, or the Reichstag if you're into history. Public transport from the airport is well connected, so it's easy to reach the city centre. Book museums in advance to avoid queues.

What to see in Berlin in 5 days?

Spend a day on Museum Island and the historic centre, another on the Berlin Wall and East Side Gallery, one more at Charlottenburg or the Reichstag, and leave time to wander neighbourhoods like Kreuzberg, Schöneberg or Neukölln. Berlin is huge, so don't try to see it all.
