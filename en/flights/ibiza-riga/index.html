Best time to visit

Riga shines in summer (June–August) with long days and mild weather. Winter (December–February) is freezing but visually stunning with snow. Spring and autumn strike a balance between price and climate. Skip peak seasons to save more.

What to do when you arrive in Riga

Riga airport (RIX) sits 13 km from the city centre. Explore the Old Town (UNESCO World Heritage), Art Nouveau buildings, the Daugava River and museums. Eat in a local pub and taste Latvian cuisine. The capital is compact and walkable.
