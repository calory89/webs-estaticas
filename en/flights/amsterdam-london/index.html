Cheap flights Amsterdam-London: from €119 return with easyJet

Amsterdam to London is one of Europe's most competitive routes, and you'll notice it in the price. We found tickets from €119 return, with a median of €120. easyJet dominates this route with direct flights that get you out of Amsterdam and into London in just over an hour.

Price: when to book and how to save

The range we saw was €119 to €121 for the journeys we checked on 22 October 2026. The median sits at €120, giving you a clear idea of the real market price. As always on short European routes, booking early helps: the sooner you reserve, the better the price. easyJet flights on this line have limited seats and fill up fast, especially if you travel at weekends or holidays.

Airline and stops: what you need to know

easyJet operates this route with no stops. Your outbound leaves Amsterdam and arrives direct in London. No plane changes, no airport waits in between. The return works the same way. It's a short journey (just over an hour of flight time) and direct end to end. Luggage: keep in mind easyJet charges for checked baggage, so review their terms if you need to check a bag.

Best time to travel

Amsterdam and London are year-round destinations, but each season has its strengths. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) usually offer good weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is cheaper but colder and wetter. If you travel around peak dates (Christmas, Easter, bank holidays), expect higher prices. Mid-week flights usually have better price-to-availability ratios than Fridays and Sundays.

What to do when you arrive: London awaits

London has an almost infinite offer: Big Ben, Tower of London, British Museum, walks along the Thames, West End theatres, markets like Camden or Borough. The airport is accessible to the centre by bus, train or taxi. Amsterdam is also worth exploring on the way: canals, museums (Van Gogh, Rijksmuseum), Jordaan neighbourhood, typical markets. If you stop in Amsterdam after you return, make the most of it.

FAQ

€119 return with easyJet. That's the lowest we've seen in our search on 22 October. The median is €120, so expect to pay around that figure. Prices in the €119–121 range are normal for this route.

Is the Amsterdam-London flight direct with easyJet?

Yes, completely direct. No stops, no changes. From Amsterdam to London in one flight.

Which airline flies Amsterdam-London?

What to see in London besides the obvious?

Borough Market for good food, Portobello Road at weekends (vintage and curios), Greenwich for the meridian and views, Notting Hill for a leisurely stroll, Covent Garden for something more touristy. The main museums (British Museum, National Gallery, V&A) are free for general admission. Walk: London looks better on foot than by bus.
