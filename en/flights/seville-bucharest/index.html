Fly from Seville to Bucharest for less than you think: from €113 return with Wizz Air Malta. Median prices sit around €139, so if you spot that range, it's worth booking. Bucharest awaits with Balkan architecture, the Dambovita riverside and nightlife that never stops.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we've seen is €113 return. The median price is €139, with samples at €113, €139 and €159. Book as early as possible: Wizz Air's low fares vanish quickly. Search mid-week for better deals, and avoid school holidays and bank holidays.

Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Wizz Air Malta on direct flights with no stops. The journey is fast: departure from Seville around 18:30, arrival in Bucharest around 22:30 (local time). Return is at 14:30, so you have the whole morning for a last stroll. Hand baggage included; checked bag from €20.

Best time to visit

Bucharest shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather, fewer crowds and reasonable prices. January–February is colder, but if you want city without crowds and very low fares, it works. July–August is peak season with heat and tourists. Skip December if you don't fancy Christmas crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Otopeni Airport is 16 km from the centre. Take bus 783 or the train to Revolution Square. Don't miss the Palace of Parliament (Europe's largest building), Lipscani Boulevard with shops and terraces, and an evening walk along the Dambovita. Try mici (meat rolls) and tuică (local brandy) at any traditional hanu.

FAQ

Is €113 the final price or are there booking fees?

It's the return fare we recorded. Wizz Air adds airport taxes and optional insurance at checkout, so check carefully before paying. Our price is a guide; always verify on the official website.

Is it a direct flight Seville to Bucharest?

Yes, direct with no stopovers on Wizz Air Malta. No intermediate stops, so it's the most convenient option.

Which airline flies this route and is it reliable?

Wizz Air Malta operates these fares. It has good reputation on European routes, though occasional delays happen. Pack light carry-on and add checked baggage only if you need it, to save money.

What to see in Bucharest in 3–4 days?

Palace of Parliament, Lipscani (old town), Stavropoleos Church, Dorobanți district for trendy cafés, and a day trip: Brașov or Sinaia by train are 2 hours away. Eat well, walk a lot and haggle in markets.
