The €107 return is the lowest floor we've seen. Most tickets range between €107 and €143, with a median of €132. The search was run on 22 October 2026 for December travel. The earlier you book, the better: these budget prices won't wait. Check daily if your dates aren't flexible.

Airline and stopovers

Best time to go

December is mid-season in Bucharest: lighter tourism than summer but cold weather (0-5°C). If you prefer better conditions, spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) are ideal, though prices rise. Bucharest has Christmas markets in winter if you travel then.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Otopeni Airport (OTP), 16 km from the centre. Old Town, Parliament of Romania (striking communist building), Orthodox churches and museums fill your days. Danube walks, Romanian food and nightlife round it out. Three or four days cover the essentials.
