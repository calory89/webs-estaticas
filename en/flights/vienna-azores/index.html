Cheap flights from Vienna to Azores: from €263

Fly from Vienna to the Azores for just €263 return. Average price around €301. With Ryanair and one stop, this route gives you access to one of Portugal's most authentic destinations without breaking the bank.

Price and when to book

Prices start from €263 for the full trip, with a median of €301. We've also seen fares at €302. Book in advance: the earlier you lock in your dates, the better price you'll find. October is a good time to search without rush, but move quickly if you spot a fare you like.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route. Expect one stop on the outbound journey: it's not direct, but that's standard for connections to the Azores. The return departs Ponta Delgada around 09:35, and the outbound leaves Vienna around 07:35. Travel with hand luggage only to save on extra fees.

Best time to visit

The Azores are worth visiting year-round, but April to October offers the most stable weather. January, when some of these prices pop up, is low season: fewer tourists, but higher chance of rain. Book well ahead regardless of season, as pricing depends more on how far in advance you book than on the time of year.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in Ponta Delgada, the capital of São Miguel. Wander through Porta Cidade, relax in the natural pools at Poço da Dona Beija, or hike the Santiago volcano. The Azores are perfect for trekking, whale watching, and genuine disconnect. Don't miss the local food scene.

FAQ

What's the cheapest fare from Vienna to the Azores?

From €263 return with Ryanair. That's the lowest we've recorded. The median sits at €301, so there are solid options in that range.

Is the flight direct or does it have stops?

One stop on the outbound. There are no direct flights from Vienna to the Azores, but this connection is standard for reaching Ponta Delgada from Central Europe.

Which airline operates this route?

Ryanair. It's the main carrier on this link, so check their baggage policy if you want to travel without surprise cost additions.

When should I book to get the best price?

Book as far ahead as you can. There's no specific month when fares drop significantly, but the earlier you book, the more choice and better prices you'll have. If you spot a fare you like, don't wait.
