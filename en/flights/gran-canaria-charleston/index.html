Price and when to book

Flights start from €799 for a return ticket. In our price sample, we found options ranging from €799 to €909. For this transatlantic route, booking at least 6–8 weeks in advance is recommended. Prices tend to rise as departure approaches, so don't miss offers in this range.

Airline and stops

easyJet operates this route with two stops on the outbound flight. It's not a direct flight, but the price is very competitive for a transatlantic connection. Keep in mind that two stops means longer total travel time, but you'll save significantly compared to direct flights.

Best time to travel

Charleston is pleasant year-round, but the best period is October to April, when the climate is milder and less humid. Summer (June to September) is very hot and humid. Also avoid hurricane season (June to November). Check for deals in winter when tourist demand is lower.

What to do when you arrive in Charleston

Charleston is known for its colonial history, gastronomy and beaches. Don't miss the historic district with colourful houses, the Port of Charleston and nearby forts. Charleston airport (CHS) is about 20 km from the city centre, well connected by bus, taxi or car hire.
