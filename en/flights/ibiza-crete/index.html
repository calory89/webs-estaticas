Fly from Ibiza to Crete with Vueling from €264 return. Prices average around €269, so booking with plenty of notice is essential to grab the best fares to this Greek island.

Price and when to book

Flights start from €264 return. The median price sits at €269, and we've seen fares up to €286. Advance booking is your best strategy: reserve 2–3 weeks ahead to lock in low prices. The longer you wait, the higher the ticket climbs.

Airline and stops

Vueling is your carrier on this route. Expect 2 stops on the outbound journey, so it's not direct. The itinerary includes intermediate stops, but the price makes up for it if you're chasing budget travel. Total flight time is considerable, but it's what's available on this connection.

Best time to visit

Crete enjoys pleasant weather almost year-round. October and November are ideal: comfortable temperatures, warm seas, and fewer crowds than summer. April to June are also excellent months. Skip July and August unless you don't mind peak season crowds and inflated prices.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Heraklion airport (HER) on the north coast. Visit Knossos (Minoan palace), explore whitewashed towns like Rethymno, swim at pristine southern beaches, and taste local cuisine. Hire a car to move around freely without relying on schedules.

FAQ

Can I find cheaper flights from Ibiza to Crete?

The lowest recorded price is €264 return. The median sits at €269. Book well in advance (2–3 weeks) and compare flexible dates to catch even lower deals if available.

Is the Ibiza to Crete flight direct?

No. Vueling operates this route with 2 stops on the outbound leg. It means more travel time, but it's the available option at this price point.

Which airline flies Ibiza to Crete?

Vueling is the carrier connecting these two destinations. It offers competitive pricing, though with stops included.

What should I see in Crete?

The Knossos Minoan palace is essential. Explore Rethymno and Chania, swim at pink-sand beaches in Elafonissi, hike gorges like Samaria, and visit traditional villages. Cretan cuisine is excellent: try local feta cheese, Greek salad, and olive oil.
