Best time to visit

Phu Quoc has a tropical climate. The dry season (November to April) offers more stable conditions for enjoying beaches and outdoor activities. Outside those months, especially May to October, rainfall increases. Choose based on your weather tolerance and travel flexibility.

What to do in Phu Quoc

The island is known for pristine beaches, clear waters and marine life. You can explore the night market, visit pepper plantations, go scuba diving, or relax at local spas. Phu Quoc Airport (PQC) is the main entry point to the island. From there, reach resorts and attractions using local transport.
