Best time to visit

Mauritius is tropical with warm weather year-round. The dry season (May to November) offers pleasant temperatures and less rain—ideal for beaches and diving. The wet season (December to April) is warmer but still enjoyable. Avoid European peak tourism to find better prices on accommodation and activities.

Things to do on arrival

Mauritius is a paradise: white-sand beaches (Grand Baie, Trou d'Eau Douce), coral reefs perfect for snorkelling and diving, Black River Gorges national park, Pamplemousses Botanical Garden, and Port Louis's cultural hub. Hire a car to explore the island or book tours covering multiple spots. Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam Airport is 65 km from Port Louis.
