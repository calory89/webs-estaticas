Best time to visit

Crete is a Mediterranean destination with mild weather year-round. May to September brings beaches and long daylight, though it gets hot. October and April offer moderate temperatures and fewer crowds. December to March is cooler and rainier, but also quieter and cheaper. Choose based on what you want: packed beaches or authentic Greece.

What to do when you arrive

Crete is Greece's largest island, with Heraklion as your entry point. Explore the old town, taste local food, swim at pink-sand beaches or dramatic cliffs. Mountain villages like Rethymno hold medieval charm. If archaeology interests you, visit the Minoan Palace of Knossos. A week in Crete balances beaches, hiking and culture nicely.
