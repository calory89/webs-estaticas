Fly from Gran Canaria to Rio de Janeiro with Iberia Express from €805 return. The median price is €822. This is a journey with 3 stops, perfect if you're looking for the best value to reach Brazil.

Price and when to book

The lowest price found is €805 per person (return trip), although the median hovers around €822. In the price sample there are options up to €1,320, so booking in advance makes a real difference. Ideally, purchase at least 3-4 weeks ahead to secure the best fares to such a distant destination.

Airline and stops

You'll travel with Iberia Express, and the journey includes 3 stops on the outbound leg. This type of connection is standard on intercontinental routes from the Canaries to South America. The stops add travel hours but significantly reduce the ticket cost compared to direct options.

Best time to visit

Rio de Janeiro has a tropical climate with good weather almost year-round. Summer in Rio (December to March) is peak season and pricier. For lower fares, choose April to September when international tourism dips. The rainy season runs December to March but doesn't prevent you from enjoying the city.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Rio de Janeiro International Airport (Galeão). Direct access to the city by metro, bus or taxi. Must-sees: Christ the Redeemer, Sugarloaf Mountain, Copacabana and Ipanema beaches, favela tours with organised operators, museums like MASP or Niterói. Eat at local restaurants in Lapa and enjoy Brazilian music and cuisine.

FAQ

What is the cheapest price for Gran Canaria to Rio de Janeiro?

The lowest price found is €805 per person on a return trip. Most offers hover around €822. These prices include the 3 stops on the outbound leg.

Is it a direct flight or does it have stops?

The flight has 3 stops on the outbound leg. This is standard on such long routes from the Canaries to South America, and it's why the price is more affordable.

Which airline operates this route?

Iberia Express is the airline for this journey. It's the low-cost brand of the Iberia group, ideal for economical international routes.

What is there to see in Rio de Janeiro?

The most visited spots are Christ the Redeemer, Sugarloaf Mountain, Copacabana and Ipanema beaches, and historic neighbourhoods like Lapa. The Museum of Art of São Paulo (MASP) and favela tours with local operators are also worth your time.
