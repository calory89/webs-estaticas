Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Iberia Express. This journey includes 2 stopovers, so it's not a direct flight, expect a longer trip in exchange for a lower fare. Stopovers mean you can stretch, grab a decent coffee, and reset. Iberia Express is Iberia's budget subsidiary, so expect the low-cost basics: checked baggage costs extra, food and drinks are pricey.

Best time to visit

Rio works year-round, but February (like your sample dates) is summer in Rio: hot, humid, with brief heavy rain. For drier, cooler weather, aim for May–June or October–November. December–January is peak season, crowded and expensive. Prices drop in the southern winter (June–August), but it's cooler.

What to do in Rio de Janeiro

Christ the Redeemer statue and Sugarloaf Mountain are the classics, but also visit Copacabana, Ipanema, and Rio's historic centre. Modern art museums are solid. With extra time, Angra dos Reis is just over 2 hours away and offers proper beaches. Rio's food scene is an attraction itself: olives on everything, caipirinhas by the sea, and ice-cold beer with a view from Santa Marta favela tour.

Is €788 return realistic?

Are there direct flights?

No on this route. You have 2 stopovers with Iberia Express, so it's a long haul. Budget 10–15 hours total (stopovers included). Not a direct, but that's the trade-off for these fares.

Which airline flies this route?

Iberia Express. It's Iberia's budget subsidiary, so it's reliable but with stripped-down service. Standard cabin bag included. Checked baggage, meals and drinks cost extra.

When is the best time to visit Rio?

May–June or October–November offer pleasant weather (mild, dry) and lower fares than December–January. February is Rio summer, hot, humid, rainy, but lively. For decent weather and cheap fares, aim for June or early November.

How far is Rio de Janeiro from Gran Canaria?

About 6,390 km in a straight line between LPA and RIO airports.

Is there a train from Gran Canaria to Rio de Janeiro?

No. There is an ocean between Gran Canaria and Rio de Janeiro (about 6,390 km between the airports), so the practical way to get there is by plane.
