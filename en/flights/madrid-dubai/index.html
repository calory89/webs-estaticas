Price and when to book

Madrid-Dubai flights start at €335 per person return, with a median of €375. Prices we've seen range between €335 and €378. As with most routes, booking ahead usually pays off: the earlier you reserve, the less you'll pay. Avoid leaving your purchase for the last minute.

Airline and layovers

You'll fly with Pegasus, a Turkish low-cost carrier connecting Madrid and Dubai with one layover. It's not a direct flight, but the price makes up for it. The layover is part of the standard route on this airline and poses no problem if you plan your time well.

Best time to visit

Dubai shines in winter (November to March), when heat is bearable and humidity drops. In summer it reaches 45°C and is oppressive. December and January see peak tourism and prices, but the weather is perfect. For lower fares, try May, September or October.

What to do when you arrive in Dubai

The international airport (DXB) is well connected to the city. Explore the historic Al Fahidi quarter, climb the Burj Khalifa, relax at Jumeirah Beach or wander the Grand Bazaar. Traditional markets (souks) are worth visiting, especially the spice souk. Dubai is pricey overall, but some museums and beaches are free.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for Madrid-Dubai flights?

The lowest price we've recorded is €335 return per person with Pegasus. Typical prices hover around €375. Booking in advance maximises your options.

Does Pegasus fly direct from Madrid to Dubai?

No, it includes one layover. Pegasus is a Turkish budget carrier and this route has a stop. Total flight time is longer, but the price saving makes it worthwhile for many travellers.

Which month is cheapest to travel to Dubai?

Generally prices drop in low season: May, September and October. December-January is expensive because the weather is ideal. No month guarantees lower fares; it depends on weekly demand.

What must I see in Dubai?

Burj Khalifa (the world's tallest building), Jumeirah beaches, the historic Al Fahidi neighbourhood and the souks are essential. The desert is also a unique experience. The metro is practical and cheap for getting around.
