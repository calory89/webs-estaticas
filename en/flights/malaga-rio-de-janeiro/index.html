Price and when to book

The lowest fare we found is €802 return, though most tickets hover around €810. Some fares can reach €1,248 depending on dates and availability. For these intercontinental routes, booking 2-3 months ahead is ideal. Dates closer to departure tend to be pricier, so plan ahead if you want the best deal.

Airline and connections

Iberia Airlines operates this route. The outbound journey has 3 stops, so it is not a direct flight. This is standard for routes from Europe to Brazil: typically includes connections within Europe and possibly an intermediate city before reaching Rio. Check your booking for specific connection details.

Best time to visit

Rio de Janeiro is a year-round destination, but the dry season (May to September) offers better weather and less rainfall. European summer (July-August) coincides with winter in Brazil, bringing pleasant temperatures. Avoid the rainy months (November to March) if you prefer sunny days. Prices usually vary with seasonal demand.

What to do when you arrive

Rio de Janeiro awaits with Christ the Redeemer, Copacabana beach, Ipanema and Sugarloaf Mountain. The international airport (Galeão) is about 20 km from the centre. From there you can take the bus, metro or taxi to your hotel. Remember this is a long journey with connections: arrive with plenty of time to rest and enjoy the city.
