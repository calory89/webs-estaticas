Fly from Paris to Bucharest with Ryanair from €65 return. The median price hovers around €88, enough to grab a deal without hanging about. Direct connection saves time and headaches.

Price and when to book

Base price starts at €65 return, though you'll also find tickets at €88 and €118 depending on dates. Book early to lock in lower fares. Ryanair refreshes deals constantly—set up price alerts on flight comparison sites so you don't miss last-minute bargains.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route direct, no stops. Straight flight from Paris to Bucharest, no plane changes. The airline keeps costs lean, so check what your fare covers: carry-on, seat, refreshments. It all adds up if you're not careful.

Best time to visit

Bucharest shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): decent weather, fewer crowds than summer, reasonable prices. Winter works if you're after cold and aggressive deals. Skip Christmas and August if you want to travel without the rush.

What to do when you land

You'll land at Henri Coandă airport (OTP), about 16 km from the centre. The Old Town (Lipscani) is unmissable: churches, squares, cafés. Visit the Palace of Parliament, walk along the Danube, eat your way through Romanian dishes. Bucharest has character, quality museums, and buzzing nightlife.

FAQ

Is the Paris-Bucharest flight direct?

Yes, Ryanair flies direct from Paris to Bucharest with no stops.

What's the cheapest price you've seen?

From €65 return. Prices vary by date; the median sits around €88.

What baggage is included on Ryanair for this route?

It depends on your fare. Check Ryanair's website for what's included: basic fares usually cover a small carry-on, the rest costs extra.

When should I book to pay less?

Low fares don't stick around long. Book as soon as you spot a price you like. Ryanair typically drops deals mid-week, especially Tuesday and Wednesday.
