Best time to visit

December is a good time to visit Kuala Lumpur. The climate is tropical and warm year-round, but the dry season (November to March) brings less rain. Christmas in Malaysia's capital is festive with plenty of activity. If you prefer to avoid crowds, consider travelling between February and September, though those months are hotter and more humid.

What to do in Kuala Lumpur

The city has much more than the iconic Petronas Towers. Explore Chinatown, wander the street markets of Petaling Street, visit the Royal Palace and relax in the Perdana Botanical Gardens. If you're up for it, climb KL Tower for 360-degree views. The Batu Caves on the outskirts are a spiritual must-see. Eat at local food courts: Malaysian food is excellent and cheap.
