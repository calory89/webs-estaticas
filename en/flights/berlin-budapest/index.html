Best time to visit

Hungary is pleasant year-round, but October-November and March-April offer good weather without crowds. July and August are pricier and busier. Winter (December-February) is cold, but prices drop and the city glows with festive lights. Always check the price calendar before picking your dates.

What to do when you arrive

Budapest is perfect for 3-4 days. Széchenyi thermal baths, Parliament, Danube river walk, local markets (seasonal). The city is compact and walkable. Ferenc Liszt airport is 16 km from the centre; bus, taxi or train connect you quickly.
