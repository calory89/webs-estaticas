Best time to visit

Cyprus offers pleasant weather nearly year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: comfortable temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. Winter (December–February) is mild, perfect if you want to dodge heat and find lower prices on the ground.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Larnaca Airport (LCA), about 5 km from the city centre. Larnaca is your gateway: stroll the Promenade, visit the Church of Saint Lazarus, or relax on nearby beaches. From there, Paphos, Troodos mountain villages, and Nicosia are all within easy reach. Public buses are affordable and well-connected from the airport to town.
