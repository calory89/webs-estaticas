Best time to visit

Rome and Milan are active destinations year-round. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is quieter and colder. Check major events in each city when choosing dates, especially if travelling for cultural or fashion reasons.

What to do when you arrive in Milan

Milan is the capital of design and fashion. Visit the Cathedral (Duomo), the Vittorio Emanuele Gallery, Leonardo da Vinci's Last Supper (book ahead), the Navigli and Brera neighbourhoods. Shop for luxury brands and enjoy quality gastronomy. Malpensa Airport (MXP) is about 50 km from the city centre; connect via metro, bus or taxi quickly.
