Flying from Galicia to Vietnam is possible from €636 return with Ryanair. The median price hovers around €678, so you've got solid options if you're flexible with dates.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €636 return. The median sits at €678 and we've tracked fares up to €690. As with any long-haul route, the rule is simple: book as early as possible for the best price. Avoid booking fewer than 2–3 months ahead and check the fare calendar regularly.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route with 2 stops on both outbound and return flights. There's no direct option, so expect intermediate layovers. Ryanair fares are budget-friendly, but watch their baggage policy: carry-on is free, but checked baggage costs extra. Verify what's included in your base fare.

Best time to visit Vietnam

Vietnam works year-round, but the sweet spot depends on where you go. In the north (Hanoi, Halong Bay), October to April brings pleasant temperatures and less rain. In the south (Ho Chi Minh, Mekong), the dry season runs November to April. Skip the northern summer months, heat and humidity are brutal. November and December are perfect if you want mild weather across the entire country.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

Hanoi deserves at least 3 days: wander the Old Quarter, visit the Temple of Literature, climb Turtle Tower for views of Hoan Kiem Lake, and eat street food in the 36 Streets district. From here it's easy to reach Halong Bay (UNESCO, essential), Ninh Binh or catch an overnight bus south. Noi Bai International Airport is 30 km from the centre; taxis, buses and trains connect smoothly to the city.
