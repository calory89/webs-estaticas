Best time to visit

Rome works year-round, but October–November and February–March offer good weather without summer crowds. If you're after low fares, watch for last-minute deals in shoulder season. The Seville-to-Rome journey is short, so almost any time suits you.

What to do in Rome

The Colosseum, Roman Forum, Vatican, and Sistine Chapel are essential. Walk the historic centre, eat at local trattorias, climb Castel Sant'Angelo, and spend time at squares and fountains. Two or three days cover the basics; a week lets you enjoy without rushing.
