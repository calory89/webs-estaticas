Best time to visit

Maldives has two seasons: dry (November to April) and wet (May to October). Dry season is best for beaches and diving, though prices rise. For savings, try May-June: fewer tourists, slightly lower fares, and good weather. November is excellent: it's the start of dry season without peak-season prices yet.

What to do when you arrive

Maldives International Airport (MLE) is in Malé. Most resorts are on nearby islands: ferry, speedboat, or seaplane will get you there. Diving, snorkelling, white-sand beaches, and coral reefs are essential activities. You can also explore Malé or simply relax at an all-inclusive resort. Ten days (as in your example) is the perfect length for a proper escape.
