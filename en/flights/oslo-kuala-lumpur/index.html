Airline and stopovers

Best time to travel

Kuala Lumpur is tropical with warm weather year-round. The best time is November to February, when rainfall decreases and humidity is lower. Avoid September and October if you prefer less moisture. Travelling in low season (May to August) may offer cheaper fares, though it rains more.

Things to do when you arrive

Kuala Lumpur has plenty to offer: the Petronas Twin Towers are a must, the colonial district of Merdeka Square is worth exploring, and the Batu Caves are just outside the city. Night markets like Jalan Alor are perfect for cheap, authentic food. With extra days, the Perhentian Islands or Penang are nearby and stunning.
