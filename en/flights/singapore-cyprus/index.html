Airline and stopovers

Best time to visit

Cyprus enjoys a Mediterranean climate. Winters are mild and perfect if you want to avoid crowds, whilst summer offers beaches and real sunshine. Choose based on your preferences: quieter vibes in low season, more activities in peak season.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in Larnaca. The Cypriot coast awaits with sandy beaches, medieval towns like Nicosia and ancient Greek ruins. The Akamas cliffs, Troodos monasteries and Limassol harbour are unmissable. Eat souvlaki, try halloumi cheese and relax on the Mediterranean.
