Best time to visit

Tallinn shines in summer (June to August) with long days and pleasant temperatures. Winter (November to March) is cold and perfect if you want to see the city snow-covered and enjoy festive cheer. Spring and autumn mean fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Tallinn's old town is a UNESCO World Heritage site: walk the cobbled streets, climb the Church of Saint Olaf for views, and explore the medieval harbour. Don't miss Soviet occupation museums and the Kalamaja neighbourhood. Eat dark rye bread and try local beer.
