Berlin and Amsterdam are just an hour's flight apart. With KLM you'll find direct flights from €169 (return) and a median price of €246. Book well in advance: this route rarely has very cheap fares during peak season.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €169 for a return trip. The median sits around €246. These prices cover your full round trip. As a general rule, book 2–3 months ahead to secure the best rates. Avoid last-minute bookings: whilst occasional deals pop up, prices are usually higher closer to travel dates.

Airline and stopovers

Fly with KLM Royal Dutch Airlines. The best part: all flights on this route are direct (no stopovers). Departure from Berlin around 14:25, arrival in Amsterdam around 16:25. It's a short journey, so minimal time in the air and you'll land with plenty of energy to explore.

Best time to visit

Amsterdam is lovely year-round, but April–May (spring) and September–October (autumn) are ideal: mild weather, less rain and fewer crowds than summer. Winter (December–February) is cold but magical if you fancy Christmas lights and ice skating. Skip July–August if you're not keen on tourist throngs.

What to do when you arrive

Amsterdam needs no introduction: canals, museums (Van Gogh, Anne Frank), bikes everywhere and cosy cafés. Explore the Jordaan neighbourhood, climb church towers for panoramic views and grab stroopwafels at any corner. The airport is 9 km from the city centre; the train gets you there in 15 minutes.

Yes, all flights on this route with KLM are direct. No stopovers. The flight takes just over an hour.

What's worth seeing in Amsterdam?

The canals (Unesco heritage), major museums (Van Gogh, Rijksmuseum), churches with views, the Jordaan neighbourhood and cycling culture are must-sees. Set aside at least 2–3 days to make the most of it.
