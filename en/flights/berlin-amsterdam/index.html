Airline and stopovers

Fly with KLM Royal Dutch Airlines. The best part: all flights on this route are direct (no stopovers). Departure from Berlin around 14:25, arrival in Amsterdam around 16:25. It's a short journey, so minimal time in the air and you'll land with plenty of energy to explore.

Best time to visit

Amsterdam is lovely year-round, but April–May (spring) and September–October (autumn) are ideal: mild weather, less rain and fewer crowds than summer. Winter (December–February) is cold but magical if you fancy Christmas lights and ice skating. Skip July–August if you're not keen on tourist throngs.

What to do when you arrive

Amsterdam needs no introduction: canals, museums (Van Gogh, Anne Frank), bikes everywhere and cosy cafés. Explore the Jordaan neighbourhood, climb church towers for panoramic views and grab stroopwafels at any corner. The airport is 9 km from the city centre; the train gets you there in 15 minutes.
