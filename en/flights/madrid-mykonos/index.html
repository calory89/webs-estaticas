Best time to visit

Mykonos is a beach and party destination year-round, but July and August are peak season (and pricey). Spring and autumn offer fine weather with fewer crowds. December also draws visitors seeking mild winter weather. Choose based on your preference for busy periods and climate.

When you arrive in Mykonos

Mykonos airport (JMK) is just 2 km from town. From there, bus, taxi or car rental get you to the centre. Once on the island, beaches like Paradise and Super Paradise, nightlife in Chora (the main town), and whitewashed villages are must-sees. Book accommodation well ahead during peak season.
