What to do when you arrive

Sofia airport (SOF) is 10 km from the centre. Spend a day at Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, Boyana Church and Banya Bashi Mosque. Take a trip to Rila Monastery (1 hour away) if you have time. Stroll the bars and restaurants around Vitosha Boulevard. Try banitsa pastry and local rakija. Sofia is cheap: accommodation, food and public transport won't drain your wallet.
