Best time to travel

Manila has a tropical climate. The best time is November to April, when there is less rain and temperatures are more pleasant. Avoid the monsoon season (June to September). November is a good month based on the example checked. If you travel during these dates you will have more stable weather.

What to do when you arrive

In Manila and surroundings: visit Intramuros, the walled historic quarter; cruise the bay by ferry; climb Taal Volcano if you fancy an adventure day; explore the islands of Palawan or Boracay (short trips from Manila). The international airport is easy to reach from the capital by bus or taxi.
