Price and when to book

Prices range from €52 to €76 in our sample. The best strategy is to book in advance: the earlier, the less risk of price hikes. Don't wait until the last minute or expect magical drops. If you see the flight at €52–54, go for it. Anything above €76 is expensive for this route.

Airline and stops

Ryanair is the sole operator on this route and flies direct from Brussels to Marrakech. No stops, no plane changes. Cabin bag is included; checked baggage costs extra. Arrive at the airport at least 2 hours early.

Best time to visit

Marrakech is pleasant year-round, but October to April is most comfortable: mild temperatures, no summer heat extremes. July and August can be very hot. Book well ahead regardless of when you choose to travel.

What to do when you arrive

In Marrakech you'll find the Medina (old town with souks and squares), Bahia Palace, Majorelle Gardens, and easy access to the Sahara and Atlas Mountains. It's a tourist-friendly city perfect for 4–5 days. The airport is 6 km from the centre; taxi or shuttle will get you there easily.
