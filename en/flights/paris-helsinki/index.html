Best time to visit

Helsinki shines in winter (December to February) if you want to see the Northern Lights, though prices go up then. For warmer weather, go in summer (June to August): incredibly long days with daylight until nearly 23:00. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer competitive prices and a good balance of comfort.

What to do when you arrive

Helsinki blends Nordic design, historic architecture and Finnish nature. Visit the White Cathedral, Suomenlinna Fortress (an island fort), art and design museums, and try Nordic cuisine. The airport is 19 km from the centre and well connected by train, bus and taxi. From Helsinki it's easy to visit Turku or explore Finland's lakes.
