Airline and stopovers

Pegasus operates this route with 1 stopover on the outbound flight. It's not direct, but the price is very competitive. Pegasus is a Turkish low-cost carrier, so check what's included in your ticket, baggage, seat selection, etc., before you buy.

Best time to visit Dubai

Dubai's summer heat is extreme. October to April offers pleasant weather between 20–30°C. November, December and January are peak tourist months. If you want fewer crowds, May to September works, but expect intense heat. Prices don't drop in low season, so choose based on your heat tolerance.

What to do when you arrive in Dubai

You'll land at Dubai International Airport (DXB), about 9 km from the city. The metro connects directly downtown. Must-sees include Burj Khalifa, Dubai Museum, Jumeirah Beach, Deira Souk and Dubai Marina. Desert safari tours depart from the city too.
