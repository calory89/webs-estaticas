Best time to visit

Las Vegas is a year-round destination, but November–March brings mild temperatures (15–20°C). Avoid July and August due to extreme desert heat. December–January prices spike for Christmas and New Year's. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) hit the sweet spot: pleasant weather and more moderate fares than summer.

What to do when you arrive

Las Vegas awaits with the Strip, luxury casinos, world-class shows and legendary nightlife. Beyond the buzz, the Grand Canyon (45 min), Red Rock Canyon (30 min) and Valley of Fire National Park await. Harry Reid Airport (LAS) sits 6 km from town; shuttle, taxi or car hire are your moves. Don't miss themed hotels, the Bellagio fountains and live performances.

TAP Portugal is the Portuguese national carrier, an IATA member with decades of operation. It's a reliable option for international flights, though like all airlines it's subject to occasional delays inherent to air travel.

Is the flight direct or does it have stopovers?

What's worth seeing in Las Vegas besides casinos?

The Grand Canyon is a must (1-day trip). Red Rock Canyon offers hiking and stunning views 30 min away. Valley of Fire National Park combines red rock and fossils. In town, there are museums, cirque shows and themed hotel architecture. The older Fremont Street downtown feels more authentic than the Strip.

How far is Las Vegas from Barcelona?

About 9,300 km in a straight line between BCN and LAS airports.

Is there a train from Barcelona to Las Vegas?

No. There is an ocean between Barcelona and Las Vegas (about 9,300 km between the airports), so the practical way to get there is by plane.
