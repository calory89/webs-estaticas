Best time to visit

Naples is pleasant year-round, but avoid August for heat and crowds. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer perfect weather and fewer tourists. Winter is quiet and cheaper, though some rain is possible.

What to do when you arrive

Naples is the heart of Campania. Visit San Gennaro Cathedral, the National Archaeological Museum, and eat real pizza in the old quarter. From here you can easily reach Pompeii, Herculaneum, and the Amalfi Coast. Capodichino airport is 5 km from the city centre with good public transport links.
