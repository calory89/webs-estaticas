What to do in Seoul

South Korea's capital blends tradition and modernity: Buddhist temples like Jogyesa, Gyeongbokgung Palace, vibrant neighbourhoods (Gangnam, Hongdae), traditional markets (Myeongdong, Namdaemun) and world-class food. Incheon Airport (ICN) is about 60 km from the city centre with excellent train and bus connections.
