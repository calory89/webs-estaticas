Price and when to book your flight

Prices range from €1,210 to €2,225 in the sample checked. There's no guaranteed pattern for when prices drop on long-haul routes like this, but booking as early as possible is your best bet. Around 30 days before departure is a solid window to secure a decent fare.

Airline and number of stopovers

You'll fly with Fiji Airways throughout. The outbound flight has 3 stopovers, so prepare for a lengthy journey. Multiple connections are standard over this distance (more than 15,000 km), and Fiji Airways is a reliable, well-regarded operator.

Best time to visit Nassau

Nassau has a tropical climate year-round. November to April is peak season: lower humidity and reduced hurricane risk. June to October is hurricane season with slightly cheaper prices, but higher risk. The example booking in November is a smart window.

What to do in Nassau and beyond

Nassau is the Bahamas capital with white-sand beaches, world-class snorkelling, and nightlife. Visit Paradise Island, the Blue Holes of Exuma (day trip), swim with dolphins, or explore the historic centre. Nassau International Airport (NAS) is about 25 km from the city.
