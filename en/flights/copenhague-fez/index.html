Best time to visit

Fez is a year-round destination. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant temperatures, perfect for exploring the medina. Winter (December–February) is mild but wetter. Summer is hot, especially July–August. Check the climate for your planned dates.

What to do when you arrive in Fez

Fez's medina is a UNESCO World Heritage Site: a fascinating maze of souks, traditional shops and hammams. Don't miss the Al-Qarawiyyin Mosque and the tanneries district. Fez airport (FEZ) is roughly 15 km from the centre; reach it by taxi or bus. Spend 2–3 days exploring the city properly.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for this route?

The lowest fare we've logged is €129 return. Most offers hover around €135, though prices can climb to €151 or higher depending on dates. Book early for the best rates.

Is it a direct flight from Copenhagen to Fez?

No, there's 1 stopover on the outbound flight. You won't arrive direct, but it's Ryanair's most budget-friendly option on this route.

Which airline flies this route?

Ryanair operates the Copenhagen–Fez flights shown here. Keep in mind Ryanair charges for luggage, seat selection and other add-ons.

What else is there to see in Fez besides the medina?

The medina is essential, but you can also visit the Royal Palace, Jnan Sbil Botanical Garden and the imperial palaces. Many travellers take a day trip to Meknes or Ifrane from Fez.
