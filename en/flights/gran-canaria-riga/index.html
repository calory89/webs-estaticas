Best time to visit

Riga is spectacular in winter for snow and Christmas markets, or summer for mild temperatures (15–22°C). Spring and autumn are pleasant too. Avoid peak tourist season dates if possible to keep prices down.

What to do in Riga

Latvia's capital blends medieval architecture with art déco. Walk through the Old Town (UNESCO World Heritage), visit Riga Cathedral, explore Christmas markets (if travelling in winter) or enjoy the Daugava riverside. Riga Airport (RIX) is 13 km from the centre, well served by bus and taxi.
