Price and when to book

The lowest price found is €47, but the median sits at €60. With sample prices of €47, €60 and €116, you see the real range. Book as early as possible from your search: Ryanair often discounts tickets weeks ahead. Avoid last-minute bookings.

Airline and direct flights

Ryanair operates this route non-stop. Typical departure from Milan at 06:00 and return from Stockholm at 09:10. You fly direct with no plane changes, saving you time and hassle.

Best time to travel

Stockholm in autumn and winter is stunning: long nights, the northern lights from October onwards, and snowy landscapes. Book ahead if travelling in high season. Flights are usually pricier in summer and European bank holidays.

What to do when you arrive

Land in Stockholm and explore Gamla Stan's old town, the archipelago islands, or the Vasa Museum. Public transport is excellent. From Milan, you'll be back with plenty of time if your return flight is in the morning.
