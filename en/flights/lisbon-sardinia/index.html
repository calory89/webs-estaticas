Connect Lisbon with Sardinia from €120 return with Ryanair. The average price sits around €135, though you'll find options up to €187 depending on dates. With one stopover, it's the most budget-friendly option to reach the Italian island.

Price and when to book

Flights start from €120 (return). The median price is €135, giving you a solid idea of the usual range. Book 4-6 weeks in advance for the best fares. Avoid peak season (July-August) if you're hunting for cheaper tickets.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route. Flights include 1 stopover, so you won't get a direct service. The break is short but adds to your total travel time. Remember that Ryanair charges separately for checked baggage, so check what's included in your fare type.

Best time to visit

Sardinia shines from May to September, with perfect beaches and sunny skies. To skip crowds and pay less, aim for May-June or September-October. Winters are mild but wetter. Book early no matter the season.

What to do when you arrive

Cagliari airport (Elmas) is 7 km from the city. From there, rent a car to explore: wild beaches in the south, mountain villages inland, crystal-clear coves along the east coast. Cagliari city deserves a day: medieval Castello, seafront walk and archaeological museums. Don't miss the Basilica of San Saturnino or views from Elefante Tower.

FAQ

Are there direct flights Lisbon-Sardinia?

No, this route has 1 stopover. It's what Ryanair offers on this leg.

What does the €120 price include?

It's the base return fare. Ryanair charges extra for checked baggage, meals and seat selection. Check which fare type you're booking.

When is it cheapest to fly to Sardinia?

May-June and September-October typically offer better value. July-August is pricier. Book 4-6 weeks ahead.

What's the best airport in Sardinia?

Cagliari (CAG) is the main hub and puts you near beaches and towns. Good transport links inland.
