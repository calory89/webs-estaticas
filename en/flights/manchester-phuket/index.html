Best time to travel

Thailand's dry season runs November to February, the best time to visit Phuket. May to October is more humid with higher rainfall. For fewer crowds and potentially better prices, consider shoulder months like November or late February to March.

What to do when you arrive in Phuket

Phuket is the gateway to islands in Krabi, Phang Nga and southern beaches. From the airport, hire a car, take a taxi or use transfer services. The area offers Patong Beach, Buddhist temples, night markets and access to diving and snorkelling tours around nearby islands.
