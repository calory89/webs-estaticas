Best time to visit

Dubai is a sun-and-luxury desert destination. Winter months (November to March) offer the most pleasant climate, with temperatures around 20-25°C. Summer (June to August) can exceed 45°C. For lower prices, fly in shoulder or low season, but always book ahead to secure the best rates.

What to do when you arrive

Dubai is famous for the Burj Khalifa, the world's largest shopping mall (Dubai Mall), white-sand beaches, and traditional souks in Deira and Bur Dubai. You can also visit Dubai Museum, take a desert safari, or enjoy the skyline from Palm Jumeirah. The airport has good metro and bus connections to the city.
