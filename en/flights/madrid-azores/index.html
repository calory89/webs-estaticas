Best time to travel

The Azores have an Atlantic climate with mild temperatures year-round. Avoid the wetter months (October to March) if you want maximum sunshine, though rainfall is fairly common. Spring and autumn offer a good balance of price and weather.

What to do when you arrive

The Azores are a haven for hiking, whale watching and diving. Ponta Delgada on São Miguel is the main entry point. Visit volcanic craters, hot spring lakes and dramatic coastal cliffs. The fishing villages are ideal for fresh seafood and unwinding.

When is the best time to visit the Azores?

Spring (April–June) and autumn (September–October) offer the best weather. Winter is wetter but still viable and often cheaper.

Hiking around volcanoes, whale watching, thermal baths, diving and exploring coastal villages. São Miguel is the most visited island, accessible via Ponta Delgada.
