Fly direct from Brussels to Agadir with Ryanair from €80 return. Median price is around €83, so you have solid options to book. Agadir awaits with beaches, markets and plenty of sunshine.

Price and when to book

The prices you see (€80, €83, €91) are real and achievable. For this route, book as early as possible; Ryanair fares climb as departure dates approach. Look for off-peak times—night flights or early morning departures often have better rates. Compare dates nearby: sometimes a week's difference makes a real difference to the price.

Airline and layovers

Ryanair operates this route non-stop. You depart Brussels and land direct in Agadir. Night departures are common on this airline (see example: 19:20 takeoff), which typically keeps prices lower. Hand luggage only is included; checked baggage costs extra.

Best time to visit

Agadir has decent weather year-round, but October to April are ideal: mild temperatures, no extreme heat and minimal rain. The city is relatively quiet outside European school holidays, so avoid July, August and bank holidays if you want to sidestep crowds and price spikes.

What to do on arrival

Agadir means beach, markets and Moroccan food. Stroll the Medina (reconstructed old town), walk the seafront promenade, visit the fishing port and local souks. The airport is 20 km from town; buses, taxis and car hire are available. If you want countryside, the Atlas Mountains and Essaouira are a few hours' drive away.

What is there to see in Agadir?

Beach and seafront promenade (corniche), Medina (restored market), fishing port with seafood restaurants, local crafts. The Atlas Mountains are nearby if you fancy trekking. The weather is mild almost all year.
