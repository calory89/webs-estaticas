Price and when to book

Fares begin at €98, though most hover around €138. Prices shift by date; the earlier you book, the less you pay. Vueling is your only option on this route, so compare dates before committing. Don't wait for last-minute deals if the price looks fair.

Airline and stops

Vueling operates this route with 1 stop. It's not direct, but it's the option available from Palma. The layover adds a few hours, but the fares make up for it. Pack essentials in your carry-on to speed through connections.

Best time to visit

Krakow is stunning year-round. Winter (December to February) is cold but magical, with snow blanketing the Old Town. Spring and autumn offer mild weather and fewer crowds. Summer is peak season and prices climb. Avoid European holidays if you're chasing low fares.

What to do when you arrive

Krakow airport (Balice) is 15 km from the centre. Take the train or direct taxi into town. Must-see: the Main Market Square (Rynek Główny), Wawel Cathedral, and the Jewish Quarter. Eat pierniki (local pastries) and try zurek (rye soup). Krakow will surprise you.

3–4 days is ideal. The Old Town takes 1–2 days, and the rest can be spent visiting Auschwitz, Wieliczka Salt Mine, or just soaking in the local vibe.
