Airline and number of stops

Best time to travel

Manchester to London runs year-round. Colder months (November to February) typically have lower fares, while spring and summer push prices up. If you're after value, travel in low season; if you want decent weather, aim for May or September.

What to do when you arrive in London

Check which terminal you land at (London airports have multiple). From the city centre you'll find world-class free museums, the Thames for a stroll, neighbourhoods like Shoreditch or Camden on a budget. Covent Garden and Big Ben are must-sees, though increasingly touristy. Eat at street markets like Borough Market: cheaper than restaurants.
