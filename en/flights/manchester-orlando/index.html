Price and when to book

The lowest price is €586 for a return ticket. Our sample prices range from €586, €589, and €666, giving you options to choose from. The golden rule: book as far in advance as possible, especially if you're travelling during peak season or near bank holidays. The price tends to climb week by week as your travel date approaches.

Airline and stopovers

KLM Royal Dutch Airlines operates this route with 1 stopover on the outbound journey. It's not a direct flight, but a single stop means your total travel time is reasonable. KLM is a trusted airline known for comfort and customer service.

Best time to visit

Orlando is a year-round destination, but May to September bring heat and frequent rain. October to April is ideal: pleasant weather and lower humidity. For cheaper prices, travel during the low season (avoid Christmas, Easter, and July–August).

What to do when you arrive

Orlando is famous for theme parks: Magic Kingdom, Epcot, Hollywood Studios, and Animal Kingdom. The Kennedy Space Center Launch Complex is about 90 km away. If you prefer to relax, Atlantic beaches are under two hours away, plus natural springs and pools across Florida. The city also has shopping, dining, and free attractions.

FAQ

What is the cheapest flight price from Manchester to Orlando?

The lowest price we've found is €586 return with KLM. Fares vary by date and availability, but this is the current base rate.

Is it a direct flight from Manchester to Orlando?

No, it includes 1 stopover. KLM makes one stop before reaching Orlando, which is standard for transatlantic flights from the UK.

When is the best time to buy my ticket?

The sooner, the better. Prices rise as your departure date gets closer. If you have flexible dates, compare different days – you might save €50 or more.

What is there to see in Orlando besides theme parks?

Kennedy Space Center, natural springs and swimming holes, the International Drive area with shopping and entertainment, and Atlantic beaches (Cocoa Beach, Daytona) within easy reach.
