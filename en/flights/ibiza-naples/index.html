Fly from Ibiza to Naples from €94 return with Iberia Express. Average price hovers around €112, so you have room to find good deals. A perfect escape to the Amalfi Coast without breaking the bank.

Price and when to book

Prices start at €94 return, with an average around €112. Book as early as possible: fares climb closer to departure. Check offers 4–6 weeks ahead for the best rates. January flights are typically cheaper than summer services.

Airline and stops

You'll travel with Iberia Express, operating this route with 1 stop on the outbound leg. It's not direct, but a manageable connection that barely adds time. Iberia Express delivers reliable low-cost service.

Best time to travel

January is ideal: low fares, fewer crowds, and mild weather (10–15°C). For warmer temperatures, aim for April–May when conditions are pleasant and before summer prices soar. Skip July and August when fares spike and Naples gets packed.

What to do when you arrive

Naples is your gateway to southern Italy's charm. Explore the historic centre, visit the Cathedral of San Genaro, climb Castel Sant'Elmo for views, and taste authentic Neapolitan pizza. The city puts Pompeii, Herculaneum, and the Amalfi Coast within easy reach by train or hire car.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Ibiza to Naples?

No, this Iberia Express route includes 1 stop on the outbound leg. It's not direct, but the connection is straightforward and doesn't add much journey time.

What's the average price I should expect?

Average price sits around €112 return, though you can find fares from €94 if you book well in advance.

Which airline serves this route?

Iberia Express operates this route. It offers budget-friendly flights with solid reliability. Always check baggage allowances and change policies before purchasing.

What should I see in Naples besides the city centre?

Pompeii and Herculaneum (essential Roman ruins), the Amalfi Coast (Positano, Amalfi), Capri and Ischia islands, and the Campi Flegrei. All are within an hour of Naples on public transport.
