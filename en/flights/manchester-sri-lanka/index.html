Best time to visit

Sri Lanka is beautiful year-round, but be aware of monsoons depending on the region. November to March is generally drier and more comfortable. These months can be busier, so if you want fewer tourists consider April-May or September-October, when prices may also be less pressured.

What to do when you arrive in Colombo

You'll land at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB), the country's main hub. From there you can hire a car, take a taxi or bus to Colombo's centre or southern beaches. Don't miss the Temple of the Tooth in Kandy, Mirissa's beaches, tea plantations in Nuwara Eliya and the ruins of Sigiriya. Sri Lanka is compact: most attractions are just a few hours apart.

FAQ: Manchester to Sri Lanka flights

Are there direct flights from Manchester to Sri Lanka?

No, this Ryanair route includes 2 stops. Direct flights from the UK to Colombo do exist with other airlines, but they're more expensive. With stops you save considerably, even though the journey takes longer.

What's the lowest price for this route?

From €716 return based on latest data. The median is €760, so expect to pay around that, though there are occasions with lower fares if you book with flexible dates.

How far in advance should I book?

The sooner the better for long-haul routes like this one. Booking 2-3 months ahead usually gives better prices. If you're travelling November-December (peak season), book even earlier.

What to see in Sri Lanka besides beaches?

The country offers plenty: ancient temples, tea plantations, wildlife in national parks, waterfalls and mountains. Kandy, Sigiriya and Nuwara Eliya are must-sees. You can also go on safari at Yala and Udawalawe to spot elephants, crocodiles and leopards.
