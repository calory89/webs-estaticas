Ryanair connects London with Prague on a direct flight from €45 per person return. The median price sits around €62, so if you find this fare, it's a steal. Book in advance to secure the best deals.

Price and when to book

The base price is €45 return, though most searches fall between €62–63. The earlier you book, the better your chances of landing a low fare. Check weekly: Ryanair prices change constantly based on demand and availability.

Airline and flight type

Ryanair is the sole operator on this route, offering a direct flight with no stopovers. That's one hour of flight time without stops, unless you choose alternative routes on your own. Ryanair charges extra for hand luggage and checked bags, so review their terms before buying.

Best time to visit

Prague is beautiful year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather without summer crowds. Avoid European holidays for better prices. Book 2–3 months ahead to fly at a competitive rate.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Prague International Airport, 20 km from the city centre. Take a public bus, taxi or private transfer in 30–40 minutes. In the city, walk across Charles Bridge, see the Astronomical Clock, explore the Jewish Quarter and climb up to St Vitus Cathedral. Prague is compact: you can cover the highlights on foot or by tram.
