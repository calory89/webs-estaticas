Best time to travel

Vienna shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), when the weather is mild and crowds are lighter than in summer. Winters are cold but magical if you fancy the Christmas charm (December). July and August can be pricier and busier. Check availability for your dates to secure the best fare.

What to do when you arrive in Vienna

Vienna Airport (VIE) is 16 km from the centre. Explore Schönbrunn, the most iconic imperial palace. Visit St. Stephen's Cathedral in the Old Town. Take walks along the Danube, enjoy a traditional Viennese coffee, and make time for the museums on the Ringstrasse. Three to four days allows you to see the highlights.
