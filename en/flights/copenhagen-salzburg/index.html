Connect Copenhagen and Salzburg for €147 return. Median prices hover around €191, so booking early saves you money. Ryanair operates this route with one stop, perfect for reaching the heart of Austria without breaking the bank.

Price and when to book

The lowest recorded price is €147 return, with the median at €191. Prices range from €147 to €221 depending on your travel dates. Book as early as possible for the best fares: aim for 6–8 weeks in advance. Avoid peak dates like bank holidays and school holidays, when prices climb significantly.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route. The flight includes 1 stop on the outbound journey, so it's not direct but remains budget-friendly. Bear in mind that Ryanair charges separately for checked baggage and seat selection, so factor those into your total budget. The stopover is usually short, but always check your booking details.

Best time to visit

Salzburg shines year-round. Winter (December–February) is magical with snow and Christmas markets; summer (June–August) offers fine weather but heavier crowds. Spring and autumn provide pleasant weather with slightly better prices. Pick whatever suits your schedule and budget—any season works well for exploring the city.

What to do when you arrive

Salzburg welcomes you with the magnificent Hohensalzburg Fortress, a UNESCO-listed old town, and views over the Salzach River. Don't miss Mirabell Gardens, the cathedral, and Mozart's birthplace. Nature lovers should venture to the Lakes District (Salzkammergut) an hour's drive away. Salzburg Airport (W.A. Mozart) is 4 km from the city centre, easily reached by bus or taxi.
