Best time to travel

Stockholm in winter (December–February) offers snowy landscapes and Nordic charm. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal for milder weather. Summer is peak season and prices rise. February can be a good option for budget-conscious travellers.

What to do when you arrive in Stockholm

Walk through Gamla Stan, the old town with medieval streets and colourful buildings. Visit the Royal Palace, the museums on Djurgården island and take a lake swim if you're travelling in summer. Arlanda airport is 20 km from the city centre; buses, trains and taxis provide good connections to the city.
