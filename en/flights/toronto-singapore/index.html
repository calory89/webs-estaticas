Price and when to book

The lowest recorded price is €988 return, with median prices at €1,087 and highs around €1,119. For this long-haul route, booking 4-6 weeks ahead is recommended. Prices tend to stabilise from early October, but the most competitive fares are usually available 30-45 days before departure.

Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Korean Air, one of Asia's most punctual carriers. This route includes 2 stopovers on the outbound leg, which extends your travel time but significantly reduces the cost. Korean Air is known for quality service and included meals on long flights, even in economy.

Best time to visit

Singapore is tropical with warm weather year-round. February to April and July to August are the driest months. Avoid monsoon season (November to January) if you prefer less rain. For cheaper fares, travel in shoulder season (May, June, September, October).

What to do when you arrive

Singapore is compact and easy to explore. Don't miss Gardens by the Bay, Marina Bay Sands, Chinatown and night markets. Changi Airport connects to the city centre by metro (red line, 30 minutes). Plan at least 3-4 days for the essentials, though some spend a full week.
