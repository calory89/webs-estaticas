Best time to visit

Hamburg is especially appealing in spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October), when the weather is mild and crowds are smaller. Winter can be very cold (between -2°C and 4°C), but it's ideal if you want to visit the Christmas markets. Summer (June-August) is warm but also peak season with higher prices.

What to do when you arrive in Hamburg

Hamburg is a charming port city with plenty to offer. Don't miss the harbour (Hafen), the St. Pauli district with its canals, St. Michael's Church, or the Speicherstadt museums. Wander through the historic warehouses, eat fresh herring at the fish market, and enjoy the Jugendstil architecture. The city has excellent public transport connections from the airport.
