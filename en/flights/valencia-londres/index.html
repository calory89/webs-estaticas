Best time to visit

November works well: mild autumn weather in London, fewer tourists than summer months. Pack a light jacket and umbrella. Late October and early November often see competitive pricing before the Christmas rush.

What to do in London

Big Ben, Tower Bridge, the British Museum, walks along the Thames. Wander Covent Garden, check out Camden or Portobello Road markets. Visit Shoreditch or Brixton if time allows. Public transport (Underground and buses) is quick and reliable. The main airports are Stansted or Luton, both connected to the city centre by train and coach in 45–60 minutes.
