Best time to travel

Bucharest is accessible year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Winter can be cold, but if you don't mind the chill, it's low season for prices. Avoid December if you want to skip Christmas crowds.

What to do on arrival

Bucharest has plenty to offer: Revolution Square, the Palace of Parliament (one of the world's largest buildings), Stavropoleos Church, and the historic Lipscani district. Enjoy local cuisine, Communist-era architecture, and the city's cultural scene. Henri Coandă Airport is about 16 km from the city centre.
