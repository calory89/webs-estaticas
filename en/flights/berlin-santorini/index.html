Best time to visit

Santorini is stunning year-round. April to June and September to October offer perfect weather with fewer summer crowds. December and January are quieter and cheaper, though some bars and restaurants may have reduced hours. Skip July and August if you want to avoid peak tourism.

What to do in Santorini

The airport is on the island, easy to reach from the centre. Spend time in Oia for the sunset, explore Kamari and Perivolos with their black sand beaches, taste local wines in vineyards, and wander the white villages on the heights. Eat at tavernas by the sea and swim in the crystal waters near the volcano.
