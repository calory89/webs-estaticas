Best time to visit

Singapore and Kuala Lumpur have tropical weather: hot and humid year-round. The dry season is most comfortable (February–April and July–September in Singapore; June–August in Kuala Lumpur). Avoid monsoon months if you dislike heavy rain. December to January also attracts more tourists and prices may rise slightly. Check local festival calendars to make the most of your trip.

What to do when you arrive in Kuala Lumpur

Kuala Lumpur is modern and easy to navigate. The Petronas Towers are the city's iconic landmark; you can walk the skybridge connecting both towers. Central Market is perfect for local food and souvenirs. If time allows, the Batu Caves are 15 km north: a stunning Hindu sanctuary inside a limestone cavern. Menara Kuala Lumpur observation tower gives 360° views. Public transport is efficient: the airport has the KLIA Express train to the city centre in 28 minutes.
