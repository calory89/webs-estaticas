Best time to visit

Kyoto shines in spring (March–May) with cherry blossoms, and autumn (September–November) with crimson leaves. Winter (December–February) is quiet and cold but less crowded. Avoid summer (June–August), which is humid. If you're purely focused on flight savings, book 2–3 months ahead; if you want good weather and value combined, aim for October.

What to do when you arrive

Kyoto is temples and tradition: Fushimi Inari with its thousands of red torii gates, the Golden Pavilion (Kinkaku-ji), the bamboo forest of Arashiyama, and the Imperial Palace. Walk through the Gion geisha district at dusk. Kyoto Station connects you to all of Japan by train in minutes. Eat at local markets like Nishiki Market and try kaiseki (haute Japanese cuisine).

What airport do I land at near Kyoto?

The closest airport to Kyoto is Kansai (KIX), about 75 km away. From there, a high-speed train takes you to central Kyoto in just over an hour. You can also fly into Osaka (near Kansai) and access the region from there.
