Best time to visit Riga

Riga in autumn and early winter (November) has a special charm with early Christmas decorations. Prices remain competitive before peak December dates. For milder weather, spring (May) and summer (June-July) are ideal, though fares rise. Travel outside school holidays for better rates.

What to do when you arrive in Riga

Riga International Airport is 13 km from the city centre. Latvia's capital boasts a well-preserved medieval old town, traditional food markets, art museums, and distinctive Art Nouveau architecture. Stroll along the Daugava River, visit Riga Cathedral, and sample local cuisine. The city is compact and walkable or easily navigable by public transport. Perfect for a week or long weekend.
