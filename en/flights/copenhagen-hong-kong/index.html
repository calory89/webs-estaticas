Copenhagen to Hong Kong starts at €591 return. Median price sits at €650. With Air China and one stopover, you'll connect two vibrant cities on a tight budget.

Price and when to book

The cheapest ticket is €591, though prices typically hover around €650. We've seen fares up to €756. Book 2–3 weeks ahead for the best rates; last-minute tends to cost more. Fly October to March (Hong Kong's dry season) to skip peak pricing.

Airline and stopovers

Air China operates this route. Your flight has 1 stopover, so it's not direct. Expect a technical stop at an intermediate hub; it adds hours but cuts costs versus a nonstop flight. Check which airport you'll stop at when you book.

Best time to travel

October to March delivers dry, mild weather (15–25°C) and fewer crowds. April to September brings heat, humidity and occasional typhoons. January and February spike in price due to Chinese New Year. Consider October, November or March to balance weather and fare.

What to do on arrival

Hong Kong is unmissable: ride the Star Ferry across Victoria Harbour, climb to Peak Victoria for panoramas, explore Central and Sheung Wan's streets, roam Mong Kok and Causeway Bay for local buzz. Don't skip Kowloon, Victoria Harbour sunset cruises or a day trip to New Territories. Public transport is efficient and cheap.

FAQ

Is the Copenhagen–Hong Kong flight direct?

No, it has 1 stopover. Air China operates this route with a technical stop along the way, which extends travel time but keeps fares lower.

Can I find cheaper flights than €591?

Unlikely. The lowest price is €591 return. We've sampled €591, €650 and €756. Book early, these are tight fares for this distance.

Which airline flies Copenhagen to Hong Kong?

Air China covers this route according to our data. A solid carrier with strong Asia–Europe connectivity.

What's the best season to visit Hong Kong?

October to March: cool, dry, stable weather. Avoid April–September (heat, humidity, typhoons). January–February sees prices jump for Chinese New Year.
