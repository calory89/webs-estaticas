Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €135 per person return. Fares on this route range from €135 to €148 depending on your chosen date. The median sits at €139. As a rule of thumb, book as early as possible for the best price. If you spot a tariff you like, buy it without hesitation. Dates with cheaper availability tend to be less touristy ones, so avoid bank holidays and bridges.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route. The flight has 1 stop, so it's not direct. The stopover is brief but saves you significant money compared to a nonstop. Check what your fare includes for baggage: Ryanair charges extra for checked luggage and a carry-on, so read the conditions carefully before purchasing.

Best time to visit

Prague is beautiful year-round, but the colder months (December, January, February) usually offer lower prices because there are fewer tourists. Spring (April, May) and autumn (September, October) are ideal: pleasant weather and less crowded than summer. For the best price, travel between November and March, but prepare for cold and short daylight hours.

What to do when you arrive

Prague airport (Václav Havel) is 20 km from the city centre. You can take public buses or the train from the terminal. Once downtown, don't miss Charles Bridge, Old Town Square and Prague Castle. Czech beer is legendary and available in any bar, so make the most of it. The city is very walkable and prices are far lower than in Western Europe.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Valencia to Prague?

No, this route has 1 stop. It's typical for this connection and the reason why prices are so competitive.

What is Prague's airport?

It's Václav Havel (PRG), the country's main airport. It's well connected to the city centre by public transport.

Is Ryanair the only option for this route?

Based on our data, Ryanair operates these prices. You can always check other airlines, but they're likely to be more expensive.

How many days should I spend in Prague?

Three to four days are enough to see the main sights. If you want to take trips nearby (Kutná Hora, Český Krumlov), add another one or two days.
