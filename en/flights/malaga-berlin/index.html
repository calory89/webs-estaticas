Best time to visit

Berlin works well year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) deliver pleasant weather and fewer crowds than July–August. If you fancy winter travel on a budget, pack warm—but Christmas markets, museums and nightlife make it worth it.

What to do when you arrive

Berlin has plenty on offer: the Jewish Quarter, Brandenburg Gate, Berlin Wall remnants, world-class galleries and excellent food. Public transport is excellent; grab a multi-day travel pass at the airport to get around the city hassle-free.
