Fly from Santiago to Miami with Vueling from €631 per person return. The median price is €654, giving you a solid reference point for this route. With one stopover included, it's the most affordable way to cross the Atlantic from Galicia.

Price and when to book

The base price is €631 return, with options ranging from €631 to €657 depending on dates and availability. Book as far in advance as possible, the earlier you reserve, the better your chances of securing these fares. Check regularly as prices shift with demand. Winter trips (like the 8 January example) often offer good value.

Airline and stopovers

You'll travel with Vueling, which operates this route with 1 stopover. It's not a direct flight, but the stopover is part of achieving these low prices. Vueling is a reliable budget carrier, so expect basics: seat included, but hand luggage is limited. Extra services cost extra.

Best time to visit

Miami has tropical weather year-round. Northern winter (December to March) is peak season but also when you'll find better flight prices from Europe due to higher competition. Summer is cheaper for accommodation but hotter and more humid. For flight savings, travel between November and March.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Miami International Airport (MIA), about 6 km from the city. Take a taxi, rideshare, or public bus to Miami Beach or Downtown. South Beach is what everyone heads for first; also worth visiting are Wynwood Walls (street art), Vizcaya Mansion (history and gardens), and the Everglades. Hit Little Havana for genuine Cuban food.

FAQ

Can I find cheaper flights from Santiago to Miami?

The base price is €631. Prices vary by exact travel date, so you might find different fares, but this is the lowest starting point. Booking well in advance gives you the best options.

Are there direct flights from Santiago to Miami?

No. This Vueling route includes 1 stopover, so you'll have at least one stop on your journey.

Why does Vueling operate this route?

Vueling is a budget airline connecting European cities to long-haul destinations. They operate these routes through strategic partnerships and stopovers, offering competitive fares compared to traditional carriers.

What can I do in Miami on a tight budget?

Many beaches are free (South Beach, Lummus Park), museums have reduced admission days, Wynwood Walls is free street art, and waterfront walks cost nothing. Eat in Little Havana for cheap meals. Public transport (Metrorail) is affordable.
