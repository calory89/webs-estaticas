Best time to travel

Mauritius is a year-round destination, but pick your season wisely. Avoid the southern summer (October to April) if you're concerned about cyclone risk. The sweet spot is May to September: dry weather, pleasant temperatures, and calm seas for diving or sailing. Any season works for beaches, but the dry season is your safest bet.

What to do when you arrive

Mauritius delivers stunning beaches like Pereybere and Trou aux Cerfs, but there's far more: snorkelling on coral reefs, hiking in the mountains, museums on the nation's history. Don't miss Port Louis (the capital), coastal villages, and nearby islands reachable by boat. It's perfect for switching off and exploring Indian Ocean biodiversity.
