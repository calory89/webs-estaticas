Best time to visit

Hong Kong has a subtropical climate. October, November, March, and April are most comfortable: mild temperatures and less rain. Skip summer (June to September) for extreme heat and humidity. December and January are cool but peak season. Book well ahead depending on when you want to travel.

What to do when you arrive

Hong Kong is packed with attractions: boat rides on Victoria Harbour, Peak Tram for panoramic views, historic temples, traditional markets, and shopping in winding streets. Hong Kong International Airport connects easily to the city centre via fast rail link, buses, and taxis.
