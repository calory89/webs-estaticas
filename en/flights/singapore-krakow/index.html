Best time to visit

Krakow is spectacular from November to March: white winters, Christmas markets if you travel in December, and snowy landscapes. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are also good, with mild weather. Summer is peak season with higher prices and crowds. Travel in low season to save.

What to do in Krakow

Krakow is a medieval gem: the Main Market Square (Rynek Główny) with art galleries, Wawel Cathedral and castle are unmissable. Explore the old town on foot, eat pierniki (dumplings) and a proper żurek (rye soup). Weather permitting, many take a day trip to Auschwitz (60 km away). Museums are accessible and affordable.
