Best time to visit

Lisbon is pleasant year-round. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. January and February are cheaper if you don't mind cooler temperatures. Skip July and August for peace and quiet.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Humberto Delgado Airport, 7 km from the centre. Visit Alfama district, try pastel de nata, take a boat trip on the Tagus and climb to São Jorge Castle. Belém, with its tower and monastery, is a must. The metro is quick and cheap to get around.
