Best time to visit

Vietnam has a tropical climate. November to April brings dry, mild weather—perfect for Hanoi without the heat. May to October is rainy. Plan ahead to catch low-season offers if possible.

What to do in Hanoi

Explore the Old Quarter, visit the Temple of Literature, relax at Hoan Kiem Lake and sample local food in the markets. Noi Bai international airport is 25 km from the city centre; taxis and buses are the most affordable options.
