Best time to visit

Helsinki is beautiful year-round, but the best season depends on your interests. For the Northern Lights, visit October to March. Summer (June-August) offers clear nights and mild temperatures. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September) are quieter with fewer tourists. Choose based on the climate you prefer.

What to do when you arrive

Finland's capital blends Nordic design, historic architecture and nature. Visit the white cathedral, Suomenlinna fortress (island stronghold), the artistic Kallio district and art museums. Don't miss a traditional sauna bath. Outside the centre, you'll find lakes and forests ideal for hiking. The city is compact and easy to explore on foot or by public transport.

What should I see in Helsinki in 5 days?

Spend 2-3 days in the centre: cathedral, Kallio district, Suomenlinna. Use 1 day for a traditional sauna tour or public bathhouse visit. Reserve another for day trips to nearby lakes or forests. Helsinki is easily explored on foot and by public transport.
