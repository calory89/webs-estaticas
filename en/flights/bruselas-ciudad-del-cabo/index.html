Airline and stopovers

Best time to travel

Cape Town has two clear seasons: summer (December to March) is warm and busy; winter (June to August) is cooler but dry. November and April are transition months with good weather and fewer crowds. Travel when it suits your schedule and budget—don't wait for specific dates, as prices always rise then.

What to do when you arrive

Cape Town mixes mountains, beaches and wildlife. Table Mountain dominates the landscape with 360-degree views. Camps Bay and Clifton beaches are perfect for relaxing. Robben Island offers history. Safaris in nearby reserves like Kruger can be booked from the city. The historic Waterfront area combines shopping, restaurants and sea views.
