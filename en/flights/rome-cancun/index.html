Best time to visit

November is a good month: moderate prices combined with warm weather in Cancún. Avoid December and January when prices peak during high season and occupancy rises. February to April is dry and pleasant, though pricier. June to October is hurricane season with lower fares but weather risk.

What to do when you arrive

Cancún is the gateway to the Riviera Maya: beaches, cenotes and Mayan ruins like Chichén Itzá or Tulum are nearby. The international airport is 25 km from the centre. Rent a car or use colectivos to get around the area. Protect yourself from the sun and bring insect repellent. The currency is the Mexican peso, though euros are accepted in tourist areas.

Is the Rome to Cancún flight direct?

No, it has one stopover. Ryanair does not operate direct flights on this route, so expect at least one connection. Total duration depends on the connecting airport, which varies by date.

Which Rome airport does the flight depart from?

The route is from Rome (ROM), which includes Rome Fiumicino and Rome Ciampino airports. Ryanair typically uses Ciampino. Confirm your terminal when you receive your flight confirmation.

What is the best month to travel to Cancún for price and weather?

November is excellent: warm weather, less rain than summer and lower prices than December–January. April and May are dry but more expensive. June to October is hurricane season with reduced fares but weather risk.

How far is Cancun from Rome?

About 9,200 km in a straight line between ROM and CUN airports.

Is there a train from Rome to Cancun?

No. There is an ocean between Rome and Cancun (about 9,200 km between the airports), so the practical way to get there is by plane.
