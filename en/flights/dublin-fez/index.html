Best time to visit

Fez shines between October and April: mild temperatures (15–25°C) without the summer heat. April and May are ideal if you fancy spring. Skip July and August when temperatures hit 35°C and the Medina fills with tourists. Prices tend to be keener in low season (November to January), though December edges up a bit for the holidays.

What to do when you arrive in Fez

The Medina of Fez is a UNESCO World Heritage site: winding alleys, ancient riads, the Weavers' Souk. Don't miss Al Quaraouiyine University (the world's oldest continuously operating university), the Royal Palace or Chaouara Tannery. Outside the old town, New Fez has a more relaxed vibe. Eat in the souks, try harira soup and sip mint tea under the stars.

FAQ

How much is the cheapest flight Dublin to Fez?

From €114 return. Sample prices range €114 to €143, so there's variation. Booking early boosts your odds of landing the lowest fare.

Does Ryanair fly direct Dublin to Fez?

No, there's 1 stopover included. It's not direct, but the price makes up for it. Plan a buffer if connection concerns you.

What's the best time to visit Fez?

October to April is ideal: mild weather and moderate tourism. April and May are especially lovely. Avoid July–August for extreme heat and crowds.

What can I see and do in Fez?

The Medina comes first: an ancient labyrinthine city with palaces, mosques and souks. Al Quaraouiyine University, Chaouara Tannery and the Royal Palace are must-sees. Eat in the souks and soak up authentic Moroccan atmosphere.
