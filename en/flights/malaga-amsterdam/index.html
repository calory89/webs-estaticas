Best time to visit

Amsterdam is lovely year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: mild weather, less rain than winter, and fewer crowds than summer. If you travel in January–February like the example we found, expect cold and frequent showers, but also fewer tourists and more stable prices. July–August is peak season: flights are pricier and the streets are busier.

What to do when you arrive

Amsterdam airport (AMS) has excellent public transport links to the city centre. Cycle or take a boat along the canals, visit the Van Gogh Museum and the Anne Frank House, explore the Red Light District, and try croquettes and Dutch pastries. The city works well for a short 3–5 day break or a full week if you want to include Rotterdam or Delft.
