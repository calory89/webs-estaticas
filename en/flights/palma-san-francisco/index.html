Best time to visit

San Francisco is a year-round destination, but May to September (summer and autumn) offer the most stable weather. November is also appealing: less peak tourism than summer, mild temperatures (15–20 °C) and good visibility. Skip December and January if you're seeking lower prices—that's the holiday rush. February to March is another window with good fares and pleasant weather.

What to do when you arrive

San Francisco has plenty to offer: the Golden Gate Bridge, Alcatraz, Chinatown, Mission District with its street art, and Ocean Beach cliffs. The international airport (SFO) is roughly 20 km from the city centre, well served by BART (rapid transit), bus and taxi. The city is compact and walkable in many areas. Spend at least 3–4 days to enjoy it properly.
