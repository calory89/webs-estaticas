Best time to visit Nassau

Nassau enjoys tropical weather year-round. High season is winter (December to March), with warm temperatures and less rain; it's also the most expensive. To save on accommodation and tours, consider May-November, though this includes Atlantic hurricane season (monitor forecasts). September-October is the quietest and most budget-friendly period.

What to do in Nassau and the Bahamas

Nassau blends white-sand beaches (Paradise Island, Cable Beach), snorkelling on nearby reefs, and nightlife in the historic centre. Atlantis Resort is iconic but pricey; public beaches are free. Visit the Straw Market for local crafts, try fresh seafood, and relax in turquoise waters. Nassau airport (NAS) is 12 km from the city centre with good taxi and bus connections.
