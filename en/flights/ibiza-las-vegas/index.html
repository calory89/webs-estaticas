Best time to visit

The Nevada desert is scorching in summer (over 40°C). October through April is ideal: comfortable temperatures (15-20°C), fewer crowds and lower prices. If you skip the July-August peak, you'll save money and enjoy a better experience overall.

What to do when you arrive

Las Vegas is more than casinos. The Strip is the main tourist hub, but Valley of Fire National Park, Hoover Dam, and the Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area are worth the trip. For relaxation, resorts offer luxury spas. Downtown's Fremont Street Experience captures old Vegas. Shows rotate constantly: circus, concerts, magic.
