Price and when to book

The lowest price we found is €694 return. Fares vary between €694 and €761 depending on dates and availability. For this route, the earlier you book, the better: low-cost seats sell out fast. Keep checking flight search engines to catch deals.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route. The outbound flight includes 2 stopovers. There is no direct service Gran Canaria-Miami, so add travel time. Stopovers may be at airports across Europe or North America: check your booking to confirm where you'll stop.

Best time to visit

Miami is a year-round destination, but winter (December to February) is peak season: warm, sunny days and high prices. Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) offer good weather at better rates. Avoid hurricane season (June to October).

What to do when you arrive

Miami Beach is the obvious first stop: sandy shores, Art Deco architecture and vibrant nightlife. Little Havana pulses with Cuban culture. Wynwood Walls is a must for street art lovers. Vizcaya Museum and the Pérez Art Museum offer quieter cultural pursuits. The Everglades, just outside the city, deliver spectacular wildlife and nature.
