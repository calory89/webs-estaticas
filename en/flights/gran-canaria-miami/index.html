Best time to travel

Miami is a year-round destination, but keep the climate in mind: June to November is hurricane season. Winter months (December to April) are pricier but best for beaches and outdoor activities. Prices often drop in May, June and September if you skip holidays and long weekends.

What to do when you arrive in Miami

Miami offers white-sand beaches, Art Deco architecture in South Beach, world-class nightlife and Cuban cuisine in Wynwood. Explore Little Havana for Caribbean culture, visit the Pérez Art Museum, or unwind in Coconut Grove. Miami International Airport (MIA) is 20 km from the city centre; bus, taxi or car rental are quick options.
