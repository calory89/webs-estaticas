Best time to travel

Rio de Janeiro is a year-round destination, but each season has its appeal. Spring and autumn offer pleasant weather without the crowds or peak-season prices of summer. Check fares regularly: prices vary with demand, local holidays, and school holidays.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Rio's International Airport (Galeão). From there, bus, taxi and transport services are available into the city. Once in Rio, don't miss Christ the Redeemer, Sugarloaf Mountain, the beaches of Copacabana and Ipanema, and historic neighbourhoods like Lapa. Local cuisine and nightlife are essential too.
