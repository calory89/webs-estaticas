Best time to visit

Lisbon is pleasant nearly year-round. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) offer comfortable temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. July and August are hot and busy with tourists. If you want quieter streets and potentially cheaper deals, winter works, though expect more rain.

What to do in Lisbon

Portugal's capital has plenty to offer: the tiles of Sintra, iconic yellow trams in Alfama, views from São Jorge Castle, and those addictive Belém pastéis you can't skip. The Tagus riverside is perfect for strolls, and viewpoints (miradouros) are scattered everywhere. A week gives you time to explore the city centre and day-trip to Cascais or the Sintra mountains.
