Best time to visit

Berlin is appealing year-round. April to October usually offers the best weather, while winter (December to February) brings festive Berlin but colder temperatures. Avoid school holidays and bank holidays if you want better prices. Weekday flights tend to be cheaper than weekends.

What to do when you arrive in Berlin

Berlin is a must-see historic and cultural hub. Visit the Berlin Wall, Brandenburg Gate, world-class museums and a vibrant food scene. Berlin airport (BER) is well-connected to the city centre by train and bus. Plan ahead to make the most of your time.
