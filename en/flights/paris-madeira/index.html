Transavia France connects Paris to Madeira on a direct flight from €122 return. The median price sits at €127, so you've got solid options in the lower bracket. A 10-day trip to the Portuguese islands needn't break the bank.

Price and when to book

€122 is the lowest price you'll find on this route. The sample prices we've seen (€122, €127, €159) tell you availability exists at that low rate, but it won't last forever. Book 2–3 months ahead and you'll secure the best fares. Leave it to the last minute and prices climb fast.

Airline and stopovers

Transavia France is the operator here, and it's a direct flight: no changes, no stopovers. You depart Paris at 06:00 and land in Madeira without hassle. It's the quickest, most straightforward way to get there.

Best time to visit

Madeira is worth visiting year-round thanks to its mild climate. May to October brings warmer temperatures and less rain, ideal if you want guaranteed sun. January (like the example search) is low season for tourism, which explains the bargain fares; you'll get some rain but fewer tourists and a slower pace. Spring offers the sweet spot: decent weather and reasonable prices.

What to do when you arrive

The airport (Cristiano Ronaldo International) sits in Funchal. Once you're there, world-class hiking on the island's coastal trails is waiting, plus a stroll through Funchal's old town and traditional markets. Day trips to mountain villages like Câmara de Lobos, botanical gardens, and Portuguese cuisine round out the offer. Madeira suits both active travellers and those after a slower pace.
