Best time to travel

Luxembourg is worth visiting most of the year. Spring and autumn bring pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Prices don't follow a fixed low season, so your own schedule matters most. Book early and stay flexible with dates if you can.

What to do when you arrive

Luxembourg is small but packed with culture. The Old Town, a Unesco World Heritage site, deserves a full day: wander narrow streets, visit the Cathedral, descend into the Casemats (underground galleries). The Museum of Modern Art (MUDAM) and the Grand-Ducal Palace are must-sees. Rent a car to explore medieval castles in nearby towns like Vianden.
