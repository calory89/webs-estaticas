Ryanair connects Dublin with Madeira on a direct flight starting from €55 per person. The median price hovers around €80, so you have room to find good fares. Funchal awaits with beaches, volcanoes and hiking.

Price and when to book

Prices start from €55 for the full journey, though you'll also find options at €80 and €149 depending on dates and availability. Ryanair usually offers lower fares the earlier you book, especially for seasonal routes. Check and compare regularly if you're flying soon: flexibility is your asset.

Airline and direct flights

Ryanair operates this route with no stops, so you fly direct from Dublin (DUB) to Funchal (FNC). A straightforward flight of a few hours without changes—ideal to save time and hassle. Always check what baggage is included in your base fare.

Best time to travel

Madeira has mild weather year-round, but October to April offers pleasant temperatures (18–22°C). Spring and autumn are ideal: less rain, fewer crowds and generally lower prices than summer. Winter is low season for tourism, which can help your search for cheap flights.

What to do in Funchal and beyond

Funchal is the perfect base: stroll the historic centre, visit the cathedral and Madeira's gardens. Hike the levadas (ancient irrigation channels) or climb Pico do Arieiro. Natural swimming pools, cable cars with views and local food round out your trip. The airport is 15 minutes from the centre by car.

The lowest recorded is €55 per person return. Average prices hover around €80, but fares at €149 are also available depending on dates and availability.

What to see and do in Madeira?

Funchal has museums and a historic centre. Hiking the levadas is essential. Pico do Arieiro offers stunning views. Natural pools and cable cars are popular attractions. Mild weather allows outdoor activities year-round.
