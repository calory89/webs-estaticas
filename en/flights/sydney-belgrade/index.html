Best time to visit

Belgrade shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with mild weather and less mass tourism than summer. November is also viable if you don't mind the cold. Winter is cheaper but rainy. Avoid July and August if you want to skip crowds and higher prices.

What to do when you arrive

Belgrade is a Balkan capital with character. Stroll through Kalemegdan Fortress, explore the bohemian Žižkovac neighbourhood, visit the city's museums and enjoy nightlife on Danube river boats. Serbian cuisine is tasty and cheap. The international airport is about 20 km from the centre, well-connected by public transport.
