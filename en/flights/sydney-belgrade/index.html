Price and when to book

The lowest price we've seen is €1,018 return. Typical prices sit at €1,192, with some up to €1,302. On such long intercontinental routes, booking 6-8 weeks ahead is ideal. The earlier you book, the better fares you'll secure. Prices tend to be more competitive if you avoid school holidays and public holidays.

Airline and stopovers

China Southern Airlines operates this route with 2 stopovers. No direct flights exist on this connection, so you'll change planes twice. Stopovers are normal on such distant routes. Check with the airline for connection times and stopover locations to plan your luggage and rest properly.

Best time to visit

Belgrade shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with mild weather and less mass tourism than summer. November is also viable if you don't mind the cold. Winter is cheaper but rainy. Avoid July and August if you want to skip crowds and higher prices.

What to do when you arrive

Belgrade is a Balkan capital with character. Stroll through Kalemegdan Fortress, explore the bohemian Žižkovac neighbourhood, visit the city's museums and enjoy nightlife on Danube river boats. Serbian cuisine is tasty and cheap. The international airport is about 20 km from the centre, well-connected by public transport.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Sydney to Belgrade?

No. This route requires 2 stopovers with China Southern Airlines. Direct flights between Australia and Serbia do not currently exist.

What is the cheapest price I can find?

The lowest recorded price is €1,018 return. Typical prices are around €1,192. Booking in advance is key to securing a better fare.

Which airline flies this route?

China Southern Airlines. Check directly with the airline for baggage policies, meals and in-flight services.

What to see in Belgrade in a week?

Kalemegdan Fortress, main museums, the Žižkovac neighbourhood, Danube walks and local cuisine. A week is enough to enjoy without rush.
