Price and when to book

Fares start from 923 € return. Our price sample ranges from 923 € to 963 €, with a median of 955 €. Book as soon as possible: prices tend to climb closer to departure. October and November are good months to search before New Year price spikes.

Airline and stopovers

Lufthansa operates this route. You'll have one stopover on your outbound journey: it's not a direct flight, but that's standard between these two cities. The journey is long (around 11–12 hours of flight time plus the stopover), so expect a full travel day.

Best time to visit

November and December are ideal for Mexico City: mild weather, less rain than summer, and festivities start building up. Avoid July and August if heat and humidity bother you. January also attracts tourists after the holidays, so prices can climb.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Mexico City International Airport (Benito Juárez). From there, take an official taxi, Uber, or bus to the centre. Don't miss the Historic Centre, the Museum of Anthropology, Xochimilco, and the Frida Kahlo Museum. The food is spectacular: eat tacos, mole, and street food in the Coyoacán and Roma neighbourhoods.
