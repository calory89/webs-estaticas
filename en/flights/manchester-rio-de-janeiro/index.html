Prices on this route range from €762 to €1,350. The median is €878, giving you a realistic idea of typical costs. Book as far ahead as possible—the earlier you reserve, the lower the fares tend to be. Ryanair's low-cost availability varies, so don't delay.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with 2 stopovers on the outbound journey. It's not a direct flight, but that's how these competitive prices stay low. Check connection times carefully, and remember Ryanair charges for checked baggage—pack light or budget for that extra cost.

Best time to visit

Rio de Janeiro is pleasant throughout most of the year. To save money, travel outside European school holidays and Rio's peak season (Carnival in February/March). April to October typically see fewer crowds and more stable airport pricing.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Rio de Janeiro International Airport (Galeão). From the city centre, use bus, train or taxi. Must-see attractions include Christ the Redeemer, Sugarloaf Mountain, Copacabana and Ipanema beaches, and the National Library. Buy a metro/bus pass to get around easily.

FAQ

How many days should I spend in Rio de Janeiro?

3–5 days is usually enough to see the main sights: Christ the Redeemer, Sugarloaf, beaches and the historic centre. With more time, you can explore the surrounding area or combine Rio with other Brazilian cities.
