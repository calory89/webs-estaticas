Best time to visit

Sri Lanka has two monsoons: the southwest (May to September) and the northeast (December to February). Best periods are March to April and October to November, when there's less rain and more stable temperatures. Outside these windows, you'll face less tourist competition and possibly better availability, though some areas will be more humid or rainy.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB), about 30 km from Colombo. The capital is the ideal starting point: visit Galle Face Green, Buddhist temples, the National Museum, and the harbour district. From here you can explore the rest of the island: southern beaches, tea plantations in the central highlands, or the cultural triangle with Anuradhapura and Polonnaruwa. Remember the country drives on the left.
