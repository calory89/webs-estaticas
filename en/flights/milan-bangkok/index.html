Best time to travel

Thailand has a tropical climate with a dry season (November to February) and monsoon season (May to October). For pleasant weather, travel between November and February when temperatures are comfortable and rainfall is minimal. These dates are also popular, so prices tend to be higher; if you're after cheaper fares, consider May–June or September–October and be prepared for occasional rain.

What to do in Bangkok

Bangkok is a vibrant city: don't miss the Grand Palace and Temple of the Emerald Buddha, floating markets like Damnoen Saduak, and Chinatown. Enjoy street food, relax with a traditional Thai massage, and explore Buddhist temples. Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK) is well connected to the city by train and taxi.
