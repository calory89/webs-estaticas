Best time to visit

Singapore is a year-round destination, but October to February are the most comfortable months: lower temperatures and less rain. Avoid the peak monsoon between November and January if you prefer completely clear days. Travel between May and September to find lower prices, though rain showers are more frequent.

What to do when you arrive

Singapore is compact and highly accessible. Don't miss Marina Bay (and its iconic Marina Bay Sands hotel), the Supertree gardens in Gardens by the Bay, and historic Chinatown. Changi Airport is 20 minutes from the city centre by metro or taxi. Spend at least 3–4 days to enjoy the city: street food, museums, temples and world-class nightlife.
