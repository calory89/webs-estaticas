Best time to visit

Kyoto shines in spring (March–April, cherry blossoms) and autumn (October–November, fall colours). Winter (December–February) is quiet and cold. July and August bring heat and humidity, less ideal. Plan months ahead for peak season; in low season (January–February, June) prices drop and crowds thin out.

What to do when you arrive

Osaka Kansai Airport (KIX) is 75 km from Kyoto; the Haruka Express train gets you to Kyoto in 90 minutes. Visit Fushimi Inari (thousands of red torii gates), Arashiyama (bamboo grove), temples like Kinkaku-ji and Ryoan-ji, and Gion (geishas at dusk). Stay central; transport is cheap and efficient.
