Price and when to book

Prices start at €116 for the complete round trip, with mid-range options at €135 and €151. These figures were checked on 23 October, so book early: the sooner you reserve, the better rate you lock in. Travel in December if you spot these prices—like the example from 5 to 15 December.

Airline and stops

Ryanair is your only option on this route, operating with 1 stop on the outbound leg. There are no direct flights Oslo-Venice, but the connection is manageable and prices make up for it. The return also runs through the same stopover system.

Best time to travel

December works if you catch deals like those shown. Shoulder months (April-May and September-October) usually offer a good balance between pleasant weather and reasonable fares. Skip July-August if you're hunting cheap tickets: Venice gets rammed and flights spike. Check regularly from two months before your target date.

What to do when you arrive in Venice

You'll land at Marco Polo airport, 13 km from the centre. From there, bus, vaporetto (water bus) or water taxi get you to the lagoon. Explore St. Mark's Basilica, Doge's Palace, the canals, and the islands of Murano and Burano. Venice is best explored on foot: wear comfy shoes and don't expect cars.

FAQ

What's the minimum price for flights Oslo to Venice?

The lowest recorded price is €116 return with Ryanair. The median sits at €135, so don't expect much lower. Sample prices: €116, €135, €151.

Are there direct flights from Oslo to Venice?

No. Ryanair operates this route with 1 stop on the outbound leg. No direct options are currently available.

Which airline flies Oslo to Venice?

Ryanair is the airline connecting both cities in the available data. It's a low-cost carrier, so expect paid baggage and basic services.

What can I see in Venice over 10 days?

St. Mark's Basilica, Doge's Palace, Rialto Bridge, gondola or vaporetto rides, the islands of Murano (glass) and Burano (lace), and churches like Santa Maria della Salute. Spend at least 3–4 days in the historic centre.
