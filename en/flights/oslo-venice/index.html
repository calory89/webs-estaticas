What to do when you arrive in Venice

You'll land at Marco Polo airport, 13 km from the centre. From there, bus, vaporetto (water bus) or water taxi get you to the lagoon. Explore St. Mark's Basilica, Doge's Palace, the canals, and the islands of Murano and Burano. Venice is best explored on foot: wear comfy shoes and don't expect cars.
