Best time to visit

Berlin is a fantastic destination year-round. Autumn (September–October) and early winter (November) offer pleasant weather, fewer crowds and lower prices. Spring (April–May) is also excellent if you don't mind sharing the city with more tourists. Skip July–August if you're after peace and better rates.

What to do when you arrive

Berlin is waiting with history, culture and nightlife. Explore Museum Island, climb the Reichstag dome, walk the Wall remnants and dive into the art scene in Kreuzberg. Berlin Brandenburg Airport (BER) is 25 km from the centre; reach it easily by regional train or shared shuttle bus.
