Best time to visit

Bologna appeals year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) bring pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Low season (November–February) means lower fares but expect cold and rain. July–August is expensive and busy.

What to do when you arrive

Bologna is Italy's food capital. Walk the medieval porticoes, climb Torre Asinelli, visit the Basilica di San Petronio, and eat authentic tagliatelle alla bolognese. Marconi Airport is 4 km from the centre; bus or taxi takes 20 minutes.
