Flights between Sydney and Hanoi start from €448 return, with a median price of €610 return. Xiamen Airlines is your carrier, with one stopover on the outbound flight. Book in advance to secure the best fare.

Price and when to book

We found flights ranging from €448 to €831 in our sample. The median price sits at €610. For this route, book as early as possible: the sooner you reserve, the better your chances of a low fare. Avoid booking at the last minute.

Airline and stopovers

Xiamen Airlines operates this route with 1 stopover on the outbound leg. The outbound flight departs around 12:35 and the return around 15:40. One stopover is standard for this intercontinental connection; it's not direct, but the connection time is reasonable.

Best time to travel

Vietnam is pleasant from October to April, with drier weather and moderate temperatures. November to January typically see mid-range hotel and flight occupancy. If you're hunting for lower fares, travelling outside peak season (July-September) may help, though the climate is more humid.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

Noi Bai International Airport is about 25 km from the city centre. Once in Hanoi, explore the Old Quarter, Ha Long Bay (2-3 days from Hanoi), temples and local cuisine. The currency is Vietnamese dong. Vietnam is very affordable and accessible once you're on the ground.

FAQ

Is the Sydney-Hanoi flight direct?

No, it has 1 stopover. Xiamen Airlines operates this connection, with one intermediate stop before reaching Hanoi.

What's the cheapest price you've found?

€448 return. Prices in our sample range from €448 to €831, so bargains are possible, but they require flexibility and early booking.

Which airline flies from Sydney to Hanoi?

Xiamen Airlines. This is the carrier connecting both cities on this route with 1 stopover.

What's worth seeing in Hanoi besides the centre?

Ha Long Bay is a must-see (about 3 hours away), Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum, Temple of Literature, Hoan Kiem Lake and traditional villages nearby. Hanoi is the starting point for exploring northern Vietnam.
