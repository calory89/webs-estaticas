Best time to travel

Vietnam is pleasant from October to April, with drier weather and moderate temperatures. November to January typically see mid-range hotel and flight occupancy. If you're hunting for lower fares, travelling outside peak season (July-September) may help, though the climate is more humid.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

Noi Bai International Airport is about 25 km from the city centre. Once in Hanoi, explore the Old Quarter, Ha Long Bay (2-3 days from Hanoi), temples and local cuisine. The currency is Vietnamese dong. Vietnam is very affordable and accessible once you're on the ground.
