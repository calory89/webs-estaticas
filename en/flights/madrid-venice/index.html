Best time to visit Venice

January and February are low season: fewer tourists, lower prices and quieter streets. If you prefer warm weather, May–June and September–October are ideal: less crowded than July–August with decent conditions. Skip Carnival if you can't stand crowds (though it's spectacular). Winter lagoon has its own gloomy charm; summer brings heat and heaving masses of people.

What to do when you arrive in Venice

No cars: the city runs on boats and feet. Hop in a gondola, explore St Mark's Basilica, the Doge's Palace and the Rialto Bridge. Wander the backstreets to find real trattorias. If you arrive early, catch the sunset from San Giorgio Maggiore. Skip the tourist trap restaurants on St Mark's Square: the best food is tucked away in the side alleys.
