Best time to travel from Sydney to Tokyo

Japan shines in spring (March-May) and autumn (September-November), when the climate is mild and rainfall is lower. These periods coincide with spectacular natural events like cherry blossoms or autumn foliage. Winter (December-February) also attracts visitors, especially Australians seeking snow. Plan ahead to find better prices.

Things to do in Tokyo when you arrive

Tokyo is a captivating metropolis: explore temples like Senso-ji and Meiji, stroll through Shibuya and Shinjuku, eat at local markets such as Tsukiji, and relax in a traditional onsen. Haneda Airport connects well to the city centre by metro and train. Don't miss Harajuku, Asakusa, and Ginza. If you have time, consider a day trip to Kyoto or Hiroshima from Tokyo.
