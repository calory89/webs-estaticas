Best time to visit Phuket

Phuket's dry season runs November to April: pleasant temps, less rain, calm seas. It's peak season, so hotel prices climb. If you want good weather without the crowds, aim for November or March. Monsoon season (May to October) is cheaper but wetter.

What to do when you arrive

Phuket is the gateway to Thailand's beaches. From the airport you're in town in under an hour. Don't miss Patong, Kata and Karon beaches, Buddhist temples like Wat Chalong, or day trips to the Phi Phi Islands. The Phuket Town night market and Rawai port give you a taste of real local life.
