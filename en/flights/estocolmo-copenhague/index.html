Best time to visit

Copenhagen is lovely all year, but the best price-to-weather balance is April-May and September-October. Winter (December-February) can offer lower fares, though it's cold. Summer (June-August) pushes prices up and brings crowds. For budget travel, avoid European school holidays.

What to do in Copenhagen

Nyhavn is essential: colourful waterfront charm and lively restaurants. Explore Christianshavn, visit Rosenborg Castle and Tivoli Gardens (seasonal). Vesterbro is trendy for eating and drinks. Danes love cycling—hire a bike and get around like a local. Don't miss Nørrebro: independent cafés and design shops galore.
