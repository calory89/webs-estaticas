Price and when to book

Prices on this route range from €1,090 to €1,240 depending on availability and dates. The median is €1,137, giving you a realistic budget figure. Booking 3–4 weeks ahead usually works well for long-haul international routes like this one. Avoid European school holidays and Colombian peak season if you're chasing the lowest base fares.

Airline and stops

Icelandair operates this route with 2 stops. While they add travel time, these intermediate connections keep fares competitive compared to direct flights. Check with the airline for exact connection times on your chosen date to plan luggage handling and terminal transfers if needed.

Best time to travel

Bogotá is year-round friendly thanks to its altitude (2,640 metres). July–August and December tend to cost more due to European summer holidays and Christmas. Consider May, June, September or October for tighter pricing, though no automatic discount is guaranteed.

What to do when you arrive

Colombia's capital boasts world-class museums (Gold, Botero), historic neighbourhoods like La Candelaria, local food in Zona T, and vibrant nightlife. El Dorado airport is 15 km from centre; use authorised transport. Allow a day or two to adjust to altitude and enjoy the city's mild climate.
