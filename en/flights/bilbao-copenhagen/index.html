Best time to visit

Copenhagen is appealing year-round. Winter (February onwards) is cold but quieter. Spring and autumn offer pleasant temperatures and fewer crowds. Summer is peak season, so if you want lower prices, skip June, July and August.

What to do when you arrive

Stroll around Nyhavn, the historic harbour with colourful townhouses. Visit Amalienborg Palace and Tivoli Gardens. Don't miss the Little Mermaid and the bohemian neighbourhood of Christiania. For food, try smørrebrød (open-faced sandwiches) and local pastries. The airport is 8 km from the city centre with good public transport links.

What to see in Copenhagen in 3 days?

Nyhavn, Amalienborg Palace, Tivoli and Fredens Kirke Gardens are must-sees. Spend one day exploring the centre, another visiting palaces and historic neighbourhoods, and a third browsing cafés and Danish design shops.
