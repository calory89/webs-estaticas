Airline and stopovers

Air Canada operates this route with one stopover. It's not a direct flight, but that's standard for Europe-South America connections. The stopover adds travel time but makes these competitive prices possible. You'll arrive at El Dorado, Bogotá's main airport.

Best time to travel

Bogotá sits in the Andes with mild temperatures year-round (12-21°C). Best seasons are December-February and July-August for less rain. On a tight budget, March-May or September-October still offer good weather and fewer crowds. Avoid Easter week if you want lower fares.

What to see and do in Bogotá

La Candelaria is the historic centre with colonial architecture, museums and dining. Museo del Oro holds the world's largest collection of pre-Hispanic gold art. Monserrate, a mountain sanctuary, provides city views. Usaquén is a bohemian neighbourhood perfect for walking, eating and shopping. La Macarena is artistic and lively. Don't miss markets like San Alejo and try local food: bandeja paisa, ajiaco bogotano and single-origin coffee.
