Best time to visit

Tallinn shines in summer (June to August) with long days and pleasant temperatures. In winter (December to February) the city turns white and Christmas lights are magical, though it's very cold. Autumn (September to October) brings beautiful colours and fewer crowds. Avoid the Christmas season if you're price-conscious: it tends to be pricier.

What to do on arrival

Tallinn is a medieval gem with a UNESCO-listed Old Town. Stroll its cobbled streets, climb Pikk Hermann Tower, visit Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, and eat in traditional taverns in the old quarter. For beaches, try Käsmu, 30 km away. Public transport from the airport is excellent and affordable.

What can I see and do in Tallinn?
