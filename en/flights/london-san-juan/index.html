Best time to visit

Puerto Rico enjoys warm weather year-round. The dry season runs from December to April, ideal weather, but also peak tourist season and higher prices. For cheaper fares, consider travelling outside those months, though you'll risk occasional tropical rain. Steer clear of hurricane season (June–November) if safety is a priority.

What to do when you arrive in San Juan

Old San Juan is unmissable: cobblestone streets, colourful colonial buildings, and historic forts. Nearby beaches like Isla Verde, plus day trips to Flamenco (Culebra) and Vieques' bioluminescent waters, are essential. El Yunque National Rainforest offers lush hiking, and Puerto Rican food, mofongo, alcapurrias, is reason alone to visit.
