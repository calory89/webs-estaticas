Airline and stops

Best time to visit

Rome is pleasant year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer perfect weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is cheaper, though rain happens. Skip August if you want to avoid peak tourist season.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Leonardo da Vinci Airport (Fiumicino). Don't miss the Colosseum, Roman Forum, Vatican, and Trevi Fountain. Eat authentic cacio e pepe in Trastevere's backstreets. Public transport is cheap and reliable; buy a ticket at the airport.
