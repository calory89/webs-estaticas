Best time to visit

Cancun is a year-round destination, but December is peak season with higher prices. For better fares, consider April, May or September. Note that June to October is hurricane season in the Riviera Maya, though tourist services are well-prepared for it.

What to do when you arrive

Cancun International Airport (CUN) is 25 km from the city centre. From there you have easy access to luxury beaches, theme parks like Xcaret and Xel-Há, Mayan ruins in Tulum and cenotes. The Riviera Maya is perfect for diving, snorkelling and relaxation. Hire a car or use local transport to explore the area.
