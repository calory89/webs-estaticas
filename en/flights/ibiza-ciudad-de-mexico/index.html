Best time to visit Mexico City

November to April is ideal: dry weather, pleasant temperatures and less rainfall. June to September is the rainy season. If you travel in December or January, expect crowds and higher prices. Spring (March-April) is a good balance between climate and fares.

What to do when you arrive in Mexico City

Benito Juárez international airport is 20 km from the city centre. Explore the Historic Centre, visit Xochimilco, Frida Kahlo Museum, or the nearby volcanoes Popocatépetl and Iztaccíhuatl. Local food is essential: try tacos, tamales and traditional markets. Plan at least 4-5 days to make the most of the city and surroundings.
