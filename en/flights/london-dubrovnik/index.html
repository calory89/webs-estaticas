Best time to visit

April to October is peak season in Dubrovnik. April–May and September–October offer good weather without peak summer crowds. November marks low season with fewer flights and less predictable pricing, so check availability carefully. Avoid December–January if you want decent flight options.

What to do when you arrive

Dubrovnik is unmissable: walk the city walls, explore the Old Town, and sit at a terrace on Stradun. Banje Beach is 10 minutes on foot. With time to spare, ferries to the Elafiti or Lokrum islands leave from the harbour. The airport is 20 km out; bus or taxi gets you to town in 20–30 minutes.
