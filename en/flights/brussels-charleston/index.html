Fly from Brussels to Charleston from €823 return with Aer Lingus. The median price sits around €851, so there's room to find good fares if you're flexible. Plan ahead: flights with 3 stops require patience, but the savings are worth it.

Price and when to book

Prices start from €823 for a return trip. Our data shows a median of €851, with samples reaching €856. Book as early as possible: this route has strong demand. Check regularly for price drops, but don't leave it to the last minute. Flights with multiple connections are cheaper precisely because they take longer to complete.

Airline and stops

You'll travel with Aer Lingus, which is your option on this route. Keep in mind there are 3 stops on the outbound flight, so it's not direct. This adds several hours to your journey, but the price makes up for it if saving money is your priority. Check connection times when booking to ensure they suit you.

Best time to go

Brussels and Charleston have very different climates. Charleston in winter (January) is mild and pleasant, ideal if you're escaping Belgian cold. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) offer perfect temperatures. Skip summer if you can't handle the heat and humidity of the American South. Any time of year works for booking—just search far ahead to secure availability.

What to do when you arrive in Charleston

Charleston is a colonial jewel with historic architecture, riverfront walks and upscale dining. Explore the old town centre, visit Fort Sumter, browse the public market. Coming from Brussels, the pace shift is real—prepare yourself for Southern hospitality. The airport (CHS) is about 20 km from downtown, with easy taxi or rental car links.

FAQ

The lowest recorded price is €823 for a complete return journey. The median sits at €851, so if you spot fares above €860, it might be worth waiting a bit or trying different dates.

Is the flight Brussels to Charleston direct?

No, it has 3 stops. It's a longer journey, but the cheap prices make up for the extra travel time. A direct flight would cost more.

Which airline flies this route?

Aer Lingus operates these flights. Book directly with them or use flight comparison sites to shop around for the best price.

What can I do in Charleston?

Charleston has intact colonial charm: cobbled alleyways, lowcountry cuisine restaurants, river cruises, antique shops and nearby beaches. It's very walkable and packed with American civil history.
