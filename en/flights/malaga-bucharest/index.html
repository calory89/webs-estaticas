Best time to visit

Bucharest is attractive year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant temperatures and fewer crowds. Winters are cold but perfect if you want to experience the city with less tourism. Avoid peak-demand dates to keep prices low.

What to do when you arrive

Otopeni airport (Aurel Vlaicu) is about 16 km from the city centre. Bucharest is known for the Salvation Church, the Palace of Parliament and the Old Town. Eat in Lipscani, walk along the Danube and discover communist-era architecture. The city is affordable: accommodation, food and activities are inexpensive.
