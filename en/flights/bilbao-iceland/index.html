Best time to visit

Iceland appeals year-round: Northern Lights between October and March, waterfalls and nature in summer. We cannot predict which months are cheapest without historical data, but booking early is key. January, as in the example, can work well if you reserve in advance. Avoid Christmas and New Year, peak price season.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Keflavík (KEF), 50 km from Reykjavík. The capital is small and welcoming. From there you reach the famous Golden Circle trip (Gullfoss, Geysir, Þingvellir waterfalls), or explore the south coast. Hire a car in advance. The Ring Road (circular route) is essential for touring the island over several days.

FAQ

What is the cheapest price from Bilbao to Iceland?

From €160 return with easyJet, though the median is €192. Spot offers crop up, so check multiple dates and book ahead.

Is the Bilbao to Iceland flight direct?

No, it has 1 stopover. easyJet operates with a stopover on this route, which explains the budget-friendly price compared to non-stop flights.

Which airline flies from Bilbao to Reykjavík?

easyJet runs this connection. Verify schedules and stopover points in your search results before booking.

What to see in Iceland in one week?

The Golden Circle (Gullfoss, Geysir, Þingvellir), south coast waterfalls (Seljalandsfoss, Skógafoss), glaciers, canyons and black sand beaches. With a rental car you can cover 500–800 km across several days. Book accommodation centrally in Reykjavík or spread across the island depending on your itinerary.
