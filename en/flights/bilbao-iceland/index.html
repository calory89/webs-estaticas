Best time to visit Iceland

Iceland is stunning year-round. Winter (Nov–Feb) offers northern lights but it's cold and dark. Summer (Jun–Aug) has almost 24-hour daylight and better weather, though prices rise. Spring and autumn are sweet spots: fewer crowds and fair prices. February, like in the example, is winter: prepare for cold and possible auroras.

What to do in Keflavík and around

Keflavík is your gateway to Iceland. Reykjavík, the capital, is 50 km away. Don't miss the Blue Lagoon (15 km from the airport), the Golden Circle (Þingvellir, Geysir, Gullfoss), Skaftafell cliffs, black sand beaches and, in winter, northern lights hunting. Rent a car if you want to explore freely.
