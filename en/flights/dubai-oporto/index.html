Best time to visit

Porto shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) with mild weather and less rain. Winter is wetter but quieter. Skip peak summer if you want lower prices and fewer crowds. Every season has charm in this riverside city.

What to do when you arrive

Porto deserves 3–4 days minimum. Wander the Ribeira, the historic quarter by the Douro River. Cross the Dom Luís II bridge, explore the cellars of Porto in Vila Nova de Gaia and taste port wine. Climb the Clérigos Tower, visit Livraria Lello bookshop, and eat francesinha (a local sandwich). Matosinhos beach is nearby if you fancy sea air.
