Best time to visit

Miami is a year-round destination. Northern winter (December–February) is peak season: perfect weather and strong European tourism. Summer is cheaper but hotter and more humid. Hurricane season runs June–November in the Atlantic: factor this into your plans.

What to do when you arrive

Miami delivers beaches in Miami Beach, art and museums in Wynwood, nightlife in South Beach, and shopping at The Shops at Midtown Miami. Coral Gables (30 minutes by car) has historic architecture. The Florida Keys lie 4 hours south for a day trip. Miami International Airport (MIA) is 10 km from downtown, served by bus, taxi, and car hire.
