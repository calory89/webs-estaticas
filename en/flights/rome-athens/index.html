Best time to visit

Athens and Rome enjoy enviable Mediterranean weather almost year-round. Autumn (September to November) and spring (March to May) offer pleasant temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. November, when the flight example occurs, is an excellent choice: fewer tourists and still fine weather.

What to do in Athens

The Acropolis and the Parthenon are must-sees, but Athens is so much more: stroll through Plaka, dine on neighbourhood terraces, explore the Acropolis Museum, climb Lycabettus Hill at sunset, or relax on nearby Glyfada beaches. A week's trip lets you combine the capital with close-by islands.
