Best time to visit

Warsaw is worth visiting year-round. Autumn (September to November) and spring (April to May) offer pleasant temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. Winter brings festive charm if you travel in December, though it's cold. Book ahead in any season to lock in the best price.

What to do in Warsaw

Poland's capital is a must-see: the Old Town (UNESCO World Heritage), the Palace of Culture and Science with 360° views, the Ghetto and its museum, and the Vistula River for walks. Eat local dishes at Old Town Square restaurants, visit Lazienki Park with its Palace on the Water, and explore contemporary art galleries. Three to four days gives you a solid visit.
