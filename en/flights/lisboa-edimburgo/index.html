Price and when to book

Prices range from €72 to €116 depending on dates. Booking 2-3 weeks ahead usually yields good results on this route. The sooner you lock in your ticket, the better: Ryanair keeps availability open but prices climb as departure approaches. November is a reasonable time in terms of fares.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair is your option at these prices. The flight is direct from Lisbon (LIS) to Edinburgh (EDI), no connections, so you save time. The airline is a solid choice if you travel with just a small bag, but bear in mind that checked luggage carries an extra charge.

Best time to visit

Scotland in November offers shorter days but fewer crowds. Temperatures hover around 6–8°C, so pack layers and a warm jacket. Spring (April–May) and early summer (June) bring milder weather but higher fares. Winter (December–February) is cheaper but colder and wetter.

What to do in Edinburgh

Edinburgh deserves 3–4 days minimum. Must-see: the Castle, the Royal Mile with its historic closes, Holyrood Palace, and the Leith waterfront. Viewpoints at Calton Hill and Arthur's Seat offer stunning vistas. Spend an afternoon in free museums and an evening in local pubs. The airport is 13 km from the city centre with good bus links.
