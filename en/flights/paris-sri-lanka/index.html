Airline and stopovers

Air India is your carrier on this route. You'll find flights with 1 stopover, meaning one intermediate stop before Colombo. Layovers typically range from 2–4 hours depending on the service. Direct flights aren't available at these prices, so factor in a connection.

Best time to travel

Sri Lanka has a tropical climate. The dry season in the south and west runs December to March: hot but rain-free, ideal for beaches and hiking. April and November are cheaper alternatives, though showers are possible. Avoid October if you can—it's the wettest month across most regions.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB), roughly 30 km north of Colombo. Endless options await: Mirissa and Unawatuna beaches on the south coast, the Cultural Triangle with ancient temples, tea plantations in Nuwara Eliya, or Sigiriya's famous rock fortress. Colombo itself is chaotic but has solid museums and Buddhist temples. Rent a car or hop on local buses: it's cheap and safe.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Paris to Colombo?

Not at these prices. You'll find flights with 1 stopover via Air India. It's standard for this route: major carriers don't offer direct connections at this fare level.

Which airport do I land at in Sri Lanka?

Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB), about 30 km north of Colombo. It's the country's main hub. Buses, taxis and car services run from there to anywhere on the island.

How far ahead should I book to get these prices?

Aim for at least 1–2 months' notice. Long-haul routes like this get expensive fast. If you spot a low fare, grab it: tickets to Sri Lanka sell out quickly.
