Best time to visit

Milan is worth visiting year-round, but October and April-May offer mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. December brings Christmas lights and luxury shops beautifully decorated, perfect for shoppers. Skip August if you prefer fewer tourists. Prices don't swing much by season on this short route, so choose based on what appeals to you.

What to do when you arrive in Milan

The Cathedral (Duomo) is a must: go inside for the stained glass or admire the facade. Teatro alla Scala for opera lovers. Walk through Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, a historic shopping arcade. Sforza Castle and Renaissance art in the museums. Try risotto alla milanese and panettone. Milan is compact: walk or use the metro and trams to get around fast.
