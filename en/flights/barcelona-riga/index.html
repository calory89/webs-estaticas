Best time to visit Riga

Riga shines in summer (June to August) with long days and mild weather, though it draws more tourists. Spring (May–June) and autumn (September–October) are excellent: fewer crowds, pleasant climate and less price pressure. Winter brings intense cold and short daylight, but if you want snowy Riga and Christmas market charm (December especially), that's your season.

What to do in Riga

Latvia's capital blends medieval architecture in the Old Town (UNESCO heritage site) with Art Nouveau buildings you won't see elsewhere. Walk along the Daugava riverbank, try local Baltic cuisine in neighbourhood bars, and if you visit at Christmas, the Town Hall Square markets are unmissable. Just 40 km away is Sigulda, home to fairy-tale castles set in pine forests.
