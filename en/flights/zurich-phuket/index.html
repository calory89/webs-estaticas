Best time to travel

Thailand is a winter escape for Europeans: the weather is dry and pleasant from November to February. October and March are also good, though rainier. For tighter budgets, travel in low season (May to September), though there's more rain. December and January are pricier because many Europeans flee the cold.

What to do when you arrive in Phuket

Phuket is your gateway to Thailand's beaches. From there you easily reach Patong Beach, the Phi Phi Islands by boat, or Krabi in about an hour. The town has nightlife, street markets and temples. The international airport is roughly 30 km from the centre; take a taxi, bus or private transfer. Eat well and cheap, explore the nearby islands and enjoy the sea.

Is Etihad Airways reliable?

Yes, Etihad Airways is an established airline with a strong reputation. It flies regularly on long-haul international routes. Check the baggage allowance included in your fare before buying, as you may need to pay extra for checked luggage.

How many stopovers? Is the journey very long?

You have 1 stopover, so it's not direct. Total journey time usually runs 15–18 hours of flight time plus stopover time. It's not the quickest, but the price makes up for it. Pack essentials in your carry-on to stay comfortable.

What else can I see in Phuket besides beaches?

Buddhist temples like the Big Buddha, night markets with cheap local food, old Phuket Town with colonial buildings, and nature safaris. The Phi Phi Islands are an hour away by boat. Tours to everywhere else leave from Phuket.
