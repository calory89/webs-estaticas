Best time to visit

Belgrade is a lively city almost year-round. For pleasant weather and smaller crowds, April, May, September and October are ideal. November through February are the coldest months, but Belgrade isn't a beach destination, so it's still worth a trip then. Avoid July and August if you can't handle intense heat and peak summer crowds in the city.

What to do when you arrive

Belgrade is history, art and nightlife rolled into one. Kalemegdan Fortress dominates the Danube, the Temple of Saint Sava stuns with its scale, and the Savamala district buzzes with galleries, bars and indie culture. Walk around Ada Ciganlija island for nature close by, sample street food in Skadarlija and don't miss the Museum of Yugoslav History. The city is affordable, locals are welcoming and there's something for everyone.
