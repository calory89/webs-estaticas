Best time to visit Bologna

Bologna shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), when the weather is mild without summer extremes. Avoid August if you want fewer crowds in the old town. Flight prices don't drop by season, so choose based on your weather preference.

What to do on arrival

Bologna airport (Guglielmo Marconi) is 6 km from the centre. Head straight to Piazza Maggiore, the heart of the city surrounded by medieval arcades. Don't miss San Petronio Basilica, the leaning towers (Torre Garisenda and Torre degli Asinelli) and the Pinacoteca museum. Local food is essential: try authentic bolognese, tagliatelle and tortellini.
