Flights start from €119 return; the median is around €140. We searched on 23 October 2026 and found availability. Book early: these low fares tend to rise as your travel date approaches. Aim to book 6–8 weeks ahead to grab the best deals.

Airline and stopovers

easyJet operates this route non-stop. Departure from Zurich (ZRH), direct arrival in Lisbon (LIS). A direct flight means less hassle, no plane changes, no waiting in other airports. Great if you travel light or want to arrive quickly.

Best time to visit

Lisbon has pleasant weather year-round. Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to October) are ideal: mild temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is wetter but flights are often cheaper. Avoid July and August if you dislike peak season tourism.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Lisbon Airport (Portela, just a few kilometres from the city centre). The city boasts historic trams, stunning azulejo tiles, viewpoints over the Tagus River, charming neighbourhoods like Alfama and Belém, and excellent Portuguese cuisine. Public transport is affordable and reliable.
