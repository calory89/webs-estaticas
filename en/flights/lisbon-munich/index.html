Best time to visit

Munich is wonderful year-round, but autumn (September to November) and spring (April to May) offer great weather without summer crowds. Winter months are cold but beautiful if you fancy snow and Christmas markets. Skip July and August if you want lower prices and fewer tourists.

What to do when you arrive

Munich is the gateway to Bavaria. Visit Marienplatz and the Rathaus, stroll through the English Garden (one of Europe's largest), enjoy local food in biergärten, and explore world-class museums. With extra time, the Bavarian Alps and Neuschwanstein Castle are within easy reach by car or train.

What should I see in Munich over a weekend?

Marienplatz, the Cathedral, Nymphenburg Palace and a traditional Bavarian biergarten are must-sees. With more time, Neuschwanstein Castle is just over an hour by train.
