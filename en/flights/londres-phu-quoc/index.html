Best time to visit

Phu Quoc enjoys tropical weather. The dry season from November to April offers the most comfortable beach and diving conditions. Summer (May–October) brings fewer crowds and potentially lower prices, but rain is frequent. December, as in the example, is ideal: sunny, warm, and moderately busy.

What to do when you arrive

Phu Quoc is known for white-sand beaches, snorkelling and diving on coral reefs, and pepper plantations. Visit An Thoi Islands, the marine national park, Duong Dong fishing village with its local market, and nuoc mam (fish sauce) distilleries. Don't miss the sunset from any western beach.
