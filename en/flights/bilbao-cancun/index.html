Airline and stops

Best time to visit

Cancún is a year-round destination, but May to October is hurricane season, though it doesn't mean you can't travel. December to April is peak season: dry weather, pleasant temperatures and busy tourism. January, February and Easter week push prices up. June, July and September have lower flight fares, but expect more rain.

What to do when you arrive

Cancún is the gateway to the Riviera Maya. Beaches and resorts are minutes from the airport. Don't miss cenotes (natural freshwater pools), the Mesoamerican Coral Barrier for diving, Mayan ruins at Tulum and Chichén Itzá, and towns like Playa del Carmen. The airport has good transport links and car rental options.
