Best time to visit Hamburg

Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant temperatures. Winter is cold but charming with festive markets. Summer (June-August) tends to have higher prices and more crowds.

What to do in Hamburg when you arrive

Visit Miniatur Wunderland, the world's largest model railway exhibition. Walk through Speicherstadt, the UNESCO-listed warehouse district. Enjoy the Elbe riverside, try local fish and chips at Fischmarkt, and relax in Planten un Blomen gardens.
