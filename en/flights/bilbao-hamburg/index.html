Best time to visit

Hamburg shines in spring (April–May) and summer (June–August), when the weather is mild and outdoor activities are plentiful. For cheaper tickets, consider shoulder months (March, September, October) over peak summer. Avoid Christmas and public holiday dates if you want lower prices.

What to do when you arrive

Hamburg is northern Germany's gateway. Stroll the port (Hafen), climb St. Michael's Church for 360° views, explore the canals and warehouses of Speicherstadt. The Alster lake in the city centre is perfect for unwinding. Eat fresh herring at market stalls and try local beer. Culture, nature, and food are all within reach from the airport.
