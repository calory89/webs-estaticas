What to do in Bruges

The city is an open-air museum: stroll along its canals, visit the Basilica of the Holy Blood and the Belfort tower. Taste Belgian chocolate, try local beers and explore the main square. You'll land in Brussels (around 90 km away), where a direct train to Bruges takes just 20 minutes.
