Best time to travel

Azores enjoys mild weather year-round. Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to October) offer pleasant temperatures and less rain. Summer months (June to August) are warmer but busier. Avoid winter if you're seeking guaranteed good weather.

What to do on arrival

Azores is ideal for hiking, whale watching and volcanic landscapes. São Miguel island, where Ponta Delgada airport is located, boasts crater lakes, beaches and charming villages. The Ponta da Cabeça Nature Park and volcanic calderas are must-sees. Hire a car to explore the island at your own pace.
