Airline and stopovers

Air Europa operates this route with 2 stopovers. No direct flights from Palma to Buenos Aires at these fares, so expect over 20 hours total travel time. Typical connections are through Europe (Madrid or similar) then another hub before Argentina. Bring a charger, comfy clothes and patience.

Best time to visit

Buenos Aires is pleasant year-round, but November to March (spring-summer) offers good weather and energy. May to August (winter) is cooler but still worth visiting. Avoid Christmas and New Year if you don't want peak prices. Check your dates carefully: fares swing hard with demand.

What to do when you arrive

Buenos Aires has plenty: La Boca's colourful houses, San Telmo for tango and history, Palermo for shopping and food. Don't miss Teatro Colón or proper Argentine beef with Malbec. Outside the city, Mendoza (wine), Córdoba and Iguazú Falls are essential. Ministro Pistarini airport (Ezeiza) is 35 km from centre: taxi, remise or bus are budget options.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Palma to Buenos Aires?

No. Air Europa operates this route with 2 stopovers, so it's a long journey. Rest at connections and stretch your legs.

What's the cheapest price you've found?

€1,096 return. It's the low point in our sample. The median is €1,135, so there's flexibility if you shift dates.

What's included in the Air Europa ticket?

It depends on the fare: some include checked baggage, others don't. Check when booking how many bags you can take and whether seats, meals or in-flight entertainment are charged separately.

What to see in Buenos Aires in a week?

Centre and La Boca (2 days), San Telmo and tango (1 day), Palermo for shops and bars (1–2 days), Teatro Colón, La Recoleta cemetery. If you have spare days, a trip to Mendoza or Iguazú. Try the steak, alfajores and mate.
