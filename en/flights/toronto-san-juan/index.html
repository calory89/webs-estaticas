The lowest price we found is €300 return, with a median of €314. Our sample prices range from €300 to €366. The earlier you book, the better your chances of landing these fares. December is peak season for Caribbean travel, so don't wait until the last minute if you're planning a festive trip.

Airline and stopovers

Air Canada is your carrier on this route. The outbound flight includes 1 stopover, so it's not direct. This is typical for this pairing and helps keep fares down. Always check your booking confirmation for the exact connection airport.

Best time to visit

December is high season in San Juan, good weather but busy and pricier. If you prefer fewer crowds and lower fares, consider May to November (avoid September–October due to hurricane season). Whatever time you choose, book well ahead to lock in your rate.

What to do when you arrive

San Juan is your gateway to Puerto Rico. Explore Old San Juan's colourful colonial streets, hike El Yunque tropical forest, enjoy beaches at Isla Verde or Luquillo, and sample local food. The airport is Luis Muñoz Marín (SJU), roughly 10 km from the centre.
