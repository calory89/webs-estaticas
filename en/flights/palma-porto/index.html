Best time to visit

Porto is worth visiting year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is wetter but prices may be lower. Plan ahead: booking 2–3 months in advance gives you more price and schedule options.

What to do when you arrive

Porto is the gateway to Portugal. Don't miss Ribeira, the historic quarter by the Douro River; Clérigos Tower with its distinctive spire; the Luis I Bridge with spectacular views; and traditional Porto wine cafés. The airport (OPO) has good public transport links to the centre. Take time to explore the wine lodges or venture into the Douro Valley.

What can I see and do in Porto?
