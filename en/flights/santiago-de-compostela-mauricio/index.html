Best time to visit

Mauritius is a beach and adventure destination year-round, but the driest and most pleasant weather runs from May to November (our autumn and winter). These months coincide with European school holidays, which may affect availability and prices. For lower fares and fewer crowds, consider June, July, or September. Avoid cyclone season (November to April).

What to do in Mauritius

Mauritius offers white-sand beaches, coral reefs for diving and snorkelling, and trekking in the interior mountains. Don't miss Piton de la Petite Rivière Noire (Black River Peak), the coloured sands of Chamarel, and Île aux Cerfs nature reserve. Coastal towns like Grand Baie and Flic en Flac are perfect for unwinding. The south and east coasts have wilder, less touristy beaches.
