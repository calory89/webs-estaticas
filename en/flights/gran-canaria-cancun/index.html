Best time to travel

Cancún and its beaches are appealing year-round. Hurricane season runs June to November, though the coast remains safe. January to April offers the best weather and lowest rainfall, but higher prices. Travel in May, September or October for fewer crowds and better value.

What to do on arrival

Cancún is your gateway to the Riviera Maya: beaches, Mayan ruins (Tulum, Chichén Itzá), cenotes and underwater parks. The international airport is 25 km from town. Bus, taxi or car rental available. From there, reach Playa del Carmen, Cozumel and nearby islands easily.

What to see in Cancún and nearby?

The Riviera Maya is the main draw: white-sand beaches, Mayan ruins (Tulum is 1.5 hours away), cenotes for swimming, theme parks like Xcaret. Cozumel is excellent for diving. Transport from the airport reaches all these spots.
