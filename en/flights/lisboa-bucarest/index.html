Best time to travel

Bucharest is worth visiting year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is colder but fares may drop. Check the fare calendar before picking your dates.

What to do in Bucharest

Romania's capital blends architecture from different eras: the massive Palace of Parliament, the Old Town with traditional pubs, Stavropoleos Church and walks along the Dâmbovița River. Try local food and visit Snagov Monastery if you have extra days.
