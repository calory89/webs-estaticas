Best time to visit

Zurich shines in spring (April–May) with mild temperatures and snow-capped Alps in the background. Autumn (September–October) is also excellent. Winters are cold but ideal if you want skiing or snowy landscapes. Avoid August if you dislike crowds.

What to do in Zurich

Explore the Altstadt (old town) and its historic churches, stroll along the lakeside, visit museums like the Kunsthaus. Swiss trains take you easily to the Alps, Lucerne or St Gallen. World-class food: fondue, raclette and Swiss chocolate at every corner.
