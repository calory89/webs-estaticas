Best time to go

Athens is at its best in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), when weather is mild and you avoid the intense summer heat. Winter (November–March) also draws visitors seeking fewer crowds and pleasant temperatures. Any season works; just pick based on your climate and crowd preferences.

What to do when you arrive

The Acropolis and Parthenon are essential; set aside a full day. The Greek Agora, the Acropolis Museum, and the winding streets of Plaka deserve hours of wandering. Eat in a traditional taverna, climb Lycabettus Hill at sunset, and relax on nearby beaches like Glyfada or Vouliagmeni. The airport is 30 km east; buses, taxis and metro get you to the centre in 30–50 minutes.

What else is there to see in Athens besides the Acropolis?

The Greek Agora, the Acropolis Museum, Plaka district, Lycabettus Hill, Glyfada beach, and Syntagma Square. Athens is packed with history on every corner and vibrant street life. Three to four days covers the essentials well.
