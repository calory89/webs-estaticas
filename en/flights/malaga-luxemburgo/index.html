What to do when you arrive

Luxembourg is small but charming. The capital, also named Luxembourg, boasts a spectacular UNESCO-listed Old Town with medieval ramparts and palaces. Walk the city centre, visit the Grand Ducal Palace and stroll the Pétrusse Gardens. The airport is 6 km from the centre; bus or train takes 30 minutes.
