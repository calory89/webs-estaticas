Best time to visit

Sardinia shines in spring (March to May) and autumn (September to October): mild temperatures and beaches without crowds. Summer (June to August) is hot and pricier; winter is mild but rainy. November (your example is 13-11) is late autumn, a great choice if you prefer peace and quiet.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Cagliari (Elmas airport). Explore the old town, the Basilica of San Saturnino and the harbour. Rent a car to visit beaches like Villasimius or Costa Verde. Don't miss the Roman ruins of Nora or the south-west cliffs. Sardinia blends beaches, history and local food: malloreddus, culurgiones and local Vermentino wine.
