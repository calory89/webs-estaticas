Best time to travel

Singapore has tropical weather year-round; Iceland is very different. Travelling in autumn (October–November) as in the example offers more stable skies and a chance to see the Northern Lights. Winter (December–February) is cold and very dark but incredible for aurora viewing. Summer (June–August) has bright nights but fewer aurora odds. Book well in advance: long-haul international flights need planning.

What to do in Reykjavik and beyond

Reykjavik is the capital and entry point. Keflavík airport (KEF) is roughly 50 km from the city. From there explore the Golden Circle route (geysers, waterfalls, tectonic sites), southern glaciers, geothermal pools like the Blue Lagoon, or the city itself. Iceland is perfect for a rental car tour. Check transport and accommodation deals after you secure your flight.
