Airlines and stops

Best time to visit

Buenos Aires is worth visiting year-round, but remember December is summer there. Spring (September–November) and autumn (March–May) are typically ideal for Argentina's weather and crowds. Avoid July–August if you prefer warmer temperatures, though winter is still perfectly enjoyable.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Ezeiza Airport (BUE), 35 km from the city centre. Buenos Aires offers tango in La Boca, colonial neighbourhoods like San Telmo, shopping on Calle Florida and world-class dining. Nearby attractions include traditional estancias, vineyards in Mendoza and Patagonia. With 9 days in this example trip, you can explore the city and take a short excursion.
