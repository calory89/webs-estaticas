Best time to visit

Buenos Aires is worth visiting year-round, but the Southern summer (December to February) is peak season with higher prices and more tourists. For savings, consider March to May (autumn) or September to November (spring), when the weather is pleasant and there's less competition. Southern winter (June to August) is cheaper but colder.

What to do when you arrive in Buenos Aires

You'll land at Ministro Pistarini International Airport (also known as Ezeiza), about 35 km from the city centre. The city offers live tango, belle époque architecture, sprawling parks and world-class gastronomy. Explore La Boca, San Telmo and Recoleta neighbourhood. Museums, the Colón Theatre and bookshops are must-sees. Book accommodation in advance if travelling during peak season.
