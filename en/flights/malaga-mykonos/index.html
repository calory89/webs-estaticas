Best time to visit

Mykonos is a high-season destination in summer, especially July and August, when prices peak. May, June, September and October offer good weather with fewer crowds and better value. Winter (November to March) is cheaper but weather is less predictable. This late-December offer could be attractive if you fancy New Year's Eve on the island.

What to do when you arrive

Mykonos is famous for its beaches, nightlife and the classic whitewashed villages of the Cyclades. Wander the old town's narrow streets, visit the Church of Paraportiani, enjoy beaches like Paradise or Psarou, and sample local Greek cuisine. For quieter vibes, explore nearby islands like Delos (ancient ruins) or Rheneia. Hire a car or scooter to explore the island freely.
