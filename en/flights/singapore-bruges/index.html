What to do when you arrive in Bruges

Bruges is a UNESCO World Heritage site: walk along the canals, explore the Market Square and climb the Belfry Tower for stunning views. Taste Belgian Trappist beers, eat mussels and buy artisan chocolate. Antwerp is 30 km away and easy to reach by train. Ghent, another medieval gem, is even closer. The city is perfect for 2–3 days, whether you move fast or take it easy.
