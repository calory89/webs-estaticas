Best time to visit

Dubrovnik works year-round, but peak season is June to September: guaranteed sun, beaches, and outdoor activities—though expect crowds and higher prices. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer good weather with fewer tourists. Winter is cooler and wetter, but prices drop significantly.

What to do when you arrive

Dubrovnik is famous for its medieval Old Town, a UNESCO World Heritage site surrounded by walls. Walk the city walls, visit the Cathedral and Church of St. Blaise, or relax on nearby beaches like Banje. If you have time, day trips to the Elafiti Islands or Cavtat are worthwhile. Dubrovnik Airport is about 20 km from the city centre; bus, taxi, or car rental are your options.
