Best time to travel

Kuala Lumpur is hot and humid year-round. The best months are November to March when rainfall is lower and conditions are more bearable. December and January see more tourists, pushing prices up. If you want to save and can handle heat and humidity, July and August are also viable. Check offers several months ahead.

What to do when you arrive

Kuala Lumpur has the iconic Petronas Twin Towers—a must-visit. Chinatown is perfect for cheap food and market exploration. Don't miss the Royal Palace, Batu Caves and the Botanical Gardens. Public transport is affordable and reliable. Short on time? Take a train to the Selangor Islands for tropical beaches just 45 minutes away.
