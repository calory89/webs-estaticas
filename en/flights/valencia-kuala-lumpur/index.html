Best time to visit

Kuala Lumpur has a tropical climate year-round. The best period is November to February, when rainfall is lower and temperatures slightly milder. Avoid the southwest monsoon (May to September). Book flights well in advance if travelling during European holidays or school breaks.

What to do on arrival

Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) is well-connected to the city centre. Visit the Petronas Twin Towers, shop at Pavilion KL, explore Chinatown's food scene, or climb the KL Tower. Nearby attractions include the Batu Caves, nearby beaches and national parks within a few hours' drive.
