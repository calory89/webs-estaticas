Best time to travel

Buenos Aires is worth visiting year-round, but remember seasons are opposite to Europe's. Local spring and autumn (September–November and March–May) offer mild weather and fewer crowds than the Buenos Aires summer (December–February). Prices don't shift by month alone; school holidays, local festivals and events also play a role. Check your destination's calendar to dodge peak tourism periods if you want a quieter trip.

What to do when you arrive in Buenos Aires

You'll land at Ministro Pistarini Airport (Ezeiza), 35 km from the city. Buses, taxis and private transfers connect it to town. Once in Buenos Aires, walk through La Boca and San Telmo for tango and heritage architecture; stroll the Old Quarter and visit the Cathedral; enjoy art at MALBA and local life in traditional cafés. Don't miss the asados (grills) or Malbec wines from Mendoza if you take a day trip. Three days cover the essentials; a week lets you explore nearby provinces.

What to see in Buenos Aires besides tango?
