Best time to visit

Santorini shines nearly year-round, but skip August if you want fewer crowds and better prices. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: perfect weather and manageable tourism. Winter (November–February) has fewer visitors, though some services close.

What to do when you arrive

Santorini airport is in Kamari, about 10 km from Fira. Get there by bus or taxi. Don't miss Oia at sunset, volcanic sand beaches like Perissa, whitewashed villages such as Kamari and Imerovigli, and clifftop views. Divers and snorkellers will love the underwater sites.
