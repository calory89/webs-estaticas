Best time to visit Jacksonville

Jacksonville has a subtropical climate: mild winters (15–20°C) and humid summers with tropical rain. November to April is the most pleasant season, with temperatures between 12–22°C and lower humidity. If you travel in these months, you'll avoid intense summer heat and storm risk. November is perfect: good weather and not yet peak season.

What to do in Jacksonville

Jacksonville is the largest city by area in the continental U.S. You can explore the historic downtown (Riverside), visit museums like the Cummer Museum of Art and Gardens, enjoy southern beaches (Jacksonville Beach), or discover nature along nearby rivers and state parks. The San Marco district offers shops, restaurants and lovely colonial architecture. Don't miss a sunset walk along the St. Johns River.
