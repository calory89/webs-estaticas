Airline and connections

Best time to visit

Mexico City is a year-round destination. November to April brings mild, dry weather. Avoid the rainy season (May to October) if you prefer clear skies. Low season in January and February may offer more flight availability, though it doesn't guarantee cheaper prices.

What to do when you arrive

Mexico City International Airport (MEX) is 13 km from the centre. Head to the heart of the capital: Historic Centre, Teotihuacán, Frida Kahlo sites. Plan visits to Coyoacán and Xochimilco. The city offers museums, gastronomy and nightlife. Arriving after 3 connections means you'll need rest: use your first day to acclimatise.
