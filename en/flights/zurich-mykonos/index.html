Best time to go

December is excellent for Mykonos: well outside the summer crush, quiet beaches and genuine local life. Weather is mild (15–16°C average), perfect for walking and exploring without stress. Accommodation prices drop compared to summer, though some venues close early or reduce hours.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Mykonos airport (JMK). From there, take a bus or taxi to town. Don't miss the old harbour at sunset, traditional churches, nightlife (quieter in December), and southern beaches if weather permits. Coastal villages like Ano Mera are worth a day trip.
