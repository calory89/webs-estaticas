Price and when to book

The cheapest flights start from €226 return. The median price sits at €265, with sample prices up to €284. Prices vary significantly by date: the more flexible you are, the better. Book 2–3 months ahead to secure price and availability.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with one stopover on the outbound flight. The sample departure from Brussels is 7 January at 14:40, arriving in Azores the same day. The return flight (14 January, 09:35) also includes a stop. Always check total journey time and the connection airport when booking.

Best time to visit

The Azores are worth visiting year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild weather and less rain. Summer is peak season; winter is wetter but less crowded and often cheaper. Check the forecast before you book.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Ponta Delgada Airport (PDL), your gateway to São Miguel island. Walk the city, visit Santiago lake, try local food at harbourside restaurants, or hike to volcanic calderas. The archipelago is ideal for hiking, whale watching and natural pools.
