Price and when to book

Flights start from €125 (return). Our sample shows prices of €125, €132 and €149. As a general rule, booking 1-2 months ahead usually delivers better fares. Be flexible with dates: switching to a different day often means the difference between €125 and €150.

Airline and flight type

Ryanair operates this route with no stopovers: you depart Paris and arrive in Vilna direct. It's a short flight, ideal for saving time. Bear in mind that Ryanair charges for large carry-on bags and checked luggage, so factor that in if you need more than the basics.

Best time to visit

Vilna is beautiful in summer (June–September) and also in winter if you fancy cold and snow. Prices tend to be higher in peak season. For savings, travel in spring (April–May) or autumn (September–October): pleasant weather and lower demand.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Vilna International Airport (VNO), about 4 km from the city centre. The Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage Site: wander the medieval lanes, visit the Cathedral and Gediminas Castle. The Neris River and green parks are perfect for unwinding. Lithuania is cheap: eat well, drink local beer and enjoy without breaking the bank.
