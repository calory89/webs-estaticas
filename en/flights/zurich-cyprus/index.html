Best time to travel

Cyprus shines from April to May and September to October: perfect weather, no extreme heat. April and May offer ideal beaches and spring landscapes. For lower prices, skip July and August when rates climb. December is also an option: mild winter on the island.

What to do when you arrive in Larnaca

Larnaca is your gateway to Cyprus. Stroll the seafront promenade, visit the Church of Saint Lazarus (UNESCO site) or enjoy nearby beaches. From here it's easy to reach Paphos to the west or Nicosia inland. The airport is 5 km from the centre, well served by bus and taxi.
