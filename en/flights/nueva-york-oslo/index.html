Best time to visit

Oslo shines in summer (June–August) with long days and pleasant temperatures, ideal for exploring the city and beyond. What to do in Oslo

Oslo is a modern city with plenty of character: visit the City Hall, the Munch Museum (home to The Scream), and stroll around the harbour. Norway's fjords are within easy reach if you want to extend your trip. The Viking Ship Museum and Oslo Opera House are must-sees. Public transport is convenient and well-integrated.
