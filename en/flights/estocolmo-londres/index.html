Price and when to book

Prices range from €47 to €56 depending on dates. We've seen deals from €47 when searched on 23 October 2026. The earlier you book, the better: Ryanair cuts fares for flights more than a month ahead. If you travel in low-demand periods (January, February, September), you'll find better deals.

Best time to travel

January to March and September to October typically have lower fares and fewer crowds in London. Avoid July and August if you're after bargains. Late October to early November, like in the example, is a sweet spot between price and pleasant autumn weather.

What to do when you arrive

From the airport you reach central London in 30–45 minutes by train or bus. Walk through the City, see Big Ben and Parliament, cross Tower Bridge, or spend a day on free museums (British Museum, National Gallery). With 4–5 days you can fit in Greenwich and Camden Market.
