Best time to visit

The Philippines has a tropical climate. Dry season runs from November to April, ideal for avoiding monsoons. Travelling in low season (May to October) may offer tighter prices, though there's more humidity. Choose based on your heat tolerance and budget.

What to do when you arrive in Manila

Manila is chaotic but fascinating: explore Intramuros (walled colonial city), visit Rizal Park and enjoy local food in the markets. From Ninoy Aquino airport you can take a bus or taxi to the city centre. The city is your gateway to the Philippines; from here it's easy to travel to other islands like Palawan or Boracay.
