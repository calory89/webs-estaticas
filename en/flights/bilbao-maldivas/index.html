Best time to visit

Maldives has two seasons: dry (November to April) and wet (May to October). December to March is peak tourist season with higher flight prices. For better value, consider November, April or May. Travel before peak dates to lock in cheaper airfares.

What to do when you arrive

Velana International Airport (MLE) is in Malé and connects to resorts by speedboat or seaplane. Maldives is famous for white sand beaches, coral reefs and diving. Most visitors spend time in Malé then head to a resort in the atolls. Don't expect traditional tourist attractions: the appeal is marine nature and relaxation.

FAQ

Does Pegasus fly direct from Bilbao to Maldives?

No, the outbound flight has 3 stops. It's not a direct route, so be ready for multiple plane changes. Check the full itinerary with stops before you buy.

What is the best time to visit Maldives?

The dry season (November to April) offers the best weather and diving, but is pricier. November and April give good conditions with fewer price spikes. The wet season (May to October) is cheaper but wetter.

What is there to see in Maldives?

Diving and snorkelling on coral reefs, pristine white sand beaches, and luxury resorts in the atolls. Malé, the capital, has a few museums and mosques. Most activities centre on water: it's a marine nature destination, not an urban tourist spot.
