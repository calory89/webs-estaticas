Best time to travel

What to do in Kuala Lumpur

Malaysia's capital features the Petronas Towers (with 360° views), the Royal Palace, Botanical Gardens, and the lively Jalan Alor night market. The Batu Caves temple complex is nearby, and the Perhentian Islands offer stunning beaches. Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) is well connected to the city centre.
