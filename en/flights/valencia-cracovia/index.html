Airline and stopovers

Best time to visit

Krakow shines in spring and autumn when prices drop and weather is mild. Winter is magical but freezing; summer is crowded. Any season has charm, but October and November offer better value than December or July.

What to do on arrival

Krakow airport (Balice) is 15 km from the centre. The old town is UNESCO heritage: visit the Main Market Square, climb Wawel Castle, explore the Wieliczka salt mines. Auschwitz is 1 hour by train. Eat pierniki and drink Polish beer guilt-free.
