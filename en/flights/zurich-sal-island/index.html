What to do when you arrive

Sal Island is famous for white-sand beaches and crystal-clear water. You can try windsurfing and kitesurfing in Santa Maria, visit Espargos town, explore Pedra Lume Natural Park, or relax in Palmeira. Amílcar Cabral International Airport is the main entry point.
