Best time to visit

Prague is beautiful year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: pleasant weather and less crowding than summer. Winter brings snow and freezing temperatures, but if you fancy that festive vibe, Prague's Christmas markets in December are legendary. Skip July–August if you prefer fewer tourists.

What to do when you arrive

Prague deserves at least 3–4 days. The Old Town, the Castle, Charles Bridge and Old Town Square are must-sees. Museums, Czech breweries and walks along the Vltava river round out a full visit. Václav Havel Airport is about 20 km from the centre and well connected by bus and metro.
