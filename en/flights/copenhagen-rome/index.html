Best time to visit

Rome works year-round. For cheaper prices and fewer crowds, January and February are worth considering. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather without summer heat. Avoid July and August if you dislike crowds and extreme temperatures.

What to do when you arrive

Rome has it all: the Colosseum, Vatican, Trevi Fountain, Roman Forum. Fiumicino airport connects well to the centre by train. You won't need a car, the city is walkable, well-served by public transport, and taxis are available. Plan at least 3 days to see the essentials without rushing.
