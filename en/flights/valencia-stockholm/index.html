Best time to visit Stockholm

Stockholm shines in winter (December–February) with Christmas markets and northern lights viewing in nearby areas. Spring (April–May) brings longer days and mild temperatures. Summer is short but intense, with bright nights. Autumn (September–October) is perfect if you prefer fewer tourists and warm-coloured landscapes. Any season works; choose based on your climate and activity preferences.

What to do when you arrive in Stockholm

Walk around Gamla Stan, the medieval old town. Visit the City Hall and climb its tower for views. The Vasa Museum houses a unique 17th-century ship. Enjoy Djurgården, a green island with museums and attractions. The nearby archipelagos are perfect for boat excursions. Sample Swedish food at traditional and modern markets throughout the city.
