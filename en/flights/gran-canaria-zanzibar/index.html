Best time to visit

Zanzibar has a tropical climate. June to October is the dry and cool season—ideal for visiting. November and December mark the start of short rains. January to March are more humid. Avoid April and May, months of heavy rainfall. Book two to three months ahead for the best fares.

What to do when you arrive in Zanzibar

Zanzibar Airport (ZNZ) is 5 km from Stone Town, the historic centre. Explore its coral alleyways, spice markets and sultan palaces. Northern beaches like Nungwi and Kendwa are perfect for diving and snorkelling. Try kite surfing in Paje. Don't miss Pemba and Unguja islands or the red cliffs on the east coast.
