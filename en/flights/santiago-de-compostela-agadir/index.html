Best time to visit

Agadir has mild weather year-round. October to March offers pleasant temperatures (20-25°C) and less heat than summer. August and July are very hot. February-March and October-November are good months with moderate pricing outside school holidays.

What to see and do in Agadir

Agadir is a beach destination: strolls along the Promenade, fine sandy beaches and paragliding. Visit Kasbah Agadir for panoramic views. Short trips away: Essaouira (coastal towns), Atlas (hiking) and traditional Moroccan markets. Perfect for combining beach, nature and culture.
