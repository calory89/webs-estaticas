Best time to travel

San Francisco enjoys pleasant weather year-round, but autumn (September to November) and spring (April to May) are ideal: mild temperatures and less peak-season crowding. Avoid peak July and August dates if you want better prices. November (the example month) is a good choice before the festive season.

What to do when you arrive

San Francisco offers the Golden Gate Bridge, Alcatraz, the Castro neighbourhood, and beaches like Ocean Beach. The international airport (SFO) is 25 km from the city centre; bus, train, and taxi are all viable. Spend at least 3–4 days to enjoy the city properly.
