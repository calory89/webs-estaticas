Fly from Dublin to Malta for just €50 return with Ryanair on direct flights. The median price is €67, so you have good options if you book in advance. Malta awaits at a price you didn't expect.

Price and when to book

Prices range from €50 to €103 return. For short routes like this, Ryanair usually keeps deals running year-round if you book 4-6 weeks ahead. The sooner you buy, the better rate you lock in. Weekends and public holidays tend to cost more, so flexible dates save money.

Airline and flight type

You travel with Ryanair, operating this route with direct flights (no stops). The journey takes just over 2 hours, perfect for a quick getaway. Ryanair is budget-friendly: free hand baggage, but checked luggage costs extra. Pack smart.

Best time to visit

Malta has Mediterranean weather year-round. April-May and September-October are ideal: pleasant temperatures, less rain than winter, and cheaper than summer (June-August). Travelling in shoulder season also means lower hotel rates and less crowded beaches.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Valletta Airport (MLA), 5 km from the capital. Malta is small but intense: red-sand beaches at Ramla, turquoise coves in Comino, Valletta (UNESCO heritage), prehistoric temples and underwater caves. Rent a car or use cheap public transport (buses). Three to four days covers the essentials.

Valletta (historic capital), Ramla and Mellieha beaches, Comino island, prehistoric temples and coves. Three to four days covers the main sights. Malta is small and easy to explore.
