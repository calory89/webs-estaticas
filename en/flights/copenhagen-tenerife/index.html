Fly Copenhagen to Tenerife for under 130 € on Ryanair. Median price is 140 € return. Pick your dates wisely and save on your Canaries getaway.

Price and when to book

The lowest price is 129 € (return), though most searches hover around 140 €. We've also seen fares at 198 €. Book early: Ryanair adjusts prices based on availability. Reserve 2–3 months in advance for the best deals.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with 1 stop. It's not direct, so expect added journey time. Ryanair is budget-friendly but charges for luggage, seat selection and extras: check what's included in your fare. A stopover is standard on low-cost routes from northern Europe southwards.

Best time to visit

Tenerife has subtropical weather year-round, so any time works. Winter (December–February) beats Nordic cold. July and August are peak season and pricier. October, November, March and April strike a good balance: pleasant weather and steadier prices.

What to do in Tenerife

Tenerife is more than beaches: hike Mount Teide (Spain's highest peak), explore Los Gigantes cliffs or relax on Playa de las Américas. Landing at Tenerife South (TCI) puts the tourist south coast within reach. Visit coastal villages and local markets too.
