Best time to visit

Bulgaria shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather, fewer crowds, and stunning scenery. Winter is cold but worthwhile if you want to see snow in Sofia or ski in nearby mountains. July and August are hot. Book well ahead if travelling during European holidays or long weekends.

What to do when you arrive

Sofia deserves 2–3 days: Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, the Church of Boyana (UNESCO-listed) and the Basilica of Sveta Sofia are must-sees. Museums are cheap, food is excellent value, and the airport is 10 km from the city centre with a direct bus. From there, explore Bansko (mountains), Rila Monastery or venture into the Balkans.
