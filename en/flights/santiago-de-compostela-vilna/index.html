Best time to visit

Vilna is pleasant year-round. Summer (June–August) draws more tourists and higher prices. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer good weather and fewer crowds. Winters are cold but the city has charm under snow if you don't mind the chill. Plan ahead: the more time you spend searching, the better your chances of finding a good fare.

What to do when you arrive in Vilna

Vilna airport (VNO) is about 5 km from the centre, well served by bus and taxi. The Altstadt (Old Town) is a UNESCO site: cobbled streets, baroque churches and cafés. Climb Gediminas Tower for panoramic views. Visit the Gate of Dawn, the Cathedral and explore neighbourhoods like Uzupis. Vilna is compact and walkable, and Lithuanian cuisine is a treat—try cepelinai (potato dumplings) and local beer.
