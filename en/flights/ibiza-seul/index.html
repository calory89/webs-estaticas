Best time to visit

South Korea has four distinct seasons. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and less rain. Winter is cold and dry, summer hot and humid. February is generally the cheapest month to travel, though any season can offer good prices if you're flexible.

What to do when you arrive

Seoul is vibrant: explore Gangnam, visit palaces like Gyeongbokgung, wander traditional markets such as Myeongdong. Outside the capital, Bulguksa temple in Gyeongju and Hwaseong Palace in Suwon are must-sees. Korean food alone is worth the trip: eat at noraebang and try local kimchi. Incheon airport connects well to central Seoul by metro and bus.

What should I see in Seoul?

Gangnam and its skyscrapers, historic palaces like Gyeongbokgung, neighbourhoods like Itaewon and Hongdae packed with shops and nightlife, markets such as Myeongdong and Namdaemun. Eat street food, visit noraebang (Korean karaoke) and try homemade kimchi in local markets.
