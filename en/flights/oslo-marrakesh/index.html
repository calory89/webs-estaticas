Best time to visit

Marrakech is ideal from November to March: mild temperatures (20–25°C by day), no rain, and no extreme summer heat. December is perfect if you want fewer crowds but still great weather. July and August are scorching; April to October is mid-season with more tourists.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Marrakech-Menara (5 km from the centre). The medina is essential: Jemaa El-Fnaa square with juice vendors and acrobats, the artisan souks, and the mosques. Don't miss the palaces (Bahia, Badi), gardens (Majorelle), and the city walls. Try street food: tagine, couscous, harira. Traditional riads make great stays in the old town.
