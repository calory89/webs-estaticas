Best time to travel

Sydney is in the Southern Hemisphere, so November is Australian spring: pleasant weather and sunny days. What to do in Burbank

Burbank is the gateway to Los Angeles: 30 km from downtown, with direct access to Hollywood, Griffith Observatory and Santa Monica. Visit film studios, the Walk of Fame, Southern California beaches, or coastal drives. Burbank Airport (BUR) is smaller and faster than LAX, ideal for starting your trip without queues or delays.
