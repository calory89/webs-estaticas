Airline and connections

Vueling operates this route direct, with no stops. That means you leave Bilbao and arrive in Paris without changing planes. A flight of just over 2 hours with no hassle. Vueling is a budget airline with a solid European network.

Best time to visit

Paris is lovely year-round, but winter (January-February) has fewer tourists and lower flight prices. If you prefer good weather, spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) are ideal: pleasant temperatures and less crowded than summer. In July and August, flight prices tend to rise.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Paris airport (Charles de Gaulle or Orly, depending on your flight). From there, direct public transport to the city centre by train or bus. Don't miss the Eiffel Tower, the Louvre, Notre-Dame and a stroll along the Seine. Grab a real croissant at a local boulangerie, explore the Marais neighbourhood and head up to Montmartre at sunset. Three or four days is ideal to see the essentials without rushing.
