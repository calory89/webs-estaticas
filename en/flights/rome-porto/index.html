Ryanair takes you direct from Rome to Porto from €50 per person (return). The average sits at €56. Fly with no fuss and discover the Portuguese city for less.

Price and when to book

Fares range from €50 to €60 return depending on dates. From €50 you get a complete return ticket. Book as early as possible; Ryanair raises fares as departure approaches. Search 2–3 months ahead for the best rates.

Airline and direct flight

Fly with Ryanair with no stops (direct). ROM to OPO is non-stop: depart from Rome, land in Porto with no aircraft changes. Departure is morning (10:55) and return afternoon (16:35) subject to availability. Check baggage policy: Ryanair charges for checked luggage.

Best time to visit

Porto is pleasant year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild weather without crowds. Avoid July–August if you want lower fares; the city fills up and prices rise. Winter is quieter and cheaper, though rainier.

What to do when you arrive in Porto

Porto airport is 11 km from the centre; bus or taxi takes 20–30 minutes. Stroll the Ribeira (old town with azulejo tiles), taste port wine in Vila Nova de Gaia cellars, cross the Dom Luís Bridge and climb the Clérigos Tower. Eat francesinha (local sandwich) and walk the Monumental stairs.
