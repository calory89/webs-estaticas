Best time to visit

Las Vegas enjoys sunshine year-round. October to April offers comfortable temperatures, while summer (June–August) can exceed 40°C. Christmas and New Year are busy and pricey. Travel in shoulder season (May, September, or October) for better value and pleasant weather.

What to do when you arrive

Explore the iconic Strip with its casinos and shows, visit Fremont Street Experience downtown, or take day trips to the Grand Canyon and Lake Mead. Enjoy world-class shows, celebrity chef restaurants, and outlet shopping. Harry Reid Airport is 6 km from the centre; use a taxi, rideshare, or bus to reach your hotel.
