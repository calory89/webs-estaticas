Best time to visit

Singapore is tropical year-round. June to August is driest but very hot. December to February is cool and dry, more comfortable for walking around the city. Skip October and November if you want less rain. Book well ahead if you're flying near European holidays.

What to do when you arrive in Singapore

Changi Airport (SIN) is 18 km from central Singapore with excellent connections. See Marina Bay Sands, the Botanic Gardens, Masjid Sultan, Chinatown and Little India. Eat at hawker centres and try street food. Singapore is compact, safe and easy to navigate by metro, bus or on foot.
