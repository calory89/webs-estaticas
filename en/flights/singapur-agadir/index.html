Airline and stopovers

Best time to visit

Agadir has mild weather almost year-round. October to April is ideal: pleasant temperatures (18-25 °C) and low rainfall. Skip July and August if you don't handle heat well. Tourism is steady throughout, so choose based on your own availability.

What to do when you arrive in Agadir

Agadir is the gateway to southern Morocco's beaches. Enjoy Agadir Beach, explore the New Medina, visit the port to see the fishing fleet, or take a trip to Souss-Massa National Park. A short drive away is Taghazout for surfing. Don't miss local souks and try Moroccan cuisine at beachfront restaurants.
