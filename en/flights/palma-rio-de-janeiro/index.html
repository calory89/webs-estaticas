Price and when to book

From €786 you'll find return tickets. Most fares sit around €805. If you see prices of €1,319 or higher, availability is lower on those dates. Book as early as possible: check today because these prices can rise. December is peak season in Brazil, so demand climbs quickly and prices follow.

Airline and stopovers

Fly with Iberia Airlines. This route includes 3 stops on the outbound flight, so it's not direct. Expect connections along the way to Rio: total travel time will be longer than a direct flight, but the price makes up for it. Check connection times when booking to avoid surprises.

Best time to visit

December is summer in Rio de Janeiro, with high temperatures, long days and perfect beaches. It's peak tourist season with occasional rain. Prices spike because everyone wants to go then. For fewer crowds and lower fares, consider May to September (autumn and winter), though it'll be cooler. January is just as expensive as December.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Galeão (Rio de Janeiro International Airport). The city centre is about 20 km away: take a taxi, Uber or bus. Stroll along Copacabana and Ipanema beaches, ride up to Christ the Redeemer, visit Sugarloaf Mountain, explore Lapa's nightlife and food scene. Rio is huge: spend at least 4-5 days to enjoy it properly.

We don't have the exact duration, but with 3 stops the trip will be lengthy: between 16 and 24 hours total time depending on where you stop. When you book, you'll see the full itinerary.

Are there direct flights Palma to Rio?

This Iberia route has 3 stops. You can search other airlines to compare, but most flights from Spain to Rio include at least one connection. What matters is the total price.

What's essential to see in Rio de Janeiro?

Christ the Redeemer (iconic), Sugarloaf Mountain (cable car views), Copacabana and Ipanema beaches (swim and stroll), Lapa (nightlife and historic street), and Santa Teresa neighbourhood. If you have time, head to Niterói or nearby beaches.
