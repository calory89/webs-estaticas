Best time to visit

Kyoto is beautiful year-round, but each season offers something different: spring (March–May) brings cherry blossoms; autumn (September–November) delivers warm colours and perfect weather. Avoid local school holidays if you want lower prices and fewer crowds. Book your flight early to access more cheap date options.

What to do when you arrive in Kyoto

Kyoto is Japan's cultural heart: ancient temples, geishas in Gion, the Arashiyama Bamboo Grove and historic castles await. The nearest airport is Kansai (Osaka), about 75 km from Kyoto, with a direct train (90 minutes) that's very convenient. Bring yen, download transport apps and have a must-see temples list ready.
