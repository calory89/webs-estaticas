Price and when to book

Return fares range from €648 to €893. Booking 2 months ahead typically yields better prices. Monitor fares now if your travel date is coming up; the earlier, the better. Prices fluctuate based on availability and seat load, so don't wait until the last minute.

Airline and stops

Air China operates this route with 1 stop, which extends your journey but cuts costs significantly versus a direct flight. The connection typically routes through mainland China. Check your layover time at booking—it usually ranges from 2 to 4 hours.

Best time to visit

Phuket's dry season runs November to February: clear skies and moderate waves. July and August bring monsoon rains and higher prices due to European summer holidays. March to May is hot and humid, but fewer tourists. December is ideal: perfect weather and beaches, though fares climb.

What to do when you arrive

Phuket offers white-sand beaches, Buddhist temples and vibrant nightlife. Phuket International Airport (HKT) is 32 km from town; use a taxi, bus or private transfer. Visit Phi Phi Islands, Patong Beach, Big Buddha and Old Phuket Town. Eat fresh seafood at night markets and sample spicy Thai cuisine.
