Best time to visit

Kyoto is gorgeous in spring (March–April, cherry blossoms) and stunning in autumn (October–November, red foliage). These peaks are busy and pricier. After cheap fares, travel in winter (January–February) or summer (July–August), though it will be colder or hotter.

What to do when you arrive

Kyoto is Japan's former capital: temples, Shinto shrines, geishas in Gion and zen gardens. The Imperial Palace, Fushimi Inari Temple and Arashiyama bamboo groves are must-sees. Use public transport (bus, local train)—it's efficient and cheap. Stay near the main station to reach other cities like Osaka or Hiroshima easily.

What airport serves Kyoto?

Kyoto has no international airport of its own. The OSA code (Osaka International Airport, also called Itami) is closest, about 75 km away. From there, train or bus to Kyoto takes 60–90 minutes.

When is the best time to visit Kyoto?

Spring (March–April) and autumn (October–November) are stunning but pricey. For bargains, go in winter or summer. Local transport is cheap and it rains less than you'd think.
