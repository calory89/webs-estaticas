Best time to visit Denmark

Denmark is stunning in autumn and winter if you're after Northern Lights or festive Christmas magic; summer (June-August) brings very long daylight hours. Winter is cold with short days. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer mild weather and fewer crowds. Flight prices don't follow a strict seasonal pattern on this route, though it varies with peak holiday periods.

What to do when you arrive in Copenhagen

Copenhagen is a lively city: explore Nyhavn, the historic colourful harbour; visit Amalienborg Palace or cycle through the capital like locals do. Malmö (Sweden) is just 30 minutes by train. Denmark is pricey for food and drink, so budget carefully. The airport connects to the city centre in 12 minutes by train. Grab a transport card (City Pass) to move around hassle-free.
