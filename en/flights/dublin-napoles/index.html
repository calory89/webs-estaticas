Cheap flights Dublin–Naples: from €60

Ryanair connects Dublin and Naples on direct flights at very competitive rates. The lowest fare is around €60 return, with a median of €87. Travel to the Amalfi Coast and the Bay of Naples without breaking the bank.

Price and when to book

Cheapest flights start from €60 return; the median sits at €87. To find these fares, book as far in advance as possible: Ryanair usually has lower prices 4–8 weeks before departure. February, March, October and November are budget-friendly months. Avoid July and August if you're looking to save. Compare several dates in Ryanair's calendar to spot the best deals.

Airline and direct flights

Ryanair is the sole operator on this route with direct flights (no transfers). The journey is swift: morning departure from Dublin, afternoon arrival in Naples the same day. No plane changes or terminal transfers. Hand baggage is included in the base fare; checked luggage from approximately €15.

Best time to visit

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer ideal weather and moderate prices. Naples in these seasons has pleasant temperatures without summer crowds. Winter is cheaper but rainier and greyer. Summer is pricey and packed with tourists. On a tight budget, travel in November or March.

What to do on arrival in Naples

Capodichino Airport (NAP) is 5 km from the city centre; bus (€3) or taxi (€20–25) in 15–20 minutes. In Naples: authentic pizzeria, Cathedral, Royal Palace, Spaccanapoli district. Day trips: Pompeya and Herculaneum (nearby train), Mount Vesuvius, Island of Capri, Amalfi (bus or train from the station). Eat fresh pasta and seafood by the port.
