Budapest awaits you with direct flights from Valencia from just €65 return. The median price sits around €75, so you've got solid options for affordable travel. One of the cheapest destinations from Spain's east coast.

Price and when to book

Prices range from €65 to €76 based on our sample data. From Happy Low Cost, the golden rule is to book early: the sooner you purchase, the better the fare. Flights booked 1-2 months ahead typically offer the best deals. Monitor the route regularly if your dates aren't fixed yet.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route. The highlight: direct flights from Valencia (VLC) to Budapest (BUD), no stopovers. Flight time is short (around 2 hours) and you'll arrive fresh. Ryanair keeps fares tight, so check baggage policy before purchase if you need a checked bag.

Best time to visit

Budapest shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter travel (like the 4 January example) means cold but less tourism and lingering festive vibes. Avoid July–August if you'd rather skip peak season crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Ferenc Liszt Airport is 16 km from the city centre. Take the 100E bus (cheap) or taxi. In the city: historic thermal baths (Széchenyi, Gellért), Parliament, Danube views at night, alternative ruin bars, Hungarian food (goulash, langos). Budapest is compact and walkable or easy by public transport. Keep costs low with hostels or Airbnb accommodation.

FAQ

Can I find cheaper Valencia to Budapest flights than €65?

Unlikely. €65 return is the floor price we've recorded. Most fares hover around €75. Book early, compare flexible dates, and set alerts to catch any spot deals.

Is Ryanair the only airline on this route?

According to our data, Ryanair operates this connection. Other sites may list carriers flying with a stop (Wizz Air, Lufthansa, etc.), but those add a layover and extra travel time.

Is the flight direct or do I need a stopover?

Direct. Ryanair flies nonstop from Valencia (VLC) to Budapest (BUD). You board at one and land at the other. Much more convenient and quicker.

What's worth seeing in Budapest in 3–4 days?

The thermal baths (Széchenyi is the largest), Hungarian Parliament, St Stephen's Basilica, Fisherman's Bastion with Danube views, the historic Jewish quarter, Buda Castle, and a sunset river cruise. Three to four days covers everything at a relaxed pace.
