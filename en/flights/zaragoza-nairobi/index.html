Best time to visit

Nairobi is worth visiting year-round, but the dry seasons (June to October and January to February) offer better weather and ideal conditions for safaris. What to do when you arrive

Nairobi is your gateway to Kenya. From here you can access the country's top destinations: safaris in Masai Mara, Amboseli or Tsavo, the Giraffe Centre, the National Museum, and the old town and local markets. Jomo Kenyatta International Airport is about 20 km from the city centre.
