Best time to visit

Venice works well almost year-round, but prices and experience vary widely. Spring and autumn (April–May and September–October) are peak seasons with less acqua alta (high water) and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is quieter and flights are often cheaper, though rain and low canal levels are more likely. January and February see fewer visitors.

What to do when you arrive

Venice is small but dense: St Mark's Square, the Basilica, Rialto Bridge and the canals are must-sees. Hire a gondola if your budget allows, wander aimlessly through backstreets, and try cicchetti (Venetian tapas) at any waterside bar. Marco Polo Airport is 13 km away; take a vaporetto (water bus) or water taxi to reach the centre. Spend 2–3 days to enjoy it properly.
