Best time to travel

Phu Quoc is a tropical destination with pleasant weather year-round. October to April brings less rainfall and comfortable temperatures, ideal for beaches and outdoor activities. Travelling in shoulder season keeps prices down and crowds manageable without sacrificing good weather.

What to do when you arrive

Phu Quoc is known for white-sand beaches, clear waters and resorts. Explore Duong Dong, the main town, with its night market and seafood restaurants. Don't miss nearby islands accessible by boat, historic pepper plantations and national parks. The island is compact and easy to navigate; renting a motorbike or taking a taxi are common options.
