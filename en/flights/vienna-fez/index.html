Best time to visit

Fez has pleasant weather in autumn (September to November) and winter (December to February), with mild temperatures and less extreme heat than summer. Spring (March to May) is also excellent. Avoid July and August if you don't handle heat well.

What to do when you arrive

Fez is one of Morocco's most fascinating imperial cities. The Medina of Fez is a UNESCO World Heritage site: wander its winding streets, visit the Royal Palace, the University of Al Quaraouiyine, and traditional leather tanneries. Fez airport is 15 km from the centre with bus and taxi options available.

What's there to see in Fez?

The Medina of Fez is essential: historic streets, the University of Al Quaraouiyine, leather tanneries, and the Royal Palace. You can also explore the French Nouvelle Ville district and take day trips to nearby towns like Ifrane or Mérida.
