What to do when you arrive in Nassau

Nassau is the capital of the Bahamas and a perfect entry point. Enjoy white sand beaches, reef diving and the famous Blue Hole. Visit the historic centre, try local cuisine with conch salad and explore nearby islands. Lynden Pindling Airport (NAS) is 8 km from downtown; taxis and rental services get you where you need to go.
