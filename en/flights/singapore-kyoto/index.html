Best time to visit Kyoto

Kyoto shines in spring (March–April, cherry blossoms) and autumn (October–November, warm colours). Winter can be cheaper but colder. The example here is January, after the holiday peak. Avoid the rainy season (June) and intense summer heat if possible. Check the lunar calendar: many traditional festivals follow it.

What to do when you arrive in Kyoto

You'll land at Kansai Airport (Osaka), about 75 km from Kyoto. A direct train takes under 90 minutes. Kyoto has thousands of temples (Fushimi Inari, Arashiyama, Ginkakuji), bamboo groves, museums, and traditional cuisine. Walk through the Gion geisha district at dusk. Museums are open year-round. Carry cash; not everywhere takes cards. Rent a bicycle, it's the most efficient way to explore the city.

What can I see and do in Kyoto?

Kyoto has over 2,000 temples. Visit Fushimi Inari (with thousands of red toriis), Arashiyama's bamboo grove, the golden Kinkakuji pavilion, and the silver Ginkakuji. Walk through Gion in the evening to spot geishas. Try traditional kaiseki dining and public baths (onsen). It's 75 km from Kansai Airport.
