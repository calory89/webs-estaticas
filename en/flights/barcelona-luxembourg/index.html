Best time to visit

Luxembourg is a year-round destination. If you're hunting for lower prices, avoid peak seasons like Christmas, Easter and summer. Autumn and winter months (outside public holidays) typically offer more competitive fares. Check regularly: prices change based on availability.

What to do when you arrive

Luxembourg Airport (Findel) is 6 km from the city centre. Direct buses run to the city in about 20 minutes. Once in Luxembourg, don't miss the UNESCO-listed Old Town, the Grand Ducal Palace, medieval fortresses and walks along the Alzette river. It's compact and easily explored on foot.
