Best time to visit

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is cheaper on flights but cold and rainy some days. July and August mean peak heat and packed tourist spots.

What to do when you arrive

Rome is essential: Colosseum, Roman Forum, Pantheon, St Peter's Basilica, Trevi Fountain. The Vatican Museums deserve a full day. Wander Trastevere, eat at local trattorias, stroll along the Tiber. Three to four days covers the basics; a week lets you breathe it in.
