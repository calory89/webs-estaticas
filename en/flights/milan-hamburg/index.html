Best time to visit

Hamburg shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter months are cold but perfect for Christmas markets or if you prefer quieter tourism. Avoid August unless you enjoy peak season crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Don't miss the historic port (Hafen City), Speicherstadt with its red-brick canals and warehouses, or St Michael's Church. The Kunsthalle art museum and Natural History Museum are essential. Eat at restaurants along the Elbe and sample local beer. Hamburg is walkable and well-served by public transport.
