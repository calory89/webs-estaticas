Best time to visit

Mauritius enjoys a tropical climate year-round. The dry season runs from May to November, ideal for beaches and outdoor activities without constant rain. December marks the start of the wetter season, but it remains viable. Check specific weather conditions before travelling, as they are crucial.

What to do in Mauritius

Mauritius boasts white-sand beaches, coral reefs for diving and snorkelling, and the Tamarin Bay lagoon. Don't miss the volcanic island of Île aux Cerfs, the Pamplemousses Botanical Gardens, and Port-Louis, the capital, with its cultural blend. The international airport is well connected to the centre; choose accommodation based on beach or city plans.
