Best time to travel

November is a solid choice: fewer tourists than summer, pleasant weather in Crete, and more competitive prices. May-June and September-October also offer good balance between climate and fare. Avoid July and August if you're hunting bargains, it's peak season and prices soar.

What to do when you arrive in Crete

Crete is Greece's largest island: pink sand beaches at Balos, Chania's old town, Minoan ruins at Knosós, and Samaria Gorge for hiking. The airport (HER) is close to Heraklion, the island's capital. Car hire gives you freedom to explore, or use local buses if you prefer something more budget-friendly.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price from Lisbon to Crete?

€247 return is the lowest we've found. Most tickets hover around €258. Prices vary by date, the earlier you book, the better the fares tend to be.

Does SKY express fly direct Lisbon to Crete?

No, this route includes 1 stop. It's what SKY express offers on this run. The stopover is necessary but worth it price-wise compared to other options.

When is the best time to visit Crete?

September to October and May to June offer pleasant weather and steadier prices. November is also a good bet. July and August see higher fares because it's peak season in Greece.

What should I know about Crete airport?

Crete's international airport (HER) is in Heraklion. Bus connections and taxis are available. It's well linked to the rest of the island, though hiring a car gives you more flexibility.
