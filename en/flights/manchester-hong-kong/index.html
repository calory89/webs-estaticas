Best time to visit

Hong Kong has pleasant weather from October to April, with mild winters. Avoid the rainy months (May to September). Travelling outside school holidays is usually cheaper. If you're interested, check when Chinese New Year falls for a unique experience.

What to do in Hong Kong

Don't miss Victoria Harbour, nearby islands like Lantau, Stanley Market, or a trip to Shenzhen (mainland China). Eat at street markets, ride the Peak Tram, and take in the blend of tradition and modernity that defines the city.
