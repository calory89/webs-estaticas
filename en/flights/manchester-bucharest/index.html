Price and when to buy

Flights start from €81 return; most fall between €81 and €102. The earlier you book, the lower the price. Don't wait until the last minute, fares climb as your departure date approaches. Check offers 6–8 weeks ahead.

Airline and flight type

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight: no stops, no changes. Departures from Manchester (MAN), arrivals at Bucharest Otopeni (OTP). With Ryanair, bear in mind cabin baggage is limited; add luggage costs if you need a checked bag.

Best time to visit

Bucharest appeals year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild weather and fewer crowds. Winter (November–February) is cheaper but cold. Summer is peak season: pricier and busier. Plan based on your weather preference and budget.

What to do when you arrive

Bucharest has the Brâncovenesc Church, Snagov Monastery, the bohemian Lipscani district, and a vibrant food scene. The airport is 16 km from the city centre; grab a taxi, bus, or train. The city is affordable: accommodation, food, and attractions cost far less than Western Europe.
