Airline and stops

Scoot operates this route as a direct flight with no stops. You travel from Vienna airport to Singapore without intermediate layovers. Best time to visit

Singapore is tropical year-round, but avoid monsoon months (November to March can bring heavy rain). Prices vary with tourist season. Travel shoulder months (April, May, August, September) if you want good weather without peak tourism. Check the destination climate before you buy.

What to do when you arrive in Singapore

Singapore is compact and efficient. Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay, Chinatown and Little India are must-sees. Public transport is excellent: fast metro and cheap buses. Eat at hawker centres (local food courts) to spend little and taste authentic local food. Museums, temples and parks complete the experience in 3–5 days.
