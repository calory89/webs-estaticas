Best time to travel

San Francisco is pleasant most of the year, but May to September offers the most stable and sunny weather. Prices tend to be higher in summer and around US holidays. If you're after low fares, consider travelling in winter or spring.

What to do when you arrive

San Francisco awaits with the Golden Gate, Alcatraz and Embarcadero walks. Explore historic neighbourhoods like Mission or Castro, try local sourdough and ride the iconic cable cars. From the airport (SFO) you have good public transport links to downtown.
