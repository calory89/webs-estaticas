Price and when to book

Amsterdam to Malta flights range around €125–€127 return. We've spotted prices from €125 to €173 in our sample. The trick: book early. The sooner you lock in your ticket, the better your chances of hitting the lowest fares. Don't wait until the last minute.

Airline and direct flights

easyJet is your carrier on this route. Best part: no stops. Direct flights from Amsterdam (AMS) to Malta (MLA), no middle points. A clean, hassle-free journey that saves you time.

Best time to visit

Malta shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): perfect weather, fewer tourists than summer, and fairer prices. Travel then and you stand a better shot at good fares. Skip July–August if you want peace and savings.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Malta International Airport (MLA), 5 km from Valletta. Explore the UNESCO capital, discover Mdina (the "silent city"), relax at Popeye Village or dive in crystal waters. Gozo and Comino islands are must-sees. Rent a car or hop on local buses: cheap and reliable.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Amsterdam to Malta?

Yes. easyJet flies non-stop between Amsterdam and Malta. One flight, no stops.

What's the lowest price you found?

From €125 return. The median sits at €127. Prices shift by date and availability, so book ahead.

Which airline operates this route?

easyJet. It's the carrier we track for Amsterdam–Malta with the sharpest fares.

What to see in Malta in a short trip?

Valletta (capital), Mdina, Popeye Village, Mellieha and Comino beaches, and diving. If you have time, visit Gozo. Three days covers the essentials.
