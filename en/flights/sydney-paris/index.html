Airline and stopovers

Best time to visit

Paris shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), when the weather is mild and crowds are thinner. Winter (December–February) is cold but charming with festive vibes. Avoid July and August if you prefer fewer tourists and potentially lower prices.

What to do when you arrive in Paris

The French capital speaks for itself: Eiffel Tower, Louvre, Notre-Dame Cathedral, strolls along the Seine, world-class museums and unmatched gastronomy. Direct connections from the airport lead to the city centre. Allow at least 4–5 days to enjoy yourself without rush.
