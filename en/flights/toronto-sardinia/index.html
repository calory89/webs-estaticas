Best time to visit

Sardinia shines from May to September: crystal-clear waters, ideal temperatures, and lively coastal towns. June and September are golden months (less crowded than July–August but great weather). If you travel in low season (October to April), you'll find cheaper fares but shorter daylight hours. From Canada, note that December is winter here but school holidays there, so demand may be higher.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Cagliari Airport (Elmas) in southern Sardinia. From there, visit untouched beaches in Villasimius, hike the cliffs at Baunei, explore fishing villages like San Sperate, and see ancient nuraghe ruins across the island. The northeast coast (Costa Smeralda) is famous but pricey; the Orosei area to the east offers better value. Rent a car to explore freely.

What should I see in Sardinia?

White-sand beaches and turquoise water (Villasimius, Chia), stunning coastal trails, ancient towns, mountain hiking, snorkelling and diving. The island is small but packed; with a car you can reach anywhere in 2–3 hours.
