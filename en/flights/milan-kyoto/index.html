Best time to visit

Kyoto is stunning in spring (March-May) with cherry blossoms, and in autumn (September-November) with vibrant nature colours. Avoid summer (July-August) if you don't like high temperatures and humidity. Winter is peaceful and less touristy. Check local festival calendars before booking.

What to do when you arrive

Kyoto is Japan's cultural heart. Visit Fushimi Inari temple with its thousands of bright red torii gates, the Imperial Palace, Ryoan-ji gardens, and traditional geishas in Gion. Walk through Arashiyama Bamboo Grove and try local cuisine. The nearest international airport is Kansai (KIX), about 75-90 minutes from Kyoto by train.
