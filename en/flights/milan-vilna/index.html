Best time to visit

Vilna shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather, fewer crowds than summer, less cold than winter. Winter means snow and sub-zero temperatures; summer brings peak tourism and higher fares. Travel off-season for better prices and a quieter experience.

What to do when you arrive in Vilna

Vilna airport is 4 km from the centre. The Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage site: medieval lanes, baroque churches, cosy squares. Visit the Cathedral, Gate of Dawn, and Gediminas Tower. Eat in Uzupis, the bohemian district. Vilna is compact and walkable.
