Best time to visit

Vienna is beautiful any season. February can be cold, but the city has fewer tourists and accommodation prices drop. For better weather, spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal, though usually pricier. Winter in Vienna has its charm with traditional Christmas markets.

What to do when you arrive

Vienna awaits with its imperial palaces, especially Schönbrunn and Hofburg. Don't miss St. Stephen's Cathedral in the historic centre, the Ringstrasse museums and traditional cafés serving Sachertorte. The airport (VIE) is 16 km from the city; take the fast train or bus to reach the centre in 30–45 minutes.
