Best time to visit

Cape Town has a Mediterranean climate. Southern summer (December to February) is warm and dry, perfect for beaches and outdoor activities. Spring (September to November) and autumn (March to May) offer pleasant temperatures and fewer crowds. Winter (June to August) is cooler but still worth visiting. What to do when you arrive

Cape Town is the gateway to South Africa's Western Cape. Don't miss Table Mountain (cable car or hiking), Cape Point, Clifton beaches, the Winelands for vineyards, and the Bohemian Woodstock neighbourhood for art and food. The airport (CPT) is 20 km from the centre, with buses, taxis, and car hire available.
