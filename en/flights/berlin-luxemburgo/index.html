Flying from Berlin to Luxembourg costs from €132 return. The median price sits at €180. With Ryanair and one stop, you've got budget-friendly options to escape to the Grand Duchy.

Price and when to book

Flights start at €132 return, with prices climbing to €181 depending on availability. The median sits at €180. Book early for the best deal: this route isn't super frequent and cheap seats go fast. Aim for two months ahead to lock in a good fare.

Airline and stops

Ryanair is your carrier. Flights include one stop: not direct, but the price makes up for it. Outbound departs around 08:15 and the return leaves early, roughly 06:05. Always check exact times when you book, as Ryanair adjusts schedules by date.

Best time to visit

Luxembourg is lovely year-round, but spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer mild weather and fewer crowds. Winter means cold and possible snow; summer (July-August) is warm but busier. Christmas brings festive markets, though prices climb then too.

What to do when you arrive

Luxembourg airport is 5 km from the capital. The old town is a UNESCO World Heritage site: cobbled alleys, fortresses and Alzette Valley views. Don't miss Notre-Dame Cathedral, the Corniche viewpoint and the Musée d'Art Moderne. The Ardennes to the north have hiking trails and medieval villages. Three days covers the essentials well.
