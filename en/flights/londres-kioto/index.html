Best time to visit

Japan has four distinct seasons. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) offer mild weather and less rain—ideal for sightseeing. Winter (December–February) is colder but less crowded. If your example is early December, expect cold temperatures in Kyoto, though it's a nice window before the holidays.

What to do when you arrive

Kyoto is Japan's cultural and spiritual heart. Don't miss its thousands of temples (Fushimi Inari is essential), bamboo walks in Arashiyama, geishas in Gion and the Imperial Palace. Eat at local markets, try sake and cycle through country lanes dotted with rice fields. Spend at least three or four days to enjoy without rushing.

FAQ

Which airport do I land at in Kyoto?

You'll arrive at Kansai International Airport (KIX), the region's main airport. Direct trains run from there to Kyoto (70–90 minutes). There's no commercial airport within Kyoto itself.

The prices shown are the base fare. When you confirm on the airline's or agency's website, check if airport taxes, checked baggage or other fees are added. Always review the breakdown before paying.
