Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Ryanair. This route includes 2 stopovers on the outbound flight, so prepare for a longer journey. Use the stops to rest and explore the airports. Check what baggage is included in your fare before travelling.

Best time to visit

Peru is a year-round destination, but the dry season (May to October) offers better weather in the highlands and rainforest. These months typically have higher fares. On a tight budget, consider travelling in the low season (November to April), when there's more rain but fewer tourists and cheaper flights available.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in Lima, the capital. From the airport you can reach the city centre on foot or by local transport. Lima is your gateway to Machu Picchu, the Sacred Valley and the Amazon rainforest. The city has world-class museums, renowned gastronomy and nearby beaches. Domestic connections from Lima reach other Peruvian destinations.
