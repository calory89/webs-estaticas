Best time to visit

Crete is open year-round for beaches and culture. April-May and September-October deliver perfect weather: warm but not oppressive. July-August means higher prices and crowded beaches. Winter (November-March) is cheaper but expect occasional rain. Pick based on your budget and preferred climate.

What to do when you arrive

Heraklion is your gateway to Crete. Airport to city centre takes 20 minutes. Explore pink sand beaches at Balos, Minoan ruins at Knossos, white villages in Lefka Ori mountains, hidden coves and Cretan cuisine. Rent a car for freedom or use local buses to get around the island.

What can I see and do in Crete in 5 days?

Beaches (Balos, Elafonissi), Knossos ruins, mountain villages, gorges for hiking and local tavernas. Crete is large; a car lets you cover more ground. Using buses? Stick to the north or south coast depending on where you base yourself.
