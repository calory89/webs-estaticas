Best time to visit

December is peak season in Egypt for weather. It's cooler than summer, perfect for exploring Cairo without the heat. Prices go up then, but travelling in European winter beats the summer slog. Shoulder months (October, November, March, April) offer good prices and pleasant conditions.

What to do when you arrive

Cairo International Airport (CAI) is roughly 20 km from downtown. Head straight to Khan El-Khalili (the historic bazaar), the Citadel of Saladin, and of course the Pyramids of Giza. If you have extra days, pair Cairo with Luxor or Aswan. The Egyptian Museum is unmissable. Hire a local guide to make the most of your time.
