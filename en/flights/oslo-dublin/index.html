Airline and stopovers

Best time to travel

Dublin is pleasant year-round, but October to March brings more rain. Spring (April–May) and early autumn (September) offer good weather and fewer crowds than summer. Skip December if you want lower fares—it's peak season. Book 6–8 weeks ahead for the best deals.

What to do when you arrive

Dublin Airport (DUB) is 10 km from the city centre. Bus, taxi or train are your main options. Must-sees: Temple Bar, Trinity College, Guinness Storehouse, and the Cliffs of Moher (day trip). Explore Dublin's literary heritage (Joyce, Beckett) and soak up the pub culture.

No, this Ryanair UK route includes 1 stopover. If you need a direct flight, check other airlines, though they're likely to be pricier.

Is it really safe to book Ryanair UK at €105?

Yes. Those prices (€105–€119 return) are confirmed and real. Remember Ryanair charges extra for checked baggage, seat selection, and priority boarding. Review what your fare includes before paying.

What's the best month to visit Dublin at these prices?

We can't say which month is cheapest, but outside peak season (July–August, December) you'll find more offers. Compare prices for your specific dates on your search engine.

What will I find at Dublin Airport?

A modern airport with direct public transport (bus, train) to Dublin city centre in 20–40 minutes. Shops, restaurants and car hire desks are available. It's well signposted and easy to navigate.
