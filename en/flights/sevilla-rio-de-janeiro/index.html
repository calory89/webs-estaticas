Best time to visit Rio de Janeiro

November is ideal: Brazilian summer begins, it's warm and rainfall is lower than peak months (December-March). It's high season, but not peak. Prices ease slightly outside July-August and December-January, though international routes maintain steady demand year-round.

What to do when you arrive

Rio's international airport (Galeão) is 20 km from the city centre. Head to the Distrito Federal or beaches like Copacabana and Ipanema. Don't miss Sugarloaf Mountain, Christ the Redeemer, Rodrigo de Freitas lagoon, and beach life. Rio is chaotic and vibrant: plan transport carefully and stay alert in certain areas.
