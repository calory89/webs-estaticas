Best time to visit

Norway shines in summer (June to August) with long days and mild weather, but it's peak season. For better value, try spring (May) or autumn (September–October) for good weather and fewer crowds. Winter is magical if you want to see the Northern Lights, though you'll need warm layers.

What to do when you arrive in Oslo

Gardermoen airport is 25 km from the city centre; take the Flytoget train or bus to get in. Once there, stroll the Old Town (Gamlebyen), visit the Opera House and the Royal Palace Park. Try fresh salmon at the markets, take a fjord boat tour and don't miss the National Gallery. Oslo is compact and walkable: a weekend goes a long way.
