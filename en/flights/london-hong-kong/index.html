Fly from London to Hong Kong with China Eastern Airlines for €437 return. Median price sits around €477. Perfect for travellers seeking an Asian escape without breaking the bank.

Price and when to book

The cheapest flight costs €437 return. Prices in the sample range from €437 to €479, with a median of €477. For this route, the earlier you book, the better. Check flights 2–3 months ahead; prices climb as the departure date approaches. December is peak season, so availability thins out fast.

Airline and stopovers

You'll travel with China Eastern Airlines. The outbound flight includes 1 stopover, so it's not direct. The typical route connects through one of their Asian hubs. This adds travel time but enables these competitive fares. Always verify your stopover details in your confirmation.

Best time to visit

December is a solid choice if you want warm weather in Hong Kong (15–20°C), though it's peak tourist season. Avoid July and August for extreme heat and humidity. March to May offers more pleasant conditions. Bear in mind December brings more travellers and fuller flights, so snap up this price if you spot it.

What to do when you arrive

Hong Kong blends cutting-edge skyscrapers with tradition. Don't miss Victoria Harbour views from The Peak Tram, the neon-lit night markets of Temple Street or Mong Kok, and temples like Wong Tai Sin. Ride the iconic double-decker tram, ascend Victoria Peak, and explore Central and Wan Chai. The airport connects to the city centre by express train in 24 minutes.

FAQ

What is the cheapest price to fly London to Hong Kong?

The lowest price is €437 return. The median price in the sample is €477. These fares are for December departures with China Eastern Airlines.

Is it a direct flight from London to Hong Kong?

No, it's a one-stopover flight. China Eastern Airlines operates this route with a single connection, which helps keep the price competitive. Total travel time depends on your stopover location and layover duration.

Which airline flies London to Hong Kong?

China Eastern Airlines operates this route. It connects through one of its Asian hubs. Check your booking confirmation to confirm your exact stopover airport.

What should I see in Hong Kong in 3–4 days?

Day 1: The Peak Tram and stroll through Central and Wan Chai. Day 2: Night markets (Temple Street or Mong Kok). Day 3: Wong Tai Sin Temple and local neighbourhoods. Day 4: Nearby islands by ferry or the New Territories. Transport is efficient and affordable with an Octopus Card.
