Best time to visit

December is Copenhagen's festive season: Christmas markets are in full swing, squares are lit up, and the atmosphere is magical. If you prefer milder weather, spring (April-May) or autumn (September-October) work well, though prices may climb. Winter suits those keen on Nordic charm, pack warm layers.

What to do when you arrive

Copenhagen Airport Kastrup is the main hub, well-connected to the city centre. Don't miss Nyhavn with its colourful townhouses, the Little Mermaid statue, Tivoli Gardens (especially festive in December), and Christiania neighbourhood. Stroll the waterfront, try Danish open sandwiches, and explore Strøget, Europe's longest pedestrian street. Christmas markets pop up across the city in December.
