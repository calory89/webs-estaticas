Ryanair connects Dublin and Bologna with direct flights priced from €95 per person return. The median price sits at €118, and alternative dates are available around €166 if you need more flexibility.

Price and when to book

The lowest price is €95 return. Ryanair offers these base fares on this route if you book 8–12 weeks ahead. The earlier you reserve, the higher your chances of hitting that minimum. October to November and January to March often see lower demand and cheaper seats, though this shifts with market conditions. Check regularly: Ryanair refreshes fares frequently and prices can climb fast.

Airline and flights

Ryanair operates this route with direct flights, no stops, no hassle. The carrier is famous for lean pricing, but remember that baggage usually costs extra depending on your ticket type. The direct Dublin-Bologna journey lands you at Bologna Airport (BLQ), well connected to the city centre by bus or train in under 30 minutes.

Best time to visit

Bologna shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) with mild weather and fewer crowds. Winter (December–February) is quieter and cheaper locally, though rain is common. Summer can be scorching. If your goal is pure savings, travel January–February or November: fewer tourists and cheaper accommodation, though Ryanair's absolute lowest fares don't always align with these months.

What to do in Bologna

Bologna is Italy's gastronomic capital and a UNESCO World Heritage site. Wander Piazza Maggiore (the medieval heart), climb Torre Asinelli for city views, explore 40 km of arcaded streets (pórticos), and eat authentic tagliatelle alla bolognese in local trattorias. The Sanctuary of the Madonna di San Luca outside town is worth the trek. The centre is compact and walkable, everything is accessible on foot.
