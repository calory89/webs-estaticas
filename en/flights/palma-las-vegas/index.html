Best time to visit

Las Vegas offers sunshine and shows year-round. Weather is milder from November to March, whilst summer (June-August) is very hot. Prices are often more competitive in low season (January-February and September-October). Avoid late December if you're looking for an economic fare.

What to do when you arrive

Las Vegas offers casinos, shows on the Strip, Bellagio fountains, aquariums, museums, and non-stop nightlife. The Grand Canyon is just hours away. For something quieter, visit Red Rock Canyon or Valley of Fire. Harry Reid Airport is about 6 km from the centre; take a taxi, rideshare, or rent a car.
