Best time to travel

November is a solid choice: less tourism than summer, lower fares and early autumn in northern Italy. Weather in Milan is cool (5–10°C) but dry. For warmer weather, spring (April–May) is ideal, though prices rise. Avoid December and July–August if you want to save.

What to do when you arrive in Milan

You'll land in Milan (likely Malpensa or Linate). The city is Italy's design and fashion capital: the Duomo, Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II and La Scala are must-sees. From Milan you can easily reach lakes Como and Garda, plus medieval towns like Bergamo and Cremona. Try risotto alla milanese and panettone if travelling in autumn–winter.
