Best time to visit

Marrakech is pleasant nearly year-round, but October–November and March–April offer ideal temperatures (20–25°C). Skip July–August for extreme heat. Check fares regularly: prices fluctuate based on demand, school holidays, and Moroccan festivals.

What to do when you arrive

Marrakech airport (RAK) is 6 km from the medina. Head straight to Jemaa el-Fnaa square, the city's tourist heart with markets and authentic buzz. Don't miss the palaces of Bahia and El Badi, Majorelle Gardens, or day trips to the Atlas Mountains or the Sahara. Three days cover the city well; extend if you want nature.
