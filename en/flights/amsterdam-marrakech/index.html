Fly from Amsterdam to Marrakech from €130 return with Transavia. The median price sits at €147, so booking ahead and staying flexible with dates gives you solid options. Direct, no hassle.

Price and when to book

Flights start from €130 (return). The median price is €147, and we've also seen fares at €164. Transavia is competitive on this route. Book 6–8 weeks ahead for the best deal; last-minute options aren't reliable here. Search midweek and avoid peak demand windows.

Airline and stops

Transavia operates this route as a direct flight: departs Amsterdam, arrives in Marrakech with no stops. It's a budget carrier, so expect a no-frills cabin, but the journey is short (under 2 hours). Pack liquids and restricted items in checked luggage per EU rules.

Best time to visit

October, November, March and April are ideal: mild weather (20–25°C), no extreme summer heat. December and January are fine too if you don't mind 15–18°C at night. Skip July and August if you can't take 35°C+ heat. Demand peaks over Christmas and Easter.

What to do when you arrive in Marrakech

The airport (RAK) is 6 km from the medina. Walk through Jemaa el-Fnaá square, browse the spice and textile souks, visit the Bahía Palace and Majorelle Gardens. Ait Benhaddou kasbah is 3 hours by car. Try tagine, mint tea and date pastries. The city is accessible, safe and very photogenic.

FAQ

How much does a flight from Amsterdam to Marrakech cost?

From €130 return with Transavia. The median price is €147. Fares vary by date; we've seen prices up to €164 during peak demand periods.

Does Transavia fly direct from Amsterdam to Marrakech?

Yes, it's a direct flight with no stops. The journey takes under 2 hours, very straightforward.

What's the best month to visit Marrakech?

October, November, March and April are best: pleasant temperatures (20–25°C) and less crowding than Christmas. July and August are very hot (35°C+).

What's worth seeing in Marrakech in 3–4 days?

Jemaa el-Fnaá square, traditional souks, Bahía Palace, Majorelle Gardens and the red kasbah. If you have time, take a day trip to Ait Benhaddou (3 hours). Eat tagine in the medina and drink mint tea in a riad.
