Best time to travel

November is ideal: mild temperatures in New York (10–15 °C), fewer crowds than summer and before the Christmas rush. Prices tend to be more stable in autumn than in peak season. Avoid July–August if you want cheap flights.

What to do when you arrive in New York

New York has endless options: Manhattan (Times Square, Central Park, Broadway), world-class museums, food from everywhere, neighbourhoods like Brooklyn and Queens, and views from the Empire State or One World Observatory. Your arrival airport is typically Newark (EWR), LaGuardia (LGA) or JFK; all well connected to the city centre by subway, taxi or bus.
