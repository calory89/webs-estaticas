Best time to visit

London is attractive year-round. April–May and September–October offer mild weather and fewer crowds than July–August. Winter (December–February) appeals to those seeking Christmas or New Year events, with slightly higher prices. Watch for Ryanair deals in low season to save more.

What to do in London

Big Ben, Tower Bridge, National Museums (many free), Buckingham Palace, markets like Camden or Borough. The destination airport will leave you 15–40 km from the centre depending on the terminal; budget for the Underground, bus, or taxi. An Oyster card for public transport is essential.
