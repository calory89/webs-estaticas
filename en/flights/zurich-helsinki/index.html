Best time to visit

Helsinki is a winter and summer destination. Travelling in December (like the example from 6–17) means Christmas markets, festive atmosphere, and chances to see the Northern Lights outside the city. What to do in Helsinki

Finland's capital blends modern architecture with history. Visit the White Cathedral, the Rock Church (Suomenlinna) and the Kiasma Museum. Local food markets at Kauppatori are a must. Winter travel? Head north to spot the Northern Lights. The region also offers traditional saunas, Nordic design museums and nature nearby.
