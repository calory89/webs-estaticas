Best time to visit

Portland is a year-round destination. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild weather and less rain. Winters are wet but mild, and summers are dry and pleasant. Travelling outside peak tourist season may help you find better prices and enjoy fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Portland is known for its cultural scene, indie cafés and nearby nature. The historic city centre, urban parks like Forest Park, the Portland Art Museum and the food cart scene are must-sees. A short distance away you'll find hiking, the Columbia River Gorge and charming small towns across Oregon. PDX airport is about 10 km from the centre with good public transport links.
