Best time to visit

Mauritius has tropical weather year-round. May to October is cooler and drier; November to March is warmer and more humid, with cyclone risk in February–March. For beaches and diving, the dry season is ideal. Avoid February and March if you can't handle intense heat and humidity.

What to do when you arrive

Mauritius offers white-sand beaches, coral reefs for diving, the Trou aux Cerfs volcanic crater, Chamarel waterfall and historic towns like Port Louis. The international airport is 45 km from the capital. Hire a car or use local transport; the road network is good and buses are affordable.
