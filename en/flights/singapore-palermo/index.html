Singapore-Palermo flights: from €603 return

The Singapore (SIN) to Palermo (PMO) route starts from €603 for return flights. The median price sits around €692, so you have room to find good deals. Flying from Southeast Asia to Sicily requires patience: these journeys typically have 2 stopovers on the outbound leg.

Price and when to book

Fares range between €603 and €748 depending on dates. In the sample checked on 23 October 2026, trips departing 12 November and returning 21 November held within that range. There's no fixed rule about which month prices drop, but booking 2–3 weeks ahead usually helps. Check fares regularly: small date shifts can save €50–€100.

Airline and stopovers

Oman Air operates this route with 2 stopovers on the outbound leg. The sample flight departs Singapore at 20:50 and arrives in Palermo on 21 November at 22:15. With two stops in between, the journey is long but competitive in price. Stopovers are standard on this intercontinental route; pick flight times that let you rest between connections.

Best time to visit

Sicily shines from October to April, when the weather is mild and there's less mass tourism than in summer. November, as in the sample, works well: pleasant temperatures, clear skies and lower fares than peak season. Avoid July and August if you want peace and better prices.

What to do when you arrive

Palermo is your gateway to Sicily: walk the historic centre, try street food at the markets (Ballarò, Capo), visit the cathedral and relax on nearby beaches like Mondello. From there you can reach the whole island: Monreale, Cefalú, Mondello or the ruins of Agrigento are a short drive or bus ride away.

FAQ

Is the Singapore-Palermo flight direct?

No. Oman Air makes 2 stopovers on the outbound leg. Direct flights Singapore–Europe are extremely rare; stopovers on this route are the standard.

What's the cheapest price you've seen?

From €603 return on the dates checked. But check regularly: there's variation depending on your exact departure and return day.

Which airline flies this route?

Oman Air is the operator in the available data. Always verify on the search engine if other options exist for your travel dates.

When is the best time to visit Palermo?

October to April: mild weather, fewer crowds and more reasonable prices. November is ideal if you want a balance between comfort and fare.
