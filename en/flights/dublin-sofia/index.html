Price and when to book

From €68 return is the lowest we've seen. The median sits at €75, with options up to €134 depending on dates and flexibility. Check regularly: flight prices change with availability. Book as soon as possible from now (October), especially if you're travelling in December or peak season.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with direct flights from Dublin to Sofia. No intermediate stops: you board at DUB and land at SOF. Direct means less hassle, less airport time, and more hours at your destination.

Best time to visit

Sofia is lovely year-round, but October to April offers fewer tourists and better prices. Winter (November-February) is cold but charming. Spring and autumn are ideal: mild weather and good availability. December can spike in price due to holidays, so check with flexibility.

What to do when you arrive

Alexander Nevsky Cathedral is a must. Walk through Saint George Basilica, visit Rila Monastery (1.5 hours away) and try local food at traditional markets. Sofia is compact: most attractions are walkable or a short public transport ride away.
