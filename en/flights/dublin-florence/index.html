Best time to visit

Florence shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with fewer crowds than summer. Prices may be higher during peak season. Winter offers better value for money with fewer visitors, though it's colder. Choose based on your budget and climate preference.

What to do when you arrive

Florence is the cradle of the Renaissance. Visit Florence Cathedral, the Uffizi Gallery, Ponte Vecchio and the Basilica of Santa Croce. The city is compact and walkable. Try Tuscan cuisine and explore the museums and art galleries scattered throughout. Florence Airport (Peretola) is about 4 km from the city centre.

What should I see in Florence in 3 days?

Spend the first day in the historic centre: Cathedral, Piazza della Signoria and Ponte Vecchio. On day two, visit the Uffizi Gallery and explore museums. On day three, head to Piazzale Michelangelo for panoramic views and explore neighbourhoods like Oltrarno. With more time, take a trip to Siena or the Chianti region.
