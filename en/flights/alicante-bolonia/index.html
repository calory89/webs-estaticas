Best time to visit

Bologna is attractive year-round, but October, November and April offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. June to August see significant price increases and tourist congestion. For savings, travel in low season (November to March, except Christmas and New Year).

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Bologna-Marconi airport, 6 km from the centre. Bus or taxi takes you there in 20 minutes. The city is small and the old town is pedestrian: explore the medieval towers, the Basilica of San Petronio, the monumental porticoes and try fresh homemade pasta (tortellini, Bolognese lasagne). Museums and galleries will keep you busy in the afternoons.
