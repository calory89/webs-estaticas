Best time to visit Crete

Crete is at its best from April to November, with warm weather and beaches in full swing. Travelling outside July and August means you'll dodge peak prices and tourist crowds. Spring and autumn deliver the sweet spot: pleasant climate and better-value flights.

What to do in Crete

Beyond beaches, Crete offers Minoan history at Knosós, charming coastal towns like Chania and Rethimno, plus mountains for hiking. Heraklion Airport (HER) is well-linked by bus and car hire to anywhere on the island.
