Best time to visit

Dubrovnik sits on the Adriatic and shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) with pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Summer is beautiful but packed and pricey. Winter (November–March) is quieter and cheaper, though rain is common. Choose based on your priorities: beaches or cultural break.

What to do when you arrive

Dubrovnik Airport is roughly 20 km from the city. Take a bus or taxi to the walled Old Town (a UNESCO site). Walk the walls, explore Gothic palaces, swim at nearby Banje Beach, or hop a ferry to nearby islands. Dubrovnik is small and walkable. Book museums and restaurants ahead during peak season.
