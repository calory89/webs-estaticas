Best time to visit

Dubrovnik is beautiful year-round, but July and August are the most expensive and crowded months. April, May, September and October offer good weather and fewer tourists. If you're after lower prices, October and November can surprise you. Avoid Christmas if you don't want to pay more.

What to do when you arrive

Dubrovnik is the jewel of Croatia: walk the ancient walls, stroll down the Stradun (main street), visit the museums, enjoy nearby beaches like Banje or Lokrum. The Adriatic is at your doorstep. Boats leave Dubrovnik for nearby islands such as Elafiti or Lopud. The old town is a UNESCO World Heritage site and deserves at least two days.

What to see in Dubrovnik in 3 days?
