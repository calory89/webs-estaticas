Air Europa takes you from Alicante to Cancún for €866 return. The median price sits around €878. This route is perfect for a Caribbean escape without breaking the bank.

Price and when to book

Flights start from €866 return. In our sample we found fares at €866, €878 and €969. The difference is small: if you spot a ticket below €900, it's a good time to book. Book early and you'll have more choice and better prices.

Airline and stops

Air Europa operates this route with 3 stops on the outbound flight. It's not a direct flight, so expect intermediate stops that vary by date. This type of journey is standard on long-haul routes to Mexico and is where you'll usually find the best value.

Best time to travel

Cancún is a beach destination year-round, but the dry season (November to April) offers the best weather. August and September are hurricane months, so avoid them. For prices, travelling outside school holidays is usually cheaper.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Cancún International Airport (CUN), 25 km from the centre. In the area you can visit the Riviera Maya, pristine beaches, cenotes, Mayan ruins in Tulum or Playa del Carmen. If you prefer the city, Cancún centre has restaurants, nightlife and shops. Hotels vary widely in price: there are options for every budget.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Alicante to Cancún?

Not at this fare. Air Europa offers flights with 3 stops from €866. Direct flights, if available, usually cost more.

What's the best month to buy these flights?

We can't confirm a specific cheaper month. What works is booking in advance: you'll have better availability and wider choice of prices.

How long does the Alicante-Cancún flight take with stops?

With 3 stops, the journey will be long. Total travel time depends on intermediate stops. Generally, expect 12 to 16 hours of actual flight time plus layovers.

What to see in Cancún besides beaches?

Cenotes (natural freshwater pools), Mayan ruins in Tulum less than 2 hours away, parks like Xcaret and Xel-Há, fishing villages in Playa del Carmen, and Isla Mujeres. There's something for every interest.
