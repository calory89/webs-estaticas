Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €813 for a complete round trip. Average prices hover around €978. Book as early as possible: Ryanair releases its best fares 2-3 months in advance. January, February and November tend to be cheaper months on this route because they fall in Cancun's low season, and the weather is still excellent.

Airline and stopovers

We fly with Ryanair, the budget carrier behind these competitive prices. This route includes 2 stopovers, so total flight time is longer than a direct flight, but the price difference is worth it if you're saving money. The stopovers are part of Ryanair's standard routing from Alicante to the Caribbean.

Best time to visit

Cancun is stunning year-round, but February-March and October-November offer the best weather: pleasant temperatures (25-28°C) and less rain. February is especially good because flight prices are low. Avoid September and October for Atlantic hurricane season. Sargassum seaweed blooms vary yearly, so check ahead if that concerns you.

What to do when you arrive

Cancun is the gateway to the Riviera Maya. Don't miss Chichén Itzá and Tulum for Mayan ruins. Playa del Carmen is 45 minutes by car. Cenotes at Xel-Há and Xplor are perfect for swimming in freshwater and snorkelling. For untouched beaches, Isla Mujeres and Cozumel are accessible by ferry. The airport is 25 km from the city centre; take a taxi, shuttle or hire a car.
