Best time to visit Dubrovnik

December is a solid choice if you want fewer crowds and keen pricing. The Adriatic coast shines year-round, but May–September offer warm weather and clear skies. Winter (December–February) is cool and wet, but perfect if you prefer quiet and don't mind layering up. April–June and September–October strike the sweet spot: pleasant weather without peak-season tourists.

What to do when you arrive in Dubrovnik

Dubrovnik is the Adriatic's crown jewel: medieval walls, a film-set old town and nearby beaches. Walk the city walls, stroll the Stradun and ferry out to the Elafiti Islands. Don't skip Lokrum's cliffs or the villages of Cavtat and Ston. The airport sits 20 km away; a bus or taxi takes you to the centre in 30 minutes.
