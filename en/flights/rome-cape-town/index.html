Ryanair connects Rome with Cape Town with 1 stop from €696 return. The median price sits around €740, so there's room to find good deals if you adjust your dates. This price is competitive for an intercontinental route.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €696 return. The median stands at €740, and options go up to €930 depending on availability and dates. As always, the earlier you book the better: prices rise as departure approaches. Check regularly 2 to 8 weeks before travel to grab the best fares.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route with 1 stop, so it's not a direct flight. Depending on the connection point, total journey time will be longer than a direct flight, but the price saving makes up for it if you have flexibility. The airline uses a low-cost model: baggage, meals and seat selection cost extra.

Best time to travel

Cape Town is a year-round destination, but the Southern summer (December to February) is peak season and pricier. For better prices, consider travelling in autumn (March to May) or spring (September to November) in the Southern Hemisphere, when the weather is pleasant and flights cheaper.

What to do when you arrive

Cape Town is a vibrant city: Table Mountain (cable car or hiking), Robben Island, Cape Point, beaches like Camps Bay, artistic neighbourhoods like Bo-Kaap, and nearby wine estates. The airport is 20 km from the centre, with public transport, shuttles and car hire available.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Rome to Cape Town?

No, according to current data Ryanair operates this route with 1 stop. Direct flights between Rome and Cape Town are very limited or non-existent with this airline on these dates.

What is the cheapest price you've seen?

The lowest price is €696 return. The sample we checked also includes options at €740 and €930, so there's quite a range. Booking in advance maximises your chances of finding prices in this range.

What does the Ryanair ticket include on this route?

The base Ryanair fare includes transport. Small cabin baggage is included; checked baggage, meals, seat selection and other services are paid separately based on your needs.

How long does the total journey take with a stop?

The exact time depends on the connection airport and layover length, but with 1 stop you should allow 14 to 18 hours total. A direct flight would be shorter, but this Ryanair option offers better value.
