Best time to visit

Iceland is a year-round destination. Summer (June–August) offers more daylight and better weather, but also peak prices. For value, consider April–May or September–October, when there are fewer tourists and leaner fares. Winter (November–February) is prime time for northern lights, though it's colder.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Keflavík (KEF), 50 km from Reykjavik. From the airport, take a bus, taxi or hire a car. The Golden Circle route (Þingvellir, Geysir, Gullfoss) is unmissable. Don't skip Reynisfjara's black sand beach, the geothermal Blue Lagoon or the south coast. In winter, chase the northern lights.
