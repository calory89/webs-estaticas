Best time to visit

Tokyo shines in spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November). November is ideal: mild weather, autumn colours, and fewer crowds than peak season. To avoid crowds and get better prices, travel between November and February or in June–July. Keep checking fares to spot price drops.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in the Tokyo area. Explore Shibuya, Shinjuku and the Imperial Palace. Try local cuisine, visit ancient temples and wander neighbourhoods like Harajuku. Tokyo has something for everyone: from pure Japanese tradition to cutting-edge technology. Getting around is simple thanks to excellent public transport.
