Best time to visit

Charleston has a subtropical climate. Winter (November to February) is mild and dry: ideal for walking without oppressive heat. Spring (March to May) is perfect if you want flowers and pleasant temperatures. Summer is very humid with hurricane risk. Book in advance regardless of your month: early booking always lowers the price.

What to do when you arrive

Charleston is famous for its historic centre with colourful houses, tree-lined squares and Southern food. Walk Rainbow Row, visit Fort Sumter by boat, try the lowcountry boil (local dish) and relax at nearby Folly Beach. The airport is 20 minutes from the centre; you can hire a car or use public transport.
