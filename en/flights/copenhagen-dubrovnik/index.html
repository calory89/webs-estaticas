Best time to visit

Dubrovnik is excellent from May to September, when it's warm and days are long. To avoid crowds and higher prices, choose May, June, or September. Winter is quieter but cooler. Check prices for your dates: they vary significantly.

What to do when you arrive

Dubrovnik airport is 20 km from the city. Take a bus or taxi. Must-see: walk the Old City walls, swim at Banje and Lapad beaches, visit Lokrum island, and dine at waterfront restaurants. Dubrovnik is small and walkable—plan at least 2-3 days.

FAQ

What's the cheapest Copenhagen-Dubrovnik flight?

The lowest price found is €146 return. Most options hover around €148, so book as soon as possible to secure these fares.

Does Ryanair fly direct from Copenhagen to Dubrovnik?

No, it has 1 stop. The flight is not direct, so it adds time to your journey. Check the exact schedule when booking.

When is the best time to visit Dubrovnik?

May to September offer good weather. June and September provide a good balance between warmth and fewer crowds than July-August. Flight prices may rise in summer.

What can I do in Dubrovnik in 3 days?

Walk the Old City walls, swim at Banje or Lapad, take a ferry to Lokrum, eat by the harbour, and explore the narrow streets. Dubrovnik is compact and very walkable.
