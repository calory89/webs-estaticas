Best time to travel

Buenos Aires has a temperate climate year-round. Spring (September to November) and autumn (March to May) offer pleasant temperatures and less rain. Southern summer (December to February) is hot and humid, though it is peak season. Consider travelling in April, May, September or October for better weather and value.

What to do when you arrive

Buenos Aires is Argentina's capital and a gateway to the region. The city offers historic neighbourhoods like San Telmo and La Boca, vibrant culture, world-class dining and tango. From there you can reach Iguazu Falls, Patagonia or wine regions in Mendoza. Ministro Pistarini airport is 35 km from the centre; buses and transfers are available.
