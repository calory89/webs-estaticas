Best time to visit

Bruges is beautiful year-round, but each season offers something different. Spring and autumn usually bring pleasant weather and moderate prices. Winter brings snow and Christmas markets if you travel in December, while summer is peak season with higher fares. Avoid booking at the last minute: short-notice flights are significantly more expensive.

What to do when you arrive

Bruges is a medieval Belgian gem. Stroll the Main Square (Grote Markt), climb Belfry Tower for panoramic views, and take a boat tour of the canals. Visit the Béguinage and the Basilica of the Holy Blood. The city is compact and fully walkable. From there, you can easily reach other Belgian cities like Antwerp or Ghent.
