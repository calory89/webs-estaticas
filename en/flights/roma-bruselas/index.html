The lowest price we've seen is €50 return; median sits at €60. With a range from €50 to €90, there's room to manoeuvre. Book 2–3 months ahead: you'll have flexibility on dates and avoid last-minute charges. Watch for mid-week deals; Fridays and Sundays tend to be pricier.

Airline and flight type

Ryanair is your option on this route: direct flights, no stopovers. The example outbound departs at 07:35 from Rome and the return at 17:55 from Brussels, giving you full days in the city. Note Ryanair's baggage policy: carry-on is free, but checked luggage costs extra.

Best time to visit

Brussels shines year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) deliver pleasant weather and fewer crowds. December gets busy with Christmas markets if you prefer peace and quiet. Winter is cold but charming.

What to do in Brussels

Grand Place is essential: the main square is a Unesco World Heritage site. Don't miss Manneken Pis, try Trappist beers, eat waffles and Belgian chocolate. Sablon district is perfect for a stroll. Time permitting, Basilica of Sacré-Cœur and the Royal Palace are worthwhile. The airport is 15 km from the centre with good public transport links.
