Best time to visit

Fort Lauderdale shines in winter (December to March): mild temperatures, lower humidity, and perfect beach weather. January is ideal if you want to escape Canadian cold without paying peak summer rates. Skip October and November due to hurricane season.

What to do when you arrive

Fort Lauderdale's beaches and nearby areas (Pompano, Deerfield) are stunning. Stroll the waterfront, enjoy fresh seafood, or take a day trip to the Everglades. Airport FLL is 30 km from the city centre; public transport, taxis, and car rental are all available.
