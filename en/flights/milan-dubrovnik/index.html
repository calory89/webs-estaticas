Best time to travel

Dubrovnik is stunning year-round, but October and November offer mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. December-February is low season with lower fares, though peak days (Christmas, New Year) may spike. Spring (April-May) is another excellent choice to avoid peak tourism.

What to do when you arrive

Dubrovnik is a Unesco World Heritage site: walk the City Walls, stroll Stradun Street, and climb Fort Lovrijenac. Nearby are the Elafiti Islands, caves, and hiking trails. Dubrovnik Airport is 20 km south; reach the city centre by bus or taxi in 30-45 minutes. Dine at waterfront restaurants and taste local wines from the region.
