Best time to visit

Tenerife has good weather year-round. December to March is peak season for Northern Europeans escaping cold. These months attract more visitors and have higher prices. Consider travelling in low season (May to October) for better deals.

What to do when you arrive

Tenerife offers beaches, hiking in Teide National Park, coastal villages and nightlife. Tenerife South airport (TCI) is well connected by road to main tourist areas. Hire a car or use local transport depending on your plans. The island suits all types of traveller.
