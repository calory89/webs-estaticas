Best time to visit

Budapest appeals all year round. Winter (November–March) brings snowy views and fewer crowds, ideal if you want a quieter Danube experience. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild weather and busy streets without summer heat. Summer is peak season with more events but also more tourists. Low-season prices are your friend, so winter is when you'll find the best fares.

What to do when you arrive

Ferenc Liszt Airport is 16 km from the city centre. Take the minibus, public transport or a taxi to town. Must-sees include Parliament (Buda side), Széchenyi Thermal Baths, St. Stephen's Basilica and a Danube cruise. Budapest is compact and walkable; the metro is fast and cheap. Eat at the Central Market Hall to taste local food without breaking the bank.
