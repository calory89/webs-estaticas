Best time to visit

Budapest is lovely year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: pleasant weather, fewer crowds than summer, and better prices. Skip July–August if you want peace and value.

What to do when you arrive

Budapest is a gem: the Danube splits the city into Buda and Pest. Walk along the riverbank, visit Buda Castle, explore the Parliament building and enjoy thermal baths. Eat at street markets, try goulash and discover historic cafés. Three or four days covers the essentials.
