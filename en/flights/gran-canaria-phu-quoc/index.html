Best time to visit

Phu Quoc has a tropical climate. The dry season runs November to May: less rain, pleasant temperatures. June to October is wetter, though prices may drop. December and January peak with tourists, so expect crowds if you travel then.

What to do when you arrive

Phu Quoc is a beach island. Head to Long Beach to unwind, explore Phu Quoc Market for local food, or visit Dinh Cau for harbour views. Diving and snorkelling trips to nearby islands are popular. The airport connects easily to the island centre.
