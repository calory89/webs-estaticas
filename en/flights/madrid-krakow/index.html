Best time to visit

Krakow is beautiful year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and more moderate prices than peak summer. Winters are cold but atmospheric, especially if you're after festive charm. Avoid July and August if you want to fly for less.

What to do on arrival

Krakow airport is 20 km from the city centre. The city deserves at least 3–4 days: Rynek Główny (Main Square) is unmissable, Wawel Castle dominates the skyline, and history museums are profound and moving. Try pierniki (traditional pastries) and sample local beer. The surrounding areas offer interesting visits if you have more time.
