Best time to visit Vienna

Vienna is stunning year-round. Winter (December–February) is magical if you like Christmas markets and festive vibes, though it's peak season. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild weather and fewer crowds. Summer is warm and perfect for Danube strolls and palace visits.

What to do when you arrive

Vienna deserves 3–4 days minimum. Must-see: Schönbrunn Palace, St. Stephen's Cathedral, Hofburg Palace, and a Danube river cruise. Viennese cuisine is excellent (schnitzel, strudel, Viennese coffee). Vienna International Airport (VIE) connects well to the city centre by regional train or coach.
