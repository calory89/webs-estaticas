Best time to travel

Sal Island enjoys tropical weather year-round, but January to May offers milder temperatures and less rainfall. December is also a solid choice if you want to escape the Spanish cold without waiting for summer. These periods see moderate demand, so advance booking remains essential.

What to do when you arrive at Sal Island

Sal Island boasts white-sand beaches and crystal-clear waters. Santa Maria is the main tourist hub, perfect for shops and nightlife. Espargos, the capital, keeps a more authentic local feel. Windsurfers and kite surfers will find excellent conditions. The international airport connects easily to the north of the island; renting a car or taking a taxi are practical options.
