Price and when to book

The lowest price recorded is €563, though typical fares range between €634 and €747 for a return ticket. As a general rule, book as early as possible: this route rewards advance planning by several months. Check back in October and November if your dates are flexible.

Airline and stopovers

Oman Air operates this route with 2 stopovers. There are no direct flights Singapore–Bologna, so prepare for transfers. The stopovers add time to your journey, but Oman Air has a solid reputation for punctuality and service on long-haul connections.

Best time to visit

Bologna shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), when the weather is pleasant and crowds smaller than summer. Winter is cheaper but grey. If your trip falls in November, like the example, take advantage of the mild early-month temperatures.

What to do when you arrive

Bologna is Italy's gastronomic capital: try tortellini, ragù and local mortadella. Walk through Piazza Maggiore, climb Torre Asinelli for city views, and explore the medieval arcades. The Museum of the History of Bologna and Basilica di San Petronio are worth your time.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Singapore to Bologna?

No. Oman Air operates this route with 2 mandatory stopovers. It's the available option for this connection.

What's the average price for a flight to Bologna from Singapore?

The median is €634 return. Prices range from €563 (lowest) to €747 depending on date and availability.

Which airline flies Singapore to Bologna?

What can I see and do in Bologna?

Bologna offers medieval architecture, famous arcades, excellent food (tortellini, Bolognese ragù), Piazza Maggiore and historical museums. It's perfect for mixing culture and gastronomy.
