Best time to visit

Riga is beautiful year-round, but summer (June to August) offers long days and pleasant temperatures, though prices tend to rise. If you're budget-conscious and don't mind the cold, winter is quieter. Spring and autumn strike the perfect balance: decent weather and fewer tourists.

What to do when you arrive

Riga airport is about 13 km from the city centre. Once there, Old Town (Vecrīga) is unmissable: wander medieval streets, visit Riga Cathedral and try Latvian cuisine. Climb St Peter's Church tower for panoramic views. Museums, parks and the nearby Daugava river round out a short but rewarding visit.
