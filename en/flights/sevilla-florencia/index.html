Best time to visit

Florence shines in autumn (September-November) and spring (April-May), when the weather is pleasant and crowds are smaller than in summer. Winters are short and mild, perfect for exploring museums without the queues. Skip July and August if you want peace and quiet.

What to do when you arrive

Florence airport (Peretola) is 4 km from the city centre. Head straight to the Cathedral of Santa Maria del Fiore; climb Brunelleschi's Dome if you're up for it. The Uffizi Gallery is essential—book your ticket in advance. Walk across the Ponte Vecchio at sunset, explore San Marcos and its museum, and don't miss the views from Piazzale Michelangelo.
