Best time to visit

Bruges is stunning in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with pleasant temperatures and less rain. Winter months are cold but the city's medieval charm stands out even more. Avoid July and August if you dislike crowds. Book your flight two to three months in advance for the best prices.

What to do when you arrive

Bruges is a medieval gem: canals, the Grote Markt, St. Blood Cathedral and Flanders museums await. Wander the cobbled streets, try Belgian beer and local cuisine. The city centre is close to Brussels-Zaventem airport (take the train to Bruges, 15 minutes). Bruges is perfect for a long weekend.
