Best time to travel to Sri Lanka

The island has two monsoons: avoid May-September on the west coast and April-September on the east coast. The driest and most stable months are December-March (west coast) and May-September (east coast). Price depends on availability, not season, so compare options year-round.

What to do upon arrival in Colombo

You'll land at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB), 30 km from Colombo. The city offers museums, Buddhist temples, nearby beaches and lively markets. Easy access to central highlands (Kandy, Nuwara Eliya), southern beaches (Mirissa, Unawatuna) and UNESCO World Heritage sites.
