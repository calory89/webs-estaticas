Best time to visit

Berlin is incredible year-round, but December brings a festive atmosphere with Christmas markets. For good weather and peak tourism, aim for May to September. Shoulder months (April, October) often have more reasonable prices.

What to do on arrival

Berlin is an essential cultural capital: Brandenburg Gate, Pergamon Museum, Reichstag, alternative neighbourhoods like Kreuzberg. Eat at street food markets, stroll through Mauerpark, and discover history at every corner. Note that restaurant and accommodation prices are quite reasonable compared to other European capitals.
