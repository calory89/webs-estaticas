Best time to visit

Lisbon is pleasant most of the year. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer good weather and fewer crowds than July–August. Winter is cheaper but wetter. Choose based on your budget and weather preferences.

What to do when you arrive

Barrio Alto is essential: steep lanes, viewpoints, and restaurants. Take the historic tram 28. Visit Mosteiro dos Jerónimos and Museu de Arte Antiga. Grab pastéis de nata from any bakery. Nearby coastal towns (Cascais, Sintra) make perfect day trips from the city.
