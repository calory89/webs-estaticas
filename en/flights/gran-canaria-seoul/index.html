Best time to visit

Seoul is excellent in spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November), when the weather is mild and rainfall is lower. Winters are cold but dry, and summers are hot and humid. These pleasant periods usually coincide with higher demand, so if you're travelling then, book even further ahead.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Incheon International Airport (ICN), about 50 km from Seoul's centre. The city offers historic palaces like Gyeongbokgung, modern districts like Gangnam, and traditional markets such as Myeongdong. Don't miss Korean cuisine, Buddhist temples, or the Dongdaemun Design Plaza. Public transport is excellent and very affordable.
