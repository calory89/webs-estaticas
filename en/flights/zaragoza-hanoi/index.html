Best time to visit

Vietnam is pleasant from October to April, when temperatures are moderate and rainfall is lower. These months typically see higher demand and prices. May to September are more humid and hot, but you may find cheaper fares if offers arise. Choose based on your climate tolerance and budget.

What to do in Hanoi and nearby

Hanoi, the capital, features the Old Quarter with centuries-old temples, the Tran Quoc Pagoda and Hoan Kiem Lake. Halong Bay, a UNESCO World Heritage site, is within reach. You can also explore Sapa in the north for rice terrace landscapes, or travel south to Hoi An and Ho Chi Minh City. Local transport is affordable and accessible.
