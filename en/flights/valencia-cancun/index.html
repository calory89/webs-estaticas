Best time to travel

Cancún is a year-round destination. The dry season runs from November to April with ideal weather. July and August are rainier and pricier. December and January tend to be expensive due to holiday season. Consider shoulder months like May or September for better value and pleasant climate.

What to do when you arrive

Cancún is the gateway to the Riviera Maya. From the airport you have access to beaches, cenotes, Mayan ruins and the Coral Reef. Playa del Carmen, Tulum and Xel-Há are just hours away. The Cancún hotel zone offers restaurants, shops and nightlife. Rent a car or book local tours to get around.
