Best time to visit

Luxembourg shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with mild weather and lighter crowds. Winter is cold and grey, but if you want solitude and potentially lower prices, it's worth considering. Summer brings more tourists and possibly higher fares. The search was run on 23 October; track winter dates for possible discounts.

What to do when you arrive

Luxembourg is small but packed with culture. Explore the Old Town (UNESCO heritage), medieval fortifications, and Moselle vineyards. The airport is well connected by bus and train to the city centre, just 15 minutes away. Currency is the euro; French is the main language, with German and Luxembourgish spoken too. A week gives you time to see it properly.
