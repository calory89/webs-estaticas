Best time to visit

Sal Island enjoys a tropical dry climate year-round. December to February brings pleasant temperatures (25–27°C) and lower rainfall. July to October is hotter and more humid. Plan according to your climate preference, but remember peak season (Christmas, February) can push prices up.

What to do when you arrive

Sal Island is famous for pristine beaches: Santa Maria is ideal for families, while Pedra Lume offers stunning views. Don't miss the Parque Natural de Isleta de Aves. Espargos is the main town. Crystal-clear waters are perfect for diving and snorkelling, and the vibe is relaxed and laid-back.
