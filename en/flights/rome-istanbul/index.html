Best time to travel

Istanbul is worth visiting year-round. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) are ideal: mild weather, fewer crowds than summer, and more accessible prices. Winter can also surprise you with good discounts, though daylight hours are shorter.

What to do when you arrive

Istanbul Airport (IST) drops you right at the city's doorstep. Spend time at the Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia, and Topkapi Palace. Wander through the Grand Bazaar, cruise the Golden Horn, and enjoy Turkish cuisine at every corner. Airport transfers to the city centre are fast and affordable.
