Best time to visit

Vienna is lovely year-round, but December brings festive Christmas markets. May to September offer fine weather and are perfect for spring or summer travel. Check prices across different dates: shoulder seasons (January–February, November) often surprise with deals. Avoid peak summer if you prefer fewer crowds.

What to do in Vienna

Austria's capital is essential for music and architecture lovers. Visit Schönbrunn Palace, St. Stephen's Cathedral and the State Opera House. Museums like the Kunsthistorisches and the Natural History Museum are worth hours. Enjoy the Danube ambiance, have sachertorte in a historic café and catch a classical concert. Public transport is excellent and the international airport is about 20 km from the city centre.
