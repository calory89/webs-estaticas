Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €184. The average sits around €202, so expect to pay thereabouts. Book 2–3 months ahead for better rates. Avoid school holidays and public holiday periods if you're chasing the cheapest fares.

Airline and stopovers

Vueling operates this route with 2 stopovers. It's not a direct flight, but that's typical for Ibiza–Salzburg connections. Check your layover time when booking: aim for at least 1.5 hours between flights to avoid stress and baggage issues.

Best time to visit

Salzburg shines in winter (Christmas markets and festive charm) or spring–summer (pleasant weather and calm strolling). July and August are pricier and busy. February and March usually offer tighter fares. Pick based on what appeals: snow and markets, or sunshine and hiking.

What to do when you arrive

Salzburg is compact and beautiful: Old Town, Hohensalzburg fortress, baroque churches, Mozart's birthplace. The train station is close to the centre, so you can get around easily without a car. December brings festive Christmas markets. The surrounding Salzburg region offers hiking and picture-postcard villages.

FAQ

Is it a direct flight from Ibiza to Salzburg?

No, there are 2 stopovers. Not a direct route, but standard for this connection. Allow enough time between connections when you buy your ticket.

What should I see in Salzburg in a few days?

You can cover the city in 2–3 days: Old Town, Hohensalzburg fortress, cathedral, Christmas markets (if visiting in winter). Close by are Alpine scenery and villages like Hallein. Public transport works well—you don't need a car.
