Best time to visit

Kuala Lumpur is pleasant year-round, but avoid the monsoon rains from September to November. December to February offers the best weather: less humid and less rain. Although November (your sample flight date) is still workable, expect higher rainfall. European spring (March–May) is also excellent. Travelling June–August is pricier but drier.

What to do when you arrive

Malaysia's capital awaits with the Petronas Towers, the country's most iconic skyline. Explore the Old Quarter (Chinatown), Batu Caves, and Perdana Park. Local transport is cheap and efficient. From KUL International Airport to the city centre, take a direct bus or train. Eat at night markets and try local food at rock-bottom prices. With €454 for your flight, you have budget to enjoy.
