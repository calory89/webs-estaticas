Best time to travel

Vilna is beautiful year-round, but winter (December to February) has a special charm with snow and festive lights if you travel in January. Spring and autumn typically offer better prices than summer. Check flexible dates to match your budget and schedule.

What to do on arrival

Vilna deserves 3–4 days minimum. The medieval Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage site: cobbled streets, Baroque churches, historic walls. Don't miss the Gate of Dawn, the Cathedral, the Jewish quarter, and views from Gediminas Tower. Lithuanian food is affordable and authentic; museum entry is budget-friendly too.
