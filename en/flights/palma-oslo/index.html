Ryanair connects Palma to Oslo for €164 return. The median price hovers around €165, so now is the time to book. Travel to Norway's capital with one stop and explore its fjords and urban charm.

Price and when to buy

Base fare starts at €164 return. Flights checked on 22 October 2026 ranged from €164 to €166. Ryanair is cheaper the sooner you book, so don't miss this rate. Keep in mind Ryanair charges for oversized cabin bags and checked luggage; review their terms before confirming.

Airline and layovers

Ryanair operates this route with one stop on the outbound journey. It's not a direct flight, but the price makes up for it. Outbound departs Palma at 06:10 and return leaves Oslo at 12:20. Check your confirmation for the layover location and connection time.

Best time to travel

Norway is stunning in winter (northern lights, snow) and summer (midnight sun, hiking). Prices vary by season. Booking in advance is key, whenever you plan to go. Avoid European peak periods if you're chasing lower fares.

What to do when you arrive

Oslo offers the City Hall, the Royal Palace and museums like the Munch. Stroll through Grünerløkka, explore the harbour and nearby fjords. Gardermoen Airport is 20 km from the city centre; take the train or bus to get in quickly. Try Norwegian cod and local beer at any bar.

FAQ

Which airline flies from Palma to Oslo?

What to see in Oslo in 3 days?

City Hall, the Opera House, the Royal Palace, the Munch Museum, Grünerløkka and strolls along Oslofjord. If you have time, day trips to nearby fjords or ski mountains. Eat at local markets and try smoked salmon.
