Best time to travel

Istanbul is pleasant almost year-round, but May, June, September and October offer ideal temperatures without peak-season crowds. For the lowest prices, November through March tend to be cheaper, though winters are colder. Avoid July and August if you don't want crowds and higher fares.

What to do when you arrive

Istanbul is a must: Hagia Sophia, Blue Mosque, Topkapi Palace, the Grand Bazaar. The airport (IST) is well-connected by public transport to the city centre. Spend at least 3-4 days exploring the main attractions. Local cuisine and Bosphorus cruises are experiences you shouldn't miss.
