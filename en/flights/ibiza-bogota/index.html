What to do when you arrive in Bogotá

Bogotá is a high-altitude capital (2,640 m) packed with things to see: the Gold Museum, La Candelaria and its colonial streets, Monserrate with panoramic views, and neighbourhoods like Usaquén and La Macarena buzzing with energy. Eat well, explore art galleries, and give yourself a few days to adjust to the altitude. El Dorado airport sits 12 km from the city centre.
