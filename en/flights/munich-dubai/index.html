Price and when to book

The base price is €333 return. Samples checked on 23 October 2026 show fares between €333 and €342. The earlier you book from today, the better: budget airlines raise prices as departure approaches. Look for dates outside public holidays and school breaks for better availability.

Airline and stopovers

You'll travel with Pegasus. The route includes 1 stopover. The outbound flight departs Munich on 9 December at 14:40, and the return flight leaves Dubai on 13 December at 11:10. Check your booking confirmation for the stopover location and total journey time.

Best time to visit

Dubai offers pleasant weather from October to April. Summer months (June to August) are very hot. December, as in this example, is ideal: mild temperatures, pleasant beaches and no rain. Avoid Ramadan if you prefer standard opening hours for restaurants and shops.

What to do when you arrive

The Burj Khalifa is a must; go up to the observation deck for city views. Jumeirah beaches and Palm Jumeirah are perfect for relaxing. Don't miss the historic Al Fahidi district, traditional souks and the Dubai Museum. The desert offers sunset safaris. Visit Dubai Mall and stroll past the Burj Khalifa fountain light show at night.
