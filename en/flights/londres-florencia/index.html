The lowest price is €59 return; we've seen fares up to €115. Book as far ahead as you can: this route works best when you secure your ticket early. Vueling typically offers better prices to those who book 2–3 months in advance. Avoid the final days before departure.

Airline and flight details

Best time to travel

December is a good window if you're after low fares outside summer. Spring (April–May) and early autumn (September–October) also offer a solid mix of weather and prices. Skip July and August if you want to save: peak season hikes fares. Check your favourite search engine regularly to catch spot deals.

What to do in Florence

You'll land at Florence-Peretola Airport, 4 km from the city centre. The Uffizi Gallery, the Cathedral, the Ponte Vecchio and Renaissance museums are must-sees. Walk the historic centre, try Tuscan food and climb the Duomo at sunset. With extra days, venture to the Chianti hills or Siena.
