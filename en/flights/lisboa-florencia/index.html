Best time to travel

Florence is stunning year-round, but autumn (September–October) and spring (April–May) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter (December–February) is cool and wet, but fewer tourists mean quieter museums. Steer clear of Christmas and New Year weeks if you're hunting low prices. Book well ahead regardless of season: date flexibility is your strongest weapon.

What to do in Florence

The Uffizi Gallery, the Cathedral and Duomo are essential. Cross the Ponte Vecchio, climb Giotto's bell tower, visit the Accademia Gallery (home to Michelangelo's David). Explore the Oltrarno neighbourhood, try bistecca alla fiorentina in any trattoria. Florence airport (Peretola) is your arrival point, well connected to the city centre by bus (10–15 minutes).
