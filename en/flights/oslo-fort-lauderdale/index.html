Best time to visit

Fort Lauderdale has tropical weather: northern winter (December–March) is dry and pleasant, perfect for beaches. North American summer (June–August) is hotter and humid, with hurricane risk. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer good balance. Prices vary by tourism demand, so compare several dates.

What to do on arrival at Fort Lauderdale

FLL is a gateway to Florida's coast. Visit Fort Lauderdale and Hollywood Beach, Sawgrass Mills shopping mall, or take a day trip to Miami (30 km). The Everglades and Key West are popular for multi-day trips. The airport is 30 km from the centre; rent a car or use public transport.
