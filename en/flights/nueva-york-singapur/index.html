Best time to visit

Singapore is a tropical destination year-round. February to April and July to August tend to be peak season with higher prices. Consider travelling between November and January or May to June for better deals and fewer crowds at major attractions.

What to do when you arrive

Singapore offers Marina Bay Sands with 360-degree views, the Gardens by the Bay with illuminated giant trees, Chinatown, temples of all faiths and a world-class airport. It's a compact city-state: public transport is efficient and you can see the essentials in a few days. Try street food and explore night markets.
