Quick answer: The cheapest return flight from Seville to Marrakesh we found costs 40 € (search on 18 Nov 2026, Kiwi.com), 20% below the usual 50 €. It is a nonstop flight with Ryanair.

Direct flights from Seville to Marrakech start at €40 return with Ryanair. The median price hovers around €50, so booking in advance lands you good deals. Less than 400 km and a single flight: one of the most affordable routes from southern Spain.

Price and when to book

Prices range from €40 to €98 return depending on date. The earlier you book, the better price you'll get. This route tends to be cheaper in low season (autumn and winter, excluding Christmas holidays). Check weekly: Ryanair updates fares constantly and last-minute offers pop up occasionally.

Airline and connections

Ryanair operates this route with direct flights, no connections. The journey is short (under 1 hour of flight time), making it efficient both for time and cost. Travel with hand luggage only to save more, or add checked baggage if needed.

Best time to visit

Marrakech is pleasant most of the year, but the best months are October, November, March and April: mild weather without summer's extreme heat or winter chill. Avoid July and August if you don't handle dry heat well (40°C+). December and January can see price hikes due to Christmas and New Year.

What to do when you arrive

Menara airport is 6 km from the centre. From there, reach the Medina (old town), Jemaa el-Fnaa square with its markets and street shows, or Majorelle Gardens. Marrakech is compact: you can explore on foot or by local taxi. Two or three days are enough for the essentials.

FAQ

What is the cheapest price I've seen on this route?

The lowest price recorded is €40 return. This happens at certain times, especially when Ryanair launches low-season offers. The median sits at €50, so that range is realistic for most bookings.

Is it a direct flight Seville-Marrakech?

Yes, it's direct with no stops. Ryanair operates this route as a single connection with no intermediate stops.

Which airline flies Seville to Marrakech?

Ryanair is the operator of this route. It's a low-cost airline, so check what's included in your fare (baggage, seat, food) before you buy.

How many days do I need to see Marrakech?

Two full days are enough for the essentials: the Medina, Jemaa el-Fnaa, souks and main gardens. With three days you can add the Atlas or nearby villages. It all depends on your pace, but the city isn't very large.
