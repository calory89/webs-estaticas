Prices range from €45 to €72 depending on your dates. Book early: fares climb as departure approaches. Check regularly because Ryanair updates offers constantly. Avoid last-minute purchases if you want to keep the price down.

Airline and flight type

Ryanair is your only option on this route. The flight is direct with no stopovers, so you'll depart Seville and arrive straight into Marrakech. Check carefully what your fare includes: some tickets don't cover checked baggage or seat selection.

Best time to visit

Marrakech is pleasant year-round, but October to April is ideal: mild temperatures and no extreme heat. Skip July and August if you can't handle intense heat (often above 35°C). Check the flight calendar to find dates that match your budget.

What to do when you arrive

Marrakech Menara airport is 6 km from the city centre. You'll find buses, taxis or car rental available. Once in the city, the Medina (old town) is a must: squares, souks and traditional palaces. Also worth visiting are the Koutoubia mosque, Bahia Palace and day trips to the nearby Atlas Mountains or desert.

What's worth seeing in Marrakech?

The Medina is the city's heart with its souks, Jemaa el-Fnaa square and palaces like Bahia. The Koutoubia is the most important mosque. With more time, the Atlas Mountains and nearby desert are perfect for day trips.
