Best time to travel

Miami is sunny year-round, but avoid June to October due to hurricane season. December to April is peak season: warm weather, perfect sea conditions, and lower storm risk. January and February are priciest, so aim for early December or late April to save money. Prices tend to drop outside peak holiday periods.

What to do when you arrive

Miami offers white-sand beaches, street art in Wynwood, nightlife in South Beach, and shopping in the Golden Mile. Don't miss the Everglades if you want nature. The Pérez Art Museum and the Cuban quarter of Little Havana are worth visiting. Rent a car to explore freely or use local public transport. Miami airport (MIA) is well-connected to the city.
