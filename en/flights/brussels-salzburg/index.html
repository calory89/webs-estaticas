Best time to visit

Salzburg shines in winter with its world-famous Christmas markets, especially November to December. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Summer months are the priciest and most crowded.

What to do when you arrive

Salzburg is Mozart's birthplace and features a Unesco-listed old town. Visit Hohensalzburg Fortress, the Cathedral, and Mirabell and Helbrunn palaces. In winter, don't miss the Christmas markets. The Alps are within easy reach for hiking or skiing.

What to see and do in Salzburg?

Salzburg blends musical heritage, baroque architecture, and alpine nature. Explore its old town (Unesco site), visit Hohensalzburg Fortress, the Cathedral, Mirabell and Helbrunn palaces, and enjoy proximity to the Alps. In winter, the Christmas markets are a must-see.
