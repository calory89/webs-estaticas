Price and when to book

The prices you see (€67, €74, €100) shift based on your travel dates and how far ahead you book. Reserve 4–6 weeks in advance to nail cheaper fares. Prices climb during peak season (July, August, bank holidays) and drop in winter, January and early spring. Check back regularly if you've got a date in mind.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route with direct flights Berlin to London—no intermediate stops. You'll depart Berlin at 10:10 (per the example) and land in London the same day. These rock-bottom prices don't usually include checked baggage; any suitcase costs extra.

Best time to visit

London's worth visiting year-round, but winter (January to March) and autumn (September to early November) often have cheaper flight fares. Want sunshine and crowds? Summer's your bet, though pricier. For value and fewer tourists, aim for May or early June, or late August.

What to do when you arrive

London has four main airports; Ryanair typically lands at Stansted, Luton or Gatwick depending on the flight. All have bus, train or taxi links to central London. Once there: Big Ben, Tower Bridge, free museums (Natural History, British Museum), Hyde Park, Portobello and Camden markets. Buy an Oyster Card for public transport.
