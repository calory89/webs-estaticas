China Southern Airlines connects Sydney to Tenerife from €977 return. The median price is €1,313, so you have budget options if you're flexible with dates. The journey includes 2 stops on the outbound and offers reasonable duration for this long-haul route.

Price and when to book

The lowest price found is €977, but the median sits at €1,313, giving you a realistic expectation. Fares go up to €1,509 depending on dates. For this long route, booking 2–3 weeks ahead typically works well. Compare prices across different travel days: they fluctuate significantly on intercontinental routes.

Airline and stops

China Southern Airlines operates this route. Note that the outbound flight includes 2 stops, so it's not a direct flight. This adds to total journey time, but it's standard for Australia–Canaries connections. Check the stopover cities when you book to choose schedules that suit you.

Best time to visit

Tenerife enjoys a subtropical climate year-round. Autumn and winter (October to March) see fewer tourists and milder temperatures if you're coming from Australian heat. Spring offers sunshine without crowds. Avoid August if you prefer quieter beaches. The sample date is November, a solid choice.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in the north of the island (Tenerife Norte). Mount Teide is essential: take the cable car up Spain's highest volcano. Beaches like Los Cristianos and Playa de las Américas in the south are popular. Explore colonial towns such as La Orotava and La Laguna. Local food (papas arrugadas, mojo sauce) is excellent. Hire a car for freedom to explore.
