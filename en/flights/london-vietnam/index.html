The base price for this route is €469 return. Prices we've seen range from €469 to €565 depending on travel dates. Generally, booking further in advance gives you better rates. Avoid searching and purchasing close to UK holiday periods, when demand increases.

Airline and stopovers

Air China operates this route with 1 stopover on the outbound journey. It's not a direct flight, but it's a competitive option price-wise. The stopover is common on routes between the UK and Vietnam, so factor in extra time when planning your travel itinerary.

Best time to travel

Vietnam has a tropical climate. The best time to visit is October to April, with less heat and rainfall. November to January are particularly pleasant months. If you travel during this period, plan further ahead as demand is higher and prices may increase.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

You'll land at Noi Bai International Airport (HAN), about 25 km from Hanoi city centre. The city offers Ha Long Bay (UNESCO World Heritage), ancient temples, local markets and authentic Vietnamese cuisine. From Hanoi you can easily reach Ho Chi Minh City, Sa Pa or the coast.
