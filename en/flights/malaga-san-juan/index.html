Best time to visit

San Juan and Puerto Rico are destinations year-round. November to April is dry season with higher prices (as you see here). May to October sees more rain and potential hurricanes, but fares drop. Want stable weather and don't mind paying more? Go November-March. Flexible on climate? Other months can be cheaper.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport (SJU), about 15 km from San Juan. Old San Juan is a must: walls, colours, cafés and nearby beaches. El Yunque (rainforest), Camuy Caves and Fajardo beaches are short excursions. Puerto Rico has Caribbean rhythm, creole food and nightlife. Four to five days covers the essentials.
