Price and when to book your flight

Fares for this route range from €608 to €617 based on the sample checked on 22 October 2026. The key to saving is booking as early as possible: long-haul international flights tend to rise closer to departure. Check offers regularly, as Iberia Airlines adjusts its prices according to availability.

Best time to travel

Miami is a year-round destination, but the dry season (October to April) is ideal: perfect weather, fewer hurricanes and more stable prices. If you travel in December as in the example, you'll encounter Christmas tourism, but the weather is splendid. Avoid summer if you want to escape extreme heat and humidity.

What to do when you arrive in Miami

You'll land at Miami International Airport (MIA). The city offers luxury beaches in South Beach, colourful Wynwood with its murals, Little Havana for Cuban culture, and cruise options to the Caribbean. Don't miss Miami Beach's Art Deco Historic District or the bars and restaurants of Brickell.

FAQ

What is the cheapest price to fly from Palma to Miami?

The lowest fare recorded is €608 return with Iberia Airlines. Keep in mind this can vary depending on dates and availability, but this is the base rate we've found.

Is the flight from Palma to Miami direct?

No, Iberia Airlines offers this route with 1 stopover. Although it's not direct, it is the most economical option available from Palma to Miami.

When should I book to find the best prices?

The sooner you book, the better. For long-haul international flights, it's ideal to purchase 2-3 months in advance. Prices typically rise as departure approaches, especially during school holidays or public holidays.

What can I see in Miami besides beaches?

Miami has much more to offer: Wynwood neighbourhood with street art, Little Havana with Cuban gastronomy, Miami Art Museum, Miami Beach's Art Deco District, and Caribbean cruise options. It's a vibrant city with comprehensive cultural and leisure offerings.
