Best time to visit

Bali is a tropical destination that welcomes tourists year-round. The dry season (April to October) is ideal for avoiding heavy rainfall. If you're looking for fewer crowds and potentially lower prices during certain periods, travel outside European school holidays. Check the calendar well ahead to pick your dates.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS), about 13 km from the tourist areas. Bali offers volcanic sand beaches, temples like Tanah Lot and Besakih, rice terraces in Ubud, and vibrant nightlife in Seminyak and Kuta. Arrange transport in advance or use reliable taxi apps. Local food is affordable and delicious.
