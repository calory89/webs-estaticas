Airline and connections

You'll travel with Swiss International Air Lines, Switzerland's national carrier. This route is non-stop from Palma to Zurich with no connections, saving you time and hassle. Evening departure (around 21:00) and morning return (11:30), perfect for maximising your time in the destination.

Best time to visit

Zurich is worth visiting year-round, but each season has its appeal. Winter (December–March) offers Alpine skiing and snowy landscapes. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal for hiking and enjoying the city without crowds. Summer is peak season. Flight prices tend to stay similar; what varies is accommodation and activity pricing.

What to do when you arrive

Zurich Airport (ZRH) is 12 km from the city centre, well connected by train (20 minutes). Don't miss the old town, Zurich Lake, and museums. For mountains, the Jungfrau or Rigi are reachable in a few hours. If you have extra time, Bern or Lucerne are 1–2 hours by train. Try fondue and Swiss chocolate.
