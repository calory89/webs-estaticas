Prices range between €712 and €719 for the full journey. Book at least 3–4 weeks ahead to secure the best fares. The sooner you lock in your ticket, the fewer nasty surprises on the final price.

Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Etihad Airways. Your outbound flight includes 1 stopover, so it's not direct, but it's a competitive option from Amsterdam. Expect a connection on the itinerary, typically swift with Etihad.

Best time to visit

Zanzibar is stunning year-round, but June to October offers more comfortable temperatures and lower humidity. Avoid April and May if you'd rather skip the heaviest rains. Any time works if you embrace tropical weather.

What to see and do in Zanzibar

Don't miss Stone Town, its Old Town of cobbled streets and carved Zanzibari doors. Nungwi and Kendwa beaches up north are perfect for diving and swimming. Sample local spices at the markets and visit the clove plantations that made the island famous.
