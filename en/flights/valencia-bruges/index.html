Best time to travel

Bruges is beautiful year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Prices tend to be lower outside July and August. January and February see fewer tourists, though it's bitterly cold. Avoid Christmas dates if you're hunting for deals.

What to do in Bruges

Bruges is a fairytale medieval city. Stroll along its canals, visit the Basilica of the Holy Blood, climb Belfort Tower for panoramic views, and sample Belgium's finest chocolate. The Grote Markt (Main Square) is the tourist heart. Brussels Airport is 90 km away; take a direct train to Bruges in roughly 50 minutes. Don't miss Flemish art museums and traditional Belgian beer halls.
