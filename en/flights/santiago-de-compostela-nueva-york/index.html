Best time to visit

New York is attractive year-round, but spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer the best weather without extreme summer crowds. Winter is cold but iconic (Christmas, New Year's). Avoid July-August if you're after fewer tourists. Prices tend to fluctuate more by demand than by season, so your ideal date depends on your schedule.

What to do when you arrive in New York

You'll land at one of New York's airports (JFK, LaGuardia or Newark). From there, the metro or express bus connects you to Manhattan in 30-60 minutes. Must-sees: Times Square, Central Park, the Statue of Liberty, Brooklyn Bridge. World-class museums (MoMA, Natural History), neighbourhoods like SoHo and Lower Manhattan. Book early and grab transport passes to save money.
