Cheap flights from Vienna to Porto: from €124

Ryanair connects Vienna to Porto on direct flights with fares around €124–€130 return. It's the cheapest route between these two cities and great value if you travel in low season.

Price and when to book

The median price sits at €130 for a round-trip ticket. Cheapest fares start from €124, though we've also seen quotes up to €142. Book at least 4–6 weeks ahead: Ryanair adjusts prices by demand. Check their fare calendar and set up alerts so you don't miss flash deals.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route non-stop. Direct flight from Vienna to Porto saves time and hassle. It's a budget carrier, so checked baggage, seat selection and meals are extras. Pack light for cabin only and watch out for surprise fees at the desk.

Best time to visit

Porto is pleasant most of the year, but October–November and February–March offer the best price-to-weather ratio. Summer months (June–August) are pricier. Winter (December–January) is cold but fewer tourists. Check fares shortly before travel to pick your ideal date.

What to do in Porto

Ribeira is a must: wander cobbled streets and catch Douro views. Taste Port wine in a Vila Nova de Gaia lodge. Climb Clérigos Tower for city panoramas. Livraria Lello bookshop is iconic but charges entry. The airport sits 10 km north of the centre; bus, taxi or car rental are all handy.

FAQ

What is the cheapest fare from Vienna to Porto?

We've found tickets from €124 return with Ryanair. The median hovers around €130. Prices vary by date.

Is the Vienna-Porto flight direct?

Yes, Ryanair flies it non-stop. You depart Vienna and arrive straight in Porto.

Which airline flies Vienna to Porto?

Ryanair operates this route. Remember it's low-cost: you'll pay extra for checked bags, seat assignment and add-ons.

Where can I stay in Porto on a budget?

Ribeira and Miragaia have cheap hostels and 2–3-star hotels. Further out in Cedofeita or Massarelos you'll find cheaper options. Booking and Airbnb are your best bets.
