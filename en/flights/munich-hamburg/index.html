Best time to visit

Hamburg is worth visiting any time of year. Summer (June-August) sees the most tourists, so expect crowds. March to May and September to October have fewer visitors and pleasant weather. If you want to save money, travel in winter, though temperatures will be low.

What to do when you arrive

Hamburg is Germany's second-largest city. Walk the canals in Speicherstadt, visit the port (one of Europe's busiest), stroll the Reeperbahn (nightlife district), and climb the St. Michael's Church. Hamburg Airport (HAM) is about 9 km from the city centre; take the train or a taxi to reach the city.
