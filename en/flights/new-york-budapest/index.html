Best time to visit

Budapest shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Summer is peak season with more tourists and potentially higher prices. Winter is cold but perfect if you want to see Budapest snow-covered and enjoy the thermal baths without the masses.

What to do when you arrive

Budapest awaits with the Danube separating Buda and Pest. Must-sees: Széchenyi Thermal Baths (relax in hot springs), Buda Castle with river views, St Stephen's Basilica, and a nighttime walk past the illuminated Parliament. Eat authentic goulash anywhere and try langos (fried bread). Ferenc Liszt Airport is 16 km from the centre; bus, taxi or transfer get you there quickly.
