Price and when to book

The cheapest flight found costs €461 (return), though most are around €556. We've seen fares up to €615 depending on dates. We're already in October: December-January flights are available now. The sooner you book, the better rate you'll get. Check multiple departure days to find the best price.

Airline and stopovers

You'll travel with Shenzhen Airlines. The flight includes 1 stopover (not direct), which extends your journey but cuts the price significantly. Example departure is 2 December at 11:35, return on 14 December at 15:10. Always confirm times and stops when you book.

Best time to visit

December and January are excellent for Vietnam: dry weather and cool temperatures, especially in the north. Avoid July and August (monsoon rain). If you travel in peak season (December-February), prices rise, so book early. September and October (before Christmas) often have better offers.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Noi Bai (HAN), Hanoi's airport. The Old City has temples, traditional markets and street food. Short bus rides reach Ha Long Bay (UNESCO site), the Red River and rural villages. Rent bikes or use local buses for cheap transport. One to two weeks lets you explore without rush.
