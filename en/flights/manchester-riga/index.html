Best time to visit

Latvia shines in daylight months. June to August offer long days and pleasant weather (15-22°C), though it's peak season. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) are quieter. Winter suits those after snow-covered landscapes and Christmas markets, but it's cold: pack layers.

What to do in Riga

The capital is the main draw: UNESCO-listed Old Town, the Dome Cathedral, nearby canals. Art museums, café culture in the squares, Art Nouveau architecture everywhere. If you have extra time, Gauja national park or the Baltic coast are within reach.

What to see in Riga over a weekend?

The Old Town is unmissable: walk its medieval streets, climb the Dome Cathedral for views, eat at a Town Hall square café. Spend a day on Art Nouveau architecture (Quiet District, Elizabeta Street). If time allows, Kipsala island is lovely at sunset.
