Price and when to book

Tickets from €202 for the full journey. Median sits at €216—very competitive for this route. Book in advance: we found these prices checking in late October for mid-November travel. The sooner you book, the better your chances of securing a low fare.

Airline and direct flights

Icelandair operates this route non-stop. Your outbound flight is direct from Oslo (OSL) to Keflavík (KEF), saving time and hassle. No stopovers, no plane changes. This keeps your journey smooth and costs lean.

Best time to visit

November is low season in Iceland, which explains these bargain prices. Want northern lights? September to March is your window. Prefer daylight and outdoor activities? Summer costs more but offers long hours and better weather. These €202–€216 fares are typical of cold, quieter months.

What to do when you arrive

Keflavík is 50 km from Reykjavík. From there: the Golden Circle (geysers, waterfalls, Þingvellir national park), the Blue Lagoon, and the south coast with black-sand beaches. November means very short days but aurora potential. The interior needs 4x4 in winter. Reykjavík is compact, walkable, and food-focused.

FAQ

What's the cheapest flight Oslo to Iceland?

From €202 return with Icelandair. These prices appear in low season (like November). Booking well ahead is essential.

Does Icelandair fly direct Oslo to Keflavík?

Yes, non-stop. It's the quickest and most comfortable way from Norway.

What can I see in Iceland in 3 days?

The Golden Circle (Þingvellir, geysers, Skógafoss waterfall), the Blue Lagoon, and Reykjavík fill three days nicely. Longer stays allow south coast, Jökulsárlón, northern lights.

Which month has the best northern lights?

September to March, depending on cloud cover and solar activity. November–February are dark but also pricier and colder.
