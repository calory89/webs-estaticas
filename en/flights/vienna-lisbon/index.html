Best time to visit

Lisbon shines year-round, but spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) are unbeatable: perfect weather without summer crowds. July and August are hot and rammed with tourists. Winter is mild but wetter. Check your flight price across different dates: sometimes a week's difference means the gap between €107 and €160.

What to do when you arrive

Lisbon greets you with the Tagus, Alfama's narrow lanes and views from Mirador Senhora do Monte. Must-sees: Belém Tower, Jerónimos Monastery, Terreiro do Paço. Eat pastéis de nata on every corner, ride the yellow trams and wander Chiado's streets. Humberto Delgado Airport (LIS) is just 7 km from the city centre; metro or bus gets you downtown in 30 minutes.
