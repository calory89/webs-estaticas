Best time to visit

January is Scotland's low season: fewer tourists, winter landscapes and cheaper fares. If you like cold weather and early sunsets, it's perfect. Prefer longer days and milder conditions? Try April or May, though prices usually climb. October is also excellent, with autumn at its peak.

What to do when you arrive

Edinburgh awaits with its castle perched on the hill, the bustling Royal Mile, and art galleries around every corner. Walk the Old Town, visit Leith Docks and, if you fancy it, explore the Pentland Hills just outside the city. The city is compact and easily navigated on foot from the airport.
