Best time to visit

Buenos Aires shines in spring and autumn (September–November and March–May in the Southern Hemisphere). Skip peak summer (December–February) if you want fewer crowds, though prices don't always drop then. The example date of 11 November is solid: early spring in Buenos Aires, mild weather and less tourist overload than midsummer.

What to do when you arrive

Spend at least a week in Buenos Aires. Explore La Boca (colourful and photogenic), San Telmo (bohemian alleyways), historic San Nicolás. Eat proper Argentine beef, catch live tango, wander Palermo. Ministro Pistarini airport is about 35 km out; take the Buen Ayre bus or a remise taxi. The city comes alive late: many spots open after 22:00.
