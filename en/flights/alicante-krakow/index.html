Best time to visit

Kraków is stunning year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winters are cold but magical with snow. Check availability for your preferred climate and activities.

What to do on arrival

Kraków blends medieval history with modern life. Rynek Główny (Main Market Square) is the heart; from there, explore Wawel Castle, Gothic churches, and the Jewish Quarter. Sample local food in traditional bars. Kraków Airport (KRK) is 15 km away; take the train or bus to the centre in 20–30 minutes.
