Best time to visit

Prague is an attractive destination year-round. Autumn (September-October) and spring (April-May) offer pleasant weather without peak summer tourism. Winters are cold but the city sparkles with snow. Avoid July-August if you prefer fewer crowds and potentially lower prices.

What to do upon arrival

Prague awaits with Prague Castle, Charles Bridge, Old Town Square and the Astronomical Clock. Explore the Jewish Quarter, relax in the Thermal baths or savour Czech beer at a local brewery near the airport. The city centre is easily accessible by public transport from Václav Havel airport.
