What to do when you arrive in Vienna

Vienna awaits with imperial palaces like Schönbrunn, St. Stephen's Cathedral and classical concerts. Vienna Airport (VIE) is 16 km from the city centre; the City Airport Train reaches the station in 16 minutes. Stroll the old town, enjoy traditional cafés and walk along the Danube. It's a city made for wandering and discovering at your own pace.
