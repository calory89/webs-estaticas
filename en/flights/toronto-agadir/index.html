Flights start from €647. Prices in our sample range from €647 to €726, with most clustered around €653. Book as early as possible: the sooner you lock in your ticket, the fewer surprises. Check regularly for sudden price drops.

Airline and stopovers

Porter Airlines operates this route with 2 stopovers on the outbound leg. It's not a direct flight, but it gives you flexible scheduling and often more competitive fares. Factor in total journey time when planning your departure.

Best time to visit

Agadir is a beach and sunshine destination. Avoid the wettest months and pick pleasant temperatures: spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) are ideal. Prices tend to spike during summer high season, so travelling outside June, July and August usually costs less.

What to do when you arrive

Agadir is known for its seafront promenade, golden-sand beach and nearly year-round sunshine. Explore the harbour, enjoy fresh seafood in local restaurants, and if you fancy adventure, book excursions to the Sahara or nearby Berber villages. Agadir Al Massira Airport is 30 km from the city centre.
