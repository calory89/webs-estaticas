Best time to visit

Denmark enjoys pleasant summers (June to August) with very long days, though it's also peak season and prices climb. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) strike a good balance between weather and crowds. Winter (November-February) is colder and darker, but if you don't mind the cold, fares tend to drop. Travel to suit your climate preference, but remember: book early regardless of season.

What to do when you arrive in Copenhagen

You'll arrive at Copenhagen Airport (CPH), well connected to the centre. The city is perfect for cycling, visiting Tivoli, strolling round Nyhavn, or exploring neighbourhoods like Vesterbro. With more time, Denmark offers coastal towns, castles and stunning Nordic landscapes. Public transport is excellent and the city is very safe and accessible.
