Flights from Lisbon to Los Angeles start at €585 return, with a median price around €813. Royal Air Maroc is the available airline with 1 stopover on the outbound journey. Book as soon as you find a price that fits your budget.

Price and when to book

The lowest price found is €585 return, though the median hovers around €813. Prices vary by date: we've also seen fares at €856. The best approach is to keep an eye on prices over the next few weeks and book the moment you spot a fare within your budget. This route doesn't have dramatic price swings, so don't wait too long—early booking is your safest bet.

Airline and stopovers

Royal Air Maroc operates this route with 1 stopover on the outbound flight. It's not a direct flight, but the price makes up for it if saving money is your goal. The stopover also gives you a chance to stretch your legs before reaching the West Coast. Accept the connection and pocket the savings instead of paying double or more for a nonstop option.

Best time to visit

October, November, April and May offer the best weather in Los Angeles: pleasant temperatures, not too hot, fewer crowds than summer. December-January is possible if you avoid peak holiday prices. Summer (July-August) is expensive and busy. Since Lisbon enjoys good weather most of the year, choose dates based on Los Angeles rather than your origin.

What to do when you arrive

Los Angeles has beaches (Santa Mónica, Venice Beach), hiking with views (Griffith Observatory), museums and galleries, and the iconic Route 66. LAX airport is 20-30 km from the city centre; public transport is available, but hiring a car gives you more freedom to explore. Eat well, enjoy the sunshine and catch the sunset over the Pacific.
