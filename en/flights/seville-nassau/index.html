Best time to visit

Nassau and the Bahamas have a tropical climate year-round. The most pleasant season is November to April, with milder temperatures and less rain. These months coincide with peak season, so prices may be higher. Consider travelling in shoulder months like October or May for better value.

What to do when you arrive

Nassau is the capital of the Bahamas and gateway to the archipelago. Visit Paradise Island, enjoy white-sand beaches and turquoise waters, or explore the historic centre with colonial buildings. Don't miss the Exuma Cays or snorkelling and diving activities. Lynden Pindling International Airport is just a few km from Nassau centre.
