Best time to visit

Lisbon is pleasant year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (October–November) hit the sweet spot: mild weather, fewer crowds, lower fares than summer. Skip July and August if you're budget-conscious. Winter is quiet, cooler (15–18°C), but you might see rain.

What to do when you arrive

Land at Humberto Delgado (main airport). Explore Alfama's narrow cobbled streets, the Tagus waterfront, pastéis de nata in any bakery. Climb Castelo de São Jorge, ride the famous Tram 28, cross the 25 April Bridge. Day trip to Sintra (30 km) for palaces and forests. All cheap and easy by public transport.
