Best time to visit

Kuala Lumpur is tropical and rainy year-round, but the driest months are June to August and January to March. These periods tend to have higher airfares. To save on flights, aim for May, September or November when tourism is lower. Avoid local holidays and festivals if you're hunting for budget tickets.

What to do when you arrive

Kuala Lumpur is your gateway to Malaysia. The Petronas Towers are iconic, but the real value lies beyond: Petaling Jaya, Subang Jaya, and further into Cameron Highlands (tea plantations) or the islands of Langkawi. Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) has good public transport connections. From there, explore markets, Hindu temples and local cuisine.
