Fly from Palma to Helsinki from €148 return with Ryanair. The median price sits at €177, giving you room to snag a good deal. One stop is standard on this route to Finland's capital.

Price and when to book

The lowest price recorded is €148 return. Sample prices range from €148 to €177, with a median of €177. Book as early as possible: flights to Helsinki have less regular availability than other European routes, so purchasing 2-3 months ahead will give you better options. Check fares mid-week, especially Tuesdays and Wednesdays.

Airline and stopovers

You'll travel with Ryanair, connecting Palma to Helsinki via one stop. The outbound flight typically departs Palma at 10:20 and arrives around 18:00 (local Finnish time). The return flight leaves Helsinki at 23:40. One stop is the norm on this route; look for options with short layovers to minimise waiting time.

Best time to visit

Helsinki shines in summer (June to August) with long days and mild temperatures. To avoid extreme winter cold (down to -15°C), travel between May and September. December is magical for spotting northern lights, though it's bitterly cold. Prices tend to drop outside July and August. Note that winter days are very short (barely 6 hours of daylight in December).

What to do on arrival

Helsinki impresses with unique architecture and islands. Visit Uspenski Cathedral (striking red Orthodox church), the Kalevantalo neighbourhood, and the wooden cabins of Porvoo. Don't miss the archipelago and small islands reachable by ferry. The city is compact and easy to explore on foot. The airport is 20 km from the centre; direct train costs €6.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price from Palma to Helsinki?

The lowest price on record is €148 return. Fares vary by date and availability; the median is €177. Book early to catch the best deals.

Is the flight from Palma to Helsinki direct?

No, it has one stop. Ryanair operates this route with one stopover. Total journey time will be 3 to 4 hours depending on where the layover is.

Which airline flies from Palma to Helsinki?

Ryanair operates this route. It offers competitive fares, but bear in mind it's a budget airline, so paid baggage can increase your final ticket price.

What should I see in Helsinki?

Uspenski Cathedral, the White Cathedral, the bohemian Kalevantalo neighbourhood, and the archipelago are must-sees. Helsinki blends modern architecture with Finnish tradition. The train to Porvoo takes you to storybook wooden villages.
