Best time to visit Fez

Fez is beautiful year-round, but October to April is ideal: pleasant temperatures (15–20°C) without oppressive heat. Skip July and August due to scorching conditions. The medieval medina is best visited early in the morning before crowds build. Keep in mind January can be chilly in the region.

What to do in Fez

The medina of Fez is a UNESCO World Heritage site: getting lost in its alleys, tea houses and souks is a must. Visit the Museum of Arms, the Bou Inania Madrasa and the Chouara Tannery (artisan tanners). The Royal Palace and its gates are stunning. From Fez you can take day trips to Meknes, Ifrane or Moulay Idris. Local cuisine is excellent and very affordable.

Which airport do I arrive at in Fez?

You'll arrive at Fez-Saïss (FEZ), the city's only commercial airport. It's about 15 km from the medina centre. From there you can take a taxi or book a transfer in advance; it's the most convenient way to reach your accommodation.

What's the best time to visit Fez?

October to April is ideal: temperatures of 15–20°C, clear skies and far less oppressive heat than summer. January is cold but dry. July and August are very hot (35°C+). Rainfall is minimal. If you travel in winter, bring a light jacket; in spring and autumn, pack layers.
