Best time to travel

November is ideal: mild temperatures (17-21°C), low rainfall and far fewer tourists at monuments and beaches. Skip July-August if you want to avoid crowds and peak prices. October and November offer the best mix of climate, price and peace.

What to do when you arrive

Palermo is Sicily's gateway. Explore the old town (Ballarò and Vucciria markets), eat arancini and pasta con le sardine, visit Mondello. Monreale's Byzantine mosaics are 30 minutes away. Rent a car to roam freely: Cefalù and the north coast are brilliant day trips or longer stays.
