Best time to visit

Dubai shines from November to March, when heat is most bearable (25-30°C). June to September is scorching (40°C+) and less appealing. If you travel in European winter (high season), prices rise, but the experience is worth it. Outside those months, it's cheaper but harsh. Choose based on your heat tolerance and budget.

What to do when you arrive

Dubai Airport is well connected by bus, metro and taxi. In the city you'll find the Burj Khalifa, Burj Al Arab, Mall of the Emirates and beaches like Jumeirah. The desert and desert safaris are popular for a different experience. Dubai is pricey for dining and attractions, but budget options exist if you plan ahead.
