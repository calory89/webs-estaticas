Best time to visit Fez

Fez is pleasant most of the year, but autumn and winter months (October to March) offer milder temperatures for exploring the medina. Spring is also excellent. Avoid summer if you don't cope well with intense heat. Travelling off-season will help you find better prices on accommodation and activities.

What to do when you arrive in Fez

The medina of Fez is a UNESCO World Heritage site: explore its maze-like souks, visit the Royal Palace and the Karaouine Mosque. Don't miss the traditional leather tanneries. On the outskirts, Fez el Jadid (new Fez) offers a different perspective. Transport from the airport to the city centre is well connected, so arriving is straightforward.
