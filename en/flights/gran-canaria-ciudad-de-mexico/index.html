Best time to travel

Mexico City is worth visiting year-round, but October to April is most pleasant: cooler and less rain. July and August are hot and rainy. Avoid Christmas and Easter holidays if you're seeking tighter prices.

What to do on arrival

Mexico City deserves several days. The Historic Centre, Paseo de la Reforma and Coyoacán are must-sees. Don't miss the museums (Anthropology, Frida Kahlo House), Chapultepec Park or La Condesa neighbourhood. Benito Juárez International Airport is well connected to the city centre by public transport.
