Best time to visit

Maldives has two monsoons: the northwest (November to March) brings clear skies and calm seas, while the southwest (May to October) brings more rain but fewer tourists. November to February is perfect for diving and beaches, though prices rise. For budget travel, consider April, May or October as shoulder seasons with good deals and decent weather.

What to do when you arrive

Maldives is all about beaches and reef. Land at Velana International Airport (MLE) and take a speedboat or seaplane to your resort. Dive, snorkel right from your bungalow, explore Malé. Baa, Ari and Malé atolls have the best wall dives. You don't need tours—the sea is the destination itself.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for flights Lisbon to Maldives?

€826 return. That's the lowest fare we've tracked. Most offers fall between €826 and €916.

Is the Ryanair flight to Maldives direct?

No, it has 2 stops. That's why the price is so competitive. The full journey is long, but it's the most budget-friendly option from Lisbon.

When do prices to Maldives go up?

Dry season (November to March) sees peak demand and higher fares. Travel in May, September or October for better rates, though rainfall is more likely.

What is there to do in Maldives besides the beach?

Diving and snorkelling on the reefs, a walk through Malé the capital, water bungalows at resorts. Atolls have protected marine parks with rich coral life.
