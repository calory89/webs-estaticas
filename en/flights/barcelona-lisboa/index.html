Barcelona to Lisbon costs from €41 return with Vueling. Median prices hover around €52. It's a short, direct route perfect for a weekend break or quick getaway with no hassle.

Price and when to book

Prices start at €41 but most searches fall between €41 and €65. There's no particular day of the week that guarantees lower fares, so compare multiple dates. The golden rule is to book 2–3 weeks ahead for better rates. Vueling often has last-minute deals, but if you're travelling during peak season (summer or public holidays), book in advance.

Airline and stops

Vueling operates this route with direct flights, no stopovers. Remember that checked baggage costs extra with Vueling; only a small cabin bag is included free. Typical departure from Barcelona around 07:15; return from Lisbon around 22:00. Always check exact times when you book.

Best time to visit

Lisbon is pleasant almost year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer great weather without summer crowds or peak prices. Winter is cheaper but rainier. Skip July–August if you want peace and value. The Barcelona–Lisbon route runs year-round, so choose based on your climate and budget preferences.

What to do in Lisbon

The airport (Portela) is 7 km from the centre. Bus, metro or taxi take 20–30 minutes. Explore the Alto neighbourhood, see Mosteiro dos Jerónimos, stroll Belém, relax in Príncipe Real cafés. On a first visit, spend one day downtown, one further out (Sintra, Cascais). Three or four days lets you see the best without rushing.
