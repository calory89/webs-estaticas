Best time to visit

Santorini welcomes visitors year-round, though the Aegean has a Mediterranean climate: mild winters and hot summers. Travel outside peak season (April–October) is usually cheaper and less crowded. Bear in mind that winter in the Aegean can bring strong winds and choppy seas, but Santorini remains accessible. Check the forecast before you go.

What to do when you arrive at Santorini

Santorini's international airport (JTR) puts you right at the island's doorstep. From there, buses or taxis head to the main towns: Fira (the capital, famous for clifftop views), Oia (the iconic village), Kamari or Perissa (black sand beaches). The island is small but intense: beaches, blue-domed churches, ancient ruins, volcanic crater, waterfront dining. With six days (arriving 13 Jan, leaving 19 Jan) you can explore without rushing.
