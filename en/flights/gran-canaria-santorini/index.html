Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €263 return. Prices typically hover around €329 at the median, with peaks up to €365. To grab the best fares, book as early as possible: Ryanair rewards advance purchases. Avoid last-minute bookings if budget matters.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair is your sole option on this route. The flight includes 2 stopovers, so it's not direct: you'll have intermediate stops that will lengthen your journey, but they compensate with a price you won't find elsewhere. Settle in for a longer journey in exchange for a bargain ticket.

Best time to visit

Santorini shines from May to September, with stable weather and warm seas. If you prefer fewer crowds and steadier prices, April and October are smart months. Winter (November to March) brings less tourism but also greyer days. Bear in mind your search date: November is low season and can offer decent fares.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Santorini Airport (JTR), 5 km from the island. Santorini is small but intense: don't miss the caldera views from Oia, the red cliffs at Akrotiri, black sand beaches and blue-domed churches. Fira and Kamari are solid bases. Eat fresh at local tavernas: prices are reasonable if you avoid the obvious tourist traps.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Gran Canaria to Santorini?

No, this journey includes 2 stopovers. There is no direct connection between these two cities, so you'll have intermediate stops.

What's the cheapest price I can find?

The lowest price on record is €263 return. The median sits at €329, so if you spot that fare, it's a solid opportunity. Book early to lock in the best rates.

Does Ryanair fly this route?

Yes, Ryanair operated this route during the period checked. Confirm current availability on their website, as routes can change.

What can you visit in Santorini?

Oia for sunsets, Akrotiri for Minoan ruins and red cliffs, Fira for the hub, and black sand beaches like Kamari. A caldera boat trip is worth it too. Santorini is small: you can cover the highlights in 3–4 days.
