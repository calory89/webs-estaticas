Best time to visit

Amsterdam is beautiful year-round, but avoid the rain of November–February if you can. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is magical if you love Christmas markets and ice skating, but prepare for cold.

What to do when you arrive

Amsterdam awaits with canals, museums and bicycles everywhere. Stroll the canal rings, visit the Van Gogh Museum or Anne Frank House, rent a bike like locals and cycle the dikes. Eat stroopwafels and Dutch cheese. The airport (AMS) is 15 km from the city centre: train, bus or taxi depending on your budget.
