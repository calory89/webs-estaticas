Price and when to book

Prices range from €629 to €692 for return flights. The median hovers around €662, so if you spot fares near this price, it's a solid deal. Book as far ahead as possible: the earlier you purchase, the lower the final price. Check regularly for price drops, especially during low season.

Airline and stopovers

Norse Atlantic UK operates this route with 2 stopovers on the outbound leg. This means intermediate stops before reaching Fort Lauderdale, which extends your journey but significantly reduces ticket costs. Confirm the stopover locations when you book.

Best time to visit

Fort Lauderdale is a beach and sunshine destination. Avoid hurricane season (June to November). The best months are December through April, when the weather is perfect. January is especially popular but pricier; consider February or March for better fares without sacrificing good weather.

What to do when you arrive

Fort Lauderdale boasts white-sand beaches, waterfront promenades and vibrant nightlife. The airport is well connected to the city centre; from there you can reach Las Olas Beach, explore the port or take day trips to the Everglades. The city is also a jumping-off point for Caribbean cruises and island escapes.

FAQ

What is the cheapest price for this flight?

The lowest recorded price is €629 return. The median price is €662, so the lowest fares aren't always available. Booking early gives you the best chances of catching a bargain.

Does Norse Atlantic UK fly direct to Fort Lauderdale?

No, this flight includes 2 stopovers. You'll have intermediate stops before reaching Fort Lauderdale, which extends total travel time but enables much cheaper fares.

What is the best time to visit Fort Lauderdale?

December through April is the best season: perfect weather and no hurricane risk. If you're hunting bargains, skip January (the priciest month) and aim for February or March instead.

What airport serves Fort Lauderdale?

Fort Lauderdale Airport (FLL) is Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport. It's about 30 km from the city centre and well served by public transport and taxis.
