Best time to travel

Dubrovnik offers sunshine and culture year-round, but peak season runs June through September—prices climb and crowds swell. For better fares, travel in spring (April–May) or autumn (October–November), when weather is still pleasant. Winter is the cheapest option, though rainfall is higher.

What to do when you arrive

Dubrovnik airport is roughly 20 km from the city centre. Once in Dalmatia, don't miss the UNESCO-listed Old Town with its walls and historic streets, or ride the cable car up Mount Srđ for panoramic views. Take a boat to the nearby Elafiti Islands. Fresh seafood is a must. Plan at least 4–5 days to explore at ease.

What is the best time to visit Dubrovnik?

Spring (April–May) and autumn (October–November) offer good weather with fewer crowds and lower fares. Summer (June–September) is pricier and busier. Winter (December–March) is the cheapest season, though rainfall is more frequent.
