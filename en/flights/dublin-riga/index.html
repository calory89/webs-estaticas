Price and when to book

The lowest price we've tracked is €103 for a return ticket. The median sits at €116. Fares climb quickly—we've seen options jump to €185. For the minimum fare, book well in advance; the last few days before travel tend to be pricier. Check often: Ryanair deals shift without notice.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route nonstop—no layovers. Clean flight with a low base fare, though the airline charges extra for luggage, seat selection and priority boarding. Travel with just a small personal item free, and your total cost drops significantly.

Best time to visit

Dublin and Riga both have long, dark winters. June, July and August see peak tourism and higher flight prices. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) strike a good balance between weather and cost. Winter in Riga is stunning if you love Christmas markets and snow, but prepare for extreme cold.

What to do when you arrive

Riga is a UNESCO World Heritage site: wander the medieval Old Town, explore the Art Nouveau avenue (the densest in the world), and climb the Dome Cathedral. Museums are affordable and restaurants cheap. Riga International Airport (RIX) sits 13 km from the centre; bus, taxi or transfer are quick options.
