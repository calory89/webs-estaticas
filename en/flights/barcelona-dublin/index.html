Fly from Barcelona to Dublin from just €46 return with Ryanair. The median price sits at €68, so if you find tickets around that figure, you're buying well. Direct flights, no surprises.

Price and when to book

Sampled prices range from €46 to €112 return. The lowest recorded is €46, median is €68. Book 6–8 weeks ahead to secure base fare. Avoid weeks before holidays and Christmas, when prices spike. Check regularly: Ryanair flash sales don't last long.

Airline and stops

Ryanair is the sole operator on this route, offering direct Barcelona–Dublin flights. No stops, no plane changes: you board at BCN and land at DUB. Bear in mind Ryanair charges extra for oversized hand luggage and checked bags, so check their policy before you travel.

Best time to visit

Dublin is worth visiting any time, but October, April and May see fewer tourists and more stable prices. December and holiday weeks cost more. Winter is cold and rainy; spring and autumn offer better weather and fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive in Dublin

Dublin airport is about 10 km from the city centre with good bus and train links. In the city: Temple Bar for atmosphere, Trinity College for culture, Howth cliffs for nature. Walk the historic centre, try Guinness in proper pubs, climb the Spire. Day trips to Glendalough or the Cliffs of Moher are a must if you have time.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price I can find for Barcelona–Dublin flights?

The lowest recorded is €46 return. The median sits at €68, so that's the typical price you'll see. Tickets at €112 are still very competitive for this route.

Does Ryanair fly direct from Barcelona to Dublin?

Yes, Ryanair operates direct flights with no stops on this route. You board at Barcelona (BCN) and land in Dublin (DUB).

When is the best time to book flights to Dublin?

Book 6–8 weeks ahead. Avoid school holidays and dates close to bank holidays. October, April and May tend to have lower fares than other months.

What should I see in Dublin in 3 or 4 days?

Temple Bar and the nightlife, Trinity College, the Book of Kells, the Guinness Storehouse, the Spire. With more time, visit the Cliffs of Moher or Glendalough. The airport has good bus and train connections to the city centre.
