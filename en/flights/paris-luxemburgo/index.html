Best time to visit

Luxembourg is worth visiting any time of year. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Winter brings fewer tourists and festive charm. Summer is busier but offers long daylight hours.

What to do when you arrive

Luxembourg is small but packed with culture. The old town is a UNESCO World Heritage site with fortresses and dramatic cliffs. Explore the Vieille Ville, descend into the Casematas (underground galleries), and walk the Pétrusse Park. With more time, the Ardennes region offers castles and countryside. The airport is about 6 km from the centre with direct public transport.
