Best time to visit

Puerto Rico has pleasant weather year-round, but hurricane season runs June to November. The driest and most comfortable months are December to April. August and September see fewer tourists and may occasionally offer better prices, though they bring higher rainfall. Plan ahead to secure good fares during your preferred dates.

What to do on arrival in San Juan

San Juan is Puerto Rico's capital and entry point via Luis Muñoz Marín Airport. From here, explore Old San Juan with its colourful colonial streets, beaches like Flamenco (in Culebra), tropical forests, coral reefs, and the iconic Morro Fort. Puerto Rico delivers nature, culture, beach and food all in one destination. There's no need to island-hop; variety is everywhere on the island itself.
