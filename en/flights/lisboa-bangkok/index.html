Price and when to book

Flights start from €684 return, with a median of €745. We found fares up to €923 depending on date and availability. Book well ahead: this route improves if you buy 6-8 weeks early. October and November usually offer good prices before peak season kicks in.

Airline and stopovers

easyJet is the main player here with flights featuring 1 stopover. Departures from 06:10 and arrivals in the small hours (around 03:00 on the return). One stopover means longer transit time, but it balances out against price compared to other market options.

Best time to travel

Thailand shines November to February, with dry weather and pleasant temperatures. Skip May to October (monsoon). These cold European months align perfectly with Bangkok's peak season, so book now if you want to escape the winter chill.

What to do when you arrive in Bangkok

Bangkok is chaotic, vibrant and addictive. Temples like Wat Arun and the Grand Palace are unmissable. Eat at street stalls, explore the canals, visit floating markets. Suvarnabhumi airport is 30 km from the centre; grab the train or a metered taxi. A week minimum to enjoy it properly.
