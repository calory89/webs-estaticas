Best time to visit

Jacksonville enjoys a subtropical climate that's pleasant most of the year. Winter (November to March) is ideal: mild temperatures, less rain, and fewer crowds than summer. Avoid June to October if you want to skip extreme heat and hurricane season. November is a solid choice, with moderate prices and comfortable weather.

What to do when you arrive

Jacksonville is the largest city by area in the continental United States. Explore historic Riverside's downtown, stroll the Riverwalk, and visit Jacksonville Beach. Museums, art galleries, and local dining are worth your time. Nature lovers will find state parks and wildlife viewing in the surrounding area.
