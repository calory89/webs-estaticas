Best time to visit

Seoul shines during spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with mild temperatures and clear skies. Winter is cold but dry, and summer is hot and humid. Check price calendars across several months: low season (January–February) may yield even cheaper fares, though the weather is harsh.

What to do when you arrive

Seoul is unmissable: from the Gyeongbokgung Palace to the futuristic Gangnam district, traditional temples and markets like Myeongdong. Incheon Airport (ICN) is well connected to central Seoul by train and bus. Spend several days exploring, as the cultural, gastronomic and entertainment offer is enormous.
