Best time to visit

San Juan has good weather year-round, but February is shoulder season with less rain and hurricanes than summer. Prices tend to be higher in winter (December-March) when Europeans escape the cold. Consider April-May or September-October to balance price and climate. Avoid hurricane season (June to November) if you prefer weather certainty.

What to do when you arrive

San Juan offers stunning beaches, Old San Juan with colonial architecture, El Yunque National Forest for hiking, and coral reef snorkelling. Luis Muñoz Marín Airport (SJU) is 8 km from the city centre. Taxis and buses run from there to town. Puerto Rico is perfect for mixing beach relaxation with cultural exploration and local Spanish flavours.
