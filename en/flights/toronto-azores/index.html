Best time to visit

Azores is a year-round destination thanks to its Atlantic climate. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant temperatures and less rain. Summer is warmer but busier with tourists. Winter is cooler but ideal if you want peace and quiet, and lower prices.

What to do when you arrive

Azores is a paradise for hiking, whale watching and volcanic landscapes. Visit Lagoa do Fogo on São Miguel, the craters of Terceira, or Pico's rugged coastline. There are natural hot springs, stunning viewpoints and traditional villages where life moves at a different pace. Rent a car if you want to explore the island thoroughly.
