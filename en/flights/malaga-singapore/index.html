Best time to visit

Singapore is tropical year-round. January-February is dry and pleasant: around 25-30°C, less rainfall. December-February is most comfortable. Humidity is high but bearable. Pack light clothes, sunscreen and an umbrella.

What to do when you arrive

Changi is one of the world's best airports. In the city: Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay, Chinatown, Little India temples, Sentosa Island for beaches and attractions, Orchard Road for shopping. Eat at hawker centres (cheap and excellent). MRT is fast, clean and easy. Daily budget €40-60 eating local.
