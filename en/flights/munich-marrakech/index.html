Best time to visit

Marrakech is pleasant almost year-round, but October to April offer the most comfortable weather (20-25°C). Avoid July and August, when heat is intense. January and February can have cold nights. Spring (March-April) and autumn (September-October) are ideal for exploring the medina and visiting the nearby desert.

What to do on arrival

Marrakech airport (RAK) is 6 km from the city centre. The medina is unmissable: explore the souks, visit the Koutoubia Mosque and stay in traditional riads. From Marrakech, tours depart to Agafay Desert (30 km) and the Atlas Mountains. The city works well for 3-4 days.
