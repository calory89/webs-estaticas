Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Iberia Express on this route. The journey includes 3 stopovers, so prepare for a longer trip: you'll depart Gran Canaria around 20:10 and arrive in Mexico City past noon after intermediate stops. Though it means more travel time, the low fares make up for the duration.

Best time to travel

Mexico City is pleasant year-round. November to April brings mild temperatures and less rain. If you're budget-conscious, avoid Christmas, New Year and Easter holidays when prices climb. Flights typically offer better rates between May and September if you're comfortable with heat and rainy season.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Mexico City International Airport (Benito Juárez), about 5 km from the centre. Metro, bus and taxi connections run from the airport to the city. Don't miss the Historic Centre, Coyoacán, Frida Kahlo Museum, Teotihuacán and Xochimilco. Local food is outstanding and museums are world-class. Bring sunscreen and stay hydrated at altitude (2,250 m above sea level).
