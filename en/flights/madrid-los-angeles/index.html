Best time to travel

Los Angeles is worth visiting most of the year. January-February is winter in California, with mild temperatures (15-20°C). If you prefer heat, go for June-September. October is especially recommended: great weather and fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive in Los Angeles

LAX is the main airport in the area. From there, reach iconic beaches (Malibu, Santa Monica), Hollywood, Griffith Observatory, Downtown LA and the Getty Center museums. The city is best explored by car or ride-sharing apps. Spend at least 4-5 days to enjoy it properly.
