Tway Airlines takes you direct from Barcelona (BCN) to the heart of South Korea with no stopovers. Prices around €624 return, with a median of €650. Book early if you're travelling in December: it's peak season and tickets rise fast.

Price and when to book

Flights range from €624 to €694 depending on your exact dates. The earlier you book, the better the price: 6-8 weeks ahead is ideal. December is peak season, so the cheapest tickets sell out quickly. If you spot a flight at €624, grab it.

Airline and stopovers

You're flying with Tway Airlines, South Korea's low-cost carrier that connects Barcelona to Seoul directly. No stopovers, no plane changes: you board in BCN and land in Incheon. Check what's included in your fare before you buy, especially baggage—checked luggage may cost extra.

Best time to visit

December is winter in Seoul, with cold temperatures (between -5°C and 3°C) but clear skies. It's tourist season, so prices are high. For better fares, consider May-June (late spring/early summer) or October-November (autumn), though these figures are December-only. Each season has its appeal in South Korea's capital.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Incheon Airport (ICN), about 60 km from the centre. Public transport is efficient and affordable. Explore Gyeongbokgung Palace, stroll through the traditional Bukchon neighbourhood, relax in jjimjilbang (Korean spas), or spend an afternoon at Myeongdong market. Street food is essential: try tteokbokki and kimchi anywhere you can find it.

FAQ

What should I see in Seoul in a week?

Gyeongbokgung Palace, Buddhist temples, Bukchon's traditional streets, markets like Myeongdong and Namdaemun, jjimjilbangs (Korean spas), modern art museums. Seoul is compact and well-connected by metro. Food is part of the experience.
