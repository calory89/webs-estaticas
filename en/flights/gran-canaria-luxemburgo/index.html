Best time to visit

Luxembourg is best enjoyed in spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October), with mild temperatures and long days. Winter is cold but festive, perfect for the Christmas market if you travel in December. Skip July and August if you want fewer crowds and lower prices. The price check conducted on 22 October shows availability from November onwards.

What to do when you arrive

Luxembourg is small but intense: the Ville Vieille (Old Town) is a UNESCO World Heritage site with fortresses and spectacular views. Walk the Chemin de la Corniche, explore Place d'Armes, visit the Grand Ducal Palace. The country is ideal for hiking in the Moselle valley and the Ardennes. Try local cuisine in traditional restaurants. Take advantage of its size: you can explore multiple regions in just a few days from the capital.
