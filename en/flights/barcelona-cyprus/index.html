Best time to visit

Cyprus has a Mediterranean climate with mild winters. November to March is quieter and prices are often lower than summer. If you want guaranteed sunshine, travel May to September, though fares will be higher. Avoid Christmas, New Year, and Easter week if you want to save money.

What to see when you arrive

Larnaca is your gateway to Cyprus. Explore the beach and seafront promenade (Finikoudes), visit the Church of Saint Lazarus, or head to Nicosia, the capital. Akamas National Park, the beaches near Paphos, and mountain villages like Lefkara are worth visiting. The airport is 6 km from Larnaca, with good bus and taxi links.
