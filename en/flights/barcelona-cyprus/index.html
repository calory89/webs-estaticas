Best time to visit Cyprus

November is a strong choice: warm weather without summer's intense heat, beaches still pleasant, and moderate fares. Summer months (June to September) are pricier and crowded. Spring (March to May) and late autumn offer a good balance of temperature and visitor numbers. Skip Christmas and New Year if you're chasing low prices.

What to do when you arrive in Larnaca

Larnaca International Airport is Cyprus's main hub. From there, the city centre and beaches are close by. Don't miss the sandy beaches, Orthodox churches, mountain villages towards the Troodos range, Kourion ruins, and Larnaca's salt flats. Eat traditional meze and enjoy sunset beach life.

What can I see in Larnaca and the surrounding area?

Sandy beaches, the Church of Saint Lazarus, salt flats and historic mills, Kourion ruins further west, mountain villages in the Troodos range, and charming coastal towns. Local food—meze, halloumi, fresh fish—is a must.
