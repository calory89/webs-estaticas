Best time to visit

Sofia enjoys mild winters (2–5°C average) and warm summers (24–28°C). If you don't mind the cold, January is cheap and less crowded. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: pleasant weather and better prices than summer. Skip July and August if you're after deals.

What to do when you arrive

Sofia is your gateway to Bulgaria. Stroll past the Alexander Nevsky Basilica, explore the old town with the Banya Bashi Mosque, and soak up local life in traditional cafés. From the capital, reach Rila Monastery (UNESCO site) or Vitosha Mountains easily. Sofia Airport is 10 km from the centre; bus, taxi, or car rental get you into town quickly.
