Best time to visit

Istanbul is appealing year-round. Autumn (September to November) and winter (December to February) tend to be quieter with less mass tourism than spring and summer. Mild weather in these seasons makes exploring the city comfortable without extreme heat.

What to do on arrival

Istanbul awaits with the Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia, Topkapi Palace, and the Spice Bazaar. Istanbul Airport (IST) connects directly to the city via public transport. One week is enough to cover the main highlights at a relaxed pace, or adjust to your schedule.
