Best time to visit Sofia

Sofia is worth visiting year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer the most pleasant weather for exploring the city. Winter is cold, though prices may drop further. Book well in advance regardless of season: cheap flights don't hang around.

What to do when you arrive in Sofia

Bulgaria's capital delivers. Visit the Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, stroll through the Vitosha neighbourhood, explore archaeological museums, and taste local food at traditional markets. Sofia Airport is about 10 km from the centre and well served by public transport. With a few days, you can see Orthodox churches, parks, and the bohemian side of this increasingly popular city.

FAQ

Does Ryanair fly direct from Valencia to Sofia?
