Cheap flights Valencia to Sofia from €72

Ryanair connects Valencia with Sofia at very tight prices: from €72 return. Direct flights get you to Bulgaria's capital in just over 3 hours. Book in advance and grab the best fares.

Price and when to book

Flights range between €72 and €100 in the sample checked. The median sits at €80 return. To get the best price, book in advance (minimum 4-6 weeks) and stay flexible on your travel dates: Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Thursdays are usually cheaper. Avoid school holidays and bank holidays.

Airline and connections

Ryanair is your only option on this route and operates direct flights (no stops). Departure from Valencia (VLC) in mid-afternoon, arrival in Sofia (SOF) at night. It's the best option if you value speed and convenience: direct, no time wasted on connections. Remember Ryanair charges for oversized hand luggage and checked bags.

Best time to visit

Bulgaria is worth visiting year-round, but spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer good weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is cheaper, though it gets cold. If you're chasing low prices, fly between November and March, skipping Christmas and New Year. Sofia is enjoyable without the masses during these periods.

What to do when you arrive in Sofia

Sofia airport is 10 km from the city centre; reach it by bus (quick and cheap) or taxi. Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, Boyana Church and the bohemian Vitosha neighbourhood are must-sees. Eat at local markets and try authentic Bulgarian food. Sofia is cheap: accommodation, restaurants and activities won't drain your wallet.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Valencia to Sofia?

Yes, Ryanair operates this route non-stop. No connections—you fly from Valencia (VLC) straight to Sofia (SOF) in around 3–3.5 hours.

How much does a Valencia to Sofia flight cost?

From €72 return. The median price is €80. Fares vary by date: the earlier you book and the more flexible you are, the cheaper you'll get.

Which airline flies Valencia to Sofia?

Ryanair operates this route. Direct flights only, and it's the most budget-friendly option available.

What can I see in Sofia in 3 days?

Alexander Nevsky Cathedral (the most famous landmark), Boyana Church (UNESCO World Heritage), the Archaeological Museum, Vitosha neighbourhood with cafés and local markets, and nearby thermal baths. Sofia is compact and easy to explore.
