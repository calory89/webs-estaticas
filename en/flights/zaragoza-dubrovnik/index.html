Price and when to book

The lowest price we've seen is €161 return, though the median stands at €182. There are also options at €190. On routes to Croatia it's worth not waiting too long: the market moves quickly, especially in peak season. If the date suits you, best decide in the next few days.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with one stopover on the outbound leg. It's not a direct flight, but the price makes up for it. The stopover is usually brief and well-coordinated. Check your baggage: Ryanair charges extra for checked luggage, so if you travel light you save more.

Best time to visit

Dubrovnik is beautiful year-round, but May–June and September–October are ideal: less crowding than July–August and better weather. If you're chasing low fares, travel outside Spanish school holidays. Winter (December–February) also brings fewer tourists and mild temperatures for exploring.

What to do when you arrive

Dubrovnik is the star: medieval walls, a storybook old town, and a bustling harbour. From there, day trips to the Elafiti Islands or the Pelješac Peninsula. The city is 20 km from the airport; bus, taxi or hire car gets you there in under half an hour.

FAQ

Are there direct flights Zaragoza–Dubrovnik?

Not in this search. Ryanair operates the route with one stopover. It's the most affordable option from Zaragoza.

What's included in the €161 ticket?

Return flight. Hand luggage is included; checked baggage and seat selection are paid extras with Ryanair.

How many days is it worth spending in Dubrovnik?

At least 3 days to see the old town at leisure. With 5–7 days you can explore nearby islands and coastal villages.

What's the best way to get from Dubrovnik airport to the city centre?

Public bus, taxi or hire car. The bus is the cheapest option and takes around 30 minutes. The airport is well connected.
