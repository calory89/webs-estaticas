Ryanair connects Paris and Edinburgh with direct flights starting at €57 return. The median price sits around €87, so you'll find good deals if you search smartly. Fly in December or earlier to secure these fares.

Price and when to book

Flights start from €57 return, with a median of €87. Prices in our sample range from €57 to €93. The best strategy is to book as early as possible: the sooner you reserve, the lower the fares tend to be. Search mid-week and avoid peak demand periods like bank holidays and school breaks.

Airline and direct flights

Ryanair operates this route with direct flights—no layovers required. You depart Paris and arrive in Edinburgh without intermediate stops, making your journey straightforward. Bear in mind that Ryanair charges separately for checked baggage and cabin bags, so review what's included in your base fare.

Best time to visit

December shows strong availability in our data, but Scotland's winters are cold and days are short. For more daylight and milder weather, consider spring (April–May) or autumn (September–October). Check prices for those periods: budget fares often drop when demand is lower.

What to do when you arrive in Edinburgh

Edinburgh is a stunning medieval city with a castle, historic closes (narrow lanes), and world-class museums. Walk the Royal Mile, climb the castle, explore the modern food scene in Leith. If time allows, venture to Stirling, the Highlands, or the Fife coast. The airport is 12 km from the centre and well served by buses and trains.

What should I see in Edinburgh in 3–4 days?

Edinburgh Castle, the Royal Mile, Calton Hill for panoramic views, the Leith waterfront, and the National Museum of Scotland. With an extra day, day-trip to Stirling or the nearby Highlands.
