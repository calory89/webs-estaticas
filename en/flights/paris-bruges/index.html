Best time to visit

Bruges is beautiful year-round, but October to March offers less mass tourism and cool temperatures ideal for walking. December brings Christmas markets and special decorations, though it's pricier season. Book with maximum notice if you travel then.

What to do when you arrive

Bruges is a medieval city with picturesque canals, cobbled squares and storybook architecture. Visit the Beguinage, the Basilica of the Holy Blood, the Belfry and explore its canals by boat. Belgian food won't disappoint: chocolate, local beers and chips. From Brussels Airport (roughly 70 km away), reach Bruges by train or coach in under an hour.
