Best time to visit

Bruges is beautiful year-round, but weather is most pleasant April to October. July and August bring peak tourism and potentially higher prices. Spring and autumn offer good weather with fewer crowds. Winter is cold but stunning if you want to see the medieval streets blanketed in snow.

What to do in Bruges

Bruges is a compact medieval gem: walk the historic centre, climb Belfry Tower for 360-degree views, take a boat tour along the canals, and sample Belgian beer and chocolate. Minnewater (Lake of Love) is unmissable at sunset. Most attractions are walkable, perfect for a long weekend escape from Barcelona.
