Best time to travel

Athens is a year-round destination, but shoulder months (April-May and September-October) offer pleasant weather without summer crowds or extreme heat. Winters are mild. Avoid July and August if you're seeking lower prices and fewer crowds. December can be appealing if you don't mind cooler weather.

What to do when you arrive

Athens International Airport 'Eleftherios Venizelos' is 24 km from the city centre, with metro, bus or taxi access. The Acropolis and Parthenon are unmissable, plus the Acropolis Museum. Stroll through Plaka, explore the ancient agora, and catch views from Lykavittos Hill. Local cuisine is excellent: try moussaka, souvlaki and fresh feta cheese.
