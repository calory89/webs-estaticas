Best time to visit

Athens is beautiful year-round, but autumn (September–November) and spring (March–May) offer perfect weather without summer's intense heat. Skip July and August if you dislike crowds. December is quiet and mild, ideal if you want fewer tourists.

What to do when you arrive

Athens awaits with the Acropolis, the Parthenon and the bohemian Plaka neighbourhood. Don't miss the Ancient Agora, the National Archaeological Museum or a stroll around Syntagma. Take a ferry to Aegean islands (Mykonos, Santorini) if you have extra days. The airport is 35 km from the centre; bus, taxi or metro get you downtown.
