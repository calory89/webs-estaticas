Best time to visit Iceland

Winter (November to March) offers northern lights and snowy landscapes, though with few daylight hours. Summer (June to August) is peak season with endless daylight and better conditions for hiking and camping. Spring and autumn bring tighter prices and mild weather. Choose based on what you want to see.

What to do when you arrive in Iceland

You'll land at Keflavík, 50 km from Reykjavik. The Golden Circle (Þingvellir, Geysir, Gullfoss) is a must. Don't miss black sand beaches, waterfalls (Skógafoss, Seljalandsfoss) and glaciers. Hire a car to move freely or take buses if you prefer not to drive. Iceland is pricey for food, so eat well in Reykjavik before exploring.
