Best time to visit

Mexico City is a destination year-round, though the dry season (October to April) brings less rain and clearer skies. Northern summer months (June–August) coincide with Mexico's rainy season, but the capital stays lively. Avoid Christmas and New Year weeks if you're chasing the lowest fares.

What to do on arrival

Mexico City's international airport (MEX) is about 5 km from the city centre. The capital boasts world-class museums, from the National Museum of Anthropology to Frida Kahlo's house. Stroll through Coyoacán, Xochimilco and the Historic Centre. Local food is exceptional: street taqueríasand high-end restaurants alike. Use the metro to get around; it's fast, cheap and reliable.
