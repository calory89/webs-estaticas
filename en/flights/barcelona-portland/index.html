Best time to visit

Portland has mild weather year-round. Summer (June to September) is peak season with higher prices. Autumn (September to November) and spring (March to May) offer good weather and more moderate fares. Winter can be rainy but works if you want peace and lower prices. Avoid peak holiday periods like Christmas and New Year for better deals.

What to do when you arrive

Portland is known for its cultural scene, vintage shops and nightlife. Walk through the historic downtown, visit Powell's Books (one of the largest independent bookstores in the US), enjoy local food cart cuisine and urban parks. Further out, Mount Hood and Columbia River gorge waterfalls are essential. Also explore the nearby Willamette Valley wine region.
