Price and when to book

The best price we've seen is €185 return. The median is €186, with sample fares ranging from €185 to €204. Book as early as possible: prices tend to climb as departure approaches. Although it's impossible to predict exactly, reservations 4–6 weeks ahead are often cheaper.

Airline and stops

Best time to visit

Copenhagen is lovely year-round, but winters are harsh (December to February). For milder weather, travel in spring (April–May) or autumn (September–October). Prices don't fluctuate predictably by season: book in advance regardless.

What to do when you arrive

Copenhagen is compact and walkable—or cycle like a local. See Nyhavn (the colourful harbour), Amalienborg Palace, Tivoli Gardens and the Little Mermaid statue. Eat smørrebrød (open-faced sandwiches) everywhere. The airport (CPH) is 8 km from the centre; bus and train connect easily.
