Best time to travel

Mexico welcomes visitors year-round, but each season has its perks. Hurricane season (June to November) often brings lower fares, though weather risk is higher. For guaranteed good weather, aim for December to April: hot, dry and fewer showers. November is particularly smart—decent weather and gentler prices before the festive rush.

What to do when you arrive in Mexico City

You'll land at Benito Juárez International Airport (MEX). The capital packs serious attractions: Historic Centre, Metropolitan Cathedral, Templo Mayor, world-class museums (Frida Kahlo, Anthropology), boat rides in Xochimilco and bohemian neighbourhoods like Coyoacán and Roma. Eat at street markets, try fine-dining Mexican cuisine and soak up the nightlife. The city is vast—plan carefully based on your priorities.

What should I see in Mexico City if I have limited time?

Prioritise the Historic Centre, Cathedral, Templo Mayor and Frida Kahlo Museum. With more time, add Xochimilco (boat rides), Coyoacán and the Anthropology or Modern Art museums. Eat at Mercado de la Paz or Roma neighbourhood restaurants for genuine local flavours.
