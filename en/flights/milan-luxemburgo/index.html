Best time to visit

Spring and autumn offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Luxembourg is lovely in May, June, September and October. Avoid December if you're hunting for low fares, though it's festive season. Check the flight calendar with some lead time to pick the right dates.

What to do when you arrive in Luxembourg

Luxembourg Airport is 6 km from the capital. The old town is a UNESCO World Heritage site, with medieval alleyways and views over the Alzette river. Walk the historic fortifications, visit Notre-Dame Cathedral and relax at cafés on Place d'Armes. If you have time, take the Corniche, the promenade with the best city views.

What to see in Luxembourg in a week?

Spend 2–3 days in the capital: old town, museums, fortifications. Use the rest to explore medieval villages like Vianden or Echternach in the north, or wine estates in the Moselle valley. Luxembourg is small, easy to get around by bus or train.
