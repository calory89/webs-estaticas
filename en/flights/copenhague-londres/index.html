Fly Copenhagen to London for under €100 return per person with Ryanair. Direct flights with no stops, evening departures and early morning returns. The best option for a quick getaway to the British capital.

Price and when to book

The lowest price is €98 per person (return), with a median of €100. Prices range between €98 and €100, very tight margins. To secure these fares, book as far in advance as possible: the earlier you purchase, the better your price. Avoid booking at the last minute.

Airline and connections

Ryanair is the only airline on this route with direct flights and no stops. The journey is short (roughly 1h 20 min flight time), perfect for avoiding connection time. Evening departures from Copenhagen and early morning arrivals in London make good use of your time.

Best time to visit

London is enjoyable year-round, though spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. July and August attract more tourists, so you'll have less choice of low fares. Winter is cheaper but cold and dark early.

Things to do when you arrive

You'll land at Stansted Airport, 47 km from the city centre. From there, bus or train take you downtown in 40–60 minutes. Don't miss Big Ben, Tower Bridge, the British Museum (free entry), Thames walks and markets at Camden or Borough. London has excellent public transport (Oyster card) and plenty to see on any budget.
