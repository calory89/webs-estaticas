Best time to visit

Hong Kong is pleasant year-round, but October to March offers drier, cooler weather. Skip September (typhoon season) and summer (June-August) if you prefer less heat and humidity. December and January are peak tourist season, so prices are higher. For a balance between cost and weather, April-May or November are solid choices.

What to do when you arrive

Hong Kong is a must: Star Ferry across the harbour, Causeway Bay for shopping, Temple Street Night Market, ancient temples in New Territories. Take the Peak Tram for panoramic views. Eat dim sum in local restaurants and explore the islands by ferry. Transport is efficient, so getting around is easy.
