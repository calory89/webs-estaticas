Airline and stops

The layovers extend your travel time but make the competitive price possible. Always check connection times when booking to decide if the journey suits you.

Best time to visit

Phu Quoc has a tropical climate. The dry season runs November to April, with warm weather and less rain—your ideal window. Travelling these months means more days for beach time and outdoor activities. Avoid the rainy months if you prefer uninterrupted plans. Remember these are peak dates, so book early.

What to do when you arrive

Phu Quoc is an island with stunning beaches, national parks and bustling markets. The international airport (PQC) connects well to the centre. Stay in Duong Dong for the main tourist hub or explore the quieter north. Dive, visit pearl farms, sample local food or unwind on the beach. Plenty to do at a relaxed pace.
