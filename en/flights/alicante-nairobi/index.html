Best time to visit

Kenya is a year-round destination, but the dry seasons (January–February and July–October) offer better conditions for safaris and beaches. Prices may fluctuate with demand, so flexible dates will help you find a bargain. Avoid peak season if you're chasing low fares.

What to do when you arrive in Nairobi

Nairobi is your gateway to incredible safaris: the Maasai Mara National Reserve is just a few hours away. Explore the city, visit museums, enjoy local food and arrange excursions to nearby reserves. Jomo Kenyatta Airport is about 20 km from the city centre, well connected by bus, taxi or shuttle.
