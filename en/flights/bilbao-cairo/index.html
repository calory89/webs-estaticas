Best time to visit

Egypt is a year-round destination. Weather is most pleasant from November to March, with daytime temperatures around 20–25 °C. Avoid July and August due to extreme heat. Rainfall is virtually non-existent, so thermal comfort and tourist crowds are the main deciding factors.

What to do on arrival

Cairo is your gateway to Egypt. Access the Pyramids of Giza, the Sphinx and the Egyptian Museum from here. Wander Khan El-Khalili bazaar, cruise the Nile, or visit the Citadel. Cairo Airport (CAI) is 20 km from the city centre; buses, taxis and transfer services are available.
