Prices and when to buy

Fares on this route range from €264 to €325 depending on availability and dates. Book as early as possible, especially if you're travelling in peak season. Generally, booking 2–3 months ahead secures better prices on international routes.

Airline and stops

Iberia Airlines operates this route with one stop. There are no direct flights from Valencia to Cairo, so allow extra time at the connection airport. Layovers typically last 1–3 hours depending on the connection.

Best time to visit

Cairo is a year-round destination, but October to April offers the most pleasant weather with moderate temperatures. Avoid summer if you can't handle extreme heat. Check availability for your preferred dates to adjust your budget accordingly.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Cairo International Airport (CAI), about 25 km from the city centre. The Pyramids of Giza, the Egyptian Museum and Khan El-Khalili are must-sees. You can also explore the Citadel of Saladin and take a felucca (traditional sailboat) on the Nile. Arrange transport in advance: taxi, Uber or included tours are standard options.
