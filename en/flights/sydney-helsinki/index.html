The airport is 20 km from the city centre, with good train and bus links. Explore Helsinki's White Cathedral, the underground Kamppi temple, and the bohemian Kallio district. Visit the fortress island of Suomenlinna, experience a traditional Finnish sauna, and savour Nordic cuisine. From Helsinki, you can easily reach Finland's famous lake region, wooden cabins, and authentic Lapland experiences in winter.
