Best time to visit

Charleston is pleasant most of the year, but October through April offers milder temperatures and lower humidity than summer. January (when we've seen example bookings) is solid if you don't mind mild winters in the South. Spring (March–May) is also excellent: blooming gardens, local events, and perfect weather.

What to do when you arrive

Charleston is a gem of the American South: walk Rainbow Row's colonial townhouses in pastel colours, visit Fort Sumter by boat, enjoy local cuisine on King Street, and relax on nearby beaches like Folly Beach. The airport (CHS) is 20 km from the centre; grab a taxi, Uber, or hire a car to explore the Lowlands region.
