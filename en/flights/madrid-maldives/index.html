Fly from Madrid to Maldives from just €657 with Etihad Airways. Prices average around €692, so if you spot tickets below that, it's time to book. One stopover awaits on the way.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €657 per person for a return trip. The median sits at €692, giving you a clear benchmark for this route. Book as early as possible: prices tend to rise closer to your departure date. Avoid last-minute bookings if you want to save.

Airline and stopovers

You'll travel with Etihad Airways on both legs. Your journey includes one stopover, so it's not direct, but it's the most economical option from Madrid. Plan for a plane change during your trip.

Best time to visit

Maldives is a tropical destination, so choose your dates based on your climate preference. The dry season (November to April) is generally the best time to avoid heavy rain. Regardless, book as far ahead as you can to lock in the lowest fares.

What to do when you arrive

Maldives means white-sand beaches, coral reefs, and luxury resorts. Dive, snorkel, relax in overwater bungalows, or explore the capital, Malé. Most resorts are spread across different atolls, so pick your base depending on the experience you're after.
