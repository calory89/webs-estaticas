Best time to visit

November is good for low prices and fewer crowds. For more activities and pleasant weather, choose summer (June–August), though fares rise. Winter (December–February) brings Christmas markets from late November onwards and festive vibes, but with more tourists. Spring (April–May) balances mild temperatures and moderate prices.

What to do when you arrive

Hamburg deserves 2–3 days. Don't miss the historic port and restored warehouses of Speicherstadt (Unesco World Heritage site). Stroll Reeperbahn (nightlife hub), explore Alster canal and its museums. If travelling in winter, Christmas markets fill the city.
