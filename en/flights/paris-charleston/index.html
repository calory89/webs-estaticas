Best time to visit

Charleston has pleasant weather year-round. Winters (December to February) are mild and ideal for avoiding intense heat and summer hurricane season. Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) offer excellent conditions. Check the availability calendar to compare prices across your preferred dates.

What to do on arrival

Charleston is a historic city with colonial architecture, nearby beaches and local cuisine. Explore the historic promenade district, visit Fort Sumter, relax on Folly Island or Kiawah beaches, and enjoy Carolina Low Country food. Charleston International Airport (CHS) is about 20 km from the city centre; bus, taxi and car rental services are available.
