Price and when to book

Prices range between €97 and €137 based on the samples we've tracked. Book early to lock in a bargain, this route fills up fast because it's short, competitive, and popular. Don't leave it to the last minute.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair runs this route with direct flights. You leave Dublin and land in Athens, no transfers, no stops. Pack smart though: cabin baggage is included, but checked luggage costs extra.

Best time to visit

May, June, September and October offer the best weather, fewer crowds than summer, and calmer seas. Price shifts depend on demand and booking timing, not the calendar month itself, so track fares week by week.

What to do in Athens

The Acropolis is non-negotiable, but don't skip the Parthenon, museums, and the bohemian Plaka district. Greek food, rooftop terraces and sunset views over the city justify the trip alone. Eleftherios Venizelos airport is 30 km out; bus or metro gets you downtown in half an hour.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for Dublin to Athens flights?

We've seen return flights from €97 with Ryanair. Price depends on your travel dates and how far ahead you book; advance booking saves money.

Are Dublin to Athens flights direct?

Yes, Ryanair flies direct between Dublin and Athens. No stopovers, no plane changes.

Which airline flies Dublin to Athens?

Ryanair is the main carrier on this route, offering direct flights at competitive fares.

What should I see in Athens in 3 days?

Acropolis, Parthenon, Acropolis Museum, Ancient Agora, Plaka, and Piraeus waterfront. Add dinners on terraces and local cafés to soak up the real Greece.
