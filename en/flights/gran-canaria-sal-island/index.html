Best time to visit

Isla de Sal is a year-round destination: beach, diving and fishing are the main draws. December to March offer dry weather and perfect beaches. July and August are hotter and busier. February often strikes a good balance between climate and prices. Book ahead if travelling during school holidays or public holidays.

What to do when you arrive

Sal is all beach and relaxation: Punta Preta, Ponta Leme and Santa María are must-visit beaches. Crystal-clear waters are perfect for diving and snorkelling. Pedra Lume salt ponds are unique for a buoyant swim in hyper-salty water. Hire a car or scooter to explore the island freely. The international airport (SID) is 10 km from the main town.
