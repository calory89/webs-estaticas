Connect the Canary Islands to the French capital with no fuss: direct flights with easyJet from €109 return. The median price sits around €125, so you have room to find good deals if you book in advance.

Price and when to book

Prices found range from €109 to €161 for return flights. The classic rule still holds: book 6–8 weeks ahead for better fares. Prices tend to climb as departure approaches, so don't ignore offers you see today.

Airlines and stopovers

easyJet is your option on this route and offers direct flights between Gran Canaria and Paris, saving you time and hassle. No stopovers means fewer lost bags and zero connection stress.

Best time to travel

December is mid-to-high season in Paris (cold and Christmas ahead), but perfect if you want Christmas markets and festive buzz. Prices tend to be higher then and in summer. Consider spring (March–May) or autumn (September–October) for a good balance of weather and cost.

What to do when you arrive in Paris

You'll land at Paris airport (likely Charles de Gaulle or Orly). Direct train or bus to the city centre in 30–45 minutes. Visit the Eiffel Tower, Louvre, Notre-Dame, stroll Montmartre, and eat at local bistros. Paris deserves at least 3–4 days.
