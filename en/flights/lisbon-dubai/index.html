Best time to visit

Dubai is best from November to March: pleasant temperatures (20–30°C) versus the scorching summer heat. December is ideal if you want beaches and outdoor activities without intense heat. Avoid June to September, when temperatures exceed 40°C.

What to do when you arrive

Dubai offers the Burj Khalifa (the world's tallest skyscraper), Jumeirah Beach, Dubai Marina waterfront, and the world's largest shopping mall. For culture, explore the Old Town with its traditional souks. The airport is 15 km from the city centre, well connected by metro and bus.
