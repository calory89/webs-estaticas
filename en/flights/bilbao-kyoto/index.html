Price and when to book

Prices start from €687 return, with fares also available at €806 and €849. As an intercontinental route with stopovers, book early to lock in the best rate. Check regularly for new departures; fares shift with availability and season.

Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Vueling. This journey includes 2 stopovers, so it's not a direct flight. Whilst it adds time, it's the cheapest option from Bilbao. The stopovers give you a chance to stretch your legs before reaching Kyoto via Osaka (OSA).

Best time to visit

Kyoto shines in spring (March–May) with cherry blossom season and autumn (September–November) with mild weather and vibrant colours. Skip summer (June–August) due to heat and humidity. Winter is calm and less crowded. Compare offers for your preferred dates.

What to do when you arrive

Kyoto is Japan's cultural heart. Visit ancient temples like Fushimi Inari, walk through Arashiyama's bamboo grove and explore the geisha district of Gion. Local trains connect well with Osaka (OSA) and beyond. Bring cash: many traditional spots only take yen in hand.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Bilbao to Kyoto?

No. This route requires 2 stopovers with Vueling. There's no direct connection from Bilbao to Kyoto/Osaka.

What's the cheapest price you found?

€687 return. The median sits at €806, so expect to pay between €700 and €850 in most cases. Higher prices occur during peak season.

Which airline operates this route?

Vueling. They offer budget fares, though remember stopovers mean longer total travel time than a direct flight.

When is the best time to visit Kyoto?

Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) are ideal: lovely weather, stunning landscapes and without summer's intense heat or winter's deep cold.
