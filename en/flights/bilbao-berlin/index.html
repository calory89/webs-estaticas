Best time to visit

Berlin is worth visiting any time of year. Winter (November–March) tends to be cheaper than spring and summer. Travel during these months and you'll face fewer crowds at monuments and museums. Pack a warm coat, temperatures are low.

Things to do when you arrive

Berlin airport (BER) is about 25 km from the city centre. Visit the Brandenburg Gate, Reichstag, Museum Island and the Berlin Wall remnants. Eat in Kreuzberg, stroll around Prenzlauer Berg and experience the nightlife. Three or four days covers the essentials.
