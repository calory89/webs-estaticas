Best time to visit

Krakow shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Prices tend to be lower outside peak season. Avoid July and August if you're after peace and cheap fares.

What to do when you arrive

Krakow welcomes you with its medieval Old Town, the Market Square and Gothic churches. The airport is 15 km from the centre, well connected by bus and train. Spend time on the Royal Route, visit Wawel Castle and taste local cuisine. It's a compact city, perfect for exploring on foot.
