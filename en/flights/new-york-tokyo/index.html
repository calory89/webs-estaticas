Best time to visit

Tokyo is beautiful in spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November), with mild temperatures. Winter (December–February) is dry and cold, perfect if you don't mind bundling up. Avoid the summer monsoon (June–September). Book in advance to secure the best prices, regardless of season.

What to do on arrival in Tokyo

You'll arrive in the Tokyo metropolitan area. The centre features historic temples, world-class gastronomy, modern districts like Shibuya and Shinjuku, and traditional experiences in Asakusa. Public transport is excellent: trains, metro, and buses connect any part of the city. Spend days exploring museums, parks, and the nightlife.
