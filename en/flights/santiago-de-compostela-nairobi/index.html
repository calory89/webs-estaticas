Airline and stops

Wizz Air Malta operates this route. The journey includes 2 stops at connecting airports. It's not a direct flight, but it's the budget option available from Santiago. Note that connection times vary; review your booking carefully to avoid missing your next flight.

Best time to travel

Nairobi welcomes visitors year-round, though rainy seasons (March to May and October to November) typically offer lower fares. Dry season (June to October and December to February) is pricier but features better weather. For savings and if you don't mind occasional rain, travel during wet months. Flexible dates help: shifting your departure or return by one day can save dozens of euros.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport. Nairobi is your gateway to Kenya's best: safaris in Amboseli or Masai Mara, local museums, traditional markets, and urban life. Excursions to national parks depart from here. Arrange transport ahead and consult local operators about the best routes for your budget and time.
