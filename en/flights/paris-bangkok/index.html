Best time to visit

Thailand shines from November to February, with pleasant temperatures and less rain. December-January is peak season, so expect crowds at temples and beaches. Want cheaper prices and fewer tourists? Try November or February instead. Monsoon season (May-October) is cheapest but comes with constant rain.

What to do in Bangkok

Bangkok has stunning temples (Wat Arun, Wat Phra Kaew), boat rides through floating canals, and vibrant night markets. Beaches in Phuket and islands like Koh Samui are a few hours away. Eat at street food stalls, visit the Royal Palace, and explore neighbourhoods like Chinatown. It's not just ruins—it's living culture.
