Best time to visit

Vilna is worth visiting year-round. Autumn (September–October) and spring (April–May) offer mild weather and fewer crowds. Winter is very cold but the city sparkles with Christmas lights in December. Flight prices tend to be lower in off-season (January–March, November).

What to do when you arrive

Vilna airport is 4 km from the centre; bus or taxi is quick. The Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage site with medieval lanes, Baroque churches and quiet squares. Don't miss the Cathedral, the Gate of Dawn and the bohemian neighbourhood of Užupis. Vilna is compact and affordable; walks, museums and local food won't break the bank.
