Best time to visit

Hong Kong is ideal from October to March, when temperatures are lower and humidity is reduced. November (the sample flight month) is excellent: pleasant weather and clear skies. Avoid June to September due to typhoons and extreme heat and humidity. Prices typically rise around Christmas and New Year, so travelling outside these dates saves more.

What to do when you arrive

Hong Kong offers total contrast: skyscrapers next to pristine islands. Don't miss Victoria Peak for bay views, Victoria Harbour via the Star Ferry, night markets (Temple Street), historic temples, and neighbourhoods like Sheung Wan and Central. If you have extra days, take a trip to Lamma or Lantau Islands. Street food is essential.
