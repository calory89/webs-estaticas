Best time to visit

Zanzibar is a beach destination year-round, but dry season is best: June to October and January to February. Rain falls March to May. Water is always warm. Choose based on your climate preference and flight availability.

What to do in Zanzibar

Stone Town is essential: narrow lanes, palaces and spice markets. Northern beaches like Nungwi and Kendwa are peaceful. Pemba and Unguja are nearby islands ideal for diving and snorkelling. Visit the Pemba Archipelago for pristine coral reefs.
