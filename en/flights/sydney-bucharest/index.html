Best time to visit

Bucharest is pleasant in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with mild temperatures and less rain. Skip winter if you want to avoid heavy snow and cold. Summer is warm but crowded. Any season works if you plan what to see.

What to do when you arrive

Bucharest has the Palace of Parliament (Europe's largest building by volume), a medieval old town with charming lanes, stunning Orthodox churches, and art museums. The city has great Romanian food: try mici (grilled meatballs) and local restaurant dining. Henri Coandă airport is 16 km from the centre, well served by bus and taxi.
