Best time to travel

Naples shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. For budget travel, go in low season (November–February), though it'll be cooler. Regardless of season, book ahead: cheap direct flights don't wait around.

What to do in Naples

The Cathedral of San Gennaro, the National Archaeological Museum, and Pompeii (24 km away) are must-sees. Eat authentic Neapolitan pizza—it's not optional. Wander the old town, hit the waterfront, and spend time in the side streets: Naples happens outdoors. Capodichino airport is 6 km from the centre; bus or taxi gets you there in 15–20 minutes.

The old town (UNESCO site), the Cathedral of San Gennaro, the National Archaeological Museum, and a day trip to Pompeii. Don't miss the food: pizza, fresh pasta, and espresso. If you have extra time, the Amalfi Coast is an hour away.
