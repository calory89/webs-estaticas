Fly from Munich to Tenerife for just €139 return with Condor on direct flights. Median price sits around €237, so book early for the best deals. Tenerife awaits with beaches, volcanoes and year-round sunshine.

Price and when to book

The lowest price recorded is €139 for a return trip. Median price is €237 and we've seen fares up to €244. The classic advice still applies: book well in advance to get the best fares. Tenerife is a destination year-round, but the cheapest prices often fall during low season (October to April, excluding Christmas).

Airline and flight type

Condor is your airline on this route. The flight is direct from Munich to Tenerife South, no stops. This saves you time and hassle: you leave MUC and arrive direct at TCI. Choose seat, luggage and options on Condor's website according to your needs.

Best time to travel

Tenerife enjoys subtropical weather year-round: average temperatures 20–25°C even in winter. If you're chasing cheap prices, aim for October, November, February or March. December to January and July–August are high season and pricier. Beach weather is always good; if you want the mountains (Teide), go May to September when there's less snow.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Tenerife South (Reina Sofía). Southern beaches (Los Cristianos, Playa de las Américas) are 20 km away. In the north, La Laguna and Puerto de la Cruz are charming coastal towns. Mount Teide is a must: climb to 3,718 metres or drive the national park. Los Gigantes cliffs, markets, Canarian food: there's something for everyone.
