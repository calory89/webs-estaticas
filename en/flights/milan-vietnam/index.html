Best time to visit

Vietnam has a tropical climate. The best season depends on where you're going: the north (Hanoi) is pleasant from October to April when it's cooler and drier. December is ideal: dry weather, pleasant temperatures, and it's not peak season yet. Avoid May to September due to monsoons.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

You'll land at Noi Bai International Airport, about 30 km from the city. The Old Quarter, Hoan Kiem Lake, the French Quarter, Ho Chi Minh's Mausoleum, and nearby temples are must-sees. Many travellers head to Halong Bay or visit Sapa in the mountains. Local transport is cheap and easy to use.
