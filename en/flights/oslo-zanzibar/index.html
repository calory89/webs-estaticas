Best time to visit

Zanzibar is a tropical destination year-round. The dry season (June to October) and transition months (December to February) offer less rain and better beach weather. December strikes a balance: fewer crowds than July–August and still reasonable prices if you skip peak Christmas weeks.

What to do in Zanzibar

The airport (ZNZ) is 50 km from Stone Town. Visit the old town (Unesco site), beaches at Nungwi and Kendwa, the Changuu Prison ruins, or take a spice tour. Zanzibar is a perfect springboard for safaris in northern Tanzania (Arusha is a short flight away).

What can you see in Zanzibar besides beaches?

Stone Town (the historic capital, a Unesco site), Changuu Prison, spice tours, diving, snorkelling, and island excursions. Many travellers continue to northern Tanzania afterwards (Kilimanjaro, Serengeti).
