Best time to travel

Dubai enjoys comfortable weather from October to April when temperatures are mild. These months are perfect for exploring the city and surroundings without extreme heat. Plan ahead with enough time to secure the best flight prices.

What to do when you arrive in Dubai

Dubai offers everything from iconic skyscrapers like the Burj Khalifa to beaches and traditional markets like the Spice Souq. The airport is well connected to the city centre. Enjoy shopping, water sports, desert experiences and diverse cuisine.
