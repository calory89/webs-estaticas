Best time to visit

Krakow shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather and fewer crowds. Winters are harsh but create a magical atmosphere if you don't mind cold. July and August push prices and tourism up. Skip June if you're chasing a bargain.

What to do when you arrive

Krakow is a UNESCO gem: the Main Market Square, Cathedral and Wawel Castle are unmissable. Walk the Jewish Quarter, visit the Wieliczka Salt Mines just outside, or sip a craft beer in the old town. Three or four days lets you explore it properly.
