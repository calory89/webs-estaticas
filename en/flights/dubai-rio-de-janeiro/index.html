Fly from Dubai to Rio de Janeiro from €1281 return. The median price sits around €1383. With Pegasus and 3 stopovers, this is the most budget-friendly option to cross the Atlantic to Brazil.

Price and when to book

Prices range from €1281 to €1773 return. Book as early as possible: the sooner you purchase, the better the fare. Avoid peak dates (Christmas, Carnival) if you're hunting for the lowest prices.

Airline and stopovers

Pegasus is the carrier offering these prices. The flight includes 3 stopovers on the Dubai-Rio route, which increases total travel time. If you prefer fewer stops, check other options, but expect to pay more.

Best time to visit

Rio is a beach and culture destination year-round. The driest months are May to September, ideal if you want clear skies. Travel outside peak season (January-February and July) and you'll find better deals on accommodation and activities.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Rio's international airport (Galeão). From there, the city is accessible by bus, taxi or private transfer. Don't miss Christ the Redeemer, Sugarloaf Mountain, the beaches of Copacabana and Ipanema, or the bohemian neighbourhood of Lapa. Travel in February and Carnival is unmissable.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price from Dubai to Rio?

From €1281 return with Pegasus. This is the lowest found; most searches centre around €1383.

Is the flight direct or with stopovers?

The flight has 3 stopovers. It's not direct, so total travel time will be substantial. It's the most budget option, but requires more time in the air and on the ground.

Which airline operates this route?

Pegasus is the carrier offering these prices. It's a Turkish low-cost airline with competitive fares on international routes.

What's the best month to fly from Dubai to Rio?

May to September offers dry weather. January-February is Carnival (spectacular but pricey). July is also peak season. April, October and November are shoulder months with fewer crowds and lower fares.
