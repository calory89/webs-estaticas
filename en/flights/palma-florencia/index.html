Price and when to book

The lowest price we've seen is €136 per person (return). Median prices hover around €151. Fares vary by date: the closer to your trip, the more expensive. If you travel in low season (January, February, November), you'll find tighter rates. Book at least 6–8 weeks ahead to secure the best price.

Airline and stops

This route is operated by Vueling with one stop. There are no direct flights Palma–Florence, so you'll have an intermediate connection that adds 2–4 hours depending on the layover. Vueling is a low-cost, reliable carrier; check your carry-on allowance to avoid surprises at the desk.

Best time to visit

October, November, March and April are ideal: pleasant weather, fewer crowds than summer, and lower fares. Skip December and July–August if you want to save. Winter (January–February) is very cheap but cold. Check if any festivals in Florence align with your dates.

What to do when you arrive in Florence

Florence is small but packed. The Duomo, Uffizi Gallery, Ponte Vecchio and Palazzo Pitti are essential. Walk the old city, climb Brunelleschi's dome, grab coffee in a piazza. Eat fresh pasta and try bistecca alla fiorentina. Florence airport (Peretola) is 4 km away; a bus or taxi gets you to the centre in 20 minutes.
