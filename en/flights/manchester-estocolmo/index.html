Best time to visit

Stockholm is beautiful in late autumn and spring. November offers low prices like these examples. Winter is very cold (down to -10°C) but magical if you fancy snow. Summer is peak season and pricier. For good prices and moderate weather, April–May or September–October work best.

What to do when you arrive

Stockholm is a captivating capital: Gamla Stan (medieval old town), the Royal Palace, museums like Vasa and art galleries. Ferries connect to archipelago islands. Stroll Djurgården, eat at markets and learn Swedish history. With one stop you won't lose much time: nearby airports get you to the city fast.
