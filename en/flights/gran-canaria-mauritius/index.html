Price and when to book

The cheapest flight costs €937 return, median price €974. Fares range from €937 to €1,040 depending on dates. Book in advance: the earlier you purchase, the better the rate. October and November often offer good value before demand rises heading into December.

Airline and stopovers

Vueling is your only option on this route. It includes 1 stopover on the outbound flight, which extends your journey but reduces the fare compared to direct flights. The return also has a technical stop. Not the quickest option, but the savings are worth it.

Best time to visit

Mauritius has pleasant weather year-round. The southern summer (November to March) is warmer and more humid; winter (May to October) offers comfortable temperatures and less rain. Avoid January and February if you prefer fewer showers. Book months ahead to secure a low fare.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport (MRU), about 60 km north of the island. Rent a car or take a shuttle from there. Don't miss Port Louis (the capital), Beau Bassin beaches, the coral reef at Grand Baie, or the Pamplemousses Botanical Garden. Central Mauritius is perfect for diving and snorkelling.
