Vueling offers direct flights between Palma and Paris from €51 return per person. Median prices hover around €60, so you have budget-friendly options to travel in December. Book early because this route fills up fast.

Price: when and how to book

Tickets start from €51 return (base fare, one person). Median prices sit around €60, so there's fluctuation. Check the data as of 22 October 2026: if you spot a similar price, don't wait because Vueling tends to raise fares quickly on short routes. Book directly on Vueling's website or via flight comparison sites. Example journeys depart 5 December (outbound at 20:00) and return 15 December (departure at 07:10), perfect for a week-long Paris break.

Airline and stops

Vueling operates this route with direct flights, no stops. That means you depart Palma de Mallorca and arrive in Paris without changing planes. Vueling is a budget carrier, so baggage allowance is minimal (check what's included in your fare), but at this price it's what you'd expect. The airline is reliable and punctual on this route.

Best time to visit Paris

December is high season but flights are still affordable if you book ahead. The city is decorated and full of festive cheer, though it's cold (4–8 °C) and rainy. For fewer crowds and lower fares, try May or September on future searches. Summer prices spike sharply and it gets very busy.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at a Paris airport (CDG, Orly or similar depending on your flight time). The city centre is well connected by train and metro, roughly 30 minutes away. Explore Île de la Cité, climb Notre-Dame Cathedral, stroll the Champs-Élysées and visit the museums. In December, Christmas markets are essential. Eat in a traditional bistro and grab coffee at a café. A week gives you time to enjoy the city properly.
