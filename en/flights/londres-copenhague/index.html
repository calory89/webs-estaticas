Price and when to book your flight

Prices range from 85€ to 108€ return depending on your chosen date. The longer you wait, the dearer they get: book at least 2–3 months ahead if you want the lowest fares. We've seen example flights departing on 8 January at 11:15 and returning two days later at 08:30.

Airline and whether there are stops

Norwegian Air Sweden operates this route non-stop: no plane changes, no waiting around. It's the quickest way from London to Copenhagen. A smooth, straightforward journey for those who value time.

Best time to visit

Copenhagen shines all year round. The warmest months (June to August) pull in more tourists and typically push fares up. For decent price and good weather, aim for May or September. Winter is bitter cold but the city glows in December. Avoid school holiday peaks if you're after cheap tickets.

What to do in Copenhagen

Don't miss Nyhavn, the historic harbour with colourful townhouses. Amalienborg Palace, the royal residence, and Tivoli Gardens are essentials. Cycle through Christianshavn like the locals do, taste smørrebrød sandwiches and wind down on a rooftop with a view. The city is compact and easily walkable or bikeable.
