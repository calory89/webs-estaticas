Best time to visit

Fort Lauderdale has subtropical weather: dry and warm winters (December to April) and hot, humid summers (June to August). October and November offer good balance before peak season. Avoid hurricane season (June to October) for greater safety.

What to do on arrival

Fort Lauderdale is your gateway to Broward's beaches and Miami. Explore the historic district, Las Olas Boulevard shops or cruises from the port. The region offers nature parks, nightlife and access to the Florida Keys. The airport (FLL) is well connected to the city centre.
