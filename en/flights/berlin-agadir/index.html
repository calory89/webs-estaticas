Best time to travel

Agadir is pleasant year-round, but November to March offers mild temperatures and less intense heat. Skip July and August if you don't handle heat well. Book well in advance for better prices, especially in peak season.

What to do when you arrive

Agadir is your gateway to Morocco's coast. Enjoy its beaches, explore the medina, visit the fishing port and taste local food. From there you can take excursions to the Draa valley or the Atlas mountains. The airport is well connected to the city centre.
