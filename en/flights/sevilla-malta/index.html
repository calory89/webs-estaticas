Best time to visit

Malta enjoys a Mediterranean climate year-round. Spring (March to May) and autumn (October to November) are ideal: pleasant temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. If you want sea and sun without the masses, skip July and August. Winter is mild, so it's viable if you're chasing bargains.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Malta Airport (MLA), 5 km from Valletta. Valletta, the capital, is a UNESCO heritage site and unmissable: stone alleyways, churches and Mediterranean views. Explore Mdina (the ancient city), Comino's beaches and Popeye Village. Three islands in one: Malta, Gozo and Comino. Rent a car or use local buses to reach anywhere quickly.

What should I see in Malta in 4 days?

Spend one day in Valletta and its churches; another in Mdina and nearby villages; a third at Comino beaches or Popeye Village. On the fourth, rest or explore the south: Marsaxlokk, Ghar Dalam or the Blue Grotto.
