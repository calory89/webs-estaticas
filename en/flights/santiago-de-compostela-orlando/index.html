Best time to visit

Orlando is a year-round destination, but prices are generally more competitive outside school holidays and peak periods (Easter, Christmas, summer). If you can travel between September and October or February and March, you'll likely find better rates. October is a solid choice: fewer crowds at theme parks and more reasonable fares than peak season.

What to do when you arrive

Orlando is famous for its theme parks: Disney World, Universal Studios and SeaWorld are the main draws. For something quieter, downtown offers restaurants and nightlife. Orlando International Airport (MCO) is about 25 km from the centre, plan for transport (car rental, shuttle or taxi). Many travellers combine Orlando with other Florida cities like Miami or Key West.
