Best time to visit

Kyoto shines in spring (March–May, cherry blossoms) and autumn (October–November, autumn colours). Both are peak seasons but the weather is perfect. Winter (January–February) and summer (July–August) often have cheaper flights, though conditions are less ideal.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in Osaka (Kansai International Airport). Kyoto is 75 minutes away by train. Visit the temples of Arashiyama, the Golden Pavilion, Fushimi Inari with its thousands of red torii gates. Enjoy local cuisine, street markets and traditional gardens. Kyoto is Japan's cultural heart.
