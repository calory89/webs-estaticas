Airline and stops

Best time to visit

The Azores enjoy a temperate climate year-round. May to September brings more sunshine and less rain, ideal for beaches and hiking. If you travel in winter (December to February) you'll dodge crowds, though the weather is less predictable. Pick based on what suits you.

What to do when you arrive

The Azores are a nature-lover's dream. Explore volcanic lakes on São Miguel, walk the cliffs of Faial, spot whales in the Atlantic, or soak in natural hot springs on Graciosa. Every island has its own appeal. Ponta Delgada is your arrival airport, and excursions to the whole region are easily arranged from there.

The islands are perfect for hiking, whale watching, natural pools, and volcanic lakes. São Miguel is the largest, with Ponta Delgada as the main hub. Each island has its own character, from quiet beaches to dramatic cliffs.
