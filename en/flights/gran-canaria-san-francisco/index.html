Best time to visit

San Francisco enjoys stable weather year-round, but September and October typically offer pleasant temperatures and less fog. Summer is peak season and tends to push ticket prices higher. Travelling in winter or spring can be more economical.

What to do when you arrive

San Francisco has the Golden Gate, Alcatraz, cable cars and bohemian neighbourhoods like Mission and Castro waiting for you. The international airport (SFO) is 25 km from the city centre. Train, bus or car will get you downtown quickly. Spend at least 3–4 days exploring the city; if you have more time, venture to Big Sur, Yosemite or the Wine Country.
