Best time to visit Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka has two monsoon seasons: southwest (May-September) and northeast (December-February). The best time depends on where you go. The west and south coasts work best November to March; the north and east, April to September. July and August tend to have fewer tourists and lower prices, though rainfall is heavier.

What to do in Colombo and beyond

Colombo is your gateway to Sri Lanka. Walk along Galle Face Green, visit Kelaniya Temple or the National Museum. From there, you can reach the tea plantations of Nuwara Eliya, Mirissa and Arugambe beaches, the ancient kingdom of Polonnaruwa, or the Cultural Triangle (Sigiriya, Dambulla). Hire a car or use local buses—they're very affordable.
