Best time to visit Oslo from Lisbon

Oslo shines in summer (June-August) with bright evenings and outdoor activities. Winter (December-February) brings snow and northern lights, but cold weather. Prices tend to be higher during peak tourist season. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer a good mix of pleasant weather and moderate fares.

What to see and do in Oslo

You'll land at Oslo Airport (Gardermoen), 64 km from the city centre. Must-sees: Munch Museum, Oslo Opera House and City Hall. Explore Grünerløkka district, eat at Mathallen food market, and if you're there in winter, search for northern lights outside the city. Norway is expensive, so budget carefully for food and transport.
