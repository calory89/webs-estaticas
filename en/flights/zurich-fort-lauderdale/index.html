Best time to visit

Fort Lauderdale is a year-round beach destination. October to April is ideal: perfect weather, no hurricane season, and less rain. May to September is hurricane season and hotter. Christmas and New Year push prices up sharply, so consider November or January–March instead.

What to do when you arrive

Fort Lauderdale offers white-sand beaches, vibrant nightlife, and cruise ships. Las Olas Beach and Port Everglades are close to the airport. You can also explore the Everglades, visit Miami (30 minutes away), or simply relax on the Florida coast. The airport (FLL) has taxis, buses, and car rental options.
