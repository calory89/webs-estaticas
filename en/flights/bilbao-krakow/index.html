Best time to visit

Krakow is stunning year-round, but each season has its own appeal. Off-peak months typically offer better prices. Plan according to your preferences: spring and autumn provide pleasant weather; winter brings snow (magical city views); summer is high season with higher prices. Book in advance regardless of when you're travelling.

What to do when you arrive in Krakow

Krakow airport (KRK) is about 15 km from the city centre. The medieval old town is a UNESCO World Heritage Site: explore the Main Market Square, visit Wawel Castle, wander the Jewish Quarter, and enjoy the vibrant café culture. Krakow is very walkable with exceptional cultural attractions. Local prices are budget-friendly, perfect for backpackers and cost-conscious travellers.
