Best time to travel

Sri Lanka has good weather June to September on the west and southwest coast, and December to March on the east coast. Outside monsoon seasons, the island is accessible year-round. Check the annual calendar to avoid heavy rains depending on your region. Flight prices depend more on demand than season.

What to do on arrival in Colombo

You land at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB), 30 km north of Colombo. The city has beaches, Buddhist temples and colonial markets. From here you reach the whole island: southern beaches, Kandy mountains, Sinharaja reserve. Rent a car or use buses to explore. Sri Lanka is compact—you can cover it well in one or two weeks.
