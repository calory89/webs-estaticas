Flights start from €740 return. The median price sits at €824, though options go up to €1,212 depending on dates. The earlier you book, the better price you'll find: these long-haul routes with stops are cheaper when you reserve 2-3 months ahead. Check prices midweek; weekends tend to be pricier.

You'll travel with Ryanair on a route with 2 stops. This low-cost model keeps fares competitive, but expect longer total travel time than a direct flight. Stops are technical layovers where you'll change planes. Check baggage allowance on Ryanair's website: many fares include cabin baggage only.

Best time to travel

Sri Lanka has a tropical climate. Flight prices vary by demand, not season. What matters is your destination: check the best time to visit Colombo and Sri Lanka before you go. Regardless of when you travel, buy your ticket well in advance.

What to do when you arrive in Colombo

Colombo is the capital and gateway to Sri Lanka. From Bandaranaike Airport, hire a car, catch a bus or taxi into the city. Walk the port, visit Buddhist temples, nearby beaches or plan trips to Sigiriya, Kandy or the central highlands. Sri Lanka can be explored well in one or two weeks.
