Best time to travel

The Philippines has a tropical climate. The dry season (November to April) is best for avoiding heavy rain. December and January are popular months but prices tend to be higher due to demand. If you're after lower fares, consider May to October, though expect more rainfall. The search was made on 23 October—check dates from then onwards to compare offers.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Ninoy Aquino International Airport (MNL), Manila's main hub. From there, explore the city: Intramuros (historic walled quarter), Ayala Museum, or nearby beaches like Boracay. The Philippines offers beaches, diving, pristine islands and unique cuisine. Plan ahead which areas to visit: Manila, Palawan, Cebu and the Visayas are classic destinations.
