Best time to visit

November is a good choice to visit Mykonos outside the peak summer season. The weather is milder and the island is less crowded than in July and August. If you prefer to avoid crowds and enjoy more moderate prices, autumn and winter are recommended seasons. Note that some venues may have reduced hours outside peak season.

What to do upon arrival in Mykonos

Mykonos International Airport (JMK) is just a few kilometres from the capital. Once there, you can explore Little Venice, visit beaches such as Psarou and Paradise, see Orthodox churches and enjoy local cuisine. The port also connects to other Cycladic islands if you want to expand your trip across the region.
