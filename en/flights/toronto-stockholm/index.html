Best time to visit

Stockholm shines in summer (June to August) with long days and mild weather, and in winter (December to February) if you want to see a snowy north. November and early December offer lower prices and less tourism. Spring (April–May) is pleasant and quieter. Avoid July if you're seeking to dodge crowds.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Arlanda Airport (ARN), 20 km from the city centre. Stockholm deserves at least 3–4 days: wander through Gamla Stan (the old town), visit museums like the Vasa, climb the city hall for views, and take a ferry into the archipelago. Try crayfish in summer, Swedish meatballs, and cosy cafés.
