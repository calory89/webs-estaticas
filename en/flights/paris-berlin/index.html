Best time to visit

January is Berlin's low season: fewer tourists, cheaper accommodation and attractions. Weather is cold (around 0–5°C), but the city looks beautiful with snow. Perfect if you want to skip museum queues and crowds. Pack warm layers.

What to do when you arrive

Berlin has plenty: the Wall, Museum Island, Checkpoint Charlie, the Reichstag and its terraces. Brandenburg Airport connects easily to the city centre by train (30 min). Bars and restaurants are affordable compared to other European capitals. Rent a bike or buy an unlimited transport pass to get around smoothly.
