Price and when to book your flight to Peru

The cheapest flight we found is €701 return. The median is €759, so it's worth comparing and booking with a bit of time to spare. The earlier you purchase, the better your chances of catching a good deal. Check fares regularly: prices shift with availability.

Airline and number of stopovers

You'll travel with Ryanair. The route includes 2 stopovers, so it's not a direct flight. This explains the competitive price: it's a low-cost carrier with connections involved. Double-check where the stopovers are and how much time you have between them before you book.

Best time to visit Peru

Peru is worth visiting year-round, but the dry season (May to October) is ideal for Machu Picchu and trekking. Prices tend to be higher then. If you want to dodge crowds and save cash, consider the rainy season (November to April), though some trails may be closed.

What to do when you arrive in Lima

Lima is your gateway. Explore the old town, wander through Miraflores and eat at Peru's best restaurants. From there you can connect to Cusco (for Machu Picchu), Arequipa or travel along the coast. Spend at least 2–3 days in the capital before heading further.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for flights from London to Peru?

The lowest fare we've seen is €701 return with Ryanair. The median hovers around €759, so booking early gives you a shot at that price. Fares vary by date and availability.

Is it a direct flight London to Lima?

No, the flight includes 2 stopovers. This affects total journey time but keeps the price competitive thanks to Ryanair as the operator.

Which airline operates the London–Peru route?

Ryanair is the carrier with the cheapest fares we found. Remember it flies with 2 stopovers, so check where the connections are.

How many days should I spend in Peru to make it worthwhile?

At least 5–7 days to get the most out of it: 2–3 in Lima, 2–3 in Cusco (Machu Picchu) and time to rest. With more time, add the Amazon or the Sacred Valley.
