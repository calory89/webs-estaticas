Best time to visit Peru

Peru is worth visiting year-round, but the dry season (May to October) is ideal for Machu Picchu and trekking. Prices tend to be higher then. If you want to dodge crowds and save cash, consider the rainy season (November to April), though some trails may be closed.

What to do when you arrive in Lima

Lima is your gateway. Explore the old town, wander through Miraflores and eat at Peru's best restaurants. From there you can connect to Cusco (for Machu Picchu), Arequipa or travel along the coast. Spend at least 2–3 days in the capital before heading further.
