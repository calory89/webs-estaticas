Best time to visit

Bogotá enjoys a stable subtropical mountain climate with consistent temperatures throughout the year. There are no hurricane seasons affecting the region. Check local event calendars and consider avoiding Australian school holiday periods for better fares. Availability varies by seasonal demand.

What to do on arrival

Bogotá is Colombia's capital, set at 2,640 metres altitude. Explore the Gold Museum, Monserrate Sanctuary, the historic La Candelaria district, and vibrant street art in neighbourhoods like San Alejo. The international airport is roughly 15 km from the city centre with easy access by taxi, Uber or public transport.

What is there to see in Bogotá?

Bogotá boasts the Gold Museum (one of the world's finest pre-Hispanic art collections), Monserrate Sanctuary overlooking the city, Plaza de Bolívar, the colonial-era La Candelaria neighbourhood, and thriving street art scenes in areas like San Alejo and Usaquén.
