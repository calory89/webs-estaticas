Best time to travel

Northern hemisphere winter (November to March) is prime time for Egypt: mild temperatures of 15–25°C, without summer heat. Skip July and August. Midweek flights often offer better pricing.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Cairo International Airport, gateway to the Pyramids of Giza, the Sphinx and the Egyptian Museum. Spend days exploring Cairo, then head to Luxor and the Valley of the Kings, or cruise the Nile. Cairo buzzes day and night: bazaars, mosques and brilliant food.
