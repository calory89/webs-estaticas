Best time to visit

Warsaw shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter brings festive decorations and magic, though it's cold. Summer (June–August) is peak season and prices tend to climb.

What to do when you arrive

Warsaw deserves at least 3 days. The Old Town (Stare Miasto) is a must, along with the Market Square and Uprising routes. Don't skip the Palace of Culture and Science, the Warsaw Uprising Museum, and strolls along the Vistula. Chopin Airport is 10 km from the centre; fast trains or taxis reach it in 30 minutes.
