Best time to visit

The Azores are a year-round destination, each season with its own charm. The Atlantic climate brings frequent rain, but temperatures stay mild (12–20°C even in winter). Winter (December–February) offers lush green landscapes and fewer tourists; spring and autumn are stable and pleasant. Summer brings drier weather but higher prices and crowds. For budget travel, January through March typically offer good deals and thin tourist numbers.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in Ponta Delgada, capital of São Miguel. Explore the old town, the Basílica do Senhor Jesus Cristo, and the harbourfront. Don't miss the hot springs at Furnas, the Sete Cidades lakes, and the wild north coast. If you've got time, other islands like Faial are reachable by ferry. Local food (tuna, sweet potato) is worth the detour. Car hire is the most flexible way to explore the island.
