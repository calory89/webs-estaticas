Price and when to book

The lowest price recorded is €674 for a return ticket, whilst the median reaches €738. Fares on this route vary significantly depending on season and booking timing. Reserving 2–3 months in advance increases your chances of finding competitive prices. Avoid booking at the last minute: prices spike dramatically in the final 2–3 weeks.

Airline and stopovers

China Southern Airlines operates this connection with 1 stopover en route to Belgrade. There are no direct flights on this route, so you'll have an intermediate stop. Whilst it means more travel time, stopovers are standard on long-haul intercontinental routes and allow you to travel at a more competitive price.

Best time to visit

Singapore has a tropical climate year-round, so any time works. Belgrade enjoys spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) with pleasant temperatures. Summer months in Belgrade can be scorching. Avoid travelling in peak winter if you prefer to dodge freezing temperatures in the Serbian capital.

What to see when you arrive

Belgrade is Serbia's cultural and historical heart. Visit Kalemegdan Fortress with views over the Danube, walk through the bohemian Žižkovac district and explore the Austro-Hungarian architecture of the centre. The Church of Saint Sava is a must, and restaurants and bars lining the Danube are perfect for sunset relaxation. Belgrade's nightlife is legendary.
