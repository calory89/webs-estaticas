Best time to visit

December brings festive vibes to Las Vegas, though it's peak season. If you prefer fewer crowds and pleasant weather, try May or September; October is lovely too. Skip July and August if you can't handle 45°C desert heat. Low season fares dip slightly, but €841 is already excellent value.

What to do when you arrive

Las Vegas is far more than casinos: the Strip is essential, but don't miss Fremont Street Experience. Red Rock Canyon is 30 km away; the Grand Canyon (with Skywalk) is slightly further. The Neon Museum is brilliant for curious minds. Stay slightly off-centre to save on accommodation. Hire a car to explore Nevada's desert freely.
