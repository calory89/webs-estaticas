Price and when to book

Prices range from €701 to €778. Booking 1–2 months ahead usually delivers better fares; skip last-minute impulse buys. Check regularly: Ryanair pricing shifts with demand, flight day of week and season.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route. Your outbound flight includes 1 stopover, so it's not a direct flight. Use that layover time to change terminals if needed or grab a quick bite. Return flight departs in the morning at 13:10.

Best time to visit

South Korea shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather and stunning landscapes. Winters can be harsh (freezing) and summers humid and wet. Pick your season based on what you want to see, and book with lead time to negotiate price.

What to do when you land in Seoul

You arrive at Incheon (ICN), 60 km from the city. Take the express train or direct bus to central Seoul (it's vast). Visit Gyeongbokgung Palace, Myeongdong for shopping and street food, Hangang Park at sunset, and markets like Dongdaemun. Korean cuisine is affordable and excellent on a budget.

From €701 return. Our sample prices are €701, €709 and €778, so you can catch that minimum if you book at the right moment.
