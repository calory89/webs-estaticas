Best time to visit

Phu Quoc's finest weather runs from October to March, with mild temperatures and little rain. These months are peak tourism season, which reflects prices like €657 median in December. To save, avoid Spanish holidays and bridges. The island is perfect November through February.

What to do when you arrive

Phu Quoc is a Vietnamese island famous for pristine beaches, luxury resorts and laid-back vibes. Visit the National Park, explore Phu Quoc Prison (historical site), taste local fish sauce, or relax at Long Beach and Sao Beach. It's an ideal spot to unwind after your long journey.
