From €641 you get a return Rome-Maldives ticket. Prices in our sample range from €641 to €659, so there's no wild swing. Airline and stopovers

Etihad Airways is your carrier on this route. Flights have 1 stopover (not direct), which is standard for such long-haul journeys from Europe. Expect one intermediate stop, but it's a fair trade-off for flying cheap to such a distant destination.

Best time to visit

Maldives has two distinct seasons. The dry season (November to April) is ideal: clear skies, calm seas, and perfect visibility for diving. May to October brings more rain, but prices drop and tourism thins out. Choose based on your priorities: good weather or better value.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Velana International Airport (MLE), your gateway to the Maldives. From here, speedboats and seaplanes whisk you away to resort atolls. Diving, snorkelling, pristine beaches, and coral reefs are your main draws. Travelling in dry season means underwater conditions will be exceptional.
