Best time to visit

Warsaw is worth visiting year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather without harsh winters or summer heat. Prices tend to be lower in off-season (November–March), though temperatures drop below freezing. If you travel in January or February, expect very cold conditions.

What to do when you arrive

Warsaw Chopin Airport is 10 km from the city centre. Take the S2 train or bus to reach the centre in 20–30 minutes. Don't miss the Old Town (Stare Miasto), rebuilt after WWII, the Palace of Culture and Science, the Jewish quarter and the Vistula for evening walks. Warsaw has quality museums, street markets and authentic Polish food.
