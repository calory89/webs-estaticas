Best time to visit

Los Angeles has pleasant weather year-round, but avoid peak summer (June–August) if you want fewer crowds. Autumn (September–November) and winter (December–February) are ideal: mild temps, fewer tourists, clearer skies. November, like in the example, is a strong window.

What to do when you arrive

LAX is about 30 km from central LA. Santa Monica Beach, Griffith Observatory, Hollywood, Downtown LA and Venice Beach are must-sees. Rent a car for freedom; public transport exists but LA is sprawling. Spend 5–7 days to enjoy it properly.
