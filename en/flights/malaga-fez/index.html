Best time to visit

Autumn and winter (October to March) offer the most pleasant weather for Fez: mild temperatures, less extreme heat than summer, and fewer crowds in many areas. November is particularly good. Book 2–3 months ahead for the best fares.

What to do when you arrive

Fez is one of Morocco's most authentic imperial cities. The Medina (old town) is a UNESCO World Heritage Site: wandering through its souks, visiting the al-Qarawiyyin Mosque, and exploring the historic leather tanneries are must-dos. Also worth visiting are the Kasbah of the Sharifs and the Royal Palace. Fez airport is about 15 km from the city centre; taxis and transfers are available.
