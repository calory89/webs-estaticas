Best time to visit

Mykonos shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): pleasant weather, nice beaches and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is cheaper and quieter, though many venues close. July–August is peak season with busy beaches and top prices. January–February often has good value deals.

What to do when you arrive

The airport is 2 km from the port. Explore Mykonos Town's historic centre with its whitewashed lanes. Famous beaches: Paradise, Super Paradise, Psarou. Eat at local tavernas by the harbour. Watch sunsets in Little Venice. Take day trips to ancient Delos and Rheneia. Nightlife in bars and clubs if you fancy it. Bring sunscreen and water.
