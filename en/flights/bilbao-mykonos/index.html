Airline and stops

Vueling operates this route. Be aware the outbound flight has 2 stops, so it's not direct: add 5–8 hours depending on connection times. The return will have its own connection pattern. Not the fastest, but the price makes up for it.

Best time to travel

December in Mykonos is low season: quieter beaches, cheaper hotels and restaurants, mild weather (around 15–18°C). If you want sun and swimming, wait for May–September, but expect to pay much more. December is perfect if you want the Cyclades without the crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Mykonos is known for its beaches (Paradise Beach, Super Paradise), the white village of Chora with its windmills, and nightlife. The airport (JMK) is about 2 km from the port. Ferries from there reach other Cycladic islands (Delos, Tinos) in 30–45 minutes. December isn't beach season, but perfect for relaxed sightseeing.

FAQ

What's the cheapest flight price Bilbao-Mykonos?

€273 return is the best price on offer now (October search). Median sits at €280, so you'll find options at that level. Higher fares reach around €302.

Are there direct flights Bilbao-Mykonos?

No, this route has 2 stops with Vueling. You'll make intermediate stops before reaching Mykonos. Total journey time will be longer, but that's how these low prices are possible.

What month is cheapest for Mykonos?

We can't say which month is cheapest, but December (when you see these prices) is low season in Greece. Booking with flexible dates usually gets you better rates than targeting specific months.

What's there to see in Mykonos besides beaches?

The town of Chora with white Cycladic architecture, the windmills, Paraportiani church. Ferries from the port go to Delos (ancient ruins) and other islands. Cultural and food scene is strong year-round.
