Best time to visit

Luxembourg is lovely in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) when the weather is mild and the city feels relaxed. Winter is cold but magical if you fancy Christmas markets. Summer brings more tourists and prices may rise. Avoid European school holidays if you're after better prices.

What to do on arrival

Explore the UNESCO-listed Old Town with its fortresses and Petrusse Gorge views. Visit Notre-Dame Cathedral and the Grand-Ducal Palace. Head to the Corniche viewpoint for stunning photos. Try local specialities like luxemburgeois pastries at any café. The city is compact and easily walkable or navigable by public transport.
