Best time to visit

Belgrade appeals year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is cheaper but cold. Avoid August if you want peace and quiet, as the city fills with tourists. Check availability for your dates to find the best balance of price and climate.

What to do on arrival

Belgrade awaits with Kalemegdan Fortress, the Danube and intense nightlife. Stroll the old town, try local food in Skadarlija and unwind at one of the river spas. Nikola Tesla International Airport is 12 km from the centre; taxis and buses connect you quickly to the city.

What to see in Belgrade in 3 days?

Visit Kalemegdan, walk along the Danube, explore Stari grad (old town) and experience the city's nightlife. Don't miss Skadarlija, the traditional bohemian quarter, and spend time in local museums if you're interested in Balkan history.
