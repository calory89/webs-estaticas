Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €135 for a return ticket. The median price is €140, and we've also recorded fares at €168. Book early: airlines raise prices closer to departure. Purchase 6–8 weeks ahead to maximize savings.

Airline and stopovers

Fly with Vueling, Spain's budget airline. This route includes 1 stopover, so it's not direct. The journey is reasonable for the distance: you'll have one intermediate stop that extends travel time, but it's an economical option from Bilbao.

Best time to visit

Belgrade is lovely in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is cold but the city has festive charm. Skip July and August if you prefer to avoid heat and peak tourism, though flights tend to be pricier anyway.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Belgrade 'Nikola Tesla' Airport, 12 km from the centre. Stari Grad (Old Town) is a must: Kalemegdan Fortress, Church of Saint Sava, traditional markets. Enjoy local food in Dorćol and Beton Hala, walk along the Danube, and explore Belgrade's vibrant nightlife.
