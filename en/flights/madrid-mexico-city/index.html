Best time to travel

Peak season in Mexico City runs November to March: dry weather, sunny days, pleasant temperatures (15–25°C). December and January spike in price due to Christmas and New Year. For quieter crowds and lower fares, consider May–September, though afternoons see more rain. Avoid booking last-minute; give yourself time and budget flexibility.

What to do in Mexico City

The capital offers world-class museums (Frida Kahlo, National Anthropology), a UNESCO-listed historic centre, globally-recognised cuisine, and vibrant nightlife. The Zócalo, Coyoacán, Xochimilco and local markets are must-sees. The airport is about 12 km from the centre; use official transport or private transfer.
