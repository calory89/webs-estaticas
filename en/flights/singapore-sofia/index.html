Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €629 per person return. Average prices sit around €706, and can rise to €719 depending on availability. For this long-haul route, booking 2–3 months ahead is ideal. Prices are often more competitive mid-week. If your dates are flexible, try different combinations: small shifts of 1–2 days can deliver significant savings.

Airline and stopovers

Scoot operates this route with 1 stopover. There are no direct flights available on this connection. The stopover is typical on routes linking Asia with Eastern Europe, and usually lasts 2–4 hours depending on the intermediate hub. Scoot is a low-cost carrier, so remember that baggage, seat selection and onboard services carry extra charges.

Best time to visit

Sofia is pleasant in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather and fewer tourists than summer. July and August are pricier and busier. Winter (December–February) is cold but offers lower fares if you avoid Christmas dates. There's no particular low season where prices drop dramatically on this route; the key is booking early, regardless of when you travel.

What to do in Sofia

Sofia is Bulgaria's capital, featuring Ottoman heritage architecture, Orthodox churches (including Alexander Nevski Cathedral), museums and affordable nightlife. Sofia Vasil Levski Airport is 10 km from the centre; bus, taxi or private transfer get you into town in 30–40 minutes. The city works well combined with trips to Bansko (mountains) or Croatian Plitvice.
