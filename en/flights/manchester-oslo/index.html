Best time to visit

Oslo shines June to August (long days, mild weather) and winter if you chase the northern lights. December and January are cold but magical. Skip peak dates if you want peace. Booking early keeps prices down—they climb as departure nears.

What to do when you arrive

Gardermoen airport sits 50 km from the city centre. Oslo rewards walks along the fjord, the Viking Ship Museum, Vigeland park (Europe's largest sculpture park) and Nordic pubs. Stroll Grünerløkka, try fresh salmon, soak in modern architecture. Norway isn't cheap, but it's worth it.
