Travel from Dublin to Los Angeles for from €540 return with SAS. The median price is €648, with options up to €850. Book well in advance for the best fares across the Atlantic.

Price and when to book

Prices start at €540, but the median sits at €648, with fares reaching €850. The route checked on 22 October shows departures in December. Book 6-8 weeks ahead for the best transatlantic rates. Prices climb as the date approaches, especially during the Christmas period.

Airline and stopovers

SAS (Scandinavian Airlines) operates this route with one stop on the outbound leg. It's not a direct flight, but typical for transatlantic journeys from Dublin. The return departs at 19:30 from Los Angeles. Check exact schedules when you book, as the stopover may be Copenhagen, Oslo or Stockholm depending on the connection.

Best time to travel

European winter (December to February) is peak demand from Dublin to California, pushing prices up. Spring and autumn are more affordable. Note that Los Angeles has pleasant weather year-round: mild winters (15–20°C) and hot summers (28–30°C). Avoid school holiday periods for better rates.

What to do when you arrive

Los Angeles offers beaches in Santa Monica and Malibu, the iconic Hollywood sign, Downtown LA culture and museums like the Getty. LAX airport is 30 km from the city centre; use public transport, shuttle or car rental. Spend at least 4–5 days to enjoy it without rushing.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Dublin to Los Angeles?

Not on this route. SAS operates with one stop on the outbound leg. Direct flights from Dublin to LAX are limited and typically with other carriers.

Why does it cost more than €540?

€540 is the base price, but the median of €648 reflects reality: specific dates, preferred times and proximity to holidays push fares up. Look for flexibility in your travel dates.

What does the SAS ticket include on this route?

It varies by fare type: baggage, meals and services differ. Check the details when booking; many include only hand luggage as standard.

Which airport serves Los Angeles?

LAX (Los Angeles International Airport), the city's main hub. It's well connected by public transport, taxis and car rental.
