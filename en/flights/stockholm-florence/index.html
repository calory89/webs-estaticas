Best time to visit

Florence is beautiful year-round, but April–May and September–October offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than July–August. Prices tend to be more moderate in low season (November–February), although it's cooler. If you fly in winter from Stockholm, you'll notice the temperature change in Florence, which enjoys milder conditions.

What to do in Florence

The Cathedral of Santa Maria del Fiore, the Uffizi, Ponte Vecchio and the Accademia Gallery (home to Michelangelo's David) are unmissable. Walk around the historic centre, eat at local trattorias and, if you have time, head to Piazzale Michelangelo for sunset views. Amerigo Vespucci Airport is 4 km from the centre, easily reached by bus or taxi.

What to see in Florence in 2–3 days?

Day 1: Duomo, Baptistry and Campanile. Day 2: Uffizi and Ponte Vecchio. Day 3: Accademia (David) and the Oltrarno neighbourhood. Spend time wandering and eating at local trattorias. Florence is compact and very walkable.
