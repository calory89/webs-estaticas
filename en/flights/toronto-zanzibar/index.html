Best time to visit

Zanzibar has a tropical climate year-round. The main dry season runs June to October with less rain and pleasant temperatures. December to March is another dry period but warmer and busier with tourists, which can push up hotel and activity prices. Avoid April, May and November if you prefer fewer showers. What to do when you arrive

Zanzibar is renowned for white-sand beaches, the UNESCO-listed Stone Town old town, spice markets and coral reefs. It's ideal for diving, snorkelling, traditional dhow sailing and beach relaxation. Zanzibar International Airport (ZNZ) is roughly 50 km from Stone Town; reach it by taxi, bus or private transfer.
