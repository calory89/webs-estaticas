Best time to visit Zanzibar

Zanzibar has a tropical climate. The dry season is ideal (June to October and January to February), with less rain and pleasant temperatures. November, when your example flight departs, is in the transition: moderate rainfall but less crowded beaches. If you're after peaceful shores and reasonable prices, November and December are worth considering. Avoid April and May (heavy rains).

What to do in Zanzibar

White-sand beaches at Nungwi and Paje, snorkelling in coral reefs, dhow sailing trips (traditional sailing boats), the historic medina of Stone Town with narrow streets and bazaars. You can also explore Pemba and Mafia islands from Zanzibar. It's a world-class diving destination and a gateway for expeditions into northern Tanzania.
