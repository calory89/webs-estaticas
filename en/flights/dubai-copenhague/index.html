Best time to visit Copenhagen

Denmark shines in spring (April–May) and summer (June–August) when weather is pleasant and daylight extends late. For budget-conscious travel, shoulder months (September–October and March–April) are ideal: fewer crowds, moderate fares, and still-decent temperatures. Winter is dark but charming if you fancy Nordic Christmas markets.

What to do when you arrive in Copenhagen

Copenhagen Airport (CPH) is well-connected to the city centre by metro and train. Once in town, stroll Nyhavn (the historic canal with colourful row houses), visit Amalienborg Palace, cycle through trendy Norrebro and Vesterbro neighbourhoods, and don't skip Grundtvig's Church. With extra time, Tivoli Gardens is a classic must-see.
