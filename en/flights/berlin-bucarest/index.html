Best time to visit

Bucharest is pleasant in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with mild temperatures and less rain. Winter is cold but ideal if you want lower prices and fewer crowds. Avoid August if you're after cheaper fares, though the weather is best then. January to March typically offer particularly competitive rates on this route.

What to do in Bucharest

Bucharest awaits with the Palace of Parliament (the world's largest administrative building), Lipscani Street with shops and restaurants, and Stavropoleos Church. Stroll through Cismigiu Park, try Romanian cuisine at any local tavern, and climb Televiziunii Tower for panoramic views. Don't miss the art museums and nightlife on Banu Mihalache Avenue. The airport is 16 km from the centre; buses and taxis run direct.
