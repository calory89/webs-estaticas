Best time to travel

Fort Lauderdale has decent weather year-round. December is high season (pre-Christmas and mid-winter in Europe), so it's not the cheapest period, but it's workable. Avoid October and November if you want to steer clear of hurricane season. Book well ahead regardless of the season.

What to do when you arrive

Fort Lauderdale is known for beaches, cruises (major port), and marine life. Visit Las Olas Boulevard, mangrove boat tours, or day trips to the Everglades. The airport (FLL) is 30 km from the centre; public transport, taxis and car hire are all available.
