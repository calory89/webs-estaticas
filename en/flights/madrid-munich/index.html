Best time to visit

Munich is worth visiting year-round. For good weather, spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant temperatures. In winter, especially December, the city hosts traditional Christmas markets. Summer is pricier and busier. Choose based on your budget and what you'd like to see.

What to do in Munich

The city is famous for its architecture: the Church of Our Lady, the nearby Neuschwanstein Castle, the royal palaces. The English Garden is perfect for a stroll. If you love beer, try authentic Weissbier in traditional pubs. The Egyptian Museum and the Pinakothek are worth visiting too. Munich blends history, art and fine dining.
