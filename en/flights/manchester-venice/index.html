€57 return is the lowest recorded price. Most tickets fall between €57 and €75. Book as soon as you can from now on to secure cheaper options. Check Ryanair's calendar for off-peak days (typically mid-week).

Airline and stops

Ryanair is your only option on this route. Best part: direct flight, no connections. You'll depart Manchester around 19:45 and arrive in Venice early morning, roughly 06:00. Hand luggage is included; checked baggage has an extra fee.

Best time to visit

Venice is lovely year-round. Winter (December–February) is cheaper and less crowded, though colder and wetter. Spring and autumn offer good weather and reasonable fares. July and August are pricey and packed. Avoid cruise season if you want a quieter experience.

What to do when you arrive

Marco Polo airport is 13 km from the city centre. Take a bus or water taxi into Venice. Once there, explore St Mark's Square, ride a gondola through the canals, eat in Cannaregio, and visit nearby islands like Murano and Burano. Wear comfortable shoes and bring water.
