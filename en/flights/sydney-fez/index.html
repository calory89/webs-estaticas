Best time to visit

Fez offers pleasant weather nearly year-round. If you choose the milder months (October to April), you'll avoid summer heat and enjoy ideal conditions for exploring the medina. November, as in your example, is a good option: moderate temperatures and fewer crowds than peak season.

What to do when you arrive

Fez's medina is a UNESCO World Heritage site: explore the souks, craft shops and ancient mosques. The Jewish Quarter, the University of Al-Quaraouiyine and the traditional tanneries are must-sees. Spend 2–3 days wandering the old city at a leisurely pace. You can also take day trips to Méknes, Ifrane or Moulay Idrís.
