Best time to visit

Stockholm shines in summer (June–August) with long days and mild temperatures. For budget-conscious travellers, shoulder months like May and September offer good weather with fewer crowds. Winter (December–February) is magical for northern lights, though it's cold.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Stockholm Arlanda Airport, about 40 km from the city centre. Train or bus will get you in 20–45 minutes. In the city, don't miss Vasa Museum, the Royal Palace, Gamla Stan (old town) and boat trips through the archipelago. Local museum passes often offer good value.

FAQ

Yes, Ryanair operates direct flights on this route. No stops, so you'll arrive in one go.

Which airport do I land at in Stockholm?

You'll arrive at Stockholm Arlanda (ARN), the city's main airport. Train or bus connect you to the centre in under an hour.

Is Ryanair the only option?

Based on the data we have, yes. Ryanair operates this route at those prices starting from €96.

What must I see in Stockholm?

Gamla Stan (the historic old town), Vasa Museum, the Royal Palace, City Hall and boat tours through the archipelago are must-sees. Allow 3–4 days to enjoy at a relaxed pace.
