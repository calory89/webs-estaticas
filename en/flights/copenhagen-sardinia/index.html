Best time to visit

Sardinia offers beaches and scenery year-round. November, like the example, is quieter: fewer tourists, mild weather (18–22°C), and moderate prices. For warm beach days, May to September are ideal, though fares climb. Winter (December–February) is cheapest if you don't mind cooler conditions.

What to do on arrival

Cagliari airport is 4 km from the city centre. Visit the Cittadella dei Musei (panoramic views), the Basilica di San Saturnino, and beaches like Poetto. Don't miss the Costa Smeralda in the north, towns like Alghero, and local traditions. Car hire gives you freedom to explore.
