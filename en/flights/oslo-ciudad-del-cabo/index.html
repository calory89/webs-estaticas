Fly from Oslo to Cape Town with Ethiopian Airlines from €578 return. The median price sits around €666. It's the perfect route to escape to the southern tip of Africa without breaking the bank.

Price and when to book

The cheapest flights start from €578 for return journeys, though the median hovers around €666. Book at least 3–4 weeks in advance: the earlier you reserve, the better fares you'll secure. Prices climb as departure dates approach, so don't wait until the last moment.

Ethiopian Airlines and stopovers

You'll travel with Ethiopian Airlines on this route, which includes 2 stopovers. It's a long journey, but the airline offers solid value for money. Stops typically occur in Addis Ababa or other African hubs. Expect a full day of travel: departures around 18:45 and arrivals in the late afternoon the following day.

Best time to travel

Cape Town enjoys pleasant weather year-round. Southern summer (December to February) is hot; spring (September to November) and autumn (March to May) are ideal: mild temperatures, less rain, and stunning Table Mountain views.

What to do when you arrive

Cape Town is a must: don't miss Table Mountain, Cape Point, Robben Island, and Camps Bay beaches. The international airport is 24 km from the city centre; take a taxi, arrange a transfer, or hire a car. The city deserves 4–5 days minimum.
