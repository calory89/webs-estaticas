Fly Dublin to Edinburgh with Ryanair from €42 return. Median price sits at €45, giving you scope to find good deals. It's a short, direct route perfect for a weekend break or quick getaway.

Price and when to book

Flights start from €42 return. Median price is €45, with samples going up to €51. When you spot fares in this range, that's your cue to book. The earlier you buy (2–3 months ahead), the better your chances of base fares. Avoid last-minute bookings; prices climb fast.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with no stopovers—it's a direct flight from Dublin to Edinburgh. No airport waits in between. Bear in mind Ryanair charges extra for cabin bags and checked luggage, plus seat selection and changes come at a cost. Check the terms carefully before paying.

Best time to visit

Edinburgh shines year-round. October and November bring autumn and slightly softer prices than summer. December is peak Christmas season (pricey) but the city looks stunning with festive lights. Spring (April–May) and summer (June–September) push prices up due to demand, though weather is more reliable. Winter is wet but quieter and cheaper.

What to do when you land

Edinburgh Airport (EDI) is 13 km from the city centre. The tram whisks you downtown in 30 minutes. Once there, the Royal Mile is a must, the Castle dominates the skyline, and Holyrood offers great views. The Meadows are ideal for strolling. Spend 2–3 days exploring Old and New Town. Tours to the Highlands leave from the city.
