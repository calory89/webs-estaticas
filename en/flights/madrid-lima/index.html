Best time to visit

Peru is year-round, but climate varies by region. May to October is the dry season in the Andes—great for Machu Picchu and Cusco. The Amazon is wetter December–March but still worth it. Every season has its appeal; it depends what you want to explore.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Jorge Chávez Airport in Lima. Don't skip the capital—colonial architecture and world-class food. Head up to Cusco for Machu Picchu next. The Peruvian Amazon, Lake Titicaca, and Colca Canyon round out the classics. Plan ahead if you're hitting multiple sites.
