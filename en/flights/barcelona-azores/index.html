Best time to visit Azores

January is low season in Azores, which explains these competitive prices. The archipelago enjoys mild weather year-round. For drier days and sunshine, May to September are ideal; winter brings more rain but fewer tourists and lower fares. Choose based on whether you prefer beach and hiking or quieter, more relaxed trips.

What to see and do in Azores

Azores is 9 volcanic islands with black-sand beaches, crater lakes, whale watching and top-notch hiking. São Miguel is the largest; visit Ponta Delgada and Sete Cidades lakes. Terceira offers its volcanic caldera. Pico is for those keen on climbing the volcano. Bring waterproof gear: the climate is changeable. Island-hopping is easy via regional airports served by Vueling and TAP.

Which Azores island is best for a first visit?

São Miguel. It's the largest, with established tourist infrastructure, beaches, volcanoes and the capital Ponta Delgada. From here you can easily travel to other islands. For pure nature and peace, Graciosa or Faial are also excellent. Allow a week to explore 2–3 islands properly.
