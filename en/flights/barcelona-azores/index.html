Best time to travel

Azores enjoys temperate weather year-round. Summer (June to September) is warmest but also busiest. If you prefer fewer crowds and good weather without excessive heat, May and September-October are ideal. Winters (December to February) are cooler and cloudier, though less touristy.

What to do in Azores

The archipelago offers nature at its finest: whale watching, volcanic hiking, natural pools and black sand beaches. Ponta Delgada (on São Miguel island) is the main tourism hub. Dramatic cliffs, freshwater lakes in volcanic calderas and hot springs on Terceira are must-sees. Rent a car to explore the islands at your own pace.
