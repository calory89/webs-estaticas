Price and when to buy your NYC-VCE flight

The cheapest flight we've spotted is around €538 (return). Prices typically range between €538 and €609 depending on the exact date and how far in advance you book. Airline and stopovers: TAP Portugal

TAP Portugal operates this route with 2 stopovers. This means it's not a direct flight: you'll have intermediate stops before reaching Venice. With two connections, total travel time will be longer than a direct flight, but the savings are significant. Typical stopovers on this route are usually in cities like Lisbon or other European hubs.

Best time to travel to Venice

Venice is beautiful year-round, but April-May and September-October offer pleasant weather without peak summer crowds. August can be very hot and packed with tourists. Winter (December-February) is quieter and prices dip, though there's more rain and the Adriatic is cold. Plan your trip based on your preferences for weather and crowds.

What to do in Venice after landing

You'll arrive at Marco Polo Airport, 13 km from the centre. From there, take a bus, boat (vaporetto) or water taxi straight into the city. There are no cars in Venice: you'll walk, use vaporettos and gondolas. St Mark's Basilica, the Doge's Palace and the Grand Canal are essential. The bridges, squares and canals are the real attraction; spend time wandering without a fixed route.
