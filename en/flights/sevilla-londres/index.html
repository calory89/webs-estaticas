Best time to visit

London is a year-round destination, but competitive prices tend to fall in autumn and winter outside public holidays. Choose weekdays when possible: less airport traffic and potential for better fares. Avoid bank holidays and school holidays if you're chasing the cheapest tickets.

What to do when you arrive

London has 5 major airports. Check your exact terminal: some are closer to the city centre than others. Once on the ground, public transport (Underground, bus) gets you around fast. Big Ben, Tower Bridge, free museums and parks are essential. Grab proper fish and chips in a traditional neighbourhood.
