Best time to visit

Marrakech shines in autumn (September–November) and winter (December–February): mild temperatures, clear skies and the medina far more bearable than in summer. Avoid May to August if you can't handle Saharan heat. Spring is also good, though March–April can be windier. Check prices regularly as demand shifts with holidays and local events.

What to do when you arrive

Marrakech welcomes you with the Medina (Jemaa el-Fnaa bustling day and night), traditional souks, the Bahia Palace and Menara Gardens. The airport (RAK) is about 6 km from the centre. From there, taxi, bus or private transfers get you into town in 15–20 minutes. The city is compact and walkable; you don't need much more to enjoy it. If you want peace, the Atlas Mountains nearby are perfect for a day trip.
