Best time to travel

Edinburgh is worth visiting any time, but October and November offer a good balance: less crowded than summer and more reasonable prices. Avoid July–August if you're after savings. Winter (December–February) is colder and wetter, but with fewer tourists.

What to see and do in Edinburgh

Edinburgh Castle is essential, with views across the city from the top. Walk the Royal Mile, explore historic neighbourhoods and visit the Scottish Parliament building. The museums are free and of high quality. If you have time, climb Arthur's Seat for the best views at sunset.
