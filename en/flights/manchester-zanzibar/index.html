Best time to visit

Zanzibar shines from June to October, when the climate is drier and cooler. If you're travelling in December like the example, you'll hit the warm and humid season, though it's still pleasant. Avoid April and May due to heavy rains. Prices fluctuate with seasons, so book early for the best rates.

What to do when you arrive

Zanzibar is pure Indian Ocean: white-sand beaches, coral reefs and the historic Stone Town with its winding alleys. Dive, snorkel, visit uninhabited islands or unwind at east-coast resorts. Zanzibar International Airport (ZNZ) is close to major attractions. Don't miss the spice markets or the sunsets.

What is there to see in Zanzibar?

Stone Town and its historic monuments, east-coast beaches, snorkelling and diving on reefs, the islands of Pemba and Unguja, and the famous spice market. It's a complete destination for beach, adventure and culture.
