Best time to visit

Bologna shines in spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October): pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter travel means less tourism and possibly better prices, though daylight hours are shorter.

What to do when you arrive

Bologna's historic centre is just a few kilometres from the airport. Explore the medieval towers (Garisenda and Asinelli), visit the Basilica of San Petronio, and eat tortellini and ragù at any local trattoria. The porticoed galleries are perfect for a leisurely stroll.
