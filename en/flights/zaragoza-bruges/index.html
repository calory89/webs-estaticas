Best time to visit

Bruges looks good year-round, but autumn and winter stand out. October to March brings fewer crowds and lets you wander its medieval canals in peace. Winter prices tend to be lower than summer. Skip July and August if you're hunting deals.

Things to do on arrival

Bruges is small and walkable or bikeable. Its canals, cobbled squares and Gothic buildings are the real deal. Try Trappist beers, climb Belfort Tower for 360° views, see the Basilica of the Holy Blood, and lose yourself in Rozenhoedkaai. Pure medieval magic. Brussels Airport is about 100 km away; direct bus service runs there.
