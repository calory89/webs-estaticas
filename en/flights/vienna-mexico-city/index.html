Best time to travel

Mexico City is appealing year-round, but October, November and April offer good weather and less rain. Avoid hurricane season on Mexico's coast (June to October). November and December are popular but prices tend to rise, so if you want to save, consider travelling between October and April, outside the Christmas period.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Benito Juárez International Airport in Mexico City. The city centre is about 25 km away. Visit the Zócalo, Metropolitan Cathedral, Teotihuacán ruins and pre-Hispanic art museums. The city is at altitude (2,250 m), so take a day to acclimatise. The food is excellent and very affordable.
