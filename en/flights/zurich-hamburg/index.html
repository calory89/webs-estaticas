Best time to visit

Hamburg shines in summer (June–August) and at Christmas, when the port and city centre glow. To avoid price peaks and crowds, pick April–May or October–November: pleasant weather, fewer tourists and kinder fares than peak season. Winter is quiet but cold.

What to do in Hamburg

Don't miss the Port (Hafen), a UNESCO World Heritage site with spectacular views, and Speicherstadt district, packed with historic warehouses and museums. St. Michael's Church offers unique panoramas, and the Alster Lake is perfect for strolls. Eat fresh fish at waterfront restaurants and try local beer.
