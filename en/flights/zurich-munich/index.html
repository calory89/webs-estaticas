Airline and stops

Lufthansa operates this connection and flies direct from Zurich to Munich. No stops, no plane changes, no hassle. The journey is very short (around 350 km), perfect if you prefer to keep things simple.

Best time to visit

December draws leisure and business travellers alike. Temperatures are cold (0–5°C), but the city transforms with Christmas markets and festive atmosphere. If you're after lower prices, consider October–November or January–February, though the weather is harsher then.

What to do when you arrive in Munich

The airport is 28 km from the city; train and bus connections are straightforward. Don't miss Marienplatz with its neo-Gothic town hall, Nymphenburg Palace and the royal residences. Traditional beer halls (Hofbräuhaus) and local beer are a must. The Deutsches Museum is one of Europe's largest science museums.
