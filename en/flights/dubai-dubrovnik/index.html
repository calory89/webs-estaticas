Best time to visit

Dubrovnik is beautiful year-round, but the weather is warmest from May to September. To avoid crowds, consider travelling in April, May, or October. Prices tend to be lower outside summer. November and December offer a quieter experience if you don't mind cooler temperatures and occasional rain.

What to do in Dubrovnik

Walk the medieval city walls, explore the Unesco-listed Old Town, and visit Dubrovnik Cathedral. Take a ferry to the Elafiti Islands or climb up to Fort Lovrijenac. Dine at waterfront restaurants and enjoy the Adriatic. The city offers nearby beaches, baroque churches, and stunning views from every corner.
