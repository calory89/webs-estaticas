Best time to travel

Nairobi has a tropical highland climate year-round. Best months are January, February and August, when rainfall is lower and prices may shift slightly. Avoid April and May, the wettest seasons. Search the tool for fares on your specific dates.

What to do in Nairobi

The National Museum of Kenya and Giraffe Centre are must-sees. From Nairobi you can easily reach Tsavo National Park and safaris in Masai Mara. Jomo Kenyatta Airport is 18 km from the city centre. Book transfers in advance if you don't hire a car.
