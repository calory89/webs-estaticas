Best time to visit

Malta enjoys good weather almost year-round, but May to October are the sunniest and warmest months. For fewer crowds and slightly better prices, April, September and October are smart choices. Winter (November to March) brings more rain and fewer tourists, but also quieter beaches and monuments.

What to do when you arrive

Malta is small but packed with history: Valletta (capital with baroque architecture), Mnajdra megalithic temples, Mellieha beaches, the island of Comino and its crystal waters. The airport is 5 km from the centre, well served by bus and taxi. Try pastizzi (local pastries) and soak in the cultural blend that runs through the island.
