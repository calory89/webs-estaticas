Best time to visit Vietnam

November is ideal: mild temperatures, less rain than other months, and the best season for the north. The weather is dry with sunny days. Perfect for exploring Hanoi, Halong Bay, and the Mekong Delta without summer heat.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

Hanoi is your gateway to Vietnam. Explore the Old Quarter with narrow streets, Hoan Kiem Lake, and the Temple of Literature. Tours to famous Halong Bay depart from here. Don't miss night markets or street food. A classic trip also includes Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon) and Mekong Delta villages.
