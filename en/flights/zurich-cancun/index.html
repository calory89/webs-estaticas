Best time to travel

Cancun shines year-round, but November to April is best: dry weather, comfortable temperatures (25–30°C), and fewer rainy days. Avoid September and October if you want to dodge hurricane season. Beach season is pricey; for better fares, consider May to August, though it's hotter and more humid.

What to do on arrival

Cancun is your gateway to cenotes, pristine beaches, and Mayan ruins. Playa del Carmen is 45 minutes away. Tulum blends archaeology and sea. Cenotes in the Riviera Maya are a must for diving or swimming. Try ceviches and cochinita pibil at local markets. The airport is 20 km from downtown; use bus, safe taxi, or car rental to suit your plans.

FAQ

Is the Zurich to Cancun flight direct?

No. The outbound flight includes 1 stop. It's not direct, but this is the price we found starting from €776.

What's the best time to fly cheaply?

November to April offers ideal weather but is pricier. For better fares, May to August is cheaper, though hotter and more humid.

Which airline operates this route?

Condor flies ZRH–CUN with 1 stop. Condor is a reliable carrier on long-haul routes.

What can I visit in Cancun besides beaches?

Cenotes, Mayan ruins at Tulum and Chichén Itzá, Playa del Carmen, snorkelling on reefs, theme parks (Xcaret, Xel-Há), and local markets for authentic Mexican food.
