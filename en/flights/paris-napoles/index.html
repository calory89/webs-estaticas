Best time to visit

Naples is pleasant year-round, but avoid July and August if you want to escape the heat and crowds. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer good weather and fewer tourists. Book further ahead if travelling in these periods, as prices rise.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Capodichino Airport (NAP), about 7 km from the centre. Naples is the birthplace of pizza, the gateway to Pompeii and Herculaneum, and the perfect base for exploring the Amalfi Coast. Wander the historic centre, eat authentic pizza in Spaccanapoli, and visit the National Archaeological Museum.
