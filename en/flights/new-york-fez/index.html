Best time to visit Fez

Fez is pleasant year-round, but October to April is the prime season: mild temperatures, no oppressive heat. December offers perfect weather (15–20°C) and the city is less crowded than summer. Ideal for exploring the medina without battling the heat.

What to do when you arrive in Fez

The medina of Fez is a UNESCO World Heritage Site: wander its alleys, visit leather tanneries, mosques and souks. Don't miss Al Quaraouiyine University, one of the world's oldest. The new town has modern shops and restaurants. From Fez you can day-trip to Meknes, Volubilis or the Rif.
