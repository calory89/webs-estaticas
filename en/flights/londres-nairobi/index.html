Best time to visit

Nairobi is accessible year-round, but the best seasons are the dry months: June to October and January to February. Rain falls March to May and again October-November. Low season bargains often appear in April, May, and early June when fewer tourists travel.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO), Nairobi's main hub. The capital is the gateway to safaris in the Masai Mara and other reserves. Visit the Nairobi Museum, explore the old town, or catch a domestic flight to national parks. Many travellers combine city days with time on the savanna.
