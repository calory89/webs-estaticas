Best time to visit

Sardinia is a beach and nature destination. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant weather without summer's extreme heat or winter rain. July and August are peak season with crowded beaches and higher prices. For tranquillity and good weather, choose April, May or September.

What to do on arrival

Sardinia boasts spectacular coastlines: white sand beaches on the Costa Smeralda (northeast), turquoise coves in the south. The interior features mountains, traditional villages and nuragic archaeology. Cagliari airport is 4 km from the city centre. From there you can hire a car to explore the island or use local buses.
