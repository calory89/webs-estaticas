Best time to travel

Cancún is a beach and sun destination year-round. Winter season (December to March) is the most popular with the best weather, though it's also peak season. If you're looking for cheaper prices, consider May to October, though it's rainy season and early hurricane season. October and November offer the best price-to-weather ratio.

What to do when you arrive in Cancún

Cancún is the gateway to Mexico's Caribbean beaches. From the airport you can easily reach the Riviera Maya, Cozumel, Tulum and Playa del Carmen. Cancún city centre has shopping, dining and nightlife. Don't miss the cenotes, Mayan archaeological sites and nearby theme parks.
