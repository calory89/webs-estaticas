Best time to visit

Cancún is a year-round beach destination, but avoid June to October due to hurricane season. December to April offers the best conditions: dry weather, warm seas and perfect beaches. January is peak season and prices rise; consider February or March if you want good weather and steadier fares.

What to do in Cancún and nearby

Cancún is your base for Riviera Maya beaches, cenotes, Mayan ruins at Tulum and Playa del Carmen. The international airport is 25 km from the centre. From there, easy access to hotels, all-inclusive resorts and tours. Use car rentals or shuttle services to get around. Tourist areas are safe; check current news before travel.
