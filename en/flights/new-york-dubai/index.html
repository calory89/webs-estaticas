Best time to travel

Dubai is a year-round destination, but October to April offer pleasant weather (20–30 °C). May to September is very hot. Avoid school holiday weeks if you want fewer crowds at the airport and more stable prices.

What to do on arrival

Dubai combines luxury and modernity: Burj Khalifa, Emirates Palace, Dubai Mall, shopping in The Gold Souk. Beaches like Jumeirah, desert safaris, artificial islands. The international airport (DXB) is 5 km from the centre; bus, metro and taxi are available.
