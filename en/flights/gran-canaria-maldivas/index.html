Best time to travel

Maldives is a year-round destination, but each season has its appeal. The dry season (November to April) is ideal for perfect diving and clear skies. May to October brings rain, fewer tourists, and tighter prices. Remember that booking 2 months ahead gives you more date options and better fares.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Velana International Airport (MLE), Maldives' main hub. Most resorts and hotels arrange transfers by speedboat or seaplane from here. Maldives is renowned for world-class coral reefs, diving, and pristine beaches. If you stay on a local island (not a private resort), you'll have better access to local life, markets, and Maldivian cuisine.
