Best time to visit Peru

February falls in the southern summer, with high temperatures on Lima's coast. The driest season in the Andes runs May to September, ideal if you want to visit Machu Picchu without rain. These months are peak tourist season, though, so accommodation prices rise. To balance climate and budget, consider April–May or October–November.

What to do when you arrive in Lima

You'll land at Jorge Chávez International Airport, 16 km from the city centre. Lima is your springboard to explore Peru: stroll the historic centre, take a train to Machu Picchu via Cusco, venture into the Amazon, or see the Nazca Lines. Peru offers world-class food and unique archaeological routes. Affordable domestic flights connect Lima to other parts of the country.
