Best time to visit

Phu Quoc is a tropical beach destination. The best season is November to April, when it's dry and temperatures are pleasant (25–30°C). June to September is monsoon, with heavy rain. October to early November is a transition: still rainy, but less so. Your example trip (15–20 November) falls in peak dry season, perfect for beaches and outdoor activities.

What to do when you arrive

Phu Quoc is Vietnam's top island destination. Expect white-sand beaches, clear waters, diving and snorkelling on coral reefs. Visit the pearl breeding farm, tour the coast by boat, sample local cuisine at Duong Dong harbour. Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC) is 30 km from town; use shared taxis or rent a motorbike to get around the island. Budget 5 to 10 days to make the most of it.
