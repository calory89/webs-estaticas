Best time to visit

Istanbul works year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild weather and lighter crowds than summer. Winter is cooler but quieter too. Regardless of when you go, prices don't drop by month: book ahead and keep an eye on weekly swings.

What to do when you arrive

You land in Istanbul (IST). This city bridges Europe and Asia with millennia of history: Hagia Sophia, Blue Mosque, Topkapi Palace. The Grand Bazaar is a must for shopping and food. Galata and Balat are charming neighbourhoods for walking. See the Bosphorus best from a ferry crossing. Eat authentic kebab, sip Turkish tea, taste the pastries.
