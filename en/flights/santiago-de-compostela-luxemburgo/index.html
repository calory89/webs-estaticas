Best time to visit

November is a solid window: less tourism than summer, tighter prices, and late autumn in Luxembourg is picturesque. Book a few weeks ahead to secure a good fare. If you're flexible, mid-month often works out cheaper than early or late November.

What to do when you arrive

Luxembourg is small but packed. The capital's Old Town (UNESCO heritage) is unmissable, plus Grund's colourful riverside houses and the Grand Palace. Outside the city, the Ardennes deliver hiking trails and medieval villages. Public transport is free, so getting around is straightforward.
