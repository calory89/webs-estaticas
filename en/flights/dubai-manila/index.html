Best time to travel

Manila is a tropical destination year-round. The dry season (November to April) is ideal: less rain, pleasant temperatures and lower typhoon risk. If you're seeking lower fares, travelling outside peak season (May to October) can offer competitive pricing, though with higher rainfall chances.

What to do in Manila and beyond

Manila blends colonial history with modern energy. Walk through the historic Intramuros district, visit the Rizal Monument in Rizal Park, and explore colonial churches. Beaches at Boracay, Palawan and Siargao are within reach on domestic flights. Nínoy Aquino International Airport (MNL) sits 7 km from the city centre with good public transport links.
