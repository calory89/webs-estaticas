Best time to visit

South Carolina enjoys pleasant weather year-round, though autumn and winter have their own appeal: November brings mild temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. April to May and September to October are ideal if you want warm weather without summer heat. Note that hurricane season runs from June to November.

What to do in Charleston

Charleston is a destination full of character. Stroll the historic district, visit local plantations, eat seafood at the waterfront, and enjoy art galleries. Charleston Airport (CHS) is just over 20 km from the city centre. The city offers everything from museums to nearby beaches at Folly Beach or Kiawah Island.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for flights Stockholm to Charleston?

The lowest price we've seen is €739 return. The median sits at €769, so it's realistic to expect around that figure if you book in advance.

Does Icelandair fly direct from Stockholm to Charleston?

No, this route has 2 stops on the outbound journey. Icelandair connects both cities through intermediate connections.

Which airport do I arrive at in Charleston?

You arrive at Charleston International Airport (CHS), located about 20 km north of the city centre. From there you can hire a car, take a taxi, or use public transport.

When is the best time to visit Charleston?

October and November are ideal: mild temperatures, less humidity than summer, and fewer tourists than peak season. Avoid June to September if you don't cope well with extreme heat.
