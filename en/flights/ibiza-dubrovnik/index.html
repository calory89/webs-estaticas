Best time to visit

Dubrovnik shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): fewer crowds than summer and perfect weather. November is also viable if you want peace and lower prices. Skip July–August if you can't stand peak season crowds in the Old Town.

What to do when you arrive

Dubrovnik is Croatia's crown jewel: walk the medieval walls, explore the red-brick Old Town, climb Fort Lovrijenac for stunning views. Ferry to the Elafiti Islands for quiet beaches or hiking. The harbour is perfect for kayaking. Try local cuisine at waterfront restaurants.
