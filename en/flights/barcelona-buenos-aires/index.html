Best time to visit Buenos Aires

Buenos Aires has a temperate climate. Spring (September to November) and autumn (March to May) are ideal: pleasant temperatures and fewer rainy days. Southern summer (December to February) is hot and humid, though it's peak tourist season. Winter (June to August) is cooler, but still navigable. Choose based on your heat tolerance and availability; any season works for exploring the city.

What to do when you arrive in Buenos Aires

You'll land at Ministro Pistarini International Airport (also called Ezeiza), about 35 km from the centre. From there, you can take a bus, remise (metered taxi alternative) or train into town. The city has it all: La Boca with its tango and colourful houses, San Telmo with its fairs and galleries, the Recoleta neighbourhood with Parisian-style cafés, and the newly renovated Puerto Madero. Eat well, drink coffee, enjoy Argentine beef and local wine. It's a vibrant city where football, music and gastronomy are a way of life.
