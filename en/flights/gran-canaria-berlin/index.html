Best time to visit

Berlin is brilliant year-round, but autumn (September to November) and spring (March to May) offer pleasant weather without peak summer crowds. Want lower prices? Travel outside July, August and school holidays. Winter brings fewer tourists and festive decorations, plus deep-freeze temperatures—pack layers.

What to do in Berlin

Brandenburg Gate, Reichstag, Berlin Wall remnants and street art murals are must-sees. World-class museums on Museum Island, green spaces like Tiergarten, edgy neighbourhoods in Kreuzberg and Friedrichshain. Grab currywurst from a street cart, drink beer in biergärten and dive into the city's arts scene. Berlin Airport (BER) sits 22 km from the centre; buses, trains and taxis reach the historic core in 30–45 minutes.

What's the cheapest price for flights on this route?

How many days do I need for Berlin?

A 3–4 day weekend covers the essentials. A full week lets you explore museums, nightlife and alternative neighbourhoods without rushing.

What's the best month to visit Berlin?

April, May, September and October are ideal: mild weather, less rain and far fewer summer crowds. Book ahead to lock in lower fares.
