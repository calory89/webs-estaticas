Fly from Barcelona to Cape Town from €645 return with easyJet. The median price sits around €706. Book in advance and grab the best fares to South Africa's capital.

Price and when to book

The cheapest flight is around €645, though typical prices hover near €706. With easyJet, booking 2–3 months ahead is your best bet. Prices fluctuate by season and seat availability, so if you spot a good deal, don't delay.

Airline and connections

easyJet operates this route with 1 stop on the outbound flight. It's not non-stop, but it's an economical way to reach Cape Town. Always check your connection time and baggage allowance, as easyJet applies restrictions depending on your fare type.

Best time to visit

Cape Town is enjoyable nearly year-round. Southern summer (December to February) is warm and peak season. Winter (June to August) is cooler but brilliant for whale watching. Travelling in shoulder months (April–May or October–November) keeps costs down.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at OR Tambo or Cape Town airport (25 km from the centre). Don't miss Table Mountain with its cable car views, the harbour walks, Robben Island, Camps Bay beaches, and Cape Point. Rent a car to explore the coast properly.

Table Mountain, Robben Island, the harbour and waterfront, beaches like Camps Bay, and Cape Point. The city blends nature, history, and great food.
