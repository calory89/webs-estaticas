Price and when to book

Flights start from €584 return, with prices around the €587 median. Sample fares we've seen range between €584 and €720. Book as early as possible: international fares to Manila climb as departure dates approach. October and November are particularly competitive months for this route.

Airline and stopovers

Air China operates this route with 1 stopover. It's not a direct flight, but the connection is manageable on a long-haul route. The typical stopover is in Beijing, Air China's main hub. Always check your booking details to see where your connection is and how much time you have between flights.

Best time to travel

The Philippines has a tropical climate. The dry season (November to April) is ideal: less rain, pleasant temperatures, and perfect for beaches and outdoor activities. July and August bring typhoon season, so best avoided. For the lowest prices, fly in low season (May to June), though it's more humid.

What to do when you arrive

Manila is your gateway to the Philippines. Explore Intramuros, the walled old city; relax along Roxas Boulevard seafront; or visit Taal Volcano, just outside the city. From Manila, local flights and ferries connect easily to Boracay, Palawan, and Cebu. The international airport is 7 km from the centre.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for flights from Brussels to Manila?

From €584 return with Air China. This is the lowest we've found; the median sits at €587, so keep price alerts active to catch the best deal.

Does Air China fly direct from Brussels to Manila?

No, Air China operates this route with 1 stopover, typically in Beijing. Although it's not direct, it's a competitive option price-wise for this distance.

What's the best time of year to visit Manila?

November to April is ideal: dry weather, warm temperatures, and no typhoon risk. If you travel in May or June you'll save more, but it's the more humid season.

What can I see in Manila?

Intramuros (the historic walled district), Rizal Park, Roxas Boulevard, the National Museum, and Divisoria market. From there, it's easy to take day trips to Taal Volcano or hop on a ferry or short flight to other islands like Boracay or Palawan.
