Best time to visit

Spring and autumn are ideal for Paris: mild temperatures, less rain, and fewer crowds than summer. October, November, March and April offer pleasant weather without peak pricing. Winter brings snow or frost in Paris—beautiful but colder. July and August see higher fares and peak tourism.

Things to do when you arrive

Paris has it all: Eiffel Tower, the Louvre, Montmartre, Seine strolls, Le Marais shops. Paris airport (either Charles de Gaulle or Orly) is well connected by metro and train into the city. Book accommodation in advance if travelling in high season. A long weekend covers the essentials; with extra days, make the most of them.
