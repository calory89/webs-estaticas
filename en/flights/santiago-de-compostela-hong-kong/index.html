Best time to visit

Hong Kong has a subtropical climate. The best months are October to November and March to April, with pleasant temperatures and less rain. December to February is cool but dry; May to September is hot, humid and typhoon-prone. Travelling during low season (January, February, June, July) may help you find lower prices, though the weather won't be ideal.

What to do when you arrive

Hong Kong is a city of contrasts: watch Victoria Harbour at sunset, ride the Peak Tram to Victoria Peak for panoramic views, explore traditional markets in Mong Kok and Ladies Market, visit temples like Man Mo, and enjoy street food dim sum. The international airport is well connected by bus, train and metro to the city centre.
