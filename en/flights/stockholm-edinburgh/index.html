Best time to visit

Edinburgh shines in summer (June–August) with long days and mild weather, ideal for exploring. For fewer crowds, spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) strike a good balance. Winter is greyer but less touristy. Adjust your dates to match your interests: Edinburgh Festival in August or peaceful autumn.

What to do when you arrive

Edinburgh Airport is 8 km from the city centre. The Airlink train gets you there in 30 minutes. Don't miss Edinburgh Castle, the Royal Mile, Holyrood Palace and Arthur's Seat for panoramic views. Scottish beer and food deserve your time. Book museums in advance during peak season.
