Alicante and Milan are just one direct flight apart with Wizz Air Malta. Prices start from €36 return, with a median of €36 too, so you've got very competitive options for this route.

Price and when to book

This route typically costs €36–43 return depending on dates. Book early: cheap seats sell out fast on routes this affordable. Check the calendar regularly if you're travelling in peak season.

Airline and flight type

You'll fly Wizz Air Malta on a direct flight from Alicante to Milan, no stops. You depart from ALC and arrive straight in Milan. Check their website for baggage allowance; Wizz Air usually charges for checked luggage.

Best time to visit

Milan attracts business and fashion crowds year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is cheaper if you don't mind the cold, and Christmas peaks prices.

What to do in Milan

The Duomo is a must-see. Walk through the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, visit Castello Sforzesco, and grab a coffee in Brera. For museums, the Pinacoteca di Brera and La Scala theatre museum are worth hours. Eat well: Milanese food is addictive.

FAQ

Yes, Wizz Air Malta operates this route with no stops. Departure from Alicante (ALC), direct arrival in Milan.

The lowest on record is €36 return. Prices typically range from €36 to €43 depending on availability and date.

How long is the flight?

It's a short flight, under 2 hours. Being direct, you save time on connections.
