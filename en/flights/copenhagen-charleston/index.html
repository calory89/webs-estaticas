Best time to visit Charleston

Charleston is pleasant nearly year-round. Spring (March-May) and autumn (September-November) offer mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter (December-February) is cooler but less busy, and flights are often cheaper. Avoid July and August if you can't handle heat and humidity. Prices drop outside peak season, so February and September are smart choices.

What to do in Charleston

Charleston is a gem of the American South. Stroll the Historic District's colourful streets, eat fresh seafood at the harbour, visit colonial churches and historic forts like Fort Sumter. South of Broad is perfect for photography. Nearby beaches like Folly Beach and Sullivan's Island are minutes away. Don't miss the public gardens and local bourbon and sweet tea distilleries.
