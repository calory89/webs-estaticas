Quick answer: The cheapest return flight from Seville to Paris we found costs 65 € (search on 19 Nov 2026, Kiwi.com), 6% below the usual 69 €. It is a nonstop flight with Ryanair.

The cheapest flights from Seville to Paris start at €65 per person (return). The median price hovers around €69, so you have solid options for little money. Ryanair connects both cities with direct flights: late evening departures and afternoon returns.

Price and when to book

We found return flights from €65, with sample prices of €65, €69, and €81. The median sits at €69, giving you a solid reference point. Check regularly: Ryanair prices climb as your travel date approaches, especially for weekend trips. Book with enough time (at least 4–6 weeks ahead) to lock in the lowest fares.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair is your option on this route and offers direct flights (no stopovers). Typical departures leave Seville late afternoon (around 19:30) and arrive in Paris the following morning after just over 2 hours flying. The return is usually mid-afternoon (around 18:15), giving you a full day in the French capital.

Best time to visit

Paris is beautiful year-round, but spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) offer the best weather and fewer crowds than summer. February and November tend to be cheaper than peak seasons. Scan the fare calendar regularly: small date shifts can mean €10–20 savings on your ticket.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Paris airport (CDG or Orly, depending on your flight). From there, public transport reaches the centre in 30–45 minutes. Spend time at the Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, Notre-Dame Cathedral, strolls along the Seine, and neighbourhoods like Marais or Montmartre. A week lets you explore at ease; 3–4 days means focusing on essentials and maybe a Versailles trip.
