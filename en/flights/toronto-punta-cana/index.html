Flair Airlines flies you direct from Toronto to Punta Cana at prices around €315 return. The median sits at €318, so you'll find very competitive options to escape to the Caribbean without breaking the bank.

Price and when to book

Prices range between €315 and €340 return depending on dates. The typical price is €318. Book well in advance: the sooner you lock in your ticket, the better the fare. October and November often have good deals before winter and summer demand pushes prices up.

Airline and flight type

You're flying with Flair Airlines on a direct flight from Toronto to Punta Cana with no stopovers. Departure from YTO is at 09:00 and arrival at PUJ. Return flight departs at 20:04, giving you time to enjoy the destination. Flair is a budget carrier, so check what's included in your ticket type before travel.

Best time to visit

Punta Cana is a beach destination year-round, but December to March is the dry season with warm weather. June to November is hurricane season in the Caribbean, though Punta Cana is relatively protected. January is peak season and pricier; September and October tend to offer better value. Choose based on your budget and weather tolerance.

Things to do on arrival

Punta Cana is all about beaches, resorts and water activities. You'll find pristine beaches, theme parks, cenotes, diving, water sports and nightlife. The airport (PUJ) is 20 km from main resort areas. Hire a car or arrange transport to your hotel.
