Best time to visit

Nassau has tropical weather year-round. The dry season runs November to April—ideal for avoiding hurricanes and enjoying beaches. Summer (May to October) is hotter and more humid. Travel in European winter for guaranteed sunshine, though prices may rise during Christmas and New Year.

Nassau International Airport (NAS) is 10 km from the centre. Arrive in the Bahamas for white-sand beaches, snorkelling at Blue Holes, exploring the colonial capital, or day trips to nearby islands.
