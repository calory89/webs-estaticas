Best time to visit

Sofia is worth visiting any time of year. If you prefer mild weather, May-June and September-October are ideal. Winters (December-February) are cold but perfect if you want to skip the crowds and prefer the city quieter. Either way, check prices weeks before you travel: on short routes, differences can be several euros.

What to do when you arrive in Sofia

The airport is 10 km from the centre. The city offers the Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, the Basilica of Sofia, and the thermal waters of Bansko if you fancy a day trip. Eat grilled meats (mesiski), explore the bohemian quarter near Vasil Levski, and don't miss the Monument to the Motherland. Sofia is compact—walking is your best bet.
