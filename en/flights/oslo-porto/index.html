Best time to visit

Porto is pleasant almost year-round. Autumn (September to November) and spring (March to May) are ideal: mild temperatures, less rain than winter and fewer crowds than summer. For beach weather and sunshine, July and August are warmest but also busier. Winter (December to February) is damp and cool, but accommodation prices drop.

What to do in Porto

Explore Ribeira, the old town along the Douro River, a UNESCO World Heritage site. Taste Port wine at the cellars in Vila Nova de Gaia, climb Torre dos Clérigos for city views and browse Livraria Lello, one of the world's most beautiful bookshops. Matosinhos beaches are 20 minutes from the centre. Eat cheap by trying francesinha (a local sandwich) at any local tavern.

Which airline operates this route?

What is the best month to visit Porto?

October and May are perfect: mild weather, less rain and fewer crowds than summer. November is also good but colder. Avoid December–February if you dislike rain, but hotel prices will be lower then.
