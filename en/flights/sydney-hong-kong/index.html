Best time to visit

Hong Kong has a subtropical climate. October to November brings pleasant temperatures and lower humidity, ideal for walking the city and exploring. December to February is also good, though peak season. Skip June to September if you don't enjoy intense heat and moisture.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Hong Kong International Airport (HKG), well connected to the city centre by train (35 minutes). Hong Kong is a must: ride to Victoria Peak, cross by Star Ferry between the island and Kowloon, browse Temple Street and Mong Kok night markets, and eat dim sum. You can also base yourself here to visit Macau or Shenzhen on the mainland.
