Best time to visit

Naples is pleasant year-round, but prices and crowds shift seasonally. Outside peak season (June–August), expect lower fares. February, March, October and November typically see fewer tourists and lower prices. The Campania coast enjoys mild weather throughout the year.

What to do when you arrive

Naples is your gateway to the Campania region. From the airport, you reach the city centre quickly. Don't miss Pompeii, the Amalfi Coast cliffs, Capri island or Herculaneum. Try authentic Neapolitan pizza, visit the National Archaeological Museum and walk through the UNESCO-listed historic centre.
