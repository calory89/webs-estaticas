Price and when to book

Flights from Berlin to Mauritius start from €670 return, though most hover around €775. We've seen fares up to €829. The earlier you book, the less you pay: prices rise as departure approaches. For long-haul routes like this one, it's wise to purchase 2–3 months in advance.

Airline and connections

You'll fly with Condor on a route with 1 stopover. It's not a direct flight, but that's standard on this route from Berlin. The connection makes the journey longer, but the price makes up for it. Condor is an established German carrier for international flights.

Best time to travel

Mauritius is a winter destination in the Northern Hemisphere. The best period is October to April, when the climate is dry and temperatures pleasant. Avoid January and February if rain isn't your style. December is a good bet: reasonable prices and fine weather. May to September are cooler but cheaper.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Mauritius International Airport (MRU). The island boasts postcard-perfect beaches in the north and east, world-class diving and snorkelling, nature reserves like Black River Gorges, and colonial towns. Port Louis, the capital, deserves a day for museums and dining. Rent a car to explore at your own pace.
