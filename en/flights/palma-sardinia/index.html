Best time to visit Sardinia

Sardinia works as a destination almost year-round. Summer (June–August) is pricier and busy; spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant beaches without crowds. Winter is peaceful but the sea is cold for swimming. Pick based on whether you fancy sun or peace and quiet.

What to do in Cagliari and nearby

Cagliari is your gateway to white-sand beaches and turquoise water. Walk the old town, climb to the Castello district for bay views, and eat proper Sardinian food in local restaurants. Nearby you'll find Poetto beach (very popular) and coastal villages like Villasimius. The airport (CAG) is 7 km from the centre and well connected.
