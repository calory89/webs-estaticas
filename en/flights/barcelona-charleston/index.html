Best time to visit

Charleston has a subtropical climate. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: pleasant temperatures and less rain. Summer is hot and humid; winter is mild but variable. Avoid hurricane season (June–November). Choose based on your heat tolerance and budget.

What to do when you arrive

Charleston is a historic city with colonial architecture, nearby beaches and excellent local food. Stroll the historic centre, visit the Historic City Market, explore plantations, or relax at Folly Beach. The airport is 20 km from the city. Various transport options are available to reach the centre.
