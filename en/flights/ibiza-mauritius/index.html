Best time to visit

Mauritius enjoys a tropical climate year-round. The best period is May to October, when temperatures are milder and rainfall is lower. These months offer a good balance between pleasant weather and accessible prices. Avoid December to February if you prefer to escape humid heat and cyclone season.

What to do on arrival

Mauritius is a paradise for beaches and nature. Don't miss Port Louis, the capital, with its street markets and museums. The beaches at Beau Bassin and Trou aux Biches are spectacular. Visit the Pamplemousses Botanical Garden and Black River Gorges Nature Reserve. Snorkelling and diving are must-do activities on this island. If you enjoy hiking, exploring the northern cliffs is essential.
