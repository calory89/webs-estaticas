Flights from Toronto to Bucharest cost from €649 return. The median price hovers around €693. Getting to Romania is more affordable than you'd expect if you plan ahead.

Price and when to book

We found fares ranging from €649 to €713 in our sample. The median sits at €693. For this route, book at least 6–8 weeks in advance; the earlier you purchase, the better the price typically is. Check multiple search engines regularly: fares fluctuate based on availability.

Airline and stops

TAP Portugal operates these flights with 2 stops. Although not direct, it's an economical option on this route. The stops extend your journey but significantly reduce the cost compared to direct flights. Confirm the number and location of stops when booking, as they may vary by date.

Best time to visit

Bucharest shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): pleasant weather and fewer crowds. July and August are peak season, pricier and hot. Winter (December–February) is cold but magical if you enjoy snow. Since this data was checked in October, year-end flights may rise—don't delay.

What to do when you arrive

Bucharest has plenty: Revolution Square, the Village Museum (unique Romanian ethnography), stunning Orthodox churches, and vibrant nightlife. The Danube is nearby for day trips. The city is highly walkable and public transport is cheap. Museums and attractions cost little—perfect for tight budgets.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Toronto to Bucharest?

The fares we found (from €649) include 2 stops with TAP Portugal. Direct flights, if available, would cost more. These stops are why the price is competitive.

What's the best month to find cheap flights to Bucharest?

We can't say which month has lower prices without historical data. What does work is booking 6–8 weeks ahead. Avoid July, August, and December if you want the best rate.

Is TAP Portugal a reliable airline?

TAP Portugal is Portugal's national carrier with decades of history and a solid reputation. It's a dependable choice. As with any connecting flight, check total journey time and layover durations when you book.

What to see in Bucharest in 4–5 days?

Palace of Parliament, Old Town (Lipscani), Stavropoleos Church, Village Museum, Danube walks, and exploring neighbourhoods like Dorobanți. The city is very walkable and public transport is affordable.
