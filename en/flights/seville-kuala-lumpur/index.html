Best time to visit

November is a good window: stable weather, less monsoon rain than other months. Kuala Lumpur is warm year-round, but June to September sees heavier rainfall. Fly in early November to escape European winter and avoid January-February, when tourist numbers and prices climb.

What to do in Kuala Lumpur

The Petronas Towers are the city's calling card: climb to the connecting bridge for 360-degree views. Stroll the colonial district (Masjid Jamek, Merdeka Square). Jalan Alor night market buzzes with authentic street food. If city life tires you, Batu Caves sits 30 minutes away—a striking Hindu shrine carved into rock. Unwind at Bukit Nanas park.
