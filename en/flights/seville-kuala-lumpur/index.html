Best time to visit

February and March offer dry weather and pleasant temperatures in Kuala Lumpur. Avoid September and October due to higher rainfall. Flying mid-week is usually cheaper than weekends. Book 2–3 months ahead for the best fares.

What to do when you arrive

Kuala Lumpur is your gateway to Malaysia. Visit the Petronas Twin Towers, explore Petaling Street's night market, and visit Batu Caves. The international airport is about 50 km from the city centre with direct public transport. Don't miss Bukit Nanas, the urban jungle with tower views.
