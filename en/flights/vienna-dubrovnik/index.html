What to do when you arrive

Dubrovnik is known for its walled old town, beaches along the Dalmatian coast and access to islands like Lokrum. Walking the city walls, exploring the old port and discovering the surrounding area are must-do activities. Buses and transfer services run from the airport to the city centre.
