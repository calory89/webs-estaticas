Best time to visit

Rio has a tropical climate. Best months are May to September (austral winter, dry and mild). December to March is summer—hot, humid, and peak season for prices. November and April are reasonable transitions. Avoid booking last-minute if you're travelling around holidays or long weekends.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Rio de Janeiro (Galeão), about 20 km from the centre. Must-sees: Christ the Redeemer, Sugarloaf Mountain, Copacabana and Ipanema beaches, responsible favela tours, museums and nightlife. Public transport, taxis and ride apps available. Allow at least a day to recover; a week is better.

How long does the total journey take with stopovers?

What can I visit in Rio de Janeiro?
