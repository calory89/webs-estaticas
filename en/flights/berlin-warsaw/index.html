The Berlin-Warsaw route starts at €121 return, though the median price sits at €180. easyJet operates this connection with 1 stop, perfect if you're after a quick getaway between two European capitals packed with things to do.

Price and when to book

Fares range from €121 to €204 depending on availability and dates. The median is €180, a good benchmark for budgeting. Book as early as possible: easyJet releases budget seats several weeks ahead. If you spot a price near €121, grab it, those are limited to awkward times (early mornings, late nights). Summer and Christmas fares climb significantly; winter and spring are kinder to your wallet.

Airline and stops

easyJet operates this route with 1 stop on the outbound leg. It's not a direct flight, but one stop is standard at this price point. Total journey time will be longer than a direct, but the savings are worth it if you're flexible. Pack what you need in hand luggage; checked bags cost extra with easyJet.

Best time to visit

Warsaw appeals year-round. October to March (outside summer and holidays) offer the lowest fares and fewer tourists. Winter brings Christmas markets in November-December. Skip Easter and European school holidays if price is your priority. Spring (April-May) is sweet spot: decent weather, fewer crowds than summer, and moderate fares better than July-August.

What to do when you arrive

Warsaw's Chopin airport (WAW) is 10 km from the centre; direct train or bus gets you in 20-30 minutes. Spend two or three days in Poland's capital: the Old Town (UNESCO site), Warsaw Uprising Museum, Palace of Culture and Science, shopping, nightlife. Try local pierogi and barszcz at informal bars. The currency is Polish złoty, though euros work in many places. Warsaw is cheap compared to Western Europe, your budget stretches far.

When is it cheapest to fly to Warsaw?
