Best time to visit

The Azores are worth visiting year-round, but the most stable and sunny weather runs from May to September. If you travel in November, as in the example, you'll find fewer tourists and lush green landscapes thanks to Atlantic humidity. It's ideal for hiking and nature with less crowding.

What to do on arrival

You'll land in Ponta Delgada (PDL), São Miguel's capital. The Azores offer volcano hiking, thermal spring bathing, whale and dolphin watching, crater and mountain lake visits. Rent a car to explore the island or take local tours. Don't miss Lagoa das Sete Cidades or the natural pools of Porto Moniz on Madeira island (short flights from PDL).
