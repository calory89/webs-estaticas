Best time to visit

Estonia shines in summer (June to August) with long days and mild temperatures. Winter is cold but the city glows with festive lights. If you want fewer crowds and better value, shoulder months (April-May and September-October) work well. Any time of year is fine to see Tallin—pick based on your weather preference and tolerance for tourists.

What to do when you arrive

Tallin airport (Lennart Meri Tallinn Airport) is about 4 km from the centre. The Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage site: a medieval maze perfect for wandering, city walls, Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, and charming squares. Ferry trips to Helsinki or coastal villages are straightforward. Eat well—Estonian cuisine is traditional and affordable.
