Best time to visit

Florence is stunning in autumn (October–November) and spring (April–May): mild weather, fewer crowds than summer. The sample checked is November, a solid choice. Avoid July and August if you can't handle heat and long queues at the Uffizi.

What to do in Florence

The Cathedral and Duomo, Ponte Vecchio, the Uffizi Gallery (book ahead), the Accademia Gallery for Michelangelo's David. Walk through Oltrarno, eat at a local trattoria, climb to Piazzale Michelangelo at sunset. Three to four days covers the essentials; a week at a relaxed pace is better.
