Flying from Copenhagen to New York costs from €477 return with Icelandair. The median price sits around €484, so you've got solid options without breaking the bank. With one stopover, you'll reach the Big Apple for less than you'd think.

Price and when to book

The lowest price recorded is €477 return. Sample fares range from €477, €484 to €499, so the spread is quite tight. Book as far in advance as possible: the earlier you purchase, the more cheap options you'll find. Avoid the two weeks before departure, when prices jump significantly.

Airline and stopovers

Icelandair is your option on this route, with one stopover on the outbound journey. It's not non-stop, but Icelandair maintains good service standards and reliability. One stopover gives you a chance to stretch your legs, though it will add a few hours to your total travel time compared to a direct flight.

Best time to visit

New York shines year-round, but winter (January to March) has real charm: fewer crowds, lower prices, and festive vibes if you go early in the year. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer perfect weather, though prices rise. Summer is pricey and packed with tourists.

What to do when you arrive

New York awaits with Central Park, Times Square, the Met, and Manhattan's iconic skyline. Your airport (likely JFK, LaGuardia or Newark) connects easily to the city centre by subway or taxi. Walk through Brooklyn, explore the High Line, wander SoHo and Greenwich Village. Grab street pizza, sit in a classic diner, and soak up the energy of the city that never sleeps.
