Munich-Riga flights start from €170 return. We've tracked prices of €170, €173 and €224. Book at least 6-8 weeks ahead for the best rates. Off-season (October to March) tends to offer more deals.

Airline and stops

Best time to visit

December and January are excellent: fewer tourists than summer, lower fares and Riga glows with festive lights. Spring (April-May) works well too if you want mild weather. Avoid July-August if you want to pay less.

What to do in Riga

The Old Town is essential: medieval churches, cobbled squares and traditional cafés. Walk Alberta Avenue for Art Nouveau architecture, climb the Cathedral tower and stroll the Daugava riverside. Local food, markets and museums round out your visit.
