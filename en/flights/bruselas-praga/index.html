Fly Brussels to Prague with Ryanair from just €61 return. The median price hovers around €73, so you have solid options for budget travel. A classic European break at pocket-friendly rates.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €61 return, with a median of €73. Prices peak around €79. To grab the best deals, book 6–8 weeks ahead and pick dates in low season (November to March, except Christmas). Midweek flights tend to be cheaper than weekends.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight. No stops, no plane changes: you leave Brussels and arrive straight in Prague. It's the quickest and easiest way to make the journey without hassle at connecting airports.

Best time to visit

October to November and February to March offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Winter months (December except Christmas, January) are coldest but cheapest. Skip June to September if you want low fares—that's peak season in Prague. Spring (April–May) is lovely if you don't mind paying a bit more to see the city in bloom.

What to do when you arrive

Prague is pure medieval magic: the Charles Bridge, Old Town Square and the Astronomical Clock are must-sees. Climb Prague Castle for panoramic views, stroll through Nerudova, try trdelník (Czech pastry rolls) and relax in thermal baths if you head to Budapest afterwards. The airport is 20 km from the centre; bus, train or Uber are your cheapest options.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for Brussels to Prague?

From €61 return with Ryanair. This is the lowest fare we've recorded; booking well ahead in low season can yield similar deals.

Does Ryanair fly direct from Brussels to Prague?

Yes, it's a non-stop flight. You depart Brussels and arrive in Prague with no plane changes.

When's the best time to buy flights to Prague?

Book 6–8 weeks ahead if you can. Aim for October–November or February–March to mix low prices with decent weather. Avoid July and August (peak season, expensive).

What's worth seeing in Prague over a weekend?

Charles Bridge, Old Town Square, Astronomical Clock, Prague Castle and old town walks. Two to three days covers the highlights; it's a compact, walkable city.
