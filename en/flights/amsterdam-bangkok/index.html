Best time to visit

Thailand is a year-round destination, but November to February is ideal—dry season with cool temperatures. March to May is hot and humid. The monsoon season (May to October) brings fewer tourists and better hotel prices. Factor these conditions into your travel dates when booking.

What to do in Bangkok

Bangkok is chaotic, vibrant and addictive. The Grand Palace and Temple of the Emerald Buddha are must-sees. Explore floating markets (Damnoen Saduak or Amphawa), eat street food in Chinatown (Yaowarat) and treat yourself to traditional Thai massage. Day trips to ancient temples or nearby islands are also worth planning. Arrive ready to explore on foot and by tuk-tuk.

When is the best time to visit Bangkok?
