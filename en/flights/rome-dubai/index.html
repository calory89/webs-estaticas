Cheap flights Rome to Dubai: from €304 return

Rome to Dubai for just €304 return is a real chance to reach the Emirates without breaking the bank. With Royal Jordanian and a single stopover, prices stay very competitive. Book with a decent lead time and secure your seat.

Price and when to book

The lowest fare hovers around €304 for the full trip. Although the market moves constantly, the general advice is not to wait: the sooner you book, the better your chances of catching the best deals. Avoid last-minute purchases, which tend to be pricier. A sensible strategy is to search 2–3 months before your planned departure date.

Airline and stopovers

Royal Jordanian operates this route, with one stopover on the outbound leg. It's not non-stop, but a single intermediate stop is standard for this journey and won't significantly add to your total travel time. Always check baggage terms and flight change conditions before you confirm your booking.

Best time to visit

Dubai is a year-round destination, but October to April offers the most pleasant weather (20–30°C). Summer (May–September) is very hot and fares may vary. Your best bet is to pick dates outside school holidays and bank holidays, when demand and prices tend to climb.

What to do when you arrive

Dubai has iconic sights waiting: the Burj Khalifa, Palm Jumeirah and the city's traditional souks. The international airport (DXB) is well connected by metro, taxi and bus. Spend time in the markets, enjoy the beaches, and if you fancy it, book a desert excursion.

FAQ

What's the minimum fare from Rome to Dubai?

From €304 return with Royal Jordanian. This is the lowest price on record; other samples checked were around the same figure.

Is it a direct flight or with stopovers?

One stopover on the outbound leg. Not non-stop, but this is standard for the route and total flight time remains reasonable.

Which airline flies Rome to Dubai on this route?

Royal Jordanian is the carrier currently available for this connection.

When should I book to get the best price?

Ideally book 2–3 months before your trip. Don't wait until the last minute: fares typically rise as your departure date approaches.
