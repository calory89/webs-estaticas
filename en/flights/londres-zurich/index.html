Best time to visit Zurich

Zurich is lovely year-round, but prices climb in summer (June–August) and around year-end holidays. Travel in spring (April–May) or autumn (September–October) for fewer crowds and better value. Winter is ideal if you fancy festive cheer or skiing in the nearby Alps.

What to see and do when you arrive

Walk through the Altstadt (old town), visit Grossmünster cathedral and relax on the lakeside. The main train station is a perfect starting point. From Zurich, fast connections reach other Swiss cities and the Alps. Eat well: Zurich is pricey, but the local food is worth it.
