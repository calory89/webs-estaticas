The lowest price found is €124 return, with a median of €130. The sample of quotes checked (€124, €130, €162) shows there's variation in fares. Book as soon as possible: these mid-January prices tend to climb closer to travel dates. Look for flexible date combinations to catch the lowest rates.

Airline and stopovers

Wizz Air UK is the carrier on this route. You'll have 1 stopover on the outbound leg, so it's not direct. Connections are usually manageable if you book with enough layover time. Check the flight details when purchasing to see the connecting airport and waiting times.

Best time to visit

January and February are ideal for Florence: less peak-season crowds than summer, cheaper accommodation and flight prices, and mild weather (though cool). Avoid June to August if you're watching your budget. Spring (April–May) is lovely but fares rise considerably.

What to do when you arrive

Florence airport (Peretola) is 4 km from the city centre. You can take a bus, train or taxi. Don't miss the Duomo, the Uffizi Gallery, the Ponte Vecchio and Palazzo Pitti. Florence is compact, you can cover the highlights on foot in a few days.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Málaga to Florence?

Not on this route. Wizz Air UK operates the journey with 1 stopover. Direct flights between these two cities don't run regularly, so a stopover is standard.

Can I fly cheaper with a different airline?

Wizz Air UK is the carrier in this search. If you want to compare other options, try different dates or search platforms; alternative connections sometimes appear depending on when you look.

What's the lowest price you've seen?

The lowest recorded price is €124 return. That's a solid deal for this route; €130 median is also good value. Anything above €160 starts to feel pricey for Málaga–Florence.

What else is there to see in Florence besides the main monuments?

Beyond the Duomo and the Uffizi, explore galleries like the Accademia, stroll through the Oltrarno neighbourhood, visit the churches of Santa Croce and Santo Spirito, and wander the San Lorenzo market. And eat well, Florentine cooking is exceptional.
