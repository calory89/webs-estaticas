Best time to visit Punta Cana

December to April is dry season and ideal: guaranteed sun, minimal rain. July and August are popular too but hotter and more humid. September to November carry higher rainfall risk. Consider your budget: peak season (December–February) pushes flight prices up. Book ahead if travelling then.

What to do when you arrive

Endless beaches: Bavaro, Arena Gorda, Macao. Diving and snorkelling on the reefs. Los Haitises National Park for nature lovers. Old Santo Domingo is 2 hours by road. Underground cenotes and caves. Local food is simple and tasty (mofongo, salt-baked fish). Currency: Dominican peso, though tourist areas often take euros.
