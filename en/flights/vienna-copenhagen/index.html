Best time to visit

Copenhagen shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with mild weather and less rain. Summer is peak season with higher prices. Winter (December–February) is colder, but the festive Christmas vibe and fewer tourists make up for it. Choose based on your climate preference and tolerance for crowds.

What to do in Copenhagen

Stroll through Nyhavn, the historic colourful harbour lined with cafés. Visit Amalienborg Palace, the royal residence, and Tivoli Gardens, a classic amusement park. Copenhagen is a cyclist's dream, rent a bike and explore quiet streets. Explore Christiania, an alternative neighbourhood with street art. It's unique in Europe.
