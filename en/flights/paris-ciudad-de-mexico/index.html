Best time to travel

December is peak tourist season in Mexico with dry weather and pleasant temperatures (20–25°C in Mexico City), but prices are higher. For the best balance between price and climate, consider May–June or September–October. Avoid April (Easter holidays) and July–August due to crowds. Rain in Mexico is mainly September–October, so this is a secondary factor if you time your trip right.

What to do when you arrive

Mexico City is essential: Historic Centre with the Metropolitan Cathedral, National Palace and Zócalo. Templo Mayor Museum and Frida Kahlo are nearby. Outside the centre, Teotihuacán (pyramids 50 km away), Xochimilco (trajineras boats) and Coyoacán are classic visits. Food is affordable and excellent. The airport (MEX) is 5 km from the centre; metro or collective taxi are the cheapest options. Plan at least 4–5 days.
