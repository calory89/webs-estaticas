Best time to visit

Malta enjoys mild weather year-round. April-May and September-October offer ideal temperatures without summer crowds. July and August are hot and packed with tourists. Winter is quieter but wetter. Check availability for your preferred dates to see pricing.

What to do when you arrive

Malta airport (MLA) is 5 km from Valletta. Visit the Unesco capital, explore Comino and Gozo's beaches, dive in crystal-clear waters, tour the neolithic temples (World Heritage Site), or unwind at Popeye Village coves. Malta is compact; you can see plenty in a few days.
