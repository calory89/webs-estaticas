Best time to travel

Orlando has sunshine year-round. November and April offer pleasant weather with less rain than summer. For lower prices, travel outside Spanish school holidays and US public holidays. Avoid December and July–August, when fares spike significantly.

What to do when you arrive in Orlando

Orlando is famous for its theme parks: Magic Kingdom, Epcot, Hollywood Studios and Animal Kingdom. Beaches are nearby (Clearwater, Cocoa Beach). The Tree of Life Park, shopping on International Drive and attractions like The Wheel or iFLY round out your options. Hire a car if you want to explore the region comfortably.
