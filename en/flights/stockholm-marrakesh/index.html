Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route non-stop. You fly direct from Stockholm to Marrakech on a single flight, saving you time and hassle. A clear advantage on a journey of nearly 4,000 km.

Best time to visit

Marrakech works year-round, but October, November, April and May offer ideal temperatures (25–30°C) without summer's intense heat. Prices tend to be gentler during shoulder season than at Christmas or peak summer. If you're budget-conscious, skip July, August and major holidays.

What to do in Marrakech

You land at Marrakech Menara International Airport, 6 km from the centre. In the city itself: the Medina (souk maze), Jemaa el-Fnaa square (cultural heart), Bahia and Badii palaces, and Majorelle Gardens. Sixty km away lie the Ourika Valley and the Atlas. A perfect base for mixing culture, nature and downtime.
