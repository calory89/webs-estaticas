The lowest price we've found is €651 return. We've spotted fares at €659 and €706 within the same search range. Airline and stops

Etihad Airways operates this route with one stop. It's not direct, but one stopover is standard on this route and usually fits schedules well. Always check where the stop is and how long it lasts so you can plan without surprises.

Best time to go

Maldives is tropical. Plan around the weather you prefer: dry season (November to April) is most comfortable and also pricier. Rainy season (May to October) is cheaper but wetter. Bear in mind prices spike during Christmas, New Year and school holidays, so avoid those if you're hunting for deals.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Velana International Airport (MLE) in the capital, Malé. Maldives is about luxury and nature: snorkelling and diving in coral reefs, private island resorts, white sand beaches and crystal water. On your first trip, book excursions through your hotel. Most travellers go for all-inclusive resorts, so arrange that in advance.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Milan to Maldives?

No, this route has one stop. It's standard for this journey, and total travel time varies depending on where the stop is. Always check the details when you book.

What's the cheapest price you've seen?

€651 return. But prices swing a lot based on dates and availability. We've spotted fares up to €706 in nearby searches.

Which airline flies this route?

Etihad Airways. It's a quality carrier, so you'll enjoy the journey even with the stop.

What else is there to do in Maldives besides beaches?

Snorkelling and diving are the main draw: coral reefs, tropical fish and spectacular marine life. Malé, the capital, has museums and local markets. You can also take trips to nearby islands or enjoy water activities offered by resorts.
