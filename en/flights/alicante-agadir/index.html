Flights start from €78 return; we've seen fares at €78, €84 and €144. There's no specific cheapest day, but booking early usually pays off. Check regularly: prices on this route change based on availability. Book at least 2–3 weeks ahead for better rates.

Airline and connections

Best time to visit

Agadir has mild weather year-round. October to April are ideal: comfortable temperatures without extreme heat. July and August are very hot. Travel when suits you best: prices don't swing by season, but by flight availability and demand.

What to do when you arrive in Agadir

Agadir is beach, sun and relaxation. Walk Agadir Beach, stroll the Promenade lined with cafés and shops, or climb the Kasbah for panoramic views. After surfing nearby beaches or exploring local souks, eat fresh seafood by the shore. It's the perfect break-away destination.
