Best time to visit

Fort Lauderdale shines in winter (December to February) when New York shivers. Beaches are perfect, weather is warm, and it's cruise season. If you want fewer crowds and slightly lower prices, May, June, or September are solid choices.

What to do when you arrive

Fort Lauderdale is far more than beaches. Explore the historic downtown and the yachts at the sports marina, swim in the clear waters of Las Olas Beach, or visit the colourful Davie neighbourhood. Miami is nearby (half an hour's drive), Disney World in Orlando (3 hours), and the Keys if you fancy scenic driving. FLL airport is 30 km from the city; bus, car hire, or Uber get you there quickly.
