Price and when to book

Lowest fares start at €171 return. The median hovers at €187, and we've also seen offers at €235. The gap is significant: booking well in advance and staying flexible with dates make all the difference. Don't wait until the last minute; secure your ticket several weeks ahead if you can.

Airline and no stopovers

Pegasus operates this route as a direct flight. No intermediate stops: you leave Copenhagen and arrive straight in Istanbul. That means less travel time, single check-in, and fewer complications. Simple and efficient.

Best time to visit

Istanbul appeals year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Prices tend to be more competitive outside July and August. January can also be budget-friendly if you don't mind the cold.

What to do when you arrive in Istanbul

Visit the Blue Mosque and Hagia Sophia in the historic heart. Explore the Grand Bazaar to connect with local life. The Golden Horn offers stunning sunset views. Don't miss traditional Turkish baths, Topkapi Palace, and a Bosphorus cruise. Turkish food is essential: try kebab, pastries, and Turkish tea.

What to see in Istanbul in 3–4 days?

Spend one day in the historic core: Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia, and Topkapi. Another exploring the Grand Bazaar and streets in Sultanahmet. A third on a Bosphorus cruise and neighbourhoods like Galata or Beyoğlu. Eat at local restaurants: Turkish cuisine is affordable and superb.
