Best time to visit

Sofia shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), when weather is mild and crowds thinner. Winters are harsh and summers sweltering. Plan ahead to lock in competitive fares during shoulder season.

What to do in Sofia

Sofia is soaked in history: the Alexander Nevsky Cathedral is stunning, Boyana Church is a UNESCO World Heritage site, and Banya Bashi Mosque takes you back centuries. Wander the old town, dip into nearby thermal baths, and taste authentic Bulgarian food. The city is compact and easy to navigate on foot.
