Norwegian Air Sweden operates this route as a direct flight. No awkward stopovers: you leave Berlin and arrive in Stockholm without stops. It's the quickest and most comfortable option for this journey.

Best time to visit

December in Stockholm is magical: Christmas, festive markets, and a chance to see the northern lights if you venture north. Fair warning: it's cold and daylight is limited. For more daylight and milder weather, try spring (April–May) or autumn (September–October).

What to do when you arrive

Stockholm is built on islands: wander Gamla Stan (the old town), visit the Royal Palace, or grab a coffee in Södermalm's cafés. The Vasa Museum and City Hall are must-sees. You'll land at Stockholm Arlanda Airport (ARN), 40 km from the centre; trains and buses connect the city efficiently.
