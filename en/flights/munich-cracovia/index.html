Best time to visit

Krakow is beautiful year-round. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter (January-February) is colder but also cheaper and perfect if you like snow. Avoid bank holidays and peak seasons if you're after lower prices. Book 4-6 weeks ahead for the best fares.

What to do in Krakow

The Main Market Square (Rynek Główny) is the heart of the old town, lined with palaces and cafés. Climb St Mary's Basilica for city views from above. Wawel Castle deserves a full visit. The Vistula riverside is perfect for walks. Kazimierz, the Jewish district, has rich history, galleries and nightlife. Eat pierogis and try local beer.
