Price and when to book

Prices range from €631 to €1,060 in the sample consulted. Booking in advance remains the general recommendation: the earlier you purchase, the better your chances of finding economy fares. Check regularly from October onwards to spot occasional price drops.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with one stopover. It's not a direct flight, but it's the available option to connect Palma with Cape Town whilst maintaining budget prices. Review connection details when booking to manage waiting times and baggage handling.

Best time to travel

Cape Town offers good weather between October and April. November and December are ideal: pleasant temperatures, less rain, and summer in the Southern Hemisphere begins. Avoid June to August if you're not seeking austral winter. High-season demand (December–January) may push fares up.

What to do on arrival

Cape Town is a benchmark destination in South Africa. Visit Table Mountain, Stellenbosch vineyards, Robben Island, beaches like Camps Bay, and Cape Point. Plan tours and overland transport in advance to make the most of your stay.
