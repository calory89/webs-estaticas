Best time to visit

San Francisco enjoys good weather year-round, though spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) are ideal: mild temperatures and less rain. Winter is wetter, and summer can be windy and cool on the coast. Plan around your climate preferences. January tends to be quieter after the holidays.

What to do when you arrive

San Francisco awaits with the Golden Gate, Alcatraz, and iconic neighbourhoods like Mission District and Castro. Eat at food trucks, explore the waterfront, ride the historic cable cars, and visit beaches like Ocean Beach. SFO airport is well connected by public transport to the city centre. It's pricey, but worth every euro.
