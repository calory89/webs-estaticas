Best time to visit Munich

Munich is worth visiting year-round, but May to September offer pleasant weather. Oktoberfest runs in September and October. Winter is cold but perfect if you enjoy the festive Christmas atmosphere in December. Check flight availability for your preferred dates.

What to do when you arrive in Munich

Munich airport is well connected to the city centre in around 40 minutes by train. The city features Nymphenburg Palace, Marienplatz with its Gothic cathedral, traditional beer halls, and the English Garden. The Bavarian Alps are a few hours away if you want nature. Explore Schwabing and Au neighbourhoods for local cafés and shops.
