Price and when to book

The lowest price recorded is €520, though most searches range between €620 and €696. For this multi-stop route, book as far in advance as possible (at least 2–3 months). Off-peak seasons like November tend to be more affordable than high season.

Airline and number of stops

You'll fly with Ryanair on this route. There are 3 stops on the outbound journey before reaching Hanoi. Check your booking confirmation for the intermediate airports so you can plan your total travel time and luggage connections properly.

Best time to visit Vietnam

November is excellent for Vietnam: mild temperatures, less rain than other seasons, and clear skies. Avoid the monsoon (May to September) if you want comfort. Humidity is lower and you'll enjoy outdoor activities more.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

Hanoi is your gateway to Vietnam. Explore the Old Quarter, Halong Bay (UNESCO World Heritage, hours away), historic temples and Hoan Kiem Lake. From there, travel to Ho Chi Minh City or Siem Reap. Noi Bai Airport is about 30 km from the centre; buses and taxis available.
