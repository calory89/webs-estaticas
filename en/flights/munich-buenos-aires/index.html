The Munich-Buenos Aires route costs from €1,082 return with Eurowings. The median sits at €1,248. Book early and grab the best fares for your trip to Argentina.

Prices and when to book

Prices range between €1,082 and €1,348 depending on dates. The median hovers around €1,248. The earlier you book, the better the fare; this multi-stop route allows room for rate negotiation. Checked on 23 October, example outbound 16 November and return 21 November.

Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Eurowings. This route includes 3 stopovers on the outbound, which lengthens your journey but cuts costs significantly compared to direct alternatives. Connections are standard on this intercontinental route from Germany to South America.

Best time to travel

Buenos Aires offers great weather in spring (September-November) and autumn (March-May). November, like in the example, is shoulder season: pleasant temperatures without the summer peak (December-February). Avoid July-August if you want warmth, though those months are cheaper. Book 6-8 weeks ahead.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Ministro Pistarini (also called Ezeiza), 25 km from central Buenos Aires. The city offers tango, world-class dining, neighbourhoods like La Boca and San Telmo, museums and nightlife. From the airport, buses, remis and direct transport to the city centre are available.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Munich to Buenos Aires?

No. This route includes 3 stopovers, which is standard from Germany to Argentina. Direct flights from Europe to Buenos Aires are very limited and far more expensive.

How long is the total journey time with stopovers?

With 3 connections, total time usually falls between 20 and 26 hours depending on layovers. Eurowings manages these stopovers as part of the route.

Why is Eurowings cheaper than other options?

Eurowings is a low-cost airline within the Lufthansa group. It offers competitive fares on multi-stop routes, balancing long-haul distances with attractive prices.

What does the €1,082 price include?

The price is return. Check with Eurowings what's covered in your specific fare (cabin bag, checked luggage, seat) as budget airlines charge supplements for extras.
