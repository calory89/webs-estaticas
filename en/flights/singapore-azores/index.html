Best time to visit

The Azores have a temperate oceanic climate year-round. September to October and April to May are ideal: pleasant temperatures (18-22°C) and less rain than winter. July-August is peak season with higher prices. If you prefer to save on your ticket, travel between November and March, though expect more rain and cloudy days.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in Ponta Delgada (São Miguel island), the main hub of the Azores. Walk through the historic centre, swim at Piscinas Naturais de Poço da Pedra or take a trip to Lagoa das 7 Cidades. On Terceira island, the town of Angra do Heroísmo is a Unesco World Heritage site. Hiking, whale watching and local food round out the experience. Hire a car to move between islands comfortably.
