Best time to visit

Budapest shines in autumn and winter. November (like in the example) strikes a good balance: fewer tourists than summer, mild temperatures and better prices. For extra savings, avoid July–August and Christmas. Search with flexible dates to lock in the best fares.

What to do when you arrive

Budapest is Hungary's capital and a Central European gem. Széchenyi Thermal Baths are essential, along with Parliament, Buda Castle and the Danube itself. Wander the Jewish Quarter, try goulash and Dobos cake. It's highly walkable with cheap public transport. Spend at least 3–4 days to enjoy it properly.
