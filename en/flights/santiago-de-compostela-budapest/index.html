Best time to visit

Budapest is appealing year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is ideal if you want a quieter trip and potentially lower fares.

What to do when you arrive in Budapest

Don't miss the Parliament, views of the Danube from the riverside promenade, Széchenyi and Gellért thermal baths, and the Old Town of Buda and Pest. Hungarian cuisine (goulash, lángos) is a must. Budapest Ferenc Liszt Airport is 15–20 km from the city centre with good public transport links.
