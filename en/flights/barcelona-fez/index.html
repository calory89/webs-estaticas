Best time to travel

Fez is pleasant most of the year, but October-November and March-April have ideal weather: mild temperatures, no scorching heat. January-February can be chilly (down to 10°C at night) but hotels are less crowded. July-August is very hot and peak tourist season. For rock-bottom prices, winter works; for comfort, spring is your pick.

What to do in Fez

Fez's Medina is a UNESCO World Heritage site: a maze of narrow streets, souks and traditional riads. Al-Qarawiyyin University is one of the world's oldest. The Jewish Quarter, the blue Bab Boujloud gate, the Chouara leather tanneries. Outside the medina: the Royal Palace and spice markets. One or two nights cover the essentials; if Islamic history fascinates you, add another day.
