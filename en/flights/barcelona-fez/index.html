Best time to visit

Morocco is pleasant year-round, but October–November and March–April are ideal: mild temperatures and fewer crowds. Avoid July–August if you don't handle heat well. Check the flight calendar to pick your dates with flexibility.

What to do when you arrive in Fez

Fez is Morocco's medieval gem. Explore the walled medina (UNESCO World Heritage), traditional tanneries, Al-Qarawiyyin Mosque, and souks. An hour's drive away lie Meknes and Volubilis. The airport is 15 km out, connected by taxi and bus to the city centre.
