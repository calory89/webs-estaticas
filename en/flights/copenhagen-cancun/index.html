Price and when to book

Prices range from €867 to €1,001 based on the sample checked on 23 October. Book as early as possible: budget airlines like Ryanair raise fares as departure dates approach. For the best price, avoid peak demand periods (school holidays around Christmas and Easter in the northern hemisphere).

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route with 1 stop. It's not a direct flight, but it gives you access to very competitive pricing. The stopover usually lasts 1–2 hours depending on the connection, so download some entertainment and keep your documents handy.

Best time to visit

Cancun offers sun year-round, but the best period is November to April: dry weather and pleasant temperatures (25–28°C). Avoid September and October due to hurricanes. June to August is low season for prices but expect frequent rain. Travel in November or early December for good weather without peak-season fares.

What to do when you arrive

Cancun is your gateway to the Riviera Maya. White-sand beaches, freshwater cenotes, Mayan ruins (Chichen Itza, Tulum), theme parks and coral reef snorkelling are all within reach. The airport is 20 km from town; bus, taxi or car rental get you to your hotel quickly.
