Vueling connects Ibiza to Munich for €150 return, though the median sits at €169. This route includes one stop, ideal if you want to reach Germany on a tight budget.

Price and when to book

Fares start from €150 return. Our sample shows options between €150, €169 and €185. Book as soon as possible from today: these prices tend to rise as departure dates approach. Don't wait until the last minute if you want to keep the fare low.

Airline and stops

Vueling operates this route with one stop. Example itinerary: departure from Ibiza on 11 November at 20:10, arrival in Munich on 12 November; return on 17 November at 21:05. Factor this connection into your travel plans.

Best time to visit

Munich is worth visiting year-round. Autumn (September to November) offers pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter brings possible snow and Christmas is peak season. Book well ahead regardless of the season to secure these fares from €150.

What to do when you arrive

Munich deserves several days: Marienplatz and its cathedral, Nymphenburg Palace, the Pinakothek museums, walks through the English Garden or day trips to nearby Neuschwanstein Castle. Munich airport (MUC) is well connected by public transport to the city centre.
