Best time to visit

Mexico is a pleasant destination year-round. The rainy season runs May to October, whilst November to April offers drier, more stable weather. To avoid crowds, travel January to March or September. December and July are pricier due to peak tourism.

What to do upon arrival in Mexico City

Once you land, there's plenty to explore. The historic centre is essential: Zócalo, Metropolitan Cathedral, National Palace. The museums are world-class (Frida Kahlo, Anthropology). Walk through Coyoacán and Condesa for local vibes. Eat at traditional markets like Oaxaca or La Merced.
