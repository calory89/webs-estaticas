Best time to visit Fez

Fez shines in spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November), when the weather is mild and heat isn't overwhelming. Winter works too, though evenings can be cool. Skip July and August if you can't handle intense heat in the medina.

What to do when you arrive in Fez

The old medina is essential: narrow alleyways, bustling souks, the famous Chouara leather tannery. Don't miss Al Quaraouiyine University, one of the world's oldest. From the airport, reach the centre by taxi or bus. Explore the Jewish quarter and traditional riads turned into guesthouses.
