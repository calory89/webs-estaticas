Barcelona and Sardinia are just one direct flight apart. Prices start from €53 for a return ticket, with an average of €59, so now's the time to plan a budget getaway to this Italian island without draining your wallet.

Price and when to book

The cheapest ticket we've found sits around €53 (return), with an average of €59. Prices in our sample reach up to €86, depending on dates and flexibility. As a general rule, booking 2–3 months ahead gives you better options. The sooner you lock in your dates, the more control you have over the final price.

Airline and stopovers

Vueling is your option on this route and offers direct flights (no stopovers) from Barcelona to Cagliari. This saves you time in transit airports and gets you to your destination fresher. Outbound flights depart early morning (around 07:45) and return flights land late evening (near 23:35), letting you make the most of your time in Sardinia.

Best time to visit

Sardinia shines between May and September, when weather is warm and dry. If you prefer fewer crowds and milder temperatures, April and October are solid choices. Winters are mild, perfect for those who don't mind moderate cold and fancy quiet beaches. Pick based on your heat tolerance and whether you're after beach time or cultural exploration.

What to do when you arrive

Cagliari, the capital, blends urban beaches with museums and local neighbourhoods. Sardinia's coast is the real draw: white sandy beaches at La Maddalena, dramatic cliffs at Orosei, hidden coves dotted around the island. Taste malloreddus (local pasta), swim in crystal waters and explore stone villages inland. The airport is 7 km from the city centre, well connected by bus and taxi.

What's there to see and do in Sardinia?

Sardinia offers white sandy beaches and turquoise waters (La Maddalena, Orosei), virgin coves around the coast, museums in Cagliari, stone villages inland and local cuisine. Perfect for mixing beach time, nature and culture in one island.
