Airline and stops

Best time to visit

Sardinia shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather, pleasant beaches and fewer crowds than summer. Winter trips, like the January example, offer lower fares, though the sea will be cold. Skip August if you want to avoid peak season.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Cagliari airport (CAG), gateway to the south. Explore white-sand beaches in the southeast (Villasimius, Pula), the northeast coast (Costa Smeralda), or Cagliari's old town with its historic Castello district. Rent a car for freedom to explore the island.
