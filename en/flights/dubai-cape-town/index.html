Best time to visit

Cape Town has mild weather year-round, but the southern hemisphere summer (November to February) brings sunny days and longer daylight, perfect for beaches and wildlife spotting. Winter (June to August) is cooler but ideal if you prefer less heat. No clear off-peak pricing season exists: book according to your availability and with good advance notice.

What to do when you arrive

Cape Town airport sits about 20 km from the city centre. From there: take the cable car up Table Mountain, stroll the Waterfront, explore Camps Bay and its beaches, book a tour to Robben Island, or drive the Cape Route to see where two oceans meet. South African landscapes are stunning; spend at least 3–4 days to make the most of the area.
