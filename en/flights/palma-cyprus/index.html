Price and when to book

Flights start from €172 return. The median price is €195, and our sample shows fares up to €246 depending on dates. Book early: long-haul beach routes fill up quickly. Check the same day of the week across different weeks to spot the best deals.

Airline and stops

Vueling operates this route with 2 stops on the outbound flight. It's not direct, so add a few hours to your journey, but the saving makes it worthwhile. Stopovers are usually brief; check your booking for exact stopover details and transfer times.

Best time to visit

Cyprus enjoys sunshine almost year-round. October to April brings mild weather and fewer crowds; May to September is peak beach season. Prices climb in July–August and around bank holidays, so travelling outside those windows means cheaper fares. January is a solid bet for lower prices than summer.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in Larnaca (LCA). The city has accessible beaches and sits near Nicosia. Don't miss Paphos (Roman archaeology), Ayia Napa's beaches and the Troodos mountains. Hire a car for freedom of movement; buses also connect the main towns well.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Palma to Cyprus?

No, this Vueling route has 2 stops. It's standard for flights from Spain to Cyprus; direct services are rare and pricier. The stopovers are usually short, so total travel time is manageable.

What's the cheapest price found on this route?

We've seen tickets from €172 return on this route. The median sits around €195, so if you spot fares near that level, it's a good time to book.

Which airline flies from Palma to Cyprus?

Where do you land in Cyprus?

You arrive at Larnaca airport (LCA), about 50 km south of Nicosia. It's the island's main hub with good bus and taxi links to anywhere on Cyprus.
