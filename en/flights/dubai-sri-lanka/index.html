SriLankan Airlines takes you from Dubai to Colombo non-stop from €297. Average fares sit around €321, so there's room to find bargains. Direct flights with night departures that save you time.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we've seen is €297 (return), but typical fares hover around €321. Some tickets climb to €392 depending on dates and availability. The gap between cheapest and most expensive is significant: if you spot a ticket at €297, don't sleep on it. Book early whenever possible, especially if you're travelling in peak season.

Airline and connections

SriLankan Airlines runs this route with direct flights, no changes, no wasted time in connection halls. The sample flight departs Dubai at 23:10 and arrives in Colombo after a few hours, leaving your overnight and next morning free to rest. A straightforward way to travel.

Best time to visit

Sri Lanka works year-round, but prices shift with the season. November is strong: we've spotted fares from €297 mid-month. Skip the absolute peak if you want to save, though even then €321 is competitive for an international route. Book ahead and stay flexible on exact dates.

What to do when you arrive in Colombo

You'll land at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB), roughly 30 km from Colombo city centre. The capital awaits with its historic port, ancient temples and bustling markets. From here it's easy to explore the rest of the island: southern beaches, central highlands and tea plantations. Most visitors hop on a local bus or train, it's cheap and genuine.

FAQ

Is the Dubai to Sri Lanka flight actually non-stop?

Yes, SriLankan Airlines operates this route without stops. You leave Dubai in the evening and arrive in Colombo the next morning. No plane changes, no annoying layovers.

Why do prices vary so much (€297 to €392)?

Fares depend on your exact travel date, when you book and seat availability. Mid-November we found the lowest prices. The longer you wait, the fewer cheap options remain.

When is the best time to book this flight?

We can't predict price dips, but €297 is already a market steal. Book well in advance (weeks ahead) to lock in availability and price. Last-minute bookings tend to cost more.

Which airport do I land at in Sri Lanka?

You'll arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB), about 30 km from Colombo. It's the country's main hub. From there, taxis, buses and trains connect you to the city centre and beyond.
