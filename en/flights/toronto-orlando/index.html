Flair Airlines connects Toronto to Orlando with direct flights from €195 return. The average price sits around €213, so if you spot a fare under €200, it's time to book.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €195 return. Best fares typically range between €195 and €213. Book as early as possible (ideally 6–8 weeks ahead) for more economical options. If you see a price under €220, grab it: this route doesn't hold low fares for long.

Airline and direct flight

Flair Airlines operates this route with non-stop flights from Toronto. That means 8–9 hours of flying without layovers, perfect if you want to save time as well as money. A direct flight is the best option for this distance: less hassle, less time hanging around airports.

Best time to travel

Orlando is a year-round destination, but the cheapest fares usually fall in low season (August to October, January to March). Summer months and Christmas holidays push prices up. If your dates are flexible, aim for September or October; February and March also see lower demand and better prices.

What to do in Orlando

Orlando's famous for theme parks, but there's plenty more: natural springs like Wekiwa Springs, the charming Winter Park with its vintage shops and lakes, or the beach just over an hour away (Cocoa Beach). Grab food from downtown food trucks, explore Winter Park's galleries, or spend a day by the lakes. Most attractions don't need a car if you stay central.
