The cheapest flights are around €93 return. Most tickets range between €170 and €199. The sooner you book after spotting a price, the better: airfares rise as your travel date approaches. Check regularly for price drops.

Airline and stopovers

Vueling operates this route with direct flights, no stopovers. It's the most convenient: boarding, disembarking and baggage go straight to your destination. You save time and avoid connection hassles.

Best time to visit

Zurich is attractive year-round, but October to March offers lower prices and fewer crowds. December has Christmas charm, though markets boost demand. Summer is expensive. Avoid Spanish and Swiss public holidays to find better deals.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Zurich Airport, 12 km from the city centre. The train gets you there in 12 minutes. Explore the Altstadt (old town), Bahnhofstrasse, Kunsthaus and the lake. Eat fondue and raclette. From Zurich, the Alps are an hour away by train. Buy the Zuri Card for discounts on transport and attractions.
