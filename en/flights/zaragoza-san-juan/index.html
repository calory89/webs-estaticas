Price and when to book

Flights from Zaragoza to San Juan cost from €817 return, with the median price at €821. Our sample prices range between €817 and €824. Book as far in advance as possible: the earlier you secure your ticket, the better the fare. Avoid last-minute purchases, when prices climb.

Airline and number of stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with 3 stopovers on the outbound flight. Although there are several stops, it's the cheapest way to connect Zaragoza with San Juan. Be prepared: a multi-stop journey increases total travel time, but the savings on the ticket make up for it if your budget is tight. Always check Ryanair's baggage allowance before you travel.

Best time to visit

San Juan and Puerto Rico are destinations year-round. Bear in mind that hurricane season in the Caribbean runs June to November, though it's not a guarantee of bad weather. December to April tend to see higher demand and steeper prices. Travel outside peak season if you want to combine good value with stable weather.

What to do on arrival in San Juan

You'll arrive at Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport (SJU), in Puerto Rico's capital. Old San Juan is a must: cobblestone streets, colonial architecture and nearby beaches. Explore Flamenco Beach on Culebra island, visit El Yunque rainforest, or unwind in turquoise waters. Local cuisine and music are part of the place's soul.
