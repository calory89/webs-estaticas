Best time to visit

Bruges is lovely year-round, but spring (April–May) stands out with canals lined with flowers and pleasant temperatures. Autumn (September–October) is also excellent: less summer crowds and mild weather. Skip December–January if you prefer warmth, though Christmas transforms the city into a magical setting.

What to do when you arrive in Bruges

Brussels Airport is about 60 km from Bruges (well connected by train). Once in the city: walk the medieval centre, take a boat tour along the canals, visit the Basilica of the Holy Blood and Grote Markt (main square). Eat Belgian waffles, chocolate and sample local beers. Bruges is small but charming; two or three days are enough to enjoy it fully.
