Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route with 1 stop. It's not direct, but it's the most affordable option from Valencia to Seoul. Factor in total journey time including the layover and check Ryanair's baggage policy (checked luggage is charged).

Best time to visit

South Korea shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather and stunning scenery. Avoid January–February if you're not keen on extreme cold. Travel demand shifts prices, so compare fares based on your weather preference.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Incheon, about 60 km from Seoul. The airport connects well by train (around 45 minutes) and bus to the city centre. Don't miss Gangnam, royal palaces, Bukchon's traditional streets, Myeongdong, and noraebang (karaoke). South Korea is safe with excellent public transport and world-class food.
