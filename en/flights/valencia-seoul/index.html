Best time to visit

Seoul is stunning in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), when the weather is mild and crowds more manageable. Winters are cold but clear; summers are hot and humid. Regardless of season, check available dates in your search engine: sometimes you'll find pleasant surprises on pricing.

What to do when you arrive

Seoul is a metropolis with endless attractions: from traditional palaces like Gyeongbokgung to the bustling Gangnam district. Incheon Airport (ICN) connects well to the city centre by train and bus. Don't miss the night markets, local food scene, and Buddhist temples. Two weeks is a solid minimum if you want to explore the city without rushing.
