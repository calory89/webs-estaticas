Best time to visit

Madeira has mild weather year-round. If you're looking for the most competitive prices, try travelling in low season (outside July, August and Christmas holidays). Book well in advance regardless: direct flights to islands tend to fill up quickly.

What to do when you arrive in Madeira

Funchal Airport (FNC) is your gateway to the island. From there you can visit Cabo Girão cliffs, enjoy botanical gardens, hike along historic levadas (water channels), or simply relax on beaches and coves. The capital, Funchal, deserves a morning to explore the old town and try local cuisine.
