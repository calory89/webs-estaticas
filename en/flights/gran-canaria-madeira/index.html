Best time to visit

Madeira enjoys good weather year-round with mild temperatures. The climate is stable, so there's no 'bad' season. If you want fewer crowds and better prices, skip peak Christmas and Easter weeks. January is a quiet month for exploring without mass tourism.

What to do when you arrive in Madeira

Madeira offers laurel forests, mountain trails and ocean views. Explore Funchal, hike the levadas (historic water channels with scenic routes), visit the Cabo Girão cliffs or relax in the natural pools at Porto Moniz. Madeira Airport (FNC) is about 20 km from Funchal with good public transport connections.
