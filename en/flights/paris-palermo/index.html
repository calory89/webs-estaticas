Airline and stopovers on your journey

Best time to visit Palermo

Sicily shines from May to September: warm weather, clear skies and azure seas. For fewer crowds and slightly gentler prices, April and October deliver pleasant temperatures. Avoid August if you dislike busy tourist spots. Winter is mild but wetter.

What to do and see in Palermo

Teatro Politeama and the Metropolitan Cathedral anchor the centre. Stroll through Ballarò market for authentic Palermo. Mondello beach is 30 minutes by public transport. Try pasta alla Norma and arancini on any street corner. Visit the Palatine Chapel if Norman-Arab art interests you.
