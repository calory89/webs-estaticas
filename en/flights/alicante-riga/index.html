What to do when you arrive

Riga airport (RIX) is 13 km from the city centre. Head straight to the Old Town, a UNESCO World Heritage site, and walk its medieval streets. Visit the Dome Basilica, eat at the Central Market for local food, and climb the Freedom Tower for panoramic views. Riga is compact and walkable.
