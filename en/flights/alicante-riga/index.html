Price and when to book

Prices on this route range from €158 to €183 for return trips. Book as far in advance as possible: Ryanair fares drop the earlier you reserve. Avoid last-minute bookings unless you accept awkward connections. Check the price calendar weekly to catch the best deals before cheap seats sell out.

Best time to travel

December offers competitive prices and Riga's festive winter atmosphere. Pack warm clothes as temperatures drop below zero. May to September are milder months, though fares tend to be higher. Fly midweek when possible to avoid peak demand periods.

What to do when you arrive

Riga airport (RIX) is 13 km from the city centre. Head straight to the Old Town, a UNESCO World Heritage site, and walk its medieval streets. Visit the Dome Basilica, eat at the Central Market for local food, and climb the Freedom Tower for panoramic views. Riga is compact and walkable.
