Best time to visit

Kuala Lumpur is warm year-round, but the driest season runs May to September. October through April sees more rain and humidity. Late March to April or September to October offer lighter crowds and more bearable weather, though tropical humidity is always present.

What to do on arrival

Kuala Lumpur has the Petronas Twin Towers, Batu Caves (Hindu temple in limestone caverns), and Taman Negara National Park for nature lovers. Public transport is reliable and tourist-friendly. Don't miss Petaling Street night market and the Perdana Gardens. From KUL you can reach other parts of Malaysia or hop to Borneo on short flights.
