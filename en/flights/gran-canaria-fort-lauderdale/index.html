Price and when to book your flight

The lowest price is €790 return, with a median of €853. Fares recorded range from €790 to €887. To secure the best rates, book as far in advance as possible: international flights from the Canary Islands drop significantly when you book 2–3 months ahead. Check regularly from October if your trip is in December or later.

Airline and stops

Iberia Express is your carrier on this route. Expect 2 stops on the outbound journey, so this is not a direct flight. The connections are usually manageable, though total travel time will be longer than a non-stop option. Confirm your stopover details at checkout.

Best time to travel

December to March is ideal for Fort Lauderdale: warm weather, minimal rain, and perfect beaches. Travel during these months means high demand and stable pricing. June to September are warmer and more humid but often feature aggressive deals. Avoid hurricane season (August–October) if you prefer peace of mind.

What to do when you arrive in Fort Lauderdale

Fort Lauderdale is the gateway to south Florida. Explore white-sand beaches, visit the marina and waterways. It's just 30 km from Miami, making it an ideal base to discover the region. The airport is connected to the city by bus and taxi. Enjoy the coastal atmosphere, local restaurants, and day trips to the Everglades or Miami without straying far.
