Price and when to book

The lowest price we found is €42 return, with an average around €61. For this Ryanair route, the earlier you book, the better the price. Check regularly: low-cost fares vary significantly based on availability and how quickly the flight fills up.

Airline and flight features

Fly with Ryanair on a completely direct route from Brussels to Cagliari with no stops. The budget airline gets you there quickly, though remember hand luggage has limits and checked baggage costs extra. Check their website for last-minute deals.

Best time to visit

Sardinia shines year-round, but the coast is best from May to September with warm weather and clear seas. For fewer crowds and lower prices, consider April, May, September and October. Winters are mild, perfect if you want peace and quiet.

What to do when you arrive in Cagliari

You'll land at Cagliari airport. The city has nearby beaches, a historic centre with views, and is the gateway to southern coves. From here you can hire a car to explore Chia, Pula or white sand beaches. Good connections to other parts of the island too.

FAQ

Is the Brussels-Sardinia flight direct?

Yes, Ryanair flies non-stop between Brussels and Cagliari. You'll arrive quickly without a transfer.

Which airline operates this route?

It's Ryanair, specialising in cheap low-cost flights. Remember that checked baggage costs extra.

How far in advance should I book to get the best price?

With Ryanair, booking in advance generally pays off. The further ahead you book, the better the fare usually is. Check often: prices rise as seats become scarce.

What can I see and do in Sardinia?

Cagliari has nearby beaches, a medieval castle and a seafront promenade. Southern Sardinia is famous for stunning coves like Chia and Pula. The coast is the main draw, but you'll also find great food and charming coastal villages.
