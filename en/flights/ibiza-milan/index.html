What to do on arrival

Milan awaits with its iconic Duomo, the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II and the Teatro alla Scala. Explore the charming Navigli and Brera neighbourhoods, visit the Basilica of Santa Maria delle Grazie and enjoy local cuisine. The nearest airport is Milan-Malpensa (MXP), well connected to the city centre.
