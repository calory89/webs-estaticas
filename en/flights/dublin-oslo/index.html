Best time to visit

Oslo is stunning in summer (June to August) with nearly continuous daylight. If you prefer fewer crowds and milder temperatures, spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are excellent. Winter is cold but spectacular if you're keen on snow and the northern lights.

What to do in Oslo

Norway's capital offers world-class museums (Munch, Viking Ship), parks like Vigeland with outdoor sculptures, and the Opera House with fjord views. Wander through Grünerløkka, try fresh seafood, and if visiting in winter, try spotting the northern lights from the outskirts.
