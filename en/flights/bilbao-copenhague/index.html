Price and when to book

The lowest price is €137 return, with a median of €138. The sample of prices checked on 22 October shows options between €137 and €195. Book with time to spare: letting only days pass usually pushes the fare up. Comparing prices across multiple days helps you spot the best deals without relying on fixed drop patterns.

Airline and stops

Vueling operates this route. The outbound flight includes 1 stop, so it's not direct but still quick. You depart at 11:20 from BIO and return at 06:45 from CPH, taking in Denmark in just over a week.

Best time to visit

Copenhagen shines in summer, but the example flight is in November, offering fewer crowds and moderate prices. Denmark's capital is beautiful even in autumn with its colours and less mass tourism. For maximum savings, avoid peak July-August and December.

What to do on arrival

Copenhagen is small and walkable: the Old Town, the colourful Nyhavn harbour and Tívoli Gardens are must-sees. The airport CPH is 8 km from the centre, easy to reach by public transport. Try Nordic cuisine, cycle like the locals and relax in Norrebro cafes.
