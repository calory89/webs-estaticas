Prices start at £126 return. The median sits at £129, with options up to £131 in our sample. As with any low-cost flight, booking early usually means better fares. Set up price alerts and check regularly to catch deals.

Airline and flight type

easyJet operates this route with direct flights, no stops, saving you time and hassle. Remember easyJet is low-cost, so baggage, seat selection and extras cost extra. Check what's included in your base fare before purchasing.

Best time to visit

Amsterdam is appealing year-round, but spring (March-May) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter can bring frost and snow on the canals, but has its own charm. Avoid last-minute bookings if you want these low prices.

What to do when you arrive

Amsterdam airport (AMS) is well-connected to the city centre by train (15-20 minutes). The canals, museums (Van Gogh, Anne Frank), bicycles and cafes are must-sees. It's a compact city, perfect for 3-5 days. Public transport is efficient and reasonably priced.

FAQ

How many days should I stay in Amsterdam?

3-4 days cover the highlights: canals, museums and local life. For a slower pace, 5-6 days are ideal. The airport is very close, making it easy to arrange arrivals and departures.
