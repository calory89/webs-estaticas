Best time to travel

Salzburg is a year-round destination. Winter (December–February) attracts visitors for snow and Christmas; spring and autumn offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds; summer is peak season with higher prices. Plan your trip based on your priorities: if price matters, avoid July–August and holiday periods.

What to do when you arrive

Salzburg is Mozart's birthplace and a UNESCO World Heritage site. Visit Hohensalzburg Fortress, the Old Town with its Baroque squares, the Cathedral, and Mirabell Palace. The area offers Alpine lakes and mountains perfect for hiking. Salzburg airport is 4 km from the centre; bus or taxi reach it in 15–20 minutes.
