Fly direct from Palma to Hamburg with Ryanair from €92 return per person. Median fare sits around €102, so you have budget-friendly options if you book in advance. A short hop to reach the heart of northern Germany.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we found was €92 per person (return), with fares averaging around €102. Prices climb to €114+ if you leave it to the last minute. Book 4–6 weeks ahead to secure the best rates; Ryanair charges heavily for last-minute bookings.

Airline and direct flights

Ryanair operates this route non-stop. Early morning departures from Palma (our example was 05:50) and afternoon arrivals in Hamburg. No stops means less hassle and less time in the air. Note: Ryanair charges for cabin and checked baggage, so check their terms before you fly.

Best time to visit

Hamburg shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with mild weather and less rain. December is full winter: expect bitter cold and short days, but the city fills with Christmas markets. Skip July–August if you want lower prices; it's peak tourist season.

What to do in Hamburg

The city blends port history with modern culture. Explore the Harbour (Hafen), visit the Philharmonie (stunning glass building), walk through the Reeperbahn red-light district, and relax by Lakes Alster and Außenalster. Art museums and Miniatur Wunderland are must-sees if it rains.
