Best time to visit

Malta is a year-round sun destination. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer ideal temperatures without peak summer heat. Winter is wetter and cooler but less crowded. July and August are hottest and busiest. Consider travelling outside peak season for better prices and fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Malta Airport (MLA) is 5 km from Valletta city centre. From there you reach the historic capital—a Unesco World Heritage site with charming narrow streets and sea views. Mdina, the former capital, is unmissable. Mellieha beaches and Popeye Village appeal to families. Comino's archipelago offers crystal-clear waters. Rent a car or use local buses to get around the island easily.
