Best time to travel

Punta Cana is a year-round beach destination. Bear in mind that hurricane season in the Caribbean runs from June to November, so travelling in winter (December to April) is safer. Those dates are pricier, but the weather risk is lower. Balance price and climate based on your priorities.

What to do when you arrive

Punta Cana is famous for white-sand beaches and resorts. You can visit Santo Domingo's historic centre, go diving on the reefs, explore the Blue Lagoon or visit caves with Taíno art. Punta Cana International Airport (PUJ) is about 20 km from the hotel zone, well connected by road.
