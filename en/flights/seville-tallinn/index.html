Best time to visit

Estonia shines in summer, but Tallinn is captivating year-round. Winter brings snow and very short days; spring and autumn offer mild weather and fewer crowds. If you travel in shoulder season, you'll often find good deals. Check availability for your preferred dates.

What to do when you arrive

Lennart Meri Tallinn Airport (TLL) is 4 km from the city centre. The Estonian capital is compact and walkable: medieval old town, 13th–15th century churches, history and art museums, modern cafés. Spend time in the historic centre, visit nearby islands, and explore the city's tech and cultural scene.
