Best time to visit

Munich is beautiful year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather with fewer crowds than summer. Travel in December for a unique Christmas atmosphere. Keep in mind that high season can push prices up, so book with plenty of time to spare.

What to do when you arrive

Munich awaits with legendary beer halls, Marienplatz and its Gothic town hall, the English Garden, and world-class museums. The airport (MUC) is well connected to the city: train, bus, and taxi options. Plan a 4–5 day trip to make the most of it without rushing.
