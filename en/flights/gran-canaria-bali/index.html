Airline and stops

You'll travel with Iberia Express, Iberia's low-cost subsidiary. This route involves 3 stops, meaning intermediate layovers before reaching Denpasar (Bali). It's not direct, so total journey time is longer, but it's the most economical option. Check your confirmation to see where the stops are so you can manage connection times properly.

Best time to visit

Bali's dry season runs April to October: perfect for beaches, hiking, and temples without heavy rain. Wet season is November to March. If you want low prices and good weather together, May, June, and September are your sweet spot. December prices spike for Christmas. Avoid February if you don't want constant rain.

What to do when you arrive

Denpasar international airport (Ngurah Rai, DPS) is 13 km south of the island. From there, rent a scooter or car, or use local transport apps. Ubud deserves 2–3 days: rice paddies, monkeys, temples. Seminyak and Canggu offer beaches and nightlife. Tanah Lot is the postcard-perfect shot. Temples like Besakih and Tirta Empul are stunning. Eat cheap at warungs (local eateries) and haggle at markets.

What must I see in Bali?

Ubud and its rice fields, the temples (especially Tanah Lot for sunset), Seminyak for beaches and restaurants, and Tirta Empul for its sacred water pools. With 1–2 weeks you'll cover the essentials comfortably.
