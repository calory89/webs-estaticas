Ryanair flies direct from Alicante to Rome from €45 return. The median price is around €61. Book in advance and fly without intermediaries.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we found is €45, though most searches hover around €61. We've seen fares up to €70. The earlier you book, the better deal you'll get: start looking at least 4–6 weeks ahead. Prices climb as your departure date approaches.

Airline and direct flights

Ryanair operates this route. The flight is direct: no stops or changes. Departures are mid-afternoon from Alicante, arriving in Rome early evening. Check which baggage allowance is included with your ticket type.

Best time to visit

Rome shines in spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November). Winter is cheaper but wetter; summer is hot and crowded with tourists. Skip European holidays and bank holidays if you want better fares.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Fiumicino Airport (FCO), 26 km from the city centre. Take the direct train downtown in 30 minutes. Don't miss the Colosseum, the Vatican, the Trevi Fountain, and neighbourhoods like Trastevere and Campo de' Fiori. Roman food is essential.
