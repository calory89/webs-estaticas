Best time to visit

Vilnius shines in autumn (September–October) and spring (May–June) with mild temperatures. Winter is cold but atmospheric if snow appeals to you. November straddles autumn and winter, so pack a coat. Outside summer you'll find fewer tourists and more affordable accommodation.

What to do when you arrive

Vilnius impresses with its medieval Old Town (UNESCO-listed), baroque churches and street art. Walk the Pilies Street, climb Gediminas Tower for panoramic views, and explore the Gates of Dawn. The international airport is 4 km from the centre; bus and taxi are quick and affordable.
