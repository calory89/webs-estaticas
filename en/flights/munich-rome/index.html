Best time to visit

Rome is appealing year-round, but October and November offer mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Spring (April–May) is also excellent. Avoid July and August if you don't want peak season heat and queues. Prices are usually higher during peak season, so travel in low or shoulder season for better rates.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Rome Fiumicino (FCO) or Ciampino (CIA) airport. The city awaits with the Colosseum, the Vatican, Trevi Fountain and the Roman Forum. Eat cacio e pepe in any corner, stroll through Trastevere at sunset and don't miss the Renaissance churches. Three to four days covers the essentials.
