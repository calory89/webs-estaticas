Best time to visit

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer the best conditions: pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. If you want beach days and nightlife, July and August are peak season but busier. Winter is quieter and cheaper if weather isn't your main concern.

What to do when you arrive

The Acropolis is a must; spend at least half a day there. Plaka is perfect for wandering, dining on terraces and exploring traditional Athens. Don't miss the Acropolis Museum, ancient temples scattered throughout the city or a sunset from Likavittos. Piraeus is ideal for an evening stroll by the port.

What to see in Athens in two or three days?

The Acropolis, Parthenon and Acropolis Museum are essential. Spend time in Plaka for strolling and dining, climb Likavittos at sunset and explore Piraeus. With more time, visit Delphi or the Saronic Islands.
