Best time to visit

Phu Quoc is tropical. The ideal period is November to March: dry weather and pleasant temperatures. May to September is monsoon season: rain and humidity. Peak season (December–February) has high demand and pricier tickets, but travelling between November and March gives you great weather. February and early March are busiest.

What to do when you arrive in Phu Quoc

Phu Quoc means white-sand beaches, coral reefs and jungle. Visit Sao and Long beaches, snorkel or dive at nearby islands. Explore fishing villages, try local fish sauce and unwind at spas and resorts. The airport is 30 km from town; take a taxi, Grab or rent a motorbike to get around. Plan at least 4–5 days to enjoy it properly.
