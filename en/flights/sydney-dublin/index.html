Best time to visit

Dublin is an attractive destination year-round, but spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) offer better weather and fewer crowds than summer. Plan with extra time if travelling in peak season (June–August). Remember Ireland is rainy: bring a coat and waterproof jacket regardless of season.

What to do upon arrival in Dublin

Dublin Airport (DUB) is 10 km from the city centre. The capital offers visits to Trinity College, the Guinness Storehouse, strolls along the Liffey, and neighbourhoods like Temple Bar. From Dublin you can take day trips to the Cliffs of Moher, Galway, or the east coast. The city is a perfect base for exploring all of Ireland by public transport or car hire.
