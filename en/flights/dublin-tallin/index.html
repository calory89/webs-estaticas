Best time to visit

Tallinn is especially lovely in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with pleasant weather and less rain. Summer (June–August) is peak season with more tourists and potentially higher prices. Winter (November–February) is cold but offers Christmas markets if you travel in December. Check flexible dates to find the best price for your availability.

What to do when you arrive

Lennart Meri Tallinn Airport is 4 km from the centre. Explore the medieval Old Town (Unesco World Heritage), visit Alexander Nevsky Cathedral and walk the city walls. The harbour offers great photo spots. Eat at traditional restaurants in the old town and try local specialities. Tallinn is small and very walkable; a weekend is enough to see the essentials.
