Price and when to book

Málaga-Dubai flights start from €405 return, though most hover around €451. Recent samples show fares up to €508. It's worth booking 3-4 weeks ahead to catch lower prices. The earlier you secure your ticket, the more bargains you'll find.

Airline and stopovers

Vueling operates this route. Expect 2 stopovers on the outbound flight. Multiple stops lengthen your journey but slash the final price. If comfort matters more than savings, ask about direct options (they'll cost more).

Best time to visit

Dubai is scorching May to September. The sweet spot is October to April with pleasant temperatures for exploring. November is ideal: perfect weather and still decent flight fares. Steer clear of July and August if you can't handle intense heat.

What to do when you arrive

Dubai airport (DXB) sits 20 km from the city centre, well served by metro and taxi. The city delivers Burj Khalifa, Palm Jumeirah beaches, and traditional souks in Deira. Don't skip desert safaris or camel rides. Shopping malls are world-class.
