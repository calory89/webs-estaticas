Best time to travel

Prague is attractive year-round, but October to March are the cheapest months for flights. Winter (December–January) usually has low prices except around Christmas. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are pleasant with good value. Avoid July–August if you're after bargains: it's high season.

What to do when you arrive

Prague is compact and walkable. The Old Town, Charles Bridge, Prague Castle and Old Town Square are must-sees. Czech beer and local food deserve attention. Václav Havel Airport is 20 km from the centre; bus, train or taxi get you in 30–40 minutes. A week is comfortable time to enjoy at leisure.
