Best time to visit

Cairo is best October through April, when temperatures are moderate (20–25°C) and rainfall minimal. June to August exceed 35°C. Consider avoiding Ramadan if you prefer regular restaurant and shop hours. Prices tend to spike during European winter holidays.

What to do on arrival

Cairo International Airport (CAI) sits 25 km from the city centre. Visit the Pyramids of Giza, the Sphinx, the Egyptian Museum and Coptic Cairo. The Nile, traditional bazaars (Khan el-Khalili) and Luxor temples are must-sees on longer stays. Cairo is chaotic but rewarding: eat at local spots and haggle in markets.
