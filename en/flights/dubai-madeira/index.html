Best time to travel to Madeira from Dubai

Madeira enjoys mild weather year-round, but autumn (September to November) and spring (March to May) are ideal with pleasant temperatures and less rain. December to March is the coolest season, with possible showers but fewer tourists. Summer is hot in Dubai, so a trip to Madeira can be a welcome escape. What to do in Madeira when you arrive

Madeira is a destination for nature, hiking and stunning views. You'll land at Cristiano Ronaldo Airport (FNC), in the south of the island. Don't miss Pico Ruivo, the cliffs of Cabo Girao, the UNESCO laurel forests, and the Levada waterfalls. Funchal's harbour offers fresh fish restaurants, and the pebble beaches are perfect for relaxation. The Atlantic Ocean vistas from almost any point on the island are spectacular.
