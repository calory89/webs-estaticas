Best time to go

Warsaw shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather and fewer crowds. Winter (like our January example) is cold but magical until mid-January with festive vibes. Skip summer if you want lower fares and smaller queues. Prices dip in low season: January, February and November.

What to do when you arrive

Warsaw will surprise you: the rebuilt Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage site, perfect for strolling. The Palace of Culture, Second World War museums, the Chopin Trail, and modern art galleries are must-sees. Eat pierniki (Polish dumplings) and sample local vodka. Two or three days and you'll have covered the essentials.
