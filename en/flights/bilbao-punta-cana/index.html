Best time to travel

Punta Cana works year-round, but bear in mind: December to March brings drier, more pleasant weather; June to October is hurricane season in the Caribbean, though Punta Cana is somewhat sheltered. Prices drop in low season (May, September, October), but watch the weather. Late October or early November is ideal: fewer tourists, better fares and favourable conditions.

What to do when you arrive in Punta Cana

Punta Cana is all about beaches: coral reefs, diving, snorkelling and white sand. Visit Isla Saona or Isla Catalina for day trips. The tourist hub is the Punta Cana riviera with all-inclusive resorts. Cenotes and nature parks are nearby too. The airport is 30 km from the centre; book a private transfer or share shuttles with other travellers.
