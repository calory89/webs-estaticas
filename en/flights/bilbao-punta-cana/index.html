Best time to visit

Punta Cana is a year-round destination, though the dry season (December to April) brings better weather and less rain. July and August are peak with higher fares. January is particularly busy. Consider shoulder months (May-June, September-October) to balance good weather with more affordable tickets.

What to do when you arrive

Punta Cana means white-sand beaches and turquoise waters. Enjoy all-inclusive resorts, diving, snorkelling, or excursions to Saona Island. Punta Cana Airport (PUJ) is 30 km from town and well connected. Hire a car or book private transfers to explore the area.
