Best time to visit

Dubai is perfect between November and March, when temperatures are comfortable (around 25-30°C). Avoid May to September: heat exceeds 40°C and is brutal. Flight prices may vary by season, but cheap fares give you flexibility. European winter = your window to go.

What to do when you arrive

Dubai awaits with skyscrapers, beaches and shopping. Burj Khalifa, Palm Jumeirah and The Dubai Mall are must-sees. Don't miss a desert sunset if you want genuine local flavour. You'll land at the international airport (DXB), well connected to the city by metro and taxi.
