Dublin to Luxembourg for €38 return. It's real, and it's Ryanair. Typical prices hover around €61 on this direct route across the North Atlantic in just a few hours.

Price and when to book

Fares range from €38 to €81 depending on your travel dates. The median sits at €61 for the full trip. Book early: Ryanair raises prices as departure approaches. Two months in advance is standard for the best deals on this route.

Airline and direct flight

Ryanair is the sole operator. Direct flight DUB-LUX: no stops, no changes. Sample itinerary: departure 14 November at 10:15, arrival 18 November at 18:20. Always check exact timings in your search, they shift by date.

Best time to visit

Luxembourg shines from April to October when the weather is mild and daylight plentiful. Autumn months (September-October) are ideal: fewer tourists than summer, still reasonable fares, and beautiful scenery. Skip December unless you fancy inflated prices.

What to do in Luxembourg

This tiny country packs plenty: Luxembourg City's Old Town (UNESCO World Heritage) with medieval ramparts, the Casemates du Bock, the Adolphe Bridge, and views from the Corniche. Beyond the capital, the Luxembourgish Ardennes offer hiking and charming villages. The airport is 6 km from the city centre; bus and taxi connect you quickly.

What should I see in Luxembourg?

The capital is a must: medieval streets, 17th-century ramparts, the Adolphe Bridge, and stunning views from the Corniche. With extra days, explore Ardennes villages and nearby hiking trails.
