Best time to visit

European spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant weather in Amsterdam without summer crowds. Winter (December-February) brings more rain but fewer tourists and sometimes better prices. The search was run on 23 October 2026, so monitor prices during these seasonal windows.

What to do when you arrive

Amsterdam is the gateway to the Netherlands: museums like the Rijksmuseum, canal cycling, street markets and nearby villages such as Volendam. Schiphol Airport connects to the city centre in 20 minutes by train. Consider extending your stay to explore Utrecht, Rotterdam or the tulip region.
