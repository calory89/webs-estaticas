Prices range from €142 to €230 for return flights. As always, book early: fares rise as departure approaches. If you spot €142, don't hesitate. Most users find tickets around €163, which remains very competitive for this route.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair is your only option at these prices. The outbound flight includes 1 stopover, so it's not direct: expect an intermediate stop. It's standard for European-to-Morocco routes with this airline, but the price makes up for it.

Best time to visit

Agadir is a beach destination year-round. Weather is pleasant even in winter (15-20°C), though summer gets very hot. Spring (March-May) and autumn (September-November) offer ideal temperatures without crowds. Avoid August if you dislike extreme heat.

What to do when you arrive

Agadir is more than beaches: the fishing port, the Casbah with panoramic views, and local markets await. Water sports enthusiasts can surf nearby beaches. Marrakech is a few kilometres away for a day trip. Agadir-Al Massira airport is close to the city centre.

The Casbah (historic fortress with views), the port, traditional markets and the seafront promenade. Many travellers take day trips to Marrakech or Atlas villages. Agadir is the perfect base to explore the region.
