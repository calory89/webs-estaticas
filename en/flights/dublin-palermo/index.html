Best time to visit

Sicily enjoys pleasant weather almost year-round. Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) are ideal: fewer crowds than summer and very comfortable temperatures. Winter is quiet but rainy some days. Summer is hot and busy with tourists.

What to do when you arrive in Palermo

Palermo airport is 35 km from the city centre. The city has plenty to offer: the Cathedral, Palazzo dei Normanni, traditional markets like Vucciria or Capo, Mondello beach nearby, and excellent Sicilian cuisine. Explore the island: Monreale, Cefalú or Mondello are easily accessible.
