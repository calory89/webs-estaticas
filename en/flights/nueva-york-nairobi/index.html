Best time to visit Nairobi

Nairobi can be visited year-round. The dry months (June to October and January to February) are ideal for safaris and outdoor activities, with less rain and pleasant temperatures. Rainy season is cheaper, but the weather is more unpredictable. Choose based on your plans.

What to do in Nairobi and surroundings

Nairobi is the gateway to Kenya. Visit the National Museum of Kenya, the Giraffe Centre to see giraffes up close, and David Sheldrick's Elephant Orphanage. Just outside the city, Nairobi National Park offers safaris without leaving town. Local craft markets are perfect for authentic souvenirs. From here, tours depart to Masai Mara Reserve and Lake Nakuru.
