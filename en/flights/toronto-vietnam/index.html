Best time to visit Vietnam

Vietnam is best visited between October and April, when the climate is drier and temperatures are pleasant. Avoid the monsoon season from May to September. Travelling in November, as in the booking example (16 November), is ideal: moderate temperatures and less rain. This season also offers reasonable prices if you book ahead.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

Hanoi, Vietnam's capital, has plenty to offer: the Old Quarter with its narrow streets and traditional markets, Ho Chi Minh's Mausoleum, the Temple of Literature and Hoan Kiem Lake. From the city you can take trips to Halong Bay, one of Vietnam's most iconic destinations, or visit the rice fields of Sapa. Noi Bai International Airport is about 25 km from the city centre.
