Best time to visit

Hamburg shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. The cold months (November to February) are cheaper but bring proper German winter. December has Christmas market charm, though expect rain and cold. For better value, consider shoulder season or off-peak.

What to do when you arrive

Hamburg has Europe's biggest northern port, canal walks (Spreekanal), the State Opera House and the bohemian Altona district full of cafés. Airport HAM is 9 km from the centre with good rail links. Museums, markets and Hanseatic food: you won't run out of things to do.
