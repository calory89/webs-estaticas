Best time to visit

Norway shines in summer (June to August) with long days and pleasant temperatures, perfect for exploring cities and nature. Winter (December to February) attracts those chasing the Northern Lights, though it demands heavy clothing. Autumn (September to October) brings colourful landscapes with fewer crowds. Choose based on your interests and cold tolerance.

What to do when you arrive in Oslo

Oslo Airport (OSL) sits roughly 50 km from the centre. The capital is worth exploring: walk the Old Town (Gamlebyen), visit the Opera House, check out museums like Munch or the Viking Ship. With more days, Norwegian fjords, Bergen and inland mountains are unmissable. Hire a car or use public transport; distances are vast but well connected.
