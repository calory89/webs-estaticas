Ryanair connects Málaga and Oslo with direct flights from €98 return. The median price sits around €101, so you've got solid opportunities to fly to Norway without breaking the bank.

Price and when to book your flight

The lowest price we've found is €98 return. The median sits at €101, with sample prices of €98, €101 and €115. Book well in advance, especially if you're travelling near holidays or weekends. The earlier you purchase, the better the fare. Direct flights typically hold competitive pricing better than routes with stopovers.

Ryanair: direct, no stopovers, gate to gate

Ryanair operates this route without stopovers. It departs Málaga early morning and arrives in Oslo with plenty of time. Travel light: Ryanair charges for checked luggage, so stick to essentials in your carry-on. Online check-in is free and available 24 hours before departure.

Best time to travel to Norway from Málaga

Norway is spectacular in summer (June to August) with long days and mild temperatures. Winter offers better chances of seeing the Northern Lights, but it's cold. Autumn and winter tend to be low seasons with tighter pricing. Avoid Christmas week and Easter if you're after the best fares.

What to do in Oslo when you arrive

Oslo deserves 2-3 days minimum. Visit the Munch Museum and National Gallery, explore the bohemian Grünerløkka neighbourhood, eat fresh salmon at the waterfront (Vaterland) and climb Akershus Fortress. The airport is 20 minutes from the city centre by train. From Oslo you can take day trips to the southern fjords or ski resorts.

FAQ
