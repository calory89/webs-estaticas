Best time to visit

Fort Lauderdale has a tropical climate: rain in summer (June–October) and warm temperatures year-round. November to April is the dry season and most pleasant, ideal for beaches and outdoor activities. If you're after low prices, travel outside school holidays; if you want guaranteed good weather, choose the Northern Hemisphere winter (December to February).

What to do when you arrive

Fort Lauderdale and surroundings offer white sand beaches, snorkelling on coral reefs, water parks and marine life. The international airport (FLL) is about 30 km from the city centre. It's the gateway to the Bahamas and Florida's keys. Book ground transport from the airport in advance if you're travelling on a tight budget.

When is the best time to visit Fort Lauderdale?
