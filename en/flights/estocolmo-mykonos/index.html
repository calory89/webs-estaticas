Cheap flights from Stockholm to Mykonos start from €225 return. The median price sits at €266, so you have budget-friendly options. Wizz Air Malta operates this route with 2 stopovers, ideal if you're looking for a low-cost fare.

Price and when to book

The base price is €225, with the median around €266. The highest sample fare reaches €275. Book early to lock in the best prices—advance booking is essential on this route. October and November are typically competitive months before the high winter season kicks in.

Airline and stopovers

Wizz Air Malta operates this route. All flights include 2 stopovers, so there are no direct options. The journey is longer, but the savings on airfare make up for it if you're flexible with time. Check connection times carefully when booking to avoid tight transfers.

Best time to visit

Mykonos shines from May to September with perfect weather and warm sea. If you travel shoulder season (April-May or October-November), you'll find fewer crowds and more competitive fares. Winter is quiet and cool, ideal if you want relaxation without mass tourism.

What to do when you arrive

Mykonos Airport (JMK) is 2 km from town. Explore Psarou and Paradise beaches, stroll the old town with its iconic windmills, and savour local cuisine. The island's bars and clubs are world-famous, but quiet coves away from the hustle are equally rewarding.
