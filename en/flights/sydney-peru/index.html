The cheapest ticket we found is €1,687 (return). Prices range between €1,687 and €2,110 depending on availability and dates. The sooner you book, the better: recent data shows the median fare sits at €2,001. For such a long route, booking 2–3 months in advance usually yields good results.

Airline and stopovers

The sample itinerary shows departure from Sydney at 14:00 on 11 November 2026 and return from Lima on 17 November at 12:00. Stopovers are typical for such a long intercontinental journey, so factor in connection time.

Best time to visit

Peru welcomes visitors year-round. The dry season (May to September) is ideal for Machu Picchu and the Andes: less rain and pleasant temperatures. If you're heading to the highlands, avoid the rainy season (November to March). The Peruvian coast is nice any time, though warmest December to March. Plan based on your interests: heritage, nature, or beaches.

What to do on arrival in Lima

You'll land at Lima's Jorge Chávez International Airport. The capital is your gateway to explore Peru. In the city: Larco Museum, Historic Centre, Miraflores district. From Lima, you can travel to Machu Picchu (train to Cusco), Nazca, the Sacred Valley, or the Amazon. Even if it's a stopover, Lima deserves 1–2 days before heading to your final destination.
