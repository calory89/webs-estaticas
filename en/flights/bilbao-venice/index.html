Best time to visit

Venice is beautiful year-round, but avoid peak season (June to September) if you want fewer crowds and lower prices. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: good weather, fewer tourists and more reasonable fares. Winter is the cheapest option, though expect more fog.

What to do when you arrive

Venice airport is 15 km from the city. Take the vaporetto (water bus) or fast boat directly to St Mark's. Visit the Basilica, Doge's Palace, Rialto Bridge and the Bridge of Sighs. Wander the narrow streets, eat fresh pasta in small restaurants and climb a bell tower for views across the city. Travel light—Venice is explored on foot and by boat.
