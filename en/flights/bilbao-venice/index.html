Best time to visit

Venice works year-round. October, November, February and March usually see fewer tourists than summer. Avoid July and August if you don't want crowds. December has a festive atmosphere if that appeals, though prices may rise on specific dates.

Things to do when you arrive

Venice awaits: St. Mark's Basilica, Rialto Bridge, Doge's Palace. Walk the canals, eat at waterside terraces, explore art galleries. Venice airport (Marco Polo) is well connected to the city. Move around on foot or by vaporetto through the canals.
