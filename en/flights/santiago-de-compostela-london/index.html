Fares range from €83 to €109 in the sample checked (22 October 2026). Finding a ticket at €83 is a steal; from €104 onwards it's mid-to-high pricing. Golden rule: book 2–3 weeks ahead if you can. Best time to visit

Spring (April–May) and early autumn (September) typically see lower price peaks. Skip December, Easter, and July–August if you want cheap fares. Winter (November–February) is generally affordable, though London appears grey. Weekends cost more than weekdays.

Things to do when you arrive

Your flight lands at one of London's Ryanair airports: Stansted, Luton or Gatwick. From there, public transport gets you downtown in under an hour by train or bus. Must-sees: Tower Bridge, British Museum, Thames riverside walks, Borough Market. Try proper fish and chips, explore Camden or Shoreditch for alternative vibes.
