Flying from Oslo to Bali costs from €673 return per person with Wizz Air Malta. The median price sits at €830. With some date flexibility and advance booking, you'll find options that fit your budget.

Price and when to book

The lowest recorded price is €673 per person (return). Most searches land between €673 and €831. Book 4 to 8 weeks ahead to access the best fares. The earlier you purchase, the more budget-friendly options you'll have.

Airline and stops

Wizz Air Malta operates this route with 3 stops on the outbound flight. It's not direct, but it's the most economical option available. Always check connection times when booking: with 3 layovers, the total journey will be long, so bring medicine, a charger, and entertainment.

Best time to travel

Bali has a dry season from April to October with clear skies and less rain. The wet season (November to March) is cheaper because there's less tourism, but expect heavy showers. Choose based on your priority: stable weather or even lower prices.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Denpasar (Ngurah Rai International Airport). From there, rent a scooter or car to explore beaches like Seminyak and Canggu, temples such as Tanah Lot, rice fields in Ubud, or simply relax at resorts. The island offers diving, hiking, and local cuisine at very affordable prices.
