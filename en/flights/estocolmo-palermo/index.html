Best time to visit

Sicily is pleasant most of the year, but May, June, September and October offer the warmest weather and least rain. Winter (December to February) is cooler and wetter but has fewer tourists and lower prices. Choose spring or autumn for sun and beaches; winter if you want to save money and don't mind cooler weather.

What to do in Palermo

Palermo is Sicily's capital with world-class food, Norman and Baroque architecture, and bustling markets like Vucciria. Don't miss the Cathedral, the Church of La Martorana, and grab arancini and pasta alla norma anywhere. Mondello beach is 30 minutes by bus. You can also take day trips to Monreale or the Aeolian Islands.

FAQ

Is the flight from Stockholm to Palermo direct?

No, it has 1 stopover. The outbound journey is completed in one day, but it is not a non-stop flight.

Which airline flies from Stockholm to Palermo?

What is the best month to visit Palermo?

May, June, September and October offer good weather and fewer crowds than summer. December and January are cheaper but cooler with a risk of rain.
