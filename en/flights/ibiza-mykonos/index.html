Best time to visit

December is low season in Mykonos, away from the summer rush. That means fewer crowds, cheaper prices, and a quieter island vibe. If you want beaches and nightlife, May to September is your window; if you're after peace and savings, winter (November–March) suits you better.

What to do when you arrive

Mykonos has beaches like Paradise Beach and Psarou, each with its own character. The old town features white-washed streets, shops and waterfront tavernas. Panagia Paraportiani church is an icon. In low season, you'll enjoy a slower pace and more genuine connections with the island.
