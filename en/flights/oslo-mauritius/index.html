The Oslo-Mauritius route is available from €915 return with Norwegian Air Shuttle. The median price sits around €933, giving you a solid reference point for booking decisions. It's a beach and adventure destination worth 7-10 days of your time.

Price and when to book

Flights start from €915, with a median of €933 and a top price of €975 in recent searches. Norwegian Air Shuttle operates this route. For a distance of over 8,000 km, these fares are competitive. If the dates work for you, book now: long-haul international flights like this tend to rise in price as departure dates approach, especially in November and December.

Airline and stops

You'll fly with Norwegian Air Shuttle. The outbound flight includes 2 stops, so it's not direct: expect several hours of connections. The sample outbound departs Oslo on 13 November at 07:30, and the return is 20 November at 09:25. Stops are necessary on routes this long, and Norwegian typically handles connections smoothly.

Best time to visit

November is a solid choice: it's the transition into austral summer in Mauritius, with pleasant temperatures (24-28°C) and less rain than December-January. The prices you're seeing (€915-€933) reflect mid-season, not peak or low. If you want drier beaches and fewer crowds, November-December is your window: go for the €915 price point.

What to do when you arrive

Mauritius means beaches, coral reefs and nature. Spend days exploring the north (Grand Baie, Cap Malheureux), east (Palmar, Belle Mare) and south (Le Morne, Bel Ombre). Don't miss the Coloured Mountain in Black River Gorges, Port Louis market and the Botanical Gardens at Pamplemousses. Eat Mauritian curry, try local rum and dive or kitesurf if it's your thing. Seven nights is enough.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Oslo to Mauritius?

No, this route includes 2 stops. It's normal given the distance (over 8,000 km) and few airlines operate routes this long. Budget 20-26 hours total travel time door to door.

Why do prices vary between €915 and €975?

Variation depends on exact date, booking in advance, seat availability and baggage policy. €915 is the lowest found; if you wait, prices are likely to go up. Norwegian usually offers better prices with significant advance booking.

Is Norwegian Air Shuttle reliable on this route?

Norwegian is a Scandinavian low-cost carrier that operates international flights. As with any budget airline, check what's included in your fare (baggage, meals, seats) at booking. Connections are typically well managed.

What must-see places in Mauritius in 7 days?

Beaches in the north (Grand Baie), south (Le Morne) and east (Belle Mare), the Coloured Mountain, Black River Gorges, Port Louis and Pamplemousses Gardens. This covers beach, nature and culture in one trip. It's a compact destination; with a rental car you can see it all in a week.
