Best time to visit

Crete is a great destination year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: pleasant weather, fewer crowds than summer, and better prices. Summer can be scorching. Winter offers peace and quiet beaches, though the sea is cold.

What to do when you arrive in Crete

Crete offers stunning beaches, charming traditional villages, and excellent food. Visit Chania, hike Samaria Gorge, enjoy beaches like Balos or Elafonissi, and taste authentic Cretan cuisine in local tavernas. Heraklion airport (HER) puts you close to major attractions.
