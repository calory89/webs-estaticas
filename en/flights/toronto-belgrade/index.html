Best time to travel

Belgrade shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather, fewer crowds and better prices than summer. Winter is cold but budget-friendly if you don't mind the season. July and August are pricey and busy.

You'll land at Nikola Tesla Airport (BEG), about 18 km from the city centre. Belgrade offers Kalemegdan Fortress, the Danube, solid museums and proper Balkan food. It's an underrated European capital, walkable, affordable and less crowded than the usual suspects.
