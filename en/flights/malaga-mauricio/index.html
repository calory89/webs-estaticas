Best time to visit

Mauritius has a tropical climate. May to November (Southern Hemisphere winter) offers milder temperatures and less rainfall—the ideal season. December to April is Southern Hemisphere summer with more heat and humidity. Bear in mind the journey will take several days due to the stops, so plan accordingly.

What to do when you arrive

Mauritius is a destination for beaches, reefs and nature. The arrival airport (MRU) is near the capital, Port-Louis. From there you can explore Coral Island, visit Black River Gorges, enjoy the beaches of Flic en Flac or Belle Mare, or dive and snorkel. Local food and the island's cultural blend are must-sees.
