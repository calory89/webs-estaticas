Best time to visit

Las Vegas is a year-round destination. Autumn (October-November) and spring (March-May) offer pleasant temperatures and less extreme heat than summer. October to November is a good window if you want mild weather and non-peak prices.

Las Vegas is more than casinos: stroll the Strip to see the hotels and fountains, visit Fremont Street Experience, explore the Neon Museum, take day trips to the Grand Canyon or Valley of Fire.
