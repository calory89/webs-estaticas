Best time to travel

Japan shines in spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November), when the weather is mild and landscapes are stunning. Kyoto and its surroundings are magnificent in these seasons. Travel in winter (December to February) for fewer crowds, though outdoor attractions can be cold.

What to do on arrival

Kyoto is a treasure trove of temples, traditional shrines and gardens. Don't miss Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, Fushimi Inari shrine and the Imperial Palace. Museums, street markets and local food complete an unforgettable experience. Kansai Airport (OSA) connects well to Kyoto by train.
