Airline and stops

Icelandair operates this route. The flight includes one stop, so it's not direct, but it's an economical option to Orlando. The stopover means longer total travel time, but the price saving usually makes up for it.

Best time to travel

Orlando is worth visiting year-round. November to January is peak season (Disney and Universal are busy and pricier). May to September sees more tropical rain but fewer crowds. April, May and October often offer the best balance of weather and tourism levels.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Orlando airport (MCO), in the heart of Florida. Disney World, Universal Studios and SeaWorld are 30 minutes away. If theme parks aren't your thing, explore nearby beaches (Clearwater, Cocoa Beach), freshwater lakes for kayaking, or nature at Wekiwa Springs State Park.

Disney World and Universal Studios are the main draws. If you prefer nature, there are water parks, lake kayaking, Florida's coastal beaches within an hour, and nightlife in downtown.
