Best time to travel

Bucharest has appeal year-round. Autumn and spring offer pleasant weather for exploring the city. Winters are cold but perfect if you don't mind harsh conditions. Summer is peak tourist season. Whatever your preference, these prices make visiting Romania affordable any time of year.

What to do when you arrive in Bucharest

You'll land at Henri Coandă Airport (Otopeni), about 16 km from the centre. The city deserves several days: explore the historic centre, visit the Palace of Parliament (Europe's largest building), stroll through the Old Town and enjoy Romanian food. Museums, ancient churches and nightlife will round out your stay.
