Airline and stopovers

Vueling operates this connection with 3 stopovers on the outbound flight. No direct flights on this route, so prepare for a long journey. Stopovers are standard on flights to the Philippines from Spain; what matters is that the total price is competitive and connection times are reasonable. Check the full itinerary before you confirm.

Best time to visit

The Philippines is tropical; the dry season (November to May) is best: less rain and pleasant temperatures. Avoid the typhoon season (June to October). Prices spike in December and January (Christmas and New Year), so book February, March or April if you want to save. September and October are cheaper but rainier.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Ninoy Aquino International Airport in Manila. The city is chaotic but vibrant: visit Intramuros, the walled old town, and Manila Bay waterfront. From Manila you can easily island-hop: Boracay for stunning beaches, Palawan for diving, or Sagano for mountains. Eat well: Filipino food is cheap and delicious. Local buses and jeepneys are affordable, perfect if your budget is tight.

FAQ

Why are flights to Manila so cheap?

The 3 stopovers mean a long journey (typically 20+ hours), but that lowers the price compared to fewer stops. Vueling offers good value on Asian routes if you're flexible with connections.

Is it safe to fly with 3 stopovers?

Completely safe. Stopovers are standard on long international routes. Check connection times in your itinerary; if they're tight (under 2 hours), consider switching flights. Vueling is an established European airline, so no risk there.

What does the ticket include at this price?

That depends on Vueling's specific fare type. Some prices include hand luggage only; others might include checked baggage or seat selection. Always check the price breakdown before you buy.

What's the best time to book?

Book 6–8 weeks ahead if you can; prices usually rise 2–3 weeks before departure. February to April is shoulder season in the Philippines (good weather, reasonable prices). Avoid last-minute bookings on this route.
