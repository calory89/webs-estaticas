Paris to Lima flights on Air Canada start from €797 return, with a median price of €812. Travelling to Peru is affordable if you book well in advance and stay flexible with dates.

Price and when to book

The cheapest flight we've tracked costs €797 return. The median sits at €812, so expect to land around that range on most searches. We've seen prices climb to €1,011 in peak season. Book 2–3 months ahead: it's the golden rule for a golden fare. October, November and early December are strategic months, but advance booking matters more than the month itself.

Airline and stopovers

Air Canada operates this route with 2 stopovers. There are no direct Paris-Lima flights, so expect a total journey time of 15–18 hours. Multiple stops are standard on intercontinental routes like this one; connection times are usually reasonable when Air Canada handles them.

Best time to visit Peru

The dry season (May to October) is ideal: clear skies, mild temperatures on the coast, and perfect conditions for Andean trekking. December to April brings rain to the jungle and cloudier skies around Machu Picchu, but fewer crowds. Flight prices tend to peak in July–August (European summer holidays) and Christmas. Shoulder seasons (April–May, September–October) often mean cheaper fares.

What to do when you arrive in Lima

You'll land at Jorge Chávez international airport, 16 km from the city centre. From there, explore Lima (world-class food, museums, Miraflores district), head to the highlands for Cusco and Machu Picchu, or visit Nazca. Most travellers spend 2–3 days in Lima, then head to the mountains. Airport buses are cheap; official taxis are also safe and affordable.

What can I see in Peru?

Must-sees include Machu Picchu (from Cusco), the Sacred Valley, Nazca Lines, the Amazon rainforest from Iquitos, and Lake Titicaca. Lima deserves 2–3 days. Plan for 10–14 days or longer depending on your time.
