Airline and stopovers

Best time to travel

Luxembourg is beautiful in autumn and winter, when flight and hotel prices drop. Skip July and August if you're after savings. Spring is also a solid choice with pleasant weather. December and January tend to have lower fares, perfect for quick trips or working remotely.

What to do on arrival

Luxembourg is small but packed with interest: the Ville Vieille (old town) is a UNESCO World Heritage site, the Casemates are fascinating underground galleries, and the MUDAM modern art museum is worth your time. The surrounding area offers vineyards and castles. Getting around by bus or rental car is straightforward.
