Price and when to book your flight

Prices range between €817 and €867 based on sample fares. As this is an international route with stopovers, it's wise to book as far in advance as possible, ideally two to three months before departure. Off-peak months (outside summer and Christmas) typically offer better prices. Compare flexible dates: a couple of days' difference can save you dozens of euros.

Airline and stopovers

Iberia Express operates this route with 3 stopovers on the outbound leg. It's not a direct flight, so allow extra time for connections. The return flight also includes stopovers. If you want more options, compare alternative dates: different routes may offer different stopover combinations.

Best time to visit Fort Lauderdale

Fort Lauderdale enjoys warm weather year-round. The ideal period is November to April: pleasant temperatures and less rainfall. July and August are very hot and humid. Avoid December to January if you're seeking quieter travel, as it's peak season. May, June, September and October can offer good fares and fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive at Fort Lauderdale

Fort Lauderdale International Airport (FLL) is well-connected. Explore Fort Lauderdale and Deerfield Beach, visit the historic district and Las Olas Boulevard shops, or take a day trip to the Everglades. For food lovers, the marina and waterfront restaurants are essential. Miami is just 30 minutes away if you want to explore further.
