Vienna and Sardinia connected from €133 return. Average price sits around €145, so you've got room to spot bargains. One stopover with Ryanair as your operator—the most budget-friendly option to reach the Italian island.

Price and when to book

Tickets start from €133 return (two passengers, full journey). Median price hovers around €145, so if you spot fares near that range, it's time to lock it in. Ryanair usually offers keener prices the earlier you book, especially on quieter routes like this one. Avoid leaving it until close to your travel date.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair is the sole operator on this route. Expect one stopover on the outbound leg—it's not direct, but that's standard at this price point with budget carriers. Connection times are normally short but workable. Return flights follow a similar pattern depending on availability.

Best time to travel

Sardinia shines from May through September, with beaches at their best and warm weather. For peace and quiet at lower fares, try spring (April–May) or autumn (September–October). Winter months (December–February) are cheapest but the sea is cold for swimming.

What to do when you arrive

Cagliari is your entry point. Wander the old town, explore the Roman amphitheatre, or relax at Poetto beach. From there, hire a car and discover the coast: the Costa Smeralda in the north is famous, but the south and inland areas have hidden gems too. Untouched beaches, prehistoric nuraghe ruins, and local food await.

FAQ

Is €133 the total price or per person?

It's the total return price per passenger. Two people means you double it. Ryanair doesn't include checked baggage or seat selection—those cost extra.

Is the flight direct?

No, you have one stopover on the outbound leg. Ryanair operates this route with a connection, which is normal at this price. The stopover is usually brief but keeps costs down.

Which airline flies this route?

Only Ryanair. It's the sole operator between Vienna and Sardinia, so it's your pick if you want the lowest fare.

When is the best time to visit Sardinia?

May to September offers the finest weather and peak beach season. For lower costs, spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) work well: good weather, fewer crowds.
