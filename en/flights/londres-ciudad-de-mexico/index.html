Best time to travel

The best time to visit Mexico City is October to April, when temperatures are milder and there's less rain. April can get warm, so October-March is the most comfortable period. Travel between January and March tends to see higher prices, while September-October and April offer a good balance between weather and fares.

What to do when you arrive

You'll arrive at Benito Juárez International Airport, 12 km from the city centre. Mexico City offers the Teotihuacán Archaeological Zone, Chapultepec Castle, world-class museums and exceptional gastronomy. The historic centre, Coyoacán and Paseo de la Reforma Avenue are essential. The public transport network is extensive and affordable.
