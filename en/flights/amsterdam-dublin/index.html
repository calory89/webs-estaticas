Cheap flights from Amsterdam to Dublin from €61

Ryanair operates direct flights between Amsterdam and Dublin from €61 for a return ticket. The median price hovers around €68, so you have room to find deals if you're flexible with your dates.

Price and when to book

The minimum price of €61 return is genuine, but you need to be quick: many tickets at this price sell out within hours. If you find fares between €61 and €82, you're in a good range. Book at least 4 to 6 weeks ahead to maximise options. Prices climb as your departure date approaches, so don't leave it until the last minute.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair is the operator on this route and offers direct flights: no stopovers, no changes. The sample outbound departs Amsterdam on 5 December at 18:20 and arrives direct. The return on 16 December takes off at 09:30. Direct flights save you time and hassle.

Best time to visit

Dublin appeals year-round, but autumn (September–October) and spring (April–May) are ideal: less rain than winter, fewer crowds than summer. Winter is cheaper and atmospheric if you fancy the cold and lively pub culture. Summer is peak tourist season, so expect bigger crowds and higher prices.

What to do when you arrive in Dublin

The airport sits 10 km from the centre; take a bus or taxi. Ireland's capital has world-class museums (Guinness Storehouse, Trinity College), walks along the Liffey, Temple Bar for live music, and shops on Grafton Street. The surroundings are worth exploring too: Howth cliffs, Dún Laoghaire coast. Get around by bus or on foot; the city is walkable.

FAQ

Are there really flights at €61 Amsterdam to Dublin?

Yes, but in limited numbers. Ryanair has options from €61 return, though not every day or every week. The median is €68, so expect to pay around that range. The earlier you book, the better your chances.

Are these direct flights or do they have stopovers?

Direct. Ryanair flies non-stop between Amsterdam and Dublin. The journey is short, so there are no intermediate stops.

Which airline operates this route?

Ryanair. It's a low-cost carrier, so check what's included in your fare: hand luggage usually yes, checked baggage often costs extra.

When should I book this route?

Between 4 and 6 weeks before your trip. Within that window you'll find solid prices and availability. Booking less than 2 weeks ahead typically pushes costs up.
