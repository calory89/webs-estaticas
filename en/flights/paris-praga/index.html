Best time to visit

Prague is beautiful year-round, but October to March is cheaper and less crowded. Winters are harsh but magical (especially around Christmas). Spring (April-May) and autumn (September) offer good weather without peak-season prices. July-August is high season: expect crowds and higher fares.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Václav Havel Airport (PRG), 20 km from the centre. Best options: direct bus, train or shared taxi. Once in the city, Prague hooks you: Prague Castle, Charles Bridge, Old Town Square, medieval alleys and Czech beer for three euros. Two or three days cover the essentials; with more time, explore Kutná Hora (Gothic ossuary) or the Moldava valley.
