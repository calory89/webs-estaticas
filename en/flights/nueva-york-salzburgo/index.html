Best time to visit

Salzburg is worth visiting year-round. Winter (December to February) brings snow and Christmas markets; spring and autumn offer mild weather and fewer crowds. Summer is peak season with higher prices. Consider the destination and your climate preference when choosing your dates.

What to do when you arrive

Salzburg is Mozart's birthplace and a baroque gem. Explore the old town, Hohensalzburg Fortress, the Cathedral, and palatial squares. Lake Fuschl and the Berchtesgaden Alps are nearby for day trips. W.A. Mozart Airport (SZG) is 4 km from the centre with direct bus and taxi links.
