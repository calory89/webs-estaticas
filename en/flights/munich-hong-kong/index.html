Air China connects Munich with Hong Kong from €518 return. Most tickets on this route average €519. Fly in December or after summer peak season for the best value.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €518 for return flights. The median sits at €519, so there's not much variation between the cheapest and typical fares. Other sample prices: €568. Book as early as you can; long-haul routes like this tend to climb as departure approaches, especially during peak season.

Airline and stops

Air China operates this route with one stop. It's not a direct flight, but the price is competitive for a distance of nearly 10,000 km. The connection adds travel time, though many passengers accept it for the savings.

Best time to travel

December and the months after summer peak (August–September) typically offer lower fares. Avoid July and August if you're budget-conscious. Hong Kong has a tropical climate; winter (December–February) is cooler and drier, ideal for sightseeing.

What to do when you arrive

Hong Kong is a must for its Victoria Harbour views, the Peak Tram, and vibrant neighbourhoods like Mong Kok and Central. Eat at street stalls on Temple Street and Temple Fair, explore traditional temples, and take the Star Ferry between Hong Kong Island and Kowloon. The airport is well connected by public transport to the city centre.
