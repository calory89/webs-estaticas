Best time to visit

Madeira has mild weather year-round. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) bring good conditions and less rain. Winter is wetter but perfect for lush hiking. July and August are warmest and busiest.

What to do when you arrive

Cristiano Ronaldo Airport (FNC) is 17 km from Funchal centre. Hike the laurel forest, swim in natural pools, ride the cable car in Achada, spot whales on boat tours. Local food is bold: espetada (scabbardfish), sweet cakes and poncha (local spirit). Transport card available for bus and funicular.
