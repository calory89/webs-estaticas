easyJet connects Gran Canaria with London on direct flights from just €51 return. The median price sits around €53, so you have real chances to travel on a tight budget. Check dates and book in advance.

Price and when to book

Prices start from €51 return, with a median of €53. We've also spotted fares at €95. Book as early as possible: direct routes with budget airlines sell out fast, especially if you're travelling in shoulder or peak season. Aim to book 4–6 weeks ahead to secure the best deal.

Airline and stops

easyJet operates this route with zero stops: you depart Gran Canaria (LPA) and arrive direct in London (LON). No changes, no hassle. It's the quickest and most convenient way to get there. Remember that easyJet charges for large cabin bags and checked luggage: check their website before buying.

Best time to travel

Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) offer the best balance of price and comfort. Winter is peak season and prices climb. Summer is busy too. If your dates are flexible, avoid Spanish and UK school holidays and bank holidays to find cheaper fares.

What to do when you arrive

London has free museums (British Museum, National Gallery, V&A), markets like Camden and Portobello Road, big parks (Hyde Park, Richmond Park), and Thames riverside walks. Use an Oyster Card for public transport or walk—many neighbourhoods are pedestrian-friendly. Grab fish and chips at a local pub and spend at least 3–4 days enjoying the city at a relaxed pace.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for Gran Canaria to London flights?

The lowest fare we've seen is €51 return with easyJet. The median is €53, so if you find tickets in that range, you've got a solid deal. Some prices reach €95 if you book late or during peak season.

Are there direct flights from Gran Canaria to London?

Yes, easyJet flies direct (no stops) from Gran Canaria to London. It's the quickest option and you won't need to change planes.

When should I book to get the best price?

Book 4–6 weeks in advance. The earlier you purchase, the more options and lower fares you'll have. Direct budget airline flights sell out quickly, especially in spring and autumn.

Which is the main airport in London?

easyJet arrives in London (LON), which refers to one of its major airports. From any of them, you'll have good public transport connections to the city centre.
