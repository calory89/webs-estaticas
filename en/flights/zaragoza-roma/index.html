Rome is within reach from Zaragoza for just €73 return with Wizz Air Malta. The median price hovers around €76, so if you spot that figure, you've got a solid deal. Book early and fly direct to Italy's capital.

Price and when to book

The cheapest flight found is €73 return. Prices climb to €95 during peak demand periods. The earlier you book, the better: fares tend to rise as departure dates approach. Check deals midweek, as weekends are typically pricier.

Airline and stops

You'll travel with Wizz Air Malta, a reliable low-cost carrier. The route is direct from Zaragoza (ZAZ) to Rome (ROM), no layovers, saving time and hassle. Hand luggage is included; checked baggage comes at an extra charge.

Best time to visit

Rome works year-round, but cheap flights are especially common in winter (January to March) and autumn (October to November). Skip Christmas, Easter and summer for lower fares. February and March see fewer tourists and cheaper flights overall.

What to do when you arrive

From Fiumicino airport, reach the centre by train or bus in 30–60 minutes. Rome has the Colosseum, Roman Forum, Trevi Fountain, the Vatican, and Tiber walks. Eat pasta in backstreet trattorias, visit baroque churches and wander medieval alleys. Three days cover the essentials.

FAQ

Is it safe to fly with Wizz Air Malta?

Yes. Wizz Air Malta is an established low-cost carrier across Europe with a solid track record. It meets international safety standards. Bear in mind it's a budget airline, so extras like luggage and add-ons cost extra.

Is the Zaragoza to Rome flight direct?

Yes, it's a direct flight. It departs Zaragoza (ZAZ) and arrives in Rome (ROM) with no stops in between.

Which Rome airport does it land at?

You'll land at Leonardo da Vinci Fiumicino Airport (FCO), Rome's main hub. From there, the Leonardo Express train or buses connect quickly to the city centre.

What are the best months to fly to Rome cheaply?

February, March, October and November typically have fewer tourists and lower flight prices. Avoid December, Easter and July–August if you want to save on the ticket.
