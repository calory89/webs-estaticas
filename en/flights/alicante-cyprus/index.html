Best time to visit

Cyprus is a sun and beach destination year-round. The prime season is April to October, when weather is warm and swimming is perfect. July and August bring peak heat and crowds. For low prices and fewer tourists, April–May is unbeatable. Winter is cooler but still highly rewarding.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Larnaca International Airport. Ayia Napa and Protaras beaches are famous and buzzing with bars. For history, head to Nicosia, the split capital. The Troodos mountains offer hiking and charming villages. Don't skip the ruins at Kourion or Aphrodite's Baths. The quieter north holds hidden gems.
