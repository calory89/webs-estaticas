Fly Berlin to Bologna for just €124 return. The median price sits around €158, so there's room to find real bargains. Wizz Air Malta operates this route with one stop along the way.

Price and when to book

The lowest price is €124, but most searches land around €158. Prices climb to €185 during peak dates. As of late October, book at least 4–6 weeks ahead to lock in a good rate. Less than 3 weeks away, expect to pay more.

Airline and stopovers

Wizz Air Malta operates this route with 1 stop. It's not a direct flight, so add 1–3 hours to your journey depending on the connection airport and layover time. As a low-cost carrier, Wizz Air charges separately for large baggage and seat selection—a small cabin bag is included.

Best time to visit

Bologna shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter brings cheaper fares but shorter days. Skip August if you want to avoid peak season crowds and higher prices across the city.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Guglielmo Marconi Airport (BLQ), 6 km from the city centre. Train or bus will get you there in 20 minutes. Bologna is famous for medieval towers, 40 km of arcaded streets (porticoes), incredible food (Bolognese sauce, tortellini) and Europe's oldest university. The old town alone will keep you busy for days.
