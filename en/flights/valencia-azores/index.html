Best time to visit

The Azores have mild Atlantic weather year-round, but May to September offers the least rain and most daylight. If you travel in winter (like the January example), you'll pay less but expect cloudy skies and occasional showers. Spring and autumn are a good balance between price and weather.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in Ponta Delgada (São Miguel island), the largest of the Azores. Visit Lagoa de Santiago, stroll the historic centre, hike to Pico da Vara, or explore the volcanic coastline. With time to spare, take a ferry to islands like Faial or Terceira. Local food (caldo verde, tuna, seafood) is essential.
