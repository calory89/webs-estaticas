Airline and stopovers

Scoot operates this route with one stop on the outbound flight. There are no direct options, so factor in the connection time to your total journey. Scoot is known for competitive long-haul pricing, though services are low-cost and extras are charged separately.

Best time to travel

January offers a quieter escape from summer crowds. Naples weather is mild (10–15°C) and perfect for sightseeing. Avoid July and August when tourist numbers and prices peak. April–May and September–October are balanced months with good weather and fewer visitors.

What to do when you arrive in Naples

Capodichino airport is 6 km from the centre. Visit the Cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta, the National Archaeological Museum and Spaccanapoli's historic street. Don't miss Pompeii (25 km south) and the Amalfi coast. Local food is essential: authentic pizza, fresh pasta and limoncello.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Singapore to Naples?

No. Scoot operates this route with one stop on the outbound flight. Non-stop options are not available on this connection.

What's the lowest price you've seen?

The cheapest fare on record is €657 return. The median sits at €697, so use that range as your benchmark.

Is Scoot a good airline for such a long flight?

Scoot is a low-cost carrier specialised in Asian long-haul routes. It offers competitive pricing, but onboard services (meals, baggage, premium seats) are limited and charged separately. If budget is your priority, it's a solid option.

What else can I see in Naples besides Pompeii?

The Cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta, the National Archaeological Museum, Spaccanapoli, royal palaces and the Amalfi coast are must-sees. Food culture is also a major draw: pizza, homemade pasta and local produce.
