Best time to go

Zanzibar works year-round, though the dry season (June to October) offers the best weather. January is also excellent, with warm temperatures and calm seas. Choose based on your heat tolerance and weather preferences.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land near Stone Town, the historic old quarter with winding stone streets, bustling bazaars and fascinating maritime history. Nungwi and Paje beaches are perfect for relaxation. Take a spice plantation tour or venture to nearby Pemba island for diving and culture.
