Best time to travel

Sicily shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): perfect weather, fewer crowds than summer, and more contained prices. Summer is peak season with more tourists and potential fare increases. Winter is cheaper but some attractions reduce their activity.

What to do when you arrive

Palermo is your gateway to Sicily. Explore the historic centre, try street food at Vucciria market, visit Palermo Cathedral and stroll around the harbour. From the capital you have easy access to nearby beaches, coastal towns like Mondello and the Tyrrhenian coast. The airport is 35 km from the city centre; buses and taxis get you to town quickly.
