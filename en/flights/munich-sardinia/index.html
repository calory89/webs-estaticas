Best time to visit

Sardinia shines year-round, but May to September is peak season: perfect weather and warm sea. For fewer crowds and solid prices, aim for shoulder months like April-May or October. Winter is quieter but cooler. Plan ahead: flights in high season sell out fast.

What to do when you arrive

Cagliari, the capital, greets you with urban beaches and a charming old town. From there, head north to the Costa Smeralda for upmarket beaches and white sand, or east to Baronia for wild coves. Don't miss Orosei, Gola di Gorropu, or the white sands of Piscinas. With more time, explore Olbia or Alghero in the north. Try malloreddus and bottarga, drink local vermentino.
