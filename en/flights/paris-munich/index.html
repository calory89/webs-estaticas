Best time to travel

Munich is appealing year-round. In winter, enjoy a festive atmosphere if you travel December–January, with Christmas markets and a quieter Oktoberfest season. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild weather and fewer crowds. Summers are warm but busy. Choose based on your preferences: visiting in lower-demand periods often brings better prices for accommodation and activities.

What to do when you arrive in Munich

Munich airport is well connected to the city centre by train and bus (about 40 minutes). Spend time at Marienplatz with its striking Gothic cathedral, Nymphenburg Palace, and the Pinacotheca museums. If you love beer, visit Hofbräuhaus. The Bavarian Alps are less than two hours away, perfect for hiking. Public transport is efficient and distances are short, making it easy to get around.
