What to do in Salzburg

Salzburg is Mozart's birthplace: visit his house on Getreidegasse, explore the UNESCO-listed Old Town, and climb Hohensalzburg fortress. Lake Fuschl and the Alps are close by. Eat apple strudel and schnitzel. A 24 or 48-hour city card saves you money on museums and transport.
