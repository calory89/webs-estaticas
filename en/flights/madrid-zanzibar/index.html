Best time to visit

Zanzibar shines between June and October—dry weather and clear skies. December marks the start of the rainy season, though tourism peaks then too. If you want fewer crowds and better rates, try April–May or September.

What to do when you arrive

Zanzibar airport is on the main island. From there, explore Stone Town's winding streets, relax on white-sand beaches like Nungwi or Paje, or take a spice island tour. Snorkelling and diving are essential here.
