Best time to visit

Santorini is a seasonal destination. Prices are lower outside July and August, and also in November-December before Christmas holidays. If your travel dates are flexible, avoid peak tourist season: you'll get better flight deals and enjoy the island more.

What to do when you arrive

Santorini airport (JTR) is 5 km from Fira, the capital. Explore white and blue villages like Oia, hike down into the caldera, taste local wines at wineries, or relax on volcanic sand beaches. Rent a car or use local buses to get around the island. Don't miss the sunset from any vantage point.
