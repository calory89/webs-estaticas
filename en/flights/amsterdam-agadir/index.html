Transavia connects Amsterdam to Agadir non-stop with no layovers. Prices start at €159, with an average of €179 for return flights. Beach life in Morocco has never been this close to the Netherlands.

Price and when to book

Fares range from €159 to €202 depending on date and availability. Booking 2-3 months ahead typically offers the best balance of price and options. Check regularly: this route has price swings, especially in peak season. Tuesdays and Wednesdays are usually cheaper booking days.

Airline and connections

Transavia operates this route as a direct service. Non-stop from AMS to AGA means no layovers or transfers. Transavia is a budget carrier, so food and drinks are purchased separately; checked baggage may incur fees.

Best time to visit

Agadir enjoys mild weather nearly year-round, but October to April brings the sweetest temperatures (18-25°C) without summer extremes. Skip July-August if you dislike intense heat. The coast is the main draw: beaches, seafront walks, local markets. Winter is ideal: fewer crowds than peak European summer.

What to do in Agadir

Wide, well-kept beaches; the Medina for shopping and street food; the Fish Port for fresh seafood daily catch; the Kasbah for city panoramas and sunsets. Perfect for evening strolls. Close to nearby coastal villages for day trips—Essaouira is about 3 hours away. Local currency: Moroccan dirham (MAD).

FAQ

Is Amsterdam-Agadir a direct flight?

Yes, Transavia operates this route non-stop. Direct flight from AMS to AGA with no layovers or connection hassle.

What's the cheapest price you've found?

The lowest price recorded is €159 return. Average is around €179. Prices rise above €200 in peak demand (high season, weekends).

Which airline flies this route?

Transavia operates it. It's a low-cost carrier owned by Air France-KLM. Modern Boeing 737 fleet. Remember: meals, drinks and checked bags come with extra charges.

What can I see and do in Agadir?

Golden-sand beaches, the Medina for shopping and local food, the Fish Port with a daily market, the Kasbah for panoramic views, and day trips to nearby coastal towns. Plenty of nightlife and entertainment options too.
