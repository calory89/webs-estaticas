Best time to visit Maldives

November to April is peak season: dry weather, clear skies and calm seas. May to October brings rain and monsoons. If cheap fares are your priority, June to August can have lower prices, though the weather is less stable.

What to do when you arrive

Maldives means beaches, coral reefs and diving. Malé airport (MLE) is your entry point. Most resorts offer boat or seaplane transfers from there. Snorkel, dive, fish, enjoy spa treatments and sample local food. Accommodation ranges from budget guesthouses to luxury resorts.
