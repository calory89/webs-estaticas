Best time to visit

Portland is attractive in autumn and winter to see leaves change and enjoy mild weather. Spring is also a good option. Avoid peak tourist months if you're looking for lower prices, though the difference depends on many factors. Book months ahead to secure your fare.

What to do when you arrive

Portland is known for its cultural scene, independent cafés and parks. Portland International Airport (PDX) is about 10 km from the city centre. From there you can take public transport, a taxi or hire a car. The city has plenty to offer: Powell's Books (a massive bookstore), the Portland Art Museum, Forest Park (one of the largest urban parks in the USA) and the Oregon coast is nearby for day trips.
