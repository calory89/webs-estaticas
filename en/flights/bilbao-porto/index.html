Price and when to book

Prices range from €47 to €52 at the median. Most searches fall within that bracket. Book in advance: the earlier you buy, the better the fare. Avoid last-minute changes and watch out for low-season deals.

Airline and stopovers

Vueling operates this route. The best part: the flight is direct from Bilbao to Porto, no stopovers. Departure at 19:10 and arrival at 20:55 (example times). Short flight, no connection stress.

Best time to visit

Porto is lovely year-round. Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) offer great weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is cheaper but rainier. Book several months ahead if you're travelling in peak season.

What to do when you arrive

Porto deserves at least 3–4 days. Explore Ribeira (the historic centre), climb Torre dos Clérigos, sample Port wine in the cellars of Vila Nova de Gaia, and cross the iconic Dom Luís I bridge. The city is compact and walkable. The airport is 15 km away; bus and taxi options available.
