Best time to visit

Mauritius has a tropical climate. November falls in the warm, humid season but remains viable. May to October (Southern Hemisphere winter) offers milder temperatures and less rain. Plan based on your heat tolerance and budget: peak seasons are generally pricier.

What to do when you arrive

Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam Airport (MRU) is about 60 km from the coast. From there, explore northern beaches like Trou aux Biches, visit Hindu temples in Port-Louis, snorkel or dive at coral reefs, or tour sugar cane plantations. It's perfect for mixing nature, culture and relaxation.
