Find direct flights from Madrid to Budapest with Ryanair from just €61 return. The median price is €75, making this route highly affordable. Fly to Hungary's capital without stops and explore one of Central Europe's best destinations.

Price and when to book

Prices start from €61 return; the median sits at €75 and can reach €117 depending on dates. For these competitive fares, book as far in advance as possible. Ryanair typically offers better prices the earlier you purchase, so don't wait until the last minute. Compare flexible dates: small changes to your travel dates can make a significant difference in the final price.

Airline and direct flights

Ryanair is the airline operating this route and offers direct flights from Madrid to Budapest with no stops. Madrid-Barajas (MAD) connects directly to Budapest Ferenc Liszt Airport (BUD), making for a quick, hassle-free journey. Remember Ryanair is a low-cost carrier, so check their baggage policy carefully: cabin luggage is included, but checked baggage incurs an additional fee.

Best time to visit

Budapest is an attractive destination year-round. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter months are cold but magical, especially during the Christmas markets. Avoid major holidays and bank holidays to find better prices and less congestion in the city.

What to do in Budapest

Budapest is a must-see: the Széchenyi Thermal Baths, Parliament, the Danube, St Stephen's Basilica and Buda Castle are essential visits. Stroll through the Old Town, try Hungarian cuisine (goulash, langos) and cross the bridges that connect Buda and Pest. The city has vibrant nightlife, unique ruin bars and art galleries. With such cheap flights, it's worth extending your stay.
