Airline and stopovers

Best time to visit

Budapest is lovely year-round. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer perfect weather and fewer crowds than summer. Flight prices are usually lower outside June, July, August and December. Winter is cheaper but cold (around 0°C).

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Ferenc Liszt International Airport (BUD), 16 km from the centre. From there, bus 200E goes directly to Blaha Lujza Station (30-40 min) or use a taxi or rideshare. Budapest deserves at least 3 days: Parliament, Széchenyi Thermal Baths, views from Chain Bridge, Jewish Quarter, Buda caves. Hungarian food is excellent and cheap.

Prices generally drop if you book 6-8 weeks in advance. They tend to be lowest between November-March (except Christmas) and in spring. July-August and December-January are peak season.

What to see in Budapest in 3-4 days?

Parliament, Széchenyi Thermal Baths, Chain Bridge, Fishermen's Bastion, Jewish Quarter, Buda Castle, Margaret Island, and an evening stroll along the Danube. The city also has vibrant nightlife and unique ruin bars worth exploring.
