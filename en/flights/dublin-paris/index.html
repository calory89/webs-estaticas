Cheap flights Dublin to Paris from €38

Ryanair connects Dublin to Paris from €38 return. Flights are direct and fares on dates you'll find hover around that price. Book now: less than a month before departure, good seats vanish.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we found is €38 return. Fares on this route range from €38 to €53. As a rule, book as far in advance as possible: Ryanair typically raises prices as departure approaches. Check the search engine daily if dates are flexible; occasional deals pop up, but don't expect bargains in the final two weeks.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route non-stop. You fly direct from Dublin (DUB) to Paris. Check your booking to see which Paris terminal you arrive at; Ryanair often uses secondary airports. Bear in mind baggage: on low-cost fares, checked luggage costs extra.

Best time to visit

Paris is worth visiting year-round. April, May, September and October are ideal: pleasant weather, less summer crowds and better value than July-August. If you travel in November (like the example), expect cold temperatures and more rain, but fewer tourists. January and February are the coldest months.

What to do when you arrive

Arrange your airport transfer beforehand (metro, bus or taxi). Paris has the Eiffel Tower, the Louvre, Notre-Dame, Montmartre, the Musée d'Orsay and Rodin Museum, Seine walks and cafés on every corner. Buy a Museum Pass if you're visiting several attractions. Walking is the best way to explore the city.

FAQ

Is the Dublin-Paris flight really €38 all-in?

Yes, that's the return price we found on this search. Heads up: it's Ryanair's base fare. Baggage, reserved seat and other services cost extra. Always check the price breakdown before you pay.

Does Ryanair fly direct from Dublin to Paris with no stops?

Yes, it's a direct flight. It departs Dublin and lands in Paris with no intermediate stops. The journey is around 2 hours.

Which airline operates this route?

Ryanair is the carrier offering these fares. It's a European low-cost airline, so basic services are included but many add-ons cost extra (checked baggage, seat selection, food, drinks).

What's the best time of year for this flight?

Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) are ideal: pleasant weather and moderate tourism. Avoid July-August if you want to skip peak crowds. Winter (January-February) is colder but cheap and quieter.
