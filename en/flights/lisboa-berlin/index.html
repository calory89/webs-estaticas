Best time to visit

Berlin works year-round. Winter (December–February) is cold but atmospheric, with fewer crowds. Spring and autumn offer mild weather and lower prices than summer. July and August are pricey and packed. For authentic experiences at good value, skip summer and book 6–8 weeks ahead.

What to do in Berlin

Berlin won't break the bank. Must-sees: Brandenburg Gate, Jewish Quarter, Mauer Park (Wall remnants), Museum Island, Charlottenburg Palace. Eat at street markets in Kreuzberg, drink beer in Friedrichshain. Public transport is cheap and reliable. Stay in Mitte or Wedding for central location without tourist prices.
