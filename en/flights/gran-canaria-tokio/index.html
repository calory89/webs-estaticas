Best time to visit Tokyo

Tokyo shines in spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November), when the climate is mild and days are clear. Avoid summer (very humid) and harsh winters. If you travel in peak season, expect more crowds and potentially higher prices. Flexibility with dates remains your best weapon.

What to do when you arrive in Tokyo

You'll land at Tokyo airport (TYO covers Narita and Haneda). From the centre, explore Shibuya and Shinjuku, visit traditional temples like Senso-ji, try cuisine at Tsukiji, and don't miss the Shibuya crossing. Tokyo blends tradition and modernity at every turn. Check local public transport and museum opening hours before you go.
