Airline and stops

Vueling is your carrier. Flights include 2 stops on the outbound leg. Although they take longer than a direct flight, the savings are substantial. Check your booking to see where the stops are and how much time you'll spend on the ground.

Best time to visit

Santorini shines in April-May and September-October: perfect weather, fewer crowds than summer, and better prices than July-August. Winter is cooler with occasional rain, but you'll find less tourism and good price opportunities.

What to do when you arrive in Santorini

You'll land at Santorini Airport (JTR). From there, rent a car or use public transport to reach the white villages of Oia and Fira, the black sand beaches in Kamari, or the clifftop views. Taste local cuisine and don't miss the sunset in Oia, it's one of the most stunning in the world.

FAQ
