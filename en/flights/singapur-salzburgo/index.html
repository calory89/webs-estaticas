Best time to visit

Salzburg shines in winter (December to February) with snow and Christmas markets, and in summer (June to August) for Alpine exploration. Autumn (September to October) is pleasant and less crowded. Avoid July and August if you want fewer tourists. From Singapore, European winter aligns with your spring.

What to do when you arrive

Salzburg is Mozart's birthplace and the Sound of Music filming location. Walk the Altstadt (old town), climb Hohensalzburg fortress for panoramic views, and explore baroque palaces. The Salzburg Alps are 30 minutes by train away. The city is small, walkable on foot. W.A. Mozart Airport (SZG) is 4 km from the centre, with bus and taxi nearby.
