Ryanair connects Barcelona and Rome with direct flights from €39 return. The median price hovers around €41, so you'll regularly find deals in this range. Here's everything you need to book at the best rate.

Price and when to book

Flights start from €39 return, with a median of €41. Prices vary by date: we've seen fares of €39, €41 and €45 in our sample checked on 21 October 2026. Book in advance and stay flexible with dates; early-morning flights tend to be cheaper. Check regularly—slight variations in your search can save you a few euros.

Airline and stops

Ryanair is the airline on this route, offering direct flights with no stops. You depart Barcelona and arrive in Rome without intermediate landings, saving time and hassle. Bear in mind that Ryanair charges for hand luggage and checked bags, so factor this into your budget.

Best time to visit

Rome is worth visiting year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. In winter (November–February) prices tend to drop, ideal if you're chasing savings. Avoid August if you want to skip extreme heat and tourist masses.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Fiumicino Airport (FCO), about 30 km from the centre. There's a direct train to Termini central station in 30 minutes. Don't miss the Colosseum, Trevi Fountain, the Vatican and the Roman Forum. The city is perfect for walking or using public transport. Eat carbonara, cacio e pepe and authentic gelato.
