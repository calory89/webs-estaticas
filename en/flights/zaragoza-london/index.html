Best time to visit

October to March is London's low season: fewer tourists, lower prices and cold but manageable weather (bring a coat). April and May are spring: flowers, long days and mid-range prices. June to August is summer: pricey, crowded and good weather. To save, travel outside Spanish school holidays and bank holidays. Easter, Christmas and half-terms spike prices, so avoid them if your budget is tight.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at one of London's airports (Stansted, Luton, Gatwick or Southend, depending on the day). From there, fast public transport to the centre: train, bus or Tube. London offers free museums (British Museum, National Gallery, V&A), the Thames, Tower Bridge, Covent Garden, Camden Market and West End theatres. Eat authentic fish and chips, try local beer and walk around neighbourhoods like Shoreditch or Notting Hill. Three days are enough to see the essentials without rushing.
