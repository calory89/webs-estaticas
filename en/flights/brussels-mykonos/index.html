Best time to visit

Mykonos shines in summer (June, July, August) if you want busy beaches and nightlife. Shoulder season (May, September) delivers sun with fewer crowds. Winter is quieter and cheaper. The route runs year-round: choose what suits you. Note: December–February the sea is cold for swimming, but perfect for strolls without queues.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Mykonos Airport (JMK). White-washed villages of Santorini are 1 hour by ferry. In Mykonos: sandy beaches (Paradise, Super Paradise), the old port with cube-shaped houses, shops and tavernas. Delos (nearby island) is an essential archaeological site. Rent a quad or car: the island is small (170 km²) but spread out. Sunsets over Oia (Santorini) or the Mykonos port are classic. Eat fresh: grilled octopus, Greek salad, local wine.
