Bologna is beautiful year-round, but skip July and August if you want fewer crowds and better accommodation rates. Autumn and winter are quieter seasons. Prices shift with holidays and long weekends, so flexibility with dates is your edge.

What to do when you arrive

Bologna airport (Marconi) is 4 km from the centre. Bus, taxi and shuttle all available. The city is a UNESCO heritage site: Torre Asinelli, Piazza Maggiore, Basilica di San Petronio. Eat proper tortellini and walk Via Pescherie Vecchie. For art, Pinacoteca is essential.
