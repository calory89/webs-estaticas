Price and when to book

The lowest fare found is €768 return, with prices ranging from €768 to €795. Booking 3–4 weeks ahead is your best bet to secure these rates. Avoid last-minute purchases; they're significantly more expensive. November is a good window if you want reasonable prices before the high Christmas season.

Airline and stopovers

Lufthansa operates this route with 2 stopovers on both outbound and return. There are no direct flights Berlin–San Juan, so expect a longer journey, but the fares are very competitive. Stopovers are typically at European or American hubs, which adds travel time but substantially cuts the price compared to fewer-stop operators.

Best time to travel

November and December offer good weather in San Juan, though December hits peak season with steeper prices. Rainy season runs May to October. If you want beaches and outdoor activities without paying double, aim for November or early December before fares skyrocket.

What to do when you arrive

Old San Juan is a must—cobbled streets and colonial architecture make it unforgettable. Castillo San Felipe del Morro is perfect for a half-day visit. Luquillo Beach is under an hour away, and if nature appeals to you, the Dry Forest of Guánica or the islands of Vieques and Culebra are stunning day trips from San Juan.

No. This route requires 2 stopovers on both legs. Lufthansa is the available option at these prices, so expect around 14–16 hours total travel time including layovers.

The lowest recorded is €768 return. The median is €787, so if you find fares in that range, book without hesitation. Prices can climb quickly.

Will the price drop in the coming days?

There's no way to predict price movements. If you find €768–€787, it's a good moment to buy. Waiting is risky; fares usually rise as your departure date approaches.

Is it better to travel in November or wait for December?
