Best time to visit

Denmark shines year-round. Summer (June-August) brings long days and pleasant weather, but it's peak season. Spring and autumn offer fewer crowds and steadier prices. Winter has Christmas charm and northern lights further north, though it gets cold.

Things to do in Copenhagen

See Nyhavn's colourful facades, visit Amalienborg Palace, explore the bohemian Christiania district and savour Nordic cuisine. Danes excel at 'hygge' (comfort and cosiness), so spend time in local cafés. Rent a bike like a local and discover the city at Copenhagen's pace.
