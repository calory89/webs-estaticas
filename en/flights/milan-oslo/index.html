Price and when to book

Milan-Oslo flights start from €41, with mid-range prices around €57 and highs near €100 return. Ryanair is your only option on this route. To secure the best price, book as far in advance as possible; demand on international routes grows closer to departure. If your dates are flexible, watch for flash sales, though no guarantee the price will drop below €41.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route with direct flights—no intermediate stops. You board in Milan and land in Oslo without changing planes, simplifying your journey and saving ground time.

Best time to visit

Norway shines in winter for Northern Lights (September to March), though peak season hikes prices. Autumn (September–October) and spring (April–May) balance pleasant weather with better fares. Summer is peak tourist season. Avoid travelling without flexible dates; if you can choose, pick periods outside school holidays.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Oslo Airport (Gardermoen), about 60 km from the city centre. Fast trains reach the city in 20 minutes. In Oslo, visit the Viking Ship Museum, explore Grünerløkka neighbourhood, climb the Opera House, or venture to nearby fjords. The City Hall is iconic. With more time, head north for Northern Lights or to the Flåm fjords.
