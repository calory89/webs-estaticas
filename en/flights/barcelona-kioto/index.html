Price and when to book

The lowest price on record is €616, but you'll find fares ranging from €616 to €814. The median hovers around €720. For this intercontinental route, aim to book 2–3 months ahead. Prices fluctuate, but the earlier you secure your ticket, the fewer surprises you'll face.

Airline and stopovers

Air China is your best-value option on this route. All flights include 1 stopover, typically in Beijing or Shanghai, which is standard for Europe–Japan journeys. Long-haul trips from Europe to Japan almost always route through central Asia. Accept it as part of the experience: stops are brief and well-coordinated.

Best time to visit

Kyoto shines in spring (March–May) and autumn (October–November): mild weather and stunning scenery. Winter (December–February) is cold but less crowded. Skip July and August if you can't handle extreme heat and humidity. Check the festival calendar before you decide.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in Osaka (Kansai), just 75 km from Kyoto. Take the Haruka train for 75 minutes and you're downtown. Kyoto is a must: historic temples, geishas in Gion, bamboo groves in Arashiyama, and traditional markets. Plan at least 3–4 days. From there, Osaka, Nara or Hiroshima are within easy reach.
