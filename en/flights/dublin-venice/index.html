Price and when to book your flight

The lowest price recorded is €47, whilst the median price sits at €55 return. Some flights reach €140 depending on dates. For these tight fares, it's ideal to book in advance (6-8 weeks) and be flexible with dates. Keep an eye on Ryanair promotions mid-week, when they tend to be lower.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route non-stop: you depart Dublin at approximately 09:20 and arrive in Venice at 21:45. A direct flight that saves you time and hassle. Bear in mind that Ryanair charges for hand luggage if you exceed dimensions, and for checked baggage.

Best time to travel

Venice is beautiful any time of year, but prices drop outside peak season (avoid July, August and Easter). Autumn (September–October) and early winter (November–December) typically offer cheaper flights and fewer tourists. Check specific dates to find your best deals.

What to do when you arrive in Venice

You'll arrive at Venice Marco Polo Airport. From there, take the ACTV bus or vaporetto (water transport) towards the historic centre, 15 km away. In Venice there are no cars: everything is done on foot or by boat. Visit the Basilica di San Marco, the Rialto Bridge, cross the canals and enjoy local dining in the trattorias.
