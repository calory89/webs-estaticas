Best time to visit

Bucharest is pleasant year-round, but spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer ideal temperatures for sightseeing. Winter is cold with occasional snow. Summer is warm but can be busy. Always check the weather forecast for your exact travel dates before you go.

What to do in Bucharest

The Palace of Parliament is essential: one of the world's largest buildings. Stroll through the Old Town, visit Stavropoleos Church and Stavropoleos Monastery. The Village Museum preserves traditional rural architecture. Eat at local restaurants in Lipscani and enjoy the nightlife. Bran Castle, Romania's most famous fortress, is 140 km away.
