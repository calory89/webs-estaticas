Best time to visit

Bologna is appealing year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: mild weather, fewer crowds than summer, and better prices than July–August. Winter (December–February) can also be budget-friendly if cold doesn't bother you. Skip August if you want better rates.

What to do when you arrive

Bologna prides itself on food: tagliatelle al ragù, tortellini, mortadella. Tour the Two Towers (city symbols), the Basilica of San Petronio, and the Portico of San Luca. Piazza Maggiore is the heart where you'll feel the city's pulse. Bologna airport (BLQ) is 6 km from the centre, with quick bus or taxi links.
