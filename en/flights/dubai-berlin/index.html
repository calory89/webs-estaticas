Price and when to book

Prices range from €304 to €385 for return flights. The sooner you book, the better: recent data shows available fares at competitive rates, but prices tend to rise closer to departure. Aim to reserve 2–3 weeks in advance to maximise savings. Late-November trips often deliver excellent value.

Airline and stopovers

Eurowings operates this route with direct flights—no stopovers. You'll travel in a single leg from Dubai (DXB) to Berlin (BER), saving you time and hassle. If you want reliability, Eurowings is a solid choice on Gulf-Europe routes.

Best time to travel

November and early December are ideal: you escape Dubai's extreme heat, and Berlin's cold arrives gently. Prices also tend to be competitive outside holidays and public holidays. Berlin shines with its Christmas markets from mid-November onwards.

What to do when you arrive

Berlin deserves at least 3–4 days. Explore Museum Island, visit the Brandenburg Gate, climb the Reichstag, and wander Kreuzberg's alternative neighbourhoods. Christmas markets (if travelling then) are unmissable. Berlin airport connects to the city centre in around 40 minutes by public transport.
