Price and when to book

Prices range between €139 and €150 for a return journey. The median stands at €148. Booking 2 months in advance is usually a solid strategy for this route; as with any short-haul European flight, the closer to departure, the fewer cheap options remain. Check flexible dates carefully: a single day's difference can mean €10–15 in savings.

Airline and no stopovers

Austrian Airlines operates this route non-stop. No stopovers means one landing in Belgrade and a flight of around 1 hour. It's comfortable, quick and hassle-free for your luggage. If your ticket includes a checked bag, verify it in your confirmation.

Best time to travel

Belgrade is worth visiting almost year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant temperatures and less extreme heat. Avoid July–August if you don't handle hot weather well. Prices tend to be more competitive outside peak tourist seasons, so mid-week trips often cost less than bank holiday getaways.

What to do when you arrive in Belgrade

Belgrade Airport (Nikola Tesla) is 12 km from the city centre. Bus, taxi or private transfers get you there in 20–40 minutes. In the city, explore Kalemegdan Fortress, the bohemian Dorćol neighbourhood, the Danube in Zemun, and the nightlife at Beton Hala. Balkan food of excellent quality at very local prices.
