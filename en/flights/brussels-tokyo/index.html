Best time to travel

December is a good option if you're looking for low-to-mid season pricing. Japan at that time is cold but dry, perfect for visiting without massive crowds. Spring (March-April) and autumn (October-November) are pricier, so travelling in winter gets you better fares and fewer tourists.

What to do when you arrive

Tokyo offers historic temples, futuristic districts, world-class food and flawless transport. Main airports are Haneda (more central) and Narita (further out). Before booking, check which one you're landing at. The city has museums, markets, parks and vibrant nightlife. Bring cash: many places don't take cards.
