Best time to travel

Mexico City is worth visiting year-round, but certain periods are more pleasant: the dry season runs November to March, with mild weather and less rain. Avoid hurricane season (June to October) if you prefer stable weather. Christmas and New Year push prices up sharply. May-October is usually cheaper because fewer people travel, though the climate is more humid and rainy. Choose based on your heat and rain tolerance.

What to do in Mexico City

Mexico's capital is packed with culture: the Historic Centre, Metropolitan Cathedral, Templo Mayor and Palacio de Bellas Artes are must-sees. World-class museums include the National Museum of Anthropology, Frida Kahlo Museum and Tamayo Museum. Walk through Coyoacán and La Roma for local life, shops and food. Xochimilco's trajineras (boats) are a classic. Eat at markets like Central de Abastos or neighbourhood restaurants for authentic Mexican food at good prices. Benito Juárez Airport (MEX) is 5 km from the centre, well connected by metro and taxi.
