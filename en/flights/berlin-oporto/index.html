Best time to visit

Porto is pleasant almost year-round. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) offer ideal temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is cooler and wetter, but cheaper if you're after a bargain.

What to do when you arrive

Porto enchants with its historic Ribeira centre, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Cross the iconic Don Luís I bridge, taste port wine in the Vila Nova de Gaia lodges, admire traditional azulejo tilework, and savour Portuguese cuisine. The airport is 11 km from the centre with good public transport links.
