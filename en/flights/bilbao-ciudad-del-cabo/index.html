Best time to visit

Cape Town offers pleasant weather throughout the year. Southern summer (December to February) is warm and busy; spring (September to November) and autumn (March to May) provide comfortable temperatures and fewer crowds. Skip peak months if you prefer quieter landscapes and potentially lower fares.

What to do when you arrive

Cape Town is a must: Table Mountain, Camps Bay, Victoria & Alfred Waterfront. The Cape of Good Hope route, vineyards in Stellenbosch and Franschhoek, and Robben Island are within easy reach. The international airport is 20 km from the city centre with straightforward road or public transport access.
