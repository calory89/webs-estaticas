Fly to Sofia from Zaragoza from €133 return with Wizz Air Malta. Median price sits around €142. One stop on the outbound will get you to Bulgaria's capital in just over 6 hours.

Price and when to book

Flights start from €133 return based on sample prices (€133, €142, €154). Book early: Wizz Air Malta's low fares fill up quickly. Look for flexibility, shifting your dates by one or two days can save you €10–20.

Airline and stopover

You'll fly with Wizz Air Malta, a reliable low-cost carrier across Europe. Your outbound flight includes one stop, so it's not direct. The return may vary. Factor in Wizz Air's charges for oversized hand luggage and checked bags, they don't come free.

Best time to visit

Sofia shines year-round, but April, May, September and October offer the best weather: mild temperatures and no harsh winters. Travel in these months for stable prices and fewer crowds than summer.

What to do when you arrive

Sofia has the Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, the Banyá Mosque and Rila Monastery waiting for you. Stroll around Alexander Batenberg Square, eat at local restaurants in the Vitosha district and soak in the bohemian vibe. The airport is 10 km from the city centre; bus, taxi or ride-share gets you downtown in 30 minutes.

FAQ

Is the Zaragoza-Sofia flight direct?

No, the outbound flight includes one stop. Total travel time is around 6–7 hours depending on the layover length.

Which airline flies Zaragoza-Sofia?

Wizz Air Malta operates this route. It's a budget carrier, so check what your fare includes: small hand luggage is usually free, but they charge for checked bags.

Is €133 the final price or are there extra charges?

€133 is the base return fare. Wizz Air charges extra for checked luggage, seat selection and optional insurance. Always factor these in when you book.

What can I see and do in Sofia?

The Alexander Nevsky Cathedral is unmissable, as are the Banyá Mosque and Rila Monastery just outside the city. Spend a day exploring historic squares and another sampling local shops and Bulgarian food.
