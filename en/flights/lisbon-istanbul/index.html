Flying from Lisbon to Istanbul starts at €225 per person (return). The median price in our sample sits at €228, giving you a realistic picture of what you'll pay. With Pegasus and one stopover, this route is affordable and straightforward to manage.

Price and when to book: get your flight from €225

Prices on this route range between €225 and €232 in our sample. Whilst we can't predict which day the price will drop, booking in advance is the way to go. The further ahead you travel, the more low-fare options you'll find. Check on Tuesdays or Wednesday mornings: airlines often release deals mid-week.

Pegasus and stopovers: travel with one stop

Pegasus operates this route with one stopover. It's not a direct flight, but the low-cost carrier keeps your price tight. The stopover is quick to handle and part of your journey to Istanbul. If you want non-stop options, you might find them with other carriers, but expect a higher fare.

Best time to visit: spring and autumn

Istanbul is worth visiting in spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November): mild temperatures, fewer crowds than summer, and clear skies. July and August are hot and packed with tourists. Winter is quiet, but rainier. Any time works for this route, but those months will give you more comfort.

What to do when you arrive: airport and the city

You'll land at Istanbul Airport (IST), well connected to the centre by metro, bus and taxi. Once in the city, don't miss the Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia, the Grand Bazaar and a Bosphorus cruise. Istanbul blends Ottoman, Byzantine and modern history on every corner. Eat at small restaurants in Balat or Galata neighbourhoods for fair prices and local vibes.

FAQ

Per person, return. If you're travelling as two, multiply by two; if solo, that's your final price plus airport taxes.

Can I find a direct flight from Lisbon to Istanbul?

This Pegasus route includes one stopover. Other airlines may offer direct connections, but they're usually pricier. Compare on your usual flight search engine to see all options.

What travel dates did you see the €225 price?

Which airport do I arrive at in Istanbul?

Istanbul Airport (IST), the city's main hub. It's about 25 km from the centre. Good public and private transport links available.
