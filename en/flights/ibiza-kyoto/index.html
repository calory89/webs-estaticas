Best time to travel

Japan offers pleasant conditions in spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November), with mild weather ideal for sightseeing. Kyoto is spectacular in both seasons. What to do when you arrive in Kyoto

Kyoto is Japan's cultural capital. Visit thousands of temples and shrines, wander through the bamboo forest of Arashiyama, explore geishas in Gion, and discover zen gardens. Local transport by bus and train is straightforward. Stay central for easy access. Sample local cuisine at traditional markets and restaurants.
