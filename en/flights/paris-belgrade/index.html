Airline and direct flights

Best time to visit

Belgrade shines in spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October): pleasant weather, fewer crowds and moderate prices. June to August can be pricier and hotter. Winter is quiet but cold. For savings, travel between October and November, or March and April.

What to do when you arrive in Belgrade

Serbia's capital will surprise you. Walk around Kalemegdan, the medieval fortress with Danube views. Explore the old town (Stari Grad) with its cafés and galleries. Nightlife is legendary: floating bars on the river and clubs until dawn. Don't miss the Church of Saint Sava, the city's museums or Zemun, the bohemian neighbourhood across the river.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price Paris-Belgrade?

What can I see in Belgrade in a few days?

Kalemegdan and Stari Grad are essential. Spend one afternoon walking by the river, another on museums and local food, and evenings at the bars. Zemun is perfect for a short trip. Three to four days covers everything comfortably.
