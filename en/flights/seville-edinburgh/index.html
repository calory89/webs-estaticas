Flights from Seville to Edinburgh are available from €81 return with Ryanair. The median price sits around €157, leaving room to find good deals if you book ahead. Direct and hassle-free to reach Scotland's capital.

Price and when to book

From €81 is the lowest recorded price for this return route. The median stands at €157, with samples also at €168. Book 6-8 weeks in advance: it tends to cut costs noticeably. Avoid buying very close to departure (last week) unless you spot a flash deal. Tuesdays and Wednesdays usually offer tighter prices than Fridays and Sundays.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route direct from Seville (SVQ) to Edinburgh (EDI). No stopovers, meaning faster travel and fewer complications. Note: Ryanair charges for checked baggage and oversized cabin bags, so review what your fare includes before paying extras.

Best time to visit

Edinburgh shines between May and September with long days and milder weather. November to February is colder and darker, though less crowded with tourists. For price-hunting, low season (January, February, July) offers better value for money. Skip local holidays if you want to find affordable accommodation more easily.

What to do on arrival

Edinburgh airport sits 12 km from the city centre: train, bus or taxi reach town in 25-40 minutes. Don't miss Edinburgh Castle, the Royal Mile, Holyrood Palace and viewpoints from Calton Hill or Arthur's Seat. Museums have free entry. Spend at least 3 days to capture the capital's essence.
