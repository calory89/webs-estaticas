Best time to visit

Oslo draws visitors in winter and summer alike. Winter (December–February) offers northern lights and snow sports, though it's cold. Summer (June–August) brings long daylight and pleasant weather, but it's peak season. Spring and autumn are quieter with kinder prices, perfect if you land a deal like this one.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Oslo Gardermoen, 60 km from the city. Train or bus will get you downtown in 20–30 minutes. In the city: explore the Opera House district, Vigeland Park with its sculptures, and the museums in Bygdøy. In winter, venture out for the northern lights. Eat at street food markets and enjoy waterfront terraces by the fjord when weather permits.
