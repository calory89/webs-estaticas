Best time to visit

Kyoto is stunning in spring (March–April, cherry blossoms) and autumn (October–November, red foliage). These periods attract more tourists and higher prices. To save money, consider winter (December–February) or summer (July–August), though weather is more extreme. Avoid international holidays when fares spike sharply.

What to do in Kyoto

Kyoto is Japan's former imperial capital, renowned for temples, Shinto shrines and geisha tradition. Don't miss the Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, the thousands of red torii gates at Fushimi Inari Shrine, the Imperial Palace, and geisha walks in Gion district. Train connections from Kyoto reach nearby Osaka, Nara and Kansai region cities easily.

What is there to see in Kyoto besides temples?

Geishas in Gion, Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, boat trips on the Kamogawa River, art museums and traditional streets in Higashiyama. It's also an excellent base for day trips to Nara (deer park), Osaka (food scene) and Hiroshima (historical sites).
