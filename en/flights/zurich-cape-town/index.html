Price and when to book

The lowest price found is €639 return. The median is €644, with some fares up to €915 on specific dates. Book early: long-haul international flight prices tend to rise as departure approaches. Check options 3-4 weeks ahead for better rates.

Airline and stopovers

Ethiopian Airlines operates this route with 2 stopovers on the outbound flight. It's not a direct flight, but Ethiopian Airlines is a reliable carrier on Africa-Europe routes. Stopovers extend your journey, but that's the trade-off for these accessible fares. Check the stopover breakdown when booking to plan your time.

Best time to visit

Cape Town is a year-round destination, but April to September (Southern Hemisphere autumn and winter) offer dry weather and pleasant temperatures. These months typically see medium-to-high demand, so booking ahead is key. Avoid Christmas and New Year if you want lower prices.

What to do when you arrive

Cape Town airport (CPT) is 20 km from the city centre. From there, access to Table Mountain, Cape Peninsula, Clifton beaches, and Robben Island. The centre is walkable and the food scene is excellent. A week is the minimum to enjoy the city without rushing.
