Fly direct from Lisbon to Krakow with Ryanair from €91 return. The route sits at a median of €99, making it an accessible getaway to one of Eastern Europe's most captivating destinations.

Price and when to book

Prices range from €91 to €102 based on sample fares checked on 22 October 2026. The median sits at €99. For this route, the recommendation is to book well in advance: the earlier you purchase, the better fares you'll find. Ryanair typically offers its best prices 6-8 weeks ahead of departure, so don't leave it to the last minute.

Airline and direct flights

Ryanair operates this connection with no stopovers. The outbound flight departs Lisbon (LIS) at 13:45 arriving the same day in Krakow (KRK), whilst the return leaves Krakow at 10:00. Being direct saves you time and hassle; you simply board and disembark without aircraft changes or connection delays.

Best time to travel

December is a magical time in Krakow, with its Christmas markets and historic decorations. If you prefer warmer weather and fewer festive crowds, consider May-June or September-October. In these months the weather is stable, there's less peak tourism and prices remain reasonable. Avoid August when fares spike and visitor numbers surge.

What to do when you arrive

Krakow airport (KRK) is 15 km from the city centre. You have bus, taxi and shuttle services available. Once in the city, don't miss the Main Market Square (Rynek Główny), St Mary's Basilica and Wawel Castle. The World War II Museums and Auschwitz (70 km away) are profound experiences. Try Polish cuisine: pierniki (dumplings) and zurek (rye soup). The city deserves 3-4 days to explore at a proper pace.
