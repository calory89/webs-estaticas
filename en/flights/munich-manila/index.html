Best time to visit

The Philippines has a tropical climate. The dry season runs from November to April, with pleasant temperatures and less rainfall. June to September sees monsoon season with heavy rains. Travel between October and May for the best weather and to avoid rainy periods. High demand around Christmas and New Year may push prices up.

What to do on arrival

Manila offers museums, historic churches and the famous Intramuros walled district. Short distances away lie spectacular beaches on Boracay, Palawan and the El Nido islands. Eat local street food, visit Buddhist temples and explore city markets. Ninoy Aquino International Airport is about 15 km from the city centre.
