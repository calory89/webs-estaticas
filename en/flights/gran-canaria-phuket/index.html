Price: when to book your ticket

The lowest price we found is €667 return. The median price sits at €768, so if you spot fares in this range, book ahead. Prices can reach €883 during peak season or near-date bookings. Search in October for December travel: you'll have 6–8 weeks' notice, the sweet spot for securing good fares without leaving it too late.

Iberia Express: airline and stops

You'll travel with Iberia Express, Iberia's low-cost subsidiary. This route includes 2 stops on the outbound leg, so it's not a direct flight. The intermediate stops will extend your journey (expect 20–30 hours total travel time), but it's the trade-off for budget fares. Check connection times when booking: longer layovers mean less stress.

Best time to visit Phuket

December is a solid choice: mild temperatures and less rain than monsoon season. Avoid September and October if you want reliable weather. Bear in mind prices spike around Christmas and New Year, so if you're travelling then (like the 6–10 December example), book early. January to March is peak season with excellent weather.

What to do when you arrive in Phuket

Phuket airport connects directly to the island. From there, explore Patong, Kata and Karon beaches; visit the Big Buddha; head to nearby Krabi; snorkel around the Phi Phi Islands. Shuttle, taxi and car rental are available from the airport. Rest on your first night: jet lag after two stops is real.
