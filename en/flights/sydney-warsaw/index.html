Best time to travel

Warsaw shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) when the weather is mild and crowds are lighter than summer. Winter brings cold but charm, especially if you enjoy the city's festive atmosphere. These seasons don't guarantee lower prices, but the experience is more pleasant.

What to do when you arrive

Warsaw is Poland's capital with plenty to explore: walk the Old Town (Unesco World Heritage site), visit the Palace of Culture and Science, stroll along the Vistula River, and eat your way through traditional Polish bars. Chopin Airport is 10 km from the centre with good train and bus links. From Warsaw, day trips to Kraków or Gdańsk are easy.
