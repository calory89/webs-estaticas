Best time to visit

Edinburgh is worth visiting year-round, but timing shapes your experience. November and early December bring autumn colours and fewer crowds than summer. Avoid July–August unless you like packed museums and peak prices. Spring (March–May) is pleasant, but bring a jacket: it's chilly even in May.

What to do when you arrive

Edinburgh delivers. Climb Edinburgh Castle, walk the Royal Mile, explore Leith, and admire Georgian terraces. The airport connects to the city centre by bus or train (roughly 30–40 minutes). Leave time for free museums like the National Gallery of Scotland. Local food is hearty: try haggis if you're game.
