Fly from Lisbon to Cyprus from just €203 return with easyJet. The median price sits at €216, giving you solid budget options. With one stop, the journey is economical if you book well in advance.

Price and when to book

The cheapest flights hover around €203, with a median of €216 return. We've recorded prices reaching €254 in peak season. Book at least 6-8 weeks ahead; easyJet fares climb as departure approaches. Skip July and August for better deals, and opt for travel between October and April instead.

Airline and stopovers

easyJet operates this route with one stop. Some flights may pause at European hubs like Madrid or Barcelona before reaching Larnaca. Although not direct, the price makes up for the stopover. Pack light to keep costs down; small cabin bags and personal items fly free.

Best time to visit

Cyprus shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): fine weather, fewer crowds, and cheaper flights than summer. Winter is mild but wetter in the north. December–January and August draw peak tourism and inflated prices; avoid them if you want a bargain.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Larnaca Airport. Enjoy Paphos (crystal waters), Nicosia (medieval history), Ayia Napa (nightlife and coves), or the Troodos mountains. Hire a car or use local buses. Eat meze (local tapas), try halloumi, and relax by the Mediterranean.
