Best time to visit

Riga shines in summer (June to September) with long days and pleasant weather, ideal for exploring the city. If you prefer fewer crowds and lower prices, autumn (September to October) is a solid choice. Winter is cold but magical if you enjoy snowy settings.

What to do when you arrive

Riga International Airport is 13 km from the city centre. Riga's Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage site: wander its medieval streets, see the Freedom Monument and enjoy Latvian cuisine in local restaurants. The Museum of Occupation and Art Museums are also worth visiting.
