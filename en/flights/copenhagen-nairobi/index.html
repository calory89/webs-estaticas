Best time to travel

Nairobi has a highland tropical climate: mild temperatures year-round. The long dry season (August to October) and short dry season (January to February) are ideal for safaris and minimal rain. November, as in your example, marks the start of the wet season, but landscapes are fresh and green. Avoid April and May if you prefer drier weather.

What to do in Nairobi

Kenya's capital is your gateway to major parks: Masai Mara reserve is just hours away. In the city, the National Museum and Giraffe Centre are must-sees. Markets like Nairobi City Market put you in the thick of local life. Keep in mind Jomo Kenyatta Airport is about 15 km from the centre, so plan transfers ahead.
