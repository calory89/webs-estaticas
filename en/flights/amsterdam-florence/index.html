Best time to visit

Florence is beautiful year-round, but spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) are ideal: pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. If you travel in winter (like the example on 4 January), you'll enjoy low season with fewer tourists, though it's cooler. Avoid July and August if you don't want large crowds.

What to do in Florence

The Duomo Cathedral is essential. Walk through the historic centre, cross the Ponte Vecchio at sunset, visit the Uffizi if you love Renaissance art. Head to Piazzale Michelangelo for the best city views. Grab authentic gelato at any corner and try bistecca alla fiorentina. Your arrival airport is Florence-Peretola (FLR), very close to the centre.
