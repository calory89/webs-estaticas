Airline and stops

You'll travel with Ryanair. This route includes 3 stops on the outbound, which lengthens the journey but cuts costs. Prepare for stopovers; keep connection documents handy and check baggage if checked through. Ryanair charges extra for large suitcases, so pack light or budget for that.

Best time to visit

November is autumn in Argentina, mild and pleasant in Buenos Aires (no heavy rain). It's shoulder season, not peak tourism, which favours pricing. December to January are pricier due to summer. Spring (September-October) is also solid if you need flexibility.

What to do when you arrive

Buenos Aires awaits with San Telmo, La Boca and tango; Recoleta and its cafés; Teatro Colón. Eat medialunas and asado, catch free museum hours on Thursdays, explore La Cantera. Ministro Pistarini airport (Ezeiza) is 35 km out; take bus 8 straight to centre or book a remis upfront.
