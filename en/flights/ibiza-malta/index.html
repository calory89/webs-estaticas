Best time to travel

Malta is a year-round destination thanks to its Mediterranean climate. Shoulder months (April-May and September-October) typically offer good weather with less tourism than summer. If you're after low prices, avoid July and August when tourist demand peaks. Winter is quiet but with occasional rain.

What to do in Malta

You'll arrive at Malta International Airport (MLA), 5 km from Valletta. The capital is a UNESCO World Heritage site with spectacular medieval architecture. Visit the Mnajdra Temples, Dingli Cliffs, Mellieha Bay beaches, and explore Comino. The historic town of Mdina and coastal villages deserve full days. A week gives you plenty of time for the essentials.
