Fly Manchester to Bogotá with Air Canada from €888 return. Typical fares sit around €1,100, so booking ahead saves money. Colombia awaits: the capital opens doors to jungle, coffee and mountains.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we found is €888. Median price is €1,100, and some fares reach €1,196. Book early for better rates. There's no fixed rule about which month prices drop, but planning a few weeks ahead protects you from last-minute spikes. All prices shown are return.

Airline and stopovers

Air Canada operates this route with 2 stopovers on the outbound. Sample flight departs Manchester 12 November at 11:30 and returns 20 November at 09:00 from Bogotá. You'll change planes twice, extending travel time, but that's the trade-off for these low fares.

Best time to visit

Bogotá has mild weather year-round (12–20°C). December to March and July to August see less rain. The city sits at 2,640 metres, so pack a jacket even on sunny days. Book in advance for better pricing, regardless of season.

What to do in Bogotá and beyond

The Gold Museum is unmissable. La Candelaria is the historic quarter. Monserrate offers views from 3,152 metres. With extra days, explore the Coffee Triangle or San Andrés. El Dorado airport is well connected to the city centre (30 km).

What to see in Bogotá?

The Gold Museum (pre-Hispanic treasures), La Candelaria (historic centre), Monserrate (sanctuary at 3,152 m with panoramic views), parks like Simón Bolívar and local food. Bogotá is the jumping-off point for the Coffee Region and beyond.
