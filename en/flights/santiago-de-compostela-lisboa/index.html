Best time to visit

Lisbon is pleasant most of the year. Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) offer excellent weather and fewer crowds than summer. Travel in shoulder season to combine lower fares with a calmer experience.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Humberto Delgado, Lisbon's main airport. The city has Belém, Sintra, the Tagus river, and neighbourhoods filled with azulejo tiles and pastéis de nata. From the airport, buses, metro, and taxis reach the centre in under 30 minutes.
