Best time to visit

Tokyo is worth visiting year-round, but spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) are ideal: mild weather and less rain. Winter is dry but cold; summer is hot and humid. Prices tend to spike in these peak seasons, so if you're budget-conscious consider travelling outside these months.

What to do when you arrive

Tokyo needs no introduction: ancient temples like Senso-ji, modern districts like Shibuya and Shinjuku, world-class food, and unique experiences around every corner. Narita or Haneda airport connect you well to the city centre by train. Stay central and walk: it's the best way to discover the city.

FAQ

Is €879 really the final price?

Yes, we've seen return fares at that price. Bear in mind baggage allowance may or may not be included: check Turkish Airlines' terms. The median of €949 is a more realistic expectation if you want date flexibility.

Is Turkish Airlines direct from Bilbao to Tokyo?

No, there's one stopover. It's standard for this route. The stopover adds time but is a necessary connection point: Turkish usually offers smooth connections.

When should I book to get the best price?

The sooner the better. Intercontinental fares climb as your departure date approaches. Book 2–3 months in advance if you can, especially if you're travelling in peak season.

What else is there to see in Tokyo besides the main spots?

Beyond Senso-ji and Shibuya: explore tiny ramen shops in Shinjuku, the museums of Ueno, quieter temples in Asakusa outside peak hours, and spend time in local cafés. Japan rewards those who wander off the beaten track.
