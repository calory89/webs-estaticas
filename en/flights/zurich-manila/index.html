Price and when to book

Flights ZRH-MNL start from €672 return. The sample of prices checked on 23 October 2026 shows options at €672, €695 and €818. Book as early as possible: long-haul fares to the Philippines rise quickly. Look for deals 2-3 months ahead to secure the best price.

Airline and stops

You'll fly with Etihad Airways on a route with 1 stop. It's not direct, but it's the cheapest option from Zurich to Manila. The connection is manageable and the price makes up for the extra travel time. Etihad is a quality carrier with a solid reputation on long-haul routes.

Best time to travel

The Philippines has a tropical climate. The dry season runs from November to April, ideal for visiting without heavy rain. If you travel outside peak season (May-October), you'll find lower prices, though there's more chance of rain. December and January are peak tourist times with higher fares.

What to do when you arrive

Manila is your gateway to the Philippines. From Ninoy Aquino Airport (MNL) you can explore the capital, visit Intramuros (the old walled city), or head to nearby islands like Boracay, Palawan or Siargao for stunning beaches. Local transport is cheap and easy to use.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for Zurich-Manila flights?

The lowest price recorded is €672 return with Etihad Airways. Prices vary depending on travel date and booking in advance; the median sits around €695.

Does Etihad Airways fly direct from Zurich to Manila?

No. This route includes 1 stop with Etihad Airways. There is no non-stop option on this route at present, but the connection is well-organised and the price is very competitive.

When is the best time to fly cheap to Manila?

The dry season (November-April) is ideal for travel. May to October typically has lower prices due to the rainy season, but the risk of typhoons is higher. Book in advance in both cases.

What is there to see in Manila and the surrounding area?

Manila has Intramuros, museums and nightlife. From MNL airport you can easily connect to islands like Boracay (white sand beaches), Palawan (caves and lagoons) or Siargao (surfing). Domestic transport is very affordable.
