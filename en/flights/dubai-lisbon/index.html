Best time to visit

Lisbon is beautiful year-round, but the best months are April-May and September-October: perfect weather, fewer crowds and lower prices than summer. Avoid July and August if you're looking to save. Winter (December to February) can also offer good rates, though it rains more. February is usually a solid choice: low prices and better weather than January.

What to do when you arrive

Land at Humberto Delgado Airport (LIS), 7 km from the centre. Don't miss the steep streets of Alfama, Mosteiro dos Jerónimos and Torre de Belém. Enjoy the best pastéis de Nata at the original pastry shop, try bacalao à Brás and take a boat ride on the Tejo. Tram 28 is iconic. Sintra is 40 minutes by train: Palácio da Pena and Quinta da Regaleira are worth the trip.
