Best time to visit

Miami is a year-round destination. October to April brings dry weather and pleasant temperatures (22–28°C). June to September is hurricane season with rain, though fewer tourists and lower prices. Winter (December–February) attracts more visitors, so expect higher fares and crowded airports.

What to do on arrival

Miami has iconic beaches like South Beach and street art in Wynwood Walls. Little Havana preserves Cuban culture. From Miami International Airport (MIA), there's bus, taxi and car hire. Remember you're arriving with a stopover, so add extra time to your itinerary.
