Best time to visit

Bucharest shines almost year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant temperatures and fewer crowds. Winters are cold but the city glows with festive decorations. Skip July and August if you prefer fewer people in museums and squares.

What to do when you arrive

The Palace of Parliament is essential: Europe's largest building after the Pentagon. Walk through Old Town Bucharest with its colonial churches and bohemian cafés. Visit the National Art Museum. Climb to the TV Tower viewpoint for 360° city views. Try mici (local grilled meat rolls) at any authentic restaurant. Everything is close by, the city is very walkable or use the metro.

What's the best date to book?

What to see in Bucharest besides the Parliament Palace?

Old Town is a charming walled neighbourhood with colonial churches, art shops and restaurants. The National Art Museum has an excellent collection. Revolution Square is historically significant. The TV Tower offers panoramic views. Lipscani is perfect for antiques and local crafts. Two or three days gives you time to see everything without rushing.
