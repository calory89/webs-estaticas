You'll find the lowest fares from Munich to Phuket at €541 return. Sample prices range between €541 and €670, indicating a favourable window. Book well in advance (at least 2–3 months) to secure the best rate. Avoid peak travel dates (Christmas, New Year, Easter).

Airline and stopovers

Air China operates this route with 1 stopover at its hub (typically Beijing). Although it means longer journey time, the price combination is competitive. Check where the connection takes place and ensure adequate time between flights.

Best time to visit

Thailand's dry season runs November to February: pleasant temperatures and less rain. April to May is very hot (39–40 °C). June to October is monsoon season, with heavy rainfall but fewer tourists and cheaper accommodation. Travel December to January for the best weather.

What to do in Phuket

White-sand beaches (Patong, Kamala, Karon), snorkelling on nearby islands (Phi Phi, Similan), Buddhist temples, nightlife and local markets. Phuket International Airport (HKT) is 30 km from town; take a taxi, minibus or hire a car. Eat well: pad thai, Massaman curry and fresh seafood are essential.
