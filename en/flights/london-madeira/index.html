Best time to visit

Madeira enjoys mild weather year-round. Driest months are May through October. For warmth and clear skies, July and August peak, but prices rise accordingly. Spring (March-May) and autumn (September-November) offer good weather, fewer crowds and better rates.

What to do in Madeira

Madeira is a hiking and nature paradise with black-sand beaches and dramatic cliffs. Visit Funchal, explore historic levadas (irrigation channels), trek to Pico Arieiro or soak in natural pools. Try espetada (grilled fish) and Madeira wine. Madeira Airport (FNC) sits 15 km from Funchal city centre.
