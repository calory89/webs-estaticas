Best time to visit

Cape Town works as a destination almost year-round, but the austral summer (November to February) delivers good weather and long daylight hours. June to August is cooler. Check prices regularly as they shift with demand—you'll often find better deals in low season or with plenty of advance notice.

What to do on arrival

Cape Town is a gateway to South Africa with plenty to explore: Table Mountain (cable car or hiking), Cape Point, beaches like Camps Bay, and the colourful Bo-Kaap neighbourhood. The Waterfront is a hub for shopping and dining. With extra time, the Winelands are just over an hour away. The international airport (CPT) is about 20 km from the city centre, connected by bus, taxi or hire car.
