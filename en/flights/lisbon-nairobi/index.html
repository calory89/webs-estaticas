Best time to visit Nairobi

Kenya is a year-round destination. Dry seasons (June to October and January to February) offer the best weather and are ideal for safaris. November marks the start of short rains, but travel is still feasible. Go when your budget allows and confirm any weather impacts with your airline.

What to do when you arrive in Nairobi

Nairobi is Kenya's capital and gateway to the country's great safaris (Masai Mara, Amboseli). In the city, visit the National Museum of Kenya, Karen Blixen Museum or the Westlands neighbourhood. Safaris and national park trips depart from Nairobi. Book tours in advance to make the most of your stay.
