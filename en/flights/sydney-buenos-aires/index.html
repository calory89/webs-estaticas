Best time to travel

Buenos Aires shines in spring (September–November) and autumn (March–May) in Australian terms, when weather is mild. Skip Buenos Aires winter (June–August) if you want pleasant temperatures. High season demand can push prices up, so travel in shoulder months if you can.

What to do when you arrive

Buenos Aires awaits with tango, belle époque architecture in La Boca and San Telmo, and world-class dining. Stroll through Recoleta, savour an asado, visit the Casa Rosada, and get lost in Palermo's bookshops. The city breathes culture at every turn, so give it at least a couple of days.

No. This route requires 2 stopovers with LATAM Airlines, so travelling without stops isn't possible. It's standard for routes between Australia and South America: airlines need those stops for distance and operations.

What should I see in Buenos Aires over a weekend?

Historic centre (Casa Rosada, cathedral), La Boca (Caminito), San Telmo (tango and antiques), Recoleta (museums and cemetery). Grab a proper asado, try medialunas with café, and soak up the porteño vibe.
