Best time to visit

Tallinn shines in summer (June to August) with white nights and pleasant weather. Winter (December to February) is cold but magical if you fancy snow. Spring and autumn are comfortable and less crowded. Pick based on your climate and crowd preferences.

What to do when you arrive

Tallinn airport is about 4 km from the city centre. The Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage Site: stroll the cobbled streets, climb Hermann's Tower for views, visit Kumu Museum and try local food. If you have time, explore nearby islands or the Baltic coast.

Is the Madrid to Tallinn flight direct?

No, the flight includes 1 stopover. Ryanair connects both cities with an intermediate hub, so total travel time is longer than a direct flight would be.

What can I see in Tallinn over a weekend?

The Old Town is a must: medieval walls, historic centre, Toompea Tower. Walk through Kalamaja or Rotermann for modern Tallinn. With more time, Saaremaa or Hiiumaa islands are popular day trips from the harbour.
