Best time to travel from Valencia to Luxembourg

Luxembourg is attractive in spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October), with mild weather. December can be cold but the city has charm during the festive season. Summer sees more tourism and higher prices. Consider travelling in low season or midweek for generally better fares.

What to do when you arrive in Luxembourg

Luxembourg Airport (LUX) is 6 km from the capital city. The historic Old Town is worth exploring: scenic views over the valley, Grand Ducal Palace, Fortress of the Old Town. The country is small but well-connected by public transport. Perfect for 3-4 days. It's also a good base for visiting Belgium or France if you have more time.
