Flights Rome to Bogotá: from €935 return

Flying Rome to Bogotá costs from €935 return. The median price sits around €947, giving you room to find competitive fares. With one stop, round trips range from €935 to €1,122.

Price and when to book

The best-value range is €935–€947 for return tickets. Fly 2–3 months ahead to secure lower fares; booking too close to departure pushes prices up. Flexible dates are your secret weapon: shifting travel by a couple of days can save €100–€200.

Airline and stops

KLM Royal Dutch Airlines operates this route with one stop. Departures leave Rome around 13:30 and arrive in Bogotá after 16:00 local time (next day, accounting for time difference). The single connection extends journey time but the pricing makes it worthwhile.

Best time to visit

December is a common travel month on this route. Note Bogotá's climate: it sits in a tropical highland zone with mild temperatures year-round (12–26°C). Check local events and festival calendars to avoid peak tourism periods.

What to do in Bogotá

Bogotá has world-class museums (Gold Museum, Botanic Museum), La Candelaria with colonial architecture, and diverse local and international dining. El Dorado airport lies 12 km from the city centre; use taxis, ride apps or pre-arranged transfers. Allow 24–48 hours to acclimate to the 2,640 m altitude.

FAQ

What's the cheapest Rome to Bogotá flight?

From €935 return with KLM. This is the lowest recorded; most fares hover around €947. Higher prices (€1,122) appear when availability tightens.

Is it a direct flight or with stops?

It includes one stop. KLM handles the connection; you typically depart Rome around 13:30 and arrive Bogotá after 16:00 (next day).

Which airline flies Rome to Bogotá?

KLM Royal Dutch Airlines. Check their website or flight search engines for exact schedules and baggage policies based on your fare type.

What to see in Bogotá in 1–2 days?

Gold Museum, La Candelaria (historic centre with churches and plazas), Monserrate (hilltop shrine), and restaurants in Usaquén or Rosario. Rest well on day one due to altitude (2,640 m).
