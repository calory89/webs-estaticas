Best time to visit

Phuket shines November–February: dry weather, mild temperatures, calm seas. December is peak season (hotel prices spike), but the weather is flawless. July–October is monsoon season; skip it if you want reliable beach conditions.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Phuket International Airport (HKT), 30 km from town. Famous beaches: Kata, Karon, Patong. Day trips to nearby islands, Phi Phi, James Bond Island. Explore Buddhist temples, eat at street markets, try Muay Thai. The airport runs direct transport: fixed-rate taxi, bus, or private transfer to your hotel.
