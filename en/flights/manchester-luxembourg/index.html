Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route. Outbound includes 1 stopper, so it's not direct; return also has a connection. Expect 3 to 5 hours total travel time depending on your connecting airport. Mind the baggage: Ryanair charges for hold luggage.

Best time to visit

Luxembourg is lovely year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer good weather without crowds. Winters are cold but perfect for festive Christmas vibes in the capital. Summer is peak tourist season with higher prices.

What to do when you arrive

The airport is 6 km from the city centre. Luxembourg is compact: walk the Ville Vieille (old town with stunning views), visit the Cathedral, explore Petrusse Casemates. Hiring a car to explore villages in the Luxembourg Ardennes is highly recommended. A 3–4 day weekend gives you plenty of time.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Manchester to Luxembourg?

No. Ryanair connects this route with 1 stopper on the outbound and return. Total travel time is 3 to 5 hours.

When is it cheapest to fly to Luxembourg?

Prices drop when you book 2 to 3 weeks in advance. Dates outside summer and bank holidays tend to be cheaper.

Can I take checked baggage with Ryanair?

Yes, but you'll pay extra. Check Ryanair's website before buying; it typically costs €10–€20 per bag depending on size.

What can I visit in Luxembourg over a weekend?

The capital is the main draw: old town, cathedral and views from the Casemates. If you have time, villages in the Luxembourg Ardennes (Vianden, Echternach) are worth a day trip. Everything is very close.
