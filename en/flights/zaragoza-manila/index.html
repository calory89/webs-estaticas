Price and when to book

The lowest price we found is €694 per person return, with a median around €722. Remember this route has stopovers, which affects the final price. There's no clear pattern for price drops on such long-haul routes, so the classic advice applies: book early, the sooner the better, especially in high season.

Airline and stopovers

Wizz Air Malta operates this route. The outbound flight includes 2 stopovers, so it's not direct. This is standard for Europe-Philippines connections; most routes have intermediate stops. Always check the total duration and layover times when you book, as they vary by date.

Best time to visit

Manila has tropical weather year-round, but the dry season runs from November to April: the best months with less rain and pleasant temperatures. July and August attract more European travellers. If you want fewer crowds and potentially better prices, try March-April or September-October, though there's no guarantee of discounts.

What to do when you arrive

Manila is your gateway to the Philippines. In the city itself, explore Intramuros, the walled old town, visit Rizal Park and museums. But Manila is a base: cheap ferries and domestic flights leave from here to islands like Palawan, Boracay and Siargao, which is what most travellers want. The airport has good links to the city; use Grab (ride-hailing app) to get around.

FAQ

Is the Zaragoza-Manila flight direct?

No, it has 2 stopovers. That's standard for such a long route; there are no direct flights from Zaragoza to Manila at these fares.

Which airline operates this route?

Wizz Air Malta, a sister brand of Wizz Air, the European low-cost carrier. Check what's included in your fare (baggage, seat) before booking.

When is the best month to visit Manila?

November to April, when it rains less and the weather is pleasant. Travel then for a better experience, though cheap flight availability depends on seat availability, not the month itself.

Is €694 a real price or just marketing?

It's the lowest price we've found in our sample, return per person on this route. The median is €722, so there are plenty of options in that range. However, it varies by exact dates and availability; always check the search tool for confirmation.
