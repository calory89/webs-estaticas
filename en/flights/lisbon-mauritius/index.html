Airline and stops

Best time to visit

Mauritius has tropical weather year-round. Avoid December to February if you prefer less rain. May to November is the dry season and cooler—perfect for beaches and nature without cyclone risk. Travel within that window and you'll combine better prices with better weather.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Mauritius International Airport (MRU). The island boasts white-sand beaches, coral reefs for diving, waterfalls in Black River Gorges, and the charming port of Port Louis. Piton de la Petite Rivière Noire and the wild south coast are must-sees. Hire a car for freedom to explore.
