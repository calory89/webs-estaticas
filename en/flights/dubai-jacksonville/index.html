Best time to travel

Jacksonville has pleasant weather year-round. Winter (December to February) is mild and dry, perfect for tourists escaping European cold. Summer (June to August) is very hot and humid, with storm risk. Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) are pleasant. Avoid peak holiday weeks if you want lower prices.

What to do when you arrive in Jacksonville

Jacksonville offers wide beaches like Jacksonville Beach, ideal for relaxation. Downtown riverfront has shops and restaurants along the St. Johns River. For museums, visit MOSH or the Cummer Museum of Art & Gardens. Nature lovers can explore nearby state parks. Less than an hour away sits St. Augustine, America's oldest Spanish colonial town with historic forts and charming streets.
