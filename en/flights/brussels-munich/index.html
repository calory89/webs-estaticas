The starting price is €220 return. In our sample searches, prices range between €220 and €221, so margins are very tight. The earlier you book, the better: prices tend to rise as the flight date approaches. If you have flexibility, check options one or two weeks in advance.

Best time to visit

Munich is worth visiting year-round. If you prefer to avoid business travel rush and crowds, go for June, September or October: pleasant weather and fewer travellers. Winter months (November to February) can have snow in the nearby Alps, ideal if you like skiing or snowy landscapes. December peaks with Christmas markets.

What to do when you arrive

Munich offers everything from world-class museums (Pinakothek) to traditional breweries like Hofbräuhaus. Walk around Marienplatz and Nymphenburg Palace, or escape to the Bavarian Alps 90 minutes away. The airport is well connected by train to the city (45 minutes).
