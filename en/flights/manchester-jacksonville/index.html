Best time to visit

Florida is most pleasant from October to April—mild weather, less rain. Skip July and August due to extreme heat and hurricane season. Autumn and spring offer the best mix of climate and outdoor activities.

What to do in Jacksonville

Jacksonville is the largest U.S. city by land area. Explore the St. Johns River, wander the historic downtown, enjoy nearby beaches like Jacksonville Beach, or visit the port. Most attractions are easily accessible from the airport.
