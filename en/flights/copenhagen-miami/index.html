Prices and when to book your ticket

The cheapest fares start at €570, though most hover around €596. We've seen prices up to €641. The earlier you book, the better: prices rise as the date approaches. Check regularly over the coming weeks and grab the tightest rates. Flexibility helps—shifting your dates by a day or two can save you tens of euros.

Airline and stopovers

TAP Portugal is your carrier on this route. All flights include 1 stopover, typically in Lisbon. It's not direct, but TAP offers solid value and handles connections smoothly. Remember the total journey takes longer, but the price saving makes up for it.

Best time to visit

Miami boasts great weather year-round, but the Northern Hemisphere winter (November to March) brings milder temperatures. Avoid hurricane season (June to November) if you prefer peace of mind. Low-season months like May, September and October sometimes offer cheaper fares, though rainfall is higher. December and January are peak tourist season with higher prices.

What to do when you arrive in Miami

Miami delivers white-sand beaches, street art in Wynwood, Cuban cuisine in Little Havana, and shopping in Design District. Miami airport (MIA) sits 8 km from the centre; grab a taxi, rideshare or bus. Don't miss South Beach, Vizcaya Museum or the nearby Keys by rental car.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Copenhagen to Miami?

No. All the flights we've found include 1 stopover, normally in Lisbon with TAP Portugal. It's the most affordable option on this route.

What's the minimum price for a return ticket?

From €570 per person return. The median price is €596, so expect to pay around that figure if you don't land the lowest fare.

Which airline operates this route?

TAP Portugal flies these routes. You can book direct or through online travel agencies.

What's the best time to visit Miami?

November to April is the dry season and most pleasant. May, September and October often have lower fares, though it rains more. Avoid June to November due to hurricane season.
