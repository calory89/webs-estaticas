Malta shines year-round, but October to April offer gentle weather: comfortable temperatures, fewer crowds than peak summer. What to do when you arrive

Malta is small but packed: Valletta (the capital, UNESCO-listed), Mdina's cathedrals, beaches at Mellieha and Popeye Village. The airport sits 5 km from town; bus, taxi or car hire are your moves. Three to five days covers the essentials at a comfortable pace.
