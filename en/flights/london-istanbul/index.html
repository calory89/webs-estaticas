Best time to visit

Istanbul is stunning year-round, but spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer perfect temperatures, fewer crowds at major sites and better price-availability balance. Summer months (June-August) are pricier and busier. Winter is cold but less touristy.

What to do when you arrive

Istanbul awaits with the Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia and Topkapi Palace in the historic peninsula. The Grand Bazaar is perfect for souvenirs and local food. Cross the Bosphorus on foot via Galata Bridge and explore Galata and Beyoğlu neighbourhoods. Try kebab, Turkish tea and relax in a traditional hammam.
