Airline and stops

You'll fly with Iberia Express throughout. Expect 2 stops on the outbound leg, so it's not direct but standard for this route from Ibiza. The journey is long (over 16 hours total with stops), but the fares make up for it.

Best time to visit Bangkok

December to February is peak season: dry weather, pleasant temperatures (25–30°C) and clear skies. Avoid May to October if you can't handle extreme heat and humidity. Travelling in shoulder months (April, November) gives you more fare options and fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive in Bangkok

You'll land at Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK). Metro or taxi to the city takes 30–60 minutes. Don't miss the Grand Palace, temples (Wat Pho, Wat Arun), Chao Phraya river cruises, floating markets and street food. Bangkok is intense: plan your days well.
