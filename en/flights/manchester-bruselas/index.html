Airline and flight type

Best time to visit

Brussels is pleasant almost year-round. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is colder but perfect if you want a festive atmosphere and fewer tourists. Avoid July-August if you dislike peak season crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Brussels is small and easy to explore. Grand Place (UNESCO World Heritage), Manneken Pis, St Michael's Cathedral, local markets. Public transport works well from the airport. Eat Belgian chocolate, try craft beers and the best fries in Europe.
