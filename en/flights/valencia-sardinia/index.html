Best time to travel

Sardinia works year-round. Summer (June–August) brings peak tourism and higher prices, but you get guaranteed sunshine and beaches at their best. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) strike a balance: pleasant weather, fewer crowds, and generally lower fares than peak season.

What to do when you arrive

Cagliari is your gateway to Sardinia. Elmas airport is 7 km from the city centre. Explore the old town, museums, and Poetto beach. From there, reach coastal areas (Chia, Villasimius), San Pietro island, and inland mountains. Hire a car to make the most of the island.

What's worth seeing in Cagliari and around it?

Cagliari has a historic old town, archaeological museums, and urban beaches. Nearby: Costa Rei beaches, Villasimius coast, San Pietro island, and charming coastal towns like Pula. Renting a car gives you freedom to explore the island.
