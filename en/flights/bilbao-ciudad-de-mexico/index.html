Best time to travel

December and January are peak season in Mexico, so expect higher prices. May to September are usually cheaper, though it's the rainy season. April and October strike a balance: decent weather and moderate fares. Plan ahead for the best value-for-money trip.

What to do in Mexico City

Benito Juárez airport (MEX) is the main hub. From there, reach the centre in 30-40 minutes by metro or taxi. Visit the Historic Centre, Teotihuacán, the Anthropology Museum and Xochimilco. The food scene is world-class and neighbourhoods like Roma and Condesa buzz with restaurants and nightlife. October to April is peak season: mild weather and low rainfall.
