Best time to visit

Venice is beautiful year-round, but spring (April and May) and autumn (September and October) offer pleasant weather without peak summer crowds. December to February is low season: rain and cold, but fewer tourists and lower prices. Avoid July and August if you dislike queues and heat.

What to do when you arrive in Venice

You'll land at Marco Polo Airport. From there, take a bus, water taxi or water launch to central Venice (20–30 minutes). Once in the city, visit St Mark's Square, climb the Campanile, take a gondola ride if your budget allows, and wander the Rialto alleys. Eat well: risotto, squid ink pasta, and espresso at any café corner.

FAQ

Which months are cheapest to fly to Venice from Santiago?

What can you see and do in Venice?

St Mark's Square, the Basilica, the Campanile, historic bridges, gondola rides, churches like the Frari, museums, and dining along the canals. It's a city for walking and savouring, not rushing.
