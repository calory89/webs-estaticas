Best time to visit

Jacksonville has a subtropical climate. November to April are ideal months: mild temperatures (15–23°C) and less rainfall. July and August are very hot and humid. Winter travel (your likely window) avoids extreme heat and hurricane season. Book well ahead if you're travelling over Christmas or school holidays.

What to do when you arrive

Jacksonville is your gateway to quiet beaches (Jacksonville Beach, Neptune Beach) and the historic San Marco district. Visit MOSH museum, Riverside gardens, or take a day trip to St Augustine (40 km away). The airport is about 13 km from downtown; you can hire a car or use public transport to get around.
