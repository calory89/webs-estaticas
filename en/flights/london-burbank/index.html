Price and when to book your London-Burbank flight

Base price starts at €537, but we've seen fares up to €801. The median lands at €561, giving you a realistic benchmark. Book early: prices climb as departure approaches. October and early November are key windows to catch discounted tickets.

Alaska Airlines: your option on this route

Alaska Airlines operates these flights. Your outbound includes 1 stopover, so it's not a direct flight, but it's the best value option from London to Burbank. Always check your confirmation to see where that stop is before travelling.

Best time to travel: autumn and winter

November and December are your ideal months: less summer tourism, more budget flight availability, and pleasant Los Angeles weather (18–24°C). Avoid high season (June–August) when prices spike. Skip holidays that drive demand up.

What to do on arrival: your gateway to Los Angeles

Burbank airport sits just 15 km from central Los Angeles. From here you're minutes from Hollywood, the Walk of Fame, Griffith Observatory, and Santa Monica beaches. Rent a car for flexibility or use ride-sharing. Burbank itself is worth exploring: historic film studios, vintage shops, and excellent food.
