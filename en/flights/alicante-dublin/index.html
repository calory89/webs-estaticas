Best time to visit

Dublin is lovely in spring (April–May) and summer (June–August) with longer days and mild temperatures. Autumn and winter can be rainy but attract fewer crowds, and shoulder months like October offer a good mix of pleasant weather and reasonable fares.

What to do when you arrive

Dublin Airport is about 10 km from the city centre. Explore Temple Bar, visit the Guinness Storehouse, walk along the Liffey, and sample traditional pubs. If you have extra time, Dublin Bay and Howth Lighthouse are easy trips by public transport.
