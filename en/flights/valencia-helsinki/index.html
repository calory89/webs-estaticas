Best time to visit

Helsinki shines in winter (December–February) for Northern Lights and Christmas charm, though it's cold (-5 to -15°C). Summer (June–August) offers long days, nearly 24-hour daylight, and mild temperatures (15–20°C). Spring and autumn are ideal if you prefer fewer crowds and tighter budgets.

What to do when you arrive

The airport is 20km from the city centre; reach it by train in 30 minutes. Visit the white Cathedral at Senate Square, Suomenlinna fortress (UNESCO site) by ferry, Kiasma contemporary art museum, and traditional Finnish public baths. Don't miss the Christmas market if you travel in December, and try Nordic cuisine.
