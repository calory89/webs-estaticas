Price and when to book

The base price is €115 return, but most tickets hover around €131. You'll spot fares up to €157 too. Book ahead and dodge peak dates (holidays, bank holidays). The more flexible you are with dates, the better you'll fare. Morning flights tend to be cheaper than afternoon or evening ones.

Airline and layovers

easyJet is your carrier. The best bit: direct Paris-Athens flight, zero layovers. That means no airport hassle, no hours killing time between connections and more kip on the plane. Smooth sailing from start to finish.

Best time to visit

October, April and May offer the sweet spot: great weather, pleasant seas and fewer crowds. July and August are baking hot and pricey. Skip those peak summer months if you want value. Winter (December-February) is cheap but chilly, though Athens is fine even then.

What to do when you arrive

The Acropolis, Parthenon, Ancient Agora—the classics do what they say on the tin. Plaka is perfect for wandering and eating. Museums (National Archaeological, Acropolis) are must-sees. Nearby beaches like Glyfada or Vlihotis for a swim. Public transport is cheap (metro and buses). Three or four days gives you a decent hit of the essentials.

FAQ

At least 3–4 days for the big hitters (Acropolis, Agora, museums, Plaka). Throw in a beach day and more breathing room? Add another couple. A four-day long weekend hits the spot nicely.
