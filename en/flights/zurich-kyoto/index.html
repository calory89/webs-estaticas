Best time to visit

December is peak season in Kyoto: mild temperatures (5-10°C), festive illuminations in temples and late autumn colours. Prices are higher. For savings, spring (March-May) and autumn (September-November) offer excellent weather and lower fares. Your arrival airport is Osaka (OSA/Kansai), 75 km from Kyoto; easy train connection in 90 minutes.

What to do when you arrive in Kyoto

Kyoto is Japan's cultural capital. Visit 2,000+ temples, the Golden Pavilion (Kinkaku-ji), Arashiyama bamboo forest and the red-orange gates of Fushimi Inari shrine. Stroll past geishas in Gion at dusk. Local transport (bus and train) is cheap and reliable. Allow at least three days in the city; many travellers combine it with Osaka, Nara or Tokyo during the same trip.
