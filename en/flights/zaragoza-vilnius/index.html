Best time to travel

Vilna is worth visiting any time of year. For lower prices, travel outside peak season (July-August). Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer good weather and fewer crowds. Winter is colder, but the city has charm if you visit in December during the festive season.

What to do in Vilna

Lithuania's capital blends medieval history with contemporary art. Stroll through the Old Town, a UNESCO World Heritage site, with its cobbled streets and picturesque squares. Visit the Cathedral, climb Gediminas Castle for panoramic views, and explore the alternative galleries and cafés in the Uzupis neighbourhood. Vilna airport is 4 km from the centre, well connected by bus.
