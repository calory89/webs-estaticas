Best time to visit

Japan shines in spring (March–May, cherry blossoms) and autumn (September–November, mild weather and autumn colours). November is ideal: pleasant temperatures, less rain, and beautiful autumn foliage in Kyoto. Avoid summer (heat and humidity) and winter is freezing but quieter. Book 4–8 weeks ahead for international flights.

What to do when you arrive in Kyoto

Kyoto is Japan's cultural heartland: ancient temples (Fushimi Inari, Kinkaku-ji), geishas in Gion, zen gardens and traditional markets. You can easily get there by train from Osaka Airport (OSA, your arrival point). Carry cash (many places don't take cards), respect temple opening hours and be prepared for plenty of walking.
