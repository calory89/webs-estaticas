Best time to visit Salzburg

December is stunning for Christmas markets. January–February: cold but fewer tourists and lower fares. April–May and September–October are peak season with pleasant weather. August is expensive and crowded. For the best prices, travel January to March or November.

What to do when you arrive

Salzburg is Mozart's birthplace and home to a UNESCO-listed Old Town. Walk Getreidegasse (the prettiest street), visit Hohensalzburg Fortress from above, and explore Mirabell Palace. The Alps are 90 minutes away, ideal for hiking or winter skiing. The city is compact and walkable from the airport (15 km, direct bus available).

No, Ryanair connects this route with one stop. It's not direct, but a single stopover is quick and the price from €159 makes up for it.

How long is the journey with a stopover?

Total time varies depending on your stopover airport, but it typically runs 4–6 hours of flight time plus the layover. It's not a long haul, though the stop adds to the duration.

What else is there to see in Salzburg?

Hohensalzburg Fortress (funicular access, 360° views), Mirabell Palace and gardens, the Cathedral, and if you have time, the nearby Alps. Salzburg is compact; 2–3 days cover the main sights.
