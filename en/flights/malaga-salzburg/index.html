Best time to visit

Salzburg shines year-round. Winter (December–February) brings Christmas markets and Alpine snow; summer offers perfect weather for sightseeing. Avoid July and August if you want fewer crowds and better prices.

What to do on arrival

Spend 2–3 days minimum. Visit the Cathedral, Mirabell Palace, Hohensalzburg Fortress and the Altstadt (old town). If you have time, take day trips to Salzkammergut lakes or the nearby Alps. Public transport is efficient and affordable.
