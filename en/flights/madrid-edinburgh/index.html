Ryanair connects Madrid with Edinburgh on a direct flight and prices start at €66 return. The median sits at €116, so it's realistic to find a ticket under €130 if you search early.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €66 return. The median is €116 and there are options at €130. For these fares, book with at least 3–4 weeks' notice. Ryanair has occasional flash sales, so set alerts and check every few days. Short trips (like the example: Saturday departure, Tuesday return) tend to be cheaper than long weekends.

Airline and stops

Ryanair is the sole operator on this route and offers a direct connection with no intermediate stops. The outbound departs Madrid around 20:00 and the return leaves Edinburgh around 06:00 in the morning. Hand baggage is included; any checked luggage carries an extra fee.

Best time to visit

Edinburgh is worth visiting any time, but autumn (September–October) and spring (April–May) offer good weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is dark but magical, especially if you catch the Edinburgh Festival in August (though that's peak season). Book in advance to avoid inflated prices.

What to do when you arrive

Edinburgh is compact and walkable. The historic centre with the Castle, Royal Mile, and Holyrood Palace are must-sees. The airport is 10 km from the centre (train or bus in 30 minutes). Explore the closes (alleyways) of the old town, climb Calton Hill for panoramic views, and don't miss Greyfriars Kirkyard. With 3 days, add a day on the coast or in the Pentland Hills.

FAQ

Is it really possible to fly Madrid–Edinburgh for €66?

Yes. We've logged this price in our tracking. It's a Ryanair base fare on that route, but it depends on the date and how far ahead you book. It tends to appear in searches 3–4 weeks out and on weekdays or mid-week.

Is the flight direct or does it have stops?

It's direct. Ryanair flies non-stop between Madrid and Edinburgh.

What price should I realistically expect?

The median is €116 return, so that's a realistic and frequent price. Some days will be more expensive (€130+) and others will have bargains, but €116 is what you can plan for as normal.

What must I see in Edinburgh?

Edinburgh Castle and the Royal Mile are the highlights. Then Holyrood Palace, Calton Hill for views, and wandering the closes of the old town. With 3 days, you'll cover the centre and can take a day trip to the surroundings.
