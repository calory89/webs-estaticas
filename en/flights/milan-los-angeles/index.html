Price and when to book

The base price starts from €405 return; the median sits at €520. Prices checked on 23 October show a range of €405, €520 and €621. There's no fixed rule about which month is cheapest, but booking well ahead usually reduces your final price. Compare flexible dates if you can: shifting your trip a few days may make a difference.

Airline and stops

Royal Air Maroc operates this route. The flight has 1 stop, so it's not direct: expect one stopover on the Milan-Los Angeles route. Stopovers can add time, but they're standard on transatlantic flights. Check your booking for the stopover location and duration.

Best time to visit

Los Angeles has mild weather almost year-round. Winter (December-February) tends to be rainier but with pleasant temperatures. Summer is dry and warm; avoid August if you can't handle high heat. Any season works: choose based on your preferred activities and budget.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at LAX (Los Angeles International). From here you have beaches in Santa Monica and Malibu, the cultural heart of Downtown, Griffith Observatory with city views, film studios at Universal and Warner Bros., and areas like Venice Beach or Hollywood Boulevard. The city is sprawling: rent a car or use public transport depending on your route.
