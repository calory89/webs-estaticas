Paris to San Francisco starts from €590 return with TAP Portugal. The median price hovers around €659, though you'll also find options at €778 depending on dates. With one stop, it's an achievable route for those seeking California without breaking the bank.

Price and when to book

Tickets from €590 appear around mid-December. The median sits at €659, and higher fares reach €778. Booking 6-8 weeks ahead typically yields better results; the sooner after checking, the better. Check regularly: long-haul pricing like this shifts with demand and availability.

Airline and stops

TAP Portugal operates this route with one stop. It's not direct, but TAP is reliable and its connections (typically via Lisbon) are smooth. One stop lets you stretch your legs and doesn't add excessive time to a transatlantic journey.

Best time to travel

December looks promising based on these prices, though it brings winter cold in Paris. San Francisco has pleasant weather year-round. For more daylight, spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) are ideal, though usually pricier. Avoid July and August if you want to save.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at San Francisco International Airport (SFO), 25 km from downtown. The Golden Gate, Alcatraz, Fisherman's Wharf and the Mission District are must-sees. Don't skip the cable cars or the bay viewpoints. Eat well, the food scene is excellent. Allow 4-5 days minimum to see the highlights.

FAQ

Can I find direct flights from Paris to San Francisco?

No; this route includes one stop with TAP Portugal. Direct flights from Europe to San Francisco are limited and usually pricier. The stop is manageable for this leg.

Is TAP Portugal a good airline?

Yes, TAP is Portugal's flag carrier and has a solid reputation on transatlantic routes. It offers reasonable service and reliable punctuality.

What does the €590 ticket include?

That depends on TAP and the fare type. It typically includes cabin baggage; check whether checked luggage is included or if you pay extra. Meals and drinks vary by class.

How many days do I need in San Francisco?

To see the essentials (Golden Gate, Alcatraz, main neighbourhoods), 4-5 days. If you like a slower pace or plan trips to Napa or Big Sur, better to allow 6-7 days.
