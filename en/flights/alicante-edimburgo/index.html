Best time to visit

Scotland is stunning year-round, but July and August are peak season with premium prices—and August is rainy too. April–May and September–October strike the sweet spot: cooler, fewer crowds, and cheaper fares. Winter (December–February) is the most budget-friendly if you don't mind cold and short daylight.

What to do when you arrive in Edinburgh

Edinburgh is compact and walkable. The Castle crowns the skyline; the Royal Mile links Castle to Holyrood Palace. Arthur's Seat rewards you with 360° views (45 mins' hike). Traditional Scottish pubs, especially in Grassmarket, are essential. With extra days, the Highlands are 1–2 hours by train or car: Loch Lomond, Glen Coe, Isle of Skye. The airport is 10 km from the centre; bus, train, or taxi gets you downtown in 25–30 minutes.
