Best time to visit

Berlin is captivating year-round. Autumn and winter offer a different vibe: fewer crowds and Nordic skies. November is a solid choice if you want to escape summer heat but prefer avoiding harsh winter conditions. Prices tend to be gentler outside peak summer (June to August). Always check Berlin's event calendar for festivals or activities that might interest you.

What to do when you arrive

Berlin delivers art, history and nightlife. Don't miss the Brandenburg Gate, the Reichstag (with dome views), the East Side Gallery (Berlin Wall turned art) and the Museum Island. Walk through neighbourhoods like Kreuzberg and Friedrichshain for alternative vibes. Sample local food and explore its rich cultural spaces. Berlin Airport (BER) is well served by public transport to the city centre.
