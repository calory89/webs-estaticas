Best time to visit

Tenerife is a year-round destination thanks to its subtropical climate. November to March offers pleasant temperatures and lighter crowds. Travel during shoulder or low season if you want fewer tourists and better value for your stay.

What to do when you arrive

Expect black-sand beaches, Mount Teide (Spain's highest peak), and charming villages. Santa Cruz and Puerto de la Cruz are key entry points. Spend days exploring volcanic landscapes, hiking, or relaxing on the coast. North and south Tenerife offer very different experiences.
