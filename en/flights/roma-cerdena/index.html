Price and when to book

Prices start at €41 return, though you may see options up to €59 in our sample. The earlier you book, the better: fares typically rise as departure approaches. This trip is especially worth it if you plan ahead and have flexible dates.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route. The good news: direct flight between Rome and Sardinia, no stops. You'll depart Rome and arrive straight into Cagliari, Sardinia's main airport. With Ryanair, watch your cabin baggage allowance and note that extras like meals and checked bags carry additional charges.

Best time to visit

Sardinia shines from May to September, with perfect beaches and stable weather. For bargains, shoulder months (April, October) offer a sweet spot between price and climate. Winter (November to February) is cheaper but more unpredictable. Choose based on your priorities: guaranteed sun or a lighter wallet.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in Cagliari, in southern Sardinia. From there, stunning beaches await (Poetto is 10 minutes away), plus the Basilica of Bonaria in the old city and the Pisan Walls. Venturing further, don't miss the Costa Smeralda coast in the north, Cala Luna cliffs, or inland towns like Oristano. The island is compact: all areas are well-connected.
