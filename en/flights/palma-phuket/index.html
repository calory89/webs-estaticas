Prices in the sample range from €610 to €845. The median sits at €696, so use that as your realistic benchmark. For intercontinental routes like this, book well in advance: the earlier, the better. Check fares regularly from 2-3 months before travel.

Airline and stops

You'll travel with Vueling, which operates this route with 2 stops. There are no direct flights from Palma to Phuket, so expect two plane changes. Total journey time will be substantial, but that's standard for this distance from a regional airport.

Best time to visit

Phuket works year-round, but the dry season (November to February) offers the best weather and least rain. It's also peak season and pricier. For cheaper fares and don't mind monsoon showers, consider May to October instead.

What to do when you arrive

Phuket is your gateway to Thailand's islands. From the airport, reach Patong Beach easily, visit local temples, or catch a ferry to Phi Phi or Similan for diving. The city has nightlife, spas and traditional markets. Hire a car or use taxis and ride-apps to get around.
