Best time to visit

Zanzibar has a tropical climate. The dry season (June-October) is most comfortable: pleasant temperatures, less rain and better sea visibility. December-February is also possible, though more humid. Avoid April and May if you prefer fewer showers.

What to do when you arrive in Zanzibar

Stone Town, the historic capital, is a must: wander its coral streets, spice markets and 19th-century palaces. Nungwi and Padua beaches are perfect for relaxing. Snorkelling and diving on coral reefs, trips to nearby islands like Pemba, and dolphin-spotting excursions round out the offer. Your arrival airport is Zanzibar Stone Town (ZNZ), with transfers to your accommodation taking between 20 and 45 minutes depending on location.
