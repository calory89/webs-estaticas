Best time to visit Cape Town

South Africa is a year-round destination, but the best period is the Southern summer (December to February) with long, warm days, though it's peak season. Autumn (March to May) and spring (September to October) offer pleasant temperatures and fewer crowds. Winters (June to August) are cool but dry, ideal for whale watching near the coast.

What to do in Cape Town

Table Mountain is essential: take the rotating cable car or hike for 360° views. Tour Robben Island (UNESCO heritage site), walk the Waterfront, visit Camps Bay or Muizenberg beaches, and don't miss Cape Point. Local markets, city dining, and nearby wine estates round out the attractions.
