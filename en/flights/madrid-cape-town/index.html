Airline and stopovers

Best time to travel

Cape Town works year-round. Southern summer (December–February) is hot and busy; southern winter (June–August) brings milder temps and rain. Pick based on what suits you: beaches and hiking in summer, greener landscapes and fewer crowds in winter.

What to see and do when you arrive

Cape Town delivers Table Mountain, the Garden Route (towards Hermanus), beaches like Camps Bay, Robben Island, and world-class food. The international airport is 20 km from the city centre; grab a taxi, hire a car or take a shuttle based on your budget.
