Best time to visit Miami

Miami works year-round, but December and January are peak season with high prices and crowds. For fewer tourists and better deals, try September or October, though hurricane season runs then too. Spring (March–May) balances good weather with more reasonable fares.

What to see and do when you arrive

Miami delivers picture-perfect beaches at South Beach, urban art in Wynwood, cosmopolitan Calle Ocho in Little Havana, and shopping in the Design District. Tropical weather means outdoor plans work any time. Skip airport delays: rent a car or use Uber to move freely around the city.
