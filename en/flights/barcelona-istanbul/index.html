Airline and direct flights

You'll fly with Pegasus on a direct Barcelona-Istanbul route with no stopovers. You leave BCN and arrive at IST in a single journey. Pegasus is a low-cost carrier, so expect essentials: hand baggage usually included, but checked luggage and extras come at additional cost.

Best time to visit

Istanbul is a year-round destination, but spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer ideal weather: mild temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. Winter (January-February) is cooler but less touristy. July and August are hot and busy. Book ahead regardless of season to lock in price.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Istanbul's international airport (IST). The city offers Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, Topkapi Palace, and the Grand Bazaar. Explore Galata, relax in Turkish baths, or take a Bosphorus cruise. Local food is outstanding: kebab, meze, and Turkish coffee are must-tries. Use public transport with an Istanbulkart.
