Best time to travel

Fort Lauderdale is a beach destination with pleasant weather year-round. The dry season (November to April) offers comfortable temperatures and less rainfall. What to do when you arrive in Fort Lauderdale

Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood Airport (FLL) is well connected to the city. Fort Lauderdale's beaches are the main draw: wide, clean and popular. You can also explore the historic downtown (Las Olas Boulevard), museums, water parks and shopping. The area is ideal for relaxing after a 3-stop flight.
