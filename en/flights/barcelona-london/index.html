Cheap flights Barcelona to London: from €49 with Ryanair

49 € returnTypical price: 54 €See flights and dates ✈️

Ryanair connects Barcelona and London non-stop from just €49 return. Median prices sit around €54, making this one of Europe's most budget-friendly routes. Book in advance to secure the best fares.

Price and when to book

The cheapest flights start from €49 return, with typical prices hovering around €54. The earlier you book, the better the price: the standard recommendation is 2–3 months in advance. Avoid booking the final week before departure, when prices spike significantly.

Airline and flight details

Ryanair is the operator on this route, offering direct connections with no stops. The journey is quick: departs Barcelona (BCN) at 12:45 and arrives in London (LON) at 14:35, giving you the afternoon to explore. Remember Ryanair charges for checked baggage and seat selection.

Best time to visit

Barcelona to London runs year-round, but February (as in the example 2027-02-01) is typically quieter than summer with stable pricing. October, November and March are also excellent: decent weather, fewer crowds and reasonable fares. July and August are priciest.

What to do in London

You'll arrive at a London airport, central connections take 15–45 minutes depending on the terminal. See Big Ben, the Tower of London and the British Museum (free entry), then stroll South Bank. West End theatres offer matinee deals. Try fish and chips at any pub and afternoon tea. The Oyster Card makes public transport affordable for getting around.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price Barcelona to London?

The lowest recorded price is €49 return with Ryanair. Similar deals appear regularly if you book 2–3 months ahead.

Is the Barcelona to London flight direct?

Yes, it's a non-stop flight with no stops. Ryanair flies from Barcelona (BCN) to London (LON) on a single route.

Which airline flies Barcelona to London?

Ryanair operates this route, offering the market's most competitive fares for this connection.

How many terminals does London airport have?

London has multiple airports. Terminal details aren't specified here, but Ryanair historically flew to Stansted or Luton. Confirm your landing airport at booking and check journey times to the city centre.
