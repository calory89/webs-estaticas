Price and when to book

Prices found range from €41, €51 and €75 for return flights. The lowest fare is exceptional; average prices are very competitive. Generally, booking 5–8 weeks ahead tends to yield better fares. Check regularly as Ryanair adjusts prices based on availability.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight with no stopovers. Departures like 14 November at 09:10 take you straight to Milan. It's the quickest and hassle-free option.

Best time to visit

Milan is attractive in spring and autumn, when the weather is mild and it's less crowded than summer. If you're after low fares, avoid July and August. Late October and November offer a good balance between pleasant climate and competitive prices.

What to do when you arrive

Milan is the fashion and design capital. Visit the Cathedral, the Duomo, the Vittorio Emanuele II Gallery and Sforza Castle. The Brera neighbourhood is perfect for walks, dining and art. If you have time, take a regional train to Como or Bergamo.
