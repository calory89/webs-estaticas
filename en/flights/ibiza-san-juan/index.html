Best time to travel

San Juan enjoys warm weather year-round. The dry season runs December to April: less rain, crisper air and clear skies. July and August are hot and humid but quieter. Flight price will depend more on your flexibility than the season; choose based on the climate you prefer.

What to do when you arrive in San Juan

Luis Muñoz Marín international airport (SJU) is 10 km from the city centre. In the old town, walk colourful narrow streets, visit El Morro and the colonial walls. Try local cuisine, enjoy turquoise Caribbean beaches and explore Camuy Caves or El Yunque rainforest if time allows. Most attractions are well connected from the centre.

What's worth seeing in San Juan in 3–4 days?

El Morro, Paseo Piñones for sandy beaches, the old town with El Convento de los Dominicos, Creole food, and if you have time El Yunque National Forest or Camuy Caves. No rush: San Juan deserves a slow pace.
