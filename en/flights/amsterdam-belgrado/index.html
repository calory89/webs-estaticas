Best time to visit Belgrade

Belgrade is an interesting destination year-round. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winters are cold but the city has its festive charm. Book ahead to secure your flight at the lowest price, regardless of which season you choose.

What to do when you arrive in Belgrade

Belgrade airport (BEG) is about 12 km from the city centre. Once there, don't miss Kalemegdán fortress with views over the Danube and Sava rivers, the city's museums, the lively nightlife scene at Beton Hala, or a walk through the bohemian Skadarlija neighbourhood. Local cuisine is excellent and prices are very reasonable.
