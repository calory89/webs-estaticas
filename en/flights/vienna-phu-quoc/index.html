Best time to travel

November is ideal: it's the start of Vietnam's dry season, warm but manageable temperatures and less rain. Phu Quoc enjoys calm seas and pristine beaches at this time. Avoid May to September for monsoons. Prices tend to be lowest outside Christmas and New Year, so November–December is your sweet spot.

What to do when you arrive at Phu Quoc

Phu Quoc is Vietnam's largest island, famous for white-sand beaches, clear waters and budget-friendly resorts. Swim at Long Beach, explore Phu Quoc National Park, sample fresh seafood at local markets and unwind at surprisingly affordable spas. The island blends beach, hiking and food perfectly. The international airport has good connections to the island's centre.

What's the cheapest price I can find?

From €671 return based on the sample checked. Median is €743, so that's the typical fare you'll see. Booking 3–4 weeks ahead and travelling in November–December gives you better odds.

Which airline am I flying with?

Scoot, a low-cost carrier from Singapore. It's reliable, specialises in Asian routes and is known for keeping fares down. Check what's included in your ticket (baggage, meals) before booking.

What's the best time to visit Phu Quoc?

November to March: dry season, perfect beaches, pleasant temperatures. November especially is ideal because prices haven't spiked yet with the Christmas rush and the weather is excellent. May to September is monsoon, avoid it.
