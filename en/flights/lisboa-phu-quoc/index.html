Best time to visit

Phu Quoc has tropical weather. November to April is peak season: dry and pleasant. Skip May to October due to monsoon and heavy rain. December and January see highest demand and prices rise. Target September or October for balance between good weather and lower fares.

What to do in Phu Quoc

Virgin beaches, diving, snorkelling and resorts. Phu Quoc National Park is essential. Visit the fishing village of An Thoi. Explore pearl farms and night fishing courses. The airport is 50 km from the main town. Hire a scooter or car for flexibility.
