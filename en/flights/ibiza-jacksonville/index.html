Best time to visit

Jacksonville has a subtropical warm climate year-round. Winter (December–February) is peak season with potentially higher fares, but offers pleasant temperatures. For fewer crowds and drier weather, try spring (March–May) or autumn (September–November). Avoid peak hurricane season between August and October.

What to do in Jacksonville

Jacksonville is the largest city by area in the contiguous USA. Stroll the River Walk, enjoy beaches like Jacksonville Beach and Ponte Vedra, visit art and history museums, or explore the historic San Marco neighbourhood. For nature lovers, the area has parks and wildlife-viewing opportunities nearby.
