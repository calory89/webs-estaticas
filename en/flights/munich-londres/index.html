Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €97 return; the median stands at €142. We've recorded samples at €97, €142 and €152. For this route, the golden rule is to book in advance: the further ahead you travel, the better the fare. Check regularly and set up alerts so you don't miss last-minute deals.

Airline and stops

easyJet operates this route with direct flights, no stops. You travel from Munich to London in a single journey, which saves time and the hassle of connections. The airline is known for competitive pricing, but check what your base fare includes.

Best time to travel

There's no specific season when prices drop across the board. What does work is flying midweek instead of weekends, and avoiding public holidays and bank holidays. December tends to be pricier due to Christmas, so if you want a bargain, consider autumn or early spring instead.

What to do when you arrive in London

London has something for everyone: free museums like the British Museum and National Gallery, Big Ben, the Tower of London and Buckingham Palace. Public transport (Tube and buses) is straightforward with an Oyster Card. Grab fish and chips at a traditional pub, walk along the Thames and don't miss Camden Market or Portobello Road.
