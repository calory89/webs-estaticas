Best time to visit Berlin

Berlin is appealing year-round. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Winter (December-February) is cold but the city shines with Christmas markets and cultural events. July and August are warmer but busier. Choose based on your climate and activity preferences.

What to do and see in Berlin

Berlin is a capital of modern European history: world-class museums (Pergamon Museum, Neues Museum), Brandenburg Gate, Wall remnants, hipster neighbourhoods like Kreuzberg, varied cuisine and vibrant nightlife. Berlin Airport (BER) is well connected by public transport to the city centre. Allow 3-4 days to explore at ease.
