Best time to travel to Nairobi and Kenya

Kenya enjoys pleasant weather during the dry season: June–October and January–February. These periods coincide with the best conditions for safari and outdoor activities. January and February tend to be less crowded than July–August, making them ideal for a better experience and potentially less congestion.

What to do when you arrive in Nairobi

Nairobi is Kenya's gateway: from here you can access the country's top safaris, such as Amboseli National Park or the Masai Mara Reserve. The city itself offers museums, local cuisine and traditional market shopping. Jomo Kenyatta International Airport connects to the city centre in 45–60 minutes by taxi or shuttle.
