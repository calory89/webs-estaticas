Best time to visit

Prague is appealing most of the year. November (like the sample flight date) offers pleasant weather for sightseeing without summer crowds. December is magical with Christmas markets if that appeals to you. Spring (April–May) is also ideal: mild temperatures and fewer tourists. Avoid July–August if you want to escape the peak season rush.

What to do when you arrive

Václav Havel Airport (PRG) is about 20 km from the city centre. Walk across Charles Bridge, visit Prague Castle and Old Town Square with its famous Astronomical Clock. Traditional Czech restaurants are affordable and tasty. Spending little in Prague is easy: beer, food and transport are all reasonably priced.

What can I see and do in Prague?

Prague is an old city with plenty of charm. Charles Bridge, the Castle, Old Town Square and its churches are must-sees. Czech beer is excellent and cheap. You can explore the Jewish Quarter, local markets and traditional restaurants. It's a very walkable city.
