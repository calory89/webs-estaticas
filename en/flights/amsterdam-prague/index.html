Best time to visit

Prague is worth visiting year-round. Winter brings Christmas markets (if you go before January) and fewer crowds. Spring and autumn offer pleasant weather and moderate fares. Summer is peak season, with larger crowds and potential price hikes.

What to do when you arrive

Prague is compact and walkable. Charles Bridge is essential, as are the Castle and Old Town Square. Grab a cheap Czech beer, wander through Josefov and Vinohrady neighbourhoods, and spend at least a day exploring at your own pace. The metro and tram network make getting around simple.
