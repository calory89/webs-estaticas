What to do when you arrive

Mauritius is renowned for white-sand beaches, crystal-clear waters and coral reefs perfect for diving and snorkelling. The capital, Port Louis, blends colonial history with modern life. Don't miss the Pamplemousses Botanical Garden, volcanic mountains in the south and the island's cultural diversity. Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam international airport is well connected to the rest of the island.
