Best time to visit

Seoul is spectacular in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with mild temperatures and clear skies. Winter (December–February) is cold but perfect if you enjoy snow. Avoid summer if you don't fancy extreme humidity. Check the climate for your preferences before booking.

What to do when you arrive in Seoul

You'll land at Incheon International Airport (ICN), 60 km from the city centre. Take the Airport Railroad express train or a direct bus. Don't miss Seoul's historic palaces, Gangnam district, the Han River, traditional markets like Myeongdong, and Korean cuisine. It's a city that beautifully blends tradition and modernity.
