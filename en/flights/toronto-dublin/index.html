Best time to visit

Dublin works year-round, though May to September offer better weather (more daylight, less rain). Prices fluctuate by demand, so book in advance regardless of season. December can surprise you with good rates (like the example: 5–11 December at €533–534), so don't rule out winter trips if you're flexible.

What to do when you arrive in Dublin

Dublin is compact and walkable: Temple Bar, Trinity College, the Book of Kells, Guinness Storehouse. Stroll along the Liffey, visit Christ Church Cathedral. Local buses take you north and south; trains run to Galway, Cork, or Belfast for day trips. The airport is 10 km from the city centre (quick bus or taxi ride).
