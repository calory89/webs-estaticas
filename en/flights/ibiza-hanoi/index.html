Best time to visit Vietnam

Vietnam's dry season (October to April) is ideal: pleasant weather, less rain and clear skies. January and February are peak tourist months and often pricier. December to February can offer good deals if you avoid public holidays. April marks the start of heat and humidity.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

Hanoi is your gateway to Vietnam. Noi Bai International Airport is about 25 km from the city centre. Explore the Old Quarter, Hoan Kiem Lake, Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum and local temples. From here, it's easy to reach Ha Long Bay, Sapa or the Mekong Delta by bus or domestic flight. Vietnamese cuisine is a major draw.
