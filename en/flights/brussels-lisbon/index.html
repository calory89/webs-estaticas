Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with direct flights. No aircraft changes or waiting at other airports. Early morning departures (like the example on 6 January at 06:20) and afternoon returns (like 15 January at 17:50) are typical.

Best time to travel

Lisbon is pleasant almost year-round, but October, April and May offer good weather without peak tourism crowds. Summer months (June to September) draw more visitors and prices rise. Winter is cheaper but rainier.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Humberto Delgado Lisbon, well connected to the centre. Alfama is a must: narrow streets, traditional tiles and viewpoints. Visit Belém district for Mosteiro dos Jerónimos. Try pastéis de nata, hop on tram 28 and enjoy sunset drinks overlooking the Tagus.
