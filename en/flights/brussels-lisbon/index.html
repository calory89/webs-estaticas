Brussels and Lisbon connected with Ryanair for just €68 return. Prices average around €74. One of the cheapest routes between Belgium and Portugal: direct, straightforward.

Price and when to book

From €68 return is the lowest price on record. The median sits at €74, meaning most tickets range between that figure and €101. Book as early as possible for the best fares. On a route this competitive, prices tend to climb as departure approaches.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with direct flights. No aircraft changes or waiting at other airports. Early morning departures (like the example on 6 January at 06:20) and afternoon returns (like 15 January at 17:50) are typical.

Best time to travel

Lisbon is pleasant almost year-round, but October, April and May offer good weather without peak tourism crowds. Summer months (June to September) draw more visitors and prices rise. Winter is cheaper but rainier.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Humberto Delgado Lisbon, well connected to the centre. Alfama is a must: narrow streets, traditional tiles and viewpoints. Visit Belém district for Mosteiro dos Jerónimos. Try pastéis de nata, hop on tram 28 and enjoy sunset drinks overlooking the Tagus.

Yes, Ryanair operates this route without stopovers. One flight takes you straight from BRU to LIS.

€68 return is the lowest price recorded. Most tickets are around €74.

Is a weekend in Lisbon from Brussels worth it?

Absolutely. With prices from €68 and direct flights of just over 2 hours, it's a very worthwhile escape. Alfama, Belém and the Tagus terraces justify every euro.
