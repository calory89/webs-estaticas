Best time to visit

Helsinki shines in summer (June to August) with long days and mild temperatures. If you're after northern lights, winter (December to February) is your season, though it's colder. Autumn (September-October) is a good bet to dodge crowds and find more moderate prices. Check the weather when choosing your dates.

What to do when you arrive

Helsinki airport is 20 km from the city centre with excellent train connections. The capital offers design museums, Alvar Aalto's architecture, the Uspenski Cathedral and harbour walks. From there you can easily reach the Finnish archipelago islands and Laponia if you fancy exploring further.
