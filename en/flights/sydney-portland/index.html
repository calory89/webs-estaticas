Best time to visit

Oregon offers its best weather in summer (June to September) and early autumn (September to October), with mild temperatures and less rain. Winter is colder and wetter, though less crowded. Autumn is ideal if you want golden landscapes and pleasant weather without peak tourist season.

What to do when you arrive

Portland is known for its vibrant cultural scene, craft breweries and quality dining. Explore the historic downtown, visit Powell's City of Books (the world's largest independent bookstore), browse urban parks and nearby Oregon waterfalls. Portland International Airport (PDX) is 10 km from the centre with good public transport links.

What should I see in Portland besides the city centre?
