Best time to visit

Seoul is stunning in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with mild temperatures and clear skies. Winter is cold but dry, perfect for Christmas markets and ice skating. Avoid summer (June–August) for heat and humidity. Prices don't drop much by season, so choose dates based on weather and your availability.

What to do in Seoul

Visit Gyeongbokgung Palace, walk through Dongdaemun Design Plaza, eat at Myeongdong and Gangnam, and climb Namsan Tower for panoramic views. Don't miss the Hanok village (traditional neighbourhood) and Buddhist temples. The metro is efficient and cheap to get around. A week is enough time for the essentials.
