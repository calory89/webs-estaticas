Best time to visit

Rio shines nearly year-round, but the southern summer (December to March) offers peak weather and events. Avoid heavy rain in January and February if you prefer dry days. The dry season (May to October) is also excellent and typically quieter.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Tom Jobim International Airport (also called Galeão), about 20 km from the city centre. In Rio, visit Christ the Redeemer, Guanabara Bay, the beaches of Copacabana and Ipanema, and the bohemian neighbourhood of Lapa. Try ceviche or local Brazilian food. Rio has beaches, mountains, and endless energy all in one.
