Best time to visit

Jacksonville has a subtropical climate. Winter (December to February) brings pleasant temperatures (15-20°C) and less rainfall, ideal for outdoor activities. Spring and autumn are also good options. Avoid June to September due to heat and humidity.

What to do on arrival

Jacksonville is the largest US city by land area. The international airport (JAX) is 13 km from the centre. Visit Amelia Island beaches, the historic San Marco neighbourhood, the Cummer Museum of Art, and the Riverwalk. Local food is excellent, especially seafood. Rent a car or use Uber to get around; public transport is limited.
