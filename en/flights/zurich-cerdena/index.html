Prices start from €139 return, though the median sits at €177. Our sample shows fares of €139, €177 and €214. The gap between lowest and highest is substantial, so book early. Aim for 6–8 weeks ahead of travel to lock in low fares; prices jump sharply in the final two weeks.

Total travel time depends on where you stop, but Vueling's network is solid. Pick your departure time based on whether you prefer a quick turnaround or a longer layover to stretch your legs.

Best time to visit

Sardinia shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): uncrowded beaches, pleasant temps between 20–25°C. Summer (June–August) is hot and ideal if you handle heat, but busier. Winter is mild on the coast but cloudier. Either way, the Mediterranean is always inviting.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Cagliari airport in the south of the island. It's 4 km from the centre with good bus and taxi links. From there, reach white-sand beaches (La Pelosa, Tuerredda), Cagliari's historic old town with its Roman amphitheatre, and coastal villages like Villasimius. Don't miss the northern Costa Smeralda or inland routes through vineyards and hills.
