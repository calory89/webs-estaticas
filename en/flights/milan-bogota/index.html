Best time to travel

Bogotá has tropical weather year-round. The driest months are December–March and July–August, though rain can occur anytime. From a flight price perspective, avoid peak dates (Christmas, Easter, July) if you want lower fares. Keep in mind this is a long journey: plan your schedule carefully.

What to do when you arrive in Bogotá

Bogotá sits at 2640 metres altitude: take it easy the first few days while you acclimatise. Visit La Candelaria (historic centre), the Gold Museum (one of the world's finest for pre-Columbian art), and Monserrate. Try arepas, bandeja paisa, and Colombian coffee. Eldorado airport is 13 km from the city centre: taxi, Uber, or buses are available to reach your hotel.
