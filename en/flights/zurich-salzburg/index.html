Best time to visit

Salzburg is beautiful year-round. Winter brings snow and festive vibes; spring and autumn offer mild weather and fewer crowds. Summer is peak season and accommodation prices spike. If you want cheap flights and peace and quiet, avoid July–August and school holidays.

What to do when you arrive

Salzburg airport (W.A. Mozart) is 4 km from the city centre. Take a bus or taxi. In town, don't miss the Cathedral, Schloss Hellbrunn, the historic old town, and Mozart's birthplace. The Alps are accessible by train. A weekend is enough for the essentials.
