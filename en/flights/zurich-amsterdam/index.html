The base price is €266 return. The median price we found is €275, so you should expect to pay in that range. There's no reliable pattern for price drops on specific days, but booking in advance generally helps. Check frequently: prices change daily based on availability and demand.

Airline and stops

Best time to visit

Amsterdam is beautiful year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. The canals are stunning at any time. Avoid peak tourist season if you prefer a quieter experience and better value.

What to do when you arrive

Amsterdam welcomes you with its canals, art galleries and bicycles everywhere. Schiphol Airport is 15 km from the city centre, well connected by train. Explore the old town, visit museums like Van Gogh or Rijksmuseum, take a canal boat tour or simply soak in the city's vibe.
