Best time to visit

Warsaw shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather, fewer crowds than summer, and a gorgeous city. Winter is cold but magical if you like snow and Christmas lights. Summer is peak tourist season.

What to do in Warsaw

Warsaw's Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage site: cobbled streets, cafés and museums everywhere. Don't miss the Palace of Culture and Science, the Jewish Quarter and the Warsaw Uprising Museum. For food, try authentic Polish cuisine in the Old Town. Warsaw has world-class museums and a vibrant nightlife.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Bilbao to Warsaw?

Yes. Wizz Air flies direct with no stops. It's a straightforward and comfortable journey.

Is €80 the final price or are there booking fees?

€80 is from what the return flight costs. Check if you need checked baggage, seat selection or other add-ons, which will increase the price. Always compare the final cost at checkout.

Which airline flies this route?

Wizz Air operates the Bilbao–Warsaw route.

What can I see in Warsaw over a weekend?

The Old Town, Palace of Culture and Science, history museums and neighbourhoods like Praga and the Vistula riverbank are must-sees. Warsaw's centre is compact and easy to navigate: two to three days cover the essentials.
