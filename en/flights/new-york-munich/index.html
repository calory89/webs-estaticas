Best time to visit

Munich is lovely in autumn (September–October) and winter, especially December for Christmas markets. Spring (April–May) offers pleasant weather if you prefer mild temperatures. Summer (June–August) draws more tourists and usually higher fares. Avoid peak holidays to catch better deals.

What to do when you arrive

Munich airport is about 30 km from the city centre with good train and bus connections. Explore the old town around Marienplatz, visit the Munich Cathedral and Nymphenburg Palace. If you travel in autumn, Munich hosts Oktoberfest. Museums (Pinakothek, Deutsches Museum) are perfect for rainy days.
