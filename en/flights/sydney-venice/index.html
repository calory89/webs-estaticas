What to do in Venice

The city has no cars or roads. You'll land at Marco Polo Airport (VCE) and catch a boat or public vaporetto straight to the centre. St Mark's Basilica, Doge's Palace, Grand Canal, Bridge of Sighs are essentials. Museums (Accademia, Franchetti) hold masterpieces. Walk the bridges and squares; wander side streets to find authentic trattorias away from tourist traps.

FAQ

Why is this route so expensive?

The distance is massive: nearly 17,000 km from Australia to Italy. Even at €936 it's competitive for an intercontinental route with 2 stopovers. Fares on routes this long rarely drop below €800; when you see €936, you've either booked early or caught a good deal.

Is it direct or does it have stopovers?

It has 2 stopovers. No commercial direct flights exist from Sydney to Venice. Stopovers are standard on this pairing and built into the fare. Total journey time can run 24–30 hours including layovers.

Can I find a cheaper price by switching airlines?

China Southern Airlines is the option shown here from €936. Other carriers may serve the route, so check flight comparison sites. Sometimes an extra stopover lowers the fare; sometimes it's not worth the extra time.

What's at Venice airport when I land?

Marco Polo Airport (VCE) sits 13 km from the historic centre. Public buses and vaporettos (water buses) depart from the terminal. No direct train from the airport exists. The vaporetto is the most Venetian and touristy way in; the bus is cheaper. From there you can walk to anywhere in the city.
