Best time to visit

Las Vegas is a year-round destination. Keep in mind that summer is extremely hot, with temperatures often exceeding 40°C. Spring and autumn offer more comfortable temperatures. If you're planning a weekend or bank holiday trip, book early: prices rise with demand.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Harry Reid International Airport (LAS), very close to the city centre. The Strip is the main tourist area with casinos, shows and restaurants. Beyond that, you can visit Red Rock Canyon, Valley of Fire or the Hoover Dam. The city is manageable without a car if you stay on the Strip, but one is useful for day trips.
