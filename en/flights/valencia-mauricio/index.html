Best time to visit

Mauritius has a tropical climate. The dry season (May to November) offers pleasant temperatures and less rain. Southern summer (December to March) is hotter and more humid, with cyclone risk. Plan according to your heat tolerance.

What to do on arrival

Mauritius offers beaches, diving and nature. Visit coral reefs, Trois Mamelles mountain, Port Louis market or Chamarel waterfalls. The international airport is in the southeast; connections across the island are available from there. Book excursions in advance, especially during peak season.
