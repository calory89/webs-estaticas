Best time to visit

Munich is lovely year-round, but October to November offers balance: mild temperatures, less summer tourism and good flight availability. If you travel in these November dates like our example, you'll find the city quieter and transport prices lower. Avoid December if you're chasing cheap fares (Christmas pushes costs up).

What to do in Munich

Marienplatz and the Cathedral of Our Lady are must-sees. Nymphenburg Palace, the Bavarian royal residence, deserves a full morning. If you're into beer, Hofbräuhaus beer hall is a classic; if you prefer culture, the German Museum warrants over a day. The English Garden is perfect for wandering. Eat Leberkäse and Kaiserschmarrn at any Gasthof.
