Price and when to book

Flights start from €1,200 return; median price sits at €1,326. Prices rise to €1,693+ depending on availability. Book as far in advance as possible: these intercontinental routes drop when you purchase 2–3 months ahead. Since the search date is October 2026, booking for November still gives you room for good fares.

Airline and stops

Air China operates this route with 3 stops. It's not a direct flight, so total journey time exceeds 24 hours including layovers. This is the most budget-friendly option from Singapore to Punta Cana. Stop locations may vary by booking date.

Best time to visit

November and December are excellent for Punta Cana: warm weather, less rain than summer, perfect beaches. Avoid August and September due to hurricane season. Travelling outside Christmas and New Year helps lower accommodation and activity costs, though flights on this route don't vary as much by season.

What to do on arrival

Punta Cana is your Caribbean gateway. White-sand beaches, all-inclusive resorts, diving and snorkelling on reefs, trips to Bacardi Lagoon or Los Haitises National Park. Punta Cana Airport (PUJ) is 30 km from the tourist hub. Skip car rental if you stay at a resort; hire one if exploring towns like Bayahibe or Santo Domingo.
