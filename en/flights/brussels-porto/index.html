Best time to visit

Porto appeals year-round, but avoid July-August (pricier and crowded). March to May and September to October usually offer the best price-weather balance. Travel mid-week if possible: Wednesday-Sunday flights tend to be cheaper than peak weekend fares.

What to do in Porto

Porto is a northern Portuguese gem. Walk the medieval Ribeira, taste port wine at Vila Nova de Gaia cellars, climb the Torre dos Clérigos and cross the Dom Luís bridge. Hunt for traditional azulejo tiles, explore the Douro riverside and savour local food. The airport is 11 km away; bus or taxi reach the city centre in 20-30 minutes.
