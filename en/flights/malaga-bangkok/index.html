Best time to visit

Thailand has a tropical climate. The dry season (November to February) is best: pleasant temperatures and less rain. July and August are more humid but quieter. Avoid the monsoon season if you prefer fewer showers. Travel when it suits your schedule and budget.

What to do in Bangkok

Bangkok is a metropolis with temples, floating markets, museums and nightlife. Stroll around the Grand Palace, explore the Chao Phraya River, try street food at night markets and visit Buddhist temples. Bangkok's international airport (Suvarnabhumi) is modern and well-connected to the city. Spend at least 3-4 days to enjoy it properly.
