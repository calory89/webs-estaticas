Best time to visit

Miami shines year-round, but keep this in mind: June to November is hurricane season (more risk, cheaper prices); December to April offers perfect weather and higher fares. For the best balance between price and comfort, try May or early June. European winter (December–February) draws more tourists, so expect pricier flights and a crowded destination.
