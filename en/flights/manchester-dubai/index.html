Best time to visit Dubai

Dubai is a year-round destination, but October to April offers more pleasant temperatures (20–30°C). Summer months (June to September) are very hot. To avoid crowds and higher prices, travel outside Christmas, New Year and school holidays. Rainfall is minimal.

What to do in Dubai when you arrive

The Burj Khalifa, Palm Islands, Dubai Mall and Fountain Show are must-sees. Enjoy the desert on a safari, relax at Jumeirah beaches or explore the traditional Deira souk. The international airport is well connected to the centre by metro and taxi. Don't miss the Museum of the Future or a sunset dinner on a traditional dhow.
