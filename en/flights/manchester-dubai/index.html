Price and when to book your Manchester-Dubai flight

The lowest price we found is €410 return, though the median sits at €434. Our sample prices range from €410 to €439. Book as far in advance as possible: the earlier you secure your ticket, the better the price. Avoid leaving your purchase until days before travel, when fares climb.

Airline and stops: what you need to know

Pegasus operates this route with 1 stop. It's not a direct flight, but the price makes up for the extra time. The airline is a budget carrier, so note that baggage may incur a surcharge and service is basic. Example: departure from Manchester on 7 January at 14:15, return on 14 January at 05:20.

Best time to visit Dubai

Dubai is a year-round destination, but October to April offers more pleasant temperatures (20–30°C). Summer months (June to September) are very hot. To avoid crowds and higher prices, travel outside Christmas, New Year and school holidays. Rainfall is minimal.

What to do in Dubai when you arrive

The Burj Khalifa, Palm Islands, Dubai Mall and Fountain Show are must-sees. Enjoy the desert on a safari, relax at Jumeirah beaches or explore the traditional Deira souk. The international airport is well connected to the centre by metro and taxi. Don't miss the Museum of the Future or a sunset dinner on a traditional dhow.
