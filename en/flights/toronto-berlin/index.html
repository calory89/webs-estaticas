Best time to visit

Berlin shines year-round: autumn (September-October) and spring (April-May) offer ideal weather and fewer crowds. Winter (November-February) is colder but charming with festive markets. Summer is peak season with higher prices. Book flexibly across dates to catch better fares.

What to do on arrival

Berlin is history, art and nightlife. Visit the Berlin Wall, Brandenburg Gate, Museum Island and bohemian neighbourhoods like Kreuzberg. The airport (BER) is about 20 km from the city centre; bus, train or taxi get you there in 30-45 minutes. Consider the Berlin Welcome Card for transport and museum discounts.
