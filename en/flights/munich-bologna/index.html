Best time to visit

December is a smart choice to reach Bologna with fewer tourists than spring or summer. Prices drop when you travel outside peak season. If you book 3–4 weeks ahead from when you spot the offer, you'll have better chances of landing the best fare.

What to do when you arrive

Bologna welcomes you with its medieval centre full of towers, the Basilica of San Petronio and legendary food culture. Bologna airport (BLQ) is 6 km from the old town, easy access by bus or taxi. Spend time at the University, Europe's oldest, and eat tagliatelle al ragù at a proper trattoria.

What to see in Bologna in 2–3 days?

The historic centre with its towers, the Basilica of San Petronio, the Sanctuary of Madonna di San Luca, local markets and, above all, the food: mortadella, tortellini and ragù are a must.
