Best time to visit

Sal Island enjoys a dry, sunny climate year-round. December to April brings warm temperatures and calm seas, ideal conditions. June to September is windier. Travel outside peak season and you'll find better deals on both flights and accommodation.

What to do when you arrive

Sal Island is famous for white-sand beaches, turquoise waters and reefs. The airport is in Espargos. You can windsurf at Ponta Preta, dive, or simply relax in Santa Maria. It's a beach and nature destination, not museums or big cities, come to unwind.
