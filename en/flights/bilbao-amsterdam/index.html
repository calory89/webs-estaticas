Best time to visit

Amsterdam is worth visiting year-round. Autumn (September to November) and spring (March to May) offer good weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winters are cold but magical, especially around the festive season. Book ahead to access lower prices.

What to do when you arrive

Amsterdam Airport (AMS) has excellent connections to the city centre by train, bus and tram. The canals, Van Gogh Museum, De Pijp neighbourhood and Jordaan are must-sees. Grab a stroopwafel and take a boat ride along the canals at sunset. The city is very cyclist-friendly: hire a bike to get around like a local.
