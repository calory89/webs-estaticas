Best time to visit Tokyo

Tokyo offers something different each season. Spring (March–May) brings the iconic cherry blossoms; autumn (September–November) is stable and pleasant. Winter (December–February) is cold but clear; summer is warm and humid. There's no guaranteed "cheap" season: booking ahead matters more than which month you choose. Pick based on climate preference.

What to do when you arrive

Tokyo and the surrounding region offer endless options: ancient temples, parks, street food, museums, modern neighbourhoods like Shinjuku. With extra time, visit Kyoto (traditional), Hiroshima or Mount Fuji. Public transport is efficient: trains, metro and buses connect everything seamlessly.
