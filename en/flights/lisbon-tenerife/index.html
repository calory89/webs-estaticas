Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €138 per person return. The median stands at €143, with variation up to €153 depending on dates. The more advance notice you give, the better price you'll get. Low-cost fares like Ryanair reward bookings made 6-8 weeks ahead. Check daily if you're travelling on short notice, as prices often drop last-minute on routes with moderate demand.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with one stopover. Direct flights are not available. Bear in mind that a stopover adds 2-4 hours to total travel time compared to a direct flight, but the saving on fare makes up for it for most travellers. Ryanair includes carry-on baggage free (40x20x25 cm); checked luggage and seat reservations are extra.

Best time to visit

Tenerife is a destination year-round. November to March offer pleasant temperatures (20-24°C) and fewer crowds than summer. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) are ideal periods with good weather and moderate prices. Avoid July-August if you're after peace and better fares.

What to do in Tenerife

Mount Teide is the main attraction: a 3,718-metre mountain with views across the ocean. Black sand beaches in the north (Puerto de la Cruz) contrast with golden sands in the south (Playa de las Américas, Los Cristianos). Explore coastal villages, taste local cuisine and enjoy trekking in protected areas. Arrival airports are Tenerife South (TFS) or Tenerife North (TFN) depending on your flight.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for Lisbon to Tenerife?

The lowest price we've seen is €138 per person return. The average is €143. Prices vary by travel dates; book well in advance for the best fares.

Are there direct flights from Lisbon to Tenerife?

No. This route includes one stopover. Ryanair operates it that way. Total travel time is longer than a direct flight, but the price makes up for it.

Which airline flies Lisbon to Tenerife?

Ryanair is the main carrier on this route. It operates with low fares and carry-on baggage included.

What to see in Tenerife besides Mount Teide?

Teide is a must, but also explore the southern beaches (Playa de las Américas, Los Cristianos), coastal villages with Canarian tradition, trekking in protected areas and local gastronomy. Tenerife is a complete island for 4-7 days.
