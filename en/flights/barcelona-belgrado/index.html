Best time to visit

Belgrade is pleasant in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with mild temperatures and fewer crowds. Winters are cold with occasional snow; summers are hot. If you want comfortable weather and fewer tourists, aim for May or September. The city has character year-round, so it really depends on your temperature preference rather than attraction availability.

What to do when you arrive

Belgrade blends history, architecture and nightlife. Explore Kalemegdan Fortress, stroll through Dorćol neighbourhood, visit the Church of Saint Sava. Eat typical Serbian dishes at small local restaurants, try rakija and unwind at floating bars along the Danube. The city is compact and easy to explore on foot or by public transport.
