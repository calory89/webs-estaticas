Best time to visit

Agadir has mild weather year-round, but October to April is perfect: pleasant temps (18–25°C) and way less heat than summer. July and August are scorching. Travel in shoulder season (November, January–February) for fewer crowds and still-cheap flights. December and Easter can push prices up.

What to do when you arrive

Agadir Beach is your first stop: 9 km of sand with a seafront promenade and beach bars. Climb to the Kasbah for panoramic views. Explore the small old medina and eat fresh fish at harbourside restaurants. Day trip options: Tafraoute (dramatic blue-stone mountains) or Essaouira (charming coastal town). Everything's cheap, food is affordable, and locals are welcoming.

FAQ

Is it cheaper to fly midweek?

There's not enough data to confirm that any specific day is cheaper than another. What works is booking in advance and watching for Ryanair flash sales, which pop up unpredictably.

Do these prices include luggage?

With Ryanair, the base fare covers a small backpack or bag. Hand luggage and checked bags cost extra. Budget for baggage to avoid surprises.

Is this a direct flight with no changes?

Yes, completely direct. Ryanair flies DUB–AGA non-stop, so no connecting flights.

What should I see in Agadir besides the beach?

The beach is the main draw, but don't miss the Kasbah (fortress with views), the harbour and fish market, the medina, and the seafront promenade. Within 1–2 hours you'll find Tafraoute (unique mountains) and Essaouira (bohemian coastal village). All close by and affordable.
