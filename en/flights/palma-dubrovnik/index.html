Best time to visit

Dubrovnik is stunning year-round, but skip July and August if you prefer fewer crowds. Autumn (September to October) and winter (outside holidays) offer better fares and less mass tourism. Spring (April to May) combines good weather with moderate prices. Check flight availability for your preferred dates directly.

What to do when you arrive

Dubrovnik airport is 20 km from the city centre. Walk the Walls of Dubrovnik at sunset—it's unmissable. Day-trip to the Elafiti Islands, explore the Old Town, visit Lokrum cliff. If cruising, the port is central. Eat by the water and taste local Dalmatian wines. The currency is the Croatian kuna.
