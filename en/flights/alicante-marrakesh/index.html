Cheap flights Alicante to Marrakech: from €45 return

45 € returnTypical price: 56 €See flights and dates ✈️

Alicante and Marrakech are just one hour apart by air. With Ryanair you'll find tickets from €45 return; the median price sits at €56. It's a short, direct route and affordable even on a tight budget.

Price and when to book

Prices range from €45 to €59 in the sample we've checked. The median is €56 return. Ryanair releases low fares on weekdays and during low season; if you spot a price like this, don't wait, these cheap flights sell out fast. The earlier you book, the better. Check prices regularly as availability and rates change almost daily.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair is the sole operator on this route and flies direct from Alicante to Marrakech. No stopovers, no plane changes: departures at 20:25, return arrival at 14:40 (times may vary). It's the fastest and most straightforward way to reach Morocco from the Costa Blanca.

Best time to visit

Marrakech is pleasant nearly year-round, but October, November, March and April are ideal: moderate temperatures, less intense heat than summer. December to February can be cool at night in the medina or desert, though daytime is usually sunny. Skip July and August if you can't handle intense dry heat.

What to do when you arrive

Menara airport is 6 km from the city centre. Marrakech's medina is a must: Jemaa el-Fnaa square, souks, historic palaces. Majorelle Gardens are perfect for relaxing. Less than an hour away you'll find the Atlas Mountains or Sahara Desert for day trips. Local cuisine is an attraction in itself.

FAQ

What's the cheapest flight from Alicante to Marrakech?

From €45 return with Ryanair. These fares appear in low season and on weekdays. They don't last long, so if you spot this tariff, book immediately.

Does Ryanair fly direct from Alicante to Marrakech?

Yes, it's a direct flight with no stopovers. Just one hour in the air between both cities.

When is it cheapest to fly to Marrakech from Alicante?

Fares tend to be lower in low season: October, November, January, February. Weekday dates (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday) often have better prices than weekends. Book as far in advance as you can.

What's worth seeing in Marrakech in a week?

The medina with its souks and palaces, Jemaa el-Fnaa square, Majorelle Gardens. If you have time, consider a day trip to the Sahara or Atlas Mountains. With flights this cheap, you can afford to stay several days without the ticket straining your budget.
