Best time to visit

Finland is a winter destination (December to February) if you want to see the Northern Lights, and summer (June to August) for long days and good weather. Both seasons can be pricey due to demand. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) tend to be cheaper and offer pleasant conditions for exploring the city.

Things to do when you arrive

Helsinki deserves at least three days: visit the White Cathedral and the Rock Church, stroll through Kauppatori harbour, explore the Kallio district, and take the ferry to Suomenlinna Fortress. The city is compact and easy to navigate on foot or by public transport.

What is there to see in Helsinki?
