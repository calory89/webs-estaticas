Best time to visit

December is an excellent choice for Riga: the city lights up with Christmas decorations and the Christmas market in Doma Square is unmissable. The Baltic winter is cold (between -5 and 0°C), so pack warm clothes. If you prefer milder temperatures, May to September offer pleasant weather, though prices tend to be higher.

What to do when you arrive

Riga is a UNESCO World Heritage site for its Old Town and Art Nouveau buildings. Don't miss the Cathedral, Stone Bridge, and strolls along the Daugava riverbank. Eat well: Latvian cuisine is hearty and affordable. Riga International Airport (RIX) is 13 km from the centre, with direct buses reaching it in about 30 minutes.
