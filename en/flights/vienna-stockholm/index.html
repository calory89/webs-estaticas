Airline and connections

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight with no stops. You'll depart Vienna and arrive directly in Stockholm. That's a clear advantage: less hassle, shorter flight time and lower risk of accumulated delays. Remember that Ryanair is a low-cost carrier, so baggage, meals and seat upgrades come at an extra cost.

Best time to visit

Vienna and Stockholm are worth visiting year-round. Autumn (September–October) and spring (April–May) typically offer good weather without summer crowds. Winter is magical in Stockholm if you're drawn to snow and Nordic lights. Check local events and your own availability to pick the right date.

What to do when you arrive

Stockholm is served by Arlanda Airport (ARN), about 40 km from the city centre. Reach it by train, bus or taxi. Once in town, don't miss the Old Town (Gamla Stan), the Royal Palace, museums (Vasa, ABBA) and boat trips around the archipelago. The city is very walkable and has excellent public transport.
