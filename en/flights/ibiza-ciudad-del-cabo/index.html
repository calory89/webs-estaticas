Best time to visit

South Africa's spring and autumn (September to November, March to May) bring ideal weather: mild temperatures and less rain. Skip the European summer peak if you want lower fares. November is excellent: warm days and beaches at their best.

What to do in Cape Town

Table Mountain, the Victoria & Alfred Waterfront, Table Mountain National Park and Boulders Beach (home to penguins) are must-sees. Eat fresh at the waterfront market, ride the cable car at sunset and spend a day at Robben Island. The city blends nature, history and world-class food.
