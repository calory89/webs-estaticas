Best time to visit

Azores has a temperate climate year-round. Summer (June–September) brings most daylight and least rain, but higher prices. For cheap flights and decent weather, May or September are solid compromises. Winter (December–February) usually has cheaper flights and fewer tourists, though expect more rain and wind. December is brilliant for green landscapes and bargain deals.

What to do when you arrive

Azores has 9 volcanic islands. São Miguel (where Ponta Delgada airport sits) is most visited. Walk around Lagoa de Santiago, swim in thermal springs, visit Lago das Sete Cidades with its stunning views. Santa Maria has beaches; Terceira holds historic towns. Rent a small car: roads are good and it's the best way to explore. Bring a raincoat—the Atlantic climate is unpredictable.

What should I see in Azores in one week?

A week lets you cover 2–3 islands comfortably. Spend 4–5 days on São Miguel (Ponta Delgada, Lagoa de Santiago, Lago das Sete Cidades). If time remains, hop to Santa Maria (beaches) or Terceira (heritage). Inter-island ferries are cheap but slow; domestic flights are quick.
