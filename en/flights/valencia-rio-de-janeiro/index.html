Best time to travel

Rio de Janeiro has a tropical climate and good weather year-round. December to February is summer (hot and occasionally rainy). March to May and September to November are milder and drier. Choose based on your climate preference and activities: beach season in summer, hiking in autumn or spring.

What to do when you arrive

Rio offers iconic attractions: Christ the Redeemer, the Botanic Garden, Copacabana and Ipanema beaches, and the historic neighbourhood of Santa Teresa. Galeão International Airport (RIO) is 20 km from the centre; use a taxi, Uber, or bus depending on budget. Book tours or visits in advance to make the most of your time.

FAQ

Is the Ryanair flight direct from Valencia to Rio?

No, Ryanair offers this route with 2 stopovers. It's not a direct flight, but the price is attractive for a long-haul route. Decide whether you prefer more comfort (fewer stops, higher cost) or want to save money with intermediate stops.

Which airport do I fly into in Rio?

You'll arrive at Galeão International Airport (RIO), Rio's main airport. It's about 20 km from the city centre. Taxis, Uber, and buses are available for getting to your hotel. Plan your transfer before you land.

What should I see in Rio de Janeiro?

Christ the Redeemer is a must, along with Copacabana and Ipanema beaches. Don't miss the Botanic Garden, Sugarloaf Mountain, and Santa Teresa neighbourhood. If you travel in February, Carnival season is unforgettable. Book local tours for authentic and safe experiences.
