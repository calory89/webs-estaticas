Price and when to book

From €52 you'll find return tickets; most searches land around €93. The typical range goes up to €124. Ryanair usually offers rock-bottom prices well in advance, so book at least 6–8 weeks ahead if you spot a fare you like. Avoid searching late Sunday or Monday morning, when prices tend to spike.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route nonstop. Early departures (around 05:45 in the example) and morning arrivals (about 09:15 on the return). No stops, no transfers: clean direct flight from DUB to HAM in just over 2 hours. Pack liquids in your hand baggage inside a clear bag and remember that Ryanair charges for checked baggage.

Best time to travel

Hamburg shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather, fewer tourists and fares before peak demand hits. Winter is cold but magical if you like snow; summer (June–August) draws bigger crowds and ticket prices rise. Steer clear of school holidays and major holidays to find low fares.

What to do on arrival

Hamburg airport is 9 km from the city centre. Bus or S-train gets you downtown in 25–30 minutes. The city is perfect for wandering the port (Hafencity), visiting St. Michael's Church, exploring the canals and savouring local food. Hamburg blends port heritage with modernity: shops, museums, restaurants and buzzing nightlife. Ideal for a weekend break or a relaxed week.
