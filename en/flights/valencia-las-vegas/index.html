Price and when to book

The lowest price is €860 for a return ticket, whilst most flights range between €949 and €1,150. Book as far in advance as possible: international flights to the US sell out quickly and prices rise closer to departure. Compare several dates, as small changes can shift fares significantly.

Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Ryanair on a route with 3 stopovers. This means it's not a direct flight: there will be intermediate stops before reaching Las Vegas. This type of connection is typical on low-cost transatlantic routes and extends your journey, but it brings the price down considerably compared to direct flights.

Best time to travel

Las Vegas attracts tourism year-round. Prices tend to be higher during peak season (summer, Christmas, New Year). If you're looking to save, consider trips in spring or autumn, when the weather is pleasant and demand is lower. Winter also often offers good deals, though nights can be cold.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Harry Reid International Airport (LAS), about 24 km from the city centre. From there, take a bus, Uber or rent a car. In the city you have the Strip (casinos, hotels and shows), museums, fine dining, entertainment and 24-hour nightlife. The surrounding area offers desert landscapes and hiking near Red Rock Canyon or the Grand Canyon (a few hours' drive away).
