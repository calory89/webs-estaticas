Fly Paris to Mauritius from €755 return. Average fare around €819. Condor is your single option with one stop, ideal for dream beaches and pristine nature.

Price and when to book

Expect to pay from €755 for a return ticket. Median sits at €819, so waiting risks another €60–90 on price. Book 2–3 months ahead: October–November is prime for low fares if you're flying December–January. Advance booking is essential on this long-haul route.

Airline and stops

You fly Condor with 1 stop. No direct Paris–Mauritius service exists, so the intermediate stop is part of the journey. Condor is reliable on this route: departs Paris (Orly or Charles de Gaulle, check your booking) and arrives Mauritius (Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam).

Best time to travel

May–October is dry season: less rain, mild weather (22–26°C), and perfect beaches. December–January is summer but cyclone risk exists, though many visitors go anyway. July–August is peak season and pricier. Avoid November–April for stable weather.

What to do on arrival

Mauritius is paradise: white-sand beaches (Île aux Cerfs, Trou aux Biches), intact coral reef, hiking in Black River Gorges, local markets in Port-Louis, luxury resorts. Rent a car or book organised tours from your hotel. The country is safe and cosmopolitan, perfect for a 7–10 day escape.

FAQ

Can I find cheaper flights Paris to Mauritius?

Minimum fare now is €755 return with Condor. To lower cost, book 2–3 months ahead and skip school holidays. Travelling May–June or September tends to be cheaper than December–January.

How many stops does Condor's flight have?

Condor has 1 stop on Paris–Mauritius. No direct flight from Paris exists, so the stop is mandatory. Total journey time is roughly 14–16 hours.

Which airline flies Paris to Mauritius?

Condor operates this route. Departure around 11:35 (sample outbound) and arrival around 08:40 next day (sample return) are typical timings.

What's worth seeing and doing in Mauritius in one week?

Beaches like Trou aux Biches and Île aux Cerfs, hiking in Black River Gorges, wildlife at Casela World Park, markets in Port-Louis, reef diving, and spa resorts. Mauritius blends beach, nature, and culture in one compact, safe destination.
