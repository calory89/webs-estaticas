Best time to visit Fez

Fez is pleasant year-round, though the mild months (October to May) offer ideal weather for wandering the medina without intense heat. Skip peak summer if you can't handle extreme temperatures. December and January are dry and cool, perfect for exploring the city at a relaxed pace.

What to do when you arrive

Fez's medina is a UNESCO World Heritage site and the city's heart: a maze of souks, palaces, and mosques. Don't miss Bou Inania Madrasa, the Royal Palace, or the traditional leather tanneries. Saïss Airport is 15 km from the centre; take a taxi or arrange a private transfer. Spend 2–3 days in the medina and surroundings to soak it in properly.
