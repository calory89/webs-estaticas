Best time to visit

Salzburg is especially attractive in winter (December to February) for its Christmas markets and snowy Alpine backdrop. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Summer is popular too, though prices tend to rise. Travel mid-week when possible for better fares.

What to do when you arrive

Salzburg, Mozart's birthplace, welcomes you with Hohensalzburg Fortress overlooking the city, the charming Getreidegasse and the UNESCO-listed old town. Stroll along the Salzach river, visit the Cathedral and baroque palaces. During Christmas, the markets are unmissable. The Alps are just 30 minutes by train for hiking or skiing depending on season.

Hohensalzburg Fortress, the old town (UNESCO World Heritage), the Cathedral, Getreidegasse and Mirabell Palace are must-sees. In winter, the Christmas markets are outstanding. For nature lovers, the Alps are within easy reach.
