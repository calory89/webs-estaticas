Best time to travel

Cancún is a tropical destination year-round. Hurricane season in the region runs June to November, so if you want safer weather, aim for December to May. Bear in mind those months are pricier and busier. Outside school holidays (summer, Christmas, Easter), you'll find more competitive rates.

What to do in Cancún and nearby

Cancún isn't just beaches: you have cenotes (natural freshwater pools) within reach, Mayan ruins at Tulúm and Chichén Itzá, parks like Xcel Há and Xplor, plus the Riviera Maya lined with resorts. Cancún International Airport is well served with transport to the hotel zone (25 km away). Pack strong sunscreen: Caribbean sun is intense.
