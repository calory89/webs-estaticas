The Lisbon-Porto route costs from €95 return with TAP Portugal, completely direct. It's one of the shortest and most affordable journeys on the Iberian Peninsula: just 300 km between two major Portuguese cities.

Price and when to book

We found this route at €95 return across the entire period analysed. Don't wait until the last minute: book as early as possible for the best fares. With multiple daily departures, you have flexibility to choose your dates.

Airline and stopovers

TAP Portugal operates this route as a direct flight. No stopovers, no plane changes. Regular departures throughout the day, with times from morning to afternoon. It's the most comfortable and fastest way to connect the two cities.

Best time to travel

Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: mild weather, fewer crowds and stable fares. Avoid July and August if you're looking to save money and enjoy peace and quiet. December can offer good prices if cold weather doesn't bother you.

What to do when you arrive in Porto

Explore Ribeira, the historic centre by the Douro River. Taste Porto wine in the cellars of Vila Nova de Gaia, across the river. Climb the Clérigos Tower, walk through Livraria Lello (one of Europe's oldest bookstores) and dine on riverside terraces. Everything is within 15 minutes' walk from the airport.
