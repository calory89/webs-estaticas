Ryanair connects Manchester with Tenerife at prices that rarely drop below €48 return. The median sits at €58, so you have room to find good deals if you're flexible with dates.

Price and when to book

Prices we've tracked range from €48 to €67 for return trips. Book early: fares tend to climb as your departure date approaches. If your trip isn't urgent, purchase 4–6 weeks in advance. Tuesdays and Wednesdays often have cheaper options than weekends.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route direct, no stops. The flight departs Manchester (MAN) and arrives directly at Tenerife Norte (TCI). Check what's included in your ticket: Ryanair charges extra for checked baggage and cabin bags depending on your fare type.

Best time to travel

Tenerife works year-round, but winter months (December to February) draw more British visitors escaping the cold. Want better prices and fewer crowds? Try May, June or September. Skip UK school holidays if you prefer to avoid peak demand.

What to do in Tenerife

Mount Teide is unmissable: Spain's highest mountain and a Unesco World Heritage site. Beaches in North Tenerife (Playa de Las Teresitas) and South (Playa del Duomo, El Médano) deserve a day each. Explore La Laguna's old town or hike volcanic trails in the National Park. Local food and Santa Cruz markets round out the visit.

Is this a direct flight Manchester to Tenerife?

Yes, Ryanair flies direct with no stops between Manchester (MAN) and Tenerife (TCI).

Tenerife Norte (TCI). It's the closest airport to the capital, Santa Cruz. From there, a bus or hire car gets you around the island in 30–45 minutes.
