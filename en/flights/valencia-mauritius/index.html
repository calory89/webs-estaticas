Best time to visit

Mauritius has a tropical climate. Avoid cyclone season (January to March). The ideal months are May to October (austral winter), with mild temperatures and less rainfall. High season months tend to have higher fares. April or November offer a good balance between weather and price.

What to do when you arrive

Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport is 60 km from Port Louis. Mauritius is famous for white-sand beaches, coral reefs perfect for diving, the Pamplemousses Botanical Garden, and Tamarin Falls. The island also offers multicultural cuisine and trips to nearby Rodrigues and Diego García islands.
