Best time to visit

Salzburg shines year-round: autumn brings Alpine colours, winter offers Christmas markets (especially in December), spring awakens the gardens, summer draws crowds but gives long days. Book with lead time to lock in decent fares regardless of season.

What to do on arrival

Salzburg offers Hohensalzburg fortress, the baroque cathedral and Mirabell gardens. The city is compact and walkable. Taste local Mozartkugel, explore the old town and climb into the Alps if time allows. Vienna is just 2 hours by train if you want to extend your trip.
