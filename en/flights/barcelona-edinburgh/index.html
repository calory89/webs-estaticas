Price and when to book

Fares range around €56, €88 and €99 for return trips. The lowest prices are usually available if you book 2–3 months ahead. Check regularly: prices change by occupancy and dates. Avoid peak season (December and August) if you want rock-bottom fares.

Airline and direct flights

Ryanair operates this route with no stops (direct flight). Outbound departs Barcelona (BCN) at 21:15 and arrives Edinburgh (EDI); return takes off at 17:00. This saves time and avoids awkward connections. Check exact flight dates on Ryanair's website.

Best time to visit

Edinburgh is worth visiting year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter (November–February) is cold but magical, especially over holidays. Book well ahead regardless of season to secure the best rates.

What to do when you arrive

Visit Edinburgh Castle, walk the Royal Mile, explore Holyrood Palace and historic quarters. Pentland Hills are great for hiking. Try Scottish whisky and local food, soak in medieval architecture. The airport is 9 km from the city centre with good public transport links.
