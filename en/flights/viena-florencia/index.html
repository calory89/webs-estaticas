Price and when to book

Prices range from €164 to €200 based on our tracked samples. Vienna-Florence is a route where advance booking pays off: the earlier you reserve, the more options you'll find at the lower end. Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with one stopover—it's not a direct flight. Our data shows the outbound leg includes an intermediate stop. Whilst this adds one or two hours to your journey, the savings versus direct alternatives make it worth it. The Irish low-cost carrier dominates this Central European connection.

Best time to visit

Florence shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather, fewer museum queues, and perfect light for walking the streets. Avoid July–August if you dislike mass tourism. Winter is quieter but some museums cut opening hours. Regarding prices, budget fare availability depends more on your flexibility than the season.

What to do when you arrive

Florence is unmissable: the Duomo Cathedral, Uffizi Gallery, Ponte Vecchio, Palazzo Pitti. Spend at least three full days to avoid rushing. The airport (Peretola) sits 4 km northwest of the city; a bus or taxi gets you to the centre in 20–30 minutes. Use your first day to settle in, then wander unhurried through Santo Spirito and San Frediano neighbourhoods, away from the crowds.
