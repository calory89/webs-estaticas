Best time to visit

Amsterdam is lovely year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer the best weather with mild temperatures and less rain. Avoid peak summer (July–August) if you're after lower fares. Winter (December–February) is colder and darker, though less crowded.

What to do when you arrive

Amsterdam awaits with iconic canals, world-class museums (Rijksmuseum, Van Gogh Museum), and a thriving bike culture. Schiphol Airport is 9 km from the city centre with easy train access. Explore neighbourhoods like De Pijp, take a houseboat tour, sample Dutch cheese, and relax in the city's laid-back cafés.
