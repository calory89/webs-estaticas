Price and when to book

The lowest recorded price is €377 return, with a median of €423. Prices vary between €377 and €487 depending on travel dates. For this route, book as early as possible: the sooner you purchase, the better the fare. Avoid last-minute changes, which push prices up.

Airline and stops

Wizz Air Malta operates this route with 2 stops on the outbound journey. Although not direct, this configuration gives you access to competitive pricing. Outbound departs at 20:15 and return arrives in Zaragoza at 03:25 (early morning).

Best time to visit

Dubai is a winter destination: November to March offers ideal climate and prices. Summer is scorching (45°C+) and many attractions close partially. For deals, avoid Spanish school holidays and bank holidays.

What to do on arrival

Dubai ranges from Jumeirah beaches to desert and malls like the Mall of the Emirates. The DXB airport is well connected to the city centre. Plan museums, souks, beaches and desert safaris based on your stay length. Cost of living is high: budget for local transport and food accordingly.
