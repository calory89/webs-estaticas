Tickets start from €197 return, with sample prices of €197, €250 and €264. The median is €250, so deals below that mark are solid finds. As with nature-focused destinations, booking early works: seats fill fast in high season and prices climb fast.

Airline and stops

Wizz Air Malta operates this route with one stop on the outbound leg. Not a direct flight, so it adds travel time, but the price makes up for it. Wizz Air runs a no-frills model: limited baggage on base fares, but you can upgrade as needed.

Best time to visit

The Azores have Atlantic weather: rain in winter, but mild temperatures year-round. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) offer the best balance of climate and fewer crowds. Summer (June–August) draws more visitors and higher fares. For nature without the rush, aim for April–May or September–October.

What to do when you arrive

The Azores are a hiking, whale-watching and volcanic landscape destination. São Miguel island (where you land in Ponta Delgada) holds most attractions: Lake Santiago da Lagoa, Sete Cidades caldera and Capelinhos volcano. You don't need a car: local buses and tours cover everything, fares around €2 for a single ticket.

FAQ

From €197 return. The median is €250, so expect to pay between €200–€260 if you book ahead.

Is this a direct flight?

No, there's one stop on the outbound leg. The return follows the same pattern. It adds roughly 2–3 hours to your total travel, but the cost saving is substantial.

Is Wizz Air Malta reliable on this route?

Wizz Air is an established budget carrier with years in the market. On this route they're competent, but operate as a basic service: no meals or baggage included. Check their change and delay policy before you buy.

What else is there to see in the Azores?

Ponta Delgada has an old town with Portuguese colonial architecture. Traditional watermills, hot springs and the island's tea plantations are unique draws. Whale-watching and volcano tours are easy to book once you land.
