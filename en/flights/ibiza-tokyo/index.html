Best time to travel

Tokyo is pleasant in autumn (September to November) and winter (December to February), with moderate temperatures. Avoid summer if you don't tolerate humidity and heat. Book 2-3 months in advance to secure better availability and prices on this long-haul route.

What to do when you arrive

Tokyo offers ancient temples like Senso-ji, vibrant neighbourhoods like Shibuya and Shinjuku, world-class gastronomy and endless shopping. The international airport (Narita or Haneda) is well connected by public transport to the city. Spend at least 4-5 days to enjoy the capital without rushing.
