Best time to visit

Puerto Rico has tropical weather year-round. Hurricane season runs June to November, peaking August to October. Travel December to April for better weather and less rain, though prices rise then. Off-peak travel (May to July, outside holidays) offers better fares.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport (SJU), about 9 km from San Juan. Explore Old San Juan's colonial architecture, Fort El Morro, beaches, and Caribbean food. Day trips to Culebra, Vieques, or El Yunque National Rainforest are easy. Hire a car or use local transport to get around.
