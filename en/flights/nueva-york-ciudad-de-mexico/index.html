Best time to travel

Mexico City is a destination year-round. Avoid peak seasons (Christmas, Easter, July-August) if you're looking for better prices. May to October usually offer more competitive fares, though there's a higher chance of rain. November, December, January and February offer stable, pleasant weather.

What to do when you arrive

Mexico City is rich in history, art and gastronomy. Visit the Historic Centre, the Metropolitan Cathedral and the Templo Mayor. The Museum of Anthropology is essential. Explore the neighbourhoods of Coyoacán and San Ángel. Eat at local markets and try street food. Benito Juárez airport is about 5 km from the centre, with easy access by metro, taxi or bus.
