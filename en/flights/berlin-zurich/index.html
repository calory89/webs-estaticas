Best time to visit Zurich

Zurich is lovely year-round. Winter (December to February) brings fewer tourists and potentially lower prices, plus festive events. Spring and autumn offer ideal weather for exploring the city and surroundings. Summer is warmer with more crowds and higher fares. Avoid public holidays and peak dates if you're after a bargain.

What to do in Zurich

Zurich is compact and walkable. Explore the Altstadt (old town) with its medieval lanes, visit Grossmünster Cathedral, and relax by the lakeside. Museums cover every interest. Swiss trains connect Zurich to the Alps quickly: Interlaken, Jungfrau, and Matterhorn are within day-trip distance.
