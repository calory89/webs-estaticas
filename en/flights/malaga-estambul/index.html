Best time to visit

December is a great time to visit Istanbul: cool weather, fewer crowds than summer, and the city glows with its festive markets. Avoid July and August if you prefer moderate temperatures and less congestion at major monuments.

What to do when you arrive

Istanbul deserves at least 4–5 days. Don't miss the Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia, Topkapi Palace, the Grand Bazaar, and a Bosphorus boat tour. Istanbul Airport (IST) connects well to the city centre via metro, bus, and taxi. Eat in Sultanahmet or Galata for authentic Turkish cuisine.
