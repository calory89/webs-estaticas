Best time to visit

Edinburgh shines in summer (June to August) with long days and mild weather, perfect for exploring the city and surroundings. Autumn (September to October) is also excellent: fewer crowds and spectacular scenery. What to do in Edinburgh

Scotland's capital is essential: Edinburgh Castle dominates the skyline, the Royal Mile is the historic heart, and Leith (the port district) buzzes with modern energy. Climb Arthur's Seat for 360° views, explore free museums, and soak up authentic pub culture. All of Scotland lies within reach: trains run regularly to Glasgow, the Highlands, and the lochs from here.
