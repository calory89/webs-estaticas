Best time to visit Bali

November is a smart choice: the rainy season has just ended (October-April) and prices start climbing towards Christmas. Temperatures are pleasant (28-30°C) and there's less tourism than July-August. To save money, avoid European school holidays and bank holidays.

What to do when you arrive in Bali

You'll land at Denpasar international airport (DPS), 13 km from Seminyak beach. Taxis, ride-sharing and buses are available. Luxury beaches in Seminyak and Uluwatu, temples like Tanah Lot, rice paddies in Ubud, or Mount Amed are must-sees. Bali offers everything from total relaxation to adventure, depending on your pace.
