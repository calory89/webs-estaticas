Fly Barcelona to San Francisco from €547 return with SAS. Average fare sits at €642, so there's room to hunt for deals if you're flexible with dates. December is decent for this route if you don't mind peak season pricing.

Price and when to book

Fares on this route range from €547 to €677 based on our sample. The median is €642, so if you spot anything below that, it's a solid opportunity. Book as far ahead as possible: Barcelona to San Francisco is a long transatlantic route and prices climb closer to departure. If your travel dates are flexible, try shifting by a day or two—sometimes a 2-3 day change saves €50-100.

Airline and stopovers

SAS (Scandinavian Airlines) runs this route with 1 stopover—it's not a direct flight. Your layover will likely be in Stockholm or another SAS hub. Total travel time will be around 13-15 hours gate to gate, depending on connection. It's longer than direct but the price reflects that trade-off.

Best time to visit

San Francisco works year-round, but December brings mild weather (12-17°C) and holiday crowds, pushing up prices and hotel rates. For better value, consider January, February, or September-October when tourism dips. Remember this is always an expensive route because it's transatlantic: the €642 average already shows it's a decent fare within its usual range, not a seasonal bargain.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at San Francisco International Airport (SFO), about 25 km from downtown. BART (regional train) gets you to the city centre in 30 minutes for under €15. Don't miss the Golden Gate, Alcatraz, Chinatown, and the Mission District for food. December offers comfortable weather without extreme heat, though afternoons get foggy. Rent a bike or use the historic cable cars to move around the city.

FAQ

Are there direct flights Barcelona to San Francisco?

Not in this search. SAS operates with 1 stopover, typically in Stockholm. Direct flights from Barcelona to San Francisco do exist but are pricier or don't match your travel dates.

Why does it cost over €500 for a flight with a stopover?

Because it's a long intercontinental transatlantic route with steady demand. Europe to US West Coast flights rarely dip below €400-500 return. SAS is a quality airline but not the budget carrier—however, their pricing remains competitive.

Is it worth booking 2 months in advance?

Yes. Transatlantic flights are typically booked 2-3 months ahead for best prices. If your trip is late 2026, book now if the dates work. Waiting until the last minute almost always costs more.

What to see in San Francisco besides the Golden Gate?

Alcatraz (book ahead), Chinatown, the Painted Ladies Victorian houses, Mission District for street art and restaurants, Twin Peaks for panoramic views, and the Embarcadero waterfront. With extra days, explore Marin County or Santa Cruz for beaches and quieter vibes.
