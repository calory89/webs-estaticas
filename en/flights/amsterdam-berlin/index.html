Fly direct from Amsterdam to Berlin with KLM from €167 return. Median prices hover around €169, so you have solid options if you book soon. A short, hassle-free route to Germany's capital without stops.

Price and when to book

Best fares on this route start from €167 return. We've spotted prices between €167 and €169, with a median of €169. The earlier you book, the better the deal generally works out. Check regularly now: November trips tend to have good availability. Book direct with KLM for full control over changes and cancellations.

Airline and direct flights

KLM Royal Dutch Airlines operates this route nonstop. A direct flight of just over 1 hour saves you time and hassle. Hand luggage and hold baggage are typically included in the base fare, confirm when you book. KLM is a full-service carrier with solid punctuality and decent in-flight standards.

Best time to visit

Amsterdam and Berlin are year-round destinations. Autumn (September to November) offers mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is charming if you like festive markets, but expect cold snaps. Spring (April–May) delivers good weather and moderate prices. August tends to be pricier due to peak tourism.

What to do when you arrive in Berlin

Berlin is history, art, and nightlife rolled into one. Visit the Brandenburg Gate, the Wall, world-class museums like the Pergamon, and edgy neighbourhoods such as Kreuzberg. Berlin airport is well connected by U-Bahn and bus to the city centre (30–45 minutes). Book museums ahead in peak season. Food and public transport are very affordable.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for Amsterdam to Berlin flights?

From €167 return with KLM. Prices we've tracked range €167–€169. Book early to lock in the best rate.

Is there a direct flight from Amsterdam to Berlin?

Yes, KLM flies nonstop. Just over 1 hour in the air, no stops, no faffing about. Much smoother than connecting itineraries.

Which airline flies Amsterdam to Berlin?

KLM Royal Dutch Airlines is the main operator. Strong reputation, full-service network carrier, usually on time.

What should I see in Berlin in 3–4 days?

Brandenburg Gate, Berlin Wall, Museum Island, neighbourhoods like Charlottenburg, Kreuzberg and Prenzlauer Berg. Easy to navigate on foot or public transport. Eat döner and local beer.
