Best time to visit

Fort Lauderdale enjoys warm weather year-round. June to October is warm and humid (hurricane season). November to April is dry and pleasant—the best window. July and August are wettest. Book early if you're travelling during school holidays or European bank holidays, when fares spike.

What to do when you arrive

Fort Lauderdale is a beach haven with a laid-back marine vibe. Explore Las Olas Boulevard for shops and dining, relax on wide sandy beaches, or take a waterway cruise through mangroves and canals. Miami and its museums are minutes away. The airport (FLL) is 30 km from town; bus, taxi or rental car will get you there. It's an ideal base to unwind or launch trips to the Florida Keys.
