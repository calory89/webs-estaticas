Best time to visit

Salzburg is stunning in winter with festive charm and nearby skiing, though January remains peak season. Spring and autumn offer mild weather and fewer crowds. Summer is perfect for exploring the Alps region without cold, though prices can climb. Choose based on your priorities: winter for snow, spring or autumn for peaceful nature and comfortable conditions.

What to do in Salzburg

Salzburg is Mozart's birthplace and your gateway to the Austrian Alps. The old town with medieval streets and Hohensalzburg fortress is essential. The region's lakes are superb for hiking. If you love 'The Sound of Music', themed tours cover film locations. Just 30 minutes away is Lake Königssee in Bavaria, Germany, perfect for day trips.
