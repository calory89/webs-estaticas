Best time to visit

Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) are ideal: pleasant weather, fewer crowds than summer, and lower fares than July–August. Avoid December if you're chasing rock-bottom prices, though it's high season. Book as far in advance as you can.

What to do in Rome

Don't miss the Colosseum, Roman Forum, St Peter's Basilica and the Sistine Chapel. Wander through the piazzas (Navona, Trevi), sample pasta and gelato, and spend time exploring Trastevere and the Historic Centre. An absolute gem of a city for any length of stay.
