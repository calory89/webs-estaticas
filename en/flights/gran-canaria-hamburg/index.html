Best time to visit

Hamburg shines in spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October): mild weather and less rain than winter. Summer is peak season and prices rise. Winter is cold and grey, but flights may be cheaper if you avoid Christmas and New Year.

You'll land at Hamburg-Fuhlsbüttel Airport (HAM), 9 km from the city centre. The city offers the canals of Little Venice, the historic port, world-class museums and German cuisine. Try a Labskaus or Currywurst. Perfect for 3-4 days.
