The lowest price is €941, but note that the median sits at €1,009 (what most tickets actually cost). Prices reach €1,425 in the pricier samples. As with any intercontinental route, book at least 6–8 weeks in advance. Daily searches in the final month may save you money, but don't wait until the last minute on this connection.

Airline and stops

Scoot operates this route with 2 stops on the outbound leg. It's not direct, but Scoot keeps fares competitive through its low-cost model. Stops are usually technical or in Asian hubs, which extends travel time but delivers that tight price. Return flights also have stops. Always check connection times when booking.

Best time to travel

Malta has good weather almost year-round. May to October bring high temperatures and maximum sun. November to March are milder with occasional rain. Shoulder season (March–April and October–November) offers good climate balance and fewer crowds. Any time works if price is your main factor.

What to do on arrival

Malta is small but packed: Valletta (capital) blends medieval architecture with Mediterranean views. Don't miss Mdina, the walled historic city, or Ħaġar Qim temples. Popeye Village is quirky fun. Mellieha and Golden Bay beaches are excellent. The airport is 5 km from the centre; public bus, taxi, or car rental are your main options.

FAQ

Is it a direct flight from Sydney to Malta?

No, it has 2 stops (outbound). Scoot doesn't operate direct on this route, which is why the fares are so low.

How many hours does the total flight take?

I don't have the exact duration, but this is a long-haul intercontinental route with connections. Expect 20–26 hours total travel time (including layovers).

Are there flights cheaper than €941?

Not in the available samples. €941 is the lowest recorded price. Though it's always possible a deal might pop up if you search daily over several weeks.

What to see in Malta in 5 days?

Spend 1–2 days in Valletta and Mdina, 1 day at the beaches (Mellieha), 1 day at ancient temples (Ħaġar Qim), and 1 free day exploring coastal villages like Marsalforn or beach time.
