Fly from Lisbon to London with Ryanair from €59 (return). Median fares sit around €82, so there's room to spot a bargain. At this price, a getaway to the British capital won't drain your wallet.

Price and when to book

Flights start from €59 return. We've seen sample prices at €59, €82 and €97. We checked these on 22 October, so timing matters: book as early as possible. Set up price alerts on travel sites to catch a good deal on this route.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair is your carrier on this route and the best part is they fly non-stop Lisbon-London. No stopovers, no wasted time changing planes. Bear in mind that low-cost airlines have strict baggage rules, so check what's included before you pack.

Best time to visit

December is a solid option (we have a flight example on 7 December). This season brings Christmas markets, ice skating rinks and festive vibes across London. Want cheaper fares? Try travelling outside school holidays or public holidays. Weekday flights tend to be cheaper than weekends.

What to do when you arrive

London has endless options: Big Ben, Tower Bridge, free museums (British Museum, National Gallery), Thames walks and neighbourhoods like Shoreditch and Camden. Eat at Borough Market, ride the London Eye if budget allows, or stroll through Hyde Park. Three days (like the example: 7 to 10 December) is enough for the essentials.

FAQ

How much does a Lisbon to London flight cost?

From €59 return. Median price is €82. Fares vary by date and booking time, so compare and set up alerts.

Which airline flies Lisbon-London?

Ryanair operates this route with direct flights.

It's direct. Ryanair gets you from Lisbon to London without stops.

What to see in London?

Big Ben, Tower Bridge, Westminster Abbey, British Museum, National Gallery, Hyde Park, the Thames, Camden Market, Shoreditch and Borough Market. Many museums are free. December brings Christmas markets and ice rinks.
