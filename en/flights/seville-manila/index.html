Fly from Seville to Manila from €768 return. The median price hovers around €792, so you have good options to find a bargain. With 2 stops and easyJet, it's an accessible route if you prioritise price.

Price and when to book

The cheapest flight we've found is €768 return, whilst most tickets hover around €792. Prices climb if you wait: we've recorded fares up to €1,754. Book as far in advance as possible. For such a long route, booking 2-3 months ahead gives you more options and better rates. Check every Tuesday or Wednesday, when airlines typically refresh their offers.

Airline and stops

easyJet is your carrier on this route. Expect 2 stops on your outbound journey, so it's not direct. With two intermediate stops, the journey will be long, but the price makes up for it if you're flexible. Pack a power bank and entertainment; low-cost airlines rarely offer charging ports or seatback screens.

Best time to visit

Manila has a tropical climate. The dry season (November to April) is ideal: less rain and pleasant temperatures. January and February see peak demand, but offer the best weather. If you travel in low season (May to October), you'll find lower fares, though it rains more. The route is viable year-round if you choose your dates wisely.

What to do when you arrive

Ninoy Aquino Airport (MNL) is 7 km from Manila's centre. Get there by taxi, Grab or bus. Visit Intramuros, the colonial walled city, Rizal Park and local markets like Divisoria. With extra days, explore Boracay, Palawan or the Cordillera islands. The local currency is the Philippine peso; the airport has exchange booths.
