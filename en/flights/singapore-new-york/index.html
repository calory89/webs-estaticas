Best time to visit

New York is attractive year-round, but each season has its charm. Autumn (September to November) and spring (March to May) offer pleasant weather and less rain. Winter is cold but perfect for snow and holiday lights. Summer is hot and humid. Check availability for your preferred dates; prices fluctuate with the season.

What to do in New York

Times Square, Central Park, the Statue of Liberty, the Empire State Building and Brooklyn Bridge are must-sees. The museums (MoMA, Natural History) are world-class. Walk through SoHo, Brooklyn Heights and the High Line. Eat at food halls and try local pizza and bagels. Best night views from the Top of the Rock or One World Observatory. Spend at least 5-7 days to enjoy without rushing.
