Best time to visit

December is ideal for Punta Cana: dry weather, less rain, and pleasant temperatures (25–28 °C). Skip September and October if possible, peak hurricane season. Prices spike during peak periods (Christmas, Easter), so target mid-month or off-holiday dates for better value.

What to do when you arrive

Punta Cana is all about beaches: coral reefs for diving, white sand, and all-inclusive resorts. Punta Cana airport (PUJ) sits 45 minutes from major resorts. Don't miss Playa Blanca, the local cenotes, or fishing villages. For nature, Los Haitises National Park is worth the trip.
