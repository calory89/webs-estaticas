Best time to visit

Prague is worth visiting year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer ideal weather without summer crowds or harsh winter cold. January can appeal to travellers seeking fewer tourists and a festive atmosphere, though temperatures are low. Avoid August if you prefer to escape peak tourism.

What to do when you arrive

Prague awaits with Prague Castle, Charles Bridge, Old Town Square and the Astronomical Clock. Wander the Jewish Quarter, enjoy a pint of local beer in any traditional pub, and climb the Town Hall Tower for panoramic views. The Moldau River is perfect for an evening stroll. One or two nights cover the essentials; with longer stays, explore the countryside and nearby towns.
