Best time to visit

Zurich appeals year-round. Winter (Nov–Feb) brings Christmas markets, nearby ski slopes and snowy views. Spring (Mar–May) and autumn (Sep–Oct) are ideal for city walks and lakeside strolls without crowds. Summer (Jun–Aug) offers warmth but more tourists. At these fares, any season works well.

What to do when you arrive

Zurich greets you with a historic centre overlooking the lake, shopping on Bahnhofstrasse, world-class art and design museums, and flawless public transport. The Alps are 90 minutes away; the charming town of Winterthur is 15 minutes by train. Try local fondue, explore the old town and experience Swiss precision everywhere.
