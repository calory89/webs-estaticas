Airline and stops

Best time to visit

Iceland appeals to visitors year-round. Winter (November to February) offers northern lights, but it's peak season pricing and bitterly cold. Spring and autumn bring moderate fares. Summer is prime tourist season with clear skies and mild temperatures, yet pricier flights. Think about your budget and what you want to see.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Keflavík, Iceland's main international airport. Reykjavik is about 50 minutes away. The Golden Circle (waterfalls, geysers, tectonic rifts), glaciers, waterfalls and geothermal pools are must-sees. Explore the south coast and spot marine life. Car hire gives you flexibility to roam independently.
