Best time to visit

Mykonos is a beach and leisure destination year-round, though summer (June to September) is peak season. For better prices and fewer crowds, consider spring (April–May) or autumn (September–October). The dates shown are late year, when good deals often emerge.

What to do when you arrive

Mykonos is known for beaches, nightlife and whitewashed villages with shops. The international airport (JMK) is 2 km from town centre. You can take a bus, taxi or hire a car. Psarou, Ornos and Kalafatis beaches are must-sees; the old port is perfect for a stroll and dinner.
