Fly from Alicante to Fort Lauderdale from €737 return. Prices average around €753, so you have a real chance to grab a bargain if you book in advance. Royal Air Maroc is your main option on this route.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we've spotted is €737, but most travellers pay around €753. Some fares climb to €832. Book early and you'll lock in better rates; wait and prices will rise. This route has no magic discount day or month—what works is booking 2–3 months ahead.

Airlines and stopovers

Royal Air Maroc operates this route with 2 stopovers on the outbound flight. It's not direct, so expect intermediate stops, but that's standard for Europe–Florida connections. Your return will also include stopovers. Two stops mean a longer journey, but a cheaper ticket than direct options.

Best time to travel

Fort Lauderdale is a year-round destination, though December–February is peak season in Florida (perfect weather, but pricey and crowded). If you want fewer tourists, try May–June or September–October instead. But remember: prices don't drop by themselves—booking early is the real key, whatever the season.

What to do in Fort Lauderdale

Sandy beaches, a yacht-filled port, art galleries and shopping on Las Olas Boulevard. The international airport (FLL) is 30 minutes from downtown. From there, you can reach Miami (half an hour by car), the Everglades or the Florida Keys. Make the most of being on Florida's Atlantic coast.
