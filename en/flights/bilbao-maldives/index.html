Airline and stopovers

You'll travel with Wizz Air Malta. The outbound flight includes 2 stops, so it's not a direct journey. This multiple connection format is what makes these prices so competitive. Allow enough time at each stop and always check what baggage is included in your fare.

Best time to visit

The Maldives has a tropical climate with two monsoon seasons. The best time is November to April, when there's less rain and the sea is calmer. These months also coincide with peak tourism season. If you want to avoid crowds and rain, aim for December, January or February. Keep in mind these are the most expensive months for this route.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Velana International Airport (MLE), the main airport in the Maldives. From there, speedboat or seaplane connections take you to your resort. The Maldives offers diving, snorkelling, white sand beaches, spas and luxury resorts. You can also visit Malé, the capital, to experience local culture. Many resorts include water activities in the price.
