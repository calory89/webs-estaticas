Best time to travel

Porto is pleasant nearly year-round. Autumn (September–October) and spring (April–May) offer good weather without crowds. Summer is busier but pricier. Winter is damper, though prices drop.

What to do in Porto

Explore Ribeira, the historic centre by the Douro River. Climb the Clérigos Tower, taste Porto wine in Vila Nova de Gaia cellars, and stroll through Livraria Lello bookshop. The airport is 10 km from the centre with direct metro access.
