Best time to visit Nairobi

Nairobi welcomes visitors year-round, but the dry seasons (January-February and July-October) are ideal for safaris and outdoor activities. Temperatures are mild during these periods. Avoid the rainy seasons if you plan to explore nearby reserves. December can be a great choice, with less rain and pleasant temperatures.

What to do when you arrive

Kenyatta International Airport will receive your flight. From there, you have access to Masai Mara safaris, Amboseli National Park and Tsavo National Reserve. In the city, visit the Giraffe Centre, the National Museum or Karen Blixen Market. For getting around, use recommended taxis or pre-booked transport services. Nairobi is your gateway to African wildlife.
