Best time to visit Kenya

Kenya is a year-round destination. Dry season (June to October and January to February) offers the best weather and prime safari viewing. Green season (March to May and November to December) brings rain but lush landscapes and fewer tourists. Choose based on your priorities: safari or quieter cultural exploration. Most international flights arrive at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport near Nairobi.

What to do in Nairobi and beyond

Nairobi is your gateway to Kenya's legendary safaris. From the capital, access Nairobi National Park, Amboseli National Park, and the Masai Mara National Reserve. The city has museums, local markets, and vibrant urban life. Many travellers use Nairobi as a hub to reach Kenya's top wildlife destinations. The city has mild weather; pack a light jacket for mornings and evenings.
