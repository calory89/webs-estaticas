Best time to visit Oslo

Norway shines in winter if you're after northern lights (December to February), though it's bitterly cold. Spring and summer (May to August) bring long days, lush scenery and pleasant temperatures. Autumn (September to October) is stunning with fewer crowds. Plan based on your priorities: snow and night sky or hiking under the midnight sun.

What to do in Oslo and beyond

Oslo is compact and walkable. Visit the Munch Museum, the City Hall with fjord views, and explore nearby fjords by train or cruise. Grünerløkka district has indie shops and cafés. The Nordic Museums are must-sees. With extra time, catch the train to Bergen or visit Lillehammer. Food is pricey: eat at local spots to save money.

Is it safe to travel to Oslo? What should I pack?

Oslo is one of Europe's safest cities. Pack warm clothes (especially in winter), sunscreen in summer and note that living costs are high, budget well for food and activities. Carry euros or a card, though NOK (Norwegian krone) is the official currency.
