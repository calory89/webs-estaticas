Best time to travel

Mexico City is accessible year-round. The dry season (November to March) offers pleasant weather, whilst June to October brings rain but fewer tourists and potentially lower fares. Avoid December–January if hunting for cheaper prices, as it coincides with peak demand periods.

What to do when you arrive

Mexico City blends pre-Hispanic ruins, colonial architecture and modern life. Don't miss the Zócalo, Teotihuacán, the National Museum of Anthropology, Coyoacán and Frida Kahlo's House. Benito Juárez is the main airport with direct transport to the city centre. Sample local food: tacos, mole and traditional markets are essential.
