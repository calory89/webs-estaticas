Fly Amsterdam to Brussels for €162 return with Ryanair. The median price sits at €173, so booking well ahead secures good fares. This route of just over 200 km is perfect for a weekend trip.

Price and when to book

Flights start from €162 return. Our sample prices range from €162 to €181, with a median of €173. To get these fares, book 6-8 weeks in advance. Ryanair adjusts prices by demand, so if you spot something around €162–170, don't delay. Tuesdays and Wednesdays are usually cheaper than Fridays and weekends.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair is the sole operator on this route. The outbound flight includes 1 stopover, so it's not direct. Though it adds travel time, the price is competitive. Always check Ryanair's baggage terms: cabin bag is included, but checked luggage carries an extra fee.

Best time to visit

Brussels is pleasant in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild temperatures and less rain. December has charm with Christmas markets, though it's peak demand. July and August tend to be pricier due to summer tourism. January–February is the cheapest season if you don't mind cold weather.

What to do when you arrive

Brussels awaits with the iconic Grand Place, the Atomium, and museums like Magritte's. Belgian beer and waffles are essential. Brussels-Zaventem airport is 12 km from the city centre; train, bus, or taxi get you there easily. With one stopover and short distance, you can reach the city and make the most of your time.

FAQ

€162 return is the lowest price in our sample. The median is €173, so if you find something in that range, it's time to book.

Which airline flies this route?

What's the best time to visit Brussels?
