Best time to visit Kenya

November and December are excellent months: warm temperatures, less rain and perfect for safari. Dry season extends through February. Avoid April and May due to long rains. Prices may fluctuate by season; booking 6–8 weeks ahead is usually safe.

What to do when you arrive in Nairobi

Nairobi offers museums (National Museum), parks (Nairobi National Park with city views) and nightlife. It's the gateway to Masái Mara and Mount Kenya safaris. Arrive with time to spare: Jomo Kenyatta Airport is about 18 km from the centre. Plan excursions in advance if you want tours outside the city.
