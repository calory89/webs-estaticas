Best time to visit

Mykonos shines in summer, but October and November (as in the 14 November example) offer a sweet spot: fewer crowds than July–August, pleasant temperatures, and beaches still worth enjoying. Spring (April–May) is another solid choice if you prefer to avoid intense heat.

What to do when you arrive in Mykonos

Mykonos is famous for its beaches (Paradise, Super Paradise, Elia), nightlife at the port, and the Chora village with its iconic white-washed houses. Don't miss the sunset in Little Venice or visit Delos, the ancient archaeological site just a short boat ride away. If you seek quiet, the northern beaches are less crowded.
