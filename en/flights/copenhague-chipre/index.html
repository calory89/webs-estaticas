Price and when to book

The price range we found runs from €175 to €220 return. The median sits at €191. To secure the cheapest tickets, it's wise to reserve well ahead of time. Ryanair maintains competitive fares on Mediterranean routes, so monitor prices regularly if you have flexible travel dates.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with 1 stopover. The journey is not direct, but the prices make up for the extra time. Example: departure from Copenhagen at 06:35 and return from Cyprus at 10:45. Always check the details of your stopover when booking.

Best time to travel

Cyprus enjoys good weather almost year-round. The warmest months (June to September) are ideal for beach and sun, though peak season prices apply. If you prefer fewer crowds and milder temperatures, April, May, October and November are excellent choices. Winter is quiet but with shorter daylight hours.

What to do on arrival in Cyprus

You'll land at Larnaca International Airport (LCA). From there you have access to the island's main beaches: Nissi Beach, Ayia Napa. Also visit Nicosia, the divided capital with fascinating history, or explore the mountain villages of Troodos. The ancient ruins of Kourion and Paphos are unmissable for archaeology enthusiasts.
