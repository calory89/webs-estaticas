Airline and stopovers

Eurowings operates this route with 1 stopover on the outbound leg. Your outbound flight departs Palma at 08:25 (example: 3 December) and return at 09:30 (example: 16 December). The stopover adds roughly 2–3 hours to total journey time. You'll land in Istanbul (IST) with ample connection time.

Best time to travel

December is workable if you're okay with 8–12°C temperatures. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: 20–25°C and less rain. Skip July and August, peak tourism crowds. Book 2–3 months ahead: that's the sweet spot for this route.

What to do on arrival

Istanbul awaits with the Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia, and the Spice Bazaar on the European side. The Bosphorus divides Europe from Asia: cross the Galata Bridge or take a ferry. Eat in Balat, buy ceramics in Kütahya, and catch sunset from Pierre Loti cable car. The airport (IST) is 25 km from centre; direct bus or shared minibus at fair rates.

What to see in Istanbul in 5 days?

Days 1–2: Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia, Topkapi Palace, Spice Bazaar. Day 3: Bosphorus cruise. Day 4: Balat quarter and Galata. Day 5: Museums or shopping on Istiklal. Metro and tram make getting around easy.
