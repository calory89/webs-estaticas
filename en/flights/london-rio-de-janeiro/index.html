Best time to travel

November is pleasant in Rio de Janeiro: it's spring in the Southern Hemisphere, with warm temperatures and less rain than the summer months (December to March). Flight prices tend to be lower during shoulder season, the transition between peak and low season, so November-December and February-March offer good value alongside favourable weather. Avoid local and European school holidays if you want the best fares.

What to do in Rio de Janeiro

Rio is a must-visit: the Christ the Redeemer statue, Copacabana beach, Ipanema, Sugarloaf Mountain with sunset views, and the bohemian Santa Teresa neighbourhood. Visit the Rio Museum of Art, relax on Leblon or Arpoador beaches, and sample local dining in Lapa's bars and restaurants. Galeão International Airport (GIG) is about 20 km from the city centre; buses, metro, and taxis are available to reach your accommodation.
