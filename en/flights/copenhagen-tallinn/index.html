Prices start at €182 return. Our data shows the median at €189, so advance booking is essential. The closer to your travel date, the higher the cost. Use flexible date search: shifting 2–3 days can save you €20–30.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route with 1 stop on the outbound flight. It's not direct, but it delivers the most competitive price. Travel light to avoid checked baggage fees. The stopover is usually short at one of Ryanair's European hubs.

Best time to visit

Tallinn shines in summer (June–August) with long days and mild weather, but it's peak season with higher fares. Spring (May) and autumn (September–October) offer better value: fewer crowds, moderate prices and pleasant climate. Winter appeals to those wanting snow and a true Nordic experience.

What to do when you arrive

Lennart Meri Tallinn Airport (TLL) is 4 km from the city centre. Head straight to the Old Town (UNESCO World Heritage), walk the medieval walls and climb to Alexander Nevsky Cathedral. Try local dining at Kumu or explore Kadriorg Palace. Tallinn is compact and walkable; three days is plenty.
