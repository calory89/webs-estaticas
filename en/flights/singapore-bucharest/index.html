Best time to visit

Romania shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), when weather is mild and crowds are lighter than summer. Winter is cheaper but daylight is scarce. Skip December–January if you're on a tight budget, as heating and indoor activities drive up costs.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Henri Coandă Airport in Bucharest, 16 km from the city centre. Bucharest has plenty: the Parliament Palace (one of the world's largest buildings), Old Town with cobbled streets, ancient Orthodox churches, and buzzing nightlife. Easy access to the Carpathians and Brașov for nature lovers. Romanian food is hearty and cheap.
