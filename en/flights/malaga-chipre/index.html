Best time to travel

Cyprus is a year-round destination, but April–May and September–October offer the best climate with pleasant temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. July and August mean hot weather and beach season, though prices rise. Winter is quieter and cheaper, ideal if you want to save money and don't mind milder temperatures.

What to do when you arrive in Larnaca

Larnaca is your gateway to Cyprus. Ten minutes by bus or taxi gets you to Larnaca Beach, perfect for a first swim. Explore the old town with its Byzantine churches, stroll the seafront promenade, and taste Cypriot cuisine. From here you can easily reach Paphos, the Troodos Mountains, or the famous Fig Tree Bay. Cyprus is compact; with a rental car, the whole island is within reach.

What's the best month to visit Cyprus?
