Fly Milan to Sal Island from €195 return with no stops. Median price sits around €208, so booking in advance gives you real options. Sal is pure beach: calm waters, white sand, and year-round sunshine.

Price and when to book

This route starts at €195, though most fares hover around €208. We found samples at €195, €208, and €239. Book 6–8 weeks ahead for the best rates. easyJet's flash sales and Black Friday deals can bring extra savings, but last-minute bookings rarely pay off.

Airline and stops

easyJet operates this route nonstop: no changes, no layovers. Perfect for a transatlantic journey. Being a budget carrier, cabin bags are included but checked baggage costs extra.

Best time to visit

Sal has no real rainy season. November to June offers drier, more comfortable conditions; July to October gets hotter and more humid. The trade winds make it a windsurfing destination almost year-round. Skip peak season if you want better prices.

What to do when you arrive

The airport is on the island itself. Sal is small but packed: volcanic-sand beaches in Santa Maria, sea turtle spotting at Buracona, diving and snorkelling in crystal waters. Historic salt ponds and the village of Pedra Lume are must-sees. Stay in Santa Maria or Espargos depending on your budget.

FAQ

Is the Milan to Sal Island flight actually direct?

Yes. easyJet flies nonstop. Depart Milan, arrive in Sal, no stops.

What's the cheapest round-trip price recorded?

€195 return. Fares climb closer to travel dates and with demand, but €208 is the realistic median.

Does it include checked luggage?

No. easyJet includes cabin baggage only (55×40×20 cm). Checked bags cost extra, typically €25–40.

What's there to do on Sal beyond the beach?

Buracona natural pool, Santa Maria village, Pedra Lume salt ponds, sea turtle spotting, windsurfing, snorkelling, and diving. The island is compact but packed with water activities.
