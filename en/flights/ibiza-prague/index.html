Best time to visit

Prague is lovely year-round, but October to March is when you'll find the lowest prices and fewer crowds. Avoid Christmas and New Year if you're hunting for budget fares. Spring (April–May) is ideal if you want good weather without the premium price tag. The example flight departs on 7 January, a perfect date for rock-bottom post-holiday prices.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Václav Havel Airport (PRG), 20 km from the city centre. Take a direct bus or train for just a few euros. Wander the Old Town Square, climb the Astronomical Clock Tower, cross the Charles Bridge and explore Prague Castle. Eat at local pubs and try trdelník (traditional pastry). Three days are enough to see the highlights.
