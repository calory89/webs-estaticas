Airline and stops

SAS (Scandinavian Airlines) operates this route with 2 stops on the outbound flight. There's no direct service Oslo–Santorini, so expect connections that add travel time, but the price makes up for it. Layovers typically connect through major European hubs.

Best time to visit

Santorini shines May to September: sun, crystal sea, and postcard views. To skip crowds and high prices, try April or October—solid weather and fewer tourists. Winter (December–February) is cheaper but wetter, and some shops close. Pick based on your budget and heat tolerance.

What to do in Santorini

Landing at Santorini (JTR, Thíra airport) opens doors to jaw-dropping cliffs, whitewashed villages like Oia and Fira, black-sand beaches, and an active volcano. Taste local tomatoes and volcanic wine. Ferry to nearby islands if you have extra days. Sunsets from the clifftops are worth every euro.

FAQ

Is €277 the final price or are there hidden fees?

€277 is the return fare we show. Check SAS's website for extras: hand luggage may be included, but checked bags often carry a surcharge depending on your ticket type.

Does SAS fly direct from Oslo to Santorini?

No, there are 2 stops on the outbound leg. No direct route exists on this pairing. Total journey time will be longer, but the price compensates.

What airport do I land at in Santorini and how do I reach my hotel?

You arrive at Thíra (JTR), Santorini's only airport. From there, grab a public bus, taxi, or book a private transfer. Taxis are pricey; the bus is the budget choice.
