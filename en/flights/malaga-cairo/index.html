Best time to visit

January is ideal: pleasant daytime weather (20–25°C), cool nights, and no extreme heat. Avoid June to August, when temperatures reach 40°C or higher. January is also peak tourist season, so expect crowds at monuments. February and March remain good options with fewer visitors.

What to do when you arrive

Cairo awaits with the Giza Pyramids just 20 km away, the Egyptian Museum in Tahrir, and Khan el-Khalili bazaar. The international airport is about 25 km from the city centre. Use a taxi, Uber, or pre-arranged transfers. Spend at least 3–4 days to see the essentials.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for flights from Málaga to Cairo?

From €292 return. This is with Wizz Air Malta, includes one stopover, and applies to January 2027 departures. Book early if you want this fare.

Does Wizz Air Malta fly direct from Málaga to Cairo?

No, this route has one stopover. Total travel time depends on where the connection is made, but budget between 5 and 8 hours airport to airport.

When is the best time to visit Cairo?

October to March is perfect: pleasant days and cool nights. January is a good choice but the busiest. If you prefer fewer tourists, try February or March with similar good weather.

What can I see in Cairo in a week?

The Giza Pyramids, the Egyptian Museum, Khan el-Khalili bazaar, the Citadel of Saladin, and a day trip to Saqqara or Memphis are must-sees. Spending 3–4 days in the city and the rest on day trips gives you plenty of time.
