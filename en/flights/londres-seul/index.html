Best time to visit Seoul

Seoul shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): pleasant weather and clear skies. Winter (December–February) brings snow and intense cold, but it's brilliant for snowy landscapes and nearby ski resorts. Skip the summer monsoon (June–August). Book flights well ahead: the earlier you reserve, the better your fare.

What to do when you arrive in Seoul

You'll land at Incheon Airport (ICN), well-connected to the city centre by rapid train (45 minutes). Don't miss Gyeongbokgung Palace, Bukchon Hanok (traditional neighbourhood), Cheonggyecheon (urban creek), Myeongdong for shopping, and Gangnam for modern Seoul. Street food is incredible and affordable. Public transport is excellent and cheap.
