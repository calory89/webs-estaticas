Paris-Lisbon flights start from €78 return, with typical prices around €86. In our sample we've seen fares of €78, €86 and €92. Airline and what the journey is like

Ryanair operates this route with direct flights, no stops. The example flight departs Paris at 14:20 and arrives in Lisbon at 15:25 (local time). No aircraft changes, so you save time and hassle. Keep in mind Ryanair charges for checked baggage and seat selection; factor in these extras before you complete your booking.

Best time to visit

Lisbon is pleasant year-round, but April to June and September to October offer mild weather without extreme tourist crowds. January is low season, with mild temperatures (8-14°C) and occasional rain. Winter is ideal if you want peace and these contained prices. Avoid July and August if you dislike heat and congestion.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Lisbon Humberto Delgado airport, about 7 km from the city centre. Take the metro (red line), bus or taxi. Once in the city, head to the Tejo on the Sintra train to see Pena and Quinta da Regaleira, stroll through Alfama with its tiles and viewpoints, try pastéis de nata at any local shop, or relax in a tasca with local wine. The Museu de Arte Oriente and Jerónimos Monastery are must-sees. Four to five days gives you plenty of time.

FAQ

In our sample, the lowest price is €78 return with Ryanair. The median sits at €86. Prices vary depending on dates and availability; booking several weeks in advance usually yields better fares.

Are there direct flights from Paris to Lisbon?

Yes, Ryanair flies direct between Paris and Lisbon with no stops. The journey takes approximately one hour, depending on weather conditions and winds.

Which airline offers the cheapest Paris to Lisbon flights?

Ryanair is the airline on this route offering prices from €78 return. It's a low-cost carrier, so check what's included in your fare (baggage, seat) before you buy.

What to see in Lisbon in 5 days?

Alfama with its narrow streets and tiles, the Tejo and its bridges, Jerónimos Monastery, Basílica da Estrela, Belém with its famous pastries, a day trip to Sintra (Pena Palace, Quinta da Regaleira), and the modern Parque das Nações neighbourhood. Museums, terraces and local dining will fill the rest.
