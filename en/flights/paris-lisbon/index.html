Best time to visit

Lisbon is pleasant year-round, but April to June and September to October offer mild weather without extreme tourist crowds. January is low season, with mild temperatures (8-14°C) and occasional rain. Winter is ideal if you want peace and these contained prices. Avoid July and August if you dislike heat and congestion.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Lisbon Humberto Delgado airport, about 7 km from the city centre. Take the metro (red line), bus or taxi. Once in the city, head to the Tejo on the Sintra train to see Pena and Quinta da Regaleira, stroll through Alfama with its tiles and viewpoints, try pastéis de nata at any local shop, or relax in a tasca with local wine. The Museu de Arte Oriente and Jerónimos Monastery are must-sees. Four to five days gives you plenty of time.
