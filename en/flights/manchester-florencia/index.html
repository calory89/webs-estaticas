Best time to visit

Florence is beautiful year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer the best balance: pleasant temperatures and fewer crowds than July–August. Prices tend to rise in summer and around public holidays. Weekday travel is usually cheaper than weekends.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Florence Airport (FLR), just 4 km from the centre. The Cathedral and Duomo are must-sees, Ponte Vecchio is iconic, and the Uffizi holds masterpieces. Don't miss the old quarters, Renaissance churches and Tuscan food. Vasari's corridor connects historic palaces: walk it if time permits.

No, this route includes 1 stopover. Ryanair operates it, so the connection is part of the journey. Total travel time remains competitive for the price you pay.

What should I see in Florence?

The Cathedral, Uffizi Gallery, Ponte Vecchio, Palazzo Pitti and the Oltrarno neighbourhood are essential. Tuscan food (bistecca, wines) is outstanding. Three to four days covers the basics; a week is ideal if you want a slower pace.
