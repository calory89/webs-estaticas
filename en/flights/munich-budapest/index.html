Best time to visit

Budapest shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather, fewer crowds than summer. Winter is cold but magical if you like snow-covered sights. Avoid July–August if you want peace; it's peak season. Winter and early spring often have lower fares.

What to do when you arrive

Budapest is a must: thermal baths (Széchenyi, Gellért), Parliament, the Danube from Chain Bridge. Explore the Jewish Quarter, St Stephen's Basilica, eat goulash and Dobos cake. Ferenc Liszt Airport is 16 km from the centre; quick bus or taxi ride in.
