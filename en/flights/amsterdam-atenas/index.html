Best time to visit

Athens enjoys pleasant weather year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer comfortable temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. December is quiet and the city has special festive charm. Skip August if you'd rather avoid peak tourist season.

What to do in Athens

The Acropolis and Parthenon are unmissable. Wander through Plaka, the old neighbourhood with narrow streets and traditional tavernas. Visit the Acropolis Museum and explore Monastiraki. Day trips to nearby Attica and beaches are easily done. Try local food: souvlaki, moussaka and feta.
