Airline and stopovers

Ryanair is your only option on this route, and the good news is it flies direct: 0 stopovers. Night departure from Madrid (around 23:25) and mid-afternoon arrival in Porto (around 18:50). No hassle with connecting flights.

Best time to visit

Porto is pleasant year-round, but skip July and August if you want fewer crowds and better prices. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: lovely weather and quieter streets. Winter is cheap but expect rain and shorter days.

What to do in Porto

Wander through Ribeira, the old town by the Douro River, packed with cafés and blue tiles. Taste Porto wine in the cellars of Vila Nova de Gaia. Climb the Tower of Clérigos for 360° views and relax on nearby beaches like Foz do Douro. Three or four days is enough to settle in without rushing.
