Best time to travel

Fort Lauderdale has pleasant weather year-round; Northern winter (December to February) is peak season but appealing if you're escaping European cold. Autumn (September to November) and spring (March to May) often strike a balance between weather and crowds.

What to do on arrival

Fort Lauderdale offers sandy beaches, the world's busiest cruise port, and vibrant nightlife downtown. The airport is roughly 30 km from the centre; easy access to Miami, the Everglades and the Keys from there. Rent a car if you plan to explore Florida freely.
