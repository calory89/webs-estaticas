Price and when to book

Prices for this route range from €48 to €70 based on samples we've seen. The golden rule: book as early as possible for the best fares. Ryanair typically offers its lowest prices 2-3 months in advance, so if you're planning winter or spring travel, start searching now. Weekday flights are often cheaper than weekend services.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight with no stopovers. The outbound flight (example: 5 December, departure 17:15) and return (example: 12 December, arrival 20:30) are both direct DUB-LIS. Ryanair dominates this connection and offers good value if you travel with just a cabin bag.

Best time to visit

Lisbon is pleasant year-round. Spring (March-May) and autumn (September-October) offer lovely weather without summer heat. Winter (December-February) brings cooler temperatures, fewer crowds, and often competitive fares. July and August are peak season with higher prices and busier streets.

What to do when you arrive

Lisbon has plenty to offer: walk through the Bairro Alto, climb up to Castelo de São Jorge, try pastéis de nata in Belém, and ride the iconic Tram 28. The airport is 7 km from the city centre; the Aerobus is quick and affordable. A week gives you plenty of time to see the highlights and relax.
