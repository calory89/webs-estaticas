Best time to visit

December in Cape Town is southern summer: long days, pleasant temperatures (24-26°C average) and peak tourist season. If you prefer fewer crowds, consider travelling in March-April or September-October, though prices may vary. The climate is good year-round, but November to February is the warmest season.

What to do on arrival

Cape Town is spectacular: Table Mountain (accessible by rotating cable car), beaches like Camps Bay and Boulders Beach with penguins, Cape Point, Constantia Valley for wine, and Robben Island. The airport is about 20 km from the centre; use shuttle, taxi or hire a car to get around the region.
