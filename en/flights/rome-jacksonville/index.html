Airlines and connections

Aer Lingus operates this route with 3 stops on the outbound journey. This means it is not a direct flight, you will have intermediate stops before reaching Jacksonville. Although it requires more travel time, these connections typically allow for more competitive fares than direct flights.

Best time to travel

Jacksonville enjoys good weather most of the year. November to April are ideal if you prefer mild temperatures and less rainfall. Avoid September and October, hurricane season. While there is no guarantee of lower prices during low season, travelling outside school holidays and European bank holidays increases your flexibility.

What to do when you arrive

Jacksonville is the largest city by area in the US and stands out for its beaches, the Riverwalk and its food scene. Visit St. Augustine, Europe's oldest settlement in the country, less than an hour away. The Cummer Museum and nearby nature reserves are other worthwhile options to enjoy your stay.
