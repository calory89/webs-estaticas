Price and when to book

Prices start from €155 return. The median is €166. The earlier you book, the better the fare, Ryanair adjusts prices dynamically based on availability. Avoid booking last-minute if you're looking for savings.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with one stop on the outbound flight. Departing from Santiago de Compostela (SCQ) and arriving at Dublin (DUB), the journey includes an intermediate stop. Whilst longer than a direct flight, the price makes it worthwhile.

Best time to visit

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is cheaper but wetter. Avoid Christmas and New Year if you want lower fares.

What to do when you arrive in Dublin

Dublin Airport is about 10 km from the city centre. Don't miss the historic centre, Temple Bar, the Book of Kells at Trinity College, the Guinness Storehouse, and the Howth or Cliffs of Moher nearby. Dublin is compact and well-connected.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price from SCQ to Dublin?

The lowest price is €155 return with Ryanair. The median is €166, so you can find good deals around that figure if you book in advance.

Does Ryanair fly direct from Santiago to Dublin?

No, this route includes one stop on the outbound flight. It's not a direct flight, but the price is very competitive because of this.

When should I book for the best price?

Book as far in advance as possible. Ryanair raises prices as your departure date approaches. Booking 2–3 months ahead is ideal for this route.

What is there to see in Dublin?

The Book of Kells, Trinity College, Temple Bar, the Guinness Storehouse, and the historic centre are must-sees. The Cliffs of Moher are 2–3 hours away. Dublin is easy to explore on foot or by public transport.
