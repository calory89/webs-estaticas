Best time to visit Palermo

Sicily shines year-round, but October to April is ideal: mild weather, fewer crowds, and lower fares than peak summer. Skip July and August if you want to avoid heat and tourist masses. Spring (March–May) is also brilliant.

What to do when you arrive

Palermo is unmissable: wander the old town, grab arancini at Vucciria and Capo markets, see the Cathedral and Palatine Chapel. Mondello Beach is 30 minutes by bus. Catch the sunset at Mondello and explore the baroque palaces nearby.
