Best time to visit Kraków

Kraków is beautiful year-round. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winters are cold but perfect if you want to see the city dusted with snow. Avoid July and August if you prefer lighter tourist traffic.

What to do in Kraków

Don't miss the Main Market Square (Rynek Główny), a UNESCO World Heritage site, Wawel Castle, and the historic Kazimierz district. Kraków Airport (Balice) is 15 km from the centre; take the train or bus to reach the city heart quickly.
