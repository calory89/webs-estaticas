Best time to visit

Kuala Lumpur has a tropical climate year-round. If you want to avoid rain, aim for the drier months (January to March and July to August). Outside those windows, monsoon showers are more likely. Bear in mind that demand peaks during European holidays, which can push prices up. Travelling between peak seasons often saves money.

What to do on arrival

Kuala Lumpur is Malaysia's capital and a perfect entry point. Visit the Petronas Towers (unmissable), the Royal Palace and the National Museum. Explore Chinatown and Little India for street food and local life. From there, beaches like Langkawi or Penang are easy to reach, as are primary rainforests. The international airport (KUL) is 75 km from the centre; direct train connections are available.
