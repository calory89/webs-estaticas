Best time to visit

Malaysia has a tropical climate year-round. June to September are drier months in the Kuala Lumpur area, though hotter and more humid. December and January see peak tourism. Choose based on your heat tolerance and book several weeks in advance.

What to do when you arrive in Kuala Lumpur

You'll land at Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KUL), about 75 km from the city centre. Visit the Petronas Twin Towers, explore Chinatown, enjoy local street food at night markets, and discover Hindu and Buddhist temples. The nearby islands and Malaysia's natural attractions are also worth exploring.
