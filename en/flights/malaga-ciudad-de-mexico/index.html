Best time to travel

December is peak season in Mexico, so prices spike. For savings, consider May–June or September–October. Mind the climate: the rainy season runs June–September, but showers are brief afternoon bursts. December–March offers perfect weather but premium prices.

What to do in Mexico City

The capital is vast and unmissable. Centro Histórico and Zócalo are essential. World-class museums: Frida Kahlo, Museum of Anthropology, Tamayo. Neighbourhoods like Coyoacán, Condesa and La Roma have great food, art and nightlife. Xochimilco for boat tours on the canals. Day trips to Teotihuacán's pyramids are an easy 50 km away. Carry cash for markets and local restaurants.
