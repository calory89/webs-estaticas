Best time to visit

Nairobi is a safari and wildlife gateway, so migration season in July–October and January–February is peak tourist season: pricier, busier parks. Shoulder months like April–May or November offer stable weather and fewer crowds. No severe weather restrictions: any month is viable, though long rains are April–May. From Dubai the journey is brief (4 hours), so choose dates based on price availability and your preference for what to see in Kenya.

What to do on arrival in Nairobi

You'll land at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO), roughly 18 km from the city centre. Nairobi is the springboard for safaris in Masai Mara, Amboseli and Tsavo. In the city itself: museums, markets and dynamic urban life. Many visitors arrive, hire a car and head to the parks; others spend 1–2 days in Nairobi first. Climate is subtropical highland (1,600 m), pleasant year-round.
