Price and when to book

Flights from Zurich to Sri Lanka start from €591 return. Sample prices range between €591, €601 and €610, so you have options within a tight band. As a general rule, book 2–3 months ahead to secure better fares. The longer you wait, the more you'll pay.

Airline and stopovers

Etihad Airways is your option on this route with one stopover. Outbound departs Zurich at 10:30 and return departs Colombo at 04:00. One stopover is standard on this route from Zurich, so you won't change planes more than once. Check in your booking manager exactly where the stopover happens.

Best time to visit

Sri Lanka is a pleasant destination year-round, but the dry season (January to March and July to September) is most comfortable. Rainfall is lower and weather more stable in those months. Whenever you go, book in advance to avoid missing out on price deals.

What to do when you arrive in Colombo

Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) is about 30 km from Colombo city. From there you have easy connections to main destinations: south coast beaches, the cultural triangle (Sigiriya, Kandy, Polonnaruwa), tea plantations in the centre, or scenic train rides. Colombo itself merits a day to explore Galle Face Green and the colonial quarter.
