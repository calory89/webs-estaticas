Best time to visit

Berlin is attractive year-round. December brings Christmas markets and festive vibes, though it's peak season. For lower prices, consider May-June or September-October: pleasant weather and fewer crowds. That said, availability and prices shift weekly, so compare alternative dates.

What to do when you arrive

Berlin is a capital of history and culture. Brandenburg Gate, Reichstag, the Wall and street art galleries are must-sees. Eat in Kreuzberg, stroll through Charlottenburg, visit world-class museums. Public transport is efficient; a travel pass is worth it. Spend at least three or four days to enjoy without rushing.
