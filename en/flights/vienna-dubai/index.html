Price and when to book

Fares start from €324 per person (return). Our sample showed prices between €324, €334 and €349. Booking well in advance is your best bet: the earlier you purchase, the better your chances of avoiding price hikes. December and January are peak months in Dubai, so for softer pricing, aim for shoulder season instead.

Airline and stops

Pegasus operates this route with 1 stopover. Direct flights aren't available in this price bracket, but one stop is standard for this journey. Check where the connection is when you book, as layover times vary. Pegasus is a Turkish low-cost carrier that keeps fares competitive in exchange for basic services.

Best time to travel

Dubai is a winter destination: October to April brings pleasant temperatures (20–30°C). July and August are brutal (45°C+). Travel November–February and expect crowds and higher prices. For a balance between weather and cost, target May or September: it's still warm, but demand drops.

What to do when you arrive

Dubai International Airport (DXB) is 10 km from the city centre. From there, visit the Burj Khalifa (world's tallest building), Dubai Mall, Palm Jumeirah, the desert (safaris and 4x4 drives) and the Gold Souk in Deira. The city runs on metro, taxi and bus. Book major attractions in advance if you have a tight itinerary.
