Fly from Stockholm to the Azores from €242 return with Ryanair. The average price sits at €267, so you'll find very competitive options if you're flexible with dates. A perfect destination to escape into Atlantic nature.

Price and when to book

The cheapest return flight we found is €242, while most hover around €267. Peak dates push prices up to €303. Book at least 6–8 weeks ahead for the best fares; Ryanair increases prices as departure approaches. Date flexibility is your strongest weapon.

Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Ryanair, the budget carrier that dominates this route. Expect 1 stopover on the way (usually Lisbon or Porto), so it's not a direct flight but total travel time remains reasonable to reach the Azores. Check baggage allowance: Ryanair's base fare includes small cabin luggage only.

Best time to travel

The Azores enjoy mild weather year-round, but May to September are the sunniest months. For lower fares, fly in winter (November to March): fewer tourists and reduced prices, though expect more rain. The landscape stays lush and beautiful even in low season.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in Ponta Delgada on São Miguel island. The Azores are a paradise for hiking, whale watching, geothermal baths and volcanoes. Visit Sete Cidades crater lakes, walk around Santiago Lake and soak in the thermal springs at Furnas. Hire a car to explore the island freely.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Stockholm to the Azores?

No, this route includes 1 stopover. Ryanair doesn't offer a direct connection, but total travel time is manageable—around 5–6 hours door-to-door.

Why is the flight so cheap?

Ryanair operates a low-cost model with aggressive pricing on European routes. The base price excludes checked baggage, so factor that in if you need a large suitcase. It's still economical overall.

When is the best time to buy this flight?

Book 6–8 weeks before departure. Ryanair prices climb as your travel date approaches. Tuesday and Wednesday bookings often beat weekend rates.

What can I see in the Azores?

Volcano hiking, whale watching, thermal baths, crater lakes, dramatic cliffs and charming fishing villages. It's an ideal destination for nature lovers and anyone seeking peace away from crowds.
