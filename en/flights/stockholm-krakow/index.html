Fly from Stockholm to Krakow non-stop from €43 return. The median price sits at €63, so there's room to find deals. This route is affordable year-round if you book in advance.

Prices and when to book

The lowest price we've seen is €43 return. Most flights hover around €63, though some reach €78. The earlier you book, the better the fare. If you spot a ticket below €60, that's a good time to buy. Check Ryanair directly to confirm availability and pricing for your exact dates.

Airline and flight type

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight with no stops. The outbound departs Stockholm at 13:20 (example: 10 November) and lands in Krakow without intermediate halts. Ryanair is known for low fares, so check what's included in your ticket: cabin baggage yes, checked luggage may cost extra. Flight time approximately two hours.

Best time to visit

Krakow is lovely in any season. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is magical if you enjoy snow, though temperatures drop sharply. Book 2–3 months ahead for the best prices, regardless of your travel date.

What to do when you arrive

Krakow airport (Balice) is 15 km from the city centre; reach it by bus, train or taxi. Don't miss the Main Market Square, St. Mary's Basilica and the Jewish Quarter. Auschwitz, a memorial site, is 70 km outside the city. Krakow is budget-friendly: good meals are cheap and wandering the old town is free.

FAQ

What is the cheapest price for a flight from Stockholm to Krakow?

From €43 return with Ryanair. This is the lowest price we've recorded. The median is €63, so this price is exceptional and rare.

Is the flight from Stockholm to Krakow direct?

Yes, Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight with no stops. You depart Stockholm and arrive directly in Krakow.

Which airline flies from Stockholm to Krakow?

Ryanair connects these two cities. It's a low-cost carrier, so check what's included in your ticket (cabin baggage yes, checked luggage may have a charge).

What should I see in Krakow on a short trip?

The Main Market Square with St. Mary's Basilica is essential. Walk the old town, explore the Jewish Quarter, and if time permits, visit Auschwitz 70 km away. Three days are enough for the highlights.
