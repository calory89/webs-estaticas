Best time to visit Malta

Malta is a year-round destination thanks to its Mediterranean climate. The best period is October to April, with mild temperatures (15-20°C) and little rain. July and August are very hot (30°C+) and crowded. December is a good option if you avoid local Christmas festivities. Travel in shoulder season to balance price and comfort.

What to do in Malta

You'll land at Malta International Airport (MLA), 6 km from the centre. Valletta, Europe's capital in 2018, is a must: fortified walls, cathedrals and Grand Harbour views. Visit the Three Villages, Mdina (medieval silent city), Popeye Village and beaches in Comino and Gozo. Malta is small but packed with history, diving and beaches within short distances. Rent a car or use cheap local buses.
