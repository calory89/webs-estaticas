Best time to travel

Phuket has a tropical climate: the best months are November to February, with less rain and comfortable temperatures. It's peak season, but you'll find more flight options. Travel in low season (May-October) for cheaper accommodation and activities, though there's more rainfall. Plan ahead: long journeys need rest before and after.

What to do when you arrive at Phuket

Phuket is the gateway to Thailand's islands. From Phuket International Airport, you can easily reach beaches like Kata, Karon and Patong. Don't miss the Phi Phi Islands or Phang Nga. There are also Buddhist temples, local markets and nightlife. Rent a scooter or use shared taxis to get around: it's cheap and safe.
