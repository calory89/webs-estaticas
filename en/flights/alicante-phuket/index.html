Best time to travel

Thailand has distinct seasons. The dry season (November to February) is ideal: clear skies, low humidity and perfect conditions for beaches and temples. Expect higher demand and prices in those months. The rainy season (May to October) is cheaper but wetter.

Things to do in Phuket

Phuket is your gateway to southern Thailand's beaches. From here you can visit Patong Beach, night markets, Buddhist temples and take trips to nearby Phi Phi islands. The international airport connects to Bangkok and other cities if you want to explore more of the country.

FAQ

What's the lowest price on this route?

The lowest recorded price is €654 return with Vueling. The median sits at €772, so fares close to that figure are realistic deals.

Does Vueling fly direct from Alicante to Phuket?

No, this flight has 2 stopovers. Allow enough time between connections and check where your stops are when booking.

When is the cheapest time to fly to Phuket?

We can't guarantee a specific month, but the rainy season is usually cheaper. The dry season (November to February) is pricier due to demand, though the weather is better.

What can I see and do in Phuket?

Beaches like Patong, Buddhist temples, night markets and trips to nearby islands like Phi Phi. It's an excellent starting point to explore southern Thailand.
