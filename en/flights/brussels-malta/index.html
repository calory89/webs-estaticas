Best time to visit

Malta shines year-round, but October to April offer gentle weather: comfortable temperatures, fewer crowds than peak summer. December is mild winter by Mediterranean standards, ideal if you're escaping Belgian cold for some warmth without the July-August heat. Flight prices don't shift by season, so choose based on your preferred climate and available dates.

What to do when you arrive

Malta is small but packed: Valletta (the capital, UNESCO-listed), Mdina's cathedrals, beaches at Mellieha and Popeye Village. The airport sits 5 km from town; bus, taxi or car hire are your moves. Three to five days covers the essentials at a comfortable pace.

FAQ

Is €44 really the full return fare?

Yes, from €44 return. Keep in mind Ryanair charges separately for luggage, seat picks and extras. That's the base airfare, check what's included before you commit.

Is this a direct flight or are there stops?

Direct, no stops. One flight Brussels to Malta with Ryanair, no aircraft change.

What should I know about Malta before travelling?

It's a small archipelago in the heart of the Mediterranean with thousands of years of history. You've got Valletta (the capital), film-set beaches, turquoise coves and world-class diving. Three days minimum if you don't want to miss everything.
