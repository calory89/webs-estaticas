Airline and stopovers

Best time to visit

Portugal is worth visiting year-round, but spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) offer pleasant temperatures without summer crowds. These seasons often align with budget airline promotions too. Winter is cooler and wetter, though less touristy.

What to do when you arrive in Lisbon

Portugal's capital blends historic heritage with modern vibrancy. Walk through Barrio Alto and Alfama, hop on the famous Tram 28, visit Mosteiro dos Jerónimos and Belém Tower. Pastéis de Nata from Pastéis de Belém are unmissable. From Lisbon, it's easy to reach Cascais or Sintra by public transport.
