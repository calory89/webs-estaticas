Best time to travel

Estonia shines in summer (June to August) when white nights offer light almost all day. Tallinn is spectacular in spring (May-June) and autumn (September). Winter is harsh but magical if you fancy snow. Check prices across seasons: summer isn't always the most expensive on Ryanair.

What to do when you arrive in Tallinn

Estonia's medieval capital is compact and perfect for walking. Explore the Old Town (UNESCO World Heritage) with cobbled streets, Alexander Nevsky Cathedral and city walls. Soak in saltwater spas, try traditional Estonian cuisine in local restaurants, and head to the revolving restaurant atop the TV Tower for 360-degree views.
