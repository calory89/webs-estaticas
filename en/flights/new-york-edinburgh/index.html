Best time to visit Edinburgh

November in Edinburgh is late autumn: cold temperatures (5–8°C), short days and more rainfall. If you want better weather, summer (June to August) is ideal, though pricier. Winter brings snow and a Christmas festival if you travel in December. Spring (April–May) balances price and climate well. Edinburgh is a popular destination, so book ahead.

What to do in Edinburgh

Edinburgh is Scotland's capital and a UNESCO World Heritage site. Visit Edinburgh Castle, the Royal Mile, the medieval Old Town and the modern Leith district. Museums, galleries and cultural life are excellent. Plan 5–7 days comfortably. The international airport is 13km from the city centre with good bus and train links.
