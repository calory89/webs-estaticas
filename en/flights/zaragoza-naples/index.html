Best time to visit

Naples is pleasant almost year-round. April, May, September and October offer good weather without July and August crowds or peak prices. Winter (November to March) is quieter and cheaper, though rainier. Choose based on what you want: beaches and sunshine, or fewer tourists and lower fares.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Capodichino (NAP), 7 km from the centre. From there, metro or taxi gets you easily into the city. Must-sees: the Cathedral, National Archaeological Museum and the historic centre. The nearby coast (Positano, Amalfi) and Pompeya are reachable as day trips. Eat genuine Neapolitan pizza and fresh seafood.
