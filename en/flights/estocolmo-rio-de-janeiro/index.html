Price and when to book

The base price found is €838 per passenger return, with a median of €881. The price range we've seen spans from €838 to €1,376. To get these low fares, book as far in advance as possible, as flights to distant destinations like Rio increase in price as departure approaches.

Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Ryanair, which operates this route with 3 stops. Keep in mind the journey will be lengthy with intermediate stops. Carry documentation for each stopover and check your baggage allowance when booking, as Ryanair charges for excess luggage.

Best time to visit

Rio de Janeiro has a tropical climate. The best period to visit is October to March, when rainfall is lower and weather is pleasant. July and August are winter in the Southern Hemisphere but remain viable. Avoid carnival week if you're seeking lower prices and fewer crowds.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Tom Jobim International Airport (Galeão), located in Rio's northern zone, about 40 km from the city centre. Use bus, taxi or shared transport services. Don't miss Christ the Redeemer, Sugarloaf Mountain, Copacabana and Ipanema beaches, or the bohemian neighbourhood of Lapa. Local cuisine is exceptional: try feijoada and caipirinhas.

FAQ

Can I fly direct from Stockholm to Rio de Janeiro?

No, this Ryanair route requires 3 stopovers. There is no direct option at the shown prices. The journey will be longer, but the price from €838 compensates.

Which airport will I arrive at in Rio de Janeiro?

You'll arrive at Tom Jobim International Airport, also known as Galeão Airport. It's Rio's main airport and is connected to public transport towards the city centre.

What baggage can I bring with Ryanair?

Ryanair allows a small backpack or handbag. Checked baggage and additional suitcases incur extra costs that vary by itinerary. Check your booking for specifics on this route.

When should I book to get the €838 price?

The sooner you book, the better. Prices to intercontinental destinations like Rio rise as departure approaches. Booking 2-3 months in advance is ideal for securing these low fares.
