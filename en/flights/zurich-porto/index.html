Price and when to book

The lowest price recorded is €107 return, with a median of €112. Sample fares range from €107 to €128. As always, book as far in advance as possible for the best rate. Check regularly 2–3 months before your trip.

Airline and stops

easyJet operates this route with direct flights, no stops. You depart Zurich (ZRH) and land directly in Porto (OPO). Cabin baggage is included; checked luggage depends on your fare type. The journey is short and hassle-free with no connection worries.

Best time to visit

Porto is pleasant year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is quieter and cheaper. Avoid July–August if you want lower prices and less tourist traffic.

What to do on arrival

Porto airport (OPO) is 11 km from the centre. Bus, metro or taxi gets you there in 20–30 minutes. Once in the city, visit Ribeira with its tiled townhouses, Clérigos Tower, Dom Luís Bridge and Vila Nova de Gaia's wine cellars. Explore the old neighbourhoods and try francesinha in local tascas.
