Best time to visit

Copenhagen is beautiful in spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October), with pleasant temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is magical if you want Christmas lights, festive markets and pure Nordic atmosphere. Avoid July and August if you're after fewer tourists and better prices.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Copenhagen Airport (CPH), well connected to the city centre in 25 minutes by train. Visit Nyhavn, the iconic harbour with colourful townhouses; Amalienborg Palace; and Tivoli and Bakken parks. Cycle like the locals, try smørrebrød (traditional open sandwiches) and explore the alternative neighbourhood of Christiania. The food scene is world-class.
