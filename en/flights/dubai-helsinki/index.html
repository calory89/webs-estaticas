Price and when to book

The lowest price on record is €338 for a return ticket. Prices range from €338 to €458 depending on dates. Book 3–4 weeks in advance to land the best fares. Compare different departure days: slight date shifts can make a difference to your final price.

Airline and stopovers

Pegasus operates this route. Expect 1 stopover on the outbound journey, so it's not a direct flight. Check what's included in your fare: Pegasus charges for checked baggage on lower-cost tickets. Pack light in your carry-on to avoid extra fees.

Best time to visit

Helsinki shines in summer (June to August) with nearly continuous daylight, though prices climb and tourism peaks. Winter (December to February) brings a chance of northern lights but extreme cold. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) strike a balance: mild weather, fewer crowds, and more reasonable prices.

What to do when you arrive

Helsinki greets you with Nordic design and classical architecture. Walk Senate Square, step into the white Lutheran cathedral, explore the independent shops and cafés of Kallio. Take a ferry to Suomenlinna fortress or soak in public saunas—very Finnish. If winter and skies are clear, head north to Lapland for a shot at the aurora.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for Dubai to Helsinki?

The lowest fare we found is €338 return. The median hovers around €416. Price depends heavily on your travel dates: the earlier you book and the more flexible you are with dates, the better deal you'll get.

Is this a direct flight from Dubai to Helsinki?

No, this route has 1 stopover. Pegasus operates these flights. Check the total duration and layover time when you book.

What does the Pegasus ticket include?

Pegasus is a budget airline, so your seat and carry-on bag are included, but checked luggage costs extra on base fares. Choose your baggage carefully to avoid surprises.

What should I see in Helsinki in a few days?

The Lutheran cathedral, Senate Square market, the Kallio neighbourhood, and Suomenlinna fortress by ferry are must-sees. Try public saunas and taste rye bread and reindeer stew. Two days covers the essentials; three is comfortable.
