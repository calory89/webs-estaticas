Best time to travel

Sydney enjoys spring and summer from September to February with pleasant temperatures. Milan shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), though hotel prices rise. Winter in Milan (December–February) can be cold, but coincides with fashion sales.

What to do when you arrive

Don't miss Milan's Cathedral (Duomo), the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele and Castello Sforzesco. Malpensa and Linate are the main airports; Linate is closer to the city centre. Book now and enjoy fashion, art and Italian cuisine.
