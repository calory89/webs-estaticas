Best time to visit

Marrakech is an attractive destination year-round, though autumn (September to November) and winter (December to February) offer the most pleasant temperatures for exploring the city. Spring is also excellent. Summer months are quite hot. Check specific offers for your preferred dates.

What to do when you arrive

Marrakech is a fascinating city: the Old Medina, Jemaa el-Fnaa square, the Menara Gardens and the Gueliz neighbourhood are must-sees. Marrakech International Airport is about 6 km from the centre. From there you can take a taxi, bus or book a transfer. The city is perfect for a minimum of 2–3 days.
