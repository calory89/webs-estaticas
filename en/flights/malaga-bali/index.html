Best time to visit

Bali has two seasons: dry (April to October) and wet (November to March). The dry season brings less rain and better visibility for beaches and temples. Avoid Christmas and New Year weeks if you want fewer crowds. Check local festivals if you're keen to experience Balinese culture firsthand.

What to do when you arrive

Bali's international airport (Ngurah Rai) is 20 km from Denpasar. From there, choose between beaches like Seminyak or Uluwatu, or head inland to Ubud for rice paddies and temples. Bali is affordable, with plenty of budget accommodation and cheap local transport. Three or four days work for a first visit, though many stay longer.

FAQ

Does Ryanair fly direct from Málaga to Bali?

No. Ryanair on this route includes 3 stops. The journey is longer than a direct flight, but the price is far more competitive. Always check the exact stops when you book.

When is the best time to visit Bali?

The dry season (April to October) is ideal: less rain, clear beaches and good visibility for temples. Avoid peak tourist times like Christmas and New Year if you prefer quieter crowds.

What can you do in Bali on a tight budget?

Local transport is cheap, as is food and accommodation. Visit temples like Tanah Lot or Besakih, beaches in Seminyak, rice paddies in Ubud and waterfalls. Most attractions have very low entry fees or are free.
