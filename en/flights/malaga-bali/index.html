Best time to visit Bali

Bali has a tropical climate year-round. The dry season (April to October) is best: less rain, clear skies and perfect beaches. The wet season (November to March) brings rainfall but fewer tourists. Either works; choose based on your weather and crowd preferences.

What to do when you arrive at Bali airport

You'll land at Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS). From there, transport to your hotel is available: taxis, private transfers or public transport. Bali offers stunning beaches, ancient temples, rice fields, nightlife in Seminyak and Ubud's cultural scene. Plan at least 5–7 days to make the most of such a long journey.
