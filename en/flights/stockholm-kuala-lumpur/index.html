Best time to travel

Kuala Lumpur has a tropical climate year-round. The best season is November to March, with less rainfall and slightly cooler temperatures. Avoid June to September if you want to skip heavy monsoons. Travel in shoulder season (outside European school holidays) to find lower prices and fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive in Kuala Lumpur

Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) is about 75 km from the city centre. Once there, don't miss the Petronas Twin Towers, the Blue Mosque, traditional markets in Petaling Jaya, and the colonial Merdeka district. If you have time, take a trip to the Batu Caves or coastal beaches. Local food is excellent and very affordable.
