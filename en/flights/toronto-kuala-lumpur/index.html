Best time to travel

Kuala Lumpur is warm year-round, but November to February is best: less rain and pleasant temperatures. Avoid April and October if possible (monsoon season). Plan ahead for better prices and seat availability.

What to do in Kuala Lumpur

See the iconic Petronas Twin Towers. Don't miss Batu Caves (stunning Hindu temple), the old Chinatown district, and local markets. With extra days, head to coastal beaches or nearby islands. Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) is 60 km from the city centre; take the express train or shuttle to get in quickly.
