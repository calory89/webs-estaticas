Price and when to book

The lowest price we've seen is €143 return, though the typical range sits around €160. Book as early as you can: check daily for price drops. Airline and stops

SAS is your option on this route with 1 stop. There's no direct flight from Oslo to Larnaca, so expect a connection midway. A single stop isn't a hassle if the layover is short; check your booking for the stopover location and waiting time to plan your connection properly.

Best time to visit

Cyprus has sunshine year-round. December is still pleasant with 15–18°C during the day—perfect if you're escaping Nordic cold without midsummer heat. Beaches are quiet and prices lower than July–August. Spring (April–May) is another winner: ideal temperatures and fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in Larnaca (LCA), Cyprus's main gateway. Nearby you'll find beaches, the seafront Promenade with bars and restaurants, and the medieval fort. For more action, Limassol is an hour's drive away. Don't skip Nicosia (the capital) inland or the Troodos mountains if you fancy cooler scenery. The airport has public transport, taxis and car hire.

Larnaca's Promenade is great for walks, nearby beaches for relaxation, and the medieval fort for history. Limassol, Nicosia and the Troodos are within reach if you want more options during your stay.
