Best time to visit

Dubai enjoys year-round sunshine, but October to April offers the most pleasant weather (25–30°C). Summer is extremely hot. Budget travel in May to September may stretch further, though temperatures exceed 40°C. October and November strike a good balance between comfort and savings.

What to do when you arrive

Visit the Burj Khalifa and The Dubai Mall, explore Jumeirah beaches, or head to the historic Al Fahidi district for traditional architecture. Desert safaris and quad biking are minutes away. DXB airport connects easily to the city centre via taxi, metro and bus.
