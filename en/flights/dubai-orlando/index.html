Best time to travel

Orlando is a year-round destination. To dodge crowds, consider September to November or January to February. July and August are hottest and rainiest. December peaks with holiday tourism.

What to do when you arrive

Orlando is known for theme parks, but there's plenty more: airboat rides in the Everglades, Atlantic beaches under 2 hours away, shopping on International Drive, and nightlife on Church Street. MCO airport is 25 km from the city centre; hire a car for freedom of movement.
