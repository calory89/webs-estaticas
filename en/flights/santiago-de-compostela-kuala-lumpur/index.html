Best time to visit Kuala Lumpur

Kuala Lumpur has a tropical climate year-round. The best time to visit is between November and February, when there's less rain and the weather is more pleasant. June to September is the wettest season. If your goal is to save money, travelling outside European school holidays gives you more flexibility on prices. Avoid local festivities if you want to find accommodation at the best rate.

What to do when you arrive in Kuala Lumpur

You'll land at Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA). The city offers must-see attractions: Petronas Twin Towers, the Royal Palace, Batu Caves and night markets. To get around, use local public transport or taxi apps. The city centre is about 50 km from the airport, accessible by train, bus or taxi.
