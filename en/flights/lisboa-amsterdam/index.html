Best time to visit

Amsterdam is worth visiting any time, but November–February see fewer tourists and lower hotel rates (though flight prices don't always drop). Spring and autumn offer good weather and reasonable fares. Summer is peak season. December is special for Christmas markets but pricier overall.

What to do when you land

Amsterdam has it all: canals, world-class museums (Van Gogh, Anne Frank), bohemian neighbourhoods and cycling routes everywhere. The airport (AMS) is 20 minutes from the centre by train. Worth visiting: Zaanse Schans, Volendam, Edam. Eat well, try croquettes and stroopwafels.
