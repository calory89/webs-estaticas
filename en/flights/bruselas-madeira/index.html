Best time to visit

Madeira has mild weather year-round, but April to October offers the most sunshine and least rain. For warm temperatures and reasonable prices, travel in May, June or September. December and January are cooler but still pleasant compared to Brussels. Avoid February if you prefer dry weather.

What to do when you arrive

Madeira is a nature and adventure destination. Hiking the levadas (historic water channels), black volcanic sand beaches, exploring Funchal and its traditional market, and boat trips are must-dos. Cristiano Ronaldo Airport (FNC) is about 16 km from Funchal centre. Rent a car or use the bus to get around the island. Don't miss the botanical gardens and viewpoints overlooking the ocean.
