Price and when to book

Flights start from €160 return, though most hover around €204. Book as far in advance as possible: the earlier you purchase, the cheaper. Avoid last-minute bookings. Compare across different days of the week to find the best fares.

Airline and stops

Wizz Air UK operates this route with 1 stop. There are no direct flights Madrid-Riga in this offer. The stopover adds travel time, but the price makes up for it. Wizz Air is a low-cost carrier, so check what's included in your fare (baggage, seat selection, etc.).

Best time to visit

Riga shines in summer (June to September) with long days and pleasant temperatures. Winter is very cold but the city glows with Christmas lights. Spring and autumn are ideal if you want good weather without crowds. Book flights several weeks ahead to secure better prices.

What to do when you arrive

Riga International Airport is about 13 km from the centre. The Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage site with medieval charm perfect for strolling. Visit St Peter's Church, relax at Livu Square and walk along the Daugava riverside. Try local rye bread and Latvian beer. Riga is compact, 3 to 4 days cover the essentials.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Madrid to Riga?

Not in this offer. Wizz Air UK provides 1 stop on this route. It's standard for connections to the Baltic cities from Spain.

What's the cheapest price you've found?

€160 return. The median is €204, so it depends on your travel dates and how early you book.

What's included in a Wizz Air UK ticket?

It's a low-cost carrier. Check carefully: some fares include only a small cabin bag. Checked baggage and seat selection may cost extra.

What else is there to see in Riga besides the Old Town?

The Freedom Monument, the Art Nouveau district (the world's largest), the Occupation Museum and canal walks. The city is very walkable.
