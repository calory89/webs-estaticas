Best time to visit

Munich shines in autumn (September–October) and especially during winter for Christmas markets (November–December). Skip August: it's busy and hotels cost more. Spring (April–May) offers mild weather and fewer crowds. Check local events—beer festivals and other attractions might tie in nicely with your trip.

What to do in Munich

Marienplatz is the city's heart: Gothic town hall, churches, and local buzz. Neuschwanstein castle (the fairytale one) is 90 minutes by train. Historic beer halls like Hofbräuhaus are must-visits. Art museums and the Alte Pinakothek attract culture lovers. Englischer Garten is one of Europe's largest parks. The central station has excellent public transport links.
