Best time to visit

Palma and Lisbon enjoy pleasant weather most of the year. October and April–May offer mild temperatures with less tourist crowding than summer. Skip August if you want lower prices and fewer crowds. December brings rain to Lisbon, but the weather stays mild and prices are attractive outside the Christmas rush. No time difference between the two cities (both GMT/GMT+1).

What to do when you arrive

Lisbon deserves 3–4 days minimum. Wander through Alfama's narrow streets, climb to São Jorge Castle for river views, and visit the Jerónimos Monastery and Belém Tower. Eat pastéis de nata in any café, try grilled sardines, and explore the quirky Príncipe Real neighbourhood. Humberto Delgado Airport is 7 km from the city centre with good public transport connections.

How many days do I need to visit Lisbon?
