Price and when to book

The cheapest ticket we've spotted is 946€ return. Prices in our sample range from 946€ to 1,388€. The golden rule: book early to maximise your options for price adjustments. Long-haul routes like this one reward flexibility—shifting your trip by a week can easily swing prices by hundreds of euros.

Airline and stopovers

Iberia Express operates this route. All flights include 2 stopovers, so you're not getting a direct connection. This stretches your journey but is standard for transatlantic routes from the Canary Islands. Budget at least 15–20 hours total travel time including airport waits.

Best time to go

Bogotá's climate stays consistent year-round thanks to its equatorial location and high altitude. The best time depends on your budget and schedule. Cheaper flights often appear outside European and Colombian school holidays. Book well ahead if your date falls near Christmas or summer breaks.

What to do when you arrive in Bogotá

You'll land at El Dorado, Bogotá's main airport. The capital delivers world-class museums (Gold Museum, Botero Museum), vibrant neighbourhoods like La Candelaria, and easy day trips. The historic centre and Colombian cuisine are must-dos. From Bogotá, reaching other regions is straightforward if your return flight allows.
