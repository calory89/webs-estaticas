Fly from Palma to the Azores for from €242 return. The median price sits at €252. A perfect route to escape to an Atlantic archipelago without breaking the bank.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €242 return. The median sits at €252, so realistically you'll pay between €242 and €254 for a full ticket. Book as far in advance as possible: the earlier you purchase, the more cheap options you'll have. October and November typically offer good fares before prices climb in high season.

Airline and stopovers

Wizz Air Malta operates this route. Keep in mind the outbound journey includes 2 stopovers, so it's not direct. That's why the price is so competitive: you have intermediate stops, but you reach the Azores without paying direct flight prices.

Best time to visit

The Azores are enjoyable nearly year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer good weather without summer's tourist peak. Winter is wetter but less crowded. Summer is ideal if you don't mind sharing the islands with more visitors. Check fares regularly: prices drop in low season.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in Ponta Delgada (island of São Miguel). It's the main entry point to the Azores. From here you have world-class hiking (Caldera of Sete Cidades, Pico da Vara), thermal baths in Furnas, spectacular viewpoints and botanical gardens. The archipelago is perfect for nature, whale watching and getting away from the noise. Spend at least 3–4 days exploring the main island.

FAQ

The lowest price we've seen is €242 return with Wizz Air Malta. The median is €252, so that's what you'll likely pay if you book well in advance.

Is it a direct flight or does it have stopovers?

It's not direct. It has 2 stopovers on the outbound leg. That's why the price is so competitive compared to non-stop flights.

Which airline operates this route?

Wizz Air Malta is the carrier for this connection between Palma and the Azores.

What can you do in the Azores?

The Azores are ideal for hiking, thermal spring bathing, natural viewpoints and whale watching. The island of São Miguel (where you arrive) is the largest and most accessible. Plan at least 3–4 days to enjoy it without rushing.
