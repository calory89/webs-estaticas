Best time to visit

Dublin is charming year-round, though the weather is milder from May to September. Outside peak season (autumn and winter) you'll find cheaper flights and fewer crowds. These months are also perfect if you prefer a quieter trip.

What to do when you arrive in Dublin

Dublin Airport is 10 km from the city centre. You can take a bus, train or taxi. Don't miss Temple Bar, the Guinness Storehouse, Trinity College and the River Liffey. Dublin is compact and easy to explore on foot or by public transport.
