Price and when to book

The lowest price we've seen is €645 return. Prices vary: you'll find options at €645, €739 and €806. Booking well in advance is your best weapon—the earlier you secure your ticket, the better the fare. Avoid last-minute purchases; check regularly to spot price drops.

Airline and stopovers

You'll travel with China Eastern Airlines with 2 stopovers on the outbound flight. It's not direct, but it's the most affordable option on this route. Bear in mind that layover times can vary; allow a buffer if you have tight connections. China Eastern is a solid airline with a good reputation on Asian routes.

Best time to visit

The Philippines has a tropical climate: dry from November to May, and wet from June to October. December to February are ideal—pleasant temperatures and less rain. Avoid typhoon season (July-October) if you want stability. These months also tend to have higher prices, so balance climate and budget.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Ninoy Aquino International Airport (MNL), about 7 km from Manila. The city offers Intramuros (the old quarter), museums, nightlife and local cuisine. Nearby are Tagaytay (volcano and views), Boracay (picture-postcard beaches) and Palawan (for divers). With 2 stopovers, you might want to rest on day one before exploring.
