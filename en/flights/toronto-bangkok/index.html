Flights from Toronto to Bangkok starting at €931 return. The median price is €1,184. With 2 stopovers on the outbound, this route connects Canada to the heart of Thailand affordably.

Price and when to book

Prices range from €931 to €1,193 in the sample checked on 23 October 2026. The median sits at €1,184. Book early: long-haul international flights usually have better availability and prices 2–3 months ahead. Watch your travel dates; small changes can make a difference.

Airlines and stopovers

Porter Airlines operates this route with 2 stopovers on the outbound leg. The example flight departs 14 November at 18:25 and returns 18 November at 19:55. Stopovers extend total journey time but often keep prices lower than direct flights. Confirm stopover airports and connection times with the airline.

Best time to visit

Bangkok is warm year-round. The dry season (November to February) is most comfortable: moderate temperatures and less rain. Avoid September and October, the wettest months. Travel outside local Thai holidays if you want better accommodation availability. Demand varies by Canadian holidays and school breaks.

What to do when you arrive in Bangkok

You'll land at Suvarnabhumi International Airport (BKK), 25 km from the city centre. The Golden Buddha temple, Royal Palace, floating markets and the Chao Phraya river are must-sees. Bangkok offers world-class cuisine, traditional Thai massage and nightlife. From the city you can easily reach Phuket, Pattaya or southern islands.
