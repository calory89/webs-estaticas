What to do when you arrive

Orlando is the theme park destination par excellence: Walt Disney World, Universal Studios and SeaWorld concentrate most attractions. You can also enjoy pools, shopping at The Mills (outlet), fine dining and nightlife. The airport (MCO) is about 25 km from the centre; rent a car or use transfers to move between attractions. Plan your itinerary in advance to make the most of your stay.
