Best time to travel from Málaga to Orlando

Orlando is a prime winter destination (November to March) when the weather is mild and theme parks less crowded than in summer. Avoid July and August due to intense heat. Christmas and New Year are peak season with higher prices. Travel between October–November or January–February is typically more affordable and comfortable.

What to do in Orlando on arrival

Orlando is the world capital of theme parks: Walt Disney World, Universal Studios and SeaWorld are the main attractions. Beyond these, the historic downtown offers galleries, bars and restaurants. Natural lakes in the area invite water activities. Orlando International Airport (MCO) is about 20 km from the city centre; car rental or public transport get you to your destination quickly.
