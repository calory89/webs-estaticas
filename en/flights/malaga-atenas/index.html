Best time to visit

Athens is a year-round destination. If you want to avoid crowds and catch better prices, travel between mid-September and late November, or January to February. The weather is mild all year, though December to February is cooler. May to September is peak season and pricier.

What to do in Athens

The Acropolis and Parthenon are must-sees. Explore Plaka's cobbled streets, visit the Ancient Agora, the Acropolis Museum and grab a coffee in Monastiraki. Cape Sounion, home to the Temple of Poseidon, is an hour by bus away. Saturday markets are lively and local food is excellent and affordable.
