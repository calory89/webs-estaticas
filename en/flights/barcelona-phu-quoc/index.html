Best time to visit Phu Quoc

Phu Quoc is a beach destination year-round, but the best season is November to April: dry weather, less rain and pleasant temperatures (25–30°C). Summer (May to October) brings the monsoon, with more rainfall. December and January see peak tourism. For fewer crowds, travel in November or March–April. Today's search is October; you have a window to book trips for late 2026 or spring 2027.

What to do in Phu Quoc

Phu Quoc is Vietnam's largest island, known for pristine beaches, national parks and diving. Visit Bai Sao beach, Phu Quoc National Park for hiking, or northern fishing villages and markets. The main tourist hub is Duong Dong. You can snorkel, take boat trips, and sample local cuisine at nearby floating markets. It's a relaxing and affordable destination once you arrive.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Barcelona to Phu Quoc?

No. Available flights on this route have 2 stopovers. Direct flights from Barcelona to Phu Quoc are not offered by major carriers; connections are the norm for Asia-Europe travel.

Is Shenzhen Airlines reliable?

Yes. Shenzhen Airlines is China's national carrier, approved by CAAC (Civil Aviation Administration of China). It has operated international routes for decades with certified safety standards. While less known in Europe, it is safe and regularly operates long-haul flights.

Which airport do I arrive at in Phu Quoc?

Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC) is the gateway. It's about 30 km north of the main tourist area of Duong Dong. Taxis and buses connect the airport to the centre. It's a modern, well-linked facility.

Can I find cheaper prices at other times?

Prices vary with demand. Booking well ahead (2–3 months) is usually worth it. Low season (May–October) may have occasional deals, but weather is poorer. If your date is flexible, compare prices across different periods before confirming your booking.
