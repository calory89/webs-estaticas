Best time to visit

Zanzibar is a year-round destination, but June to October is the dry season with clear skies and less rain. December to February is also good, though warmer and more humid. Avoid April and May, when rainfall is heaviest.

What to do when you arrive

Stone Town is the historic heart with Arab and Swahili architecture. Northern beaches (Nungwi, Kendwa) and eastern shores (Paje, Jambiani) are stunning. Don't miss the spice market, coral reefs for diving, and a trip to nearby Pemba island. Abeid Amani Karume Airport is 50 km from Stone Town.
