Best time to visit

Brussels is pleasant year-round, though October to March can be cold and rainy. Spring (March–May) and early autumn (September–October) offer nice weather without peak tourist crowds. For lower prices, midweek travel usually beats weekends, and flying outside school holidays always helps the wallet.

What to do when you arrive

Brussels awaits with the Grand Place, a UNESCO World Heritage site, and the Manneken Pis. Taste Belgian chocolate, craft beer and the best waffles. The Magritte Museum, the Atomium and the neighbourhoods of Ixelles and Saint-Gilles are worth exploring. From Brussels, day trips to Antwerp, Bruges or Leuven by train are quick and cheap.

Three to four days covers the main sights: Grand Place, museums, neighbourhoods and food. If you add trips to Bruges or Antwerp, add one to two days more. It depends on your pace.
