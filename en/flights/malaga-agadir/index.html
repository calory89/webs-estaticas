Best time to visit

Agadir has mild weather almost year-round. November to March are ideal: pleasant temperatures (20–25°C) and fewer crowds than summer. Skip July and August if you don't handle heat well. The route runs all year, so choose based on your climate preference and availability.

What to do when you arrive

Agadir is a beach and relaxation destination. Stroll the Corniche, enjoy the beaches, visit the port and try local Moroccan food at restaurants. For more activity, Essaouira is just over an hour's drive away. The airport is 20 km from town; reach it by taxi, bus or car hire.
