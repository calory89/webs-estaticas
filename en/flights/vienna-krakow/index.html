Best time to visit

Vienna and Krakow are worth visiting almost any time of year. Winter (November to February) is cold but festive; spring and autumn offer pleasant temperatures. Prices tend to be lower outside peak season (July-August and Christmas), so aim for October, November or April for better fares.

What to do when you arrive in Krakow

Krakow is a medieval gem. Walk through the Main Market Square, explore St. Mary's Basilica and climb the Town Hall Tower for 360° views. Wawel Castle dominates the skyline; spend at least half a day there. Don't miss the Jewish Quarter of Kazimierz, full of cafés and galleries. With more time, Auschwitz is 70 km away and unmissable.
