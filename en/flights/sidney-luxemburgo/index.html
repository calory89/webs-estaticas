Best time to visit

Luxembourg is worth visiting year-round. If you prefer to avoid extreme cold, travel between May and September. Winter months (December to February) offer a festive atmosphere and fewer tourists. Check flight calendars regularly to spot price drops.

What to do when you arrive

Luxembourg is small but charming. Explore the Old Town (UNESCO World Heritage), visit the Grand Duke's Palace and walk across some of Europe's most famous bridges. Public transport is free nationwide. Make time for the Ardennes region or Moselle wine cellars nearby.
