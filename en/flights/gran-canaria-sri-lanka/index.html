Prices start from €703 for return flights. The median hovers around €777. We've seen fares up to €848. While we can't predict exact price drops, booking 6–8 weeks ahead typically improves your budget. Long-haul routes with stopovers need more planning, so locking in your ticket early is wise.

Best time to travel

November is promising: our sample price was for 11 November. Sri Lanka enters its dry season on the east coast then, though the northeast remains wet. If you want to dodge crowds and find moderate prices, transition months like October and November offer good value. Always check weather patterns before booking.

What to do in Colombo and Sri Lanka

You'll arrive at Bandaranaike Airport (CMB), 30 km from Colombo. The capital is your gateway to ancient temples, tropical beaches and tea mountains. From there explore Sigiriya, southern beaches or the Cultural Triangle. Sri Lanka is compact: a week covers most highlights. Cost of living is very low, perfect for extending your trip.
