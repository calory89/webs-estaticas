Best time to visit

Warsaw is lovely year-round, but autumn (September-October) and spring (April-May) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is spectacular if you enjoy cold and snowy streets, especially around December. Avoid July and August if you prefer fewer tourists and lower prices outside peak season.

What to do when you arrive

Warsaw deserves at least 2-3 days. The Old Town (Stare Miasto) is a UNESCO World Heritage site. Stroll the Royal Route, visit the Museum of the Warsaw Uprising, eat piernik in a traditional milk bar, and relax in parks along the Vistula River. Chopin Airport (WAW) is 10 km from the city centre; bus, train or taxi are affordable options to get in.
