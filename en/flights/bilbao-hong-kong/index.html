Best time to visit

Hong Kong has a subtropical climate. Skip summer (June to September) due to extreme heat and humidity. The sweet spot is October to April: pleasant temperatures and less rainfall. January is mild, dry winter in Hong Kong—ideal for sightseeing. Book well ahead, especially if you're travelling during these popular months.

What to do on arrival

Visit Victoria Harbour for the iconic skyline, historic temples like Man Mo, vibrant neighbourhoods such as Mong Kok and Causeway Bay, night markets, and savour local cuisine. Hong Kong is compact and easy to explore with efficient public transport.
