Price and when to book

The cheapest ticket we've found is €493 return (checked 23 October). Prices in our sample range from €493 to €689. Don't rush: booking earlier (ideally 2–3 months ahead) almost always pays off. Check daily if your dates are flexible—transatlantic fares can swing noticeably in just a few days.

Airline and stopovers

TAP Portugal operates this route with 1 stopover, meaning one intermediate stop before reaching London. It's not direct, but the price difference makes up for it. A typical TAP stopover from New York adds roughly 2 to 4 hours to your total journey. Always check your booking for details about where you stop.

Best time to travel

New York and London are worth visiting year-round. Autumn (September to November) and spring (April to May) bring pleasant weather in both cities and typically lower fares than summer. Winter months (December to February) can surprise you with occasional deals, though weather is harsher. Avoid school holiday weeks if you're hunting for cheap prices.

What to do when you arrive in London

You'll land in the London area. The capital offers world-class museums (British Museum, National Gallery), iconic markets like Camden and Borough, West End theatres, Tower Bridge and Buckingham Palace. Jump on the London Eye for 360-degree views. The famous red double-decker buses and Underground metro get you everywhere quickly and affordably.
