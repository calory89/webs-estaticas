Best time to travel

Vienna shines in autumn (September-October) and winter (December-February), especially for Christmas markets. Spring (April-May) offers mild weather and blooming parks. Skip July-August if you prefer fewer crowds. Check prices regularly: fares can dip or climb based on demand and seat availability.

What to do when you arrive

Vienna awaits with its enchanting old town: St Stephen's Cathedral, Schönbrunn Palace and Hofburg Palace are must-sees. Stroll the first district on foot, taste Viennese apple strudel, enjoy the Danube, and don't miss the Christmas markets if travelling in winter. Vienna International Airport (VIE) is 16 km from the centre; metro and buses connect well to the city.
