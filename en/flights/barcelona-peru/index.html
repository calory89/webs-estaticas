Price and when to book

The best price we've found is €676 return. The median sits at €719, so you've got room to negotiate. The earlier you book, the better: purchasing weeks ahead is always cheaper than waiting until the last minute. Barcelona to Lima is a long-haul route, so avoid deciding in the final month.

Airline and stops

You'll travel with Avianca. The outbound flight has 1 stop, it's not direct, but that's standard on this route from Barcelona. The layover adds hours to your journey, but it's the trade-off for keeping fares low. Always check total flight time in your search.

Best time to travel

Peru is worth visiting year-round. The dry season (May to October) is ideal for trekking to Machu Picchu or exploring the jungle. June and July see peak tourism in the Andes. For fewer crowds, try April or November. Flight prices don't drop by season on intercontinental routes, so pick based on weather and what you want to see.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in Lima, the capital. Explore the historic centre, visit Miraflores, taste Peruvian cuisine. From there, take domestic flights to Cusco for Machu Picchu or head to the Amazon. Lima is your gateway, don't skip it.
