Best time to travel

January, as in the sample, is a solid choice to avoid crowds and secure competitive prices. Summer months and peak holidays (Christmas, Easter) push fares up. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) offer good weather and moderate prices in Ireland. Skip December if you're looking for savings.

What to do when you arrive in Dublin

Dublin airport is 10 km from the city centre: reach it easily by bus or taxi. The city blends medieval history, traditional pubs and vibrant nightlife. Don't miss Trinity College, Guinness Storehouse, the River Liffey and bohemian neighbourhoods like Temple Bar. A week gives you time to explore the south coast (Wicklow) or nearby cities such as Galway.
