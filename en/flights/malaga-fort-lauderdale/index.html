Best time to travel

Fort Lauderdale offers sunshine year-round. Northern winter (December to February) is peak season: comfortable temperatures and fewer hurricanes. June to September is hurricane season, but prices are lower. Plan based on your budget and tolerance for heat and humidity.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Fort Lauderdale International (FLL), gateway to Broward's beaches. Explore Fort Lauderdale's sands, visit Miami Beach (30 km away), or discover the Everglades. The area offers diverse food, nightlife and water activities. Tropical climate lets you enjoy yourself any time of year.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for Málaga to Fort Lauderdale?

From €688 return with Air Europa. This is the lowest recorded price. Average sits around €707, so expect to pay roughly that on most dates.

Does Air Europa fly direct from Málaga to Fort Lauderdale?

No, the route includes 2 stops on the outbound leg. That's why the price is competitive. Although you'll spend more time travelling, the savings beat direct flights by a wide margin.

Which airport do I arrive at in Fort Lauderdale?

Fort Lauderdale International Airport (FLL). It's about 30 km from Fort Lauderdale city centre and connects easily to Miami Beach and the whole Broward area by car or public transport.

When is the best time to visit Fort Lauderdale?

December to February gives perfect weather: 20–25°C and low rain risk. If you're after cheaper prices, consider June to September, though it's hotter and hurricane season runs through this period.
