Best time to visit

Bulgaria enjoys summer from June to September with warm weather and ideal beaches. If you prefer fewer tourists and more peace, spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) are excellent. Winter is cold but perfect if you want to ski in Bansko. Always check prices for your target dates: they vary greatly by season.

What to do in Sofia

Bulgaria's capital offers Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, spectacular Orthodox churches and art museums. Stroll the historic centre, try local cuisine in markets and relax in cafés. From Sofia you can take trips to Rila Monastery or Vitosha mountains. Bulgaria remains affordable: your money goes much further than in other European capitals.
