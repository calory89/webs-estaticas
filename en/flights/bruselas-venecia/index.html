Best time to visit

Venice is a year-round destination, but skip peak season if you want quieter streets and steadier prices. Spring and autumn offer pleasant weather without summer chaos. Winter brings occasional flooding to some squares, but prices drop significantly and the city breathes easier with fewer tourists.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Marco Polo, 13 km from the centre. Take the water bus (vaporetto) or coach towards St Mark's Square. Walk the canals, enter St Mark's Basilica (paid entry), cross the Rialto Bridge and wander the backstreets. Eat in local bacari, not on the plaza. Venice is best enjoyed on foot, without rushing.
