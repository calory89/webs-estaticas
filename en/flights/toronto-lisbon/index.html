Best time to visit Portugal

Lisbon has pleasant weather most of the year, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: mild temperatures and less peak tourism than summer. If you want beaches and packed terraces, July and August deliver, though with bigger crowds. Winter is peaceful and cool, perfect if you don't mind the odd shower.

What to do when you arrive in Lisbon

Wander Alfama, the historic neighbourhood with cobbled lanes. Climb Castelo de São Jorge for panoramic views. Try Pastéis de Nata at any bakery. Take a sunset cruise on the Tagus. Visit Mosteiro dos Jerónimos and Torre de Belém. Ride tram 28, it's iconic. Eat bacalau à Brás and drink Douro wine in Ribeira restaurants.

What should I see in Lisbon in 3 days?

Day 1: Alfama, Castelo de São Jorge, tram 28. Day 2: Belém (Mosteiro dos Jerónimos, Torre de Belém, Pastéis de Nata). Day 3: Bairro Alto, Rossio, nightlife or a beach day at Costa da Caparica. Don't miss the views from Miradouro de Santa Justa.
