Best time to visit

Marrakech is enjoyable almost year-round. Avoid the hottest months (June to August), when temperatures exceed 35°C. Ideal is October to April: mild weather, sunny days and cool nights. November is particularly recommended: pleasant climate and fewer crowds than peak season.

What to do on arrival

Marrakech offers plenty in a compact space. The ancient medina with its souks, Jamaa el-Fna square buzzing with life, historic palaces (Bahia, Badi) and gardens (Menara, Majorelle) are essential. Menara airport is 5 km from the centre, easy taxi or bus access. Spend at least 3 days to explore at a relaxed pace.
