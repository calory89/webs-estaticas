Best time to visit

Dubrovnik is beautiful year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer good weather without peak summer crowds. Prices tend to be more reasonable outside July and August. Winter is low season and often has very competitive fares.

What to do in Dubrovnik

The walled old town is essential: walk the walls, explore the harbour, visit the cathedral. The Dalmatian coast offers beaches, coastal towns and sailing opportunities. Dubrovnik Airport is about 20 km from the centre, well served by bus and taxi.
