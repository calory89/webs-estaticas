Best time to visit Brussels

Brussels is pleasant year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild weather and less rain. Winter months are colder and greyer, though less crowded. July and August see more tourists and higher prices. Check availability based on your flexible schedule.

What to do on arrival in Brussels

After landing at Brussels airport (Zaventem), the historic centre is 30 minutes by train. The Grand Place (Grote Markt) is a must: medieval architecture ringed by cafés. Try Belgian beers, moules marinières and Belgian fries with sauce. The European Quarter, art museums and the Atomium are other classic stops without stretching your budget too far.
