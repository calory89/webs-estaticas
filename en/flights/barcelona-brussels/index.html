Best time to visit

Brussels is lovely in spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) with pleasant weather. Winter is cold but perfect for Christmas markets. July-August brings rain and crowds, so skip Easter holidays if you prefer peace and quiet.

What to do on arrival

Don't miss the Grand Place (UNESCO World Heritage), the Manneken Pis statue and the Magritte Museum. Eat Belgian waffles, try local craft beer and wander the medieval quarter. Brussels is compact: you can explore it on foot in 2-3 days. Use public transport or rent a bike like the locals do.
