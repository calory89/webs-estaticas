Best time to visit Zanzibar

Zanzibar has a tropical climate. The dry season from June to October offers warm weather and less rain. December to February is hot and humid. Prices tend to spike during European school holidays (December, Easter, July-August), so if you can travel in shoulder season (March-May, November), you'll likely find better fares.

What to do in Zanzibar when you arrive

Stone Town is unmissable: walk its historic narrow streets, visit the Sultan's Palace and the spice market. Beaches at Nungwi and Kendwa in the north are perfect for unwinding. Dive or snorkel at nearby reefs. Pemba Island is ideal if you want more wilderness. Don't miss Jozani National Park to spot the endemic red colobus monkey.
