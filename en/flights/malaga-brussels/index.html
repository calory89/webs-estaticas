Best time to visit

Brussels shines in spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) with mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter brings Christmas markets and lighter tourism. Avoid July–August if you dislike peak season crowds. Demand and prices shift by season; check regularly.

What to do when you arrive

Brussels offers the iconic Grand Place surrounded by guildhalls, Manneken Pis, the Atomium, and world-class museums. Eat authentic Belgian waffles and beer. Brussels Airport (BRU) connects well to the centre: train, bus or taxi in 20–40 minutes. Spend 2–3 days to explore properly.
