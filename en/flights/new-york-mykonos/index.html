Best time to visit

Mykonos is peak season June to September, when prices skyrocket. To save, consider May, October or November: decent weather, fewer crowds and lower fares. Avoid December–February unless you enjoy cool weather and closed businesses. Check the forecast before you book.

What to do when you arrive

Mykonos is known for beaches, nightlife and whitewashed villages. The airport is 2 km from the port and 15 km from the capital. Rent a car, take a taxi or use local buses to get around. Don't miss Paradise Beach, Little Venice sunsets, Boni Windmill and Ano Mera village. The island is small: you can see the highlights in 3–4 days.
