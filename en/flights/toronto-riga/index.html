Best time to visit

Riga shines in summer (June to August) with long days and pleasant temperatures. Autumn (September-October) is also lovely with fewer tourists. Winter is cold but magical if you enjoy snow. Avoid spring unless you're keen on muddy streets and unpredictable weather.

What to do when you arrive

Riga's Old Town (Vieille Ville) is a Unesco site: explore medieval lanes and historic churches. Don't miss the Freedom Monument, art nouveau buildings along Kronvalda Avenue, and the Central Market (Tirgus Laiki). Sample fresh herring, drink Latvian beer, and unwind on the beaches of the Gulf of Riga.
