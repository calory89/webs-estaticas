Best time to visit

Bali shines year-round, but has two main seasons: dry season (April to October) with clear skies and pleasant temperatures, and wet season (November to March) with rain and humidity. Dry season attracts more tourists and prices rise. For fewer crowds and better value, try the shoulder months (March-April and September-October).

What to do in Bali

Dream beaches in Seminyak and Canggu, ancient temples like Tanah Lot and Ubud, rice terraces, mountains for trekking, and vibrant culture. Eat well, surf, do yoga or just unwind. Living costs are very low compared to London, so your budget stretches much further once you arrive.
