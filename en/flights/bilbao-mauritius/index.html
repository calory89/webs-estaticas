Best time to visit

Mauritius has a tropical climate year-round. The driest and most pleasant season runs from May to November, ideal for avoiding heavy rainfall. The cyclone season is November to May. Plan your trip based on your preferred weather and flight availability.

What to do when you arrive

Mauritius is known for white-sand beaches, coral reefs and cultural diversity. Mauritius International Airport (MRU) connects to the island's centre. From there you can explore Port Louis, visit the Pamplemousses Botanical Garden or enjoy northern beaches. The island also offers hiking, diving and marine wildlife spotting.
