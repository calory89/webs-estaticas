Best time to visit

Charleston is pleasant year-round, but spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) offer the most comfortable weather. Winters are mild and snow-free, perfect for a January trip like our example. Summers are warm and humid, so skip June–August if you dislike heat. Off-season travel often means cheaper flights too.

What to do when you arrive

Charleston is a gem of the American South: stroll the Historic District, explore King Street's art galleries, enjoy local food at Market Hall, or tour nearby plantations. The waterfront is perfect for leisurely walks, and Folly Beach is just 15 minutes away. Don't miss the colonial churches and museums documenting the city's history.
