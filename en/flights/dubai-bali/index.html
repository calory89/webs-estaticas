Best time to visit

Bali has a tropical climate year-round. The dry season (April to October) is ideal if you prefer less rain. The wet season (November to March) attracts fewer tourists and prices sometimes dip, but expect more rainfall. Choose based on your tolerance for heat and crowds.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Bali's international airport (Ngurah Rai, DPS). From there, buses, taxis and car rentals take you to areas like Seminyak, Ubud or Kuta. Beaches, ancient temples, rice fields and volcano trekking are some of the main draws. Three to four days minimum is worth it to enjoy the island without stress.
