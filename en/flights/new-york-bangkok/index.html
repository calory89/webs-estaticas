Best time to travel

Bangkok is best visited between November and February, when the weather is dry and pleasant. Avoid May to September if you dislike heavy rain. Prices tend to be steadier outside peak tourist seasons; check regularly to spot occasional drops.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK), 25 km from Bangkok. From there, take a taxi, bus or train to the centre. Don't miss the Grand Palace, Buddhist temples, Damnoen Saduak floating market and street food. Bangkok is a city that captivates: spend at least 3-4 days there.
