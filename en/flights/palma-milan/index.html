Best time to fly to Milan

Milan is an urban destination with pleasant weather year-round. October and May are ideal: mild temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. February brings Carnival if you're into festive atmosphere. Avoid August if you'd rather skip peak tourist season. Prices tend to spike during high season, so spring and autumn are your friends.

What to do in Milan when you arrive

Milan is more than fashion: the Cathedral (Duomo) is stunning, the Vittorio Emanuele Gallery is a must-walk. Santa Maria delle Grazie houses Leonardo's Last Supper. For art lovers, the Pinacoteca di Brera and Castello Sforzesco museums are worth your time. The Navigli area is perfect for dinner by the water.
