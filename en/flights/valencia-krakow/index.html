Price and when to book

Prices range between €65 and €94 depending on availability. The best strategy is to book as far in advance as possible: the earlier you buy, the lower the fares. Wizz Air's low-cost tickets work this way. Check daily for offers, but don't wait too long if you spot a price that suits you.

Airline and direct flight

Wizz Air operates this route. The journey is direct from Valencia (VLC) to Kraków (KRK), with no stopovers or changes. You depart in the afternoon and arrive early the next morning. Hand luggage is included; checked baggage is charged separately.

Best time to travel

Kraków is beautiful in spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October): mild temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is cold but magical if you enjoy snow. Avoid July and August if you're looking for lower prices and fewer tourists. February, when these example flights depart, often has good fares.

What to do when you arrive in Kraków

Kraków International Airport (Balice) is 15 km from the city centre, well served by bus and taxi. In the city, visit the Main Market Square, St. Mary's Basilica and Wawel Castle. Stroll through the Jewish Quarter (Kazimierz) and try local food: pierniki (pastries) and żurek (rye soup). Auschwitz is also 60 km away if you're interested.
