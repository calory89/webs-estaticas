What to do when you arrive

Agadir airport (Driss el Harti) is 20 km from the city. Agadir beach is the main draw: 10 km of sand with a seafront promenade, restaurants and shops. Visit the kasbah, explore the medina, or day-trip to Essaouira (100 km). The fishing port has authentic vibe and fresh seafood. Hire a car to explore nearby beaches and the Anti-Atlas mountains.
