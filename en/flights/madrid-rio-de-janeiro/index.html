Best time to visit

Rio de Janeiro works year-round, but it has a tropical climate. Keep in mind summer (December to March) is hot and rainy; winter (June to August) is milder and drier. Plan based on your weather preference and how busy you'd like it to be.

You'll land at Rio de Janeiro International Airport (Galeão). From there, reach the city centre by bus, metro or taxi. The city offers beaches like Copacabana and Ipanema, Tijuca National Park, Christ the Redeemer statue and the lively Lapa neighbourhood. There's something for every budget.
