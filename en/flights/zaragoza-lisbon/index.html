Best time to visit

December is low season in Lisbon: fewer crowds, leisurely walks and cheaper accommodation. The weather is cool but pleasant for sightseeing. If you want more sun and warmth, aim for May or September; prices will creep up slightly. Skip July and August if you're after peace and quiet.

What to do when you arrive

Lisbon deserves at least 3 days. Take the ferry across the Tejo from Belém, explore the colourful tiles of Alfama, taste custard tarts at Confeitaria de Belém, and grab coffee on a riverside terrace. With more time, hop a local train to Cascais or Sintra.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Zaragoza to Lisbon?

No. Vueling flights include 2 stops, so factor in that extra time. Plan for 4-5 hours total journey time from departure to arrival.

What's the cheapest price for this route?

The lowest we've recorded is €142 return. Most deals sit around €145–149, so if you spot something near €142, don't wait: they vanish fast.

Which airline flies Zaragoza to Lisbon?

Vueling operates this route with the lowest fares. Check their baggage allowance and change policies before you book.

What should I see in Lisbon over a weekend?

In 2–3 days, cover the historic centre, Belém and Alfama. Eat in local tascos, ride tram 28 and cross the Tejo. If time allows, take a train to Sintra; it's worth the trip.
