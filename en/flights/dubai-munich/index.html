Best time to visit Munich

November, like the example, is excellent: temperatures drop to 10–15°C, heat fades, and tourist crowds thin out compared to summer. December brings the iconic Christmas markets at Marienplatz, stunning but pricier. Spring (April–May) offers mild weather and city in bloom, though flights rise. Avoid July–August: heat, queues, and steep fares.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Munich Airport (MUC), about 28 km from the city centre. The S-Bahn train takes you straight downtown in 40 minutes. Don't miss Marienplatz, the Cathedral of Our Lady, and the English Garden. If you visit in November, the city feels more relaxed and museums less packed. Bavarian beer and local food await at every corner.
