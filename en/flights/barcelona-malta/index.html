Price and when to book

Barcelona-Malta flights start from €42 return. Sample prices range from €42 to €50, with a median of €45. Book early: prices generally rise closer to departure. If your schedule allows, search for weekday dates to spot lower fares.

Ryanair direct, no hassle

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight: no stops, no aircraft changes. The journey is short and straightforward. Bear in mind that Ryanair charges for large hand luggage and checked bags, so review their baggage policy before purchasing. If you travel with just a small backpack, your final price stays low.

Best time to visit Malta

Malta shines in spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October): fine weather, less extreme heat than summer, and pleasant beaches. Skip July and August if you dislike intense heat. Winter is mild but cloudier. Book ahead to secure the best prices during shoulder seasons.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Malta International Airport (MLA), about 5 km from Valletta. Spend time in the capital, a Unesco World Heritage site, with its baroque churches and harbour views. Golden Bay and Ghajn Tuffieha beaches are perfect for a swim. Don't miss Gozo island, known for its cliffs and the famous Azure Window (partially collapsed).
