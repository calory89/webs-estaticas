Best time to visit

Colombia welcomes visitors year-round. The dry season in the Andean region (December to March and July to August) offers the best weather. Keep in mind that travelling in peak season pushes flight prices higher. Plan ahead if you're flying during those months.

What to do in Bogotá

La Candelaria is the historic heart: Gold Museum, Botanic Museum, colonial squares. Monserrate offers city views from the mountains. Zona Rosa has dining and nightlife. Explore local markets in neighbourhoods like Usaquén.
