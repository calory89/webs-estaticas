Price and when to book

Flights from Copenhagen to Dubai start at €288 return. Most tickets fall between €300 and €349. The earlier you book, the better: prices climb as departure approaches. Check regularly if you have flexible dates, as small day shifts can save or cost you money.

Airline and stops

You'll travel with Pegasus, which operates this route with 1 stop. It's not a direct flight, but the price makes up for the intermediate landing. Bring a portable charger and keep documents on your phone for the stopover. Always check what your fare includes: Pegasus sells stripped-down tickets, so baggage or seat selection may cost extra.

Best time to visit

Dubai is a beach and city destination year-round. Weather is most comfortable from November to March, when it's not too hot. In summer (June-August) temperatures exceed 45°C, but prices drop. Avoid Ramadan if you prefer more freedom to eat and drink at regular times.

What to do when you arrive

Dubai offers white-sand beaches (Jumeirah, Palm Beach) and urban attractions (Burj Khalifa, The Dubai Mall). Don't miss the old Al Fahidi district, spice and gold souks, or a stroll through Marina. Taxis and metro are cheap and convenient from Dubai International Airport (DXB).
