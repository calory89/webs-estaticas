Price and when to book

Prices start at €483 per person return. The median sits at €543, with sample fares at €569. Book in advance (we're currently at 22 October 2026): the sooner you reserve, the better rates you'll get. November flights offer solid fares before high winter season kicks in.

Airline and stops

Iberia Airlines operates this route with one stop. That means one intermediate landing, not a direct flight, but it's standard for connecting Seville to New York via a European hub. Check your booking to see where the connection is so you can plan your time.

Best time to travel

November is a solid window: New York weather is mild (13–18°C), perfect for walking around, and it's just before Black Friday deals. Skip summer (pricey and humid) and December–January (premium rates for Christmas). For deals, travel October–November or April–May.

What to do when you arrive in New York

You'll land at one of NYC's area airports (EWR, LGA, or JFK). From there, Manhattan awaits: Times Square, Central Park, Brooklyn Bridge are essential. Use the subway, grab pizza anywhere, visit world-class museums. A week covers the basics; ten days is better if you can.
