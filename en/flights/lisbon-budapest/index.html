Best time to visit

Winter (January-February) offers the cheapest fares, though it's cold. Spring (March-May) and autumn (September-October) balance decent weather and reasonable prices. Avoid summer if you want a bargain: July and August are peak season. Budapest is beautiful year-round.

What to do when you arrive in Budapest

Ferenc Liszt Airport is 16 km from the city centre. Take the bus, train, or taxi. Once downtown, explore the Danube, soak in Széchenyi Thermal Bath, climb Buda Castle, and wander the Jewish Quarter. Eat goulash, drink local Hungarian wine. Budapest is cheap: eat and drink well without breaking the bank.
