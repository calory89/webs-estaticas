Best time to visit

Hong Kong works year-round, but November and December (autumn–winter) bring the best weather: temperatures between 15–25°C and lower humidity than summer months. These seasons also align with local festivals and cultural events. Avoid July and August if you dislike extreme heat and humidity.

What to do when you arrive

Hong Kong delivers the Victoria Peak lookout with 360° views, Victoria Harbour ferries, historic neighbourhoods like Stanley and Causeway Bay for shopping. The international airport (HKG) connects to the city centre by train in 24 minutes. Explore traditional temples, eat at local dim sum stalls, and soak up the blend of modernity and heritage that defines the city.
