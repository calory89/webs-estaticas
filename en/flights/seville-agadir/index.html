Best time to visit

Agadir has pleasant weather almost year-round, but October to April offers the most comfortable conditions: mild temperatures and calm seas. July and August are scorching. Prices climb during school holidays, so aim for shoulder season to save money.

What to do when you arrive

Agadir is all about beach and relaxation: the bay is perfect for swimming and strolling the promenade. Visit the medina and fish market at dawn, enjoy the seafront at sunset. If beaches bore you, nearby fishing villages and the Anti-Atlas interior offer authentic exploration.

What should I know before travelling to Agadir?

It's a safe, tourist-friendly coastal city. The airport (Agadir–Al Massira) is 20 km from centre, reachable by taxi or shuttle. Bring sunscreen: the sun is intense year-round. Beaches are the main draw, though the interior is worth exploring too.
