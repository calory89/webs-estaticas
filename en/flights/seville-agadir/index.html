Best time to visit

Agadir offers beaches and warm weather year-round. October to April is ideal: comfortable temperatures without extreme heat. Skip July and August if you dislike intense heat. Rain is minimal, so choose based on heat tolerance, not weather concerns.

What to do when you arrive

Agadir is all about beaches: the Agadir Bay offers 9 km of sand. Walk the Kasbah, explore the medina and harbour. Nearby Essaouira's coast is worth a day trip. Souks are essential for local food and handicrafts. It's a laid-back beach destination, not a monument-heavy city.
