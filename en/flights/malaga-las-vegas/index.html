What to do when you arrive in Las Vegas

You'll land at Harry Reid International Airport (LAS). From there you have easy access to the Strip with casinos and major hotels. Walk it or take the monorail. You can also visit downtown Las Vegas (Fremont Street), catch shows, or take day trips to the Grand Canyon or Red Rock Canyon if time allows.
