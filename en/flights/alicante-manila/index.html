Best time to visit

The Philippines has a tropical climate. The dry season and best time to visit is from November to May, when there is less rain. These months typically see higher demand, so prices may be higher. If you're looking to save, consider travelling during the rainy season (June to October), though the weather will be more humid and unstable.

What to do when you arrive in Manila

Manila welcomes you with a blend of modernity and tradition. Stroll through Intramuros to explore colonial history, visit Rizal Park to relax, or enjoy local cuisine in the city's markets and restaurants. Don't miss the National Museum and nearby temples. From Manila it's easy to make excursions to beaches and towns in the region.
