Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route. The good news: all flights are direct, with no intermediate stops. Milan-London is a quick connection that saves you time in transfers. Note that Ryanair flies from Milan's airports (Malpensa or Bergamo) to London's (Stansted, Luton or Gatwick, depending on the date).

Best time to travel

November is an excellent season: mild temperatures in both cities and fewer crowds than summer. London in autumn-winter has a special atmosphere with parks and museums less packed with tourists. Milan is pleasant year-round, but November lets you skip the summer heat and peak season.

What to do on arrival

In London don't miss Big Ben, the Palace of Westminster, the British Museum (free entry) and a stroll along the Thames. If you have days in Milan before departure, the Duomo Cathedral, Sforza Castle and fashion shopping in Brera are must-sees. With a direct flight you have more time to enjoy both cities.

FAQ

From €42 return with Ryanair, according to our data. Book well in advance to secure these fares, as they increase closer to the departure date.

Is Milan-London a direct flight?

Yes, completely direct. Ryanair flies with no stops between these two destinations, saving you travel time.

What to see in London with limited time?
