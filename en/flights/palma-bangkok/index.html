Price and when to book

The cheapest ticket we found is €633. The median price is €842, with options up to €980. These are full return fares. As with any international route, book as early as possible: long-haul flights rise in price closer to departure. Check regularly for price changes.

Airline and stops

You'll fly with Ryanair, offering this route with 1 stop. It's not direct, but the price makes up for it. One stop is standard on such long routes from Spain; you'll get a chance to stretch your legs before continuing to Bangkok.

Best time to go

Thailand is a destination year-round, but mind the climate. November to February is most comfortable (less rain, moderate temperatures). The rainy season runs May to October. Book ahead regardless of season; international flights booked weeks in advance tend to be cheaper.

What to do when you arrive in Bangkok

Landing at Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK), you have direct access to the capital. Visit the Grand Palace, temples (Wat Phra Kaew, Wat Arun), floating markets, Chinatown, or relax at spas. Bangkok mixes tradition and modernity: ancient shrines alongside shopping malls and nightlife. Transport is cheap and easy to navigate.

The Grand Palace, Buddhist temples (Wat Phra Kaew, Wat Arun), floating markets, Chinatown, shopping malls and nightlife. Bangkok has museums, local food and lively street culture.
