Price and when to book

The lowest recorded price is €125 return, though most searches hover around €152. We also found options at €208. Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route with one stop—it's not direct. The example flight departs Manchester on 15 November at 19:05 with an intermediate stop and returns from Tallinn on 20 November at 12:55. Total travel time typically ranges from 6–8 hours depending on the connection airport. Always check exact durations and terminal changes, as Ryanair sometimes uses secondary airports.

Best time to visit

Tallinn shines in summer (June–August) with long days and mild weather, but it's peak season and pricier. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer good conditions with fewer crowds and lower fares. Winter (November–February) is cold and dark, though prices drop significantly and the city has charm during the festive season if you travel in December. Any time works; choose based on your budget and preferred climate.

What to do upon arrival

Lennart Meri Tallinn Airport (TLL) is 4 km from the city centre. You can reach it by bus, taxi or on foot if travelling light. The medieval Old Town is essential: city walls, cobbled streets and charming squares. Visit Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, stroll the harbour district and eat at local markets. Tallinn is compact; you'll see the highlights in 2–3 days. Accommodation and food prices are very reasonable compared to the rest of Europe.
