Best time to travel

Cape Verde enjoys a tropical climate year-round. November to June offer milder temperatures and less rainfall. Avoid September and October if you prefer drier conditions. Travelling in low season may help you find better prices.

What to do in Isla de Sal

Isla de Sal is famous for white sand beaches and crystal-clear waters, perfect for diving, kitesurfing and snorkelling. Santa Maria is the main village with shops, restaurants and nightlife. Don't miss Salinas de Pedra Lume, a unique salt lagoon. The international airport (SID) is just 10-15 minutes from the island.
