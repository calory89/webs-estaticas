Nassau awaits from €902 return with Iberia Express. Prices average around €911. A long journey but affordable to escape to the beaches of the Bahamas.

Price and when to book

The cheapest flight costs €902 return; the median sits at €911 with options at €919. These prices vary by availability and travel date. Book as early as possible: aim for 2–3 months ahead to secure competitive fares on a long-haul route like this one.

Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Iberia Express. The outbound flight includes 3 stopovers, making the journey long but affordable. Example: departure from Ibiza on 8 January at 09:05 and arrival in Nassau the same day. Return departs 12 January at 17:30.

Best time to go

Nassau shines from November to April, when the weather is dry and warm (25–28°C). Avoid June to October if you prefer to skip hurricane season. January is ideal: escape European cold and enjoy Caribbean sunshine, though it's peak season so prices may rise outside special offers.

What to do on arrival

Lynden Pindling International Airport is about 25 km from the centre. Nassau offers white-sand beaches (Paradise Island, Cable Beach), reef diving and the famous blue holes of nearby islands. The historic downtown deserves an afternoon: Straw Market, Fort Fincastle and British colonial architecture are must-sees.

FAQ

What is the cheapest price Ibiza to Nassau?

The base price is €902 return. Sample prices range from €902 to €919 depending on dates and availability. The earlier you search, the better your chances of finding this rate.

Is it a direct flight from Ibiza to Nassau?

No, the flight includes 3 stopovers. It's a long journey (full routing exceeds 12 hours), but it's Iberia Express's option from Ibiza to the Caribbean.

Which airline operates this route?

Iberia Express operates these flights. It's Iberia's low-cost subsidiary, which is why prices are competitive even with stopovers.

When is the best time to visit Nassau for weather?

November to April offers the best conditions: dry weather, pleasant temperatures (25–28°C) and no hurricane risk. January combines good weather with the chance to find deals if you book well in advance.
