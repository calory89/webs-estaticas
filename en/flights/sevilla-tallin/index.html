Best time to visit

Tallinn is spectacular at Christmas: the Old Town fills with festive markets and lights from November to January. If you prefer milder weather, May to September offer gentler temperatures and longer daylight. Bear in mind winter is peak season, so book with plenty of notice.

What to do when you arrive

Tallinn's Altstadt (Old Town) is a UNESCO World Heritage site: medieval walls, towers, cobbled streets and stunning churches. Climb Toompea for 360-degree views. Explore digital-era Estonia or discover local museums. Lennart Meri Tallinn Airport is about 4 km from the centre with good bus connections.
