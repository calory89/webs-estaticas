What to do when you arrive in Crete

You land at Heraklion airport, the perfect entry point. From there, explore Knossos (Minoan ruins), Balos beaches or Pink Lagoon. Wander through Chania or Rethymno, charming coastal towns with history. Taste local food: cheeses, dakos, Cretan wine. Hire a car for freedom to explore.
