Best time to visit Tallinn

Tallinn shines from May to September, when days are long and the climate mild—perfect for walking. For fewer crowds, April and October work well with steadier prices. Winter is cold but snow and northern lights create a magical atmosphere if you can handle the temperatures.

What to do in Tallinn

The Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage site: wander its cobbles, visit Alexander Nevsky Cathedral and climb Kiek in de Kök tower for panoramic views. The medieval centre is perfect for getting lost between cafés and local shops. Winter brings a stunning Christmas market. Beyond the centre, St Brigitta's Convent and the Baltic forests are worth a day trip.
