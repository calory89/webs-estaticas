Best time to visit

Dublin appeals year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Avoid December–January if chasing lower prices, though Christmas in Dublin has its charm. Travel in low season to maximise savings.

What to do when you arrive in Dublin

You'll land at Dublin Airport, roughly 10 km from the city centre. Train, bus or taxi get you downtown in 20–40 minutes. Visit Temple Bar, walk along the Liffey, visit the Guinness Storehouse and stroll through Trinity College. Dublin is compact and walkable, or use public transport.
