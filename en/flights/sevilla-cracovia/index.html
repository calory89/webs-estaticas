Best time to travel

Krakow shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather, fewer crowds than summer, and steadier prices. Winter brings snow and intense cold, but if you love Christmas magic, December's markets are worth it. July and August are pricey and packed with tourists.

What to do on arrival

Balice airport is 18 km from the centre. Head to the Market Square (Rynek Główny), the historic heart of the city. Walk through the Jewish Quarter, climb up to Wawel, try zurek (rye soup) at a local restaurant. The city is small, very walkable and prices are low. Spend at least 3 days to enjoy it properly.
