Best time to go

Ireland welcomes visitors year-round, but spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) offer better weather and less rain. Summer is peak season with higher fares. Winter is cheaper but features short days and cold temperatures. Weigh your priorities: budget versus climate comfort.

What to do when you arrive

Dublin is less than 10 km from the airport with easy access to the centre: bus, taxi, or hire car. The capital delivers medieval history, traditional pubs, world-class museums, and vibrant nightlife. From Dublin you can explore the coast (Howth, Dun Laoghaire) or venture inland (Wicklow, Galway).
