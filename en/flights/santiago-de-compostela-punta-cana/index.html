Best time to visit

Punta Cana has warm weather year-round. Peak season runs December to March (less rain, busier). If you want fewer crowds and slightly lower fares, May to November works, though rainfall odds rise. Plan based on your heat tolerance and crowd preference.

What to do on arrival

Punta Cana is known for white-sand beaches and resorts. Beyond sunbathing, try the Blue Lagoon, scuba diving, Isla Saona, or coastal towns. The airport is modern and well-connected with transfers to hotels. Budget accordingly, it's a luxury tourist zone.
