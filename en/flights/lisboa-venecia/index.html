Price and when to book your flight

Base price starts at €78, but the median sits around €116. We've seen flights up to €130 in our sample. For short routes between Portugal and Italy, booking as early as possible works best: 2–3 months ahead puts you in a stronger position. Prices tend to spike in peak season (summer and public holidays), so if you have flexibility, travel outside peak times.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with direct flights—no stopovers. You board in Lisbon and land directly in Venice with no transfers. Ryanair runs a low-cost model, so check what's included in your ticket (baggage, seat selection, etc.) before confirming to avoid surprises at the desk.

Best time to travel

Venice is beautiful year-round, but summer is the priciest and most crowded. If you want cheaper flights and fewer crowds, aim for autumn (September–October) or spring (April–May). Winter offers the best value, though you'll face more rain and unpredictable high-water conditions.

What to do when you arrive in Venice

You'll land at Marco Polo Airport. From there, catch a bus, water shuttle, or private transport into central Venice (about 30 km). Once in the city, stroll St. Mark's Square, cross the Rialto Bridge, explore the canals by gondola, or climb the Basilica di Santa Maria della Salute. Water transport is the norm: a multi-day travel pass saves you money.
