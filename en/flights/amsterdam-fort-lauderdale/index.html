Airlines and stops

easyJet operates this route with 3 stops, so it's not direct. This contributes to the attractive price but does extend your total journey time. Check where the connections are when you book so you can manage your layovers and keep tabs on your hand luggage.

Best time to visit

Fort Lauderdale shines in winter (November to April): warm beaches, low humidity and low hurricane risk. January gets busy with Europeans escaping the cold, so expect higher fares. For better value, consider May or October when the weather is still lovely but fewer tourists arrive. Summer (June–October) is hurricane season—prices drop but the meteorological risk is real.

What to do on arrival

Fort Lauderdale is your gateway to the Everglades, broad beaches and vibrant nightlife. It's home to the world's second-largest cruise port, perfect if that's your thing. Downtown shopping and Sawgrass Mills outlet centre are popular. Prefer nature? Book an Everglades tour. Beach lovers should head to Deerfield Beach or Las Olas Boulevard.

FAQ

Is €765 the final price or are there hidden fees?

The price shown usually includes airport taxes and basic fees, but always check the full breakdown in easyJet's checkout. Checked baggage, flight changes and seat selection cost extra and can push the total up noticeably.

Does this easyJet route have mandatory stops?

Yes, this route includes 3 stops—it's not a direct flight. Verify the connecting airports when you book so you know how much time you'll spend on the ground at each layover.

What's the cheapest month to fly to Fort Lauderdale?

We can't say for certain when prices drop, but European winter (January–February) brings higher demand and fares. Spring (March–April) or early autumn (September–October) often offer better value for money without sacrificing weather.

What can I do in Fort Lauderdale besides beaches?

Explore the Everglades on alligator tours, stroll Las Olas Boulevard, visit the Fort Lauderdale Museum of Art or shop at outlets. It's also a major cruise port, so you could combine your visit with a Caribbean cruise if you fancy it.
