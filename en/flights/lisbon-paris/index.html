Best time to visit

Paris shines year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter can be cold but the city looks magical dusted in snow. Skip July–August if you want to avoid peak season and higher prices.

What to do when you arrive

Paris awaits: Eiffel Tower, Louvre, Notre-Dame Cathedral, strolls along the Seine, café culture and French cuisine. You'll land at Paris–Charles de Gaulle or Paris–Orly airport. Quick public transport links take you to the city centre. Budget at least 3–4 days to see the highlights without rushing.
