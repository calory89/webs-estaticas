What to do when you arrive on Isla de Sal

Sal is pure beach: Santa Maria and Ponta Leme are the main draws. Snorkelling, diving, kitesurfing and dune walks are classic activities. The island is small (24 km²) but packs plenty in. The airport links well to the hotel zone. Try peixe à sal (salt-crusted fish) and soak up Cape Verde's laid-back vibe.
