Best time to travel

December to April is ideal: dry weather, pleasant temperatures (23–26 °C), and calm seas. Avoid August and September (rain and extreme heat). February offers a good balance between fine weather and competitive fares.

What to do when you arrive

Isla de Sal is known for white-sand beaches, turquoise water, and diving. Santa Maria is the most touristy town; Pedra Lume offers caves and natural pools. Visit the Arquipelago Natural Park and enjoy water sports or simply relax on the beach.
