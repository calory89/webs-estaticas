Best time to visit Hamburg

Hamburg appeals year-round. Autumn and spring offer mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Colder months (November to February) are cheaper but pack warm clothes. If you fancy Christmas markets, travel in late November or early December. Book ahead: fares rise as the date approaches.

What to do when you arrive in Hamburg

Hamburg is Germany's second city with plenty of charm: canals, the historic port and Speicherstadt architecture are must-sees. Stroll through St. Pauli, eat fresh herring at Fischmarkt and grab a drink on the Elba waterfront. The airport (HAM) is about 9 km from the centre, well served by train and bus.
