Finnair connects New York with Helsinki on direct flights from €570 return. The median price sits at €583. An under-the-radar route that gets you straight to Finland's capital in just over 9 hours.

Price and when to book

Prices range from €570 to €680 depending on availability. For this route, booking 2–3 weeks ahead is standard practice. The sooner you lock in your dates, the better: long-haul flights from New York tend to climb as departure approaches, especially during peak season or school holidays.

Finnair: your direct carrier

Finnair is the sole operator on this route and offers non-stop flights between New York and Helsinki. The Scandinavian airline has a solid reputation for punctuality and on-board service. With no stops, you save both time and hassle—unless you're deliberately hunting a connection to cut costs.

Best time to travel

Helsinki shines in summer (June to August), when nights are nearly endless and the weather is mild. If you fancy winter, December brings Christmas markets and snowy landscapes, though daylight is scarce. Prices are typically keener in low season (January–March and September–October). The route runs year-round.

What to do when you arrive

You'll touch down at Vantaa Airport, 20 km from the city centre. Helsinki greets you with the White Cathedral, Uspenski Cathedral, and the design-forward Punavuori neighbourhood. Walk the coastline, visit the UNESCO-listed Suomenlinna fortress, or soak in a Finnish sauna. Public transport is seamless: tram, bus, and metro get you everywhere.
