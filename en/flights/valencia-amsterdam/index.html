Best time to visit

Amsterdam is attractive year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) typically offer the best balance of pleasant weather and reasonable prices. Avoid peak summer weeks (July–August) and the Christmas period, when flights get expensive. January and February also offer good prices, though it's colder.

What to do when you arrive

Amsterdam is small and easy to explore. Walk along the canals, visit the Van Gogh Museum or Anne Frank House, rent a bike like the locals, or dive into world-class museums. The airport (AMS) is 9 km from the centre and well connected by train, bus or taxi. With a morning flight from Valencia, you'll have the whole afternoon ahead.
