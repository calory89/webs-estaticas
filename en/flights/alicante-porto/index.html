Price and when to book

The cheapest ticket in the sample is €38, but you'll also find options at €42 and €76. Book early for the best fares. Travel midweek if you can: weekends tend to be pricier. The route was searched in October for November availability, so plan 2–3 weeks ahead to secure a low price.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair is the option here, and it's straightforward: the outbound flight is direct (0 stopovers). Departure from ALC at 07:05, arrival at OPO at 08:10 on the return flight. No stops, no plane changes.

Best time to visit

Porto is pleasant year-round. For good weather and less rain, pick May through September. Autumn (October–November) and spring are also ideal, with fewer crowds. Winters are mild but greyer. Be flexible with your dates: there's often a price difference between Tuesday and Friday flights.

What to do in Porto

Explore Ribeira, stroll across the Dom Luís II bridge and visit Porto wine lodges in Vila Nova de Gaia. Clérigos Tower, Livraria Lello (one of the world's most beautiful bookshops) and Portuguese tiles around every corner will impress you. Don't miss a night in Baixa or try francesinha, an irresistible local sandwich.
