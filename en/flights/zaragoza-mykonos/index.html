Best time to visit

Mykonos offers beaches, nightlife and events year-round, but July and August are peak season (busy and pricey). May, June, September and October deliver good weather without the crowds or high-season fares. Prices genuinely drop November to March, though the sea is cold. December can be a nice surprise if you want festive parties with less chaos.

What to do in Mykonos

Fine-sand beaches (Psarou, Paradise, Super Paradise), whitewashed Old Town with tavernas, windsurfing at Kalo Livadi, walking trails to Armenistis lighthouse. Sunsets at the harbour are free and stunning. You'll arrive at Mykonos Airport (JMK), about 2 km from town.
