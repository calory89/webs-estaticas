Best time to travel to Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka is a year-round destination, but the best season depends on which area you're visiting. The west and south coasts are driest from November to March. The central highlands stay cool throughout the year. Avoid monsoon season if you prefer less rain. Check the climate for your specific destination before booking.

What to do in Colombo and surrounding areas

Colombo is your gateway to Sri Lanka. Walk along Galle Face Green, visit Buddhist temples like Kelaniya Raja Maha Vihara, and explore the old quarter. From Colombo, it's easy to reach nearby beaches, the central highlands with Kandy and Nuwara Eliya, or the south towards Mirissa and Unawatuna. Rent a car, use local trains or buses to explore.
