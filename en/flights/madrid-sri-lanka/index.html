Best time to visit Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka has two monsoon seasons: May to September on the west coast and October to April on the east coast. The best time depends on where you're headed: the south and west coasts are ideal from December to March, whilst the north and east are better from May to September. Travel between November and February for dry weather and sunny days.

What to do in Colombo and beyond

Colombo is your gateway to Sri Lanka. Explore the colonial Fort district, walk along Galle Face Green overlooking the Indian Ocean, and sample local cuisine at the markets. From there, it's easy to reach beaches like Mirissa, Buddhist temples like Kandy, and tea plantations in Nuwara Eliya by train or bus. Sri Lanka fits into two weeks and every corner is worth your time.
