Best time to visit

November to April is ideal for Maldives: dry weather, calm seas and perfect beach conditions. December and January tend to be pricier due to European school holidays, but the weather is at its best. Consider travelling in May or June if you're after better value, though there's more rain. Avoid July to October, which coincides with the southwest monsoon.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Maldives International Airport (MLE) on Hulhumalé island. From there, take a speedboat or seaplane to your resort. The islands offer snorkelling, coral reef diving, luxury spas and pristine white-sand beaches. Most resorts include transfers in their packages. Spend time exploring the coral reef, joining a night tour to see bioluminescence, or simply unwinding in an overwater villa.
