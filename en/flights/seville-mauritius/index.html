Best time to visit

Mauritius is a year-round destination, but the best season is May to November, with less rain and more pleasant temperatures. Avoid December to March (cyclones and higher humidity). The trip we're showing you departs in November, an ideal time. Prices don't drop drastically in any particular season, but booking in advance (2–3 months) is usually key.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport (MRU), about 65 km from the capital, Port Louis. The island offers white-sand beaches, coral reefs, Parliament Mountain and Port Louis's historic centre. Rent a car to explore the coast, visit Île aux Cerfs or relax in the Indian Ocean waters. Local cuisine blends African, Indian and French influences.
