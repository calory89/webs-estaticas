Best time to visit

Marrakech offers ideal conditions from October to April, with mild temperatures and dry weather. Avoid July and August due to intense heat. Prices tend to be more competitive during low season; travel between November and February for better fares.

What to do upon arrival

Marrakech awaits with the iconic Jemaa el-Fnaa Square, the historic Medina, Majorelle Gardens and the Koutoubia Mosque. Wander through the souks, taste local cuisine in traditional riads and restaurants, or take a trip to the Atlas Mountains. Menara Airport is 6 km from the city centre, accessible by taxi or bus.
