Best time to visit

Seoul shines in autumn (September to November) and spring (March to May), with mild temperatures and clear skies. Winter is cold but stunning if you want snow and ice skating. Avoid the rainy monsoon season (June to August). Every season has its appeal, just plan based on what experience suits you best.

What to do when you arrive

Seoul is a blend of old and new: ancient temples alongside glass towers, late-night clubs in Gangnam and traditional markets like Myeongdong. Taste street food, climb Namsan peak, walk through Gyeongbokgung Palace. Incheon airport has fast metro and bus links to the city centre. Seoul is safe, clean and easy to navigate for travellers.
