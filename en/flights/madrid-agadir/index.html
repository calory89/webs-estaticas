Price and when to book

Madrid-Agadir flights start from €42 return, with a median price of €54. Our tracking records fares of €42, €54 and €77 on different dates. Airline and flight type

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight with no stopovers. It departs Madrid and lands directly in Agadir in just over 2 hours. With Ryanair, note that cabin baggage is included, but checked luggage costs extra. Priority boarding and other services are also paid additions. Check your fare conditions before you buy.

Best time to visit

Agadir is a beach destination with pleasant weather most of the year. October to April offer comfortable temperatures (20-25°C) and less heat than summer. June to September is peak season with hot temperatures (30°C+). To fly cheap without sacrificing good weather, aim for November, March and April: you'll find lower prices and perfect conditions for beach and city life.

What to do in Agadir

Agadir is known for its 9 km urban beach, perfect for relaxation. The Kasbah of Agadir offers panoramic views of the city and bay. Explore the medina, try Moroccan cuisine at local restaurants, and catch the fishing port at dawn. For nature lovers, the nearby Souss-Massa National Park is ideal for bird watching and walks. Agadir is also a base for desert excursions and trips to nearby coastal villages.
