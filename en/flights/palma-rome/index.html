Best time to visit

Rome is a year-round destination, but October, November, March and April offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Prices tend to spike in July and August. Winter (December–February) can surprise with bargains if you're comfortable with mild temperatures (8–12°C). Avoid Easter week and Christmas if you're hunting budget flights.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Rome Fiumicino or Ciampino. The historic centre is 25–30 km away: train, bus or taxi are your options. Don't miss the Colosseum, Roman Forum, Vatican and Trevi Fountain. Rome deserves a minimum of 3 days. Tourist cards (Roma Pass) include transport and museum entry. Eat at local trattorias away from the centre to save money.
