Best time to travel

Puerto Rico welcomes visitors year-round. April to June and September to November tend to be more budget-friendly. Avoid December and January if seeking lower prices, that's peak season. Summer (July–August) also sees higher flight demand.

What to do in San Juan

Old San Juan blends colonial architecture, galleries and restaurants. Fort El Morro and Castillo San Cristóbal are must-sees. Nearby you'll find Caribbean beaches, the Bioluminescence of El Yunque Tropical Forest and Río Piedras neighbourhood with local character.
