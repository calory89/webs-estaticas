Best time to visit

Malaysia has a tropical climate year-round. The driest season on the peninsula is June to August, though some areas like east Borneo run best from April to October. Skip the heavy rain season if outdoor activities matter to you. Peak season (December–January) pushes prices up, so consider travelling outside that window.

What to do when you arrive

Kuala Lumpur is your gateway to Malaysia. See the Petronas Towers, the Royal Palace and the Blue Mosque. From here, it's easy to reach Penang, Melaka or Langkawi island. For nature lovers, Taman Negara is one of the world's oldest tropical rainforests. Local transport is cheap and reliable: monorail, taxi and buses cover the entire area.
