Connect Madrid with London from €48 return. The typical price range sits around €65, with options up to €76 depending on dates. Ryanair operates this route with direct flights.

Price and when to book

Flights start from €48 return. The standard range is between €48 and €76. Book 4–6 weeks ahead: earlier reservations mean lower fares. Trips in January through early February tend to be cheaper than peak season.

Airlines and stops

Ryanair operates this route with direct flights from Madrid to London, no intermediate stops. Examples: departure at 21:25 with next-day arrival, or return at 06:15. No transfers means a quicker journey.

Best time to travel

Winter (January–February) offers the lowest fares, though expect cold and rainy weather. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) balance decent weather with moderate prices. Avoid July–August if you're price-hunting: peak season drives costs up.

What to do when you arrive in London

Big Ben, Tower Bridge, the British Museum and Buckingham Palace are must-sees. The Tube (underground) connects the airport to the centre in 15–45 minutes. Westminster, Camden Market and the royal parks are worth your time. Buy a Travelcard for cheaper public transport.

FAQ

Madrid flights arrive at London's main airports, typically Stansted, Luton or Gatwick, depending on the flight. All connect to the city centre via public transport (Tube, bus or train) in under an hour.
