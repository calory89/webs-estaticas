Best time to visit

Phu Quoc is a tropical destination. November and December are ideal months for pleasant temperatures and less rain. These months also tend to have less extreme humidity than May to September. Check the local climate before travelling to pack appropriately.

What to do when you arrive

Phu Quoc stands out for its white-sand beaches (Long Beach), tropical forest and marine life. Phu Quoc Airport (PQC) is the main entry point. From there you can access resorts, diving excursions, pearl farm visits and strolls through local markets. The island is compact, perfect for exploring by motorbike or organised tours.

Are there direct flights from Seville to Phu Quoc?

No. The outbound route includes 3 stopovers, which means two plane changes. It's an economical but long connection. If you prefer fewer stops, check alternative dates or airlines.

What is there to see in Phu Quoc besides the beach?

The island has jungle, nature reserves, pearl farms, local temples and traditional markets. Long Beach is the main beach. Many accommodation providers offer activity packages. Allow at least 4-5 days to explore without rushing.
