Best time to visit

Phu Quoc is a tropical destination with warm weather year-round. The best time to visit is November to April, with less rainfall and milder temperatures. The sea is also calmer during these months for water activities. Avoid May to October if you want to guarantee good weather, as this is monsoon season.

What to do when you arrive

Phu Quoc is an island with white-sand beaches, crystal-clear waters and a laid-back atmosphere. Enjoy diving, snorkelling, visits to fishing villages and pearl farm tours. Duong Dong is the main hub with services, local markets and restaurants. It's also worth exploring Ong Lang Beach and visiting local temples to experience Vietnamese culture.
