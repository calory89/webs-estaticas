Best time to visit

Berlin is worth visiting any time of year, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer better weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter prices dip, though January and February are cold. December is expensive due to Christmas.

What to do when you arrive

Berlin deserves 3–4 days minimum. Visit the Wall, Brandenburg Gate, Museum Island, Checkpoint Charlie and street markets. Public transport is reliable: grab a tourist pass. Eat in Kreuzberg and explore the city's alternative scene.
