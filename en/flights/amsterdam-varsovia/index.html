Best time to visit Warsaw

Warsaw shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather, fewer tourists and clear skies. Winter is cold but magical if you fancy snow. Summer (June–August) brings crowds and higher prices. Late-year flights (like the December example) often come with good fares because it's not peak tourist season in Warsaw.

What to do when you arrive in Warsaw

Warsaw has a stunning rebuilt Old Town—a UNESCO World Heritage site. Don't miss the Palace of Culture, the Uprising Museum, and walks along the Vistula. Chopin Airport is 10 km from the centre: fast train, bus or taxi all straightforward. It's a compact, walkable city where 3–4 days covers the essentials.
