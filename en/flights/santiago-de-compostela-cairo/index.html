Best time to visit

October, November, February and March are ideal: pleasant weather (20–25°C) without summer heat. Avoid June to September if you can't handle temperatures above 35°C. December and January are cool but also peak season. Book ahead regardless of when you travel.

Things to do on arrival

Cairo International Airport (CAI) is 20 km from the city centre. The Pyramids of Giza, Egyptian Museum and Saladin Citadel are must-sees. Walk through Khan El-Khalili bazaar and sail the Nile at sunset. Cairo is intense: pace your activities and enjoy local food in traditional cafés.
