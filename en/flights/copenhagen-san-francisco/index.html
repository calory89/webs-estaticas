Best time to visit

San Francisco is a year-round destination. Summer (June–September) is peak season with higher prices and heavy tourism. Autumn (September–November) and spring (March–May) offer good weather and lower fares. Winter (December–February) is the cheapest period, though fog and cloudy days are common. December and January are cold but clear.

What to do when you arrive

San Francisco International Airport (SFO) is 21 km from the city centre. Easy access to the Golden Gate, Alcatraz, Mission District and major tourist areas. The city is best explored on foot or by tram. Don't miss the Embarcadero, Chinatown and the charming Victorian townhouses.
