Price and when to book

The lowest price on record is €889 return. The median sits at €1,247, and prices can reach €1,277 during peak demand periods. Book as far in advance as possible: fares tend to increase as your travel date approaches. Avoid purchasing within the last 14 days before departure if you're looking for the best deals.

Airline and stopovers

You'll travel with Iberia Express. This route includes 1 stopover. Example flights depart Gran Canaria on 11 November 2026 at 21:30 and return on 22 November at 19:10. Stopovers allow for better prices, making this a solid option if you're budget-conscious.

Best time to visit

Peru appeals year-round, but the dry season (May to October) is ideal for trekking and outdoor activities, especially if you plan to visit Machu Picchu. November to April brings more rain but fewer tourists and lower prices. Check fares regularly: prices shift based on weekly demand.

What to do when you arrive in Lima

You'll land at Jorge Chávez International Airport in Lima. The city offers world-class museums of pre-Hispanic art, world-leading gastronomy, and colonial neighbourhoods like Barranco. From here, you can easily reach Machu Picchu, the Sacred Valley, Nazca, or the Amazon. Lima is the perfect starting point to explore Peru.
