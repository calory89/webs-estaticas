Best time to visit

Sal enjoys good weather almost year-round, but the dry season (November to July) is ideal. The example flight (13 November) falls right in that window, when rainfall is sparse and sunshine dominates. August to October can be more humid, though still worth visiting. Avoid the Christmas rush if you prefer fewer crowds.

What to do on Sal Island

Sal is perfect for dreamy beaches and water sports. Santa Maria, in the south, is the main tourist town with restaurants and nightlife. Try windsurfing and kitesurfing in sheltered bays, visit Pedra Lume Salt Lagoon (a historic saltwater lake), or simply lounge on white sands. The international airport connects easily to the tourist hub.
