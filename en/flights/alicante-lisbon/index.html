Best time to travel from Alicante to Lisbon

Lisbon is attractive year-round, but skip July and August if you want fewer crowds. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) offer good weather without the masses. Low prices like these €46 fares often show up in low season, so grab them when you find them rather than waiting for the "perfect time."

What to do in Lisbon

Straight from the airport, explore Belém for the Jerónimos Monastery and Belém Tower. Climb up to São Jorge Castle for 360° views of the city, stroll through the shops and taverns of Baixa Pombalina, and don't miss the Tagus at sunset from any viewpoint. The historic tram line 28 is a tourist classic.
