Best time to visit Hong Kong

Hong Kong has a subtropical climate. October to April offer pleasant temperatures and less rainfall. December and January are ideal if you want to avoid the extreme summer heat. Book well in advance as these dates attract high demand and prices rise. July and August are cheaper but very hot and humid.

What to see and do in Hong Kong

Hong Kong is a vibrant metropolis with spectacular viewpoints (Peak Tram), traditional neighbourhoods (Central, Mong Kok), historic temples and world-class cuisine. A stroll along Victoria Harbour is essential, as is exploring street markets and iconic transport like the Star Ferry. Lantau Island, the Thousand Steps Route and the Tian Tan Buddha are popular visits. Spend at least 5-7 days to enjoy the city without rushing.
