Best time to visit

Jacksonville has a subtropical climate that's pleasant almost year-round. The coolest months are November to February, whilst summer (June to August) is very hot and humid. Hurricane season runs from June to November, so keep this in mind. Autumn and winter trips offer the best balance between weather and availability.

What to do when you arrive

Jacksonville is the largest city in the continental US by area. Enjoy beaches like Jacksonville Beach, explore the historic riverside district, visit museums and galleries, or relax in nearby parks. For nature lovers, state parks are just minutes away. Jacksonville International Airport (JAX) is well connected to the city centre.
