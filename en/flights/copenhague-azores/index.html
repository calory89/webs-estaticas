Cheap flights Copenhagen to Azores: from €248

248 € returnTypical price: 278 €See flights and dates ✈️

Copenhagen to Azores starts at €248 return with Ryanair. Median prices hover around €278, so you'll find good deals across most dates. A weekend or week on the islands without breaking the bank.

Price and when to book

The cheapest ticket hovers around €248. Prices don't leap much: between €248 and €284 you'll find most options. Book early: Ryanair's cheap seats go fast, especially January and February. Midweek (Tuesday–Thursday) tends to be cheaper than Friday and Sunday.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with 1 stopover. It's not a direct flight, but the price makes up for it. The stopover adds journey time, though it's standard for this route. Carry-on baggage is free; checked luggage costs extra.

Best time to visit

The Azores work well year-round. January and February often have competitive fares (though more rain). Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: mild weather and fewer tourists. Summer is pricier and busy. Prices also drop in low season: November and December.

What to do when you arrive

Ponta Delgada airport (PDL) lands you on São Miguel, the largest island. Crater walks, hiking, hot springs, black-sand beaches and fishing villages await. Ferries connect the islands if you want to explore Terceira, Pico or Faial. Car hire is handy for getting around.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Copenhagen to Azores?

No. This route includes 1 stopover, which is standard for Copenhagen–Azores. The price (from €248) makes it worthwhile.

How far in advance should I book to get the €248 price?

The earlier the better. Ryanair releases cheap fares weeks or months ahead, but they fill quickly. Look with flexible dates in low-season months (autumn–winter) to spot bargains.

What does the Ryanair ticket include on this route?

One small carry-on bag (40×20×25 cm). Checked bags and other backpacks cost extra. Check fees when booking; Ryanair charges each add-on separately.

When is the best time to visit the Azores?

May to September is dry and warm, though pricier. October and November remain good with fewer crowds. Winter (December–March) is rainy, but prices drop and tourism is lighter.
