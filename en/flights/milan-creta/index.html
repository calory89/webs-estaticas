Airlines and stops

easyJet operates this route direct: no stops, no plane changes. Departure from Milan (terminal varies by booking), landing direct in Heraklion. Straight journey, no surprises or layover waits.

Best time to visit

Crete shines May to October: warm temps, clear skies, perfect beaches. April and November are solid too if you want fewer crowds and lower prices. July and August spike costs and tourism; if you can travel June, September or October, you'll find better fares and less crowding.

What to do when you arrive

You land in Heraklion, the capital. Explore Knossos Palace (pure Minoan history), walk Balos or Elafonissi beaches, taste local food in Rethimno tavernas, and tackle Samaria Gorge if you're fit. The island has public buses, car hire and taxis: getting around is straightforward.
