Price and when to book

The cheapest ticket we've found is €710 return, though most fall around €777. Options go up to €830 depending on dates. Book 2 months ahead for better price chances. Rates update constantly, so compare with airlines before finalising your purchase.

Airline and stops

You travel with Iberia Airlines for the entire route. You'll have 3 stops on the outbound journey SCQ-DPS, which means intermediate landings. It's not a direct flight, but it's the most economical option from Santiago de Compostela. Always check what baggage is included in your fare.

Best time to visit Bali

The dry season runs from April to October, with less rain and more sunshine. These months are ideal if you want beaches and outdoor activities. The wet season is November to March, with lower prices but more rainfall. Book in advance regardless of season: prices don't drop by specific months, only by availability.

What to do when you arrive in Bali

You land at Bali International Airport (DPS). From there, transport to your hotel is well served. You can visit temples like Tanah Lot, beaches in Seminyak or Kuta, rice terraces in Ubud, and dive near nearby islands. The island offers everything from resort relaxation to trekking and water sports. Your journey from SCQ will take time, so rest your first day.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price SCQ-Bali?
