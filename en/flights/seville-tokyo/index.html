Flights from Seville to Tokyo start from €693 return with KLM. The median price hovers around €877, so you have solid options to reach Japan without overspending. Book early and lock in the best fares.

Price and when to book

From €693 you'll find a return ticket with KLM. Prices climb to €877 at the median and can reach €1,244 during peak season. For the best rate: book at least 8 weeks ahead, skip Japanese holidays (New Year, Golden Week in May) and stay flexible with travel dates. The sharpest deals usually drop mid-week.

Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with KLM Royal Dutch Airlines with 1 stopover on this route. It's not a direct flight, but KLM is a reliable carrier with solid service. The stopover lets you stretch, though it adds to total travel time. If speed matters, check other dates for more direct options.

Best time to visit

Skip January, May (Golden Week) and late July–August: they're the priciest periods. Best value comes in October, November, March and April: mild weather, fewer crowds and lower fares. February and December cost slightly more due to holidays, but offer pleasant weather.

What to do when you arrive in Tokyo

Tokyo is vast but compelling. Spend a day in Shibuya, Harajuku and Shinjuku (urban chaos at its peak), another at temples like Asakusa and Senso-ji, visit the Imperial Palace, Meiji Shrine and museums. Eat at izakayas, try ramen at Ramen Yokocho, and don't miss Tsukiji or Toyosu fish market. The local train is fast, cheap and reaches everywhere.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Seville to Tokyo?

No. With KLM you have 1 stopover, which is standard for this route from Spain. Direct flights from Spanish cities to Tokyo are very limited, so a stopover is the norm.

Can I find flights cheaper than €693?

Unlikely. €693 is the minimum price we've seen on this route. From there, fares go up. To bring it down, try being more flexible with dates or consider flying from another Spanish airport nearby.

How far in advance should I book?

At least 8 weeks ahead for the best rates. With KLM and a stopover, booking early gives you more control over schedules and connections. Wait until the last minute and you'll pay €300–400 extra.

Which Tokyo airport do I arrive at?

Usually Narita (NRT) or Haneda (HND). Haneda is closer to the centre, just 14 km away. Narita is about 60 km out and takes longer to reach the city, but all Tokyo trains connect it well.
