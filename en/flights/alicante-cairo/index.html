Best time to visit

October, November, February and March are ideal: mild weather, less scorching heat than summer, and the pyramids are more enjoyable. July and August are brutal. Ramadan affects opening hours and activities, so check beforehand. Prices don't drop by season on this route; buy when you spot a fare that suits you.

What to do in Cairo

The Pyramids of Giza, Giza Plateau and the Sphinx are unmissable. The Egyptian Museum holds pharaonic treasures. Stroll Khan El-Khalili bazaar, cruise the Nile at sunset, or climb the Citadel of Saladin. The airport (CAI) is about 25 km from the centre; taxi or Uber are quick. Spend at least 3-4 days if it's your first visit.

What can I see in Cairo in 4 days?

Day 1: Giza (pyramids, plateau, Sphinx). Day 2: Egyptian Museum and historic centre. Day 3: Khan El-Khalili and Citadel. Day 4: Nile cruise or Tutankhamun museum. That's plenty. Your arrival airport is CAI, about 25 km from downtown.
