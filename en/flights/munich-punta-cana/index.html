Best time to visit

Punta Cana is a year-round beach destination. Atlantic hurricane season runs June to November, though the risk is lower in the Dominican Republic. December to March brings dry weather and pleasant temperatures—ideal for sea and sun. Summer (July–August) sees fewer tourists and more frequent showers, but also better hotel deals.

What to do when you arrive

Punta Cana means white-sand beaches, turquoise waters, and resorts. Beyond the beach, you can visit Saona Island, snorkel or dive, explore cenotes, or tour Los Haitises National Park. Punta Cana International Airport (PUJ) is roughly 30 km from the hotel zone.
