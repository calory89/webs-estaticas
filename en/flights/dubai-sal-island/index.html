What to do when you arrive

Amílcar Cabral International Airport (SID) is your entry point. Sal Island is famous for pristine beaches, coral reef diving and traditional villages. Santa Maria is the main tourist town with shops and restaurants. Don't miss Pedra Lume with its historic salt ponds and underground lagoon.
