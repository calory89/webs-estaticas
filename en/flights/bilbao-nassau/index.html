Flights start from €795 return, with a median of €870. We've tracked prices between €795 and €885. The sooner you book from today, the better: long-haul international fares climb fast as departure date approaches. A golden rule: purchase 2 to 3 months ahead of travel.

Airline and stopovers

Air Europa is your carrier on this route. Expect 3 stopovers on the outbound journey, it's not a direct flight. Connection times vary, so allow a comfortable buffer for terminal changes and procedures. Check your baggage allowance in your fare before buying.

Best time to visit

Nassau enjoys good weather year-round, but skip June to November (Atlantic hurricane season). November through April is prime: pleasant temperatures and clear skies. If you travel in shoulder season (April or November), you'll often spot better prices than peak European winter.

What to do when you arrive

Nassau is your gateway to the coral-fringed beaches of the Bahamas. From the airport (NAS), you're 30 minutes from the city centre. Explore Paradise Island, swim in turquoise waters, sample local cuisine at Junkanoo Beach, or take a day trip to Exuma's Pig Beach. The US dollar is legal tender; exchange at the airport or use ATMs.
