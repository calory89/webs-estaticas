Best time to visit

Cape Town has a Mediterranean climate. December to February is summer (warm, 25–28°C). Winter (June–August) is cooler (12–18°C) but dry. Spring (October–November) and autumn (March–May) are ideal: pleasant temperatures and fewer crowds. Avoid December if you're hunting for low fares—it's peak season.

What to do when you arrive

Cape Town is a must: Table Mountain by rotating cable car, Cape of Good Hope, Robben Island, Camps Bay and Boulders Beach. Explore the City Center's museums and galleries. Combine with wine tastings in Stellenbosch, game reserves nearby, or whale watching depending on season.
