Best time to travel Oslo to Kyoto

Kyoto shines in autumn (October–November) with red maples and spring (March–April) with cherry blossoms. Winter (December–February) is cold but quieter. Skip summer (July–August) for extreme heat and humidity. Any season works; choose based on which scenery appeals to you. Prices don't follow a predictable monthly pattern, so check your specific dates.

What to do when you arrive in Kyoto

Kyoto is Japan's cultural heart. Visit Fushimi Inari with its thousands of red torii gates, Kinkaku-ji (the Golden Pavilion), Ryoan-ji's gardens and the Arashiyama Bamboo Grove. Walk through Gion, the geisha district, at dusk. Local transport is reliable with buses and trains; your hotel will guide you. Plan to spend at least 3–4 days.
