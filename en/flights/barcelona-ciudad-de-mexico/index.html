Best time to travel

High season to Mexico City is December, Easter week, and European summer. For better prices, consider travel in September–October (outside school holidays) or January–February. Mexico City's climate is mild year-round, so your choice depends more on availability and budget.

What to do when you arrive

Mexico City offers world-class museums (Frida Kahlo, National Museum of Anthropology), Paseo de la Reforma, the Coyoacán neighbourhood, and Xochimilco. The food scene is essential. Benito Juárez airport is about 5 km from the city centre; use public transport or organised services to reach the city.
