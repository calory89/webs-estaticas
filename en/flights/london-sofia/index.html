Cheap flights London-Sofia: from €62 return

Sofia awaits you from €62 return. With Ryanair and a direct flight, you'll reach Bulgaria's capital in just over 3 hours. The median price hovers around €66, giving you room to pick dates without breaking the bank.

Price and when to book

Prices hover around €62, €66 and €70 return. Whilst we cannot say which season brings lower fares, booking well in advance always helps. The sample checked on 22 October 2026 confirms tickets at that price are real, not fantasy.

Airline and stops

Ryanair runs this route direct: no stops, no plane changes, no connection hassle. Depart from London (any of its airports) and land in Sofia (BOJ) on a single flight.

Best time to visit

Sofia shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with mild weather. Winter (December–February) is cold but can suit your wallet if you don't mind layers. Skip August if you want fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Spend a day at Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, the Basilica of Sofia and the old town. The nightlife is lively; try traditional Bulgarian restaurants. The Balkans are reachable by bus (Rila, Pirin, mountain villages). Cheap food, affordable lodging, great vibe.

FAQ

Can I find tickets at €62 on any date?

No. That is the lowest price in the sample, but it depends on availability, your chosen date and how far ahead you book. The earlier you reserve, the better the rate. The examples from this search (4 December outbound, 11 December return) show that fare was live on those dates.

Is the London-Sofia flight direct?

Yes, direct with Ryanair. No stops, no changes. London to Sofia in one leg.

Which airline flies this route?

Ryanair is the carrier on this route. Check their website directly or flight comparison sites to confirm availability on your dates.

What to see in Sofia for a weekend?

Alexander Nevsky Cathedral is essential, as is a stroll through the historic centre. Taste Bulgarian food, grab coffee on terraces in the Vitosha district. With more time, Rila Monastery or Pirin mountains are nearby.
