Best time to visit

December brings Christmas markets and festive charm to Prague, but it's peak season. If you want cheaper fares, skip holiday periods. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Prague is a must: the Old Town Square and Astronomical Clock, Charles Bridge, Prague Castle. Explore the Jewish Quarter, taste Czech beer in a traditional pub, and stroll along the Vltava. It's a walkable city packed with character.
