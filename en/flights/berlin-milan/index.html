Price and when to book

Flights on BER-MIL start from €38 return, with a median of €49. Our sample prices range between €38, €49 and €58. Book early: the last two months before departure is when fares rise most, so if your trip is flexible, lock in your ticket 2–3 months ahead. Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route with direct flights between Berlin and Milan. No stops means more comfort and less time in the air: the journey is short and seamless. Note that Ryanair charges for oversized cabin bags and checked luggage, so pack smart to avoid unnecessary extras.

Best time to visit

Milan is lovely year-round, but April–May and September–October bring pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. If you're watching your budget, winter and early spring tend to be cheaper. Avoid December and Easter holidays if you want to sidestep peak tourism and price spikes.

What to do when you arrive

Milan's Duomo is one of Europe's most stunning cathedrals, and the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II is perfect for shopping and classic cafés. The Brera district pulses with art galleries and local character. Fashion lovers should stroll Via Montenapoleone. Leonardo's Last Supper is in the refectory of Santa Maria delle Grazie (book ahead online). Use the metro—it's fast, clean and gets you everywhere.
