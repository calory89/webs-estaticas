Price and when to book

The cheapest flight we found was €75, though most fares range between €106 and €123. Ryanair typically offers its best prices 6-8 weeks in advance, so don't wait until the last minute. Prices tend to rise as your departure date approaches.

Ryanair direct, no transfers

This is a direct Rome-Tenerife flight. Ryanair is the only airline on this route, so they're your single option. No stopovers or lengthy connections. Check what baggage is included in your fare before purchasing.

Best time to travel

Tenerife is a year-round destination. Winter is peak season due to warm weather across Europe, which pushes prices higher. Spring and autumn offer better value whilst you still enjoy good conditions. Summer months (July-August) are expensive and crowded.

What to do when you arrive in Tenerife

Tenerife offers volcanic black sand beaches, Teide National Park (UNESCO site), historic towns like La Laguna, and hiking routes in the north. The island has airports in the south and north. Check which one you land at to plan your transfer properly.
