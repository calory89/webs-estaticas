Price and when to book

Flights start from €551 return. Median price is €575, with some fares reaching €827 depending on dates. Book as early as possible—prices generally rise closer to departure. Aim to search 2–3 months ahead for the best deals.

Airline and stopovers

Eurowings operates this route with one stopover on the outbound leg. It's not direct, but it's the most affordable option from Gran Canaria. The stopover adds travel time, but the price difference is usually worth it. Always check connection times when booking.

Best time to visit Dubai

Dubai is a year-round destination, but October to April offers the best weather: pleasant temperatures (25–30°C) without extreme heat. Avoid May to September if you dislike intense heat. Prices can fluctuate during local holidays and peak tourist seasons, so compare several dates before committing.

What to do when you arrive

Dubai has beaches, shopping at Mall of the Emirates, Burj Khalifa, waterfront walks, desert safaris and traditional souks. Dubai International Airport (DXB) is well-connected by public transport and taxi. Plan based on your interests: leisure, culture, nature or a mix.
