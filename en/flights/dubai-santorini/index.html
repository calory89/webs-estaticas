Best time to travel

Santorini is peak in summer and shoulder season in spring and autumn. For lower prices, go between November and March, though weather is more changeable. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) balance good weather with moderate fares.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Santorini airport (JTR). Buses and taxis connect to Fira, the island capital. Don't miss the sunset at Oia, Amoudi Bay cliffs, volcanic sand beaches and whitewashed villages. Local food and wineries are also essential stops.
