Best time to visit

Sofia is lovely in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with mild temperatures and fewer tourists. Winters are cold but the city buzzes with life. Skip summer if you want peace and lower prices. Search flights with date flexibility to catch price drops depending on the season.

What to do when you arrive in Sofia

Sofia airport (SOF) is about 10 km from the city centre. Once there, don't miss Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, Banya Bashi Mosque, or the hot springs of Bansko if you venture out. Eat cheaply at local canteens and try a rakija. Sofia is compact, most places are reachable on foot or by public transport.
