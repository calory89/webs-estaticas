Best time to visit

Kuala Lumpur has tropical climate year-round. June to August offer less rain on the peninsula, while the east coast is drier from May to September. Avoid the northeast monsoon (November to March) if you prefer drier days. Any time works well to visit—plan according to your tolerance for heat and humidity.

What to do when you arrive

Kuala Lumpur is vibrant: the Petronas Twin Towers are unmissable, and you can enjoy the views from the observation bridge. Explore the colonial Merdeka district, the night markets at Jalan Alor, and the Hindu and Chinese temples. Outside the city, the Batu Caves are worth a day trip. Local food is outstanding: don't miss laksa soup and satay.
