Best time to visit

Mauritius has a tropical climate year-round. May to November (southern hemisphere winter) offers milder temperatures and less rainfall. Demand and prices are often higher during these months. December to April is warmer and more humid. Choose based on your weather preference and availability.

What to do when you arrive

Your arrival airport is Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam, about 45 km from the centre. From there you can reach the east coast beaches, visit Port Louis (the capital), explore coral reefs and snorkel, or hike through Black River Gorges. The island offers beaches, trekking and water sports.
