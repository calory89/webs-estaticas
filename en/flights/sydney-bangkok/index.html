Best time to visit

Thailand works best October to February, cooler, less rain. November and December are peak season and pricier, but the most pleasant. Budget hunters should look at the green season (June to September): fewer tourists, lower hotel rates, but more rainfall. From Australia, you can make this trip work year-round though.

What to do when you arrive

Bangkok is your entry point: temples (Wat Arun, Wat Pho), floating markets, nightlife scene. From here, short flights or buses head north (Chiang Mai), south to islands (Phuket, Krabi), or east coast (Trat). Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK) is well-connected, about 30 km from the city centre.
