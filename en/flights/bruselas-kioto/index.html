Best time to visit Kyoto

Kyoto is stunning in spring (March to May) with cherry blossoms, and autumn (September to November) with mild weather and spectacular colours. Winter (December to February) also attracts visitors for fewer crowds and serene atmosphere. Avoid summer (June to August) if you dislike heat and humidity. Whenever you go, book early to secure your flight.

What to do upon arrival in Kyoto

You'll land at Osaka airport (KIX), roughly 75 km from Kyoto. Direct trains take you there in 45–90 minutes depending on service. Once in the city, explore thousands of temples and shrines, wander through the geisha district of Gion, walk the red torii gates at Fushimi Inari, or unwind with traditional tea ceremonies. Kyoto is accessible, affordable, and captivating for European travellers.
