Best time to visit

Punta Cana is a year-round beach destination. The dry season (November to April) offers better weather and less rain; it's also busier and pricier. For lower fares, May to October is cheaper, though there's more rainfall. Plan ahead: flights sell out quickly in peak season.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Punta Cana International Airport (PUJ), gateway to the eastern Dominican beaches. Enjoy white sand, cenotes, theme parks and diving. The area has all-inclusive resorts and independent options alike. Just a few kilometres away you'll find Bávaro, Uvero Alto and nearby islands for day trips.
