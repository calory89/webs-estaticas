Fly from Barcelona to Punta Cana for €659 return. The median price sits around €661, so now is a good time to book. Air Canada gets you there with one stop to the Dominican Republic's stunning beaches.

Price and when to book

The base fare for this route is €659 return. Prices we've tracked range from €659 to €1,071, with a median of €661. To secure the best rate, book as far ahead as possible: this is a long-haul international flight, so earlier bookings win. Avoid last-minute purchases if you can.

Airline and stops

Air Canada operates these flights. The outbound journey includes 1 stop, so it's not a direct flight. Factor this into your planning: the journey will take longer than a direct flight, but the price makes up for it. Sample departure is 25 January at 13:35 and return is 2 February at 15:15.

Best time to visit

Punta Cana has tropical weather year-round. November to March sees the least rainfall and lowest hurricane risk. January and February are ideal: warm temperatures, minimal rain and excellent conditions. If budget matters more than weather, consider May to October, though expect more showers and higher humidity.

What to do when you arrive

Punta Cana is a beach destination above all: Bávaro and Playa Blanca offer white sand and turquoise water. You can also explore Los Haitises National Park, snorkel in cenotes, visit colonial towns like Altagracia or simply relax at your all-inclusive resort. Punta Cana International Airport (PUJ) is less than 30 km from most hotels.

FAQ

Is the flight Barcelona to Punta Cana direct?

No. Air Canada operates this route with 1 stop, so you'll have an intermediate stop. This extends your journey, but the price is very competitive at from €659.

What's the best month to visit Punta Cana?

January and February are excellent: warm temperatures, clear skies and minimal rain risk. They're peak season months, but the weather is perfect for beach and water activities.

Are there cheaper flights or is this the best fare?

€659 is the lowest price we've seen for this route. Most searches hover around €661. Above €1,000 is significantly pricier, so if you see that, wait or try different dates.

What should I know when I arrive in Punta Cana?

The airport (PUJ) is well connected to hotels and resorts. Punta Cana is a safe, tourism-focused destination with solid infrastructure. Bring sunscreen: Caribbean sun is intense. Most resorts are all-inclusive, which makes budgeting easier once you're on the ground.
