Airline and stops

Best time to visit

Phu Quoc has a tropical climate. October to March is dry and sunny, perfect for beach and water activities. May to September is rainy but quieter, and hotels often cost less. Pick based on your budget and travel style: pure relaxation or exploring with fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Phu Quoc is known for pristine beaches, snorkelling and diving on coral reefs, Phu Quoc National Park, and night fishing trips. Explore local villages, try Vietnamese food, and visit markets. Most resorts and tours can be booked on arrival without advance booking. The airport (PQC) is just a few kilometres from the main tourist areas.
