Best time to visit

Orlando draws visitors year-round, but October–November and January–March offer fewer crowds at the theme parks and pleasant weather. Skip July–August for extreme heat and peak season. Check bank holidays in both Ireland and the US to spot cheaper travel windows.

What to do when you arrive

Orlando is the world's theme park capital: Walt Disney World, Universal Orlando and SeaWorld are must-dos. Beyond the parks, explore historic Winter Park, Atlantic coast beaches (an hour's drive) or natural freshwater springs. Orlando International Airport (MCO) connects well to the city; rent a car or use local transport to suit your plans.
