Best time to visit

Kuala Lumpur has tropical weather year-round. Best months are June to September (drier northeast monsoon) or January to March (slightly cooler temperatures). Avoid September and October if you can, when rainfall is heavier. Bear in mind it's a tourist destination during European holidays, so prices rise in July–August and Christmas. Low season (April–May, November) is usually cheaper.

What to do when you arrive

Kuala Lumpur is modern with plenty to explore: the Petronas Twin Towers, Menara KL, markets like Petaling Street, and nearby nature like Taman Negara rainforest. The airport (KLIA) is 75 km from the city centre; use licensed taxis or transport services. The city is easy to navigate and a good base for day trips to Selangor or Pahang.
