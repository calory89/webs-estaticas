Best time to travel

Kuala Lumpur and Malaysia have good weather year-round. June to August are the driest months, though potentially pricier. December to February offer the coolest and driest season, ideal for exploring without the heat. Avoid September and October, which see heavier rainfall. Flight prices vary more than the weather does, so your dates matter more than the season.

What to do when you arrive

Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) is 75 km from the city centre. Take the bus, express train, or taxi. In the city, visit the Petronas Twin Towers, Petaling Street's night market, Hindu and Chinese temples, and the Batu Caves. The Perhentian Islands and Penang are a few hours by bus if you want beaches. Local food is spectacular and cheap.
