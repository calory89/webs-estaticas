Best time to visit

Dubrovnik shines in May, June and September: mild weather, fewer crowds than July and August, beaches and Old Town walks without stress. If you want intense heat, July and August are yours, but expect heavier tourism. December is quiet, mild winter on the Dalmatian coast, though some days rain. Winter is ideal if you prefer history and architecture without the chaos.

What to do when you arrive in Dubrovnik

Čilipi International Airport (DBV) is 20 km from the Old Town. Walk the medieval walls, visit the Rector's Palace, take a ferry to Lokrum or Cavtat. Eat by the bay, swim on nearby beaches and spend an afternoon in harbourside cafés. The old city is a UNESCO World Heritage site and doubles as an open-air film and TV studio. Book hotels in the Old Town but venture out to eat well and cheaply.
