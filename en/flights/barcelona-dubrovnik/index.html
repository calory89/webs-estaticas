Best time to travel

Dubrovnik is a year-round destination, but prices typically drop in low season (November to March). Travel in autumn or winter for fewer crowds in the Old Town and better accommodation rates. Weather remains pleasant in autumn.

What to do on arrival

Dubrovnik Airport is about 20 km from the city centre. Direct buses, shared shuttles and taxis are available. Once in town, explore the city walls, Stradun street, the old port and nearby islands. Dubrovnik's centre is compact; you'll walk most sights on foot.

What to see in Dubrovnik?

The Old Town (UNESCO World Heritage), the city walls, Stradun street, the port, churches and monasteries. Boat trips to islands like Lopud and Koločep depart from here. Plan at least 3 days to explore without rush.
