Best time to travel

Mykonos peaks in summer, when prices soar from June to August. May, September and early October offer the best value: decent weather, pleasant sea, and fewer crowds than peak season. For maximum savings, travel between November and March, prices drop, though weather is less predictable.

What to do when you arrive

Mykonos means beaches, nightlife and whitewashed villages. Paradise Beach and Psarou are popular. Chora old town buzzes with bars and shops. For quieter vibes, try Ano Mera or Ftelia. Mykonos airport is 2 km from the port; ferries depart to other Cycladic islands if you want to extend your trip.

FAQ

No, these Ryanair flights include 2 stops. This is typical for budget routes from Paris to Mykonos.

What's the cheapest price I can find?

From €249 return, based on this search. The median is €259, giving you a realistic idea of average cost.

When is the best time to visit Mykonos on a budget?

May, September and early October offer good weather and lower prices than peak summer. Winter is cheaper still, though weather is less stable.

What can I see and do in Mykonos?

Beaches (Paradise, Psarou), Chora village with white-washed streets, nightlife, and ferry trips to other Cycladic islands like Delos or Santorini if you extend your stay.
