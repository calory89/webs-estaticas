Best time to visit

Phuket is a tropical beach destination. November to February offer the best weather and least rain, though these months see peak season and higher prices. For better value, consider shoulder seasons: March to May or September to October can balance acceptable weather with lower fares. Check the airline's calendar for dates with more availability at competitive prices.

What to do on arrival in Phuket

Phuket is your gateway to Thailand's islands. Beaches like Patong, Kata and Karon are essential. From here, explore Phang Nga Bay, the Phi Phi Islands or reef snorkelling. Old Phuket Town features colonial architecture and Thai temples. The international airport is about 30 km from the centre; taxis, buses and private transfers are straightforward options.
