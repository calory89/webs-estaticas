Best time to visit

Naples is pleasant almost year-round, but October to April offers mild weather without summer heat. December and January are cooler and perfect for dodging crowds. The low fares you see now (checked 23 October) typically hold in low season. Expect higher prices in July and August.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Naples Airport (Capodichino). The city offers the Cathedral, the Royal Palace, and the National Archaeological Museum. Grab authentic pizza, wander the old town (UNESCO site) and, if time allows, take a ferry to Capri or Ischia. The train station connects easily if you fancy exploring the Amalfi Coast or Pompeii.
