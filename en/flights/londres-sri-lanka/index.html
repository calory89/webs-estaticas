Best time to visit

Sri Lanka is most comfortable during the dry season. The west and south coast are best from December to March, while the north and east work well April to September. Prices peak during these high seasons, so consider shoulder months like October or May if you want to save and still find decent weather across many regions.

What to do when you arrive

Colombo is your entry point to Sri Lanka. From the international airport, take a train or minibus to the capital or beyond. Must-sees: Sigiriya (rock fortress), temples in Kandy, beaches at Mirissa or Unawatuna, and tea plantations in the highlands. It's a compact country, easy to explore in 2–3 weeks on a tight budget.

A week is tight but doable. Cover Colombo, Sigiriya, and Kandy in 3–4 days, then beaches (Mirissa or Unawatuna) in 3 days. With 10 days, add the scenic train ride through the mountains (Kandy to Nuwara Eliya) and extra beach time.
