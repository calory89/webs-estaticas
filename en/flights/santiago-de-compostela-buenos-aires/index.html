Best time to travel

Buenos Aires is a year-round destination. Southern summer (December–February) is peak season with higher fares. Spring (September–October) and autumn (March–May) offer pleasant weather and moderate prices. Winter (June–August) is low season with fewer tourists and lower prices, though it's cold (8–15°C).

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Ministro Pistarini (Ezeiza), 35 km from central Buenos Aires. Must-sees include iconic neighbourhoods like La Boca, San Telmo and Palermo, plus the Teatro Colón. Enjoy local cuisine, live tango and walks through Recoleta. Patagonia and Iguazu Falls make great extensions if you're staying longer.
