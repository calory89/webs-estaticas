Best time to travel

Kuala Lumpur has a tropical climate and is warm year-round. The best season is November to February, with less rain and slightly cooler temperatures. From late October onwards is already good timing—that's why you see offers then. Avoid June to September if you prefer drier weather.

What to do on arrival

Kuala Lumpur boasts the iconic Petronas Twin Towers, the Royal Palace, bustling Chinatown street markets and Batu Caves for hiking. Nearby Selangor has beaches and Pulau Ubin island. The international airport is well connected to the city centre by train. Eat at hawker centres for cheap, authentic local food.
