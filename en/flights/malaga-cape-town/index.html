Best time to visit

South Africa is a year-round destination, but Cape Town shines from October to April (spring and summer in the Southern Hemisphere). The weather is dry, temperatures pleasant and light excellent. Winter (June to August) is cool but also appealing if you want fewer crowds. Plan your trip around climate and activities: hiking, beaches or safari call for different seasons.

What to do on arrival

Cape Town is essential: Table Mountain, beaches like Boulders (penguins), Camps Bay or Muizenberg, the Victoria & Alfred waterfront, Table Mountain, and the Cape Point route. Nearby areas offer wineries in Stellenbosch, the coastal town of Hermanus (whales in season) and wild landscapes. Cape Town Airport is 20 km from the city centre, well served by shuttle, taxi or car hire.
