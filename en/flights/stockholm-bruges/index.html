Flying from Stockholm to Bruges is very affordable: from €55 return with Ryanair. The median price sits at €56, so you'll find budget options throughout the year. Book in advance and save on your Belgian getaway.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we found is €55, with a median of €56. Sample prices range from €55 to €96. To secure the best fare, book at least 4–6 weeks ahead. Check at month-end or midweek: demand is usually lower and prices drop.

Airline and flight features

Ryanair operates this route. Flights are direct (no stops), cutting travel time. In our example, outbound was 5 December at 07:30 and return 14 December at 21:40. Remember Ryanair charges separately for carry-on and checked baggage: factor that into your budget.

Best time to visit

Bruges is charming year-round, but December brings festive markets and Christmas atmosphere. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Summer is peak season with higher prices. Plan ahead to lock in better rates.

What to do when you arrive

Bruges is medieval and compact: explore its canals, visit the Basilica of the Holy Blood and Market Square. Taste Belgian beers, waffles and artisan chocolate. Brussels Airport (BRU) is roughly 60 km away; bus, train or car hire are available. Spend 2–3 days to enjoy it properly.

Walk the canals, visit the Basilica of the Holy Blood and Market Square, browse local markets. Sample Belgian beers, waffles and chocolate. It's a small, walkable city, 3 days is enough to soak up its medieval charm.
