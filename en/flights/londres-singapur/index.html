Best time to travel

Singapore is tropical year-round. November (the example date) is dry and pleasant, with temperatures around 26–27°C and less rain than monsoon season. December to February also offer good weather. Avoid September and October if you prefer less rain. Book well in advance regardless of season.

What to do when you arrive

Singapore is compact and accessible: Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay, Chinatown, Kampong Glam, and nearby beaches on Sentosa. Public transport is excellent and taxis are cheap. Eat at hawker centers and street food stalls. Three to four days cover the essentials; a week lets you enjoy at a relaxed pace.
