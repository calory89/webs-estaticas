Best time to travel

Porto appeals year-round. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is quieter but wetter. Skip August if you want fewer tourists and potentially lower prices.

What to do when you arrive in Porto

You'll land at Porto Airport (OPO), 11 km from the city centre. Explore the historic old town, stroll along the Douro riverfront, climb the Clérigos Tower and taste port wine at the local lodges. The metro or bus will get you to town in 30–40 minutes.
