Best time to travel

Jacksonville enjoys good weather year-round. Autumn (October–November) and spring (March–May) are ideal: pleasant temperatures and less rain. July and August are very hot and humid. Hurricane season runs June to November, though it doesn't stop travel. Check the forecast before booking, but it's not a price-determining factor.

What to do when you arrive

Jacksonville offers beaches in Riverside, a revamped historic downtown, and the Cummer Museum. For nature lovers, Tillie K. Fowler State Park is nearby. Local dining focuses on fresh seafood. The international airport (JAX) is 20 km from downtown; taxi, Uber or shuttle are quick options.
