Price and when to book

The cheapest flights hover around €137 return, though most available options fall between €154 and €157. Book as far ahead as possible for better prices. Avoid last-minute bookings; fares climb as your departure date approaches.

Airline and stopovers

Wizz Air Malta operates this route. All flights include 1 stop, so it's not a direct journey. You'll have an intermediate landing before reaching Berlin, which adds to your total travel time.

Best time to visit

Berlin shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Winters are cold but charming around the festive season. Skip July and August if you dislike peak tourism. Check availability for your preferred dates.

What to do when you arrive

Berlin has it all: Brandenburg Gate, East Side Gallery (painted wall), world-class museums on Museum Island, and vibrant neighbourhoods like Kreuzberg and Charlottenburg. Grab authentic currywurst, explore weekend markets, and soak up the cultural scene. Your main airport is Berlin Brandenburg.
