Flights from Vienna to Brussels with Ryanair cost from €67 return. The median price is around €71, perfect for travelling without breaking the bank. It's a direct route connecting two of Europe's most interesting cities.

Price and when to book

Prices range between €67 and €81 in the sample checked. To get the best fares, book as far in advance as possible: budget airlines drop prices as seats fill up, but also raise them when availability gets tight. Compare nearby dates to find the right balance between availability and cost.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with direct flights. Departure from Vienna at 18:45, arrival in Brussels at 19:10 (example schedule). As a direct flight, you save travel time and terminal hassles. Check what luggage is included in your fare: Ryanair charges for checked baggage.

Best time to travel

Vienna and Brussels are charming destinations year-round. Spring and autumn offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter months can be cheaper, but verify available flights match your dates. Avoid peak tourist seasons if you're hunting for the lowest prices.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Brussels-Charleroi Airport (CRL), 46 km from the centre. Direct buses run to the city affordably. In Brussels, don't miss the Grand Place, the Atomium and Sint-Jans canals. In Vienna, before you leave, enjoy the cafés, the Danube and imperial architecture. Both cities deserve at least 2–3 days each.
