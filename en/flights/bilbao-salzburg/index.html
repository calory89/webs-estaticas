Best time to visit Salzburg

Salzburg shines in winter (Christmas markets and New Year) but also in spring and autumn if you want fewer crowds. Summer is peak season and pricier. Pick dates outside school holidays to fly cheaper and enjoy a more relaxed visit.

What to do when you arrive in Salzburg

Salzburg is Mozart's birthplace and the Sound of Music backdrop. Don't miss Hohensalzburg Fortress, the UNESCO-listed old town, Lake Hallstatt (just over 1 hour away), and the nearby Alps. The airport is 4 km from the centre; reach it easily by bus or taxi.
