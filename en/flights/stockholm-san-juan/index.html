Best time to travel

San Juan enjoys tropical weather year-round. November to April is the dry season with cooler temperatures, ideal for avoiding heavy rain. If you travel in low season (May to October), outside peak tourism, you'll find fewer crowds at beaches and attractions, though airfares vary. Book well ahead for the best price.

What to do when you arrive

San Juan offers Old San Juan with colonial streets and El Morro fortress, Caribbean beaches and snorkelling. Camuy Caves and Guánica Dry Forest are nearby. Local food is essential: try mofongo and alcapurria.
