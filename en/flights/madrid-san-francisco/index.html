Best time to travel

San Francisco is a variable-season destination. Winter (December to March) tends to be cheaper, whilst summer (June to September) pushes prices up. Spring (April–May) is a sweet spot: good weather and still reasonable fares. Avoid public holidays and bank holidays to keep prices low.

What to do when you arrive

San Francisco is the gateway to the Bay Area. Don't miss the Golden Gate Bridge, Alcatraz, Mission District, and street art murals. Within 1–2 hours you'll find beaches, Napa Valley vineyards and stunning natural parks. The airport (SFO) is well connected: BART (train) gets you to the centre in 30 minutes.
