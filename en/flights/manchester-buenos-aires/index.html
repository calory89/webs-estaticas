Best time to visit

Buenos Aires enjoys good weather year-round. Spring (September–November) and autumn (March–May) are ideal: pleasant temperatures and less rain. Winter (June–August) is cooler but less crowded. Check deals based on your availability.

What to do in Buenos Aires

Argentina's capital offers tango, European architecture, quality museums, world-class food and vibrant markets like La Boca. Teatro Colón, San Telmo neighbourhood and Recoleta are must-sees. Stroll down Avenida 9 de Julio and enjoy café with medialunas. From Ministro Pistarini airport, the city centre is 45–60 minutes away.

FAQ

Is the flight from Manchester to Buenos Aires direct?

No, the outbound flight has 3 stopovers. There's no direct connection from Manchester. Plan time for connections and rest before travel.

Which airline flies Manchester to Buenos Aires?

Ryanair. Check what baggage is included (Ryanair is strict on luggage), as base fares don't always cover a checked suitcase.

What can I see in Buenos Aires in a week?

A week covers the city centre well: La Boca, San Telmo, Recoleta, Teatro Colón, MALBA and museums. Enjoy tango, local food and cafés. With more time, add a trip to Colonia (Uruguay) or Tigre.
