Best time to visit

Punta Cana works year-round, but the dry season (November to April) brings less rain and perfect beach weather. November and December are especially good: ideal climate before peak Christmas prices. May to October is hurricane season, so avoid if you're looking for safety. The November departure in our example is spot-on timing.

What to do when you arrive

Punta Cana is your gateway to white-sand beaches and turquoise waters. From the airport (PUJ) you reach main resort zones in 20-30 minutes. You can explore natural parks, cenotes, colonial Santo Domingo, or simply lounge by the coast. The Dominican Republic offers solid value for accommodation and activities, perfect to stretch your budget after the flight.
