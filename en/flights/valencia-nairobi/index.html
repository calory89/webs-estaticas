Best time to travel

Nairobi and Kenya are destinations year-round, but the dry seasons (June to October and January to February) are best for safaris and outdoor activities. These periods see higher demand. The green season (April to May) has fewer tourists and still offers good weather for exploring.

What to do when you arrive in Nairobi

Visit the National Museum of Kenya, explore the old town, dine at Karen Blixen restaurants, or climb to the viewpoint at Nairobi National Park. From here, day trips run to Masai Mara Reserve, Lake Nakuru and other must-see destinations across Kenya.
