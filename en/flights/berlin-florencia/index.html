Best time to visit

Florence is beautiful year-round, but outside July and August (peak season) you'll have fewer crowds and better prices. January, as in the examples we see, is quiet and affordable. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) are also excellent: pleasant weather and less congestion than summer.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Florence Airport (Peretola), 5 km from the city centre. The Cathedral of Santa Maria del Fiore, the Uffizi Gallery, Ponte Vecchio and Palazzo Pitti are must-sees. But Florence deserves to be explored on foot: wander its narrow streets, try Tuscan food at a local trattoria and climb Brunelleschi's dome if you're up for it.
