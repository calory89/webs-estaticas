Flights start from €36 return. We've seen samples at €36, €40 and €65. The earlier you book, the better: Ryanair fares rise as your departure date approaches. Airline and connections

Ryanair is your sole option on this route and operates direct flights. No stopovers, no hassle. Departure from Málaga-Costa del Sol (AGP) around 23:10 and arrival in Marrakech (RAK) early morning. A short journey: you barely cross the Mediterranean.

Best time to travel

Marrakech is pleasant almost year-round. October, November, March and April offer ideal temperatures and fewer crowds. Avoid July and August for extreme heat. December and January are cool but with occasional rain risk. The most competitive prices are usually found outside July-August and school holidays.

What to do when you arrive

Marrakech awaits with the Old Medina, a maze of souks bursting with life. Don't miss Jemaa el-Fnaa Square, the pulsing heart of the city with acrobats and juice vendors. Visit the Bahia Palace, the Majorelle Gardens or venture into the nearby Atlas Mountains. The airport is just 10 km from the city centre, easy access by taxi or bus.
