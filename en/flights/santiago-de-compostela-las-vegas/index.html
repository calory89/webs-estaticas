Best time to visit

Las Vegas has sunshine year-round, but extreme heat hits June to September (40–45°C). October, November, April and May are ideal: pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. December and January also work but are peak season. Book ahead regardless.

What to do when you arrive

Las Vegas is far more than casinos. The Strip hosts iconic resorts; explore downtown's Fremont Street Experience too. Outside the city: Red Rock Canyon (30 min), Valley of Fire (45 min) and the Grand Canyon (45 min by plane or 4.5 hours by car). The city never sleeps, eat, shop and play 24/7.
