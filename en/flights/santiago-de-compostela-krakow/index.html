Best time to visit Krakow

Krakow is beautiful year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Winters are cold but charming if you want snow. Avoid July–August for lower tourist numbers. Book in advance regardless of season.

What to do in Krakow when you arrive

The Main Market Square (Rynek Główny) is unmissable, lined with galleries and cafés. Climb St. Mary's Basilica for city views. Wawel Castle dominates the skyline from its hilltop. Explore Kazimierz's Jewish quarter, packed with history and nightlife. Museums deserve a full afternoon if you have time.
