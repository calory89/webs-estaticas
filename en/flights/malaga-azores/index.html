Best time to travel

The Azores enjoy a temperate climate year-round. January, our example date, is winter but the islands don't experience extreme cold. The archipelago is green and rainy, so expect humidity. For drier weather, consider spring (April–May) or autumn (September–October). Prices tend to be lower outside summer months.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Ponta Delgada (PDL), the main city on São Miguel island. Explore volcanic lakes (Lago de Santiago and Lagoa das Sete Cidades are must-sees), waterfalls, Nordeste cliffs and botanical gardens. Fishing villages and natural pools complete the experience. Hire a car: public transport is limited.
