Best time to visit Maldives

Dry season runs November to April: clear skies, calm seas and superb visibility for diving and snorkelling. December and January are peak season with ideal temperatures (26–30°C) and minimal rain. For fewer crowds and less inflated prices, consider March or April. Outside dry season the sea can be rougher, though still suitable for island enjoyment.

What to do when you arrive in Maldives

Malé International Airport (MLE) is your gateway. From there, a seaplane or speedboat takes you to your resort; most sit in nearby atolls (15–60 minutes away). Spend days diving, snorkelling at coral reefs, boat trips, night fishing or simply relaxing on white-sand beaches. Local island tours let you experience authentic Maldivian culture. Bring high-SPF sunscreen and insect repellent.
