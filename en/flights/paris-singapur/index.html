Best time to visit

Singapore is a tropical destination worth visiting year-round. The dry season (February to April and July to August) has the least rain, though it's still hot and humid. Choose your dates based on your heat tolerance: prices don't swing wildly by season, so the best time is whenever your schedule allows.

What to do when you arrive in Singapore

Singapore is a city-state packed with attractions: Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay, colonial quarters, historic temples, and world-class food. The airport has excellent public transport and taxis. It's a perfect hub to explore Southeast Asia if you want to extend your trip.
