Best time to visit

Bologna is beautiful year-round, but prices typically drop during low season (October to March, outside holidays). Avoid peak demand like Christmas or long weekends. For pleasant weather, April to October is ideal, though prices will be higher. Spring and autumn offer a good balance: nice weather and moderate prices.

What to do when you arrive in Bologna

Bologna airport (BLQ) is 6 km from the historic centre and well connected by bus and train. In the city, don't miss Piazza Maggiore, the medieval towers, the Basilica of San Petronio and the famous arcades. Local food is legendary: tagliatelle al ragù, mortadella, Parmigiano Reggiano. Bologna is compact and walkable; perfect for 2-3 days.
