Ryanair connects Gran Canaria to Milan with a direct flight from €61 return. Median prices hover around €117. Book early to lock in the lowest fares.

Price and when to book

Prices start at €61 (return), with a median of €117. Options go up to €140. The earlier you book, the better the rate. Cheap tickets sell out quickly, especially in peak season. Check fares mid-week: they're usually cheaper than Fridays and weekends.

Airline and flight type

Fly with Ryanair, which operates this route as a direct flight with no stops. That means outbound and return without plane changes. The sample shows departure at 15:35 from Gran Canaria and return at 06:40. With Ryanair, note that cabin baggage is limited: check what's included in your fare.

Best time to travel

The route runs year-round. Milan appeals most in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), when weather is pleasant without extremes. Summer is pricier and busier. Winter has fewer tourists but long nights; note it gets cold in northern Italy.

What to do on arrival

Milan is a global hub for fashion, design and art. Visit the Cathedral (Duomo), the Opera House (La Scala) and the Pinacoteca di Brera. Leonardo da Vinci museums and Sforza Castle are must-sees. Eat well: risotto alla milanese and ossobuco are local classics. Milan airport is connected to the city centre by train in 15–20 minutes.
