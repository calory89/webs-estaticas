EgyptAir flies nonstop from Berlin to Cairo from €305 return. Average prices sit around €310. If you want to reach Egypt without connections, here's what you're paying right now.

Price and when to book

Fares on this route start at €305 and climb to €328 depending on dates and availability. The median sits around €310. Book 8 weeks ahead if possible: long-haul flights from Berlin typically offer better rates with advance notice. Travel between mid-October and March for lower prices in the shoulder season.

EgyptAir and nonstop service

EgyptAir operates this route nonstop: departure from Berlin mid-afternoon (around 14:55), arrival in Cairo the next morning. No plane changes, no airport queues. Return flight is also direct. This saves you time and hassle.

Best time to visit

October to March is peak season in Egypt: dry weather and pleasant temperatures (20–25°C). July and August are scorching and pricey. Book early if travelling December–January. Outside European school holidays, fares drop noticeably.

What to do when you arrive

Cairo airport (CAI) is 15 km from the city centre. Head to the Islamic quarter, museums (Egyptian Museum, Citadel of Saladin) or the Giza pyramids. Spend at least 3 days in the capital and surroundings. The Nile, souks, Muhammad Ali Mosque, the classics you shouldn't miss.

FAQ
