Best time to travel

Vietnam is most pleasant October to April, cooler and drier. Prices climb during these months because it's peak season. To save on flights, aim for May, June, or September: it's wetter, but fares drop noticeably.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

Hanoi is the capital and natural entry point. Wander the Old Quarter, visit Ho Chi Minh's Mausoleum, and eat street food at the markets. From here, most travellers head to Halong Bay, Sapa's mountains, or the Mekong Delta towns in the south. Hanoi International Airport (HAN) is 30–45 minutes from the city centre by taxi or bus.
