Best time to visit

Rome is ideal from October to April, when the weather is cooler. November is perfect: mild temperatures (15–20°C), fewer crowds than summer and clear skies. Skip July and August, heat is intense and tourist numbers peak. Prices tend to stay steadier outside school holidays and Christmas.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Fiumicino Airport (FCO), 30 km from the centre. Take the Leonardo Express train (15 mins to Termini) or a bus. In Rome, don't miss the Colosseum, Roman Forum, St Peter's Basilica, Trevi Fountain and the Vatican Museums. The city is walkable; spend at least 3–4 days to enjoy it properly.
