Best time to visit

Jacksonville is in Florida, so it's warm year-round. November to April is ideal: pleasant temperatures (20–25°C) without the stifling heat and humidity of summer. Skip June to September if you can't handle tropical heat. January, like in the example, is a solid pick: European winter, Florida spring already in full swing, and fewer crowds than Christmas holidays.

What to do when you arrive

Jacksonville International Airport (JAX) sits about 20 km from the city centre. You've got nice beaches at Jax Beach and Neptune Beach, museums like the Cummer Museum of Art & Gardens, and historic St. Augustine close by (45 minutes' drive). Natural spots like Timucuan Preserve are worth a visit too. It's less touristy than Miami or Orlando, so hotels and activities tend to be friendlier on the wallet.
