Best time to visit

Jacksonville is lovely in winter (December–February): mild temperatures, less rain than summer, and no extreme heat from June to September. Avoid hurricane season (August–October). January is a solid choice if you want beaches without crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Jacksonville has decent beaches at Jacksonville Beach and Amelia Island. Stroll the historic River District, check out the Cathedral Basilica of St. Augustine, or explore Riverside. If you've got time, drive to St. Augustine—America's oldest city—just over an hour away.
