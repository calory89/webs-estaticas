Best time to visit

Belgrade is appealing year-round, but April-May and September-October offer the best weather: mild temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. December is also pleasant if you enjoy festive decorations, though winters are colder. Skip July-August if you prefer to avoid peak season.

What to do when you arrive

Belgrade's Nikola Tesla Airport is 12 km from the city centre. Once there, visit Kalemegdan Fortress, stroll through the bohemian Zemun district, dine along the Danube, and explore the Church of Saint Sava. Belgrade is compact and easy to navigate on foot or by public transport.
