Best time to visit

Salzburg is stunning year-round. Winter (November–February) brings Christmas markets and snowy landscapes, ideal for festive vibes. Spring and autumn offer pleasant weather with fewer crowds. Summer is peak season with higher fares. Check availability for your preferred month.

What to do when you arrive

Salzburg deserves 3–4 days minimum. Hohensalzburg Fortress, the UNESCO Old Town, and Mozart's birthhouse are must-sees. Wander baroque streets, try Austrian strudel, and take a Sound of Music tour. Salzburg Airport (W.A. Mozart) is 4 km from the centre; direct buses and trains run into town.

What to see in Salzburg in 3 days?

Hohensalzburg Fortress, the Old Town, the Cathedral, Mozart's birthhouse and walks along the Salzach River. The city is small and walkable. Many visitors spend a day exploring the nearby Salzkammergut lakes.
