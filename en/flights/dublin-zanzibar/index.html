Best time to visit

Zanzibar has a tropical climate. The dry season offers sunny days and less rain—ideal for sightseeing. Check the weather calendar before choosing dates. Avoid the rainy season if outdoor activities matter to you. Prices also fluctuate between peak and off-season travel.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Zanzibar International Airport (ZNZ). Stone Town, the historic capital, is a must: wander narrow coral-white streets, visit palaces and the spice market. Nungwi and Kendwa beaches are perfect to unwind. Try snorkelling and diving on the coral reef, or visit the slave fort. Book excursions in advance.
