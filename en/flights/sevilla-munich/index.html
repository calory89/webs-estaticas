Best time to visit Munich

Munich is worth visiting year-round. Summer brings fine weather for exploring the city and surroundings. Winter, especially December, is magical thanks to the Christmas markets. Avoid Oktoberfest dates (September–October) if you want lower prices and fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive in Munich

Munich airport (MUC) is 28 km from the centre. Quick access via S-Bahn train (about 40 minutes). Once in the city, don't miss Marienplatz with its Gothic cathedral, Nymphenburg Palace and English Garden. The Pinakothek and Deutsches Museum are worth visiting. Eat at traditional beer halls like Hofbräuhaus.
