Best time to visit

Las Vegas has good weather most of the year, but October to April is peak season: mild temperatures (15–20°C average) and less extreme heat. Summer (June–August) can exceed 40°C. Hotel and activity prices tend to be more competitive during shoulder seasons than during peak holidays like New Year or major event weekends.

What to do when you arrive

Harry Reid International Airport is about 20 km from the centre. The Strip is your first sight: casinos, casino-hotels and nonstop entertainment. Outside the core, Red Rock Canyon, Valley of Fire, and Hoover Dam waterfalls make perfect half-day trips. Downtown (Fremont Street) offers cheaper food and cocktails than the Strip.
