Best time to visit

Charleston has a subtropical climate. October to April is ideal: pleasant temperatures (15–20°C) and less humidity. May to September is hot and humid, with hurricane risk. Prices are often more competitive in winter (January–February) than in cruise and spring tourism season (March–April).

What to do when you arrive

Charleston is known for its well-preserved historic district, art galleries on King Street and the waterfront. Visit Fort Sumter, stroll through Waterfront Park at sunset, try Low Country cooking, and explore nearby plantations. The airport is 20 km from downtown; bus, taxi or car hire are all viable options.
