Best time to travel

November is a solid choice if you want low prices and prefer avoiding peak tourism season. Scotland in autumn has its charm: shorter days but fewer crowds at monuments and castles. The weather is cold, so pack a coat. Avoid December and January if budget flying matters to you.

What to do in Edinburgh

Edinburgh Castle is a must, reachable from Waverley Station in the city centre. Walk the Royal Mile, explore the old and new towns. If you like views, climb Arthur's Seat for panoramic sights across the city. Museums, galleries and character pubs complete your visit. Edinburgh works well for 3–4 days.
