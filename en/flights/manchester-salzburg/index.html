Best time to visit

Salzburg is a year-round destination. Winter (December-February) brings Christmas markets and nearby skiing. Spring and autumn are ideal for exploring the old town and nearby lakes. Summer attracts more tourists, so consider travelling off-season for better prices and fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Salzburg Airport is 4 km from the city centre. Hohensalzburg Fortress, the Cathedral and the old town are must-sees. Don't miss Mozart's birthplace, Mirabell Gardens and Lakes Fuschlsee and Königssee just a few kilometres away. The region is perfect for hiking and exploring nearby Alpine villages.
