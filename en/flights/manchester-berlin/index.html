Best time to travel

Berlin is worth visiting year-round, but autumn (September–October) and early winter (November–December) usually offer a good balance between price and weather. Summer months tend to be pricier. Plan ahead to secure the best fares.

What to do when you arrive

Berlin awaits with its history, world-class museums and nightlife. Walk past the Brandenburg Gate, the Wall and Museum Island. The city is walkable and well-served by public transport: you can reach the city centre from the airport by regional train quickly.
