Best time to visit

Mykonos is a beach and nightlife destination, so summer is peak season. For lower prices, consider spring (April–May) or autumn (September–October), when the weather is still excellent but crowds are smaller. Winter is quieter and cheaper, though daylight hours are shorter.

What to do when you arrive

Mykonos is small but lively: white-sand beaches (Paradise, Super Paradise), a fishing village with whitewashed churches, and a vibrant nightlife. The airport is 2 km from the main town. Rent a car or use local buses; many visitors walk the town centre. Don't miss the sunset views and consider a day trip to Delos, an important archaeological site.
