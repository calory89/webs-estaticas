Best time to visit

Jacksonville has a subtropical climate. The area is pleasant year-round, but summer (June to September) is very hot and humid. Autumn and winter are more comfortable. Regardless of season, search ahead: booking in advance helps find those €119–150 bargains.

What to do when you arrive

Jacksonville is the largest city by area in the continental US. Stroll downtown, visit the Cummer Museum of Art, explore Amelia Island beaches or relax in riverside parks. The airport (JAX) is about 20 km from the centre; bus, car hire or ride-sharing are common options.
