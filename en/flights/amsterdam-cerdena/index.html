Best time to visit

Sardinia works well almost year-round. June to September are peak (beaches, busy), while April–May and September–October bring pleasant weather with fewer crowds. Winter (December–February) is quieter and laid-back, perfect if you want peace. The sea is cooler, but you still enjoy the island fully.

What to do when you land

You arrive in Cagliari, the capital in the south. From there, explore La Pelosa beach (north), the village of Bosa, Costa Smeralda, or stay in Cagliari itself (old town, Roman amphitheatre). Food is excellent: malloreddus, bottarga, local cheeses. Rent a car for freedom or use buses to hop between towns.
