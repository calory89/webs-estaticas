Best time to travel

Phu Quoc has a tropical climate. The dry season runs from November to April with warm temperatures and less rainfall: the ideal time to visit. May to October is the monsoon season with frequent rain but potentially lower prices if budget is tight. December is peak season: good prices start from October, but rise towards year-end.

What to do on arrival

Phu Quoc offers pristine beaches, national parks and floating markets. Phu Quoc National Park and Duong Dong Beach are must-sees. You can visit pearl farms, dive or simply unwind on white sand. Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC) is well connected to the tourist centre, about 30 km away.
