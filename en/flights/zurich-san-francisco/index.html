Flights from Zurich to San Francisco start at €643 return with SAS. The median price sits around €751, so booking early and staying flexible with dates makes a real difference. Journey includes one stop and totals roughly a full day of travel.

Price and when to book

From €643 up to €760 in the samples we've tracked. The gap between the lowest fare and the median is around €100, which shows how much advance booking and date flexibility matter. Reserve at least 2-3 weeks ahead; the closer you get to departure, the higher the price climbs. Weekends and dates near holidays tend to cost more.

Airline and stops

SAS (Scandinavian Airlines) operates this route with one stop. Not a direct flight, but it's the option we're presenting. One intermediate stop is standard for this intercontinental connection; total travel time is around 14-16 hours door-to-door, depending on layover length.

Best time to visit

San Francisco has pleasant weather most of the year, but October to April brings cooler days and more fog. May to September is sunnier and warmer. November, as shown in the example (16 November), is late autumn: good prices, fewer crowds, mild temperatures. Avoid December-January if you're chasing a bargain.

What to do when you arrive

San Francisco airport (SFO) is 21 km from the city centre. Direct BART (rapid transit) connection into town. Don't miss the Golden Gate Bridge, Alcatraz, Castro neighbourhood, the Victorian houses at Painted Ladies, and Fisherman's Wharf. The city centre is highly walkable; hire a bike for more freedom to explore.
