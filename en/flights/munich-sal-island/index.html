What to do on Sal Island

Sal Island is all about beaches: crystal-clear waters, white sand and beachfront resorts. Santa Maria is the most touristy town, perfect for diving and snorkelling. Pedra Lume has abandoned salt mines you can explore. The island is small but ideal for a week off on a budget when you combine cheap flights with basic accommodation.
