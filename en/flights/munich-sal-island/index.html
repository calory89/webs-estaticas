Fares for this route range around €355, €368 and €403 return. Book as early as possible: prices climb as departure approaches. Check dates around mid-January (like 6–16 January) for the best rates. Avoid changing dates at the last minute, as it pushes costs up significantly.

Best time to travel

January is ideal: dry weather and pleasant temperatures on Sal Island, no rain. Winter flights often come at competitive fares since it's low season in Europe. Skip Christmas and New Year dates if you want the lowest price.

What to do on Sal Island

Sal Island is all about beaches: crystal-clear waters, white sand and beachfront resorts. Santa Maria is the most touristy town, perfect for diving and snorkelling. Pedra Lume has abandoned salt mines you can explore. The island is small but ideal for a week off on a budget when you combine cheap flights with basic accommodation.
