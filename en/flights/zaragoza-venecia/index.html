Price and when to book

The flight starts from €142 return. Prices in the sample range from €142 to €161, with a median of €160. To grab these fares, aim to book at least 6-8 weeks in advance. The earlier you reserve, the better your chances of avoiding surprise price hikes.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with one stopover. There are no direct flights, so you'll need to make a connection. Total travel time depends on your transit point and layover length. Check your booking to see where you'll change planes and plan your luggage accordingly.

Best time to travel

Venice is appealing year-round, but dodge peak summer (June-August) if you want fewer crowds and lower prices. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer good weather and fewer tourists. Winter is colder and damper, but often cheaper.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Marco Polo Airport, 13 km from Venice. From here, take water taxis or the public water bus to the historic centre. Once in the city, explore on foot: St Mark's Basilica, the Doge's Palace, iconic bridges and small squares. Transport is by boat—no streets, no cars, everything moves across the canals.
