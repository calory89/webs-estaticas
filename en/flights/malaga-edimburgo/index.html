Best time to visit Edinburgh

Edinburgh appeals year-round. Summer (June to August) offers long days and mild weather, though it's busier with tourists. Autumn (September to October) brings beautiful colours and pleasant temperatures. Winter and spring are quieter, ideal if you prefer fewer crowds. Choose based on your climate and activity preferences.

What to do in Edinburgh

Scotland's capital blends medieval history with modern life. Visit the iconic Edinburgh Castle, stroll the Royal Mile lined with shops and pubs, and climb Calton Hill for panoramic views. The surrounding area offers castles, Highlands scenery and charming villages. Don't miss Leith with its restaurants and galleries.
