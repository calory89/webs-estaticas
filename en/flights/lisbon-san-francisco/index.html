Price and when to book your flight

The cheapest ticket costs €655 return. The median price sits at €664, meaning half the searches fall above and half below that mark. We've seen fares up to €797, so book as soon as possible for better deals. Booking further ahead typically reveals more budget options.

Airline and stops

Royal Air Maroc is your carrier on this route. Your outbound flight includes 2 stops, so it's not direct. This extends your travel time but brings the final price down significantly. If comfort matters more than journey time, you can explore other options, but at this price point it's a solid trade-off.

Best time to visit

San Francisco enjoys mild weather most of the year. Summer and early fall (July to October) offer pleasant temperatures but draw crowds. Winter (December to February) is rainy and cold, though fares may dip. Avoid peak tourist seasons if you prefer a quieter visit.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at SFO international airport, 20 km from the city centre. Walk across the Golden Gate Bridge, ride the historic cable cars, explore Mission and Castro neighbourhoods, stroll the Embarcadero, and taste local food. Spend a few days in the city before exploring the wider Bay Area.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Lisbon to San Francisco?

No, this route includes 2 stops. Connections are necessary to achieve such a competitive price as €655.

Is Royal Air Maroc reliable?

Yes, it's Morocco's national airline and operates international routes. Check baggage policy and change terms on their website before booking.

What's the typical cost for a return ticket?

The median price for this route is €664. Some fares climb to €797, so prices near €655 are quite sharp. Booking well ahead increases your chances of finding cheaper options.

What should I know about San Francisco?

Weather is cool even in summer (15–20°C). The city is pricey but offers accommodation for all budgets. Public transport (BART, buses) connects airport and centre well. A transport pass is worth it if you're staying several days.
