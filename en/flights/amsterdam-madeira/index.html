Airline and stops

easyJet operates this route direct from Amsterdam (AMS) to Madeira (FNC). No stops, no plane changes, no wasted time on connections. Direct flights around 4 hours. The Irish carrier is your go-to for this link, with departures leaving around midday.

Best time to visit

Madeira enjoys mild weather year-round, but October, November, April and May are ideal: pleasant temperatures and less rain. Winter (December to February) is cooler and wetter, though still viable. Summer is warm but sunny. Book 2-3 months ahead for the best fares.

What to do when you arrive

Cristiano Ronaldo Airport (FNC) is 17 km from Funchal. Hire a car, take a taxi or use the bus from there. Madeira is a hiking paradise: Pico do Arieiro, Pico Ruivo or the levadas (water channels with views). Volcanic black sand beaches are stunning. Funchal, the capital, has markets, local food and nightlife. Don't miss the cable car over the city.

What to see in Madeira in 3-4 days?

Hiking Pico Ruivo or Arieiro, Funchal stroll, cable car over the capital, volcanic black sand beaches, and a levadas route. With more time, explore the north (São Vicente, Porto Moniz) and east. The island is compact yet packed with things to do.
