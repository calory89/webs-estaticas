Airline and connections

Air France operates this route with 2 stops on the outbound leg. It's not a direct flight, but connections are integrated, so your baggage goes all the way to Tokyo. Multiple stops are standard on long-haul Asian routes. The return leg may have a different number of stops depending on your chosen date.

Best time to visit

Tokyo shines in spring (March–May) and autumn (October–November), when weather is mild, though prices tend to rise. To save, travel in winter or summer, though July and August can be hot and humid. Avoid Japanese national holidays if you prefer fewer crowds at major attractions.

What to do when you arrive

Tokyo offers ancient temples, cutting-edge technology, world-class dining and unique neighbourhoods like Shibuya and Akihabara. Both Narita and Haneda airports are well connected to central Tokyo by train. Despite the long flight, it's worth it: you can experience a completely different country just 24 hours of time difference away.
