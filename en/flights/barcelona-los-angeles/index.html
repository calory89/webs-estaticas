Best time to visit

Los Angeles has good weather year-round. The best period is October to April, when temperatures are mild (15-20°C). Avoid July and August if you don't handle heat well. You'll arrive at Los Angeles International Airport (LAX), west of the city, about 30 km from downtown. From there, rent a car, take a bus or taxi. Don't miss Santa Monica, Griffith Observatory, Downtown LA, Malibu beaches and the Getty Center. LA is sprawling: plan your route by neighbourhood.
