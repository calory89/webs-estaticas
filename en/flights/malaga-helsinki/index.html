Best time to visit

Helsinki is stunning in winter if you want to chase the Northern Lights, though it gets very cold. Spring and autumn offer milder temperatures for exploring the city. Summer is short but has near-daylight around the clock. Choose based on what experience appeals to you most.

What to do when you arrive in Helsinki

Finland's capital is modern and welcoming. Visit the White Cathedral in Senate Square, the Rock Temple carved into granite, and browse contemporary design galleries and shops. Museums, speciality cafés, and vibrant nightlife make Helsinki a complete destination for 4–5 days.
