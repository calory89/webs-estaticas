Best time to visit

Tokyo is worthwhile year-round, but spring (March-May) and autumn (September-November) offer the best weather. If you travel in low season (summer or deep winter), you'll find more competitive fares. Avoid peak weeks around New Year and Golden Week (late April) if you're hunting bargains.

What to do when you arrive

Tokyo is massive: ancient temples like Senso-ji sit alongside ultra-modern districts such as Shibuya and Shinjuku. Don't miss the Imperial Palace, museums, parks (especially Ueno in spring), and the food scene. Narita Airport is about 60 km from central Tokyo; take the Narita Express train for quick, affordable access into the city.
