Price and when to book

The cheapest tickets on this route are around €592 per person return. The median sits at €684 and we've seen fares up to €812. For long-haul travel, the earlier you book, the better: prices rise as departure approaches. Search with 2–3 months' notice if possible. Sample flights shown are in January, low season in Tokyo.

Airlines and stops

Air China is your option here, with 1 stop on the route. It's not a direct flight, but it's the most economical choice. Total journey times can be lengthy on intercontinental flights; check the layover duration when reviewing your full itinerary.

Best time to visit

January is low season in Tokyo: fewer tourists, cheaper hotels, shorter queues. For milder weather, spring (March–April) and autumn (September–October) are ideal, though pricier. Tokyo winters are cold but dry; pack layers.

What to do on arrival

You'll fly into Tokyo's metropolitan area (TYO covers multiple airports; Narita and Haneda are the main hubs). In the city: temples like Senso-ji, iconic neighbourhoods like Shibuya and Shinjuku, parks, museums and world-class food. Japan is accessible and safe; public transport is superb.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Barcelona to Tokyo?

Not in this search. Air China offers 1 stop. Direct flights from Europe to Tokyo do exist with other carriers, but they're typically more expensive.

What's the cheapest price you've found?

€592 return per person with Air China. Prices vary by availability and travel dates; the median is €684.

When should I book to get the best price?

For long-haul routes like this, aim to book 8–12 weeks ahead if you can. From 3–4 weeks before departure, fares tend to climb. Set up price alerts so you don't miss the best deals.

What do you recommend seeing in Tokyo?

Senso-ji and Meiji Shrine, Shibuya Crossing, Shinjuku, the Imperial Palace, art museums and neighbourhoods like Harajuku. A JR Pass makes it easy to explore nearby cities such as Kyoto or Hiroshima.
