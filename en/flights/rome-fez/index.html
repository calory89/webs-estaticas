Best time to travel

Fez shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), when temperatures are mild. Avoid July and August if you don't handle intense heat well. Winter (December–February) is pleasant in Morocco and may see fewer tourists, although some travellers seek this out to enjoy it peacefully.

What to do in Fez

Fez is Morocco's cultural jewel. Don't miss the medina, a Unesco World Heritage site, with its souks, historic tanneries and palaces. Climb to a café terrace to see the city from above. The Karaouine Mosque and traditional riads round out the authentic experience. Visit the new town (Fez-Nouvelle) for shops and restaurants.
