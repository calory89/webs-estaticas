Airline and stops

Vueling operates this route. Your outbound flight includes 2 stops, so it's not direct. These connections are necessary to link Ibiza with Kuala Lumpur. Check the layover times when you book—with two stops, there's usually enough buffer if everything runs on schedule.

Best time to go

Kuala Lumpur is tropical and humid year-round. The sweet spot is November to March: less heat, less rain. Skip May to September if monsoon season doesn't appeal. November is especially good—mild temperatures and clearer skies. Prices in this window are usually reasonable if you book early.

What to do when you arrive

Kuala Lumpur will impress you: the Petronas Twin Towers dominate the skyline, night markets are unmissable, and the Batu Caves are 30 minutes out. Nature lovers can hop to the Perhentian Islands a short flight away. Street food is incredible—try laksa and enjoy rock-bottom local prices. Kuala Lumpur International Airport is well-served by public transport into town.
