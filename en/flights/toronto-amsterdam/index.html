Best time to visit

Amsterdam shines all year round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is colder but magical with lights and Christmas markets. Travel when it suits your plans best: prices don't drop by season, but by availability and how far ahead you book.

What to do when you arrive

Amsterdam awaits with canals, world-class museums (Van Gogh, Rijksmuseum) and bikes everywhere. Amsterdam Airport (AMS) is 10 km from the city centre, connected by direct train in 15 minutes. Explore the Red Light District, visit Anne Frank House, rent a bike and soak up Dutch life. Three, four or five days cover the essentials.
