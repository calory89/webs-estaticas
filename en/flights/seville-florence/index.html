Best time to visit Florence

Florence shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): good weather, far fewer crowds than summer, and hotel prices are more reasonable. Skip July and August if you don't fancy battling tourist hordes. Winter is quiet and cool, but monuments are less congested too.

What to do in Florence when you arrive

Florence Cathedral (Brunelleschi's dome), the Uffizi Gallery, Ponte Vecchio, and the Basilica of Santa Croce are must-sees. Climb the dome for 360° views. Stroll the historic centre, try bistecca alla fiorentina at a local spot, and spend an afternoon walking both sides of the Arno. Florence Airport (Peretola) is 4 km from the centre; bus or taxi takes 15–20 minutes.
