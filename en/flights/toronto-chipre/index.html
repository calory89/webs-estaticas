Toronto and Cyprus are closer than you think: Porter Airlines gets you there from €816 return. Median price sits around €871, so you have room to find good deals. Three stops is standard on this route, but the price makes up for it.

Price and when to book

€816 is the best you'll find on this route. Prices range from €816 to €906 depending on dates and availability. Book as early as possible: flights 2–3 months ahead tend to have better fares. Check weekday options; weekends are usually pricier.

Airline and connections

Porter Airlines is your option on this search. Expect 3 stopovers on the outbound leg, meaning at least two aircraft changes. Not a direct flight, but competitive at this price. Bring a charger and patience: ground time at connections varies, so check your booking confirmation for where you'll change planes.

Best time to visit

Cyprus shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): pleasant weather, uncrowded beaches, and fairer prices than summer. July and August are scorching and expensive. Winter (November–March) is quiet and budget-friendly, though rain is possible. Skip Christmas and New Year if you're chasing a bargain.

What to do when you arrive

Larnaca International Airport (LCA) is 6 km from town. Cyprus offers white-sand beaches in Ayia Napa, history in Nicosia and Paphos, hiking in the Troodos Mountains, and Mediterranean food in any taverna. Don't miss Kolossi Castle or wine-village routes. Car rental or taxis are affordable.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Toronto to Cyprus?

No. This route has 3 stops with Porter Airlines. Direct Toronto–Cyprus flights are uncommon; connections are the norm.

Why are these flights so cheap?

Porter Airlines and multiple stopovers keep costs down. Travelling with connections is more economical, though total journey time is longer.

When is the best time to book?

Book 2–3 months ahead. Avoid Christmas, New Year, and July–August if price is your priority.

What can you do in Cyprus?

Beaches in Ayia Napa and Paphos, Kourion ruins, mountain villages with wineries, Troodos hiking, and local food. It's a full destination for 7–10 days.
