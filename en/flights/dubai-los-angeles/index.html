Los Angeles boasts iconic beaches (Santa Monica, Malibu), attractions like the Hollywood Sign, world-class museums and vibrant nightlife. LAX airport is 30 km from the city centre; arrange transport ahead of time. Spend at least a week enjoying the city and surrounding areas.
