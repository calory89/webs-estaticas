Best time to travel

Hamburg shines from May to September: pleasant temperatures and long daylight. For cheaper prices, travel in winter (November to March), though the weather is cold and grey. Skip German end-of-year festivities if you want to avoid crowds and price spikes.

What to do in Hamburg

The port is the city's heart: walk the Landungsbrücken docks and climb the Michels (Germany's largest church). Explore Speicherstadt, a Unesco-listed neighbourhood with canals and historic warehouses. World-class museums, shopping on Möhlendamm avenue, and great food: try smoked eel and local gingerbread.
