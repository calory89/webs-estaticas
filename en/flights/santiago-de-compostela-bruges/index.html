Best time to visit

Bruges is beautiful any time of year, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than July–August. Winter has its charm too, especially if you catch the Christmas market season.

What to do in Bruges

Don't miss the Main Square (Grote Markt), the medieval canals that shape the city, the Basilica of the Holy Blood, and the Belfry of Bruges. Wandering aimlessly through cobbled streets is practically mandatory. Grab a Belgian waffle and try the local beer.

You'll land at Brussels-Zaventem airport (BRU), about 15 km from Bruges. Direct train to the city takes roughly 20 minutes.
