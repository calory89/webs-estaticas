Price and when to book

Tickets start from £132 per person (return). The median price sits at £177, with variations up to £196. Booking as far in advance as possible is your best strategy to keep costs down. The earlier you purchase, the more cheap options you'll find.

Airline and flight type

You'll travel with Iberia Airlines. The outbound flight includes 1 stopover, which is standard for this route. It's not direct, but it remains a competitive option in terms of price and reliability.

Best time to visit

Bruges is beautiful any time of year, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than July–August. Winter has its charm too, especially if you catch the Christmas market season.

What to do in Bruges

Don't miss the Main Square (Grote Markt), the medieval canals that shape the city, the Basilica of the Holy Blood, and the Belfry of Bruges. Wandering aimlessly through cobbled streets is practically mandatory. Grab a Belgian waffle and try the local beer.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Santiago to Bruges?

No, with Iberia Airlines this route includes 1 stopover. That's standard between these two cities.

What's the cheapest price you've seen?

From £132 return. It depends on dates, availability, and how far in advance you book.

Is it safe to book now for December?

Yes, the more advance notice you give yourself, the better. Flight prices are usually more competitive when you book 2–3 months ahead.

Where do I land in Bruges?

You'll land at Brussels-Zaventem airport (BRU), about 15 km from Bruges. Direct train to the city takes roughly 20 minutes.
