Best time to visit

Helsinki shines in winter (December to February) if you chase the Northern Lights and snow, and in summer (June to August) for long daylight and mild weather. Spring and autumn are peaceful and less crowded. Each season has its charm: choose based on what you want to experience.

Things to do when you arrive

Helsinki airport (HEL) is 20 km from the city centre. Visit the white cathedral and the Church in the Rock, wander the harbour and traditional markets, or explore the Design Museum. In winter, book trips to Laponia to see the Northern Lights. In summer, the Finnish archipelago islands are perfect for getaways.
