What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Amílcar Cabral International Airport on Sal Island. Easy access to Santa Maria and Ponta Leme beaches, perfect for diving and snorkelling. Visit Santa Maria town for fresh local seafood, explore the historic salt ponds, or relax on the coast. The climate is dry and windy, ideal for kitesurfing if that appeals to you. It's a small island but has everything you need for a hassle-free beach break.
