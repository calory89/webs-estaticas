Fly direct from Gran Canaria to Venice with Ryanair for just €55 return. Prices typically hover around €81, making this an affordable escape to the Venetian lagoon.

Price and when to book

Tickets start from €55 (return) if you catch them at the right moment. Most searches land around €81, so that's your realistic target. Book as far in advance as possible—the earlier you purchase, the better the price. Avoid last-minute bookings when fares jump to €153 or higher. Check Ryanair's calendar regularly several weeks before your trip.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route, and the best part is it's a direct flight with no stops. You'll depart from Gran Canaria airport (LPA) and arrive directly in Venice (VCE). Typical flights leave late afternoon (like the 11 November example at 16:40) and touch down early morning the following day (like 17 November at 07:10). Check Ryanair's luggage policy: hand baggage is included, but checked bags incur a fee.

Best time to visit

Venice welcomes visitors year-round, but skip July, August and school holidays for lower prices and fewer crowds. Autumn (September to November) and spring (March to May) offer pleasant weather and fewer tourists. Winter (December to February) is the quietest season, though temperatures drop and fog is common.

What to do on arrival

Venice airport is about 13 km from the city. Get there by bus, express water taxi or water taxi. Once in Venice, take a gondola ride down the Grand Canal (or the cheaper vaporetto), explore St Mark's Basilica, the Doge's Palace, and wander the narrow streets. Eat at local osterie and try authentic tiramisu before you leave.

What else is there to see in Venice besides the city centre?

The lagoon islands: Murano for glassblowing and Burano for colourful houses. The Lido for beaches, museums inside St Mark's Basilica and the Doge's Palace, and historic bridges like the Rialto.
