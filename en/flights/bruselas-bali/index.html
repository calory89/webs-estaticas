Price and when to book

Brussels to Bali flights with Ryanair start from €628 as the lowest fare, with a median of €689. Prices reach up to €781 in high-demand periods. Booking 2-3 months ahead secures better rates. Final price depends on dates, flexibility and baggage, which Ryanair charges separately.

Airline and stops

You'll fly with Ryanair, which operates this route with 2 stops. It's not direct, but it's the cheapest option on the market. The stops add travel time (typically 20-30 hours total), but the cost savings are significant compared to other carriers.

Best time to travel

Bali has tropical climate year-round. The dry season (April to October) is most popular and stable. November to March is wetter but cheaper for accommodation. Flight prices vary by tourist season, so check fares week by week.

What to do upon arrival

You'll land at Bali International Airport (Denpasar). Ubud is ideal for temples, nature and culture. Beaches at Seminyak, Canggu and Uluwatu offer surfing and resorts. Don't miss Tanah Lot temple on clifftops and Tegallalang rice fields. Rent a scooter or arrange local transport to get around.
