Best time to travel

January offers good prices, as shown by the sample flight on 8 January. Winter is generally cheaper than spring and summer. If your schedule is flexible, avoid July and August to find better rates. The route runs year-round.

What to do when you arrive in Zurich

Zurich is your gateway to Switzerland. The airport is well connected to the city centre by train in 10 minutes. The city boasts world-class museums, lakeside walks, luxury shopping and quality Swiss cuisine. From Zurich, you can easily reach the Alps, Bern or Lucerne by train.

Lake Zurich is a must for a stroll. Visit the Altstadt (old town) with its historic churches, the Swiss National Museum and the well-maintained lakeside. The city is also a centre for luxury shopping and fine dining.
