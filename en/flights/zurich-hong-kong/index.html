Best time to visit

Hong Kong is pleasant October to March, with mild temperatures and low humidity. Avoid May to September due to extreme heat and typhoon season. Travelling in shoulder season (October–November or February–March) usually balances weather and availability well. Check the price calendar for the dates you're considering.

What to do when you arrive

Hong Kong is a city of contrasts: skyscrapers next to ancient temples. Take the Peak Tram for the most iconic views, explore Victoria Harbour's markets, visit the fishing villages on nearby islands, or relax on Lamma's beaches. The MTR is fast and cheap; most signs are in English and Chinese. Plan for 4–5 days or longer.

What else is there to see in Hong Kong besides the Peak?

Victoria Harbour is essential for ferry rides and views. Night markets (Temple Street, Ladies' Market) are authentic experiences. Lantau and Lamma islands offer beaches and quiet villages. Tai Lam Shan is great for hiking with views. Don't miss Wong Tai Sin and Man Mo temples, full of history.
