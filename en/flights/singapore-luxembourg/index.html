Best time to visit Luxembourg

Luxembourg shines in autumn (September to November) with mild temperatures and changing landscapes. Spring (April to May) is also excellent: less summer crowds and the Ardennes forests are spectacular. If you want to avoid peak season and save money, skip July and August. Winter is cold but magical, especially around Christmas with the festive markets.

What to do when you arrive in Luxembourg

Luxembourg airport is 6 km from the capital. The old town is a UNESCO World Heritage site: walk the Corniche (stunning views), explore the ramparts and visit the casemates below ground. The Luxembourg Ardennes are perfect for hiking. From here you can day-trip to Brussels (45 minutes by train), the German Moselle wine region, or stay in the capital, small but charming for 2-3 days.
