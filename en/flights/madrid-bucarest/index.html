Best time to visit

Bucharest is lovely almost year-round, but spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Winter is cold but perfect if you fancy the festive atmosphere. Skip August if you prefer fewer tourists.

What to do when you arrive

Bucharest airport (Otopeni) is about 16 km from the city centre. Head to the Old Town to wander medieval streets and charming bars. Don't miss the Ceaușescu Monument, Stavropoleos Church and a stroll through Herăstrău Park. Romanian food is affordable and delicious.
