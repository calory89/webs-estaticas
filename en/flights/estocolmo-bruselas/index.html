Price and when to book

The lowest price found is €55 return, though most dates hover around €56. We've also seen fares at €96 during peak demand periods. Book at least 4–6 weeks ahead to lock in the best rates. Prices climb as departure dates approach, so don't ignore good deals when you spot them.

Airline and layovers

Ryanair is your option on this route and it operates direct flights with no layovers. The outbound departs early morning (around 07:30) and the return leaves in the evening (around 21:40). Flying direct saves time: no terminal shuffling and you get to your destination faster.

Best time to visit

Brussels is worth visiting year-round, but flight prices tend to drop in winter (January–February) and early spring (March–April), away from peak summer and Christmas periods. If you prefer pleasant weather and fewer crowds, consider May, June or September. Steer clear of December, Easter and August if you're budget-conscious.

What to do when you arrive

Brussels is Belgium's heart: the Grand Place is essential, along with St Michael's Cathedral and the Royal Palace. Taste Belgian beer, waffles and artisan chocolate. Brussels airport (BRU) connects well to the city centre by train, bus or taxi. Use Brussels' central location to day-trip to Antwerp, Bruges or Ghent.

What should I see in Brussels?

The Grand Place is the main draw, a UNESCO World Heritage site. Don't miss St Michael's Cathedral, the Royal Palace, Manneken Pis and art museums. Dine at Grand Place terraces, buy Belgian chocolate from old town shops and enjoy local beers at any café.
