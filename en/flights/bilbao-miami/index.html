Best time to travel

Miami has sunshine year-round, but avoid June to October due to hurricane season. December to April is peak season with the best weather, though prices are higher. If you want a balance between good weather and better fares, September and November can be solid choices with fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive in Miami

Miami offers beaches for all tastes at South Beach and Miami Beach, vibrant neighbourhoods like Wynwood with its murals, the Design District for shopping, and lively Latin culture in Little Havana. Miami International Airport (MIA) is 6 km from the city centre, well-connected by public transport and taxi.
