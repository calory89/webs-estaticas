Best time to travel

Miami is a year-round destination, but the climate is most pleasant from November to April: warm temperatures without summer extremes. If you travel during European winter, you'll escape the cold. Note that June to September is hurricane season in Florida. Year-end and Easter holidays tend to bring pricier fares.

What to do when you arrive

Miami offers beaches in South Beach, the Arts District in Wynwood, Little Havana for Cuban culture, and the historic Coral Gables neighbourhood. Miami International Airport (MIA) is 15 km from the city centre; you have transport options: taxi, bus or car hire. Walking the waterfront and sampling local cuisine are musts.
