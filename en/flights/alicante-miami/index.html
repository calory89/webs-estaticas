Best time to travel

Miami is a year-round destination, but October to April is dry season with the best temperatures. April-May and September-October are quieter than January-February. Summer (June-September) brings more storms and slightly lower fares, but also higher humidity. December peaks with holiday pricing.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Miami International Airport (MIA), 10 km from the centre. The beach is the first thing most visitors head for: South Beach, Miami Beach, Wynwood for street art and galleries. Vizcaya Archaeological Park, Little Havana for Cuban food and Miami Beach's Art Deco Historic District are classics. You'll need transport from the airport: taxi, rideshare or car rental.
