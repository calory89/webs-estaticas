Best time to visit

Hamburg is beautiful year-round, but May to September offer the best weather and more activities. If you're after cheaper fares, winter months (November to February) and early spring tend to be more economical. Any time is good to explore the canals and architecture.

What to do when you arrive

Hamburg has plenty to offer: stroll the port (Hafen), visit St. Michael's Church for panoramic views, explore St. Pauli and its famous canals, and wander the Elba museums. Hamburg Airport connects well to the city centre by train and bus. Don't miss the fish market at sunrise or the museums along the Elba.

What to see in Hamburg in 4 days?

Four days is plenty. Spend one day at the port and St. Pauli, one exploring the canals and Elba museums, one visiting churches and shopping (Möhlenhof, Colonnaden), and the last discovering local neighbourhoods like Eppendorf or making nearby trips.
