Best time to visit

Hamburg is attractive year-round, but May to September offer the most pleasant weather. Prices tend to be higher in summer and European holidays. For the best fares, travel in low season: November, January or February. Winter also brings the charm of Hamburg's Christmas Market if you travel in December.

What to do when you arrive in Hamburg

Hamburg Airport (Fuhlsbüttel) is 9 km from the city centre. Explore the port, canals and historic Speicherstadt warehouses. Don't miss Lake Alster, St. Michael's Church and the nightlife of Reeperbahn. Hamburg blends Hanseatic history, art and top-class German cuisine.
