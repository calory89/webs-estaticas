What to do in Bologna

Bologna stands out for its medieval old town, leaning towers, the Basilica of San Petronio and Italy's longest arcades. It's the cradle of Emilian gastronomy: try tagliatelle al ragù, tortellini and mortadella. The city's museums and university atmosphere make Bologna a vibrant destination. Guglielmo Marconi international airport is just a few km from the centre.
