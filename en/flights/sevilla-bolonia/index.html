Airline and direct flights

Ryanair operates this route with no stops. Your outbound flight departs at 20:45 from Seville and arrives in Bologna. The return leaves at 17:30. Being a short, direct journey saves you time and skips hassle. Remember Ryanair charges for large carry-on and checked baggage: check their website to see what's included in your fare.

Best time to visit

Bologna shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather, fewer crowds than summer, and more reasonable prices. Winter is cold and grey, but also less touristy. Avoid August if you want peace and better value. Christmas and New Year push fares higher.

What to do when you arrive

Guglielmo Marconi Airport is 6 km from the centre. Bologna is Italy's food capital: eat tortellini, mortadella, and ragù bolognese. Stroll through medieval towers, Piazza Maggiore, and the Basilica of San Petronio. The porticoed galleries shelter you from rain. Rent a bike: it's the perfect city for it.

The historic centre: towers, Piazza Maggiore, Basilica of San Petronio. Taste local food at any osterìa. Walk under the porticoes. If you have time, visit the Pinacoteca di Bologna or take a short train trip to Modena (30 minutes).
