Best time to travel

November is a smart choice: you'll dodge summer crowds and prices are lower than peak season. Venice in autumn has genuine charm with fewer tourists. Be aware of acqua alta (high water flooding) if you travel late October or early November, though it's unpredictable. December tends to be pricier due to Christmas.

What to do when you arrive

Marco Polo Airport is 12 km from the city centre. Take the ATVO bus, train, or a direct water taxi to the Grand Canal. Once in Venice, forget cars: everything is on foot or by boat. Visit Basilica di San Marco, the Doge's Palace, cross the Rialto Bridge. Take a gondola if you wish, but it's pricey and touristy. Eat at small trattorias away from San Marcos Square for better value.

FAQ

Is €587 per person or total?

Per person, return. If two of you travel, multiply by two. Always check the breakdown on the booking page before confirming.

Can I avoid the stopover?

No. This route requires 1 stop with SAS. There is no direct flight available in our data.

Which day of the week is cheapest?

We can't guarantee which specific day is cheaper. What works: book early and be flexible with dates. Compare several consecutive days on the search engine.

What should I know about Venice before going?

Venice is unique but pricey. Buy the Venice Card if you'll use public transport a lot. Carry cash alongside your card. November brings more rain and possible acqua alta. Streets are a maze: download an offline map. Eat away from the main square to save money.
