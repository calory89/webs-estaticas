Best time to travel

November is a good window: it's spring in the southern hemisphere and Florida hasn't hit peak tourist season yet. Prices are often more competitive than high season (December-January). Avoid July-August in Sydney (winter) if you want good weather.

What to do when you arrive

Fort Lauderdale puts you at the gateway to Miami and its iconic beaches. Explore Fort Lauderdale's beachfront, Miami Port, Wynwood with its street art murals, and day trips to the Everglades. If you dive, the area is excellent for reef trips.
