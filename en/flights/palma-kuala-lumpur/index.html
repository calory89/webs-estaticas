Best time to travel

Kuala Lumpur is tropical year-round. The dry season on the west coast runs from June to September, though it's always warm and humid. Avoid local festivals if you prefer fewer crowds, or seek them out for cultural experience. Regardless of season, prices fluctuate, so keep monitoring fares.

What to do when you arrive

Kuala Lumpur is Malaysia's beating heart. The Petronas Towers are the city's icon. You'll also find Batu Caves (temple caves), the Central Market for local shopping, and gardens like Perdana. Outside the city, Malaysian rainforest offers trekking and wildlife. Eat at local food courts: excellent food and very cheap.
