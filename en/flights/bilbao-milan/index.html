Best time to travel

Milan is attractive year-round: spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer ideal weather and fewer crowds. December has festive charm but more tourists. Avoid August if you prefer less tourism. At these prices, nearly any date is affordable.

What to do when you arrive

Milan is the capital of design and fashion. Don't miss the Duomo, the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele, La Scala and the Church of Santa Maria delle Grazie (home to Leonardo's The Last Supper). Brera is the perfect neighbourhood for strolling, eating and soaking up the atmosphere.
