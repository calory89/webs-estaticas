Best time to visit

Kuala Lumpur is tropical and wet year-round. November–February is less humid (29–31°C). March–October brings stronger heat and monsoons. Sydney in November is late spring (18–25°C, pleasant). Travel during Malaysia's wet season and prices may dip slightly, though pack a raincoat.

What to do on arrival

Kuala Lumpur is compact: Petronas Towers, Batu Caves, Chinatown markets. Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) is 75 km out; direct train to city centre in 30 mins (cheap, fast). Beaches nearby in Selangor. For nature, Peninsular Malaysia has jungle, islands and caves. From Sydney, short flights to Brisbane or Canberra if you extend your trip.
