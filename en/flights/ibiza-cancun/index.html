Fly from Ibiza to Cancun from €805 return. The median price sits at €905. With one stop and Iberia Express, it's the most affordable option to reach the Riviera Maya from the Balearics.

Price and when to book

Flights start from €805 return, though most hover around €905. Book as early as possible: search 4–5 weeks ahead to secure the best fares. Travel in November, December or January for more competitive pricing on this long-haul route.

Airline and stopovers

Iberia Express operates this route with one stop. It's not direct, but one layover is standard on this connection. Check your booking for the stopover location and ground time.

Best time to visit

November, December and January are ideal for flying to Cancun: dry weather, pleasant temperatures (25–28°C) and less rainfall. Avoid September and October (Atlantic hurricane season). Book well ahead if travelling at Christmas or New Year.

What to do when you arrive in Cancun

Playa del Carmen and the Riviera Maya are under an hour away. Visit Tulum (Mayan ruins by the sea), Cozumel (diving), Xel-Há (nature park) or cenotes. Cancun city itself has beaches, nightlife and shopping. The international airport is 25 km from the centre.

FAQ

What's the cheapest flight price from Ibiza to Cancun?

From €805 return. This price is achievable by booking well in advance and staying flexible with dates. The median price is €905.

Is the flight from Ibiza to Cancun direct?

No, it has one stop. Iberia Express operates this route with a layover. Check connection times when booking.

Which airline flies from Ibiza to Cancun?

Iberia Express. They connect these two cities on this route.

What to see in Cancun and nearby?

Playa del Carmen, Tulum (ruins), Cozumel, Xel-Há, cenotes, and Cancun's hotel strip. Everything is between 30 minutes and 2 hours from the airport.
