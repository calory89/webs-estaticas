Air Arabia: direct with no stops

Air Arabia operates this route as a direct flight with no layovers. You board in Dubai and land in London without changing planes. It's the most comfortable and fastest option for this journey. Air Arabia is the UAE's low-cost carrier, which explains these competitive fares.

Best time to visit

Dubai is warm year-round, but October to April offer more pleasant temperatures. London is colder in winter and experiences rain most of the year. To avoid crowds and high prices, travel during low season (January–February or September). Fares typically spike during school holidays and festive periods, so plan well ahead.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in the London area (LON), which includes major airports: Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, Luton or City. London offers world-class free museums (British Museum, National Gallery), Big Ben, Tower Bridge, Hyde Park and endless cultural and dining options. Public transport is excellent: get an Oyster Card for the tube and buses.
