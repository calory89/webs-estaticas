Ryanair connects Dublin to Dubrovnik with no stops from €64 per person (return). The median sits around €72, perfect for travellers seeking the Dalmatian Coast without breaking the bank. We've spotted fares up to €78 in our sample checked on 22 October.

Price and when to book your ticket

Fares range from €64 to €78 return. The earlier you book, the better your chances of grabbing the lowest prices: the €14 spread between minimum and maximum is noticeable. Reserve well in advance and check fares regularly; small fluctuations are normal on budget routes.

Airline and connections

Fly with Ryanair, which operates a direct service from Dublin to Dubrovnik. No stops means less hassle, shorter flight time and quicker arrival on the Dalmatian coast. Ryanair rules Europe's budget market: take advantage of competitive pricing but check what's included (hand luggage, checked baggage).

Best time to travel

November is shoulder season in Dubrovnik: fewer summer crowds, pleasant weather and lower fares than July–August. Autumn is ideal for exploring the Old Town without throngs of tourists. If you want guaranteed beach weather, May to September works; if you prefer fewer people and better prices, autumn and early winter are your best bets.

What to do when you arrive

The airport is 20 km from the city. Walk the Old Town walls for Adriatic views, an unmissable 1.9 km loop. Visit the Rector's Palace, cathedral and seafront cafés along Stradun. Take a boat to the Elafiti islands for quiet beaches, or explore Cavtat, a coastal town 20 minutes away. Try local seafood stew and regional wine.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for Dublin-Dubrovnik flights?

€64 return per person with Ryanair. This is the lowest in our sample; book early to have the best shot at securing it.

Is Dublin to Dubrovnik a direct flight?

Yes, Ryanair operates a non-stop service between the two cities. Departure from Dublin around 16:00 and arrival in Dubrovnik the following morning (e.g. return 11:50).

Which airline flies this route?

Ryanair is the main operator on this connection and offers the market's most competitive fares for this route.

What can I visit in Dubrovnik?

The Old Town walls are the must-see attraction, encircling the city with Adriatic views. Stroll Stradun, visit the Rector's Palace, sample local cuisine and take a boat to the Elafiti islands for quiet beaches.
