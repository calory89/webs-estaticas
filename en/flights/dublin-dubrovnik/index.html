Airline and connections

Fly with Ryanair, which operates a direct service from Dublin to Dubrovnik. No stops means less hassle, shorter flight time and quicker arrival on the Dalmatian coast. Ryanair rules Europe's budget market: take advantage of competitive pricing but check what's included (hand luggage, checked baggage).

Best time to travel

November is shoulder season in Dubrovnik: fewer summer crowds, pleasant weather and lower fares than July–August. Autumn is ideal for exploring the Old Town without throngs of tourists. If you want guaranteed beach weather, May to September works; if you prefer fewer people and better prices, autumn and early winter are your best bets.

What to do when you arrive

The airport is 20 km from the city. Walk the Old Town walls for Adriatic views, an unmissable 1.9 km loop. Visit the Rector's Palace, cathedral and seafront cafés along Stradun. Take a boat to the Elafiti islands for quiet beaches, or explore Cavtat, a coastal town 20 minutes away. Try local seafood stew and regional wine.
