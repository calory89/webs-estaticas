Best time to visit

Bali is tropical with dry season (April–October) and wet season (November–March). Dry season is busier and pricier, but drier. For beach time without crowds and good weather, aim for April-May or September-October. The example trip (December) falls in wet season, with more rain likely but fewer tourists. Choose based on what matters to you.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Bali's Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS), about 13 km from Denpasar. Taxis and buses connect to town. Bali has beaches in Seminyak and Kuta, temples like Tanah Lot, rice paddies around Ubud, and diving off Amed. The south is beach tourism; inland is nature and culture. Three or four days covers the basics; a week lets you enjoy without rushing.

What to see in Bali in a week?

Beaches in the south (Seminyak, Kuta), temples (Tanah Lot, Besakih), rice fields and villages in Ubud, plus trips to Mount Agung or diving in the northeast. A week gives you time to see it all without rush.
