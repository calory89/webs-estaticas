Price and when to book

Fares start at €264 return, with real examples at €264, €278 and €367. Search at least 3–4 weeks ahead: prices climb as departure approaches. October is a good month to book November flights. Compare flexible dates within the same week to find cheaper combinations.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with 2 stops on the outbound flight. It's not a direct service, but the price is competitive. Connecting flights take longer, so book if you're willing to spend extra time at layovers in exchange for lower fares.

Best time to travel

November is ideal: Greek summer is over, Mykonos sees far fewer tourists, and flight prices drop. Temperatures hover around 20°C, perfect for exploring without summer heat. Avoid July and August if you want cheap flights; those months see peak demand and peak prices.

What to do when you arrive

Mykonos is known for beaches, whitewashed villages and nightlife. The international airport (JMK) is 2 km from the port. Wander Little Venice, visit blue-domed churches, enjoy beaches like Paradise or Psarou, and sample Greek cuisine. November means fewer crowds than summer.

FAQ

What's the cheapest flight price from Gran Canaria to Mykonos?

The lowest recorded fare is €264 return with Ryanair. The median price is €278. Book early to secure the best rates.

Is it a direct flight or does it have stops?

Ryanair operates this route with 2 stops on the outbound leg; it's not direct. Connecting flights take longer but offer significantly lower fares.

When is the best time to visit Mykonos?

November is excellent: summer crowds are gone, flight prices are lower, and weather is mild (around 20°C). July and August are peak season and much more expensive.

What can I see and do in Mykonos?

Mykonos has stunning beaches, typical Cycladic whitewashed villages, Little Venice, blue-domed churches, and good food options. It's versatile: beach days, sunset walks through town, and evening atmosphere.
