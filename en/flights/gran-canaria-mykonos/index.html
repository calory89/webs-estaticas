Best time to visit

Mykonos is a beach and party destination, ideal in summer (June to September). If you want fewer crowds and better prices, consider May, October or November. The weather is pleasant and locals get a breather. Winter (December to February) is low season: cooler and some businesses close, but prices drop.

What to do when you arrive in Mykonos

Pristine beaches at Kalafatis or Kalo Livadi, white villages like Ano Mera, nightlife on the coast, Panagia Paraportiani church, museums and local food. Mykonos airport (JMK) is 2 km from the port and about 15 km from the capital. Rent a car or use the bus to get around.
