What to do in Bucharest

Romania's capital blends communist architecture with bohemian neighbourhoods. Visit the Palace of Parliament (Europe's largest building), stroll through Lipscani (the old town), explore art galleries and try local cuisine in Obor or Lipscani restaurants. Henri Coandă Airport is 30 minutes away, well served by taxis and bus.
