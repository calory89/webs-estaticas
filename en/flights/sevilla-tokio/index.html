Best time to visit Tokyo

Tokyo is worth visiting any season, but spring (late March to April) and autumn (September to November) offer ideal weather and fewer crowds in some spots. Winter is dry with cool temps (5–10°C), perfect if you like cold. Summer is hot and humid. Check availability based on the dates you're interested in.

What to do when you arrive in Tokyo

Tokyo is a global capital: ancient temples like Senso-ji, vibrant neighbourhoods like Shibuya and Shinjuku, world-class food, museums and parks. The biggest airport is Narita, 60 km from the centre; Haneda is even closer. Fast trains run direct from the airport to central Tokyo. Budget at least 4–5 days to cover the basics without rushing.
