Best time to visit

Sardinia is a beach destination, making summer peak season. For fewer crowds and potentially lower flight prices, consider trips outside July and August. Spring (April–May) and early autumn (September) offer excellent weather with less tourism congestion. Flight prices vary with demand, so flexibility with dates is essential.

What to do when you arrive in Cagliari

Cagliari, Sardinia's capital, blends beaches with culture. Poetto Beach is the highlight: 8 km of golden sand right at the city's doorstep. Explore the medieval Castello quarter, visit the Cathedral of Santa Maria, and enjoy views from the Basílica di Bonaria. Sample local Sardinian cuisine: malloreddus, bottarga, and regional wine are must-tries. If time allows, visit nearby beaches like Chia or Villasimius, or venture inland to discover charming villages.
