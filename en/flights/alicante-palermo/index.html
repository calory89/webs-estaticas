Best time to travel

Sicily is a sun and sea destination. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer perfect weather without summer crowds. Winter is mild on the coast; if you go November–February, temperatures are pleasant but rain is more likely. July and August are hot and very touristy. Pick based on whether you prefer quiet beaches or busier atmosphere.

What to do when you arrive

Palermo is Sicily's capital: walk the historic centre, visit the Cathedral, explore traditional markets (Ballarò, Capo). Food is stunning: arancini, pasta alla Norma, granita. From here it's easy to reach Mondello beach (30 km away) or take trips to Monreale. The airport is about 30 km from the centre; bus or car hire are straightforward options.
