What to do in Jacksonville

Jacksonville is your gateway to Florida's coast. Walk the River Walk, visit beaches like Jacksonville Beach and Neptune Beach, and explore the Historic San Marco neighbourhood. Great as a base to combine with other Florida destinations if you have extra days.
