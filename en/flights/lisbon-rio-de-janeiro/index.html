Best time to visit

Rio has a tropical climate. The southern summer (December to February) is hot and rainy; spring (September to November) and autumn (March to May) offer pleasanter temperatures. November is a strong choice: less rain, fewer crowds and gentler prices than peak season. Avoid Carnival if you seek peace and quiet (February/March).

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Tom Jobim International Airport. Don't miss Cristo Redentor, Sugarloaf Mountain, Copacabana and Ipanema beaches, and the bohemian Lapa neighbourhood. The city blends culture, nature and beach life. Book accommodation early in busy periods and use the metro or buses to get around.
