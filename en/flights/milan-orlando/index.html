Airline and stops

You'll travel with Ryanair. The outbound flight includes 1 stop, so it's not direct. Check where the stopover is: Ryanair typically stops at European airports. Verify the total journey time when booking, including connection time.

Best time to visit

Orlando is a year-round tourist destination. Winter (December to February) is cooler with less rain. Summer is hot and humid. Outside school holidays and festive periods (spring and Christmas), crowds tend to thin out and prices can be more moderate.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Orlando International Airport (MCO). The area is famous for theme parks, especially Disney World and Universal Studios. For a quieter break, explore downtown Orlando, the region's fountains and lakes, or day trip to nearby Florida coast beaches.
