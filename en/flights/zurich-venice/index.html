The cheapest flight we've found is €158 return, and most tickets hover around €179. We've seen prices reach €231. As always: the earlier you book, the more you save. Buy well in advance and avoid last-minute changes.

Airline and stops

easyJet operates this route. You have one stop on the outbound flight, so it's not direct. Hand baggage is included; check the exact weight allowance on their website. The return flight takes you from Venice to Zurich with the same airline.

Best time to visit

Venice is beautiful year-round, but choose based on your preferences: spring and autumn offer mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is quieter and flights often cost less. Avoid peak tourist seasons if you prefer to move around comfortably.

What to do when you arrive

Venice (VCE) awaits. St Mark's Basilica, Doge's Palace, Rialto Bridge and the Grand Canal are essential. Take time to wander the narrow streets: you'll find local bars, galleries and squares soaked in history. Tip: climb a bell tower for views of the lagoon from above.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price Zurich to Venice?

€158 return. It's the lowest we've recorded. The median sits around €179, so book early if you want that deal.

Does easyJet fly direct from Zurich to Venice?

No. The outbound flight includes one stop. Check your booking confirmation to see where you stop.

What should I see in Venice in 3 or 4 days?

St Mark's, Doge's Palace, Rialto, the Grand Canal, the Basilica di Santa Maria Gloriosa dei Frari and the Basilica di Santa Maria della Salute. Spend time wandering neighbourhoods like Cannaregio or Dorsoduro. Vaporettos (water buses) take you everywhere.
