Airline and stopovers

Best time to visit

December brings cold weather but Bruges is spectacular: Christmas markets, ice skating and the city lit up. If you prefer milder weather, May, June and September are pleasant. Avoid July and August if you dislike crowds. Whenever you go, book in advance for the best rate.

What to do when you arrive

Brussels Airport is about 25 km from Bruges, with direct bus connections. Once in Bruges: explore the Grote Markt (main square), climb the Belfry, boat rides on the canals, sample Belgian triple beers in medieval taverns and visit Hospice Saint-Jean. The city is small and walkable or easy by bike.

What to see in Bruges in 3 days?

The Grote Markt, the Belfry (366 steps to climb), boat rides through canals, medieval churches like Our Lady, local taverns and Flemish art museums. The city is compact and pedestrian-friendly.
