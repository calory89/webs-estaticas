Best time to visit

Hamburg is attractive all year round, but autumn and spring offer pleasant weather. Winters are cold but festive. Summer is warm with more tourism. Travel outside school holidays if you want better prices on transport and accommodation.

What to do in Hamburg

Walk through Speicherstadt (historic warehouses), the port, and the modern HafenCity district. Visit the Kunsthalle for classical art or Miniatur Wunderland, the world's largest miniature railway. Try Fischbrötchen (fish sandwich) at local markets and relax by the canals.
