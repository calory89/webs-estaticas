Best time to visit

Mauritius has a tropical climate and warm weather year-round. The dry season runs May to November, ideal for outdoor activities and beaches. December to March is more humid and cyclone season in the Indian Ocean, though still visitable. Choose based on your heat and rainfall tolerance.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport (MRU). Mauritius offers white-sand beaches, coral reefs, hiking in the Moka range, Waterfall gardens and Port Louis. It's perfect for relaxation, snorkelling or diving. Hire a car to explore the island freely.
