Best time to visit

Florida works year-round, though winter (December to February) is peak season with higher prices and perfect weather (20–25°C). Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) offer good conditions and fewer crowds. Summer (June to August) is hot and rainy, but sometimes with deals. Plan according to your climate and crowd preferences.

What to do in Orlando

Orlando is the world's theme park capital: Walt Disney World, Universal Studios and SeaWorld host the most-visited attractions. Beyond the parks, enjoy the historic downtown, lakes for water activities and shopping on International Drive. Orlando airport (MCO) is 24 km from the city centre; rent a car or use public transport to get around.
