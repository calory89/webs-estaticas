Best time to visit

Bogotá is a year-round destination thanks to its highland climate. December to March and July to August are peak season. For fewer crowds and compressed pricing, consider shoulder months (April-May, September-October). More flight availability often means more price options.

What to do when you arrive

Bogotá offers world-class museums like the Gold Museum, characterful neighbourhoods such as La Candelaria, and quality dining. Eldorado international airport is 15 km from the centre. Public transport, taxis, and ride apps connect you to the city. The altitude is significant (2,600 m), so take it easy your first day.

What to see in Bogotá in a week?
