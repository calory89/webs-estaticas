Best time to visit Hamburg

Hamburg shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): pleasant weather, less rain, and prices kinder than summer. Skip July–August if you want to dodge crowds and high fares. Winter is cold but magical during the festive season (November–December).

What to do when you arrive in Hamburg

From Hamburg Airport (HAM, Fuhlsbüttel), the city centre is 25 minutes by metro. Don't miss the Port (Hafen), St Michael's Church, the canals and historic Speicherstadt (UNESCO World Heritage), and the modern Elbtowers. Try fresh herring at the harbour market and stroll the Landungsbrücken.
