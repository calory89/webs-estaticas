Best time to visit

Maldives has a tropical climate with two seasons: dry (November to April) and wet (May to October). December to March are best for beach trips, with clear skies and calm waters. If you're seeking low prices and good weather, aim for January, February or early March. Avoid peak Christmas holidays if you want fewer crowds and potentially better fares.

What to do when you arrive

Maldives is a dream beach destination: snorkelling, diving and luxury island resorts. Malé International Airport (MLE) connects to the islands by speedboat and seaplane. The crystal-clear waters are perfect for exploring coral reefs and spotting manta rays. If you're travelling on a tighter budget, consider staying on Malé island or in guesthouses on local islands, far cheaper than luxury resorts.
