Best time to visit

Mauritius is a beach destination year-round, but the dry months (May to November) offer the best weather. June to September has lower rainfall and less peak tourism. To save money, avoid December–January (peak demand). Prices tend to stabilise in shoulder season (April–May and September–October).

What to do when you arrive in Mauritius

You'll land at Mauritius International Airport. From there, explore white-sand beaches, coral reefs, hiking in Black River Gorges, and cities like Port Louis. Take your time: relax on Grand Baie or Belle Mare beaches, soak in local culture. Hire a car or use taxis to get around at ease.
