Best time to visit Sri Lanka

November and December are ideal: dry season for the south and southwest coast. January to March are also excellent, with warm temperatures and low rainfall. Avoid May–June and October–November if you prefer less rain. The island is humid year-round, but these periods are most comfortable for beaches and temples.

What to do when you arrive in Sri Lanka

Colombo is your entry point. From Bandaranaike Airport (CMB) it's 30–40 minutes to the city centre. Don't miss the Cultural Triangle (Dambulla, Sigiriya, Kandy), southern beaches (Mirissa, Unawatuna), the train to Nuwara Eliya highlands, and Buddhist temples. Hire a car or use affordable local transport.

Does Pegasus fly direct from Seville to Sri Lanka?

What must I see in Sri Lanka?

The Cultural Triangle is essential: Sigiriya (rock fortress), Dambulla (cave temples), and Kandy (sacred lake and Temple of the Tooth). Add southern beaches, the train ride to the highlands, and Buddhist temples. One week covers basics; two weeks is ideal.

Is Sri Lanka expensive for European tourists?

Sri Lanka is very affordable. Accommodation, street food, and transport cost roughly a third of Spain. A 3–4-star hotel runs €30–50 per night. A good meal costs €5–15. It's an excellent destination if you want to travel on a tight budget.
