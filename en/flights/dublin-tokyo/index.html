Best time to visit

Tokyo is wonderful in spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November), when the weather is most pleasant. These periods tend to have higher demand, so prices may be steeper. If your budget is tight, consider travelling in low season, though Japan always has something special to offer.

What to do when you arrive

You land in Tokyo, a city blending ultra-modern with ancient shrines. Don't miss Shibuya, the Imperial Palace, Asakusa temples or museums. From Tokyo it's easy to reach other Japanese cities like Kyoto or Osaka. The main airports (Narita or Haneda) have excellent transport links to the city centre.
