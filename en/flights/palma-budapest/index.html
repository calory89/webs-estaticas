Best time to visit

Budapest shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), with mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is cold but the city glows with festive lights through January. Skip July and August if you dislike peak tourism. The data snapshot is from October 2026, so you have plenty of time to plan.

What to do in Budapest

Hungary's capital is a gem: the Parliament overlooking the Danube, Széchenyi Thermal Baths for relaxation, the Jewish Quarter's nightlife, Buda Castle, and river cruises. Food is outstanding, try gulasch, palacsinta and freshwater lobster. Public transport is efficient and costs for eating and staying are low compared to Western Europe.
