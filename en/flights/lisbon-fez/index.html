Best time to visit

Fez is pleasant from October to May with mild weather. Skip July and August due to intense heat. Prices tend to be better outside Easter and school holidays. December, the example month, is mid-season: good weather and fewer crowds than summer.

What to do when you arrive

Fez is Morocco's cultural capital. Explore the medina (old city) and its traditional souks, visit Al Quaraouiyine University and Karaouiyine Mosque. Don't miss the leather tanneries and local cuisine. Fes-Saïss Airport is about 15 km from the centre, with taxis and shuttles available.
