Best time to visit Singapore

Singapore is a tropical destination with warm weather year-round. The best period is November to February, with less rainfall and more comfortable temperatures. Avoid the wetter months (May to October). Peak season prices are highest in December–January, so November is a smart alternative.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Changi International Airport, one of the world's finest. In the city, visit Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay, Chinatown, Hindu temples and local markets. Singapore is highly accessible by public transport and walking. Eat at hawker centres for authentic local food at great value.
