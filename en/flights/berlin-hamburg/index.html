The lowest price is €145 return. Sample prices we've tracked are €145, €151 and €215. Book as early as possible: this route gets expensive during peak season. Check fares regularly, especially midweek, to grab the best deals.

Airline and stops

Wizz Air Malta operates this route. You'll travel with one stop, meaning one intermediate airport before Hamburg. Whilst not direct, the prices make up for the travel time. Departure is around 18:55 and return around 09:05, so you have flexibility with your schedules.

Best time to visit

Hamburg is attractive year-round. May to September offer better weather and more sunshine to explore the city and surrounding areas. January and February are quieter months if you want fewer crowds. Avoid Christmas dates if you're looking to escape the highest prices.

What to do when you arrive

Hamburg is Germany's second-largest city with plenty to offer: stroll the Speicherstadt canals, climb St. Michael's Church tower for panoramic views, explore Altona's markets or enjoy the nightlife. Hamburg Airport (HAM) has good bus and train connections to the city centre.
