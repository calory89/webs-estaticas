Best time to visit

Hamburg is an attractive destination in autumn and winter, especially at Christmas when the port and city fill with Christmas markets. Spring is also pleasant. Avoid summer if you're after lower prices: it's peak season. October, November and January typically have lower fares than July and August.

What to do on arrival

Hamburg is the gateway to northern Germany. Explore the port (Hafen), visit St Michael's Church, walk through canals and districts like Altstadt and HafenCity. Don't miss Miniatur Wunderland, the world's largest model railway. Hamburg Airport is 9 km from the city centre; bus or taxi gets you there quickly. The city is compact and easy to navigate.
