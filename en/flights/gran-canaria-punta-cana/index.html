Best time to visit

November is ideal: less rainfall than summer, pleasant temperatures (28–30°C), and excellent beach conditions. December and January see higher prices due to Christmas and New Year demand. Hurricane season ends in November, making it a safe time to travel to the Caribbean.

What to do in Punta Cana

Punta Cana is known for white-sand beaches and turquoise waters. Enjoy your resort, take excursions to Isla Saona, snorkel at coral reefs, or tour Los Haitises National Park. Punta Cana International Airport is just a few kilometres from the main tourist zone.

FAQ

The lowest recorded price is €683 return. Most searches show prices around €860. Booking 2–3 months in advance increases your chances of finding lower fares.

Does Iberia Express fly direct from Gran Canaria to Punta Cana?

No, this route includes one stop. Iberia Express connects via an intermediate airport, but total travel time remains competitive for the distance.

When should I book a flight to Punta Cana?

Reserve 6–8 weeks ahead for better price options. Avoid last-minute bookings when fares spike. November and early December offer good value and pleasant weather.

What is there to do in Punta Cana besides the beach?

Visit Isla Saona (full-day tours with snorkelling), Los Haitises National Park, cenotes, theme parks, and go diving on coral reefs. Explore local towns like Higüey or relax at your all-inclusive resort.
