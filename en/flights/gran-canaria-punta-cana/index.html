Best time to go

Punta Cana enjoys good weather year-round, but the dry season (November to April) is ideal for avoiding heavy rain. December and January are busiest and most expensive. For quieter beaches and lower fares, consider May to October, though it's hurricane season in the Caribbean (though Punta Cana is geographically protected).

What to do when you arrive

Punta Cana is synonymous with virgin beaches and beachfront resorts. Beyond the coast, visit the Laguna de Ojos Indigenas, fresh-water cenotes of great beauty, or Isla Saona by boat. Nearby villages like Bayahibe offer authenticity. If you dive, the coral reefs are spectacular. The area is very touristy, so everything is geared towards travellers.
