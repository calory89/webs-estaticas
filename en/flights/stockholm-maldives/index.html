Cheap flights Stockholm to Maldives: from €750

Flights from Stockholm to Maldives start from €750 return, with a median of €769. This is a multi-stop route, perfect for travellers prioritising price over direct convenience. You travel 12 days (example: 4th–16th December).

Prices and when to book

Prices range from €750 to €802 in the analysed sample. Book as far in advance as you can: earlier bookings secure better fares. This route is price-sensitive to availability and demand, so don't leave it until the last minute.

Airline and stopovers

Pegasus operates this route with 3 stopovers on the outbound journey. It's not a direct flight, but the price makes up for the extra connections. Expect intermediate stops and connection times between flights.

Best time to visit

Maldives is tropical with a dry season from November to April. This period offers stable weather, less rain, and warm waters ideal for diving and resorts. December (as in the example) is excellent but busier and pricier. Consider January to March for better weather-to-price balance.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Velana International Airport, Maldives' main gateway. From there, speedboats or domestic flights connect you to resorts or local islands. Dive the atolls, relax on white-sand beaches, or explore marine life. Most resorts arrange airport transfers.

FAQ

Is the Stockholm to Maldives flight direct?

No, it has 3 stopovers. Pegasus operates this route with intermediate stops, which keeps the price more competitive than fewer-stop alternatives.

What's the cheapest price for this route?

The lowest price is €750 return. Sample prices range from €750 to €802, so booking early makes the real difference.

Which airline flies Stockholm to Maldives?

Pegasus is the main carrier on this route. It offers competitive pricing, though with a 3-stopover itinerary.

When is the best time to visit Maldives?

November to April is the dry season, ideal for weather. December has great conditions but more tourists. January–March can be cheaper. Avoid May–October due to rain and cyclones.
