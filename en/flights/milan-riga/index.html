Best time to visit

Riga shines in summer (June to August) and December with its Christmas market. Autumn draws visitors for the colours. Travel off-season for quieter streets, though expect colder weather. Whenever you go, book ahead: cheap flights on this route sell out fast.

What to do in Riga

Latvia's capital won't disappoint. The Old Town (UNESCO World Heritage) blends medieval and Art Nouveau architecture. Walk along Daugava, the river. Eat street market food and local fare. Visit St. Peter's Church or Riga Cathedral. Bar and pub nights in the old quarter. Close by: Jūrmala beaches, 30 minutes by train.
