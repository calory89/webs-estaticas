Fly from Brussels to Miami from €610 return. The median price sits at €644, giving you a solid benchmark for your booking. SAS operates this route with one stopover, connecting two of the Atlantic's most vibrant cities.

Price and when to book

The cheapest flights start from €610 return. The median price is €644, and we've seen fares up to €662. Check regularly from now on: the further ahead you book, the better control you'll have over prices. There's no fixed rule about which month is cheaper, but booking well in advance usually pays off.

Airline and stopovers

SAS operates this route. You'll have one stopover on the outbound journey, so it's not a direct flight. You'll touch down at an intermediate airport before reaching Miami. Although it adds travel time, a stopover lets you stretch your legs and often helps keep prices competitive.

Best time to visit

Miami is attractive year-round, but bear in mind: June to September is hurricane season. October to April offers milder temperatures and lower storm risk. Travel when it suits you best; the price doesn't rise or fall by month—it depends more on how far ahead you book and how flexible your dates are.

What to do when you arrive

Miami awaits with beaches, art déco design in South Beach, and relentless nightlife. The Wynwood Walls are essential for street art lovers. Enjoy Cuban cuisine, visit the Vizcaya Museum, or wander through Little Havana. The airport is well connected, so getting to the city centre is straightforward.
