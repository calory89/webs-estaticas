Best time to visit Marrakech

Marrakech is pleasant almost year-round, but October and November are ideal: mild temperatures, fewer crowds than December-February, and without summer's oppressive heat. If you travel during these months you'll find better flight availability and more stable pricing than peak season.

What to do when you arrive

Marrakech awaits with its medina, the vibrant Jemaa el-Fnaa square, the Menara gardens and historic palaces. Marrakech Menara airport is 6 km from the city centre, well served by taxi and bus. Spend at least 3–4 days exploring the city at ease and the nearby Atlas Mountains.
