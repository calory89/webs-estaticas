Best time to visit

November is a solid choice: the scorching summer heat has faded and the weather is pleasant. Sicily in autumn is perfect for walking the city, visiting beaches without crowds and enjoying cheaper rates than peak season. Mid-November onwards guarantees fewer tourists. Avoid July and August if you want cheap fares; high season pushes prices up. Mid-October through late November typically offers good rates.

What to do when you arrive in Palermo

Palermo is Sicily's capital and worth every euro of your ticket. The Gothic Cathedral, the Politeama Theatre and the street markets (Ballarò, Vucciria) are essential. Eat arancini, panelle and pasta con le sardine at any corner. Palazzo dei Normanni, the Palatine Chapel and Mondello beach are classics. With extra days, day-trip to Mondello or villages like Cefalú. The airport is 30 km from the city centre; take a coach or shared taxi to keep costs down.
