Best time to visit

Malta enjoys warm weather year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant temperatures without summer heat. Outside July and August, you'll encounter fewer crowds and potentially better accommodation deals. Winter is mild and perfect if you're escaping Berlin's cold.

What to do in Malta

Malta is compact yet history-rich. Explore the Three Cities (Vittoriosa, Senglea, Cospicua) with baroque architecture, Popeye Cove or Golden Bay beaches, and the Hal Saflieni Hypogeum, a subterranean Neolithic temple. Valletta, the capital, is a UNESCO site walkable on foot. Diving, local food, and ancient churches round out the experience.
