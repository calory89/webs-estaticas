Best time to travel

Fort Lauderdale shines in winter (December to February) with pleasant temperatures and minimal rainfall. It's peak tourist season. If you prefer fewer crowds and don't mind more heat and humidity, September to November is quieter. Avoid June to August due to hurricane risk.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Fort Lauderdale International Airport (FLL), roughly 30 km from the centre. The area is famous for its beaches, boat tours along mansion-lined canals, marine life at coral reefs, and tropical weather. From here it's easy to reach Miami, the Florida Keys, or nearby theme parks.
