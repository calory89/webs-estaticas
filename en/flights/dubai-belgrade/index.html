Best time to travel

Belgrade is lovely in spring and autumn when the weather is mild. Winter (December–February) can be cold, but the city has fewer tourists. Skip summer if you want lower prices and less crowding. Check prices regularly: rates fluctuate based on availability.

What to do when you arrive

Belgrade awaits with Kalemegdan Fortress, Orthodox churches, and the vibrant nightlife along the Danube riverfront. Nikola Tesla Airport is 12 km from the centre; take a taxi, bus, or private transfer. The city is affordable, the food is outstanding, and the locals are welcoming.

What else is there to see in Belgrade?

Kalemegdan Fortress is a must. Walk Knez Mihailova Street, eat in local kafana (taverns), and explore the nightlife on river boats. The Museum of History and the artistic Beton Hala district are also worth your time.
