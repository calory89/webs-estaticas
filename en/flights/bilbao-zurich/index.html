Best time to visit

Zurich works year-round, but winter is harsh (snow, cold). Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) deliver pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Summer is peak tourist season. Booking with plenty of notice gives you flexibility to choose dates based on your climate preference.

What to do when you arrive

Zurich is compact and easily walkable or served by public transport. Old Town (Altstadt), luxury shopping on Bahnhofstrasse, world-class museums and top-tier food. Nearby lakes (Zurich, Linth) are ideal for a break. The main train station is a hub for the rest of Switzerland if you want to expand your trip.
