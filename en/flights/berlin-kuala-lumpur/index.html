Price and when to book

Prices range from €642 to €738 return on our sample. Booking 8-12 weeks ahead typically secures better rates for international long-haul routes. The earlier you book, the more flight times and price options you'll have.

Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Pegasus. The journey includes one stop, so it's not non-stop. You'll have an intermediate connection en route to Kuala Lumpur. Check layover times when booking to make sure they suit your plans.

Best time to visit

Kuala Lumpur is tropical: rain almost year-round, heavier from May to September. Driest months are December to March. Fewer tourists between June and August. Consider these windows to avoid crowds, though airfare depends more on when you book than the season.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Kuala Lumpur International Airport, well-connected to the city. Visit the Petronas Towers, Chinatown, Hindu and Buddhist temples, night markets. Ipoh, the Perhentian Islands, and the Batu Caves are easy day trips by local transport. Street food is excellent and cheap.

FAQ

How much is a Berlin to Kuala Lumpur flight?

From €642 return. We've seen prices up to €738 in our sample. Median is €670. Book as early as possible for the best fare.

Does Pegasus fly direct to Kuala Lumpur?

No, there's one stop on the route. It's not a non-stop flight, so you'll have one layover.

Which airport serves Kuala Lumpur?

Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KUL) is the main hub. It's about 70 km from the city centre, with train, bus, and taxi links.

What should I see in and around Kuala Lumpur?

The Petronas Towers, Chinatown, temples, night markets. Nearby: Batu Caves, Perhentian Islands for beaches, and Taman Negara national park. All accessible by affordable local transport.
