Best time to visit

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) are ideal: mild weather and fewer crowds. Winter (December–February) has competitive prices if you don't mind the cold. Avoid July–August if you want peace and quiet.

What to do in Budapest

The Danube splits the city into two halves: Buda (with the Royal Palace and Fishermen's Bastion) and Pest (museums and urban life). The Széchenyi Thermal Bath is famous for soaking in hot springs. Walk past Parliament, visit the synagogues, and eat at local markets.
