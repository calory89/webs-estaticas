Air Arabia: direct, no stopovers

Best time to visit the Maldives

The Maldives enjoy clear skies from November through April, outside the monsoon season. These months bring calm seas, excellent visibility for diving, and bright sunshine. If your flight is mid-November (like the example), you're landing in the dry season. Travel in shoulder months for lower fares, though weather is less stable.

What to do on arrival in the Maldives

Malé Airport (MLE) is your gateway. Fast ferries and speedboats connect to resorts from there. The Maldives mean diving, snorkelling, white-sand beaches, and turquoise waters. Relax at your resort, explore reefs, taste local food, or take boat tours to see dolphins and turtles.

What can you do in the Maldives?

Diving and snorkelling on coral reefs, white-sand beaches, boat tours, spotting marine life (dolphins, turtles, sharks), spa treatments at resorts, and local dining. Perfect for both relaxation and water adventures.
