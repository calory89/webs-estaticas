Best time to visit

Cyprus is a year-round sunshine destination. April to June and September to October offer ideal temperatures without extreme heat. July and August are scorching but also pricier. Avoid peak tourist seasons like Easter and August if you want better rates. Autumn is excellent: great weather and lower fares.

What to do when you arrive at Larnaca

Larnaca International Airport (LCA) is the island's main gateway. From there, buses, taxis and car hire take you to the beaches of Ayia Napa and Protaras, the coastal towns of Paphos, or the Troodos mountains. Historic Nicosia city centre and monasteries scattered across the island are must-sees. Three or four days give you time for beach, culture and nature.
