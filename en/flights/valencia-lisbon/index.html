Best time to visit

Lisbon is attractive year-round, but spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) offer perfect weather without peak summer crowds. Winter is cheaper if you don't mind mild temperatures (8–14°C). Check availability for your preferred dates; sometimes there are pleasant surprises outside peak season.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Humberto Delgado Lisbon Airport. From there, the Aerobus reaches the city centre in 20 minutes. Lisbon won't let you down: Belém with its monuments, the Tagus for strolls, azulejo tiles everywhere, neighbourhoods like Alfama and Príncipe Real, and custard tarts around every corner. At €47 for your flight, you've already got half the trip sorted.
