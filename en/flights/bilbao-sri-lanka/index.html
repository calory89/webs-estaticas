Best time to visit

Sri Lanka has a tropical climate year-round, but the dry season in the southwest runs from December to March. February is an excellent choice: pleasant temperatures, less rainfall and pristine beaches. It's shoulder season, so you'll find fewer crowds than during Christmas. Avoid the southwest monsoon between May and September if you prefer more stable conditions.

What to do when you arrive

Colombo, Sri Lanka's capital, is your entry point. Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) is about 35 km north. From there you can explore Colombo's old town, nearby Negombo beaches, or head to Kandy, Sigiriya or the southern coasts. Sri Lanka is compact: everything is a few hours away by bus or train. Local cuisine, Buddhist temples and mountain scenery await you.

FAQ

What's the lowest price for Bilbao to Sri Lanka?

The lowest recorded price is €642 return. This is exceptional and very date-dependent: February usually offers good opportunities. Check multiple dates before deciding.

Does Pegasus fly direct from Bilbao to Colombo?

No. This route includes 2 stopovers. It's standard for long-haul connections to Sri Lanka from northern Spain. Stopovers extend the journey but are necessary on these types of international flights.

When should I book to get the best price?

The earlier the better, especially if you're travelling in February. Prices rise as departure approaches. If you find something below €850, consider it good value.

What airport do I land at in Colombo?

Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) is Sri Lanka's main airport. It's well connected with taxis, buses and transfer services to central Colombo and other island destinations.
