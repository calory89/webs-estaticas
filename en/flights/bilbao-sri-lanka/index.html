Airline and stops

You'll fly with Pegasus. The flight includes 2 stops, so expect a longer journey: typical departure from Bilbao around 13:40, arrival in Colombo near 18:50 (local time, next day). Stops are functional, not sightseeing, but they give you a chance to stretch your legs.

Best time to visit Sri Lanka

November is a decent option: the dry season begins on the east and central coasts. For ideal conditions, December to March is best. Minimal rain, beaches at their peak. Bear in mind those are busier months and prices rise. Shoulder season (April, October) also works and is cheaper.

What to do when you arrive in Colombo

You'll land at Bandaranaike Airport (CMB), about 30 km from Colombo. The city has temples, coastal walks and museums. But Sri Lanka shines on its beaches (Mirissa, Unawatuna), hill country (Kandy, Nuwara Eliya) and whale watching. Hire a car or use local buses to get around. One week lets you blend beach and mountains without rush.
