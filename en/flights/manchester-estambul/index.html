Airline and direct flight

Best time to visit

Istanbul is worth visiting year-round, but April–May and September–October offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than July or August. Winter brings lower prices, though it's cooler. Choose based on your priorities: climate or budget.

What to do when you arrive

Istanbul awaits with the Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia and the Grand Bazaar. A Bosphorus sunset cruise is a must. Don't miss the colourful streets of Balat or the Galata neighbourhood. Istanbul Airport (IST) is well connected to the city centre by metro and bus, roughly 40 minutes depending on traffic.
