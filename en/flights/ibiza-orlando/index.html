Best time to visit

Orlando is worth visiting year-round, but November to February offers pleasant weather and lower humidity. Avoid July-August (intense heat and hurricane season). Christmas (December) is peak season at theme parks: book well ahead. May-June is also ideal with fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Orlando is famous for theme parks: Disney World, Universal Studios, and SeaWorld. Beyond parks, explore downtown, quality restaurants, natural springs in the area, and nightlife. The international airport (MCO) is about 25 km from the centre. Rent a car if you want freedom to move around.
