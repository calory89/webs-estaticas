Airline and stopovers

Best time to visit

Mexico City is a year-round destination, but September and October see more rain. December to February is peak season and prices climb. May to July and November typically offer better value. Avoid local holidays if you want cheaper fares.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Benito Juárez International Airport, about 10 km from the city centre. Mexico City boasts world-class museums (Frida Kahlo, Anthropology), incredible food, historic markets like La Merced, and colonial architecture downtown. Spend at least 3–4 days to make the most of such a long journey.

FAQ

Are there direct flights Sydney to Mexico City?

No, Fiji Airways includes 2 stopovers. Direct flights don't exist on this route, and stopovers are standard for such a long distance.

Why are Sydney to Mexico flights so expensive?

It's one of the longest routes in the world (16,000+ km). Stopovers, fuel costs and low flight frequency make it inherently pricey. At €1,200+ you're already getting a bargain.

Is Fiji Airways reliable for this route?

Yes, Fiji Airways operates regularly on long-haul routes. It's Fiji's national carrier and connects Oceania with Latin America.

How many days should I spend in Mexico City?

With a return journey that eats up nearly a full day each way, plan at least 4–5 complete days in the city. That way the long flight is worth it.
