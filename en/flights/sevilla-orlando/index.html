Best time to travel

What to do in Orlando

Orlando is the theme park capital. You'll find Magic Kingdom, EPCOT, and Hollywood Studios in Walt Disney World, plus Universal Studios and Islands of Adventure. For nature lovers, crystal-clear springs and mangrove areas are nearby. Downtown Orlando has museums, shops, and restaurants if you want something quieter.
