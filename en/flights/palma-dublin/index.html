Best time to visit

Dublin is lovely year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer the most pleasant weather and less rain. If you're after low fares, November to February tends to be cheaper, though it's colder. Skip major holidays if you prefer fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Dublin Airport. The city centre is compact and walkable: don't miss Guinness Storehouse, Temple Bar, the River Liffey and Grafton Street shops. If you have extra days, Cliffs of Moher and Wicklow Coast are classic day trips. Use buses, tram or your feet—Dublin rewards exploration.
