Best time to visit

Prague works year-round. Winter brings Christmas markets and fewer crowds; spring and autumn offer pleasant weather and lovely scenery. Summer is peak season. Pick based on your preference and budget: outside July and August you'll typically find better prices on flights.

What to do in Prague

Czech capital packs history into every corner: Prague Castle, Charles Bridge, Old Town Square with its astronomical clock. Wander through neighbourhoods like Malá Strana, try local beer in any pub, visit the Spanish Synagogue or simply lose yourself in medieval alleys. Prague is made for wandering on foot.
