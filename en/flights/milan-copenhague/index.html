Airline and stops

Best time to visit

December is winter in Copenhagen: cold, but charming if you're drawn to Nordic Christmas. Christmas markets and festive decorations add a special touch. If you prefer milder weather, consider spring (April-May) or autumn (September-October). Prices typically shift with holidays and tourism season.

What to do when you arrive

Copenhagen awaits you with Nyhavn (historic harbour), Amalienborg Palace, Tivoli, and an atmosphere of bicycles and design. The city is highly walkable with excellent public transport. Don't miss local cafés and the food scene. Direct trains run from the airport to the city centre in under 15 minutes.

What's worth seeing in Copenhagen over 4 days?

Nyhavn, Amalienborg Palace, Vor Frelser Church (Round Tower), Tivoli, modern art museums, and neighbourhoods like Nørrebro and Vesterbro. The city is very accessible by bike or on foot. Eat at food markets and try Nordic cuisine.
