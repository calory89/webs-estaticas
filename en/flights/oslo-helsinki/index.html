Best time to visit

Helsinki shines in winter if you're after northern lights, though the weather is harsh. Spring (May–June) and autumn (September–October) offer milder temperatures and long days. Summer brings nearly endless daylight and peak tourism. Prices on this route stay fairly stable year-round, so choose based on your preferred climate.

What to do in Helsinki

The white cathedral dominates Senate Square, Kiasma Museum appeals to modern art fans, and Suomenlinna fortress island is unmissable. Stroll the harbour, sample local food at markets, and if you're there in winter, book a northern lights tour from the outskirts. The airport is 20 minutes from the city centre by bus or train.
