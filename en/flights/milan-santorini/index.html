Best time to visit

Santorini shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): pleasant weather, fewer crowds than July–August, and often better prices. If you want summer, book as early as possible. Winter brings peace and quiet, though some tourist spots close.

What to do when you arrive

Santorini airport (JTR) connects to the centre by ferry or local bus. Once on the island, Oia and Fira are must-see whitewashed villages for stunning sunsets. The volcanic sand beaches, cliff edges and local tavernas make every hour count. Three to four days are enough to enjoy without rushing.

FAQ

Is the flight from Milan to Santorini direct?

No, easyJet operates this route with 1 stop. Check the connection details when you book so you can plan your time properly.

When is it cheaper to fly to Santorini from Milan?

Booking as early as possible is key. We can't say one month is always cheaper than another, but travelling outside summer months often gives you more competitive fares.

What should I see in Santorini in three days?

Oia and Fira are the most famous whitewashed villages. Don't miss sunsets on the cliffs, the volcanic sand beaches and local wineries. The ferry to the island of Nea Kameni is also popular for those keen to explore further.
