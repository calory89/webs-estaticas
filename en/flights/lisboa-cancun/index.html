Price and when to book

Return tickets from Lisbon to Cancún start at €704. Our sample fares show €704, €769 and €830, giving you a sense of the range. Book 6–8 weeks ahead if you can; prices often jump in the final two weeks before departure. Travel in May–June or September–October to find lower fares than winter months.

Airline and stopovers

Lufthansa operates this route with 1 stop. There are no direct flights from Lisbon to Cancún, so plan for at least one connection (likely at a Lufthansa European hub). It extends your journey, but the pricing makes up for it compared to more direct alternatives.

Best time to visit

High season in Cancún runs November to March, with perfect weather and peak prices. To save money, consider May–June (early hurricane season but fewer tourists) or September–October (climate transition, cheaper flights). Avoid Christmas, New Year and Easter holidays if price is your priority.

What to do when you arrive in Cancún

Cancún is a gateway to stunning beaches, water parks and Mayan ruins. From the airport (25 km from downtown) you can easily reach Playa del Carmen, Tulum and the Riviera Maya. For nature lovers, Xcaret and Xplor are must-sees. If you dive or snorkel, the Mesoamerican Barrier Reef is unmatched in the Caribbean.
