What to do when you arrive in San Juan

San Juan is Puerto Rico's capital with plenty to explore: Old San Juan's colonial streets and historic forts, nearby beaches like Flamenco, El Yunque rainforest, and excellent local food. Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport (SJU) is about 10 km from the city centre. From there you can hire a car, take a taxi, or use ride-sharing services to get around the island.
