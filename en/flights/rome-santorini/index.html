Best time to visit

December is a great month for Santorini: fewer crowds than summer, mild weather (though cooler) and lower fares. If you want beach time and strong sun, aim for May to September, but expect higher prices. April and October are well-balanced months with good weather and fewer masses of tourists.

What to do on arrival

Santorini awaits with its white villages of Oia and Ia, black volcanic sand beaches, legendary sunsets and local wine. The airport (JTR) is well connected by bus and taxi to Fira and main villages. Don't miss the old town, blue-domed churches or a caldera cruise. Eat on terraces overlooking the Aegean Sea.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Rome to Santorini?

In this search, SKY express offers 1 stop on the outbound journey. Direct flights between Rome and Santorini are rare or non-existent in regular commercial offer; stops are standard on this route.

What's the cheapest price found?

€208 per person return. This is the lowest fare in the search, valid for travel from mid-December onwards.

How many days in advance should I book?

Ideally 4–6 weeks ahead, especially for Cyclades routes. The sooner, the better the availability and price options. Avoid last-minute purchases unless exceptional deals appear.

What else is there to see in Santorini besides beaches?

Oia and Ia with their white houses and blue domes, the volcano and caldera, local museums, wineries, Akrotiri archaeological remains and caldera cruises. The sunset from Fira's cliff is unmissable.
