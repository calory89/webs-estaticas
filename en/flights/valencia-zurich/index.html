Best time to travel

Zurich is attractive year-round. Autumn and winter bring spectacular Alpine scenery and fewer tourists. Spring offers green landscapes and mild weather. If you want to dodge crowds and grab lower fares, low season (November to March, excluding Christmas) is typically more budget-friendly. Summer is peak season and pricier.

What to do in Zurich

Zurich blends modernity with tradition. Explore the Altstadt (old town) with its medieval lanes, visit Zurich Cathedral and world-class museums like the Kunsthaus. Don't miss nearby lakes (Zurich, Lucerne) for day trips or the Alps within easy reach. Swiss gastronomy, watches and chocolate are must-tries.
