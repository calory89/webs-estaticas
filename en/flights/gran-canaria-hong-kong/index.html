Best time to visit

Hong Kong has a subtropical climate year-round. November to March offer milder temperatures and less rainfall, which most consider the best season. April to September are hot and humid, plus typhoon season. If you're looking to save and have no time constraints, less touristy periods (January–February after Chinese New Year, or June–August despite the weather) may see lower flight demand.

What to do when you arrive

Hong Kong is a city of contrasts: skyscrapers, traditional markets, temples and stunning harbours. Chek Lap Kok International Airport is well connected by public transport. You can visit Victoria Harbour, climb Victoria Peak, explore Central and Sheung Wan neighbourhoods, or take a ferry to nearby islands. Local food is an experience in itself, from street stalls to fine dining. Don't miss night markets like Temple Street or Ladies' Market.
