Best time to visit

Sardinia shines from May through October: clear waters, stunning beaches and sunny weather. Skip July and August (peak season, pricier) and you'll dodge crowds plus find better fares. For value and good weather, aim for June, September or October. November and April work too if you don't mind slightly cooler conditions.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Cagliari airport (CAG), your gateway to authentic Sardinia. Explore Poetto beach, the cliffs at Cala Luna, the old town of Alghero or Tharros ruins. The island is perfect for mixing beach time, hiking and local food. Hire a car if you want freedom to roam without constraints.
