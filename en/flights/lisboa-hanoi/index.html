Fly from Lisbon to Hanoi from €698 return. The median price sits around €742. With one stopover on the outbound journey, this route opens the door to one of Southeast Asia's most captivating destinations.

Prices range from €698 to €819 depending on dates. We've seen fares from €698 (outbound 3 Dec, return 13 Dec 2026). The median is €742. For this long-haul route, booking in advance is highly recommended; the earlier you reserve, the better the price. Monitor price changes in the weeks before your trip.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route with one stopover on the outbound leg. It's not a direct flight, but it's the most affordable option from Lisbon. The stopover extends your journey time, but gets you access to very competitive fares for an intercontinental flight.

Best time to visit

Vietnam is a year-round destination, though each region has its peak season. Northern Vietnam (including Hanoi) enjoys pleasant weather from October to April, with moderate temperatures and less rainfall. Flying during these months lets you avoid the summer monsoon and enjoy ideal conditions for exploring. December is particularly popular for its dry climate.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

Hanoi's Noi Bai International Airport is about 25 km from the city centre. From there, you can easily reach the Old Quarter, Hoan Kiem Lake, Thang Long Citadel and local temples. Many travellers use Hanoi as a base for trips to Ha Long Bay, northern rice fields or Sapa mountains. The city offers unique cuisine, vibrant streets and rich history.

FAQ

What is the cheapest price for flights from Lisbon to Vietnam?

The lowest fare we've recorded is €698 return with Ryanair. Prices vary by exact booking and travel date; the median is around €742.

Does Ryanair fly direct from Lisbon to Hanoi?

No, this route includes one stopover on the outbound leg. It's a connection of flights that keeps the price low for such a distant destination.

Which airport do I arrive at in Hanoi?

You arrive at Noi Bai International Airport (HAN), Hanoi's main airport. It's about 25 km from the city centre with connections by bus, taxi and train to the city.

When is the best time to visit Vietnam?

October to April is the best season for northern Vietnam (Hanoi). You'll have dry weather, pleasant temperatures and less rainfall. December is especially popular for its stable climate.
