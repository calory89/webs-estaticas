Fly Singapore to Madeira with Oman Air for just €713 return. The median price sits at €795, so you've got scope to find deals. Madeira awaits with volcanoes, laurel forests and stunning beaches.

Price and when to book

Tickets start from €713 return. The median price stands at €795 (samples at €713, €795 and €820). For long-haul flights like this, booking 2–3 months ahead is ideal to secure better fares. Watch for price shifts: tariffs can rise depending on availability.

Oman Air: layovers and route

You'll travel with Oman Air with 2 layovers on the outbound leg. Sample departure is 12 November 2026 at 20:50 and return on 21 November at 15:40. With two stops en route, expect a journey over 20 hours. Oman Air delivers solid value on long-haul routes to Europe.

Best time to visit

Madeira enjoys subtropical weather year-round, but April–May and September–October are ideal: pleasant temperatures (20–24°C) and less rain. Winter (November–March) is wetter but still warm. Book in advance during peak months, as prices climb.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Cristiano Ronaldo airport (FNC), 30 km from Funchal. Explore the Laurisilva (UNESCO heritage), hike volcanic mountains, walk Funchal's streets and duty-free shops, sample fresh fish on the coast, and take in views from Pico do Arieiro. Local buses (guaguas) are cheap and reliable.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Singapore to Madeira?

No. This route has 2 layovers with Oman Air. Flights across such distances between Asia and a Portuguese island require intermediate connections.

Why is Oman Air cheaper than other airlines?

Oman Air leverages strong connections in the Persian Gulf and offers competitive fares. It's not about lower quality, but business model and strategic routes. Check baggage allowance and services before booking.

How long will my layovers be?

It varies by the exact itinerary offered when you search. With 2 layovers, expect stops of 1.5 to 3 hours each. Times shift based on availability when you book.

What's the best view in Madeira if time is short?

Pico do Arieiro and the Laurisilva are must-sees. If you have just one day, combine a short hiking trail in the mountains with a stroll through Funchal's markets and streets.
