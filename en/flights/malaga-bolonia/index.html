Best time to travel

Bologna is pleasant year-round, but avoid July and August if you want fewer crowds and lower prices. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer good weather and less mass tourism. Book several months ahead for these popular periods.

What to do in Bologna

Bologna is a medieval Italian gem: explore its towers and arcades (UNESCO World Heritage), visit the Basilica of San Petronio, try fresh pasta at local markets, and discover Europe's oldest university. The centre is fully pedestrian and reachable from the airport by bus or train in under 30 minutes.
