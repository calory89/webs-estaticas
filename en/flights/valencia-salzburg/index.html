Fly from Valencia to Salzburg for just €175 return. The median price sits around €214, so if you spot fares in this range, it's worth booking. Ryanair operates this route with one stop, perfect if you want great value without compromising on reaching Austria.

Price and when to book

The cheapest tickets start at €175 per person return, with a median of €214. Sample fares we've seen are €175, €214 and €228. Don't wait until the last minute: cheap seats sell out fast. Book well in advance to secure the best price, especially during peak travel seasons.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route. The outbound flight includes 1 stopover, so it's not direct, but the price makes up for it. Check your connection time and which airport you'll stop at so you can plan accordingly.

Best time to visit

Salzburg shines in winter if you fancy the Christmas Market or skiing in the nearby Alps, and in summer for exploring lakes and hiking. Prices tend to be more competitive if you skip school holidays and long weekends. Check the calendar before you book.

What to do when you arrive

Salzburg is Mozart's birthplace and the Sound of Music city. Wander the Altstadt, climb Hohensalzburg Fortress, explore Lake Königssee or take a day trip to the Alps. Salzburg Airport is just 4 km from the city centre, very accessible.
