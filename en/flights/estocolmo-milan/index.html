Cheap flights from Stockholm to Milan from €38

Ryanair takes you from Stockholm to Milan non-stop from €38 return. The median price is €43, so you'll find affordable fares throughout the year. Pick your dates wisely and fly direct to Italy without hassle.

Price and when to book

Prices range from €38 to €86 return. Best booking strategy: reserve 6–8 weeks in advance. Ryanair fares rise sharply as departure approaches. Use flexible date search—shifting your travel by just one day can save you noticeably.

Airline and direct flights

Fly with Ryanair on a non-stop route, no plane changes. Departure from Stockholm (STO), arrival at Milan (MIL). Ryanair is a budget airline, so check baggage rules beforehand: carry-on is included, but checked luggage and extras are charged separately.

Best time to travel

Autumn and spring offer pleasant weather in Milan without summer crowding. Skip July and August if you want lower fares. Northern European winters can make Stockholm flights pricier, but December–January sometimes surprise with deals.

What to do in Milan

The Cathedral (Duomo) is essential, and Castello Sforzesco is worth a visit. Eat and explore Brera, a bohemian district with shops and restaurants. Easy train access to Como and Bergamo. Malpensa airport is 50 km away; trains reach the city centre in 45 minutes.

FAQ

Is the Stockholm to Milan flight really non-stop?

Yes, Ryanair operates this route without stopovers. Direct departure from Stockholm (STO), direct arrival at Milan (MIL).

When is the best time to book this flight?

Book 6–8 weeks ahead to get the best price. Ryanair fares climb closer to departure, so don't leave it to the last minute.

Why is there such a big price difference between €38 and €86?

Season, day of the week, and advance booking time all matter. Italian and Swedish bank holidays push prices up. Midweek flights (Wed–Thu) are usually cheaper than weekends.

What is included in the Ryanair ticket price?

Price covers the seat only. Bring a small carry-on bag, as checked luggage and other extras cost extra. Check Ryanair's website for current baggage policy.
