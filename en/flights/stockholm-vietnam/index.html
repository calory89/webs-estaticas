Air China connects Stockholm to Hanoi from €567 return. With one stop, this journey costs between €567 and €604 depending on dates. Fly in November for the best prices.

Price and when to book

The cheapest flight is around €567 return. Sample fares range from €567 to €604, a tight spread that signals good value on this route. Check now: the closer your departure date, the less room for price drops. If you travel late November, you'll catch deals at the lower end.

Airline and stops

Air China is your option at these prices. The journey includes 1 stop, so it's not direct. The sample departure is 10 November at 18:10 from Stockholm, arriving in Hanoi on 19 November at 04:30 local time. Despite the stop, the total travel time is reasonable for a long-haul connection.

Best time to visit

November is ideal: mild temperatures (20–25 °C), less rain than summer, and before the Christmas rush. It's peak season in Vietnam, lush landscapes and perfect weather for exploring. Skip September and October (monsoon) and December–January (crowded).

What to do when you arrive

Hanoi is your gateway. Walk the Old Quarter, eat at street stalls around Hoan Kiem Lake, visit Ho Chi Minh's Mausoleum. From there, head to Ha Long Bay (UNESCO), Sapa or Hoi An. Three weeks cover a classic route. Vietnam is cheap: accommodation, food and local transport cost little once you've paid for your flight.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Stockholm to Vietnam?

Not at these prices. Air China offers 1 stop, which is standard on Nordic-to-Asia routes. Direct flights, if available, would cost more.

Is Air China a safe and quality airline?

Yes. Air China is China's national carrier, internationally certified. It has a solid reputation on long-haul routes.

What was the most expensive flight you found?

€604 return. It's only €37 more than the cheapest, so the range is very tight.

Which airport is Hanoi (HAN)?

Noi Bai International Airport, 45 km north of the city centre. Take a bus (cheap) or taxi into town. Well connected to local transport.
