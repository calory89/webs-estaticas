What to do upon arrival in Seoul

You'll land at Incheon (ICN), about 50 km from the city centre. The express train gets you downtown in 45 minutes. Seoul boasts royal palaces, trendy districts like Gangnam, Buddhist temples and world-class food. The metro is efficient and affordable. December means Christmas lights in shops and traditional Korean festive markets to explore.

FAQ

Why do Rome to Seoul flights cost from €637?

It's the base fare recorded for return travel with Air China. The Europe–Asia sector requires a stopover, which affects the price. Flexible travel dates, early booking and low season within December help you hit this tariff.

Is Air China reliable on this route?

Air China is China's flag carrier and a Star Alliance member. It operates regularly on Europe–Asia routes. Always check baggage policy, change and cancellation terms before confirming. Passenger reviews are typically available on aviation forums.

Can I fly direct from Rome to Seoul?

No. This route requires 1 stopover with Air China. There are no regularly scheduled direct flights linking Rome and Incheon. A transit stop is mandatory on this journey.

Which airport should I use in Rome?

Rome's IATA code is ROM. It has two main terminals (Fiumicino and Ciampino). Confirm on your ticket which Air China uses; international flights typically depart from Fiumicino (FCO).
