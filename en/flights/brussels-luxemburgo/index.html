The Brussels-Luxembourg route costs from €134 return. Median prices sit around €138, so you'll find competitive fares year-round. With one connection on the outbound, this short journey is accessible and practical.

Price and when to book

Prices range between €134 and €164 for return tickets. The lowest fare is €134; if you spot €138 or €164, it's still good value for this distance. Book as far ahead as possible to catch the best deals. Cheapest seats sell out quickly on short routes between European capitals.

Airline and connections

Ryanair operates this route with one connection on the outbound flight. It's not a direct journey, but the price makes up for it if you have flexible timing. The stopover will add a few hours to your travel time, but that's standard at this price point.

Best time to travel

Brussels and Luxembourg are city destinations that work well year-round. October to April tends to have fewer tourists and steadier prices. Avoid European bank holidays and long weekends if you're chasing low fares. Check the holiday calendar before picking your dates.

What to do when you arrive

Luxembourg is small but packed with medieval history. Explore the old town (Unesco World Heritage), walk the Petrusse cliffs and visit the capital's museums. From Brussels, see the Grand Place, Atomium or Bruges' canals. Both cities are perfect for weekend getaways.

FAQ

What's the lowest price from Brussels to Luxembourg?

The lowest fare we've recorded is €134 for a return trip. Most common prices hover around €138. Book early to secure these rates.

Are there direct flights from Brussels to Luxembourg?

No, this route includes a connection. Ryanair operates it with one stop on the outbound, which makes the journey longer but the price very competitive.

Which airline flies Brussels to Luxembourg?

Ryanair is the main carrier on this route. You get low prices, but watch out for potential charges on extra baggage.

How many days do I need to see Luxembourg and Brussels?

A 3–4 day weekend is enough to explore both capitals. The historic centres are compact and distances short, so you'll make great use of your time.
