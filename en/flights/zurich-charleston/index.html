Best time to visit

Charleston shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather, no brutal summer heat, and no winter chill. These seasons are ideal for exploring the city comfortably. Avoid summer hurricane season if possible.

What to do when you arrive

Charleston airport (CHS) is 20 minutes from downtown. Walk historic King Street, explore the waterfront, visit the parks, and eat at bay-side restaurants. The city blends colonial architecture with modern life. If you have time, check out nearby beaches.
