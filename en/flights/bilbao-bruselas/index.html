Best time to visit

Brussels is appealing year-round, but October and November often offer good weather without crowds. Summer months (July-August) see more tourism and generally higher fares. Winter is Christmas markets season; adjust your dates based on what you'd like to see.

What to do when you arrive

Brussels airport (Zaventem) is 12 km from the city centre. Take the train (fast and cheap) or bus to get downtown. Visit the Grand Place, stroll the Marais neighbourhood, try Belgian beers and don't miss the Atomium. Brussels is compact and easy to explore on foot.
