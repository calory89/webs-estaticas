Best time to visit

Helsinki is spectacular in winter if you want to see the Northern Lights (November to February). If you prefer milder weather, go in summer (June to August), though prices usually rise. Autumn and early winter offer a good balance between price and experience.

What to do when you arrive in Helsinki

Finland's capital has plenty to offer: the White Cathedral, Nordic design, traditional saunas, and easy access to nature. Helsinki Airport (HEL) is 17 km from the city centre and well connected by train and bus. Don't miss local cuisine and design museums.
