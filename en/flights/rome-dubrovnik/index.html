Best time to visit

Dubrovnik is beautiful year-round, but April to October offers the best weather and most events. If you want to skip crowds and cut accommodation costs, May and September are ideal: pleasant temperatures without July and August's peak tourism. Winter is quiet and cheaper, though fewer daylight hours.

What to do when you arrive

Dubrovnik airport is about 20 km from the Old Town. Walk the city walls, visit the Cathedral, climb Lovrijenac Fortress, or relax on Banje and Lapad beaches. Fresh seafood and local wines are a must. Consider a day trip to the Elaphiti Islands or a visit to Cavtat nearby.
