Best time to travel to Sal Island

Sal Island enjoys good weather almost year-round thanks to the trade winds. The dry season (November to July) is best for avoiding rain, though the island is very arid. December, as in your example, is excellent: pleasant temperatures and little rainfall. Plan ahead if travelling in high season (December-January), when prices rise.

What to do on Sal Island

Sal Island is known for its white-sand beaches and turquoise waters, perfect for sun and beach tourism. Santa Maria is the main tourist town, with amenities and nightlife. The international airport is on Sal, Cabo Verde's entry point. Enjoy diving, kitesurfing and snorkelling. It's a laid-back destination ideal for getting away.
