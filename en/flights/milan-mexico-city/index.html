Price and when to book

The lowest price on record is €1,021 for the complete journey. Prices on this route range from €1,021 to €1,025, a very tight spread. The earlier you book, the more options and better prices you'll find. There's no clear pattern for specific days or months when prices drop, so keep an eye on offers as they come.

Airline and connections

Aer Lingus operates this route. The journey includes 2 stopovers on the outbound leg, which extends the total travel time. Although it's not a direct flight, it's a competitive option price-wise for such a long distance. Always check the stopover details when booking to plan your connections.

Best time to travel

Mexico City works well year-round thanks to its high-altitude climate. The dry season (November to April) is especially pleasant: less rain and comfortable temperatures. If you can, avoid European school holidays to escape crowds and potentially find better prices.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Benito Juárez International Airport (MEX), about 5 km from the city centre. Mexico City boasts world-class museums, top-tier food, colonial and modern architecture, traditional markets, and vibrant cultural life. The Zócalo, Frida Kahlo Museum, Teotihuacán and Xochimilco are must-sees.
