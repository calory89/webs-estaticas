Best time to visit Bogotá

Bogotá has mild weather year-round (altitude 2,640 m). Dry months are July–August and December–January, ideal if you prefer less rain. Try to avoid peak rainy periods (April–May and October–November) if possible. Bear in mind Bogotá is an urban destination, so weather affects your plans less than in other Colombian regions.

What to do in Bogotá

Colombia's capital offers world-class museums (Gold, Botero), the Monserrate quarry with panoramic views, historic districts like La Candelaria, and modern areas such as Usaquén. Enjoy local cuisine in city-centre restaurants and the nightlife scene. The city is safe in tourist areas if you take standard precautions.
