Fly from Amsterdam to Hanoi from €552 with China Eastern Airlines. The median price sits around €558. Book at least two months ahead to secure the best fares.

Price and when to book

Prices range between €552 and €653 depending on dates. The most common fare hovers around €558. The earlier you buy, the better: booking two months in advance usually offers the best conditions. Avoid last-minute bookings, as prices rise significantly.

Airline and stopovers

You'll travel with China Eastern Airlines, with 1 stopover on the outbound leg. This model is common on Europe-Asia routes, giving you access to competitive fares. Always check the connection point when booking to plan transit times.

Best time to travel

Vietnam is a year-round destination, but October to April offers drier weather and pleasant temperatures. These months tend to be pricier due to peak season. For lower prices, consider travelling May-September, though expect more rain. Always check the forecast before you go.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

You'll land at Noi Bai International Airport, about 25 km from the city centre. Explore the Old Quarter, Hoan Kiem Lake, Ngoc Son Temple and Ho Chi Minh's Mausoleum. From Hanoi, you can take trips to Ha Long Bay or visit Sa Pa. Street food is excellent and very affordable.

FAQ

What's the lowest price for flying from Amsterdam to Vietnam?

The lowest price is €552 for return flights. You'll find fares in this range if you book in advance and have flexible travel dates.

Which airline will I fly with?

You'll fly with China Eastern Airlines, a reliable option for connecting Europe and Asia with good value for money.

Is the flight direct or does it have stopovers?

It has 1 stopover on the outbound leg. This is standard for this route and is how you access more competitive fares from Amsterdam to Hanoi.

What's there to see in Vietnam after landing?

Hanoi has plenty to offer: historic temples, traditional markets and unique cuisine. If you have more time, Ha Long Bay is a must-see, and Sa Pa is perfect if you want nature and mountain scenery.
