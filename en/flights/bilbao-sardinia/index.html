Best time to visit

Sardinia is a year-round destination for beaches and culture. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant weather with fewer crowds than summer. July and August are peak season and most expensive. For cheaper prices, consider November to March, though expect cooler weather.

What to do when you arrive

Sardinia offers crystal-clear beaches, coastal towns like Porto Cervo, nuraghe archaeological sites, and unique local cuisine. Cagliari airport connects well to main tourist areas. Hire a car to explore the northern Costa Smeralda or inland regions where you'll find authentic culture and wild landscapes.

What can I see in Sardinia?

White sand beaches, the Costa Smeralda, traditional coastal villages, nuraghe archaeological remains, hidden coves, and excellent local food. Cagliari airport is your entry point.
