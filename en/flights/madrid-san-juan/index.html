Best time to travel

San Juan enjoys tropical weather year-round. The dry season (November to April) is best: less rain and pleasant temperatures. For smaller crowds and better value, spring (March–April) can be a smart pick. Try to avoid September and October if you can, as that's hurricane season in the Caribbean.

What to do when you arrive in San Juan

Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport (SJU) is about 10 km from the city centre. Explore Old San Juan with its cobbled streets, Fort El Morro, and colonial galleries. Beaches like Flamenco (in Culebra, a short hop away) are stunning. Don't miss El Yunque, the national tropical rainforest, or the bioluminescent Laguna Grande.
