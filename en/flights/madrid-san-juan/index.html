Best time to visit

San Juan is a year-round destination, but November to April is ideal: pleasant temperatures and lower hurricane risk. July and August are hot and humid. December fills with holiday crowds and higher fares. Consider shoulder months (May–June or September–October) to balance weather and cost.

What to do on arrival

Old San Juan is unmissable: colonial architecture, urban beaches and nightlife. El Yunque tropical rainforest, 45 min away, offers hiking and natural pools. Flamenco Beach (Culebra) and Vieques are iconic. Eat mofongo and alcapurrias at local spots. Luis Muñoz Marín Airport (SJU) is 15 km from centre; take a taxi, Uber or hire a car.
