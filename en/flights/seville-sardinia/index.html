Best time to visit

Sardinia offers beaches, nature, and hiking year-round. July–August is peak season, pricey and crowded; April–June and September–October strike a balance with decent weather and lower fares. Winter (November–March) has discounts, though sea temperatures drop.

Things to do in Sardinia

Sardinia is a beach and culture hub: explore the Costa Smeralda, visit Cagliari port, taste Sardinian food (malloreddus, culurgiones), and discover nuragic ruins. Cagliari–Elmas airport is 4 km from the capital. Hire a car for flexibility or use local buses.
