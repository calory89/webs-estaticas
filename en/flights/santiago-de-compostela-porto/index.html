Things to do in Porto

Once you land at Porto airport (OPO), head to Ribeira with its medieval lanes, Clérigos Tower, and Dom Luís Bridge. The Douro River is perfect for boat trips. Don't miss the Port wine cellars in Vila Nova de Gaia, across from the old town. Everything is well served by public transport from the airport.
