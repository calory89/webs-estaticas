Best time to visit

Milan is pleasant year-round. October–November and March–April are ideal: mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. If you fly in winter (December–February), expect cold temperatures. July–August is peak season and pricier. Book in advance regardless of when you travel.

What to do in Milan

The Cathedral is a must; climb to the roof for city views. Visit Castello Sforzesco, the Duomo, and Pinacoteca di Brera. Leonardo's Last Supper is at the Refectory of Santa Maria delle Grazie (book ahead). Explore the Navigli district for cafés and nightlife. The main airport is Malpensa; Linate is closer to the city centre.
