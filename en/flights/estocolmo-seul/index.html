Best time to visit

South Korea shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather and stunning landscapes. Winters can be harsh (freezing) and summers humid and wet. Pick your season based on what you want to see, and book with lead time to negotiate price.

What to do when you land in Seoul

You arrive at Incheon (ICN), 60 km from the city. Take the express train or direct bus to central Seoul (it's vast). Visit Gyeongbokgung Palace, Myeongdong for shopping and street food, Hangang Park at sunset, and markets like Dongdaemun. Korean cuisine is affordable and excellent on a budget.
