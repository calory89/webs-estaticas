Best time to visit

The Philippines has tropical weather. The dry season (November to May) is best: less rain, comfortable temperatures, and perfect visibility for beaches and outdoor activities. December and January are peak months with higher prices. February to April is a solid compromise.

What to do when you arrive

Manila is your gateway to the Philippines. The international airport is well-connected to the city. From here, reach Palawan's beaches, the Cordillera mountains, or islands like Siargao and Boracay. The city itself has museums, local restaurants, and nightlife. Plan ahead to make the most of your trip.
