Best time to visit

Helsinki shines in winter if you want to see the Northern Lights (December to February), though prices climb then. In summer (June to August) you'll enjoy the midnight sun and milder weather, but prices are higher too. Shoulder seasons (spring and autumn) offer a good balance between price and experience.

What to do when you arrive

Helsinki has it all: the white cathedral on Senate Square, the Sibelius Monument, the waterfront markets and the Suomenlinna fortress. Travel in winter and look for Northern Lights excursions. The city is compact with excellent public transport. Try fresh salmon at the local markets.
