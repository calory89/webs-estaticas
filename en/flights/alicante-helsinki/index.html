Best time to visit

Helsinki is spectacular in winter if you want to see the northern lights (December to February), though it gets extremely cold. In summer, June to August, you'll enjoy the midnight sun phenomenon and pleasant temperatures. Spring and autumn are milder but less touristy. Choose based on what experience you prefer.

What to do on arrival

Helsinki airport (HEL) is 20 km from the centre. Visit the White Cathedral, Uspenski Temppeliaukio Church, and explore the Kallio district. Don't miss the archipelago islands by ferry or traditional markets. If you travel in winter, many tours offer trips north to chase the northern lights; in summer, beaches and lakes are ideal.
