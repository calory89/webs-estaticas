Best time to visit

Nairobi and Kenya work year-round, but dry season (June to October and January to February) offers better weather and wildlife visibility. Rainy months (April-May and November) are cheaper but less predictable. Choose based on your priorities: wildlife and clear skies versus budget.

What to do when you arrive

Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO) is 18 km from the city centre. Explore Nairobi National Park, visit the National Museum of Kenya, stroll the Karen neighbourhood, or take a day trip to Masai Mara National Reserve. The city is the ideal base for safaris. Try local restaurants and Kenyan cuisine.
