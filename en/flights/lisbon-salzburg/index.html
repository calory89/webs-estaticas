Best time to visit

Austria shines in winter (snowy Salzburg is stunning) and summer. December brings unique Christmas markets. Spring and autumn are quieter and prices may be slightly lower. Avoid August if you want to escape crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Salzburg is small and beautiful: the Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage site. Don't miss Hohensalzburg Fortress (incredible views), the Cathedral, and Mozart's birthhouse. The airport is 4 km from the centre, easily accessible by bus or taxi. Perfect for 3–4 days.
