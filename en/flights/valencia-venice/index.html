Best time to travel

Venice is stunning year-round, but November-February is off-season: fewer crowds and slightly lower prices (like these). Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer mild weather. July-August is packed and pricier. If you're hunting deals like these €37-40€ fares, travel in low season or during short breaks.

What to do in Venice

You'll land at Marco Polo, 12 km from the city centre. The vaporetto (water bus) whisks you downtown cheaply. St. Mark's Square, the Basilica di Santa Maria della Salute, gondola rides, the Rialto Bridge: classics for a reason. Eat where locals eat, away from tourist traps. St. Mark's Square is pricey but unmissable at sunset.

What to see in Venice in 1-2 days?

St. Mark's, the Basilica of the Salute, the Rialto Bridge and a gondola ride are the stars. Wander the side streets without a map; that's where Venice lives. Eat at local trattorias, not plaza terraces. Wear comfy shoes, lots of walking. The vaporetto is your best friend.
