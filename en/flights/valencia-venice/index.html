Best time to visit

Venice is stunning year-round, but the quietest months with lower prices are October, November, February and March. July and January are peak season. If you travel in winter, prepare for the occasional "acqua alta" (flooding), though it's brief. Spring is ideal: mild weather and fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive in Venice

Venice airport (Marco Polo) sits in the lagoon. From there, take the vaporetto (water bus) or fast launch towards the historic centre. Once at St Mark's Square, walk to the Doge's Palace, St Mark's Basilica and iconic bridges like the Rialto. The islands of Murano and Burano are must-sees: one for blown glass, the other for colourful houses. Eat fresh pasta anywhere and take a gondola along the Grand Canal if your budget allows.
