Best time to visit

Luxembourg shines in spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October): mild weather and less rain. Winter is cold with short days; summer is busy but pleasant. No season guarantees lower airfares, early booking matters more than timing.

What to do when you arrive

Luxembourg Airport sits 6 km from the city with good public transport links. The capital deserves 2-3 days: explore the Ville Vieille (old town), national museums and the Corniche viewpoint. The Ardennes lie close if you want forests and castles. Accommodation and dining are pricey, but free museum days help offset costs.
