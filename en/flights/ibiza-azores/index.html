Prices start from €241 for return flights, with a median of €257. We've seen fares up to €265 depending on availability. The sooner you book from today, the better rate you'll lock in. Trips from January to March usually offer good value for money, so don't miss these prices if you find them.

Airline and stopovers

Vueling operates this route with 2 stopovers on the outbound leg. Although it's not direct, the prices make up for the overall journey time. Check your booking to find out the exact cities where you'll stop; connections are typically smooth with an established carrier like Vueling.

Best time to visit

The Azores enjoy mild weather year-round, but winter (December–February) and spring (March–May) bring less rain and clearer skies. January is a good month to find deals after the Christmas rush. If you're chasing lower fares, avoid July and August when prices climb sharply.

What to do when you arrive

The Azores impress with volcanic landscapes, crater lakes, whale watching and hiking trails. Ponta Delgada Airport (PDL) on São Miguel Island is your gateway. From there, explore Furnas (thermal baths), Lagoa de Santiago or take ferry trips to other islands. Local food, unspoilt nature and black sand beaches round out the experience.

Volcanic crater lakes, hiking through calderas, whale watching, thermal baths at Furnas, black sand beaches and picturesque villages. São Miguel is the main island, reachable from Ponta Delgada Airport. You can take ferry trips to other islands for exploration.
