Fly from Paris to Cyprus from €171 return with Ryanair. Average price sits at €203. Take advantage of this low-cost route to reach the Mediterranean island for less.

Prices and when to book

Prices range from €171 to €226 depending on availability. The median of €203 is your benchmark: below it is a good deal, above it, wait. Book as far in advance as possible to secure better fares. The journey checked in October 2026 shows December availability, so plan ahead.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair is your only option on this route. The outbound flight includes 1 stopover, so it's not direct. Factor in total journey time and arrive at the airport with plenty of margin. Travel with hand baggage only to avoid extra costs, or add checked luggage to your budget.

Best time to visit

Cyprus is a year-round sun destination, but April to October offers the best weather. December is low-to-mid season, which aligns with these competitive prices. For quiet beaches and savings, winter is your window. Spring and autumn strike a perfect balance between pleasant warmth and fewer crowds.

Things to do on arrival

You'll land at Larnaca Airport (LCA), 45 km from the capital Nicosia. Cyprus offers white-sand beaches, Kition ruins, picturesque monasteries and Mediterranean cuisine. Rent a car to explore the island at your own pace or use public transport. Turquoise coves in the north and local gastronomy are must-sees.

FAQ
