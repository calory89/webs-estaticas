Best time to visit

Manila has a tropical climate with dry season (November to May) and wet season (June to October). The dry season is most comfortable for tourism: less rain and pleasant temperatures. November to February offers the best weather balance. Plan your trip in these months if you want good conditions, though prices may vary depending on demand in each period.

What to do in Manila

Manila is the gateway to the Philippines. Don't miss Intramuros (historic walled city), Manila Bay promenade, art and culture museums, and local markets like Quiapo. The city is chaotic but vibrant, with good food and nightlife. Make the most of your trip by exploring nearby island beaches or Malacañan Palace. Ninoy Aquino International Airport (MNL) is a short distance from the city centre.
