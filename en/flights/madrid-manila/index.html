Best time to visit

The Philippines has a tropical climate. The dry season (November to May) is best: fewer rainstorms and pleasant temperatures. Outside these months, monsoons can be heavy. November through January is peak tourist season with higher fares, but the weather makes it worthwhile. Always check the forecast before you go.

What to do on arrival in Manila

Manila is your gateway to the Philippines. Ninoy Aquino International Airport is well connected to the city centre. From there, visit Intramuros (the walled old town), Manila Bay, museums and temples. Many travellers fly on to other islands: Cebu, Palawan or Boracay. Exchange money at the airport or find ATMs, and use local transport (jeepneys, taxis) or ride-hailing apps.
