What to do when you arrive

Zanzibar awaits with white sand beaches, coral reefs and historic Stone Town. It's ideal for diving, snorkelling and relaxation. The international airport is about 50 km from the capital. From there you can hire a car, take a taxi or use local transport to get around the island.
