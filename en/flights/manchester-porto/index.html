Price and when to book

The lowest fares we've spotted start at €58 per person return, though most tickets hover around €62. Check regularly: Ryanair prices shift with availability and demand. Book early when you can, especially if you're travelling in peak season.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route, the sole carrier in our data. The outbound flight is direct: departs Manchester at 08:45 and lands in Porto with no intermediate stops. Return departs Porto at 09:00. No layovers mean less hassle and more time to enjoy your break.

Best time to visit

Porto is pleasant year-round, but spring (March to May) and autumn (September to October) deliver ideal weather and fewer crowds than summer. After July and August, you'll find more competitive fares. School holiday periods tend to push prices up.

What to do when you arrive

Porto is compact and walkable: stroll through Ribeira, cross the Luis I bridge, visit the port wine lodges, and try francesinha (the local sandwich). Livraria Lello is one of Europe's most stunning bookshops. The airport is 11 km from the centre with bus and metro links.

What should I see in Porto in 4 days?

Ribeira, the Luis I bridge, port wine lodges, Livraria Lello, Torre dos Clérigos, and walks along the Douro. Porto is compact: everything is within walking distance or a short metro ride. Eat francesinha and try pastéis de nata.
