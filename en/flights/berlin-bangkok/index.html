Best time to travel

Bangkok and Thailand have a tropical climate. The most comfortable season is November to February, when temperatures drop and rainfall decreases. In summer (May-October) it's very hot and humid. If you travel in December like the example, you'll hit the peak season: pleasant temperatures, clear skies and fewer mosquitoes. Book 2-3 months ahead to improve your price.

What to do when you arrive

Bangkok is your gateway. Don't miss the Grand Palace, temples like Wat Arun, floating markets and nightlife in Silom. From BKK you have cheap domestic flights to Chiang Mai (temples and mountains), Phuket or Krabi (beaches). Eat street food, haggle at markets and explore local neighbourhoods away from the tourist centre. The airport has quick train access to the city via Skytrain.
