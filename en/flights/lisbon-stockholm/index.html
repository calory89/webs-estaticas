The lowest price on record is €179 return. Fares on this route range between €179 and €185 from the samples checked. Booking 6-8 weeks ahead generally delivers better rates. Check regularly in October and November if you're travelling in winter: prices can shift day by day.

Best time to visit

Stockholm shines in winter if you're chasing northern lights or Christmas markets, though it's the coldest season. Spring and autumn offer pleasant temperatures with fewer crowds. Summer is peak season but also priciest. For value, travel between October and March (excluding Christmas holidays).

What to do when you arrive

Arlanda is Stockholm's main airport, roughly 40 km from the centre. It connects well via train, bus and taxi to Gamla Stan's medieval old town. Wander the narrow streets, visit the Royal Palace, explore Djurgården's museums or soak up modern architecture in Norrmalm. Three to four days covers the city well.
