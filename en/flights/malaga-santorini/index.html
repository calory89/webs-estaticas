The lowest price we've found is €292 return, though the median price stands at €477. For this route, book at least 3–4 weeks ahead. Prices are usually more competitive if you avoid peak season (July and August). December is a good month to find deals, as shown by the example flight on 6 December.

Airline and stopovers

Wizz Air Malta operates this route with 2 stopovers on the outbound leg. Sample flights depart Málaga on 6 December at 22:15 and arrive in Santorini on 7 December. The return departs 17 December at 08:15. Stopovers are common on routes to Greece from Spain, so allow extra time for connections.

Best time to visit

Santorini shines from May to October, but if you're after low prices, October is your month. April, May and September–October offer good weather without crowds. Winter months (November to March) have fewer tourists and cheaper flights, although the sea is cold for swimming. December combines attractive prices with a festive atmosphere.

What to do when you arrive

Santorini awaits with its white-washed villages: Oia is a must for sunset, Fira offers clifftop views. Black sand beaches (Kamari, Perissa), local wineries and archaeological museums round out the experience. The airport (JTR) is 10 km from the cruise port; rent a car or use the local bus. Spend at least 3–4 days to enjoy without rushing.

How much does a flight from Málaga to Santorini cost?

What to see in Santorini besides beaches?

Oia for sunset, Fira for clifftop views, traditional villages like Kamari, Akrotiri ruins, volcanic wine cellars and a boat trip to nearby volcanic islands are must-sees.
