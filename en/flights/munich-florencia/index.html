Cheap flights from Munich to Florence

Fly direct from Munich to Florence with Lufthansa from €177 return. The median price is around €198, so you have affordable options to explore the cradle of the Renaissance without breaking the bank.

Price and when to book

The base price is €177 return, though the median sits at €198 and you'll find options up to €273. Searches from 23 October show it pays to book ahead: the sooner you reserve, the better the fare. Allow at least 2-3 weeks from your search date to secure good prices and peace of mind.

Airline and stopovers

Lufthansa operates this route non-stop, with no stopovers. You board in Munich (MUC) and land directly in Florence (FLR) in just over two hours. It's the most comfortable way to travel: no waiting, no plane changes.

Best time to visit

Florence is beautiful year-round, but spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) are ideal: mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. If you fly in low season (November to February), you'll enjoy lower fares and avoid the queues at the Cathedral or the Uffizi.

What to do when you arrive

Florence airport (Peretola) is 4 km from the city centre. Take a bus or taxi to reach it in about 20 minutes. Don't miss the Cathedral of Santa Maria del Fiore, the Ponte Vecchio, the Uffizi Gallery and Palazzo Pitti. Florence is compact: you can walk almost everywhere.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for Munich-Florence flights?

Fares start from €177 return with Lufthansa. The median price is around €198. Higher options go up to €273, so booking early really matters.

Does Lufthansa fly direct from Munich to Florence?

Yes, Lufthansa offers direct flights with no stopovers on this route. The journey takes around two hours.

What can I see in Florence in 3 days?

In 3 days you can cover the Cathedral, the Campanile, the Baptistry, the Uffizi, Palazzo Vecchio, the Ponte Vecchio and a stroll through the bohemian Oltrarno district. Don't try to see everything; pick what appeals to you most.

Where does the plane land in Florence?

At Florence-Peretola Airport (FLR), 4 km from the city centre. It's well served by bus connections and very close to town.
