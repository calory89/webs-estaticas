Best time to travel

Bologna shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather, fewer crowds. Winter is cold but peaceful; summer is hot and packed with tourists. For value and comfort, skip July–August. Mid-season months (like November, when this data was gathered) usually strike a good balance between price and climate.

What to do when you arrive

Bologna is a medieval gem: wander Piazza Maggiore, climb Torre Asinelli, explore 40 km of arcades that ribbon through the city. Bolognese food is legendary, tagliatelle, mortadella, handmade tortellini on every corner. Bologna Airport (BLQ) sits 6 km from the centre; bus, taxi or train get you there quickly.
