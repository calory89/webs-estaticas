Price and when to book

Fares range from €660 to €784 depending on your travel dates. Finding that €660 price requires flexibility; the median (€765) is far more realistic. For such a long-haul route, booking 4–6 weeks ahead typically beats last-minute deals. Check prices mid-week; long-haul fares fluctuate more than short routes.

Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Wizz Air Malta. This journey includes 2 stopovers, so it's not direct: expect a lengthy trip with intermediate stops. That's the trade-off for the budget price. Check what baggage is included in your fare; Wizz Air has strict carry-on and checked luggage policies.

Best time to visit

Phuket is tropical year-round, but the dry season (November–February) brings better weather and less rain. It's also peak demand, so flights cost more. The rainy season (May–October) offers lower prices but less stable weather. Data checked in late October: good timing to hunt for year-end or spring trips.

What to do and see in Phuket

Phuket is your gateway to Thailand's beaches: Patong, Karon and Kata are the main strips. Day trips reach Phi Phi, Similan and James Bond islands. In town, the Old Phuket district keeps Sino-Portuguese architecture. Buddhist temples (Big Buddha, Chalong) and night markets round out the offer. For diving, snorkelling and water sports, it's one of Southeast Asia's top draws.
