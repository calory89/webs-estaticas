Best time to visit

Sardinia is a sun-and-sea destination. High season runs June through September, fares climb but weather is excellent. April, May and October strike a balance: warmer waters than winter, fewer crowds than summer, and moderate prices. Winter months are wetter but quieter and cheaper if you want relaxation over nightlife.

What to do when you arrive in Cagliari

Cagliari, Sardinia's capital, blends urban beach, old town and Gulf views. Poetto Beach is 10 minutes by bus from the airport. The medieval quarter (Castello) winds uphill through narrow streets, churches and the San Pancrazio Tower. Hire a car to explore: Arzachena, Bosa and the Emerald Coast are close by. Local food is stellar, malloreddus, bottarga, mirto liqueur.

What's there to see in Sardinia besides beaches?

Cagliari has a charming old town and museums. Across the island you'll find nuragic ruins (prehistoric stone towers), coastal towns like Bosa and Alghero, and wild inland landscapes. The Emerald Coast is famous, but leave time for the rest of the island too.
