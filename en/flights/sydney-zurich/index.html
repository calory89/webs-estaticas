Best time to go

Zurich is stunning in winter with snow on the Alps and in spring when the lakes thaw. Summer is peak season: if you can, fly in autumn or winter to skip crowds and find better prices. From Sydney, it's a long journey, so pick a time when you can enjoy the destination properly.

What to do when you arrive

Zurich has an old town by the lake, medieval churches and world-class museums. From there you can train to Interlaken or Montreux for the Alps. The airport is 13 km from the centre, with good rail links. Don't miss the Botanic Museum or a walk down Bahnhofstrasse.
