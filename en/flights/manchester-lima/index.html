Airline and stopovers

Best time to visit Peru

Peru shines during the dry season, May to October. The weather is stable, roads are in better condition, and the sky's clear for photographing ruins. Outside these months it rains more, especially in the highlands. Avoid peak international tourism if you want fewer crowds; May and June are ideal.

What to do on arrival in Lima

You'll land at Jorge Chávez Airport. From there, connect to Cusco or Puno to reach Machu Picchu, Lake Titicaca, or the Sacred Valley. In Lima itself, explore the historic centre, Miraflores district, and museums. Book tours to ruins and trains to Cusco well in advance; they fill up quickly, especially in high season.

FAQ

What's the cheapest flight price Manchester to Peru?

From €666 return. These fares are typically limited and require early booking. Check availability for your exact dates.

Does Ryanair fly direct from Manchester to Lima?

No, there are 2 stopovers. Ryanair connects through other airports, which extends the journey but keeps costs down. Total travel time depends on your stopover locations.

What should I see in Peru besides Machu Picchu?

Lake Titicaca in Puno, the Sacred Valley near Cusco, the Nazca Lines, and Lima. If you have time, the Amazon from Puerto Maldonado. Plan at least 10-14 days to explore without rushing.

When are flights to Peru cheapest?

No specific month guarantees lower prices. What matters is booking early: reserve 2-3 months ahead. Travel in dry season (May-October) for better weather and activity availability.
