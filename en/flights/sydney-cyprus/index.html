Best time to visit

Cyprus is a beach and culture destination year-round. June to September are the hottest months but also pricier and busier. Spring (April–May) and autumn (October–November) offer pleasant weather with fewer crowds. Winter is mild and fares drop. Plan according to your temperature preferences and tolerance for crowds.

What to do upon arrival in Cyprus

The international airport is in Larnaca (LCA), about 45 minutes from the city centre. From here you can explore Ayia Napa beaches, Kourion ruins, Nicosia (the capital), Kolossi Castle, or mountain villages like Omodos. The western coast around Paphos has resorts and archaeological sites. Local transport includes buses, taxis and car hire.
