Price and when to book

Milan-Agadir flights start from €38 return. We've seen prices at €38, €42, and €55 depending on availability and date. Book early: Ryanair releases fares months in advance. Search October or November for more budget-friendly options than summer. The savings can add up fast if you wait.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route with direct flights, no stops. Departures from Milan (Malpensa or Bergamo) to Agadir airport. The Irish carrier is known for rock-bottom fares: remember that cabin baggage is usually included, but checked luggage costs extra. Always confirm baggage policy when you book.

Best time to visit

Agadir is a coastal destination with mild weather nearly year-round. October to April is best: temperatures 20–25°C and little rain. Prices tend to be sharper outside summer. Travel in November or March and you'll get good weather plus better fares than June–August.

What to do when you arrive

Agadir beach is the main draw: 9 km of golden sand for lounging or water sports. The port and old medina are worth a stroll. From here you can head inland: markets in Taroudant (1 hour), hiking in the Atlas, or nearby beaches like Essaouira. The airport is 20 km from town, linked by bus and taxi.
