Best time to visit

Dubai is a warm destination year-round. The best season is October to April, when temperatures are moderate (25–30°C) and exploring the city and beaches is pleasant. Summer (May to September) is extremely hot, with temperatures exceeding 40°C. December is ideal: perfect weather and festive atmosphere.

What to do in Dubai

Dubai offers Jebel Ali beach (premium), Burj Khalifa (iconic skyscraper), Gold Souk (traditional market), Palm Jumeirah (artificial island), and the desert for safaris. Don't miss the Dubai Museum, shopping at Dubai Mall, or a walk through the old Al Fahidi district. The city blends modern luxury with Arab tradition.
