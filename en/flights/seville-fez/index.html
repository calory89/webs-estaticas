Best time to visit

Fez is pleasant year-round, but spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) offer ideal temperatures and fewer crowds. Summer is scorching in the medina. Avoid July and August for comfort. Book 2–3 months ahead to lock in the base fare.

What to do when you arrive in Fez

Fez's medina is a UNESCO World Heritage Site: wandering its souks, mosques and riads is a must. The Jewish Quarter, Karaouine Mosque and traditional tanneries are essential. Outside town, visit Volubilis or Meknes. Local transport is cheap: taxis and buses get you everywhere without breaking the bank.
