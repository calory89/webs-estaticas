Flights from Seville (SVQ) to Fez (FEZ) are available from €144 return, with a median price of €147. Ryanair operates this route with one stopover. Night departures and early-morning returns maximise your stay.

Price and when to book

The lowest price found is €144 return, ranging typically from €144 to €187. Book 2–3 months ahead for these fares. January and February offer good value if you skip public holidays. Night flights (like the 29 January departure at 22:20) let you make the most of your time.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair serves this route with one stopover; direct flights are not currently available. The stopover adds journey time but reflects Ryanair's network model for North African connections. Keep luggage within your ticket allowance to avoid extra charges.

Best time to travel

October, November, March and April offer mild weather (20–25°C) without summer heat or winter chill. January and February are cooler (12–15°C) but cheaper and less crowded. Skip July and August: heat is intense and tourist numbers peak.

What to do when you arrive

Fez is a Unesco World Heritage site: explore the Medina, one of Morocco's oldest souks, with its maze-like alleys. Visit Al Quaraouiyine University, the Royal Palace and traditional tanneries. Meknès is 30 minutes by car; Marrakech 1.5 hours. Fez Airport is 15 km from the centre; local taxis and buses connect it.

What else can you visit in Fez besides the Medina?

Al Quaraouiyine University, the Royal Palace, traditional tanneries and the Weapons Museum. From Fez you can take day trips to Meknès, Marrakech or the Rif mountains. Travel by local bus or private taxi is straightforward.
