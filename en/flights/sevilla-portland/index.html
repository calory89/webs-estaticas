Best time to travel

Portland offers pleasant weather year-round, but each season has its appeal. For drier conditions and moderate temperatures, June to September are ideal. Spring (March–May) brings blooming nature, whilst autumn (September–November) offers spectacular colours. Winter is wet but less crowded with tourists. Check availability according to your preferred dates.

What to do when you arrive in Portland

Portland is known for its alternative culture, exceptional food scene and easy access to nature. Explore the historic downtown, visit Powell's Books (the world's largest independent bookstore), wander through urban parks and try the craft breweries. Not far away lie the Columbia River, Multnomah Falls and the Oregon coast. PDX airport has good public transport links to the city centre.
