Airline and stopovers

Vueling is your airline on this route with 3 stopovers. It's not a direct flight, so prepare for connecting flights. Multiple stops extend your journey, but they're why these fares are so competitive. Always check connection times when booking to avoid delays.

Best time to visit

Orlando is a year-round destination. The climate is warm and humid, with rainy summers. Autumn and winter months (October to April) are drier and more comfortable. Skip peak holiday season if you want to keep prices down. Hurricane season runs June to November.

What to do when you arrive

Orlando is the world capital of theme parks: Walt Disney World, Universal Orlando Resort, and SeaWorld are must-dos. If parks aren't your thing, enjoy lakes, nature, and downtown. The international airport (MCO) is around 20 km from the city centre. Rent a car or use public transport to get around.

FAQ

How much does a flight from Zaragoza to Orlando cost?

From €597 return with Vueling. Most tickets hover around €670. Prices vary by date and how far in advance you book.

Are there direct flights from Zaragoza to Orlando?

No, Vueling operates this route with 3 stopovers. The journey is longer, but fares are cheaper than direct flights.

What's the best time to visit Orlando?

October to April is ideal: dry and comfortable weather. Avoid June to November due to hurricane season and hot, humid summers.

What to see in Orlando besides the parks?

Kayaking on lakes, Florida nature, downtown with restaurants and shops, and nearby beaches just one or two hours' drive away.
