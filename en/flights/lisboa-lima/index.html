Best time to visit

Peru is a year-round destination, but remember the Andes (Machu Picchu, Cusco) have a dry season from May to October: less rain, clear skies. Lima and the coast are enjoyable any time. Avoid rainy season in the highlands (November to April) if you're planning trekking or mountain activities. Travelling from Europe between September and November often gives better prices than summer.

What to do when you land

You'll arrive at Lima airport (Jorge Chávez). From there, take a bus, taxi or ride-share app to the city centre. Spend 2-3 days in Lima: world-class food, museums (Larco, Anthropology), neighbourhoods like Miraflores and Barranco. Then fly to Cusco (short domestic flight) for Machu Picchu, or explore the Amazon, Lake Titicaca or the southern coast. Most European visitors book domestic flights once they land.
