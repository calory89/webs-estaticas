Airline and stopovers

ITA Airways operates this route with 1 stopover. The 10 November example departs Rome at 10:20; the 17 November return leaves at 16:52. One stopover adds roughly 2–4 hours to your total journey time depending on the connection, but cuts costs significantly versus a direct flight.

Best time to travel

November falls into Puerto Rico's shoulder season: fewer tourists than summer, fewer hurricanes than earlier autumn. Weather is warm and humid, ideal for beach time. If you're hunting savings, these in-between months offer better value than December or July.

What to do on arrival

You'll land at Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport (SJU), 10 km from San Juan. Old San Juan is unmissable: walls, colonial squares, local food. Beaches like Flamenco (Culebra) or El Yunque (tropical rainforest) are classic day trips. Hire a car to explore beyond the city centre, or walk round San Juan itself.
