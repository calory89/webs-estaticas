Fly from Santiago to Amsterdam from €176 return. The median price sits at €186, giving you solid savings options. Iberia Airlines runs this route with one stopover, perfect for connecting to northern Europe.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €176 return, with a median of €186. Sample prices range from €176 to €219. Book 6-8 weeks in advance for the best fares; prices climb as departure approaches. Midweek searches (Tuesday–Thursday) often pull cheaper rates than weekends.

Airline and stopovers

Iberia Airlines is your carrier on this route with one stopover. It's not a direct flight, but one stop is reasonable for a European route. The sample outbound departs at 13:30 from SCQ and the return leaves at 07:20 from Amsterdam, giving you scheduling flexibility.

Best time to visit

Amsterdam is lovely year-round, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) deliver good weather and fewer crowds than July–August. Prices often dip outside peak season, particularly in early winter and late August. Skip Spanish and Dutch public holidays if you're chasing low fares.

What to do when you arrive

Amsterdam Schiphol (AMS) is 9 km from the centre; direct train in 15 minutes. The city thrives on cycling, canal walks and museums like the Rijksmuseum. Nearby towns (Marken, Volendam) are a half-hour away. Book accommodation in De Pijp or Jordaan for a local vibe.
