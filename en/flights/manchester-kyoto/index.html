Best time to visit Kyoto

Kyoto shines in spring (March to May) with cherry blossoms and in autumn (October to November) with mild weather and colourful scenery. Winter is quieter but cold; summer is hot and humid. If you travel in December, like the example date, you'll arrive as Japanese winter begins: low rainfall but cool temperatures.

What to do when you arrive in Kyoto

Kyoto is Japan's former capital, full of temples, shrines and traditional gardens. Walk through Arashiyama's bamboo grove, visit Fushimi Inari temple with its thousands of red gates, explore the geisha district of Gion and unwind at nearby hot springs (onsen). Local cuisine, including kaiseki and yudofu, won't disappoint.

What else can I see in Kyoto besides temples?

Traditional cuisine, markets like Nishiki, boat trips on the Hozu river, seasonal festivals, Nijo castle, art museums and the machiya (old wooden house) district. You can do a day trip to Nara to see Todai-ji temple and free-roaming deer.
