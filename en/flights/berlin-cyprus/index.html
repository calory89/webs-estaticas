The cheapest flights from Berlin to Cyprus are on easyJet from €120 return. The median price sits around €182, giving you varied options across different dates. With direct flights, this route is straightforward and time-efficient.

Price and when to book

The lowest recorded price is €120 return, though the median stands at €182. We've seen fares reach €303 depending on availability and travel dates. Book as far in advance as possible: the earlier you purchase, the better your rate. Prices typically rise closer to departure.

Airline and stops

easyJet operates this route with direct flights, no stops in between. You travel from Berlin to Cyprus without intermediate landings, saving time and hassle. Check what baggage is included in your chosen fare, as easyJet charges for checked luggage.

Best time to visit

Cyprus has mild weather year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant temperatures without summer extremes. These periods also tend to have fewer crowds than peak July–August, which can affect flight availability.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Larnaca International Airport (LCA), about 30 km from the centre. From there, explore Ayia Napa's beaches, stroll through Limassol, hike the Troodos mountains, or wander Nicosia's old town. Cyprus blends medieval history, Mediterranean coastlines, and solid local cuisine.
