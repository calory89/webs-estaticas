Airline and stops

Lufthansa operates this route with direct flights. No intermediate stops, so you'll land in Luxembourg in just over an hour. That means less airport hassle and more time to enjoy your destination.

Best time to visit

Luxembourg is pleasant year-round, but October and November offer mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. December brings festive charm, though fares climb. Skip July and August if you want lower prices and less tourist congestion.

What to do when you arrive

The airport is 6 km from the city centre. Luxembourg City is compact and walkable. Don't miss the Casemates du Bock (fortified galleries), the Grand Ducal Palace, and the lively neighbourhoods of Ville Haute and Grund. Art museums are excellent. Train connections to Brussels, Paris and nearby cities are quick if you want to extend your trip.

What can I see in Luxembourg?

The city is small but rich in history. Visit the Casemates (underground galleries), the Grand Ducal Palace, the cathedral, the suspension bridges and the old quarters of Grund and Ville Haute. It's also a good base for exploring the Ardennes and nearby villages by train.
