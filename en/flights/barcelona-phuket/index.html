Best time to visit

Thailand is best from November to February: dry weather, comfortable temperatures, no monsoons. Phuket has beaches year-round, but European summer (June-August) coincides with the rainy season. If you want good prices and good weather, December and January are ideal, though busier. March to May can be very hot.

What to do when you arrive in Phuket

Phuket is the gateway to Thailand's beaches. Patong Beach is the most famous and lively. From there, it's easy to take trips to the Phi Phi Islands, Phang Nga, or Phang Nga Bay. Phuket Old Town has shops and authentic local life. Options range from beach relaxation to scuba diving and trekking.
