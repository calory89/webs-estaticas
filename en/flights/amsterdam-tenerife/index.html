Price and when to book

We found tickets from €127 return, with typical prices around €138. Fares range from €127-205 depending on your chosen dates. Book 2-3 months ahead to secure the best rate. Avoid Easter week and Christmas if you want to keep costs down, prices spike then. Tuesdays and Wednesdays tend to offer better fares than weekends.

Airline and stops

easyJet operates this route without stops. Direct Amsterdam-Tenerife flights save you time and hassle. As a low-cost carrier, hand luggage is included, but checked baggage costs extra. Pack carefully and check size limits for your carry-on before heading to the airport.

Best time to visit

Tenerife has pleasant weather year-round, but October, November and April are typically the cheapest months outside peak season. January and February attract more tourists thanks to the warmth, so prices rise. Travel in low season (September-October or April-May) for lower fares and quieter beaches and villages.

What to do when you land

Tenerife South airport (TCI) is your entry point. Hire a car to explore whitewashed villages up north, visit Mount Teide, relax on beaches like El Médano or stroll around Santa Cruz. Book accommodation and activities in advance, especially in mid-season. Public transport exists but is limited, a rental car gives you more freedom to explore.
