Best time to visit Rio de Janeiro

Rio is worth visiting year-round. The dry season (April to October) offers stable weather and less rain. Rio's summer (December to February) is hot and humid. Avoid Carnival if you don't want crowds and high prices, though it's the most iconic time. Consider shoulder months (March, November) for better weather-to-price value.

What to do when you arrive in Rio de Janeiro

Rio de Janeiro International Airport (GIG) is well connected to the city. Visit Christ the Redeemer, Copacabana Beach, Guanabara Bay, and the Lapa neighbourhood. Take the cable car up Sugarloaf Mountain for panoramic views. Enjoy carioca cuisine and samba bars. The city is vibrant with options for all budgets.
