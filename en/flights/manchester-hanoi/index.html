Travel to Vietnam for less than you'd expect: from €655 return. The median price sits at €672, so with advance booking and date flexibility you'll grab solid deals. easyJet is your main option on this route.

Price and when to book

From €655 you'll find combinations with easyJet. Sample prices hover around €655, €672 and €723, giving you room to choose. The further ahead you book, the better the price: reserve 2–3 months in advance if you can. Avoid booking too close to departure.

Airline and stopovers

easyJet operates this route with 2 stopovers on the outbound flight. It's not direct, but the prices make up for it. Example outbound departs Manchester 8 December at 13:25, return 11 December at 22:40. Always check easyJet's website for exact stopover locations and total journey times.

Best time to travel

Vietnam is worth visiting year-round depending on your climate tolerance. October to April is the dry season and most comfortable. If you're hunting low prices, availability depends more on your flexibility than any one season. Check the price calendar on booking sites to spot week-to-week variations.

What to do when you arrive in Hanoi

You land at Noi Bai International Airport, 30 km from the city centre. The Old Quarter is essential: narrow streets, temples and street food. Hoan Kiem Lake, the Temple of Literature, Ha Long Bay (3–4 hours away) and Ho Chi Minh City (short domestic flight) are classics. Arrange ground transport in advance.
