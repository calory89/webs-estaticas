Price and when to book

Flights we've tracked range from €144 to €179 return. The sooner you book from today (21 October), the better your rate. There's no magic day when prices drop, but booking 3-4 weeks ahead generally works. Check regularly to spot a good deal.

Airline and stops

Air Europa operates this route with 1 stop. It's not direct, but the price makes up for it. The stopover adds time to your journey, so check the full schedule: outbound departs 17:05, return arrives just after midnight (20:25). Leave buffer time if you have onward plans from Malaga that day.

Best time to visit

Belgrade is worth visiting any time of year, but October and April offer mild weather and fewer crowds. Winter is cheap but cold; summer draws tourists. Don't book by price alone—choose dates that fit your calendar and the climate you prefer in Serbia.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at 'Nikola Tesla' Airport (BEG), 18 km from the city centre. Take bus 72 or a taxi into town. Explore Kalemegdan Fortress, wander the bohemian Zemun neighbourhood, visit the Temple of Saint Sava. Local food (ćevapčići, rakija) is worth every penny. It's a Balkan capital with real character.

FAQ

Does Air Europa fly direct Malaga-Belgrade?

No, this route includes 1 stop. It's not direct, but the price is very competitive for what you get.

What's the best airline from Malaga to Belgrade?

What can I visit in Belgrade?

Kalemegdan Fortress (Danube views), the historic Zemun neighbourhood, the Temple of Saint Sava, museums and riverside nightlife. It's a compact city, easy to explore on foot.
