Price and when to book

Prices range from €160 to €218 in our sample. For this route, booking with good notice pays off: the earlier the better. Don't wait until the last minute. Flexible dates help too: a Tuesday or Wednesday outbound is usually cheaper than Friday or Sunday.

Airline and stops

Wizz Air Malta operates this route with one stop. It's not a direct flight, but the prices make up for it. Cabin luggage is free; checked bags and seat selection have extra charges. Check the terms carefully before you buy.

Best time to visit

Belgrade is lovely in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) when the weather is mild and crowds are lighter. Winters are cold but the city has character. Skip summer dates if you want the best prices: July and August get pricey. January and February are cheaper, though chilly.

What to do when you arrive

Belgrade deserves 3–4 days. Don't miss Kalemegdan Fortress, the bohemian Zemun district, the Church of Saint Sava and local museums. Eat at a traditional kajmakčnica, walk along the Danube and enjoy the nightlife. The airport is about 20 km from the centre; bus or taxi are affordable.
