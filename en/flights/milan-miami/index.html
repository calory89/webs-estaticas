Best time to visit

Miami is a year-round destination. October to April brings pleasant weather: mild temperatures and less rain. May to September is hurricane season with hot, humid conditions, though prices may dip. Winter is peak season and pricier.

What to do when you arrive

Miami offers white-sand beaches, nightlife in South Beach, the Wynwood Walls for street art, Vizcaya Museum for history, and easy access to the Florida Keys. The airport (MIA) is about 13 km from downtown; use public transport, taxi, or car rental depending on your preference.
