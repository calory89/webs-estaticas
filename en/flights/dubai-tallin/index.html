Best time to visit Tallinn

Summer (June to August) is stunning—long days and packed terraces—but it's peak season. May and September offer pleasant weather with fewer crowds and lower fares. Winter (December to February) is for those who want to see the city blanketed in snow and experience traditional festive markets. Each season has its charm.

What to do when you arrive in Tallinn

Lennart Meri Tallinn Airport (TLL) is 4 km from the city centre. The Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and deserves 2–3 days: medieval walls, ancient churches, cobbled squares. Explore the Kalamaja neighbourhood, hunt for coffee at Telliskivi, and climb up to Toompea for city views. Winter markets here are fantastic.

Pegasus is a Turkish low-cost carrier meeting European safety standards. As a budget airline, baggage and extras cost extra. Read the terms carefully when you book.

What can I see in Tallinn in 4–5 days?

The Old Town is essential: walls, Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, St Nicholas Church. Spend time in Kalamaja, wander Telliskivi, and if you have time, take a short ferry to Helsinki or explore the coastal towns to the east.
