Best time to visit

Phu Quoc has a tropical climate. The best season is the dry months (November to April), when there's less rain and beaches are at their finest. December, the month in your sample flight, is ideal: pleasant temperatures and low monsoon risk. Avoid May to October if you prefer less rainfall.

What to do when you arrive in Phu Quoc

Phu Quoc is known for pristine beaches, crystal-clear water and coral reefs. Visit Duong Dong (the main town, with markets and local life), go snorkelling, diving or simply relax on beaches like Long Beach or Sao Beach. There are also pepper plantations and the Phu Quoc Prison Museum for cultural interests. The airport (PQC) is well connected with taxis and transport services to town.
