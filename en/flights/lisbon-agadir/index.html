Lisbon-Agadir flights start at €125 return, with a median of €127. Royal Air Maroc operates this route with one stopover. A bargain for a break on Morocco's coast.

Price and when to book

Tickets from €125 return. Median sits at €127, so most trips are in that range. Book 3-4 weeks ahead to lock in these fares. The later you book, the steeper the prices climb.

Royal Air Maroc and your stopover

Royal Air Maroc operates this route with one stopover. The sample outbound departs Lisbon at 17:55 and arrives in Agadir; the return leaves at 12:00. One stopover is standard on this link, so total journey time is reasonable. Arrive early at check-in.

Best time to travel

Winter (November to February) is prime season: mild weather, accessible beaches and keener fares. Autumn works well too. Skip peak summer if you're hunting deals. Always check for flash promotions.

What to do on arrival in Agadir

The beach comes first: golden sand and warm waters. The medina is a must for winding streets, handicrafts and local food. The fishing port is picturesque at dusk. Inland, Amazigh villages and the Anti-Atlas mountains deserve a trip. Hire a car for freedom.

What is there to see and do in Agadir?

Wide beaches, medina with shops and restaurants, fishing port, local crafts. From there it's easy to reach Essaouira, Tafraoute or the mountains. Great for beach time, relaxation and cultural exploration.
