Best time to travel

November is a smart choice: fewer tourists than summer, cool weather (bring a coat), and competitive prices. Skip bank holidays and public holidays for cheaper fares. January–February and September–October also tend to offer good value.

What to do when you arrive

Paris offers the Eiffel Tower, the Louvre, Notre-Dame, and charming Latin Quarter cafés. Cycle along the Seine, explore Le Marais, or wander up Montmartre. Eat well: croissants, French ham, local cheeses. Four to five days is plenty for the highlights.
