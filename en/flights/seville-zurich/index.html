Best time to travel

Zurich is attractive at any time of year. Winter (as in this January example) offers snowy landscapes and Christmas markets in the surrounding area through mid-January. What to do when you arrive

Zurich is perfect for exploring on foot. Wander the Altstadt (old town), climb the Cathedral and enjoy Lake Zurich. If you fancy mountains, the Alps are just a few hours away. The city's museums and Swiss gastronomy round off an 8-day escape like this example.
