Best time to visit Cape Town

Cape Town is a year-round destination. Southern summer (December to February) brings peak season, higher prices and crowds. Spring (September to November) and autumn (March to May) offer pleasant weather and fewer tourists. Winter (June to August) is cooler but still enjoyable, and flights are cheaper.

What to do when you arrive at Cape Town airport

Cape Town International Airport is around 25 km from the city centre. Reach it by bus, taxi or hire a car. The city centre is walkable along the coast: Camps Bay beach and the Waterfront promenade are essential. Must-see excursions include Table Mountain, Robben Island and the Stellenbosch wine route, all reachable as day trips or short stays from the city.
