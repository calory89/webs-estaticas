Fly from Dublin to Cagliari airport in Sardinia from €116 return. The median price sits around €134, so book early to grab the best fares. One stopover lies between you and Sardinia's beaches and heritage.

Price and when to book

Fares start from €116 return, with a median of €134. As your travel date approaches, prices climb to €155. The key rule is simple: book as far ahead as you can. Ryanair uses dynamic pricing, so don't wait around.

Airline and stopovers

You'll travel with Ryanair on both legs. Your outbound flight includes one stopover, so it's not direct, but prices compete at the lower end of the market. The return departs 21 November at 18:25, giving you a clear window to explore the island at a leisurely pace.

Best time to visit

November is a solid choice: mild temperatures (16–20°C), fewer crowds than summer, and the island remains fully accessible. If you prefer warmth, May to September offers sunny days, though prices rise. Aim to book several months ahead for peak-season dates.

What to do in Sardinia

Cagliari airport welcomes you in the south. Explore Villasimius and Maddalena beaches, Cagliari's old town, the Costa Smeralda to the north, or prehistoric nuragic towers. Local food, malloreddus, botarga, Vermentino wine, is worth the trip alone.
