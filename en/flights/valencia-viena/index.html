Best time to travel

Vienna in November is crisp autumn weather (8–15°C), perfect for sightseeing without heat. December brings Christmas markets but higher prices. October–November tends to be cheaper than spring. Skip July–August (peak season) if you want a bargain fare. Travel mid-week when you can.

What to do when you arrive

Vienna has St Stephen's Cathedral in the city centre, the palaces of Schönbrunn and Hofburg to explore. Walk the Old Town (Unesco World Heritage), have coffee at a traditional Viennese café, and try Wiener Schnitzel. The airport (Schwechat) is 19 km out; bus or train gets you downtown in 20–40 minutes.
