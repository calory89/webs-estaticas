Best time to visit

Bruges is beautiful year-round, but skip summer crowds (June–August) and Christmas. Autumn (September–October) and spring (April–May) offer good weather and fewer tourists. Winter is magical if you like the festive vibe, but it's also peak season for prices.

What to do when you arrive in Bruges

Brussels-Zaventem airport is 30 km from Bruges; there's a direct train every 30 minutes (20-minute journey). Once in Bruges, walk the medieval old town, climb Belfort belfry for city views, drink Belgian beer on a terrace and try the waffles. The Grand Place is unmissable at sunset. Two to three days is enough to see it all properly.
