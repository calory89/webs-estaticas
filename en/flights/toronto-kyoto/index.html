Best time to visit

Japan is stunning year-round, but spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) are peak seasons with high demand and good flight availability. Winter and summer can offer lower fares, though weather is more extreme. Book well ahead to secure the best prices.

What to do when you arrive

Kyoto is Japan's cultural heart: historic temples like Fushimi Inari and Kinkaku-ji, traditional gardens, historic districts such as Gion, and exceptional local cuisine. Kansai Airport connects to Kyoto by public transport in 75–90 minutes. Carry a valid passport and check entry requirements before travelling.
