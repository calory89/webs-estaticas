Best time to visit

October, November, March and April are ideal: mild weather (20–25°C), no extreme summer heat. December and January are fine too if you don't mind 15–18°C at night. Skip July and August if you can't take 35°C+ heat. Demand peaks over Christmas and Easter.

What to do when you arrive in Marrakech

The airport (RAK) is 6 km from the medina. Walk through Jemaa el-Fnaá square, browse the spice and textile souks, visit the Bahía Palace and Majorelle Gardens. Ait Benhaddou kasbah is 3 hours by car. Try tagine, mint tea and date pastries. The city is accessible, safe and very photogenic.

What's the best month to visit Marrakech?

October, November, March and April are best: pleasant temperatures (20–25°C) and less crowding than Christmas. July and August are very hot (35°C+).

What's worth seeing in Marrakech in 3–4 days?

Jemaa el-Fnaá square, traditional souks, Bahía Palace, Majorelle Gardens and the red kasbah. If you have time, take a day trip to Ait Benhaddou (3 hours). Eat tagine in the medina and drink mint tea in a riad.
