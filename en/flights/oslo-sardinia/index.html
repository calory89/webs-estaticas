What to do in Sardinia

Sardinia boasts stunning beaches, picturesque coastal villages, and rich culture. Visit Porto Cervo and the Costa Smeralda for upmarket views, or explore Cagliari, the capital, with museums and medieval architecture. La Maddalena and Arzachena beaches are unmissable. You'll land at Cagliari-Elmas airport (CAG), well connected to the city centre and main attractions.
