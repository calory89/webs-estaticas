Best time to visit

Iceland is a year-round destination. For northern lights, choose winter (October–March), though it'll be cold with fewer daylight hours. Summer (June–August) brings midnight sun and better weather, but higher prices. January, February and April often have good flight deals. Book early regardless of season.

What to do when you arrive in Iceland

You'll land at Keflavík International Airport (KEF), 50 km from Reykjavik. The Golden Circle is essential: hot springs, waterfalls and geysers. Don't miss Reynisfjara's black sand beach, Vatnajökull glacier and Fjaðrárgljúfur canyon. The Blue Lagoon thermal baths are perfect to relax after flying. Iceland is wild: hike your own routes or book local tours.
