Airline and stopovers

Ryanair is your only option on this route. The journey includes 1 stopover, so it is not direct. Connection times may vary depending on your chosen date, so verify the layover duration when purchasing. Although it is not a non-stop flight, the final price makes up for the intermediate stop.

Best time to travel

Iceland is a seasonal destination. Summer (June-August) is the best time to see the midnight sun and enjoy milder temperatures. Winter (October-March) is spectacular if you're after the northern lights, though days are very short and the climate more harsh. Each season has its charm, so choose based on which natural phenomenon you want to experience.

What to do when you arrive in Iceland

You'll land at Keflavík Airport (KEF), about 50 km from Reykjavik. From there you can explore the Golden Circle (Þingvellir, Geysir, Gullfoss), visit waterfalls like Skógafoss or Seljalandsfoss, bathe in the Blue Lagoon or hike glaciers in the south. Iceland offers unique landscapes: cliffs, glaciers, hot springs and black sand beaches.
