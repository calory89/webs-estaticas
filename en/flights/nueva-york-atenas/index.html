Best time to visit

Athens enjoys Mediterranean weather. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. Winter is cheaper but with shorter days. Plan based on your climate preferences and budget.

What to do in Athens

Don't miss the Acropolis and Parthenon, the winding streets of Plaka, the Ancient Agora, and the Acropolis Museum. Eat mezze (Greek tapas) at a traditional taverna, stroll the Piraeus waterfront, and climb Lycabettus Hill at sunset. The city blends ancient ruins with modern life.

What should I see in Athens in 3 days?

Day 1: Acropolis, Parthenon and Acropolis Museum. Day 2: Ancient Agora, Plaka and Lycabettus Hill. Day 3: Piraeus, Temple of Poseidon at Cape Sounion, or nearby islands like Hydra. Eat at local tavernas every day.
