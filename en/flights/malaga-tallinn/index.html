What to do when you arrive

Tallinn Airport (Lennart Meri Tallinn Airport) is 4 km from the city centre. The Old Town is a UNESCO site—wander medieval lanes, visit Alexander Nevski Cathedral, and grab a drink at Town Hall Square. Don't miss Kalamaja's bohemian vibe or a coastal stroll. It's a compact capital, easily explored on foot or by bike.
