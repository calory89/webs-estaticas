Best time to visit

South Korea shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October), when weather is mild and rainfall is lower. Winter (December–February) brings snow to many areas and good skiing conditions. Summer is warm and humid. Choose your season based on climate preference, but remember prices rise during peak season. Booking well ahead protects you from fare hikes.

Things to do in Seoul and beyond

You'll land at Incheon International Airport (ICN), about 60 km from Seoul. In the capital, don't miss the palaces (Gyeongbokgung, Changdeokgung), Gangnam district, the traditional Bukchon hanok village, and Myeongdong's nightlife. It's easy to reach the Demilitarized Zone, Bulguksa Temple, or the coastal town of Busan from Seoul. Public transport is excellent and very affordable.
