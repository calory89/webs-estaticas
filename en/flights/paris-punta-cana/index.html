Air Transat: stopovers and service

Air Transat operates this route with 1 stopover. Outbound departs at 09:15 from Paris and return at 14:10 from Punta Cana, with reasonable connection times. They specialise in transatlantic routes to the Caribbean at low cost.

Best time to visit

Punta Cana is a year-round beach destination. The dry season (November to March) offers ideal weather and moderate prices before Christmas. Avoid September and October due to hurricane season. April to June sees fewer tourists and potentially cheaper fares.

What to do when you arrive in Punta Cana

Punta Cana International Airport is modern and well-connected. Direct access to stunning beaches (Bavaro, Cabeza de Toro), natural parks (Ojo Indígena, Hoyo Azul) and all-inclusive resorts. Perfect for diving, snorkelling and day trips.
