Best time to visit

Vilna shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): mild weather, uncrowded streets and reasonable prices. Winter brings intense cold but special charm if you like snow. Summer (June–August) is peak season, busier and more expensive.

What to do when you arrive in Vilna

The airport is 4 km from the city centre. The Old Town, a UNESCO site, is unmissable: wooden churches, medieval squares and cosy cafés. Climb Gediminas Hill for panoramic views, explore Užupis (a bohemian neighbourhood with its own declared independence) and browse vintage markets. Vilna is compact and walkable.

Old Town (museums, churches, walks), Gediminas Hill (panoramic views), Užupis neighbourhood (street art), markets and vintage shops. The city is small and very walkable. Three to four days cover the essentials well.
