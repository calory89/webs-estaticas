Best time to go

Nairobi works year-round. The dry seasons (July–October and January–February) offer clearer skies and better safari visibility. Prices climb during these months, so if your budget is tight, consider May–June or November–December. Avoid rainy seasons if you want peak comfort.

What to do when you arrive

Nairobi is your gateway to Kenya. From here you reach major parks: Amboseli (elephants), Masai Mara (wildebeest, lions) and Tsavo. In the city, visit the Nairobi National Museum, Karen Blixen Museum and Giraffe Centre. The airport (NBO) sits 15 km from the centre; taxis and shuttles are available at arrivals.
