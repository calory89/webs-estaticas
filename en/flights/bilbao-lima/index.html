Best time to visit

Peru is a year-round destination, but the dry season (May to September) is best for trekking Machu Picchu and the Cordillera Blanca. For low fares, travel in off-peak: October, November, April or May. The humidity and rain from December to March doesn't deter everyone, so there are deals to be found.

What to do when you arrive

You land in Lima, the gateway. Enjoy the food scene and colonial museums, then head to Machu Picchu (train from Cusco), trek the Cordillera Blanca, or visit Lake Titicaca. Peru offers archaeology, high-altitude nature and living culture. Plan carefully as internal travel takes time.
