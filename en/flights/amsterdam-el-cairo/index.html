Price and when to book

Flights range from £273 to £355 in our sample. The average price hovers around £284—excellent value for this route. Airlines typically release discounted seats 2–3 months in advance: booking in early October for January–February travel puts you in a strong position. Skip the last two weeks before departure, when fares tend to climb.

Airline and stops

Fly direct with Transavia from Amsterdam to Cairo. No stops means fewer surprises, less time in terminals and greater comfort. Transavia is Air France's low-cost subsidiary, combining decent service with tight pricing. Always check what's included in your fare (carry-on bag, seat, meals) before you confirm.

Best time to visit

January and February are ideal: pleasant temperatures (20–25°C daytime), light rainfall and fewer crowds than peak European winter. July and August are unbearable (40°C+). The Nile and temples are far more enjoyable from autumn through spring. Travel during these prime months and you'll sidestep seasonal fare hikes.

What to do on arrival

You land at Cairo International Airport (CAI), about 25 km from town centre. The Egyptian Museum, Giza pyramids and Khan El-Khalili bazaar are unmissable. The Nile offers spectacular cruises. Book accommodation ahead: prices rise in peak season, even if your flight was cheap.
