Best time to visit

Peru is worth visiting year-round. The dry season (May to October) offers better weather in the highlands and Machu Picchu, ideal for trekking. The rainy season (November to April) attracts fewer tourists and has lower prices on the ground. Consider your preference for climate or tourist density when choosing dates; this affects your experience more than flight price.

What to do upon arrival in Lima

Lima is the gateway to Peru. Spend days exploring the historic centre, museums like Larco or the National Museum, and local cuisine. Domestic flights depart from here to Cusco (for Machu Picchu), the Amazon, and Nazca. Jorge Chávez International Airport is 16 km from the centre, well connected by bus, taxi, and private services.
