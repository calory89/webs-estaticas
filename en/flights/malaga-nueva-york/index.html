Best time to visit

New York is a 12-month destination, but each season has its charm. Winter (December-February) is cold but magical with holiday decorations; spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer perfect weather and stunning scenery; summer is warm with plenty of events. Prices tend to be higher during holidays and peak season, so if you want to save, avoid school breaks and public holidays.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at one of New York's airports (JFK, LaGuardia or Newark depending on your route). Times Square, Central Park, the Statue of Liberty and Brooklyn are must-sees. Eat at food courts in neighbourhoods like East Village or Astoria if you want to spend less. The subway is the cheapest and fastest way to get around. Book museums and attractions in advance to avoid queues and secure availability.
