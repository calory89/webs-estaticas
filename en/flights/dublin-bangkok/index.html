Airline and stopovers

China Eastern Airlines operates this route with 1 stopover on the outbound leg. It's not direct, but that's standard for ultra-long haul routes. The stopover lets you move around and stretch out, though it adds total travel time. Check your booking confirmation for the stopover location and connection time.

Best time to visit

Thailand welcomes visitors year-round, but December is prime: dry weather, mild temperatures, ideal for sightseeing without the sticky season. It's also peak season, so the city is lively. If you prefer fewer crowds and potentially lower fares, consider April–May, though it'll be hotter.

What to do when you arrive

Bangkok is sprawling and energetic. The Grand Palace and Wat Arun are worth the visit, if touristy. Hunt for street food at the floating markets, cruise the Chao Phraya River, and explore local neighbourhoods beyond the old quarter. Suvarnabhumi is the main airport; take the airport rail link or taxi into town in roughly 30–45 minutes.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Dublin to Bangkok?

No, this route includes 1 stopover. Long-haul intercontinental flights typically require a technical stop, so it's standard for this journey.

Is China Eastern Airlines reliable?

Yes, it's a major Chinese carrier with a long track record. It offers standard service quality on long-haul routes, though comfort varies by ticket class.

When are flights to Bangkok cheapest?

We can't guarantee specific months are always cheaper, but booking in advance definitely helps. As you can see here, fares are available weeks ahead of travel.

What else is there to see in Bangkok besides temples?

Floating markets (Damnoen Saduak is the most famous), the old quarter at Talad Noi, the National Museum, canal boat tours, nightlife along Sukhumvit, and modern fusion restaurants. There's far more than traditional sightseeing.
