Price and when to book your flight

The fares you see (from €129 to €134) come from recent searches. There's no fixed pattern for price drops, but the golden rule remains: book early, pay less. Travel between January and March or in autumn to find the best deals; summer prices climb steeply.

Transavia France: direct and straightforward

Transavia France operates this route with direct flights, no stops. You depart Paris in the morning (example: 08:00) and arrive in Crete without changing planes. On the return, your flight leaves mid-afternoon (example: 13:20). A simple and quick way to reach the Greek island.

Best time to visit Crete

January is a good window for cheap flights, as shown in the example dates. February and March are also quiet months with controlled pricing. If you prefer better weather and don't mind spending more, April–May and September–October shine. Summer is warm and crowded; skip it if you want calm and low fares.

What to do in Crete

Crete is Greece's largest island. In the north, Heraklion (your arrival point) and Chania offer beaches, dining and nightlife. Inland, mountain villages, gorges (such as Samaria) and Minoan ruins (Knossos) are must-sees. The pink-sand beaches of Balos and the maze-like old town of Rethymno round out the highlights.

FAQ

How many days should I spend in Crete?
