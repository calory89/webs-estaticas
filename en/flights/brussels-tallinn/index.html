Price and when to book

Flights start from €190 return, with median price at €195. Current market fares range between €190 and €196. For this route, book at least 2-3 weeks ahead; the more time you give yourself, the better. November is low season in Estonia, so prices tend to stay lower than in summer months.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route at €190 base fare. The flight includes 1 stopover, so it's not direct. Departure from Brussels is around 6:30 am and return is mid-afternoon (16:10). Despite the connection, total travel time remains competitive at this price point.

Best time to visit

November and December offer lower fares and fewer tourists. Don't expect warm weather: Tallinn averages 0-5°C. However, it's perfect if you want to avoid crowds and experience the medieval Christmas markets in the old town. Spring (April-May) and early autumn (September) are also good options, though slightly pricier.

What to do when you arrive

Lennart Meri Tallinn airport is 4 km from the city centre. Spend at least one day in the medieval old town, a UNESCO World Heritage site, with cobbled streets and city walls. Visit Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, stroll the harbour and try traditional Estonian cuisine. Winter visitors can enjoy festive markets in Town Hall Square.
