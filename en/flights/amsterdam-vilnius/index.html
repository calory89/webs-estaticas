Best time to visit

Vilna shines in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October): pleasant temperatures and less rain. Winter is cold but charming if you enjoy snow and festive lights. Fly midweek when possible to avoid crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Vilna Airport (VNO) is 4 km from the centre. The Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage site: cobbled streets, stunning churches and cosy cafés. Climb Gediminas Tower for panoramic views, wander the Cathedral Square and taste Lithuanian cuisine. Vilna is small but soaked in history.
