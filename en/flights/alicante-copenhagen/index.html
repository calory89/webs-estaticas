Best time to visit

Copenhagen appeals year-round. Winter (December–February) brings cold and short days, but fewer tourists. Spring and autumn offer mild temperatures and clearer skies. Summer is peak season and prices rise. Travel in low or shoulder season to save on accommodation.

What to do in Copenhagen

Nyhavn's coloured townhouses, Amalienborg Palace, Tivoli (the world's oldest amusement park) and the Little Mermaid are must-sees. Don't skip art museums, bike rides—the city's main transport—and Christiania's cafés. Eat smørrebrød (open sandwiches) and try local beer.
