Best time to visit

Thailand welcomes visitors year-round, but November and December (like the example date of 15 November) offer dry weather, less rain and pleasant temperatures. These months are the busiest, so prices tend to sit in the mid-to-upper range. Summer months (June–September) see more rainfall but fewer tourists.

What to do in Phuket

Phuket is your gateway to Thailand's beaches. Visit the famous Patong, Kata and Karon beaches, explore Buddhist temples like Wat Chalong, catch the sunset at Promthep Cape, and take a boat trip to Phi Phi or Phang Nga islands. Thai cuisine is outstanding—eat at local markets and beachside stalls. Phuket Airport is about 30 km from town; grab a taxi, bus or ride-share app to reach your hotel.
