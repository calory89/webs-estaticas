Airline and stopovers

Best time to visit

Sri Lanka is tropical with two monsoon seasons. The west coast (Colombo, beaches) is best December to March with less rain. The east coast suits May to September. December is peak season but weather is excellent on the west side. Choose your timing based on which region you want to explore.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB), about 30 km from Colombo. From there explore Colombo's old town, Negombo beaches, Galle Fort on the coast, or head inland for Buddhist temples, tea plantations and wildlife reserves. The country is compact and easy to navigate.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Brussels to Sri Lanka?

No. Etihad Airways operates the route with 1 stopover, typically in Abu Dhabi. Nonstop flights from Brussels to Colombo aren't available at the prices we show.

Which airline flies Brussels to Colombo at the cheapest price?

Etihad Airways, from €641 return. That's the carrier in this search and offers good value for long-haul travel.

How many hours does the flight take with a stopover?

Around 15–18 hours total, including the stopover. Exact time depends on how long you spend on the ground in Abu Dhabi.

What is there to see in Sri Lanka?

Colombo's old quarter and seafront, Negombo and south-coast beaches (Galle, Mirissa), the Sigiriya Rock Temple, tea estates in Nuwara Eliya, and wildlife at Yala National Park. You can fit the whole country into 1–2 weeks easily.
