Best time to visit Egypt

November to February is ideal: mild weather (20–25°C), no summer extremes. This explains why prices rise then (see the €365 sample). To save money, travel March–April or September–October, though it's warmer. July–August is doable but scorching. Cairo runs year-round, so it depends on your heat tolerance.

What to do in Cairo

The Giza Pyramids, the Sphinx, and the Egyptian Museum are must-sees. The Old City, Khan El-Khalili bazaar, and Citadel of Saladin deserve a full day. A Nile cruise at sunset is worthwhile. Eat in the Coptic Quarter and sample local food. Cairo International Airport (CAI) is 25 km from the city centre; negotiated taxis or transfer services are common options.
