Best time to visit

Tallinn shines in summer (June to August) with long days and mild weather, perfect for walking the Old Town. Winter (December to February) brings snowy landscapes and a magical Christmas atmosphere. Spring and autumn are quieter and less crowded.

What to do when you arrive in Tallinn

Explore the UNESCO Old Town with its medieval walls and cobbled streets. Visit Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, climb up to Toompea for views, and stroll the harbour. Local cafés let you try Estonian food at reasonable prices.
