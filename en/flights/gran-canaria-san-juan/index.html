Best time to visit

Puerto Rico has great weather year-round and is a beach destination. The dry season runs December to April with less rainfall. July and August are hot and humid. If you're after lower prices and don't mind heat, summer is cheaper; winter is peak season with higher fares.

What to do when you arrive in San Juan

San Juan is the capital and your entry point. Old San Juan awaits with cobbled streets, Creole food and beaches. From here, rent a car to explore El Yunque rainforest, Culebra or Vieques beaches. The island pulses with Caribbean rhythm, live music and excellent cuisine.
