Best time to travel

San Juan has tropical weather year-round. February offers pleasant temperatures (24–28°C) and less rain than summer months. It's shoulder season: fewer cruise ship tourists than December–January, but excellent weather. Avoid September to November due to higher hurricane activity.

What to do when you arrive in San Juan

You land at Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport. San Juan offers beaches (Condado, Ocean Park), Old San Juan with colonial architecture, restaurants and nightlife. Nearby coral reefs are ideal for diving. It's a gateway to explore the rest of Puerto Rico.
