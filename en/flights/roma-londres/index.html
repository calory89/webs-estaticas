No seasonal restrictions on this route. Your choice depends on preferences: winter can offer cheaper fares outside holiday peaks (Christmas, New Year), while spring and autumn are pleasant. London works well year-round. If possible, avoid bank holidays or long weekends: prices rise.

What to do in London

With a direct connection from Rome, you save travel time. London has free museums (British Museum, National Gallery), the Thames, markets like Camden Market, and historic neighbourhoods. The arrival airport has good connections to the city centre. Plan ahead: London is expensive, but plenty of activities are free or low-cost.
