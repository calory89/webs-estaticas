Best time to visit

Madeira enjoys pleasant weather year-round, but the best period is April to October, with less rain and more sunshine. December to March are cooler and wetter months, though the island remains green and attractive. If you want to escape winter in Spain, any month works, but July and August are pricier.

What to do on arrival

Madeira Airport (Cristiano Ronaldo) is on the east side of the island. From there, hire a car, take a bus, or grab a taxi. Don't miss Funchal's Old Town, tropical gardens, levadas (ancient irrigation channels with hiking trails), and views from Pico do Arieiro. If you enjoy hiking or nature, Madeira is a 10/10 destination.
