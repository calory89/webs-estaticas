Vienna and Zurich are just over an hour's flight apart, and the good news is the prices are very affordable: from €207 return. Non-stop flights with Austrian Airlines, no stopovers or hassle. At this price, booking in advance is your best move.

Price and when to book

The base price is €207 return. October data shows consistency in this range. Although this is a short route, as with any European flight, booking earlier is better: typically 4–6 weeks before travel gives you more room to find a good fare. Also check mid-week flights; they're usually cheaper than Friday and weekends.

Airline and stopovers

Austrian Airlines operates this route with non-stop flights (no stopovers). A Vienna–Zurich direct takes just 1 hour, saving you time and complications. Austrian is the national airline of Austria, with a solid reputation on short-haul European routes.

Best time to travel

Zurich is a city destination year-round. In winter (November–March) you'll find Christmas markets and snow in nearby mountains; spring and autumn are ideal if you prefer walks without excessive rain. Summer (June–August) attracts more tourists, so prices may be slightly higher. Avoid booking just weeks before if you want the best fare.

What to do when you arrive

Zurich is compact and perfect for 2–3 days. The Old Town (Altstadt), the lake, and the Limmat River are must-sees. Museums (Kunsthaus, Transport Museum) are excellent. If you have extra days, Swiss public transport connects easily to Interlaken, Lucerne, or Appenzell. Zurich Airport is 15 km away; bus, train, or taxi reach the city centre in 30 minutes.
