Airline and flight type

Ryanair operates this route as a direct flight with no stops. You'll fly Milan to Fez non-stop, saving time and hassle. Keep in mind Ryanair charges separately for cabin baggage and checked luggage, so factor that into your total budget.

Best time to visit

Fez is rewarding year-round. October, November, March and April offer pleasant temperatures and fewer crowds than summer. Winter (December–February) is cooler but cheaper. July and August bring intense heat; skip them unless you thrive in it.

What to do when you arrive

Fez's Medina is a UNESCO World Heritage site: winding alleys, bustling souks, mosques and traditional workshops. Don't miss the Medersa Bou Inania, the Jewish Quarter and the Tanners' Souk. For a change of pace, the Rif Mountains are close by with stunning scenery. Three to four days lets you soak in the atmosphere properly.
