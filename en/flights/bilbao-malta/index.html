Best time to travel

Malta enjoys good weather almost year-round. April to June and September to October are ideal: pleasant temperatures without summer extremes. Travel during these periods for better value. Winter (December–February) is milder than northern Spain, perfect if you're fleeing the cold.

What to do when you arrive

Malta is small but intense. Explore Valletta, Europe's smallest capital, stroll through Mdina, visit the Ħal Saflieni temple, or relax on Comino and Gozo beaches. Malta International Airport (MLA) is 5 km from Valletta, well connected by bus and taxi. Prices are low, so you can spend more on experiences.
