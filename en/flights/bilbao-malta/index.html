Best time to visit

Malta has a Mediterranean climate: mild in winter (12–17°C) and very hot in summer (28–35°C). January is a good month to avoid crowds and enjoy pleasant temperatures. If you want beach and sunshine, April to June or September to October are ideal. August is peak season and prices rise. Book early: the cheapest low-cost tickets sell out fast.

What to do in Malta

Malta is small but packed: three islands (Malta, Gozo, Comino) filled with history, beaches and coves galore. Valletta has 16th-century fortifications. The Dingli Cliffs are spectacular. Popeye Village in Gozo is a classic. Crystal-clear waters are perfect for diving or snorkelling even in winter. Rent a car or use the local bus: everything is close by.
