Best time to visit

Peru is a year-round destination. The dry season in the highlands (May to October) offers ideal conditions for mountaineering and trekking. For the Amazon rainforest, any season works, though October to March is warmer. Book 2–3 months ahead to secure availability at the best prices.

What to do in Lima and beyond

Lima is your entry point. Explore the historic centre, visit museums and enjoy local cuisine. From here, access Machu Picchu (domestic flight recommended), the Sacred Valley, Nazca and the Amazon. A classic 7–10-day itinerary: 2–3 days in Lima, 3–4 in the Sacred Valley/Cusco, 2–3 exploring the rainforest or Nazca lines.
