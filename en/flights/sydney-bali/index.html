Price and when to book

The cheapest flight is €392, but most routes on the dates checked hover around €427. We found samples ranging from €392 to €569. The earlier you book, the better: fares typically rise closer to departure. Search 4 to 8 weeks ahead to maximise your savings.

Airline and stopovers

Jetstar Airways operates this route non-stop: you'll reach Bali without plane changes or endless waiting. It's the best option to save time and money. The sample flight departs Sydney on 10 November at 17:10 and arrives on 18 November at 21:25.

Best time to travel

Bali is a year-round destination, but the dry season (April to October) offers better weather and less rain. The monsoon season (November to March) is cheaper with fewer tourists, though expect more rainfall. Avoid school holidays if you're chasing lower prices.

What to do on arrival in Bali

Explore Ubud's temples, discover the beaches of Seminyak and Canggu, or climb Mount Agung. The terraced rice fields are essential. The airport (DPS) is about 30 km from main beaches. Rent a scooter or use an online taxi app to get around without surprises.

FAQ

Are there direct flights from Sydney to Bali?

Yes, Jetstar Airways flies non-stop between Sydney and Bali. It's the quickest and most efficient flight for this route, taking approximately 4 hours.

What is the cheapest price you found?

The lowest recorded return flight is €392. The median sits at €427, so you have good chances of finding deals under €430 if you book in advance.

Which airline operates this route?

What to see in Bali besides beaches?

Don't miss Tanah Lot temple, the terraced rice fields of Tegallalang, Ubud as the cultural hub, and Mount Agung if you fancy a trek. There's something for every budget.
