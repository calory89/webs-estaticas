December is workable but daylight is limited in Oregon. June to September offer long days and dry weather, ideal for exploring Portland. What to do in Portland

Portland International Airport (PDX) lies 10 km from the city centre. The city is famed for its coffee culture and food scene, parks (Forest Park, Laurelhurst), independent shops in Pearl District and vibrant arts. The Oregon coast, Mount Hood and Willamette Valley wineries are close by. Car hire is practical for exploring the state.
