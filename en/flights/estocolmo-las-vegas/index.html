Best time to visit

Las Vegas works well year-round, but note that summer (June to August) is very hot. Autumn and winter offer comfortable temperatures and less mass tourism. December is festive and prices can spike, so consider November or February to avoid peak rates.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Harry Reid International Airport (LAS), about 6 km from the centre. The Strip is Vegas's heart with casinos, hotels and shows. Beyond that, Fremont Street Experience offers retro vibes. Red Rock Canyon is 30 minutes away if you want nature. Many hotels have free airport shuttles.
