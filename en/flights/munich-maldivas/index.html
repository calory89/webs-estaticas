Best time to visit

Maldives has dry season from November to April, with clear skies and minimal rain risk. These months, especially December to March, are priciest. Late February to March and early November can offer good weather with slightly lower fares. Avoid May to September: monsoon season brings heavier rainfall.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in Malé (MLE), Maldives' capital. From the airport, take a ferry, seaplane or speedboat to your atoll resort. Luxury resorts are famous, but budget-friendly options exist too. Snorkelling, diving, white-sand beaches and unforgettable sunsets await. Island prices can be steep: eat at your resort or explore local options in Malé before departing.
