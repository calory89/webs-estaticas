Best time to visit

Tokyo shines in spring (late March to April, cherry blossoms) and autumn (October to November, mild weather). Winter is dry and less crowded. Avoid the monsoon season (June–July). Peak seasons mean higher fares, so plan your dates accordingly.

What to do in Tokyo

Tokyo has it all: temples like Senso-ji, modern districts like Shibuya, world-class food, museums and parks. Haneda Airport is well connected to the city centre by train. Spend at least 4–5 days exploring. Don't miss nearby spots: Nikko, Kamakura or Mount Fuji are a couple of hours away.
