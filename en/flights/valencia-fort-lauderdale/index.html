Check the layover duration when selecting your fare, some have 3-4 hours, others longer.

Best time to travel

Fort Lauderdale has tropical weather: mild winters (December to February, 24-26°C) and hot, humid summers (June to September with rain). Peak season is December-February (pricier, busier) and low season September-October. Avoid hurricane season (June to October). For the best price-to-climate balance, aim for March-May or November.

What to do when you arrive

Fort Lauderdale is your gateway to Florida's coast. Las Olas Boulevard offers shopping, restaurants and waterfront walks; the port has yachts and cruises; Miami is just 30 km south. Also nearby: the Florida Keys, water parks and shopping centres. The airport (FLL) is 30 km from downtown, grab a taxi, Uber or rent a car to get around.
