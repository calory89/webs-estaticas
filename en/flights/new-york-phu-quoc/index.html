Air Premia connects New York with Phu Quoc from €905 return. The median price sits around €952, so booking early is key to grab the best fares. Examples checked on 23 October for January show there's room to find deals with flexible dates.

Price and when to book

Prices range between €905 and €974 for the round trip. Book as early as possible: this route is infrequent and seats are limited. Avoid the final weeks before departure. January is high season in Phu Quoc, so factor this into your budget if you're travelling then.

Airline and stopovers

You'll fly with Air Premia, operating this route with 2 stopovers on the outbound leg. It's not a direct flight, but that's standard for such a long connection. Stopovers can add 8–12 hours to your total journey time, depending on layover duration. Keep travel documents in order and check transit requirements for stopover countries.

Best time to visit

Phu Quoc has a tropical climate. The dry, cooler season runs November to February, ideal for beaches and diving. March to May is intensely hot. June to October is rainy season, but prices drop. Want good weather and don't mind paying more? Aim for December–January. Prefer savings? Try May or September with fewer tourists.

What to do when you arrive

Phu Quoc is famous for pristine beaches, especially Sao and Kem. Visit Phu Quoc National Park for hiking and wildlife spotting. Pearl farms are a unique attraction. Try local cuisine at Duong Dong markets. Don't miss sunsets at the harbour. The airport (PQC) is about 30 km from town; take a taxi, private transfer, or local bus.
