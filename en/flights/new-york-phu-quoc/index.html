Airline and stopovers

Best time to visit

Phu Quoc has a tropical climate. The dry, cooler season runs November to February, ideal for beaches and diving. March to May is intensely hot. June to October is rainy season, but prices drop. Want good weather and don't mind paying more? Aim for December–January. Prefer savings? Try May or September with fewer tourists.

What to do when you arrive

Phu Quoc is famous for pristine beaches, especially Sao and Kem. Visit Phu Quoc National Park for hiking and wildlife spotting. Pearl farms are a unique attraction. Try local cuisine at Duong Dong markets. Don't miss sunsets at the harbour. The airport (PQC) is about 30 km from town; take a taxi, private transfer, or local bus.
