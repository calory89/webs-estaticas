Best time to visit Santorini

Santorini shines in May–June and September–October: comfortable temperatures, fewer crowds than July–August, and clear skies. If you fly in low season (November to March), prices drop but daylight is shorter and some businesses close. Summer is hot and packed with tourists.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Santorini National Airport (JTR). From there, rent a car, catch a bus or book a transfer. Don't miss Oia at sunset, Akrotiri with its Minoan ruins, volcanic sand beaches and a boat trip around nearby islands. Eat on the terraces in Fira overlooking the caldera.
