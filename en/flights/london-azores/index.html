Fly from London to the Azores for just €216 return. The median price sits around €234, so if you spot fares in this range, it's time to book. Getting to this Portuguese archipelago is more affordable than you'd think.

Price and when to book

Fares start from €216 for return flights. The median price is €234, with sample fares reaching €258. Book early, Ryanair typically increases prices as your travel date approaches. Check regularly and be flexible with dates if possible to secure the best deal.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route. Your outbound flight includes 1 stopover, so it's not direct, but the price makes up for it. Remember that Ryanair charges for hand luggage and checked baggage, so add those costs to your budget if needed.

Best time to visit

The Azores enjoy mild weather year-round. Summer (June to August) is peak season with higher prices and more tourists. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer good weather and fewer crowds. Winter is wetter but ideal if you want peace and quiet, plus cheaper fares.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Ponta Delgada airport (PDL), the gateway to the Azores. From here, explore São Miguel, the largest island, with volcanic calderas, crater lakes and charming villages. Hiking, whale watching, pineapple plantation tours and thermal spring baths are the highlights. Rent a car for freedom or use local buses to get around.

No, the outbound flight includes 1 stopover. Although not direct, the final price is very competitive, making it worth it for the saving.

What can I see in the Azores?

Hiking in volcanic calderas, freshwater crater lakes, whale watching, picturesque coastal villages and stunning green landscapes. It's a perfect destination for nature lovers and getting away from it all.
