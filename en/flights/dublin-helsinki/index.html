Best time to visit

Helsinki shines in summer (June to August) with white nights and mild weather. Winter (December to February) offers northern lights if you're lucky. Autumn (September-October) and spring (April-May) are quieter and often cheaper. Check prices across different dates, demand significantly affects costs.

What to do on arrival

Land at Helsinki-Vantaa. Finland's capital blends modern Nordic architecture with tradition. Visit the White Cathedral, Kiasma museum, archipelago islands, and Kauppatori market. Turku and Tampere are within reach. In winter, book trips to Finnish Laponia for the northern lights.
