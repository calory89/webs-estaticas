Price and when to book

Prices range from €47 to €76 depending on your travel dates. To secure the lowest fares, book 4–6 weeks in advance: Ryanair typically offers its best prices well ahead of departure. The closer you get to your travel date, the higher prices climb. Check regularly: last-minute deals do pop up occasionally, but the general trend is upward as departure approaches.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair is your carrier on this route and operates direct flights Gran Canaria to Marrakech. No stopovers or connections: board at LPA, land at RAK. Direct flights save you time and hassle, though remember Ryanair charges for oversized cabin bags and checked luggage. Review their baggage fees before you buy your ticket.

Best time to visit

Marrakech is pleasant year-round, but October, November, March and April are ideal: mild temperatures (20–25°C) and far less heat than summer. June through September brings intense heat that can be exhausting. January and February are cool but offer clear, sunny days. Time your trip around Ramadan if you prefer standard restaurant and shop hours, though it's also a unique cultural experience.

What to do when you arrive

Marrakech is compact and the airport sits 6 km from the medina. Explore Jemaa el-Fnaa square, visit the Bahia Palace, Majorelle Gardens or the traditional souk (market). The city is perfect for 2–3 days. Arrange excursions to the Atlas Mountains or Sahara Desert from the city itself, though these tours need at least an extra day.
