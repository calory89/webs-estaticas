Best time to visit

Marrakech is a year-round sunshine destination, but November to March offer pleasant temperatures without the scorching heat of summer. October to April is ideal: fewer crowds than peak summer, balanced prices and perfect weather for exploring the medina and desert. Skip July and August if you can't handle extreme heat.

What to do when you arrive

Marrakech's medina is a must: winding alleys, souks, Jemaa el-Fnaa square with acrobats and orange juice vendors. Visit the Royal Palace, Majorelle Gardens and the Koutoubia. Want to escape? The Sahara is a few hours away on an organised tour. Marrakech Menara airport is 6 km from the city, easy access by taxi or transfer.
