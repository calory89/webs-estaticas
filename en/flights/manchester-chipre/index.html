Best time to visit Cyprus

Cyprus shines from October to April: mild weather, calm beaches and lower prices than summer. November is ideal if you want to escape extreme heat but still enjoy the sea. Peak season (June-August) is pricier and hotter. Spring (March-May) is also excellent with fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land in Larnaca, Cyprus's gateway. From here: relax on sandy beaches at Nissi Beach (Ayia Napa), explore Kourion's ruins, stroll medieval Paphos or visit mountain villages in the Troodos. Nicosia's old town and Kykkos monastery are worth a stop. Eat meze, try halloumi and enjoy the sun year-round.
