Best time to visit

Hong Kong has a subtropical climate: mild winters (October to March) and very hot, humid summers. October to November and February to March are ideal: pleasant temperatures and minimal rainfall. If you travel in summer, expect heat and monsoon rains. Check local festival calendars to avoid peak season crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Hong Kong International Airport (HKG) has excellent public transport connections to the city centre. Don't miss Victoria Harbour, traditional markets like Mong Kok, Victoria Peak for panoramic views, and temples such as Man Mo. If you have spare days, nearby islands like Lantau are perfect for a break.
