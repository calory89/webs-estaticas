Best time to visit

Manila has a tropical climate. The dry season runs from November to May, with December to February being the best months: less rain and pleasant temperatures. These months see higher tourist numbers, so expect higher prices. For quieter travel and cheaper fares, consider June to October (monsoon season), though rain is more frequent.

What to do when you arrive

Ninoy Aquino International Airport (MNL) is 7 km from central Manila. The city has museums, nearby Boracay beach, and historic Intramuros. Siargao Island attracts many travellers. Eat street food at local markets and try adobo. Good euro exchange rates for European travellers.
