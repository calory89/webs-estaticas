Best time to visit

November and December offer good prices. Copenhagen shines in winter with Christmas markets and Tivoli lit up for the season. Spring and autumn have milder weather but slightly higher fares.

What to do in Copenhagen

Don't miss Nyhavn with its coloured townhouses, Rosenborg Castle and Tivoli Gardens. Explore Christiania district, try smørrebrød (traditional rye bread sandwich) and relax in design cafés. It's a bike-friendly city perfect for getting around.
