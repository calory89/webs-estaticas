Best time to visit

Zanzibar is a tropical archipelago with warm weather year-round. The dry season (June to October) is most popular, with less rain and pleasant temperatures. The rainy season runs March to May, with potentially lower prices but more rainfall. Bear in mind January is peak season with higher fares.

What to do when you arrive

Zanzibar is an island paradise off Tanzania's coast featuring stunning beaches, the historic Stone Town medina (UNESCO World Heritage), spice markets and coral reefs. Perfect for diving, snorkelling, beach relaxation or cultural exploration. Abeid Amani Karume International Airport connects well to the island.
