Airline and flight type

Ryanair is your option on this route. The flight is direct with no stopovers. Night departure from Paris (around 22:50) and arrival in Milan just after midnight (approximately 21:10 on return). Check Ryanair's baggage policies before you buy, as hold luggage may incur extra fees.

Best time to travel

Milan is worth visiting year-round. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter months are quieter and may offer lower prices, though it's colder. Avoid local holidays if you want better deals.

What to do in Milan

The Duomo is a must, as are La Scala and Sforza Castle. Stroll through the Victor Emmanuel II Gallery for shopping and food. Don't miss Navigli District with its canals. Milan is a fashion and design hub, perfect for shopping. Milan airport has fast rail and bus connections to the city centre.
