Best time to travel

Tokyo is appealing year-round, but spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) offer the most pleasant weather with moderate temperatures. Summer is humid and pricey; winter is dry and cold. For lower fares, consider travelling in shoulder season (January–February or June–August).

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Tokyo's international airport (Narita or Haneda, depending on your stopover). From the city centre, explore Shibuya, Shinjuku, historic temples in Asakusa, and museums. Tokyo blends tradition and modernity: ancient shrines next to neon skyscrapers. Public transport is excellent and very affordable. If you venture beyond Tokyo, grab a JR Pass for trips to Kyoto and Osaka.
