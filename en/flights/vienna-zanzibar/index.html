Best time to travel

Zanzibar has a tropical climate. The dry season is ideal, with fewer rains and more sunshine. Plan your trip during those months for guaranteed good weather. High season brings higher prices, so consider travelling in shoulder months to balance cost with climate.

What to do when you arrive

Zanzibar is known for white-sand beaches, coral reefs and the historic Stone Town. Abeid Amani Karume International Airport (ZNZ) connects well to the island. From there you can dive, snorkel, explore the spice forests and wander through Swahili architecture. Book local tours and enjoy the island's relaxed pace.
