Best time to visit

Jacksonville enjoys subtropical weather year-round. October to April is dry and pleasant; May to September is hot, humid, and prone to storms. Prices tend to drop outside European peak summer. Travel in autumn or spring for comfortable weather and reasonable fares.

What to do when you arrive

Jacksonville is your gateway to Florida beaches, a historic downtown with red-brick architecture, and the St. Johns River. From JAX airport, the city centre is about 30 minutes away.
