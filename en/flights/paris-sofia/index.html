Best time to visit

Sofia has a continental climate: cold winters and hot summers. Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to October) are ideal, with pleasant temperatures and fewer crowds. July and August can be very warm.

What to do when you arrive

Sofia is compact and walkable. Visit Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, the Basilica of Saint Sophia (the city's namesake) and the Soviet Army Monument. Eat at local markets, explore the Vitosha neighbourhood, and sit in the cafés around Vitosha Avenue.
