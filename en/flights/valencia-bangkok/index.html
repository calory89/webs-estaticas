Best time to travel

Bangkok is a year-round destination, but the dry season (November to February) offers more pleasant weather and less rain. Avoid the monsoon season (May to October) if you're after better conditions. These details help us know when is best to visit, not when flights are cheaper.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Suvarnabhumi (BKK), Bangkok's main airport. The city centre is about 30 km away. From the airport you have bus, train or taxi options. Once in the city, the Grand Palace, Buddhist temples, the Chao Phraya River and floating markets are must-sees. Thai cuisine is incredible and prices are very affordable.
