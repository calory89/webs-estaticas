Best time to visit

Budapest is beautiful in spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October), when the weather is mild and the city is less crowded than in summer. Winters are cold but perfect if you want to see the city under snow and enjoy the Danube with fewer tourists. Skip July and August if you want to avoid the masses.

What to see and do in Budapest

Don't miss the Parliament building—one of Europe's most stunning. The thermal baths (Széchenyi is the most famous) are a must for relaxation. Walk or take a boat along the Danube to see both banks, visit Fishermen's Bastion, and try Hungarian food at the Great Market. The city is compact and easy to get around using public transport.
