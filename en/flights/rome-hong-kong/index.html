What to do when you arrive in Hong Kong

Hong Kong is a metropolis of skyscrapers, traditional markets and nearby beaches. Take the Peak Tram to Victoria Peak for bay views, hop on Star Ferry, explore neighbourhoods like Mong Kok and Causeway Bay, and visit temples such as Thean Hou. The international airport (HKG) is well connected to the city centre by metro and bus, giving you quick access to everything.
