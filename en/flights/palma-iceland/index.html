Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €179 for a return trip. Prices range from €179 to €222 depending on dates and availability. As a golden rule, book as far in advance as you can: the earlier you purchase, the better your price. October is a good time to start hunting for deals for winter and spring travel.

Airline and stopovers

easyJet operates this route. Bear in mind that flights include 1 stopover, so it's not direct from Palma to Reykjavik. Total journey time will be longer than a direct flight, but the price makes up for it if you're budget-conscious.

Best time to travel

Iceland is spectacular year-round, but each season has its charm. Winter (November to February) brings long nights and chances to see the Northern Lights, though weather is harsh. Summer (June to August) guarantees 24-hour daylight and better conditions, but costs more. Spring and autumn are quieter sweet spots with fewer tourists.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Keflavík, about 50 km from Reykjavik. From there, you have buses, taxis, or car hire. The Golden Circle route (Þingvellir, Geysir, Gullfoss) is a must; the Blue Lagoon combines geothermal bathing with nature; the south coast impresses with black sand beaches. Winter trips suit aurora hunting; summer is best for hiking and glaciers.

FAQ

Is €179 the real return price?

Yes, €179 is the lowest price we've found for a round trip. The median sits at €199, so affordable options exist, though prices vary by exact travel date.

Is the flight direct from Palma to Iceland?

No, there is 1 stopover. easyJet operates this route with an intermediate stop, so total travel time is longer than a direct flight, but the final price is much more competitive.

Which airport do I use in Iceland?

You'll arrive at Keflavík (KEF), Iceland's main international airport, about 50 km from Reykjavik. From there you can catch a bus, taxi, or hire a car.

What's the best time of year to fly cheaper?

We can't confirm which months have lower prices, but booking early is key. March to May and September to October are often more affordable than summer (June-August) or Christmas, though it depends on availability.
