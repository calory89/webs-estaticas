Best time to visit

Zurich shines year-round. The Alps are stunning in winter for skiing and summer for hiking. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild weather and fewer crowds. December is popular but cold; book early if you're flying during holiday periods.

What to do when you arrive

Zurich is compact and easily reached from the airport. Explore the Altstadt (old town) with churches and shops, stroll the lakefront, and visit art and design museums. The Alps are hours away by train. Swiss cuisine and chocolate are musts. Public transport is excellent; a day pass pays off quickly.
