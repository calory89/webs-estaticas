Airline and stops

Pegasus is your carrier on this route, with one stop on the outbound leg. It's not direct, but the price makes up for it. Allow extra time in your itinerary for the connection.

Best time to visit

Dubai works year-round, but November to March is ideal: cooler weather (25–30°C) and pleasant conditions. June to September are very hot. Shoulder months like October and April offer good value and fewer crowds.

What to do when you arrive

Dubai has plenty: the Burj Khalifa, Jumeirah beaches, the historic old town (Al Fahidi), shopping at The Dubai Mall, and traditional souks. Nearby emirates like Abu Dhabi and Sharjah are easy trips. Dubai International Airport (DXB) is 30 km from the centre, well served by metro and taxis.
