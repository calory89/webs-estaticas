Fly from Palma to Mykonos with Ryanair from €234 return. The median price sits at €265. With two stops along the way, this is your most affordable gateway to the Cyclades.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €234 per person (return). The median stands at €265, with a third search tier at €297. As a general rule, book 4–6 weeks in advance to secure better fares. Prices shift with the season and Ryanair seat availability, so set up price alerts if your dates are flexible.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route with 2 stops. It's not a direct flight, but the price makes up for it if you're budget-conscious. The stops are part of the low-cost model: they keep fares down. Always check Ryanair's terms on hand luggage, checked bags and flight changes before you buy.

Best time to visit

Mykonos peaks in summer (June–September) when prices climb and the island fills with tourists. To fly cheaper, consider May, late September to early October, or even winter (November–March). These periods offer better value and pleasant weather. Skip European holidays and long weekends if you want a bargain.

What to do when you arrive

Mykonos is famous for beaches, nightlife and whitewashed villages. Walk through Chora's old town, visit the Paraportiani church, swim at Psarou or Kalafatis beach. Don't miss nearby Delos (UNESCO site) and Rheneia island. Eat fresh fish and Cycladic dishes at local tavernas. The airport (JMK) is 2 km from the port with good bus and taxi links.

No. This route operates with 2 stops on Ryanair. If you need a direct flight, you'd need to check other airlines or connections, though they'll likely cost more.

The €234 is Ryanair's base fare. A checked bag (up to 20 kg) is an add-on. Check Ryanair's website for current baggage costs before you book.

Which month is cheapest for Mykonos?

The lowest fares are typically outside peak season (May, October, November). Avoid June–August and European holidays to find better prices.
