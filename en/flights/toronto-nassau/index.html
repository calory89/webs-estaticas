Best time to visit

Nassau has tropical weather year-round. January is peak season and pricier, but if you spot deals like these, go for it. Avoid September and October due to hurricane season. The sweet spot for value and good weather is usually May–June or November.

What to do when you arrive

Nassau Airport (NAS) is 25 km from the city centre. Expect white-sand beaches, snorkelling at reefs, diving with sharks, the famous Blue Lagoon Island, or simply unwinding on Paradise Island. The city offers casinos, duty-free shops, and brilliant Caribbean food. Seven to ten days gives you plenty of time to make the most of it.
