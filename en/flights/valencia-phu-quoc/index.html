Best time to visit

Phu Quoc has a tropical climate. The best time is November to April, when rainfall is lower and temperatures are pleasant. These months often align with good offers like those you see here. If you travel in high season (December-January), prices may rise, so book further in advance.

What to do when you arrive

Phu Quoc is famous for white sandy beaches, crystal-clear waters and marine life. Visit Duong Dong, the main fishing port, or explore the night market. Don't miss nearby islands on diving and snorkelling tours, Phu Quoc National Park and cultured pearl farms. The airport (PQC) is well connected to the island centre.
