Phu Quoc is a tropical island gem with white-sand beaches, jungle, diving, and marine parks. Explore Long Beach, visit Phu Quoc National Park, see the pearl farms, or unwind at resorts. Phu Quoc International Airport is close to the main areas; grab a taxi or tour. Plan for a proper stay, this isn't a weekend trip.
