Price and when to book

Toronto-Cancún flights start at €264 per passenger return. We've seen prices up to €332 depending on dates. The median sits at €291. Book as early as possible; fares rise as departure approaches. Compare flexible dates to find the best price.

Airline and stopovers

Flair Airlines operates this route with direct flights, no stopovers. This means fewer hassles and more rest before reaching Cancún. Typical departure is at 09:40 with afternoon arrival.

Best time to visit

Cancún is a year-round beach destination. High season (November to April) offers dry weather and perfect beaches, but also more tourists. May to October is hurricane season, though with fewer crowds and lower fares. Choose based on your tolerance for heat and rain.

What to do when you arrive

Cancún airport is 25 km from the hotel zone. You can rent a car, take a shuttle, or taxi. The Riviera Maya features endless beaches, cenotes, Mayan ruins (Tulum, Chichén Itzá), and parks like Xcel Há. The hotel zone has nightlife and shopping. Coastal towns like Playa del Carmen offer a relaxed vibe.
