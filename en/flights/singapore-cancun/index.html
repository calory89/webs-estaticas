Best time to visit

Cancún is a Caribbean destination with good weather year-round, but hurricane season runs June through November. If you book in advance, you can avoid both crowds and peak prices. Your best bet is travelling outside school holidays and major festivities.

What to do in Cancún

Cancún offers turquoise beaches, water parks like Xcaret and Xel-Há, Mayan ruins in the Riviera Maya, and lively nightlife. The international airport is 25 km from the centre with good connections. Staying in the hotel zone is convenient for beaches and entertainment; downtown is cheaper and more authentic.
