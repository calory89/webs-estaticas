Best time to visit Paris

Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) are ideal: mild weather and fewer crowds than summer. Winter (December-February) attracts tourism for Christmas lights and decorations, but it's cold. July and August have higher prices due to peak season. Plan according to your preferences for weather and budget.

What to do when you arrive in Paris

Paris is Europe's cultural capital: Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, Notre-Dame Cathedral, Arc de Triomphe. Walk through the Latin Quarter, stroll along the Seine, explore Montmartre. Eat at local cafés, enjoy museum art and wander the streets. The arrival airport (CDG or Orly) has good public transport to the city centre. A city that won't leave you indifferent.
