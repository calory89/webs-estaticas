Best time to visit

Peru is a year-round destination, though the dry season (May to October) in the highlands offers better weather for trekking and sightseeing. In the Amazon, Peru's summer (December to March) brings more rain. Prices don't follow a predictable seasonal pattern, so focus on dates that suit your plans and book ahead.

What to do when you arrive in Lima

Lima is your gateway to Peru. Jorge Chávez airport is 16 km from the city centre. From there, explore the historic centre, Miraflores with Pacific views, or the art and anthropology museums. For Machu Picchu or the Sacred Valley, you'll need to connect to Cusco. Lima's food scene is world-class, try the local ceviche spots.
