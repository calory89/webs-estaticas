Best time to visit

Punta Cana has good weather most of the year, but October to November is ideal: fewer tourists, warm temperatures and less rain than summer. December to March is high season with more crowds and higher prices. Consider travelling between May and October for better fares.

What to do when you arrive

Punta Cana is picture-perfect beaches: Cabeza de Toro, Macao, Arena Gorda. Dive in cenotes, visit natural freshwater springs. The international airport is 30 km from resorts. Explore Santo Domingo or Saona island if you have extra days. The Caribbean awaits.
