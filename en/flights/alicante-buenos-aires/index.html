Best time to travel to Buenos Aires from Alicante

Buenos Aires offers pleasant weather in spring (September-November) and winter (June-August), with mild temperatures and less rain than summer. Southern spring is ideal: sunny days and cool nights perfect for exploring. Avoid January-February due to intense heat. Check in advance: prices vary significantly depending on peak tourist season.

What to do when you arrive in Buenos Aires

You'll land at Ministro Pistarini International Airport (Ezeiza), 35 km from the city centre. Explore La Boca with its famous Caminito, enjoy tango in San Telmo, walk down the world's widest avenue (9 de Julio), and relax in iconic cafés. Don't miss antique markets or local cuisine. Buenos Aires is the «Paris of South America»: each neighbourhood has its own character.
