Best time to visit

Egypt is most pleasant from November to February: moderate temperatures, no extreme summer heat. December is especially good if you want to avoid peak crowds at certain tourist sites. July and August are very hot; May and June too.

What to do when you arrive

Cairo is your gateway to Egypt. Within reach: the Giza Pyramids, the Egyptian Museum, and the Citadel of Saladin. Rent a car or book a tour to reach Saqqara and Memphis in just over an hour. The international airport is well connected to the city.
