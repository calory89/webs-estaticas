Best time to visit

Istanbul works year-round. Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds than summer. Prices are often sharper outside Spanish school holidays and bank holidays. Travel midweek when possible—weekend flights tend to cost more.

What to do when you arrive

You land in Istanbul. From Istanbul Airport (IST) to the centre: metro, bus or taxi available. Must-see: the Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia, the Grand Bazaar, a Bosphorus cruise, plus Galata and Balat neighbourhoods. Change money at the airport or withdraw lire from ATM. Buy an Istanbulkart (transport pass) if staying several days.
