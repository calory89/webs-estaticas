Best time to visit

Sal Island enjoys sunshine almost year-round. December to March is driest and coolest, ideal if you prefer less heat. Summer months are hotter and more humid, but also quieter for European tourists. Plan according to your climate preference and holiday availability; there's no 'bad' season to visit.

What to do when you arrive

Sal Island is small but packed: stunning beaches in Santa Maria, diving and snorkelling in crystal-clear water, and Sal Island Natural Park for hiking. Don't miss Pedra Lume village with its historic salt pans. Amílcar Cabral International Airport is 10 km from the capital. Rent a car or use local taxis to get around; the island is accessible and safe.
