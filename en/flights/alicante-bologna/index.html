Best time to visit

What to do in Bologna

Bologna is famous for its cuisine, medieval architecture and university atmosphere. Don't miss Piazza Maggiore, the Asinelli and Garisenda towers, the Basilica of San Petronio and historic arcades. The city is perfect for walking. Eat tortellini alla bolognese and taste local wines. The airport (BLQ) is about 6 km from the centre, with easy bus or train connections.
