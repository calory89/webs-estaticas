Best time to visit

November is a smart choice: San Francisco's autumn is pleasant, with mild temperatures (14–20°C) and less rain than winter. Summer crowds are gone, and fares are friendlier. Skip July and August if budget is tight. December climbs sharply for the holidays.

What to do in San Francisco

The Golden Gate, Alcatraz, and the iconic cable cars are must-dos. Walk the Embarcadero, climb to Twin Peaks for the views, and explore Mission District and Chinatown. If you have extra days, the Bay is stunning. The international airport (SFO) connects easily to downtown via BART (rapid transit).
