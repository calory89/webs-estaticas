Price and when to book

Flights start from €647 return, with the median around €685. Some go up to €926 depending on dates. Book early: the sooner you purchase, the better the price. Long-haul tickets climb as departure approaches, so don't delay.

Airline and stopovers

You'll travel with TAP Portugal, which offers solid value on long-haul routes. Expect 1 stopover on the way (likely in Lisbon, TAP's main hub). Not direct flights, but the prices make up for the extra time in the air.

Best time to visit

San Francisco is beautiful year-round, but spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October) offer the best weather and less rain. Summer is peak season and prices rise. Winter (December–February) is cooler but can offer good deals if you're flexible.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at SFO (San Francisco International Airport), 20 km from the centre. Golden Gate Bridge, Alcatraz, Fisherman's Wharf, Cable Cars, and Silicon Valley are must-sees. The city is pricey, but worth every euro. Bring a jumper, the bay wind is deceptive.
