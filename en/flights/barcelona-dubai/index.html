Price and when to book

Flights cost from €368 to €381 depending on dates. Book now to secure these prices: the median is €378 and cheapest seats sell fast. Travel in November and take advantage of autumn fares before Christmas prices spike.

Airline and stops

You'll fly with Pegasus. The outbound has one stop, so it's not direct: adds hours to your journey but cuts cost versus other options significantly. Return departs early morning (06:45), so plan your connection timing accordingly.

Best time to visit

November is ideal for Dubai: pleasant temperatures (25–30°C), no rain, no summer extreme heat. Avoid June to August (over 40°C). Low season (October to March) brings cheaper fares, so November gives you the sweet spot between price and weather.

What to do on arrival

Dubai offers beaches, shopping at Dubai Mall, Burj Khalifa, Dubai Museum and old town Al Fahidi walks. If you have long stopovers, rest up. The international airport is 20 km from centre with easy links into the city.
