Stockholm to Nairobi starts at €628 return with Ethiopian Airlines. The median price is €656. A one-stop route that takes you straight to the heart of Kenya.

Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €628. The median sits at €656 and higher prices reach around €733. Like any flight route, booking 4–6 weeks ahead usually gets you better fares. The sooner you lock in your ticket, the fewer nasty surprises at checkout.

Airline and stops

You fly with Ethiopian Airlines on a one-stop flight. It's not direct, but Ethiopian is a reliable airline on the Africa–Europe route. The example outbound departs 16 November at 21:20 and arrives 17 November. The return departs 23 November at 17:25. Total journey times vary depending on your connection.

Best time to go

Nairobi and Kenya work well most of the year. If you want less rain and active wildlife, the dry season is your friend: June to October is prime time. Prices tend to be higher then because demand peaks. November is a solid choice: the green season begins, prices drop, and visibility is still good.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO), just outside Nairobi. The capital is your launch pad for safaris in the Masai Mara, Amboseli or Tsavo. If you stay in the city, the National Museum of Kenya and Karen Blixen Museum are worth your time. Accommodation and activity prices are far cheaper than in Europe.
