Cheap flights London-Stockholm: from €51 return

Ryanair connects London and Stockholm with direct flights at very competitive prices. From €51 return depending on availability; the typical price sits around €72. Travel to Sweden's capital without stopovers and without breaking the bank.

Price and when to book your ticket

Prices range from €51 to €91 return depending on date and how far in advance you book. Ryanair usually offers the best fares with 4-6 weeks' notice; the earlier you book, the more cheap options you'll find. Avoid last-minute bookings unless you spot a flash sale. Check regularly because prices change daily.

Airline and no stopovers

Ryanair is the sole operator on this route and flies it non-stop: departing from London (LON) and arriving directly in Stockholm (STO). A direct flight saves you time and hassle; the journey gives you a chance to watch a film or rest without changing planes.

Best time to fly to Stockholm

Stockholm shines in summer (June-August) and winter if you're after the Northern Lights. Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October) are ideal: fewer crowds, pleasant temperatures, and moderate prices. Winter (November-February) is cheaper but very cold; still, if you fancy snow and Nordic lights, it's worth it.

What to do when you arrive in Stockholm

Explore the Old Town (Gamla Stan), stroll through Djurgården, visit the Vasa and Fotografiska museums, or relax in Södermalm's cafés. In winter, the city turns into a fairy tale; in summer, make the most of long daylight and archipelago cruises. Arlanda Airport is 20 km from the centre; trains, buses or taxis connect you easily to the city.

FAQ

What's the cheapest price for flights from London to Stockholm?

From €51 return depending on availability. That's a rare fare; you'll typically find tickets between €72 and €91. Booking in advance improves your chances.

Does Ryanair fly direct from London to Stockholm?

Yes, it's a direct flight with no stopovers. It departs from London (LON) and lands in Stockholm (STO) in a single leg.

When is the best time to visit Stockholm?

Summer (June-August) for long daylight and mild weather; winter (November-February) if you want to see the Northern Lights and snow. Spring and autumn offer a good balance of pleasant weather and fewer tourists.

What to see in Stockholm in 3-4 days?

Gamla Stan (Old Town), Djurgården (island with museums and nature), the Vasa Museum, the bohemian Södermalm district, and if possible an archipelago cruise. The city is compact and easy to explore on foot or by public transport.
