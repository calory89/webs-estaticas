Fly London to Krakow for just €46 return with Ryanair. The median price sits around €65, so there's room to find a bargain. A classic budget escape to Poland.

Price and when to book

Base price is €46 return per person. In our sample, fares ranged from €46 to €71. We checked on 22 October for November travel (outbound 10th, return 18th). Book early: Ryanair's low fares sell out fast. Don't leave it to the last minute if you want to keep costs down.

Airline and stops

Ryanair operates this route direct: no stops, point-to-point flight. That's a plus—you save time at airports and the journey is smoother. Ryanair is Europe's low-cost king, so these prices are typical for London-Krakow deals.

Best time to visit

November is solid: the price we saw was for those dates (10–18 Nov). Krakow shines in autumn and winter when masses of tourists leave. It's cold but the city is beautiful, and hotel and restaurant rates drop. Skip July–August if you want peace and quiet—and a better deal.

What to do when you arrive

You'll land at Krakow-Balice Airport (KRK), 15 km from the centre. The Main Market Square is essential: Unesco heritage, lined with galleries and cafés. Climb the Town Hall Tower for 360° views. Visit Wawel Cathedral, explore Kazimierz (the historic Jewish district) and eat pierniki (dumplings) everywhere. Three to four days gives you enough to see it all.

FAQ

Is €46 the final price or are there extras?

The €46 is per person return. Ryanair then adds airport taxes and may charge for cabin bags or checked luggage if not included. Check what baggage you're allowed before you buy.

Is this a direct flight London to Krakow?

Yes, it's direct. No stops in between. Ryanair flies LON to KRK non-stop.

When do prices drop most for this route?

Autumn and winter are cheaper than summer. But there's no fixed month that's always lowest. What works: book 6–8 weeks ahead and stay flexible with dates.

What can I see in Krakow over a weekend?

The Main Market Square, Wawel Cathedral, the Town Hall Tower, Kazimierz district (historic Jewish quarter), St Mary's Basilica. Three days covers the essentials with time left over for good food.
