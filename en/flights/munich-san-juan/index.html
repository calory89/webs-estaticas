Best time to visit Puerto Rico

Puerto Rico has good weather year-round, but hurricane season runs from June to November. December to April are the driest months with lower risk. If you're after cheaper fares, travel outside European school holidays (Christmas, Easter, summer). Avoid long weekends and public holidays, which drive up demand.

What to do in San Juan and nearby

San Juan is Puerto Rico's capital and the perfect entry point. Stroll through the historic Old San Juan, visit Fort San Felipe del Morro, enjoy nearby beaches like Luquillo or Icacos, or explore the Camuy Caves. Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport (SJU) is 10 km from the city. Rent a car to explore the island or use local taxis and transport services.
