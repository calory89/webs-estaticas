Best time to travel

Cairo is best visited between October and April, when temperatures are milder (25–30°C) and exploring the city and archaeological sites is more comfortable. May to September is very hot. Avoid Ramadan if you want museums and restaurants with normal opening hours. Book with some date flexibility to catch deals.

What to do when you arrive

Cairo International Airport is about 20 km from the city centre. You'll find a city with 5,000 years of history: the Pyramids of Giza, the Sphinx, the Egyptian Museum, and the Coptic Quarter with ancient churches are must-sees. Khan el-Khalili bazaar is perfect for souvenirs and street food. Travel by metro (cheap and fast) or book organised tours from your hotel.

What is there to see in Cairo?

The Pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx are the top attractions. Also visit the Egyptian Museum (home to Tutankhamun's treasures), the Citadel of Saladin, the Coptic Quarter with 4th-century churches, and Khan el-Khalili to walk around and eat. Everything is reachable in 2–3 days if you plan well.
