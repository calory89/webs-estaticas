Best time to go

Prague is beautiful year-round, but each season has its charm. Winter (like the January example) is quieter and cheaper, though cold. Spring and autumn offer pleasant temperatures. Summer is peak season: more tourists, more noise, higher prices. Travel off-season and your money goes further.

What to do when you arrive

You land at Václav Havel Airport (20 km from the city centre). The Old Town, Charles Bridge and Prague Castle are must-sees. Wander medieval streets, taste Czech beer (it's excellent) and eat trdelník (a pastry roll). Museums, galleries and nightlife cluster around Old Town Square. On a tight budget, most main attractions are cheap.
