Best time to visit

Vienna is beautiful year-round. Winter (December to February) features festive Christmas markets and is prime for palaces and concerts. Spring and autumn offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Summer can be hot and busy. Prices don't vary much on this route, so choose based on your preferred climate.

What to do when you arrive

Vienna airport connects well to the city centre by train and bus (20-30 minutes). Must-see: Schönbrunn Palace, St. Stephen's Cathedral, museums and Hofburg. Walk or cruise the Danube, enjoy Viennese coffee and pastries in historic cafés. Vienna is perfect for 2-3 days.
