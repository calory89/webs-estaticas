Price and when to book

The lowest price we've found is €136 return, with a median of €148. Sample prices range from €136 to €206. Book with the maximum advance (3–4 weeks minimum) to secure the lowest fares. Prices tend to climb as your travel date approaches, especially during peak season.

Airline and stopovers

Ryanair operates this route. The outbound flight includes 1 stopover, so it's not direct. Check your exact flight times in advance to plan your layover and ground time. Ryanair pioneered budget fares across Europe, so expect basic service with paid extras (checked baggage, seat selection, onboard food).

Best time to visit

Dubrovnik is a year-round destination, but spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer the best value and pleasant temperatures. Summer (June–August) sees peak tourism and higher prices. Winter has fewer flights and more unpredictable weather.

What to do on arrival

Dubrovnik Airport is about 20 km from the city. Use the bus, taxi, or shuttle services. Once in the centre, walk the City Walls (Unesco site), visit the Rector's Palace, St Blaise Church, and explore cafés on the Main Street. Don't miss a boat trip to the nearby Elafiti Islands. Bring euros or a card; Croatian kuna is becoming less common.

What's worth seeing in Dubrovnik?

Dubrovnik is renowned for its walled old town (Unesco site), the City Walls, the Rector's Palace, St Blaise Church, and Adriatic views. The Elafiti Islands are perfect for a day trip by boat.
