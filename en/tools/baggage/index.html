FAQ

Which low-cost airline has the biggest free bag?

easyJet: a free under-seat bag of 45×36×20 cm, up to 15 kg.

Which airlines include a cabin bag in the fare?

In our table: Eurowings, Air Europa, Air France. On low-cost airlines the cabin bag is paid separately.

What is the standard free bag size?

40×30×20 cm on 6 of the 11 airlines: Ryanair, Vueling, Wizz Air, Volotea, Transavia, Norwegian.
