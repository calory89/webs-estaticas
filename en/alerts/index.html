Let the deal find you.

Pick your airport, the place you dream of and the most you want to pay. When we hunt down a flight that fits, we tell you. No spam: one alert a day at most, and you leave with one click.

Is it free?

Yes. We send at most one email a day and you unsubscribe with one click.

Where do your deals leave from?

Most of them from Spain, and a growing number from the United States, Canada, Australia and the rest of Europe. The airports above are the ones in today's deals, so you can see what we actually cover before you leave your email.

What counts as a deal?

A return flight at least 40 % below the usual price for that route, with the price checked again before it is published and every day after that.

Can I get alerts without giving an email?

Yes. Use the button on this page to turn on notifications in this browser and the alert arrives as a push notification. You can turn it off from the browser settings.

Which destinations can I choose?

Any city, island or country, or simply "anywhere". If you leave it open, we send the best deal from your airport within your price.

What price limit should I pick?

The most you would pay for a return flight. The quick options go from €100 to €1,000; we only tell you about deals at or below your limit.

How do I stop the alerts?

Every email has an unsubscribe link: one click and you are out. Push alerts stop when you block notifications for happylowcost.com in your browser.
