About Happy Low Cost

Short answers to what people ask most about the site, who runs it and where the prices come from.

What is Happy Low Cost?

A flight deals website founded in 2012 by Carlos de Ory. We publish return flights that cost at least 40% less than usual, checked on Kiwi.com, plus destination travel guides.

Who is behind it?

Carlos de Ory, founder (carlosdeory.com). More than 420,000 travellers follow us on Facebook.

How does Happy Low Cost make money?

Through affiliate links: if you book through them we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We also publish sponsored articles, always labelled as such. Whether a deal pays a commission does not affect whether we publish it.

Do you sell flights?

No. We find and check the price; you book on the airline or agency website we link to.

Where do the route prices come from?

From our own searches on Kiwi.com: lowest return fare, usual fare (median) and cheapest month. Each page shows the date of the search. The data can be reused crediting Happy Low Cost (CC BY 4.0).

How long does a deal last?

By default, one week from publication. We re-check it every day and remove it if it rises more than 10% or disappears.

How often are prices and guides updated?

Deal and route pages are rebuilt every 4 hours with the latest prices checked, and every deal is re-checked daily. Each guide shows the date it was last reviewed and each price the date it was checked. If something is wrong, write to us from the contact page and we will fix it.

How do I hear about deals?

With a free email or push alert, choosing a destination or departure city.
