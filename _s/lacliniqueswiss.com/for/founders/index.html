When can I go to the island?

Once your membership is paid in full. With 12 monthly payments, that is in your second year. Your dietitian and coach start in 2027, before your first stay.

What do I pay when I come?

€150 housekeeping per stay of six nights. Flights and personal extras are yours. Additional treatments are available at member prices.

What if I can't come one year?

Nothing happens and nothing is charged. Your week for that year is released and you come back the next year.

Can I get my money back?

You can transfer your membership to another member from your country at the price you paid. The transfer happens when there is an interested buyer. You can also leave it to your heirs.

Is this an investment?

No. It is a membership for medical services and stays. It does not promise any return or profit.

Will there be other places?

Yes. Residences in Europe and Latin America are planned next. When each opens, members can spend nights there too, subject to availability and to the membership terms.

Where is the clinic, and why "Swiss"?

The island clinic is in Karimunjawa, Indonesia. Medical direction and protocols come from our team and company in Switzerland.

Do you guarantee health results?

No one honestly can. We guarantee a careful measurement every year, a physician who reads it, and a team that helps you act on it.

What happens after 30 years?

You can extend your membership for another period with a renewal fee, if you wish. Or pass it on to someone you choose. The renewal terms are set out in your membership terms.

What does the €150 per stay cover?

Cleaning, fresh linen and towels, and the turnover of your cabin. It is the same every year, and you only pay it on the years you come.

What is not included?

Flights, travel insurance, personal extras, and optional treatments beyond your annual programme, which members get at member prices.

Can I bring someone?

Yes. Your cabin is for up to 2 people, included in the €6,500. Your guest stays with you at no extra cost. The medical programme is for the member; your guest can add their own check-up at member prices.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes. Choose "A gift" in the form and tell us who it is for. Many people give it to their parents.

What if I move to another country?

Your membership stays yours. Transfers are made to members of the country where you joined, so tell us if you move and we will look at your case.

What if the opening is delayed?

Your membership terms set out the planned opening and what happens if it moves, before you sign. Nothing is charged before you sign.

How do I get there?

Fly to Semarang, transfer to Jepara and take the fast boat, about two hours. We arrange the transfers and meet you at the harbour.

Is it suitable if I have limited mobility?

Tell us before your stay. We plan the cabin, the transfers and the programme around you.

Can you handle diets and allergies?

Yes. Your dietitian knows your needs before you arrive, and the kitchen cooks for them.

Which languages does the team speak?

English for everyone. German, French, Spanish and Indonesian within the team.

What happens to my health data?

It is used only for your care and your plan. It is never sold, and it is shared only with your consent.

What if I fall ill on the island?

Our medical team is on site during your stay. For anything serious, we arrange transfer to a hospital on Java. We recommend travel insurance that covers medical evacuation.

Who is behind La Clinique Swiss?

La Clinique Swiss is a project of Equinox Investment Group, LLC, with medical direction from Switzerland.
