Contact usOur approach

Most medicine waits for something to go wrong. We prefer to look earlier.

Many of the changes that shape how we age can be measured years before they are felt. Our work is to measure them carefully, explain them clearly and follow them over time.

The annual longevity assessment

A structured yearly assessment, designed and supervised under Swiss medical standards and offered to individuals through our partner programmes.

  • Complete laboratory studies: heart, metabolism, hormones, inflammation, vitamins and organ function.
  • Body composition, grip strength and mobility.
  • Cardiorespiratory fitness and blood pressure.
  • Sleep and recovery.
  • A physician review of the results and a personal plan for the year.

Long-term longevity research

The most useful answers about healthy ageing come from time. We are building a long-term study that follows participants year after year. Participation is always voluntary, with informed consent, and data is analysed anonymised.

Evidence first

We measure what research links to a longer, healthier life, and we change the protocol as the science moves.

The least intervention that works

Habits before prescriptions. Nothing is added that does not need to be.

No miracle promises

No one can promise extra years. We promise careful measurement, honest explanation and follow-up.

Your data is yours

Results belong to the person measured. Research only ever uses anonymised data, with consent.
