Feature

Ownership Type

The VIP Co-Owner Membership is a collaborative ownership that grants each participant a 1/12 stake in a studio for 76 years, enabling multiple owners (maximum of 12 owners) to share the property.

The VIP Owner Membership is an individual ownership that grants exclusive control of a studio for the same duration.

Payment Flexibility

As a VIP Co-Owner, investors can enjoy flexibility in payment. You can choose between a full or installment payment:

1. Full Payment: Pay the entire amount at the time of reservation.2.Installment: Pay a down payment of €9,950, followed by ten monthly installments of €655 each. There are no extra costs associated with this option.
The VIP Owner investors have no option for the installment payment, and have to pay the entire amount at the time of reservation.

Affordable Price

As a VIP Co-Owner Membership, investors can enjoy a more affordable price for owning the studio, as the amount they have to pay will be shared equally with 11 other investors. Not only will you share the price of the studio, but you will also share all the maintenance fees, electricity, water, and internet consumption.

The VIP Owner Membership has to pay all costs and fees individually.

Studio Refund in 12 Years

If the estimated Return on Investment is not met in 12 years, VIP Co-Owner investors can have the option to refund their studio, however this option is not available for the VIP Owner.

Steady Earnings

Both ownership types offer the opportunity to generate passive income through studio rental. The Startup Island will take care of everything, starting from the rental process, studio maintenance, to marketing campaigns, to ensure the studio is rented consistently and provide annual income for investors.

Venture Shares

Both types of investors will be given shares if they participate in the Hackathon Program. Your investment in innovation is rewarded with a 70% shares in your own company, as well as up to 0.1% stake in each of the 50 startups created every month on the Hackathon Program. It’s a unique opportunity for substantial returns as you support groundbreaking ventures.

Investors can also sell their shares through The Startup Island with a 10% commission.

Studio Usage

Investors on VIP Co-Owner Membership can enjoy the studio for 1 month per year, rotating among owners.

Investors on VIP Owner Membership can enjoy exclusive year-round access to the studio.

Hackhathon Free Participation

VIP Co-Owner investors can enjoy complimentary participation in the hackathon program during their ownership month, while the VIP Owner investors can join complimentary participation in any month for 12 months in a year.

Est. Return of Investment

The estimated Return on Investment (ROI) ranges between 6% to 10% for VIP Shared Membership and (TBD%) for VIP Private Membership, annually for a defined period. Additionally, as the land and housing market values in Indonesia evolve, these ROI percentages are anticipated to increase over time. The annual percentage increase in property prices in Indonesia ranges from 10% to 15%, depends on some factors such as:
1. Location
2. Market inflation
3. Growing demands
4. Infrastructure development

What is the difference between VIP Co-Owner and VIP Owner?

VIP Owner membership provides you with sole ownership of a studio, giving you the freedom to use or rent it whenever you like. VIP Co-Owner membership involves shared ownership with 11 other investors, offering each investors access for one month per year to the studio.

How does VIP Co-Owner work?

Each owner has access to the studio for one month per year. A raffle system is used to determine which investor gets to stay in the studio during each month. This adds a fair and exciting element to the ownership experience.

Which membership option is better for me?

Choosing between VIP Co-Owner and VIP Owner membership depends on your preferences. VIP Co-Owner membership provides a more affordable entry and flexibility in the studio ownership compared to the VIP Owner membership.

Is there a payment option?

As an investor with VIP Co-Owner membership, you can choose between two methods of payment:

  1. Full Payment: Pay the entire amount at the beginning of ownership.
  2. Installment: Pay a down payment of €9,950, followed by ten monthly installments of €655 each. There are no extra costs associated with this option.

The VIP Owner do not have this option, and should pay the entire amount at the beginning of ownership.

Can I switch between ownership types?

VIP Co-Owner investors have the flexibility to switch to full ownership if space permits, while the VIP Owner investors can’t change their status and switch to time-sharing ownership.

Can I earn passive income with both ownership types?

Yes, both ownership types offer the potential for passive income, if investors rent their studios.

The Return on Investment (ROI) during the first 12 years of renting (12 months/year) ranges from 6% to 12%, providing a rewarding return on your investment. Furthermore, the promising aspect is the likelihood of this ROI increasing over time.

As the Indonesian real estate landscape evolves and the market matures, the potential for higher returns is accentuated by land revaluation and burgeoning market opportunities.

What if I do not rent my studio? Can I still get passive income?

TSI guarantee each investors to earn a steady income even if they are not renting their studio. TSI will distribute yearly income of all studios rented each month, equally to all investors.

How do I make a decision between the two ownership types?

Consider your lifestyle, preferences, and investment goals.

See our MEMBERSHIP PLAN  for comparison.

Reach out to our team if you have specific questions, and we’ll help you make an informed decision that suits your vision and aspirations. Email us to: info@thestartupisland.com

What is the expected rental income for the studios?

The projected monthly rental income is estimated to be between 800€ to 1000€ and is expected to grow over time as the market evolves and demand for the property increases.

The estimated rental income provided is on a gross basis. This means that it doesn’t include any associated expenses.

How are you going to rent the studio?

Our target rental audience encompasses two primary segments:

  • Entrepreneurs (Hackathon Program Participants):

    Individuals selected to participate in The Startup Challenge.

  • Tourists:

    Visitors seeking unique experiences and high-quality accommodations.

The studio will be marketed through various avenues:

  • Corporate Retreats:

    We will offer packages tailored to companies organizing retreats and immersive experiences.

  • Online Portals:

    Our listings will be featured on platforms like Booking, Hotels.com, Airbnb, Agoda, and more.

  • Curated Experiences:

    We will organize our own specialized experiences, ranging from marketing courses and yoga retreats to fitness sessions, cooking classes, and diving activities.

If I rent my studio, how about water, electricity and Internet consumption?

All expenses for water, electricity and internet is apart from the 65€/month maintenance fee, and will be divided equally to all investors. All bills will be sent to your email in every month.

Do I have to equip the studio to rent it?

The house come in fully equipped and furnished. It is ready for use or rent, all of which are included in the price.

What if I want to stay in my studio?

If the owner wishes to stay for less than a full month, TSI cannot guarantee that the remaining days will be rented out.

How do you send me the rental income from my studio?

Every year, TSI will provide the annual accounting book of rental incomes, signed by a notary, to all owners for complete transparency. The annual income will be distributed equally among all owners.
