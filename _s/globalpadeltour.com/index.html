FAQ

Straight answers.

How exactly do I earn?

You earn a commission on every player from your city who registers for a Global Padel Tour event. Each Head gets a personal code and link so every registration is tracked to the right city. The rate and how it is paid are written in your licence contract.

Do I have to live in the city?

No. You need to be able to bring players from it: club owners, coaches, organisers and well-connected players are the ideal Heads.

What does the €3,500 pay for?

Your exclusive city licence for the tour, your place in the final with nights at Eco Beach City, and the right to run official qualifiers. Licence money is used to build the courts and run the tour, starting at Eco Beach City.

What if my city is already taken?

Then pick a city nearby. There is room for many cities per country, and a big city can be split into areas if demand is there.

Is this a professional tour?

No. Global Padel Tour is an independent international amateur circuit for club players of all levels. It is not affiliated with the FIP, Premier Padel or any professional tour.

What happens after I apply?

We confirm your city is free, then you book a 15-minute call. If it is a fit, you get your licence contract and your city is yours once it is signed and paid.
