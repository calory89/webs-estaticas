How the RealHunt score works

A person looks at every home before it goes up. Homes that need major work, ruins, bare land and listings with one photo are turned down. That is why the grade starts at 70: a home on RealHunt has already passed.

The grade then compares the home with every other home on the site, from 70 to 98. The home pages, the country pages and the front page are all ordered by this grade, best first. Votes and saves only move the buyer-interest sub-score; nobody pays to rank higher.

The 11 sub-scores (0 to 100)

  • Price: How cheap it is next to every other home here and in its country
  • Price per m²: How little you pay per square metre
  • Space: Size of the house
  • Move-in condition: Renovated or ready to live in scores more; homes that need work score less
  • Extras: Garden, terrace, pool, sauna, fireplace, views, land and more
  • Sea & lakes: How close it is to the sea or a lake
  • Good weather: Good-weather days a year where the home is: a high of 18–33 °C, dry, light wind (climate normals)
  • Airport: Nearest airport with scheduled flights: 20 km or less scores 100, then it drops evenly to 0 at 200 km or more
  • Buying costs: Taxes and fees to buy in this country: lower scores more
  • Listing detail: Photos, size and rooms published, verified by us
  • Buyer interest: Views and saves from RealHunt buyers

What the score is not

It is not a survey or a valuation. Photos can flatter and listings go out of date. Before you buy anything, get an independent inspection and a local lawyer or notary. If a home is sold or wrong, use the report button on its page.
