How the RealHunt score works: method, checks and limits

The RealHunt score is a grade from 70 to 98 that every home on RealHunt carries. A person first decides whether a home is listed at all; the grade then averages up to 11 sub-scores from 0 to 100, all with the same weight, and compares the home with every other home on the site.

Version 2026-11-14 · figures from 726 homes on 27 November 2026 · Markdown version

Step 1. A person checks the home

A person looks at every home before it goes up. Homes that need major work, ruins, bare land and listings with one photo are turned down. That is why the grade starts at 70: a home on RealHunt has already passed.

Old is fine; neglected is not. RealHunt turns down about three listings for every one it publishes (why we say no to most cheap houses).

A sub-score is left out, not guessed, when the listing lacks what it needs: a home with no published size has no price per m² and no space sub-score, and a home with no sea or lake recorded nearby has no water sub-score. Today 214 of 726 homes have all 11.

Step 3. From sub-scores to the grade

The grade is the plain average of the sub-scores a home has, each with the same weight, placed on the 70 to 98 scale: the home with the lowest average on the site gets 70, the one with the highest gets 98, and the rest fall in between in proportion. So a grade compares a home with the other homes listed, and it can move when homes are added or removed. No sub-score counts double and none can be bought.

Home pages, country pages and the front page are ordered by this grade, best first. Votes and saves only move the buyer-interest sub-score; nobody pays to rank higher.

What the score is not: limits

  • It is not a survey or a valuation. It cannot see damp, structure, title problems or debts.
  • It rests on the listing. Photos can flatter and listings go out of date; a home can be sold or re-priced before the page changes.
  • It is relative. A grade compares a home with the other homes on RealHunt, so it can move when homes are added or removed.
  • It describes a chosen set. RealHunt lists cheap, move-in-ready homes picked by a person, not the whole market of a country.
  • Homes with fewer sub-scores are averaged over fewer facts, so their grade is less informative.
  • Before you buy anything, get an independent inspection and a local lawyer or notary.

Errors and corrections

If a home is sold or wrong, use the report button on its page. For anything about the method or a figure, write to carlos@equinoxinvestmentgroup.com. See the editorial policy.
