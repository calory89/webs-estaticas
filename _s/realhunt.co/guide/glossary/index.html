Glossary

Property glossary by country: the local words of buying a home abroad

Every country names the same steps differently: the paper that shows who owns the house, the contract before the contract, the tax on the purchase. These glossaries keep each word as it is written where you will hear it and explain it in plain English.

All 90 words, A to Z

acte de vente · France
The final deed, signed before the notary (notaire).
AFM · Greece
The Greek tax number.
Agentsiya po vpisvaniyata · Bulgaria
The Registry Agency, where the notary files the deed.
akiya · Japan
A vacant house.
APE · Italy
The energy performance certificate of the home.
arras · Spain
A private contract with a deposit that reserves the home before the deed is signed.
Auflassungsvormerkung · Germany
A notice entered in the land register that protects the buyer's claim until the transfer is registered.
bank- og advokatforbehold · Sweden and Denmark
Clauses that make a Danish purchase contract depend on the approval of your bank and your lawyer.
bedenktijd · Netherlands
Three days in which the buyer can cancel without a reason and without paying the seller anything.
BSN · Netherlands
The citizen service number used for tax and official business.
budgivning · Sweden and Denmark
The round of bids for a home in Sweden.
buyer's agent agreement · Mexico and the United States
The written agreement a buyer signs with an agent before being shown homes in the United States.
caderneta predial · Portugal
The tax office's record of the property, with its tax value and whether it is urban or rustic.
certidão permanente · Portugal
The land registry certificate of a property, kept online and always up to date.
chūkai tesūryō · Japan
The estate agent's fee, paid by the buyer and capped by rule.
closing · Mexico and the United States
The meeting at which the balance and the closing costs are paid and the sale completes.
codice fiscale · Italy
The personal tax code you need for any contract in Italy.
compromesso · Italy
The preliminary contract that binds both sides to sign the deed later.
compromis de vente · France
The preliminary contract in which seller and buyer both commit to the sale.
conditions suspensives · France
Conditions written into the contract that must be met for the sale to go ahead, such as getting the mortgage.
conformità urbanistica e catastale · Italy
Proof that the building as it stands matches its permits and its cadastral plans.
CPCV · Portugal
The promissory contract that fixes the price and the date of the deed.
danachna otsenka · Bulgaria
The tax assessment value of the property, on which the yearly municipal tax is charged.
danak pri pridobivane na imushtestva · Bulgaria
The municipal tax on acquiring property.
délai de rétractation · France
Ten calendar days in which a private buyer of a home can withdraw without giving a reason.
DPE · France
The energy performance survey of the home, one of the surveys the seller must provide.
E9 · Greece
The declaration in which an owner lists the properties held in Greece.
ejendomsværdiskat · Sweden and Denmark
The two yearly property taxes in Denmark: one on the value of the home and one on the land.
ejido · Mexico and the United States
Communal agricultural land, which cannot be sold in the normal way.
ENFIA · Greece
The unified yearly property tax.
escritura · Spain
The deed of sale, signed in front of a notary.
escritura · Portugal
The deed of sale, signed at a notary or at a Casa Pronta desk.
escritura pública · Mexico and the United States
The public deed, prepared and signed by a public notary.
escrow · Mexico and the United States
An account set up by the mortgage lender to pay property taxes and home insurance out of your monthly payment.
fideicomiso · Mexico and the United States
A bank trust in which a Mexican bank holds the title for a foreign buyer in the restricted zone.
FIRPTA · Mexico and the United States
The rule under which the buyer generally withholds 15% of the sale price when a foreign owner sells.
fóros metavívasis · Greece
The property transfer tax, paid before the final deed.
frais de notaire · France
Everything paid through the notary at the sale: mostly transfer taxes, plus the notary's own fee.
Grundbuch · Germany
The land register, where owners and charges are entered.
Grunderwerbsteuer · Germany
The transfer tax on buying property, set by each federal state.
Grundsteuer · Germany
The yearly municipal property tax.
HOA dues · Mexico and the United States
The fees paid to the owners' association of a condo building or a planned community.
home inspection · Mexico and the United States
An inspection of the condition of the house, often part of the buying process.
IBI · Spain
The yearly property tax charged by the town hall.
IMI · Portugal
The yearly municipal property tax.
imposta di registro · Italy
The tax on buying from a private seller.
IMT · Portugal
The transfer tax on the purchase, on a sliding scale.
IMU · Italy
The yearly municipal tax on second homes.
ISAI · Mexico and the United States
The tax on acquiring property in Mexico.
ITP · Spain
The tax the buyer pays on a resale home, set by each region.
jūyō jikō setsumei · Japan
The explanation of the property's legal status that a licensed specialist must give the buyer before the contract.
Kadaster · Netherlands
The national land registry.
kommunal fastighetsavgift · Sweden and Denmark
The yearly municipal property fee on houses in Sweden.
koopovereenkomst · Netherlands
The written purchase agreement; only this, once signed, binds a private buyer.
köpekontrakt · Sweden and Denmark
The purchase contract signed by buyer and seller in Sweden.
kosten koper · Netherlands
A price marked k.k. means the buyer pays the transfer tax and the notary's transfer deed on top.
kotei shisan zei · Japan
The yearly tax on land and buildings.
Ktimatológio · Greece
The Hellenic Cadastre, where deeds are registered.
lagfart · Sweden and Denmark
The registration of the buyer as owner at Lantmäteriet, the Swedish land registry.
NIE · Spain
The identity and tax number Spain gives to foreigners.
NIF · Portugal
The Portuguese tax number, needed before anything is signed.
nota simple · Spain
A short extract from the Land Registry that identifies the property, who holds rights over it and what charges it carries.
Notar · Germany
The public official who must record the purchase contract for it to be valid.
Notar- und Grundbuchkosten · Germany
The notary's and the land register's fees for the purchase.
notarialen akt · Bulgaria
The notarial deed that transfers the property.
nōzei kanrinin · Japan
A person with a Japanese address who receives and pays the tax bills of an owner who lives abroad.
overdrachtsbelasting · Netherlands
The transfer tax on buying property.
OZB · Netherlands
The yearly municipal property tax, charged on the official value of the home (the WOZ value).
pantbrev · Sweden and Denmark
A mortgage deed on the property, needed if you borrow against it.
paramethóries periochés · Greece
Designated border areas, where buyers from outside the EU and EFTA need a permit.
plusvalía municipal · Spain
A municipal tax on the increase in value of urban land, which comes due when the property changes hands.
predial · Mexico and the United States
The yearly local property tax in Mexico.
predvaritelen dogovor · Bulgaria
The preliminary contract signed before the notarial deed.
promesse de vente · France
A contract in which only the seller commits, giving the buyer an option to buy for a set time.
proposta d'acquisto · Italy
The written offer to buy, usually with a small deposit.
prosýmfono · Greece
The private preliminary agreement signed before the notary's deed.
rogito · Italy
The final deed of sale, signed before a notary (notaio).
samordningsnummer · Sweden and Denmark
The identity number Sweden gives to people who are not registered as living there.
shihō shoshi · Japan
The judicial scrivener, the professional who registers the transfer of ownership.
skøde · Sweden and Denmark
The Danish deed; its digital registration (tinglysning) with the Land Registration Court makes you the legal owner.
sommerhus · Sweden and Denmark
A Danish holiday home.
suelo rústico · Spain
Land classed as rural, where building is restricted.
taxe foncière · France
The yearly property tax paid by the owner, set by the local authorities.
tetsukekin · Japan
The deposit paid when the sale contract is signed.
tilladelse fra Civilstyrelsen · Sweden and Denmark
The permission that a buyer without Danish domicile, or without five years of past residence, needs to buy a home in Denmark.
title insurance · Mexico and the United States
Insurance against problems with the title, such as someone with a legal claim on the home.
visura catastale · Italy
The extract of the cadastre for a property: its identification, class and cadastral income.
VvE · Netherlands
The owners' association of an apartment building.
zemya · Bulgaria
Land, which a buyer from outside the EU cannot own in their own name.
zona restringida · Mexico and the United States
The strip within 50 km of the coast and 100 km of a border where foreigners cannot hold title directly.
