Buying a home in Bulgaria: the local words, explained

Bulgaria has some of the lowest yearly costs in our files and one rule that changes everything for buyers from outside the EU: buildings yes, land no. The words below are in Cyrillic with their reading.

Reviewed on 14 November 2026 · Rules and figures as of November 2026 · General information, not legal or tax advice

predvaritelen dogovor preliminary contract

Also: предварителен договор

The preliminary contract signed before the notarial deed.

You pay a deposit, usually 10%. If you pull out you lose it, and if the seller pulls out they owe you double.

Source: RealHunt country file: Bulgaria

notarialen akt notarial deed

Also: нотариален акт

The notarial deed that transfers the property.

It is signed before a notary in the district of the property, with a sworn translator if you do not speak Bulgarian. Payments of EUR 5,113 or more must go by bank transfer.

Source: RealHunt country file: Bulgaria

Agentsiya po vpisvaniyata Registry Agency

Also: Агенция по вписванията

The Registry Agency, where the notary files the deed.

Filing usually takes one to three business days. Before that, a lawyer checks the title and the debts, which usually takes five to ten business days.

Source: RealHunt country file: Bulgaria

zemya land

Also: земя

Land, which a buyer from outside the EU cannot own in their own name.

Non-EU citizens can buy apartments and buildings in their own name. To buy a house with its plot, they usually set up a Bulgarian company, which adds about EUR 500 to 1,500 a year in running costs.

Source: RealHunt country file: Bulgaria

danachna otsenka tax assessment value and the yearly tax on it

Also: данъчна оценка

The tax assessment value of the property, on which the yearly municipal tax is charged.

The municipal property tax is 0.01% to 0.45% of that value, with a 5% discount if paid in full by 30 April, plus a municipal waste collection fee. You must file a tax declaration with the municipality within two months of buying.

Source: RealHunt country file: Bulgaria

danak pri pridobivane na imushtestva municipal transfer tax

Also: данък при придобиване на имущества

The municipal tax on acquiring property.

Each municipality sets it between 0.1% and 3%; Sofia charges 2.5%. Notary fees of 0.1% to 1.5%, legal fees of 0.5% to 1% and an agent's 2.5% to 3% come on top.

Source: RealHunt country file: Bulgaria
