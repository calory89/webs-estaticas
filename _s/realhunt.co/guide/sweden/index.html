How to buy a home in Sweden as a foreigner

The steps, papers and taxes, in plain words. General information, not legal advice: use a local lawyer before you sign.

Can foreigners buy?

Yes. There are no citizenship or residence rules for buying a home in Sweden, but you need a Swedish personal identity number or coordination number (samordningsnummer) to register ownership.

The steps

  1. Get a coordination number: Book an identity check at a Swedish Tax Agency service centre to get a samordningsnummer, which you need to register as owner.
  2. Open a Swedish bank account: A Swedish account is normally needed to complete the payment.
  3. Find a home and arrange money: Listings are on Hemnet and Booli; foreign buyers may face a larger deposit if a bank lends at all.
  4. Inspect before you commit: Visit the property and consider hiring an inspector before signing.
  5. Sign the purchase contract: You and the seller sign the contract, usually with the seller's agent handling the paperwork.
  6. Apply for title at Lantmäteriet: Apply to Lantmäteriet for lagfart and pay the 1.5% stamp duty to be registered as owner.

Costs on top of the price

About 1.5-3.5%: Stamp duty on the title (lagfart) of 1.5% of the price or tax value, whichever is higher, plus a SEK 825 fee; if you borrow and need new mortgage deeds (pantbrev), 2% of their amount plus a SEK 375 fee; optional legal advice; the seller normally pays the agent, and a cooperative flat (bostadsrätt) has no lagfart.

Every year

House owners pay the municipal property fee and must file a Swedish tax return if they owe it or have rental income, even when living abroad. Add insurance, utilities and upkeep.

Yearly property tax: Houses pay a municipal property fee (kommunal fastighetsavgift) of SEK 10,425 in 2026, or 0.75% of the tax value if that is lower.

Tax on rental income: Non-resident individuals pay 30% tax on net rental income as capital income; for a private home you can first deduct SEK 40,000 per property and 20% of the rent (the 20% does not apply to a bostadsrätt).
