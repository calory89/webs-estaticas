Buying costs in Georgia: calculator and worked examples

Buying a home in Georgia costs about 0.6 to 1.1% of the price in taxes and fees. On a €100,000 home that is €600 to €1,100, so €100,600 to €101,100 in total. Type your own price: the result has its own link, to send to whoever is buying with you.

How it is worked out

  • Where the money goes: No transfer tax or stamp duty; registry fee 50-200 GEL, notary under 0.1%, legal 0.5-1%; agent 2-3% if buyer-paid.
  • Taxes and fees = price × the range above. They are a share of the price, so the sum works in any currency; the currency box only changes the symbol.
  • The range covers new and resale homes and different regions. A cheap home is usually nearer the high end, because several fees have a minimum.
  • Comparing with another country? Put two homes side by side in compare the total cost of two homes.

Every year after you buy

Yearly property tax: Only for tax residents: 0.05-1% of value depending on household income; non-residents generally exempt.

Tax on rental income: 5% of gross rent on residential property (long- or short-term), residents and non-residents alike.

Questions people ask

How much are the taxes and fees on a €100,000 home in Georgia?

About 0.6 to 1.1% of the price: €600 to €1,100, so €100,600 to €101,100 in total. On €150,000 it is €900 to €1,650.

What do I pay every year after buying in Georgia?

Only for tax residents: 0.05-1% of value depending on household income; non-residents generally exempt. Add up your own bills with the running costs calculator.

Can a foreigner buy a home in Georgia?

Yes, residential and commercial property freely; agricultural land is reserved for citizens. See the rule for your nationality in can I buy there?
