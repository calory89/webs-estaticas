Can a French citizen buy property in Mexico?

Short answer: Yes, with conditions.

Yes, with conditions. In the Restricted Zone (within 50 km of the coast or 100 km of the borders, which covers Tulum and Playa del Carmen) French buyers buy through a fideicomiso bank trust or a Mexican company. Inland you can own directly, with a permit from the Foreign Ministry.

General information, not legal advice. Updated November 2026.

What it costs to buy

About 3.5-8% on top of the price. On a €150,000 home that is €5,250 to €12,000, so €155,250 to €162,000 in total.

Acquisition tax 2-5% + notary 0.5-1% + legal 1-1.5% (GPG 3.5-7.5%), plus 0.5-1.5% for fideicomiso/permit setup; TheLatinvestor budgets 5-8%.

Work it out for your price

Every year

Yearly property tax: Municipal predial, set locally on cadastral value; usually small, often less than HOA fees.

Yearly costs include the local property tax (predial), usually 0.05% to 1.2% of the official cadastral value, which is often well below market value. In the restricted zone add the bank trust fee, about US$450 to US$1,000 a year, plus HOA fees, insurance and upkeep.

Tax on rental income: Non-residents: 25% ISR withholding on gross rent; plus 16% IVA on short-term stays (Airbnb remits on its bookings).

Living there

Buying a home gives no residency in Mexico; temporary residency is usually based on income or savings and applied for at a Mexican consulate.
