Can a Spanish citizen buy property in Cyprus?

Short answer: Yes.

Yes. EU citizens buy homes in Cyprus without restrictions.

General information, not legal advice. Updated November 2026.

What it costs to buy

About 2.5-3.5% on top of the price. On a €150,000 home that is €3,750 to €5,250, so €153,750 to €155,250 in total.

Land Registry transfer fees of 3% up to €85,000, 5% to €170,000 and 8% above, halved for resales without VAT (1.5%, 2.5% and 4%); stamp duty abolished from 1 January 2026; lawyer about 1% plus VAT; small Land Registry charges.

Work it out for your price

Every year

Yearly property tax: There is no annual national property tax (abolished in 2017); owners pay municipal fees of about €90 to €300 a year, plus sewerage and local authority charges.

Yearly costs are municipal, sewerage and local authority fees, plus income tax and GHS on any rent.

Tax on rental income: Non-residents pay income tax on Cyprus rental income at 20% to 35% after deducting 20% of the gross rent and loan interest, plus a 2.65% health contribution (GHS); the special defence contribution does not apply to them.

Living there

As an EU citizen you have the right to live in Cyprus; stays over three months usually need registration with the local authorities. Buying a home is not needed for that, and does not change it.

Good to know as a Spanish citizen

  • Spanish residents with assets abroad above €50,000 in a category (such as property) must file form 720.
