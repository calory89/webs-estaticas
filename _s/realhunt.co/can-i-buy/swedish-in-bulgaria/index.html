Can a Swedish citizen buy property in Bulgaria?

Short answer: Yes.

Yes. Since 2012 EU and EEA citizens can own both buildings and land in Bulgaria on the same terms as Bulgarians.

General information, not legal advice. Updated November 2026.

What it costs to buy

About 3.2-8.5% on top of the price. On a €150,000 home that is €4,800 to €12,750, so €154,800 to €162,750 in total.

Transfer tax 0.1-3% (municipal; Sofia 2.5%) + notary 0.1-1.5% + legal 0.5-1% + agent 2.5-3%.

Work it out for your price

Every year

Yearly property tax: 0.01-0.45% of the property's tax value plus a municipal garbage fee.

Yearly costs are low: municipal property tax is 0.01% to 0.45% of the tax assessment value, with a 5% discount if paid in full by April 30, plus a municipal waste collection fee. Owning through a company adds about EUR 500 to 1,500 a year in running costs.

Tax on rental income: Non-residents: 10% final tax on gross rent, paid quarterly, no deductions.

Living there

As an EU citizen you have the right to live in Bulgaria; stays over three months usually need registration with the local authorities. Buying a home is not needed for that, and does not change it.
