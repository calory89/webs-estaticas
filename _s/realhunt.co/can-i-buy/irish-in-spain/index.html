Can an Irish citizen buy property in Spain?

Short answer: Yes.

Yes. EU citizens buy in Spain on the same terms as Spaniards. You need an NIE, your foreigner tax number.

General information, not legal advice. Updated November 2026.

What it costs to buy

About 7.5-14% on top of the price. On a €150,000 home that is €11,250 to €21,000, so €161,250 to €171,000 in total.

New build: 10% IVA + 0.5-1.5% AJD stamp duty + notary 0.5-1% + legal 1-2%; resale: ITP transfer tax 6-11% instead of IVA/AJD.

Work it out for your price

Every year

Yearly property tax: IBI 0.4-1.1% of cadastral value (municipal), plus imputed income tax on unlet homes.

IBI (council tax) and waste collection. Non-residents who do not rent the house out also file a small yearly imputed-income tax.

Tax on rental income: Non-residents: EU/EEA 19% on net income; non-EU 24% on gross with no deductions.

Living there

As an EU citizen you have the right to live in Spain; stays over three months usually need registration with the local authorities. Buying a home is not needed for that, and does not change it.

The steps to buy in Spain

  1. Get an NIE
  2. Open a Spanish bank account
  3. Reserve with an arras contract
  4. Check the house
  5. Sign at the notary and register
  6. Pay the transfer tax
