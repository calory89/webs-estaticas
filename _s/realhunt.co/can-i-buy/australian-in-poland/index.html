Can an Australian buy property in Poland?

Short answer: Yes, with conditions.

Partly: Australians can buy an apartment without a permit, but usually need a permit from the Ministry of the Interior (MSWiA) to buy a house or land.

General information, not legal advice. Updated November 2026.

What it costs to buy

About 2.5-4% on top of the price. On a €150,000 home that is €3,750 to €6,000, so €153,750 to €156,000 in total.

Tax on civil law transactions (PCC) 2% of the value, paid by the buyer within 14 days of the deed; notary fee (taksa notarialna) capped at PLN 1,010 plus 0.4% of the value above PLN 60,000, plus 23% VAT; court fee of PLN 200 to enter ownership in the land register (plus PLN 100 for a new register); optional lawyer about 1%; the agent fee (about 2-3%) depends on the agreement.

Work it out for your price

Every year

Yearly property tax: Owners pay a local property tax (podatek od nieruchomości) set by the commune, capped in 2026 at PLN 1.25 per m² of usable floor space for flats and houses.

Yearly costs are the local property tax, which is low for flats, plus income tax on any rent.

Tax on rental income: Private rental income is taxed as a flat lump sum (ryczałt) of 8.5% of revenue up to PLN 100,000 a year and 12.5% above that.

Living there

Buying a home gives no right to live in Poland.

Poland is in the Schengen area: with an Australian passport you can usually stay up to 90 days in any 180 days across the whole area without a visa.

Good to know as an Australian

  • Australian tax residents are taxed on worldwide income, so rent from a home abroad goes on your Australian return, usually with a credit for tax paid abroad.
