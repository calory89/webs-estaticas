Can a Dutch citizen buy property in Portugal?

Short answer: Yes.

Yes. Dutch buyers buy in Portugal with the same rights as Portuguese citizens. You need a Portuguese tax number (NIF).

General information, not legal advice. Updated November 2026.

What it costs to buy

About 3-12% on top of the price. On a €150,000 home that is €4,500 to €18,000, so €154,500 to €168,000 in total.

IMT transfer tax 0-8% (progressive) + stamp duty 0.8% + notary/registry 0.9-1.2% + legal 1.25-2%.

Work it out for your price

Every year

Yearly property tax: IMI 0.3-0.45% of taxable value (urban), set by municipality.

IMI (council tax), usually 0.3–0.45% of the tax value for urban property.

Tax on rental income: Long-term: 25% flat for non-residents; short-term (AL) under the simplified regime only 35-50% of gross is taxable.

Living there

As an EU citizen you have the right to live in Portugal; stays over three months usually need registration with the local authorities. Buying a home is not needed for that, and does not change it.

Good to know as a Dutch citizen

  • Dutch residents declare a home abroad in box 3 (savings and investments) of their Dutch tax return.
