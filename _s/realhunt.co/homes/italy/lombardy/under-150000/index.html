Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Italy

Can foreigners buy? Yes for EU citizens; non-EU buyers need reciprocity (US, UK, Canada, Australia qualify).

Taxes and fees add about 4 to 15% to the price: Registration tax 9% on a second home (resale), or VAT on new builds; notary 1-2% + legal ~1% + agent ~3% (GPG roundtrip 10-18%). On the median home on this page (€100,000) that is €4,000 to €15,000, so €104,000 to €115,000 in all.

To keep the whole purchase within €150,000, look at asking prices up to about €130,400: all 3 homes on this page fit.
