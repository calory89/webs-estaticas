Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Poland

Can foreigners buy? EU, EEA and Swiss citizens buy on the same terms as Poles. Other foreigners can buy an apartment without a permit, but usually need a permit from the Ministry of the Interior (MSWiA) to buy a house or land.

Taxes and fees add about 2.5 to 4% to the price: Tax on civil law transactions (PCC) 2% of the value, paid by the buyer within 14 days of the deed; notary fee (taksa notarialna) capped at PLN 1,010 plus 0.4% of the value above PLN 60,000, plus 23% VAT; court fee of PLN 200 to enter ownership in the land register (plus PLN 100 for a new register); optional lawyer about 1%; the agent fee (about 2-3%) depends on the agreement. On the median home on this page (€55,978) that is €1,399 to €2,239, so €57,377 to €58,217 in all.

To keep the whole purchase within €60,000, look at asking prices up to about €57,600: all 3 homes on this page fit.
