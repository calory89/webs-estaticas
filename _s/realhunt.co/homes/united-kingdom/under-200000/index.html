Buying costs and rules for foreigners in the United Kingdom

Can foreigners buy? Yes. There are no nationality restrictions on buying homes anywhere in the UK and no visa is needed, but buying does not give a right to live there.

Taxes and fees add about 3 to 9% to the price: In England and Northern Ireland, Stamp Duty Land Tax (SDLT) is 0% up to £125,000 and 2% from £125,001 to £250,000, plus a 2% surcharge for non-UK residents and a further 5% if you already own a home anywhere, so a non-resident who owns another home pays 7% up to £125,000 and 9% to £250,000; solicitor (conveyancer) about 0.6-1% with a typical minimum of around £2,100 including VAT; Land Registry fee £20 to £230; searches extra; the seller pays the estate agent. On the median home on this page (€126,500) that is €3,795 to €11,385, so €130,295 to €137,885 in all.

To keep the whole purchase within €200,000, look at asking prices up to about €183,400: 3 of these 5 homes fit.
