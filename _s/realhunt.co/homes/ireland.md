### Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Ireland

Can foreigners buy? Yes. There are no restrictions on foreigners buying homes in Ireland, and owning a home does not give a right to live there.

Taxes and fees add about 2.5 to 3.5% to the price: Stamp duty of 1% of the price up to €1 million (2% from €1 million to €1.5 million and 6% above), solicitor's fees of about 1-1.5% plus 23% VAT, and registration fees of €425 to €800; the seller pays the estate agent, whose fee is usually built into the asking price. On the median home on this page (€230,000) that is €5,750 to €8,050, so €235,750 to €238,050 in all.

At the same percentages: on €40,000, €1,000 to €1,400; on €80,000, €2,000 to €2,800; on €150,000, €3,750 to €5,250.

Yearly property tax: Local Property Tax (LPT): from 2026 a home valued up to €240,000 pays a basic €95 a year and one valued €240,001 to €315,000 pays €235; each council can raise or lower the basic rate by up to 15%, and most raised it by 15% for 2026.
