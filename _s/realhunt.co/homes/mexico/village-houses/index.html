Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Mexico

Can foreigners buy? Yes, but in the Restricted Zone (within 50 km of the coast or 100 km of borders, which covers Tulum and Playa del Carmen) only through a fideicomiso bank trust or a Mexican company; direct ownership elsewhere.

Taxes and fees add about 3.5 to 8% to the price: Acquisition tax 2-5% + notary 0.5-1% + legal 1-1.5% (GPG 3.5-7.5%), plus 0.5-1.5% for fideicomiso/permit setup; TheLatinvestor budgets 5-8%. On the median home on this page (€64,230) that is €2,248 to €5,138, so €66,478 to €69,368 in all.
