Can foreigners buy a house in Netherlands?

Yes. There are no nationality or residence rules and no surcharge for foreign buyers. Some cities, such as The Hague, restrict renting out cheaper homes after purchase without a permit (opkoopbescherming).

What are the buying costs in Netherlands?

Plan for about 2-9% on top of the price: Transfer tax (overdrachtsbelasting) of 8% if you will not live in the home, such as a buy-to-let or holiday home, or 2% if it becomes your main home; a notary fee for the transfer deed of around EUR 500; Kadaster registration fees; and mortgage costs if you borrow; the seller pays its own agent..
