Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Germany

Can foreigners buy? Yes. There are no restrictions on foreigners buying property in Germany, though non-EU buyers usually need a bigger down payment to get a loan.

Taxes and fees add about 5 to 8.5% to the price: Real estate transfer tax (Grunderwerbsteuer) of 3.5% to 6.5% depending on the state (3.5% in Bavaria, 6.5% in Brandenburg, North Rhine-Westphalia, Saarland and Schleswig-Holstein), plus notary and land registry fees (Notar- und Grundbuchkosten) of about 1.5-2%; if the seller hired the agent, the buyer pays half the commission by law, which is extra. On the median home on this page (€50,000) that is €2,500 to €4,250, so €52,500 to €54,250 in all.

To keep the whole purchase within €50,000, look at asking prices up to about €46,000: 4 of these 10 homes fit.
