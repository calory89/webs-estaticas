### Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Canada

Taxes and fees add about 1.5 to 4% to the price: Buyers usually pay provincial land transfer tax on a sliding scale (Ontario: 0.5% up to $55,000 and 1% from $55,000 to $250,000), legal fees of about $500 to $1,500, title insurance of about $250 and an inspection of $300 to $1,000, while the seller usually pays the agents; foreign buyers also pay Ontario's 25% non-resident speculation tax anywhere in Ontario and British Columbia's 20% additional tax in Metro Vancouver and four other regions. On the median home on this page (€99,500) that is €1,493 to €3,980, so €100,993 to €103,480 in all.

At the same percentages: on €40,000, €600 to €1,600; on €80,000, €1,200 to €3,200; on €150,000, €2,250 to €6,000.

Yearly property tax: Property tax is set by each city as a share of assessed value, from about 0.3% in Vancouver to 2.12% in Saint John in 2026.
