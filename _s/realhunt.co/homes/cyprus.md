### Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Cyprus

Taxes and fees add about 2.5 to 3.5% to the price: Land Registry transfer fees of 3% up to €85,000, 5% to €170,000 and 8% above, halved for resales without VAT (1.5%, 2.5% and 4%); stamp duty abolished from 1 January 2026; lawyer about 1% plus VAT; small Land Registry charges. On the median home on this page (€187,000) that is €4,675 to €6,545, so €191,675 to €193,545 in all.

At the same percentages: on €40,000, €1,000 to €1,400; on €80,000, €2,000 to €2,800; on €150,000, €3,750 to €5,250.

Yearly property tax: There is no annual national property tax (abolished in 2017); owners pay municipal fees of about €90 to €300 a year, plus sewerage and local authority charges.

### Cyprus: a home without its own title deed, and the 6 months to deposit the contract

When a home has no title deed of its own yet, for example a unit that is still being built, the Department of Lands and Surveys recommends signing a sale contract and depositing it with the Department at once, and no later than 6 months after signing. The deposit creates a charge on the property, blocks a later contract for the same unit and lets the buyer ask a court to order the transfer if the seller does not perform. A contract deposited after the 6 months pays transfer fees that are 10% higher.

Source: [Department of Lands and Surveys of Cyprus, Purchase of Immovable Property: the importance of depositing the sale contract](https://portal.dls.moi.gov.cy/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Purchase-of-Immovable-Property-The-importance-of-Depositing-the-Sale-Contract-at-the-Department-of-Lands-and-Surveys.pdf).
