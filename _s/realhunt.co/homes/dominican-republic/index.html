What are the buying costs in Dominican Republic?

Plan for about 4.25-7% on top of the price: The buyer pays a 3% transfer tax (0% for qualifying first purchases under CONFOTUR tourism incentives), legal fees of about 1-2% and notary fees of about 0.25-1%, plus registration costs, while the seller pays the 2-3% agent fee..
