### Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Mexico

Taxes and fees add about 3.5 to 8% to the price: Acquisition tax 2-5% + notary 0.5-1% + legal 1-1.5% (GPG 3.5-7.5%), plus 0.5-1.5% for fideicomiso/permit setup; TheLatinvestor budgets 5-8%. On the median home on this page (€160,200) that is €5,607 to €12,816, so €165,807 to €173,016 in all.

At the same percentages: on €40,000, €1,400 to €3,200; on €80,000, €2,800 to €6,400; on €150,000, €5,250 to €12,000.

Yearly property tax: Municipal predial, set locally on cadastral value; usually small, often less than HOA fees.
