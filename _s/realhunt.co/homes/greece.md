### Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Greece

Taxes and fees add about 5.5 to 7% to the price: Transfer tax 3.09% of the price or the taxable value, whichever is higher; notary about 0.8-1% plus VAT; land registry fees about 0.5-0.7%; lawyer 1-2% plus VAT; if an agent is used, the buyer usually pays a commission of 1-3% plus VAT on top. On the median home on this page (€135,000) that is €7,425 to €9,450, so €142,425 to €144,450 in all.

At the same percentages: on €40,000, €2,200 to €2,800; on €80,000, €4,400 to €5,600; on €150,000, €8,250 to €10,500.

Yearly property tax: Owners on 1 January pay the unified property tax (ENFIA), calculated per property from its location, size, age and official zone value, with an extra charge if total Greek property exceeds €500,000.

### The yearly property tax in Greece (ENFIA) and the E9 statement

ENFIA is calculated every year on the property held in Greece on 1 January. Owners who live abroad must file the E9 property statement like residents, through the myAADE portal, by 31 March of the year after they buy. The tax can be paid in up to 10 equal monthly instalments of at least €10 each.

Source: [Independent Authority for Public Revenue (AADE), Unified Tax on the Ownership of Real Estate (E9-ENFIA)](https://www.aade.gr/en/greeks-abroad-non-residents/property-taxation/unified-tax-ownership-real-estate-e9-enfia). Checked November 2026. General information, not legal or tax advice.
