Buying costs and rules for foreigners in the United States

Can foreigners buy? Yes. There is no federal ban on foreigners buying homes, but about 30 states have added some limits, and Texas and Florida restrict buyers from certain countries such as China.

Taxes and fees add about 2 to 5% to the price: Closing costs vary by state and typically run 2-5% of the loan amount, covering title search and title insurance, recording fees, attorney fees, appraisal and lender fees; since August 2024 the seller no longer has to pay the buyer's agent, so the buyer may pay it unless the seller agrees to; FIRPTA does not apply when you buy but does when a foreigner later sells, with the buyer generally withholding 15% of the sale price. On the median home on this page (€82,875) that is €1,658 to €4,144, so €84,533 to €87,019 in all.

To keep the whole purchase within €100,000, look at asking prices up to about €95,200: all 3 homes on this page fit.
