Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Bulgaria

Can foreigners buy? Yes, buildings and apartments, with rights similar to locals; per GPG, foreigners can't own land directly, so a Bulgarian company is the usual route.

Taxes and fees add about 3.2 to 8.5% to the price: Transfer tax 0.1-3% (municipal; Sofia 2.5%) + notary 0.1-1.5% + legal 0.5-1% + agent 2.5-3%. On the median home on this page (€66,000) that is €2,112 to €5,610, so €68,112 to €71,610 in all.
