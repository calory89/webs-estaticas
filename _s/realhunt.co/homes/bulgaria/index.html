Can foreigners buy a house in Bulgaria?

Yes, buildings and apartments, with rights similar to locals; per GPG, foreigners can't own land directly, so a Bulgarian company is the usual route.

What are the buying costs in Bulgaria?

Plan for about 3.2-8.5% on top of the price: Transfer tax 0.1-3% (municipal; Sofia 2.5%) + notary 0.1-1.5% + legal 0.5-1% + agent 2.5-3%.. This is general information, not tax advice.
