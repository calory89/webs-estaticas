Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Greece

Can foreigners buy? Yes. Buyers from outside the EU and EFTA need a permit to buy in designated border areas (parts of northern Greece and eastern Aegean islands such as the Dodecanese and Rhodes). The government has announced a 15% transfer tax for non-EU buyers of homes from 1 January 2027, not yet final in September 2026.

Taxes and fees add about 5.5 to 7% to the price: Transfer tax 3.09% of the price or the taxable value, whichever is higher; notary about 0.8-1% plus VAT; land registry fees about 0.5-0.7%; lawyer 1-2% plus VAT; if an agent is used, the buyer usually pays a commission of 1-3% plus VAT on top. On the median home on this page (€150,000) that is €8,250 to €10,500, so €158,250 to €160,500 in all.
