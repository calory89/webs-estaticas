Can foreigners buy a house in Greece?

Yes. Buyers from outside the EU and EFTA need a permit to buy in designated border areas (parts of northern Greece and eastern Aegean islands such as the Dodecanese and Rhodes). The government has announced a 15% transfer tax for non-EU buyers of homes from 1 January 2027, not yet final in September 2026.

What are the buying costs in Greece?

Plan for about 5.5-7% on top of the price: Transfer tax 3.09% of the price or the taxable value, whichever is higher; notary about 0.8-1% plus VAT; land registry fees about 0.5-0.7%; lawyer 1-2% plus VAT; if an agent is used, the buyer usually pays a commission of 1-3% plus VAT on top.. This is general information, not tax advice.
