Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Denmark

Can foreigners buy? Without Danish domicile or 5 years of past residence you need permission from the Department of Civil Affairs (Civilstyrelsen); EU/EEA citizens who live and are registered in Denmark can buy a permanent home without permission by signing a declaration to the Land Registration Court. Holiday homes (sommerhuse) need permission, which is only given to people with particularly strong ties to Denmark, such as many years of holidays there.

Taxes and fees add about 1.5 to 3% to the price: Registration fee on the deed (tinglysningsafgift) of DKK 1,850 plus 0.6% of the price, about 1% for a buyer's lawyer, and, if you borrow, a mortgage registration fee of DKK 1,825 plus 1.25% of the loan; the seller normally pays the agent. On the median home on this page (€66,400) that is €996 to €1,992, so €67,396 to €68,392 in all.

To keep the whole purchase within €80,000, look at asking prices up to about €77,600: 4 of these 5 homes fit.
