### Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Spain

Can foreigners buy? Yes, no restrictions at present; the government has proposed a 100% tax on (or ban for) non-EU non-resident buyers, not enacted.

Taxes and fees add about 7.5 to 14% to the price: New build: 10% IVA + 0.5-1.5% AJD stamp duty + notary 0.5-1% + legal 1-2%; resale: ITP transfer tax 6-11% instead of IVA/AJD. On the median home on this page (€97,000) that is €7,275 to €13,580, so €104,275 to €110,580 in all.

At the same percentages: on €40,000, €3,000 to €5,600; on €80,000, €6,000 to €11,200; on €150,000, €11,250 to €21,000.

Yearly property tax: IBI 0.4-1.1% of cadastral value (municipal), plus imputed income tax on unlet homes.

### Before you buy in Spain: the NIE, and no residence permit for buying

A foreign buyer needs a NIE, the personal number Spain gives to foreigners who have economic, professional or social interests in the country. You can ask for it in Spain or at the Spanish consulate where you live, in person or through an accredited representative, with form EX-15 and your passport. The NIE is not a residence permit and does not prove residence.

Buying a home no longer gives a residence permit: Organic Law 1/2025 ended the investor visas on 3 April 2025, including the one for property purchases above €500,000.

#### Can foreigners buy a house in Spain?

#### What are the buying costs in Spain?

About 7.5 to 14% on top of the price: New build: 10% IVA + 0.5-1.5% AJD stamp duty + notary 0.5-1% + legal 1-2%; resale: ITP transfer tax 6-11% instead of IVA/AJD. On the median home on this page (€97,000) that is €7,275 to €13,580. General information, not tax advice.
