Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Portugal

Can foreigners buy? Yes, same rights as Portuguese citizens (IMT increase for foreign second-home buyers announced Sept 2025).

Taxes and fees add about 3 to 12% to the price: IMT transfer tax 0-8% (progressive) + stamp duty 0.8% + notary/registry 0.9-1.2% + legal 1.25-2%. On the median home on this page (€117,500) that is €3,525 to €14,100, so €121,025 to €131,600 in all.

To keep the whole purchase within €150,000, look at asking prices up to about €133,900: 8 of these 12 homes fit.
