### Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Switzerland

Taxes and fees add about 1 to 5% to the price: Transfer tax (Handänderungssteuer, droits de mutation) 0% to about 3.3% depending on the canton; notary about 0.1-0.5%; land register fee, a small fixed amount or percentage; optional lawyer 1-2%; the agent fee (2-3%) is paid by the seller. On the median home on this page (€70,100) that is €701 to €3,505, so €70,801 to €73,605 in all.

At the same percentages: on €40,000, €400 to €2,000; on €80,000, €800 to €4,000; on €150,000, €1,500 to €7,500.

Yearly property tax: More than half of the cantons charge an annual property tax (Liegenschaftssteuer), usually 0.01-0.3% of the assessed value, and owners also pay cantonal wealth tax on the property value minus the mortgage.
