### Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Italy

Taxes and fees add about 4 to 15% to the price: Registration tax 9% on a second home (resale), or VAT on new builds; notary 1-2% + legal ~1% + agent ~3% (GPG roundtrip 10-18%). On the median home on this page (€85,000) that is €3,400 to €12,750, so €88,400 to €97,750 in all.

At the same percentages: on €40,000, €1,600 to €6,000; on €80,000, €3,200 to €12,000; on €150,000, €6,000 to €22,500.

Yearly property tax: IMU 0.4-1.06% on second homes (on cadastral value).
