Yearly property taxOwners pay an annual property tax (taxe foncière) set by the local authorities, and owners of a furnished second home also pay housing tax (taxe d'habitation), which some communes raise by a surcharge of 5% to 60%.Fixed asset tax is 1.4% of the government's assessed value, plus city planning tax of up to 0.3% in urban areas, and small residential plots get a lower tax base.
Tax on rental incomeNon-residents pay French income tax on French rental income at a minimum rate of 20% (30% above about €27,500), plus social charges of 17.2%, or 7.5% for people covered by social security in another EU/EEA country or Switzerland.Rent paid to a non-resident is subject to 20.42% withholding (except when an individual rents it as their own home), and the owner must file a tax return each year through a tax representative in Japan to settle the final tax.
