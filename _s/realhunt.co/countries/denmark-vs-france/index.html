Yearly property taxOwners pay property value tax (ejendomsværdiskat) of 0.51% on 80% of the official valuation (1.4% above DKK 9.2 million) plus municipal land tax (grundskyld) at a rate set by each municipality, capped at 30 per mille of the land value.Owners pay an annual property tax (taxe foncière) set by the local authorities, and owners of a furnished second home also pay housing tax (taxe d'habitation), which some communes raise by a surcharge of 5% to 60%.
Tax on rental incomeA non-resident who rents out a Danish home is taxed in Denmark on the profit under the ordinary Danish rules; staying in Denmark more than 3 months at a time or 180 days a year, or working there, makes you fully tax liable.Non-residents pay French income tax on French rental income at a minimum rate of 20% (30% above about €27,500), plus social charges of 17.2%, or 7.5% for people covered by social security in another EU/EEA country or Switzerland.
