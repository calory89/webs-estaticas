Can foreigners buy?Yes, same rights as Portuguese citizens (IMT increase for foreign second-home buyers announced Sept 2025).Under the Lex Koller law, EU/EFTA citizens living in Switzerland and holders of a C permit buy like Swiss citizens, and non-EU holders of a B permit can buy a home they live in. People living abroad need a cantonal permit and can usually only buy one holiday home in a tourist area, within national quotas; a proposed tightening was under consultation in 2026.
Yearly property taxIMI 0.3-0.45% of taxable value (urban), set by municipality.More than half of the cantons charge an annual property tax (Liegenschaftssteuer), usually 0.01-0.3% of the assessed value, and owners also pay cantonal wealth tax on the property value minus the mortgage.
Tax on rental incomeLong-term: 25% flat for non-residents; short-term (AL) under the simplified regime only 35-50% of gross is taxable.Non-residents pay tax on net Swiss rental income at ordinary cantonal and communal rates plus federal direct tax, and some cantons set the rate using worldwide income.
