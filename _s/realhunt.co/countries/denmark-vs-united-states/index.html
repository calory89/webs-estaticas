Tax on rental incomeA non-resident who rents out a Danish home is taxed in Denmark on the profit under the ordinary Danish rules; staying in Denmark more than 3 months at a time or 180 days a year, or working there, makes you fully tax liable.By default a non-resident pays 30% of gross rent (or a lower treaty rate), withheld at source, or can elect to treat the rent as business income and pay graduated rates on net income by filing Form 1040-NR and giving Form W-8ECI to the withholding agent.
