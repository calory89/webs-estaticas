Can foreigners buy?Mostly not for now: a federal ban stops non-Canadians from buying homes in cities and larger towns until January 1, 2027, with exceptions such as work permit holders and spouses of citizens or permanent residents. The ban does not cover rural areas and small towns, and the government has said it does not plan simply to extend it, but a replacement is being studied.EU, EEA and Swiss citizens buy on the same terms as Poles. Other foreigners can buy an apartment without a permit, but usually need a permit from the Ministry of the Interior (MSWiA) to buy a house or land.
Yearly property taxProperty tax is set by each city as a share of assessed value, from about 0.3% in Vancouver to 2.12% in Saint John in 2026.Owners pay a local property tax (podatek od nieruchomości) set by the commune, capped in 2026 at PLN 1.25 per m² of usable floor space for flats and houses.
Tax on rental incomeNon-residents have 25% of gross rent withheld, but they can file a Section 216 return to pay tax on net rent instead, and Form NR6 lets the agent withhold on net rent.Private rental income is taxed as a flat lump sum (ryczałt) of 8.5% of revenue up to PLN 100,000 a year and 12.5% above that.
