Can foreigners buy?Without Danish domicile or 5 years of past residence you need permission from the Department of Civil Affairs (Civilstyrelsen); EU/EEA citizens who live and are registered in Denmark can buy a permanent home without permission by signing a declaration to the Land Registration Court. Holiday homes (sommerhuse) need permission, which is only given to people with particularly strong ties to Denmark, such as many years of holidays there.Yes, same rights as Portuguese citizens (IMT increase for foreign second-home buyers announced Sept 2025).
Yearly property taxOwners pay property value tax (ejendomsværdiskat) of 0.51% on 80% of the official valuation (1.4% above DKK 9.2 million) plus municipal land tax (grundskyld) at a rate set by each municipality, capped at 30 per mille of the land value.IMI 0.3-0.45% of taxable value (urban), set by municipality.
Tax on rental incomeA non-resident who rents out a Danish home is taxed in Denmark on the profit under the ordinary Danish rules; staying in Denmark more than 3 months at a time or 180 days a year, or working there, makes you fully tax liable.Long-term: 25% flat for non-residents; short-term (AL) under the simplified regime only 35-50% of gross is taxable.
