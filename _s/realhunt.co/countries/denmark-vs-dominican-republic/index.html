Yearly property taxOwners pay property value tax (ejendomsværdiskat) of 0.51% on 80% of the official valuation (1.4% above DKK 9.2 million) plus municipal land tax (grundskyld) at a rate set by each municipality, capped at 30 per mille of the land value.The yearly IPI tax is 1% of the part of a person's total Dominican property value, as assessed by the tax office DGII, above RD$10,695,494 in 2026, so a single cheap home is often below the threshold.
Tax on rental incomeA non-resident who rents out a Danish home is taxed in Denmark on the profit under the ordinary Danish rules; staying in Denmark more than 3 months at a time or 180 days a year, or working there, makes you fully tax liable.Rent paid abroad to a non-resident is generally subject to 27% withholding.
