Can foreigners buy?Mostly not for now: a federal ban stops non-Canadians from buying homes in cities and larger towns until January 1, 2027, with exceptions such as work permit holders and spouses of citizens or permanent residents. The ban does not cover rural areas and small towns, and the government has said it does not plan simply to extend it, but a replacement is being studied.Without Danish domicile or 5 years of past residence you need permission from the Department of Civil Affairs (Civilstyrelsen); EU/EEA citizens who live and are registered in Denmark can buy a permanent home without permission by signing a declaration to the Land Registration Court. Holiday homes (sommerhuse) need permission, which is only given to people with particularly strong ties to Denmark, such as many years of holidays there.
Yearly property taxProperty tax is set by each city as a share of assessed value, from about 0.3% in Vancouver to 2.12% in Saint John in 2026.Owners pay property value tax (ejendomsværdiskat) of 0.51% on 80% of the official valuation (1.4% above DKK 9.2 million) plus municipal land tax (grundskyld) at a rate set by each municipality, capped at 30 per mille of the land value.
Tax on rental incomeNon-residents have 25% of gross rent withheld, but they can file a Section 216 return to pay tax on net rent instead, and Form NR6 lets the agent withhold on net rent.A non-resident who rents out a Danish home is taxed in Denmark on the profit under the ordinary Danish rules; staying in Denmark more than 3 months at a time or 180 days a year, or working there, makes you fully tax liable.
