Yearly property taxOwners pay property tax (Grundsteuer), calculated since 2025 as assessed value x a tax rate x a municipal multiplier (Hebesatz) that each municipality sets.Fixed asset tax is 1.4% of the government's assessed value, plus city planning tax of up to 0.3% in urban areas, and small residential plots get a lower tax base.
Tax on rental incomeNon-resident owners are taxed in Germany on net rental income at the normal progressive income tax rates, but without the basic tax-free allowance (Grundfreibetrag).Rent paid to a non-resident is subject to 20.42% withholding (except when an individual rents it as their own home), and the owner must file a tax return each year through a tax representative in Japan to settle the final tax.
