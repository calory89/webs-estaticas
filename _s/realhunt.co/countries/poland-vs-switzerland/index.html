Can foreigners buy?EU, EEA and Swiss citizens buy on the same terms as Poles. Other foreigners can buy an apartment without a permit, but usually need a permit from the Ministry of the Interior (MSWiA) to buy a house or land.Under the Lex Koller law, EU/EFTA citizens living in Switzerland and holders of a C permit buy like Swiss citizens, and non-EU holders of a B permit can buy a home they live in. People living abroad need a cantonal permit and can usually only buy one holiday home in a tourist area, within national quotas; a proposed tightening was under consultation in 2026.
Yearly property taxOwners pay a local property tax (podatek od nieruchomości) set by the commune, capped in 2026 at PLN 1.25 per m² of usable floor space for flats and houses.More than half of the cantons charge an annual property tax (Liegenschaftssteuer), usually 0.01-0.3% of the assessed value, and owners also pay cantonal wealth tax on the property value minus the mortgage.
Tax on rental incomePrivate rental income is taxed as a flat lump sum (ryczałt) of 8.5% of revenue up to PLN 100,000 a year and 12.5% above that.Non-residents pay tax on net Swiss rental income at ordinary cantonal and communal rates plus federal direct tax, and some cantons set the rate using worldwide income.
