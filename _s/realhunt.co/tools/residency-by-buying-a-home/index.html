Does buying a home give you residency? The rule in each country

Buying a home does not give a buyer from outside the EU the right to live in 18 of the 20 countries on this page. Cyprus and Greece still have a route linked to property, with a minimum price. The sentence for each country is below.

How to read the list

  • "No" means that owning the home, at any price, does not by itself let you live in the country. You visit under the normal rules for your passport and need a visa or a permit to stay longer.
  • "Only above a minimum price" means a residence route for property buyers exists, with the threshold given in the sentence. A cheap home does not reach it.
  • In a Schengen country, the days at your home count towards the 90 in any 180: use the Schengen calculator.
  • The rule for each nationality, with the conditions to buy, is in can I buy there?

Questions people ask

Which countries give residency for buying a home?

Of the 20 countries RealHunt covers, only Cyprus and Greece keep a route for buyers from outside the EU, and with a minimum price far above a cheap home. In the other 18, owning a home gives no right to stay.

How long can I stay at my home without a residence permit?

In the Schengen area, 90 days in any 180 for most visitors from outside the EU. Count your days with the Schengen 90/180 calculator.

Does this apply to EU citizens?

No. Citizens of the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway and Switzerland can live in another country of that area under the free movement rules; the list is about buyers from elsewhere.
