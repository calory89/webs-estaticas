Non-resident tax calculator for a home in Spain you do not let

An owner who does not live in Spain pays income tax every year on a home there even if it earns nothing. With a cadastral value of €40,000 the bill is €83.60 for a resident of the EU, Iceland, Norway or Liechtenstein and €105.60 for anyone else. Type the cadastral value printed on your IBI bill.

A worked example

Cadastral value €40,000, in a town that revised its values in the last ten years, owned all year. Imputed income: €40,000 × 1.1% = €440.00. Tax for an EU resident: €440.00 × 19% = €83.60. For a resident of the United Kingdom or the United States: €440.00 × 24% = €105.60. In a town without a recent revision the percentage is 2%, so that owner pays €192.00. Bought on 2 July and owned 183 days, the first case comes to €41.91.

How it is worked out

  • Imputed income = cadastral value × 1.1% if the town's cadastral values were revised in the tax year or the ten before it (the rule also covers 2023 to 2026), or × 2% otherwise. No expenses are deducted.
  • Tax = imputed income × 19% for residents of the EU, Iceland, Norway and Liechtenstein, × 24% for other taxpayers.
  • The amount is for a full calendar year and is reduced in proportion to the days you did not own the home or had it let. With several owners, each declares their share.
  • If the home has no cadastral value yet, the base is 50% of the purchase price (or of the value set by the tax office, if higher), at 1.1%.
  • It is declared on form 210 for the previous year. It is a different tax from the IBI, which the town charges, and from the tax on rent, which applies to the days the home is let.

Questions people ask

Do I pay tax in Spain if I never let my holiday home?

Yes. A non-resident who owns an urban home for own use, or leaves it empty, declares an imputed income every year. On a cadastral value of €40,000 it is between €83.60 and €192.00 depending on where you live and on the town.

Where do I find the cadastral value?

On the IBI bill the town hall sends every year, under the heading valor catastral. It is usually well below the market price.

Is this the only yearly tax on the home?

No. The IBI and the waste charge go to the town hall. The running costs calculator adds everything up, and the Spain buying guide explains the steps.
