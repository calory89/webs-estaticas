What a home abroad costs to keep each year

A home that costs 80,000 to buy and 4,130 a year to keep costs 344 a month: 5.2% of its price every year, so in 19.4 years the running costs add up to what you paid for it. Put in your own bills; the calculator shows the yearly property tax rule of each country next to them.

A worked example

The example in the calculator: property tax 400 a year, community fees 50 a month (600 a year), insurance 250, power, water and internet 90 a month (1,080), repairs 800 and four trips at 250 each (1,000). Total: 4,130 a year.

A flat in a holiday complex is the case where this sum matters most. With a yearly fee of 660 (55 a month) on a 30,000 flat, and nothing else counted, the fees alone equal the price in 45.5 years. Ask for the fee per square metre and what it covers before you compare two complexes.

A real reference from our own data: on the 35 homes in the United States on RealHunt whose listing states the yearly property tax bill, the median bill is US$4,900, from US$1,025 to US$9,000 (27 November 2026; the bill of a new owner can differ from the seller's).

How it is worked out

  • Per year = property tax + 12 × community fees + insurance + 12 × bills + repairs + trips × cost of a trip + the one-off bill ÷ the years you spread it over.
  • Share of the price = cost per year ÷ price. Years of costs that equal the price = price ÷ cost per year.
  • All figures are yours: the calculator has no rate of its own. Choose a country to read how its yearly property tax is set, with the sources on its cost page.
  • A one-off bill (a special assessment in a block of flats, a roof, a boiler) is spread over the years you choose, so one bad year does not hide in the average.

Questions people ask

What does it cost per year to own a cheap home abroad?

There is no single figure: it depends on the tax of the town, the building and how often you go. The example above, 4,130 a year on a 80,000 home, is 5.2% of the price, and travel is 24% of it.

How do I know the community or complex fee before buying?

Ask the seller or the agent for the last yearly statement of the building and the minutes of the last owners' meeting. They show the fee, any debt of the flat and the works already voted.

Is the cost of travelling really part of it?

For a second home, yes. Four return trips a year with a hire car often cost more than the property tax. Set trips to zero if you will live there.
