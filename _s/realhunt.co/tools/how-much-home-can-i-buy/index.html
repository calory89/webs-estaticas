A worked example

Take a budget of €100,000 and Spain, where taxes and fees add 7.5 to 14% to the price. Dividing the budget by 1 plus the high end of that range gives €87,719: a home at that price still fits if the bill comes out at the top of the range. With the low end the limit would be €93,023. RealHunt lists 66 homes in Spain at €87,719 or less today, out of 190.

How it is worked out

  • Highest price that fits = budget ÷ (1 + the high end of the country's buying costs). The high end is used on purpose, so the answer errs on the safe side.
  • Homes counted are the ones on RealHunt on 20 November 2026, each checked by a person, at their asking price in euros. It is not every home for sale in the country.
  • Buying costs per country (transfer tax or VAT, notary, registry, lawyer) come from the buying costs by country table, with the sources of each one.
  • Furniture, repairs, the move and the yearly running costs are not in the sum. Keep a reserve for them.

Questions people ask

Why is the highest price lower than my budget?

Because the price is not the whole bill. Transfer tax or VAT, the notary, the land registry and a lawyer add from 1 to 5% in the cheapest country to 4 to 15% in the dearest one, and they are paid in cash on the day you sign.

Does the count include every home for sale?

No. It counts the homes RealHunt lists: cheap for their country, ready to live in and checked by a person. See how homes are chosen and scored.

Can I buy in every country on the list?

Not always. Some countries ask for a permit or limit what a buyer from abroad can own. Check where foreigners can buy before you fall for a house.
