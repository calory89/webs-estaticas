Stamp duty calculator for non-UK residents buying in England

A buyer who is not resident in the UK pays £7,500 of Stamp Duty Land Tax on a £250,000 home in England or Northern Ireland, against £2,500 for a UK resident: the non-resident surcharge adds 2 points to every band. If you already own a home anywhere in the world, 5 more points apply and the bill is £20,000.

A worked example

£250,000, non-resident, no other home. The first £125,000 is taxed at 2% (0% plus the surcharge): £2,500. The next £125,000 is taxed at 4% (2% plus the surcharge): £5,000. Total £7,500, 3% of the price. On a £100,000 home the standard rate is zero, but the surcharge still applies: £2,000.

How it is worked out

  • Standard residential bands, in force since 1 April 2025: 0% up to £125,000, 2% from £125,001 to £250,000, 5% up to £925,000, 10% up to £1,500,000 and 12% above. Each rate applies only to the slice of the price inside its band.
  • Non-resident surcharge: 2 points on top of every band, since 1 April 2021. You count as non-resident for the purchase if you were in the UK fewer than 183 days in the 12 months before it.
  • Additional home: usually 5 points on top of every band if you own another residential property. The two surcharges add up.
  • The 2% can be refunded if you then spend at least 183 days in the UK in a continuous 365-day period within the two years the rules set; the claim is made by amending the return.
  • Scotland and Wales have their own taxes and are not covered. First-time buyer relief is not applied here.

Questions people ask

Do foreigners pay more stamp duty in the UK?

Nationality does not matter; residence does. A buyer of any passport who was in the UK fewer than 183 days in the 12 months before buying pays 2 points more on every band in England and Northern Ireland: £5,000 more on a £250,000 home.

Is there stamp duty on a home under £125,000?

For a UK resident with no other home, no. A non-resident pays 2% from the first pound (£2,000 on £100,000), and a buyer who owns another home pays 5% more on top.

When is it paid?

Your solicitor normally files the return and pays it for you on completion. The other costs of the purchase are on the UK buying costs page.
