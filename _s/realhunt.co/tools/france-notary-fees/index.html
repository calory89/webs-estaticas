Notary fees calculator for buying a resale home in France

On a €150,000 resale home in France the "notary fees" come to €12,315 to €12,715, about 8.2 to 8.5% of the price, in a department that charges transfer duties of about 6.3%. Most of it is tax: €9,450.00 of duties against €1,914.90 for the notary's own fee with VAT.

A worked example

€150,000 at about 6.3%. Transfer duties: €9,450.00. Notary's regulated fee: 3.870% of the first €6,500 (€251.55), 1.596% of the next €10,500 (€167.58), 1.064% of the next €43,000 (€457.52) and 0.799% of the remaining €90,000 (€719.10): €1,595.75, plus 20% VAT, €319.15. Land-registry contribution at 0.10%: €150.00. With disbursements of about €800 to €1,200, the total is €12,315 to €12,715. In a department at about 5.8% it is €11,565 to €11,965.

How it is worked out

  • Transfer duties (droits de mutation): about 5.8% of the price, or about 6.3% in the departments that raised their rate for 2025 to March 2028. The notary collects them for the state and the local authorities.
  • Notary's regulated fee (émoluments), scale in force since 1 January 2021 in mainland France: 3.870% up to €6,500, 1.596% from €6,500 to €17,000, 1.064% from €17,000 to €60,000 and 0.799% above, plus 20% VAT.
  • Land-registry contribution (contribution de sécurité immobilière): 0.10% of the price. Disbursements (débours), what the notary pays to third parties for you: about €800 to €1,200.
  • For a resale home. A new build bought from a developer pays VAT inside the price and much lower duties, so its notary fees are a smaller share. The overseas departments have their own scales.

Questions people ask

Why are notary fees a bigger share on a cheap home?

Because the scale starts at 3.870% and falls as the price rises, and the disbursements are a fixed amount. On a €60,000 home the total is €5,692 to €6,092, 9.5 to 10.2% of the price, against 8.2 to 8.5% on €150,000.

Who pays the notary in France, buyer or seller?

The buyer. The agent's commission is a separate matter: it is usually already inside the advertised price and paid by the seller.

Is the notary's fee negotiable?

The scale is set by law. Above a certain amount the notary may grant a discount on his own fee, the same for all his clients; the duties, which are most of the bill, cannot be reduced.
