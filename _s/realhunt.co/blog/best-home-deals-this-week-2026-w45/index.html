The 10 best home deals this week: 31 October to 7 November 2026

The 10 highest-scored homes that passed our check from 31 October to 7 November 2026, in Spain, Italy and Japan, priced from €7,400 to €178,000.

How we pick

A person checks every home before it goes up, and homes that need major work, ruins, bare land and thin listings are turned down. The score then compares each home with every other home on RealHunt, from 70 to 98. Homes already listed in an earlier weekly Top 10 are left out.

Prices are the asking prices when each home was added. Homes sell, so check the listing before you travel.
