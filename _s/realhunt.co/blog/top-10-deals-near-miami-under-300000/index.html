Top 10 home deals near Miami under $300,000 in good neighbourhoods

We read 8,300 listings around Miami and kept the 10 best 2 and 3-bedroom homes under $300,000 in Doral, Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Plantation, Miramar and Kendall, with what they really cost each month.

RealHunt Research · 2026-11-07

Under $300,000 within half an hour of Miami sounds impossible, and most listings at that price are either far out, in rough blocks, sold "as is" or in old towers facing big repair bills. We read about 8,300 homes for sale in ten cities around Miami, kept the 2 and 3-bedroom homes under $300,000 in safe, sought-after areas, dropped short sales, 55+ communities, "investors only" units and homes with poor photos, and published the 10 best. A person checked every one.

Where they are

Doral (two homes) is the business and family district west of Miami Airport, with good schools, CityPlace and big parks. Downtown Hollywood (two) is walkable, between Young Circle, Hollywood Boulevard and the beach, about 25 minutes from Miami. Pembroke Pines and Miramar (three, all on lakes) are quiet, green Broward suburbs with good schools, 30 minutes from Miami. Plantation (two) is leafy central Broward next to Fort Lauderdale. Kendall (one) is the established south-west Miami suburb, in a guard-gated community on a lake.

What they really cost every month

In Florida the cheap price is only half the story, so every home page on RealHunt shows the monthly costs in large type:

  • Community fee (HOA). Condos and townhouses pay one every month, often $400 to $900, sometimes including water, cable or building insurance. Two of these listings state it ($625 in Doral and $135 in Miramar); for the others we mark it as not stated, so ask the agent before you visit.
  • Property tax. Florida resets the taxable value when a home sells. Listings show what the seller pays, often with a homestead discount; a new owner pays about 1.8% of the price, roughly $4,400 to $5,400 a year for these homes.
  • Older condo buildings. Since 2022 Florida requires older condo buildings to pass structural inspections and keep repair reserves, which has led to large special assessments in some towers. We avoided any listing that mentioned one, but ask for the latest inspection, the reserve study and the board minutes before you make an offer.
  • Insurance. Home insurance is extra. Impact windows, like those in the two Westview townhouses, lower it.

How we chose them

We started from every active listing in Hallandale Beach, Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Miami Lakes, Doral, Kendall, North Bay Village, Sunny Isles Beach, Miramar and Plantation, kept 2 and 3-bedroom homes from $170,000 to $300,000 (858 homes), checked fees, year built and red flags for the most promising 193, then looked at the photos of the last 16. Six more were dropped for dark, empty or virtually staged rooms. The score is RealHunt's usual one: price for the area, condition, location and what is nearby.
