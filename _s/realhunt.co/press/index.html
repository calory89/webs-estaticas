RealHunt for journalists: facts, numbers, logos and contact

RealHunt (realhunt.co) is a website where foreigners find, check and buy property: it lists cheap homes for sale around the world, each one checked by a person and graded from 70 to 98 against every other home on the site. It is not an estate agent: every home links to its original listing and buyers pay nothing. It also publishes, free to quote, the buying rules, taxes and costs of 20 countries and data on what cheap homes cost.

Data you can quote

  • Median asking price of a cheap home, by country (2026). Lowest median price per m²: Japan, €463.
  • What €50,000, €100,000 and €200,000 buy, country by country (2026). €50,001 to €100,000 buys a median 115 m² in Denmark and 34 m² in Switzerland.
  • Buying costs by country: taxes and fees on top of the price (2026). From 0.6–1.1% in Georgia to 12–22% in Northern Cyprus.
  • Where foreigners can and cannot buy a home: the rules in 20 countries (2026). Free for any nationality in 12 of 20 countries; restricted in Canada, Denmark and Switzerland.
  • Best-scored regions for a cheap home, ranked (2026). Highest median score: West Jutland, Denmark (91).
  • Where the homes under €50,000 are: countries and regions (2026). 79 homes at or under €50,000; most in Italy (33).
  • How many years of average net pay a cheap home costs, by country (2026). 1.5 years of net pay in Germany, 8 in the Netherlands.
  • Cheap homes and flood risk: share with a medium or high indicator, by country (2026). 8% of 706 homes are marked medium or high; 13 are marked high.
  • How the price of a cheap home changes with the distance to the airport (2026). €1,460/m² under 30 km from an airport, €1,080/m² between 30 and 60 km.
  • New-build prices per m² in Bali, Phuket, Koh Samui, Tulum and Batumi (2026). From €1,366/m² in Batumi to €3,661/m² in Phuket.
  • The cheapest homes per square metre in Europe (2026). Lowest in Europe: €98/m² in Markaryd, Kronoberg, Småland, Sweden; lowest median: Sweden, €680/m².
  • Homes by the sea under €100,000: where they are, country by country (2026). 94 homes by the sea at or under €100,000; most in Spain (25).
  • New homes on RealHunt in November 2026, by country. 208 homes added in November 2026; most in the United States (38).
