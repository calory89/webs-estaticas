## Costs and taxes

- What the buying costs are made of: Notary fees of 3 per thousand of the price (2026), customarily split between buyer and seller; registry fees of 9.11 to 13.33 per thousand depending on the price, plus 2%, paid by whoever asks for the registration, usually the buyer; and the registration tax (impuesto de registro), whose rate each department sets.
