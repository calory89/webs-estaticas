## Costs and taxes

- What the buying costs are made of: Transfer tax (ITP) of 2% for the buyer and 2% for the seller, on the cadastral value (valor real) set by the Dirección Nacional de Catastro; notary fees of about 3%; and an agent's fee of about 3%.
