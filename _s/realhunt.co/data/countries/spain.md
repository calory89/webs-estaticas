## Costs and taxes

- What the buying costs are made of: New build: 10% IVA + 0.5-1.5% AJD stamp duty + notary 0.5-1% + legal 1-2%; resale: ITP transfer tax 6-11% instead of IVA/AJD.
- Every year: IBI (council tax) and waste collection. Non-residents who do not rent the house out also file a small yearly imputed-income tax.
- Tax on rental income: Non-residents: EU/EEA 19% on net income; non-EU 24% on gross with no deductions.

## How to buy in Spain, step by step

1. **Get an NIE.** Your foreigner identity number, from a Spanish consulate or a police station in Spain. You need it to sign and to pay taxes.
2. **Open a Spanish bank account.** Most notaries and councils expect payments and bills to go through a Spanish account.
3. **Reserve with an arras contract.** A private contract with a deposit, usually 10%. If the seller backs out, they normally return it twice.
4. **Check the house.** Order a nota simple from the Land Registry (owner and debts). In the countryside, check the land status (suelo rústico) and that the buildings are legal. Ask for the last IBI receipts.
5. **Sign at the notary and register.** The escritura is signed before a notario and then registered at the Registro de la Propiedad.
6. **Pay the transfer tax.** Resale homes pay ITP of 6–10% depending on the region, plus notary and registry of roughly €1,000–2,000.
