## Costs and taxes

- What the buying costs are made of: Land Registry transfer fees of 3% up to €85,000, 5% to €170,000 and 8% above, halved for resales without VAT (1.5%, 2.5% and 4%); stamp duty abolished from 1 January 2026; lawyer about 1% plus VAT; small Land Registry charges.
- Every year: Yearly costs are municipal, sewerage and local authority fees, plus income tax and GHS on any rent.
- Tax on rental income: Non-residents pay income tax on Cyprus rental income at 20% to 35% after deducting 20% of the gross rent and loan interest, plus a 2.65% health contribution (GHS); the special defence contribution does not apply to them.

## How to buy in Cyprus, step by step

1. **Hire a lawyer.** Use a Cyprus lawyer to negotiate and draft the sale contract and run the checks.
2. **Check the title.** The lawyer searches the District Land Registry for ownership, mortgages, court claims and restrictions, and checks permits and VAT status.
3. **Sign the contract.** Sign the sale agreement with the price and payment terms. It is legally binding once signed.
4. **Lodge the contract.** Deposit the signed contract at the Land Registry under the Specific Performance Law to protect your right to the title.
5. **Apply for permission if non-EU.** Non-EU buyers apply to the Council of Ministers through the District Officer, usually after signing. The title cannot be registered without it.
6. **Transfer the title.** Pay the balance and transfer fees, then transfer the title deed at the District Land Registry.
