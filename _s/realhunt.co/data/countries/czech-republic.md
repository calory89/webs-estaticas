## Costs and taxes

- What the buying costs are made of: There is no real estate transfer tax: it was abolished for purchases registered in the land register from December 2019. The land register (katastr nemovitostí) charges an administrative fee of CZK 2,000 for each application to register the sale (návrh na vklad); the notary or lawyer who draws up the contract is paid on top.
