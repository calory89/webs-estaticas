## Costs and taxes

- What the buying costs are made of: Stamp duty of 1% of the price up to €1 million (2% from €1 million to €1.5 million and 6% above), solicitor's fees of about 1-1.5% plus 23% VAT, and registration fees of €425 to €800; the seller pays the estate agent, whose fee is usually built into the asking price.
