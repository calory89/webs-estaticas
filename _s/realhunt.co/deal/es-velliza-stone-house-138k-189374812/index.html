Why it made the list

A characterful 124 m² house with thick stone walls in the village of Velliza, about 20 km from Valladolid. What it costs to buy this home

Buying in Spain adds about 7.5-14% in taxes and fees on top of the price: roughly €10,350 to €19,320 here, so plan for about €148,350 to €157,320 in total.

New build: 10% IVA + 0.5-1.5% AJD stamp duty + notary 0.5-1% + legal 1-2%; resale: ITP transfer tax 6-11% instead of IVA/AJD.

Every year: IBI 0.4-1.1% of cadastral value (municipal), plus imputed income tax on unlet homes.
