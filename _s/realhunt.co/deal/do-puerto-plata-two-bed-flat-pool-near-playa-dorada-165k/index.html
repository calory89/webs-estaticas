What it costs to buy this home

Buying in the Dominican Republic adds about 4.25-7% in taxes and fees on top of the price: roughly €6,375 to €10,500 here, so plan for about €156,375 to €160,500 in total.

The buyer pays a 3% transfer tax (0% for qualifying first purchases under CONFOTUR tourism incentives), legal fees of about 1-2% and notary fees of about 0.25-1%, plus registration costs, while the seller pays the 2-3% agent fee.

Every year: The yearly IPI tax is 1% of the part of a person's total Dominican property value, as assessed by the tax office DGII, above RD$10,695,494 in 2026, so a single cheap home is often below the threshold.

General information, not tax advice: ask a local lawyer or notary for the exact figure.
