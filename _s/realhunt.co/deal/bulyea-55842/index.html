Buyers usually pay provincial land transfer tax on a sliding scale (Ontario: 0.5% up to $55,000 and 1% from $55,000 to $250,000), legal fees of about $500 to $1,500, title insurance of about $250 and an inspection of $300 to $1,000, while the seller usually pays the agents; foreign buyers also pay Ontario's 25% non-resident speculation tax anywhere in Ontario and British Columbia's 20% additional tax in Metro Vancouver and four other regions.

Can foreigners buy a home in Canada?

Mostly not for now: a federal ban stops non-Canadians from buying homes in cities and larger towns until January 1, 2027, with exceptions such as work permit holders and spouses of citizens or permanent residents. The ban does not cover rural areas and small towns, and the government has said it does not plan simply to extend it, but a replacement is being studied.
