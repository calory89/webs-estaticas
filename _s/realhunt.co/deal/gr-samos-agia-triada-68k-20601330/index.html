What it costs to buy this home

Buying in Greece adds about 5.5-7% in taxes and fees on top of the price: roughly €3,740 to €4,760 here, so plan for about €71,740 to €72,760 in total.

Transfer tax 3.09% of the price or the taxable value, whichever is higher; notary about 0.8-1% plus VAT; land registry fees about 0.5-0.7%; lawyer 1-2% plus VAT; if an agent is used, the buyer usually pays a commission of 1-3% plus VAT on top.

Every year: Owners on 1 January pay the unified property tax (ENFIA), calculated per property from its location, size, age and official zone value, with an extra charge if total Greek property exceeds €500,000.

Can foreigners buy a home in Greece?

Yes. Buyers from outside the EU and EFTA need a permit to buy in designated border areas (parts of northern Greece and eastern Aegean islands such as the Dodecanese and Rhodes). The government has announced a 15% transfer tax for non-EU buyers of homes from 1 January 2027, not yet final in September 2026.
