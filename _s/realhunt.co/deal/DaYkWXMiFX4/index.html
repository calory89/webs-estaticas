What it costs to buy this home

Buying in Norway adds about 2.5-3% in taxes and fees on top of the price: roughly €1,636 to €1,963 here, so plan for about €67,067 to €67,394 in total.

Document duty (dokumentavgift) of 2.5% of market value, a registration fee (tinglysingsgebyr) of NOK 545 for the deed and NOK 545 for any mortgage document; a flat in a housing cooperative (borettslag) has no document duty; the seller normally pays the agent.

Every year: Some municipalities charge property tax (eiendomsskatt), at most 4 per mille of the value after a reduction of at least 30%, and some also give a basic deduction; many charge nothing.

General information, not tax advice: ask a local lawyer or notary for the exact figure.

Can foreigners buy a home in Norway?

Yes. Anyone with the funds can buy property in Norway, and a built plot of up to 2 decares normally needs no concession (konsesjon) or self-declaration; owning property gives no right to live there.
