Why it made the list

Asking US$205,000 (about €186,300) for a single-storey brick house of 113 m² on a 475 m² plot in Colonia del Sacramento, the old Portuguese riverside town facing Buenos Aires. The advert puts it 300 metres from the Rambla Costanera and a short distance from the old town. It has three bedrooms, two full bathrooms, a modern kitchen open to the living and dining room, air conditioning in the living room and main bedroom, a garage and a private patio with a built-in barbecue. The back garden is large and green. It is described as in excellent condition and ready to live in.
