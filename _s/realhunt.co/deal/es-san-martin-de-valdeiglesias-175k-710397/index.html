What it costs to buy this home

Buying in Spain adds about 7.5-14% in taxes and fees on top of the price: roughly €13,125 to €24,500 here, so plan for about €188,125 to €199,500 in total.

New build: 10% IVA + 0.5-1.5% AJD stamp duty + notary 0.5-1% + legal 1-2%; resale: ITP transfer tax 6-11% instead of IVA/AJD.
