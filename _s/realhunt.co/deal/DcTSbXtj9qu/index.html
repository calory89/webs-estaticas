What it costs to buy this home

Buying in Japan adds about 6-9% in taxes and fees on top of the price: roughly €3,367 to €5,051 here, so plan for about €59,487 to €61,171 in total.

The buyer pays an agent fee capped at 3% plus ¥60,000 plus 10% consumption tax (with a special cap of up to ¥330,000 including tax for cheap vacant homes up to ¥8 million), registration tax of 1.5% on land (until March 2029) and 2% standard on the building, real estate acquisition tax of 3% of assessed value, stamp duty, and a judicial scrivener fee of about ¥100,000 to ¥200,000.

Every year: Fixed asset tax is 1.4% of the government's assessed value, plus city planning tax of up to 0.3% in urban areas, and small residential plots get a lower tax base.

General information, not tax advice: ask a local lawyer or notary for the exact figure.
