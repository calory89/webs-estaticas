Why it made the list

A semi-detached stone house in a quiet old village in San Siro, on the western shore of Lake Como near Menaggio, renovated a few years ago and sold fully furnished. Ground floor: living room with a fireplace and a separate kitchen. Upstairs: two bedrooms, one opening onto a small balcony with open views over the lake and the mountains, and a bathroom with a tub. Small private courtyard, public parking a short walk away, south-east facing, gas heating. The price was cut from €130,000 to €95,000 (-27%). The lake is about 5 minutes away; Lugano Airport about 40 minutes and Milan Malpensa about 1 h 10. At about €1,580/m² it is roughly 40% below the San Siro average for houses (€2,690/m², RealAdvisor, Oct 2026).

What it costs to buy this home

Buying in Italy adds about 4-15% in taxes and fees on top of the price: roughly €3,800 to €14,250 here, so plan for about €98,800 to €109,250 in total.
