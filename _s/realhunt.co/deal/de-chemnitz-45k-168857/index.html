What it costs to buy this home

Buying in Germany adds about 5-8.5% in taxes and fees on top of the price: roughly €2,250 to €3,825 here, so plan for about €47,250 to €48,825 in total.

Real estate transfer tax (Grunderwerbsteuer) of 3.5% to 6.5% depending on the state (3.5% in Bavaria, 6.5% in Brandenburg, North Rhine-Westphalia, Saarland and Schleswig-Holstein), plus notary and land registry fees (Notar- und Grundbuchkosten) of about 1.5-2%; if the seller hired the agent, the buyer pays half the commission by law, which is extra.

Every year: Owners pay property tax (Grundsteuer), calculated since 2025 as assessed value x a tax rate x a municipal multiplier (Hebesatz) that each municipality sets.

General information, not tax advice: ask a local lawyer or notary for the exact figure.
