What it costs to buy this home

Buying in Spain adds about 7.5-14% in taxes and fees on top of the price: roughly €11,175 to €20,860 here, so plan for about €160,175 to €169,860 in total.

New build: 10% IVA + 0.5-1.5% AJD stamp duty + notary 0.5-1% + legal 1-2%; resale: ITP transfer tax 6-11% instead of IVA/AJD.

Every year: IBI 0.4-1.1% of cadastral value (municipal), plus imputed income tax on unlet homes.
