Why it made the list

A traditional Gallurese stazzo in the historic centre of Luras, northern Sardinia, restored with respect for its exposed granite, wood and iron: a living room with an open kitchen around a big fireplace, a reed-and-beam ceiling, bold colours and pop touches, a double bedroom, a second bedroom, two bathrooms and an attic. Flower boxes on the stone façade, and the Costa Smeralda beaches about 40 minutes away. Asking €97,000, about €1,386/m².
