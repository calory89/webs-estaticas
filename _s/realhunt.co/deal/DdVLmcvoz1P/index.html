What it costs to buy this home

Buying in Sweden adds about 1.5-3.5% in taxes and fees on top of the price: roughly €1,365 to €3,185 here, so plan for about €92,365 to €94,185 in total.

Stamp duty on the title (lagfart) of 1.5% of the price or tax value, whichever is higher, plus a SEK 825 fee; if you borrow and need new mortgage deeds (pantbrev), 2% of their amount plus a SEK 375 fee; optional legal advice; the seller normally pays the agent, and a cooperative flat (bostadsrätt) has no lagfart.

Every year: Houses pay a municipal property fee (kommunal fastighetsavgift) of SEK 10,425 in 2026, or 0.75% of the tax value if that is lower.
