What it costs to buy this home

Buying in the Netherlands adds about 2-9% in taxes and fees on top of the price: roughly €6,500 to €29,250 here, so plan for about €331,500 to €354,250 in total.

Transfer tax (overdrachtsbelasting) of 8% if you will not live in the home, such as a buy-to-let or holiday home, or 2% if it becomes your main home; a notary fee for the transfer deed of around EUR 500; Kadaster registration fees; and mortgage costs if you borrow; the seller pays its own agent.

Every year: Owners pay municipal property tax (onroerendezaakbelasting, OZB) on the official WOZ value, at a rate each municipality sets, which in practice is a per mille figure; tenants of homes do not pay it.

General information, not tax advice: ask a local lawyer or notary for the exact figure.

Can foreigners buy a home in the Netherlands?

Yes. There are no nationality or residence rules and no surcharge for foreign buyers. Some cities, such as The Hague, restrict renting out cheaper homes after purchase without a permit (opkoopbescherming).
