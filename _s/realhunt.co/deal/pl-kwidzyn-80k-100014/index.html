What it costs to buy this home

Buying in Poland adds about 2.5-4% in taxes and fees on top of the price: roughly €1,994 to €3,190 here, so plan for about €81,734 to €82,930 in total.

Tax on civil law transactions (PCC) 2% of the value, paid by the buyer within 14 days of the deed; notary fee (taksa notarialna) capped at PLN 1,010 plus 0.4% of the value above PLN 60,000, plus 23% VAT; court fee of PLN 200 to enter ownership in the land register (plus PLN 100 for a new register); optional lawyer about 1%; the agent fee (about 2-3%) depends on the agreement.

Every year: Owners pay a local property tax (podatek od nieruchomości) set by the commune, capped in 2026 at PLN 1.25 per m² of usable floor space for flats and houses.

General information, not tax advice: ask a local lawyer or notary for the exact figure.

Can foreigners buy a home in Poland?

EU, EEA and Swiss citizens buy on the same terms as Poles. Other foreigners can buy an apartment without a permit, but usually need a permit from the Ministry of the Interior (MSWiA) to buy a house or land.
