Why it made the list

A charming village house from 1900, fully renovated with a brand-new roof, keeping its Alpujarra character: chestnut beams, a fireplace and original Granada tiles. Downstairs a cosy living room and the kitchen; upstairs two bedrooms, a bathroom and a new south-facing open terrace with views to the Mediterranean on clear days and to the Sierra de Gádor. Electric heating, wooden window frames and terrazzo floors. It overlooks the village park, La Fuentecilla. Asking €49,000, about €570/m².

What it costs to buy this home

Buying in Spain adds about 7.5-14% in taxes and fees on top of the price: roughly €3,675 to €6,860 here, so plan for about €52,675 to €55,860 in total.

New build: 10% IVA + 0.5-1.5% AJD stamp duty + notary 0.5-1% + legal 1-2%; resale: ITP transfer tax 6-11% instead of IVA/AJD.

Every year: IBI 0.4-1.1% of cadastral value (municipal), plus imputed income tax on unlet homes.
