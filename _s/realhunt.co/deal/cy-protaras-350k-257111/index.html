What it costs to buy this home

Buying in Cyprus adds about 2.5-3.5% in taxes and fees on top of the price: roughly €8,750 to €12,250 here, so plan for about €358,750 to €362,250 in total.

Land Registry transfer fees of 3% up to €85,000, 5% to €170,000 and 8% above, halved for resales without VAT (1.5%, 2.5% and 4%); stamp duty abolished from 1 January 2026; lawyer about 1% plus VAT; small Land Registry charges.

Every year: There is no annual national property tax (abolished in 2017); owners pay municipal fees of about €90 to €300 a year, plus sewerage and local authority charges.

Can foreigners buy a home in Cyprus?

EU citizens buy without restrictions. Non-EU buyers need permission from the Council of Ministers, applied for through the District Officer, and are normally limited to one home or a plot of up to 4,014 m².
