What it costs to buy this home

Buying in Montenegro adds about 1-4% in taxes and fees on top of the price: roughly €1,750 to €7,000 here, so plan for about €176,750 to €182,000 in total.

New build: 21% VAT is normally included in the developer price, buyer pays notary + ~1% legal; resale: 3% transfer tax (progressive on higher values) + notary + ~1% legal (GPG 4.01%).

Every year: Municipal, ~0.25-1% of assessed value (higher rates possible for tourist properties).
