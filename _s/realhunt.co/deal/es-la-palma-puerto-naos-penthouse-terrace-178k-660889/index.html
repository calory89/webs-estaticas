Why it made the list

A modern top-floor flat of 29 m² (27 m² usable) in Puerto Naos, on the sunny west coast of La Palma (Canary Islands), with a private sun terrace of about 29 m² looking over the rooftops to the Atlantic. Living room with an integrated kitchen, one bedroom with fitted wardrobes and a bathroom, all opening onto the terrace with loungers and a dining table. What it costs to buy this home

Buying in Spain adds about 7.5-14% in taxes and fees on top of the price: roughly €13,350 to €24,920 here, so plan for about €191,350 to €202,920 in total.

New build: 10% IVA + 0.5-1.5% AJD stamp duty + notary 0.5-1% + legal 1-2%; resale: ITP transfer tax 6-11% instead of IVA/AJD.

Every year: IBI 0.4-1.1% of cadastral value (municipal), plus imputed income tax on unlet homes.
