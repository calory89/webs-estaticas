How RealHunt works

## How RealHunt makes money

Buyers pay nothing. RealHunt earns a fee only from developers of new-build projects, and only when a buyer it introduced buys. It takes no money for the resale homes it lists, for a place in a list or for a score.

Updated 27 November 2026

### Where the money comes from

- New-build projects: in the [Top 100 rankings](https://realhunt.co/top) (Bali, Phuket, Koh Samui, Tulum and Batumi), RealHunt can earn a fee from the developer if a buyer it introduced buys. The ranking is computed the same way for every project, paying or not ([how the ranking works](https://realhunt.co/top/how-we-rank)).

### Where it does not come from

- Buyers: searching, saving homes, alerts, guides, data and calculators are free.
- Resale homes: RealHunt is not an estate agent, does not sell them and takes no commission. Each home links to its original advert, and you deal with the seller or the agent directly.
- Places and scores: nobody pays to be listed or to rank higher.
