Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Cyprus

Can foreigners buy? EU citizens buy without restrictions. Non-EU buyers need permission from the Council of Ministers, applied for through the District Officer, and are normally limited to one home or a plot of up to 4,014 m².

Taxes and fees add about 2.5 to 3.5% to the price: Land Registry transfer fees of 3% up to €85,000, 5% to €170,000 and 8% above, halved for resales without VAT (1.5%, 2.5% and 4%); stamp duty abolished from 1 January 2026; lawyer about 1% plus VAT; small Land Registry charges. On the median home on this page (€341,000) that is €8,525 to €11,935, so €349,525 to €352,935 in all.
