Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Japan

Can foreigners buy? Yes. Foreigners can buy freehold land and homes on the same terms as Japanese citizens, even on a tourist visa, though buying does not give a visa or residency.

Taxes and fees add about 6 to 9% to the price: The buyer pays an agent fee capped at 3% plus ¥60,000 plus 10% consumption tax (with a special cap of up to ¥330,000 including tax for cheap vacant homes up to ¥8 million), registration tax of 1.5% on land (until March 2029) and 2% standard on the building, real estate acquisition tax of 3% of assessed value, stamp duty, and a judicial scrivener fee of about ¥100,000 to ¥200,000. On the median home on this page (€79,500) that is €4,770 to €7,155, so €84,270 to €86,655 in all.
