Questions people ask

Can foreigners buy a house in Canada?

Land transfer tax 0 to 2% depending on the province, plus a lawyer, about C$1,500. Until 1 January 2027 most non-Canadians cannot buy homes in Canadian cities (foreign buyer ban); Canadians and permanent residents can.

How much do buying costs add to the price in Canada?

Plan for roughly 1.5% of the price plus about €1,500 in fixed fees. On a €92,552 home that is around €2,888. Each home page on RealHunt shows the estimate for that exact price.

Are these homes ready to live in?

That is the rule for everything we publish: no ruins, no land, no homes that clearly need major work. Old houses are fine if they have been looked after. Always get a survey before you buy.

How often is this list updated?

Every day. Homes that sell or get reported as gone are taken down, and new ones are added after a person checks them.
