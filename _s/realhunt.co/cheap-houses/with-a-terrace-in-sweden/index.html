Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Sweden

Can foreigners buy? Yes. There are no citizenship or residence rules for buying a home in Sweden, but you need a Swedish personal identity number or coordination number (samordningsnummer) to register ownership.

Taxes and fees add about 1.5 to 3.5% to the price: Stamp duty on the title (lagfart) of 1.5% of the price or tax value, whichever is higher, plus a SEK 825 fee; if you borrow and need new mortgage deeds (pantbrev), 2% of their amount plus a SEK 375 fee; optional legal advice; the seller normally pays the agent, and a cooperative flat (bostadsrätt) has no lagfart. On the median home on this page (€82,000) that is €1,230 to €2,870, so €83,230 to €84,870 in all.
