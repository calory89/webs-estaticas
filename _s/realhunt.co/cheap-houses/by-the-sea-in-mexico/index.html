Questions people ask

Can foreigners buy a house in Mexico?

Acquisition tax (ISAI) 2 to 5% plus notary fees, about 6 to 8% in total. Foreigners can buy; within 50 km of the coast it is done through a bank trust (fideicomiso), about US$500 to 1,000 a year.

How much do buying costs add to the price in Mexico?

Plan for roughly 6% of the price plus about €1,000 in fixed fees. On a €83,448 home that is around €6,007. Each home page on RealHunt shows the estimate for that exact price.

Are these homes ready to live in?

That is the rule for everything we publish: no ruins, no land, no homes that clearly need major work. Old houses are fine if they have been looked after. Always get a survey before you buy.

How often is this list updated?

Every day. Homes that sell or get reported as gone are taken down, and new ones are added after a person checks them.
