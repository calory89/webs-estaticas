Questions people ask

Can foreigners buy a house in Japan?

Acquisition tax 3 to 4%, registration about 2%, agent 3% plus ¥60,000, stamp duty. Foreigners can own freehold.

How much do buying costs add to the price in Japan?

Plan for roughly 8% of the price plus about €1,500 in fixed fees. On a €31,415 home that is around €4,013. Each home page on RealHunt shows the estimate for that exact price.

Are these homes ready to live in?

That is the rule for everything we publish: no ruins, no land, no homes that clearly need major work. Old houses are fine if they have been looked after. Always get a survey before you buy.

How often is this list updated?

Every day. Homes that sell or get reported as gone are taken down, and new ones are added after a person checks them.
