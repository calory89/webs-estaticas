Questions people ask

Can foreigners buy a house in Italy?

9% registration tax on cadastral value for a second home (2% as main residence), notary and registry about €2,500, agent 3% plus VAT if there is one.

How much do buying costs add to the price in Italy?

Plan for roughly 10% of the price plus about €2,500 in fixed fees. On a €57,000 home that is around €8,200. Each home page on RealHunt shows the estimate for that exact price.

Are these homes ready to live in?

That is the rule for everything we publish: no ruins, no land, no homes that clearly need major work. Old houses are fine if they have been looked after. Always get a survey before you buy.

How often is this list updated?

Every day. Homes that sell or get reported as gone are taken down, and new ones are added after a person checks them.
