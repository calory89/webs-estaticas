Buying costs and rules for foreigners in the Netherlands

Can foreigners buy? Yes. There are no nationality or residence rules and no surcharge for foreign buyers. Some cities, such as The Hague, restrict renting out cheaper homes after purchase without a permit (opkoopbescherming).

Taxes and fees add about 2 to 9% to the price: Transfer tax (overdrachtsbelasting) of 8% if you will not live in the home, such as a buy-to-let or holiday home, or 2% if it becomes your main home; a notary fee for the transfer deed of around EUR 500; Kadaster registration fees; and mortgage costs if you borrow; the seller pays its own agent. On the median home on this page (€300,000) that is €6,000 to €27,000, so €306,000 to €327,000 in all.
