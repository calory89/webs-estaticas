Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Spain

Can foreigners buy? Yes, no restrictions at present; the government has proposed a 100% tax on (or ban for) non-EU non-resident buyers, not enacted.

Taxes and fees add about 7.5 to 14% to the price: New build: 10% IVA + 0.5-1.5% AJD stamp duty + notary 0.5-1% + legal 1-2%; resale: ITP transfer tax 6-11% instead of IVA/AJD. On the median home on this page (€155,000) that is €11,625 to €21,700, so €166,625 to €176,700 in all.
