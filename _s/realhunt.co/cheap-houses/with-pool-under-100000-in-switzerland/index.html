Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Switzerland

Can foreigners buy? Under the Lex Koller law, EU/EFTA citizens living in Switzerland and holders of a C permit buy like Swiss citizens, and non-EU holders of a B permit can buy a home they live in. People living abroad need a cantonal permit and can usually only buy one holiday home in a tourist area, within national quotas; a proposed tightening was under consultation in 2026.

Taxes and fees add about 1 to 5% to the price: Transfer tax (Handänderungssteuer, droits de mutation) 0% to about 3.3% depending on the canton; notary about 0.1-0.5%; land register fee, a small fixed amount or percentage; optional lawyer 1-2%; the agent fee (2-3%) is paid by the seller. On the median home on this page (€80,700) that is €807 to €4,035, so €81,507 to €84,735 in all.

To keep the whole purchase within €100,000, look at asking prices up to about €95,200: all 4 homes on this page fit.
