Questions people ask

Can foreigners buy a house in Bulgaria?

Local transfer tax 2 to 3%, notary about 1%. Foreigners cannot own land directly, apartments are fine.

How much do buying costs add to the price in Bulgaria?

Plan for roughly 4% of the price plus about €800 in fixed fees. On a €66,000 home that is around €3,440. Each home page on RealHunt shows the estimate for that exact price.

Are these homes ready to live in?

That is the rule for everything we publish: no ruins, no land, no homes that clearly need major work. Old houses are fine if they have been looked after. Always get a survey before you buy.

How often is this list updated?

Every day. Homes that sell or get reported as gone are taken down, and new ones are added after a person checks them.
