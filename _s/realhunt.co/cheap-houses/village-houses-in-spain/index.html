Questions people ask

Can foreigners buy a house in Spain?

Transfer tax 6 to 10% depending on the region, notary and registry about €1,500, agent usually paid by the seller. Add a lawyer, about €1,500.

How much do buying costs add to the price in Spain?

Plan for roughly 9% of the price plus about €1,500 in fixed fees. On a €89,500 home that is around €9,555. Each home page on RealHunt shows the estimate for that exact price.

Are these homes ready to live in?

That is the rule for everything we publish: no ruins, no land, no homes that clearly need major work. Old houses are fine if they have been looked after. Always get a survey before you buy.

How often is this list updated?

Every day. Homes that sell or get reported as gone are taken down, and new ones are added after a person checks them.
