Questions people ask

Can foreigners buy a house in Denmark?

Registration 0.6% plus about DKK 1,850. Non-residents need Ministry of Justice permission unless from the EU and moving in.

How much do buying costs add to the price in Denmark?

Plan for roughly 1% of the price plus about €1,500 in fixed fees. On a €93,200 home that is around €2,432. Each home page on RealHunt shows the estimate for that exact price.

Are these homes ready to live in?

That is the rule for everything we publish: no ruins, no land, no homes that clearly need major work. Old houses are fine if they have been looked after. Always get a survey before you buy.

How often is this list updated?

Every day. Homes that sell or get reported as gone are taken down, and new ones are added after a person checks them.
