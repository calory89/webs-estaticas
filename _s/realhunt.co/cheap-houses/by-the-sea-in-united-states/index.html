Questions people ask

Can foreigners buy a house in the United States?

Buyer closing costs are usually 2 to 5%: title insurance, escrow and recording fees. Anyone can buy, no residency or visa needed. Property tax is yearly and set by the county; the listing usually shows it.

How much do buying costs add to the price in the United States?

Plan for roughly 3% of the price plus about €1,000 in fixed fees. On a €389,800 home that is around €12,694. Each home page on RealHunt shows the estimate for that exact price.

Are these homes ready to live in?

That is the rule for everything we publish: no ruins, no land, no homes that clearly need major work. Old houses are fine if they have been looked after. Always get a survey before you buy.
