Questions people ask

Can foreigners buy a house in Poland?

Civil-law transaction tax (PCC) 2% on a resale flat, notary about 0.5 to 1% plus court registry fees. Agents often charge the buyer 2 to 3% plus VAT; some listings say no commission. EU citizens can buy flats freely; others can buy a flat without a permit but need one for a house with land.

How much do buying costs add to the price in Poland?

Plan for roughly 3% of the price plus about €700 in fixed fees. On a €68,316 home that is around €2,749. Each home page on RealHunt shows the estimate for that exact price.

Are these homes ready to live in?

That is the rule for everything we publish: no ruins, no land, no homes that clearly need major work. Old houses are fine if they have been looked after. Always get a survey before you buy.
