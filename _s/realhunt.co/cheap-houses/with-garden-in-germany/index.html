Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Germany

Can foreigners buy? Yes. There are no restrictions on foreigners buying property in Germany, though non-EU buyers usually need a bigger down payment to get a loan.

Taxes and fees add about 5 to 8.5% to the price: Real estate transfer tax (Grunderwerbsteuer) of 3.5% to 6.5% depending on the state (3.5% in Bavaria, 6.5% in Brandenburg, North Rhine-Westphalia, Saarland and Schleswig-Holstein), plus notary and land registry fees (Notar- und Grundbuchkosten) of about 1.5-2%; if the seller hired the agent, the buyer pays half the commission by law, which is extra. On the median home on this page (€45,000) that is €2,250 to €3,825, so €47,250 to €48,825 in all.
