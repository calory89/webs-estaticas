Questions people ask

Can foreigners buy a house in Netherlands?

Transfer tax 2% on a home you will live in (0% once for buyers aged 18 to 34 below €555,000), notary and land registry about €1,500 to €2,500, plus valuation and mortgage advice if you borrow. In Amsterdam many flats stand on city leasehold (erfpacht): check whether the ground rent is bought off.

How much do buying costs add to the price in Netherlands?

Plan for roughly 3% of the price plus about €2,500 in fixed fees. On a €325,000 home that is around €12,250. Each home page on RealHunt shows the estimate for that exact price.

Are these homes ready to live in?

That is the rule for everything we publish: no ruins, no land, no homes that clearly need major work. Old houses are fine if they have been looked after. Always get a survey before you buy.

How often is this list updated?

Every day. Homes that sell or get reported as gone are taken down, and new ones are added after a person checks them.
