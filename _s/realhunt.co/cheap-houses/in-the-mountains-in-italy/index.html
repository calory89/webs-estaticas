Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Italy

Can foreigners buy? Yes for EU citizens; non-EU buyers need reciprocity (US, UK, Canada, Australia qualify).

Taxes and fees add about 4 to 15% to the price: Registration tax 9% on a second home (resale), or VAT on new builds; notary 1-2% + legal ~1% + agent ~3% (GPG roundtrip 10-18%). On the median home on this page (€59,000) that is €2,360 to €8,850, so €61,360 to €67,850 in all.
