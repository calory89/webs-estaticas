Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Portugal

Can foreigners buy? Yes, same rights as Portuguese citizens (IMT increase for foreign second-home buyers announced Sept 2025).

Taxes and fees add about 3 to 12% to the price: IMT transfer tax 0-8% (progressive) + stamp duty 0.8% + notary/registry 0.9-1.2% + legal 1.25-2%. On the median home on this page (€76,500) that is €2,295 to €9,180, so €78,795 to €85,680 in all.
