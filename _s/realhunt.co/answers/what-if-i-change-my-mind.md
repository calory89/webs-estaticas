# What if I change my mind after signing?

## Short answer

Only some countries give the buyer a legal right to withdraw for free: France, 10 calendar days, and the Netherlands, 3 days. In Germany and Sweden nothing binds you until the notary's contract or the signed purchase contract. In Spain, Greece, Bulgaria and Japan, once a deposit is paid, changing your mind costs you the deposit.
