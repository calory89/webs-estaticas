## Short answer

Because there are more houses than households that want them. Japan's 2023 housing survey counted 9 million vacant dwellings, 13.8% of all homes, the highest share ever recorded. Empty houses find few buyers, so prices are low, and foreigners can buy them freehold on the same terms as Japanese citizens.
