## Short answer

Yes, in several countries the deed can be signed by someone holding your power of attorney: our files say so for Italy, France, Germany and Greece. A power signed in your own country normally needs an apostille to be accepted in another. Tax numbers can often be obtained at a consulate or through a representative, and in Japan the legal explanation can be given by video call.
