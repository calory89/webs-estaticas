## Short answer

Owning a house does not make you a tax resident; living in it for long enough can. There is no single 183-day rule: Spain counts more than 183 days in the calendar year and also looks at where your economic interests are, the United Kingdom has several tests, the United States adds up three years and Germany looks at a continuous stay of more than six months.
