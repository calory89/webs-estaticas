## Short answer

The law of the country where the house stands decides how co-owners hold it, not your relationship. In England you choose between two forms with opposite results on death. In Spain shares are presumed equal unless you write otherwise, and any co-owner can ask to divide the property at any time. Write the shares, and what happens if one of you wants out, into the deed.
