## Short answer

If you live legally in an EU country, a bank in any EU country cannot refuse you a basic payment account just because you live elsewhere. Outside that right, each bank decides. Spain, Sweden and the Netherlands in practice expect a local account or one that can pay the notary on time; the price itself almost always travels by bank transfer to the notary or the seller, never in cash.
