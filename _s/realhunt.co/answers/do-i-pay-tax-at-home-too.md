# Do I pay tax in my own country too?

## Short answer

Often yes, but usually not twice in full. The country where the house stands taxes it whoever you are. If your own country taxes residents on worldwide income, as the United Kingdom and the United States do, the rent and the gain go in your return there too, with relief for the tax already paid abroad. The relief is not automatic: you have to claim it.
