## Short answer

A court auction can be cheaper than the market, and it moves the risk to you. In Spain a bidder first deposits 20% of the valuation and, by bidding, accepts the title as it stands and the charges that rank before the debt being enforced. In Germany the security is one tenth of the market value set by the court, and a first-round bid under half that value is refused. You bid without the checks of a normal purchase.
