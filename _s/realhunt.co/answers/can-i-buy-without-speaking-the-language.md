## Short answer

Yes. Our country files list no language requirement for buyers, but the deed is drawn up in it, and the law protects you by requiring a translation. In Germany the notary must have the deed translated for a party who does not know enough German; in Spain an interpreter is required when the notary cannot explain the content personally; in Bulgaria a sworn translator signs with you.
