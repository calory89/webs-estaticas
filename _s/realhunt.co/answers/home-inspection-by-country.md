## Short answer

It depends on who the country makes responsible. France obliges the seller to give you a file of technical surveys. In the Netherlands, Sweden, Germany and the United States no report comes with the house: an inspection is something the buyer orders and pays for. In Denmark the buyer's lawyer reviews a condition report before the contract becomes final.
