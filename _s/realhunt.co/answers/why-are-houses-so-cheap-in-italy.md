## Short answer

Because more than a quarter of Italian homes have nobody living in them all year. The 2021 census counted 9.6 million dwellings not occupied by a resident, 27.2% of the total. Homes that nobody lives in find few buyers, and prices follow. The purchase tax is charged on the cadastral value, not on the price.
