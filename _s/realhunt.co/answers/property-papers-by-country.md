## Short answer

Every country keeps a public record of who owns a property and which debts sit on it, and in most of them you can read it before you pay anything. In Spain, Italy, Portugal and England a buyer can get the extract directly; in Germany you need to show a legitimate interest. Ask for it before the deposit, not after.
