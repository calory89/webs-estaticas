## Short answer

Brexit did not stop British citizens buying homes in the EU: in most countries they buy on the same terms as before. What changed is everything around the house. Without a visa you can spend 90 days in every 180 in the whole Schengen area, you are taxed as a non-EU owner and your pet needs a new certificate for every trip.
