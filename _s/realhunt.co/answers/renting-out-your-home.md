## Short answer

Before you count on any rent, check two things in the country of the house: whether a holiday let needs a licence or a registration number, and how rent paid to a non-resident is taxed. Spain, Italy and Portugal all require registration for tourist lets, and some cities have stopped giving new licences. We promise no income: the table below is about rules, not returns.
