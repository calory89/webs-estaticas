## Short answer

For the EU the order is: microchip, rabies vaccination, then the travel document. Between EU countries that document is the European pet passport, which only owners resident in the EU can get. From Great Britain and other non-EU countries it is an animal health certificate, issued by a vet for each trip. Dogs going to Finland, Ireland, Malta, Norway or Northern Ireland also need tapeworm treatment.
