How RealHunt works

What we never do

RealHunt never invents urgency, reviews, returns or numbers, never sells a place in a list, never charges buyers and never publishes a phone number. The build of the site checks every page for false urgency, promised returns, phone numbers and review markup, and reports what it finds.

Updated 28 November 2026

No false urgency

No countdowns, no "only a few left", no offers that end on a date we made up. A home is on the site until its advert goes, and we say so when it does.

No invented reviews or stars

We publish no reviews and no star ratings, and our pages carry no review markup for search engines. The score is our own grade, and it says so.

No promised returns or rents

No text says a price will rise or that a home will earn a given rent. Where we give rental tax rules, they are general information.

No paid places

Nobody pays to be listed or to rank higher, and a score cannot be bought. Our founder's own project is labelled as such and never takes part in any ranking.

No fees from buyers

Buyers pay nothing, and we do not sell the resale homes we list: each one links to its original advert (how we make money).

No phone numbers

We publish no phone numbers and no messaging links. You reach us by email: carlos@equinoxinvestmentgroup.com.

No "sold" we did not see

When an advert disappears we say it is no longer online, not that the home was sold (how we check).

No figure without a source

Prices and counts come from the homes on the site; rules, taxes and costs from the sources linked on each page. A figure we could not find in a source is left blank, not guessed (editorial policy).

No inflated numbers

We say how many homes there are: 1,058 today. Not "thousands".

No photos passed off as ours

The photos belong to the original adverts. We do not license them and we do not retouch them to make a home look better.

Seen one of these on RealHunt? Tell us at carlos@equinoxinvestmentgroup.com and we will fix it and note it in the corrections.
