Buying a home in Japan: what it really costs

Can foreigners buy?
Yes. Foreigners can buy freehold land and homes on the same terms as Japanese citizens, even on a tourist visa, though buying does not give a visa or residency.
Costs on top of the price
6-9% · The buyer pays an agent fee capped at 3% plus ¥60,000 plus 10% consumption tax (with a special cap of up to ¥330,000 including tax for cheap vacant homes up to ¥8 million), registration tax of 1.5% on land (until March 2029) and 2% standard on the building, real estate acquisition tax of 3% of assessed value, stamp duty, and a judicial scrivener fee of about ¥100,000 to ¥200,000.
Yearly property tax
Fixed asset tax is 1.4% of the government's assessed value, plus city planning tax of up to 0.3% in urban areas, and small residential plots get a lower tax base.
Tax on rental income
Rent paid to a non-resident is subject to 20.42% withholding (except when an individual rents it as their own home), and the owner must file a tax return each year through a tax representative in Japan to settle the final tax.
