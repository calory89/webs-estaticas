Buying a home in Greece: what it really costs

Can foreigners buy?
Yes. Buyers from outside the EU and EFTA need a permit to buy in designated border areas (parts of northern Greece and eastern Aegean islands such as the Dodecanese and Rhodes). The government has announced a 15% transfer tax for non-EU buyers of homes from 1 January 2027, not yet final in September 2026.
Costs on top of the price
5.5-7% · Transfer tax 3.09% of the price or the taxable value, whichever is higher; notary about 0.8-1% plus VAT; land registry fees about 0.5-0.7%; lawyer 1-2% plus VAT; if an agent is used, the buyer usually pays a commission of 1-3% plus VAT on top.
Yearly property tax
Owners on 1 January pay the unified property tax (ENFIA), calculated per property from its location, size, age and official zone value, with an extra charge if total Greek property exceeds €500,000.
Tax on rental income
Non-residents pay Greek tax on Greek rental income at 15% up to €12,000, 25% to €24,000, 35% to €36,000 and 45% above, after a flat 5% deduction for expenses.
