Buying a home in Cyprus: what it really costs

Can foreigners buy?
EU citizens buy without restrictions. Non-EU buyers need permission from the Council of Ministers, applied for through the District Officer, and are normally limited to one home or a plot of up to 4,014 m².
Costs on top of the price
2.5-3.5% · Land Registry transfer fees of 3% up to €85,000, 5% to €170,000 and 8% above, halved for resales without VAT (1.5%, 2.5% and 4%); stamp duty abolished from 1 January 2026; lawyer about 1% plus VAT; small Land Registry charges.
Yearly property tax
There is no annual national property tax (abolished in 2017); owners pay municipal fees of about €90 to €300 a year, plus sewerage and local authority charges.
Tax on rental income
Non-residents pay income tax on Cyprus rental income at 20% to 35% after deducting 20% of the gross rent and loan interest, plus a 2.65% health contribution (GHS); the special defence contribution does not apply to them.
