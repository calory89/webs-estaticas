Buying a home in the United States: what it really costs

Can foreigners buy?
Yes. There is no federal ban on foreigners buying homes, but about 30 states have added some limits, and Texas and Florida restrict buyers from certain countries such as China.
Costs on top of the price
2-5% · Closing costs vary by state and typically run 2-5% of the loan amount, covering title search and title insurance, recording fees, attorney fees, appraisal and lender fees; since August 2024 the seller no longer has to pay the buyer's agent, so the buyer may pay it unless the seller agrees to; FIRPTA does not apply when you buy but does when a foreigner later sells, with the buyer generally withholding 15% of the sale price.
Yearly property tax
Property tax is set locally and in 2024 averaged about 1.22% of value across the largest city in each state, from 0.30% in Honolulu to 3.02% in Detroit.
Tax on rental income
By default a non-resident pays 30% of gross rent (or a lower treaty rate), withheld at source, or can elect to treat the rent as business income and pay graduated rates on net income by filing Form 1040-NR and giving Form W-8ECI to the withholding agent.
