Buying a home in Sweden: what it really costs

Can foreigners buy?
Yes. There are no citizenship or residence rules for buying a home in Sweden, but you need a Swedish personal identity number or coordination number (samordningsnummer) to register ownership.
Costs on top of the price
1.5-3.5% · Stamp duty on the title (lagfart) of 1.5% of the price or tax value, whichever is higher, plus a SEK 825 fee; if you borrow and need new mortgage deeds (pantbrev), 2% of their amount plus a SEK 375 fee; optional legal advice; the seller normally pays the agent, and a cooperative flat (bostadsrätt) has no lagfart.
Yearly property tax
Houses pay a municipal property fee (kommunal fastighetsavgift) of SEK 10,425 in 2026, or 0.75% of the tax value if that is lower.
Tax on rental income
Non-resident individuals pay 30% tax on net rental income as capital income; for a private home you can first deduct SEK 40,000 per property and 20% of the rent (the 20% does not apply to a bostadsrätt).
