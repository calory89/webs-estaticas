Buying a home in Germany: what it really costs

Can foreigners buy?
Yes. There are no restrictions on foreigners buying property in Germany, though non-EU buyers usually need a bigger down payment to get a loan.
Costs on top of the price
5-8.5% · Real estate transfer tax (Grunderwerbsteuer) of 3.5% to 6.5% depending on the state (3.5% in Bavaria, 6.5% in Brandenburg, North Rhine-Westphalia, Saarland and Schleswig-Holstein), plus notary and land registry fees (Notar- und Grundbuchkosten) of about 1.5-2%; if the seller hired the agent, the buyer pays half the commission by law, which is extra.
Yearly property tax
Owners pay property tax (Grundsteuer), calculated since 2025 as assessed value x a tax rate x a municipal multiplier (Hebesatz) that each municipality sets.
Tax on rental income
Non-resident owners are taxed in Germany on net rental income at the normal progressive income tax rates, but without the basic tax-free allowance (Grundfreibetrag).
