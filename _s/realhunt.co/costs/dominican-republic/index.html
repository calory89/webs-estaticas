Buying a home in the Dominican Republic: what it really costs

Can foreigners buy?
Yes. Foreigners have the same rights as Dominican citizens to buy land and homes, with no restrictions.
Costs on top of the price
4.25-7% · The buyer pays a 3% transfer tax (0% for qualifying first purchases under CONFOTUR tourism incentives), legal fees of about 1-2% and notary fees of about 0.25-1%, plus registration costs, while the seller pays the 2-3% agent fee.
Yearly property tax
The yearly IPI tax is 1% of the part of a person's total Dominican property value, as assessed by the tax office DGII, above RD$10,695,494 in 2026, so a single cheap home is often below the threshold.
Tax on rental income
Rent paid abroad to a non-resident is generally subject to 27% withholding.
