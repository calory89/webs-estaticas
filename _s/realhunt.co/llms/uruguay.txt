### Buying costs and rules for foreigners in Uruguay

Can foreigners buy? Yes. There are no limits on the types of property foreign buyers can purchase in Uruguay, and they have the same property rights as citizens.

Taxes and fees add about 5 to 8% to the price: Transfer tax (ITP) of 2% for the buyer and 2% for the seller, on the cadastral value (valor real) set by the Dirección Nacional de Catastro; notary fees of about 3%; and an agent's fee of about 3%. On the median home on this page (€180,300) that is €9,015 to €14,424, so €189,315 to €194,724 in all.

At the same percentages: on €40,000, €2,000 to €3,200; on €80,000, €4,000 to €6,400; on €150,000, €7,500 to €12,000.
