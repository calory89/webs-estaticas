### Buying costs and rules for foreigners in the Czech Republic

Can foreigners buy? Yes. Foreigners can buy homes and land in the Czech Republic on the same terms as Czechs: the limits on homes ended on 1 May 2009 and those on farmland and forest on 1 May 2011.

Yearly property tax: Owners pay a yearly tax on land and buildings (daň z nemovitých věcí); from 2024 the rates rose to 1.8 times their old level on average, and each municipality can multiply the tax by a local coefficient between 0.5 and 5. The return is due by 31 January and the tax by 31 May.

## Costs and taxes

- What the buying costs are made of: There is no real estate transfer tax: it was abolished for purchases registered in the land register from December 2019. The land register (katastr nemovitostí) charges an administrative fee of CZK 2,000 for each application to register the sale (návrh na vklad); the notary or lawyer who draws up the contract is paid on top.
