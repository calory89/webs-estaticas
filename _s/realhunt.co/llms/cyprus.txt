### Cyprus: a home without its own title deed, and the 6 months to deposit the contract

When a home has no title deed of its own yet, for example a unit that is still being built, the Department of Lands and Surveys recommends signing a sale contract and depositing it with the Department at once, and no later than 6 months after signing. The deposit creates a charge on the property, blocks a later contract for the same unit and lets the buyer ask a court to order the transfer if the seller does not perform. A contract deposited after the 6 months pays transfer fees that are 10% higher.

Source: [Department of Lands and Surveys of Cyprus, Purchase of Immovable Property: the importance of depositing the sale contract](https://portal.dls.moi.gov.cy/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Purchase-of-Immovable-Property-The-importance-of-Depositing-the-Sale-Contract-at-the-Department-of-Lands-and-Surveys.pdf). ## How to buy a home in Cyprus as a foreigner

The steps, papers and taxes, in plain words. General information, not legal advice: use a local lawyer before you sign.

### Can foreigners buy?

EU citizens buy without restrictions. Non-EU buyers need permission from the Council of Ministers, applied for through the District Officer, and are normally limited to one home or a plot of up to 4,014 m².

### The steps

- Hire a lawyer: Use a Cyprus lawyer to negotiate and draft the sale contract and run the checks.
- Check the title: The lawyer searches the District Land Registry for ownership, mortgages, court claims and restrictions, and checks permits and VAT status.
- Sign the contract: Sign the sale agreement with the price and payment terms. It is legally binding once signed.
- Lodge the contract: Deposit the signed contract at the Land Registry under the Specific Performance Law to protect your right to the title.
- Apply for permission if non-EU: Non-EU buyers apply to the Council of Ministers through the District Officer, usually after signing. The title cannot be registered without it.
- Transfer the title: Pay the balance and transfer fees, then transfer the title deed at the District Land Registry.

### Costs on top of the price

About 2.5-3.5%: Land Registry transfer fees of 3% up to €85,000, 5% to €170,000 and 8% above, halved for resales without VAT (1.5%, 2.5% and 4%); stamp duty abolished from 1 January 2026; lawyer about 1% plus VAT; small Land Registry charges.

### Every year

Yearly costs are municipal, sewerage and local authority fees, plus income tax and GHS on any rent.

Yearly property tax: There is no annual national property tax (abolished in 2017); owners pay municipal fees of about €90 to €300 a year, plus sewerage and local authority charges.

Tax on rental income: Non-residents pay income tax on Cyprus rental income at 20% to 35% after deducting 20% of the gross rent and loan interest, plus a 2.65% health contribution (GHS); the special defence contribution does not apply to them.
