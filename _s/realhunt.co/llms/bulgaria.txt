### Bulgaria has used the euro since 1 January 2026

Bulgaria joined the euro area on 1 January 2026 at the fixed rate of 1.95583 leva per euro, and since 1 February 2026 the euro has been the only legal tender. An older listing that still shows a price in leva converts at that fixed rate, and a buyer who already holds euros has no exchange cost on the purchase.

Leva could be exchanged free of charge at commercial banks and some post offices in Bulgaria until 30 June 2026. The Bulgarian National Bank still exchanges them free of charge, with no time limit.

## How to buy a home in Bulgaria as a foreigner

The steps, papers and taxes, in plain words. General information, not legal advice: use a local lawyer before you sign.

### Can foreigners buy?

Yes, buildings and apartments, with rights similar to locals; per GPG, foreigners can't own land directly, so a Bulgarian company is the usual route.

### The steps

- Check what you can own: Non-EU citizens can buy apartments and buildings in their own name but not land. To buy a house with its plot, a non-EU buyer usually sets up a Bulgarian company.
- Hire a lawyer and check the property: A lawyer checks the title and debts, which usually takes 5 to 10 business days.
- Sign a preliminary contract: Pay a deposit, usually 10%. If you pull out you lose it, and if the seller pulls out they owe you double.
- Get your documents ready: You need your passport and a Bulgarian personal tax number from the National Revenue Agency. Payments of EUR 5,113 or more must go by bank transfer.
- Sign the deed before a notary: Sign the notarial deed before a notary in the property's district, with a sworn translator if you do not speak Bulgarian. The notary files it with the Registry Agency, usually in 1 to 3 business days.
- Declare the property to the municipality: File a tax declaration with the local municipality within 2 months of buying, and move the utility accounts into your name.

### Costs on top of the price

About 3.2-8.5%: Transfer tax 0.1-3% (municipal; Sofia 2.5%) + notary 0.1-1.5% + legal 0.5-1% + agent 2.5-3%.

### Every year

Yearly costs are low: municipal property tax is 0.01% to 0.45% of the tax assessment value, with a 5% discount if paid in full by April 30, plus a municipal waste collection fee. Owning through a company adds about EUR 500 to 1,500 a year in running costs.

Yearly property tax: 0.01-0.45% of the property's tax value plus a municipal garbage fee.

Tax on rental income: Non-residents: 10% final tax on gross rent, paid quarterly, no deductions.

## Costs and taxes

- What the buying costs are made of: Transfer tax 0.1-3% (municipal; Sofia 2.5%) + notary 0.1-1.5% + legal 0.5-1% + agent 2.5-3%.
- Every year: Yearly costs are low: municipal property tax is 0.01% to 0.45% of the tax assessment value, with a 5% discount if paid in full by April 30, plus a municipal waste collection fee. Owning through a company adds about EUR 500 to 1,500 a year in running costs.
- Tax on rental income: Non-residents: 10% final tax on gross rent, paid quarterly, no deductions.

## How to buy in Bulgaria, step by step

1. **Check what you can own.** Non-EU citizens can buy apartments and buildings in their own name but not land. To buy a house with its plot, a non-EU buyer usually sets up a Bulgarian company.
2. **Hire a lawyer and check the property.** A lawyer checks the title and debts, which usually takes 5 to 10 business days.
3. **Sign a preliminary contract.** Pay a deposit, usually 10%. If you pull out you lose it, and if the seller pulls out they owe you double.
4. **Get your documents ready.** You need your passport and a Bulgarian personal tax number from the National Revenue Agency. Payments of EUR 5,113 or more must go by bank transfer.
5. **Sign the deed before a notary.** Sign the notarial deed before a notary in the property's district, with a sworn translator if you do not speak Bulgarian. The notary files it with the Registry Agency, usually in 1 to 3 business days.
6. **Declare the property to the municipality.** File a tax declaration with the local municipality within 2 months of buying, and move the utility accounts into your name.
