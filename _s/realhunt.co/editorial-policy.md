## Editorial policy: sources, independence and corrections

RealHunt publishes figures it computes from its own listings and buying rules it compiles from named sources. Nobody can pay to change a score, a ranking or a figure, and errors are corrected on the page with its date.

Last reviewed: 21 November 2026

### Who publishes RealHunt

RealHunt is published by Equinox Investment Group LLC (Sheridan, Wyoming, United States), founded by Carlos de Ory Gordillo. Studies and guides are signed "RealHunt Research", the name RealHunt publishes its own research under. [Who we are](https://realhunt.co/about), including what you will find if you search our founder's name.

### Where the figures come from

Prices, sizes and counts are computed from the homes listed on RealHunt at the time of each update. Every figure on a data page carries its date and the number of homes behind it, and small samples are marked.

Prices are asking prices from the original listing. RealHunt does not estimate values. How homes are chosen and graded is in the [method](https://realhunt.co/method).

### Buying rules, taxes and costs

Rules for foreign buyers, taxes and purchase costs are compiled from the sources linked on each country's [cost page](https://realhunt.co/costs), with the year of the figures. Where sources disagree, a range is given. A figure that was not found in a source that was opened and read is left blank, not filled in.

This is general information, not legal or tax advice. Laws change, and a local lawyer or notary should confirm anything you rely on.

### Independence

Buyers pay nothing. Nobody pays to be listed or to rank higher, and a score cannot be bought. Sponsored cards are marked as sponsored.

RealHunt does not sell the resale homes it lists: it links to the original listing. On new-build projects it earns a fee from the developer only if a buyer it introduced buys ([how the new-build ranking works](https://realhunt.co/top/how-we-rank)). When a project of our founder appears on the site, the [about page](https://realhunt.co/about) says so.

### Corrections

Found an error? Write to carlos@equinoxinvestmentgroup.com with the page and what is wrong. For a home that is sold or wrongly described, the report button on its page is the fastest way.

When a figure, a rule or a description is corrected, the page is changed and its date updated, and the change is listed with its date in the [corrections](https://realhunt.co/corrections). Changes to how the score works are recorded in the [change log of the method](https://realhunt.co/method).

### Updates

Homes are added and removed every day, and the data pages are recomputed with each update, so two visits can show slightly different numbers. Each page shows when it was last updated.
