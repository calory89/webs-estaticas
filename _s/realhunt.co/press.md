## RealHunt for journalists: facts, numbers, logos and contact

RealHunt (realhunt.co) is a website where foreigners find, check and buy property: it lists cheap homes for sale around the world, each one checked by a person and graded from 70 to 98 against every other home on the site. It is not an estate agent: every home links to its original listing and buyers pay nothing. It also publishes, free to quote, the buying rules, taxes and costs of 20 countries and data on what cheap homes cost.

Numbers updated 15 November 2026 · Press contact: carlos@equinoxinvestmentgroup.com

- [Where the homes under €50,000 are: countries and regions (2026)](https://realhunt.co/research/homes-under-50000). 80 homes at or under €50,000; most in Italy (33).

- [Cheap homes and flood risk: share with a medium or high indicator, by country (2026)](https://realhunt.co/research/flood-indicator). 9% of 551 homes are marked medium or high; 13 are marked high.

### Facts

What | Detail
Name | RealHunt (one word, capital R and H). Web: realhunt.co
What it does | Finds cheap homes for sale abroad, has a person check each one, scores them and ranks them best first. Sends buyers to the original listing.
What it is not | Not an estate agent, a valuer or a legal adviser. It does not sell the homes.
Company | Equinox Investment Group LLC, 30 N Gould St Ste N, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States
Founder | Carlos de Ory Gordillo. [Who runs RealHunt, and what you will find if you search his name](https://realhunt.co/about)
How it makes money | Buyers pay nothing. Nobody pays to be listed or to rank higher. On new-build projects, RealHunt earns a fee from the developer only if a buyer it introduced buys. Sponsored cards are marked.
