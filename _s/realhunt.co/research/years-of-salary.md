# How many years of average net pay a cheap home costs, by country (2026)

The median home on RealHunt costs 1.5 years of average net pay in Germany and 8 in the Netherlands, among the 10 countries with at least 10 homes and Eurostat earnings data. In Germany that is a median asking price of €55,000 against net earnings of €36,535 a year.

Prices: 700 homes on RealHunt, 28 November 2026. Pay: Eurostat, annual net earnings of a single person without children on the average wage, 2025. These are cheap homes chosen by a person, not each country's average home.

Updated 28 November 2026. Source: RealHunt, https://realhunt.co/research/years-of-salary (CC BY 4.0). CSV: https://realhunt.co/research/years-of-salary.csv

## Method and limits

- RealHunt is not a census of the market. A person picks homes that are cheap for their country and ready to live in, so these are medians of 866 chosen listings (48% of them at or under €100,000), not national averages.
- Prices are asking prices in euros, as listed on the day each home was added. A sale can close lower.
- Years = median asking price ÷ annual net earnings. It ignores taxes and fees of the purchase, interest and living costs: it is a yardstick to compare countries, not the time needed to save for a home.
- Earnings are national averages for 2025; homes are where RealHunt has found them, often in cheaper regions than the national average. Countries Eurostat does not publish for that year (Norway, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, the United States, Japan and others) are left out.
- Countries with fewer than 5 homes are left out; with fewer than 10 they are marked as a small sample.
- Median: the middle home when they are sorted (with an even number, the upper of the two in the middle).
