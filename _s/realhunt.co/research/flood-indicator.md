# Cheap homes and flood risk: share with a medium or high indicator, by country (2026)

50 of 585 homes on RealHunt with a flood indicator (9%) are marked medium or high, and 13 are marked high. The share is largest in the Dominican Republic (100% of 5), the United States (31% of 61), Greece (22% of 23). No home is marked medium or high in France, Poland, Germany, the Netherlands, Canada, Portugal, Switzerland, Bulgaria and the United Kingdom.

The indicator is RealHunt's own first check, 28 November 2026: an official flood zone where one is open to query (54 homes, United States), and otherwise a rough reading of height above sea level and distance to the sea and to a large river. It is not an official flood map.

## Method and limits

- The indicator is a first filter, not a flood-risk assessment. Outside the United States it is a rough reading of the terrain: a home marked low can still flood, and a home marked medium may be perfectly safe behind a defence.
- Countries are not measured with the same ruler: the United States figure uses official flood zones for most homes, the others do not. Compare countries with care.
- 281 homes have no indicator yet and are not counted. Countries with fewer than 5 homes with the indicator are left out; with fewer than 10 they are marked as a small sample.
- RealHunt is not a census of the market. A person picks homes that are cheap for their country and ready to live in, so these are medians of 866 chosen listings (48% of them at or under €100,000), not national averages.
- Before buying, check the address on the official flood map of the country and ask the seller and the insurer about past floods.
