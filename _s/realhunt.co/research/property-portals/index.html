Property portals by country: where homes are listed for sale in 54 countries

This is a directory of 179 property portals in 54 countries: the national portals, the large classified sites and the portals made for foreign buyers, each with its web address. They are in the order RealHunt checks them when it looks for homes in that country. Being listed here is not a recommendation or a rating.

List compiled on 9 November 2026. RealHunt has homes in 22 of these countries; the counts are of 21 November 2026.

Updated 21 November 2026

How to use this list

  • Start with the first two or three portals of a country: they are the ones we check first.
  • The same home is often on more than one portal, and the price is not always the same. Compare before you call.
  • Prices on a portal are asking prices. Taxes and fees come on top: see what it costs to buy in each country.
  • RealHunt is not a portal and not an estate agent. A person checks each home before it is listed, it gets a score from 70 to 98, and the page links to the original listing. How the score works.

What is in the list and what is not

  • Each entry has the name of the portal, its country and its web address. The link goes to the portal's front page.
  • Two portals work in more than one country and appear once in each.
  • We do not rate the portals, we do not count their listings and we do not say which one is best: that changes by region and by type of home.
  • A portal is missing, has closed or has changed its address? Write to carlos@equinoxinvestmentgroup.com and we will correct the list.
