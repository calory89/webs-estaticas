# How the price of a cheap home changes with the distance to the airport (2026)

Homes on RealHunt less than 30 km from an airport have a median asking price of €1,456/m² (225 homes); between 30 and 60 km it is €1,091/m² (244 homes), 25% less, and between 60 and 100 km €1,077/m² (163 homes). The median price of a whole home goes from €129,000 to €91,000 and €85,000.

707 homes with a known distance, 21 November 2026. Distance in a straight line to the nearest airport with passenger flights. The bands mix countries, so part of the gap is that homes far from an airport are in cheaper countries and regions.

## Method and limits

- This is a description of the homes listed, not proof that distance causes the price: country, region, size and condition change at the same time.
- Distance is measured in a straight line, in kilometres, to the nearest airport with passenger flights; driving time is longer and depends on the roads. Bands include their lower bound.
- Price per m² uses only homes that state their size and is shown from 5 homes. Bands or countries with fewer than 10 homes are marked as a small sample.
- RealHunt is not a census of the market. A person picks homes that are cheap for their country and ready to live in, so these are medians of 717 chosen listings (61% of them at or under €100,000), not national averages.
- Prices are asking prices in euros, as listed on the day each home was added. A sale can close lower.
- Median: the middle home when they are sorted (with an even number, the upper of the two in the middle).
