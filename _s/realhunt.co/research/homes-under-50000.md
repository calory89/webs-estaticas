# Where the homes under €50,000 are: countries and regions (2026)

79 of the 726 homes on RealHunt cost €50,000 or less, and 28 cost €30,000 or less. Most are in Italy (33), Japan (19), Germany (10). The region with the most is Chemnitz, Germany, with 8.

Homes checked by a person and ready to live in, at their asking price on 21 November 2026. Countries and regions with fewer than 3 such homes are not listed.

## Method and limits

- RealHunt is not a census of the market. A person picks homes that are cheap for their country and ready to live in, so these are medians of 726 chosen listings (60% of them at or under €100,000), not national averages.
- Prices are asking prices in euros, as listed on the day each home was added. A sale can close lower.
- A region is the last part of each home's location before the country (a province, a state, a county or a city).
- The count describes what RealHunt lists today. A country that is missing may have such homes that have not been added yet.
- Median: the middle home when they are sorted (with an even number, the upper of the two in the middle).
