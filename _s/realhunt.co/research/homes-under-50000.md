# Where the homes under €50,000 are: countries and regions (2026)

72 of the 1,058 homes on RealHunt cost €50,000 or less, and 26 cost €30,000 or less. Most are in Italy (32), Japan (16), Germany (7). The region with the most is Calabria, Italy, with 6.

Homes checked by a person and ready to live in, at their asking price on 28 November 2026. Countries and regions with fewer than 3 such homes are not listed.

## Method and limits

- RealHunt is not a census of the market. A person picks homes that are cheap for their country and ready to live in, so these are medians of 1,058 chosen listings (45% of them at or under €100,000), not national averages.
- Prices are asking prices in euros, as listed on the day each home was added. A sale can close lower.
- A region is the last part of each home's location before the country (a province, a state, a county or a city).
- The count describes what RealHunt lists today. A country that is missing may have such homes that have not been added yet.
- Median: the middle home when they are sorted (with an even number, the upper of the two in the middle).
