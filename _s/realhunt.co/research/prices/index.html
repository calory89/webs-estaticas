Median asking price of a cheap home, by country (2026)

Among the 15 countries with at least 10 homes with a known size on RealHunt, the lowest median asking price per square metre is in Japan (€468/m²), Denmark (€790/m²), Sweden (€800/m²), and the highest in the Netherlands (€4,286/m²). The lowest median asking price is in Japan (€54,280) and Germany (€55,000).

Medians of 726 listings in 24 countries, each checked by a person, on 22 November 2026. They describe the cheap, move-in-ready homes RealHunt lists, not each country's whole market.

Method and limits

  • RealHunt is not a census of the market. A person picks homes that are cheap for their country and ready to live in, so these are medians of 726 chosen listings (60% of them at or under €100,000), not national averages.
  • Prices are asking prices in euros, as listed on the day each home was added. A sale can close lower.
  • Median: the middle home when they are sorted (with an even number, the upper of the two in the middle).
  • Countries with fewer than 10 homes are shown and marked as a small sample; with fewer than 3 they are left out.
  • Price per m² uses only the homes whose listing gives a size; the column "Homes with size" says how many.
  • In some countries every home is in one region, so the figure describes that region: Netherlands (Amsterdam).
  • The score is RealHunt's own grade from 70 to 98; it is explained in the method.
