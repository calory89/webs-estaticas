## Method and limits

- The score compares the homes on RealHunt with each other. It is not a rating of the region as a place to live, nor a valuation.
- A region is the last part of each home's location before the country (a province, a state, a county or a city).
- Regions with fewer than 5 homes are left out; those with fewer than 10 are marked as a small sample.
- RealHunt is not a census of the market. A person picks homes that are cheap for their country and ready to live in, so these are medians of 703 chosen listings (52% of them at or under €100,000), not national averages.
- Median: the middle home when they are sorted (with an even number, the upper of the two in the middle).
