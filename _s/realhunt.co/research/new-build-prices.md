# New-build prices per m² in Bali, Phuket, Koh Samui, Tulum and Batumi (2026)

In RealHunt's new-build rankings the median starting price per square metre is lowest in Batumi (€1,366/m², 100 projects) and highest in Phuket (€3,661/m², 80 projects). Koh Samui: €1,759/m²; Bali: €1,872/m²; Tulum: €2,847/m².

417 projects in 5 destinations, from the developers' published "from" prices, 20 November 2026. Some are leasehold and some are sold before they are built: a price per m² is not comparable with a finished freehold home without reading each project.

Updated 20 November 2026. Source: RealHunt, https://realhunt.co/research/new-build-prices (CC BY 4.0). CSV: https://realhunt.co/research/new-build-prices.csv

## Method and limits

- Prices are the developers' "from" prices for the cheapest unit, converted to euros when the ranking was last updated. The unit you want may cost more.
- Price per m² is the one stated for each project; projects that do not publish it count in the number of projects but not in the median.
- Ownership differs: freehold, leasehold or a structure for foreign buyers, depending on the country. Read each project and the country's rules before comparing.
- This is one reading on one day. It is not a forecast of prices or of rental income.
- Median: the middle home when they are sorted (with an even number, the upper of the two in the middle).
