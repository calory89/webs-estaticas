How to read these figures

  • RealHunt is not a census of the market. A person picks homes that are cheap for their country and ready to live in, so a median here describes those homes, not the country's whole market.
  • Prices are asking prices in euros from the original listing on the day the home was added. A sale can close lower.
  • A country with fewer than 10 homes is marked as a small sample and left out of the ranked lists.
  • Rules, taxes and costs are general information with their sources on each country's cost page, not legal or tax advice.
