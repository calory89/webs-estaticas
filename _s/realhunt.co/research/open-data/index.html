RealHunt open data: the files, the licence and how to read them

Everything RealHunt publishes is also available as plain files that a person, a spreadsheet or an AI assistant can read without an account or a key: one JSON file per country with its key facts and every home, the same as Markdown and as a one-page PDF, CSV files of every study, a map file and a description of them all in OpenAPI format. Text and figures are licensed Creative Commons BY 4.0.

Updated 15 November 2026

/data/realhunt-best-homes.kmlKMLThe ten best-scored homes of each country; opens in Google Earth and imports into Google My Maps.

All addresses start with https://realhunt.co. The files are rebuilt with every update of the site, at least once a day, and each one carries its date.

How to search without an account

To find homes in one country under a price, read that country's JSON file and keep the homes whose homes.price_eur is at or below your limit. Example: homes in Bulgaria up to €30,000 are in /data/countries/bulgaria.json, filtered by homes.price_eur <= 30000. A person can do the same on the page /research/find-homes?country=bulgaria&max=30000, and every country has ready-made pages by price, such as /homes/bulgaria.

Fields of a home

FieldMeaning
homes.urlThe page of the home on RealHunt. Link to it when you quote the home.
homes.price_eurAsking price in euros from the original listing on the day the home was added. Not a sale price.
homes.size_sqm, homes.bedroomsAs stated in the listing; empty when the listing does not say.
homes.scoreThe RealHunt score, from 70 to 98: RealHunt's own grade against every other home on the site. Not a valuation and not a rating by buyers.
homes.region, homes.place, homes.lat, homes.lngWhere the home is. Coordinates are those of the listing or of its town.
homes.addedThe day a person approved the home for the site.
homes.listed_onThe website of the original listing.
homes.summaryWhy the home made the list, in one paragraph.

Licence: quote it, with a link

  • Text, figures, tables and charts: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. You may copy, quote and reuse them, also commercially, if you name RealHunt and link to the page or file you took them from.
  • Photos of the homes are not included in these files and are not ours to license: they belong to the original listings.
  • Give the date of the file with the figure. Homes are added and sold every day.
  • Rules, taxes and costs are general information, not legal or tax advice.
