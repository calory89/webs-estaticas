Glossary

The terms you will meet in quotes and spec sheets, explained in one line each.

EXW
Ex Works: the price at the factory gate. You (or your forwarder) pay trucking to the port, export clearance and everything after.
FOB
Free On Board: the maker delivers the goods loaded on the ship at their port. Sea freight, insurance, import duty, taxes and delivery are on you. Most prices on TheGlampings are FOB.
CIF
Cost, Insurance and Freight: the maker pays the sea freight and insurance to your port. Import duty, taxes, port fees and delivery to site are still on you.
40HQ
A 40ft high-cube shipping container (about 12 × 2.35 × 2.7 m inside). Freight is charged per container, so how many units fit inside drives the freight cost per unit.
Landed cost
Factory price + freight + import duty + VAT/GST + customs and port fees: what the unit costs once it is cleared in your country, before trucking and installation.
HS 9406
The customs code for prefabricated buildings. Your duty rate depends on how your country classifies the unit under this code.
Form E
A certificate of origin for China–ASEAN trade. With it, import duty on prefab buildings into most ASEAN countries is 0%. Other regions use other certificates.
Hot-dip galvanizing
Dipping the whole steel frame in molten zinc. Far more resistant to rust than paint or powder coat, and the minimum for sites near the sea.
316 stainless
Marine-grade stainless steel for bolts and fixings. 304 or zinc-plated fixings rust quickly in salty air.
C5-M
The highest corrosion category for coatings in marine environments (ISO 12944). Ask for it on beachfront sites.
Rock wool
Mineral wool insulation: non-combustible, good for heat and sound. Preferred over EPS for hot or coastal sites.
EPS
Expanded polystyrene (white foam) sandwich panels. Cheap and light, but burns and insulates less than rock wool or PU at the same thickness.
PU
Polyurethane foam panels. Best insulation per mm; check the fire rating.
Expandable house
A container-sized unit whose side walls fold out on site, typically 20ft into about 37 m². Usually two fit in one 40HQ.
Apple cabin
A rounded or angled steel shell with a big glass front, shipped fully assembled. Usually one per container.
Space capsule
An aircraft-style aluminium pod with LED, smart glass and integrated bathroom. The most photogenic and the most expensive per m².
Beaufort scale
Wind force scale: Beaufort 10 is about 25–28 m/s (90–100 km/h); Beaufort 12 is hurricane force, 33 m/s (118 km/h) and above.
MOQ
Minimum order quantity: the smallest order the maker accepts.
Lead time
Days from deposit until the units are ready to ship, before sea freight.
