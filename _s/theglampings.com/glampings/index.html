Good to know

What is glamping?+

Glamorous camping: real beds, private bathrooms and often a pool or chef, in a tent, dome, cabin or treehouse surrounded by nature.

How much does a night of glamping cost?+

From about US$100 for a simple bell tent to over US$3,000 for all-inclusive safari camps. Most stays on this list sit between US$300 and US$1,200 a night for two.

How do you score glamping stays?+

Experience, uniqueness of the setting, design, service and value for money, from verified reviews and published prices.
