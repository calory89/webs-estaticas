    Rules in Queensland

    What applies to a claim like yours in Brisbane, with the source for each point. General information, not legal advice.

    Time limit
    A personal injury claim must be started within 3 years from the date the cause of action arose (Limitation of Actions Act 1974, section 11). Source: Limitation of Actions Act 1974 (Qld)
    Uplift fee
    In a no win, no fee agreement for a court case, the uplift fee may not exceed 25% of the legal costs, excluding disbursements (Legal Profession Act 2007, section 324). Source: Legal Profession Act 2007 (Qld)
    Percentage fees
    A fee calculated by reference to the amount of an award or settlement is prohibited (Legal Profession Act 2007, section 325). Source: Legal Profession Act 2007 (Qld)
