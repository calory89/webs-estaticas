    Country notes

    General information on how success fees work in a few countries. Rules change and have exceptions.

    United States

    Contingency fees are common in injury and many other civil claims. Agreements commonly fall somewhere around a third to 40% of the recovery, sometimes stepping up if the case goes to trial, and some states cap fees for certain claims. Check whether expenses come off before or after the percentage.

    England and Wales

    Damages-based agreements (DBAs) are capped, including VAT, at 50% of the sums recovered in most civil claims and 25% of certain damages in personal injury claims (Damages-Based Agreements Regulations 2013). Conditional fee agreements ("no win no fee" with a success fee on top of normal fees) follow different rules. Source: SI 2013/609.

    Spain

    Since a 2008 Supreme Court ruling, Bar rules can no longer prohibit a pure success fee (cuota litis), so lawyer and client agree the percentage freely. VAT is usually charged on lawyers' fees.

    Germany

    Success fees (Erfolgshonorar) are allowed only in certain cases under section 4a RVG, for example monetary claims up to EUR 2,000, or where the client would otherwise be deterred from claiming for financial reasons. Source: section 4a RVG.
