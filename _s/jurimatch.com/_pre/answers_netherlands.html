    No-win-no-fee in the Netherlands: the short answers

    Short, sourced answers to the questions people ask before hiring a no-win-no-fee lawyer in the Netherlands.

    Reviewed on 1 November 2026 by the JuriMatch editorial team.

    Are contingency fees legal in the Netherlands?

    Generally no. The rules of the Netherlands Bar prohibit Dutch lawyers from working on a no cure, no pay basis or for a percentage of the result, with limited exceptions. The success-based risk is usually carried by claims agencies or litigation funders, which are not law firms, while lawyers charge hourly or fixed fees.

    Source:Nederlandse orde van advocaten

    Who pays if I lose a case in the Netherlands?

    The loser usually pays a fixed-rate contribution to the winner's costs (liquidatietarief), set by a court scale and normally much lower than the real cost of the case. Each side therefore pays most of its own lawyer's fees, win or lose, unless a funder, a claims agency or an insurer covers them.

    Source:Nederlandse orde van advocaten
