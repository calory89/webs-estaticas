    Fees, funding and costs

    Is a CFA or a DBA cheaper?+

    It depends on the size of the damages and how much of the costs you recover. With high damages and low costs a CFA is often cheaper; with low recoverable costs a DBA can work out better. Ask the solicitor to model both on your figures.

    From: CFA vs DBA: Which No-Win-No-Fee Agreement Is Better?

    Do I pay anything if I lose under a CFA or DBA?+

    Not to your own solicitor, if the agreement says so. You may still owe disbursements such as court fees and experts, and the other side's costs unless you are protected (for example by QOCS in most personal injury claims) or insured with ATE insurance.

    From: CFA vs DBA: Which No-Win-No-Fee Agreement Is Better?

    Can a solicitor change from a CFA to a DBA mid-case?+

    Only by a new written agreement that you sign. Read how any work already done is treated before agreeing.

    From: CFA vs DBA: Which No-Win-No-Fee Agreement Is Better?

    Can I use a contingency fee and litigation funding together?+

    Sometimes. A funder may pay part of the costs while the law firm takes a reduced fee plus a success element. The total taken from your recovery should be set out in writing before you sign.

    From: Contingency Fee vs Litigation Funding: Which Is Right for Your Claim?

    Do I have to repay a litigation funder if I lose?+

    Normally not: most funding is non-recourse, so the funder loses its investment. Check the agreement for exceptions such as misconduct or abandoning the case.

    From: Contingency Fee vs Litigation Funding: Which Is Right for Your Claim?

    Which leaves me more money if I win?+

    It depends on the percentages, the length of the case and the multiple the funder charges. Ask for a worked example at several outcomes and timelines.

    From: Contingency Fee vs Litigation Funding: Which Is Right for Your Claim?

    Is no win no fee really free if I lose?+

    You pay your own lawyer nothing if the agreement says so, but you may still owe expenses such as court fees and experts, and in most countries outside the US the other side's costs. Ask who covers these before signing.

    From: No Win No Fee vs Paying by the Hour: Pros, Cons and Costs

    Why would a lawyer turn down my case on no win no fee?+

    Because they are taking the risk. Lawyers look at the chances of winning, the amount at stake, whether the other side can pay, and the time it will take.

    From: No Win No Fee vs Paying by the Hour: Pros, Cons and Costs

    Can I switch from hourly fees to no win no fee?+

    Sometimes, with a new written agreement. A new firm may also take over a case on success terms after reviewing it.

    From: No Win No Fee vs Paying by the Hour: Pros, Cons and Costs

    Do I need ATE insurance if I have a CFA?+

    Often yes, outside personal injury. A CFA only deals with your own solicitor's fees. If you lose, a court can still order you to pay the other side's costs, and ATE insurance is the usual protection.

    From: CFA vs ATE Insurance: What Each One Covers If You Lose

    Who pays the ATE premium?+

    Usually you, out of your damages if you win. Many policies defer the premium and make it payable only if the claim succeeds, so nothing is due if you lose. Check the policy.

    From: CFA vs ATE Insurance: What Each One Covers If You Lose

    Can my solicitor arrange ATE insurance?+

    Yes, solicitors usually arrange it with an insurer they work with. Ask what it covers, the limit of cover, the premium and when it is payable.

    From: CFA vs ATE Insurance: What Each One Covers If You Lose

    Can I combine a fixed fee and a success fee?+

    Often, yes. Many lawyers in Spain, France, Italy and Brazil charge a smaller fixed fee plus a success fee. In some countries this mix is the only form allowed.

    From: Contingency Fee vs Fixed Fee: Which Costs You Less?

    What does a fixed fee usually not include?+

    Court fees, expert reports and the other side's costs if you lose. Ask exactly which steps the fixed fee covers and what happens if the case goes further, such as to an appeal.

    From: Contingency Fee vs Fixed Fee: Which Costs You Less?

    Is a contingency fee always more expensive if I win?+

    Not always. It depends on the size of the award and how much work the case needs. Ask the lawyer to show you both options on your figures.

    From: Contingency Fee vs Fixed Fee: Which Costs You Less?

    Do I already have legal expenses insurance?+

    Possibly. It is often included as an add-on to home, car or bank account policies, especially in Germany, Austria and several other European countries. Check your policy documents or ask your insurer before paying a lawyer.

    From: No-win-no-fee lawyer vs legal expenses insurance

    Can my legal expenses insurer force me to use its lawyer?+

    In the EU, Article 201 of Directive 2009/138/EC gives you a free choice of lawyer for any inquiry or proceedings, and whenever there is a conflict of interest; the Court of Justice has read this right broadly. Before that point, the insurer may handle the claim itself or use a panel firm.

    From: No-win-no-fee lawyer vs legal expenses insurance

    Can I use both legal expenses insurance and a no-win-no-fee lawyer?+

    Sometimes. If the insurance limit is lower than the expected costs, a lawyer may agree success-based terms for the part the policy does not pay. Ask both the insurer and the lawyer to confirm in writing how they would work together.

    From: No-win-no-fee lawyer vs legal expenses insurance

    Does legal expenses insurance cover a dispute that started before I bought it?+

    Normally not. Policies usually exclude disputes whose cause arose before the cover began, and many apply a waiting period after purchase.

    From: No-win-no-fee lawyer vs legal expenses insurance

    Who qualifies for justicia gratuita in Spain?+

    Ley 1/1996 sets income limits linked to the IPREM index: broadly up to twice the IPREM for individuals, more for family units. Other factors, such as assets and the type of case, also count. The bar association's legal aid service can confirm whether you qualify.

    From: No-win-no-fee lawyer vs legal aid

    Does legal aid in England and Wales cover money claims?+

    Mostly not. Since the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012, most civil money claims, including most personal injury, debt and employment claims, are outside the scope of legal aid. Conditional fee agreements and damages-based agreements are the usual alternatives.

    From: No-win-no-fee lawyer vs legal aid

    If I get legal aid and lose, do I pay the other side's costs?+

    It depends on the country. In Germany, Prozesskostenhilfe does not protect you from paying the winner's costs. In other systems the protection is wider. Ask the legal aid service or your assigned lawyer before the case starts.

    From: No-win-no-fee lawyer vs legal aid

    Is JuriMatch a claims management company?+

    No. JuriMatch does not handle claims and does not take a share of compensation. It is a directory and search engine that helps you find no-win-no-fee lawyers and litigation funders, plus free guides and tools.

    From: JuriMatch, claims management companies and lawyers: the differences

    Can I make a claim without a claims management company?+

    Often yes. Flight compensation, many consumer complaints and complaints about financial services can be made directly to the company and then to an ombudsman or alternative dispute resolution body, free of charge.

    From: JuriMatch, claims management companies and lawyers: the differences

    Who regulates claims management companies?+

    In Great Britain, the Financial Conduct Authority has regulated claims management activity since 1 April 2019. Rules differ in other countries, and in many there is no specific regulator for claims companies.

    From: JuriMatch, claims management companies and lawyers: the differences

    Fraud and scams

    Can I get my money back from an investment scam?+

    Sometimes. It depends on whether the money can be traced, whether a solvent party (a bank, a regulated firm or an identifiable individual with assets) is responsible, and how quickly you act. A lawyer can tell you which of these applies to you.

    From: Lost money to investment fraud? What to do first

    Do lawyers take investment fraud cases on a no-win-no-fee basis?+

    Many do, where recovery looks realistic, for example when a regulated entity or insurer is involved, or many investors were affected by the same scheme. Where it is less certain, firms may offer a reduced fee plus a success fee, or refer you to a litigation funder.

    From: Lost money to investment fraud? What to do first

    Should I join a group claim?+

    Group or collective actions can share costs and make funding easier. Check who runs the claim, how fees are taken from any recovery, and whether you can leave.

    From: Lost money to investment fraud? What to do first

    Can stolen crypto be recovered?+

    Sometimes, if the funds can be traced to an exchange or custodian that can be compelled to freeze and disclose account information. If funds were moved through mixers or to wallets nobody controls identifiably, recovery is much harder.

    From: Crypto scam recovery: first steps and how lawyers trace funds

    Is there a no-win-no-fee option for crypto fraud?+

    Some firms work on success terms when tracing shows funds at an identifiable exchange. Initial tracing reports often carry a cost, so ask upfront what is included.

    From: Crypto scam recovery: first steps and how lawyers trace funds

    How do I spot a recovery scam?+

    Unsolicited contact, upfront fees, claims of special access to blockchains or regulators, and pressure to act fast. Legitimate law firms are regulated and can be checked with the local bar or law society.

    From: Crypto scam recovery: first steps and how lawyers trace funds

    Will my bank refund a romance scam payment?+

    It depends on your country, how you paid and the bank's rules. Some countries have reimbursement schemes for authorised push-payment fraud. Report it to the bank immediately.

    From: Romance and pig-butchering scams: what victims can do

    Debts, contracts, property and insurance

    Can I recover a deposit from a developer that went bust?+

    Possibly, if your deposit was protected by a bank guarantee, escrow or insurance, or if directors can be held personally liable. Otherwise you may be an unsecured creditor in the insolvency.

    From: Property and off-plan fraud: how to protect your claim

    Do I need a lawyer in the country where the property is?+

    Usually yes, because property claims are generally governed by local law and heard by local courts.

    From: Property and off-plan fraud: how to protect your claim

    Will a lawyer recover a debt on a no-win-no-fee basis?+

    Many debt-recovery firms work for a percentage of what they collect, particularly for undisputed business debts.

    From: Client won't pay? A guide to recovering unpaid invoices

    Can I claim interest and costs?+

    Many countries allow statutory interest and some recovery costs on late business payments. A lawyer can confirm what applies to your invoice.

    From: Client won't pay? A guide to recovering unpaid invoices

    Can a shareholder dispute be funded?+

    Yes, where the business or stake is valuable enough. Funders and success-fee firms look at what can realistically be recovered or bought out.

    From: When a business partner takes the money: your options

    Do lawyers take insurance disputes on a no-win-no-fee basis?+

    Often, because the insurer can pay. The main question is whether the policy wording supports your claim.

    From: Insurance claim denied? How to challenge it

    Who pays if I win a professional negligence claim?+

    Usually the professional's indemnity insurer, which is why these claims are often funded on success terms.

    From: Bad advice from a professional? Claiming for negligence

    Flights, banks, cars and group claims

    Do I need a lawyer to claim compensation from Ryanair?+

    Usually not for the first step: you can claim directly with the airline for free. A claim company or no-win-no-fee lawyer can help if the airline refuses or does not reply, in exchange for a share of the compensation.

    From: Claims against Ryanair: delays, cancellations and refunds

    Does Ryanair have to pay if the delay was caused by weather or strikes?+

    Not if the disruption was caused by extraordinary circumstances that could not have been avoided with reasonable measures, such as severe weather. Courts have held that strikes by the airline's own staff are not normally extraordinary, while strikes by third parties such as air traffic controllers can be. Each case depends on its facts.

    From: Claims against Ryanair: delays, cancellations and refunds

    Do I have to complain to Santander before going to court?+

    In practice, yes. A written complaint to the bank's customer service is required before you can go to the Banco de España complaints service, and since April 2025 most civil claims in Spain require a prior attempt to settle. A documented written complaint usually serves both purposes.

    Do lawyers take these cases on a no-win-no-fee basis?+

    Many do, especially floor clause and mortgage expense claims, because when the bank loses in full it is normally ordered to pay costs. Ask for the fee agreement (hoja de encargo) in writing, including what happens if the bank settles early.

    From: Claims against Santander: mortgages, cards and Banco Popular

    Can I still claim if I have paid off the loan?+

    Often yes. Having repaid or cancelled a mortgage does not by itself prevent a claim about an unfair term, but any later agreement or waiver you signed needs to be reviewed.

    Can I still claim against Volkswagen for dieselgate?+

    It depends on the country, the engine, when you bought the car and whether you joined a group action. In some countries ordinary deadlines for EA189 cars have passed; in others, or for other engines, claims may still be possible. Get a deadline check before anything else.

    From: Claims against Volkswagen: the diesel emissions scandal

    Do I need to still own the car?+

    Not necessarily. Courts in some countries have allowed claims by buyers who have since sold the car, with the sale price taken into account.

    From: Claims against Volkswagen: the diesel emissions scandal

    Do lawyers take diesel claims on a no-win-no-fee basis?+

    Many do, often with litigation funding or legal expenses insurance, because claims are numerous and similar. Read how the fee is calculated and what happens if you withdraw.

    From: Claims against Volkswagen: the diesel emissions scandal

    Is my Mercedes affected?+

    It depends on the engine, model and emissions class. Recall letters and official vehicle checkers are a starting point; a lawyer or claims group can check your specific car.

    From: Claims against Mercedes-Benz: diesel emissions

    How much could I get?+

    There is no fixed amount. In Germany, courts following the 2023 CJEU ruling have generally awarded a percentage of the purchase price for negligence claims; elsewhere it depends on national law and the claim.
