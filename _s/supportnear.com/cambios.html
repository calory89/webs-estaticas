        Some of the new places

          A place «gone» usually means it was removed or re-tagged on OpenStreetMap, or that our filters stopped counting it (for example, it started charging). It does not always mean it closed.

          How to use this

          Every number here is checkable: each new place links to its card, and each card links to the OpenStreetMap element it comes from. If you write about it, the press kit has the figures and screenshots.

          Where people found nothing

          When someone widens the search and still finds nothing, the app lets them say what they were looking for (only the area, the city and the need). The shared list appears here once the reports worker is deployed (see worker/reports.js).
