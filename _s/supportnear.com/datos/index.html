Open data: free help places by country

One CSV per country with every place SupportNear shows within 10 km of its cities: name, category, type, coordinates, address, opening hours, phone and the OpenStreetMap element. 64,253 places in 136 countries.

The data comes from OpenStreetMap and is under the Open Database License (ODbL): you can reuse it if you credit «© OpenStreetMap contributors» and share changes under the same licence. To fix a place, edit it on OpenStreetMap: SupportNear picks it up on the next update.
