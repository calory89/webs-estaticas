Put the map on your site

If you are a council, a parish, a university, a shelter or an NGO, you can put the map of your city inside your own page. It is free, you do not have to ask, and there is nothing to sign up for.

Copy this

<iframe src="https://supportnear.com/?embed=1&city=Sevilla"
        width="100%" height="600" style="border:0;border-radius:12px"
        loading="lazy" title="SupportNear">
</iframe>

Change Sevilla for your city. Any of the 1221 cities on the map works.

This is how it looks

Options

ParameterWhat it does
?embed=1Hides the header and leaves only the map. This is what turns it into a widget.
&city=SevillaOpens centred on that city instead of asking for a location.
&need=foodOpens already filtered: food, shelter, hygiene, clothes, social, legal, health, water or warm.
&color=0B7A63Your organisation’s colour (a hex code without #) for the buttons and links.

What it does and does not do

  • It does not ask your visitors for their location unless they press the crosshair button.
  • No cookies, no pixels, nothing from third parties. There is nothing to consent to.
  • It works with no connection once it has loaded.
  • It is light: the page opens in under 250 ms and only downloads the piece of map in view.

If you want your services on it

The map is fed by OpenStreetMap. If your soup kitchen, shelter or clothing bank is not on it, add it there and at the next update it will be here — and on every other site and app that uses that map, not just this one.
