About This Product

If you're looking to add some serious relaxation vibes to your space, this tabletop meditation fountain is absolutely perfect. The gentle sound of flowing water creates this amazing calming atmosphere that just melts stress away after a long day.

The USB power means you can plug it in anywhere - office desk, bedroom nightstand, living room table, you name it. No need for complicated electrical work or being limited to outlets. Just use any USB charger or power bank and you're good to go.

The blue lotus design is beautiful and adds that touch of nature to your indoor space. The combination of flowing water, stones, and the lotus flower creates this mini-ecosystem that's both visually pleasing and audibly relaxing. The waterfall sound is gentle, not overwhelming, perfect for meditation, yoga, or just creating a peaceful background ambiance.

At about 23x15x13cm for the blue version, it's compact enough for any desk or table without taking over your space. The built-in submersible pump circulates the water continuously, so you just fill it once and enjoy the soothing flow.

Whether you need something to help you focus at work, relax at home, or create a zen atmosphere for meditation, this little fountain delivers. It even helps humidify dry indoor air naturally. It makes a fantastic gift too - way more thoughtful than another boring desk accessory!
