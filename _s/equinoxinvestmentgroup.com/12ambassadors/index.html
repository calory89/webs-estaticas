    The deposit

    €6,500. Refundable. Always.

    If you are selected as one of the 12, a one-time deposit of €6,500 secures your seat for life. It funds the upkeep of the home, marketing, construction milestones, furniture, equipment and the operations behind your studio.

    Refund mechanism: recovered upon transfer of your seat to a verified successor. The deposit is non-refundable in cash by Equinox, by design — it lives in the asset that gives you the home, the equity and the network.
