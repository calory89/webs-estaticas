07 · Questions

Straight answers.

Something else? Write to carlos@equinoxinvestmentgroup.com.

What is a venture builder?
A company that builds companies. Instead of funding outside startups, Equinox designs, launches and operates its own, all on a shared team, stack and audience.
Do you invest in outside startups?
Rarely. Almost all of our time and capital goes into companies we build ourselves. If you run a company in a market we are entering, we are open to partnerships.
How do operators work with Equinox?
Operators own a company or a function across the portfolio, fully remote, with a real stake in what they build. Terms are agreed company by company.
Can I invest in Equinox?
Institutional and qualified allocators can request the investor brief. Nothing on this site is an offer to sell securities.
Where is the team?
Everywhere. The company is fully remote, with bases in Madrid, Dubai and Sumba.
