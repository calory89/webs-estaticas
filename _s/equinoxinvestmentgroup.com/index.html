01 · The Equinox method

Four moves. On repeat.

Move 1 · Find the wedge

We only build where the old way just broke.

We map verticals where distribution, trust or AI changed the rules: lawyers paid only when you win, homes scored instead of advertised, hiring by video instead of CV. No wedge, no company.

Move 2 · Build in weeks

From idea to live product in weeks, not quarters.

A small operator team on a shared technical and legal spine. Same stack, same playbooks, same publishing machine. What took 40 people in 2018 takes four today.

Move 3 · Distribute across the network

Every launch starts with an audience.

Each company launches into the traffic and trust of the rest of the portfolio. Travellers from Happy Low Cost discover Top3Hotels. Buyers from RealHunt meet Kolaboro. The network is the moat.

Move 4 · Double down

Capital goes behind the outliers.

Hiring, budget and expansion follow the numbers. What works gets more. What doesn't is retired without drama, and its lessons feed the next build.
