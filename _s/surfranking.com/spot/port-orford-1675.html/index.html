Best time to surf Port Orford

The best time to surf Port Orford is January, February, April. The season runs all year, cleanest Oct to Oct. In April the water sits around 10°C, so pack a 5/4 with boots and hood, and the typical wave is 1.8 m. The nearest airport is OTH (78 km) and the nearest town is Coos Bay (73 km).

What is under Port Orford

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor drops to 120 m, shoaling steadily over the last kilometres. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 202 m against 120 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
