Best time to surf Cobas

The best time to surf Cobas is February, March, April. The season runs all year, cleanest Feb to Apr. In February the water sits around 13°C, so pack a 5/4 with boots and hood, and the typical wave is 3.0 m. The nearest airport is LCG (27 km) and the nearest town is La Coruña (25 km).

What is under Cobas

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is still shallow, 25 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 40 m against 25 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
