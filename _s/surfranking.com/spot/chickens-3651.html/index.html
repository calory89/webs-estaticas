Best time to surf Chickens

The best time to surf Chickens is June, July, August. The season runs all year, but rarely clean. In July the water sits around 29°C, so pack boardshorts, and the typical wave is 1.3 m. The nearest airport is MLE (25 km) and the nearest town is Malé (28 km).

What is under Chickens

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is still shallow, 20 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 40 m against 20 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.

Modelled bathymetry: NOAA ETOPO 2022 global relief at 15 arc-seconds (about 460 m per cell), sampled along this spot’s swell window. At that scale the model represents broad passes and channels; it cannot see a reef ledge, so nothing here is said about how the wave breaks.
