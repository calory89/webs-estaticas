Best time to surf Teahupoo

The best time to surf Teahupoo is February, April, May. The season runs all year, cleanest Aug to Aug. In February the water sits around 28°C, so pack boardshorts, and the typical wave is 1.6 m. The nearest airport is PPT (51 km) and the nearest town is Papeete (50 km).

What is under Teahupoo

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is still shallow, 16 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 21 m against 16 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
