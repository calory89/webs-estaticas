Best time to surf Praia do Norte

The best time to surf Praia do Norte is February, March, November. The season runs all year, cleanest Mar to Mar. In March the water sits around 14°C, so pack a 4/3 with boots, and the typical wave is 2.4 m. The nearest airport is CAT (28 km) and the nearest town is Lisbon (36 km).
