Best time to surf Nahoon Beach Break

The best time to surf Nahoon Beach Break is June, July, August. The season runs all year, cleanest May to Aug. In July the water sits around 20°C, so pack a 2 mm shorty, and the typical wave is 1.9 m. The nearest airport is ELS (13 km) and the nearest town is East London (8 km).
