Best time to surf Praia dos Mosteiros

The best time to surf Praia dos Mosteiros is March, November, December. The season runs all year, but rarely clean. In November the water sits around 19°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 2.6 m. The nearest airport is PDL (20 km) and the nearest town is Ponta Delgada (21 km).

What is under Praia dos Mosteiros

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor sits over 47 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 71 m against 47 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
