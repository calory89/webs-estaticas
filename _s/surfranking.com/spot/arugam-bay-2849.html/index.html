Best time to surf Arugam Bay

The best time to surf Arugam Bay is April, July, August. The season runs all year, but rarely clean. In July the water sits around 29°C, so pack boardshorts, and the typical wave is 1.4 m. The nearest airport is BTC (98 km) and the nearest town is Badulla (89 km).

What is under Arugam Bay

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor sits over 34 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 58 m against 34 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
