Best time to surf Playa del Orzan

The best time to surf Playa del Orzan is January, February, December. The season runs all year, cleanest Oct to Oct. In February the water sits around 13°C, so pack a 4/3 with boots, and the typical wave is 3.0 m. The nearest airport is LCG (8 km) and the nearest town is La Coruña (5 km).

What is under Playa del Orzan
