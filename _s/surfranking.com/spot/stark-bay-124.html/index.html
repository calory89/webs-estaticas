Best time to surf Stark Bay

The best time to surf Stark Bay is March, April, May. The season runs all year, cleanest Jan to May. In April the water sits around 22°C, so pack a 2 mm shorty, and the typical wave is 2.1 m. The nearest airport is PER (46 km) and the nearest town is Perth (34 km).

What is under Stark Bay

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor falls away into open ocean, 725 m, deep right up to the door, so swell arrives with its energy intact.

Modelled bathymetry: NOAA ETOPO 2022 global relief at 15 arc-seconds (about 460 m per cell), sampled along this spot’s swell window. At that scale the model represents broad passes and channels; it cannot see a reef ledge, so nothing here is said about how the wave breaks.
