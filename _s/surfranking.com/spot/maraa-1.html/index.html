Best time to surf Maraa

The best time to surf Maraa is January, April, May. The season runs all year. In April the water sits around 28°C, so pack boardshorts, and the typical wave is 1.8 m. The nearest airport is PPT (22 km) and the nearest town is Papeete (24 km).

What is under Maraa

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is still shallow, 6 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 16 m against 6 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
