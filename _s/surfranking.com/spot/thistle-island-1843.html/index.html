Best time to surf Thistle Island

The best time to surf Thistle Island is April, May, July. The season runs all year, cleanest Apr to Jul. In April the water sits around 18°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 1.9 m. The nearest airport is PLO (46 km) and the nearest town is Port Lincoln (34 km).

What is under Thistle Island

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor drops to 68 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 92 m against 68 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
