Best time to surf Brimurð

The best time to surf Brimurð is January, March, April. The season runs Mar to Dec, but rarely clean. In March the water sits around 6°C, so pack a 6/5 with boots, gloves and hood, and the typical wave is 3.6 m. The nearest airport is RKV (115 km) and the nearest town is Selfoss (69 km).

What is under Brimurð

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor sits over 26 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 91 m against 26 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
