Best time to surf Bells Beach

The best time to surf Bells Beach is April, July, August. The season runs all year, cleanest Feb to Dec. In April the water sits around 17°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 1.5 m. The nearest airport is AVV (40 km) and the nearest town is Geelong (25 km).

What is under Bells Beach

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is already 191 m deep, deep right up to the door, so swell arrives with its energy intact. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 427 m against 191 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.

Modelled bathymetry: NOAA ETOPO 2022 global relief at 15 arc-seconds (about 460 m per cell), sampled along this spot’s swell window. At that scale the model represents broad passes and channels; it cannot see a reef ledge, so nothing here is said about how the wave breaks.
