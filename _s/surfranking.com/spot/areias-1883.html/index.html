Best time to surf Areias

The best time to surf Areias is January, March, November. The season runs all year, cleanest Jan to Jan. In January the water sits around 16°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 2.1 m. The nearest airport is PDL (16 km) and the nearest town is Ponta Delgada (13 km).
