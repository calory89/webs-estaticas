Best time to surf Inner kom

The best time to surf Inner kom is April, May, August. The season runs all year, cleanest Apr to Apr. In April the water sits around 17°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 2.4 m. The nearest airport is CPT (32 km) and the nearest town is Cape Town (27 km).
