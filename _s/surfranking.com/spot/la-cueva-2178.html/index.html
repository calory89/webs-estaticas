Best time to surf La Cueva

The best time to surf La Cueva is January, February, December. The season runs all year, cleanest Dec to Jan. In January the water sits around 13°C, so pack a 4/3 with boots, and the typical wave is 2.5 m. The nearest airport is LCG (12 km) and the nearest town is La Coruña (8 km).
