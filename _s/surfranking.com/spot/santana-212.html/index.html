Best time to surf Santana

The best time to surf Santana is May, June, September. The season runs all year, cleanest Mar to Nov. In May the water sits around 29°C, so pack boardshorts, and the typical wave is 1.1 m. The nearest airport is MGA (79 km) and the nearest town is Rivas (29 km).
