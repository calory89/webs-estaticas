Best time to surf Honolua Bay

The best time to surf Honolua Bay is February, March, November. The season runs all year, but rarely clean. In November the water sits around 26°C, so pack boardshorts, and the typical wave is 1.5 m. The nearest airport is JHM (7 km) and the nearest town is Wailuku (20 km).
