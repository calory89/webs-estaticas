Best time to surf Pasta Point

The best time to surf Pasta Point is April, July, August. The season runs all year, but rarely clean. In July the water sits around 30°C, so pack boardshorts, and the typical wave is 1.3 m. The nearest airport is MLE (16 km) and the nearest town is Malé (18 km).
