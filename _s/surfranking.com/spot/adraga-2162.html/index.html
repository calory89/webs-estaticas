Best time to surf Adraga

The best time to surf Adraga is March, April, December. The season runs all year, but rarely clean. In March the water sits around 14°C, so pack a 4/3 with boots, and the typical wave is 2.4 m. The nearest airport is CAT (14 km) and the nearest town is Lisbon (31 km).

What is under Adraga

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor sits over 27 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 57 m against 27 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
