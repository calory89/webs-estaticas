Best time to surf Sein Island

The best time to surf Sein Island is January, May, November. The season runs Jan to Jan, but rarely clean. In November the water sits around 14°C, so pack a 4/3 with boots, and the typical wave is 3.3 m. The nearest airport is OUI (50 km) and the nearest town is Brest (48 km).
