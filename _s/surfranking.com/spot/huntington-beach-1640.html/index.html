Best time to surf Huntington Beach

The best time to surf Huntington Beach is April, May, June. The season runs all year, cleanest Mar to Jul. In May the water sits around 18°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 0.9 m. The nearest airport is SNA (13 km) and the nearest town is Irvine (16 km).

What is under Huntington Beach

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor sits over 32 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 65 m against 32 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
