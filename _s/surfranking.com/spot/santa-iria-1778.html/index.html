Best time to surf Santa Iria

The best time to surf Santa Iria is January, February, December. The season runs all year, cleanest Dec to Mar. In January the water sits around 16°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 2.1 m. The nearest airport is PDL (22 km) and the nearest town is Ponta Delgada (19 km).

What is under Santa Iria

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is still shallow, 14 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 21 m against 14 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
