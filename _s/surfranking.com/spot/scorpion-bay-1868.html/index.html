What is under Scorpion Bay

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is already 497 m deep, deep right up to the door, so swell arrives with its energy intact. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 924 m against 497 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.

Modelled bathymetry: NOAA ETOPO 2022 global relief at 15 arc-seconds (about 460 m per cell), sampled along this spot’s swell window. At that scale the model represents broad passes and channels; it cannot see a reef ledge, so nothing here is said about how the wave breaks.
