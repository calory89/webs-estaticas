Best time to surf Point Saint George

The best time to surf Point Saint George is January, April, December. The season runs all year, cleanest Jan to Jan. In January the water sits around 11°C, so pack a 5/4 with boots and hood, and the typical wave is 3.1 m. The nearest airport is CEC (2 km) and the nearest town is Crescent City (6 km).

What is under Point Saint George

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor drops to 65 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 1694 m against 65 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
