Best time to surf Punta de Lobos

The best time to surf Punta de Lobos is February, March, May. The season runs all year, cleanest Nov to Sep. In February the water sits around 14°C, so pack a 4/3 with boots, and the typical wave is 1.5 m. The nearest airport is SCL (163 km) and the nearest town is Pichilemu (6 km).
