Best time to surf Jetty's

The best time to surf Jetty's is May, July, October. The season runs all year, cleanest Mar to Dec. In July the water sits around 28°C, so pack boardshorts, and the typical wave is 1.8 m. The nearest airport is XCH (3 km) and the nearest town is Sukabumi (414 km).

What is under Jetty's

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor sits over 27 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 63 m against 27 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
