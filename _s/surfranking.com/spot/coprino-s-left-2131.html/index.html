Best time to surf Coprino's left

The best time to surf Coprino's left is January, February, December. The season runs all year, cleanest Dec to Jan. In December the water sits around 15°C, so pack a 4/3 with boots, and the typical wave is 2.9 m. The nearest airport is LCG (10 km) and the nearest town is La Coruña (6 km).
