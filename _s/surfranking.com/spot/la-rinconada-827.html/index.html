Best time to surf La Rinconada

The best time to surf La Rinconada is February, May, June. The season runs all year, cleanest Feb to Sep. In February the water sits around 14°C, so pack a 4/3 with boots, and the typical wave is 1.8 m. The nearest airport is CCP (69 km) and the nearest town is Quirihue (28 km).
