Best time to surf Parlementia

The best time to surf Parlementia is February, March, November. The season runs all year, cleanest Nov to Apr. In February the water sits around 13°C, so pack a 5/4 with boots and hood, and the typical wave is 1.9 m. The nearest airport is BIQ (8 km) and the nearest town is Biarritz (6 km).

What is under Parlementia

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is still shallow, 11 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 24 m against 11 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
