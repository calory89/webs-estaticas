Best time to surf Faja da Areia

The best time to surf Faja da Areia is January, February, March. The season runs all year, cleanest Oct to Apr. In January the water sits around 18°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 2.1 m. The nearest airport is FNC (28 km) and the nearest town is Funchal (23 km).
