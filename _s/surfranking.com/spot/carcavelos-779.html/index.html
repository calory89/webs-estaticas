Best time to surf Carcavelos

The best time to surf Carcavelos is January, March, November. The season runs all year, cleanest Jan to Mar. In November the water sits around 18°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 2.3 m. The nearest airport is CAT (5 km) and the nearest town is Lisbon (17 km).
