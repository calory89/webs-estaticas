Best time to surf Point Venus

The best time to surf Point Venus is March, April, November. The season runs all year, cleanest Mar to May. In March the water sits around 28°C, so pack boardshorts, and the typical wave is 1.6 m. The nearest airport is PPT (13 km) and the nearest town is Papeete (8 km).
