Best time to surf Bundoran

The best time to surf Bundoran is January, February, December. The season runs Aug to Jun, cleanest Dec to Mar. In February the water sits around 8°C, so pack a 6/5 with boots, gloves and hood, and the typical wave is 1.6 m. The nearest airport is CFN (63 km) and the nearest town is Donegal (22 km).

What is under Bundoran

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is still shallow, 24 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 66 m against 24 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
