Best time to surf Skeleton Bay

The best time to surf Skeleton Bay is May, July, August. The season runs all year, cleanest Dec to Oct. In August the water sits around 12°C, so pack a 5/4 with boots and hood, and the typical wave is 1.6 m. The nearest airport is WVB (24 km) and the nearest town is Walvis Bay (9 km).

What is under Skeleton Bay

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor sits over 37 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 131 m against 37 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
