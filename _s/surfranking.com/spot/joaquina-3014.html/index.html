Best time to surf Joaquina

The best time to surf Joaquina is May, June, July. The season runs all year, but rarely clean. In May the water sits around 21°C, so pack a 2 mm shorty, and the typical wave is 1.3 m. The nearest airport is FLN (11 km) and the nearest town is Florianópolis (9 km).
