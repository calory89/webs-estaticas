Best time to surf Rincon

The best time to surf Rincon is January, May, December. The season runs all year, cleanest Jan to Jan. In January the water sits around 16°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 1.1 m. The nearest airport is SBA (34 km) and the nearest town is Santa Barbara (23 km).
