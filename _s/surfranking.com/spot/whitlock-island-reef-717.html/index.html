Best time to surf Whitlock Island Reef

The best time to surf Whitlock Island Reef is March, April, June. The season runs all year, cleanest Jan to Jun. In April the water sits around 22°C, so pack a 2 mm shorty, and the typical wave is 2.2 m. The nearest airport is GET (171 km) and the nearest town is Three Springs (114 km).
