Best time to surf The Right

The best time to surf The Right is April, May, June. The season runs all year, cleanest Mar to Jun. In April the water sits around 20°C, so pack a 2 mm shorty, and the typical wave is 2.4 m. The nearest airport is ALH (73 km) and the nearest town is Mount Barker (75 km).

What is under The Right

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor falls away into open ocean, 900 m, deep right up to the door, so swell arrives with its energy intact.

Modelled bathymetry: NOAA ETOPO 2022 global relief at 15 arc-seconds (about 460 m per cell), sampled along this spot’s swell window. At that scale the model represents broad passes and channels; it cannot see a reef ledge, so nothing here is said about how the wave breaks.
