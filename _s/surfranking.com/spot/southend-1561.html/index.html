Best time to surf Southend

The best time to surf Southend is January, March, November. The season runs all year, cleanest Jun to Jun. In March the water sits around 16°C, so pack a 4/3 with boots, and the typical wave is 2.1 m. The nearest airport is MGB (61 km) and the nearest town is Mount Gambier (64 km).
