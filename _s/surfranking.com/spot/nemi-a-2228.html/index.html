Best time to surf Nemiña

The best time to surf Nemiña is March, April, November. The season runs all year, cleanest Mar to Mar. In March the water sits around 13°C, so pack a 5/4 with boots and hood, and the typical wave is 2.7 m. The nearest airport is SCQ (70 km) and the nearest town is Santiago de Compostela (61 km).

What is under Nemiña

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor drops to 95 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 144 m against 95 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
