Best time to surf Supertubos

The best time to surf Supertubos is January, March, November. The season runs all year, cleanest Jan to Jan. In March the water sits around 14°C, so pack a 4/3 with boots, and the typical wave is 2.5 m. The nearest airport is LIS (66 km) and the nearest town is Santarém (60 km).

What is under Supertubos

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor drops to 100 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 199 m against 100 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
