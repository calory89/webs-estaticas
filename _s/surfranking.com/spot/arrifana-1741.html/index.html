Best time to surf Arrifana

The best time to surf Arrifana is February, March, November. The season runs all year, cleanest Oct to Mar. In March the water sits around 15°C, so pack a 4/3 with boots, and the typical wave is 2.3 m. The nearest airport is PRM (30 km) and the nearest town is Portimão (35 km).

What is under Arrifana

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor is still shallow, 24 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 46 m against 24 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
