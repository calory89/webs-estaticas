Best time to surf Mystery Point

The best time to surf Mystery Point is February, November, December. The season runs all year, cleanest Oct to Apr. In February the water sits around 16°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 2.1 m. The nearest airport is AGA (37 km) and the nearest town is Agadir (14 km).

What is under Mystery Point

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor falls away into open ocean, 1,129 m, deep right up to the door, so swell arrives with its energy intact. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 1841 m against 1129 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.

Modelled bathymetry: NOAA ETOPO 2022 global relief at 15 arc-seconds (about 460 m per cell), sampled along this spot’s swell window. At that scale the model represents broad passes and channels; it cannot see a reef ledge, so nothing here is said about how the wave breaks.
