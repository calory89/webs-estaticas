Best time to surf Malpica

The best time to surf Malpica is February, October, December. The season runs all year, cleanest Feb to Feb. In February the water sits around 13°C, so pack a 4/3 with boots, and the typical wave is 3.2 m. The nearest airport is LCG (35 km) and the nearest town is La Coruña (32 km).

What is under Malpica

Eight kilometres out along its swell window the sea floor sits over 55 m. Alongside the bank the survey finds a channel, 75 m against 55 m, deep enough to bend swell onto one peak instead of spreading it along the beach.
