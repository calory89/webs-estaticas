Best time to surf Queens

The best time to surf Queens is April, May, August. The season runs all year, cleanest Jan to May. In April the water sits around 17°C, so pack a 3/2 wetsuit, and the typical wave is 2.3 m. The nearest airport is CPT (22 km) and the nearest town is Cape Town (5 km).
