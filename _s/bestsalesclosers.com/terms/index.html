Terms, refunds & guarantee

These terms apply to The Closers Retreat 2027 by Best Sales Closers ("the Retreat"). Payments are processed by Eco Beach City LTD (United Kingdom). Contact: carlos@ecobeachcity.com. The contract you sign on acceptance prevails over this summary.

Price and payment

The Retreat costs €1,500 per person, paid in two parts: a €100 application fee when you apply, and a €1,400 balance only if your application is accepted. The price is the same for the in-person and the online format.

Application fee refund

If we do not accept your application, we refund the €100 application fee in full to the original payment method. If you are accepted, the €100 counts toward the €1,500 total.

What is included

3 days of live training and real call practice, access to closing positions on high-ticket offers through Best Sales Closers, and the commission guarantee below. In-person participants also get accommodation and all meals at Eco Beach City (Sumba, Indonesia) during the Retreat. Flights, visas, travel insurance and personal expenses are not included.

The €30,000 commission guarantee

If, within 12 months of the end of the Retreat, you have not earned €30,000 in commissions from closing positions offered through Best Sales Closers, we refund the €1,500 you paid. The guarantee applies only if you: attend the full Retreat; accept and actively work the closing positions we offer you during those 12 months; follow the system and the reporting we ask for; and request the refund in writing within 30 days after the 12-month period ends. The exact conditions, how commissions are counted and the refund process are set out in your contract.

Seats and dates

There are 10 seats per year in total, across both formats. In-person seats are limited. Dates are sent by email to accepted participants.

Cancellations by you

Write to us by email. Refunds for cancellations after acceptance are assessed case by case and depend on how close the request is to the Retreat date. Cancelling ends the guarantee.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for your travel documents, health and travel insurance, and for behaving respectfully toward other participants and staff.
