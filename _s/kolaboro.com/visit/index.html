Pick a .json export (yours or one you were sent). This page recomputes every fingerprint and the chain, then checks the signature. Nothing leaves your device.

How the record works

  • Each entry (a tick, a note or a photo) is written as a short canonical line with its time, GPS fix and the SHA-256 of the photo, plus the fingerprint of the entry before it. Its SHA-256 is the next link. Change, delete or reorder any entry and every later fingerprint stops matching.
  • The export is signed over the last fingerprint. With a passkey, your phone's secure chip signs after Face ID, a fingerprint or the screen lock. Without one, a signing key made on this device is used; its private half cannot be exported from the browser.
  • Time comes from your phone's clock and position from its GPS: we cannot prove either without a network. What the chain proves is that nothing was edited after it was recorded on this device.
  • Everything lives in this browser's storage on your device. Clearing site data deletes it, so export before you do.
