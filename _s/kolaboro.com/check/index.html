Analysing the project

    Check what the developer sent you

    Two checks that run on this device: nothing you choose here is uploaded.

    Permit or contract PDF: was it edited?

    Reads the file structure: saves made on top of the original, digital signatures and whether the signed bytes still match, producer and dates, and lines where the font changes part-way (what typing over a price or date leaves).

    Construction photos: when, where, and reused?

    Reads the date, camera and GPS position stored in each photo, measures the distance to the project's market, and compares each picture with the images of the projects we track.
