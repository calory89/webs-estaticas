What we shipped

Every improvement to Kolaboro, newest first.

  1. 30 Oct 2026 · evening
    • Live cases now cover 228 off-plan projects in 43 markets and 27 countries, each with a public-disclosure score (permit number, deposit protection, construction progress, plans, track record, no "guaranteed" returns) and problems reported in public sources. See the map
    • Free desk review shows results straight away (email optional) and compares the project with the others we track in the same market
    • Document reader shows plain field names; fixed an analytics error that appeared on every page
  2. 30 Oct 2026 · afternoon
    • Live video from the site: GPS and time on every frame, recorded on the phone and sealed into the case; buyers watch from their portal. Try it
    • Field tool installs on the phone and works without signal: photos and notes keep their original time and GPS and upload when the signal is back
    • Report drafts written from the evidence, edited by a reviewer; corrections are counted and published
    • Random audits: one visit in ten is repeated by a second agent
    • Share your case with a read-only link, and recommend Kolaboro for €50 off
    • Pilot API for agencies, lenders and lawyers · open weekly metrics · pay by card without a PayPal account
  3. 30 Oct 2026
    • Client portal: evidence locker with a fingerprint chain you can verify in your browser, case file PDF with a QR code to its Bitcoin timestamp
    • Document reader for contracts, permits, company deeds and invoices, as photos or PDF, answering in 8 languages
    • Plot on a satellite map, compared with the certificate area, and a satellite watch that emails you when your plot changes (Sentinel-2, radar under clouds)
    • Field tool for agents: 8-step guided visit, photos signed on the phone and rejected automatically when blurry, old, duplicated or away from the plot; quality score and earnings
    • Follow any tracked project and get an email when its completion date, price band or documents change
    • Price per m² check, a measured accuracy benchmark for the reader, email notifications, daily backups and uptime monitoring
  4. 29 Oct 2026
    • Technology page: the whole verification engine, labelled live, beta, building or next
    • Plan vs reality: computer vision compares site photos with the developer's claim and what has been paid
    • Every evidence fingerprint timestamped through OpenTimestamps and confirmed in Bitcoin blocks
    • Satellite watch: weekly Sentinel-2 composites of Bali development areas, land changed since 2023
    • Verify any report photo yourself at /verify; status page and open data docs
  5. 28 Oct 2026
    • Research agent extended to 122 off-plan projects in 9 markets
