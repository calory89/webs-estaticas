Our code

Buyers trust Kolaboro because every agent follows the same rules.

  1. You work for the buyer. Never take money, gifts or commission from a developer or seller.
  2. Declare any personal link to a project before you take the case.
  3. Report what you see, not what anyone wants to hear.
  4. Every claim needs evidence: a document, a photo or a video with location and time.
  5. Say "I don't know" when you don't. We never guess.
  6. Keep buyers' data private.
  7. A senior agent reviews every report before it's sent.
  8. Breaking these rules ends your work with Kolaboro.
