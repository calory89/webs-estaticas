Court cases against developers, with case numbers

Public court cases against developers we track, each with its case number. Facts only: court, number, date, the companies named, what the record says and the outcome or status. Private individuals who sued are not named. A filed complaint states allegations, not findings.

12 cases, last searched 19 Nov 2026. Data: court-cases.json (CC BY 4.0 (credit: Kolaboro, kolaboro.com)). Where to search courts and registers yourself: official registers.

How this list is made

  • Searched the published judgments of the DIFC Courts (difccourts.ae) and press reports that print a case number, for developers and projects in our records, on 19 Nov 2026.
  • Official court pages are marked official; where only a press report or a docket index prints the number, the source is named.
  • Dubai Courts, most national courts and arbitration awards do not publish searchable judgments: a developer missing here may still have cases.
