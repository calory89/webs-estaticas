Kolaboro / For lawyers and notaries

For lawyers and notaries

When a client buys off-plan abroad, you advise on the contract and the law. A Kolaboro case file gives you the facts around it: what the developer promised, what the registers say, and what is on site, each with its source.

Apply to the partner programmeSee a sample report

What a case file gives you

Evidence you can check yourself

Every finding in a case file points to where it came from, so you can rely on the source and not on our word.

Sources

Evidence with sources

Each check links to the register entry, regulator notice, court record, news report or developer page it rests on, with a verification level: registry, independent, developer only or contradicted.

Fingerprints

Page and file fingerprints

We record a SHA-256 fingerprint of the developer pages we read and of every photo and document, so you can show later what a page said and that a file has not changed.

Timestamps

Bitcoin timestamps

Fingerprints are anchored in Bitcoin with OpenTimestamps. Anyone can check the proof independently, without trusting Kolaboro. Try it on verify evidence.

On site

GPS-stamped photos

When a local agent visits, each photo carries its GPS position and time, so it can be matched to the plot and the date. Site visits are launching in Bali first.

Typical uses: checking a permit claim before your client signs, documenting progress before a stage payment, and building a dated record of what was promised if a project runs late.

Clear lines

What we never do

  • We never give legal advice. We verify facts and say what we found. Advice, contract review and signing stay with you.
  • We never hold client money. No deposits, no escrow, no payments pass through Kolaboro.
  • We never take money from developers. Buyers pay for checks. The evidence sets every finding. See independence.

We are building a list of independent lawyers and notaries our buyers can turn to in each market. Tell us about your practice. We read every application and reply by email; applying does not commit you to anything.
