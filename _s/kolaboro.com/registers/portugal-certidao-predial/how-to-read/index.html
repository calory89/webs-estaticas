How to read a Portuguese certidão permanente for an off-plan flat

In Portugal the land registry (registo predial) is run by the Ministry of Justice’s registry offices. Its online certificate, the certidão permanente, is ordered on Predial Online. This guide uses the Predial Online page and its FAQ and the Land Registry Code, read on 27 November 2026.

Step by step

  1. Get the registry details from the developer or the promissory contract (contrato-promessa): district, municipality (concelho), parish (freguesia) and the description number (número de descrição). For a flat in a divided building you also need its fraction letter.
  2. Open predialonline.pt and choose Certidão Permanente, then Criar. The FAQ says anyone can order it with a name and email, without a digital certificate. You can also search by the tax (matriz) article, but you must confirm the registry description before the certificate is released.
  3. Pay the fee. The FAQ gives 15 euros online and 20 euros at a registry office for six months of access, by card, Multibanco or e-banking. You receive an access code by email once the certificate is ready.
  4. Read the description of the property and who holds it. The certificate shows every registration in force on the property and every request still pending, with the number and date of its apresentação and the type of act requested.
  5. Look for horizontal property (propriedade horizontal), the division of a building into separate fractions. Under article 92 of the Land Registry Code, registering it before the building is finished is provisional by nature, and so are acts on fractions before that division is registered definitively.
  6. Read the mortgages, attachments and other charges. Then ask whether your own purchase can be registered provisionally: article 47 allows a provisional registration of acquisition before the deed, based on the owner’s declaration or on a promissory contract unless agreed otherwise.

What the fields show

Field (official label)What it tells youUse it for
Descrição (número de descrição, fração)Which property the certificate covers, and the fraction letter for a unit.Matching the contract
Aquisição (titular)Who holds the property right. For an off-plan flat this should be the company selling to you.Developer track record
Propriedade horizontal (provisória)Whether the building has been divided into fractions, and whether that registration is still provisional.Construction progress
Hipoteca, penhoraMortgages and attachments registered on the property.Seeing what must be cancelled before your deed
Apresentações pendentesRequests filed but not yet registered, with their number, date and type of act.Spotting a sale or a charge about to be registered

What it does not prove

  • It does not show planning status: whether the building licence or the use licence was issued. Those come from the municipality (câmara municipal).
  • A printout has no legal value as a certificate; the FAQ says the online consultation with the access code is what counts.
  • It does not show whether your deposits are guaranteed, the developer’s finances, or how far the works have progressed.

Red flags

  • The registered owner is a person or company other than the seller, and the contract does not explain why.
  • A pending request (apresentação) you were not told about, such as another acquisition or a new mortgage.
  • An attachment (penhora) or insolvency registration on the property.
  • The developer cannot give you a description number, or the fractions in the contract do not exist in the registered horizontal property.

How it ties to Kolaboro’s six checks

  • Developer track record: the registered owner gives you the exact company name to look up.
  • Construction progress: a horizontal property that is still provisional means the building was not finished when it was registered.
  • Permit or licence number: not in this certificate. Ask the municipality for the building licence.
  • Deposit protection, floor or site plans and no guaranteed returns: not in this certificate. Check the promissory contract and the developer’s documents.

A cheaper option, the Informação Predial Simplificada, gives non-certified information for one year: the essential description, the owners and whether mortgages, attachments, other charges or pending requests exist. The FAQ says it does not replace a land registry certificate where the law requires one.
