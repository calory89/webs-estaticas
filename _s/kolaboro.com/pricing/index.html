Pricing questions

Who pays Kolaboro?

Buyers pay for checks and monitoring. Developers never pay Kolaboro: the evidence sets every Score. Agents never take money from developers.

Is Kolaboro a lawyer or a notary?

No. Kolaboro checks facts in documents and official registers and tells you what it found; checks on site by local agents are on request: we confirm a local agent for your location before you pay. For legal advice and to sign, use an independent lawyer or notary; we can tell you what to ask them.

Do you hold my money?

Not yet: escrow is not offered today, and milestone escrow with licensed partners is on our roadmap. The plan is that a local agent confirms each milestone on site before you pay; site visits are on request: we confirm a local agent for your location before you pay.

Where do you work?

In any country. The desk review and the official-register lookups run online for off-plan projects anywhere. On-site visits by a local agent in the project's country are on request: when you send a request, we confirm a local agent for your location before you pay, and if we can't take your case you get a full refund.

How do you decide "go" or "hold"?

Nine checks, from land title to realistic promises. A red flag on title, permits or progress means "hold" until it is fixed, and every finding links to its evidence. The full method is public.

Can a developer influence a report?

Only with evidence. Developers can send documents and reply, but never pay us. Where something can't be verified, the report says so instead of guessing.

What if I already bought?

That's when monitoring matters most. Today we can check the registers, the permits and the developer's record before your next payment; checks of construction progress on site are on request: we confirm a local agent for your location before you pay.
