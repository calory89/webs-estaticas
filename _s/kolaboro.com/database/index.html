How to read it

RegistryConfirmed on an official source: a land department, permit register, licence list or company registry.
ConfirmedConfirmed by an independent source such as a regulator notice, a court record, a stock-exchange filing or a major newspaper.
Not confirmedOnly the developer, its sales agents or listing portals say so.
ContradictedSources disagree, or an official source says something different from the developer.

A published document is not proof that it is genuine; that is what a check at the registry is for, together with checks on site by local agents, which are on request: we confirm a local agent for your location before you pay. Problems are listed only from news, courts and regulators. Developers can reply on any project. Data: JSON · API · method · corrections.
