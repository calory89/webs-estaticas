What to do now

Before you pay instalment 3 on 4 November.

  1. Ask for a late-delivery penalty in writing.We sent you suggested wording: 0.1% of the price per week of delay, capped at 10%.
  2. Get the revised schedule signed.Milestone 3 was due 31 May; it was completed 5 months late. The new dates for milestones 4 to 6 should be in an annex.
  3. Pay only to PT Kenanga Bangun's account.Never a personal account, never a "new" account by message.
  4. Plan with 7 to 9% rental yield, not 14%.That's what comparable villas earn after costs.
