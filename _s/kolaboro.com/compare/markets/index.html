Where each figure comes from

  • Transparency index: the same computation as /transparency-index/ (shared code, markets with at least 3 projects), from /data/bali-cases.json.
  • Delay index: share of projects past their first advertised handover and median months late, from /data/delay-index.json (method).
  • Price index: chained index of advertised asking prices, from /data/price-index.json (method). Shown only where the market has its own series.
  • Hazards: survey areas in /data/hazards.json whose label names the market or its country, and the tracked projects of the market read at their sites (method).
  • Promises kept and broken: /data/promises.json (method).
  • Projects and checks published: /data/bali-cases.json. Register reachability: /data/register-checks.json (method), matched by market name, then by country.
