Buying property in the Algarve from abroad

Portugal lets foreigners buy property freely, which makes it simple to start and easy to skip the checks that matter.

Join the Algarve waitlistUse the free checklist

The three things that go wrong most

Registration

Check the land registry certificate (certidão permanente) and the tax record (caderneta predial) match the property and the seller.

Licences

A home needs a valid usage licence (licença de utilização). Extensions built without permission are common.

Off-plan

Tie payments to construction stages and know what happens to your money if the developer stops.

Check these before you pay
