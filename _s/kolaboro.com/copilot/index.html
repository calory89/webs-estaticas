Kolaboro / Agent copilot

A local, an AI copilot and a senior reviewer. That's how we make reliable property verifiers.

Verifying an off-plan villa abroad used to need a lawyer, a surveyor and a friend on the ground. Kolaboro trains people who already live there, and our copilot tells them on every case what to ask for, what to check on site and when to call a notary.

How someone new becomes a verifier you can trust

  1. 1 · LearnTen short lessons and an exam in the Kolaboro Academy: land titles, permits, payment plans, red flags. Free, no degree needed.
  2. 2 · Work with the copilotOn every case: a desk review of the developer's website, document cross-checks like the one above, and a milestone checklist in our field app that stamps every photo with GPS, time and a SHA-256 fingerprint.
  3. 3 · Get reviewedUntil an agent has delivered 10 reports with no material corrections, a senior reviewer checks every report before the buyer sees it. When the copilot escalates, a notary or lawyer signs off.
  4. 4 · Earn trustEach agent has a quality record: reviewer corrections, issues found, time to report. Better records mean fewer reviews and more cases. How we choose agents.
