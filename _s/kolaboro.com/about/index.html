Kolaboro / About

Why Kolaboro exists

Buying a home abroad should feel like buying one at home: you know what you're getting, and someone is on your side.

Our mission

Make it safe to buy property anywhere on Earth, by putting a trained local on the buyer's side of every deal and making every developer's track record public.

Why "Kolaboro"

It comes from collaborating. One buyer alone is easy to mislead. Thousands of buyers sharing what they found, through agents who work only for them, are not.

What we believe

  • Evidence beats promises. Every claim we make has a document, a video or a record behind it.
  • Independence is the product. Our agents are never paid by sellers.
  • Expertise can be taught. With AI, a sharp local can do work that used to need a room full of specialists.
  • Honest developers deserve to win. A public record rewards the ones who build what they sell.
