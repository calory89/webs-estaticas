Kolaboro off-plan data, November 2026
=====================================

Headline numbers

1. 8.2% of tracked off-plan projects with a usable handover date (62 of 755) are past the first handover date on record.
   Source: https://kolaboro.com/index/delays/ and https://kolaboro.com/data/delay-index.json

2. Median time past the first handover date among those 62 projects: 10.5 months (90th percentile: 35 months).
   Source: https://kolaboro.com/index/delays/ and https://kolaboro.com/data/delay-index.json

3. 4.1% (31 of 755) are past the handover date their sellers advertise today.
   Source: https://kolaboro.com/index/delays/ and https://kolaboro.com/data/bali-cases.json

4. Median transparency index across 95 markets with 3 or more tracked projects: 42 out of 100.
   Source: https://kolaboro.com/transparency-index/ and https://kolaboro.com/api/open/markets

5. Advertised asking-price index, all tracked markets: 100.03 on 2026-11-03 (base 100 on 2026-10-28, same-project pairs only).
   Source: https://kolaboro.com/index/prices/ and https://kolaboro.com/data/price-index.json

Summary

Kolaboro data show 896 tracked projects across 144 markets in November 2026. Of these, 755 projects have a handover date that can be used for the delay index. History for the delay index starts this month. Among those 755 projects, 62 are past the first handover date, a share of 8.2%, and 31 are past the current advertised handover date, a share of 4.1%. For projects past the first handover date, the median is 10.5 months and the 90th percentile is 35.0 months. In 50 markets, at least 5 projects have a handover date. The transparency index covers 95 markets, with a median of 42.0 on a scale with a maximum of 100. The price index for all markets stands at 100.03, against a base of 100.

(Every number in this summary was checked against the data by code.)
