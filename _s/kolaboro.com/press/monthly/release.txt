Summary

Kolaboro data for November 2026 cover 896 off-plan projects in 144 markets. Of the 755 projects with a usable handover date that are not completed, 62 (8.2%) are past the first handover date on record, with a median of 10.5 months and a 90th percentile of 35 months past that date. 31 (4.1%) are past the date their sellers advertise today. The median transparency index across 95 markets with 3 or more projects is 42 out of 100. The advertised asking-price index for all tracked markets stood at 100.03 on 2026-11-03, against a base of 100 on 2026-10-28. History for the delay index starts this month.
