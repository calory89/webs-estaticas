Summary

Kolaboro data show 896 tracked projects across 144 markets in November 2026. Of these, 755 projects have a handover date that can be used for the delay index. History for the delay index starts this month. Among those 755 projects, 62 are past the first handover date, a share of 8.2%, and 31 are past the current advertised handover date, a share of 4.1%. For projects past the first handover date, the median is 10.5 months and the 90th percentile is 35.0 months. In 50 markets, at least 5 projects have a handover date. The transparency index covers 95 markets, with a median of 42.0 on a scale with a maximum of 100. The price index for all markets stands at 100.03, against a base of 100.

How this release is made

A scheduled job on our build system reads the open files at the start of each month: the delivery-delay index, the asking-price index and the per-market transparency index (recomputed from the project file with the published formula). It picks the five numbers above with fixed rules, draws the charts and writes the plain text and JSON versions.

An AI model (Claude) may draft the summary paragraph from those numbers only. Code then checks the draft: every number in it must appear in the data, spelled-out numbers, dashes and loaded words such as "soaring" or "crisis" are refused, and if anything fails the check the release uses a fixed template filled from the data instead. The page says which one you are reading. Each run spends under 2 USD on AI, logged in the JSON.
