Kolaboro in one line

Kolaboro is a network of AI-trained local agents who work only for property buyers abroad, verifying land, permits and construction, and publishing a Trust Score for every project and developer.

Longer description

Buying property in another country usually means trusting photos and promises. Kolaboro puts a trained local agent on the buyer's side: they check the land title and permits, film every construction milestone with GPS- and time-stamped video, and tell the buyer when each payment is safe. Every check feeds a public Trust Score that rewards developers who deliver. Kolaboro is launching in Bali.
