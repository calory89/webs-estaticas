Every buyer makes the next buyer safer.

That's the idea behind the name. Every inspection, every delay and every finished home feeds the Kolaboro Trust Score: a public 0 to 100 rating of every project and developer, built on proof instead of price tags.

Developers can't buy it. Marketing can't fake it. Only buildings can earn it.

  • Nine checks, one number. Title, permits, delivery record, progress, money safety, contract, transparency, buyer voice and realistic promises, each scored 0 to 100.
  • Every point has evidence. A registry document, a GPS-stamped site video or a verified buyer record. You can open the reason behind each score.
  • Red flags cap the Score. If the land title can't be verified, the project can't score above 40, however good it looks.
  • Developers can reply. They can add documents and fix what's missing. Where we don't have enough data, we say so instead of guessing.

Questions buyers ask us

Straight answers, including what we don't do.

Who pays Kolaboro?

Buyers pay for checks and monitoring. Developers can pay to be verified, but paying never changes a Score: the evidence does. Agents never take money from developers.

Is Kolaboro a lawyer or a notary?

No. Kolaboro agents verify facts on the ground and in documents and tell you what they found. For legal advice and to sign, use an independent lawyer or notary; we can tell you what to ask them.

Do you hold my money?

Not yet. Today your agent confirms each milestone on site and tells you whether it's safe to pay. Milestone escrow with licensed partners is on our roadmap.

Where do you work?

We are launching in Bali first and adding cities as we train local agents. If you are buying somewhere else, tell us where: demand decides the next city.

How is the Trust Score calculated?

Nine checks, from land title to realistic promises, each scored 0 to 100 and weighted. Red flags cap the total, and every point links to its evidence. The full method is public.

Can a developer change their Score?

Only with evidence. Developers can add documents and reply publicly. Where data is missing, the page says so instead of guessing.

What if I already bought?

That's when monitoring matters most. We can verify where construction really is before your next payment.
