Questions buyers ask us

Straight answers, including what we don't do.

Who pays Kolaboro?

Buyers pay for checks and monitoring. Developers never pay Kolaboro: the evidence sets every Score. Agents never take money from developers.

Is Kolaboro a lawyer or a notary?

No. Kolaboro agents verify facts on the ground and in documents and tell you what they found. For legal advice and to sign, use an independent lawyer or notary; we can tell you what to ask them.

Do you hold my money?

Not yet. Today your agent confirms each milestone on site and tells you whether the evidence supports paying. Milestone escrow with licensed partners is on our roadmap.

Where do you work?

We are launching in Bali first and adding cities as we train local agents. If you are buying somewhere else, tell us where: demand decides the next city.

How do you decide "go" or "hold"?

Nine checks, from land title to realistic promises. A red flag on title, permits or progress means "hold" until it is fixed, and every finding links to its evidence. The full method is public.

Can a developer influence a report?

Only with evidence. Developers can send documents and reply, but never pay us. Where something can't be verified, the report says so instead of guessing.

What if I already bought?

That's when monitoring matters most. We can verify where construction really is before your next payment.

Why I started Kolaboro

For years I sold property abroad to buyers from all over the world. Almost every one of them asked me the same things before saying yes:

  • Is the land really the seller's?
  • Are the permits real?
  • Will it be built on time?
  • Where exactly does my money go?

I couldn't give them an independent answer, because I was the seller. Kolaboro is that answer: someone on the buyer's side, with evidence. If you have a question, write to me directly.
