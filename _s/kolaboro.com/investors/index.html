The trust layer for buying property abroad.

Kolaboro turns local people into AI-trained buyer's agents and turns every inspection into a public trust record of projects and developers.

The problem

People buy homes in other countries without seeing the land, the permits or the construction. The developer has lawyers, salespeople and local knowledge. The buyer has a PDF. Fear of exactly this stops many more people from buying at all.

Why now

  • Remote buying is normal. Video calls, e-signatures and international transfers made buying property from another country routine.
  • AI makes expertise cheap to train. A sharp local with an AI coach can do verification work that used to need a lawyer, a surveyor and an engineer.
  • Proof is cheap to capture. Every phone records tamper-evident, location- and time-stamped video.

What kind of company this is

  • An AI-native service. Buyers don't get software to figure it out themselves. We sell the work: a verified answer, with evidence, usually within 7 days.
  • Experts made with AI. Our own research agent reads what developers promise. Local agents without professional credentials, trained and checked by AI and by us, verify it on site.
  • Built for AI assistants too. Our research and methodology are published in machine-readable form, so the AI assistants people use to plan a purchase abroad can rely on them.

Why it compounds

Every buyer we protect adds evidence to the Trust Score. A better Score attracts honest developers and more buyers, and every agent we train opens a new city without opening an office.

Go-to-market

Our customers are Americans, Australians and Europeans buying abroad. We start in Bali, the high-risk market we know best, then Mexico, where US buyers face the same problem. Our founder has sold over €15M of real estate to international buyers and runs travel media with more than 400,000 followers (founder bio), which gives us a direct line to the people who buy abroad.

Assumptions

  1. Dubai: all 149,290 off-plan transactions, because the source does not publish buyer nationality. It includes UAE residents and nationals, so it overstates foreign buyers. That is why we also show the total without Dubai.
  2. Spain: close to 93,000 purchases by foreigners times 20.13% new homes, about 18,700. New homes stand in for off-plan. "Foreigners" includes foreigners who live in Spain.
  3. Turkey, Thailand, Portugal: all purchases by foreigners, because no source we found splits off-plan from completed homes. These counts are a ceiling.
  4. One transaction counts as one buyer. Some buyers buy more than one unit, which overstates buyers.
  5. Full ladder per buyer, €3,678: a €490 check, plus €99 a month of monitoring for 12 months (€1,188; the 12 months is our assumption, not a sourced build time), plus the €2,000 minimum buyer's agent fee (the real fee is 1% of the price, so this is a floor).
  6. Not counted: Bali, Mexico and every other market we track, B2B data revenue and repeat checks.

Business model

How Kolaboro makes money

Buyers pay. Developers never pay Kolaboro, and paying is not possible. Each step below is marked live only if a buyer or a business can use it today.

  1. Live

    Free desk review

    Paste a project's website and our research agent shows what the developer promises and what to verify, in about a minute. Free, no card. It is how buyers find us. Try it

  2. Live

    Pre-purchase check, €490

    Title, permits, developer record, contract red flags and a site visit with GPS video, usually within 7 days. The price is published and payment works; first paid checks are in progress. Pricing

  3. Live

    Construction monitoring, €99 a month

    A GPS-stamped site visit each month and a "go" or "hold" before every payment, from first payment to keys. Recurring revenue for the length of the build.

  4. Live

    Buyer's agent, 1% of the price, minimum €2,000

    Someone on the buyer's side for the whole purchase. Offered on request.

  5. Live, free

    Open data API

    Every anonymous project and market as JSON and CSV under CC BY 4.0, and a pilot API for agencies, lenders and lawyers, free during the pilot. It builds distribution, not revenue yet. API docs · Pilot

  6. Planned

    Lender and insurer data

    Project risk data for banks that finance off-plan purchases, and delay signals for insurers of buyer deposits. Roadmap

  7. Planned

    Portal and agency products

    Webhook alerts and white-label checks for portals and agencies. The live listing badge exists today and is free.

  8. Planned

    Milestone escrow with licensed partners

    Money released only when an agent verifies the work. We do not hold client money today.

Defensibility

Why this is hard to copy

A data history nobody can backfill

Every day our agent reads developer pages and fingerprints what it read. Today's seal covers … project pages, anchored in Bitcoin so nobody, including us, can change the date later. We record what changed on each project page and when: … changes detected so far. A competitor starting today cannot go back and collect what a developer said last year.

The database · Verify evidence

An open standard we wrote

The Off-Plan Disclosure Standard lists what every project should publish so a buyer can check it. It is free to cite under CC BY 4.0. When developers, portals and journalists use it, they use our definitions and point back to our data.

The standard

Agents with signed evidence

Local agents, trained and checked by AI and by us, capture photos signed on the phone with time, GPS and device. Each fingerprint goes into a chain a lawyer can check. The network is being recruited in Bali now; each trained agent opens a city without an office.

How agents work

Developers can answer, but never pay

Every developer can reply to any finding with documents, free, and the reply is shown next to the finding. Because payment is impossible, the Score cannot be bought, which is the reason buyers can trust it and a reason a paid-listing business cannot copy it.
