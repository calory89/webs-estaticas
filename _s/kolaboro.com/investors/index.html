Kolaboro / Investors

The trust layer for buying property abroad.

Kolaboro turns local people into AI-trained buyer's agents and turns every inspection into a public trust record of projects and developers.

The problem

People buy homes in other countries without seeing the land, the permits or the construction. The developer has lawyers, salespeople and local knowledge. The buyer has a PDF. Fear of exactly this stops many more people from buying at all.

Why now

  • Remote buying is normal. Video calls, e-signatures and international transfers made buying property from another country routine.
  • AI makes expertise cheap to train. A sharp local with an AI coach can do verification work that used to need a lawyer, a surveyor and an engineer.
  • Proof is cheap to capture. Every phone records tamper-evident, location- and time-stamped video.

What we sell

  • Buyers: pre-purchase checks, construction monitoring and a buyer's agent fee.
  • Developers: verification and a live Trust Score badge. Payment never changes the Score.
  • Data: developer and project trust data for banks, insurers and property portals.
  • Payments (roadmap): milestone escrow with licensed partners, released when our agent verifies the work.

Why it compounds

Every buyer we protect adds evidence to the Trust Score. A better Score attracts honest developers and more buyers, and every agent we train opens a new city without opening an office.

Go-to-market

Bali first, where foreign demand is strong and buyers' doubts are well known. Then the cities buyers ask for. Our founder has sold over €15M of real estate to international buyers and runs travel media with more than 400,000 followers, which gives us a direct line to the people who buy abroad.
