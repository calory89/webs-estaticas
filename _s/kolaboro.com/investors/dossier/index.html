History, where it exists

Kolaboro launched on 27 October 2026, so most series are days long. They are shown as recorded, without smoothing or extrapolation. Hover a bar for its value; each chart has a table.

What is deliberately not here

  • No projections, forecasts, market-size multiples or "run rate" figures.
  • No revenue or customer numbers: none are computed from public data, so none are shown.
  • No metric we cannot source. "Not measured yet" means exactly that.

For the story, team and market, see Investors. For how each number is produced, see the trust center, engineering and the open API.
