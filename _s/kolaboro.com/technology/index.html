Live · anchored in Bitcoin

Verify a piece of evidence yourself

Drop any photo or document from a Kolaboro report. Your browser computes its SHA-256 fingerprint; the file never leaves your device. We look the fingerprint up in our public timestamp record and give you the OpenTimestamps proof, which you can check with free, independent tools.

How it works: each fingerprint is submitted to independent OpenTimestamps calendars (run by the OpenTimestamps project and Eternity Wall). They aggregate it into a Bitcoin transaction within hours. After that, nobody, including us, can backdate or alter the evidence without it showing.

Why this gets harder to copy every week

Evidence nobody can backdateGPS, time, SHA-256 and a Bitcoin-anchored timestamp on every photo. A competitor can copy the screens, not two years of anchored evidence.
Every correction trains the copilotSenior reviewers correct junior agents; each correction becomes a test case. The share of reports needing review falls, and so does our cost.
Verify once, sell many timesEach project's evidence history compounds. The tenth buyer of a project gets two years of dated evidence for the cost of a short update visit.
A trained local networkAgents recruited, examined and scored by our own pipeline, in places where trusted people are the scarce resource.
