Kolaboro / Accessibility

Accessibility statement

We want every buyer to be able to use Kolaboro, including people who use a screen reader, only a keyboard, zoom or high contrast.

Our target

Our target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA, for every public page of kolaboro.com.

We do not claim full conformance. The automated results below still list open issues, and automated tests alone cannot prove conformance.

How we test

  • Every night: axe-core runs in Chrome on every page in our sitemap with the rules tagged WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A and AA. The results are published in /data/a11y.json and on /status. Our goal is zero serious or critical findings.
  • Every day: Lighthouse checks accessibility on our 20 key pages on mobile and desktop (/data/lighthouse.json), a crawl checks every page on a phone and a desktop for broken links, script errors and sideways scrolling, and visual tests compare the key pages at 390 and 1440 px with the day before.
  • When we change a page: we check it in a browser at 390 and 1440 px for keyboard focus, tap target size and text that overflows.

Known limitations

  • Automated tools find only part of what WCAG covers. Whether a text alternative is meaningful, or whether a complex widget makes sense with a screen reader, needs a person to judge.
  • The nightly axe run uses one desktop window size (1280 x 900). Phone layouts are covered by the crawl and the visual tests, which do not check every WCAG rule.
  • Maps on some pages are hard to use without a mouse or without sight. The data behind them is open: /data/bali-cases.json and the open data API.

Report a problem

If something on Kolaboro is hard or impossible for you to use, email hello@kolaboro.com with the page address, what you were trying to do, and, if you like, the browser and assistive technology you use. We will reply and tell you what we will do about it. If you need a report or a document in another format, ask in the same email.
