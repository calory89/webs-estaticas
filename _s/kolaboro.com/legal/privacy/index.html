Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and your rights.

Who we are

kolaboro.com is operated by Equinox Investment Group LLC, 30 N Gould St Ste N, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA. Contact: carlos@equinoxinvestmentgroup.com.

What we collect

  • What you send us in a form: name, email, phone (optional), place, project and message.
  • Usage data through Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity, which use cookies and similar technology to understand how the site is used.
  • Drafts and checklist ticks saved only in your own browser.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your request and provide the service you ask for (contract or steps before a contract).
  • To improve the website (legitimate interest).

Who we share it with

Our hosting and database providers (Cloudflare, Supabase), analytics providers (Google, Microsoft) and, only with your agreement, the local Kolaboro agent handling your case. We don't sell your data.

How long we keep it

Enquiries for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you become a client, in which case we keep records as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or export your data, or object to its use, by emailing us. If you are in the EU or UK you can also complain to your data protection authority.
