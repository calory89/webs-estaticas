Terms of use

The short version: we verify and inform, you decide.

The service

Kolaboro provides independent verification and information about property projects through local agents. We are not a law firm, notary, surveyor, bank or financial adviser, and nothing on this site is legal, tax or financial advice.

Trust Scores

Trust Scores and reports are opinions based on the evidence listed and the date it was collected. Projects change; always check the date. We correct errors when shown evidence, and developers can reply on every page.

Your decisions

You decide whether to buy and when to pay. Use an independent lawyer and notary for legal checks and signing.

Paying for a check

Pre-purchase checks are paid in advance through PayPal (PayPal balance or card). If we can't take your case, or can't start within 7 days of confirming it, we refund the full amount. Once the site visit has taken place, the fee is not refundable. Monthly monitoring and buyer's agent services are invoiced separately after we agree the scope.

Payments and escrow

Kolaboro does not currently hold client money for property purchases. "Go" advice is a recommendation based on what our agent verified.

Liability

To the extent the law allows, our liability for any service is limited to the fees you paid us for it.
