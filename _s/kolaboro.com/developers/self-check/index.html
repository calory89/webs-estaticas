Estimate your Trust Score in 2 minutes

Nine honest questions, the same weights we use. It shows where you stand before an agent ever visits.

1. Is the land certificate in your company's name, and can a buyer verify it at the registry?
2. Do you hold the building approval for this project, and the rental licence if buyers will rent?
3. Did you hand over your past projects on the dates in the contracts?
4. Is this project on schedule today?
5. Do buyers pay only into your company account, ideally escrow, and only by milestone?
6. Does your contract include a delivery date with a penalty or refund?
7. Are your company, directors and registration easy to find?
8. Would your past buyers recommend you if we called them?
9. Are your advertised rental yields based on real booking data?
