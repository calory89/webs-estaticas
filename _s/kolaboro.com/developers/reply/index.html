Kolaboro / Developers / Right of reply

Answer any finding, free

Every project in our database is anonymous until a check is complete and the developer has had 10 working days to reply. Send your documents here; we review them, update the verification level where they confirm a claim, and publish your reply next to the finding. Developers never pay us, and paying is not possible.

What happens next

  1. We confirm your email belongs to the developer.
  2. We check each document against the issuing register or an independent source.
  3. Within 10 working days we update the project: a confirmed document raises its verification level; your reply is published next to the finding either way.

See what we expect developers to publish in the Off-Plan Disclosure Standard, use the free templates, or compare yourself with your market in the benchmark.
