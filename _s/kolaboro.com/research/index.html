Kolaboro Research Agent

Our own software reads what developers promise and tracks it week after week. It's how the Trust Score starts: before an agent visits, we already know what was promised.

See this week's dataHow the Trust Score uses itLive report, computed in your browser

How the agent works

  1. 1

    Reads

    Every week it opens the public pages of the projects we track: developers' own sites and new-build portals.

  2. 2

    Extracts

    Our extraction model pulls out what is promised: price, title, lease years, extensions, completion date, yields, guarantees and permit claims, with the exact quotes.

  3. 3

    Compares

    It compares with last week and logs every change: a delivery date that moves, a yield that grows, a "guarantee" that appears.

  4. 4

    Hands over

    Once we confirm a local agent for your location (site visits are on request), the local agent on the case gets the list of promises to verify. What they find becomes the Trust Score.
