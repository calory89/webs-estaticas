Kolaboro Research Agent

Our own AI agent, built on Claude, reads what developers promise and tracks it week after week. It's how the Trust Score starts: before an agent visits, we already know what was promised.

How the agent works

  1. 1

    Reads

    Every week it opens the public pages of the projects we track: developers' own sites and new-build portals.

  2. 2

    Extracts

    Claude pulls out what is promised: price, title, lease years, extensions, completion date, yields, guarantees and permit claims, with the exact quotes.

  3. 3

    Compares

    It compares with last week and logs every change: a delivery date that moves, a yield that grows, a "guarantee" that appears.

  4. 4

    Hands over

    Our agents on the ground get the list of promises to verify. What they find becomes the Trust Score.
