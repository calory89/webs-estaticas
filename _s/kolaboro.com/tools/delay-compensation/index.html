Questions to ask the developer and your lawyer

    How it works

    • Months late run from the promised date to the expected or actual date.
    • Grace period: the first months of delay, during which the clause pays nothing. Compensation counts only the months after it.
    • Fixed per day: amount times days after grace. Fixed per month: amount times months after grace.
    • % of price per month: price times rate times months after grace. % of paid per year: amount paid times yearly rate, for the days after grace.
    • Cap: the most the clause will ever pay, as a % of the price or of what you paid.
    • Missed rent counts every month late, including the grace period, because you lose the rent either way.
    • Nothing you enter leaves your browser.

    Not legal advice. Your contract and local law decide. Some places add statutory rights on top of the contract, some courts reduce penalty clauses, and force majeure can stop the clock.
