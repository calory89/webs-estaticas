    How the score works

    • Up to 35 points: money due before the structure is complete. Up to 20% adds nothing, 60% or more adds all 35.
    • Up to 25 points: share of the money after signing that is due on calendar dates instead of construction milestones. Dates keep running when building stops.
    • Up to 20 points: money held back until handover or later. 20% or more adds nothing, 5% or less adds all 20.
    • 20 points with no deposit protection, 15 if you are not sure, 0 with escrow or a trust account.

    Milestones are placed on the timeline you entered: foundations at a quarter of the way to structure, finishing halfway between structure and handover. This is a planning aid, not legal or financial advice.
