Kolaboro / Tools / Insolvency check

Is the developer's company in insolvency?

Type the exact legal name of the company on your contract, for example the seller or the project company. We ask the official insolvency notices live and show you what they say, with links. Today this covers the United Kingdom and Spain. For other countries we show you the official register to check by hand.

Look up a company

Include the legal form (Limited, Ltd, PLC, LLP, S.L., S.A., Sociedad Limitada). We never search names of people. 20 lookups an hour from one address; answers are kept for a day so a repeat is instant.

What we watch every day

Every morning a job checks the companies named in our research for each tracked project in the United Kingdom and Spain against the same official sources. Company names are not listed on this page. What you see here is the count, and for a project with a hit, the date, the source and the type of notice.

Other countries: the official place to look

We did not find an open, machine-readable insolvency register for these markets. Each link is the official source; we checked that it answers.

  • IndonesiaBankruptcy and PKPU (suspension of payments) cases are heard by the five commercial courts. Case tracking for the Central Jakarta court: sipp.pn-jakartapusat.go.id. Search the company name under Perdata Khusus.
  • ThailandLegal Execution Department, which administers bankruptcy cases and publishes their notices: led.go.th.
  • PortugalCitius, the courts portal, publishes insolvency notices (publicidade de insolvencias): citius.mj.pt.
  • MalaysiaMalaysian Department of Insolvency (MdI), which runs the insolvency status search: mdi.gov.my.
  • United StatesBankruptcy filings are in PACER (an account and small fees per page): pacer.uscourts.gov.
  • United Arab EmiratesWe found no open insolvency register. Ask your lawyer to search the Dubai Courts (or the court of the emirate where the company is registered) before you pay.
