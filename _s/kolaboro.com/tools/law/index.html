What the law says, with the article quoted

41 short notes on the rules that matter most when you buy off-plan abroad: who may own what, where your payments must be held, and what happens if the project is late or cancelled. Each note quotes the official text and links to it. Our contract check shows them next to the matching clause of your contract.

Indonesia · Title type, Who is buying and selling

A foreigner can own a house in Indonesia only on Hak Pakai (right of use) land

A landed house owned by a foreigner must sit on Hak Pakai land: either plain Hak Pakai, Hak Pakai granted over someone's Hak Milik by a deed before a land deed official (PPAT), or Hak Pakai over Management Rights land under an agreement with the holder. Apartments (strata units) follow separate rules in the same article. If your contract promises you Hak Milik, or keeps the title in another person's name for you, ask a lawyer before paying.

Rumah tempat tinggal atau hunian yang dapat dimiliki oleh Orang Asing merupakan: a. rumah tapak di atas Tanah: 1. hak pakai; atau 2. hak pakai di atas: a) hak milik, yang dikuasai berdasarkan perjanjian pemberian hak pakai di atas hak milik dengan akta Pejabat Pembuat Akta Tanah; atau b) Hak Pengelolaan, berdasarkan perjanjian pemanfaatan Tanah dengan pemegang Hak Pengelolaan.

Our translation: Houses or dwellings that may be owned by a Foreigner are: a. landed houses on Land: 1. right of use (hak pakai); or 2. right of use over: a) freehold (hak milik), held under an agreement granting right of use over freehold made by deed of a Land Deed Official; or b) Management Rights, under a land use agreement with the holder of the Management Rights.

Government Regulation (PP) No. 18 of 2021 on Management Rights, Land Rights, Strata Units and Land Registration, Article 71(1)(a) JDIH Nasional (peraturan.go.id), Ministry of Law. Official text read on 2026-11-03.

Indonesia · Who is buying and selling

The foreign buyer needs an immigration document

Only a foreigner who holds an immigration document under Indonesian law can own a home. Check what the contract says about your status and which document the notary will ask for.

Orang Asing yang dapat memiliki rumah tempat tinggal atau hunian merupakan Orang Asing yang mempunyai dokumen keimigrasian sesuai dengan ketentuan peraturan perundang-undangan.

Our translation: A Foreigner who may own a house or dwelling is a Foreigner who holds an immigration document in accordance with the laws and regulations.

Government Regulation (PP) No. 18 of 2021 on Management Rights, Land Rights, Strata Units and Land Registration, Article 69(1) JDIH Nasional (peraturan.go.id), Ministry of Law. Official text read on 2026-11-03.

Indonesia · Title type

Hak Pakai on State or Management Rights land runs 30 years, then 20, then 30

Right of use with a time limit is granted for up to 30 years, can be extended for up to 20 years, and renewed for up to 30 years. An extension or renewal is an application, not automatic. Check which term your contract promises and who pays for extensions.

Hak pakai di atas Tanah Negara dan Tanah Hak Pengelolaan dengan jangka waktu diberikan untuk jangka waktu paling lama 30 (tiga puluh) tahun, diperpanjang untuk jangka waktu paling lama 20 (dua puluh) tahun, dan diperbarui untuk jangka waktu paling lama 30 (tiga puluh) tahun.

Our translation: Right of use with a time limit over State Land and Management Rights Land is granted for at most 30 years, extended for at most 20 years, and renewed for at most 30 years.

Government Regulation (PP) No. 18 of 2021 on Management Rights, Land Rights, Strata Units and Land Registration, Article 52(1) JDIH Nasional (peraturan.go.id), Ministry of Law. Official text read on 2026-11-03.

Indonesia · Price, Title type

Foreign ownership has limits: minimum price, plot size, number of units, residential use

The regulation lets the government set a minimum price, a maximum plot size, a maximum number of plots or units, and requires residential use. The current figures are set in separate ministerial rules, so check them for your region before you sign.

Kepemilikan rumah tempat tinggal atau hunian Orang Asing sebagaimana dimaksud dalam Pasal 71 diberikan dengan batasan: a. minimal harga; b. luas bidang Tanah; c. jumlah bidang Tanah atau unit Satuan Rumah Susun; dan d. peruntukan untuk rumah tinggal atau hunian.

Our translation: Ownership of a house or dwelling by a Foreigner as referred to in Article 71 is granted with limits on: a. minimum price; b. land plot area; c. number of land plots or strata units; and d. designation for residential use.

Government Regulation (PP) No. 18 of 2021 on Management Rights, Land Rights, Strata Units and Land Registration, Article 72 JDIH Nasional (peraturan.go.id), Ministry of Law. Official text read on 2026-11-03.

Indonesia · Title type

Only Indonesian citizens can hold Hak Milik (freehold)

Freehold land (Hak Milik, often shown as SHM on the certificate) can only be held by an Indonesian citizen. A foreign buyer cannot be registered as the owner of Hak Milik land.

Hanya warga-negara Indonesia dapat mempunyai hak milik.

Our translation: Only Indonesian citizens may hold freehold (hak milik).

Law No. 5 of 1960 on Basic Agrarian Principles (UUPA), Article 21(1) JDIH Nasional (peraturan.go.id), Ministry of Law. Official text read on 2026-11-03.

Indonesia · Title type

Arrangements that pass freehold to a foreigner, directly or indirectly, are void

Any sale or other act meant to transfer Hak Milik to a foreigner, directly or indirectly, is void by law, the land falls to the State, and money already paid to the owner cannot be claimed back. This is why nominee structures (an Indonesian holds the freehold for you) are a serious risk.

Setiap jual-beli, penukaran, penghibahan, pemberian dengan wasiat dan perbuatan-perbuatan lain yang dimaksudkan untuk langsung atau tidak langsung memindahkan hak milik kepada orang asing, kepada seorang warga-negara yang disamping kewarganegaraan Indonesianya mempunyai kewarga-negaraan asing atau kepada suatu badan hukum kecuali yang ditetapkan oleh Pemerintah termaksud dalam pasal 21 ayat (2), adalah batal karena hukum dan tanahnya jatuh kepada Negara, dengan ketentuan, bahwa hak-hak pihak lain yang membebaninya tetap berlangsung serta semua pembayaran yang telah diterima oleh pemilik tidak dapat dituntut kembali.

Our translation: Every sale, exchange, gift, bequest and other act intended to transfer freehold directly or indirectly to a foreigner, to a citizen who also holds a foreign citizenship, or to a legal entity other than those set by the Government under Article 21(2), is void by law and the land falls to the State, provided that rights of other parties encumbering it continue and all payments already received by the owner cannot be claimed back.

Law No. 5 of 1960 on Basic Agrarian Principles (UUPA), Article 26(2) JDIH Nasional (peraturan.go.id), Ministry of Law. Official text read on 2026-11-03.

Indonesia · Title type

Leasehold: a lease of someone else's land for building

The basic land law recognises a lease of land owned by someone else for building, paid to the owner, and a foreigner domiciled in Indonesia may hold it. A lease is a contract with the land owner, so check the owner on the land certificate, the length of the lease, and the written terms for extension.

Seseorang atau suatu badan hukum mempunyai hak sewa atas tanah, apabila ia berhak mempergunakan tanah-milik orang lain untuk keperluan bangunan, dengan membayar kepada pemiliknya sejumlah uang sebagai sewa.

Our translation: A person or legal entity has a lease right over land if entitled to use land owned by another for building, paying the owner a sum of money as rent.

Law No. 5 of 1960 on Basic Agrarian Principles (UUPA), Articles 44(1) and 45 JDIH Nasional (peraturan.go.id), Ministry of Law. Official text read on 2026-11-03.

Indonesia · Payment schedule, Permits, Title type, Completion date

Pre-sales (PPJB) of houses under construction need land status, permits and 20% built first

Homes still being built may be sold through a preliminary sale agreement (PPJB) only after there is certainty on the land ownership, what is promised, the building permit, the infrastructure, and at least 20% of the housing built. Ask the developer for proof of each. This is the 2011 wording; the Job Creation law later amended parts of this law, so have a lawyer confirm the current text.

Perjanjian pendahuluan jual beli sebagaimana dimaksud pada ayat (1) dilakukan setelah memenuhi persyaratan kepastian atas: a. status pemilikan tanah; b. hal yang diperjanjikan; c. kepemilikan izin mendirikan bangunan induk; d. ketersediaan prasarana, sarana, dan utilitas umum; dan e. keterbangunan perumahan paling sedikit 20% (dua puluh persen).

Our translation: The preliminary sale agreement referred to in paragraph (1) is made after meeting the requirement of certainty on: a. the land ownership status; b. what is agreed; c. holding the main building permit; d. availability of infrastructure, facilities and public utilities; and e. at least 20% (twenty percent) of the housing built.

Law No. 1 of 2011 on Housing and Residential Areas, Article 42(1) and (2) JDIH Nasional (peraturan.go.id), Ministry of Law. Official text read on 2026-11-03. Text as enacted in 2011. Parts of this law were later amended by the Job Creation law; we quote the 2011 text and say where that matters.

Indonesia · Permits

PBG is the building approval; SLF certifies the building is fit for use

PBG (Persetujuan Bangunan Gedung) is the permit given to the building owner to build. SLF (Sertifikat Laik Fungsi) is issued by the local government to state the building is fit for function before it can be used. Both run through SIMBG, the government's web system, so a real PBG can be checked there.

perizinan yang diberikan kepada pemilik Bangunan Gedung untuk membangun baru ... sertifikat yang diberikan oleh Pemerintah Daerah untuk menyatakan kelaikan fungsi Bangunan Gedung sebelum dapat dimanfaatkan ... sistem elektronik berbasis web yang digunakan untuk melaksanakan proses penyelenggaraan PBG

Our translation: a permit given to the owner of a Building to build new ... a certificate given by the Regional Government to state that a Building is fit for function before it can be used ... a web-based electronic system used to carry out the PBG process

Government Regulation (PP) No. 16 of 2021 implementing Law No. 28 of 2002 on Buildings, Article 1 points 17, 18 and 48 JDIH of the Ministry of Housing and Settlement Areas (jdih.pkp.go.id). Official text read on 2026-11-03.

Dubai (UAE) · Escrow or protected account, Payment schedule

Off-plan sellers in Dubai must have an escrow account for the project

A developer that wants to sell units off-plan must apply to the Land Department to open an escrow account, and buyers' payments are deposited into an account opened in the name of the project. Pay only into the project's escrow account named in your contract, never to a personal or company operating account.

Any Developer who wishes to sell Units off-plan must submit to the Department a request to open an Escrow Account.

Dubai Law No. (8) of 2007 Concerning Escrow Accounts for Real Estate Development, Articles 6 and 7 Dubai Legislation Portal (dlp.dubai.gov.ae), Government of Dubai. Official text read on 2026-11-03. Official English text published by the Government of Dubai. The Arabic text prevails.

Dubai (UAE) · Escrow or protected account

Escrow money is only for building that project and is protected from the developer's creditors

Each project has its own escrow account, used only to build that project. The developer's creditors cannot seize the money in it.

An Escrow Account will be opened in the name of the project, and will be dedicated exclusively to the construction of that Real Estate Development project. No attachment may be imposed on the payments deposited in this account for the benefit of the creditors of the Developer.

Dubai Law No. (8) of 2007 Concerning Escrow Accounts for Real Estate Development, Article 9 Dubai Legislation Portal (dlp.dubai.gov.ae), Government of Dubai. Official text read on 2026-11-03. Official English text published by the Government of Dubai. The Arabic text prevails.

Dubai (UAE) · Completion date, Payment schedule

5% of the escrow stays back for a year after units are registered

When the developer gets the completion certificate, the escrow agent keeps 5% of the account and releases it one year after the units are registered to buyers. A contract that lets the developer take everything at handover is worth asking about.

An Escrow Agent must retain five percent (5%) of the total value of each Escrow Account once the Developer obtains the completion certificate. The retained amount will be released to the Developer one (1) year from the registration of Units in the name of purchasers.

Dubai Law No. (8) of 2007 Concerning Escrow Accounts for Real Estate Development, Article 14 Dubai Legislation Portal (dlp.dubai.gov.ae), Government of Dubai. Official text read on 2026-11-03. Official English text published by the Government of Dubai. The Arabic text prevails.

Dubai (UAE) · Termination and refund, Delay penalty

If a project is not completed, the escrow agent must act to complete it or refund buyers

If the project is not completed, the escrow agent, after consulting the Land Department, must take measures to protect depositors: either get the project completed or refund their payments.

In the event of any emergency situation where the Real Estate Development project is not completed, the Escrow Agent of that project must, after consultation with the Department, take the required measures to preserve the rights of depositors, and ensure that the Real Estate Development project is completed, or depositors are refunded their payments.

Dubai Law No. (8) of 2007 Concerning Escrow Accounts for Real Estate Development, Article 15 Dubai Legislation Portal (dlp.dubai.gov.ae), Government of Dubai. Official text read on 2026-11-03. Official English text published by the Government of Dubai. The Arabic text prevails.

Dubai (UAE) · Permits

Off-plan advertising needs written permission from the Land Department

A developer may not advertise off-plan units, or promote them at exhibitions, without written authorisation from the Land Department. Dubai property ads now carry a permit QR code you can check (see our permit QR check).

A Developer may not advertise in local or international media, and may not participate in local or international exhibitions to promote the sale of Units or Real Property off-plan, unless he obtains an authorisation in writing from the Department.

Dubai Law No. (8) of 2007 Concerning Escrow Accounts for Real Estate Development, Article 5 Dubai Legislation Portal (dlp.dubai.gov.ae), Government of Dubai. Official text read on 2026-11-03. Official English text published by the Government of Dubai. The Arabic text prevails.

Dubai (UAE) · Title type

An off-plan sale must be entered in the Interim Property Register, or it is void

Every off-plan sale must be entered in the Land Department's Interim Property Register. A sale that is not registered there is void. Ask for proof of registration of your unit.

Any disposition that occurs in respect of any Real Property Unit sold off-plan will be entered in the Interim Property Register, and any sale or any other legal disposition that transfers or restricts ownership or any ancillary rights will be void unless entered in that Register.

Dubai Law No. (13) of 2008 Regulating the Interim Property Register, Article 3(1) Dubai Legislation Portal (dlp.dubai.gov.ae), Government of Dubai. Official text read on 2026-11-03. Official English text published by the Government of Dubai. The Arabic text prevails.

Dubai (UAE) · Permits, Title type

No off-plan sale before the developer holds the land and the approvals

A developer may not start a project or sell units off-plan before taking possession of the land and getting the required approvals.

No Master Developer or Sub-developer may commence a project or dispose of its Real Property Units by way of Off-plan Sale before taking possession of the land on which the project will be implemented and obtaining the required approvals from the Competent Entities in the Emirate.

Dubai Law No. (13) of 2008 Regulating the Interim Property Register, Article 4 Dubai Legislation Portal (dlp.dubai.gov.ae), Government of Dubai. Official text read on 2026-11-03. Official English text published by the Government of Dubai. The Arabic text prevails.

Thailand · Title type, Who is buying and selling

Foreigners together may own at most 49% of the unit area in a Thai condominium

In each condominium, all foreign owners together may hold at most 49% of the total area of all units. If the foreign quota is full, a foreigner cannot be registered as owner. Ask the developer for the current foreign quota figure and how your unit is counted.

อาคารชุดแต่ละอาคารชุดจะมีคนต่างด้าวหรือนิติบุคคลตามที่ระบุไว้ในมาตรา ๑๙ ถือกรรมสิทธิ์ในห้องชุดได้เมื่อรวมกันแล้วต้องไม่เกินอัตราร้อยละสี่สิบเก้าของเนื้อที่ของห้องชุดทั้งหมดในอาคารชุดนั้นในขณะที่ขอจดทะเบียนอาคารชุดตามมาตรา ๖

Our translation: In each condominium, foreigners or juristic persons under section 19 may hold ownership of units which, combined, must not exceed forty-nine percent of the area of all units in that condominium at the time of applying to register the condominium under section 6.

Condominium Act B.E. 2522 (1979), as amended, Section 19 bis Department of Lands, Ministry of Interior (dol.go.th). Official text read on 2026-11-03.

Thailand · Payment schedule

A foreign buyer must show the money came from abroad

A foreigner buying under the foreign funds route must show evidence that foreign currency was brought into Thailand, or was withdrawn from a non-resident baht account or a foreign currency account, for at least the price of the unit. Keep the bank's transfer documents for every payment.

สำหรับคนต่างด้าวและนิติบุคคลตามมาตรา ๑๙ (๕) ต้องแสดงหลักฐานการนำเงินตราต่างประเทศเข้ามาในราชอาณาจักรหรือหลักฐานการถอนเงินจากบัญชีเงินบาทของบุคคลที่มีถิ่นที่อยู่นอกประเทศหรือถอนเงินจากบัญชีเงินฝากเงินตราต่างประเทศในจำนวนไม่น้อยกว่าค่าห้องชุดที่จะซื้อ

Our translation: Foreigners and juristic persons under section 19 (5) must show evidence of bringing foreign currency into the Kingdom, or evidence of withdrawing money from the baht account of a person resident abroad or from a foreign currency deposit account, in an amount not less than the price of the unit to be bought.

Condominium Act B.E. 2522 (1979), as amended, Section 19 ter (5) Department of Lands, Ministry of Interior (dol.go.th). Official text read on 2026-11-03.

Spain · Escrow or protected account, Payment schedule, Termination and refund

In Spain, money paid before completion must be guaranteed and kept in a special account

A developer taking payments for homes under construction must guarantee their return plus legal interest, by insurance or a bank guarantee, from the moment it has the building licence, in case construction does not start or is not finished on time. The payments must go into a special bank account, separate from the developer's other money. Ask for your individual guarantee before you pay.

Garantizar, desde la obtención de la licencia de edificación, la devolución de las cantidades entregadas más los intereses legales, mediante contrato de seguro de caución suscrito con entidades aseguradoras debidamente autorizadas para operar en España, o mediante aval solidario emitido por entidades de crédito debidamente autorizadas, para el caso de que la construcción no se inicie o no llegue a buen fin en el plazo convenido para la entrega de la vivienda.

Our translation: Guarantee, from the time the building licence is obtained, the return of the amounts paid plus legal interest, by a surety insurance contract with insurers duly authorised to operate in Spain, or by a joint and several guarantee issued by duly authorised credit institutions, in case construction does not start or is not completed within the period agreed for handing over the home.

Law 38/1999 on Building Regulation (Ley de Ordenación de la Edificación), first additional provision, First additional provision, One, 1 (a) and (b) Boletín Oficial del Estado (boe.es), consolidated text. Official text read on 2026-11-03.

Spain · Termination and refund

Penitential deposit (arras): buyer walks away and loses it, seller walks away and pays it back double

If the contract uses a deposit as arras or señal, either side can withdraw: the buyer by losing the deposit, the seller by returning it doubled. Check which kind of deposit your contract describes; contracts often say.

Si hubiesen mediado arras o señal en el contrato de compra y venta, podrá rescindirse el contrato allanándose el comprador a perderlas, o el vendedor a devolverlas duplicadas.

Our translation: If a deposit (arras or señal) was given in the sale contract, the contract may be cancelled by the buyer agreeing to lose it, or by the seller returning it doubled.

Spanish Civil Code (Código Civil), Article 1454 Boletín Oficial del Estado (boe.es), consolidated text. Official text read on 2026-11-03.

Portugal · Termination and refund, Delay penalty

Promissory contract deposit (sinal): if the seller fails, the buyer can claim double

In a Portuguese promissory contract (contrato-promessa) with a deposit (sinal), if the buyer fails the seller keeps the deposit; if the seller fails, the buyer can demand double what was paid. The buyer can instead ask a court to enforce the sale.

Se quem constitui o sinal deixar de cumprir a obrigação por causa que lhe seja imputável, tem o outro contraente a faculdade de fazer sua a coisa entregue; se o não cumprimento do contrato for devido a este último, tem aquele a faculdade de exigir o dobro do que prestou

Our translation: If the party who gave the deposit fails to perform for a reason attributable to them, the other party may keep what was given; if the failure is due to the latter, the former may demand double what they paid

Portuguese Civil Code (Código Civil), Decree-Law 47344/66, Article 442(2) and (3) Procuradoria-Geral Distrital de Lisboa legislation database (pgdlisboa.pt), consolidated text. Official text read on 2026-11-03.

Florida (USA) · Escrow or protected account, Payment schedule

In Florida, deposits up to 10% of the price go into an escrow account until the condo is built

While construction of a condominium is not substantially completed, the developer must pay into an escrow account every payment you make up to 10% of the sale price, and the escrow agent gives you a receipt on request. If you properly terminate the contract, the money comes back to you with any interest earned. Ask for the name of the escrow agent and a receipt for each payment.

If a developer contracts to sell a condominium parcel and the construction, furnishing, and landscaping of the property submitted or proposed to be submitted to condominium ownership has not been substantially completed in accordance with the plans and specifications and representations made by the developer in the disclosures required by this chapter, the developer shall pay into an escrow account all payments up to 10 percent of the sale price received by the developer from the buyer towards the sale price. The escrow agent shall give to the purchaser a receipt for the deposit, upon request. [...] (a) If a buyer properly terminates the contract pursuant to its terms or pursuant to this chapter, the funds shall be paid to the buyer together with any interest earned.

Florida Condominium Act (Chapter 718, Florida Statutes), Section 718.202(1) and (1)(a) Online Sunshine, official website of the Florida Legislature (leg.state.fl.us). Official text read on 2026-11-26. The 2026 Florida Statutes.

Florida (USA) · Escrow or protected account, Payment schedule

Payments above 10% go into a special escrow account, and can fund construction only if the contract says so

Money you pay above 10% of the price before construction is completed must sit in a special escrow account controlled by an escrow agent. The developer may use it for construction costs once construction has begun, but only if your contract provides for it, and the contract must then carry a bold warning on its first page. Read the first page of the contract for that warning before paying more than 10%.

All payments which are in excess of the 10 percent of the sale price described in subsection (1) and which have been received prior to completion of construction by the developer from the buyer on a contract for purchase of a condominium parcel shall be held in a special escrow account established as provided in subsection (1) and controlled by an escrow agent and may not be used by the developer prior to closing the transaction, except as provided in subsection (3) or except for refund to the buyer. [...] (3) If the contract for sale of the condominium unit so provides, the developer may withdraw escrow funds in excess of 10 percent of the purchase price from the special account required by subsection (2) when the construction of improvements has begun.

Florida Condominium Act (Chapter 718, Florida Statutes), Section 718.202(2) and (3) Online Sunshine, official website of the Florida Legislature (leg.state.fl.us). Official text read on 2026-11-26. The 2026 Florida Statutes.

Florida (USA) · Escrow or protected account, Who is buying and selling

The escrow agent must be independent of the developer

The escrow must be held by a bank, a savings and loan association, a Florida Bar attorney, a registered real estate broker, an authorised title insurer or a lending institution worth more than $5 million, and never by the developer or anyone working for or linked to it. Check who the escrow agent named in your contract is.

Every escrow agent shall be independent of the developer, and no developer or any officer, director, affiliate, subsidiary, or employee of a developer may serve as escrow agent.

Florida Condominium Act (Chapter 718, Florida Statutes), Section 718.202(8) Online Sunshine, official website of the Florida Legislature (leg.state.fl.us). Official text read on 2026-11-26. The 2026 Florida Statutes.

Florida (USA) · Termination and refund, Escrow or protected account

If the developer breaks the escrow rules, you can void the contract and get your money back with interest

A developer that does not follow the escrow rules makes the contract voidable by the buyer, and everything you deposited or advanced must be refunded with interest. Willful failure to use escrow is also a felony of the third degree under the same section.

The failure to comply with the provisions of this section renders the contract voidable by the buyer, and, if voided, all sums deposited or advanced under the contract shall be refunded with interest at the highest rate then being paid on savings accounts, excluding certificates of deposit, by savings and loan associations in the area in which the condominium property is located.

Florida Condominium Act (Chapter 718, Florida Statutes), Section 718.202(5) Online Sunshine, official website of the Florida Legislature (leg.state.fl.us). Official text read on 2026-11-26. The 2026 Florida Statutes.

Florida (USA) · Termination and refund

You can cancel a developer contract within 15 days

A developer contract for a residential condominium unit must say that the buyer can cancel it in writing within 15 days after signing and receiving all the documents the developer must deliver, and again within 15 days of any amendment that changes the offering against the buyer. A waiver of this right has no effect.

THIS AGREEMENT IS VOIDABLE BY BUYER BY DELIVERING WRITTEN NOTICE OF THE BUYER’S INTENTION TO CANCEL WITHIN 15 DAYS AFTER THE DATE OF EXECUTION OF THIS AGREEMENT BY THE BUYER, AND RECEIPT BY BUYER OF ALL OF THE ITEMS REQUIRED TO BE DELIVERED TO HIM OR HER BY THE DEVELOPER UNDER SECTION 718.503, FLORIDA STATUTES. THIS AGREEMENT IS ALSO VOIDABLE BY BUYER BY DELIVERING WRITTEN NOTICE OF THE BUYER’S INTENTION TO CANCEL WITHIN 15 DAYS AFTER THE DATE OF RECEIPT FROM THE DEVELOPER OF ANY AMENDMENT WHICH MATERIALLY ALTERS OR MODIFIES THE OFFERING IN A MANNER THAT IS ADVERSE TO THE BUYER. ANY PURPORTED WAIVER OF THESE VOIDABILITY RIGHTS SHALL BE OF NO EFFECT.

Victoria (Australia) · Escrow or protected account, Payment schedule

In Victoria, an off-the-plan deposit is capped at 10% and held on trust until the plan is registered

A seller in Victoria may not sign an off-the-plan contract unless the deposit is paid to a legal practitioner, conveyancer or licensed estate agent acting for the seller, who holds it on trust for you until the plan of subdivision is registered, and the deposit may not be more than 10% of the price. Never pay a deposit straight to the developer.

A vendor must not enter into an off-the-plan contract with any person, except a statutory body or authority, unless [...] (a) the off-the-plan contract provides that the deposit moneys payable by the purchaser are to be paid [...] (i) to a legal practitioner, conveyancer or licensed estate agent acting for the vendor to be held by the legal practitioner, conveyancer or licensed estate agent on trust for the purchaser until the registration of the plan of subdivision; and [...] (b) the deposit moneys payable under the contract do not exceed 10 per cent of the purchase price of the lot.

Sale of Land Act 1962 (Victoria), Section 9AA(1) Victorian Legislation (legislation.vic.gov.au), Office of the Chief Parliamentary Counsel. Official text read on 2026-11-26. Authorised Version No. 174, incorporating amendments as at 9 September 2026. Other Australian states have their own rules.

Victoria (Australia) · Termination and refund, Delay penalty, Completion date

If the plan is not registered within 18 months (or the agreed period), you can rescind

If the plan of subdivision is not registered within 18 months of the contract date, or within another period the contract sets, you may rescind the contract at any time after that period and before the plan is registered. You may also rescind before registration if the seller broke the deposit or disclosure rules.

(1) If the vendor under an off-the-plan contract fails to comply with section 9AA(1) or (2), 9AB or 9ADA the purchaser may rescind the off-the-plan contract at any time before the registration of the plan of subdivision. (2) If the plan of subdivision is not registered within 18 months after the date of an off-the-plan contract for the sale of a lot on that plan of subdivision, or, if the contract specifies another period, before the end of that specified period, the purchaser may, at any time after the expiration of that period but before the plan is so registered, rescind the contract.

Victoria (Australia) · Termination and refund, Completion date

A seller can use a sunset clause only with your written consent or a court order

For a residential off-the-plan contract, the seller may rescind under a sunset clause only if the plan is not registered or the occupancy permit is not issued by the sunset date, and only after giving you at least 28 days written notice and getting your written consent. You do not have to consent. Without your consent, the seller must ask the Supreme Court, which decides whether rescission is just and equitable.

(1) A vendor must not rescind a residential off-the-plan contract under a sunset clause in that contract except as provided for in this Division. (3) Before rescinding a residential off-the-plan contract under a sunset clause, the vendor must obtain the written consent of each purchaser to the rescission after giving each purchaser, at least 28 days before the proposed rescission, written notice setting out [...] (c) that the purchaser is not obliged to consent to the proposed rescission. [...] 10E (1) A vendor under a residential off-the-plan contract that contains a sunset clause may apply to the Supreme Court for an order permitting the vendor to rescind the contract under the sunset clause.

Mexico · Governing law, Who is buying and selling

In Mexico, a developer selling homes must register its sales contracts with Profeco

The consumer law applies when the seller is a developer, builder, promoter or anyone else advising on and selling homes to the public, and those contracts must be registered with the Federal Consumer Protection Agency (Profeco). Ask for the Profeco registration of the contract you are given.

Los actos relacionados con inmuebles sólo estarán sujetos a esta ley, cuando los proveedores sean fraccionadores, constructores, promotores y demás personas que intervengan en la asesoría y venta al público de viviendas destinadas a casa habitación [...] Los contratos relacionados con las actividades a que se refiere el párrafo anterior, deberán registrarse ante la Procuraduría.

Mexico · Permits

In a presale, the developer must show the full construction project and the permits

Before you buy in a presale (preventa), the developer must make available the complete executive construction project and the model, and, for new homes or presales, the authorisations, licences and permits issued for the construction. Ask to see them, and check them with the issuing authority.

Mexico · Termination and refund, Escrow or protected account

The developer must tell you how you can cancel and what guarantee backs the deal

The developer must inform you of the conditions under which you can cancel the purchase, and of any mortgage, trust (fideicomiso) or other guarantee that exists and how it is set up. Mexican law does not require an escrow account for presales, so ask in writing where your payments go and what protects them.

XII. Las condiciones bajo las cuales el consumidor puede cancelar la operación, y XIII. Se deberá indicar al consumidor sobre la existencia y constitución de garantía hipotecaria, fiduciaria o de cualquier otro tipo, así como su instrumentación.

Mexico · Delay penalty, Completion date

The contract must set a delivery date and penalties that are equal for both sides

A registered home sale contract must state the start and end dates of the work and the delivery date of the property, and any contractual penalties for breach must be reciprocal and equivalent for seller and buyer. The developer is excused from the agreed delivery date only if it fully proves force majeure or an unforeseeable event.

Our translation: IX. The contractual penalties applicable to both the supplier and the consumer for breach of the obligations undertaken, which must be reciprocal and equivalent, without prejudice to the applicable laws; [...] XII. The start and end date of the contracted activity or service, and the date of delivery of the property covered by the contract, the latter in accordance with article 74 of this Law. The supplier is exempt from the obligation to deliver on the agreed date only when it fully proves that delivery did not take place on that date because of an unforeseeable event or force majeure directly affecting it or the property, in which case a new delivery date may be agreed without liability.

Mexico · Completion date

A new home comes with at least a 5-year structural warranty

Every home sold under the consumer law must come with a warranty of at least five years for structural matters, three years for waterproofing and one year for everything else, counted from actual delivery. During the warranty the developer must repair defects at no cost to you.

Todo bien inmueble cuya transacción esté regulada por esta Ley, deberá ofrecerse al consumidor con la garantía correspondiente, la cual no podrá ser inferior a cinco años para cuestiones estructurales y tres años para impermeabilización; para los demás elementos la garantía mínima será de un año. Todos los plazos serán contados a partir de la entrega real del bien.

Our translation: Every property whose transaction is governed by this Law must be offered to the consumer with the corresponding warranty, which may not be less than five years for structural matters and three years for waterproofing; for the other elements the minimum warranty is one year. In Japan, a developer selling before completion needs a bank guarantee or insurance for your advance payments

When a licensed real estate business sells a home it is still building, it may not take your deposit or other advance payments until a bank or other designated institution guarantees their refund, or a guarantee insurance policy covers them, and you are given the guarantee document or policy. Japan · Escrow or protected account, Payment schedule

The guarantee is required once advance payments pass 5% of the price or 10 million yen

The guarantee rule does not apply while the total advance payments are no more than 5% of the price and no more than an amount set by Cabinet Order, which the Order sets at 10 million yen, or once the title is registered in your name. Above either limit, ask for the guarantee document before paying.

法第四十一条第一項ただし書及び第四十一条の二第一項ただし書の政令で定める額は、千万円とする。

Japan · Payment schedule, Termination and refund

The deposit may not exceed 20% of the price, and you can walk away by giving it up

A real estate business selling as the seller may not take a deposit (tetsukekin) of more than 20% of the price. Whatever the contract calls the deposit, you may cancel by giving it up, and the seller may cancel by paying you double, until the other side has started performing the contract. Terms worse for you than this are void.

宅地建物取引業者は、自ら売主となる宅地又は建物の売買契約の締結に際して、代金の額の十分の二を超える額の手付を受領することができない。 ２ 宅地建物取引業者が、自ら売主となる宅地又は建物の売買契約の締結に際して手付を受領したときは、その手付がいかなる性質のものであつても、買主はその手付を放棄して、当該宅地建物取引業者はその倍額を現実に提供して、契約の解除をすることができる。 ただし、その相手方が契約の履行に着手した後は、この限りでない。 ３ 前項の規定に反する特約で、買主に不利なものは、無効とする。

Our translation: A real estate business may not, on concluding a sale contract for land or a building of which it is itself the seller, receive a deposit exceeding two tenths of the price. 2. A special agreement contrary to the preceding paragraph that is unfavourable to the buyer is void.

Signed away from the seller's office? You can cancel in writing within 8 days

If you made the offer or signed the contract somewhere other than the seller's office (for example at a hotel event or at home), you may withdraw or cancel in writing, without paying damages or a penalty, and the seller must promptly return your deposit and other money. United Kingdom · Escrow or protected account, Payment schedule

In the UK, a deposit paid to an estate agent is held on trust for you

Money an estate agent receives from you in England, Wales or Northern Ireland, including a deposit held as stakeholder until completion, is clients' money that the agent holds on trust, not the agent's own money. UK law has no general escrow rule for new-build deposits paid to a developer's solicitor, so ask in writing who holds your deposit and on what terms.

It is hereby declared that clients’ money received by any person in the course of estate agency work in England, Wales or Northern Ireland [...] (a) is held by him on trust for the person who is entitled to call for it to be paid over to him or to be paid on his direction or to have it otherwise credited to him, or (b) if it is received by him as stakeholder, is held by him on trust for the person who may become so entitled on the occurrence of the event against which the money is held.

United Kingdom · Completion date, Who is buying and selling

Builders owe every buyer a legal duty that a new home is fit to live in

In England and Wales, everyone who takes on work to provide a new dwelling owes a duty to the person who ordered it and to every later owner that the work is done in a workmanlike or professional way, with proper materials, so that the home is fit for habitation when completed. For time limits, a claim counts from the date the dwelling was completed.

A person taking on work for or in connection with the provision of a dwelling (whether the dwelling is provided by the erection or by the conversion or enlargement of a building) owes a duty [...] (a) if the dwelling is provided to the order of any person, to that person; and (b) without prejudice to paragraph (a) above, to every person who acquires an interest (whether legal or equitable) in the dwelling; to see that the work which he takes on is done in a workmanlike or, as the case may be, professional manner, with proper materials and so that as regards that work the dwelling will be fit for habitation when completed. [...] (5) Any cause of action in respect of a breach of the duty imposed by this section shall be deemed, for the purposes of the Limitation Act 1980, to have accrued at the time when the dwelling was completed [...]

Defective Premises Act 1972, Section 1(1) and (5) legislation.gov.uk, The National Archives. Official text read on 2026-11-26. Applies in England and Wales.

A claim under that duty must be brought within 15 years of when the right of action arose, which is the date the dwelling was completed. For claims that arose before section 135 of the Building Safety Act 2022 came into force (28 June 2022, as recorded on legislation.gov.uk), the limit is 30 years. Keep your completion date and documents.

(1) Where by virtue of a relevant provision a person becomes entitled to bring an action against any other person, no action may be brought after the expiration of 15 years from the date on which the right of action accrued.
