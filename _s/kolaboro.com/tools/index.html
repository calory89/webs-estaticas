Kolaboro / Tools

Free tools for buying off-plan abroad

Checks you can run yourself before you sign or pay. The document, image and account checks run entirely in your browser: your files and numbers are not uploaded.

Check what you were sent

PDF checkIn your browser

Every save inside a contract or reservation PDF, what each one changed, whether signatures still cover the file, and where metadata and fonts disagree.

Image checkIn your browser

Is this render shown on another project's page? A perceptual fingerprint compared with the images on every project we track.

Payment email checkIn your browser

Paste an email asking you to pay: Reply-To and lookalike domains against developer websites in our records, SPF, DKIM and DMARC results, domain age, changed-bank-details wording.

Account checkIn your browser

IBAN checksum, length and bank code, SWIFT/BIC format, the bank's registered name from GLEIF, account country against the project or the developer's country, and Dubai escrow rules.

Contract check with on-device AIIn your browser

Drop the contract PDF: parties, price, payments, completion, delay penalty, refund, escrow, title and governing law, each with the exact quote from your file and what the law says.

Permit photo readerIn your browser

Photo of a permit, licence or company certificate: we read the text on your device, find register numbers and look one up with one tap.

Permit QR checkIn your browser

Photo of a PBG permit, Dubai ad or title document: we read the QR code and tell you if the link belongs to the official agency.

Site visit recordPhotos stay on your phone

At the project: your GPS and photos compared with the plot location in our records, then signed and timestamped by Kolaboro. Only photo fingerprints are sent. For a private offline log, use the Site visit app.

Law notes

Foreign ownership, escrow and deposit rules in Indonesia, Dubai, Thailand, Spain and Portugal, with the official article quoted and linked.

Contract addendum builderIn your browser

Protective clauses to propose to the developer in 9 markets (escrow, delay penalty, refund with interest, completion, permits, snagging), each with the law article it relies on, as PDF or Word. A draft for your lawyer, not legal advice.

Bulk check for agents and lendersIn your browser

Paste or upload up to 100 project names, developer names or links. Matched on your device against our public index: tracked or not, handover date on record, moved dates, permit number, website health, lookalike websites. Export CSV.

Contract first passIn your browser

Paste the contract text for a quick list of buyer protections present or missing. For a clause-by-clause reading of the PDF with quotes, use Contract check.

Money and timing

Payment plan risk score

Enter the payment schedule: a 0 to 100 score and the money-at-risk curve over the construction timeline.

Payment plan checker

Paste the plan as written: flags large deposits, payments due on dates instead of construction stages, and no retention until handover.

Off-plan risk calculator

Price and timing only: money exposed before handover and what a delay of some months would cost you.

Currency risk

Your payment plan in your home currency if the rate moves 10% or 20%.

Total cost of owning

Purchase taxes, fees, service charges, financing and rent.

Rental yield check

The advertised yield against the yield after occupancy and costs.

Late handover compensation

From your own contract: what your delay clause pays, the cap, and the questions to ask.

Late handover: contract and law

From the law, not your contract: what you are owed when handover is late, quoted from the official text for each country.

Price below build cost

Your price per m² against published construction cost ranges for the city. A flag is a question to ask, not proof.

Resale exit checklist

Can you sell before handover: assignment, consent, fees and lock-in.

Choosing and tracking

Market match

Rank off-plan markets on public information for your budget and goals.

Deposit protection finder

What the law and official registers offer in each market.

Insolvency checkLive lookup

Type the developer company's legal name and see official insolvency notices (UK Gazette, Spain BORME), with links.

Is this agent licensed?Live lookup

Check a Dubai broker card or brokerage in the official register, and where to check by hand in other countries.

Your personal checklist

What to check before you sign, before each payment and before handover.

Refund and claim tracker

Log every contact, deadline and payment; stored only in your browser.

Site visit appWorks offline

Checklist, photos with GPS and time, every entry chained on your phone, exported as a signed JSON and PDF. Nothing is uploaded. For a record Kolaboro signs against the plot location, use Site visit record.

Signed buyer reportsOpens after setup

Bought into a tracked project? Report delays, payment problems or a good experience, signed with a passkey. Proof of purchase stays on your device.
