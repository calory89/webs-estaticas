At the site: record your visit against the plot

Standing at the project? Pick it, take your position and a few photos. You see how far you are from the location in our records, and you get a record signed and timestamped by Kolaboro that you can keep or send to your lawyer.

Your photos never leave this phone. This page computes a SHA-256 fingerprint of each photo. Only when you press "Seal this visit" are the fingerprints, your GPS position and your clock sent to us to be signed. We do not store them: the signed record comes back to you. Only its root hash is kept in the public timestamp log. Site analytics are masked on this tool.

  1. Pick the project

    Named projects with a location in our records. Type a name, a developer, a city or an id.

    • Take your position

      Your phone's GPS, read once. Stand on or next to the plot, outdoors if you can.

    • Take photos

      Up to 40. The camera opens on a phone. We read the time and GPS the camera saved inside each photo, if any, and compute its fingerprint here.

      Add photos

      • Seal this visit

        Kolaboro adds the time it received the record and the distance to the project location, signs it (W3C verifiable credential) and timestamps its root hash in Bitcoin (OpenTimestamps).

      What this record shows, and what it cannot

      What is compared?

      Your GPS position and the GPS saved in each photo, against the project location in our records (a pin from public sources, about 100 m accurate when it has 3 decimals). Inside means within 150 m of the pin plus your GPS accuracy. Your phone's clock is compared with the time our server received the record.

      Is this proof that I was there?

      No. A device owner can change the GPS and the clock. The record shows that the photos, position and times were consistent and that they existed, unchanged, at the time Kolaboro signed them. It is evidence to keep with your dated photos, not a verdict on the project or the developer.

      How do I check a record later?

      Open the signed JSON at /verify: it checks Kolaboro's signature. To show a photo belongs to the record, compute its SHA-256 (any tool, or the image check) and find it in the list. The timestamp can be checked at /api/kb/stamp with the root hash.

      What do you keep?

      Only the root hash, in the public timestamp log, so the timestamp can be upgraded and checked. Not the project, not your position, not the photo fingerprints. If you lose the JSON, we cannot send it again.

      For a full visit with a checklist that works offline, use /visit/.
