How we calculate

Simple sums you can check by hand

Upfront cash

Price, minus any mortgage, plus purchase taxes and fees, lawyer and other one-off costs.

Yearly running cost

Service charge per m² times size, plus property tax, insurance and maintenance. It starts at handover, so years spent waiting for construction cost nothing here.

Total over the period

Purchase taxes and fees, one-off costs, running costs and mortgage interest for the years you own it after handover, minus net rent. The price itself is not a cost: you still own the property.
