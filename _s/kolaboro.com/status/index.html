Service level objectives

Error budget policy: what we do when a budget is spent
  • Budget spent (0% left): feature work on the affected part stops. The next changes shipped there are reliability fixes only, until the measurement is back within the objective for 7 days in a row.
  • Under 25% left: changes to that part that are not reliability fixes wait for the next month.
  • An incident that spends more than half a budget: a short public write-up (what happened, why, what changes) on the changelog.
  • Objectives are not lowered to make a month look better. A change of target is announced on the changelog before the month it applies to.

Availability counts every outside probe (our database server every 5 minutes, GitHub Actions every hour). Desk review success counts every request that ends without a report, including sites that block automated readers, so it is stricter than "our servers were up". The speed objective needs at least 50 real-visitor samples in 28 days before it gives a verdict.

Public log

Site evidence sealed in Bitcoin

Every day the fingerprints (SHA-256) of all accepted photos and files from site visits are combined into one root and timestamped in Bitcoin through OpenTimestamps. Anyone can recompute the root from the list and check the timestamp. Nobody, including us, can change a photo afterwards without it showing.
