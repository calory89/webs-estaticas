Kolaboro / Company graph

Who is behind a project

For every UK project we track, we look up the companies named in our research on Companies House, read who their current directors are, and list the other companies those directors run. Dissolved and insolvent companies in that network are counted. No company names, director names, dates of birth or addresses are published here.

Outside the UK: the global company graph matches developers in every market to the LEI system (GLEIF), with parents and lapsed registrations.

How it is built

    Dissolved is not the same as failed. Many developers use one company per project and close it after handover. A pattern of short-lived companies, or insolvency, is a question to ask the developer, with the register open in front of you.

    Where it does not work

      Court records, by exact company name

      You can search public court-decision databases for the exact legal name of a company, for example the seller named in your draft contract. We list only cases whose title contains exactly that name, and we label each one as needing a check that it is the same company. Names of people are never searched.
