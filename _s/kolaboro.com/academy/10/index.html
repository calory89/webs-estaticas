Lesson 10: Working with buyers

Explaining risk calmly from thousands of kilometres away.

Be clear

Lead with the verdict, then the reasons. Buyers abroad are anxious; vagueness makes it worse.

Be fair

Say what is good as well as what is wrong. You are not there to kill deals, only bad ones.

Language and time zones

Clients choose the language of their emails and reports in the portal. Write in plain English; the system translates the structure, not your judgement. Reply within one working day, even if only to say when you'll have the answer.

Bad news

Lead with what you found, then what it means, then what the buyer can do. Never say a project is a scam: say what is missing and how to verify it.

Checklist

  • Verdict, reason, next step: in that order.
  • Quote documents exactly.
  • Say 'I couldn't verify' when you couldn't.

Exercise

Record a 2-minute voice note explaining a 'hold' verdict to a buyer.
