Lesson 8: Writing the report

Clear findings and a verdict a buyer can act on.

Structure

What the contract promised, what you found, evidence for each finding, and a verdict: go, go with a condition, or hold.

Style

Short sentences. Facts before opinions. Say 'I couldn't verify' when you couldn't.

Exercise

Write a one-page report from the visit you filmed in lesson 5.
