How it works

The same desk review as the website, for what lands in your chat

  1. Forward or send

    Text messages, links, photos of brochures (JPEG, PNG, WebP up to 4.5 MB) and PDFs up to 8 MB. Photos and PDFs are transcribed first, exactly as written.

  2. Two AI readers

    The text goes through the desk review: hidden text removed, prompt-injection scan, two models that must agree on the key facts, a strict output schema.

  3. Quotes checked, result signed

    Every promise is searched for in the original text and marked word for word or reworded. The result is signed with our Ed25519 key and checked in your browser when you open it.
