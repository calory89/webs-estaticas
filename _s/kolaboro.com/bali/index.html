Buying property in Bali, with someone on your side

Bali is where we start: strong demand from foreign buyers, and the same doubts every time. Here is what to watch and how Kolaboro helps.

Protect my purchaseCheck a project

Land and zoning

Foreigners usually buy leasehold or through a company. The zoning has to allow villas and rentals. Read the guide.

Permits

Ask for the building approval (PBG) and, once built, the certificate of fitness (SLF). Renting needs its own licence.

Construction

Delays are common. Tie payments to milestones and have each one checked on site. How to pay safely.
