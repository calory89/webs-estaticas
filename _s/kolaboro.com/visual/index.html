What changed on developer websites

When the text of a tracked developer's website changes, we photograph the whole page and compare it with the previous photograph. Here you see where the page changed and how much of it, without the page itself: a screenshot would reveal which project it is.

Why no screenshots? A screenshot of a developer's website copies its photos and renders, which are the developer's copyrighted work. So we publish only the shape of each change; what changed in the text is on each project's own page, with its source. The before and after screenshots and the highlighted diff are kept privately for 90 days, and a buyer who orders a check on that project can have them.
