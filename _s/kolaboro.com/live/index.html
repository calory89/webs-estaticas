Watch a site visit live, with proof of where and when

On a real visit your agent goes live from the plot. The phone burns its GPS position and the time into every frame, records the video, fingerprints it (SHA-256) and seals it into your case. Try it here with your own phone.

Start a demo, then scan the code with your phone.

How it works
  1. Press Start a live demo. A code and a QR appear here.
  2. Scan it with your phone and allow camera and location.
  3. Your phone's camera shows up on this screen, with GPS and time on the picture.
  4. Press Stop and seal on the phone: you see the video's fingerprint and its Bitcoin timestamp.

Demo videos stay on your phone; only the fingerprint is timestamped. On real cases the video is stored in the buyer's evidence locker.

Why it matters

Can't be staged elsewherePosition and time are drawn on the picture by the phone while it records, and the GPS track goes with the file.Can't be edited laterThe file's fingerprint is chained to the rest of the case and timestamped in Bitcoin. Change one frame and it no longer matches.Ask while they are thereThe buyer watches from their portal and can message the agent during the visit.
