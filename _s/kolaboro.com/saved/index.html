Saved for offline

Project files and guides you saved on this device. They open without a connection, for example at the site visit or on the plane.

Reading what is saved on this device.

How it works

  • Projects in My projects and the last comparisons you open are also kept on this device automatically, and the site can be installed as an app. Without a connection, pages that are not kept show the offline page with what is available.
  • Tap Save offline on a project page (for example BALI-014) or on a guide. The page, its styles and scripts and the data it shows are stored in this browser.
  • When you are online you always see the live page. The saved copy is used only when there is no connection, and a bar at the top says it is a copy and when it was saved.
  • Nothing is uploaded and nothing is saved until you tap the button. The copy stays on this device until you remove it here or clear your browser data.
  • A project file is a copy of the public database on the day you saved it. Numbers may change later: save it again to update it.
