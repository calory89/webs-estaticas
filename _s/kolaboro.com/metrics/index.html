How we count

  • Numbers come live from the production database through a read-only function that returns totals only.
  • Test and demo accounts and our own team addresses are excluded.
  • A desk review is a project website read by our research agent at a visitor's request, with their email.
  • First reply time is the median time between a client's first message in a case and our first answer after it.
  • The database figures (projects, markets, claims) are on the database page and in the open API.
