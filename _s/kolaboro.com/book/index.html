What happens after you pay

  1. Same day: we confirm your caseWe check that we can cover the project and email you a login to your case.
  2. Within 2 days: documentsYou (or the developer) share the land certificate, permit and contract. Our reader flags what does not match.
  3. Within 5 days: land office and site visitYour agent checks the title at the land office and films the plot with GPS- and time-stamped photos.
