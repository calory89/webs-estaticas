How to read this. Each project gets one point for every thing a careful buyer needs that the developer makes public: a permit or licence number, deposit protection, construction progress, plans, a track record and no "guaranteed" returns. Every project is now researched on all six; a project is "100% public" only when it publishes all six and has no problems in public reports. Problems come only from public sources such as news, courts and regulators. A high score means the documents are public, not that they are genuine; checking that is what our agents do. Projects stay anonymous until a verification is complete and the developer has had 10 working days to reply.
