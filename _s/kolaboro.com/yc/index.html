Why now

  • Buyers buy remotely. Video calls and renders replaced the visit; trust didn't follow.
  • AI makes the expert cheap. A trained local with AI does work that used to need a lawyer, a surveyor and an engineer.
  • Proof is cheap to capture. Every phone records GPS- and time-stamped evidence we can fingerprint.
