Jobersity vs job boards

LinkedIn and Indeed are good places to see jobs and get found by companies. Jobersity doesn't replace them: it's an agent that does the repetitive part of searching, tailoring and sending applications for you. Here are the differences, without overselling.

What each one does

  • Job boards: list jobs; you search, filter and apply one by one, usually with the same CV.
  • Professional networks: besides jobs, your profile lets recruiters find you and helps your network.
  • Jobersity: reads your CV, finds new jobs every day across sources, prepares a tailored CV for each one without inventing anything and, with your permission, sends it. It tells you when a company replies.

When a job board is enough

  • If you're only targeting a handful of specific jobs and want to apply by hand.
  • If your field runs mostly on contacts and referrals.
  • If you want to be found: keep your LinkedIn profile up to date either way.

When Jobersity helps

  • If you apply to many jobs and it eats hours every week.
  • If you want a tailored CV for each job but don't have the time.
  • If you're searching across several cities, countries or remote at once.

What Jobersity doesn't do

  • It doesn't invent experience or degrees.
  • It doesn't guarantee a job: the Pro guarantee is at least 1 interview a month or that month back.
  • It doesn't replace preparing for the interview, though it helps you with it.

FAQ

Can I use Jobersity and LinkedIn together?

Yes, and we recommend it. Jobersity applies for you and LinkedIn stays your professional shop window.

Are Jobersity's jobs the same as on job boards?

Many are, because we collect from boards and company careers pages. Every job links to its source.
