KlipStay: your videos can pay for your travel

Make short videos for hotels and travel brands and earn free stays, meals, activities, transport and flights. No followers required and no creator experience needed.

How it works

  1. Pick a trip or a brand with an open reward.
  2. Make the short videos the brand asks for.
  3. When your videos are approved, you get the reward, for example free hotel nights.
