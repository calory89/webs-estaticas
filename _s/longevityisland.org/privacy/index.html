Privacy

Your details, handled with care.

This page explains how Longevity Island uses the personal details you give us. Last updated: 4 November 2026.

Who we are

Longevity Island is developed by Equinox Investment Group, LLC (equinoxinvestmentgroup.com), which is responsible for your data. Contact: carlos@equinoxinvestmentgroup.com.

What we collect

When you apply: your name, email, country, age, WhatsApp number, your answers about activity, smoking and interest, and anything you write about why you want to take part. If you add the person who would come with you, their name and the contact details you give us. We also record how you reached us (for example an advert) so we know which campaigns work.

Why we use it

To review your application, contact you about it, send you your dossier and a short series of emails about the membership, and prepare the terms if you are invited. If you add a partner, we contact them once about your application, mentioning you. We do not sell your details and we do not share them for others' marketing.

Health information

The application asks only general questions (activity, smoking). Any medical assessment with La Clinique Swiss happens later, only with your separate, explicit consent, and research uses anonymised data only.

Cookies

We only use advertising cookies (Meta pixel) if you accept them in the cookie banner. Without your consent, only what is strictly necessary for the page to work is used.

Who helps us

Our providers store and process data on our behalf: Supabase (database), Brevo (emails), Cloudflare (hosting and security) and, only with your consent, Meta (advertising measurement). Some may process data outside your country, with appropriate safeguards.

How long we keep it

While your application is active and for up to 24 months afterwards, unless you become a member or ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, object to their use or ask us to stop emails at any time: use the link in any email, or write to carlos@equinoxinvestmentgroup.com. In the UK you can also complain to the ICO, and in the EU to your local data protection authority.
