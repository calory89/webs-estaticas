The island

Karimunjawa. Quiet, protected, real.

27 islands in the Java Sea, a marine national park and part of a UNESCO biosphere reserve since 2020. White sand, clear water, coral reefs and quiet roads. No mass tourism, no high-rise hotels.

2 · Drive to JeparaAbout 2 hours by road.

Questions

Good questions, straight answers

Is this a timeshare?

It is a 50-year membership that gives you the right to use one week a year in a cabin, plus the island's facilities and online coaching. You do not buy land or a building, and you can sell or transfer your membership.

Is it an investment?

No. It is a membership to use and enjoy. We do not promise rental income, returns or a resale price.

How many people can come?

Up to two adults per cabin, so €6,500 works out at €3,250 each. Families can reserve several weeks or cabins next to each other.

Which week do I get?

You choose a season when you reserve. Weeks are allocated in reservation order, so founding members choose first. Your week then repeats every year.

Are there other costs?

Your contract sets out a small service fee per stay for cleaning and energy, before you pay any deposit. Flights, extra treatments and drinks are not included.

What if I change my mind?

Reserving is free. You have 14 days to think after reserving, and we only take your €3,500 deposit after that.

When can I go for the first time?

The first member weeks are estimated for 2028. The foundations are already built, and the cabins are made in a factory and assembled on site.

Can I sell or give away my membership?

Yes. Sell it on our members' marketplace, gift a single year, or leave it to your children. We don't promise how fast it sells or at what price.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. The €3,500 deposit secures your week, and the remaining €3,000 can be paid in one go or in instalments before your cabin ships.

What if opening is delayed?

Your contract sets out what happens to your deposit if the opening date moves. Until you pay your deposit, you can walk away at no cost.

Can I come more than one week a year?

Yes. Reserve two, three or four weeks, back to back or in different seasons.

How do I know this is real?

Check it yourself. The photos marked "Real photo" were taken on our land, including the foundations and a finished show unit. The company and the founder are named on this page with an email that a person answers. You can visit the land, you read the contract before paying, and reserving costs nothing.

Why is it so affordable?

Each cabin is shared by 50 members, each with their own week, so the cost of one cabin is split 50 ways. The foundations were already built in 2023, and the cabins are made in a factory, which keeps building costs low.

Can I read the contract before I commit?

Yes. After you reserve, we send you the draft contract. You pay nothing until you have read it, signed it and your 14-day cooling-off has ended.

Is my money safe before the opening?

You pay nothing to reserve. Your contract sets out, before you pay any deposit, what happens to it if the opening date moves. If anything is unclear, ask us or your lawyer before you sign.

Can I visit the land?

Yes, you are welcome. Write to us before you travel so someone can meet you on the island and show you the site.

Is the yearly check medical treatment?

No. It measures fitness and body composition so your trainer and dietitian can plan your year. It is not a diagnosis and does not replace your doctor.

What happens after 50 years?

Your membership simply ends. There is nothing more to pay and nothing you owe.

Who is behind Longevity Island?

Longevity Island is developed by Equinox Investment Group LLC, Wyoming, USA, founded by Carlos de Ory, with La Clinique Swiss as medical partner. You can email the founder directly at carlos@equinoxinvestmentgroup.com.
