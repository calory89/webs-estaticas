Already secured · the place

The land has already been secured — on the island they call the Maldives of Indonesia.

350,000 sq ft of oceanfront land on Karimunjawa has already been acquired and titled. We are not searching for a site or speculating on a future land deal. Title documents are available for review during the application process.

Karimunjawa is an archipelago of 27 islands suspended between Java and Borneo, ringed by one of the largest coral reefs in Indonesia and known locally as "the Maldives of Indonesia." Designated a national marine park in 2001 and recognised as a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, it is one of the last places in Southeast Asia where development is strictly capped — by zoning and by topography. For more than a century, fewer than 9,000 people have lived here, scattered across five inhabited islands. The rest is forest, mangrove, and water so clear you can see your shadow on the sand at twenty metres.

Why here

A community designed around what actually adds years.

The regions where people most commonly live past 100 — the "Blue Zones" — tend to share five conditions: clean air, slow daily movement, real food, deep social bonds, and an unhurried sense of purpose. Karimunjawa, by accident of geography, offers the setting for all five.

We didn't build a resort here. We designed a community around what research associates with a longer healthspan: time in nature, time in water, time with people, and time without screens. Every home, every common space, every meal is sequenced around that one idea.
