The island

They call it the Maldives of Indonesia.

Karimunjawa is a protected marine national park of 27 islands in the Java Sea, and part of a UNESCO biosphere reserve since 2020. White sand, water so clear you can see the coral from the boat, and almost none of the crowds you find elsewhere.

Mornings start with a swim in a warm lagoon. Afternoons are for the reef, the forest paths and long lunches. Evenings end with the sun dropping into the sea. It is a place that slows you down in the best way.

Questions

What people ask us

What exactly am I getting?

A membership for life, for two people: one week of 6 days every year in a cabin on Longevity Island, with the complete La Clinique Swiss laboratory programme included every year. It is a right to use, not ownership of land or property.

Is the price per person?

No. €6,500 covers the membership for two people. That is €3,250 each, once, for life.

What does La Clinique Swiss include?

Every year: complete blood studies, body composition and strength, heart fitness, sleep and recovery, and a physician who explains your results and builds your plan. Free for members.

Why do I have to apply?

Because places are limited to 65 per country* and we want a balanced, international group of members. Applying is free and takes one minute.

How do I get there?

Fly to Semarang (about 2 hours from Singapore or Jakarta), then take the short flight to Karimunjawa. The cabins are 5 minutes from the island airport. Fast boats also run from the coast of Java. Schedules vary by season.

When can I go?

Opening is estimated for 2028. The money from memberships funds the construction of the cabins, and we keep members informed at every step.

What else will I pay?

€65 cleaning each time you stay. If your cabin needs a repair, your share is 1/60 of the cost, never more than €95 a year. There is no yearly fee. Travel to the island is not included.

Can I get my €6,500 back?

Yes, by passing your place on. You can give it to family or a friend, or we offer it to the waiting list in your country at €6,500 and that money is yours. It depends on someone taking your place. During the first 14 days after signing you can also cancel without paying anything.

Do I have to take part in the study?

No. The laboratory programme is yours either way. Adding your anonymised results to the study is voluntary, has its own consent, and you can stop at any time.

Are the years calculator numbers a promise?

No. They come from large observational studies that show links between habits and longer lives, not guarantees. They are there to show what the habits you can keep on the island are associated with.

Who is behind Longevity Island?

Longevity Island is developed by Equinox Investment Group, LLC (equinoxinvestmentgroup.com), with La Clinique Swiss as main partner. Write to us at carlos@equinoxinvestmentgroup.com.
