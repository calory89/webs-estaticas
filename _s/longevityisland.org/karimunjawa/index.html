The island

Karimunjawa. Still a secret.

27 islands in the Java Sea, a marine national park and part of a UNESCO biosphere reserve since 2020. White sand, clear water, coral reefs and quiet roads. No mass tourism, no high-rise hotels.

  • Fly to Semarang, drive 2 hours to Jepara, then a fast boat to the island
  • Seasonal small-plane flights land on Karimunjawa itself
  • Around 28 to 31 °C all year, warm sea every month

Questions

Good questions, straight answers

Is this a timeshare?

It is a 50-year membership that gives you the right to use one week a year in a cabin, plus the island's facilities and online coaching. You do not buy land or a building, and you can sell or transfer your membership.

Is it an investment?

No. It is a membership to use and enjoy. We do not promise rental income, returns or a resale price.

How many people can come?

Up to two adults per cabin, so €6,500 works out at €3,250 each. Families can reserve several weeks or cabins next to each other.

Which week do I get?

You choose a season when you reserve. Weeks are allocated in reservation order, so founding members choose first. Your week then repeats every year.

Are there other costs?

Your contract sets out a small service fee per stay for cleaning and energy, before you pay any deposit. Flights, extra treatments and drinks are not included.

What if I change my mind?

Reserving is free. You have 14 days to think after reserving, and we only take your €3,500 deposit after that.

When can I go for the first time?

The first member weeks are estimated for 2028. The foundations are already built, and the cabins are made in a factory and assembled on site.

Can I sell or give away my membership?

Yes. Sell it on our members' marketplace, gift a single year, or leave it to your children. We don't promise how fast it sells or at what price.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. The €3,500 deposit secures your week, and the remaining €3,000 can be paid in one go or in instalments before your cabin ships.

Who is behind Longevity Island?

Longevity Island is developed by Equinox Investment Group, with La Clinique Swiss as medical partner.
