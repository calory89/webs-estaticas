# Brief for Sumba Times PLACE pages (read BRIEF.md first: all its rules apply, except where this file says otherwise)

Each place gets its own page: section "places", kind "guide". One dict per place in GUIDES, same schema as BRIEF.md, with these differences:
- English body 600-1,000 words (es/id complete equivalents). At least 4 <h2>: what it is / why go; how to get there (from Tambolaka or Waingapu, km and realistic hours); when to go and best time of day; tips and etiquette (fees or donation "around", what to bring, safety, respect); nearby (2-4 other places, link their place pages as /LANG/places/<english-slug>/ if they are in the master list below).
- "places": exactly the ONE place, with coordinates verified on OpenStreetMap/Nominatim, Wikipedia or Wikidata. If you truly cannot verify, use the village/kecamatan centre and say so in your final report.
- "facts": 5 rows (Where / From the nearest airport / Best time / Entry / Good for).
- "faq": 3-5 real questions.
- title: "<Place name>, Sumba: <short promise>" e.g. "Weekuri Lagoon, Sumba: how to get there, when to go and what to know". Keep titles under 70 characters if possible.
- "image_candidate": a real Commons photo of THAT place (CC/PD, landscape, >=1280 px, no close-up people). If none exists, set "image_candidate": null.
- time: spread between "07:00" and "19:30", 15 minutes apart within your file.
- Prose that makes people want to go: one vivid paragraph first (light, colour, sound), then useful detail. No hype words like "paradise", "hidden gem", "breathtaking".

Master list of place slugs (English): weekuri-lagoon, mandorak-beach, bawana-beach, pero-beach, ratenggaro-village, wainyapu-village, praijing-village, tarung-village, lapopu-waterfall, matayangu-waterfall, waikelo-sawah-waterfall, wairinding-hill, walakiri-beach, tanggedu-waterfall, puru-kambera, tarimbang-beach, prailiu-village, rende-village, pau-village, laiwangi-wanggameti-national-park, manupeu-tanah-daru-national-park, watu-maladong-beach, kerewe-beach, marosi-beach, waimarang-waterfall, bukit-persaudaraan, kodi, waingapu-town, waikabubak-town, tambolaka-town, londa-lima-beach, purukambera-savanna (skip if same as puru-kambera), mbawana (same as bawana-beach: do not duplicate).
