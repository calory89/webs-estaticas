Why this is not a course

On ecobeachcity.com there is a list of projects open to partners. One of them is called, in those words, venture builder and founder residency. It says what already exists and what is missing. What is missing is the person who designs it and gets it running.

So we are not going to sit and talk about how a venture builder works. We are going to design mine, with you inside it, and you leave Seville having decided things that then get built.

Where this is going

The plan is to launch 100 projects a year out of Eco Beach City. That is not a figure of speech. A town of 762 owners from 40 countries needs almost everything, and every one of those needs is a company nobody has built yet.

The restaurant on the beach. The bookings engine. The island logistics. The film studio. The AI lab. The design workshop with the ikat weavers. Five of those are already published on the site, waiting for someone to run them.

Your percentage is not of one of them. It is of the vehicle they all come out of.
