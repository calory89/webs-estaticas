Eco Beach City: a town by the sea on Sumba

A town on the north coast of Sumba, one hour from Bali, with coworking, padel, solar power and homes by the sea.

FLIGHTCO 023SINCE2023DESTSUMBA, INDONESIA
Visit Eco Beach City ↗Let's talk

Eco Beach City is the project I am building now. We are building a real place on the beach in Sumba, an island one hour by plane from Bali, for people who want to live and work by the sea.

The town is designed around community: a coworking space, sports like padel, solar power and homes facing the ocean. I tell the full story, and what it takes to build a town remotely, in my talk Building a town from an island.
