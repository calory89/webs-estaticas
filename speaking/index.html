Bio for organisers

Copy it as it is for your programme.

Carlos de Ory started his first business at 16 and has been building companies for more than 20 years. He began in travel, founding Happy Low Cost, and went on to live and build with other founders in New York, Berlin, Miami and Bali. In 2022 Forbes España included him in its list of the 100 most creative people in business. Today he lives between Europe and Sumba, an island one hour from Bali, where he is building Eco Beach City. He is writing a book about building companies and a life you choose.
